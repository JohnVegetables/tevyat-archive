## summary
### LITE
The Acer Palmatum Chapter is Kaedehara Kazuha's Story Quest, released in Version 2.8, and has one act, A Strange and Friendless Road. After Kazuha blocked the Raiden Shogun's attack in Omnipresence Over Mortals he is famous in Inazuma, and a stranger starts attacking people who fight like him. The Traveler helps him trace the stranger, a samurai possessed by a cursed sword, and settle the matter without a killing.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Upon the Plain, a Purple Shadow; Through the Mist, a Name Unveiled; and Ere the End, a Glance Back. It is a quiet investigation, not an Archon-scale event. It also answers whether Kazuha will stay in Inazuma now that his clan could be cleared: he chooses to keep wandering.
### LARGE +
Its themes are reputation and inheritance: the Kaedehara name, an Isshin blade's pride in its smith's art, and the Shogun's past decrees still shaping ordinary lives. Kazuha handles the threat by testing it and offering it a way out, not by simply destroying it.

## plot
### LITE
On Byakko Plain a samurai attacks the Traveler and Paimon, then realises they are not Kazuha and leaves. They go to warn Kazuha at the Tenryou Commission Headquarters, where Kujou Kamaji has offered to restore Kazuha's clan's name. Kazuha and the Traveler trace the attacker to Amenoma Yuuya, possessed by a sword full of Tatarigami, and Kazuha beats the sword and persuades it to be reborn as a reference for the Isshin Art.
### STANDARD
Fended off on Byakko Plain, the samurai is puzzled to find he has attacked the wrong people. The Traveler notices his style resembles Kazuha's, and since the Crux is docked at Ritou they go there; Chao tells them Kazuha was called to the Tenryou Commission Headquarters. Kamaji explains that Kazuha's family was wrongly blamed in the past and the Shogun wants to restore its name and wealth. Kazuha has become the talk of the town for withstanding the Shogun's Musou no Hitotachi, which many Inazumans thought impossible. Witnesses saw him use his dead friend's Electro Vision, so many believed he used a Delusion instead. Kazuha is tempted by the offer but reluctant to leave his current life. When he hears about the mysterious samurai he investigates with the Traveler while Kamaji alerts the Tenryou Commission.

At the Bantan Sango Detective Agency, Ryuuji says two people vanished a few days ago: Nagato, a collector, and Amenoma Yuuya, a samurai of the Amenoma Clan. At the Amenoma Smithy, Yuuya's uncle Tougo says his nephew acted out of character and left saying he would bring back a great gift; he had gone to meet Nagato, and a fire broke out at the warehouse. Nagato's wife Sachiko says her husband's collecting obsession put the family in debt. At the collapsed warehouse Kazuha senses leftover Tatarigami, and a trail past some hilichurls leads to a letter showing Yuuya had attacked Nagato for his sword. The trail ends on a small island off Inazuma City, where Yuuya is found possessed by the sword.
### LARGE +
The sword says it was the prized blade of an Isshin smith who failed the Shogun's tasks and exiled himself to Snezhnaya. After the smith died, it began possessing people so it could return to Inazuma and prove to the Shogun that it was the ultimate weapon she had carelessly lost. It wants to possess Kazuha because he survived the Musou no Hitotachi. Kazuha defeats it, argues that it has neglected itself and cannot beat the Shogun, and when it threatens Yuuya's life he proposes a wager. It will possess Kazuha and fight the ronin at the Takatsukasa Clan's former secret hideout, and if it proves weak it must release Yuuya. The sword agrees.

In the hideout the sword, unmaintained for years, is hard to use and tries to imitate Kazuha instead of controlling him. After a Kairagi falls, the Tatarigami drains away and Paimon calls the blade junk. Murayama and two Nobushi arrive and are cleared easily. The sword admits its mistake, apologises and releases Yuuya, who asks for a formal duel with Kazuha and loses. Back at the smithy Tougo is relieved to have his nephew home and sorry for the sword's long life; Nagato was found badly hurt but alive. The sword chooses to give up its consciousness and be reborn as the Kagotsurube Isshin, its original name, to serve as reference material for the Isshin Art. Kazuha promises to support Tougo's effort.

Kamaji confirms the matter is settled and the Tenryou Commission will not charge Yuuya. Kazuha introduces the new sword and says he will study the Isshin Art, then declines Kamaji's offer to stay in Inazuma because he does not want to be drawn into the conflict between the Shogunate and the Sangonomiya Clan. Kamaji had not meant it that way and offers the Shogunate's help should Kazuha ever need it. Kazuha says he will go back to the Crux and wherever the wind takes him.
### ULTRA +
The sword is a victim of its own pride as much as a villain: its grievance is that its maker failed the Shogun and fled, and its remedy is to prove the Isshin Art supreme. The Tenryou Commission's decision not to charge Yuuya treats the incident as a possession, not a crime.

## revelations
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
- The Shogunate wants to restore the Kaedehara family's name and wealth, according to Kujou Kamaji.
- Kazuha's survival of the Musou no Hitotachi is known across Inazuma; witnesses saw his friend's Electro Vision, so many believe he used a Delusion.
- The sword that possessed Yuuya was an Isshin smith's prized blade, taken to Snezhnaya by its exiled maker.
- Kazuha prefers to stay out of the Shogunate versus Sangonomiya conflict.
### LARGE +
- The sword is reborn as the Kagotsurube Isshin.
- The Takatsukasa Clan's former hideout is now occupied by ronin.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Kazuha**: the wandering Crux crew member and the chapter's protagonist.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: warn Kazuha and help find the attacker.
- **Kujou Kamaji**: Tenryou Commission figure who offers to restore Kazuha's clan.
- **Amenoma Yuuya and Tougo**: samurai and his uncle at the Amenoma Smithy.
- **Ryuuji, Nagato, Nagato Sachiko**: the detective agency member, the collector and his wife.
- **Chao, Murayama**: a Crux crew member and a ronin leader.
- **The sword**: a possessing Tatarigami-filled blade, later the Kagotsurube Isshin.
### LARGE +
The Raiden Shogun appears only by reference; the sword's grudge is aimed at her.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Yuuya is freed and not charged. The sword gives up its consciousness. Kazuha turns down Inazuma's offer, keeps Kamaji's promise of help, and returns to the Crux.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.8. It requires Omnipresence Over Mortals and Reflections of Mortality, so it takes place after the Raiden Shogun's fight with the Traveler. Story order and release order match. The chapter has no later acts as of 7.1.

## entry: Kagotsurube Isshin
@keys: Kagotsurube Isshin, cursed sword, possessed sword
@keys2: Kazuha, Yuuya, Isshin Art
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
An Isshin blade whose maker fled the Shogun's tasks and died in Snezhnaya. Full of Tatarigami, it possessed Amenoma Yuuya to prove it was the ultimate weapon. Kazuha beat it, then wagered it could not win a real fight. Once the Tatarigami faded the sword apologised, released Yuuya and chose to be reborn as the Kagotsurube Isshin, its original name, as reference material for reviving the Isshin Art.

## entry: Amenoma Yuuya
@keys: Amenoma Yuuya, Yuuya
@keys2: Kazuha, sword, Amenoma
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
A samurai of the Amenoma Clan who vanished after saying he would bring back a great gift. He took Nagato's sword by force, was possessed by it, and attacked people resembling Kazuha. After release he asks for a formal duel with Kazuha and loses, and the Tenryou Commission does not charge him.
