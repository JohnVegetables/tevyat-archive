## summary
### LITE
The Vulpes Zerda Chapter is Tighnari's Story Quest, released in Version 3.0, and as of 7.1 it has one act, The Unanswerable Problems. A strange contamination spreading through Avidya Forest sends Tighnari and the Traveler deep into the woods, led by Karkata, a mechanical crab that steals machine parts. They find that it was made by a researcher Tighnari knew at the Akademiya, who died in his laboratory.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: The Start of Contamination, Into the Hinterland, Heart of the Machine and Garden Memories. It is a quiet investigation story. The culprit is not a villain but a runaway experiment and an unfinished life, and the emotional weight lies in Tighnari's decision about what to do with the dead man's work.
### LARGE +
The contamination is unlike a Withering Zone: it is caused by Ley Line energy being drawn off by extractors. The quest also shows Tighnari's habits as a forest ranger and scholar, and his wary relationship with the Akademiya's rules.

## plot
### LITE
The Adventurers' Guild reports a contaminated area in Avidya Forest. Tighnari is treating a sick boy and needs medicinal grass that merchants can no longer sell, because a crab machine stole their parts. Tighnari and the Traveler trap and follow the crab into the forest, find extractors draining Ley Line energy, and in a laboratory discover that the crab, Karkata, was built by the late researcher Abattouy. Tighnari later reveals he buried Abattouy and spared Karkata.
### STANDARD
In Sumeru City, Katheryne tells the Traveler and Paimon of a commission about contamination in Avidya Forest. They suspect a Withering Zone, but Katheryne says it looks like something else. They go to Gandharva Ville, where Tighnari is treating a boy named Yara. Tighnari wants Vasanti Grass for the cure, but the merchant Mahir has closed up shop: the merchants pooled money for machine parts that a crab-like machine stole and carried deep into the forest, and they dared not follow because of rumours of the contamination. The group lures the thief out with spare parts and drives it off; Tighnari sees that it cannot follow complex questions and is interested only in mechanical parts. He promises it more parts if it shows him its hiding place, sets traps, and recovers the stolen goods. With Collei's help he prepares Yara's medicine.

Tighnari and the Traveler return to the crab, which has not tried to escape, and it leads them on in return for more parts. They pass a glowing Sumeru Rose, which Tighnari explains is called the Ley Line Lodestar and glows unnaturally when nearby Ley Lines are dangerous. They notice no animals; Tighnari believes the animals have fled the disrupted Ley Lines. A purification device he made works and brings animals back. The crab darts away to a group of Ruin Machines, the pair defeat them and find Jasar, a herb-picker who wandered in and stayed too long in the contaminated area; Tighnari sends him to Gandharva Ville. At camp the two take turns on watch, the crab serves coffee, and Tighnari explains why the Akademiya banned research into mechanical life forms.

### LARGE +
At the source, a large nest of Ruin Machines surrounds extractors that are causing the contamination. The Traveler feels unwell but insists on helping. The crab leads them to a laboratory where a long-dead man lies. Tapes reveal the crab's name, Karkata, and the researcher's, Abattouy, whom Tighnari knew during his time at the Akademiya. Abattouy tried to make Karkata a truly living thing and had programmed it to make coffee; running short of energy and Mora, he built the Ley Line extractors as a power source. A later tape says he gave Karkata instincts and switched on the last extractor, but he overcharged it and succumbed to the energy before he could shut it off. They find the controls and shut the extractors down. Karkata tries to repair Abattouy without success and then shuts down from overdue charging and maintenance. Tighnari tells the Traveler to inform the Guild and says Akademiya protocol would have the laboratory handed over and Karkata disassembled and stored.

Back in Sumeru City the Traveler tells Katheryne the matter is solved and is rewarded. At Pardis Dhyai they find Tighnari with Karkata, repaired. He admits he did not follow Akademiya protocol: he was no longer a member and knew the Akademiya must not get Abattouy's research, so he took only a few things, buried Abattouy, and destroyed the rest and the laboratory. He chose Pardis Dhyai as a secluded spot and says he may take Karkata on as a research assistant.

Tighnari reasons throughout from observation: the missing animals, the rose's glow and the number of Ruin Machines all feed his conclusion that something is draining the Ley Lines. His purification device is his own invention and is tested in the field.
### ULTRA +
Small details: Yara's illness is why the story begins; Mahir and the other merchants had bought the parts together; and the crab is willing to be bribed with parts, which is what keeps it cooperative. The act's title points at a question it never answers outright: whether a made thing can be alive, and who has the right to decide.

## revelations
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
- The contamination comes from Ley Line extractors, not a Withering Zone.
- Karkata was built by Abattouy, a former Akademiya researcher whom Tighnari knew.
### LARGE +
- Abattouy tried to give Karkata life and instincts, and died of overexposure to the Ley Line energy.
- Tighnari does not follow Akademiya protocol and destroys the research.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Tighnari**: forest ranger and former Akademiya scholar, who leads the investigation.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: companions on the journey.
- **Katheryne**: Adventurers' Guild receptionist who gives the commission.
- **Karkata**: the mechanical crab that steals parts.
- **Abattouy**: the dead researcher, known only through recordings.
- **Collei**: Tighnari's apprentice, who helps make Yara's cure.
- **Yara, Mahir, Jasar**: a sick boy, a merchant and a herb-picker caught up in events.
### LARGE +
Other merchants of Gandharva Ville appear when they tell of the theft.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The extractors are shut down and the contamination ends. Karkata survives under Tighnari's care at Pardis Dhyai, and Abattouy's laboratory and most of his research are destroyed.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.0 and unlocked after Chapter III Act II of the Archon Quest and Adventure Rank 40. Story order and release order match. Only Act I exists as of 7.1.
