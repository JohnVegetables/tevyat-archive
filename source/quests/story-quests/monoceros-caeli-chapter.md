## summary
### LITE
Monoceros Caeli Chapter is Tartaglia's Story Quest, with one act, Mighty Cyclops' Adventure! Teucer, Childe's young brother, arrives in Liyue believing Childe is a toy seller. The Traveler and Paimon help Childe protect that story while touring Liyue, and Childe uses his Foul Legacy Transformation to guard Teucer in a Ruin Guard facility.
### STANDARD +
It requires A New Star Approaches, Bookworm Swordsman (Fabulae Textile Chapter) and Adventure Rank 40. It is a family comedy that shows a side of this Fatui Harbinger that his missions do not.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
At Lingju Pass the Traveler and Paimon help the Millelith with Ruin Guards and meet a boy, Teucer, from Snezhnaya, looking for his brother, who he thinks is a toy seller. The brother is Childe, who asks them to look after Teucer while he works, and to help him hide his Fatui identity. After a tour, a staged Fatui orientation, and a visit to a supposed toy institute, Childe protects Teucer from Ruin Guards, hurts himself, and Teucer returns home pleased.
### STANDARD +
The institute is an abandoned Ruin Guard facility left by Il Dottore, which Childe clears with the Traveler while keeping Teucer away. He uses the Foul Legacy Transformation to destroy the Ruin Guards and the machine producing them, though he had not recovered from fighting the Traveler in the Golden House. He gives Teucer a Mr. Cyclops Action Figure through the Traveler and hides to recover.
### LARGE +
**Across the Sea.** At Lingju Pass the soldier Daipai reports Ruin Guards roaming; a single ruin is behind them. After fighting one, the Traveler finds Teucer talking to an inactive Ruin Guard. He offers a bag of Mora to find his toy-seller brother in Liyue, and the search points to Northland Bank, where Teucer recognises Childe. Childe tells the Traveler and Paimon privately that his toy-selling is a front to keep Teucer from learning of his Fatui work, and asks them to babysit him while he handles business at Qingxu Pool.

**Teucer's Terrific Tour.** After a tour, Teucer wants to see his brother, so they take him to Qingxu Pool, where Childe is negotiating with Treasure Hoarders and about to fight. Seeing Teucer, he improvises and claims he wants to join them; he fetches a troublesome treasure and then declines, leaving them in his debt. Felix then reports that Fatui recruits await orientation near The Chasm, since Childe is the only Harbinger in the area after La Signora left. Teucer follows him. Childe spars with the recruits and keeps up the cover story. Teucer is impressed and says he wants to learn to fight to protect his family; he agrees to return to Snezhnaya if Childe takes him to the Institute for Toy Research.

**Defender of Childhood Dreams.** The toy institute is a Ruin Guard facility abandoned by Dottore. Childe asks Teucer to count down from sixty while he and the Traveler clear it; the Ruin Guards seem endless, so he uses the Foul Legacy Transformation, injuring himself. The Traveler finds him hiding and he hands over a Mr. Cyclops Action Figure he had ordered for Teucer. Paimon suggests a doctor, and Childe leaves discreetly. Teucer, upset that Childe left, cheers up at the figure. Andrei meets them at the bank to say the boat has arrived. Teucer admits he knew of the Traveler from Childe's letters but forgot the name. Childe reappears, thanks the Traveler, offers payment and says he would work with them again.
### ULTRA +
The quest shows Childe's habit of improvising covers and his willingness to risk injury for his brother. Teucer says he wants to learn to fight to protect his family. The Traveler is treated as a trusted but incidental partner, not a friend.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Childe pretends to Teucer to be a toy seller to hide his Fatui affiliation.
- The Ruin Guard facility posing as a toy institute was left by Il Dottore.
- Childe was still recovering from the Golden House fight when he used the Foul Legacy Transformation.

## characters
### LITE
Tartaglia (Childe), Teucer, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Also Daipai, Felix, Andrei and the Fatui recruits. Childe is portrayed as a doting older brother as well as a Harbinger.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Teucer returns to Snezhnaya with Andrei, still unaware of Childe's Fatui work. Childe is injured and recovers in secret. He and the Traveler part on cooperative terms.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.1. Requires A New Star Approaches, Bookworm Swordsman and Adventure Rank 40; it is set after the Liyue Archon Quest while Childe is lying low.
