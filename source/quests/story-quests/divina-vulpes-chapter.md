## summary
### LITE
The Divina Vulpes Chapter is Yae Miko's Story Quest, released in Version 2.5, with one act, The Great Narukami Offering. A new book, A Firsthand Guide to Summoning Spirits, spreads a wish ritual across Inazuma City and leaves some of its users acting strangely. Miko investigates with the Traveler, finds the cause is an old friend, the Daiyouko youkai Urakusai, and sees him and other spirits off with a final party.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Mysterious Urban Legend, Friendship in Writing, A Centuries-Long Dream and Banquet of Parting. It mixes a ghost-story investigation, a publishing-house rivalry in which Miko has the Traveler help write a rival novel, and a gentle farewell. Miko's wit and her affection for the mortal world are in the foreground, and Ei is only mentioned.
### LARGE +
The chapter follows the Sacred Sakura events of Transient Dreams: gnawing rifthounds stirred ancient memories along the tree's roots, and that is what woke Urakusai's spirit. It is the Story Quest where Miko is shown as she is among old friends and ordinary readers, not as the Guuji of the shrine.

## plot
### LITE
Rumour in Hanamizaka says a midnight incantation grants any wish. A book explaining it is selling out, and a swordsman who used it is possessed. Miko exorcises the possessing spirit, then tracks down the book's author, a failed novelist, and discovers the spirit behind it is her friend Urakusai. He meant no harm, and Miko promises him a final gathering before he and the other spirits leave.
### STANDARD
In Hanamizaka two men argue about a wish ritual rumoured to make people rich. At the Yae Publishing House the editors say a new book, A Firsthand Guide to Summoning Spirits, is a sensation though it did not come from their print house, and readers who follow it begin to act oddly. At the Grand Narukami Shrine, Miko is visited by Katou Youhei, whose brother Shingo was refused by the swordmaster Domon, then beat Domon's best disciple and has behaved strangely since. In Konda Village Miko makes Youhei admit that he and his brother had performed the ritual together. The spirit, not Shingo, holds the sword skill. Salt thrown by the Traveler drives it out; it challenges the Traveler, loses and fades away contentedly.

Miko shrugs off the book until Kuroda reports that it is dragging down the publishing house's shares. At the house she has read it, admires the writing, and decides to answer with a novel of their own. She sends the Traveler to a nearby convention for ideas and waits at the Uyuu Restaurant with her print-house team. The Traveler writes chapters from their journeys through Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma, Miko approves and submits it on their behalf, and after three days it is a success.
### LARGE +
Meanwhile Miko has found the book's author, Tomoyuki, a failed light novelist who had been possessed. Since the spirit sought him out, she reasons it can be called with the book's own method. Under the city she finds an underground chamber with a faded mirror and an unusual painting. Following her instructions, the Traveler thinks of wishes and is possessed. The spirit says it will bring ruin to Inazuma, then Miko and it laugh together: she recognises Urakusai, a powerful Daiyouko youkai and her friend.

Urakusai explains that when rifthounds gnawed the Sacred Sakura's roots, memories stirred along them; he heard Tomoyuki's pleas, possessed him and wrote the book so that other youkai spirits could enjoy a last measure of life and leave with no regrets, because he is fond of humanity and its aspirations. He concedes the contest and asks how Miko has been. She says she has always been content and that even Ei has changed. He asks for a "Hyakkiyakou" before they depart, and she promises one.

At the Grand Narukami Shrine Miko explains that the youkai would normally fly on their own but as spirits they need her help. She asks the Traveler to evacuate the area to avoid further possessions and to keep monsters off the Mt. Yougou road, since the concentrated youkai energy would rile them. They do so and perform the ritual, and people in Inazuma City follow the book's incantation without knowing. After the farewell Miko, asked if she is all right, asks for a meal. At the restaurant she is already inside eating a large feast, having played them. The invocations granted nobody's wishes, and she says she expects the next chapter of their novel soon.
### ULTRA +
Practical details: Takatoshi and Kiyoto start the rumour in Hanamizaka; Junkichi and Kuroda are the editors; Miko says she needs to see the Traveler's sincerity before helping with their own request, which is why she sends them to handle Shingo first.

## revelations
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
- The book was not a curse: a friendly youkai had it written so spirits could leave without regret.
- The wishes in the ritual were not granted; people were taken over by youkai spirits instead.
- The Sacred Sakura holds memories along its roots, which rifthounds disturbed.
### LARGE +
- Urakusai is a Daiyouko youkai and an old friend of Miko.
- Miko says Ei has changed.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Yae Miko**: the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine and owner of the Yae Publishing House.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: assistants, the Traveler also writing the rival novel.
- **Urakusai**: a Daiyouko youkai and Miko's friend.
- **Katou Youhei and Shingo**: brothers who used the ritual.
- **Kuroda, Junkichi**: editors at the publishing house.
- **Tomoyuki**: failed light novelist who wrote the book under possession.
### LARGE +
Domon appears only by reference. Ei is mentioned by Miko in passing.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Urakusai and other spirits depart after a final ritual. The craze fades because the wishes are not granted. The Traveler's novel is a hit, and Miko expects further chapters.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.5, together with Act II of the Raiden Shogun's chapter. It requires Transient Dreams as well as Omnipresence Over Mortals, and takes place after it. Story order and release order match. No later acts exist as of 7.1.

## entry: Urakusai
@keys: Urakusai
@keys2: Miko, Hyakkiyakou, summoning
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
A powerful Daiyouko youkai and friend of Yae Miko. His memories stirred when rifthounds gnawed the Sacred Sakura. He possessed the novelist Tomoyuki to write A Firsthand Guide to Summoning Spirits so other youkai could enjoy life and leave without regret. He yields to Miko and asks for a Hyakkiyakou before departing.

## entry: A Firsthand Guide to Summoning Spirits
@keys: A Firsthand Guide to Summoning Spirits, wish ritual
@keys2: Miko, Tomoyuki, Yae Publishing House
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
A best-selling book whose incantation was said to grant wishes. In truth it lets youkai spirits possess the reader. The book did not come out of the Yae Publishing House's own printing and cut into its shares, so Miko answered it with a rival novel.
