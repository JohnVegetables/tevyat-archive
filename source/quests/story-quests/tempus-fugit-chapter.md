## summary
### LITE
The Tempus Fugit Chapter is Lisa's Story Quest (Version 1.0), set in Mondstadt. Its single act, Troublesome Work, follows the Traveler as they help the librarian reclaim overdue books, then track down a stolen volume to the hideout of a Cryo Abyss Mage.
### STANDARD +
The act has two quests, Troublesome Work and Lost Book. It requires Adventure Rank 15 and the Prologue's Act I, The Outlander Who Caught the Wind. The Chinese chapter name means "hourglass"; "Tempus fugit" is a Latin phrase for time flying. The tone is a light, errand-based outing with an offhand dose of violence at the end.
### LARGE +
The chapter shows Lisa as a slightly bored librarian who is nonetheless sharp: she recognises the Abyss Order's work, breaks its seal and deals with its Mage without fuss. It also includes a shopping outing in which the Traveler picks Lisa a souvenir, a dish and a flower.
### ULTRA +
The chapter's structure is two quests: a day of errands that doubles as a gift-shopping outing, and a short dungeon-style trail to the Mage's hideout. The one real mystery, what 'the secret' in the book meant, is left unanswered.

## plot
### LITE
Lisa finds a number of books overdue and, to get a travel guide for the Traveler, goes to collect them. One book has gone missing, apparently stolen, and she tracks it to ruins where an Abyss Mage has taken it. Lisa electrocutes the Mage, and the book turns out to be an ordinary storybook.
### STANDARD
**Troublesome Work.** The Traveler and Paimon visit the Mondstadt library and find Lisa slightly bored. She suggests a travel guide, but it is checked out and overdue, as are several other books. She goes to visit the borrowers and asks the Traveler to come along if they want the guide. Visiting several places around the city, they reclaim the books and shop together; the Traveler chooses a souvenir, a dish and a flower as gifts for Lisa. The outing is cut short when Donna tells Lisa that the book she borrowed is missing and may have been stolen. Every library copy carries an elemental marker to prevent loss, so the group follows the thief's trail.

**Lost Book.** The trail ends at ruins where the thief lives. Lisa deduces it belongs to the Abyss Order and dispels the seal on the area. Inside is a Cryo Abyss Mage named Emery, who says the book holds "the secret" and refuses to return it, so Lisa electrocutes it. The book is an ordinary copy of the storybook The Pale Princess and the Six Pygmies. Lisa lets the Traveler take it with an indefinite due date and reminds them to register at the library.
### LARGE +
Back at the library Lisa has received a new shipment of travel guides, lets the Traveler take one and gives them some "souvenirs" for their help. She tells them to look forward to their next errand together.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Library books carry elemental markers that let the librarian trace them if stolen. Lisa can recognise and break an Abyss Order seal. An Abyss Mage was keen enough on one book to steal it, but the book proves ordinary.

## characters
### LITE
Lisa, the Traveler and Paimon; Donna, the book borrower who reports the loss; Emery, a Cryo Abyss Mage.
### STANDARD +
Lisa starts out slightly bored with her job, brightens on seeing the Traveler, and deals with the Abyss Mage decisively.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The book is recovered, the Mage defeated and the Traveler becomes an indefinite borrower. The chapter is self-contained and changes nothing in the main story.

## placement

Release order: 1.0. It can be played early, after the first Prologue act.
