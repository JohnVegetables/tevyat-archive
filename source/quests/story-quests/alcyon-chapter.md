## summary
### LITE
Linnea's Story Quest, set in Nod-Krai. The Traveler helps Linnea and her Jack Frost partner Lumi compete in the Spirit Envoy Championship, a tournament pairing people with animal companions. A rival, Celaeno, turns out to be Linnea's fae sister, and Linnea learns what kind of fae she is.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Up! Toward Homecoming Wings, made of three quests: The Championship Begins, Twice Upon a Gaze and Tread the Right Path. It was released in version 6.5 (Luna VI). The Traveler enters with Aguara, their former Tepetlisaur whelp companion, as the tournament partner.
### LARGE +
Beneath the tournament, the story is about origins: why Lumi differs from other Jack Frosts, why Linnea has wings on her head, and whether a foreseen future can be changed. It also uses cameo competitors from other regions for lighter moments.

## plot
### LITE
Katheryne mentions the Frostmoon Scions' Spirit Envoy Championship. The Traveler finds Linnea, who enters with Lumi. Over several rounds a mysterious fae, Celaeno, appears, Linnea faints and dreams, and she learns Celaeno is her sister. Celaeno explains their kind and Linnea's fate. In the semifinal Lumi grows huge and Linnea calms her. The Traveler wins the final.
### STANDARD +
The Championship Begins: at Nasha Town, Katheryne has no notable commissions but tells the Traveler and Paimon about the Spirit Envoy Championship, a Frostmoon Scions event meant to strengthen bonds between people and animals. It is normally closed to outsiders but this year has a large prize. The Traveler meets Linnea, an Adventurers' Guild advisor who lectures at Speranza, and her partner Lumi. Aguara asks to join. They sign up, meet Mualani and Itto with their own companions, and the draw begins.

Twice Upon a Gaze: over a meal at Speranza, Linnea tells the Traveler she thinks of it as home and reveals she is a changeling raised by humans, with wings on her head. She hopes to learn about Lumi's origins. During the tournament, Celaeno enters, her hat blows off to show she is fae, and Linnea faints after asking her questions. In a series of worsening dreams she sees Celaeno as her sister.

Tread the Right Path: confronted at the Flagship, Celaeno explains Augury Birds and the family history behind the swap. In the semifinal Lumi grows huge and is calmed by Linnea, who finds why Lumi differs. The Traveler wins the final and Celaeno bids farewell.
### LARGE +
The Championship Begins: Katheryne explains that the rules were changed after a past entrant brought an extremely rare and powerful partner, so now only common animal species and their variants are allowed. Linnea is introduced teaching a class about food at Speranza and is delighted by Paimon. She tells the Traveler that Lumi was bullied for being different, and that she has tried ever since to learn what makes Lumi special. Mualani, who knows of Linnea from a visit to Natlan, and Itto, who brought pooled funds from his gang, arrive with their companions Mamala and Crimson Staff. In the first draw the Traveler faces Itto, and Linnea faces Ilze and her Floating Hydro Fungus Iku-Turso. Itto also sends Ushi against Aguara, which gets Aguara disqualified for using two companions. Linnea meanwhile wins, watched by someone with evident contempt.

Twice Upon a Gaze: Jurva cooks for them and says Linnea used to be the boss at Speranza before she grew up and left. Linnea says her parents are still alive and simply left the area, and that they supported her despite her unusual features. She remembers little of Lumi's earlier life, and of her own only a lullaby she believes was meant to discourage her from seeking her past; when she once travelled to the tundra, every instinct told her to leave. She is amazed at Aguara and Lumi being evenly matched, and bothered that she cannot predict their moves. Later the Traveler and Aguara beat Haniyyah and the Sumpter Beast Fluffy the Flower King, then Mualani in the quarter-finals. A new competitor, Celaeno, defeats Jahoda and her Chic Badger with her Radiant Bladehorn Haemus, and Linnea is shocked that a Radiant Beast is so docile. When Celaeno's hat is blown off, Linnea tries to question her; Celaeno walks away, finding the moment painful. Linnea faints and is carried to Lauma, who says she just needs rest. After waking she loses to Celaeno in the next match and, in a sequence of dreams, sees Celaeno ask her to return to the tundra one last time to say goodbye to their parents, explaining that what she dreams is actually the future. Aguara has picked up her scent and leads the group onward.

Tread the Right Path: at the Flagship, Linnea demands that Celaeno open the door. Celaeno warns that curiosity will lead to regret and tells her that Augury Birds are a rare kind of fae who foresee their own futures; fewer than five exist, counting both sisters. The Traveler recalls Vedrfolnir's words about an unchangeable fate. Their parents had Linnea live among humans, swapping her with a human child, so her abilities might be delayed. That child had a rare disease that only the fae could cure; her birth parents later came for her and they moved to Snezhnograd. Celaeno admits she first resented Linnea and had formed a bitter image of her, but changed her mind on seeing how much Linnea suffered not knowing. She also says she took in Haemus after it was separated from its parents. Linnea accepts her. In the semifinal the Traveler faces Viliana and her Snow Fox Gorynishche. Lumi grows huge while fighting Haemus, deflecting blades toward spectators whom the Traveler protects, until Linnea tugs her hat and shrinks her. Linnea realises Lumi differs because she consumes Geo, and hopes to find a Jack Frost with the same tastes. Celaeno sees her vision go blank at the moment Lumi's hammer falls and asks if Linnea defied fate. Linnea says a future being set does not mean she has to accept it. Before the final, the Little One, the Traveler's Saurian former companion, is seen watching from a cliff and has helped Aguara train, bringing Grainfruit. The Traveler wins and the quarter-finalists pose for photos; Celaeno wants to release Haemus into the wild. She promises to tell their parents how Linnea is doing and says her abilities will develop in time.
### ULTRA +
The cameo layer: Mualani finds Itto's group familiar until he mentions Kuki Shinobu, who had visited Natlan and the Flower-Feather Clan for their trials, where Chasca called her a natural. Itto serves for a time as a bouncer at the Flagship, scaring off a patron who demanded compensation from Paavo. Between rounds the Traveler, Linnea and the animals practise together, and the Traveler tells Linnea how they met Aguara; Linnea wishes to travel with Lumi one day, but not before she learns Lumi's past. Paimon suggests giving Aguara a title before the quarter-final. At the end, each finalist says something: the Traveler credits hard work and friendship, Celaeno wants to free Haemus, Linnea accepts whatever fate holds, and Viliana says she enjoyed her time in Nod-Krai. Celaeno tells Linnea to write if she wants to see her, and says she will give an address when she next comes to the tundra.

## revelations
### LITE
Linnea is an Augury Bird, a rare fae who can foresee their own futures; she has an older sister, Celaeno.
### STANDARD +
Linnea was swapped with a human baby by her fae parents so her abilities would be delayed. Lumi differs from other Jack Frosts because she consumes Geo. All of this was revealed in 6.5.
### LARGE +
Celaeno says fewer than five Augury Birds exist, counting herself and Linnea. The human child exchanged for Linnea had a rare disease that only the fae could cure, and was later reclaimed by her birth parents and taken to Snezhnograd. The Traveler's own question about fate echoes Vedrfolnir's earlier words.
### ULTRA +
Celaeno's vision of the semifinal ended where Lumi's hammer would have landed, which she takes as a possible sign that fate was defied; the quest does not state so for certain. The ending says Linnea's abilities are expected to develop fully later.

## characters
### LITE
Linnea, the Traveler, Paimon, Lumi, Aguara and Celaeno.
### STANDARD +
Katheryne, Lauma, Jahoda, Jurva and Paavo (Speranza and the Flagship), Mualani and Itto (with Mamala and Crimson Staff), Ilze, Haniyyah, Viliana, Haemus and the Little One.
### LARGE +
Kuki Shinobu and Chasca appear only in conversation. Vedrfolnir is referenced by the Traveler in discussing fate.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Linnea learns her origin and reconciles with Celaeno, who promises to pass news to their parents and to give an address. Lumi's difference is explained.
### LARGE +
The chapter leaves open what Linnea's coming abilities will mean and whether her fate can be changed. According to the wiki, Celaeno afterward appears as an open-world NPC at Snezhnograd (flagged there as needing verification).

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after An Elegy for Faded Moonlight (Song of the Welkin Moon Act IV). The wiki says fully finishing the chapter also needs A Nocturne of the Far North (Act V). The event page recommends In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons first. During the Luna VI event, players at Adventure Rank 28 who have finished A New Star Approaches could use a Quick Start. Released in Luna VI, 6.5, on 2026-04-08.

## entry: Spirit Envoy Championship
@keys: Spirit Envoy Championship
@keys2: Frostmoon Scions, Nasha Town
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A tournament held by the Frostmoon Scions to improve human-animal bonds through training, with animals meant to cope with hardship and disasters. Traditionally closed to outsiders, it is open this time with a hefty prize. Entrants pair up with one companion animal; only common species and their variants are allowed, since a rare, powerful partner once entered. Using two companions gets a contestant disqualified.
### LARGE +
Rounds shown include draws the day after sign-up, quarter-finals, semifinals and a final. Competitors include Itto, Mualani, Ilze, Haniyyah, Jahoda, Viliana, Celaeno, Linnea and the Traveler.

## entry: Augury Birds
@keys: Augury Bird, Augury Birds
@keys2: Linnea, Celaeno
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Per Celaeno, Augury Birds are a rare kind of Snowland Fae who foresee their own futures, and fewer than five exist. Celaeno says the foreseen future cannot be changed. Their parents had Linnea raised among humans to delay her powers.

## entry: Celaeno
@keys: Celaeno, Celeano
@keys2: Linnea, Haemus
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Linnea's older sister, an Augury Bird from the Snezhnaya tundra. She competes with the Radiant Bladehorn Haemus and wears a hat that hides she is fae. She came to Nod-Krai resenting Linnea, but could not go through with it after seeing Linnea's distress.

## entry: Lumi's difference
@keys: Lumi, Jack Frost Lumi
@keys2: Linnea, Geo
@version: 6.5

### STANDARD
Lumi is a Jack Frost fae from the northern tundra who was bullied for being different. In the semifinal Linnea understands that Lumi's difference is that she consumes Geo, and she hopes to find a Jack Frost with the same taste.
