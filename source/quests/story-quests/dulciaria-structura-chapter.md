## summary
### LITE
Escoffier's Story Quest, set at Hotel Debord in the Court of Fontaine. Navia and the Traveler find the hotel's chefs in trouble: the current head chef, Savanna, drives them hard, and a chef has been injured. They ask Escoffier, the former head chef, for help. A cook-off over the legendary Gateau Debord follows, then a sabotage plot and a trip to a ruin where the cake's secret is buried.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Treasured Above All, of three quests: Shaped by Fame, Roasted by Flames and Ice-Frosted Endgame. It was released in version 5.6 as an event and is unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Masquerade of the Guilty. It shows Escoffier's work ethic, her fondness for the restaurant she left, and her view of food.
### LARGE +
The story is about ambition, inheritance and credit. Escoffier, Savanna and the hotel's founders each struggle with the legacy of one cake, and the plot turns on a staff member who sabotages the cook-off.

## plot
### LITE
Escoffier returns for a cook-off against Savanna for the Codex Debord, which holds the founder's recipes. Escoffier loses after someone sabotages her cake. Savanna goes missing the next day and the party follows a map to a ruin where Monica, the real culprit, has her. At the ruin Monica wants the cake's secret ingredient, but it is gone, and Savanna and Escoffier hold a fair cook-off afterwards.
### STANDARD +
Shaped by Fame: Navia introduces the Traveler and Paimon to Savanna's overbearing management and to Firmin, whose injuries stopped his work. A candy wrapper from Monica says to find Escoffier in Fleuve Cendre. Navia explains that Escoffier is the former head chef who took the blame for an illegal substance, lumiline, in the food and spent a short time in the Fortress of Meropide. When she returned Savanna had taken over. Escoffier agrees to a cook-off proposed by the hotel's boss Vaneigem, with the Codex Debord as the stake. She tests the Traveler's cooking, scores Paimon's and Navia's dishes harshly and the Traveler's at 8 out of 10, and, needing capable assistants, takes the Traveler on.

Roasted by Flames: in a double-blind cook-off, Savanna wins. Savanna reads the codex and faints on finding no secrets in it. Escoffier tastes the cake and finds it was sabotaged during preparation; Monica apologises and claims that Savanna blackmailed her into making Escoffier lose; this later proves to be a cover for her own plan.

Ice-Frosted Endgame: Savanna is missing and has received threatening letters. A map found at her home matches the sites Modeste and Savelli travelled. The party follows it to a ruin where Monica has a knife at Savanna's neck. Monica wants the cake's secret ingredient, but the container is empty. Escoffier, Savanna and the Traveler subdue her and escape, and a final fair cook-off is held.
### LARGE +
Shaped by Fame: Navia says business tripled at Fleuve Cendre once Escoffier joined it as a temporary chef. Navia explains the lumiline was a drug made from Lumitoile that heightened the senses but caused mood swings and nerve damage, and was banned quickly. The codex was handed to Escoffier as thanks for her service and only head chefs, current and former, may hold it. Escoffier says the founders Modeste and Savelli explored the world and made use of what they found, which shaped Fontainian cuisine, and that the Gateau Debord is rare because of one ingredient found on such an adventure.

Roasted by Flames: the judges unanimously agree that one cake is far below their standard. The other chefs explain that Savanna pushed herself even harder than them, working through the night. Savanna realises she is the problem and breaks down.

Ice-Frosted Endgame: Escoffier tells Savanna she will give her the scolding of her life. The ruin is a Remurian public bathhouse. Two Blubberbeasts guard the way, and Modeste's notes say they must be fed a good meal. Savanna saw through Monica's plan to poison the party's drinking water. She sets off explosives to cover her retreat. At the camp, journals show the ingredient came from the ruin.
### ULTRA +
The diaries: Savelli set up camp at the ruin and wrote that the ingredient cost Modeste his sense of taste. Modeste then died by his own hand, and sent Savelli a last letter. Savelli realised the truth and framed himself as the culprit to protect Modeste's legacy, erasing evidence. He emptied the jar so it could not harm later people. Monica, who believed Savelli had destroyed Modeste's reputation, breaks the jar in anger, which awakens two Praetorian Golems that attack her. She threatens Escoffier at gunpoint before Savanna knocks her out; the party leaves her behind.

Aftermath: Firmin forgives Savanna, saying the letters showed it was Monica who had laced his tea with lumiline. Savanna asks why her cake was not like Escoffier's and is told Monica sabotaged the ovens. Escoffier says she spent nearly three years creating her version of the cake. She insists on a fresh cook-off, and Savanna loses it. Escoffier says she left the hotel over the food-safety incident and because she felt the restaurant could no longer support her; she met a Liyue chef as adventurous as the founders, and used Romaritime Flowers on her cake. She says she believes the secret ingredient was lumiline, hyped only because sugar was less common then. A jar expert tells Navia the broken Remurian jar would have been priceless.

## revelations
### LITE
Monica sabotaged the cake and plotted against Savanna. The founders' secret ingredient was found in a Remurian ruin.
### STANDARD +
Modeste lost his sense of taste to the ingredient and died. Savelli framed himself as the culprit to protect Modeste's legacy.
### LARGE +
Monica's claim that Savanna blackmailed her was a cover. Savanna's harsh management came partly from pushing herself even harder than her staff.
### ULTRA +
Escoffier's view is that the cake's famed ingredient was only lumiline, with its fame owed to scarce sugar.

## characters
### LITE
Escoffier, Navia, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Savanna, the current head chef, and Monica, her subordinate who turns out to be the saboteur. Firmin, an injured chef. Vaneigem, the hotel's boss, and the customer Renaud.
### LARGE +
Modeste and Savelli, the hotel's founders, appear through diaries and letters. Leone and Hilaire are other chefs.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Monica is left at the ruin. The staff back Savanna as head chef and Escoffier reassures her that she has what it takes. Escoffier does not return as head chef but promises to visit the hotel from time to time to ensure its standards stay high.
### LARGE +
Escoffier gives the Traveler a revised copy of her recipe. A broken relic is lost.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Chapter IV Act V, Masquerade of the Guilty. Released in 5.6 as the event Treasured Above All.

## entry: Gateau Debord
@keys: Gateau Debord
@keys2: Escoffier, Hotel Debord
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The legendary cake of Hotel Debord, made to a recipe of its founder Modeste. Escoffier recreated it and restored the hotel's fame. It is made by three people. The secret ingredient is the story's central question, as Escoffier thinks it was lumiline while others thought it came from a ruin.

## entry: Codex Debord
@keys: Codex Debord
@keys2: Escoffier, Savanna
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A book of the founding chefs' recipes and notes, held by current and former head chefs of the hotel. The original was given to Escoffier in thanks for her service. Savanna staked the right to it in the cook-off.
