## summary
### LITE
The Trulla Chapter is Xiangling's Story Quest (Version 1.0). Although Xiangling is from Liyue, the act, Mondstadt Gastronomy Trip, takes place mostly in and around Springvale, Mondstadt. The Traveler helps the travelling chef gather ingredients for a cook-off, and they revive a hunter frozen for centuries.
### STANDARD +
The act has six quests: Little Chef on a Hunt, Cooking Appointment, Secret Ingredient, Lucky Find, Present From the Past and Cooking Showdown. It requires Adventure Rank 12 and the Prologue's The Outlander Who Caught the Wind. The tone is light, with food as the central theme and Xiangling's refusal to be limited to conventional ingredients as the running joke.
### LARGE +
Beneath the comedy the act sets out Xiangling's values: she cares about customers' happiness, not Mora, and treats any ingredient as potential food. The frozen hunter Olaf gives the chapter an unusual emotional beat about a lost era and a forgotten taste.
### ULTRA +
The act mixes small-town rivalry (Springvale's hunters and cooks against Xiangling's improvisation) with a Liyue-to-Mondstadt round trip for ingredients, and the discovery in a cave of a hunter and a boar species frozen in the same incident.

## plot
### LITE
In Springvale's forest, Xiangling tries to hunt wild boar and the Traveler helps. After an argument with the village chief over Slime Condensate, she challenges the cook Brook to a cook-off, collects ingredients in Liyue and a Cryo Regisvine's cave, finds a frozen hunter named Olaf and an extinct boar, and wins.
### STANDARD
**Little Chef on a Hunt.** In Springvale's forest the Traveler and Paimon meet Xiangling, who is drooling over wild boars she cannot catch. The Traveler hunts them and she cooks a snack she invented on the spot. Allan, a hunter, arrives and scolds them for hunting in the boars' own habitat, which hurts the hunters' trade. Xiangling apologises and goes with Allan to see the chief, Draff.

**Cooking Appointment.** Draff receives Xiangling warmly because of her reputation as a chef in Mondstadt and Liyue, and hopes her dishes will promote Springvale's meat. Xiangling will not tie herself to specific ingredients, and storms off when Draff reacts badly to her idea of using Slime Condensate. Drawn by the smell of Brook's cooking, she argues with Brook over adding the pungent Qingxin flower, which Brook calls overkill, and declares a cook-off. If Xiangling wins, Brook must stock her dish; if Brook wins, Xiangling must promote Brook's dish at Wanmin Restaurant. Xiangling leaves for Liyue to gather ingredients.

**Secret Ingredient.** The Traveler and Paimon catch up with her in Liyue and help gather Lotus Heads and Jueyun Chilis. She is still unsatisfied until she recalls a place she has not visited. At a cave entrance they find a shivering man, and Xiangling has the Traveler make Radish Veggie Soup. Warmed, he says he was tracking a boar in the ruins when a Cryo Regisvine froze him. Xiangling realises the Regisvine is what her dish needs and charges in; the man asks the Traveler to protect her.
### LARGE +
**Present From the Past.** In the ruins they defeat the Cryo Regisvine. Xiangling finds a boar with special markings, identified as a species hunted to extinction for its prized meat, and the Traveler suggests it was cryopreserved for at least 300 years. Back in Springvale, the man is bewildered that none of his friends or family are there; the Traveler concludes he was frozen along with the boar. Draff asks his name, Olaf, recognises it and realises Olaf is his ancestor.

**Cooking Showdown.** Brook brings in Siegfria as an assistant, so Xiangling has the Traveler and Paimon help: they fetch a wild radish and wash it in the lake, clear nearby slimes, and cook Jade Parcels as a side for the extinct boar. After tasting both meals Draff declares Xiangling the winner. Brook suggests the extinct boar made the difference, which alarms Draff given its value. Xiangling replies that Mora does not interest her, only her customers' happiness. Olaf mourns that he cannot taste the food of his time, and Xiangling promises to recreate the flavour with modern ingredients. She thanks the Traveler, says she will return to Liyue (with detours), and promises a meal and lessons on her unorthodox recipes at Wanmin Restaurant.

## revelations
### STANDARD
A hunter named Olaf and a boar of a species hunted to extinction were preserved by a Cryo Regisvine in a cave. Draff is Olaf's descendant. The Traveler infers the preservation lasted at least 300 years.

## characters
### LITE
Xiangling, the Traveler and Paimon; Draff, chief of Springvale; Allan, Brook, Siegfria and Olaf.
### STANDARD +
Xiangling is enthusiastic, hungry for new ingredients and stubborn about her methods, but she accepts correction when she has made a mistake, as with the hunting. Draff is hospitable but traditional about local produce. Brook is a rival cook with firm views on balance.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Xiangling wins the cook-off and leaves for Liyue, with a standing invitation for the Traveler to eat and learn at Wanmin Restaurant. Olaf is reunited with a descendant in Draff, and learns he can still taste something like his era's cooking.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: 1.0, with the other launch Story Quests. Story order: after the Prologue's The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, with an Adventure Rank 12 requirement.
