## summary
### LITE
Chiori's Story Quest, set in Fontaine ahead of Fontaine Fashion Week. A rival designer, Uther, tries to ruin Chiori's boutique through a hired attack on her courier, press smears and withdrawn suppliers. With the Traveler and friends from across Fontaine, Chiori exposes his factory and still stages her show.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, When They Talk About Tonight, released in Version 4.5, with three quests: Where Honed Blades Clash, Stained Spots and The Woven and the Cut. It is a lighter story about work, reputation and loyalty.
### LARGE +
The act shows Chiori as a proud, practical businesswoman who is also supported by many people, including Kirara, Navia, Chevreuse, Charlotte and Lyney and Lynette, whom she has helped with their outfits and a show.

## plot
### LITE
Kirara arrives late with Chiori's fabrics from Inazuma, having been attacked by bandits hired to target a courier with two tails. Uther, a rival designer, is behind it. Rumours that Chiori uses underground suppliers cost her customers, models and her accessory maker. She salvages stock into a new collection, then helps expose Uther's unsanitary factory and takes the fashion show.
### STANDARD +
Where Honed Blades Clash. At Chioriya Boutique, Chiori finishes a gown for a client, Laetitia, and explains why a designer must show new work each Fashion Week. Kirara arrives late and admits she was ambushed by bandits who had been told to target a two-tailed courier. Chiori recognises the instigator, Uther, and goes to his home; Chevreuse of the Special Patrol follows and explains that Uther is a known troublemaker who has been warned by the Maison Gardiennage. Chiori throws him out and arranges an escort for Kirara by the Special Patrol and Spina di Rosula.

Stained Spots. The next day customers cancel orders after rumours that Chiori's fabric comes from underground factories. Her accessory supplier, Wenger, withdraws, as do the models he had booked. The rumours came from minor tabloids, not The Steambird, which Charlotte tells the Traveler and Paimon she wants to help correct. Laetitia learns that Chiori covered the dress's extra cost herself and comes to defend her. The Traveler and Paimon find Chiori by the sea in the Liffey Region, where she tells them of her past. She decides to make the collection out of what is to hand, using sea finds for accessories and salvaging fabric from her ready-to-wear stock, and enlists Navia, Charlotte, Lyney and Lynette.

The Woven and the Cut. Chiori, the Traveler and Paimon tour Uther's factory at Fleuve Cendre, find a hidden lower level with unsanitary conditions and chemical treatments that would harm wearers' skin, and photograph it before being locked in. Chiori reveals that she and Uther were once partners. Uther confronts them with Clockwork Meka and a gun; Chiori parries his shot with her blade and defeats him. The fashion show succeeds, and Chiori gives the Traveler and Paimon a customised brooch.
### LARGE +
Chiori's past, as she tells it: she was a troublesome child, her parents sent her to a master tailor when her needlework showed talent, she grew angry at having to flatter arrogant clients, and after a few incidents left Inazuma for Fontaine to start over and make her brand known across Teyvat. She goes to a cliff by the sea in the Liffey Region when upset, and Eloffe, who works at the boutique, knows this.

Uther and Chiori began as newcomer designers who wanted their own take on fashion. After repeated rejections Uther gave up before a Fashion Week; Chiori kept going and gained recognition. When she opened her shop Uther began to harass her. Chiori held back during the confrontation because of their old partnership. Navia and Charlotte each first react angrily on seeing the damaged display stock, Navia assuming vandals, until Chiori says she asked the Traveler to do it.
### ULTRA +
Chevreuse says Chiori's work benefits the Special Patrol, which means she often protects Chiori from people who resent her, usually the rich and powerful, though sometimes she must protect others from her. Chiori compares Navia's determination and courage to her own. After the show Chevreuse is anxious over the risks Chiori took, and Chiori notes that Wenger sent an apology she intends to answer in her own time. Chiori ends the night saying she will never bow or scrape to anyone, confident in her work, and that it is the loveliest night she has had in Fontaine so far.

## revelations
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
Chiori and Uther were once partners. Uther ran a factory at Fleuve Cendre with unsanitary conditions and chemical treatments harmful to wearers.
### LARGE +
Chiori covered a client's dress cost from her own pocket and does not use cheap materials. She left Inazuma after conflicts with arrogant clients while apprenticed to a master tailor. The press campaign was bought: several tabloids were paid to print the same article at once.

## characters
### LITE
Chiori, the Traveler, Paimon and Kirara.
### STANDARD +
Uther (rival), Chevreuse, Navia, Charlotte, Lyney, Lynette, Laetitia (client), Eloffe (shop assistant), Wenger (accessory supplier), Leeann and Weis (customers who cancel).
### LARGE +
Spina di Rosula, the Special Patrol and the House of the Hearth support Chiori.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Uther's factory is exposed and he is defeated. Chiori's boutique gets a new sign and her fashion show goes ahead successfully. Kirara is escorted safely out of Fontaine.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocks at Adventure Rank 40 after Masquerade of the Guilty (Chapter IV Act V). Released in Version 4.5 and set in Fontaine around Fontaine Fashion Week. No later acts exist as of 7.1.
