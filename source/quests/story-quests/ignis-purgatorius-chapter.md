## summary
### LITE
Arlecchino's Story Quest, set in Poisson. A rumour of a 'phantom child' no one remembers has spread through the House of the Hearth, and Arlecchino has moved the whole House to the town to find her. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet secretly shelter the girl, Clervie, and the Traveler helps. Arlecchino reveals that Clervie was her friend, killed years ago in a duel, and the quest ends with Clervie being let go in peace.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, When the Hearth-Flame Goes Out, of four quests: The Child Who Doesn't Exist, Sea Breeze Rendezvous, Birds in a Cage and Into the Sunlight. It was released in version 4.6 and is unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Masquerade of the Guilty. It shows the Harbinger as a ruthless and honest head of a household who rewrote the cruel rules she inherited.
### LARGE +
The story is about loyalty inside a family built on fear. Lyney and his siblings risk their lives to protect one child, Arlecchino tests them, and the cost of her own survival becomes clear. It also sets up Project Stuzha as a looming threat to the House.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon visit Poisson and find the House of the Hearth staying there. Freminet has been sheltering a girl named Clervie who no one else remembers. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet try to quietly resolve it. Arlecchino learns of it, warns the Traveler, then explains that Clervie died long ago. She lets the siblings duel her, allows the 'traitors' to leave with their memories erased, and releases Clervie at dawn.
### STANDARD +
The Child Who Doesn't Exist: in Poisson the Traveler meets Lyney, who says that Father has moved the family there during trouble elsewhere. Arlecchino says a rumour that a 'phantom child' is among the House has spread and she wants to find the culprit. Freminet leads the Traveler, Paimon and Lyney to Clervie, who has been following the House for about half a month. She says there were two rosters, one for an ideal candidate and one for execution, and that no one leaves the House alive. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet agree to find a way to grant her wish to go where the sun can shine.

Sea Breeze Rendezvous: the Traveler finds Arlecchino on the shore with Childe, who has come looking for Skirk and heard a rumour that she betrayed the House. She admits she is not a Fontainian and that duels in the House once ended in death. The siblings find that Clervie cannot go into sunlight and that her name is not on the execution roster. At night the Traveler and Paimon find Clervie leaving town and learn she fears being sent to Dottore.

Birds in a Cage: Arlecchino tells them she has known all along and orders them to Palais Mermonia. She says that betrayal is not tolerated, lets the Traveler off as a guest, and promises not to punish the siblings if Clervie is gone. At the Palais she gives Neuvillette a written proposal to withdraw her forces from Fontaine over time, and he accepts. Afterwards she agrees to let the guests stay, on condition that they help the House when the time comes.

Into the Sunlight: at ruins on Mont Esus East, Arlecchino tells her history. She decides to kill Clervie's shade again. Lyney begs her to stop and Lynette, Freminet and Chapleau back him. She duels the siblings, with the Traveler joining them, and wins easily. She then offers a potion of her flames that will erase the memory of the House, and the 'traitors' take it and leave. Clervie is told the truth, accepts it, and fades at dawn.
### LARGE +
The Child Who Doesn't Exist: Lyney notices tension with some older members as he distributes supplies. Freminet lies to get food and cloth, and when Lyney sees through it he learns Freminet is hiding someone. Freminet says he could not hand her over, because he believed Clervie was the voice that helped him save Chapleau from poison. Lynette turns up unamused and joins the plan, threatening to tell Father otherwise. Asked who can vouch for her, Clervie names Perrie, whom the group has never heard of.

Sea Breeze Rendezvous: Childe says he enjoyed time with the children but would come for Arlecchino if she harmed them. She replies that her family is more complicated than he thinks, using Tonia and Teucer as examples. Lyney reports that Project Stuzha was a collaboration between Regrator and the Rooster, and that the House's acquisition of a Gnosis gave them a 'key' role that is dangerous for the House.

Birds in a Cage: Lyney says that when Arlecchino became a Harbinger Dottore proposed to work together; she turned down all but one plan. Neuvillette raises the Tartuffe assassination and asks if her proposal is sincere; she admits to nothing but says such incidents will be much less likely. A newsboy runs into the group and Arlecchino buys papers, seeing through Paimon's ruse.

Into the Sunlight: Arlecchino explains the House as a purgatory that made war orphans fight to be 'King', with the permanently injured given to Dottore or sent on dangerous missions. She tells Lyney she picked him as 'King' for his conviction and expects him to grow stronger.
### ULTRA +
Arlecchino's account in full: Clervie was the biological daughter of Crucabena ('Mother'), brought into the House at age six. She tried to change her mother and was supported only by Peruere ('Perrie'). Ten years later Peruere suggested killing Crucabena, but Clervie, who still saw her as her mother, refused. Concluding that death was the only escape, Clervie duelled Peruere and died. A year later Peruere killed Crucabena in the same place and revealed that Peruere was her real name, then took over the House and rewrote its rules. The old name brought back memories.

Aftermath: Lyney reports that those who wished to leave drank the potion while he, Lynette and Freminet declined it. Arlecchino says that with the weaker members removed the House is strong enough to turn against Project Stuzha when the time comes. She would have liked to tell Clervie she had seen the aurora of Snezhnaya, as they had promised each other, but left it unsaid before dawn would have wiped Clervie's memory. Two days later Lynette and Freminet are recovering at Hotel Bouffes d'ete; Lynette saw Filliol and the others in a cafe, living the life they wanted, with no memory of her.

## revelations
### LITE
The 'phantom child' is Clervie, the daughter of the previous Harbinger Crucabena, killed in a duel with the young Arlecchino years ago.
### STANDARD +
Arlecchino, whose real name she says is Peruere, killed Crucabena and rewrote the House of the Hearth's rules. Before that, the House made orphans fight each other, and those who survived were hurt or given to Dottore. She is also not Fontainian.
### LARGE +
Project Stuzha, a Pantalone-and-Pulcinella plan, is aimed at reining in the House now that it holds a Gnosis. Arlecchino has known of the siblings' plot from the start.
### ULTRA +
Her flames can erase memories of life in the House in those who drink them. A shade of everything she has consumed lingers in her blood and can take form.

## characters
### LITE
Arlecchino, the Traveler, Paimon, Lyney, Lynette, Freminet, Clervie.
### STANDARD +
Childe, who visits Arlecchino on the shore. Neuvillette, who accepts her proposal at Palais Mermonia. Children of the House including Filliol, Chapleau, Tonia and Teucer, as well as Crucabena (in flashback).
### LARGE +
Skirk, Regrator (Pantalone), the Rooster (Pulcinella) and Dottore are referred to but do not appear.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Clervie is released and the 'traitors' who drank the potion leave the House with no memory of it. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet stay. Arlecchino and the Traveler part with an acknowledged bond, though she says they may be on opposite sides in future.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino has agreed to withdraw her forces from Fontaine gradually and avoid special operations there unless absolutely necessary, in return for Neuvillette accepting her written proposal.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Chapter IV Act V, Masquerade of the Guilty. Released in 4.6, so it follows the Fontaine Archon Quest in release order.

## entry: Clervie's story
@keys: Clervie, Phantom Child, Crucabena
@keys2: Arlecchino, House of the Hearth
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The 'phantom child' of the Ignis Purgatorius Chapter. As Arlecchino tells it, Clervie was Crucabena's daughter and the friend of the girl who became Arlecchino. She died in a duel with that friend after deciding death was the only escape from the House. A shade of her at age six was somehow formed from the shadows in Arlecchino's blood. She fades at dawn after being told the truth.

## entry: Potion of Arlecchino's flames
@keys: Arlecchino's potion, searing flames potion
@keys2: Arlecchino, House of the Hearth
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the duel, Arlecchino offers any member who wishes to leave a potion made from her searing flames. Whoever survives it loses memories of the House, which in her words 'kills' that life so they can be released. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet each have a bottle available but decline it.
