## summary
### LITE
The Cypressus Custos Chapter is Kamisato Ayato's Story Quest, released in Version 2.6, with one act, The Firmiana Leaf Falls. Rumour says Kujou Kamaji of the Tenryou Commission and Hiiragi Chisato of the Kanjou Commission are to marry. Ayato, head of the Kamisato Clan and Yashiro Commissioner, suspects the match is a trap and, with the Traveler's help, spoils it.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: New Shoots from Old Branches, The Truth Is Also a Lie and The Wind Settles. It is a political story, not a combat one. It shows how Ayato works: quietly, through informants and public opinion, and with a wry sense of what a "wedding" between the Tri-Commission's houses really costs.
### LARGE +
It follows on from the unsettled state of the Kanjou Commission after Hiiragi Shinsuke's imprisonment and from the Kujou Clan's loss of control after Kujou Takayuki's fall. Its resolution lets Chisato assert herself for the first time and leaves Ayato crediting the Traveler and Paimon's trusted standing with Kamaji and Chisato for making his plan work.

## plot
### LITE
Two retainers, Ippei and Shinnojou, guess that Kamaji and Chisato will marry. Ayato confirms it but soon suspects a plot. Shinnojou is kidnapped, Matsuura reveals the plotters' hand, and Ayato and Kamaji arrange for public opinion to turn the match against the plotters. Two days later Chisato has the upper hand and Matsuura apologises.
### STANDARD
Leaving Tenshukaku, the Traveler and Paimon overhear Ippei and Shinnojou, who have been buying clothes for their masters and guess a wedding is coming. Shinnojou, who admires Chisato, is upset. At the Kamisato Estate they find Ayato playing chess with Thoma; Ayato confirms the rumour and says a marriage between members of the Tri-Commission deserves his personal respects. At the Kujou Estate Kamaji says Chisato proposed by letter first, but when he spoke to her she acted as though she had sent none, and they agreed anyway. Ayato finds this off, since he knows Chisato is not coy, and goes to the Hiiragi Estate. There he declines the clan representatives' hospitality, drinks Dango Milk with the Traveler, and explains that the Kanjou Commission is unstable with no replacement for Shinsuke, and some supporters of the Hiiragi would resent the match. He has a Shuumatsuban watch the representatives.

Chisato tells them Shinnojou burst in and begged her not to marry because it would harm the clan name, that Kamaji's reply was sudden, and that she did not send the letter. Ayato asks her to consider the marriage carefully. Outside Ritou he notes that a low-ranking officer objected while the higher-ups approved, and he sensed someone listening. An aide reports that Shinnojou has been taken away. Ayato says the plotters are clever and cannot be stopped until they show their fangs, because acting openly would cause public backlash.
### LARGE +
In the caves near Araumi they find and free Shinnojou, whom Ayato tells to go into hiding. Matsuura confronts Ayato, accuses him of meddling and of trying to protect his power, and says the notices have gone public so the wedding is set. Ayato is unimpressed, having thought him sharper, and says he will reply soon. Ayato tells the Traveler he wants to see how the public reacts once his plan is known. He tells Kamaji they can still turn the situation around by changing Chisato from a burden into an asset. Kamaji, who admits he moved too fast, agrees. The Shuumatsuban find a Kanjou informant who listens to the crowds, and Ayato says the plotters will have no choice but to give in, as he has done this many times; he advises waiting two days and leaves.

Two days later everyone in Ritou is talking about Chisato. At the Hiiragi Estate Matsuura comes in, accuses the Traveler and Paimon of motives, and is scolded by Chisato for the plot. He goes to the other plotters, who decide Chisato is valuable to them. Chisato notes she has used authority for the first time. Matsuura returns, apologises, and asks the Traveler to accompany him to the Kamisato Estate. There he apologises to Ayato, who asks how he will correct the error and dismisses him after hearing, saying both knew this would happen. Ayato tells Chisato that what she faces now is what he, Ayaka and Kamaji each had to face. He tells Paimon he suspected from the start but understood their feelings. Thoma comes out afterward and chats with him. Ayato says the Traveler and Paimon helped greatly, as the trust Kamaji and Chisato placed in them made it easier to accept his plan.
### ULTRA +
The scheme works through the marriage's appearance: no one can openly oppose a union between two Commission heirs, so Ayato lets it go ahead in public and instead changes what it means. His principle, as the act shows it, is that if a plan has no ill consequences it can be allowed to stand, and that people with real strength act only when the other side commits.

## revelations
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
- Chisato never wrote the letter that Kamaji says started the engagement.
- Matsuura and other plotters, with Kanjou informants watching the city, pushed the marriage.
- Ayato uses public pressure and the Shuumatsuban to counter the plot, and says he has done this many times before.
- The Kanjou Commission is unstable with no replacement for Shinsuke, and the Kujou Clan is weakened, which is what the plotters exploit.
### LARGE +
- Ayato says marriage within the Tri-Commission is important enough that he would personally visit.
- Chisato's authority is asserted for the first time in this act.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Kamisato Ayato**: the Yashiro Commissioner and chapter protagonist.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: companions and trusted friends of Kamaji and Chisato.
- **Thoma**: Ayato's household retainer, who plays chess with him.
- **Kujou Kamaji**: Tenryou Commission figure, the supposed groom.
- **Hiiragi Chisato**: Kanjou Commission figure, the supposed bride.
- **Ippei and Shinnojou**: retainers of Kamaji and Chisato.
- **Matsuura**: plotter who threatens Ayato.
### LARGE +
Ayaka and Hiiragi Shinsuke appear only by reference.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The wedding plot fails and Chisato gains standing within the Kanjou Commission. Matsuura apologises to Ayato. Shinnojou is safe.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.6. It requires Omnipresence Over Mortals, Reflections of Mortality and the World Quest Chisato's Letter, and takes place in the settled Inazuma after Chapter II. Story order and release order match. No later acts exist as of 7.1.
