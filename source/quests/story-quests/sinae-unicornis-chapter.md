## summary
### LITE
The Sinae Unicornis Chapter is Ganyu's Story Quest (Version 1.2), set in Liyue. In its one act, Sea of Clouds, Sea of People, Ganyu, half qilin and half human, doubts whether she belongs in Liyue Harbor as a secretary to the Liyue Qixing or among the adepti. After training with the adepti and helping a colleague investigate tax fraud, she chooses to keep her post.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: The Illuminated Beast of the Sea of Clouds, A Secretary in a Sea of Humanity and A Pact That Crosses Time. It requires Adventure Rank 40 and the Archon Quest A New Star Approaches. The Chinese title means roughly "Chapter of the Immortal Qilin".
### LARGE +
The act contrasts two "seas", the sea of clouds at Jueyun Karst and the sea of people in Liyue Harbor, as the places Ganyu feels adrift in. It shows how she is valued by ordinary people for a lifetime of service, and settles her choice without the Traveler deciding for her.
### ULTRA +
Beyond Ganyu's arc the act has side threads: the Fatui's low reputation in Liyue after Childe's release of Osial, a comic misunderstanding with a berry-picking Fatui skirmisher, and an Uncle Tian whose Qixing rank is a secret kept for his own comfort.

## plot
### LITE
Ningguang's secretaries cannot find Ganyu, who has gone to Jueyun Karst and fears she has been replaced. The Traveler finds her, watches her take trials from the Cloud Retainer and Xiao, and persuades her to come back to the harbor. There she helps Huixin catch two merchants cheating on taxes, hears that Huixin's family looks up to her, and decides to resume her work.
### STANDARD
At Yujing Terrace, Ningguang's secretaries Baixiao, Baishi and Baiwen ask the Traveler to find Ganyu, who has gone to Jueyun Karst convinced she has been replaced. The Cloud Retainer and Xiao give her trials, and a pilgrim's reaction to her refusal to grant a blessing deepens her doubts. Back in Liyue Harbor, with Uncle Tian's daughter Huixin, Ganyu exposes two rival shopkeepers' tax evasion by turning them on each other, after a comic clash with a berry-picking Fatui skirmisher. Huixin tells her that her family has long looked up to her, and Ganyu resolves to stay on as a Qixing secretary.
### LARGE
**The Illuminated Beast of the Sea of Clouds.** At Yujing Terrace the secretaries Baixiao, Baishi and Baiwen ask the Traveler to find Ganyu. She went to deliver a Qixing letter of respect and news to the adepti at Jueyun Karst and has not returned. They are overwhelmed by her workload. The Traveler finds her; Ganyu believes her qilin blood makes her unwelcome in Liyue and, seeing the secretaries doing her tasks, assumes she has been replaced. The Cloud Retainer tries to ease the tension by recounting Ganyu's childhood, which only embarrasses Ganyu into leaving. The Cloud Retainer then explains that she doubts relocating to Jueyun Karst would suit Ganyu's human side, but that Ganyu feels a separation from humans even among them, and has set a trial near Mt. Aocang at Ganyu's request. Ganyu passes it, then meets Xiao at Nantianmen. He is annoyed at her lateness, says her mixed heritage is the source of her inner conflict, and sets a second trial of defending a Ley Line monolith from wildlife. A pilgrim, Xin Cheng, then arrives seeking the adepti's blessing for good fortune. When it is clear that he wants material luck, Ganyu refuses a blessing and says he must make the effort himself. He leaves insulted, and his reaction deepens her doubts. The Traveler persuades her to return to the harbor, and she agrees, saying she will go back to Jueyun if it falls short.

**A Secretary in a Sea of Humanity.** In the harbor they meet Uncle Tian, whom Ganyu reveals is actually the Tianshu of the Qixing; he asks their discretion, enjoying the freedom of an unescorted stroll. He asks Ganyu to help his daughter Huixin, a junior secretary. Huixin has found tax irregularities at two rival shops, Second Life and Wanyou Boutique. Ganyu decides the best approach is to make the rivals expose each other. She tells Bolai of Wanyou Boutique that his shop's credit is not enough for Yuehai Pavilion orders, which angers him into demanding to read his rival Dongsheng's tax report, and he spots an undeclared furniture purchase from Fanmu Carpenters. At Qingce Village the carpenter Master Lu says the invoice is in a warehouse occupied by troublemakers. Ganyu suspects a Fatui Cryogunner Legionnaire in the fields below and attacks him, but he explains he only came to pick berries and that the village is of no interest to the Fatui. She apologises and suggests a complaint to the Ministry of Civil Affairs, but he says it would be ignored, since the Fatui's standing in Liyue is at rock bottom after Childe released Osial. The warehouse troublemakers turn out to be hilichurls. With the invoice, Huixin, Ganyu and the Traveler confront Dongsheng, who confesses; learning that Bolai tipped them off, he exposes Bolai's own misdeeds too. Ganyu tells Huixin that this method of turning opponents on each other was a long-standing tactic of Tian's, and Huixin invites the three to Xinyue Kiosk.

**A Pact That Crosses Time.** Huixin says she has admired Ganyu all her life from her father's stories, and that her own daughter has become a secretary in turn. Because the work is harder than she expected, she asks Ganyu to help her daughter in about twenty years. Ganyu realises that people care about her and that she has been a living witness to Liyue Harbor and its people. She decides to stay with the Qixing, returns to Yujing Terrace to relieve the grateful secretaries, and thanks the Traveler and Paimon for helping her overcome her inner conflict. She hopes to travel with them again, though Paimon points out how swamped she is.

Remarks to keep. The Cloud Retainer expresses worry rather than a demand. Xiao tells Ganyu that only she can measure her own effort, signals a harder training schedule, then leaves her to deal with the pilgrim. Ganyu's handling of the audit (work out motive, exploit rivalry, then confirm with paper evidence) is presented as a working example of her competence as a secretary.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Minor: Uncle Tian is the Tianshu of the Liyue Qixing, though few know it. Ganyu feels set apart from both adepti and humans, more strongly than others realise. The Fatui's reputation in Liyue is poor after Childe released Osial.

## characters
### LITE
Ganyu, the Traveler and Paimon. Baixiao, Baishi, Baiwen, the Cloud Retainer, Xiao, Xin Cheng, Uncle Tian, Huixin, Bolai, Dongsheng, Master Lu and a Fatui skirmisher appear.
### STANDARD +
Ganyu is dutiful and self-doubting; the secretaries are harried and fond of her; Xiao is brusque but takes her training seriously; Huixin looks up to her; Tian is relaxed and shrewd.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Ganyu stays at her post as the Qixing's secretary and deepens her trust in the Traveler. The act also gives a first look at the Cloud Retainer's and Xiao's attitudes toward her.

Release order: 1.2. Story order: after Chapter I of the Archon Quests (A New Star Approaches).
