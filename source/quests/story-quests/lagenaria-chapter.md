## summary
### LITE
The Lagenaria Chapter is Baizhu's Story Quest (Version 3.6), set in Liyue. In its one act, The Heart of Healing, a boy named Ayu seeks help for his sickly father, and Baizhu discovers that the man is being kept alive and poisoned by the remains of a dead god. The act also shows how Baizhu and Changsheng share life force, and what Baizhu hopes to achieve through immortality.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: The Search for a Physician, The Quest for a Cure and The Formula for a Miracle. It requires Adventure Rank 40, A New Star Approaches, Hu Tao's Yet the Butterfly Flutters Away and the Alatus Chapter's Butterfly's Dream. The Chinese title refers to the hanging gourd, a traditional emblem of physicians.
### LARGE +
The act shows Baizhu's habit of paying for others' cures with his own body and the pharmacy's shared secret: Changsheng's power comes with a contract that has killed her earlier hosts young. It also brings in Hu Tao, whose family history with Baizhu's master gives context to her wariness of him.
### ULTRA +
Hu Tao's account of the rift, Xiao's warning about immortality, and Baizhu's own plan to become Changsheng's last host are linked pieces of the same theme: medicine, mortality and the cost of cheating death.

## plot
### LITE
Ayu looks for his missing father Jialiang. Baizhu recognises that the man carries god remains and prolongs his life by transferring some of his own life force. The group traces the remains to a dead god beneath Qingce Village and a tunnel where Jialiang's wife Jiangli lies unconscious. Baizhu destroys the remains, and Jialiang, supposedly dying, wakes at the pharmacy thanks to an Elixir of Immortality that gives the family time to say goodbye.
### STANDARD
A boy named Ayu seeks his sickly father Jialiang at Bubu Pharmacy. Baizhu helps find him near Luhua Pool, where Hu Tao is also present, and recognises god remains inside the man. He secretly keeps Jialiang alive by transferring some of his own life force through Changsheng. They trace a hidden poison to Ayu's home in Qingce Village and to the remains of a dead god named Chi in a tunnel, where Jialiang's wife Jiangli lies unconscious. Baizhu destroys the remains, and an Elixir of Immortality prepared by Qiqi lets Jialiang say goodbye. Baizhu also reveals to the Traveler why he seeks immortality: to be Changsheng's last host.
### LARGE
**The Search for a Physician.** At Bubu Pharmacy, Qiqi and Herbalist Gui are talking to a boy, Ayu, whose father is sickly and whose mother has not returned from gathering herbs. His father had left the house, the Millelith could not spare many people, and he came to the pharmacy. Baizhu arrives with Qiqi, says he knows Ayu's mother Jiangli and father as old acquaintances, and agrees to help. The Millelith suggest Jialiang may be near Luhua Pool. The group finds a man nearly walking off a cliff and someone stopping him; it is Hu Tao, who greets the Traveler warmly and Baizhu less so. Jialiang has memory loss. Baizhu says he has god remains inside him which, if not dealt with, would cause a major outbreak, but he has a duty to his patients, especially Jialiang, per his master's dying wish. He asks for a day. Hu Tao, invited to observe, tells the Traveler that the threat of dead gods has not been seen since the Archon War, though she was taught to recognise it, and that Baizhu's master was her grandfather's younger brother. When he went to Chenyu Vale they met a pharmacist, argued, and he left the family business. Her grandfather said the pharmacist had an ominous aura and that their medicine broke natural rules, and that Baizhu sought immortality. The next day Jialiang is well, but the Traveler finds Baizhu in poor health; he admits he did not cure Jialiang but transferred some of his life force to him through Changsheng's secret arts.

**The Quest for a Cure.** At Ayu's home in Qingce Village, Baizhu and Changsheng find a hidden compartment of poison, a minor offshoot used to manipulate and control, apparently Jiangli's doing. Eliminating the source will end Jialiang's trouble. They call Xiao, who is surprised to see Baizhu, and who confirms a dead god named Chi lies under the village. He declines to meddle in mortal affairs but offers aid if needed, and warns Baizhu that immortality may not be what he imagines. Gui reports that Jialiang has worsened, earlier than the three days Changsheng predicted. Baizhu stabilises him and then suffers his own symptoms. In private he tells the Traveler that Jiangli had used Chi's remains to treat her husband's underlying heart condition, which also harmed him. Jialiang, accepting death, asks to be brought to reunite with his wife. Baizhu agrees once he consents, and asks Qiqi to prepare medicine.

**The Formula for a Miracle.** In Qingce Village, Jialiang's symptoms flare as they approach a tunnel. The tunnel suggests Treasure Hoarders, though their tools are left behind, and a censer of monster-repelling incense stands inside. At the end they find a Geovishap and an unconscious Jiangli. Baizhu says the remains are killing her as they are him. Destroying them would kill Jialiang, who accepts it, having lived longer than he should and treasured his time with his family. Baizhu destroys the remains; Jiangli wakes, and Jialiang appears to die. He instead wakes at Bubu Pharmacy, with strange marks on his body. Baizhu reveals that Qiqi prepared an Elixir of Immortality to hold him on this side of the afterlife so that he could say goodbye when it expired. Hu Tao, exasperated at another "Qiqi", says she would have taken him to the parlor had she known. Jiangli privately warns Baizhu to use Changsheng's powers wisely, as she does not want him to die either. Changsheng tells the Traveler and Paimon that she and her host share a contract and all her previous hosts died young. Baizhu intends to break the cycle by learning the secret of immortality, so that he can be her final host while ensuring that no one else follows that fate. The family and Qiqi call them to share a meal.

Details worth keeping. Baizhu's reasoning for the day's grace was that he must try to save a patient even if the remains put the area at risk, and his secret technique is performed alone to avoid distraction. Changsheng at first says Jialiang should be fine for three days. Her contract with Baizhu means his illness and his handling of others' health are linked, and the transfer of life force from Baizhu to Jialiang uses her powers.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Major for Baizhu's arc: he and Changsheng share a contract that has killed her earlier hosts young, and he seeks immortality to be her last host. A dead god named Chi lies beneath Qingce Village, and its remains are the source of Jialiang's illness and Jiangli's collapse. Hu Tao's grandfather's younger brother was Baizhu's master. All of this was revealed in Version 3.6.
### LARGE +
Jialiang is briefly kept alive by an Elixir of Immortality as a stopgap, not a cure.

## characters
### LITE
Baizhu, the Traveler and Paimon, with Changsheng, Qiqi, Hu Tao, Xiao, Herbalist Gui, Ayu, Jialiang and Jiangli.
### STANDARD +
Baizhu is calm, self-sacrificing and evasive about his own health; Changsheng is dry and protective; Qiqi prepares medicine; Hu Tao is wary but cooperative; Xiao appears briefly and warns Baizhu. Ayu is a worried child and is not described as anything beyond that.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Jialiang's family is reunited and spared from the god remains, and Baizhu's secret about Changsheng is shared with the Traveler. Hu Tao and Baizhu remain uneasy but cooperative.
