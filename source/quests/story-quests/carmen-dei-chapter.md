## summary
### LITE
Carmen Dei Chapter is Venti's Story Quest (Version 1.0), one act titled "Should You Be Trapped in a Windless Land". Venti hands the Traveler a "nirnama detector" that reveals imaginary friends, and they follow the trail from Mondstadt's children to Stanley, an old tavern storyteller who tells heroic tales to an aspiring young adventurer. In the end Venti reveals himself as Barbatos to the storyteller and speaks briefly about his own first friend.
### STANDARD
The act moves through four quests: finding imaginary friends in the city, going to the Temple of the Lion with Jack, retrieving two supposed heirlooms of the hero Vennessa from Dadaupa Gorge, and the tavern scene in which Stanley's secret comes out. The quest is a small human story that also shows Venti's gentler side, his habit of hiding who he is, and the grief behind his time with the Nameless Bard.
### LARGE +
The tone is whimsical until the last two scenes. The investigative device frames the plot, but its real function is to show Venti attentive to what people need rather than to the facts of what they did, and the closing statue scene is one of the few places where he talks about the Nameless Bard directly.

## plot
### STANDARD
**A Child's Secret.** In Mondstadt City the Traveler and Paimon see Nora talking to an imaginary friend. Venti arrives with a monocle-like nirnama detector that Lisa made from Sumeru materials, saying Lisa was too busy to use it. Through it the Traveler sees Nora's friend, then Flora's (a potted Bloatty Floatty) and Timmie's (a Ruin Guard). Adults at Angel's Share have none, so they ask Ellin, who is between the groups; her friend is Jean, whom she wants to emulate. After a demonstration she asks the Traveler to tell Jack at the Temple of the Lion that she cannot attend their planning meeting.

**The South Wind Brings Adventure.** At the temple Jack and Stanley are being attacked by a Cryo Abyss Mage and hilichurls. After the fight Stanley, hurt that the Traveler has not heard of his deeds, tells of reaching Mare Jivari with his partner. Venti senses something off; the detector shows Stanley has an imaginary friend too. Jack then explains he wants the Sword of Brilliant Valor and Shield of Magnificent Honor, once Vennessa's and supposedly discarded by Stanley at Dadaupa Gorge, to show his parents that his dream is worth taking seriously. Stanley is listening.

**Lionfang's Legacy.** In the gorge they interrupt a hilichurl ceremony and take a sword that is ordinary. Venti tells a deflated Jack there is more than meets the eye. The shield turns out to be a wine-barrel end with a handle, held by a hilichurl; the Traveler negotiates or fights for it. Jack runs off to tell his parents, and Paimon and the Traveler realise Stanley is a fraud. Venti says Stanley is stuck in the past and invites the Traveler to Angel's Share that night.

**Should You Be Trapped in a Windless Land.** Stanley is drunk, mourning the death of the real Stanley. Venti understands that the stories were true but that the man before them is not the hero. Jack arrives to say his parents now back him, so long as he explores only Mondstadt for now, thanks Stanley, and leaves. Stanley admits he did not want to crush Jack's pure dream and fears forgetting the real Stanley's life; he laments that Stanley's soul will not return to Mondstadt because he died in a windless place. To help, Venti reveals he is Barbatos and addresses him by his real name, Hans Archibald. With a breeze from Mare Jivari, Venti asks for Stanley's soul so it can rest; Hans thanks him and leaves hopeful. Diluc, watching, is moved. Venti goes to collect his wine from Jack and tells the Traveler to meet at "the usual place".
### LARGE +
At the statue of Barbatos outside Favonius Cathedral, Venti produces a partly filled bottle of ordinary cider. He tells how old Mondstadt, now Stormterror's Lair, was ruled by the tyrant Decarabian, who sealed it with a hurricane; Venti was then a formless wisp, and his present shape belongs to a friend. Paimon asks what happened to the friend, but the Traveler infers that the Nameless Bard died freeing Mondstadt. Venti is pained, the Traveler sends Paimon to fetch food, and Venti, glad to be understood, sings a short song his friend once sang. He stays in silence at the statue.
### ULTRA +
The four quest names are A Child's Secret, The South Wind Brings Adventure, Lionfang's Legacy and the act's title quest. The wiki records that the act's story card was changed in Version 1.4. The quest also mentions Jean, Lisa and Diluc only by reference or in cameo; its emotional weight is carried by Venti, Hans and Jack.

## revelations
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The storyteller Stanley is actually Hans Archibald, who tells the stories of the real, dead Stanley, and Venti is Barbatos, the Anemo Archon.
- The real Stanley died in a windless place and Venti's breeze from Mare Jivari lets his soul move on.
- Venti's present form belongs to a friend who died freeing Mondstadt from Decarabian (the Nameless Bard).
### LARGE +
Venti is willing to say who he is to a man in distress, but at the statue he avoids speaking directly about his friend's death and lets the Traveler say it.

## characters
### STANDARD
Venti (lead), the Traveler and Paimon, Lisa (maker of the detector, offstage), Nora, Flora and Timmie (children with imaginary friends), Ellin, Jack, Stanley/Hans Archibald, Diluc (listens at Angel's Share), the Nameless Bard (remembered).
### LARGE +
Vennessa is only referenced as the former owner of the heirlooms. Jack's parents are mentioned and never shown; Jean appears only as Ellin's idealised friend.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Jack gets his parents' approval to adventure around Mondstadt. Hans Archibald finds closure about the real Stanley and still has the stories to tell. The Traveler becomes a trusted confidant to Venti.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order is not numbered by canon. Released with Version 1.0 and later given a revised story card in Version 1.4. It requires Adventure Rank 36 and the first act of the Trifolium Chapter (True Treasure). The act has no sequel and no follow-up quest is listed on the wiki.

## entry: Hans Archibald and Stanley
@keys: Hans Archibald, Stanley
@keys2: Carmen Dei, Venti, Jack, tavern
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Stanley the adventurer was a real man whose tale ended in a windless place. The Stanley seen in Mondstadt is Hans Archibald, a man who keeps those stories alive and is slowly forgetting the real Stanley's personality. He keeps up the fiction to avoid crushing Jack's dreams.
### LARGE +
After Venti returns Stanley's soul with a breeze from Mare Jivari, Hans leaves calm and hopeful; Diluc hopes Stanley's tales will live on, but the wiki summary does not say whether Hans keeps telling them.
