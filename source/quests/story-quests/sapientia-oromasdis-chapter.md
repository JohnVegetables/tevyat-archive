## summary
### LITE
Nahida's Story Quest, in two acts. In Lingering Warmth (3.2) the newly dream-free Sumeru City takes up dream-sharing, and Nahida and the Traveler uncover a man who has trapped other dreamers in an artificial dream built from his uploaded mind. In Homecoming (3.6) Nahida, uneasy about a forgotten duty, helps elemental fungi from a lost homeland and finds that the dragon Apep, damaged when forbidden knowledge was purged, is in danger of dying.
### STANDARD +
Act I has three quests: Dream of Awakening, Dream of Falling and Dream of Farewell. Act II has two: Where Lies the Path Home and What Shape Does the Self Hold. A follow-up, Another Home There May Yet Be, is unlocked afterward but is outside the act.
### LARGE +
The chapter is Nahida's personal growth: learning to guide dreamers without erasing grief, and learning that being the Archon of Wisdom does not mean knowing what she has forgotten. It touches directly on the erasure of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata from memory, which only the Traveler recalls.

## plot
### LITE
Act I: A group called A Moment of Dreams meets in Sumeru City. After one member sleepwalks, Nahida and the Traveler find that several dreamers are being lured into a shared dream. Its owner, a scholar who uploaded his mind to the Akasha before it was shut down, keeps it alive with others' Jnana energy and a recreated lost wife. Nahida ends it and arranges for his consciousness to return to his body.

Act II: Nahida has a vague sense of an unfinished duty. Elemental fungi from a lost homeland lead her to Apep, the Dendro Dragon, which harbours a void left by the purge of forbidden knowledge. With a Fire Seed and her own power she helps heal it.
### STANDARD +
Dream of Awakening: Pedrush invites the Traveler and Paimon to the interest group. Nahida appears as a surprise guest and answers dream questions. Ilman says he sees his dead wife each night and continues their talk; Nahida says this is rare. The Traveler thinks privately of Rukkhadevata. Ilman sleepwalks toward a cliff and is saved; Nahida warns him off the site. The headcount shows Kathya has gone to Chatrakam Cave.

Dream of Falling: Kathya dreams of Professor Aisha, an Amurta researcher who gave her a plant that she promised would bloom as proof of her return; Nahida says it never would. The Traveler and Paimon help tell her. Nahida takes the group into the dreams of Debbi, who has lost friends in a shipwreck, and Ilman's family, then to the dream's owner, who explains how he built a 'hyper-realistic' person, his wife Emira, and planted devices in others to feed her Jnana energy. Nahida exposes him. After his creation turns into a monster and is slain, he wishes to die; Nahida refuses and promises to return his consciousness to his body in a Knowledge Capsule.

Dream of Farewell: the sleepers wake. Nahida gives the Capsule to Sitt at the Akademiya for a patient at Bimarstan, then gives the group a speech about moving forward. To the Traveler she admits she felt a warm pull in the dream that she once knew and can trace only to her unnamed past.

Homecoming: Nahida shows the Traveler a Dendro-charged object she found in the Sanctuary of Surasthana. A Floating Hydro Fungus leads her to a collapsed elemental fungus; she revives it and learns its people are an ancient race twisted by the forbidden knowledge, that she once promised to bring them home, and that home is Apep. They clear a Withering Zone, find further fungi, and learn Apep now has a large void. Nahida realises her forgotten task was to tend it. What Shape Does the Self Hold: in the Chasm they cleanse a fungus with the Fire Seed, which the Geoshroom warns is unstable. At Apep's Resort in Hadramaveth, Apep first drives them off, then swallows them. The Seed shatters, and the fungi sacrifice themselves to restore the Heart of Oasis. Apep recovers and tells its story.
### LARGE +
Details from Act I. Nahida is initially sulky when members hesitate to ask their Archon questions, and Paimon reassures them. Pedrush organises the headcount. Kathya's plant shows Aisha had cultivated it by special means, and the Traveler and Paimon break the news so Nahida does not have to. Debbi tells Nahida that Gaspar and Basimah died on a ship that struck a reef, which she learned from the newspaper The Steambird. The dream's owner says Emira was his first creation with it; he had grown close to her at the Akademiya, and she died of Eleazar complications; when Nahida shut the Akasha down he lost access to its energy and turned to other people's. The others realise they have slept too long and leave. In Dream of Farewell the dream's owner is identified as the Kshahrewar scholar Beynuni, who has been in Bimarstan since misusing the Akasha. Nahida says she will brief Cyno herself later.

Details from Act II. Nahida had been gathering hazy memories from Irminsul. The fungi can change appearance with the environment, and the Hydro fungus had a companion and a child. Paimon likens Apep to Dvalin; Nahida says Apep is older. Apep was ruler of Sumeru's once forested lands until a spike from the sky created the desert, and it never accepted the order that followed the Archon War. At Hadramaveth Apep calls both Nahida and King Deshret reliant on the heavens' glow, and warns that Buer will pay for knowing the truth. Nahida decides to use her own power in place of the shattered Seed, even though it might return her to a twig, before the fungi take her place. Apep admits its rage was amplified by corruption.
### ULTRA +
Apep's account: it and the other dragons sought forbidden knowledge to overcome the Heavenly Principles. Nibelung, who acquired it from a dark power outside Teyvat, led them against outsiders in a war that nearly destroyed the world; Nibelung died, but Apep kept collecting knowledge until the spike halted it. King Deshret, who gained forbidden knowledge from Nabu Malikata, was allowed to build his kingdom on Apep's land on condition that Apep would inherit that knowledge when he died. Apep consumed him as agreed but absorbed corruption instead, and saw it had fallen into a trap. It remains loyal to the Dragon King's cause and will watch Buer and humanity to see how life under the Heavenly Principles unfolds.

Aftermath: Nahida comforts the sad fungus Nur and finds elemental traces in her. In Another Home There May Yet Be she brings Nur and the Hydro fungi to a camp of Anemo fungi, apologises to the Traveler for nearly sacrificing herself, notices the fungi copying the elemental lifeform's farming, and resolves to monitor the camp.

## revelations
### LITE
A scholar built a hidden shared dream from others' energy, and the dragon Apep is in danger of dying after the forbidden knowledge was purged.
### STANDARD +
Nahida's forgotten duty was to tend Apep and, with the fungi, to bring them home. The fungi's people are an ancient race corrupted by forbidden knowledge.
### LARGE +
Nahida senses a 'warmth' from her past that she cannot explain, which the Traveler links to Rukkhadevata. Aisha's plant could never bloom.
### ULTRA +
Apep's history of Nibelung's war, King Deshret's pact and the trap it fell into, as told by Apep, who is an interested party. Apep's account is taken from the wiki summary and not independently confirmed.

## characters
### LITE
Nahida, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Pedrush and members of A Moment of Dreams, Ilman, Kathya, Debbi, the dream's owner and Emira, Sitt, and the dragon Apep. Cyno is mentioned as someone Nahida will brief.
### LARGE +
Floating Hydro and Anemo fungi, including Nur, the Geoshroom, and Aranara helpers. Layth, Professor Aisha, Gaspar and Basimah appear only in references or dreams.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The dream is broken and the dreamers wake; the scholar will face accountability. Apep is healed and wary. Nahida sets up a camp of fungi under her monitoring.
### LARGE +
Nahida resolves to continue learning, including from the elemental fungi, and to reunite them with each other. The fate of the Fire Seed is that it breaks.

## placement
### STANDARD
Both acts unlock at Adventure Rank 40 after Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises. Lingering Warmth was released in 3.2 on 2022-11-02 and Homecoming in 3.6 on 2023-04-12, so Act II follows Act I by several versions but takes place soon after the Sumeru Archon Quest.

### STANDARD
The Dendro Dragon once ruled Sumeru's forests and was corrupted by forbidden knowledge. When Nahida purged it from Irminsul, Apep was left with a large void. The Fire Seed is a crystal Nahida and the fungi made to aid it. Its power, taken from Nahida, can heal Apep's Heart of Oasis, but it is unstable.
