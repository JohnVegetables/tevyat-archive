## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Masters of the Night-Wind's Tribal Chronicles chapter and Citlali's story. The tribe records its history on woven scrolls colored with a hidden "color" that only certain people can perceive, and the Traveler and Paimon are asked by the Iktomisaur elder to help weave one. Their search for the lost color leads, in Act III, to the Battle of Seven Colors, the shaman Huitzilin's secret, a Fontaine publisher's fraud, and the discovery that Citlali has secretly been weaving the special scrolls herself.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three acts. Calling from the Masters of the Night-Wind and Legendary "Color" came with Version 5.2, and The Truth of the Battle of Seven Colors with Version 5.3 once the Archon Quest act Incandescent Ode of Resurrection is complete. Act III has four quests: A Commission Catches You in Natlan, Please, great shaman!, Seven-Hued Ruins and The Color of Memory. It is the main source for Citlali's relationship with her rival Huitzilin and for her feelings about outliving the people she knows.
### LARGE +
The thread running through the acts is how a people keeps its memory: the tribe insists that its scrolls are made by hand with a color that cannot be replaced by modern methods, while Citlali's long life means she carries more memory than anyone and has been reluctant to share it.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Act I a baby Iktomisaur leads the Traveler and Paimon into the Masters of the Night-Wind settlement, where the Iktomisaur elder Tlapo asks them to weave a history scroll. The tribe's chief, Biram, has them pass a test by finding a hidden color in an exhibition of old scrolls. In Act II they gather dye ingredients from a sealed cave, but the signature color still fails to appear. In Act III a Fontaine publisher, Montluson, asks Citlali's help to research the Battle of Seven Colors; the group finds the memory maze left by the shaman Huitzilin and exposes Montluson, whose real aim is to destroy evidence of an older dye that threatens his business. Citlali finally finishes the scroll in her basement.
### LARGE +
**Calling from the Masters of the Night-Wind (5.2).** At the Statue of the Seven the pair meet a baby Iktomisaur and are drawn into a vision of the elder, Great Tlapopoctiztetl ("Elder Tlapo"). In the settlement, the priest Caholom and Chief Biram explain that the tribe records major events on woven scrolls made by people the elder chooses. Despite having no experience, the Traveler and Paimon are chosen because the elder senses they can perceive a rare color. A test in a hidden exhibition of scrolls, using the Iktomisaur's special elemental sight, proves the ability. Biram sends them to find a dye.

**Legendary "Color" (5.2).** Toltec guides them to a cave near the Obsidian Totem Pole, sealed centuries ago by the shaman Huitzilin to protect ancestral graffiti; only those granted the elder's sight can enter. They collect three dye samples and test-dye fabric in the Chamber of Weaving with Biram, but the signature color does not appear. Biram says the tribe's methods should be preserved even amid reform and modernisation.

**A Commission Catches You in Natlan (5.3).** In the settlement a group of Fatui bodyguards protect Montluson, a Fontaine merchant in publishing, who wants to write a book about the Battle of Seven Colors, an ancient battle against the Abyss said to have been won when Huitzilin created a special color on the field. He has hired Allier, a researcher from the Fontaine Research Institute, and asks the Traveler to introduce him to Citlali, who has the longest memory. Citlali answers her door hungover and finds she can hear the Traveler's thoughts because of a sense-sharing spell she used earlier; she agrees to help for the Traveler's sake.

**Please, great shaman! (5.3).** While Montluson questions other tribe members, Citlali says Huitzilin was less a close friend than an archrival: students of Huitzilin's school of shamanism must hold out against Citlali in a fight before they graduate. Shamans at the Sacrificial Altar do not know the technique, but Etchemin's family tells of a version in which the battle took place in what was then Chicomostoc Cave and is now the Valley of Fog. That evening Citlali says she keeps friends at a distance because she outlives almost everyone; the Traveler replies that they treasure memories to keep people with them.

**Seven-Hued Ruins (5.3).** Inside the cave, color puzzles lead to a "realm of memory" built by Huitzilin. Citlali has already seen through Montluson, who failed to recognise two light novels she named. He reveals that he had invented a supposedly new dye, only to be told the same color appears in a centuries-old Natlan scroll, and he plans to destroy the sphere containing the maze to erase proof. His bodyguards, subordinates of the Fatui Harbinger Pantalone according to the wiki, fight and are beaten, but one throws a polearm at the sphere and sends the party into the maze. Citlali hears Huitzilin's lingering voice, learns the maze was built from the memories of many Natlanese warriors, and that Huitzilin gave Citlali the technique so that she would not become a blank in Citlali's memories. Because of the Traveler's connection to the Night Kingdom, they can see the color too, and everyone escapes before the cave collapses. Elder Tlapo and Iktomisaurs detain Montluson.

**The Color of Memory (5.3).** In Citlali's basement they find an unfinished scroll. In a memory, Huitzilin says history is the memory of a people and wants to show Citlali her technique. Citlali completes the scroll, which shows the Traveler beside Mavuika, and admits she has always secretly woven the special scrolls, hiding it with illusions, and never taught anyone because the technique involves sharing memories. She gives the Traveler her handkerchief as a memento, and offers to fix the thought-hearing spell. The Traveler reports to Biram without revealing Citlali's role. Allier interviews Citlali, and she gives the Traveler her house keys to fetch the light novels.

### ULTRA +
Finer points. Elder Tlapo has sensed a significant change in the Ley Lines before assigning the task. In Act I the Traveler is limited to solo exploration while using the Iktomisaur's elemental sight to find "Nightspirit Graffiti". Citlali's illusions hide her role as weaver. Her impatience with the bodyguard Grudoc, who interrupts her talk with the Traveler, is what draws her out to scold Montluson. After the first evening, Citlali takes the Traveler and Paimon to her hot air balloon and fails to fix the thought-hearing problem. In the maze, Huitzilin tells Citlali that she lacks the many connections Huitzilin used to shape it, which is why the Traveler's link is needed. In the finale the finished scroll is brought outside with Tlapo's help.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The special color is Huitzilin's technique, which Citlali inherited, and Citlali has been the secret weaver of the tribe's special scrolls. Huitzilin and Citlali were rivals, and Huitzilin passed on the technique to leave her own color in Citlali's memory. Montluson's research was a cover for protecting a failed business gamble.
### LARGE +
The Traveler's unusual ties to the Night Kingdom let them see the color and formed a link with Citlali that made her sense-sharing spell run out of control.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Citlali is a Cryo shaman of the tribe and the Traveler's guide in Act III. Biram is the tribe's chief and Elder Tlapo its Iktomisaur elder. Huitzilin appears only as memory and voice. Montluson, Allier and the bodyguard leader Grudoc are Fontaine visitors, along with minor shamans and tribe members: Atihk, Badru and Etchemin.
### LARGE +
Citlali is shown as hesitant about social contact, a fan of out-of-print light novels, and living in a messy home. Mavuika appears in the finished scroll beside the Traveler.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Montluson is detained and the tribe plans to work with Fontaine's authorities. The Traveler receives Citlali's handkerchief and has access to her home. The tribe keeps its scrolls and the secret of who weaves them stays hidden from most of the tribe.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: Acts I and II in Version 5.2, Act III in Version 5.3. Story order: Acts I and II only require the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame quest, while Act III requires the Chapter V finale Incandescent Ode of Resurrection, so it takes place after the war with the Abyss.

## entry: Huitzilin
@keys: Huitzilin
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Huitzilin was a legendary shaman of the Masters of the Night-Wind and Citlali's long-standing rival. She is said to have created a special color at the Battle of Seven Colors and sealed a cave of ancestral graffiti. She passed the technique to Citlali rather than her own students; her voice survives in the memory maze.

## entry: Battle of Seven Colors
@keys: Battle of Seven Colors, Seven-Hued Ruins
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
An ancient battle against the Abyss said to have been won after Huitzilin created a special color on the field. One account places it in Chicomostoc Cave, now called the Valley of Fog. The cave holds a memory maze built from many warriors' memories.

## entry: Woven Scrolls
@keys: woven scroll, woven scrolls, Chamber of Weaving
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Masters of the Night-Wind record major events on woven scrolls colored with dyes, including one special color that only certain people can perceive. Scroll weavers are chosen by the Iktomisaur elder.

## entry: Montluson
@keys: Montluson
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Fontaine merchant in the printing and publishing trade. He poses as an author of a book on the Battle of Seven Colors but really wants to hide evidence that his supposedly new dye was already known in Natlan. He is detained.
