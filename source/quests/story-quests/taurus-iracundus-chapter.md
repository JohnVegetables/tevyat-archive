## summary
### LITE
The Taurus Iracundus Chapter is Arataki Itto's Story Quest, released in Version 2.3, and as of 7.1 it has one act, Rise Up, Golden Soul. The Traveler is commissioned to arrest Itto, the brash oni who leads the Arataki Gang, for thefts and kidnappings he did not commit. The trail leads to Yashiori Island and to a second oni who feels bound by an old pact between oni and humans.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: The Oni's Evil, The Oni's Justice and The Oni's Pride. It is a story about what oni owe to the humans who live beside them, and about pride on two sides of one old bargain. Itto is the central figure but not the culprit; he is looking for the truth himself, and for the person he believes is responsible.
### LARGE +
The act is explicitly framed through a folk tale of a red oni and a blue oni, told first as a story and then, by Itto, as a record of a real agreement between clans. The conflict is therefore not good against evil but between two oni who understand their duty differently.

## plot
### LITE
The Tenryou Commission has issued a warrant for Arataki Itto for theft and kidnapping. Sango of the Bantan Sango Detective Agency tells the Traveler a tale of a red oni and a blue oni and points them to Yashiori Island. There Itto admits the crime was committed but says an oni did it and insists on clearing his own name. The group finds Takuya, the blue oni responsible, and his ronin allies. Takuya and the ronin leader Nogata are arrested, and Granny Oni resolves to look after the blue oni tribe.
### STANDARD
In Inazuma City, Katheryne passes the Traveler and Paimon a commission from the Tenryou Commission: an arrest warrant for Arataki Itto, a loud, childish oni who leads a gang of misfits and is accused of stealing goods and kidnapping people. Paimon suggests asking the Bantan Sango Detective Agency. Sango tells a story of two oni in the mountains, a red one who liked human company and a blue one who preferred solitude. Humans feared the oni and drove him away with thrown beans, so the blue oni proposed that he play the villain and be chased by the red oni. Opinion shifted, and the red oni was accepted among people, while the blue oni left on a journey. Sango adds a rumour that Itto descends from the red oni, that the blue oni were thought extinct, and that Itto is likely heading for Yashiori Island. She also gives the Traveler a bag of beans, since Itto is said to be badly allergic to them.

On Yashiori Island they confront Itto with the beans and he is knocked down by his allergy. After recovering he admits to the crime, though he says it was done by one person acting alone. Daisuke and Granny Oni come to check on him, and Itto convinces them to spare a little time to look for an onikabuto beetle for a beetle-fighting match, promising a duel with the Traveler afterward. The beetle is found and the match is played. When Daisuke realises Itto is to be taken away, Granny Oni insists that Itto tell the truth. Itto then explains that the tale hides a darker truth: the oni belong to clans, and when the red oni was allowed to live among humans, the blue oni set conditions, namely that the oni must never misuse their strength and must keep their pride. The culprit, Itto says, is a blue oni who may have been driven to act by those principles. He came to the island to meet a contact in the Watatsumi Army camp who had seen the oni.

At the camp the contact is a man named Masato, who was carrying goods when ronin attacked him, and an oni stopped them from killing him. He suspects that they come from Tatarasuna. The group ambushes the ronin at Nazuchi Beach and finds the oni, Takuya, who is bitter that the blue oni gave up so much while the present red oni seems to do nothing, damaging the old bargain. Tenryou Commission soldiers appear and Takuya flees. Itto holds off the soldiers alone, though still weakened, until much of the Arataki Gang arrives to distract them.
### LARGE +
Pursuing Takuya, the group is stopped by more ronin, defeats them and finds the hideout, where they free the hostages, among them Daisuke's parents. The hostages say that Takuya had been secretly bringing them extra food and water behind the ronins' backs. In the deepest part of the hideout, they face Takuya and the ronin leader, Nogata. Nogata warns Itto off, holding a paper seal rigged to explode the hideout if torn. When he tries, Takuya has already taken the charm, because Nogata broke their agreement. Itto punches the distracted Nogata and tries to reach Takuya, who says he is making a sacrifice of his own. Soldiers arrive, and while Itto goes to speak to them, Nogata recovers and fights Takuya for the charm, tearing it. The hideout starts to collapse in explosions, and Itto saves Takuya from being crushed, as Nogata was.

Outside, the Tenryou Commission arrests both oni. It also arrests Itto's crew for obstruction and confiscates the goods Takuya had taken for his clan. The Traveler and Paimon tell Granny Oni, and find Daisuke reunited with his parents. Granny Oni decides to look after the blue oni tribe and says she earned her nickname by caring for Itto, whom she considers her pride and joy.
### ULTRA +
Itto carries the quest in his own manner. He admits to the crime to protect another and refuses to run from the Commission, yet he still wants to find the culprit first. His reaction to Takuya is to try to reach him rather than to condemn him.

## revelations
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
- The warrant against Itto is for crimes committed by another oni, Takuya.
- The story of the red and blue oni reflects a real pact between oni clans and humans, not only a fairy tale.
### LARGE +
- Takuya is a blue oni who resents the present red oni and sees his actions as a sacrifice for his clan.
- The ronin working with Takuya are based at Tatarasuna and are led by Nogata, who is willing to blow up their own hideout.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Arataki Itto**: brash oni, leader of the Arataki Gang, wanted by the Tenryou Commission.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: the commissioned pair who track him and end up helping him.
- **Katheryne**: the Adventurers' Guild receptionist who passes on the commission.
- **Sango**: runs the Bantan Sango Detective Agency and tells the red and blue oni tale.
- **Daisuke and Granny Oni**: a boy and an old oni woman close to Itto on Yashiori Island.
- **Masato**: Itto's contact at the Watatsumi Army camp.
- **Takuya**: the blue oni behind the thefts and kidnappings.
- **Nogata**: the ronin leader who holds the explosive seal.
### LARGE +
Daisuke's parents are among the hostages. The Tenryou Commission soldiers appear as the arresting authority.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Itto's name is partly cleared. Takuya and Nogata are arrested, and Itto's crew is also detained for obstruction. Daisuke is reunited with his parents, and Granny Oni undertakes to care for the blue oni tribe.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.3. It requires Adventure Rank 40, the Archon Quest Chapter II Act III (Omnipresence Over Mortals) and the Dracaena Somnolenta Chapter's Act I (Warriors' Dreams Like Spring Grass Renewing). Only Act I exists as of 7.1.

## entry: Red oni and blue oni
@keys: blue oni, red oni
@keys2: Itto, Takuya, Sango
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
A folk tale told by Sango: a sociable red oni and a solitary blue oni, whom humans drove off with thrown beans. The blue oni proposed that he play the villain so that people would accept the red oni. Itto later explains that this reflects a real agreement between oni clans and humans, with the blue oni requiring that the oni never misuse their strength and maintain their pride.

## entry: Takuya

### STANDARD
A blue oni who works with ronin and takes goods and hostages for his clan. He felt that the current red oni was doing nothing and wasting the blue oni's sacrifice. He quietly helped the hostages, took the explosive seal when Nogata broke their agreement, and was saved by Itto from the collapse before being arrested.
