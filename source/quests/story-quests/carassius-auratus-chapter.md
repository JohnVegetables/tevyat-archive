## summary
### LITE
Yoimiya's Story Quest. In Act I the Traveler helps the Naganohara fireworks maker shelter and ship out a fugitive who slipped into Inazuma, and reunites him with a friend who now serves the Tenryou Commission. In Act II Yoimiya leaves Inazuma for the first time and travels to Sumeru, hoping to see a meteor shower.
### STANDARD +
Act I, Dreamlike Timelessness, was released in Version 2.0 and has four quests: The Firework Girl's Strange Stories, Expectations Not to Be Betrayed, A Sliver of Hope and Together Under the Fireworks. Act II, Star-Pickers' Passage, came in Version 3.7 with three: The Seeker of a Meteor Shower, All Things Flawless and Pure, and A Flower that Blooms Across the Sky.
### LARGE +
Both acts are about what a brief, bright thing is worth. Act I sets the Naganohara show against the Inazuman ideal of eternity; Act II turns the show into a trip in which Yoimiya hopes to find inspiration for a new firework, and instead finds herself carrying other people's wishes.

## plot
### LITE
Act I: during the Sakoku Decree, Yoimiya hides a wanted man, Sakujirou, and arranges a boat for him while preparing a fireworks show. His old friend Keisuke of the Tenryou Commission is hunting him. Yoimiya stops the conflict with a duel and the two part on better terms. Act II: with the Sakoku Decree gone, Yoimiya travels to Sumeru. The Traveler and Nahida stage a dream meteor shower with the Aranara, which also helps a girl named Avin.
### STANDARD +
Act I. On the beach at Inazuma City, the Traveler and Paimon meet children hunting a prank-playing youkai, then Yoimiya, who is checking materials for an upcoming show. Her father, Ryuunosuke, is deaf since a workshop accident. Yoimiya is quietly hiding Sakujirou, an Inazuman-born old customer who smuggled himself back in and is wanted by the Tenryou Commission; she means to put him on a boat after the show. She and the Traveler gather fireworks and check on a boat with Kouichi, who has been questioned because any new vessel draws Tenryou attention. Yoimiya asks the Imatani couple in Konda Village about Sakujirou and learns he once quarrelled with their son Keisuke, now a Tenryou officer, over leaving Inazuma. The Tenryou raid the Naganohara house and Sakujirou escapes. At a blockade Yoimiya fights through, finds Keisuke and Sakujirou, and challenges Keisuke to a duel so that the two will talk. She wins, Keisuke lets Sakujirou leave and lights the firework the two once planned together, and Sakujirou sees it from his boat.

Act II. Now that the Sakoku Decree is lifted, Yoimiya tells the Traveler she wants to see Teyvat's greatest meteor shower, hoping it gives her an idea for a new firework. The Traveler takes her by ship from Ritou to Port Ormos in Sumeru. Nahida, who learns of the wish, says meteor showers cannot be predicted, and proposes creating one in a dream with the Aranara, giving the Traveler an Emberglow Leaf. In the city Yoimiya befriends Avin, a young girl in a wheelchair who longs to explore. When Avin goes missing, the group finds her, calls an Aranara with the leaf, and is led to Avin's Aranara friend. The shared dream restores Avin's ability to run and ends in a field of rising stars.
### LARGE +
Act I details. The act opens lightly: children believe in the Great Mujina, and Yoimiya says they should not yet have to learn of the Vision Hunt Decree. Sakujirou says he left Inazuma to avoid responsibility but heartache brought him back. Yoimiya asks the Traveler about the wider world and, hearing of their search for a sibling, offers them a welcome and fireworks in Inazuma if they return. After the duel she gives Keisuke the finished firework that Sakujirou had ordered, so Keisuke sets it off; the Traveler watches from Amakane Island and is later given a slip for the golden firework Yoimiya made for them.

Act II details. Yoimiya asks her father to look after the shop and her mother while she is away. In Sumeru she buys Aranara carvings and quickly reads Avin's mood. Nahida warns the Traveler to get the story straight or Yoimiya will see through it, and Paimon is wary of the lie. Yoimiya admits she first wanted the trip for herself, but once people gave her wishes for the shower she felt bound to find one. Ahangar, a smith in Sumeru City, makes three lenses for a spotting device and mentions Urstone as the pinnacle of his craft. Avin's mother, Niousha, tells them Avin has been depressed since an illness; Avin tells them she used to run with an Aranara friend and fears forgetting. The Aranara Arapurva answers the leaf, deems Yoimiya trustworthy because her heart is pure and fierce like the sun, and leads them to Mawtiyima Forest, where the dream begins.
### ULTRA +
Act I: Keisuke tells Sakujirou not to throw his life away and promises help if he returns, expecting to be demoted for letting him go. Yoimiya says she does not mind her workload because she wants her customers happy. She leaves her father a note when she runs her errands.

Act II: in the dream Avin touches a great blue stone and meets her Aranara friend Arashani, then produces a lens from her own chest that Yoimiya uses to find the best place for the shower. Yoimiya says afterwards that she already knew it was a dream but is grateful for the effort. Avin leaves with her mother, one of them says they will come to Inazuma to see the show, and before sailing Yoimiya gives the Traveler another Aranara carving.

## revelations
@version: 2.0
### LITE
Yoimiya shelters a wanted man at risk to herself and her family.
### STANDARD +
Ryuunosuke has been deaf since a workplace accident. Sakujirou, an Inazuman-born man, is wanted by the Tenryou Commission for leaving and returning during the Sakoku Decree.
### LARGE +
In Act II, Nahida says no one has any precise idea when a meteor shower will occur, and they cannot be predicted. The meteor shower seen in the quest happens in a dream.

## characters
### LITE
Yoimiya, the Traveler, Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Act I: Naganohara Ryuunosuke, Sakujirou, Kouichi, the Imatani couple (Saburou and his wife), Keisuke and Yoshitaka of the Tenryou Commission. Act II: Nahida, Avin and her mother Niousha, Ahangar, the Aranara Arapurva and Arashani.
### LARGE +
Fayez, Azar, Layla, Alhaitham and Farid are mentioned or appear briefly in Act II.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Act I: Sakujirou leaves Inazuma by boat and is reconciled with Keisuke. Act II: Avin regains confidence, and she or her mother plans to see the Naganohara show in Inazuma.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act I unlocks at Adventure Rank 30 after Chapter II Act I, The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia, and takes place during the Sakoku Decree. Act II unlocks at Adventure Rank 40 after Chapter III Act V, Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises; the Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter Act II, Transient Dreams; and the Aranara World Quest Dream Nursery. It was released in Version 3.7 and takes place after the Sakoku Decree has ended.
