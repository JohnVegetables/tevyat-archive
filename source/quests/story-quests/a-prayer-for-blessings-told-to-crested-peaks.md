## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Children of Echoes' chapter of the Tribal Chronicles, which also serves as Xilonen's story. A gem artisan named Cheven, rescued by the Traveler, leads them to Tlazolli, a retired Ancient Name forger who says she is nursing her sick daughter Nechca. In Act III it emerges that Nechca died years ago and the girl in Tlazolli's house is an orphan, Nepecha, whom Tlazolli has been remaking into her daughter with memory-stealing Blazegem Inscriptions. Xilonen and the Traveler stop the ritual and Nepecha is taken in by Xilonen.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three acts: Tepetli Dissonance, Hoarse Echoes and Melodious Chant. Acts I and II came with Version 5.0 and Act III with Version 5.1, after the Archon Quest act The Rainbow Destined to Burn. Melodious Chant has four quests: Threadlike Whispers, A Faint Signal Lingers, Wailing Cacophony and Serenity's Reprise. The first two acts read as a warm, small-scale errand story, and only the third reframes them.
### LARGE +
The chapter is about the Natlanese idea of a name as a blessing from the past to the future. Tlazolli's Ancient Name is Undugu ("Kinship"), and Xilonen accuses her of turning the name she gave her daughter into a prison. The Ancient Name craft that gives Natlan its heroes is here used as a tool for harm, which is why Xilonen, who forges Ancient Names for the tribe, has to act.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Act I the Traveler and Paimon meet a crying girl, Imix, whose master Cheven has not returned from gathering volcanic crystals. They find Cheven surrounded by Treasure Hoarders, drive them off, and learn she kept the purest crystals as a birthday gift for the sick daughter of Tlazolli, a former Ancient Name forger. Act II takes them to Tlazolli, who is out looking for her daughter after the child slipped out of bed; she presents a Blazegem Inscription, a decorative piece made with Ancient Name techniques, and a birthday party is planned. In Act III Xilonen joins the Traveler in visiting Tlazolli, notices that the Blazegem Inscriptions are quietly collecting memories of Nechca, catches a slip in the story about the book "Tequil the Brave", and pursues Tlazolli to her hidden workshop, where she tries to complete a ritual that would let her dead daughter return. The ritual fails, Nechca's true voice is heard once, and Xilonen rescues the girl, Nepecha.
### LARGE +
**Tepetli Dissonance (5.0).** The Traveler follows a crying Imix to Cheven, a gem artisan who runs Muhuru's Gemporium. Cheven was attacked after a Shadow Pin crash drew monsters to her find of high-purity Volcanic Crystal. She kept the best pieces for Tlazolli and her daughter Nechca, and invites the Traveler to visit the next day.

**Hoarse Echoes (5.0).** At Tlazolli's house nobody answers. Signs of a Tepetlisaur lead the Traveler to climb a cliff by indwelling one, but Tlazolli returns with her own Tepetlisaur, Iengu, saying she had been searching for Nechca, who is still feverish. Cheven's gift turns out to be a Blazegem Inscription made by Tlazolli; Paimon asks to buy one so their names can be engraved. They plan a party around Nechca's birthday, and Tlazolli says the girl will be glad to meet them.

**Threadlike Whispers (5.1).** Xilonen is bringing Nechca a copy of the old edition of "Tequil the Brave", which Nechca asked for in a letter, rather than the revised happy-ending edition. At Tlazolli's house, over Xocoatl, Tlazolli describes the night of the Abyss attack and says the doctor told her Nechca lost her memories to Abyssal damage. She asks the Traveler and Xilonen about their memories of Nechca. Xilonen notices that the Blazegem Inscriptions share the patterns of Ancient Names and borrows one from a delivery man.

**A Faint Signal Lingers (5.1).** In her workshop Xilonen explains what Ancient Names are and takes the Inscription apart with a phlogiston engraving circle. She finds no flaw in the engraving but recognises its use: gathering memories. Tlazolli admits she wants to forge a name that holds all of Nechca's past, and Xilonen agrees to help only under strict oversight, calling any extraction beyond that scope a crime. During an outing with "Nechca" the girl reveals she knows the original ending of "Tequil the Brave", which she should not remember if she had lost everything before her illness. Xilonen presses her and the girl cries out for help.

**Wailing Cacophony (5.1).** The Traveler and Xilonen pursue Tlazolli through her supposedly closed workshop, fighting hired guards. Tlazolli reveals the girl is a child from her tribe whose parents died in the same Abyss attack as Nechca and who shares her birthday. She begins a ritual to turn the Central Inscription into Nechca's Ancient Name. Xilonen triggers a flaw in the engraving; the Inscriptions release the painful feelings collected with the memories, and Nechca's real spirit speaks, giving a blessing to the girl, Nepecha. Xilonen explains to Tlazolli that the grey figures were projections of grief and reminds her of her own Ancient Name. Afterwards Nepecha describes the isolation and the sleeping medicine, and Xilonen gives her a plain stone engraved with her name.

**Serenity's Reprise (5.1).** The next day Xilonen reports that the tribe is deciding on Tlazolli's fate, her workshop will close, and the memories absorbed by the Inscriptions will return to their owners once the Inscriptions stop working. Nepecha rests in Xilonen's workshop while Xilonen plans to investigate her history and teach her crafting skills.

### ULTRA +
Finer points. Tlazolli says on the night of the attack she was in the tribe discussing the forging of new Ancient Names and returned with guards to find Nechca gone; she had told her daughter that if she brought an Embercore Flower, it would be material for an Ancient Name for her. Xilonen explains that normal Ancient Names work with the Wayob's help to read information held in the Ley Lines, and that doing the same without a Wayob would overwhelm a person; Tlazolli's plan replaced the Ley Lines with a "memory bank" built from villagers' memories through the Inscriptions. Xilonen explains that she forges Ancient Names by gathering stories about a hero and filtering out exaggeration to find a fitting "nickname" for them. Xilonen's test of the lie was simple: if Nechca had truly lost her memory, she could not know an ending to a book published before her illness. Tlazolli tells the Traveler and Xilonen, in the final confrontation, that she wanted to invite them to the birthday party of the returned Nechca.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nechca died the night of an Abyss attack years ago. The girl in Tlazolli's house is Nepecha, an orphan from the same tribe, passed off as Nechca. Tlazolli used Blazegem Inscriptions, a deviation from Ancient Name engraving she first treated as a defect, to take the tribe's memories of Nechca and to overwrite Nepecha's own. A sleeping medicine and monitored letters kept the girl isolated and confused.
### LARGE +
The Inscriptions also soaked up the emotions of those who remembered Nechca's last days, which is why the "Nechca" the ritual called up was a grey projection of her illness rather than her true self. Early details that look benign are retroactively sinister: the sickly daughter, the letters, the birthday present and the party. Tlazolli believes she is saving her daughter; the quest treats her grief sympathetically while not excusing what she did.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon are the viewpoint pair. Xilonen, who forges Ancient Names for the tribe and grew up around Tlazolli's workshop, is the investigator and carries the chapter. Tlazolli is both antagonist and grieving mother. Nepecha is a young girl; Nechca appears only as a spirit. Cheven and Imix open the story. Xilonen says that Pacal, a tribal leader, will have a lot on his plate deciding Tlazolli's case.
### LARGE +
Xilonen calls Tlazolli "Auntie", says she did not suspect her at first, but kept a contingency plan in mind and took the Central Inscription herself so she could take it apart. She credits Nepecha's persistence for the rescue. Avilix and the merchants Iknal and Apoxpalon appear in minor roles.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tlazolli is taken to the tribe for judgement, her workshop is to be closed permanently and the unfinished Blazegem Inscriptions destroyed. Memories of Nechca held in the finished Inscriptions return to their owners when the Inscriptions stop working. Nepecha is taken in by Xilonen and, according to the wiki, later stands as an open-world NPC at Xilonen's workshop where she studies and keeps a diary.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: Acts I and II in Version 5.0, Act III in Version 5.1. Story order: Act III requires the Archon Quest act The Rainbow Destined to Burn, so it takes place after the Abyss invasion of Natlan. Acts I and II only require the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame quest.

## entry: Tlazolli
@keys: Tlazolli
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tlazolli is a retired Ancient Name forger of the Children of Echoes who lives away from the tribe, and the older woman Xilonen calls Auntie. Her Ancient Name is "Undugu" (Kinship). After her daughter Nechca died in an Abyss attack she turned a defective Ancient Name design into the Blazegem Inscription to steal memories of Nechca and replace those of the orphan Nepecha. Xilonen stopped the ritual. Her punishment is left unsettled in the quest.

## entry: Nepecha
@keys: Nepecha
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nepecha is a young girl orphaned by the same Abyss attack that killed Nechca. Tlazolli passed her off as Nechca for years, isolating her and giving her a medicine that made her sleepy and forgetful. After the rescue she remembers only her name; Xilonen engraves it on a stone as a blessing and takes her in. Her origin is unknown.

## entry: Nechca
@keys: Nechca
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nechca was Tlazolli's daughter, who died the day of an Abyss attack on the tribe, holding a dried Embercore Flower. For years the tribe was told she was alive and ill. In the ritual her real spirit briefly speaks and blesses Nepecha, saying they had known each other a while but never met.

## entry: Blazegem Inscription
@keys: Blazegem Inscription, Blazegem Inscriptions, Central Inscription
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Blazegem Inscriptions are decorative accessories the Children of Echoes make with techniques related to Ancient Name engraving. Tlazolli's versions gather memories and feelings about Nechca from their holders and erase the holders' own memories of her; they are governed by a Central Inscription. They stop working after her ritual fails and unfinished ones are destroyed.

## entry: Tequil the Brave
@keys: Tequil the Brave
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
A Natlanese storybook with two editions. In the original the brothers Tequil and Rimac are separated by tragedy and one asks the other to remember his name. A revised edition gives both brothers a happy ending but was poorly received. Nechca's knowledge of the old ending exposed that the girl in the house had read the first edition.
