## summary
### LITE
The Crystallina Chapter is Skirk's Story Quest, released in Version 5.7. It has one act, Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night. After a nightmare in which something Abyssal stabs the Traveler, Skirk appears in Liyue, says part of the Traveler's soul has been taken, and agrees to help recover it if the Traveler helps her find the thief. The thief proves to be Skofnungr, an old friend of her master Surtalogi.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: Sinister Undercurrent, Special Training, All Devouring, Heart and Mind and Unbounded. Most of it takes place in dreams and in an Abyssal space reached through Skirk, so it is a character study of Skirk as much as a case to solve. It requires the Archon Quest Masquerade of the Guilty (Chapter IV, Act V) and Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
Its central questions are what Skirk is, why she cut herself off from feeling, and how she relates to her master's legacy. The Traveler is both her student for a short time and the person who helps her accept her own emotions, and the chapter ends with a farewell that hopes for a peaceful "reborn world" at the end of the Traveler's journey.
### ULTRA +
In it the Traveler sees Skirk's inner world, and it shows her origin on a distant planet, her training under Surtalogi and her entry into Teyvat with Alice's help. The chapter has no further acts as of 7.1.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler feels unwell, and Baizhu finds nothing physically wrong. In a dream an Abyssal being stabs them. At Wanmin Restaurant, Skirk sits beside them and says the wound in their soul carries Abyssal corrosion. She trains the Traveler, enters Skirk's memories, then they track the culprit to the Chasm. After a retreat and a crisis inside Skirk's own mind, they return and unmask the culprit as Skofnungr, a Khaenri'ahn follower of Surtalogi, whom Skirk spares by destroying his consciousness.
### STANDARD
The Traveler wakes from a dream in which an Abyssal being stabbed them, and Baizhu finds nothing physically wrong. Skirk joins them at Wanmin Restaurant, says part of their soul is damaged by Abyssal corrosion, and meets them at Guili Plains, where Rifthounds are collecting god remains. She believes a god-level entity is stealing sources of power, including the Stella Fortuna, and offers to help recover the Traveler's soul if they help identify it. She trains the Traveler in bladework, Elemental Skills and reasoning, and the Traveler enters her dreams and sees her burned village, her master Surtalogi and her entry into Teyvat with Alice's help. After three answered questions (she is from outside Teyvat but not a Descender, came to learn the source of her master's strength, and hides from Tartaglia so he finds his own strength) they follow the culprit into the Chasm, where Skirk is wounded and they retreat. In her inner world the Traveler helps her accept her suppressed emotions. Back in the Abyssal space the culprit, the Dark Shadow, is revealed as Skofnungr, a Khaenri'ahn friend of Surtalogi who grafted narwhal flesh into his heart. Skirk spares him by destroying his consciousness and leaves.
### LARGE +
**Sinister Undercurrent.** The Traveler feels agitated and unwell. Paimon takes them to Bubu Pharmacy; Baizhu finds nothing physical and suggests a non-physical cause. Asleep, the Traveler stands in a foggy Abyssal realm and an Abyssal being stabs them in the back, but they wake unharmed. At Wanmin Restaurant, Paimon recognises the woman who sits down and orders wine as Childe's master, Skirk. She believes a piece of the Traveler's soul is damaged and bears the marks of Abyssal corrosion, and asks to meet at Guili Plains. Chef Mao later says only the Traveler and Paimon were at the table. At Guili Plains, Rifthounds are gathering god remains. Skirk says a possibly god-level entity is stealing sources of power across the world, including the Stella Fortuna, and offers to help recover the Traveler's soul if they help identify it. She takes them into the Abyss for safety and offers to mirror the Traveler's dreams.

**Special Training.** Skirk trains the Traveler in bladework, Elemental Skills and reasoning. When the Traveler sleeps they enter Skirk's dream, follow a strange being and see fragments of her childhood: a merchant ship arriving on her planet, her fascination with the outside, and the ship's people burning her village while her parents tell her to flee. Skirk explains that her powers are Abyssal but disguised as Cryo because of the laws of Teyvat. She notes that the Traveler uses and changes elements without a Vision, and urges them to forge their own path. A mock battle teaches that fighting means setting emotions aside: she exploits the Traveler's hesitation when their sibling is mentioned, then repeats the drill while they sort their feelings. Further dreams show her training under Surtalogi and with other disciples, and entering Teyvat with Alice's help. Afterwards she grants three questions. She is surprised that the Traveler saw her inner world and asks for secrecy, says she is from outside but not a Descender, says she came to Teyvat to find where her master's strength came from, and explains she hides from Tartaglia so he will find his own strength before they meet again.

**All Devouring.** In the Chasm domain, Skirk notices the attacks aim at her; a spike pierces her shoulder, but she crushes it and returns the stolen soul fragment to the Traveler. She reasons the thief wanted the Traveler's ability to purify Abyssal energy so as to draw power from her, asks how it came by the All-Devouring Narwhal's power, and realises it must know Surtalogi, calling him "The Foul". The entity is angered. Because Skirk is hurt and Paimon cannot defend herself, they retreat to Bubu Pharmacy, where Skirk meditates for two days. The strange being then draws the Traveler into her inner world.

**Heart and Mind.** The inner world appears as a shattered river, which the being says shows how she cut off her emotions and started over each time she met weakness. Going upstream, the Traveler relives her arrival in Teyvat, her motives and the fate of her people. The being reveals itself as her suppressed emotions. Skirk calls it weak; the Traveler answers that its ability to break through shows strength. She accepts it, and the Traveler offers to help against Surtalogi if the time comes. Back in the real world, Paimon brings snacks and Skirk warns that her power is now greater.

**Unbounded.** In the Abyssal space the fog has cleared and the culprit appears as the Dark Shadow. Skirk says those who long for Surtalogi's strength imitate him unconsciously. She wounds herself deliberately so the entity will absorb her power; its armour breaks and a Khaenri'ahn man is revealed. He names himself Skofnungr, a friend and follower of Surtalogi. He tells how he was a young heir cursed with an incurable illness and shunned until Surtalogi told him to fight for his life, and how, after the cataclysm and Ronova's curse, Surtalogi offered to lift the curse and left him a weakened narwhal. He grafted its flesh into his heart. Skirk offers him an alternative to death: destroying his consciousness to remove the stolen power, after which the curse of immortality will slowly restore body and mind. He accepts, hoping to wake in time to see her fight. Skirk then leaves, saying she and the Traveler may or may not meet again, but hoping for a reborn world of peace; Paimon proposes stargazing on the spot.

Detail on Skofnungr: he says Surtalogi quickly surpassed him and that he was embarrassed to spar him later; Surtalogi told him of his departure to the stars and that he would not return for many years. Skirk tells him that several hundred years have passed. After the deed, Skofnungr concedes that he has reached his peak and is glad others are helping Skirk reach her goals.

On the structure: the soul-theft case frames the act, but the real conflict is Skirk's. Training is used to move the Traveler into her dreams, and the culprit is defeated only after she stops separating her power from her emotions (she says she will need time to practise with her power no longer split by emotion).
### ULTRA +
The wiki summary says Skirk offers three questions "much like" she did with Tartaglia. Her lesson that a fighter must set emotion aside is later complicated by the discovery that doing so permanently was her own flaw. Skirk also asks the Traveler not to tell Paimon what they discussed.

## revelations
@version: 5.7
### STANDARD
- Skirk is from beyond Teyvat but says she is not a Descender; she is "just a visitor passing by".
- Her powers are Abyssal; she disguises them as Cryo because of Teyvat's laws.
- Her own planet's people were attacked by the crew of a merchant ship and her village burned; she was a child at the time and her parents told her to flee.
- She came to Teyvat to learn where her master Surtalogi's strength came from, after entering with Alice's help.
- She avoids Tartaglia deliberately so he forges his own path.
- A Khaenri'ahn called Skofnungr, a friend of Surtalogi, grafted flesh of a weakened All-Devouring Narwhal into his heart and became the Dark Shadow.
- Surtalogi left Khaenri'ah for the stars after the cataclysm, and Skirk says it has been several hundred years.
### LARGE +
- Skofnungr was condemned by an incurable illness as a child, was shunned by peers and family, and refused Surtalogi's offer to lift Ronova's curse because he wanted to grow stronger.
- The thief was a man in armour, not a nameless Abyssal monster; the god remains and the Stella Fortuna that Skirk said were being collected were part of the power-gathering she described.
### ULTRA +
- The Traveler's ability to purify Abyssal energy is treated as significant by Skirk.
- The being in Skirk's inner world is her suppressed emotions, which she had been dismissing as weakness.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Skirk**: the chapter's protagonist.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: the victim of the soul-theft and their guide; Skirk asks the Traveler not to tell Paimon what they discussed.
- **Baizhu**: the physician at Bubu Pharmacy who first examines the Traveler.
- **Skofnungr**: the culprit and a Khaenri'ahn friend of Surtalogi.
- **Surtalogi**: Skirk's master, shown in memories; also mentioned by Skofnungr.
- **Chef Mao**: the Wanmin Restaurant chef who saw only two people at the table.
### LARGE +
Alice appears only in Skirk's memories (of entering Teyvat). Tartaglia is discussed, not present, and Ronova is mentioned in Skofnungr's account.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler's stolen soul fragment is returned. Skirk accepts her suppressed emotions, destroys Skofnungr's consciousness to strip his stolen power, and departs, with no promise to meet again.
### LARGE +
Skofnungr is left alive but unconscious, with his curse expected to heal him slowly. Skirk's power is no longer split from her emotions, and the Traveler has pledged to help her against Surtalogi if the time comes.

## placement
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.7. It requires Masquerade of the Guilty (Chapter IV, Act V) and Adventure Rank 40, so it comes after the Fontaine Archon Quest in which Skirk first appeared. Story order and release order match. The chapter has no later acts as of 7.1.
### LARGE +
During Version 5.7 a companion event of the same name ran from 2025-06-18 to 2025-07-08, giving Primogems, ascension and level-up materials and Natlan specialties for completing the quest in time, and offered a Quick Start for players at Adventure Rank 28 who had finished Chapter I, Act III (A New Star Approaches). The quest itself remains playable afterward; only the event rewards and Quick Start window were limited.

## entry: Skirk's Inner World
@keys: Skirk's inner world, Skirk's dream, broken river
@keys2: Traveler, Skirk, Crystallina
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A dreamscape inside Skirk, seen only by the Traveler. It appears as a shattered river, each break marking a time she shut out her emotions to start again. Memories line its banks: her planet burning, Surtalogi's training, other disciples, entering Teyvat. A strange being that guides the Traveler is her suppressed emotions. She is initially hostile to being seen, and the Traveler keeps the secret from Paimon.
### LARGE +
Upstream, the Traveler reaches Skirk herself and persuades her to accept the being. She had forgotten her reason for seeking strength after severing her feelings.

## entry: Skofnungr and the Dark Shadow
@keys: Skofnungr, Dark Shadow
@keys2: Skirk, Surtalogi, Crystallina Chapter
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Skofnungr is a Khaenri'ahn, friend and follower of Surtalogi, who wore the form of the Dark Shadow after grafting weakened narwhal flesh into his own heart. He was the soul-thief in Skirk's chapter. He was an heir with an incurable illness, was helped by Surtalogi, refused to have the curse removed after the cataclysm, and hoped to grow stronger. Skirk spares him by destroying his consciousness and leaving his immortality-curse to heal him.
### LARGE +
He asked to be killed but accepted Skirk's alternative, hoping to wake in time to watch her fight Surtalogi.

Skirk's lessons to the Traveler cover bladework, Elemental Skills and reasoning. They also include a mock battle in which the Traveler is told to fight without emotion, and a second round where they must finish while sorting through feelings.
