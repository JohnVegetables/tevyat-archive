## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The Tribal Chronicles chapter of the People of the Springs, which also serves as Mualani's story quest. The tribe has an old legend of a mysterious island, hidden in a rarely visited domain, that promises great wealth and glory. After helping the chief Amina expose a ring of tourist scammers, the Traveler learns the single condition for reaching the island: travel with companions you would trust with your life. The Traveler and Paimon ask Mualani to guide them, and together with two Sumeru scholars they complete the trip.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three acts: Those Searching for the Mysterious Island, The Legend of the Mysterious Island and Journey to the Mysterious Island. It is a story about what a journey is for. The island is real, but the treasure the legend advertises is not what the first chief found, and the Traveler and Mualani choose to keep the ending secret so that the legend can go on inviting new guides.
### LARGE +
Thematically it sets "result" against "journey". Viramdra, a scholar whose earlier research was stolen, chases a result to prove himself, and the tribe's guide Koolau has claimed a result he never earned. Mualani, her teacher Nuu and the first chief all arrive at the same answer: a guide's duty is to bring clients home safely, and the companions who travelled with you are what you actually bring back.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I. While exploring the People of the Springs, the Traveler and Paimon rescue two Sumeru scholars, Karya and Viramdra, from Koholasaurs. The scholars are searching for the mysterious island for Viramdra's research, and Paimon decides she wants to see it too. They go to the tribe's chief to ask what is known.

Act II. The chief Amina tells them the legend of the first chief and the island, and asks a favour: pose as gullible tourists so that a gang of fake guides can be caught. The Traveler and Paimon are led around by Kaulu and Kaeho, who charge for photos and gear, until Kaulu's shortcut ends at a plainly fake "island" and he reveals his trick, only to be seized by Amina and a group of tribespeople. Amina explains the real requirement: the island can only be reached by a group of close friends who trust each other with their lives, so a hired guide is no use. Paimon realises they should ask Mualani.

Act III. Mualani agrees to guide the Traveler and Paimon, and the party also takes in Karya and Viramdra. Mualani buys camping gear, receives a copy of the first chief's map and a protective talisman from her teacher Nuu, then leads the group through four stages: sneaking into a valley of gathering Koholasaurs to copy a clue, working Phlogiston Nodes to open a way through a Spiritway and toward a giant whirlpool, a rescue of Viramdra, and a descent into a volcanic cave. There Viramdra recognises a golden glow as the island, Mualani turns the group back, and they discover the real answer at the tribe's founding statue.
### LARGE +
**Those Searching for the Mysterious Island (5.0).** Three Koholasaurs are attacking Karya and Viramdra when the Traveler and Paimon arrive. Viramdra, short on tact, suggests using himself as bait to capture a live Koholasaur; Karya apologises for his manner. The scholars explain they are following Koholasaur clues to the mysterious island for his research.

**The Legend of the Mysterious Island (5.0).** Amina knows the Traveler and Paimon on sight because, as chief, she keeps track of anything that might affect Natlan. She gives them a coin pouch for expenses. Kaulu first offers to take them to the island for free, then a second man, Kaeho, sells them a photo with the elder Koholasaur Ata; Kaulu hands over "expedition equipment" and the photo as supposed proof. At the end the writing on their receipts has faded and the scammers used false names. Amina's tribespeople catch Kaulu in the act.

**Someone Trustworthy (5.0).** Back at the People of the Springs the Traveler and Paimon find Mualani selling swimming floaters. She says the trip is hard, since even her teacher Nuu never reached the island, but she makes an exception because they are her friends. She insists on paying for a tent herself, saying they are travelling as friends and not on a paid commission. A record and a record player (to be played at night for safety) are bought from other shopkeepers. Nuu gives her the map and a talisman made from an unusual Spinel Fruit, telling her to return it afterwards and reminding her that a guide's only task is to bring guests safely to their destination. Koolau, the tribe's top-ranked guide, turns Viramdra down and recommends Mualani instead, and Mualani wants to prove herself against his reputation.

**Where the Koholasaurs Gather (5.0).** Mualani has the party touch the first chief's statue and pray to the ancestors before leaving. The first clue is in a valley where wild Koholasaurs gather. Viramdra wants to drive them off, which Mualani refuses on tribal custom, and Karya's remark that Koholasaurs track by smell leads to the plan of masking the party's scent with Sprayfeather Gill. The Traveler, Paimon and Mualani reach a drawing on a rock that Mualani identifies as the water-and-land boundaries of Jadeskirt Knoll, which gives her the next destination.

**The Riddle of Sea and Sky (5.0).** Viramdra proposes that the map points to a dragon-built mechanism and names it a Phlogiston Node. Only the Traveler can operate the nodes, while Mualani rides the resulting Spiritway above the water. When the nodes are spent, whirlpools form and a giant one appears, which Mualani's reading of the map says the party must enter. Viramdra disagrees and wades in to test his own theory about shallow water, is swept off, and is rescued by Mualani. The whirlpools close for the night.

**The Mysterious Island's Truth (5.0).** Next morning Karya and Viramdra operate the nodes themselves, the whirlpool reopens, and the party enters a volcanic cavern. The Spinel Fruit growing there matches the talisman. Viramdra identifies a golden glow in the distance as Vulcanite and runs for it despite Mualani's reminder that the map says to go the opposite way when the golden light appears. Monsters rise from the lava, Mualani rides her surfboard across lava to bring Viramdra back, Karya slaps him, and a whistle from Mualani summons Koholasaurs that carry the party out. At the statue the Traveler reads the first chief's message (see revelations), Nuu confirms it, and Mualani returns the talisman. The chapter ends with a group photo.
### ULTRA +
Finer points. The journey is built around a trust rule that the quest tests rather than merely states. Mualani asks the others to trust her judgment, and every time the party disagrees she reaffirms the agreement. When Viramdra tries to leave the group in The Riddle of Sea and Sky she refuses, saying a party that splits mid-journey will have the worst luck. At the whirlpool she also offers the Traveler and Paimon, as the clients, the chance to end the trip. In The Mysterious Island's Truth the temptation reappears in a harder form: Viramdra points out that Mualani alone could cross the lava, claim the highest honour a guide can have and silence Koolau's doubts, and she turns it down because the others could not get back. Viramdra's stated reason for wanting the ore is that his earlier findings were stolen and he thinks only a "result" can restore what he lost. After the first chief's message disappears, he admits the ore, which forms only at the start of fierce lava activity and is swallowed by it, could never have been brought back. He asks Karya whether she will keep working with him and she says he must first prepare a proposal. When Koolau arrives to ask whether Mualani found the island, Mualani says she found something more precious: two friends. Nuu tells Koolau that nobody would deny his abilities, but that what they would deny about him is something only he can answer.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The mysterious island of legend is the place the People of the Springs live now. At the end of the cave journey, hidden text on the first chief's statue (visible only to those whose bodies carry volcanic dust from the underground space) tells how he went in search of the island to prove himself and travelled with friends who helped and trusted him. At the end he saw that the golden glow signalled an imminent eruption, chose not to press on, returned empty-handed, and realised that his surviving friends were the treasure. They went home together and founded the tribe.
### LARGE +
The message disappears as the dust wears off, and Nuu explains that this is the final choice the first chief left: a guide may boast of the feat and receive the highest honour, destroying the legend, or stay silent and let the legend continue to draw new guides into a lesson. Nuu and other tribe members who completed the journey all kept silent. The legend and the map exist to teach younger generations what the first chief learned, and Nuu holds that guides who forget this become like Koolau. The golden glow itself is explained by Viramdra as Vulcanite, a rare ore formed when large amounts of lava cool quickly, which appears only at the start of fierce lava activity; the quest treats this as a warning rather than a treasure.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon are the viewpoint pair. Mualani, the tribe's young surfer and guide, leads the main journey and is the chapter's emotional centre. Amina, the tribe's chief, appears in Act II and sets out the requirement for reaching the island. Nuu is Mualani's teacher and the keeper of a copy of the map. Viramdra and Karya are Sumeru Akademiya scholars: Viramdra of the Spantamad Darshan specialises in ores and minerals, and Karya is his assistant. Koolau is the tribe's top-ranked guide. Kaulu and Kaeho are the fake guides in Act II.
### LARGE +
Yelafath, Anchieta and Pakaa Nui are shopkeepers Mualani buys from in Someone Trustworthy, and Suya and Seketo are tribespeople who bring Koolau news of the crystals he found. Ata is the elder Koholasaur whom Kaeho uses for his photo scam.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mualani completes her teacher's test and returns the talisman, which Nuu tells her to pass on to her own student one day. Karya and Viramdra part from the group, with Viramdra agreeing to keep the journey secret and promising to acknowledge Mualani in his next paper. The Traveler receives a commemorative group photo with Mualani. Amina's ring of scammers is exposed. The legend of the mysterious island stays in circulation, and the tribe's guides keep its true answer to themselves.
### LARGE +
Koolau protests that he has proven himself, is told by Nuu that only he can find the answer to what the tribe would deny about him, and leaves unresolved. Mualani's own view of the trip changes: she says the results no longer matter to her after what the group went through together.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: the chapter's three acts came with Version 5.0 (the wiki gives the event window as 2024-08-28 to 2024-09-17). Story order: Acts I and II require only the Archon Quest part Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, while Act III requires Into Eternal Night, in the Chapter V part Black Stone Under a White Stone, so it takes place after the Traveler and Mualani have visited the Night Kingdom together. Someone Trustworthy refers back to that trip as proof the Traveler and Paimon can trust Mualani.

## entry: The Mysterious Island and the First Chief
@keys: mysterious island, First Chief, Map Left Behind by the First Chief
@keys2: People of the Springs, Mualani, Amina, Nuu
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The People of the Springs tell of their first chief, once Natlan's most celebrated guide, who went in search of a mysterious island said to shine with gold and hold countless treasures, reached it, and left behind a map of strange symbols and riddles instead of claiming the treasure. Copies were given to the tribe's first members and are passed from teacher to student. Guides must read the map by experience. The stated requirement is to travel with friends you trust with your life. The island is in fact the tribe's own home, as revealed by a message on the founding statue only visible to those who went through the underground volcanic cave.

## entry: Viramdra and Karya
@keys: Viramdra, Karya
@keys2: Sumeru, Akademiya, mysterious island, Mualani
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two Sumeru scholars the Traveler meets in the People of the Springs. Viramdra, of the Akademiya's Spantamad Darshan, studies ores and minerals, has a blunt manner, and has lost earlier research to theft; he is driven by the belief that only a tangible result can prove his worth. Karya is his assistant, apologises for him, and eventually becomes the person who talks him down. Both travel with Mualani on the journey and part as friends of the Traveler's party.

## entry: Koolau
@keys: Koolau
@keys2: People of the Springs, Mualani, Nuu, mysterious island
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The top-ranked guide of the People of the Springs, who boasts of having been to the mysterious island alone and of having found rare crystals in an underground ruin. He turns Viramdra away as unskilled, then recommends Mualani to him, which Nuu suggests was because he thought she could not reach the island and so could not expose him. Nuu says Koolau never completed the trip, though Koolau denies it.

## entry: Nuu's Choice
@keys: Uncle Nuu, Nuu
@keys2: Mualani, mysterious island, talisman
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nuu is Mualani's teacher. He reached the mysterious island long before her and, with his companions, chose to tell no one so that the legend could keep inspiring young guides. He gives Mualani a talisman made from an unusual Spinel Fruit that turns out to match the fruit growing in the underground cave, and takes it back after she completes the journey. He tells her that a guide's duty is to deliver clients safely, not to prove anything.

## entry: The Island Scam
@keys: Kaulu, Kaeho
@keys2: scam, Amina, People of the Springs
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A small ring of tribespeople, including Kaulu and Kaeho, who sold tourists fake guided trips to the mysterious island, with photos and gear sold at inflated prices and receipts written in ink that faded. They claimed the fees as travel expenses so that the money was hard to prosecute, and used false names. Amina asked the Traveler and Paimon to pose as victims and gather evidence. Kaulu was caught after his fake island reveal.
