## summary
### LITE
Alhaitham's Story Quest. As Acting Grand Sage, he joins the Traveler and the Matra in tracing a researcher, Ilyas, who was abducted by Vahumana scholar Siraj. Siraj is building a ranked 'hive mind' of followers, and Alhaitham, who rejected Siraj's paper years ago, takes it apart.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, The Illusions of the Mob, made of three quests: Fragmented Testimony, A Thorough Investigation and Pride and Prejudice. It uses Alhaitham's blunt, unsentimental style to show how a group can become a trap for the people who join it.
### LARGE +
The story is about belonging, rank and ego. Siraj's scheme appeals to scholars who feel passed over after the sages were removed, while Alhaitham's irreverence toward both the title and the crowd is what defeats it.

## plot
### LITE
Researchers at the Akademiya gossip about Alhaitham. Ilyas says Siraj abducted him; the Matra confirm it. Alhaitham explains that Siraj was a classmate whose paper on human evolution he rejected. The trail leads from Ilyas' former colleagues to Siraj's hideout, where Ilyas' own earlier choices come out. Alhaitham undermines Siraj's system and Cyno has him detained. Afterwards Alhaitham explains at home that he has resigned the Grand Sage post.
### STANDARD +
Fragmented Testimony: Ilyas asks to speak to the Matra. Alhaitham joins Aarav's investigation, and the group go to Lambad's Tavern to question Narendra, who sold electrical parts to Siraj. Alhaitham says he and Siraj were classmates, and that he rejected Siraj's thesis on human evolution when Azar asked his opinion. Ilyas wants his paycheck for his family in Gandharva Ville.

A Thorough Investigation: Makesh says Ilyas can collect his pay if he learns why Chandra withdrew. Chandra explains that, with the sages gone and the Akasha off, researchers wait for a famous name. Ilyas recalls Siraj calling him 'Siraj #36'. Alhaitham explains that Siraj wanted a rank-based hive mind with an overmind at the top. Janaki declined a membership because she was ranked #45, based on Jnana energy once taken by the sages. The House of Daena papers show Ilyas had been honoured for reporting his instructor's fraud. At Gandharva Ville, Ilyas' family says they have received threatening letters for his 'heroism', and he guides them to the hideout.

Pride and Prejudice: Ilyas vanishes at the hideout. Visions show his past, in which his report on Jani led to Raju's death and angered other colleagues, and that he later joined Siraj willingly. In the domain Ilyas says he wanted to meet them as himself before merging into the Hive. Siraj defends his project, Alhaitham says its model is unstable, and monsters appear. The collective begins to question Siraj and collapses. Cyno arrives and the Matra detain Siraj. At home Alhaitham explains why he disapproved of the thesis and resigned.
### LARGE +
Fragmented Testimony: Paimon is surprised to find Alhaitham among the Matra, who joins Aarav's party after leaving a message for Cyno. Alhaitham says he dislikes being Acting Grand Sage. Ilyas almost recognises him but does not connect. Aarav allows Alhaitham to take Ilyas.

A Thorough Investigation: Chandra says Siraj is gaining popularity and many want to join, though he doubts he could. Alhaitham tells Ilyas not to rush regaining memories. In the Research Preparation Center, Makesh says Ilyas' coworkers took over his work while he was gone.

Pride and Prejudice: Alhaitham explains that after Ilyas met him, Ilyas' perception began to influence the other members; Siraj manages to repair this and summons monsters. Alhaitham says his 'gift' had already been sent and needed time. He claims he had known everything from the start and had never looked down on Siraj, whose fragile ego caused his downfall. He refuses to arrest Siraj himself. Cyno has the Matra take him and busy themselves detaining Siraj's associates. The group then visit Alhaitham's home, where Kaveh, embarrassed, is surprised to see guests. Alhaitham fetches books on consciousness. He says he rejected the thesis because it touched human evolution, one of the Akademiya's cardinal sins, and because the approach was conservative and the research extreme. He submitted his resignation as Acting Grand Sage because the longer he held the post, the more likely it would become permanent.
### ULTRA +
The investigation follows Ilyas' ordinary life: his pay, his workplace and his family. Siraj's ranking used Jnana energy that the sages had extracted, according to Janaki. Ilyas' report on his instructor was meant as justice but, in the visions, is followed by a colleague's death, and his later choice to join Siraj is shown as his own.

## revelations
### LITE
Siraj wanted to merge scholars into a ranked collective mind. Alhaitham rejected his paper long ago.
### STANDARD +
Siraj ranked followers by Jnana energy the sages had extracted. Alhaitham resigned as Acting Grand Sage.
### LARGE +
Ilyas joined Siraj knowingly after his own report on a colleague led to tragedy.
### ULTRA +
Alhaitham says he expected Siraj's collapse, which is his own claim and unconfirmed by others.

## characters
### LITE
Alhaitham, Ilyas, Siraj, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Matra Aarav, Cyno, Kaveh, and Akademiya figures Narendra, Makesh, Chandra and Janaki.
### LARGE +
Ilyas' former colleagues Raju, Jani and Jaap appear in visions. Azar's earlier removal is referenced.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Siraj is detained and his associates arrested. Alhaitham submits his resignation as Acting Grand Sage.
### LARGE +
The Hive collapses and members, including Ilyas, are cared for by the Matra. Alhaitham explains himself to the Traveler at his home.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises (Chapter III Act V). Released in 3.4 on 2023-01-18, so it takes place after the Sumeru Archon Quest, once Azar has been replaced.

## entry: Siraj

@keys2: Alhaitham, Ilyas
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A prominent Vahumana scholar and Alhaitham's former classmate who was expected to become a sage. His thesis on human evolution was sent to Azar, who asked Alhaitham's opinion, and Alhaitham rejected it. He built a rank-ordered collective of followers, the Hive, using their Jnana energy. It collapsed in the Vultur Volans Chapter and he was detained by the Matra.

### STANDARD
