## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Yelan's Story Quest, released in Version 2.7 and consisting of one act, Calculated Gambit. Uncle Tian, the Tianshu of the Liyue Qixing, is ready to retire, and Yelan is helping him assess three candidates. Investigating their backgrounds with the Traveler, she finds that the apparently ideal candidate, Zhiyi, has been propped up by a Fatui scheme. She feigns choosing him, lets him lower his guard, then catches him with the Fatui's ambassador at the Qingxu Pool ruins.
### STANDARD +
The chapter name is Latin, "Umbrabilis Orchis", roughly "Shadowy Orchid"; the Chinese title means the same, since "shadowy guest" is an old name for the orchid, and it is a nod to Yelan. Calculated Gambit has three quests: Seven-Star Selection, Candidature Analysis and Foregone Conclusion. It requires Adventure Rank 40 plus the Interlude Chapter act Perilous Trail and the Sinae Unicornis Chapter act Sea of Clouds, Sea of People. The Chinese act title echoes a saying from the board game Go, that a game is won at the point where an opponent's advance is cut off.
### LARGE +
The story is an investigation told as a game of deception. Yelan deliberately plays out a false verdict, and the Traveler serves as her second pair of eyes, tailing suspects and noticing details such as a cold floral scent. The chapter shows her cunning, her dry humour and her preference for the thrill of her present work over high office.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Liyue Harbor the Traveler and Paimon meet Huixin, daughter of Uncle Tian, who says her father is thinking of stepping down as Tianshu. At the Yanshang Teahouse Tian explains how the succession works and introduces Yelan, who is assisting him as an assessment official. Yelan says she would like the post herself, and Tian agrees she may have it if no candidate proves worthy. The candidates are Qianwei, clever but arrogant; Mingbo, nervous; and Zhiyi, who seems flawless. Yelan checks their backgrounds. Her informants report that Qianwei and Mingbo have faults but are decent people, while Zhiyi's reputation is suspiciously polished: tales of cruelty toward him in youth, kindness to those who disliked him, and a recent turn from bad rumours to good. Yelan connects a rumour of treasure in some ruins beneath Qingxu Pool with newcomers at the Blackcliff Forge who keep apart from the other workers. Rain lets her follow their footprints to an abandoned camp, where she and the Traveler catch a cold, floral scent that the Traveler recognises as Snezhnayan, pointing at the Fatui. She announces Zhiyi as the winner to make him complacent, has him followed, and learns of an ambassador, Yusupov, heading to the Qingxu Pool. Beneath the pool she and the Traveler overhear Zhiyi and Yusupov celebrating, and Yelan then confronts Zhiyi with the Millelith.
### LARGE +
**Seven-Star Selection.** Zhiyi admits that Tian personally tutored all three candidates. When he brings Tian a fish soup recipe, improved with seasonings he added, the recipe becomes a detail Yelan later uses. Yelan calls in her informants Wenyuan and Shanghua; the third, Wupei, is missing, so she looks into Zhiyi herself with the Traveler and Paimon, beginning with the broker Bolai on the docks.

**Candidature Analysis.** Bolai says Zhiyi's parents died when he was young and he was treated cruelly, yet he supported the family of someone who had disliked him once that person fell on hard times. The Traveler tails Zhiyi to Linlang, where he buys wineware, then to the south wharf, where Uncle Sun and Uncle Gao say he is no real fisherman and has no Mora. Dugu Shuo says Zhiyi's reputation, bad in rumour, turned positive only a few months earlier. At Blackcliff Forge, Iron Shoulder praises some newcomers' skill but says they refuse to drink with the other workers. When Wupei finally appears and says Zhiyi fell overboard and no one had interacted with him, Yelan mentions the fish soup, which Wupei has never heard of, and decides to claim Zhiyi as the winner as a trap.

**Foregone Conclusion.** At Yuehai Pavilion Yelan announces Zhiyi, to Qianwei's open anger and Mingbo's disappointment. Zhiyi suggests that his rivals work with him after his appointment, then leaves, giving her subordinates the slip. They find that he bought strong liquor at Xinyue Kiosk and the wineware at Xigu Antiques, and Yelan sends them to watch Fatui officials. That night Wenyuan reports that Yusupov has gone alone toward Qingxu Pool. Yelan, the Traveler and Paimon enter the underground ruins by an emergency exit, solve a statue puzzle, and overhear Zhiyi and Yusupov celebrating. Yusupov collapses from poison Zhiyi put in his drink; he says the Fatui will hunt Zhiyi, who answers that his subordinate Theofan, who wanted Yusupov's post, already knew about the plot.
### ULTRA +
Finer points. Yelan wanted Theofan as a source of information about the Fatui operation, and Zhiyi's reference to him is how his involvement surfaces. When she confronts Zhiyi with her subordinates and the Millelith, he first claims to have been working undercover; Yelan answers that this makes no sense after he has just confessed. Zhiyi admits poisoning Tian and promises to use his status to protect them, but gives up when she and the Traveler say he has betrayed Tian's trust. Qianwei and Mingbo, shaken, resolve to keep working.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zhiyi, the front-runner for Tianshu, is a Fatui agent, and Tian has been poisoned by him. The Fatui, working through the ambassador Yusupov, wanted a figure inside the Liyue Qixing and a treasure said to be hidden in the ruins beneath Qingxu Pool. Yelan's informants had not interacted with Zhiyi, and his glowing reputation had been built recently.
### LARGE +
The scheme was also betrayed from within: Zhiyi poisoned Yusupov at the celebration, and his subordinate Theofan had been waiting for Yusupov's post. The Fatui's higher ranks were involved too, since Harbinger Regrator stepped in and took Theofan away with the information Yelan wanted. The quest never explains the ruins' treasure.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Yelan leads the investigation. The Traveler and Paimon follow and observe. Uncle Tian is the ageing Tianshu, Huixin his daughter. Qianwei, Mingbo and Zhiyi are the candidates. Wenyuan, Shanghua and Wupei are Yelan's informants. Yusupov is the Fatui ambassador, Theofan his subordinate.
### LARGE +
Bolai, Linlang, Uncle Sun, Uncle Gao, Dugu Shuo, Iron Shoulder and Qiping are Liyue Harbor residents questioned on the way. Baizhu treats Tian after the poisoning. Regrator is mentioned, not shown.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zhiyi is taken into custody. Tian is treated by Baizhu for the poison and is shocked by the betrayal. Tian offers Yelan the Tianshu post, but she says she was joking, as she would miss the thrill of her present job. Qianwei and Mingbo agree to keep working hard. Theofan is removed by Regrator before Yelan can question him.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: all three quests came out together with Version 2.7. Story order: it needs the Interlude Chapter act Perilous Trail and the Sinae Unicornis Chapter's Sea of Clouds, Sea of People, so it takes place in Liyue after those events. Adventure Rank 40 is also required.
