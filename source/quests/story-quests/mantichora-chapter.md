## summary
### LITE
Dehya's Story Quest. The mercenary known as the Flame-Mane suspects the Eremite brigade Dakan Al-Ahmar, led by her adoptive father Kusayla, of massacring caravans. With the Traveler and her employer's daughter Dunyarzad, she learns that Kusayla is dead, that the brigade is acting to avenge itself on its own paymasters, and that Kusayla secretly left her a letter about her origins.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Lionsblood, of three quests: Shade Upon Red Silk, Annals of the Scarlet Sands and Sincerity's Facade. It is the story of a brigade's last act and of Dehya choosing to honour the man who raised her without excusing what his men did.
### LARGE +
Themes are loyalty, inherited debt and the cost of leaving a violent life. The cases are solved by talking to the former comrades Dehya knows personally rather than by overwhelming force.

## plot
### LITE
Dehya admits to the Traveler and Paimon that Dakan Al-Ahmar has been robbing and killing travellers. Tracing goods found in a looted caravan back to Dunyarzad's father Shahzaman, she learns that Kusayla died years ago and the brigade's remnants are using letters forged in his hand to turn allies against Deshret's Relics, the main brigade. Dehya helps them storm Deshret's Relics' headquarters. In the aftermath she receives Kusayla's cane and a letter revealing she was a foundling.
### STANDARD +
Shade Upon Red Silk: Dehya, with Dunyarzad at the Grand Bazaar, tells the Traveler that Dakan Al-Ahmar has been stripping and killing travellers in hundreds of incidents. She plans to face the leader, who is her adoptive father, and wants to understand why the brigade has turned violent. Outside Caravan Ribat her contacts report a camp looted with no survivors. They capture the Eremite Sattar, who refuses to talk and tries to bite off his tongue, only to lose consciousness. Goods in the caravan turn out to come from Shahzaman.

Annals of the Scarlet Sands: Shahzaman explains that bandits once attacked the family in the desert when Dunyarzad was a baby, and Kusayla's Eremites saved them. Years later, an ageing Kusayla insisted that Dehya of the Blazing Beasts be hired as Dunyarzad's bodyguard to get her out of mercenary life. Shahzaman had sent only food and medicine to the brigade and refused weapons. He points the group to Jawad, an Eremite arms supplier at Lambad's Tavern, who sends them to the Valley of Dahri. There, Dehya learns that Kusayla died years earlier after burning the 'records' at Deshret's Relics to free everyone. His men, burdened by guilt at their freedom, forged his handwriting to rally allies against Deshret's Relics.

Sincerity's Facade: Dehya and the Traveler storm the Deshret's Relics headquarters with the brigade's remnants and defeat the commander Hamzah. The loyalists, like Dakan Al-Ahmar, are prepared to die. Shakhbout directs them to the record room, where they find Kusayla's cane. Inside is a hidden sword that Dehya recognises as her childhood toy. Both brigades are taken into the Corps of Thirty's custody. Sattar gives Dehya a box of Kusayla's belongings, which contains a letter.
### LARGE +
Shade Upon Red Silk: Dehya had noticed that the main brigade, Deshret's Relics, makes those below it the 'bad guys' while taking the profit. Dunyarzad is surprised to learn of her father's possible links and promises to ask him at the city. Dehya expects that there must be a mistake.

Annals of the Scarlet Sands: Kusayla remarked to Shahzaman that he had a daughter around Dunyarzad's age, which is why he hired the family's help for a short time after the rescue. Shahzaman wanted to see Kusayla in person but was rebuffed, and guessed Kusayla wanted to keep Dehya out of it. When she hears the men's story, Dehya pities them and resolves to help while insisting that Deshret's Relics must face justice.

Sincerity's Facade: a trap separates Dehya and the Traveler inside the base before they reunite at its core. Dehya is given the cane as an inheritance. Both she and Dunyarzad are upset, the latter because her father is in custody. At the Grand Bazaar Sattar says that when he woke he tried to warn his brigade but found only the injured, since their goal was complete. He turned himself in with them. In the box, Dehya finds among the odds and ends a bottle holding the letter.
### ULTRA +
The letter reveals that Kusayla was not her birth father. He once went into the desert alone and found her as an abandoned infant in the sands, and none of his men knew. He admired the freedom and sincerity he and the others could only dream of and wanted her to live as she chose. Dehya is moved and decides to find her birth parents so that she can tell them that Kusayla made her who she is. She thanks the group for their support.

## revelations
### LITE
Kusayla, Dehya's adoptive father, died several years ago, and Dakan Al-Ahmar has acted since then under his forged letters.
### STANDARD +
Kusayla burned Deshret's Relics' records, almost dissolving that brigade. Dehya was a foundling, discovered by Kusayla alone in the desert.
### LARGE +
Kusayla arranged for Dehya to be hired as Dunyarzad's bodyguard to take her out of mercenary life, and his cane hid the toy sword she once played with.
### ULTRA +
Dehya's birth parents remain unknown, and she sets out to find them.

## characters
### LITE
Dehya, Dunyarzad, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Kusayla (dead), Dunyarzad's father Shahzaman, the Eremites Sattar, Jawad, Hamzah and Shakhbout, and the brigades Dakan Al-Ahmar and Deshret's Relics. Dehya's own Blazing Beasts are referenced.
### LARGE +
The Corps of Thirty takes the defeated and surrendered Eremites into custody.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Both Eremite brigades are broken up and their members detained. Dehya inherits Kusayla's cane and resolves to seek her birth parents.
### LARGE +
Dunyarzad is upset that her father Shahzaman is taken into custody over his links to the brigade; she and Dehya go to the Grand Bazaar to lift their spirits.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises (Chapter III Act V). Released in 3.5 on 2023-03-01, so it follows the Sumeru Archon Quest in story order.

### STANDARD
Leader of the Eremite brigade Dakan Al-Ahmar and Dehya's adoptive father, who found her as an infant in the desert. Once he grew old and weak, he asked that she be hired as Dunyarzad's bodyguard to pull her out of mercenary life. He died years before the chapter, after burning Deshret's Relics' records. His cane hid a toy sword from Dehya's childhood.

## entry: Dakan Al-Ahmar and Deshret's Relics
@keys: Dakan Al-Ahmar, Deshret's Relics
@keys2: Dehya, Eremite

### STANDARD
Two Eremite brigades. Deshret's Relics was the main brigade, which made its subordinates the 'bad guys'. Dakan Al-Ahmar, a branch led by Kusayla, was blamed for hundreds of killings and robberies in the desert. After Kusayla's death its remnants, guilty at their freedom, used forged letters to turn allies against Deshret's Relics. The Corps of Thirty detained both groups after Dehya's raid.
