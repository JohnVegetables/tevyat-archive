## summary
### LITE
Lyney's Story Quest, set in the Court of Fontaine. Ten years after a thief called the Phantom Weasel vanished, a poster announces the return. The Great Magician Lyney recruits the Traveler and Paimon as temporary assistants to find the culprit. They clear the name of Cesar, a dead stage magician wrongly branded the thief, expose his killer, and then learn the thief was someone else.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, The Forgotten Thief, made of four quests: The Phantom Thief's Reappearance, The Magician's Secret, The Truest Thing and The Interview. It was released in version 4.0 and unlocked after As Light Rain Falls Without Reason. It also reveals how Lyney and Lynette came to learn magic from Cesar, and what they hid from him.
### LARGE +
The plot is staged like one of Lyney's own shows: the real aim, restoring Cesar's name and drawing out the true culprit, is not what he first tells the Traveler.

## plot
### LITE
Charlotte reports that the Phantom Weasel, a thief never caught ten years ago, is back. Lyney offers to investigate and has the Traveler and Paimon help look into Cesar's death. They find Cesar's former apprentice Lorenzo is hiding something, are attacked, and find Cesar's diary and workshop. Lorenzo admits he killed Cesar and was the Phantom Weasel. The next day Lyney performs Cesar's old trick at the Opera Epiclese and reveals Jemma was behind it. Jemma later turns herself in.
### STANDARD +
The Phantom Thief's Reappearance: Charlotte tells the Traveler that ten years ago the magician Cesar fell to his death during a performance and the Gardes found stolen goods among his belongings, so he was thought to be the thief. A poster at the Opera Epiclese announces the Weasel is back. Lyney offers help and says they should check whether Cesar faked his death, starting with his fiancee Jemma at the Poisson cemetery. Jemma hides something.

The Magician's Secret: at Hotel Debord the Traveler meets Lorenzo, a former magician and Cesar's apprentice turned businessman, who threatens Edmondo and storms out. Lyney leads them out of town, where pursuers attack and flee on discovering he is a Vision bearer. Jemma says she is threatened whenever she looks into Cesar's death. In the sealed workshop they find Cesar's diary about two children he agreed to train. Lyney confronts Lorenzo, who admits to sabotaging Cesar from jealousy and to being the Phantom Weasel, then is taken away by Gardes. Lyney holds a memorial for Cesar.

The Truest Thing: Lyney performs a show alone at the Opera Epiclese, and at the interval Lynette reveals Jemma was the real Phantom Weasel. The twins confront her privately. Jemma tells a sad history and does not confirm the facts. Lyney reveals that he, not Jemma, wrote the letter, to draw her out because the twins felt indebted to Cesar. He then performs the final act, Cesar's old trick, and vanishes in a sea of flowers. At the cemetery he tells the Traveler about his past with Lynette and Cesar.

The Interview: at the Steambird, Charlotte tries to interview the twins and says Jemma has turned herself in and admitted to being the real Phantom Weasel. Paimon notices Lyney is now using Rainbow Roses in his tricks, which Lynette notices. She says she had only been acting to distract Charlotte, and asks the Traveler to keep the Rainbow Rose Lyney gave them.
### LARGE +
The Phantom Thief's Reappearance: Charlotte had been asking Abagnale about the Phantom Weasel before approaching the Traveler. The thief robbed rich and poor alike. Lyney is finishing a show when Charlotte spots him. Lynette at the cemetery has not seen Jemma yet; the group asks other visitors about her and Cesar.

The Magician's Secret: Edmondo says those who make an enemy of Lorenzo suffer misfortune. The pursuers say their tactics would not work on "the lady", which Lyney correctly takes to mean Jemma. In the workshop, the diary describes the children and the time spent with them, and Lyney confirms them to be himself and Lynette. Reading Cesar's notes on the trick, Lyney realised that someone sabotaged his play and caused his death. Lorenzo says he hated Cesar for his self-perceived poverty, for forbidding him to trick people out of Mora, and for entertaining crowds for free. He says he targeted the poor so he could hone his craft to steal more valuable items. Lyney suspects he also had feelings for Jemma, which Lorenzo denies. Bruno and Lori of the Gardes take him away. Lynette stays with Jemma. At the cemetery, Lynette says Lyney will host a magic show tomorrow.

The Truest Thing: the twins say the real Phantom Weasel worried about the Gardes, and, finding an aggravated pupil, used Lorenzo to kill Cesar and avoid dirtying her own hands. Jemma tells of her youth: parents lost young, betrayal and abuse, and a vow to hate all happiness and steal it. Lyney explains the letter was his, written because they felt indebted to Cesar and wanted his legacy restored. They tell Jemma she cared for Lorenzo and that her "truth" was gone now that he was arrested. Jemma asks about the secrets he and Lynette hid from Cesar and says he will end up like her; Lyney says he is nothing like her. In the finale he drops from the top of the opera, the same trick Cesar attempted, and vanishes into flowers while Charlotte watches.
### ULTRA +
At the cemetery Lyney explains that, after being taken in by Arlecchino, he and Lynette approached Cesar with an agenda in mind. He knew they had some talent but needed a professional to avoid plateauing. Cesar accepted readily, treated them well during ten days together, and asked why Lyney wanted to learn magic; Lyney said it was his passion and knew that was a lie. Lyney says selfishness and lies have followed them, but that they are grateful to have each other, unlike Jemma.

The Interview: Charlotte notes that Jemma turned herself in; she had also wanted to see Lorenzo one last time. Lyney gives his view of why Jemma turned herself in. Lynette's remark about the Rainbow Rose closes the act.

## revelations
### LITE
Cesar was killed by his apprentice Lorenzo, and Cesar was not the Phantom Weasel. The thief was Jemma, who manipulated Lorenzo.
### STANDARD +
Lyney and Lynette studied under Cesar for ten days while hiding their real purpose, and the twins arranged the letter to flush Jemma out. Jemma later turns herself in and admits to being the Phantom Weasel.
### LARGE +
Lyney's final act reprises the trick Cesar was attempting when he died. The twins' debt to Cesar is central to their motives.
### ULTRA +
The quest shows that the twins were taken in by Arlecchino before meeting Cesar and that they lied to him; the Story Quest does not detail the House of the Hearth.

## characters
### LITE
Lyney, Lynette, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Charlotte, Cesar (deceased), Jemma, Lorenzo, Edmondo, Abagnale, and the Gardes Bruno and Lori.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino is referenced by Lyney as the one who took in him and Lynette.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Cesar's name is cleared, Lorenzo is arrested and Jemma turns herself in as the real Phantom Weasel. Charlotte's article is overtaken by the news of Jemma's arrest.
### LARGE +
Lyney begins using Rainbow Roses in his tricks and gives one to the Traveler, which Lynette notices.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after As Light Rain Falls Without Reason (Chapter IV Act II). Released in 4.0 on 2023-08-16. The wiki notes it is blocked at step 5 by Unfinished Comedy.

## entry: The Phantom Weasel
@keys: Phantom Weasel
@keys2: Lyney, Cesar, Jemma, Lorenzo
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A notorious thief in Fontaine ten years before the Archon Quest who was never caught and robbed rich and poor alike. When the magician Cesar died, stolen goods were found among his things and he was blamed. His apprentice Lorenzo first confessed to being the Weasel after killing Cesar, but the twins revealed that Jemma was the real thief, using Lorenzo as a tool. She later turned herself in.

### STANDARD
A stage magician who died in a fall during a performance ten years before the quest. He agreed to train Lyney and Lynette for ten days, not knowing their secrets, and was wrongly believed to be the thief. His diary and workshop helped Lyney prove he was sabotaged by Lorenzo.
