## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The Tribal Chronicles chapter of the Scions of the Canopy, which also serves as Kinich's story quest. The tribe holds Turnfire Night to honour the hero Burkina and his Saurian companion Kongamato, who fought the Abyss, and to keep the Abyssal energy still within Kongamato stable. This year the ritual is in trouble, and the tribe wants the Saurian hunter Kinich to bear the flame. The Traveler is drawn into the matter, and Kinich solves it his own way through a deal with an Abyss Lector.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three acts: A Mysterious Visitor From Huitztlan, A Hero's Rite and Kinich's Deal. It unlocks after the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame in the Archon Quest. The chapter title refers to the Turnfire, the ritual flame; a book called "Yupanqui's Turnfire" also appears in Act III.
### LARGE +
The conflict is between two ways of dealing with Kongamato. The tribe's elder Trinidad plans a ritual that would drain the Abyssal energy even though it would probably kill Kongamato, while the elder Wayna wants it kept alive as a source of the tribe's history, pride and faith. Kinich chooses a third option, sealing Kongamato inside a Beastly Rift obtained by dealing with an Abyss Lector, and then has to improvise when his plan is broken in front of the whole tribe.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I. At the foot of Coatepec Mountain the Traveler and Paimon meet two children of the Scions of the Canopy, Huni and Toba, who are looking for baby Yumkasaurs and were told by a man named Sanka that helpers were coming. Ajaw is already with the Yumkasaur whelp Pocchi and explains that the babies' mother was overcome by Abyssal energy, attacked her tribe and her young, and died. The children say they had been telling Sanka stories about their tribe, including that Kinich is the current Turnfire bearer, when he fled. Learning that the visitors know Kinich, the children point them to the village.

Act II. In the village Trinidad, Huni's father, first doubts the visitors but warms to them when Huni says they ate with Kinich. He explains Turnfire Night, honouring Burkina and Kongamato, and the current crisis: five months after the last ceremony Kongamato has shown signs of instability, and in its rage it attacked Trinidad's own Saurian companion Nanna, whom the tribe believes died. The tribe had hoped Kinich would be the flame-bearer but he named a price, which they took as a refusal, so Trinidad asks the Traveler to take that place. At the same time Kinich and Ajaw confront Sanka. The Traveler completes a stage of the ritual at the summit of Coatepec Mountain while Kinich watches in secret.

Act III. Kinich asks the Traveler to find out what Trinidad's ritual really involves, then explains his own plan. Over the next day the chapter shifts to a search for Ponche's stolen book, a ruins mural and a meeting in a cave where Kinich reveals he has struck a deal with Sanka, who is really the Abyss Lector Enjou. On Turnfire Night Kinich produces the Beastly Rift device, Trinidad destroys the device in a panic, and Kongamato wakes and has to be calmed by the Traveler and Kinich using Kinich's Ancient Name.
### LARGE +
**A Mysterious Visitor From Huitztlan (5.0).** The Traveler and Paimon find the children at the foot of Coatepec Mountain. Ajaw tells them to question suspicious outsiders because the taint of the Abyss still lingers there. The children describe the stranger, Sanka, who tried to learn about Kinich and fled after distracting them when the Traveler arrived.

**A Hero's Rite (5.0).** Trinidad tells the Traveler that Turnfire Night honours Burkina and Kongamato. The Abyssal energy keeps Kongamato alive unnaturally long compared with other Yumkasaurs but is highly sensitive, and the ceremony keeps it at stable levels. Five months after the last ceremony it shows signs of failure. Sanka tells Kinich and Ajaw that he was tired of watching them from afar and let them catch up because he did not feel like running, and offers to negotiate. Kinich tells him to state his real purpose before he names his own price. The Traveler performs part of the rite quickly and Trinidad plans to recommend them as the flame-bearer.

**Kinich's Deal (5.0).** Trinidad intends the Traveler to purify the energy out of Kongamato, which would likely kill it. Wayna tells them he supports the Traveler's taking part but not Kongamato's death. On a walk Kinich says he knew Trinidad's plan, reveals that he gave Ajaw a teacher, and explains that he found a Beastly Rift while chasing riftwolves. He proposes to clear a rift of its inhabitants and put Kongamato inside so it can threaten no one, while admitting the plan has many flaws.

**I, Flame Bearer (5.0).** To take attention off Kinich, the Traveler and Paimon help Thiago, who has seen a mural of a human and a Saurian in ruins infested by riftwolves, and Ponche, whose book "Yupanqui's Turnfire" was taken by the Treasure Hoarder Matthias, who threw it away. Sanka finds the book in a cave and reads it aloud to Ajaw; when he mentions that all bearers of the Turnfire name die horribly, Ajaw takes interest and agrees to a deal with Sanka. Ponche thinks he has a solution that needs Kinich. Kinich shows the Traveler his reading of Burkina's memories: Kongamato killed Burkina and wishes to die, which the tribe cannot allow. The next day they open a Beastly Rift and an Abyss Lector steps out, remarking that he had delivered more than promised. Paimon recognises Enjou. Kinich had already traded the rift for being left alone in his historical research, and says Ajaw's apparent betrayal was bait. Enjou says he is a lowly clerk and not part of the inner circle, then departs. Kinich suspects he is busy with debts.

**The Last Turnfire Night (5.0).** The Traveler completes the ritual with Kinich's guidance. At Kongamato's lair, Trinidad asks the Traveler to purify the energy, but Kinich produces Enjou's device, which the tribe recognises as a Beastly Rift, and Wayna relents when Kinich says Kongamato will be fine. Trinidad, thinking the Traveler is in league with Kinich, smashes the device while Kinich is distracted. The energy collapses, Kongamato wakes and Kinich orders the tribe out, while Trinidad runs to the Turnfire and is caught by Kongamato's fire. The Traveler and Kinich fight it until it begs to be put out of its misery. Kinich uses his Ancient Name to build invisible walls between Kongamato and the Sacred Flame, tells it that it must live with the shame of its past, and it agrees on condition that Kinich uses its ruined body to train heroes until the Abyss is gone from Natlan.
### ULTRA +
Finer points. The Beastly Rift plan puts Kinich on the same side as a member of the Abyss Order against the expectations of Paimon, who is horrified by the apparent collusion; Kinich says he had wanted the Traveler to learn about the Abyss Order, which is why he had Enjou in reach. Enjou denies that he roused Kongamato. On the day after the incident Wayna reports that the elders have recovered Trinidad, who is expected to recover from severe burns, and that a transparent shell formed around Kongamato. Kinich thanks the Traveler for the Kongamato matter and says he owes them a favour; the Traveler objects that he already captured Enjou, but Kinich says Enjou gave away nothing of value. When Paimon calls them friends, Kinich cannot accept that for free and says he would do anything for them if needed. Elsewhere Enjou meets Toba and Huni, apologises for deceiving them, and says he enjoys the tribe's extreme sports after being struck by a bungee jumper falling out of the sky and learning of the practice. He says he then reunited with a friend who had missed her family and brought her to them. Huni turns round and finds Nanna, her family's Saurian companion, whom the tribe had believed dead, pressing her face against hers.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kinich bears the Ancient Name Malipo; from the memories he has of Burkina, he concludes that Kongamato killed Burkina and wants to die, and that the tribe's honour for it hides this. Sanka, the stranger who pumped the children for stories about Kinich, is Enjou, an Abyss Lector who admits to being only a clerk in the Abyss Order. The Turnfire ritual is not a normal ceremony of respect but a means of stabilising Abyssal energy in Kongamato, and the elders disagree on whether to destroy it.
### LARGE +
The tribe's version of the Turnfire legend says its bearers die horribly, which Sanka reports to Ajaw. At the story's end a transparent shell surrounds Kongamato, and the elders cannot say whether the Sacred Flame or the Turnfire produced it. Nanna, whom the family believed Kongamato had killed, is alive and is brought back to them by Enjou.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon are the viewpoint pair. Kinich, the Saurian hunter who names a price for every job, drives the plot and keeps his reasoning to himself until late. Ajaw, bound to Kinich by contract, sides with Sanka for part of the story but the betrayal is bait. Trinidad is a worried elder whose companion Nanna is hurt; Wayna is another elder who values Kongamato as tribal heritage. Huni and Toba are children of the tribe. Enjou, under the alias Sanka, is the Abyss Lector.
### LARGE +
Thiago, Ponche, Matthias and the baby Yumkasaur Pocchi have minor roles. Burkina and Kongamato are not present as characters except through the tribe's story and Kongamato's actions.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kongamato survives and agrees to remain in its ruined state to train future heroes. Trinidad is badly burned but expected to recover. Kinich owes the Traveler a favour and says he will do anything for them in future. Enjou leaves, Nanna is returned to Huni's family, and the Beastly Rift plan is spent. The tribe records the story of Malipo's manifestation, preserved by a scholar of Ancient Names.
### LARGE +
The chapter's closing text says the tale will be passed on until it becomes an enigmatic relic of a past era, to be unearthed again by later seekers of knowledge, a point about how legends outlive the events behind them.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: all three acts came with Version 5.0; the wiki gives the event window as 2024-09-17 to 2024-10-08. Story order: the chapter requires only the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame in the Archon Quest part Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn, and the wiki lists the Inazuma quest From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku as a recommended earlier quest for Act I. Trinidad recognises the Traveler from the Stadium of the Sacred Flame.

## entry: Turnfire Night
@keys: Turnfire Night, Turnfire Ritual, Yupanqui's Turnfire
@keys2: Scions of the Canopy, Kongamato, Burkina
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A ceremony of the Scions of the Canopy that honours the hero Burkina and his Saurian companion Kongamato, both of whom fought the Abyss. Burkina died and Kongamato was left with Abyssal energy that lets it live unnaturally long but is highly unstable, so the ritual flame, the Turnfire, is used to keep that energy stable. In the year of the chapter, five months after the last ceremony, Kongamato showed signs of instability and its rage hurt Trinidad's Saurian companion Nanna, whom the tribe believed it killed.

## entry: Kongamato
@keys: Kongamato
@keys2: Scions of the Canopy, Burkina, Turnfire
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Yumkasaur companion of the hero Burkina, afflicted by Abyssal energy in the fight that killed him. It lives unnaturally long, and the tribe treats it as a source of history, pride and faith. Kinich believes it killed Burkina and wishes to die. After the Last Turnfire Night it agrees to live with its shame and allow Kinich to use its ruined vessel for training future heroes, and a transparent shell forms around it that the elders interpret as insulation against Abyssal energy.

## entry: Trinidad and Wayna
@keys: Trinidad, Wayna
@keys2: Scions of the Canopy, Kongamato, Turnfire
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two elders of the Scions of the Canopy. Trinidad, Huni's father, wants the Abyssal energy purged from Kongamato even though it would probably kill it, and is badly burned when he rushes to the Turnfire. Wayna endorses the Traveler's participation but refuses to let Kongamato be killed, and afterwards reports the elders' interpretation of the shell around it.

## entry: Beastly Rift Bargain
@keys: Beastly Rift, Kinich's Deal, Sanka
@keys2: Kinich, Enjou, Kongamato
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kinich's plan to contain Kongamato. He discovered Beastly Rifts, hidden spaces opened by the Abyss, while hunting riftwolves. In exchange for being left alone in his historical research, the Abyss Lector Enjou, who had been using the alias Sanka, supplied one. The plan was to clear a rift and put Kongamato in it. Trinidad smashed the device on Turnfire Night, and the problem was then settled by Kinich's Ancient Name instead.
