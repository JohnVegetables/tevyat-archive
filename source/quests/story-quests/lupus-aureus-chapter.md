## summary
### LITE
Cyno's Story Quest, in two acts. In Sands of Solitude (3.1) the Traveler helps the General Mahamatra track a ring smuggling Canned Knowledge out of the Akademiya, and learns it was part of a wider, hidden effort connected to a banned ruin, the Court of Desolation. In Oathkeeper (4.6) a blackmail letter to Cyno's old teacher leads back to the Temple of Silence, which wants the Hermanubis power Cyno carries.
### STANDARD +
Act I has three quests: Suspicious Smuggling Case, Chain of Interests and All Returns to Silence. Act II has five: The Mysterious Letter, After Repose, Footprints Sunken Beneath the Sands, Sage of Sages and The Dead Past is the Living Present. Cyno's friends Tighnari and Collei appear in both acts; the Traveler is a close helper, not a bystander.
### LARGE +
The chapter explores Cyno's strict sense of duty in Act I, where the person he must stop is a man he respects, and his past in Act II, where the question is whether the power he carries belongs to him or to those who raised him in the Temple. Both acts end in understatement: Cyno plans to plant flowers for a fallen colleague, then spends time with his old teacher.

## plot
### LITE
Act I: Nabil, a Matra, asks the Traveler for help with smuggled Canned Knowledge. Cyno is already investigating. Following Dori's tip about an auction in the desert, the group fights off hired Eremites and scorpions, presses a smuggler in Aaru Village, and traces the Matra seal on a shipment to Taj Radkani, Cyno's former superior. At the Court of Desolation Taj's resistance ends, the ruin collapses on him, and Cyno quietly rounds up the ring.

Act II: Cyno's old teacher Cyrus receives a blackmail letter. Its pattern leads to the Temple of Silence. The Temple's leader Bamoun wants Cyno's Ba Fragment transferred to his grandson Sethos. Cyno proposes a winner-take-all Rite of Duels, wins, and gives Sethos the leadership.
### STANDARD +
Act I - Sands of Solitude. Nabil tells the Traveler that smuggled Canned Knowledge is circulating. Cyno has already found the smugglers, and Dori's tip reveals the ring is really seeking the banned Court of Desolation. Hired Eremites and scorpions try to stop them, a tracker leads to a Kshahrewar researcher, and a Matra seal on a shipment points to Cyno's former superior Taj Radkani. At the Court, Taj shows he joined the ring to expose it, and the ruin collapses on him. Cyno then quietly arrests the rest.

Act II - Oathkeeper. Cyrus, Cyno's old teacher, is blackmailed. The paper's emblem is from the Temple of Silence, and Cyrus and Cyno head into the desert. At the Temple, Bamoun tells how Cyno and Sethos were given Ba Fragments of Hermanubis and demands Cyno's be handed to Sethos. Cyno proposes a Rite of Duels, wins, absorbs the second fragment, and appoints Sethos leader after Bamoun dies. Cyno shows the Traveler his hideaway at the Sanctuary of Surasthana.
### LARGE +
Act-by-act detail.

Act I - Sands of Solitude. Suspicious Smuggling Case: Nabil tells the Traveler and Paimon that smuggled Canned Knowledge is circulating, while the Matra are short-staffed because Cyno is away. Cyno has already located the smugglers Amainu and Amenzu and their buyer Mutair; he warns them rather than arrests them. Paimon almost names Dori, who once sold Canned Knowledge to Cyno. Dori says she has already left the trade, and that the ring is really after information about the Court of Desolation, a banned ruin said to let people see beyond life and death, and that the mass dumping is a distraction. She sells them a desert survival kit and charges it to Cyno. Cyno explains the Court's origins and why research on it is banned. Leaving the outpost, they are attacked by the Eremite Jhuba, hired by the ring, and Cyno explains his wariness: an earlier ambush by mercenary remnants injured his superior Taj Radkani and ended Taj's field work.

Chain of Interests: scorpions raised by someone attack the group in the desert, and Cyno finds a tracking device on the Traveler, which he recognises as Kshahrewar handiwork. At the auction site they find only one smuggler, Bahari, who returned to loot what was left. Bahari takes them to Aaru Village; he sold Canned Knowledge to pay for his daughter Simin's chronic illness. The Matra seal on his shipment suggests insider help. Nabil and the Matra repository show that Murtada Radkani, Taj's only son, was jailed for researching the Court and died. Taj's own Eremite clothes were seen by Usem, and papers in his house reveal a wide, complex operation involving well-known figures. Cyno decides to confront Taj.

All Returns to Silence: at the Court of Desolation a short fight convinces Cyno that Taj has not truly betrayed the Matra. Taj admits that the ring had members inside the Akademiya and that the ban would have blocked a direct plan, so he joined it hoping Cyno would end the case. The ruin collapses with Taj in it. Four days later Nabil reports that a mysterious person captured the mercenaries and researchers on the list; the Matra, learning of Taj's sacrifice, acted. Cyno is seen with Tighnari, who gives him paralysing traps, and mentions Collei has been giving talks on the dangers of Canned Knowledge. Cyno plans to plant flowers for Taj.

Act II - Oathkeeper. The Mysterious Letter: Cyrus, a former Spantamad sage who taught Cyno and Lisa, got a letter demanding ten million Mora or his secret would be revealed. Cyno catches the writer, a sixteen-year-old student named Urraca with a gambling problem who overheard gossip at a tavern. The unusual pattern on the paper, however, points further. After Repose: Kaveh sketches it and Alhaitham recognises an emblem of the Temple of Silence. Cyrus has left, and Cyno has gone west after him, leaving a letter asking not to be followed. Naphis, Tighnari's master, urges the group to bring both back, noting the Akademiya's shameful history with the Temple.

Footprints Sunken Beneath the Sands: the group meets Cyno at Caravan Ribat, then goes to Thamud Oasis, where Sethos leads them into the Temple of Silence. Bamoun accuses Cyrus of betrayal and tells the history of the two Ba Fragments of Hermanubis implanted in Sethos and Cyno as children. Cyno refuses to hand his over. Sage of Sages: Cyno proposes a Rite of Duels, winner takes all. After the Traveler and Tighnari win their rounds, Cyno beats Sethos, who relinquishes his fragment; Cyno absorbs it and Bamoun dies. The Dead Past is the Living Present: Bamoun's wish makes Cyno leader of the Temple, but Cyno appoints Sethos in his place, and Sethos admits the duel was his and his grandfather's plan. Cyno shows the Traveler his retreat at the Sanctuary of Surasthana and meets Cyrus there.

Details from Act I. Nabil explains that taking too much Canned Knowledge causes health problems as well as being banned. Cyno keeps the tracking device rather than destroying it, because it might be useful later. Dori says she cashed out because a flat share was not worth it and new regulations made clients scarce, not out of conscience; Cyno does not ask where the tip came from. Utayba allows the repository search after reading Cyno's letter. Payam recalls a Kshahrewar researcher sending large shipments with little old equipment, under a Matra seal Taj told him to wave through. After the collapse, Cyno asks the Traveler to meet him at Caravan Ribat four days later while he handles the cleanup himself.

Details from Act II. Cyrus brought Cyno to Sumeru City after the trials in the Temple. Cyrus asks Cyno to bring Tighnari along to a meal at Lambad's Tavern, where Kaveh also drops by. The group camps with Tighnari and Collei in Gandharva Ville. Candace and Dehya briefly speak with Cyno at Caravan Ribat. Bamoun says the Temple's influence is fading without Hermanubis, that volunteers were rejected by the fragments, and that his two child candidates were his adopted grandson and a child whose parents accepted money. The three-phase plan was resonance, implantation, recovery and observation. He recognises Tighnari as a descendant of the Valuka Shuna. The duel is refereed by Duaenre. The Traveler faces a Grounded Geoshroom and Grounded Hydroshroom, and Tighnari two Primal Constructs. In the final round Cyno switches off Hermanubis' power mid-fight and so does Sethos.
### ULTRA +
Further details. Act I: Jhuba warns that others will come after Cyno. Nabil asks the Traveler to pass on his regards to the mysterious helper. In the closing scene Cyno asks whether the Traveler knows anything about someone billing him for survival packages, a nod to Dori's invoice. Act II: Tighnari, recalling Alhaitham's and Kaveh's remarks on desert tribes' honour customs, suggests facing the Temple on its own terms. A quick examination shows Bamoun is under heavy stress with only days to live. After the duel Bamoun, seeing the light as Hermanubis returning, dies. Sethos tells the group that, per Bamoun's wishes, anyone Sethos or Lesser Lord Kusanali deems worthy may study the knowledge the Temple holds, and that Bamoun had been pleased to meet a Valuka Shuna descendant. Cyno plans a report for Kusanali and invites Sethos to the rainforest to meet the other side. Back at the Akademiya, Naphis scolds Cyrus for recklessness, Faruzan is upset not to have been included, and Cyno has Collei distract her with an Akademiya tour. Tighnari gives Collei the password 'cup' for meeting at Puspa Cafe. Kaveh admires Cyno's decision to give up the leadership. At the Sanctuary of Surasthana, Cyno calls the Divine Tree his secret place to unwind. Cyrus and Cyno look at old photos taken by Lisa; Cyrus plans to postpone a visit to her because of a tomato-growing contest with Zaha Hadi.

## revelations
### LITE
Cyno carries a Ba Fragment of Hermanubis, implanted in him as a child by the Temple of Silence.
### STANDARD +
The Canned Knowledge smuggling was a front for research into the banned Court of Desolation, aided by insiders in the Akademiya, and Taj Radkani joined it deliberately to expose it. The Temple of Silence is a surviving secret desert order whose leader, Bamoun, held two Ba Fragments of Hermanubis.
### LARGE +
Cyno was one of two children chosen for fragments; Cyrus ended the experiment and took Cyno away. Sethos, Bamoun's adopted grandson, got the other fragment. Cyno remembers little of the Temple.
### ULTRA +
The duel was effectively staged by the Temple. Bamoun's goal was to unite both fragments in one person and to free his successor from the old order's burdens, a plan Sethos admits was shared with him. The Temple then opens its knowledge to those the Dendro Archon or Sethos approve. All of this is from the wiki summary and not independently confirmed.

## characters
### LITE
Cyno, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Act I: Nabil, the Matra Taj Radkani, Dori, the smugglers Amainu, Amenzu and Bahari, the Eremite Jhuba, Tighnari and Collei. Act II: Cyrus, Aarav, Urraca, Sethos, Bamoun, Tighnari, Collei, Kaveh, Alhaitham, Naphis, Candace, Dehya, Faruzan and Lisa (by photographs).
### LARGE +
Minor named figures include Murtada Radkani (Taj's son, deceased), Simin, Utayba, Payam, Usem, Zaha Hadi, Fodil and the duel referee Duaenre.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The smuggling ring is dismantled, with its mercenaries and Akademiya researchers arrested, and Taj Radkani dies in the Court of Desolation. In Oathkeeper Cyno absorbs the second Ba Fragment, Sethos leads the Temple of Silence, and Bamoun dies.
### LARGE +
Cyno plans to plant flowers at the Court for Taj. The Temple of Silence is brought into contact with the Akademiya and the Dendro Archon, with an official delegation expected, and Sethos is invited to Sumeru's rainforest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act I unlocks at Adventure Rank 40 after King Deshret and the Three Magi (Chapter III Act IV) and was released in 3.1 on 2022-09-28. Act II unlocks after Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises (Chapter III Act V) and was released in 4.6 on 2024-04-24, so it takes place after Sumeru's Archon Quest even though it was released much later.

## entry: Court of Desolation
@keys: Court of Desolation
@keys2: Cyno, Taj, Canned Knowledge
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A banned desert ruin said to let people see beyond life and death and, in legend, to revive the dead. The Akademiya forbids research on it. A smuggling ring used a Canned Knowledge trade to find people interested in it. It collapsed into quicksand at the end of Sands of Solitude.

## entry: Taj Radkani
@keys: Taj Radkani, Murtada
@keys2: Cyno, Matra
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Cyno's former superior in the Matra, injured in an earlier mission and removed from live work but still respected. After his only son Murtada died following a prison term for investigating the Court of Desolation, Taj joined the smuggling ring in secret to expose it, and died in the Court's collapse.

## entry: Temple of Silence
@keys: Temple of Silence, Bamoun
@keys2: Cyno, Sethos, Hermanubis
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A secret desert order tied to Hermanubis, whose influence was fading without its guide. Its leader Bamoun held two Ba Fragments and had them implanted in two children, one of whom was Cyno. After the Rite of Duels in Oathkeeper, Cyno holds both and Sethos leads the order.
