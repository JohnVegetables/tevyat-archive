## summary
### LITE
Kokomi's Story Quest, set on Watatsumi Island just after the Vision Hunt Decree ends. The Traveler joins Kokomi for the Victory Feast and finds a rumour that the Fatui and the Tenryou Commission are plotting to restart the war. The rumour proves to be a plan by grieving Watatsumi soldiers, and Kokomi turns it into a new corps to defend the island.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Warriors' Dreams Like Spring Grass Renewing, released in Version 2.1, with five quests: Watatsumi Sightseeing, Rumors Abound, Her Secret, Victory Banquet and New Beginning.
### LARGE +
Alongside the intrigue, the act shows Kokomi as a leader who never wanted the role, carries a heavy workload and relies on careful planning and the trust of Gorou and her people.

## plot
### LITE
On Watatsumi Island the Traveler helps Gorou's soldiers destroy a Fatui ship and learns that rumours of a Tenryou and Fatui plot are circulating before peace talks. Kokomi investigates a Fatui camp, rests secretly in a cave, and holds the Victory Feast. A drunken soldier admits the rumours were fake and meant to restart the war. At the talks with Kujou Sara the soldiers expose the rumour, and Kokomi settles it and forms a new defence corps.
### STANDARD +
Watatsumi Sightseeing. Arriving for the Victory Feast, the Traveler and Paimon meet Gorou, who suspects a moored Fatui ship and lacks the men to destroy it. They help him. Gorou says the Resistance is now helping civilians, and mentions that some soldiers seem secretive. The Traveler talks to Denjirou, Arisawa, and Yoshihisa and Tsuneo, who believe the Tenryou Commission still collaborates with the Fatui. At Bourou Village a trader, Naruse, is accused of dealing with the Tenryou, and Kokomi arrives and reveals she has begun peace talks, with Sara representing the Tenryou and the Kamisato Clan arranging the venue.

Rumors Abound. The three clear a Fatui camp and find Tenryou goods, expired rations and bomb-making materials. Naruse's stolen goods are returned, but he is fined for planning to sell above the fair price, against Kokomi's temporary decree on price gouging. Kokomi tells the Traveler the original shrine honouring the Raiden Shogun stood near the island's Statue of The Seven, and relations soured after Baal slew Orobashi.

Her Secret. Kokomi is missing for a morning; a soldier points the Traveler to a cave south of the shrine where she is napping. She admits she never wanted to be a leader and finds the work heavy. She asks the Traveler to find a book to restore her energy and falls asleep as they read.

Victory Banquet. At the feast, Kabasawa points the Traveler to a strange soldier, Kouzen, who is drunk and tests their warrior's will. Beaten, he admits the rumours were false and that soldiers want war again because the sudden end made their comrades' deaths feel in vain.

New Beginning. Kokomi gives the Traveler a paper of possible scenarios and the talks begin on a small island off Kannazuka. When Sara mentions returning the Resistance to Tenryou control, Kokomi's guards reveal the rumour, nearly causing a clash. Two Tenryou guards admit they were approached by Fatui seeking a restart. Sara vows to hunt them down. Kokomi refuses to release command of her soldiers for now, and afterwards announces a new corps to protect the island instead of punishing the soldiers.
### LARGE +
Details. Gorou says the soldiers have been unusually secretive. Yoshihisa and Tsuneo suspect the Tenryou and the Fatui still collude; Tsuneo discourages telling the Traveler, thinking they might have proposed the talks. Kokomi says the Kujou Clan's collusion seems unlikely given Kujou Kamaji's efforts to redeem the clan, but she still considers the possibility that the talks are a trap. Uchimura advises her to keep an open mind. Izumi Naki reminds Kokomi of a meeting with textile industry representatives, which Kokomi leaves for.

In the cave Kokomi admits that her bloodline made her leadership inevitable, and that she struggles to be the ideal leader. The Traveler finds petitions, an "energy" list and a military treatise.

Kouzen says it is too late to stop them. Kokomi tells the Traveler she wants the conspirators unharmed. At the talks, the guards Motosuke and Sue Yoshitaka say most of the Fatui left and the few that remained were Signora's forces wanting to settle a score with the Shogunate. Kokomi suggests Sara should not tip the Fatui off, so that they come to the Tenryou Commission and are captured together.
### ULTRA +
Kokomi explains that she is unwilling to give up command because of internal issues in the Tenryou Commission, and that the negotiations showed its leaders could not consistently protect Watatsumi. She tells the soldiers she apologises for not teaching them how to release their fighting will, and that she will punish any further insubordination. The act ends at the purple tree, where Kokomi thanks the Traveler, says she still has a long way to go, and asks them to stay with her a little longer.

## revelations
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
- The rumour of a Tenryou and Fatui conspiracy was invented by Watatsumi soldiers who wanted war to resume.
- Fatui remnants who stayed in Inazuma were Signora's forces seeking revenge on the Shogunate, according to the Tenryou guards.
### LARGE +
- Kokomi never wanted to lead; her bloodline made it inevitable.
- Relations between Watatsumi and Narukami soured after Baal slew Orobashi, in Kokomi's account.

## characters
### LITE
Kokomi, the Traveler, Paimon and Gorou.
### STANDARD +
Kujou Sara and the Tenryou guards Motosuke and Sue Yoshitaka; the soldiers Kouzen, Yoshihisa, Tsuneo, Matsuda and Uchimura; villagers Naruse, Denjirou, Arisawa, Kabasawa and shrine keeper Izumi Naki.
### LARGE +
Kujou Kamaji, the Kamisato Clan, the Raiden Shogun (as Baal), Orobashi and Signora appear only in conversation.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The war rumour is disarmed, peace talks conclude, and Kokomi forms a new corps to defend Watatsumi Island. Sara promises another investigation of the Fatui remnants.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocks at Adventure Rank 40 after Omnipresence Over Mortals (Chapter II Act IV) and the Story Quest Reflections of Mortality. Released in Version 2.1. Set shortly after the Vision Hunt Decree is lifted. No later acts exist as of 7.1.
