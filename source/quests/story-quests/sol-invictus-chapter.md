## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Mavuika's Story Quest, set the day after the war with the Abyss ends. The Wayob warn that the first Pyro Archon, Xbalanque, is returning to Natlan. During the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, now reworked as a friendly tournament, Mavuika finds Xbalanque living in the body of a boy named Mausau, duels him in a spirit arena and wins, and declines his offer of life force to meet someone from the Night Kingdom.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has a single act, As the Blazing Sun, released in Version 5.3 and requiring the Archon Quest act Incandescent Ode of Resurrection. Its quests are After the Peace, A New Pilgrimage and Hearken, Ye Who Have Returned. It is a calm, comic follow-up to the war and shows Mavuika at ease in peacetime with the Traveler and the tribes.
### LARGE +
The act is about what a warrior is for once the war is won. Xbalanque, who laid down the rules for the long war, meets the Archon who finished it; Mavuika's refusal of the revival offer says she wants the past kept as memory rather than relived.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Citlali wakes the Traveler and Paimon the morning after the war and brings them to Mavuika, who says the Wayob in the Night Kingdom have told Citlali that Xbalanque is returning. Mavuika has turned the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame into a friendly competition and hopes it will draw him out. She and the Traveler visit the tribes, then the tournament runs at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame and Kinich wins it. Mavuika identifies Xbalanque in the crowd inside the body of a boy called Mausau, speaks to him privately, and agrees to a duel in a spirit arena. She wins narrowly. She declines his gift of life force, they ride her Flamestrider together, and at day's end he departs; Mausau wakes remembering a dream.
### LARGE +
**After the Peace.** At the Speaker's Chamber, Citlali relays what the Wayob said and Mavuika explains the reworked Pilgrimage. At the People of the Springs, Kinich, Ajaw, Mualani and Kachina have no leads. At the Masters of the Night-Wind, Citlali asks them to check on Ororon, who is setting a trap for Xbalanque with a young qucusaur named Cacucu. Xilonen is asleep to avoid work. At the Flower-Feather Clan, Chasca takes them to Iansan, who is planning the tournament course.

**A New Pilgrimage.** Iansan's course is tested, and a dormant Secret Source Automaton wakes and attacks Mavuika, who defeats it. Mavuika treats the Traveler to a huge meal in the Scions of the Canopy and takes them to the stadium on her Flamestrider. The tournament has several events; Kinich wins and Ajaw alone does not congratulate him. In the crowd Mavuika spots Xbalanque in the body of Mausau, a young boy from the tribes.

**Hearken, Ye Who Have Returned.** Xbalanque, a joker, introduces himself as "Lanque" and explains that the child is asleep in a happy dream and is unharmed. He says he once prayed to the Ruler of Death as a mortal, struck a bargain to share her strength and let Natlan run on new rules, and bet on winning the long war; his visit is the prize. He asks how the war was won and Mavuika summarises it. He wishes to fight her. In a spirit arena of the sun they duel until the flame goes out, and he declares her the winner. He offers his remaining life force to revive someone she misses; she declines. He relays a message from her family and comrades. After riding her Flamestrider across Ochkanatlan and the People of the Springs, he leaves Mausau's body. At a feast with signal flames, Kinich is toasted, and Mausau, awake, says he dreamed of being very strong and decides to train.
### ULTRA +
Finer points. Xbalanque says the borrowed body had less than a third of his usual strength, and that he failed to reach the final 32 in the tournament. He jokes about the trouble he had after waking, with a fishing line, a trap and a coach who made him double his warm-up. He calls Mavuika the inheritor of his Ancient Name "Kiongozi" (Leader). After he leaves, Mavuika notes she can no longer sense Xbalanque's soul. Mavuika says that the signal flames at night let tribes light a path for those returning. She asks the Traveler to introduce her to the other Archons one day. Afterward the Traveler wonders aloud how she knew what they were going to say, and she answers that the night is young.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Xbalanque can return, but only for a single day and only inside a living body. His deal with the Ruler of Death let Natlan follow its own rules for the long war. Mavuika's family, comrades and friends who died are said to still call to her and watch over her from the winds.
### LARGE +
Xbalanque tests Mavuika's strength not only of body but also of spirit, and judges that the inheritance of Ancient Names has proven its worth. The scene confirms that Mavuika has chosen not to use the means that could bring a person back briefly.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mavuika leads the story; the Traveler and Paimon accompany her. Xbalanque is the visitor. Citlali, Kinich, Ajaw, Mualani, Kachina, Ororon, Xilonen, Chasca and Iansan appear as the heroes of their tribes, and Mausau is the boy whose body Xbalanque borrows.
### LARGE +
Iansan organises the tournament; Ajaw keeps mocking Mavuika; Ororon sets a trap for Xbalanque; Xilonen sleeps through the early search. Mausau remembers the experience as a dream.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kinich wins the reworked Pilgrimage. Xbalanque leaves at the end of the day and Mausau resolves to train for the next tournament. Mavuika has again chosen to keep the dead in memory.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.3 as a Story Quest. It requires the Archon Quest act Incandescent Ode of Resurrection and Adventure Rank 40, and is set right after the war with the Abyss ends.

## entry: Xbalanque Returns
@keys: Xbalanque, Lanque
@keys2: Mavuika, Pilgrimage, Mausau
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The first Pyro Archon returns for one day in the body of a boy named Mausau, enters the Pilgrimage tournament, and duels Mavuika in a spirit arena. He loses narrowly, offers his remaining life force, and leaves at dusk. Mausau is left unharmed.

## entry: Mausau
@keys: Mausau
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A boy who takes part in the Pilgrimage tournament and, unknown to him, is possessed by Xbalanque. He wakes after the feast remembering a very vivid dream and decides to train for the next Pilgrimage.

## entry: Signal Flames
@keys: signal flame, signal flames
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
Braziers that Natlan sets up to guide people home at night. Those returning light them themselves, and the light shows the tribe that the person has made it back.
