## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The Tribal Chronicles chapter of the Collective of Plenty, which also serves as Varesa's story quest. The Traveler and Paimon meet Varesa near the Great Volcano of Tollan, visit her tribe and her family orchard, and notice strange mushrooms growing there that are surprisingly delicious. When the mushrooms begin to talk, the pair find they have been eating the remains of a fungi god, Bifrons, who is trying to recover enough power for a comeback. Varesa takes responsibility for keeping an eye on it.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three acts: Ardent Land of Plenty, Orchard Repose and Field Fungal Traces. The first two are a gentle introduction to the Collective of Plenty's farms and food culture; the third turns the mushrooms into a mystery. It is told with a lot of humour about Varesa's appetite, and ends with the tribe turning the god's tool against it by simply feasting on it.
### LARGE +
Beneath the comedy it is a story about being a hero. Varesa is very strong but has never moved past the team stage of the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame because she cannot bring herself to fight her own people, and she worries about living up to a legacy. At the end she concludes that a hero is someone who makes the right choice at the right time.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I. Walking near the Great Volcano of Tollan, the Traveler and Paimon meet Varesa, a young woman of the Collective of Plenty. She offers to take them to her tribe and shows her strength on the way, smashing through rocks thrown by an eruption and dealing with some hilichurls. At the village she asks them to meet her at the arena.

Act II. Varesa shows them around the village. Iansan, who coaches Varesa, explains that Varesa has never passed the team stage of the Pilgrimage because she cannot fight her own people. At the family orchard Varesa calms her Tatankasaur guardian Vanana, explains that the area is ideal for growing fruit which the tribe sells to others, and mentions an upcoming food tournament for which the orchard supplies dessert fruit. While harvesting, Paimon spots mushrooms that Varesa says appeared when the orchard was planted. She cooks a simple meal of the harvest.

Act III. At the tournament, Varesa wins a special oversized challenge and mentions that she eats the orchard's mushrooms to balance flavours. Atolli, who cooks, says there are red and green ones and that the green ones are to be eaten at once, as someone from Sumeru advised. The Traveler eats a green mushroom, and a voice calling itself the Sage-Lord of Fungi addresses them as worthy devotees. Following its clues, they learn that the better the aroma of the cooked mushrooms the closer the god is to showing itself. The god mocks the heat of the grilled mushrooms as insufficient, so the tribe hunts for a hotter heat source and finds one in a Furnace Shell Mountain Weasel. With it the mushrooms' scent grows, the Traveler, Paimon and Varesa are drawn into a fungal realm, and they chase the Sage-Lord to its hiding place, learning that it is a weakened god named Bifrons, hoping to grow in power on a spread of spores.
### LARGE +
**Ardent Land of Plenty (5.5).** Varesa introduces herself and demonstrates her abilities on the way to the tribe's village.

**Orchard Repose (5.5).** Iansan explains Varesa's training and the obstacle she has faced in the Pilgrimage. At the orchard Varesa tells the Traveler how she began her "eating journey" and why the mushrooms there are valued even though nobody planted them.

**Sage Supreme (5.5).** Iansan and Acatl tell the Traveler that when the Abyss attacked Natlan, the Collective of Plenty had their own struggles, which Iansan believes was an effort to stop them sending reinforcements elsewhere, and that Varesa helped defend the land. Acatl describes the "Guardians of Tollan": the volcano brings destruction but also the opportunity to grow crops. A "Varesa Challenge" was created after the Speaker's Chamber noticed a single person ordering food meant for a large group and investigated it for fraud; on seeing Varesa finish the entire portion, Mavuika let it be. Not even the Pyro Archon could finish the Varesa Challenge. The Traveler eats a green mushroom, hears the Sage-Lord, and meets mushrooms that turn into fungi when examined, one of which asks a riddle to which the Traveler answers with a bad pun, and that is taken as the right answer. A mushroom explains that being eaten forms a connection between the eater and the Sage-Lord.

**The Meaning of Courage (5.5).** The Sage-Lord returns to tell the group that the heat is a fraction of the devotion required, and mocks their intelligence. Varesa takes the Traveler to the volcano, where they defeat four Avatars of Lava and, after an eruption, a huge weasel whose armoured core Varesa extracts, thinking it hotter than ordinary cores. Varesa admits she is reluctant to make her own legacy, and the Traveler and Paimon reassure her that she is acting on her own will.

**Smokeless War (5.5).** The tribe uses the weasel's armour in a cooking rig. The green mushrooms start giving off mist and the Traveler, Varesa and Paimon are dragged into another realm where the Sage-Lord says they are worthy devotees and sends clones that are easily defeated. They follow it, jump down a well, and catch up. Varesa seizes it briefly and the group sees a memory: people had eaten so much of its clones that its power was nearly spent, but it could still control a single human, Chatraka, whom it ordered to carry it somewhere hot. At a volcano a volcanic eruption buried them both. The Traveler suspects the truth, and in the realm the Sage-Lord hides inside a statue; when they destroy it, it gives its name as Bifrons, a fungi god, and reveals that the green mushrooms were meant to spread spores so it could grow in power and take revenge on Sumeru for the Archon War. Paimon tells it the Archon War ended long ago, and Bifrons says it has heard about a war that was in fact against the Abyss, though it brushes this off as a lie. The Traveler deduces it is too weak and needs a host to control, and Bifrons threatens a Jadeplume Terrorshroom but is ignored.
### ULTRA +
Finer points. Back in reality, the tribe holds a huge feast with the green mushrooms, and Saurians and even Fatui join in. Bifrons demands that they stop and is bitten by a Tatankasaur whelp. Varesa thanks the Traveler for helping her become a hero in the story, and Paimon suggests finding someone who can help with Bifrons. At the Adventurers' Guild Katheryne turns out to have found Tighnari nearby; he is surprised to see the pair in Natlan but agrees to help once he hears about the Sage-Lord. When Varesa asks him about Bifrons's clones, he confirms there is one in the Chasm named Xamaran. Because it is not a green-cap mushroom and its link to Bifrons is likely weakened, he asks Varesa not to eat it, and she says she would never eat a friendly mushroom. The chapter's closing text says Varesa learned what it means to be a hero, that the god had so little divine power left that it posed little threat, and that Varesa offered to take responsibility and keep watch over it.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The delicious mushrooms in Varesa's orchard are the remains of Bifrons, a fungi god with ties to Sumeru, so weakened by people eating its clones that it can only act through mushrooms and mind control a single host. The green ones are meant to carry its spores. Bifrons's plan is revenge on Sumeru for the Archon War, based on outdated information.
### LARGE +
Bifrons says it gave followers sustenance in proportion to their contributions. The quest shows that a human, Chatraka, was once used by Bifrons to carry it somewhere hot and that both were buried by an eruption. One of Bifrons's clones, Xamaran, lives in the Chasm and, per Tighnari, is likely disconnected from Bifrons. The god's comic threat of a Jadeplume Terrorshroom goes unfulfilled.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon are the viewpoint pair. Varesa is the heart of the chapter: a cheerful, enormously strong and hungry woman who is uncertain about her role as a hero. Iansan coaches her and Acatl explains the tribe's customs and history. Atolli is a cook who supplies Varesa with food. Bifrons is the villain, calling itself the Sage-Lord of Fungi. Tighnari arrives at the end as an expert on fungi.
### LARGE +
Vanana is Varesa's Tatankasaur guardian at the orchard. Mavuika appears only in an anecdote about how the Varesa Challenge began. Katheryne arranges the introduction to Tighnari. Chatraka is Bifrons's former human host, seen in a memory.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Bifrons is exposed as nearly powerless. Varesa takes responsibility for keeping an eye on it. Tighnari agrees to advise on Bifrons's clones. The Collective of Plenty celebrates with a feast built around the mushrooms. Varesa has learned what being a hero means. Xamaran is to be spared.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: all three acts belong to Version 5.5, with the event running from 2025-03-26 to 2025-04-15. Story order: Acts I and II require only the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame in the Archon Quest part Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn, while the chapter and event pages require Incandescent Ode of Resurrection (5.3) for Act III. The event offered a Quick Start feature to unlock Act III for players who were behind on the Archon Quest.

## entry: Bifrons
@keys: Bifrons, Sage-Lord of Fungi
@keys2: Varesa, mushroom, Collective of Plenty
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A fungi god who calls itself the Sage-Lord of Fungi. Its power was nearly spent when people ate large amounts of its clones, and it could only control a single human, Chatraka, who carried it to a volcano where an eruption buried them. It lives on in the green mushrooms in Varesa's orchard, tries to recruit worthy devotees by being eaten, and plans revenge on Sumeru for the Archon War, unaware that the war ended long ago. It is exposed and mocked by the Traveler, Paimon and Varesa, and the tribe feasts on its mushrooms.

### STANDARD
An oversized food challenge at the Collective of Plenty's food tournament that only people who have completed the normal challenge may try. It began when the Speaker's Chamber noticed that one person was ordering food meant for a large group, suspected fraud and investigated, and found Varesa eating the whole portion. Mavuika let it be. Not even the Pyro Archon could finish it.
