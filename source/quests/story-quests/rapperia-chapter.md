## summary
### LITE
Clorinde's Story Quest. She and Navia, both veterans of a local roleplaying club called the Tabletop Troupe, invite the Traveler, Furina and Lyney to play a new game about the Marechaussee Hunters. The script's author, Florian, has hidden a real purpose behind it, and the party ends up facing a real monster in a hidden vault and a church basement.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Silent Night, of four quests: Judgment Day, Hunters' Gathering, Buried Honor and Vanquishing Phantoms. It was released in version 4.7 and is unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Masquerade of the Guilty and The Little Oceanid. It shows the Champion Duelist's private life, her hobby as a Senior Game Master, and her private regard for the Hunters.
### LARGE +
The theme is honour that goes unremembered. The Hunters chose obscurity, Florian could not accept it and was corrupted, and Clorinde, who is herself a Marechaussee Hunter, decides what to do about the treasure.

## plot
### LITE
Clorinde and Navia recruit the Traveler for a game, Marechaussee Hunter: Judgment Day. Play begins in the Court of Fontaine and the party roleplays as Hunters. The party solves puzzles in an old vault, only to be attacked in the dark by a real Hunter, Florian, who is controlled by a monster. Clorinde calms him, and the party fights the Devorator in a church basement, with Florian offering himself as the target. Afterwards they learn the script was a cover.
### STANDARD +
Judgment Day: Navia and Clorinde, one player short, ask the Traveler to make a fourth. Lyney and Furina join, and Florian, the script manager, explains that the story is adapted from the real history of the Marechaussee Hunters, who hunted monsters with a swordsmanship passed down through generations. Clorinde will help by adding commentary.

Hunters' Gathering: the party roleplays as Hunters arriving in a hollow city that has sent a distress call. Players pick skills such as medicine, cooking and insight, and each can change how scenes go. They gather information, defeat monsters in a forest and find a chest.

Buried Honor: the next day the game moves to a set modelled on a prison, then a palace, and then to a hidden secret vault. The script seems to end, so Clorinde improvises. The party solves puzzles and collects three of four keys. In a darkened fight Clorinde defeats a 'guard', who turns out to be Florian. Florian says he is a descendant of Hunters, resented that his father died forgotten, and was driven by a voice. Clorinde reads it as a Devorator.

Vanquishing Phantoms: Florian asks to be the bait for the Devorator in the church basement. Clorinde accepts his request as a Champion Duelist. The monster takes him and he becomes a golem. The party defeats it by calling the names of the Marechaussee Hunters. Afterwards Florian explains and Clorinde forgives him.
### LARGE +
Judgment Day: Clorinde says she keeps work and private life separate. In court she represents the law, but privately she is an ordinary person who feels anger and sadness. Furina notes she never saw Clorinde cry. Lyney says Clorinde is one of the few Senior Game Masters of the Troupe.

Hunters' Gathering: the game master describes a legend of monsters sent by demons and sorcerers against early humanity and the rebels who became the Hunters. Combat in the actual game is against Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps and a Praetorian Golem.

Buried Honor: the fourth key is Clorinde's, and Florian says he had followed her for a while but never found an opening, so he had lured her through her hobby. Florian says his family served as Hunters for generations and were charged with maintaining a seal. After his father died he neglected it and visited it only later, where his memory goes blurred. He says the Devorator tells him to eliminate the Hunters. He took the keys by theft, from his father, another Hunter and a third Hunter on the way home from a tavern. Clorinde tells him the sword is not special and the wielder's intention is what matters, and that his 'evil sorcerer' shows who he really wanted to fight.

Vanquishing Phantoms: the stone tablet in the vault records Hunters such as Este, who inherited the duties of the Golden Hunter, and Walter, an executioner who used many tricks. The party takes some of the weapons, which glow when monsters are near.
### ULTRA +
The tablet: the names and weapons of the Marechaussee Hunters of past ages were left by their comrades, so that Hunters would be remembered by each other and not by the public. Hunters worked in the dark and gave up wealth and fame, and revealed their identity rarely; stories of kills were told at night around a bonfire.

Wrap-up: Clorinde tells the others, after Florian recovers, that the script was never a real story and was a lure. She tells him he is more use to the city above ground than below, and that he must return two stolen keys and accept any terms the wronged Hunters set. Lyney and Navia, who noticed something was wrong when Clorinde told them to close their eyes, did not break the illusion for Furina. Clorinde chooses to leave the treasure as it is, saying every name on the tablet made the same choice, and she wants only to be known as a Champion Duelist. She ends by asking the Traveler whether a journey is meaningful if no one remembers it. The Traveler says the journey and the shared time still matter, and Clorinde says she will remember the answer.

## revelations
### LITE
The script was a lure created by Florian, a descendant of Marechaussee Hunters, to get the last key from Clorinde. A Devorator was manipulating him.
### STANDARD +
The 'treasure' in the vault is a tablet of Hunters' names and their weapons. Clorinde herself is a Marechaussee Hunter, which she does not want publicised.
### LARGE +
The Devorator is a monster of several centuries ago that amplifies a person's obsessions, and Hunters sealed it with a four-part key held by separate Hunters.
### ULTRA +
Clorinde's master left her a sword and an old key before disappearing; the key is similar to the Hunter keys.

## characters
### LITE
Clorinde, Navia, Furina, Lyney, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Florian, the script manager of the Tabletop Troupe and a descendant of Hunters. Este and Walter, Hunters named on the tablet.
### LARGE +
Callas and Spina di Rosula members are mentioned in Navia and Clorinde's talk. Charlotte and Chiori are named as people Navia and Clorinde asked first.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Florian offers to turn himself in to the Garde, but Clorinde judges that he has suffered enough and sends him to return the keys. The vault is left undisturbed, with its secret kept among the party.
### LARGE +
The Devorator is defeated in the church basement by the party calling the Hunters' names and Clorinde's final blow.

## placement

Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Masquerade of the Guilty and the Animula Choragi Chapter's The Little Oceanid. Released in 4.7. The setting spans the Court of Fontaine, the Fontaine Research Institute region and the Beryl Region.

### STANDARD
A roleplaying club in the Court of Fontaine whose games let friends take part in a story. Clorinde and Navia are veteran members, and Clorinde is a Senior Game Master. Lyney is a casual member who is usually the game master of his own sibling games. Florian is a script manager who writes for the club.

## entry: Florian

@version: 4.7

### STANDARD
A script manager of the Tabletop Troupe and the son of a Hunter. After his father died forgotten, bitterness and a Devorator drove him to steal Hunter keys and lure Clorinde to a hidden vault. Clorinde forgives him in the end, and he volunteers to be the Devorator's bait in a duel to restore his honour.

### STANDARD
A monster active centuries ago that amplifies the desires and obsessions in people's hearts until they take over. Marechaussee Hunters sealed it away with a key in four parts. It reacts to the names of the Hunters of its own era.
