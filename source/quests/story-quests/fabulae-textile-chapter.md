## summary
### LITE
The Fabulae Textile Chapter is Xingqiu's Story Quest (Version 1.0), set in Liyue. In its one act, Bookworm Swordsman, the Traveler seeks the Liyue volume of the Teyvat Travel Guide and ends up helping Xingqiu defend a book collector, Chang the Ninth, from a merchant backed by the Fatui. Xingqiu defeats the merchant by cornering the market in Cor Lapis, and is revealed as heir of the Feiyun Commerce Guild.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Justice, for Books' Sake; Know Thyself, Know Thy Enemy; Calm Before the Storm; and Justice Is Its Own Reward. It requires Adventure Rank 26 and Lisa's Story Quest, Troublesome Work. The Chinese title means roughly "Chapter of the Brocade".
### LARGE +
The act is a character piece about Xingqiu's two sides: a bookish, kind swordsman who reads in the street, and a calculating merchant-heir with the resources to win without bloodshed. It shows his preference for a clever plan over brute force.
### ULTRA +
A strange bookmark left in the returned book is the last clue to Chang that Xingqiu helped him, which the act leaves implied.

## plot
### LITE
Looking for the Liyue Travel Guide, the Traveler meets Xingqiu, who wants a rare book from Chang the Ninth. Chang is being pressured to sell Cor Lapis cheaply to the merchant Gentry Maocai, who works with a Fatui agent. Xingqiu quietly buys up every scrap of Cor Lapis with Feiyun Commerce Guild money, forces Gentry to deal on his terms, and ensures Chang gets paid and keeps his books.
### STANDARD
**Justice, for Books' Sake.** At Wanwen Bookhouse in Liyue Harbor the owner, Jifang, has no Liyue volume of the Teyvat Travel Guide and suggests Chang the Ninth, a collector in Qingce Village. A passerby, Xingqiu, who has spent three years hunting the final volume of the Legend of the Shattered Halberd series, joins them. Chang's mining business has failed; he pawned most of his books, including the Travel Guide, but kept the rare sixth volume. The thug Changhu enters and says Gentry Maocai wants Chang's Cor Lapis stockpile at 30% of market price, threatening him if he refuses. Xingqiu steps in, naming himself a disciple of the Guhua Clan, which Changhu mocks as a fallen order. In thanks, Chang lends Xingqiu the book. Later Changhu returns with thugs and a Fatui Electro Cicin Mage, and Xingqiu drives them off with his sword and Hydro Vision. The Traveler suspects the Fatui are involved, and Xingqiu sends them to investigate Gentry while he finishes reading.

**Know Thyself, Know Thy Enemy.** Posing as a VIP client from Mondstadt, the Traveler and Paimon learn from Gentry's guard that he is meeting a client at Xinyue Kiosk. They eavesdrop and hear Gentry discuss an order with a Fatui agent, Javert, who offers help but urges him to finish quickly. They report to Xingqiu near Yujing Terrace, who gives them a letter written in a cipher for the Feiyun Commerce Guild.

**Calm Before the Storm.** At the Guild, Xu decodes the letter and agrees to help. Together at Liuli Pavilion they set the plan: force Gentry to desperation by buying every stock of Cor Lapis in Liyue. Xu supplies the Mora. The Traveler buys the ore from city merchants and Shizhuang the Strong in the Chasm, then visits Chang and offers market price, which relieves him; Paimon assures him Gentry's thugs will not return.

**Justice Is Its Own Reward.** At a Guild warehouse outside the city Xingqiu explains that, as the sole holder of Cor Lapis, he forces Gentry to come to him. Gentry arrives with Javert and tries to seize the ore by force, but they are beaten. Javert, judging the matter too costly, withdraws. Xu arrives and calls Xingqiu his lord, revealing him as heir of the Feiyun Commerce Guild. As punishment for intimidating Chang, Xingqiu triples the price on the stockpile, and Gentry pays it. Xingqiu asks the Traveler to return the borrowed book. Chang says his pawned books were all returned that morning and rewards the Traveler with the Liyue Travel Guide; a strange bookmark falls from the returned volume, hinting that Xingqiu was involved.
### LARGE +
Remarks to keep. Xingqiu's plan relies on the Guild's resources rather than a duel: a market cornering that leaves Gentry no alternative. Chang is at first certain that Xingqiu had little to do with it, which the bookmark contradicts.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Xingqiu is the heir of the Feiyun Commerce Guild and writes in a Guild cipher; the Guhua Clan is described by its enemies as having fallen from grace. A Fatui agent is involved in a Liyue ore deal, a recurring theme in early Liyue content.

## characters
### LITE
Xingqiu, the Traveler and Paimon. Chang the Ninth, Jifang, Changhu, Gentry Maocai, Javert and Xu appear.
### STANDARD +
Xingqiu is bookish, principled and strategic. Chang the Ninth is a genial collector in financial trouble. Gentry Maocai is a ruthless merchant. Javert is a Fatui agent who cuts his losses.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler obtains the Liyue Travel Guide and a lasting friendship with Xingqiu. Chang's business is rescued, and Gentry is forced to pay triple. Xingqiu's heirship becomes known to the people present.

Release order: 1.0. Story order: early Liyue content, after Lisa's Troublesome Work.
