## summary
### LITE
The Lepus Chapter is Amber's Story Quest (Version 1.0), set in Mondstadt. In its one act, Wind, Courage, and Wings, Amber supervises the Traveler's exam for a gliding licence. The exam is interrupted by a hunt for a criminal named Raptor, whom Amber catches by leaping into a canyon on a wind current.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: Glide With the Wind, License to Glide, Mondstadt Glider, They Call Him Raptor and Outrider Style. It requires Adventure Rank 8 and the Prologue's Act I, The Outlander Who Caught the Wind. "Lepus" is Latin for hare or rabbit; the Chinese and Japanese chapter names mean "little rabbit".
### LARGE +
The chapter is a light, comic introduction to Amber as the Knights' Outrider, showing her persistence and her love of gliding. It ends by setting up a friendly rivalry with the Traveler through the Wind Gliding Championship.
### ULTRA +
The act's main beats are a licence exam (two tests, then a modified final exam of bombing hilichurls), a case of mistaken identity involving the Knight Otto, and a chase from Springvale to Raptor's stronghold. The wiki notes the licence and championship hooks but the act does not otherwise change Mondstadt's situation.

## plot
### LITE
Amber says the Traveler needs a gliding licence and sets an exam. A Knight mistakes the Traveler for the accomplice of the thief Raptor. Amber and the Traveler track him down, catch him, and she hands over the licence.
### STANDARD
**Glide With the Wind.** Amber asks how the Traveler's gliding is going and admits she forgot to mention that a licence is required in Mondstadt, though the Traveler has glided without one. She will oversee the exam, since she gave them the glider, and hands them a guidebook. The book she gave at first was her favourite storybook, by mistake.

**License to Glide, Mondstadt Glider, They Call Him Raptor.** After studying the real manual, the Traveler passes the first test and meets Amber at Favonius Cathedral. During the next test the Knight Otto orders the Traveler down and demands the licence, taking them for an accomplice of Raptor, a thief who stole a priceless artifact the Knights had set as bait and then escaped. Amber explains herself and offers to help. Raptor was last seen in Springvale, where one of the sisters had put an Anemo mark on the artifact. They find clues and then hilichurls attacking the merchant Farrah, who says a man dropped from the sky into a hilichurl camp with a heavy object and fled.
### LARGE +
**Outrider Style.** Ambushed by hilichurls and Raptor's men, they find his stronghold. Amber changes the final exam so the Traveler must drop bombs on hilichurls along the way. At a canyon dead end Raptor tries to goad them into leaving; Amber jumps, a wind current carries her across, and she catches him, having reasoned that he could only escape if they left. At the Cathedral she gives the Traveler their licence and signs them up for the next Wind Gliding Championship, since nobody else can challenge her.

## revelations
### STANDARD
No world-level revelations. The Knights set bait traps for thieves, hilichurl camps threaten roads near Springvale, and gliding in Mondstadt requires an official licence earned by exam.

## characters
### LITE
Amber, the Traveler and Paimon. Otto, Raptor and Farrah appear.
### STANDARD +
Amber is both exam supervisor and partner, enthusiastic and stubborn. Otto is the over-zealous Knight who first threatens arrest; Raptor is the criminal who falls from the sky.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler earns an official Mondstadt gliding licence and an entry in the Wind Gliding Championship. The chapter is also a prerequisite for Razor's Lupus Minor Chapter.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: 1.0. Story order: early, after the first Prologue act and before Razor's chapter.
