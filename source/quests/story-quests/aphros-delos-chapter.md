## summary
### LITE
Aphros Delos Chapter is Eula's Story Quest (added in Version 1.5), a single act titled "The Spindrift Shall Never Return to the Sea". Jean asks the Traveler to approach Schubert, a proud member of the disgraced Lawrence Clan suspected of dealing with the Fatui. Because he despises anyone without noble manners, the Traveler is sent to Eula, a Knights of Favonius officer who was born a Lawrence, to learn etiquette.
### STANDARD
The quest has three parts. First, the Traveler fails to talk to Schubert and finds Eula fighting Fatui in the Stormbearer Mountains. Second, Eula coaches them in noble speech, then in "dignified" combat and movement. Third, the Traveler wins Schubert's trust with a formal manner and a gift, is led to a Fatui hideout, and exposes the plot with Eula's help. It also shows how hard the Lawrence name still makes life for Eula in Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
The act is a character piece as much as a mystery: the Fatui plot is small (giving the Fatui a diagram of the northern wall and its patrol routes), and most of the runtime goes to Eula's manner, her reputation among townspeople, and the gap between her stiff, formal speech and her kindness.

## plot
### STANDARD
**Shadow of the Past.** At the Knights of Favonius Headquarters, Jean tells the Traveler and Paimon that Schubert of the Lawrence Clan has been seen near Mondstadt's northern city walls. She hopes an outsider can approach him, given the hostility between the Knights and the Lawrences. Schubert takes offence at the Traveler's lack of etiquette, complains that the city is in disarray without noble guidance, and leaves. Amber suggests her friend Eula and says she is probably patrolling the Stormbearer Mountains. There the Traveler and Paimon are attacked by Fatui Skirmishers; a sniper on a tower fires on the Traveler, Eula parries the shot, climbs the tower and kills him. She scolds the Traveler for carelessness, Paimon calls her speech weird and she bristles, and once she understands their aim she offers to teach them, after returning to the city.

**Speech and Etiquette.** In the city Eula tries to teach noble speech. Paimon is confused, and when Eula approaches townspeople without asking them to take part, they react with hostility to a Lawrence. Paimon steps in by saying the Traveler is a fast learner. The second half of the lesson is on Dragonspine, where Eula teaches "dignified" fighting and travelling and the Traveler passes both trials. They then go to Good Hunter, where Eula orders Gebratenes Fleisch mit Sauerkraut as a gift; the group shares a meal, Eula says her uncle is probably camping outside Springvale, and it is explained that she is judged by her surname although she is kind.

**Through the Motions, to the Heart.** At Springvale Schubert is irritated but gives a second chance. The Traveler's formal speech and the roasted meat impress him, and he treats them as a trusted companion since he needs capable people. He takes them to a hideout that the Knights had cleared earlier and the Fatui have retaken, which confirms his collaboration. He introduces them to the Fatui agent Sergei, who is suspicious, and hands over a diagram of the northern wall and patrol routes. When the Traveler reveals themselves as the Honorary Knight, both men flee. Eula and two Knights arrest Schubert, and the two argue about right and wrong before she has him taken away. The Traveler and Eula clear the Fatui out and recover the diagram. Eula thanks them but asks them not to take her targets next time.
### LARGE +
The set-up leans on Eula's habits: she has spent a long time tracking this Fatui group, finds them only when she hears fighting, and rebukes the Traveler as a way of expressing concern. Her lessons treat noble manners as a tool the Traveler can borrow. Because the wiki summary calls Schubert her uncle, the confrontation at Springvale is a family argument as well as an arrest.
### ULTRA +
The Chinese chapter name is the "Chapter of Wave Foam" (the act title speaks of foam that never returns to the sea), and the chapter's in-game description speaks of a noble crown forged in Mondstadt from the blood and tears of the common folk, broken when the west wind blew and leaving only small spindrifts, and asks where those spindrifts will go (it does not name the Lawrences, but the act follows one of their descendants). Quest names run Shadow of the Past, Speech and Etiquette, Through the Motions, to the Heart.

## revelations
@version: 1.5
### STANDARD
- A Lawrence Clan member, Schubert, was collaborating with the Fatui and had given them information on the northern wall's defences; the Fatui had reoccupied a hideout the Knights had earlier cleared.
- Eula is a Knights of Favonius officer who is also a disavowed member of the Lawrence family; people in Mondstadt hold her family name against her.
- Eula is outwardly severe but acts to protect the Traveler and the city.
### LARGE +
The Lawrence Clan's loss of status, and the way ordinary Mondstadters transfer their old grievance onto Eula, are the quest's main social revelation; it does not retell the clan's history, which belongs to the faction entry.

## characters
### STANDARD
Eula (lead; patrols, teaches, arrests Schubert), Schubert (Lawrence Clan, Fatui collaborator), Sergei (Fatui contact), Jean (quest giver), Amber (recommends Eula and shares the meal), Paimon and the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Townspeople in the city appear as the foil to Eula's lessons. Two unnamed Knights help make the arrest. The Fatui force consists of Skirmishers in the mountains and the group in the hideout.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Fatui plot against the north wall is stopped and Schubert is in the Knights' custody. The Traveler leaves with a better understanding of Eula; the quest does not resolve her standing in Mondstadt, and the townspeople's prejudice remains.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.5. Story order is not numbered by canon. Unlocked after the Prologue's Act III "Song of the Dragon and Freedom" at Adventure Rank 40. It has only one act and is standalone among Mondstadt's story quests.

## entry: Eula's Etiquette Lessons
@keys: Eula etiquette, Speech and Etiquette, noble etiquette
@keys2: Traveler, Schubert, Aphros Delos
@version: 1.5
### STANDARD
In "Speech and Etiquette" Eula teaches the Traveler to speak like a noble, first in the city and then, as a test of dignified conduct, in combat and travel on Dragonspine. Her formal lecturing confuses Paimon, and approaching townspeople without asking them to participate angers them because of her Lawrence background.
### LARGE +
The lesson ends with Eula ordering roasted meat at Good Hunter as a present for Schubert, who is camping near Springvale.

## entry: Schubert and the Fatui Hideout
@keys: Schubert, Sergei
@keys2: Lawrence, Fatui, Springvale

### STANDARD
Schubert, a proud Lawrence who thinks Mondstadt needs nobles to run it, was passing the Fatui a diagram of the northern wall and patrol routes. He brings the Traveler to a hideout the Fatui reclaimed and introduces them to Sergei. When the Traveler reveals they are an Honorary Knight, Schubert flees and is arrested by Eula.
### LARGE +
