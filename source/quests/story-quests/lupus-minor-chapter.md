## summary
### LITE
The Lupus Minor Chapter is Razor's Story Quest (Version 1.0), set in Wolvendom, Mondstadt. In its one act, The Meaning of Lupical, the Traveler meets the boy raised among wolves, and with Amber helps clear his pack of blame for attacks on villages. Boreas, the Wolf of the North, tells Razor he is human, which leaves Razor questioning his identity.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Wolves' Territory, Another Day as an Outrider!, Ravaged by Wolves and Fate's Chosen Lupical. It requires Adventure Rank 21, the Prologue's Song of the Dragon and Freedom and Amber's Wind, Courage, and Wings. Its tone mixes comedy (Razor first takes the Traveler's friend for Klee and later jokingly forgets Amber's name) with a serious question about belonging.
### LARGE +
The theme is identity: Razor lives as a wolf in spirit but is "ultimately human and not wolf" in Boreas's words, and cannot join his pack in a fight against outsiders. The chapter ends with him unsettled but declining Amber's offer of safety in Mondstadt.
### ULTRA +
The act is entirely about trust between outsiders and the wolves: Amber's initial suspicion, the villagers' accusations, Boreas's test and Razor's own doubts. The black-leadered outside pack is raised but not resolved in this act.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler meets Razor in Wolvendom. Villagers near Springvale suspect his wolves of attacks, but Razor says an outside pack led by a black wolf is responsible. Boreas tests Razor, confirms the outsiders' guilt, then tells Razor that, being human, he cannot take part in dealing with them.
### STANDARD
**Wolves' Territory.** In Wolvendom the Traveler and Paimon are attacked by slimes, one of which grows large. A boy, Razor, cuts it back down to size and the slimes flee. He had been hunting for his wolf pack and asks the Traveler to find raw meat, which falls short of what the pack needs. He mentions a "red burny girl" who often does the same; the Traveler thinks of Amber, but he means Klee. Back at Mondstadt's gate, Amber has not heard of Razor but is investigating reports of wolf attacks at Drunkard Gorge and Springvale. They find different kinds of fur and a stray Wolfhook seed, indicating the attackers came from Wolvendom, and the younger villagers refuse to wait quietly.

**Ravaged by Wolves.** Amber meets Razor in the wilderness. He smells blood and shows an injured wolf; his poor social skills at first make Amber suspect his pack. Razor says another pack from outside, led by a black wolf, has been attacking the villages.
### LARGE +
**Fate's Chosen Lupical.** Razor has the Traveler gather Wolfhook seeds for the injured wolf while he answers Amber's questions. The Springvale villagers arrive and accuse him. Boreas, the Wolf of the North, is angered by the disturbance and orders Razor to fight him to prove he deserves to protect outsiders. With the Traveler's help Razor calms Boreas, who confirms an outside pack is at fault and says it will pay, but Razor cannot intervene because he is human, not wolf. Impressed, Boreas also lets the Traveler spar with him. Razor is upset and starts to doubt who he is. He declines Amber's offer to bring him to Mondstadt, thanks the three and jokingly forgets Amber's name.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Boreas, the Wolf of the North, is angered by disturbances and tests Razor's worth. An outside wolf pack, not Razor's, is blamed for the attacks. In Boreas's view Razor is human, not wolf, and cannot take part in the wolves' fight.

## characters
### LITE
Razor, Amber, the Traveler and Paimon; Boreas the Wolf of the North; Springvale villagers. Klee is mentioned by Razor.
### STANDARD +
Razor is protective of his wolf pack and socially awkward, which at first makes Amber suspect him. Amber tries to keep the villagers calm while she investigates.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Razor stays in Wolvendom, uncertain about his identity, and the attacks are attributed to an outside pack. Boreas also allows the Traveler to spar with him afterwards.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: 1.0. Story order: after Song of the Dragon and Freedom and Amber's Lepus Chapter.
