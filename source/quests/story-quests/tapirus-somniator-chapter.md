## summary
### LITE
The Tapirus Somniator Chapter is Yumemizuki Mizuki's Story Quest, released in Version 5.4, and as of 7.1 it has one act, Dream Eater's Melancholia. The Traveler meets Mizuki, a yumekui-baku who works as a clinical psychologist at the Aisa Bathhouse in Inazuma City, and helps her when a rival clinic draws away her patients. The rival's cheer turns out to have an inhuman source: a nightmare monster.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: Encounter in the Realm of Dreams, Crisis at the Aisa Bathhouse, A Deceit Called Happiness, Descendant of Nightmares and an Afterword. Much of it takes place in dreams. It is partly a comedy of bathhouse therapy and rivalry, and partly a study of what it costs Mizuki to digest other people's nightmares.
### LARGE +
The quest also settles the old tension between Mizuki and Yae Miko, who were friends before Miko became Guuji. Mizuki admits to feeling inferior to Miko, and Miko admits she held back out of concern for Mizuki.

## plot
### LITE
Mizuki accidentally pulls the Traveler into her dream while she is digesting her patients' nightmares, and gives them a VIP pass to the Aisa Bathhouse. The bathhouse is in financial trouble, and a rival, Emura Ichigensai, claims to remove all troubles. His patients are cheerful but forget their treatment. Mizuki and the Traveler discover that he is Emura Chuuya, possessed by a nightmare monster named Kuroyume. They fight Kuroyume in a dream, wake in time to stop Chuuya drowning, and the baku clear Narukami Island of nightmares.
### STANDARD
During a night walk in Inazuma, a floating youkai leads the Traveler and Paimon to a woman by the shore. A crowd appears as soon as eyes meet. She then looks satisfied, as if she had eaten something delicious, and expels the intruders from her dream. She introduces herself as Yumemizuki Mizuki, a yumekui-baku, and the floating youkai as her companion. They had entered her dream while she was digesting her patients' nightmares. She apologises for eating the Traveler's good dream and offers a VIP pass to the Aisa Bathhouse.

At the bathhouse, Aikawa Susumu says Mizuki is a responsible clinical psychologist and the largest shareholder and investor, yet the bathhouse is struggling. Two baku staff, Hyakujitsuhaku Shion and Shiba Murasaki, greet them. Paimon is assigned to Shion, who works with food, and Mizuki takes the Traveler for one-on-one therapy, in which shadowy figures of the Traveler appear and she is told not to look back. She finds overwhelming negative emotions but no bad dreams. In the hot springs, Yae Miko arrives, reveals that she and Mizuki were friends before she became Guuji, urges Mizuki to sell her shares and invest in Yae Publishing House, and warns of Emura Ichigensai's new psychotherapy clinic. Mizuki says he is a mysterious man who avoids her.

At the clinic, patients Susukida and Umihiko praise Emura Ichigensai for taking away their problems and call the bathhouse a scam. The Traveler falls asleep while waiting and dreams of an eye that attacks. The clinic closes before their number is called. Back at the bathhouse, Shion suspects from the story Paimon tells that Emura is Emura Chuuya, a former patient with a terrible temper whom Murasaki blacklisted. Susukida and Umihiko then arrive to book a session after all. Later Mizuki is found crying at the shore: this is the final stage of digestion, in which she feels the negative emotions of the nightmares she has eaten.

The next day Shion and Murasaki suffer extreme indigestion, which Mizuki traces to Susukida and Umihiko's nightmares. At Emura's clinic, they show only happiness and have no memory of the bathhouse visit. Mizuki enters their minds and finds that the troubles were isolated from their conscious minds, not removed, and will fester. She deduces that something inhuman is behind it. Miko, who first made things difficult with a not-so-subtle hint, eventually gives the Traveler and Paimon Chuuya's home address on condition that Mizuki not know.
### LARGE +
At Chuuya's home they are attacked by monsters, which Mizuki explains are manifestations of his suppressed emotions in his own nightmare. He insists he is healed, and an entity emerges from him: the eye from the Traveler's nightmare, a nightmare monster, the natural enemy of the yumekui-baku. It calls itself Kuroyume, the last of its kind, and escapes, saying it has found a way to revive its kind. In Chuuya's deeper dream they see fragments of his past, including rejection by his father for wanting to be a writer, and by classmates. Once Mizuki helps him open up to his mother in the last fragment, she can consume his nightmare. Kuroyume then takes them to the borderland, a hidden space in Inazuma's collective subconscious.

To match Kuroyume they gather lost sweet dreams, but these prove insufficient. Mizuki is nearly overcome until the Traveler offers a sweet dream of their own, restoring her. Together they wound Kuroyume, who survives. Hearing water, Mizuki realises Chuuya is underwater in the real world, being drowned by Kuroyume, and they must wake by jumping together. They wake underwater, Mizuki lifts them into the sky, and they see Narukami Island covered in nightmare bubbles. She summons other yumekui-baku to eat the nightmares. While Paimon watches them, the Traveler and Mizuki spend the night watching the moon from a tree. Paimon asks to book another appointment, and Mizuki says to ask again tomorrow.

In the Afterword the bathhouse is back to normal and Shion and Murasaki are recovered. Mizuki gives another VIP pass. Miko reports that Chuuya has been pulled from the water and is out of danger, but his mind has regressed to that of a young child. Mizuki sincerely thanks Miko, admits feeling inferior to her, and says that feeling gave her strength. Miko admits that she would have acted more directly were it not for fear of upsetting Mizuki.
### ULTRA +
The act is played on three levels: the bathhouse's ordinary business, the dream world, and the borderland. Mizuki's work is shown as real therapy rather than simple eating of bad dreams, since she tries to get patients to face what troubles them.

## revelations
@version: 5.4
### STANDARD
- Mizuki and Yae Miko were friends before Miko became Guuji.
- Emura Ichigensai is Emura Chuuya, a former patient of the bathhouse.
### LARGE +
- Chuuya's cheerfulness is the work of a nightmare monster, Kuroyume, who isolates troubles rather than curing them.
- Nightmare monsters are the natural enemy of the yumekui-baku, and Kuroyume says he is the last of his kind.
- The borderland is a hidden space in Inazuma's collective subconscious.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Yumemizuki Mizuki**: yumekui-baku and clinical psychologist at the Aisa Bathhouse.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: patients who become allies.
- **Yae Miko**: Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine and Mizuki's old friend.
- **Aikawa Susumu, Hyakujitsuhaku Shion, Shiba Murasaki**: the bathhouse's manager and baku therapists.
- **Emura Chuuya (Emura Ichigensai)**: the former patient who runs the rival clinic.
- **Susukida and Umihiko**: patients caught between the two clinics.
- **Kuroyume**: the nightmare monster possessing Chuuya.
### LARGE +
The floating youkai who accompanies Mizuki is called a companion in the quest; the wiki lists it as a Little Baku.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Kuroyume is wounded but survives. Chuuya is saved from drowning but his mind has regressed to that of a young child. The bathhouse recovers its business, and Mizuki and Miko clear the air between them.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.4. It requires Adventure Rank 40, the Archon Quest Chapter II Act III (Omnipresence Over Mortals) and the Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter Act II (Transient Dreams). Only Act I exists as of 7.1.

## entry: Kuroyume

### STANDARD
A nightmare monster, natural enemy of the yumekui-baku, who possessed Emura Chuuya. He makes patients cheerful by isolating their troubles from their conscious minds. He says he is the last of his kind and has found a way to revive it. He retreated to the borderland, was wounded by Mizuki and the Traveler, and survived.

## entry: Emura Chuuya
@keys: Emura Chuuya, Emura Ichigensai
@keys2: Mizuki, Aisa Bathhouse, Kuroyume
@version: 5.4
### STANDARD
A former Aisa Bathhouse patient with a terrible temper, blacklisted by Murasaki, who set up a clinic as Emura Ichigensai while possessed by Kuroyume. His nightmare reflects rejection by his father and classmates for wanting to be a writer. He is rescued from drowning, but his mind regresses to that of a young child.
