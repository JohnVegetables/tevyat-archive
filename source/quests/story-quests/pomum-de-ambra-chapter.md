## summary
### LITE
The Pomum de Ambra Chapter is Emilie's Story Quest (Version 4.8). Its single act, Floral Debt, Blood Due, takes the Traveler and Paimon to a flower exhibition in Port Ormos, Sumeru, where Emilie, a perfumer from Fontaine, is chasing rumours that the extinct flower "Auguste" has returned. A murder case follows that exposes the flower's dark origin.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests named after perfume notes: Top Note: Floral Debt, Middle Note: Blood Due and Base Note: Auguste. It requires Adventure Rank 40 and completion of Masquerade of the Guilty, the Fontaine Archon Quest finale.
### LARGE +
Thematically the chapter is about the stories and meaning people attach to objects such as flowers and scents, and what happens when a market, or a person, cannot let go of the past. It is a detective story with Emilie as the investigator.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the "Perfume Mania" resembles the historical tulip mania and that the name Auguste resembles the Semper Augustus tulip; these are wiki observations, not statements by the game.

## plot
### LITE
Emilie wants to dispel the fanatical myth around Auguste, a flower that went extinct in a fire in Fontaine. In Port Ormos deaths occur among those involved, and Emilie and the Traveler uncover that Auguste was made with a Delusion by a woman named Yelena. They catch Kirya, the man behind the scheme, before he can harm more people.
### STANDARD
**Top Note: Floral Debt.** In Port Ormos the Traveler and Paimon meet Tighnari, who is helping with a flower exhibition. Paimon begins to sneeze and a stranger gives them incense that cures it: Emilie, a Fontaine perfumer. She explains the "Perfume Mania" of twenty years ago, when perfume prices soared. Only the perfume "Auguste" never fell back, because the flower it was named for went extinct when its flowerbeds burned. Rumours say Auguste has reappeared in Sumeru, and cheap counterfeits plague Fontaine's market, so Emilie has come to dispel the obsession. Her mentor Edgar, the exhibition's flower expert and creator of Auguste, is ill; she takes the group to a hotel to tell the rest.

**Middle Note: Blood Due.** The next day a guest, Lucien, is found dead in his room with no sign of forced entry; the only way in was through the room of his companion Sylvain. Emilie reasons that Sylvain's grief is genuine, though the sheriff Shyam fears he is the suspect. Sylvain confesses and reveals the truth about Auguste: it was produced by a Fatuus named Yelena using a Delusion, which wore down her body, and the flowers and perfume could harm people over long exposure. He hides the involvement of Yelena and Kirya. Edgar allows himself to be taken into custody. Emilie later finds that a Glaze Lily had been used to hide a sample of Auguste in a Slime Condensate mixture that releases scent at night, and concludes that the hotel was a trap laid by Kirya, who had been disguised as Edgar. Soon after, Edgar is reported attacked and missing.
### LARGE +
**Base Note: Auguste.** The group chases Kirya into a domain and finds him drinking a large amount of Auguste in an attempt to end his life. He survives because the perfume was fake: Emilie had made a perfect Auguste accord from Rainbow Roses, Glaze Lilies and Cecilias, and the earlier commotion was meant to lower his guard so he would not turn to stronger measures that endangered Port Ormos. Kirya accepts his fate and Eremites take him away. A few weeks later Emilie invites the Traveler to Fontaine, where her new perfume "Yelena" is selling well. She says its flowers carry the meaning "forgetting the past and keep on living", which she believes is the legacy Yelena would have wanted. Kirya sends thanks and learns that Auguste could mean something else.
### ULTRA +
Backstory revealed along the way: Yelena did not want Kirya drawn into the matter, and when the group considered burning the evidence she did it herself, destroying the evidence and herself. She left Kirya the money, including the cuts owed to Lucien and Sylvain, who then went looking for him to get their share back.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Auguste was created by a Fatuus, Yelena, with a Delusion, which wore down her body. The flowers and perfume were not dangerous in brief contact but could harm over the long term. The exhibition's flower expert "Edgar" and the schemer Kirya are the same man. The flower's extinction followed a fire in which Yelena died.

## characters
### LITE
Emilie, the Traveler and Paimon, with Tighnari, Collei and Kirya (as Edgar). Yelena appears only through past events.
### STANDARD +
Emilie is the investigator and the emotional centre: precise with scents and people, and weary of the stories attached to her work. Tighnari and Collei assist on the Sumeru side. Kirya is both mentor and culprit. The sheriff Shyam, the Eremite Innam, the guests Sylvain and Lucien, and Oguz, Neriman and Sanjay appear in minor roles.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Kirya is taken into custody by Eremites. For Emilie the myth of Auguste is closed and she releases a new perfume named after Yelena. The case has no world-level consequences.

Release order: 4.8. Story order: after Chapter IV Act V, Masquerade of the Guilty, though events happen in Port Ormos, Sumeru. This is not an Archon Quest.
