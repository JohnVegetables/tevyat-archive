## summary
### LITE
Kamisato Ayaka's Story Quest. Ayaka finds the name Tsubaki in her late mother's notebook and asks the Traveler to help her find and visit Tsubaki, bringing a gift. The search leads to the discovery that Tsubaki was her mother's own secret persona, and Ayaka resolves to live without regrets.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, The Whispers of the Crane and the White Rabbit, released in Version 2.0, with five quests: A Private Commission, Woven Wishes, Food From Afar, Lasting Promise and With You. It is a quiet story about friendship, grief and the freedoms Ayaka's rank has denied her.
### LARGE +
The act also shows Ayaka as someone who has rarely had friends or been out in Inazuma in an ordinary way, and uses the Traveler's company to let her try ordinary things: shopping, a meal, a festival.

## plot
### LITE
Ayaka asks the Traveler to accompany her to meet Tsubaki. She commissions a kimono and recovers stolen silk from ronin at Ritou, then tries to cook foreign food and fails, so the Traveler cooks instead. Following the notebook they find a hidden box under Tenshukaku and a second notebook, which shows Tsubaki and her mother were one person. Ayaka and the Traveler finish at a festival on Amakane Island.
### STANDARD +
A Private Commission. Ayaka tells the Traveler she found a note in her late mother's things naming Tsubaki, someone neither she nor Ayato had heard of. Custom requires a gift, and she would normally bring her brother but asks the Traveler instead.

Woven Wishes. Ayaka orders a kimono to her own design from Ogura Textiles & Kimonos, but the Sakoku Decree has cut off good Liyue silk. At Ritou, Kurisu of the International Trade Association says vagrants have stolen a silk shipment. Ayaka offers to help and, without revealing herself, drives off the ronin. Kurisu offers the silk free; Ayaka pays.

Food From Afar. At Kiminami Restaurant, the owner Anna tells the Traveler that Ayaka had few friends as a child because ordinary families feared her rank. An explosion follows from Ayaka's cooking attempt. The Traveler cooks Mushroom Pizza instead, and gives Anna the recipe. Ayaka thinks Tsubaki may be a fox envoy. She gives the Traveler a white heron hairpiece.

Lasting Promise. Following the notebook, they find a hidden box beneath Tenshukaku holding a second notebook. Ayaka learns her mother and Tsubaki were the same person, who had many wishes but was bound by duties to the clan and the commission.

With You. At Amakane Island's festival, Ayaka draws a prayer panel: she wants an Inazuma where everyone is happy, while the Traveler wishes to reunite with their sibling. Ayaka performs a dance to thank them.
### LARGE +
Details. Ogura Mio is honoured by Ayaka's visit, and Ayaka whispers an additional request to Ogura. At Ritou, the ronin call Kurisu a worm and demand payment; Ayaka stops him paying. She is unconcerned that news of her intervention may spread because the ronin did not recognise her. She offers to have clothes tailored for the Traveler and Paimon, which the Traveler humbly declines. She asks what people in other nations wear, having never left Inazuma. She hopes to cook as well as the Traveler one day. Anna is happy Ayaka has made a friend and asks the Traveler to watch over her. On learning Anna spoke to them, Ayaka is sad because she knows she has few friends, until the Traveler and Paimon cheer her up.
### ULTRA +
The festival is nearly over when they arrive, and the merchants decide to stay open a little longer to keep Ayaka entertained. Ayaka decides, after reading the second notebook, to live each day without regrets. Before the search they collect the kimono and a second requested object from Ogura's shop.

## revelations
@version: 2.0
### LITE
Tsubaki was Ayaka's mother herself; the name appears in her notebooks.
### STANDARD +
The notebooks show Ayaka's mother had many unfulfilled wishes because of her duties in the clan and the commission.
### LARGE +
Ayaka does not know the truth when she sets out; she guesses Tsubaki is a fox envoy.

## characters
### LITE
Kamisato Ayaka, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Ogura Mio (Ogura Textiles & Kimonos), Kurisu (International Trade Association), Kiminami Anna (Kiminami Restaurant).
### LARGE +
Ayato and Ayaka's late mother (as Kamisato Kayo and Tsubaki) appear only in conversation or the notebooks.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Ayaka learns about her mother's hidden wishes and resolves to live without regrets. She forms a closer friendship with the Traveler, and gives them a white heron hairpiece.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocks at Adventure Rank 30 after Chapter II Act I, The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia. Released in Version 2.0 and set during the Sakoku Decree, as Ayaka's difficulty in obtaining Liyue silk shows. No later acts exist as of 7.1.
