## summary
### LITE
The Leo Minor Chapter is Jean's Story Quest, available since Version 1.0 and set in Mondstadt. Its single act, Master's Day Off, shows the Acting Grand Master worn down by the city's commissions. The Traveler and the other Knights quietly take the work off her hands and throw her a surprise party.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: When the West Wind Arises, then Margaret's Longing, Charles' Troubles and Sara's Worries (doable in any order), then Master's Day Off. It requires Adventure Rank 34 and the Trifolium Chapter's Act I, True Treasure. Its tone is warm and low-stakes, centred on how much the city relies on Jean.
### LARGE +
Its central question is whether Jean measures up to Vennessa, the first Grand Master. The chapter answers through Jean herself: she is more a Dandelion Knight than a Lionfang Knight, working to protect the future Vennessa hoped for.
### ULTRA +
The chapter is a character study rather than a plot driver. Its only combat is a lone Abyss Mage that opportunistically tries to kill the exhausted Jean, and the Knights' party is the real climax. It also shows Diluc, usually distant from the Knights, lending his tavern for her sake.

## plot
### LITE
Townsfolk send small requests that all end up on Jean's desk. She collapses at the news, the Traveler and friends handle the jobs, and Jean slips away to Windrise to think. After a brief fight with an Abyss Mage, she returns to find the Knights have organised a surprise party for her.
### STANDARD
**When the West Wind Arises.** In Mondstadt the Traveler and Paimon are asked by Sara of Good Hunter to send Jean a request to clear hilichurls from the road to Springvale, and by Charles of Angel's Share to have her send his quarterly tax records so Elzer can do the accounts. Margaret asks for help finding her cat, Prince. When the Traveler reports all this, Jean faints and Barbara tends to her. Kaeya passes the tasks on to the Traveler instead, saying he would rest easier if the Honorary Knight did them. Amber and Lisa take the original hilichurl and tax jobs.

**The three requests.** The Traveler protects Ernest's delayed delivery convoy from hilichurls, helps Sara with a flood of orders, and finds Prince; Venti explains that the cat took his astral strings, which cats are drawn to when rolled into a ball.

**Master's Day Off.** Barbara says Jean has gone to Windrise, where she always goes when troubled. There Jean admits she wants to be like Vennessa and fears her efforts fall short. An Abyss Mage appears, taunts her and flees; the Traveler helps Jean chase and defeat it. Swan then sends them to Angel's Share, where the Knights hold a surprise party. Jean learns the commissions were all done: Amber cleared the road, Lisa did the tax records and the Traveler recovered Prince and the strings. Kaeya reveals that the Traveler helped organise the party.
### LARGE +
Diluc offered his tavern as the venue despite his reluctance to deal with the Knights, but says he is grateful for the work Jean does for the people. Afterwards Lisa tells the Traveler that Jean has gone upstairs to think alone. Jean thanks the Traveler for lifting her spirits, concludes she is a Dandelion Knight rather than a Lionfang Knight, and hopes the Traveler will keep helping the Knights.

## revelations
### LITE
Jean doubts she measures up to Vennessa, the first Grand Master.
### STANDARD
The city takes Jean's tireless work for granted; Charles admits so, then feels guilty when Paimon points out the pressure on her. Jean's habit of going to the great tree at Windrise when confused is established. Her closing view of herself is as a Dandelion Knight, preserving the future Vennessa hoped for.
### LARGE +
The party shows that the other Knights, Amber, Lisa, Kaeya and Barbara among them, have been watching out for her.

## characters
### LITE
Jean is the focus. The Traveler and Paimon do most of the legwork. Amber, Lisa, Kaeya, Barbara, Venti, Diluc, Charles, Margaret and Sara also appear.
### STANDARD +
Kaeya delegates cheerfully and is revealed as an organiser of the party alongside the Traveler. Lisa quietly handles the tax returns. Barbara looks after the fainted Jean and knows where she goes to think. Venti appears with Margaret and is the one whose strings Prince took.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Nothing in the main story changes. The chapter deepens Jean's characterisation and shows the Traveler as a trusted helper of the Knights.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: Version 1.0, alongside the other launch Story Quests. It follows the Trifolium Chapter's True Treasure.

## entry: Master's Day Off
@keys: Master's Day Off, Leo Minor Chapter, Leo Minor
### STANDARD
Master's Day Off is Act I of Jean's Story Quest (Leo Minor Chapter, 1.0). Overwhelmed by Mondstadt's everyday requests, Jean is given a surprise party at Angel's Share by the Knights. She leaves with a clearer sense of her role as a Dandelion Knight carrying on Vennessa's hopes.
