## summary
### LITE
Albedo's Story Quest, set on Dragonspine. Sucrose asks the Traveler to help her master, the Chief Alchemist of the Knights of Favonius, with his research. Albedo, who believes the Traveler comes from another world, runs a series of experiments on their powers and nature, and ends the act with a quiet worry about himself.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Traveler Observation Report, of six quests: The Rumored Alchemist, The Genius Researcher and the Seed of Another World, then the First, Second, Third and Final Experiments (Elements, The World, Life, Withering Glory). It was released in version 1.2 and unlocks at Adventure Rank 40 after For a Tomorrow Without Tears.
### LARGE +
Rosaria watches the experiments with suspicion, and warns the Traveler that Albedo may have hidden motives. The act is as much a portrait of Albedo as a curious researcher who finds little lasting satisfaction in answers as it is a Traveler study.

## plot
### LITE
At Timaeus' alchemy store, Sucrose tells the Traveler her master Albedo needs help with an experiment on Dragonspine. Albedo concludes the Traveler is from another world and wants to see how life behaves outside Teyvat. Across five tests he examines their elemental ability, mind, cooking, movement and puzzle-solving. He decides they are much like an ordinary person of Teyvat, shows a flower grown from his otherworldly seed, and voices a private fear.
### STANDARD +
The Rumored Alchemist: Sucrose recognises the Traveler as the one who repelled Stormterror. She would like to study them herself, but says Albedo, a master alchemist consulted from across Teyvat, is troubled by an experiment, and is likely to reward help. He is on Dragonspine with Timaeus.

The Genius Researcher and the Seed of Another World: at the foot of the mountains the Traveler meets Barbara trying to bring Rosaria to choir practice; Rosaria refuses and is also looking for Albedo for a reason she will not share. They find Albedo sketching near a group of hilichurls, who attack; he finishes and explains alchemy until Rosaria leaves, bored. Recognising the Traveler as the Honorary Knight, he reasons their abilities mean they come from another world, and asks to experiment on how life functions outside Teyvat with a sample he has. Rosaria spies on them, wondering if he is a risk to Mondstadt.

The experiments: First, after a test against slimes, the Traveler drinks a potion extracting elemental power, with Starsilver Catalyst they find on the way to his mountain camp; Albedo remarks that peculiar people's essence differs from ordinary people's. Second, the Traveler tries and fails to break a pillar with their mind, cooks Sunshine Sprat, and finds an Anemo Crystalfly Extract and Electro Hypostasis Power with Elemental Sight; Albedo suspects Teyvat's natural laws affect them. Third, the Traveler jumps from a peak and crosses a lake; they are faster than the average Mondstadt citizen. Albedo says his interests are often fleeting because learning the truth gives him no satisfaction. Final: the Traveler solves a locked mechanism with Seelies, they find strange writing on a wall and Albedo copies it. Rosaria stops them, is persuaded the Traveler is no threat, sets up patrols and copies the script.
### LARGE +
Further details: Albedo says everything will be as safe as possible and promises compensation. Rosaria is dismissive of the alchemy talk. In the Second Experiment, Albedo expects an elemental-imbued meal to be needed and is hungry himself, and finds nothing unusual in the dish. In the Final Experiment, the water has receded when the Traveler and Albedo examine the ruins at the south end of the mountain.

Back at camp, Albedo concludes the Traveler is like an ordinary person of Teyvat, though their participation has inspired his research; he blooms a flower from the otherworldly seed. He thanks them and invites them to return when he needs help.
### ULTRA +
As they leave, Albedo notices sediment growing in the vial that he had not seen before. He says he and the Traveler are of a "different sediment", worries he will lose control and destroy everything he holds dear, and asks whether he can rely on the Traveler to stop him if that time comes. Rosaria had warned the Traveler before leaving to be wary of him, believing there is more behind his kind surface.

## revelations
### LITE
Albedo believes the Traveler is from another world and studies them for it.
### STANDARD +
He finds the Traveler ordinary in nature but not in effect on his work. Strange writing is found on the wall of ruins in the Dragonspine foothills.
### LARGE +
Albedo's sample is an otherworldly seed that flowers during the report. His closing remarks about sediment and losing control are the main hint of his hidden concern.
### ULTRA +
The act does not explain either the script or the sediment.

## characters
### LITE
Albedo, Sucrose, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Rosaria, Barbara and Timaeus.
### LARGE +
Hilichurls and slimes appear as opponents and test subjects.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Albedo invites the Traveler back for future help. Rosaria starts patrols in the area and keeps a copy of the wall script, wary of Albedo.
### LARGE +
Albedo's request that the Traveler stop him if needed is a promise left open here.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocks at Adventure Rank 40 after Prologue Act II, For a Tomorrow Without Tears. Released in 1.2, with the Dragonspine content. The wiki trivia says that during the limited events The Chalk Prince and the Dragon and Shadows Amidst Snowstorms the requirement was lowered to Adventure Rank 20 with no story keys.
