## summary
### LITE
Mona's Story Quest. A broke astrologer who knows the Traveler is not from Teyvat offers to read their fate if they help her reclaim a box her master left in Mondstadt. They travel from Liyue to Mondstadt, where the box's guardian turns out to be Klee, and Mona ends up renting a sealed laboratory.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Beyond This World's Stars, of three quests: Astrology and the 50-Year Pact, To Mondstadt and A Bewildering Fate. It was released in version 1.0 and unlocks at Adventure Rank 38 after Darknight Hero's Alibi (Noctua Chapter) and True Treasure (Trifolium Chapter).
### LARGE +
The act is mostly comedy about Mona's lack of Mora against her confident astrology, with a thread connecting her to Klee's mother and her master's diary. The Traveler's sibling is the motive: Mona promises to look into the Traveler's destiny.

## plot
### LITE
Outside Liyue Harbor Mona meets the Traveler and Paimon and says that, as a disciple, the Traveler can help her win a contest of astrology. She takes them through Liyue and over the Stone Gate to Mondstadt. There she names Klee as the guardian of her master's box, but the box holds only Klee's treasures; the real contents were a diary. Mona rents a sealed lab from Goth.
### STANDARD +
Astrology and the 50-Year Pact: Mona knows the Traveler is not of Teyvat. She wants to confront the guardian of her box to prove the power of astrology and thinks having a "disciple" helps. Before leaving she visits Mr. Zhu's inn, where he offers 20,000 Mora to find a ring a slime took. Mona first refuses, then agrees at the price. The Traveler uses leftover slime bait from the Darknight Hero's affair to draw the slimes, finds the ring and gives it back; Mona grabs the Mora, saying they must act the part of disciples.

To Mondstadt: at Wangshu Inn, Mona offers to treat them but is horrified at the cost; the Traveler and Paimon order many dishes while she asks for plain noodles. Her stomach betrays her. The Traveler cooks Golden Crab for her using a recipe from Huai'an, and she is delighted. On the road Tyler and some merchants offer them a ride, but Mona identifies them as bandits and defeats them herself. She says she reads Teyvat's people at a glance, whereas the Traveler is like an impenetrable fog to her.

A Bewildering Fate: in Mondstadt City, Klee arrives. Mona asks if her mother is Alice, then asks what she taught her; Klee knows only how to make Jumpy Dumpties. Klee remembers a fifty-year agreement and a box. The Traveler helps fetch it from Windrise, fighting Dendro slimes. Meanwhile Mona reads a diary that fell from Klee's bag. The box proves to hold only Klee's treasures, and the book is Mona's master's own diary, which she was told never to read. Klee flees. With no Mora or lab, Mona accepts help from Katheryne's commission board and dispels a Hydro seal on a house by astrology. The owner, Goth, rents it to her cheaply.
### LARGE +
Details: Mona accepts Mr. Zhu's request after first refusing, and Paimon points out the contradiction. Mona claims she eats simple food so as not to be distracted. Mona wants to combine the box guardian's knowledge with her own work to read the Traveler's fate. At the box, Klee says she replaced the original contents with her own treasures. When the Traveler wonders if the guardian is Lisa, Mona does not pry. Paimon fears Mona will faint from hunger and suggests checking on her.
### ULTRA +
The contest Mona wanted with the guardian cannot happen, since Klee knows almost nothing of what Alice taught beyond Jumpy Dumpties. Goth expects to rent at the lowest possible price; Mona had hoped for the house for free, but the alternative is being homeless.

## revelations
### LITE
The box guardian is Klee, and the box's original content was Mona's master's diary.
### STANDARD +
Mona is a friend of Klee's mother, per Mona's own question about Alice. She cannot read the Traveler's fate as she does others.
### LARGE +
Mona was told never to read her master's diary and has now done so.

## characters
### LITE
Mona, the Traveler, Paimon and Klee.
### STANDARD +
Katheryne and Goth in Mondstadt; Mr. Zhu, Yuhua and Huai'an in Liyue; Tyler and his bandit group.
### LARGE +
Lisa is only wondered about by the Traveler. Alice is mentioned as Klee's mother.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Mona now rents a lab in Mondstadt, though she can only look into the Traveler's fate later. Paimon suggests checking on her from time to time.
### LARGE +
The act leaves her master's diary and the Traveler's fate unresolved.

## placement
### STANDARD
Requires Adventure Rank 38 and both Darknight Hero's Alibi (Noctua Chapter) and True Treasure (Trifolium Chapter). The wiki notes Paimon reusing slime bait from the Darknight Hero story. Released in 1.0.

## entry: Mona's laboratory
@keys: Astrolabos Chapter, Mona's rented house

### STANDARD
At the end of Mona's Story Quest she dispels a Hydro seal on an abandoned house with astrology. Its owner Goth then rents it to her at the lowest possible price, giving her a laboratory in Mondstadt.
