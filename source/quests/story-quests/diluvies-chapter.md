## summary
### LITE
Neuvillette's Story Quest, set in the Court of Fontaine. A threatening letter targets the Melusine Kiara, and the Iudex personally investigates because a similar plot once destroyed two people he trusted. Using the Fountain of Lucine, he relives the story of the Melusine Carole and his friend Vautrin. The trail leads to a smuggler in Meropide, and Neuvillette learns that Fontaine now stands with him.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, The Remains of the Past Day, made of three quests: A Familiar Threat, Ever-Surging Memories and In a New Light. It was released in version 4.1 and unlocked after Cataclysm's Quickening. It is a quiet investigation story about trust, loneliness and how an immortal Hydro Sovereign who chose to live as a human reads his own emotions.
### LARGE +
The quest keeps one stance throughout: Neuvillette handles the case alone and quietly because he regards himself as an "outsider" by his position, and it ends with Wriothesley telling him that this is no longer true.

## plot
### LITE
Neuvillette is investigating a threat to Kiara. The Traveler and Paimon join him, and Clorinde offers to guard Kiara. At the Fountain of Lucine he accidentally pulls the Traveler into a memory of Carole and Vautrin from four hundred years ago. The letter is traced to Esseim, a freed smuggler, and then to Domenico in Meropide. Wriothesley tells Neuvillette he has overthought the case, as people now trust him.
### STANDARD +
A Familiar Threat: Sedene asks Neuvillette to leave the case to others since he is Iudex, but he says that four hundred years ago hardliners opposed to his reforms exploited the Melusine Carole's identity to cause unrest, which ended in her suicide and the exile of Vautrin, the leader of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol. He will not repeat that. He asks the Traveler and Paimon to come, leaving a note for Furina. Kiara hands over the letter. Neuvillette explains he is an outsider because of his role, and reminds Paimon he is the Hydro Sovereign. While reading old cases, he serves different waters to the guests. He explains that after his birth as a human he lost most of his memories and his connection to the Primordial Sea, and chose to become Iudex to organise his thoughts. He proposes visiting the Fountain of Lucine, where all Fontaine's water converges and strong memories and emotions can be sensed.

Ever-Surging Memories: at the fountain after sunset, Neuvillette's power draws the Traveler into the past. Carole is excited to explore the surface, while Vautrin tells her that her dream of coexistence between Melusines and humans is likely impossible. Neuvillette awards them the Medal of Peace, but Thibert and Suavegothe conspire against Carole. A mob forms, Carole sacrifices herself to prove her innocence, and Vautrin learns the truth from Rastignac, keeps it from Neuvillette and kills the culprits in revenge. Neuvillette tries Vautrin before the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale; both judge him guilty by the rules despite the audience's sympathy, and Vautrin leaves. Back in the present, Neuvillette senses resentment against Kiara in the water regarding an old smuggling case. Navia arrives with Spina di Rosula's help, and the trail leads to Esseim at Café Lutece. Esseim confirms writing the letter on Domenico's orders, and Domenico is already in the Fortress of Meropide.

In a New Light: Wriothesley brings Domenico before them. In the infirmary Sigewinne shows them criminals wearing a badge based on the Medal of Peace; it is the symbol of the Mutual Aid Network, and its founder is revealed to be Vautrin. Domenico admits he wrote the letter out of revenge and used a sympathetic guard to send it. Wriothesley tells Neuvillette that the case showed Fontaine trusts him now. Neuvillette returns to the Court understanding himself a little better.
### LARGE +
A Familiar Threat: four hundred years ago he had become Iudex with Carole, a Melusine, and Vautrin as trusted subordinates. Paimon proposes the Gardes, the Marechaussee Phantom or the Special Security, but he declines because he cannot be sure how far a conspiracy has spread. Kiara, who is roughly 400 years old and remembers Carole, insists on finishing her inspection duty with Eloffe at Chioriya Boutique, where she explains Melusines can see anomalies such as old bloodstains that humans cannot. Charlotte, who has been following to interview Neuvillette, promises to keep the case quiet and to ask colleagues at The Steambird for information. Clorinde arrives, asks why he is busy with a trivial matter, and promises to protect Kiara. The Traveler and Paimon help read case files. Paimon finds the office too quiet; Neuvillette admits few come to relax there and brings water. The water tastes the same to Paimon, which they conclude has to do with him being the Hydro Sovereign. He could ask other dragons about his origin, but they are far away and approaching them unannounced is risky. He has found that his emotions resonate with others without knowing why.

Ever-Surging Memories: Carole's first trip to the surface is the first flashback. She had spent the past twenty years in Merusea Village and wanted to cooperate with humans; the Melusines were brought to the Marechaussee Phantom for their perception. Citizens treated them with fear and blamed Neuvillette and the Melusines for strange incidents. Five years later Neuvillette gave them the Medal of Peace. Vautrin looked on Carole as his dead younger sister Illaria, whose dream had spread to him. At the trial Vautrin leaves saying her dream was naive. In the present, Neuvillette apologises for letting his emotions leak. After they get back, the case file notes Esseim was released for good behaviour.

In a New Light: Domenico had tried to incite a riot at the prison and failed. He says he first wanted revenge on Neuvillette but found nobody interested and the Mutual Aid Network against him, so he turned to the Melusines. The core values of the network are to help each other, promote justice and not resent just judgement. Carole's badge was lost in a fire, so the other medal went to Vautrin. Wriothesley adds that, from what he has read, Vautrin was disciplined and far from his courtroom behaviour, suggesting that by appearing resentful he made Neuvillette's justice look proper. Neuvillette declines tea to avoid staying long in the prison.
### ULTRA +
Closing: Clorinde sent Kiara to the Palais Mermonia because everyone wanted to protect her, but thought she would be uneasy with so many people defending her. Neuvillette is briefly rushed by memories from 500 years ago and recovers. Kiara, woken from sleep, says she met Carole in her dream and was glad to see their dream come true. Neuvillette reads Furina's reply to his note and remarks that he cannot understand how she thinks.

## revelations
### LITE
Vautrin founded the Mutual Aid Network, whose badge is modelled on the Medal of Peace.
### STANDARD +
Carole, a Melusine who worked with Neuvillette and Vautrin, was destroyed by a conspiracy of hardliners 400 years ago; Vautrin avenged her and was convicted by Neuvillette and the Oratrice. Neuvillette also states he lost most of his memories and his connection to the Primordial Sea when he was born as a human.
### LARGE +
The Fountain of Lucine holds strong memories and emotions that Neuvillette can tap with his power. The quest ends with the idea that Fontaine now trusts Neuvillette, a change from the era when people feared him.

## characters
### LITE
Neuvillette, the Traveler, Paimon and the Melusine Kiara.
### STANDARD +
Sedene, Clorinde, Charlotte, Navia, Wriothesley, Sigewinne and Furina (through a note). Villains and subjects of the case: Esseim and Domenico.
### LARGE +
In flashback: Carole, Vautrin, Rastignac, Thibert and Suavegothe. Eloffe is the shopkeeper whom Kiara inspects.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Neuvillette ends the case with a clearer view of how he is regarded and new feelings to sort through. Kiara is safe. Domenico remains in prison.
### LARGE +
The flashback explains why Neuvillette is cautious about delegating and why the Mutual Aid Network exists,.

## entry: Carole and Vautrin

@keys2: Neuvillette, Melusine
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Carole was a Melusine who became Neuvillette's subordinate about 400 years ago and hoped for coexistence between Melusines and humans. Vautrin led the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol and was a friend. After hardliners framed Carole and she died, Vautrin killed those responsible and was found guilty by Neuvillette and the Oratrice; he was exiled. The two had received the Medal of Peace.
