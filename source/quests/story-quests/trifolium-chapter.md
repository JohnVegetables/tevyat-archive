## summary
### LITE
The Trifolium Chapter is Klee's Story Quest (Version 1.0), set in Mondstadt. Its single act, True Treasure, starts from a tavern rumour about who is Mondstadt's strongest fighter and ends with the Traveler helping Klee after her buried "treasure" goes missing. Klee learns an Abyss Mage was not a playmate, and Jean again confines her to the Knights of Favonius' solitary room.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Who Is Mondstadt's Strongest Fighter?, The Mystery of the Girl in Red's Treasure and A Very Volatile Treasure. It requires Adventure Rank 32 and the completion of Diluc's Darknight Hero's Alibi and Razor's The Meaning of Lupical. The tone is comic, but the middle of the act has Klee reacting with horror to the death of the Abyss Mage.
### LARGE +
The chapter shows how the Knights treat Klee: Jean has shielded her from the Abyss Order, but she is a Knight and is cared for despite the trouble she causes. The act ends with Jean asking the Traveler to keep an eye on her.
### ULTRA +
The act's three quests move from rumour (a tavern debate), to a chase (the Abyss Mage in the Whispering Woods), to consequences (Jean's office and the confinement room), so it can be used in a scene as a single arc: gossip leads to danger, which leads to a lesson that Klee only partly absorbs.

## plot
### LITE
At Angel's Share, the Traveler and Paimon find patrons arguing over who is Mondstadt's strongest fighter. Jean warns them about a roaming Abyss Mage. Klee, whose buried treasure has vanished, joins them in the Whispering Woods and chases the mage, which sets off its own bomb and dies. Klee is horrified, Jean explains the Abyss Order, and Klee ends up confined once more.
### STANDARD
**Who Is Mondstadt's Strongest Fighter?** At night the Traveler and Paimon return to Diluc's tavern, Angel's Share, where Patton laments that he cannot join in on the current fad. The patrons give different answers, including Stanley, Diluc as the Darknight Hero, and Jean, while Six-Fingered Jose says it is a little girl who blew up the Stormbearer Mountains. Unable to settle the question, the pair ask Jean at Knights of Favonius Headquarters. She cannot answer either, but reports an Abyss Mage lurking near the city and warns them to be careful.

**The Mystery of the Girl in Red's Treasure.** Leaving Jean's office, they bump into a small girl who drops a parchment of "survival" rules. She is Klee, who recognises the Traveler as the Honorary Knight, and tells them the treasure she buried in the Whispering Woods, with a large marker, has gone missing. In the woods Paimon spots the Abyss Mage Jean mentioned. Klee calls it "Mr. Fluffball" and thinks it is only pretending to be a villain, whatever Paimon says about the Abyss Order. After a chase it retreats to its stronghold. Inside, the Traveler and Klee clear slimes and hilichurls and reach the mage, which says it has deduced from gossip that Klee is the strongest in Mondstadt and tries to use her "treasure" against them. Klee warns it not to set off the bomb; the mage reads this as fear, ignores her, and detonates it too early, killing itself. Klee, horrified that she broke one of her survival rules, runs off.
### LARGE +
**A Very Volatile Treasure.** Back at headquarters, Klee is frantically apologising to Jean for hurting someone. Jean does not understand until the Traveler and Paimon explain that the person was an Abyss Mage. Jean apologises for never having told Klee about the Abyss Order, and partly because the problem has resolved itself, allows her to attend the next Ludi Harpastum, to Klee's delight. The celebration is cut short when Jean suspects Klee meant to blow up the fish in Starfell Lake, and Klee resigns herself to solitary confinement. Jean tells the pair that Klee is a Knight, that they care for her despite the trouble, and asks the Traveler to watch over her. In the confinement room nearby, Klee says she is reflecting on her actions while also developing another bomb to show off at the next Ludi Harpastum.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Klee's first encounter with the Abyss Order: Jean had not told her about it, and she is horrified at the Abyss Mage's death and believes she has hurt someone. The act presents Klee as a Knight of Favonius whom Jean and the Order protect and discipline, not only as a troublemaker.
### LARGE +
Jean's reaction in the confinement scene shows she knows Klee's habits well enough to guess the Starfell Lake plan in advance.

## characters
### LITE
Klee, Jean, the Traveler and Paimon. Diluc, Patton, Stanley and Six-Fingered Jose appear in or are discussed in the Angel's Share scene. An unnamed Abyss Mage, nicknamed Mr. Fluffball by Klee, is the antagonist.
### STANDARD +
Klee thinks of the Abyss Mage as a playmate and keeps a list of survival rules she takes seriously. She is explosive and impulsive, and affectionate toward Jean. Jean is protective and strict. Diluc appears only through the tavern rumour about the Darknight Hero.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Klee is allowed to attend the next Ludi Harpastum but ends in solitary confinement again after Jean guesses her plan for Starfell Lake. Jean entrusts the Traveler with looking out for Klee, and Klee is already planning a new bomb for the festival.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: 1.0, with the other launch Story Quests. Story order: after the Prologue, and not before the Traveler has finished Diluc's Noctua Chapter and Razor's Lupus Minor Chapter. The act page's Adventure Rank requirement is 32.
