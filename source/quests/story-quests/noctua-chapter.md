## summary
### LITE
Diluc's Story Quest, set in Mondstadt. Rumours speak of a masked vigilante, the Darknight Hero, and the Traveler discovers it is Diluc. When the Abyss Order threatens the city and the Knights of Favonius start watching Diluc, the Traveler helps him fight in secret while giving him a public alibi.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Darknight Hero's Alibi, made of three quests: Legend of the Darknight Hero, Darknight Hero's Crisis and Darknight Hero's Alibi. It was part of version 1.0 and unlocks after Song of the Dragon and Freedom, the end of the Prologue.
### LARGE +
The tone is comic rather than grim: much of the quest is a staged alibi in which Diluc is seen serving drinks while the Darknight Hero is elsewhere, and the secret is in practice already shared by the Traveler, Kaeya and his winery staff.

## plot
### LITE
In Angel's Share, Charles tells the Traveler and Paimon about the Darknight Hero. They gather rumours, then find Diluc fighting an Abyss Mage in an alley and promise secrecy. The Abyss Order challenges the Hero; Diluc and the Traveler uncover its plan. To protect his identity from Huffman and Kaeya, they lure slimes to the city and arrange for Diluc to be seen behind the bar while he clears the horde.
### STANDARD +
Legend of the Darknight Hero: townsfolk describe the Hero as a wealthy Vision holder who works at night and is not a Knight. After waiting, the Traveler hears a commotion in a north alley and finds Diluc killing an Abyss Mage. He says he had planned his route to avoid the guards, asks them not to tell the Knights, and leaves. Huffman, a Knight, arrives a minute late.

Darknight Hero's Crisis: Huffman has a letter in which the Abyss Order declares war on the Hero, partly in cipher. Diluc, who has heard of the challenge at his winery, has found a stronghold; he and the Traveler infiltrate it and defeat Landrich, a Hydro Abyss Mage. Diluc uses his Vision on it to make it talk, and it reveals the attack is planned for the next night. Back in the city, Huffman says the Knights want to learn the Hero's identity. Kaeya orders him to keep an eye on Diluc, who has been seen working at the tavern.

Darknight Hero's Alibi: at the winery, Diluc is unsurprised by Kaeya's move. He has the Traveler gather Mist Flower Corolla; his staff had tried it to boost sales but it drew slimes with its elemental energy, which Diluc turns into bait. Diluc takes on the Abyss horde alone while the Traveler and Paimon keep Huffman in the tavern. When the slimes arrive, Diluc shames Huffman into helping defend the city and clears the horde at another gate, then returns to the bar before Huffman does.
### LARGE +
Details of the alibi: the Traveler first sets a small amount of bait on the ground and slimes converge on the winery, where the group clears them. Later, in the tavern, Paimon declines Huffman's offer to pay for her drink as part of the plan, and a guard reports that a huge number of slimes are attacking, which Huffman takes to be the Abyss Order's secret weapon. On his return Huffman sees that the Traveler is unusually tired and asks the patrons whether Diluc left; they all say no. He leaves doubtful but does not press.
### ULTRA +
Diluc then tells the Traveler that the "patrons" are his winery staff. Kaeya steps out of the crowd, having watched to see how Diluc would react. Diluc dares him to expose him; Kaeya answers that it is not his style, so the secret stays. Diluc thanks the Traveler, pays them, and says they may ask him for help if needed.

## revelations
### LITE
The Darknight Hero is Diluc.
### STANDARD +
Kaeya chooses not to expose him. The Abyss Order is using slimes as a weapon against Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
Landrich gives away the planned attack under duress. Through Kaeya's orders to Huffman, the Knights treat the Hero as a figure to be monitored.

## characters
### LITE
Diluc, the Traveler, Paimon and Kaeya.
### STANDARD +
Charles (in Angel's Share), Huffman (a Knight of Favonius) and Landrich, a Hydro Abyss Mage.
### LARGE +
Connor receives the Mist Flower Corolla at the winery. Winery staff play the tavern patrons.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler is now one of the few who know Diluc is the Darknight Hero, and Diluc says they can ask him for help. Huffman remains suspicious but without proof.
### LARGE +
The slime bait trick is recalled by Paimon in the Astrolabos Chapter, which lists this act as a prerequisite.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked after Song of the Dragon and Freedom (Prologue, Act III); the wiki lists no Adventure Rank requirement. Released in 1.0.

## entry: Darknight Hero
@keys: Darknight Hero, Darknight Hero's Alibi
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Darknight Hero is the rumoured vigilante of Mondstadt: a wealthy Vision-wielding fighter, not a Knight of Favonius, who acts at night against Abyss Order forces. Townsfolk do not know the Hero's identity. In the Noctua Chapter the Traveler learns it is Diluc, who keeps the secret with help from his winery staff and the Traveler.
