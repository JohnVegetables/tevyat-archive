## summary
### LITE
Alatus Chapter is Xiao's Story Quest, with one act, Butterfly's Dream. Near Wangshu Inn the Traveler and Paimon meet a masked man calling himself the adeptus Starsnatcher, who sells wishes for Mora. His sigil is attracting malevolent hilichurls, and Xiao, the last of the Yakshas, is the one quietly cleansing them.
### STANDARD +
The quest unlocks after Of the Land Amidst Monoliths at Adventure Rank 40. It is a light, mostly comic story that doubles as a short look at Xiao's karmic burden and at the memory of the Yakshas, ending in a small act of remembrance.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Outside Wangshu Inn the Traveler fights hilichurls radiating evil. A masked man, Starsnatcher, claims to be an adeptus and to grant wishes for faith, meaning Mora. A book at Wanwen Bookhouse about the Yakshas makes the Traveler and Paimon suspect he is a fraud. Following the evil aura, they find Xiao, who explains the trouble comes from his karma and lets them help cleanse a cavern. They then use an adeptal ritual to confront Starsnatcher, who turns out to be an ordinary man.
### STANDARD +
Starsnatcher's Sigil of Permission is the real reason the demons gather. Xiao asks the Traveler to take it from him without violence, using the adeptal art Dream Trawler. The ritual needs a censer, lamps and cold items, gathered from a shrine near the southern road of Mt. Tianheng and with Mist Flowers. At the shrine the Traveler meets Pervases, a junior student of the Yakshas. Starsnatcher turns out to be a fan of Xiao, repents, and leaves a letter, the sigil and returned money. Xiao orders a dish in memory of an old friend.
### LARGE +
**The Master of Stars' Deception.** Two evil hilichurls near Wangshu Inn are killed by the Traveler, and a masked man drives off another pair while four followers stand by. He gives his name as Starsnatcher and asks what the Traveler wishes. The followers say he performs miracles but charges Mora. The Traveler says they are searching for a lost sibling, and he agrees to help if shown faith; the Traveler offers a tasty dish instead of Mora, and Paimon proposes checking Wanwen Bookhouse for a new cookbook.

**Glimmers of the Vigilant Yaksha.** At the bookhouse the pair see Starsnatcher buy the book Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti. Reading a copy, they learn that Rex Lapis appointed adepti as Yakshas to destroy demons formed from the wrath of dead gods, that the brutality wore on them, and that only one remained, the Conqueror of Demons, whom they recognise as Xiao. Back near the inn, Starsnatcher's group is menaced by more hilichurls; his Sigil of Permission fails to drive them off, and the Traveler defeats them. Paimon no longer believes him and has the Traveler follow the evil aura using Elemental Sight.

**The Purging of Evil.** The trail ends at Xiao, standing over three dead hilichurls. He admits that the evil gathering there is a consequence of his own karma, and dismisses the book as fan fiction that his fallen comrades would find insulting to cry over. He goes to cleanse a nearby cavern with the Bane of All Evil and reluctantly accepts the Traveler and Paimon as companions. Afterwards Paimon tells him about Starsnatcher; he does not know the name, is disgusted at the wish-granting, and is alarmed to learn of the sigil, which he realises is what has been gathering the demons. To confiscate it without violence he recalls the Dream Trawler, normally used to separate a soul from the body but able to call the spirits of others.

**Insights of Drifting Dreams.** At the shrine near Mt. Tianheng's southern road, its guardian Pervases appears in human form and then speaks through a statue. He explains he was the Yakshas' junior student and is using his Mortal Lingering art to see how Liyue fares on the anniversary of his death. Sensing Xiao's karmic burden on the Traveler and Paimon, he asks them to send regards to the Vigilant Yaksha and voices a last wish to taste Grilled Tiger Fish again. When the ritual is performed, Starsnatcher's spirit appears, thinking it a dream, is subdued, and begs the supposed adepti for mercy. When told that this is Xiao, he becomes a devoted admirer. He explains that his grandfather, a folklorist, told him tales of Xiao as a child. He found the sigil among his father's belongings, began dressing as an adeptus for fun and was later swayed by greed and attention. He swears never to act under falsehoods again.

**Aftermath at the inn.** Next day Verr Goldet tells the Traveler that Starsnatcher, who stayed under the name Wang Ping'an, has checked out leaving a sincere, apologetic letter. He has gone on a journey of self-reflection, returned the money taken from followers, and left trinkets and the sigil, feeling unworthy of it. The Traveler gives the sigil to Xiao, who surprises them by ordering Grilled Tiger Fish instead of Almond Tofu, in honour of Pervases. They leave him to eat in peace.
### ULTRA +
The tone is deliberately lighter than the Archon Quest: the demons are real and dangerous, but the antagonist is a repentant impostor, and the story resolves by trickery and talk rather than a boss fight. Xiao's reluctance to be accompanied, his bluntness toward Paimon's sympathy, and his private tribute to Pervases show how he handles grief, and the encounter with Pervases is the quest's only direct link to the living world of the Yakshas.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The demons around Wangshu Inn gathered because of Starsnatcher's Sigil of Permission, in addition to Xiao's own karmic burden.
- Starsnatcher was an impostor, a man named Wang Ping'an, who had inherited the sigil.
- Pervases, a junior student of the Yakshas, remains as a lingering presence at a shrine near Mt. Tianheng.
### LARGE +
- The Dream Trawler can call the spirits of others, not only separate a soul from a body.
- Xiao calls the book Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti fan fiction, though Paimon finds it consistent with what he says about his karma.

## characters
### LITE
Xiao (the Vigilant Yaksha), Paimon and the Traveler, plus Starsnatcher (Wang Ping'an), Pervases, and Verr Goldet at Wangshu Inn.
### STANDARD +
Starsnatcher's four followers appear as extras. Xiao is shown as solitary, blunt and weary, but willing to be helped.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Wang Ping'an gives up wish-selling and returns what he took. Xiao regains the sigil and the demon gathering at Wangshu Inn ends. The Traveler gains a small personal glimpse of Xiao and the Yakshas.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.3 as Xiao's Story Quest; requires Of the Land Amidst Monoliths (Chapter I, Act I) and Adventure Rank 40. It can be played at any point after that, independent of later story.

## entry: Starsnatcher
@keys: Starsnatcher, Wang Ping'an
@keys2: Xiao, Wangshu Inn, Sigil of Permission
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
In Butterfly's Dream, Starsnatcher is a masked man near Wangshu Inn who passes himself off as an adeptus and sells wishes for Mora. He is really Wang Ping'an, a man who found a Sigil of Permission among his father's belongings and began cosplaying an adeptus out of fun before greed took over. After being confronted through a Dream Trawler ritual he repents, returns the money and sigil, and leaves on a journey of reflection.
