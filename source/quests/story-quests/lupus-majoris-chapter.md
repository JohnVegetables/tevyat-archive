## summary
### LITE
Lupus Majoris Chapter is Varka's Story Quest (Version 6.4, "Luna V"), a single act titled "To Those Who Embark on the Expedition". The Knights of Favonius' Nod-Krai expedition is about to return when Abyssal wolf-spirit forces blocking its path emerge. The Traveler helps Varka recover scattered Wolf Spirit fragments of Boreas and defeat Roland, the Abyss's replacement for the fallen Rerir, who has corrupted the remains of Andrius.
### STANDARD
The act has three quests: "Triumphant Warrior", "Fated Warrior" and "Lonely Warrior". It is split between Mondstadt, where letters from Varka carry the plot, and Nod-Krai, where Varka gathers the Wolf Spirit fragments and fights Roland. It closes with Varka returning to Windrise among his friends.
### LARGE +
The quest links Mondstadt's history of the Knights of Boreas (Ravenwood, Rostam, Roland and Varka himself) to the Abyss's attempt to reshape the wolf god Andrius, and shows that Varka's letters were also a device to anchor him.

## plot
### STANDARD
Lohen brings Varka's letters from Nod-Krai to Mondstadt, and the Traveler learns from Boreas at Wolvendom that Roland, the Abyss's replacement for Rerir, has corrupted the remains of Andrius. Dvalin carries the Traveler to Nod-Krai. There Varka and his knights gather Boreas's Wolf Spirit fragments, and Roland swallows Varka in a duel. Inside the wolf Varka draws on the longings of Rostam, Ravenwood and the Wandering Knight, calls Andrius back to the Ley Lines and fights his way out, to wake at Windrise.
### LARGE +
**Triumphant Warrior.** Venti, who has asked the Traveler about Nod-Krai, meets them at Starsnatch Cliff and remembers Varka fighting a Ruin Guard as a young man. They then see Lohen, Vice Captain of the Fifth Company, fighting a Ruin Guard at the Thousand Winds Temple. Lohen was sent from Nod-Krai to deliver ten letters from Varka and asks Venti to reply. Meanwhile Varka and his knights Winfried, Ekbert and Leifhelm destroy Wild Hunt stragglers near the Pillar of Embla. Varka finds a shard of Andrius and plants his claymore in front of it; it becomes a red wolf that asks whether the surface is still corrupt, and he readies for battle.

At Headquarters Lohen delivers letters to Jean, Lisa, Sucrose, Mika, Noelle, Eula and Amber and the Traveler volunteers to take Kaeya's. Jean explains that if Varka does not return she will become Grand Master and train his successor, and that the Boreas lineage has twice almost been wiped out. The Traveler delivers letters to Kaeya, Rosaria and Diluc at Angel's Share; Varka's note to Diluc apologises for Eroch's actions and asks if he will rejoin the Knights, which Diluc declines. Rosaria tells of Roland, a disciple of Rostam whom Boreas respected, known as the White Knight and later the Bloodstained Knight. At Wolvendom they meet a purple mist and the Wild Hunt, then Lohen, who admits the earlier meeting was a test that the Hunt had not copied them. Boreas clears them, and Razor and Venti explain that Boreas is a fragment of Andrius's soul, split on the original knight's advice. Boreas senses a blood-scented presence in the Ley Lines and confirms it is Roland, who went to Khaenri'ah. Venti sends Dvalin to carry the Traveler to Nod-Krai, and Andrius blesses Razor's claymore with a Wolf Spirit for Varka. The Traveler arrives as the knights finish an assault; the red wolf, calling itself Roland, withdraws, and they return to Favonius Keep.

**Fated Warrior.** At the keep the Traveler meets Jahoda, Ineffa and Flins. Varka tells them the attacks are led not by Rerir but by Roland, whom the Abyss installed after Rerir was removed; Albedo and Durin explain the replacement. Varka places the Wolf Spirit in his claymore and the Traveler sees Boreas's final thoughts. Varka's plan is to persuade Andrius, as Boreas did, to anchor himself in the Ley Lines and break free of corruption, but his sword needs the remaining Wolf Spirit fragments. At the Pillar of Embla the second fragment is corrupted by Roland, and the Traveler purifies without removing it; Varka compares this to fishing for an Ancient Name in the Night Kingdom, and anchors Roland with a letter Rostam wrote that Arundolyn never delivered. A voice they identify as Vedrfolnir speaks. Varka reveals a plan the Traveler considers too dangerous. When the third fragment's search party is ambushed, the Logistics Company helps, and Varka goes for the last fragment. Roland appears wanting a fair duel, and they fight. He notes Varka is holding back, destroys his sword and swallows him.

**Lonely Warrior.** Inside the wolf, Varka finds a Favonius Sword that carries Rostam's longing, a Ferrous Shadow that carries Ravenwood's, and the Wandering Knight's Wolf's Gravestone with its own, and uses them to push toward the "heart of the wolf". There he confronts Roland, who admits that he is an imperfect Abyssal copy. Varka calls out to Andrius, who chooses to return to the Ley Lines. Roland realises Varka kept Boreas's soul in himself, not in his sword. Varka reveals that the letters were meant to anchor him so he could return, reversing the process. He fights free of the souls Roland releases, finds the Black Sword and leaves Rostam's letter beside it, asking it to tell the real Roland that Rostam and Arundolyn never forgot him. He wakes at Windrise with everyone waiting and the Traveler returns his sword.

The story contains two parallel rescues: the knights and the Traveler pull Varka back from the wolf, while his letters, which seemed routine, have already given him anchors. Jean's talk about succession shows Varka's own expectation that he might not survive.
### ULTRA +
Boreas, the Wolf of the North, is described as a fragment of Andrius, with the other fragment remaining in Nod-Krai; the original Knight of Boreas persuaded Andrius to split his soul and travel south. Roland's history is told obliquely: the White Knight became the Bloodstained Knight after many battles, went to Khaenri'ah, and the wiki summary states that Roland gave everything to the Abyss after seeing its injustice. The three swords Varka finds belong to Rostam, Ravenwood and the Wandering Knight.

## revelations
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Nod-Krai expedition was partly meant to recover Boreas's Wolf Spirit fragments scattered around Nod-Krai.
- Roland, not Rerir, is the leader of the Abyssal wolf force, installed after Rerir's removal, and he has corrupted Andrius's remains.
- Boreas is a fragment of Andrius's soul and the Knight of Boreas serves as intermediary between the wolf and Mondstadt; very few know Andrius came from Nod-Krai.
- Varka's letters were meant to anchor him; he stored Boreas's soul in himself, not in his sword.
### LARGE +
The Roland met calls himself an imperfect Abyssal copy, and the real Roland's fate is left open apart from his journey to Khaenri'ah.

## characters

## consequences
### STANDARD
Andrius chooses to return to the Ley Lines, the Abyssal Roland dissipates, and Varka returns alive to Windrise. Diluc's refusal to rejoin the Knights stands.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.4 (Luna V) with an event period from 2026-02-25 to 2026-03-17; the chapter stays available. It requires Adventure Rank 40 and Act VIII of Song of the Welkin Moon, "True Moon" (quick start at Adventure Rank 28 during the event).

## entry: Wolf Spirit Fragments
@keys: Wolf Spirit, Wolf Spirit fragments
@keys2: Varka, Boreas, Andrius
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
Boreas's power and thoughts, divided into fragments across Nod-Krai. The expedition's goal was to collect them; only two remained when the Traveler arrived. Andrius gives one of his own in Razor's claymore.
### LARGE +
Varka absorbs a fragment into his own claymore. The second fragment was corrupted by Roland, so the Traveler purifies without removing the corruption.

## entry: Roland in the Wolf
@keys: Roland, Bloodstained Knight, White Knight
@keys2: Varka, Andrius, Boreas
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
In the quest Roland is the Abyss's replacement for Rerir and an imperfect copy of the historical Roland. He sees Varka as a lost soul and offers him time to rest before the duel, which Varka refuses. Inside the wolf Varka calls out to Andrius, who returns to the Ley Lines.
### LARGE +
Rostam's undelivered letter and Arundolyn's loyalty are the only thing Varka can leave for the real Roland.

## entry: Varka's Letters
@keys: Varka's letters, letters from Varka

### STANDARD
A set of letters (ten, per the wiki) sent from Nod-Krai through Lohen, including ones for Venti, Jean, Lisa, Sucrose, Mika, Noelle, Eula, Amber, Kaeya, Rosaria and Diluc, and a further one taken to Wolvendom. They announce the expedition's end and, in Varka's plan, anchor him to Mondstadt.
