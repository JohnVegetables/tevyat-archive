## summary
### LITE
Kaeya's Story Quest, set in Mondstadt. Kaeya says his pirate grandfather buried a legendary sword and asks the Traveler to find it before Treasure Hoarders do. The hunt follows map clues around Mondstadt, and at the end Kaeya reveals it was a ruse to lure out a Treasure Hoarder leader.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Secret Pirate Treasure, of three quests: Kaeya's Troubles, Mystery of the Arcadian Ruins and Kaeya's Gain. It belongs to version 1.0 and unlocks at Adventure Rank 5 after The Outlander Who Caught the Wind. The Traveler can solve the clues with or without Kaeya's help.
### LARGE +
The quest shows Kaeya using a staged story to set up an arrest and then apologising, with a promise not to deceive the Traveler again. The wiki trivia notes that the chapter title is Latin for a peacock's eye.

## plot
### LITE
After Stormterror's attack, Kaeya tells the Traveler his grandfather was a pirate who buried a sword from Celestia. Treasure Hoarders are active nearby. Following clues from an informant, the Traveler reaches the ruins, where Hoarder leader Izzard ambushes them. Kaeya arrives and reveals the treasure hunt was a trap.
### STANDARD +
Kaeya's Troubles: at Knights of Favonius Headquarters, Kaeya looks worried and leads the Traveler and Paimon to the courtyard. He says his grandfather, a pirate, buried a legendary sword from Celestia to avoid conflict among his fellows, and he fears local Treasure Hoarders will find the ruins. He suggests asking at the Adventurers' Guild.

Mystery of the Arcadian Ruins: no one at the Guild knows of the ruins. Kaeya says he found an informant who will only meet in Whispering Woods. They rescue her from monsters; she gives them a map with a riddle about three six-armed giants in the city in the lake, two guarding a statue and one the treasure. This points to the windmills of Mondstadt. A second riddle found at a windmill leads to a waterfall behind Springvale, where they find the map to the Arcadian ruins.

Kaeya's Gain: at the end of the ruins, Izzard ambushes the Traveler and Paimon. Kaeya arrives and reveals the treasure hunt was a ploy. Izzard flees and wakes a Ruin Guard, which the Traveler and Kaeya destroy before arresting him. Kaeya apologises for the deception and offers the Traveler the Harbinger of Dawn.
### LARGE +
Details: the Guild visit turns up nothing, and Kaeya then reveals the informant. The second riddle describes a woman who does not love a man and a spring whose passion is only a front; the quest resolves it to the waterfall near Springvale. After getting the final map Paimon argues they should find the sword themselves. Izzard admires Kaeya's tactics but still refuses to be taken. If the Traveler completed the hunt without Kaeya's help, he is impressed by their skill and returns to headquarters to interrogate Izzard.

### ULTRA +
The first riddle quotes: "Three six-armed giants reside in the city in the lake. Two guard the statue, one, the treasure." The informant Vile gives the map only after being rescued, and Kaeya had arranged the meeting at Whispering Woods for confidentiality.

## revelations
### LITE
The treasure hunt was a ruse to trap a Treasure Hoarder leader.
### STANDARD +
Kaeya admits he tricked the Traveler. The wiki summary does not confirm whether the sword exists or whether his grandfather was a pirate.
### LARGE +
The plan was to lure the Hoarder crew out of hiding so they could be apprehended.

## characters
### LITE
Kaeya, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Izzard, a Treasure Hoarder leader, and the informant Vile.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Izzard is captured and taken in for interrogation. Kaeya promises not to deceive the Traveler again and offers the Harbinger of Dawn.
### LARGE +
The Traveler sees that Kaeya will use half-truths to reach a goal, a trait the quest leaves unexplained.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocks at Adventure Rank 5 after Prologue Act I, The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, and takes place after Stormterror's attack on Mondstadt. Released in 1.0.
