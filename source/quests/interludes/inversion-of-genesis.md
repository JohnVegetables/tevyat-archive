## summary
### LITE
Interlude Chapter Act III. The defeated Scaramouche, held prisoner in Sumeru, joins the Traveler and Nahida in a trial run through Irminsul to look for hidden data on the Traveler's sibling. There he sees that Dottore engineered the tragedy of his past in Tatarasuna, and tries to erase himself from history.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call, A Dance of Destruction, The Kabukimono's Finale and As Though Morning Dew. It was released in Version 3.3 and is unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and the first act of the Acer Palmatum Chapter. It sits between Perilous Trail and Paralogism in the Interlude Chapter.
### LARGE +
The act is a character study as much as a mystery: it pairs a scholars' ghost story about a puppet kabukimono with the real record held in Irminsul, and ends with the former enemy being given a future instead of a punishment. It also shows how a revision of Irminsul works and where it stops (it does not touch the Traveler).

## plot
### LITE
Paimon and the Traveler visit Nahida and find Scaramouche in her sanctuary. Inside Irminsul he learns Dottore's role in his past and erases himself from Irminsul; the world forgets him, and the Traveler must find him and help Nahida restore his memories. He is then given a new name.
### STANDARD +
Four quests. The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call: two Akademiya scholars tell a ghost story about Tatarasuna, then Nahida, Scaramouche and the Traveler enter Irminsul, where he sees Dottore at work and erases himself. A Dance of Destruction: in Inazuma the Traveler checks what changed. The Kabukimono's Finale: Nahida confirms the erasure from a backup record, the Traveler finds him, and his memories are restored. As Though Morning Dew: he accepts a future and a name.
### LARGE +
**The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call.** Looking for answers about Descenders, the Traveler and Paimon head for the Sanctuary of Surasthana and overhear the scholars Aqaba and Sawada arguing about a thesis topic, the Tatarasuna incident. Aqaba's teacher chose it because even Inazumans know little: the smith Mikoshi Nagamasa forged a fine blade, then threw it into the fire and killed Katsuragi for reasons unknown, and a kabukimono, an eccentric, was involved. Sawada has checked with a friend in government that the people in the records lived at the time, among them the official Niwa Hisahide, a distant relative of the Kaedehara clan and so a practitioner of the Isshin Art; he suspects a link to the clan's later decline. Aqaba reminds Sawada of the most important point, and Sawada reveals it: the kabukimono was not a human but a puppet. The Traveler recognises Scaramouche, and Paimon bluffs that the story is just a ghost tale. Soon after, Scaramouche walks by, and the Traveler and Paimon follow him into the sanctuary to warn Nahida, only to find him already inside.

Nahida explains she struck a deal with him to investigate Irminsul for her. He expected to be killed once the job was done. She argues he is too weakened to do anything drastic. He had learned of her own deal with Dottore, from which she gained valuable information she says she will disclose over time. After his defeat, she adds, Dottore and the others discarded him, so he needs a backup plan. She proposes that they cooperate; he refuses because the Traveler and Paimon distrust him, but she begins a trial run anyway. Even with most of his powers gone he can still connect to Irminsul, and his Harbinger connections may reveal data about the Traveler's twin that others are hiding from her. She asks the Traveler to supervise him and sends them into Irminsul.

Inside, they quarrel again until Nahida reminds them of the task. She opens a connection to the interior, they locate and attach to the heart of Irminsul, and Nahida speaks privately with the Traveler and Paimon, intending that he find answers for himself. The Traveler tells her about what they saw in Haypasia's vision, which Nahida confirms was the lie that made him who he is, and she admits the scholars got one thing right: Tatarasuna was sabotaged. Scaramouche, who has overheard, pulls up strange data.

The memory it shows: an official discusses the Tatarasuna furnace with Niwa, and deeper in Niwa talks with a man called Escher about using Crystal Marrow to forge Inazuman blades and make the area prosperous. Niwa then challenges Escher about the dangers, since workers are falling sick or dying; he has sent their friend the kabukimono to Narukami Island with a golden feather to seek an audience with the Shogun as a last hope. Niwa confronts Escher about his false identity, and Escher stabs him. Escher is Dottore, who wants to see what happens when a malevolent heart is placed into an unsuspecting puppet. Niwa's dying request is that if Dottore gives the puppet his heart, he tell him that both Nagamasa and Niwa see him as one of them. Dottore reports to Pierro that his task in Inazuma is done and introduces a puppet who could become their newest comrade if he proves useful, or be turned to dust if not. Scaramouche understands and rages; the Traveler and Paimon give him space, appalled at Dottore.

After he calms down he resumes searching and finds nothing about the twin, but does learn that when the sibling arrived in Khaenri'ah they lived for a time with Pierro, who served as a royal mage at the Khaenri'ahn court. He then raises a barrier to hide them from Nahida and asks for the Traveler's hand. He asks whether the past can be changed; the Traveler's hesitation reads to him as a yes, and he goes into the heart of Irminsul to erase himself from time so that Niwa and his friends can have a better fate. They cannot find him. Nahida reconnects, understands what happened, and takes them out while she deals with it herself. Paimon, panicking, talks about food to pass the time; when she wakes she no longer remembers Scaramouche, and the alarmed Traveler heads for Inazuma to see what has changed.

**A Dance of Destruction.** At the Amenoma Smithy, Amenoma Tougo tells them about the Raiden Gokaden: a catastrophe 400 years ago still happened, but Niwa and another smith purged the corruption in the furnace at the cost of their lives. The other smith's orphaned son grew up resentful of the world and passed only hatred to his descendants. About a hundred years ago the then-head of that family began killing Gokaden smiths and was killed by the Kamisato and Kaedehara clans. Relieved, the Traveler learns that Kazuha is fine, since Tougo had tea with him. At the Kamisato Estate, Hirotatsu and Furuta confirm that Ayato and Ayaka still lead the clan and its commission. At the Tatarigami furnace, Xavier tells them someone else worked on the furnace back then, which confirms that Dottore was present and sabotaged it. Xavier does not recall a kabukimono.

**The Kabukimono's Finale.** Back in Sumeru the scholars hand over their papers, which do not mention Scaramouche, and are astonished by what Tougo said. Nahida does not remember him either. She explains that he could have revised Irminsul by erasing himself, but because Irminsul holds no information on the Traveler, revisions do not affect them. She retrieves a record and confirms the Traveler's account: she had made a backup of his memories, which revisions cannot touch. She is unsure how the Harbingers are affected. At the Grand Bazaar the Traveler recalls Rukkhadevata saying that no one can erase themselves from existence, not even her, and realises he must still be alive. Overhearing a merchant scold an employee, they spot him and follow. Nahida catches up and shows him his past, and he eventually accepts it. As the flood of painful memory hits, Shouki no Kami emerges to attack; he composes himself, a newly descended Anemo Vision in hand, shields the Traveler from its blow, and the false god is defeated. The recovery succeeds and they leave the dream.

**As Though Morning Dew.** Nahida promises to keep sifting Irminsul for data on the Traveler's twin's travels. He asks why the Traveler went to Inazuma and, told it was to see what he had changed, decides to treat it as a favour he owes. Nahida urges him to look to the future and not the past; he agrees to help where he can. She says he should have a name, and at his request the Traveler picks one. He then wants revenge on Dottore for his manipulation; Nahida urges moderation, as he still needs time to compose himself. Before leaving he asks the Traveler to tell the Raiden Gokaden's descendants that he was responsible for their downfall, since they deserve to know, and says he will accept whatever punishment they choose. Back at the inn from earlier, the vase Paimon broke is still broken, and a strange voice tells them the fate of Teyvat cannot be changed so easily and that they should believe their own eyes.
### ULTRA +
Small details worth keeping: per the wiki trivia, the notes in Aqaba's essay are a transcription of the Rather Aged Notes found in Tatarasuna. Nahida treats the Irminsul visit as a supervised trial run of his access, and she chooses to let him discover answers for himself rather than tell him. The vase at the close shows the Traveler and Paimon still remembering a world that otherwise seems unchanged.

## revelations
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Dottore, under the name Escher, sabotaged the Tatarasuna furnace, killed Niwa and picked the puppet Scaramouche as a future Fatui comrade. Scaramouche learns this inside Irminsul.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler's sibling once lived with Pierro for a time in Khaenri'ah, where he served as a royal mage. Irminsul can be revised by erasing a person from it, but the erasure does not alter the Traveler, and Nahida keeps a backup of Scaramouche's memories. Rukkhadevata told the Traveler that no one can erase themselves from existence, not even her.
### LARGE +
Revision changes who did what rather than whether disaster happened: the furnace catastrophe still occurs 400 years ago and Niwa and another smith still die purging it, but the account of the kabukimono is gone. The closing voice implies that Teyvat's fate is not easily changed.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Nahida; Scaramouche (later the Wanderer).
### STANDARD +
Dottore (as Escher, in a recorded memory), Pierro (mentioned and reported to), Niwa Hisahide (in the memory), Amenoma Tougo, Hirotatsu, Furuta, Xavier, Aqaba and Sawada. Kazuha and the Kamisato siblings are discussed but do not appear.
### LARGE +
Shouki no Kami appears as an attacker during his recovery. Katsuragi and Mikoshi Nagamasa are named in the scholars' story.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Scaramouche has erased himself from Irminsul and the world forgets him, but he is restored through Nahida's backup, gains an Anemo Vision, receives a new name from the Traveler, and agrees to help the Traveler. Dottore is his target for revenge.
### LARGE +
Nahida continues searching Irminsul for data on the Traveler's twin. The strange voice at the end sets up later events.

## placement
### STANDARD
Third act of the Interlude Chapter, released in Version 3.3 between Perilous Trail (Act II) and Paralogism (Act IV). It requires the Sumeru Archon Quest through Chapter III Act V and the first act of the Acer Palmatum Chapter.
