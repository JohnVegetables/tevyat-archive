## summary
### LITE
Interlude Chapter Act II. After the Chasm is reopened, the Traveler and Paimon meet Yanfei, Yelan, Arataki Itto and Kuki Shinobu at the Chasm Nail. The ground gives way and the group is trapped in a chaotic underground space, where they find Xiao hunting for news of his lost brother-in-arms Bosacius. Together they escape, and Xiao learns what became of him.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: The Unexpected Guest, Chasmic Maze, Danger All Around, End of the Line and At Tunnel's End, Light. It is a small-cast Liyue story that pairs the Traveler with characters who rarely meet, and it resolves a long-standing Yaksha question. During its first run it was a time-limited event with a lowered Adventure Rank requirement; the permanent act is canon for all players with the prerequisites.
### LARGE +
Most of the act is a survival puzzle in a space that ignores ordinary logic: no hunger, tiredness or passing of time as expected, illusions that prey on each character's worst fear, and passages that loop back to where they began. The solution comes from combining human knowledge (Yanfei's exorcist expertise, Yelan's resourcefulness, Itto's brute force) with Xiao's adeptal power.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Curious about the Chasm after the spiritstone incident, the Traveler and Paimon return to the Chasm Nail and run into Yanfei, then Itto and Shinobu, then Yelan. The ground collapses and the group falls into the Chasm's Bed, a looping space they cannot leave. Xiao, searching for Bosacius, helps them reach the surface with an ancient compass, nearly sacrificing himself.
### STANDARD +
Yanfei is at the Chasm Nail on secret business; Itto and Shinobu are chasing her (Shinobu came to Liyue for her Tongwen Academy certificate, which the Sakoku Decree had kept her from collecting). Yelan arrives and warns everyone off, Itto takes offense, and the ground collapses into the Chasm's Bed. There they meet Xiao, who is looking for Bosacius, and enter an illusion-filled domain that loops back on itself and keeps them from feeling hunger or time passing. Doors show each person's worst fear. Yanfei works out that it is an antlion's den of chaotic rules, they rescue Xiao from an illusion, and Itto punches open a new path. Treasure Hoarders and Fatui agents are beaten, Xiao ends a remnant of Bosacius' memory, and a Fantastic Compass lifts the group out. Xiao is hurled to safety and then rescued by Zhongli; Xiao and the Traveler agree those who died defending Liyue are heroes.
### LARGE +
**The Unexpected Guest.** The Traveler and Paimon find Yanfei at the Nail, apparently on important and secret business. Itto, who is looking for her, arrives with Shinobu. Shinobu explains she came back to Liyue to collect a Tongwen Academy graduation certificate she had been unable to pick up when the Sakoku Decree kept her in Inazuma; Itto came along as an escort and ended up in a brawl with the Millelith until Yanfei sorted it out. Shinobu slyly points Itto at the wrong target. When the Traveler meets Yanfei below the Nail, a mysterious woman, Yelan, arrives and tells everyone to leave because the Chasm is dangerous. Itto takes offense at her tone, and as the two square up the ground cracks and everyone drops into the Chasm's Bed. They find a portal and extremely old Liyue rock around it, then meet Xiao, who says he is searching for someone and tells them to leave before he vanishes. With no alternative, they enter the domain.

**Chasmic Maze.** After clearing the domain the group is back where it began. Itto calls on Ushi to find the demon he believes responsible, but Ushi finds no exit. Yanfei notes the ceiling has sealed. Yelan advises them to prepare for a long stay. The Traveler tries calling Xiao by name, but he does not come.

**Danger All Around.** A day later Paimon feels as though twenty days have passed, while the Traveler feels one. None of them has felt hunger, thirst or fatigue. Yelan finds a hidden path behind illusory magic. In the domain beyond they hear Xiao struggling, and a puzzle opens a door through which a projection of Xiao warns them before being cut off. A door in a later passage shows the opener their deepest fear: Itto faces disapproving Inazuma townsfolk and beans, Shinobu an old conversation with her mother about becoming a shrine maiden, Yanfei a civil dispute, and the Traveler the Abyss that took their sibling. Yelan declines to open it, saying she would have to kill everyone inside because she works for Ningguang and knows many Liyue secrets. Paimon grows tired and hungry, which shows the domain is targeting weaknesses. Back at camp Yanfei theorises it is a chaotic space whose rules can be turned against it, and the group drags Xiao out just in time.

**End of the Line.** Xiao explains he is looking for his old comrade Bosacius, whom he suspects died in the Chasm. His description fits Yanfei's idea that the domain is an antlion's den that uses illusions to trap victims. Xiao offers to spend all his power to push the others to the surface and stay behind; they refuse. Itto, told of the argument by Ushi, punches a path open and exhausts himself doing it. The new route leads to a large Fantastic Compass. Treasure Hoarders, hired thugs sent after Yanfei over a past case, are driven off, and Fatui agents beyond the compass are easily dispatched. They meet Xiao and a remnant of Bosacius' memory, which confirms that he was the Nameless Yaksha of the Chasm battle. Xiao ends the remnant after a short fight. In a manifestation of the Abyss the Traveler chases an illusion of their sibling and finds only a second compass. Yelan discovers someone had used it before and tries to unlock it.

**At Tunnel's End, Light.** Yelan learns the compass held only information about Boyang's fate, which discourages the group. Paimon knocks the other compass over, and it reacts to Yelan's and Xiao's energy. Yanfei realises it is an exorcism catalyst combining human magic arts and adeptal power, and that it can carry the group to the surface, though as part of the illusion it cannot be taken out. A rising platform carries them upward while Xiao channels power and malevolent energy tries to drag them back down. Xiao's energy gives out and he throws the others to the surface; the platform shatters and he starts to fall, but a swirl of yellow energy rescues him: he had told Zhongli of his plan beforehand. Afterwards Yelan leaves, Yanfei offers Itto and Shinobu a tour of Liyue, and Xiao invites the Traveler to Pervases' temple, saying not knowing Bosacius' fate had always bothered him. He and the Traveler agree that those who died defending Liyue are heroes of history.

The pairing of characters shapes the tone. Itto and Shinobu supply comedy and an outsider view of Liyue, Yanfei supplies the explanation for the chaotic space, and Yelan stays guarded and professional while hinting at her ties to Ningguang. Xiao's plan to sacrifice himself is rejected by the whole group, and Zhongli's rescue is possible only because Xiao told him of his plan before setting out.
### ULTRA +
The Nail, the Chasm's Bed and the compasses tie into the Chasm's wartime history. According to the wiki's Bosacius history, the Fantastic Compass was brought to the Chasm by Boyang and Rongzhao as a space-manipulating catalyst needing power from both a mortal and an adeptus, and Bosacius and Boyang volunteered to be sealed with the invading monsters. The act shows traces of that sacrifice rather than narrating it in full.

## revelations
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Nameless Yaksha of the Chasm battle was Bosacius, Xiao's missing comrade, who died defending Liyue.
### STANDARD +
The Chasm's depths hold a chaotic, illusion-laden space (the Chasm's Bed), which the wiki notes long predates the cataclysm and which was used to seal the invading monsters. The Fantastic Compass is an exorcism catalyst blending mortal magic arts with adeptal power.
### LARGE +
Another compass in the depths holds information about Boyang, who sealed himself in alongside Bosacius. Xiao told Zhongli of his search first and is saved by him at the end.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Yanfei; Yelan; Arataki Itto (with Ushi); Kuki Shinobu; Xiao; Zhongli (indirectly).
### STANDARD +
Bosacius appears only as a memory remnant. Ningguang is mentioned in connection with Yelan. Fatui agents and Treasure Hoarders appear as enemies.
### LARGE +
Boyang is referred to through the compass; the Millelith are mentioned in Shinobu's backstory.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Xiao finally knows what happened to Bosacius. Yanfei offers a Liyue tour to Itto and Shinobu, and Xiao invites the Traveler to Pervases' temple. The Chasm remains a dangerous place with more history below.
### LARGE +
The act deepens Xiao's characterisation and the Yakshas' legacy without changing Liyue's political situation.

## placement
### STANDARD
Second act of the Interlude Chapter, after The Crane Returns on the Wind and before Inversion of Genesis. Released in Version 2.7. It follows the Chasm world quest line (including the spiritstone) and the Traveler's search for their sibling continues in the background.

## entry: Fantastic Compass (Perilous Trail)
@keys: Fantastic Compass
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Perilous Trail the Fantastic Compass is an exorcism catalyst combining human magic arts and adeptal power. A large version and a smaller one appear inside the Chasm's illusion space. Together they carry the group to the surface, but the compass is part of the illusion and cannot be taken out. According to the wiki's Bosacius history, it was brought to the Chasm by Boyang and Rongzhao in the original battle.
