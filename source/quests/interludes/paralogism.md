## summary
### LITE
Interlude Chapter Act IV, set in Mondstadt as the Windblume Festival begins. Albedo is put on trial for murder, which proves a trap to expose an imposter made from the life force of the dragon Durin. Afterwards Albedo uses a Hexenzirkel trial to turn Mini Durin into a human.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Crimes One and Two, The Hunger of Many and The Gardener. It follows Inversion of Genesis in the Interlude Chapter and is unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after that act and Traveler Observation Report. It was released in Version 5.6. It resolves Albedo's long watch over the fallen Durin on Dragonspine.
### LARGE +
The act turns a courtroom mystery into a defence of the city and then into an act of creation. Albedo, himself an artificial human made by Rhinedottir, chooses to give a new life to a dragon-born child rather than only destroy a threat. It is the prerequisite for Song of the Welkin Moon Act I, A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves.

## plot
### LITE
Odd behaviour among Mondstadt's townsfolk and a murder trial of Albedo greet the Traveler on their return for Windblume. The trial is staged to flush out a copy of Hertha, monsters attack the city, and Albedo and the Hexenzirkel's Alice help him create a human Durin.
### STANDARD +
Three quests. Crimes One and Two: Albedo is tried for murder and the Traveler investigates, until a second Hertha exposes the first as a copy spawned by Durin's remains. The Hunger of Many: monsters attack the city, Albedo explains the trial was a trap, and the Hexenzirkel is called in. The Gardener: after a chessboard trial set by Alice, Albedo turns Mini Durin human at the start of Windblume.
### LARGE +
**Crimes One and Two.** Paimon asks the Traveler to return for Windblume. In the city people act strangely: Beatrice avoids Quinn, Miles has been sleepwalking, and Donna talks about finding her ideal man instead of Diluc. Kaeya sends them to Knights of Favonius Headquarters, where they meet Eula and the captains Note and Skeiron, and Jean tells them Albedo is on trial, a case requiring both the Knights and the Church. Varka is away on his expedition to Nod-Krai, so Jean presides, with the deacon Dahlia as assessor, Hertha as prosecutor and Kaeya as defence. Witnesses claim to have seen Albedo dispose of a body, and Diluc reports a second disposal near Dragonspine; the evidence includes a femur and teeth and a letter. Jean finds Albedo guilty but allows an appeal. Rosaria sends the Traveler to Starsnatch Cliff, where Venti and Dahlia explain that Dahlia is also Barbatos' herald, and Venti gives them a Dodocommunication Device. Jean privately commissions the Traveler to investigate. In Albedo's office a hidden compartment points to a third site on Dragonspine, an empty pit with traces of non-human diggers. At the retrial the remains are tied to the missing hunter Hanselmann. Albedo admits to killing one person on Dragonspine, a near-identical double of himself who tried to eliminate him in secret (a failed Homunculus Project specimen abandoned in Durin's stomach), and says he was stationed on Dragonspine to watch Durin. A second Hertha then arrives, and the first is exposed as a copy: Durin's spawn can mimic people. The copy summons minions, the city is attacked, and Jean sends Skeiron's 3rd Company after it.

**The Hunger of Many.** Albedo reveals the trial was staged to draw out the imposter after the Knights spread word that the real Hertha had died. He says the other two people he killed had been consumed and replaced by the spawn. The Traveler and Albedo defend the city alongside Venti, who first hides his identity, Diluc, Kaeya, Barbara, Rosaria, Amber and Mika, and they find Eula worried about Klee, who returns to help against Ruin Machines. Venti senses a child's anger in the wind, which Albedo takes to be Durin's jealousy on learning, through Subject Two's emotions, that he is no longer Rhinedottir's only child. The source, Phonia, is found hiding nearby and is taken into custody; Jean questions her about being missing for over half a year. Albedo says he can save her if the plan proceeds. Albedo then explains the Tripartite Conference: the Knights and Church, the Anemo Archon and the Hexenzirkel, an arrangement Varka had secured. Alice answers a call and attends as a giant Dodoco.

**The Gardener.** In Albedo's office Mini Durin explains that he will take over the dead Durin's body to be reborn as a human, hoping his pure side can restrain the evil instincts. At the Imaginarium Theater Albedo asks Alice for two reagents: flesh of an immortal monster from Mare Jivari and part of an embryo left by Rhinedottir. Alice sets a chessboard trial and shrinks the group. In the second half Barbeloth turns the king and queen into the Ascended King and Sublimated Queen, whom the Traveler and Albedo defeat. Alice hands over the reagents. At Durin's Core on Dragonspine Albedo draws out its energy. Two days later the festival opens, Jean names Venti the Windblume Star, and while he sings Albedo's ritual turns Mini Durin human. Venti gives the Traveler a gift from the Hexenzirkel, a Wonderland Cosmosphere that will open itself when the time is right.
Details that matter in a scene: the first trial is closed-door because parts of the case are classified; Albedo's alibi for the first charge rests on Kaeya pointing to the long Dragonspine report he submitted the next day, which Hertha dismisses, and his alibi for the second on Sucrose, whose testimony fails because she had fallen asleep after midnight; the copy Hertha is exposed when the real Hertha asks where her longest scar is, something the Knights who fought beside her would know; Miles cannot answer, the scar is on her left arm, and Miles turns out to be one of the imposter's accomplices. Albedo says the plan took more than half a year to set up. The in-game recollection summarises the act as the Traveler uncovering the culprit as the fallen dragon, then Albedo and the Traveler reviving Durin in a new form.
### ULTRA +
Jean upholds the verdict in public although she privately believes Albedo innocent, and she is the one who quietly sets the Traveler to investigate. Albedo explains that acting on Durin's power makes the other spawn unstable. When Paimon asks whether the Wanderer approved of Durin's choice, Durin believes he did, since he was told to "get a move on". Albedo says he believes Rhinedottir would be proud of the outcome and that she had devoured Naberius, Ruler of Life, though he thinks the two are now one.

## revelations
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The "murderer" Albedo is innocent of the disappearances; the strange behaviour and the Hertha accusing him come from imposters created from the dragon Durin's remains.
### STANDARD +
Albedo was stationed on Dragonspine to watch Durin, whose remains spawn mimics. Dahlia is secretly Barbatos' herald as well as an official of the Church. Mini Durin takes over the dead Durin's body to be reborn as a human with Albedo's alchemy.
### LARGE +
Albedo says Durin's jealousy comes from learning he was not Rhinedottir's only creation. He says Rhinedottir devoured Naberius, the Ruler of Life, a claim he shares in conversation and does not prove here.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Albedo; Jean; Kaeya; Venti; Dahlia; Durin (Mini Durin).
### STANDARD +
Also Diluc, Eula, Rosaria, Sucrose, Klee, Hertha and her imposter, Skeiron and Note, Alice, Barbeloth, Phonia, Amber, Barbara, Mika. Varka is voice-only by call and away at Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Minor townsfolk include Beatrice, Quinn, Miles, Donna and the hunter Hanselmann, who is among the remains.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Albedo is cleared, the city survives the attack, Phonia is held, and Durin starts a new life as a human. The Traveler receives a Hexenzirkel gift.
### LARGE +
Albedo's Tripartite arrangement with the Anemo Archon and the Hexenzirkel is confirmed. The Wonderland Cosmosphere remains unopened at the act's end.

## placement
### STANDARD
Interlude Chapter Act IV, following Inversion of Genesis. Released in Version 5.6; between 5.6 and 6.0 Quick Start could open it at Adventure Rank 28 after A New Star Approaches. Completing it is required to unlock Song of the Welkin Moon Act I, A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves.

## entry: Albedo's trial
@keys: Albedo trial, Albedo's trial, trial of Albedo
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Knights and Church jointly try Albedo for murder and illegal disposal of remains. Hertha prosecutes, Kaeya defends, Dahlia assesses and Jean presides because Varka is away. It was a pretext arranged beforehand to lure out an imposter.

## entry: Durin's spawn and imposters
@keys: Durin's spawn, Hertha imposter, imposter Hertha
@keys2: Albedo, Mondstadt, Dragonspine
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Durin's life force produces spawn that consume people and take their form; acting on that power destabilises the other spawn. Albedo killed two spawn that had consumed people and buried what remained. The double of Albedo he killed on Dragonspine was different: a failed Homunculus Project specimen abandoned in Durin's stomach.

## entry: Tripartite Conference
@keys: Tripartite Conference
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A pact among the Knights and Church of Favonius, the Anemo Archon and the Hexenzirkel for times of need. Varka secured the Hexenzirkel's aid, and Alice joins the conference as a giant Dodoco.
