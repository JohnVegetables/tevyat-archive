## summary
### LITE
Interlude Chapter Act I, set in Liyue Harbor months after the Liyue Archon Quest. Ningguang announces the rebuilding of the Jade Chamber and offers to answer any question from the first three to supply the materials. The Traveler teams up with Shenhe, a newcomer to city life, meets her master Cloud Retainer, and joins the Qixing, the Millelith and Beidou's fleet in defeating the sea monster Beisht, with Liyue's people rather than the adepti doing the fighting.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Jade Chamber Rising, Seagaze Sunset, Bygones Times Like Dust Passing and Where the Heart Finds Rest. It is partly a gentle ensemble story about Shenhe finding a place among people, and partly a test of whether Liyue under human rule can handle a threat without leaning on the adepti.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Ningguang asks for Sunset Vermillionite, Wonder Cores and Adepti Sigils to rebuild the Jade Chamber. The Traveler, Paimon and Shenhe gather them, with Yun Jin and Beidou as co-contestants. The rebuilt Chamber rises, Beisht attacks, and Liyue's defenders beat it before a victory banquet.
### STANDARD +
The contest is won by the Traveler's group, including Shenhe and Yun Jin, and the three each get a question for Ningguang. Shenhe, raised by Cloud Retainer away from society, supplies the Adepti Sigils and asks how to fit into Liyue Harbor. When Beisht, wife of the sealed god Osial, attacks as the Jade Chamber ascends, Ningguang says she prepared in advance and relies on the people of Liyue to fight. Shenhe freezes a tsunami, and the Traveler and Paimon join her in finishing the monster.
### LARGE +
**Jade Chamber Rising.** Several months after the Liyue story the Traveler overhears Ningguang's announcement: she needs Sunset Vermillionite, Wonder Cores and Adepti Sigils, and the first three people to supply them may each ask her one question, which she promises to answer honestly. The Traveler and Paimon then see two self-styled brokers, Scar Liu and Qiang the Brave, trying to fleece a young woman to cover their gambling debts. A Millelith soldier, Zheng, arrests them. The woman is Shenhe, who says she would have preferred to knock sense into them. She is hungry, not for city food but for Qingxin, Violetgrass and Glaze Lilies, which she eats at Bubu Pharmacy to everyone's surprise. She says she came to help rebuild the Jade Chamber, not to compete, but offers to repay the Traveler by eliminating other contestants, which Paimon rejects. Baizhu, collecting wound dressings Ningguang ordered, tells them Sunset Vermillionite is rare and that the Feiyun Commerce Guild may know more.

**Seagaze Sunset.** At the Guild, Xingqiu says a piece of Sunset Vermillionite is up for auction, but expects bids of at least 500 million Mora, far beyond its worth. He tells them of the late adeptus Sea Gazer, whose abode near Lisha is rumoured to hold the ore. Shenhe says Sea Gazer was close to Mountain Shaper and open-minded enough not to mind trespass. Shenhe dispels the magic around the site; inside, the abode is filled with clouds maintained by mechanisms, and after destroying them Shenhe carries a huge boulder of Vermillionite to the construction site, where Baiwen and the others assume she is an adeptus. Shenhe is mildly irritated by being worshipped as something she is not. At the inn the Traveler meets Cloud Retainer, who says Shenhe is only human, is her master and caretaker, and has come to watch how Ningguang handles the contract between Liyue and its people. The adepti sense a calamity coming; if Ningguang cannot handle it, they would not hesitate to take control. Shenhe already knew her master had spoken to the Traveler.

**Bygones Times Like Dust Passing.** At the site Beidou introduces her friend Yun Jin, who has entered to ask Ningguang for a venue for her new opera, The Divine Damsel of Devastation. They agree to split the reward into three questions and divide into teams: Beidou works through her chief smith Suling, while Yun Jin, the Traveler and Shenhe visit Master Zhang of Hanfeng's Ironmongers, who forged the first Chamber's Wonder Cores. He needs Starsplinter Iron and Subrosium. The iron is gathered on Mt. Tianheng. In a village near the Subrosium, Mingjun is shocked that Shenhe is alive; Yun Jin finds her father's diary and realises the opera's brave damsel was Shenhe, who was forced into the role rather than choosing it. Shenhe says she prefers the opera's version and wants to live up to it. Xiao is seen nearby, preparing for danger in the sea but unable to get involved. Shenhe reveals Cloud Retainer sent her with the sigils to help her rejoin society, and Beidou offers her a drink on her ship. Shenhe uses Master Zhang's smithy to craft the Adepti Sigils.

**Where the Heart Finds Rest.** The group delivers the items and is named winner. Ningguang offers Yun Jin the Jade Chamber as an opera venue. She has no leads on the Traveler's sibling, so Paimon's question is answered instead. Shenhe asks whether she can fit in at Liyue Harbor and is told to build a sense of belonging. As the rebuilt Chamber rises, a storm brings Beisht. Ningguang had been warned by Beidou months earlier. She fires the Chamber's weapons at it with Keqing, Ganyu, the Millelith and Beidou's fleet. The Traveler is hurled into a pillar by a beam; Shenhe, remembering her father leaving her to die and Cloud Retainer's words, freezes the tsunami with a talisman, the Chamber hits Beisht, and she dives into the vortex with the Traveler and Paimon. Cloud Retainer commends the preparations, which she calls considerable progress compared to last time, while crediting Shenhe. At the victory banquet Yun Jin performs her opera, and a drunk Zhu Tao is rebuked by Shenhe.
### ULTRA +
The structure echoes the Liyue Archon Quest: a puzzle-style contest in the first half, then a large monster fight that Liyue handles as a community. The key difference is who acts. Ningguang says she will rely on the people of Liyue rather than the adepti, and Cloud Retainer's visible approval is the act's answer to her warning about the adepti seizing control.

## revelations
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Shenhe is a human raised by the adeptus Cloud Retainer; the opera The Divine Damsel of Devastation is based on her childhood.
### STANDARD +
The adepti are watching how Ningguang handles Liyue and would take control if she failed. Beisht, wife of Osial, rises from the sea at Liyue Harbor as the Jade Chamber ascends.
### LARGE +
Shenhe's father left her to die as a child (a memory she recalls during the fight). Yun Jin learns her father's opera exaggerated the damsel's courage.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Shenhe; Cloud Retainer; Ningguang; Beidou; Yun Jin; Xingqiu; Baizhu; Keqing; Ganyu.
### STANDARD +
Baiwen (one of Ningguang's secretaries, who receives the materials), Master Zhang, Suling, Mingjun, Zheng, Scar Liu, Qiang the Brave, Zhu Tao, and Xiao in a brief appearance. Beisht is the antagonist.
### LARGE +
Osial and Mountain Shaper are only referred to; the adeptus Sea Gazer is known through his abode.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Jade Chamber is rebuilt and hangs again over Liyue Harbor. Shenhe gains friends and a possible place in the Harbor, and Yun Jin gets a venue for her opera. Liyue shows it can face a major monster with its own forces.
### LARGE +
The adepti's warning to Ningguang is left open: Cloud Retainer approves of the preparations but the act does not say the matter is settled.

## placement
### STANDARD
First act of the Interlude Chapter, followed by Perilous Trail. Released in Version 2.4. In story time it is several months after A New Star Approaches; players caught up at release played it after the Inazuma Archon Quest.
