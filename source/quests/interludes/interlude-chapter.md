## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The Interlude Chapter is a set of four self-contained Archon Quest acts. They do not form one story; each follows up on the quest that unlocks it. Act I, The Crane Returns on the Wind (Liyue), covers the rebuilding of the Jade Chamber and Shenhe. Act II, Perilous Trail (Liyue), sends a party into the Chasm after Xiao's lost comrade. Act III, Inversion of Genesis (Sumeru), deals with Scaramouche and Irminsul. Act IV, Paralogism (Mondstadt), is Albedo's trial during Windblume.
### STANDARD +
The chapter works as a place for stories that grew out of earlier main-story or region content but did not belong in the main chapters. Acts I and II revisit Liyue after the Chapter I finale, Act III follows the Sumeru Archon Quest, and Act IV returns to Mondstadt. The Traveler and Paimon are present throughout, and the Traveler's search for their sibling stays in the background rather than driving any act.
### LARGE +
Released across several years: Act I in Version 2.4, Act II in 2.7, Act III in 3.3 and Act IV in 5.6. As of Version 7.1 these are the only four acts.

## acts
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
**Act I, The Crane Returns on the Wind (Version 2.4, Liyue).** Ningguang announces the rebuilding of the Jade Chamber and a contest to gather its materials. The Traveler befriends Shenhe, raised by the adeptus Cloud Retainer, and the sea monster Beisht attacks as the Chamber rises.

**Act II, Perilous Trail (Version 2.7, Liyue).** The Traveler, Yanfei, Yelan, Arataki Itto and Kuki Shinobu fall into a looping space beneath the Chasm where Xiao is searching for his fallen comrade Bosacius.

**Act III, Inversion of Genesis (Version 3.3, Sumeru).** The imprisoned Scaramouche enters Irminsul with the Traveler, learns Dottore's role in his past, and erases himself; he is later restored and given a new name.

**Act IV, Paralogism (Version 5.6, Mondstadt).** During Windblume, Albedo is tried for murder in a staged trial and then uses a Hexenzirkel trial to turn the young dragon Mini Durin into a human.
### LARGE +
Quests per act: Act I has Jade Chamber Rising, Seagaze Sunset, Bygones Times Like Dust Passing and Where the Heart Finds Rest. Act II has The Unexpected Guest, Chasmic Maze, Danger All Around, End of the Line and At Tunnel's End, Light. Act III has The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call, A Dance of Destruction, The Kabukimono's Finale and As Though Morning Dew. Act IV has Crimes One and Two, The Hunger of Many and The Gardener.
### ULTRA +
Regions and focus shift each time: Act I is about Liyue's human government and the adepti, Act II about the Yaksha past and the Chasm's sealed depths, Act III about the Irminsul and the Fatui's manipulation of a puppet, and Act IV about Mondstadt's Knights, the Church and the Hexenzirkel. Each act has its own entry with the plot and revelations.

## requirements
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Each act has its own unlock conditions on the wiki. Act I needs A New Star Approaches (Chapter I Act III). Act II needs A New Star Approaches plus Transient Dreams, Rise Up Golden Soul, Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend? and A Teapot to Call Home: Part I, with Adventure Rank 40. Act III needs Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises (Chapter III Act V) and A Strange and Friendless Road, with Adventure Rank 40. Act IV needs Inversion of Genesis and Traveler Observation Report with Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
Between Version 5.6 and the Version 6.0 update, the Quick Start function let a player at Adventure Rank 28 who had finished A New Star Approaches unlock Act IV directly. Act I's Adventure Rank requirement is not stated on the chapter page beyond A New Star Approaches.

## placement
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The chapter sits beside the main Archon Quest chapters, not in sequence with them. The acts come in the order I to IV but each is timed by what it follows: Act I and Act II after the Liyue finale, Act III after Sumeru, and Act IV after Traveler Observation Report. The wiki notes that a long-time player caught up at Act I's release would play it after the Inazuma Archon Quest.
### LARGE +
For Act IV, the project notes place it before Song of the Welkin Moon Act I; the act page itself does not say so.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Recurring through all four acts are the Traveler and Paimon. Each act brings its own cast: Shenhe, Ningguang, Xiao and Cloud Retainer in Act I; Yanfei, Yelan, Itto, Shinobu and Xiao in Act II; Nahida, Scaramouche and Dottore in Act III; Albedo, Jean, Kaeya, Hertha and the Hexenzirkel in Act IV.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I shows Liyue handling a calamity as a community, and Shenhe begins to find her place among people. Act II tells Xiao what became of Bosacius. Act III frees Scaramouche from a false history and sets him on a path of his own. Act IV clears Albedo and gives Durin a new life as a human. None of the four acts changes the main Archon Quest plot.
