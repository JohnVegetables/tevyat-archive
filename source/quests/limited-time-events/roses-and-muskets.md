## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Roses and Muskets is the Version 4.3 winter flagship event. The Traveler and Paimon help the Fontaine film producer Xavier make "The Two Musketeers" for the first Fontinalia Film Festival, with Ayaka, Ayato and Yoimiya as unpaid guest actors and Furina as director. While filming, Chevreuse of the Special Patrol investigates a murder that copies the original novel, and the resolution sets two siblings' grief for their murdered mother against Fontaine's law.
### STANDARD +
The story has four acts and an afterword: The Crack of Muskets Breaking the Silence, Fiction and Reality Inside and Outside the Frame, The Lone Isle Glimpsed Through Fog, Where the Roses Bloom and The Two Musketeers' Triumph. The event's reward was the claymore "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword".
### LARGE +
The event required Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III, and recommended Chapter IV Act V (Masquerade of the Guilty), Raiden Shogun's Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter Act II and Furina's Animula Choragi Chapter Act I first. It introduced the Fontinalia Film Festival, organised by the Fontaine Film Association.
### ULTRA +
It is the origin of Musketeer Pictures, whose later productions appear in the Sunny Summer Fontinalia event.

## plot
@availability: limited
### LITE
Xavier loses his investor and cannot pay his cast, so Ayaka, Ayato and Yoimiya volunteer, and Furina is persuaded to direct. Chevreuse joins as the musketeer and asks the Traveler for help with a murder echoing the novel. While filming goes on, they trace the gun to Fleuve Cendre and a novelist's roses. At the wrap party Chevreuse stages a trap that exposes the investor Morris, who hired the killer of the novelist's mother. The film wins the festival.
### STANDARD +
**Act I.** In the Court of Fontaine Xavier cannot get funding for his film of "The Two Musketeers". Chiori has found actors from Inazuma, and Ayaka, Ayato and Yoimiya agree to work unpaid. Furina, offered a dessert and a Kamera demonstration, agrees to direct. Chevreuse, introduced by Chiori, joins and tells the Traveler about a murder that mirrors the novel.

**Act II.** Filming begins at Hotel Debord, with Chevreuse and Ayaka playing sisters Tulipe and Iris. Chevreuse, the Traveler and Paimon investigate gun parts through workshops and Fleuve Cendre.

**Act III.** The final scene is shot in one take. The trail leads to the novelist Baptiste, who confesses, then privately asks Chevreuse to kill the true mastermind.

**Act IV.** The film is stolen and recovered. At a beach party Chevreuse stages a fake murder to make the investor Morris confess; Morris hired the killer of Elisa, and Veronique, the prop manager, had killed the earlier victim.

**Afterword.** Xavier's film wins and Chevreuse hands the three to Wriothesley.
### LARGE +
**Act I in detail.** The Traveler and Paimon learn of the Fontinalia Film Festival from The Steambird. At the plaza Xavier is pleading with a would-be investor, Morris, who cannot fund the film. Chiori reassures him: the actors she found do not care about Mora. Ayaka, Ayato and Yoimiya arrive by aquabus from the Clementine Line, and Ayato explains he and Ayaka already planned to work for free, because better ties between nations matter more than wealth. Paimon volunteers as clapperboard and Yoimiya as assistant. For a director the Traveler suggests Furina, who agrees on condition of full creative freedom after the Traveler brings her Fontinalia Mousse and a Kamera sample. Furina wants an expert on muskets, and Chiori brings Chevreuse. Chevreuse, who has just stopped a Sinthe sale (production halted after Marcel's arrest), joins gladly because she likes the book. She summarises the novel and asks for help with a recent murder.

**Act II in detail.** Furina, short on sleep, rewrites the script so the sister role is not overshadowed by an overbearing brother. With Ayato busy with the Palais Mermonia, his role goes to Chevreuse, so she and Ayaka play Tulipe and Iris. Veronique is the prop manager and Bonnot the lighting technician. Filming goes on until night. Chevreuse then tells the Traveler a Rainbow Rose was left at the scene, as in the novel. At Leschots Clockwork Workshop and Beaumont Workshop nothing stands out, but Estelle sells scrap parts to Fleuve Cendre. In the sewers they find someone cleaning up practice shots, buy information from Emerald, and learn from the bar owner that a gun left three days earlier was untouched. Chevreuse theorises the killer took the gun, copied it, killed a solitary man from the east side, and left the rose. The novelist has an alibi and no motive.

**Act III in detail.** Ayaka declines Furina's offer to stay as an actress because of her Yashiro Commission duties. Emilie reports that the rose is a rare cultivar grown only in Baptiste's garden. Baptiste admits to the murder but says he cannot reach the true mastermind, and asks Chevreuse in private to kill the culprit if she can, because twenty years have passed since his mother's death.

**Act IV in detail.** The cast notices Chevreuse is missing, and Morris returns. A Kamera breaks and the finished film disappears; Chiori finds it in the sewers, undamaged. After wrapping, Furina hosts a beach party. Yoimiya leads Morris into a building; Chevreuse stages the "murder", then reveals it as a trap with a recording. She arrests Morris for Elisa's murder and Veronique, the second musketeer, who aimed a gun at Morris. Morris admits an affair with Elisa, a maid in his family's estate, paying her to leave and end the pregnancy, and later hiring an assassin when she sent him photographs of their two children. Veronique recounts hiding with her brother beneath the floor as their mother died. Chevreuse refuses revenge killings but promises to inform Wriothesley. Afterwards Chevreuse tells the Traveler about her own childhood and her father's harsh training, and they arrive at Baptiste's house where the Rainbow Roses are in full bloom.
### ULTRA +
**Clues.** Chevreuse deduced that Baptiste could not have acted alone because he failed to reassemble the musket found in his garden, that his confession was a diversion, and that the sabotage came from the investor who hoped to sink the film. She found Veronique's name next to Baptiste's in the orphanage adoption records. Letellier and Turenna of the Special Patrol take the two into custody.

**Afterword in detail.** The Traveler finds Chevreuse in the Fortress of Meropide's Administrative Area, telling Wriothesley of the three she is bringing in. She asks him to give everyone the latest Steambird so Baptiste and Veronique are seen as heroes and Morris as the villain. Back at the festival Xavier's "The Two Musketeers" wins as Musketeer Pictures; Ayaka invites Chevreuse to Inazuma, Ayato confirms the storms around Inazuma have gone, and Furina carries the Furina Award reluctantly.

## revelations
@version: 4.3
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The novelist Baptiste and the prop manager Veronique are siblings whose mother Elisa was murdered twenty years ago on the investor Morris's orders.
### STANDARD +
The "second musketeer" is Veronique, who killed the earlier victim and left a Rainbow Rose as in the novel. Baptiste's quick confession was a diversion that gave her a chance at Morris.
### LARGE +
Morris signed on as an investor in order to sink the film and bury the story, and the missing film and mishaps were his sabotage. Chevreuse admits she hesitated when Baptiste first told her the truth.
### ULTRA +
Chevreuse says Fontaine is "the Nation of Justice" because it is built on laws, and that private revenge would start a cycle. She promises Morris will not lead a cushy life in the Fortress of Meropide; the Afterword shows Wriothesley making no promise.

## characters
@availability: limited
### LITE
Traveler, Paimon, Xavier, Furina, Chiori, Chevreuse, Ayaka, Ayato and Yoimiya.
### STANDARD +
Also Morris (the investor), Baptiste (the novelist), Veronique (prop manager), Bonnot (lighting), the extra Elain, Emilie, Wriothesley and the Special Patrol members Letellier and Turenna.
### LARGE +
Furina agrees to direct only with full creative freedom. Chevreuse is calm and dutiful. Ayato keeps Inazuma's ties with other nations in mind, and Ayaka takes naturally to acting, saying that clearing her mind to become the character resembles her swordplay.

## consequences
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Morris, Baptiste and Veronique are arrested and taken to the Fortress of Meropide. "The Two Musketeers" wins the festival, and the crew is credited as Musketeer Pictures.
### LARGE +
The friendship between Ayaka and Chevreuse leads to a possible visit to Inazuma. Xavier and Musketeer Pictures return in the Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7).

## placement

## entry: Fontinalia Film Festival
@keys: Fontinalia Film Festival, Furina Award
@version: 4.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A film festival organised by the Fontaine Film Association; the entry with the highest score wins the Furina Award. The name stayed after Furina's abdication. The first winner was "The Two Musketeers" by Musketeer Pictures.

## entry: The Two Musketeers
@keys: The Two Musketeers, Musketeer Pictures, Rainbow Rose
@keys2: Xavier, Baptiste, Chevreuse
@version: 4.3
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Fontainian novel by Baptiste about sisters Tulipe and Iris, whose mother Rain is poisoned by aristocrats; the sisters become the two musketeers and leave a Rainbow Rose on each victim. Xavier produced the film, Furina directed and Ayaka and Chevreuse starred. A real murder in Fontaine copied the novel's roses.

## entry: Elisa's murder
@keys: Elisa
@keys2: Morris, Veronique, Baptiste
@version: 4.3
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Twenty years before the event, Morris hired an assassin to kill Elisa, a maid who had borne his two children. She hid the children under the floorboards and fought on. The children are Baptiste and Veronique, who grew up in an orphanage.
