## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls was a limited-time flagship event of Version 5.2 (28 November to 16 December 2024) set among the Masters of the Night-Wind in Natlan. Ororon asks the Traveler for help when a spirit-duffer named Leyla loses his spirit during a clairvoyance attempt. With Citlali the Traveler recovers it and learns that a stranger called Kokuchuu was after the location of the vanished Mare Jivari.
### STANDARD +
Alongside the story the event offered three gameplay modes tied to clairvoyance (gathering roaming spirits, banishing disruptive foes and arranging fragments of a woven scroll). Its main reward was the sword Calamity of Eshu. Because it was an event, the story is canon but not known to every player or character.
### ULTRA +
Tone is comic and warm, built on the grandmother-and-grandson dynamic between Citlali and Ororon, but the plot pivots on a real issue: a tribe where spiritual talent is the norm and a young man who lacks it. It also hands the Traveler a map and a relic shard that point towards the Mare Jivari.

## plot
@availability: limited
### LITE
Ororon invites the Traveler to the Masters of the Night-Wind. Citlali has been blocking him from saving a comatose man, Leyla, whose spirit is lost after a clairvoyance session. Citlali joins in, reads Leyla's lingering visions, discovers they lead to Sanhaj's relic and the Mare Jivari, and calls his spirit home. The stranger behind it escapes.
### STANDARD +
The story runs over three volumes: Night-Wind Letter, Vision of Ashen Desolation and The Spirit of Ah K'ulbatil. Leyla, a man with no spiritual sensitivity, is physically present but spiritless. Citlali and Ororon first disagree over how to help him, then work together through two approaches: gathering roaming spirits, and reading the visions his body and a relic produce. The trail reveals that a stranger planted the relic as bait.
### LARGE +
**Night-Wind Letter.** Ororon's letter asks the Traveler to visit the Masters of the Night-Wind. He asks them to distract Citlali with her favourite pastimes (drink, naps, light novels) so he can reach a cave where a man lies motionless. Citlali catches Ororon at the ritual site. She explains that Leyla is a "spirit-duffer", someone with no spiritual sensitivity, and that his spirit got lost. Because the tribe's healing methods depend on spiritual sensitivity, they cannot treat him directly. Citlali opposes Ororon's plan of using his own special constitution, which she considers too risky. Instead she proposes two approaches: gather the roaming spirits through a gateway to the Night Kingdom and hope to find Leyla's, and use Leyla's body as a medium for her own clairvoyance to learn what mystery he sought. The Traveler weaves the visions on the spiritloom, producing an image of Sanhaj Kompore, one of the six heroes and a Border Master. Citlali notes that his spiritual remains are still powerful.

**Vision of Ashen Desolation.** The roaming-spirit search fails to turn up Leyla. Citlali has learned from Leyla's parents that he had recently befriended an Inazuman-dressed stranger calling himself Kokuchuu. A flashback shows Kokuchuu proposing a "hypersensitization therapy" in which Leyla would immerse himself in spiritually rich objects. Citlali says the tribe once offered a similar treatment but outlawed it because of the risk and low success rate. She found a broken shard at their camp. Ororon senses it is tied to his ancient name. Using the shard as a medium, the visions show a flaming bow, confirmed as the weapon Sanhaj used in the Battle of the Mare Jivari, and then a map of the Mare Jivari's last known location in westernmost Natlan. Leyla's body reacts and a spirit approaches. Citlali needs two days to prepare the return.

**The Spirit of Ah K'ulbatil.** Citlali performs the rite with the ceremonial sword Calamity of Eshu, invoking the Ruler of Death and her title Ah K'ulbatil, and Leyla wakes. She scolds him, then credits Ororon for noticing something was wrong. Leyla insists he chose the method himself, though Kokuchuu supplied the relic. He admits he wanted not to be the odd one out in a talented tribe. Matla gives him a checkup. Then Citlali reveals she had left bait (the map and shard) in the cave and set a trap, which caught Kokuchuu. He slipped away, though she marked him so she can track him. She gives the shard and map to the Traveler. Leyla is surprised to find he can see through her illusion, and Citlali tells Ororon to teach him.
### ULTRA +
Citlali's reason for not holding Kokuchuu is that the cave is next to the hideout of a sleeping mad sage, and a fight could wake him. She also quietly admits the escape embarrassed her. After the quest the Traveler can offer her the light novels Ororon mentioned, and she tries to hide how pleased she is.

## revelations
@version: 5.2
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Kokuchuu was after the location of the Mare Jivari, and Leyla was used as bait in a plan to get a real clairvoyant to examine Sanhaj's relic.
### LARGE +
Sanhaj Kompore was one of the six heroes and a legendary Border Master of the Masters of the Night-Wind. According to Citlali, his border mastery turned the tide at the Battle of the Mare Jivari five hundred years ago: he summoned a miniature Night Kingdom and exploded it against the dark calamity, and he and Tenoch, leader of the tribal coalition forces, both died casting the spell. The bow shard is a relic of the weapon he used there.
### ULTRA +
The Mare Jivari, once a wasteland of ash, vanished from the world in recent decades. Citlali says the map points to a remote place in the far west of Natlan where few settlements stood before.

## characters
@availability: limited
### LITE
Citlali, Ororon, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Leyla, a member of the Masters of the Night-Wind who lacks spiritual sensitivity; Matla, the tribe's witch doctor; and Kokuchuu, an Inazuman-dressed stranger who is listed under Enjou on the wiki. Leyla's parents appear only in report.

## consequences
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Leyla recovers and begins training under Ororon. The Traveler keeps the shard and map. Citlali marks Kokuchuu and plans to follow him.
### ULTRA +
The shard and map are described as potentially useful if the Traveler later seeks the Mare Jivari. The event story does not resolve what Kokuchuu intends.

## placement
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Released 28 November 2024 in Version 5.2 as a flagship event. It required Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III. Completing Chapter V Act IV, The Rainbow Destined to Burn, was recommended for the best experience. It takes place in the Masters of the Night-Wind territory in the Tezcatepetonco Range.

## entry: Spirit-duffer
@keys: spirit-duffer, spirit-duffers
@version: 5.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the Masters of the Night-Wind a spirit-duffer is a person with no spiritual sensitivity: they cannot sense or command spirits and cannot cast the tribe's spells. Because the tribe's healing and problem solving all rely on dealing with spirits, ordinary treatments fail on them. Forcing a spirit-duffer into clairvoyance is possible but dangerous, and Leyla's case shows why.

## entry: Kokuchuu
@keys: Kokuchuu
@version: 5.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Kokuchuu is the name used by a stranger in Inazuman dress who befriends Leyla and proposes the dangerous hypersensitization therapy. Citlali judges that he used Leyla to get a clairvoyant to view Sanhaj's relic and learn where the Mare Jivari lay. He was never captured. In the quest he presents himself as a friendly helper.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the character as Enjou in the volume II cast. Treat the link as wiki-sourced rather than stated in dialogue.

## entry: Sanhaj's bow shard
@keys: bow shard, Sanhaj relic
@keys2: Citlali, Ororon, Leyla
@version: 5.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A broken shard of the bow Sanhaj used in the Battle of the Mare Jivari. It carries strong spiritual energy and was used as a medium by Leyla. Ororon feels it is connected to his ancient name. Citlali gives it to the Traveler together with a map of the Mare Jivari's last known location.

## entry: Hypersensitization therapy
@keys: hypersensitization therapy
@version: 5.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Kokuchuu's name for immersing a spirit-duffer in spiritually rich substances so they may glimpse spirits. Citlali says the Masters of the Night-Wind once offered a similar treatment but outlawed it, since it carried severe risk and rarely worked.
