## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
### LITE
Tournament of Glory in Bloom was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 5.5 (28 March to 14 April 2025), set in Natlan. A young Tepetlisaurus named Milray falls gravely ill, and the cure needs Sacred Flame Crystals, which in ancient times came from a six-tribe contest called the Bloomflower Trials. The tribes revive it, the Traveler and Paimon take part, and the story uncovers why the Trials were forgotten. Completing the event invited Ororon (Electro) as a reward character.
### STANDARD
The story is told in three parts: For Our Saurian Companion, Medicine Born of Guilt and Spell of Unity. Milray, a juvenile Tepetlisaurus who fought the Abyss in the war, has a rare phlogiston-related illness. The sauro-vet Ifa of the Flower-Feather Clan says the cure needs Ocean Zephyrs and Sacred Flame Crystals. The six chiefs and the Pyro Archon Mavuika agree to hold the Bloomflower Trials again, with exhibition matches in which the Traveler fights alongside, then against, the tribes' champions. Investigating a rumor that the Crystals "beguile human hearts", the group learns that the last Trials, centuries ago, ended in greed and bloodshed, and that Ifa's ancestor lost a Qucusaur companion called Antu. Antu's spirit still lingers. With the cooperation of everyone, they stage a harmless performance, the Spell of Unity, to give it peace while Milray is cured.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 20, the Prologue Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom, and, for the best experience, Mavuika's Story Quest Sol Invictus Chapter Act I. The event offered two gameplay modes alongside the story, the Bloomflower Trials (war games between the tribes) and Saurian Treasure Seeker (a route-planning mini-game with young saurians).

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Part 1, For Our Saurian Companion: the Traveler and Paimon find the Children of Echoes gathered around Milray. Ifa, a vet from the Flower-Feather Clan, says the young saurian used too much phlogiston in the war, and the cure needs Ocean Zephyrs and a pure form of the Sacred Flame called Sacred Flame Crystals. Biram knows from scrolls that the Crystals were once obtained through the Bloomflower Trials, a joint combat exercise of the six tribes not held for centuries. The chiefs agree to revive it, with voluntary entry and new rules. Milray tells the Traveler, while indwelt as a saurian, that the Crystals are dangerous and can turn friends into enemies, a warning he heard from a mysterious Qucusaur. Mavuika approves the plan but finds the rumor implausible. The tribes' champions compete to recruit the Traveler, who joins an exhibition match on the side of the Scions of the Canopy, People of the Springs and Children of Echoes, capturing the Collective of Plenty, Masters of the Night-Wind and Flower-Feather Clan bases. A hot-spring party follows, at which Kachina seems troubled.
### LARGE +
Part 2, Medicine Born of Guilt: Kachina admits she feels hollow and left behind after achieving her dream of joining the Night Warden Wars; the Traveler reassures her that others feel this way. With Citlali they search old woven scrolls at the Masters of the Night-Wind and find only that the last Trials were held to save an ailing saurian, failed, and caused a rupture between a Flower-Feather chief and his younger brother. Naiyan, Milray's friend, describes the Qucusaur as "light and floaty". Ifa is followed by a ghostly Qucusaur, which leads the group to a buried box of scrolls written by the younger brother, Nampole, Ifa's ancestor. They show how Nampole's companion Antu fell ill, how Nampole asked the tribes to take part in a scaled-down Trials while he went to find Ocean Zephyrs, and how his brother, the chief Imara, used the contest to hoard Crystals. The tribes turned on each other, Imara spent the Crystals on tribal research instead of Antu's cure, and Antu died, ashamed and believing the Crystals themselves had poisoned people's hearts. Nampole spent his life healing saurians, which Ifa's family calls "medicine born of guilt".
### ULTRA +
Part 3, Spell of Unity: Antu's spirit does not know the truth, and Ifa judges that telling it would only wound it. The Traveler, Ifa, Citlali, Kachina and friends instead plan a play in which the group pretends the Crystals have bewitched them into quarrelling, then lets Antu "cleanse" them with a Spell of Unity. Mavuika, Xilonen, Ororon (who makes smoke and light effects), Mualani and Kinich (director) join. After the second half of the exhibition match, the performance goes ahead, with Mualani, Citlali and Xilonen turning real grievances into comic arguments, and Mavuika reading prepared lines. Antu casts the spell, the "evil" fades and the squabbling stops. Ifa then mixes the medicine, Milray recovers, and Antu sees the Night Kingdom, where Nampole is waiting, waves goodbye and joins him. A feast follows, and Kachina decides she wants to bake sweets to share. Later, Ifa visits Nampole's hill to speak to his ancestor.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Bloomflower Trials were an ancient joint contest of the six tribes that produced Sacred Flame Crystals. The last one ended in greed, violence between tribes and a scandal that all six tribes wished to forget, which is why the scrolls say little. Sacred Flame Crystals are not supernatural corrupters; they are another form of the Sacred Flame, far less miraculous than once believed. Ifa's family tradition of veterinary medicine grew from his ancestor Nampole's guilt over Antu.
### LARGE +
Citlali and Biram note that the Crystals were once thought to enhance warriors, but turned out to be nearly identical in essence to ordinary Sacred Flame. Mavuika says the rules of the Ode of Resurrection have been fuelled by the Sacred Flame for ages without ill effect. In Part 3 she keeps the surplus Crystals for future use. The Qucusaur spirit, once Antu, kept lingering after death, possibly hibernating in a cave near the Remnants of the Secret Source, until the war's end let Ley Lines recover and the way to the Night Kingdom open.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Event-exclusive characters: Milray (young Tepetlisaurus), Naiyan (his friend, a companion saurian), Antu (Qucusaur spirit), Nampole and Imara (the brothers of the past), Ballo (event organiser) and Vinesh (runs the Saurian Expedition Supply Station). Ifa and his assistant Cacucu, a Qucusaur who translates for saurians, are central. The champions of the six tribes (Mualani, Varesa, Kinich, Kachina, Chasca and Citlali) are the trial characters, with Ororon, Iansan, Xilonen and the chiefs also appearing. Charlotte of Fontaine appears as a reporter covering the contest.
### LARGE +
Kachina is the emotional focus of the middle part, wrestling with uncertainty about her future. Ororon is the newly arriving character of the event and is introduced as one of the Masters of the Night-Wind; he provides the black mist illusion in the play. Ajaw, Kinich's companion, ruins arguments by appointing himself referee. Mavuika attends but declines to compete so as not to spoil others' fun.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Milray recovers and the Bloomflower Trials conclude successfully. Mavuika announces that, without arguments or scheming, all six tribes worked together. Antu's spirit finds peace. Kachina decides to pursue baking, and the chiefs are shown relaxing together. The event adds no new lasting lore beyond this history of the Trials and Ifa's family. Players who completed the stages could invite Ororon, and the event paid out 960 Primogems in total.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.5 as a Flagship Event, after the Natlan Archon Quest content. The wiki does not give an exact story position; the event assumes the war against the Abyss has ended and the Ley Lines have recovered. Treat it as a post-war interlude shared by the Traveler and Paimon.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. The event had three phases (28 March, 30 March and 1 April, 2025). In the Bloomflower Trials, the Traveler plays a six-part series of offensive and defensive battles, each with a required trial character: Offensive battles use ritual items to break barriers around enemy camps, defensive battles protect a flag. Saurian Treasure Seeker has the player steer young Tatankasaurus and Koholasaurus whelps through six stages collecting Grainfruit and avoiding hilichurls, magma and watchtower patrols. To invite Ororon, players had to clear all offensive stages, earn Bronze or better on all other stages, and complete the collection and infiltration stages.
### ULTRA +
The six trials are Attack From the Waterway (Mualani, offense), Crush the Enemy Head-On (Varesa, defense), Traverse! Infiltrate!! Surprise!!! (Kinich, offense), Like Towering Mountains and Sturdiest Stone (Kachina, defense), Air Supremacy and Close Support (Chasca, offense) and Dynamic Defense (Citlali, defense). The treasure stages are Twinkle Twinkle Little Fruit, The Truth of Churlish Treasure, Don't Panic! In the Volcano, Eyes of Ire, Aye?, Saurians, Water, and Fire and An Ending to Treasure. The quests Let the Bloomflower Trials Begin! (Ballo) and Go Go Saurian Expedition! (Vinesh) unlock the two modes.

## entry: Bloomflower Trials
@keys: Bloomflower Trials, Bloomflower Trial
@keys2: Natlan, Sacred Flame Crystal, Sacred Flame Crystals
@version: 5.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
An ancient contest in which the six tribes of Natlan fought together in organised war games, producing a purer form of Sacred Flame called Sacred Flame Crystals. The last one held, many centuries ago, collapsed amid greed and violence, and the tradition was forgotten. In Version 5.5 the tribes revive it as a voluntary tournament, with main matches between two tribes and exhibition matches between three and three, to cure a sick young saurian.
### LARGE +
The Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame later supplied enough Sacred Flame to make the Trials unnecessary.

## entry: Sacred Flame Crystals
@keys: Sacred Flame Crystal, Sacred Flame Crystals
@keys2: Natlan, Milray, Bloomflower Trials, Ifa
@version: 5.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A rarer, purer form of the Sacred Flame produced through the Bloomflower Trials. They were once believed to give warriors great power but turned out to be little different from ordinary Sacred Flame, though they can treat certain chronic phlogiston-related illnesses. A rumor that they "beguile human hearts" arises from a young Qucusaur's misunderstanding of greed; Mavuika and Citlali find no evidence for it.

## entry: Milray
@keys: Milray
@keys2: Tepetlisaurus, Naiyan, Ifa, Children of Echoes
@version: 5.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A young Tepetlisaurus of the Children of Echoes who joined the war against the Abyss despite his age and was awarded a Warrior's Medal. Overuse of phlogiston left him with a rare condition that nobody had managed to cure. He is cured by Ifa's medicine in the event, and his friends include Naiyan and Antu.

## entry: Antu and Nampole
@keys: Antu, Nampole, Imara
@keys2: Qucusaur, Flower-Feather Clan, Ifa, Bloomflower Trials
@version: 5.5
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Antu was the Qucusaur companion of Nampole, a scholar-healer of the Flower-Feather Clan, and fell ill centuries ago; Nampole organised a small Bloomflower Trials to obtain Crystals while his brother, the chief Imara, hoarded them for the tribe. Antu died believing the Crystals were evil, and Nampole wrote the story down. In the present Antu's spirit befriends Milray and Naiyan and is given peace through the Spell of Unity.

## entry: Ifa

### STANDARD
In this event Ifa, a veterinary doctor from the Flower-Feather Clan, diagnoses Milray and discovers that his family's tradition began with his ancestor Nampole. His father taught him that their skills were "medicine born of guilt". He designs the Spell of Unity plan and cures Milray.

## entry: Spell of Unity
@keys: Spell of Unity
@keys2: Antu, Ifa, Milray, Mavuika
@version: 5.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A harmless staged play devised by Ifa and Citlali in which the Traveler and friends pretend the Sacred Flame Crystals have made them quarrel, until Antu "cleanses" them. It lets the Qucusaur's spirit feel it has undone its past mistake, and allows it to rejoin Nampole in the Night Kingdom.
