## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### LITE
Three Realms Gateway Offering was a limited-time Flagship event in Version 2.5 (17 February to 30 March 2022). Enkanomiya has been wrapped in an unnatural darkness. At Kokomi's request the Traveler returns there, meets a masked shrine maiden named Tsumi, and helps relight three towers before confronting the Abyss adherent Enjou. The story chapter is called Aphotic Diffusal.
### STANDARD
The event story follows the Traveler and Paimon down to Enkanomiya after Kokomi's own investigation shows that the darkness is empowering monsters and has brought strange armoured creatures called Shadowy Husks. A shrine maiden in a snake mask, Tsumi, protects the Dainichi Mikoshi with a Sentou ceremony and gives the Traveler a device, the Bokuso Box, that gathers light and drives darkness back. By collecting Aphotium Ore and Tokoyo Legumes, defeating Husks for their gems and relighting three Towers of the Void, the Traveler weakens the darkness. In the aftermath Enjou claims the vishaps are behind the crisis and that Tsumi is one of them, while Kokomi suggests another reading of who Tsumi may be.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 30, the Archon Quest Chapter II Act II "Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow" and the World Quest "Erebos' Secret". The related world quest The Bunkoku Enigma was unlocked by finishing the Aftermath quest.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
The Eve: Kokomi tells the Traveler that the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri has stopped the bleaching of the soil rather than reversing it. Her scouts report a lasting darkness over Enkanomiya and creatures called Shadowy Husks. Inside the Dainichi Mikoshi the Traveler meets Tsumi, who agrees to work with them. Parts I to III: the Traveler restores the three towers by collecting Aphotium Ore and Tokoyo Legumes and defeating Husks. Aftermath: Enjou is cornered, defeated, and gives his own account of the vishaps and Tsumi. The Traveler lights the Dainichi Mikoshi's depths, Tsumi leaves with the vishaps, and Kokomi tells what she knows about the Vassals of Watatsumi.
### LARGE
The Eve. Paimon proposes visiting Kokomi at the Sangonomiya Shrine. Kokomi, speaking with Ueno, explains that after the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri used the Bloodbranch Coral, the soil only stopped bleaching and did not recover, which was not expected. She sent the Stingray III division into Enkanomiya, which found a lasting darkness that empowers local monsters and brings Shadowy Husks, plus a shrine maiden in a snake mask who performed a Sentou ceremony to shield the Dainichi Mikoshi. Kokomi orders Swordfish I to help with the evacuation and gives the Traveler a Fragmented Accessory, to be used when a hard decision comes. In the Dainichi Mikoshi the soldiers show their worry about Tsumi; one of them, Imaizumi, says her pupils are like a snake's, a mark the islanders associate with the Vassals of Watatsumi. The Traveler and Paimon decide to work with her anyway. Tsumi says the darkness comes from three towers at the corners of Enkanomiya's triangular border and hands over the Bokuso Box, a device found in the Dainichi Mikoshi that collects light and clears darkness in a small radius. She tells them an Abyss adherent caused the situation, and the Traveler and Paimon recognise who she means.

Parts I to III. By gathering Aphotium Ore and Tokoyo Legumes across Enkanomiya and defeating the Husks near each tower to obtain their gems, the Traveler restores the Towers of the Void one after another. The three quests are tied to the Narrows, Evernight Temple and the Serpent's Heart, with the towers in the loci of Yachimatahiko, Yachimatahime and Kunado. The quests can be completed in any order.

Aftermath. Tsumi says the darkness under the Dainichi Mikoshi is unusual and she has located the Abyss adherent. The Traveler finds Enjou, who is surprised they sided with Tsumi, suggests a fight so the Traveler can let off steam, and loses. He then tells a story. According to an old Enkanomiyan legend, after the Bathysmal Vishaps were defeated a line of them evolved to look like humans except for their pupils, the Vishap-people, who infiltrated Enkanomiya's higher ranks. He says Tsumi is one and is using the Traveler for revenge. He claims he made an alliance with the vishaps, promising darkness so their elites could enter the Dainichi Mikoshi and rescue captive kin, in return for safe passage for the Abyss Order and help rebuilding the towers, but they did not know abyssal darkness poisons elemental beings and felt betrayed. He says that once the Mikoshi's power is used to suppress the Sangacorallia and protect the vishaps, the source of the Bloodbranch Coral will be cut off. He tells them how to dispel the darkness with a mechanism in each of the three areas and leaves a gift, an accessory piece matching Kokomi's. Tsumi is gone when the Traveler returns, and most of the soldiers have been evacuated. The Traveler activates the three mechanisms with the Bokuso Box, descends into the Mikoshi, and holds off the vishap elites there. Tsumi waits above, wishing to destroy the Dainichi Mikoshi so the vishaps will not suffer again. The Traveler gives her the two fragments, which she assembles into a pendant of three entwined snakes that has been with her as long as she can remember. She says she grew up among vishaps, who taught her language and writing, and decides to return to them rather than stay on Watatsumi Island. She leaves the Mikoshi standing and warns that the vishaps will return.
### ULTRA +
Details. Tsumi says she found the methods and tools for the Sentou ceremony in the Dainichi Mikoshi and that she can read human writing. The wiki notes the Bokuso Box's upgrades give corrosion resistance and, later, the ability to disperse stronger darkness; the Sentou Trials in the Narrows, Evernight Temple and the Serpent's Heart give extra Light Realm Sigils (see gameplay).

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kokomi explains the three pendants. When the Watatsumi Omikami reorganised the court and reformed Enkanomiya, three matching pendants were given to the Dainichi Mikoshi, the Sangonomiya line and the Vassals of Watatsumi, so that commoners, officials and the divine vassals would hold one another accountable. Only the commoners have flourished since, while the other two bloodlines declined. The Vassals of Watatsumi were the familiars of the serpent god. Over time they came to look just like humans, except for their pupils. When the Watatsumi Omikami spent its power creating Watatsumi Island, it weakened and no more Vassals were born. The records mention only the last one, who was hoped to become a bridge between vishaps and humans and vanished as an infant at the Enkanomiya border after an attack by vishaps with her guardian shrine maiden. Most believe she died; some believe vishaps raised her. Kokomi says nothing outright connecting this child to Tsumi.
### LARGE +
Kokomi adds that Vishap-people and Reptilians are fiction with no evidence in Sangonomiya's classified documents. Now that she understands how the Bloodbranch Coral works, she plans to look for another way to counter the bleaching soil so the islanders no longer disturb the vishaps. She also hopes Tsumi will find where she belongs, since the Watatsumi people owe the Vassals a debt, now that the vishaps and islanders no longer dispute territory.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
Kokomi (Divine Priestess of Sangonomiya) gives the quest and the first accessory fragment. Tsumi, a masked shrine maiden with snake-like pupils, is the ally. Enjou is the Abyss adherent behind the darkness. Paimon and the Traveler carry out the work. Soldiers Imaizumi and Ueno and others from the Watatsumi Army appear in the Dainichi Mikoshi.
### LARGE +
The Eve lists Enomoto, Tsuyuko, Yabe and Imaizumi among the NPCs. In The Bunkoku Enigma Yabe and the afterimage of Koyanagi appear.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
The darkness is repelled and most evacuees are safe. Enjou disappears again. Tsumi returns to the vishaps who raised her and warns they will come back. Kokomi decides not to rely on the Bloodbranch Coral again if she can avoid it. The event unlocked The Bunkoku Enigma and a battle challenge, The Call of Ice and Thunder, against the Bathysmal Vishap elites.
### LARGE +
The Fragmented Accessory pieces Kokomi and Enjou provide form a pendant with Tsumi. The Traveler gives away both pieces, and Paimon feels slightly guilty about handing over what was Kokomi's.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.5 as a Flagship Event, set in Enkanomiya and on Watatsumi Island after the Archon Quest Chapter II Act II and the world quest Erebos' Secret, and after Kokomi's use of the Bloodbranch Coral in the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri. The reward weapon was the Oathsworn Eye. The Bunkoku Enigma could not be completed after Version 2.5.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. The Bokuso Box has 15 upgrade levels bought with Light Realm Sigils, raising Corrosion Resistance (to protect against accumulating Corrosion) and unlocking exploration effects such as marking nearby ores, chests and challenges. Bokuso Arts add combat boosts for the event. Sentou Trials, Corrupted Waypoints, Illusory Platforms and Stairways, and the Rite of Diffusal (restoring sealed waypoints with Aphotium Ore and Tokoyo Legumes) are the exploration systems. The Call of Ice and Thunder is a Normal, Hard and Extreme challenge against vishap elites. The Oathsworn Eye (Catalyst) was the main reward.

## entry: Bokuso Box
@keys: Bokuso Box, Bokuso Arts
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A light-gathering device Tsumi found in the Dainichi Mikoshi. It clears the Void's darkness within a small radius and, through upgrades paid for with Light Realm Sigils, strengthens its holder against the corrosion. It also powers the three mechanisms Enjou left for dispelling the darkness in the Dainichi Mikoshi. It disappears from the inventory after the event.

## entry: Tsumi
@keys: Tsumi
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A shrine maiden in a snake mask who protects the Dainichi Mikoshi with a Sentou ceremony. Her pupils are like a snake's, which makes some islanders think of the Vassals of Watatsumi. She grew up among vishaps, who taught her to speak and read. Enjou says she is a Vishap-people; Kokomi says that is fiction and hints she may be the lost last Vassal. She leaves with the vishaps after the Traveler helps her assemble a pendant.
### LARGE +
She can read human writing and learned the Sentou ceremony from texts in the Dainichi Mikoshi. She tells the Traveler she wants the Mikoshi destroyed so the vishaps will suffer no more, then decides against it.

## entry: Vassals of Watatsumi
@keys: Vassals of Watatsumi, Vassal of Watatsumi
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Kokomi's account the first familiars of the Watatsumi Omikami, a half-human, half-snake kind said to have helped the islanders resist the Bathysmal Vishaps. Over time they looked fully human except for their pupils. No more were born after the god exhausted itself making Watatsumi Island. Only the last is mentioned in the records, as an infant who disappeared.

## entry: Vishap-people
@keys: Vishap-people, Reptilians
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An old Enkanomiyan legend repeated by Enjou: after the Bathysmal Vishaps lost, some evolved to imitate the victors and became indistinguishable from humans except for their pupils. Kokomi says there is no evidence for them and calls them fiction. Do not treat them as established canon.

## entry: Dainichi Mikoshi
@keys: Dainichi Mikoshi
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In this event the shelter in Enkanomiya where wounded soldiers and islanders gather, where Tsumi tends them and where the Bokuso Box and the Sentou ceremony texts are found. Enjou claims the vishaps want to rescue captive kin from beneath it. Tsumi considers destroying it and does not.

## entry: The Bunkoku Enigma
@keys: Bunkoku Enigma, Bunkoku's Secret Treasure, Bunkoku Puzzle
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A world quest unlocked after the Aftermath. Yabe, who patrols near the Dainichi Mikoshi and likes mysteries, finds a stone slate and asks the Traveler to solve the Bunkoku Puzzle of eight slates and a sliding-tile mechanism. Beneath is a hidden chamber guarded by defiled spirits (a Mysterious Samurai). The afterimage of Koyanagi, who believes he attacked his friend while possessed, is found there. Yabe afterwards sums up the solution as ordinary elemental effects that kill the excitement. The story of Bunkoku, a traitor minister of Byakuyakoku, is told only in Yabe's partial version.
