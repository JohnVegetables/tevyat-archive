## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 6.0
### LITE
Clink Clank Clash was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 6.0 (Luna I), played from 2025-09-12 to 2025-09-29, set in Nod-Krai. After the Traveler and Paimon settle in, they find the young inventor Aino and her robot companion Ineffa caught up in a crisis involving stray robots, Guild commissions and a suspicious "outsourced contract". It is a light, comic workshop story told through short event quests and four minigames.
### STANDARD +
The story is spread over six event quests, each unlocking a minigame or following on from one. Treat it as a canon side story that happened during an event, not something every Nod-Krai resident would mention.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 6.0
### LITE
Aino takes on several commissions at once. A man calling himself Gaudenty says he represents the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau and wants runaway recon bots recovered. The Adventurers' Guild wants a self-defence device made for local adventurers. A Fontainian engineer, Ginette, has lost control of robots she built. In the end Gaudenty is exposed as a fraud who tried to steal Ginette's robots, and a merchant named Asikko takes him away.
### STANDARD
Gaudenty's "outsourced contract" has Aino and the Traveler chase runaway recon bots with Moonlanes and kuuhenki; the Adventurers' Guild's Sarin has them field-test a self-defence device; Ginette needs her kuuvahki robots found and debugged, and the robots turn out to want to play with the Traveler and to defend them; Ineffa shows off Aino's stealth robot Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1, built for a rescue of Aino's friends from the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau. In the finale, Unwritten Rules, Gaudenty is caught trying to steal Ginette's robots with inside help; Sarin reveals he is a disgraced ex-Guild bounty hunter, not a Bureau representative, and the merchant Asikko, who claims Gaudenty owes him, takes him away.
### LARGE +
Quest by quest, in play order:

- A Mysterious Outsourced Contract (Northern Training Ground, Lempo Isle). Gaudenty claims to be an expatriate representative of the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau. He says a batch of recon bots has escaped the Bureau and offers payment in robot parts, not Mora. Aino, who wants the parts for research, accepts and plans to use Moonlanes and kuuhenki to reach the high-flying bots. This unlocks "Pursue and Strike! The Out-of-Control Flier!".
- An Adventurer's Woes. Sarin, Branch Master of the Adventurers' Guild in Nod-Krai, explains that adventurers there cannot count on local authorities for protection. His self-defence device failed, so he commissions Aino to adapt it to kuuvahki-rich surroundings by testing it in fights. Aino will not fight herself. This unlocks "Boom! Blast! Power Test!".
- Misplaced Creativity. Ginette has built robots that use kuuvahki to communicate at a distance, but they malfunction. She also admits she lost them while testing at varied kuuvahki concentrations. Aino prepares a device that gathers kuuvahki remnants to track them. This unlocks "Seek and Find! Machines on the Fritz!".
- New Idea and The Last Step of Testing. Ginette adjusts the recovered robots and asks the Traveler to watch how the others behave. The robots gather and appear to invite the Traveler to play. Ginette concludes her design works through the robots' interaction with one another. While recovering the last batch, the Traveler and Paimon are set upon by Oprichniki, and the robots help fight them.
- Backup Plans. Ineffa shows the Traveler and Paimon Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1, a stealth robot Aino made from an abandoned cleaning bot bought on the black market. It exists because Krum-Clank 70% Sweet was stolen, and Aino wants to be able to slip into the Bureau and rescue her friends. This unlocks "Quietly Now! Sneaky Sneaking Sneaks!".
- Unwritten Rules. Gaudenty is caught trying to steal Ginette's robots, after Ineffa and Sarin warned her. Ineffa shows from repaired surveillance records that he had inside help at the workshop. Sarin says he is a former Guild member expelled for misconduct, then a bounty hunter who lost his Voynich Guild certification, and no real representative of anything. The merchant Asikko steps in, claiming the man owes him a debt, and takes Gaudenty away.
Details worth keeping straight:

- The "contract". Asikko says he got Gaudenty a subcontract from the Design Bureau, that Gaudenty failed at it and re-outsourced the work to Aino, and that Gaudenty then let decommissioned recon bots flood the black market. Gaudenty's own claim that the bots must not reach outsiders sits badly with that account.
- Asikko's notice. Ineffa points out that Asikko had posted a high price for any of Aino's creations, which could push greedy people into stealing. Asikko answers that he only praised the technology and backed it with his own money, and that anyone who oversteps is responsible for it. Ineffa admits she cannot logically refute this but asks him to withdraw the notice, since Aino treasures every robot companion. He agrees not to press Aino commercially.
- Ginette's robots. Asikko tries to recruit Ginette, hoping the combat skills the robots picked up could become a product. She explains that she wanted robots that gain a degree of intelligence through kuuvahki interaction, that in her system kuuvahki behaved more like emotion than logic, and that the robots fight to protect their favourite playmate, the Traveler. She compares them to children who will not take orders. Sarin warns that the Guild will step in if disputes are stirred up, and Asikko backs off.
- Ineffa's stance. Asked whether letting Asikko leave with Gaudenty is right, Ineffa says Nod-Krai has many unwritten rules in its shadows, that she does not agree with them, and that she only wants them kept away from Aino's life.
- Afterwards. Letti, another member of the Nod-Krai Adventurers' Guild, takes Gaudenty's place at the workshop on the Branch Master's behalf because Aino has not finished her research on the recon bots. Letti says the Moonlanes the Traveler built remain usable, the collected components need not be returned, and the Fatui side does not intend to hold them accountable.

### ULTRA +
Reward and framing details: completing the quests also granted Mora, Adventurer's Experience and Wanderer's Advice, and the event offered five tiers of Legion Morale rewards up to 1,000 (the Etherlight Spindlelute at 200 and a Crown of Insight at 1,000). The Glacial Moon Battle Pass carried four event missions. Letti's name is Finnish for "plait" or "braid" (wiki etymology note, not in-story).

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
- Gaudenty is not a real representative of the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, according to Sarin; he was expelled from the Adventurers' Guild for misconduct and later lost his Voynich Guild certification.
- Gaudenty worked with an insider, whom Paimon calls "that receptionist", to try to steal from Aino's workshop.
- Asikko had posted a notice offering a high price for Aino's creations, which may have encouraged the theft.
- Ginette's robots fight to protect the Traveler, whom they treat as a favourite playmate.
### LARGE +
Per Ineffa's account of Aino's backup plan, Aino's friends are somewhere the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau guards heavily. The event does not say who they are beyond the theft of Krum-Clank 70% Sweet, and it does not show a rescue.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
- Aino: a young inventor who runs the workshop, chooses jobs by how interesting they are, and waives extra pay when a task sounds fun. She wants the recon bot parts for her own research.
- Ineffa: Aino's robot companion, who explains Aino's projects in precise, analytical statements and handles the confrontation in Unwritten Rules.
- The Traveler and Paimon: the helpers who run the tests, recover robots and are treated as favoured playmates by Ginette's robots.
- Sarin: Branch Master of the Adventurers' Guild in Nod-Krai, who commissions the self-defence device and later exposes Gaudenty's background.
- Gaudenty: a self-styled Bureau representative and in truth a disgraced bounty hunter.
- Ginette: a Fontainian engineer-researcher whose robots communicate through kuuvahki.
- Asikko: a merchant from the black market with an unsettled debt claim on Gaudenty.
- Letti: a Guild member who appears only after the quest, in place of Gaudenty.
### LARGE +
Event-only NPCs here (Gaudenty, Ginette, Asikko, Letti) have no detailed canon profiles; do not give them backstory beyond what is stated. The wiki lists Letti with no profile. The Frostmoon Scions are mentioned only as the owners of the territory around the Light-Bathed Platform in one challenge briefing.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Gaudenty leaves in Asikko's custody, Asikko withdraws his commercial claim on Aino's work, and the Guild's contact at the workshop becomes Letti. Aino's research on the recon bots continues. No lasting change to Nod-Krai's politics is shown, and the event is not needed to follow the Archon Quest.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
(Game mechanics, not in-world fact.) The event required Adventure Rank 20 and completion of the Prologue's Act III "Song of the Dragon and Freedom"; completing Song of the Welkin Moon Act II "Elegy of Dust and Lamplight" was recommended. Players earned Legion Morale from four minigames: "Pursue and Strike! The Out-of-Control Flier!" (riding Moonlanes with kuuhenki to shoot down rogue recon bots and collect Moonlit Particles), "Boom! Blast! Power Test!" (waves of enemies, recharging a self-defence device with kuuvahki energy), "Seek and Find! Machines on the Fritz!" (following kuuvahki remnants to find malfunctioning robots) and "Quietly Now! Sneaky Sneaking Sneaks!" (steering Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1 past patrols to collect ID cards). The Catalyst Etherlight Spindlelute was the headline reward.
### LARGE +
The challenge locations include Nasha Town, the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, the Light-Bathed Platform (Frostmoon Scion territory), Maroon Basin and the Barrowmoss Barrens. Enemies in the power tests include Oprichniki (Fatui) troopers, landcruisers and Radiant beasts.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.0, "Luna I", with phases opening on 2025-09-12, 2025-09-14 and 2025-09-16. In story terms it is an optional side story set in Nod-Krai after the player has been introduced to the region. It has no confirmed place in the Archon Quest order.

@version: 6.0
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Aino's stealth robot, built by modifying an abandoned cleaning bot bought on the black market. Ineffa says the testing platform simulates hostile environments so that Aino can check the robot's ability to stay hidden, and that it is meant to get into the heavily guarded Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau and rescue friends after Krum-Clank 70% Sweet was stolen. In the minigame it has blades for spinning attacks, a short dash, and can use kuuvahki bullets against patrolling mechanisms.
