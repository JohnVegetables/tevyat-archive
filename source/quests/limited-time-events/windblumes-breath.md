## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
### LITE
Windblume's Breath was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 3.5, run during the Windblume Festival from 3 to 20 March 2023 (event shop open a week longer). Mondstadt is decorated for the festival, and the Traveler can join three activities: a balloon-chasing game called Floral Pursuit, Ballads of Breeze music performances, and Breezy Snapshots, in which Fassben the Adventurer asks for photos taken around Mondstadt and Dragonspine. It is mainly a gameplay event with little plot.
### STANDARD
The event's in-game blurb frames it as the honeyed scents of the Windblume Festival wafting on the wind again, a festival of romance in the city of freedom, with a note found by accident leading the Traveler on another fantastical journey. Floral Pursuit introduces "Floral Chess", a new game created by the Mondstadt Adventurers' Guild; Ballads of Breeze has seven songs and a score editor; Breezy Snapshots has nine photo requests for a photobook. Rewards include Primogems, a Crown of Insight and the Claymore Mailed Flower.
### LARGE +
The event is canon but was only available for a limited time, so not every character in a roleplay setting will have taken part in it; treat it as an optional, festival-time memory. The Mailed Flower's item text ties the weapon to the Windblume and to a "nameless shepherd" telling stories of the wind, linking the event's reward to the festival's old meaning.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
No continuous storyline is documented. Each segment is a self-contained activity. Floral Pursuit is a six-stage challenge (Ordered, Inverted, Observant, Obstructing, Shimmering and Pursuing Wind), unlocking daily from 3 March, in which the player collects Bloom Balloons while avoiding Ersatz Balloons. Ballads of Breeze unlocks one song per day, and Breezy Snapshots has three sets of three photo requests from Fassben.
### LARGE +
Floral Pursuit mechanics fit its stages: Galeblessings charge an "Ersatz Dispeller" skill that destroys Ersatz Balloons for a short time, Gatherblessings widen the collection range, bookcases can be shifted to open paths (Obstructing Wind) and darkened areas must be lit with light sources (Shimmering Wind). Co-op play was allowed.

Breezy Snapshots sends the Traveler to Mondstadt City's main gate and square, Cider Lake, Dadaupa Gorge, Stormterror's Lair, a viewpoint at Whispering Woods, the Snow-Covered Path on Dragonspine, a Dragonspine panorama and Wyrmrest Valley. The commissioner's comments are informal: he wants a cover photo of the city gate with its bouquets and carpets, mentions the festival crowds, jokes that a request sounds like a Guild commission, and says Cyrus has often told him not to rush to Dragonspine. After seeing the Dragonspine photos he says he should adjust his own planned photo trip.
### ULTRA +
One request has a subject at Cider Lake who says they are not photogenic and is embarrassed to be photographed. The requests attach conditions to each shot (time-of-day windows, a Mondstadt character, a Pyro character, an Elemental Skill in use, an idle animation, a subject spanning one or two photo points), but these are gameplay constraints rather than story.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The event reveals nothing new about the main story. Its only lore weight is the Mailed Flower's text: the Windblume as a symbol, once lost to time, now a sign of love and gladness in times of peace, with a legend of a nameless shepherd who handed out "flowers" and an account that a noble took Ludi Harpastum away.
### LARGE +
The Windblume Festival's wider background is covered in its own entry: Windblumes are offered to Barbatos and loved ones, and the festival's origins lie in the rebels against Decarabian using "Windblume" as a code word.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon take part. Fassben the Adventurer is the commissioner of Breezy Snapshots. Cyrus is named in his remarks. The Mondstadt Adventurers' Guild created the Floral Chess game used in Floral Pursuit. No other named characters have verified roles.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
No lasting consequences for the story. The Mailed Flower, a four-star Claymore with the passive Whispers of Wind and Flower and Elemental Mastery as its second stat, could be earned during the event. Collei's Windblume and a Windblume Kamera were also among the rewards.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.5 as a Flagship Event, requiring Adventure Rank 18 and the Prologue Act III "Song of the Dragon and Freedom". For the best experience, Chapter III Act V "Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises" and Albedo's "Princeps Cretaceus Chapter" Act I were recommended; the Quick Start option let players without them enter anyway. It follows the earlier Windblume event "Invitation of Windblume" (Version 1.4), whose title is also a Ballads of Breeze song.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Ballads of Breeze has seven songs, one unlocked per day from 3 March 2023: Invitation of Windblume, Contemplation in Snow, Dance of Aphros, Caelestinum Finale Termini, Caprice of the Leaves, Winding Through Avidya and Bard's Adventure, each on Normal, Hard and Pro. Players can edit, save (up to 50 slots) and share scores, and choose between the Windsong Lyre, Floral Zither and Festive Drum interface styles. Currencies were Festive Tickets (songs and photos) and Floral Coupons (Floral Pursuit), exchanged in an event shop for a Crown of Insight, refinement material, talent books, weapon materials, Mystic Enhancement Ore and Mora.
