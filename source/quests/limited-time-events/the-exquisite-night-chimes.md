## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 3.4
### LITE
The Exquisite Night Chimes was the Version 3.4 Lantern Rite flagship event (19 January to 6 February 2023). The Traveler and Paimon help Dvorak, a musician from Fontaine, search Liyue for the descendants of an adeptus who once saved his ancestor, prepare a Lantern Rite concert with the Qixing, and learn that the rescuer was Madame Ping, playing a tune in memory of her friend Guizhong. An epilogue is a dinner at Xinyue Kiosk.
### STANDARD
The event also offered four festival activities (Radiant Sparks, Paper Theater, Vigilance at Sea and Behind the Scenes) that earned Festive Fever, redeemable for Primogems, a Crown of Insight, furnishings and a choice of 4-star Liyue character. The story is gentle: a mystery with a human answer, a concert that goes well, and a friendly dinner with Hu Tao, Xiao, Zhongli, Venti and others.
### LARGE +
The Lantern Rite theme that year was based on Sea Gazer, as Ningguang tells the Traveler, and the music-and-memory thread is closely tied to Guizhong's legacy.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Part I, A Thousand Miles for an Enigmatic Tune: Zhongli sends the Traveler to Qingce Village to collect bamboo shoots for his soup. In the bamboo forest Yaoyao saves Dvorak, who slipped into water while admiring a waterfall. Dvorak is an organizer of the Iridescence Tour, a Fontaine music festival which he and others hope to hold in every nation, and he is also searching for the woman who saved his ancestor. The group brings the problem to Ganyu and the Qixing, who help arrange a Lantern Rite concert. Yelan and the Traveler trace a spooky tune near Qingxu Pool to a Treasure Hoarder, Fujing, who copied a childhood warning melody to scare households away and steal.

Part II, A Single Harmony for an Irreplaceable Soul: Ganyu and the Traveler seek Cloud Retainer at Mt. Aocang and find Xiao and Shenhe instead. Cloud Retainer takes them to Guili Plains and tells of Guizhong, her friend Streetward Rambler and the tune Ping composed after Guizhong's death. Back at Liyue Harbor the truth is told to Ping herself, who is Madame Ping, and then to Dvorak. After delivering the bamboo shoots to Zhongli on Mt. Hulao, the Traveler sees the concert go off well.

Epilogue, Between Facades and Familiar Faces: Hu Tao hosts a dinner at Xinyue Kiosk.
### LARGE +
Part I detail: Dvorak says his ancestor, also a traveler, nearly drowned and heard an extraordinary melody; he woke ashore, saw a woman of almost divine bearing who left without speaking and who stayed a dozen paces ahead however fast he ran. The tale became a Fontaine legend, "The Lady Overlooking the Lake." Dvorak concluded it happened in Liyue and was led there after praying in Mondstadt and following leaves blown west toward Stone Gate. Yaoyao suggests Keqing and Ningguang for the concert, and Madame Ping supports it. Fujing says he was a poor Treasure Hoarder with no nerve for break-ins; he promises to return what he took. Ningguang tells the Traveler the festival theme is based on Sea Gazer.

Part II detail: To approach Cloud Retainer, Paimon improvises a fake drowning, which brings Xiao and Shenhe. Xiao says the person sought was not a yaksha. Shenhe tells them Cloud Retainer has gone to Mt. Hulao. At Guili Plains Cloud Retainer says Guizhong, another name of the God of Dust, loved gatherings and inventions; Streetward Rambler, whom Paimon recognises as Ping, argued with Guizhong about whether mechanisms could replace human composers. Ping and Cloud Retainer avoided Guili Plains after Guizhong died in the Archon War. Cloud Retainer reveals that Ping played her new melody to the clouds and that Dvorak's ancestor probably fell in during that performance. Ping tells the group she remembers the encounter, that the tune always reminds her of Guizhong, and that she has not fully moved on; she asks the group to give regards to Dvorak and promises to play it again after practice. Dvorak says the melody was an adeptus remembering her late friend, and is surprised she is now old. Hu Tao, Yun Jin and Xinyan are rehearsing the opening act at the funeral parlor, and Zhongli is on Mt. Hulao with Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper.

Epilogue detail: Xiao is nervous and Hu Tao lightens the mood; Venti appears unexpectedly; the Traveler takes Xiao outside to calm him; the group decides on Paimon as the distinguished guest. Xiao thanks the Traveler afterward.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay: Radiant Sparks (a time trial with blossoming flames), Paper Theater (a puppet-style story played alongside craftspeople), Vigilance at Sea (time trials on the Waverider designed by an Alcor navigator) and Behind the Scenes (helping the Ministry of Civil Affairs clear enemies). Festive Fever thresholds were 200, 400, 600, 800 and 1,000; the last gave a Crown of Insight. Requirements were Adventure Rank 28 and A New Star Approaches; the quick start was meant for players who had not done The Crane Returns on the Wind or Yelan's Umbrabilis Orchis Chapter Act I. These are game mechanics, not in-universe canon.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Madame Ping is Streetward Rambler and is the one who saved Dvorak's ancestor. She and Cloud Retainer speak openly of Guizhong, and Ping says Lantern Rite exists to commemorate heroes who gave their lives for Liyue, Guizhong among them. The tune was written for Guizhong's memory, and the Fontaine legend of a fairy lady is built on a real encounter.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Dvorak is the guest, a Fontaine musician and organizer of the Iridescence Tour. Ganyu, Keqing, Ningguang and Yelan help from the Qixing side. Cloud Retainer and Madame Ping carry the lore, and Xiao, Shenhe, Zhongli, Hu Tao, Yun Jin, Xinyan, Xiangling, Xingqiu, Chongyun and Venti appear in lighter scenes.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The Lantern Rite concert proceeds without problems and Dvorak finishes it. Dvorak intends to thank Ping in person after the concert. Madame Ping promises to play the melody another time.

## entry: Streetward Rambler's melody
@keys: Streetward Rambler, Madame Ping melody, Guizhong's tune
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In The Exquisite Night Chimes Cloud Retainer says Streetward Rambler composed her tune in memory of Guizhong, after earlier disagreeing with her about the meaning of music. Ping played it to the clouds and, by Cloud Retainer's account, only once in her hearing. Dvorak's ancestor heard it while nearly drowning.
