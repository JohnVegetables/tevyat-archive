## summary
### LITE
Of Ballads and Brews was a Version 3.1 flagship event (29 September to 17 October 2022) set during Mondstadt's autumn Weinlesefest. The main story follows Razor, the wolf-raised boy, after the Knights of Favonius pass him a box his parents left behind. It holds a bottle of Thousand-Wind Wine, Mondstadt's oldest and rarest style. With the Traveler and Paimon, and with help from Klee, Bennett, Rosaria, Noelle, Sucrose, Diluc, Diona, Lisa, Kaeya and Venti, Razor brews his own bottle, buries it at Windrise, and drinks a toast at the festival's Windcoming Day.
### STANDARD +
Beside the story the event ran harvest-season activities (boar expulsion, a Knights' charity stall, gift hampers). The story is warm and family-themed, reusing known characters in small supporting roles.

## plot
### STANDARD
Act I (Sounds From Afar). With the harvest season busy, the Traveler and Paimon finish Guild errands with Katheryne. Jean summons the Knights: a letter from Grand Master Varka, read aloud by Mika, says his expedition is going well, mentions that the Fatui Harbinger "the Captain" has helped rather than harmed them, and wishes everyone a good Weinlesefest. A private note for Lisa asks the Knights to give Razor a box his parents left. Told in Wolvendom, Razor is torn but decides to see his teacher Lisa. The box contains a woolen hand puppet, a part from a Ruin Guard and a bottle of Thousand-Wind Wine. Venti, consulted at the festival, can only offer a song and tells them to try brewing it themselves.

Act II (Bonds of Melody). Paimon and the Traveler read Venti's song as a clue: the wine should carry traits of the three organising institutions: an explorer's courage (Adventurers' Guild), a love tender and true (Church of Favonius) and a defender's will (Knights of Favonius). Klee, freed from confinement for the festival, joins to help Razor find his "mommy"; Bennett brings a Small Lamp Grass as the Guild's ingredient. After they rescue a fruit cart from hilichurls, Rosaria presents a Mist Flower Corolla for the Church and bluntly tells Razor that his parents' story may not be happy. At the Dawn Winery Diluc agrees, after Kaeya's persuasion, to supply a barrel.

Act III (Aroma of the Past). Noelle and Sucrose give the Knights' ingredient, a Sunsettia improved by Sucrose's bio-alchemy ("Quadruple-Sweetness Sunsettia"). At Angel's Share the group collects the barrel and Diona mixes the brew after a misunderstanding about Razor wanting to drink. Under the great tree at Windrise, Venti gives dandelion seeds as the last ingredient to capture the wind's memory and says Thousand-Wind Wine is the original dandelion wine, tasting different each time because people add what they like. They bury the barrel there; Razor promises to open it with everyone when he is grown. On Windcoming Day Venti sings "Thousand-Wind Wine"; the wind rises, and Razor, smelling his parents' bottle, sees a vision of two adventurers on a cliff, who decided to save it for their son and chose the name Razor.
### LARGE +
Act II includes Rosaria's reasoning that the town's friendly people are too "sunny and bright" to give Razor a darker, more realistic perspective, and Paimon's observation that she is more thoughtful than she looks. Act III has Klee anxious that the Anemo Archon has not come; Paimon reassures her that Barbatos can appear in many forms, and Razor says he feels the Archon's blessing because he has grown and is no longer afraid.
### ULTRA +
The three ingredient sources come together as a patchwork of gifts: Bennett's lamp grass, Rosaria's flower, Noelle and Sucrose's fruit and Venti's seeds. Bennett and Klee also bring wines borrowed from Bennett's fathers and Albedo as an offering, which they hope will persuade the Archon to help Razor's cause. The vision of Razor's parents is brief and silent about their identity beyond their roles as adventurers who found the wine incomplete.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Razor's parents left a box with a woolen puppet, a Ruin Guard part and a bottle of Thousand-Wind Wine, which the Knights have held for him. Thousand-Wind Wine is the first and rarest wine Mondstadt learned to make, and its character depends on whatever the brewer adds. A vision shows his parents as adventurers who called their wine unfinished and gave their son the name Razor. Details of their lives remain unknown.
### LARGE +
Varka's request about the box reaches Lisa as a message on the back of his letter to the Knights; he is away on his expedition. Rosaria's remark, as the wiki summarises it, treats Varka as a father figure to both her and Razor.

## characters
### STANDARD
Razor is the central figure; he is hesitant but warm. Klee and Bennett support him; Rosaria is blunt but kind; Lisa, his teacher, anchors the Knights' side; Noelle and Sucrose, Diluc, Diona and Kaeya provide the ingredients and logistics; Venti is the wine's guide and the singer of the closing song. Jean convenes the Knights and Mika reads Varka's letter.
### LARGE +
Marcel, a merchant, asks for help finding his fruit cart in Act II. Draff hunts with Razor in Wolvendom in Act I. Other phases feature Clemens and Leah, who are not part of the Razor storyline.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The bottle Razor brewed remains buried under Windrise's great tree, to be opened with his friends when he grows up. Razor now has his parents' bottle and a vision of them. No plot beyond the festival is affected.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.1, 29 September to 17 October 2022, with the gift collection open until 24 October. The requirements were Adventure Rank 21, the Prologue Archon Quest and the Lupus Minor Chapter's first act. The story is set during Weinlesefest in Mondstadt and refers to Varka's expedition as still under way.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Labelled game content: the four phases were Autumn Crisis (expelling and capturing boars, including the Great Snowboar King), Highwind Trial (battling with Sublime Anemograna), Charity and Creativity (running a Knights' stall) and Fecund Blessings (finding gift hampers). Festive Fever points earned the Missive Windspear, a four-star polearm, and its refinement material.

## entry: Thousand-Wind Wine

@keys2: Razor, Venti, Weinlesefest
@version: 3.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
According to the event, Thousand-Wind Wine is the first and rarest wine that Mondstadters learned to make, and the many techniques involved mean each brew tastes different. Venti says it is the original dandelion wine, and that people have always been free to add what they like. In the event Razor's version takes an explorer's courage, a love tender and true, a defender's will and, last, dandelion seeds meant to capture the wind's memory.
### LARGE +
The bottle left by Razor's parents is, in Lisa's reading, a bottle they brewed themselves.
