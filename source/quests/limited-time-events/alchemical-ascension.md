## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 4.5
### LITE
Alchemical Ascension was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 4.5 (14 March to 1 April 2024). Lisa, working for the Knights of Favonius, asked the Traveler and Paimon to help run a potion shop by Cider Lake, part of a plan to promote the achievements of alchemy to the public and gather their feedback. The story chapter is called Alchemy and Business Operations.
### STANDARD
The Acting Grand Master, Jean, wanted the Knights to show ordinary people what alchemy could do and to learn how they responded. Lisa, in charge of the effort, opened a potion shop and enlisted the Traveler and Paimon. They grew herbs, brewed potions, priced them to the market and sold them, with supplies handled by the Knight Freda of the Logistics Company. Familiar characters dropped by with requests, Sucrose set alchemy exams, and the venture grew into a Teyvat-wide trade. It ends with Lisa and Jean thanking the Traveler.
### LARGE +
The entry requirement was the Prologue Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom. The wiki recommends Masquerade of the Guilty and Eula's Story Quest Aphros Delos Chapter Act I first, for the best experience. The event is a management game with a light story attached.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
Act I, Potions Most Potent: at Knights of Favonius headquarters Lisa explains the plan and recruits the Traveler and Paimon. At the ready-made shop they brew a first potion, sell the first batch and learn to watch market trends and cultivate herbs. Lisa introduces Freda, who says the Acting Grand Master ordered her to support the business. Act II, A Breakthrough in Craft: orders pile up, Paimon suggests Lisa hire more help, and Lisa and Freda propose improving brewing, cultivation and sales. Act III, International Markets: the Sumeru merchant Atefeh arrives and offers to sell the shop's potions across Teyvat if they become her regular supplier. They agree. Epilogue, A Job Well Done: once enough revenue is earned, Jean visits to thank everyone.
### LARGE +
Beyond the acts, visitors arrive in sequence. Beidou and Eula appear together (they met at Dornman Port after a misunderstanding over an entry permit and then shared a drink); Diona and Venti look for cocktail ingredients for The Cat's Tail; Diluc arrives after Elzer of Dawn Winery briefed him on the shop's success; and Gorou and Kirara come on an errand, with Kirara advising Gorou as a consultant in exchange for his help promoting Komaniya Express. Sucrose arrives with alchemy exams at four levels named Nigredo Realm, Calx, Rubedo Imminent and Citrinitas Simulated.
### ULTRA +
In the epilogue Lisa says enough sales data has been collected for the Knights' research and invites the Traveler to keep running the shop if they like. Jean says she had already heard from other knights and wanted to thank the Traveler in person; she had worried Lisa, cooped up in the library, was bored and wanted to give her talents room. Lisa jokes about losing hair to stress and being due a vacation, and gives the Traveler a memento. Jean notes the public reaction was far better than expected and that potions could become a new revenue stream for Mondstadt. The epilogue triggers once revenue reaches 2,600,000 in cycles 24 to 29, or at the end of cycle 29. A merchant from Liyue named Deyou can be engaged through the Sales Expansion Contract; he says his trades keep losing money and he wants to prove himself to his father.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 4.5
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Nothing in the event changes the wider story. Its new information is small: according to Sucrose, Jean's plan, discussed with Albedo, was not only to sell potions but to teach the public the principles and practice of alchemy, and public understanding of alchemical potions had grown as a result. Beidou and Eula hit it off after the Dornman Port misunderstanding.
### LARGE +
Diluc tells the Traveler that Elzer only briefs him like this when he thinks the winery faces a real threat, and jokes that a business with full production and supply lines and orders from across Teyvat would be a rival in the wine trade. Business Correspondence letters from Kaeya, Candace, Lyney and Xingqiu also appear, though those characters do not appear in person.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
Lisa leads the project and Jean sponsors it; Freda is the event-exclusive Logistics Company knight who supplies it; Atefeh (Sumeru) and Deyou (Liyue) are event-exclusive merchants; Sucrose sets alchemy exams. Potion-request visitors are Beidou and Eula, Diona and Venti, Diluc, and Gorou and Kirara. Paimon and the Traveler run the shop.
### LARGE +
Freda is described as a knight who likes adventure, with brown hair, golden eyes and Knights of Favonius attire; she is excited to meet the heroes whose stories she has heard. Lisa calls the Traveler and Paimon her cuties and relies on them for the work. Gorou's visit shows that Watatsumi Island had no map of Mondstadt for him, so he had to scout on the job.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
The event leaves no lasting change to the main story. The Knights gain sales data for their alchemy research, Lisa says she is due a vacation, and Jean raises the possibility of potions as a new income source for Mondstadt. As gameplay rewards, the Traveler can earn the Polearm Dialogues of the Desert Sages and other items.

## placement

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 4.5
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. The event has three phases: Getting Started (from 14 March), Gaining Traction (from 16 March, after A Breakthrough in Craft) and Red Hot (from 18 March). Three systems drive it: Cultivating Ingredients, Concocting Potions and Selling Potions. The shop has fifteen Alchemy Levels, with upgrades such as cultivation area expansions and improved alchemy cauldrons bought from Freda. Operation Bonuses and shop decorations unlock later.
### ULTRA +
Rewards include 1,000 Primogems in total, a Crown of Insight and the Polearm Dialogues of the Desert Sages. The Artisans' Gift track pays out as revenue reaches thresholds from 50,000 to 2,000,000.
