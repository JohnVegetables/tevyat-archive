## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 5.3
### LITE
Springtime Charms was the Version 5.3 Lantern Rite flagship event (24 January to 9 February 2025). The Traveler and Paimon arrive in Liyue Harbor for the festival and find a creeping "fetor" of death sickening people, a crisis tied to an old disaster at Wuwang Hill. Hu Tao, Lan Yan of Chenyu Vale and the Liyue Qixing prepare a scaled-down Sanctification of Tao Dou, and the Traveler follows Hu Tao into the border between life and death to bring her back.
### STANDARD
Alongside the story the event offered four festival activities (Immortal Combat, Drills by Lamplight, Custom Gift Envelopes and Festive Travel Journal) whose Festive Fever points unlocked Xiangling's outfit "New Year's Cheer" and a choice of 4-star Liyue character. The narrative runs in four "stanzas" under the heading Swirling Butterflies. It ends warmly: Hu Tao lives, the fetor is contained, Yun Jin performs the rite as a stage piece, and the adepti gather over tea to talk about Hu Tao.
### LARGE +
The event places Hu Tao, Lan Yan, Xiao, Ningguang and Yelan at the centre, shows how Hu Tao's family history shapes her work as funeral director, and makes the Traveler's inability to be linked to the ley lines plot-relevant.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
First Stanza, Liyue Celebrates and Eight Adepts Face a Hidden Calamity: at Wangsheng Funeral Parlor Hu Tao (in a new outfit from Xiangling) says only that she is busy. The Traveler meets Lan Yan, a young woman from Chenyu Vale, and hears the legend of Tao Dou, a tree god of the Archon War. Several people who visited Qingce Village collapse, including Timaeus and the patriarch of the Feiyun Commerce Guild, and Baizhu suspects one cause for all of them.

Second Stanza, The Funeral Parlor Has No Master, Yujing Terrace Calls the Troops: Hu Tao has not returned and Qiqi is missing after gathering herbs, with Wuwang Hill the lead. With Xiao's help the Traveler breaks into the "border" and finds Hu Tao, Lan Yan and Qiqi. Back in Yujing Terrace it is revealed that Hu Tao and Ningguang plan a Sanctification of Tao Dou to purge the fetor.

Third Stanza, Qimen Arts and the Rite of Homa, the Spirits are Calmed and Life Restored: friends take positions at the gates of the array (Terrain at Dihua Marsh, Repose at Qingce Village, Initiation at Liyue Harbor, Death at Wuwang Hill, plus the Gate of Injury in the Chasm). Hu Tao herself must be the obscured person at the centre of the rite. The Traveler cannot be linked to the ley lines, goes after Hu Tao anyway, finds the Gate of Death and pulls her out of the border.

Final Stanza, The Sanctification of Tao Dou: with the crisis contained, Yun Jin performs the rite as a stage piece at Yilong Wharf, and short epilogue scenes follow.
### LARGE +
First Stanza detail: Hu Tao had been meeting An Su, a butler of the Feiyun Commerce Guild, and then slipped out; she asks the Ferrylady to follow up the funeral arrangements. An Su fears Lan Yan has cursed the patriarch because he saw her with a rattan figure like the one he later found in the patriarch's room. Chongyun, brought by Xingqiu as an exorcist, feels a cold presence but no malice and suspects a "fetor of death." Baizhu finds Timaeus and the patriarch afflicted alike, links the cases to Qingce Village and says he had sent Qiqi to gather herbs there. Lan Yan leaves a rattan doll behind in her rush after Hu Tao. Zhongli reads the Guyan Sigil on the doll as an upstanding, auspicious sign and gives the Traveler a Rex Lapis fortune coin, which he says is only a replica from a vendor.

Second Stanza detail: Gaming mentions delivering funeral goods to the Feiyun home at Yilong Wharf. At Wuwang Hill Yelan warns the pair away, shows them Qiqi's herb basket and takes them to Yiyan Temple, where Ningguang and Beidou are talking. Xiao is reluctant to involve the Traveler but relents, destroys the fetor and temporarily shares his senses. Inside the border the Traveler breaks Lan Yan's protective illusion, Hu Tao keeps the fetor-laden Qiqi awake by startling her, and Xiao leads everyone out and takes Qiqi to Bubu Pharmacy. Ningguang explains that the Qixing have found four of eight gates. Hu Tao tells the Traveler, at the parlor, that about a dozen years ago a border incident at Wuwang Hill killed her father when he slipped while using the Art of Homa and that her grandfather, who dealt with the aftermath, did not live to sixty. She shows a photograph of them. Lan Yan separately explains that her own grandfather's fortune-telling, disturbed by the fetor, pointed to the funeral parlor.

Third Stanza detail: Lan Yan's cousin in the main family branch finds no definitive answer on why the central person must be obscured, but suspects a rule of the mountains and land. Her workaround is rattan Hu Tao figures linked to the ley lines, held by those who know the truth, in the hope that the fetor blast aimed at Hu Tao is spread among them. Hu Tao brings the Traveler along to hand out fortune coins, quietly gives the coin to Zhongli to return, and tells him that if she fails to come back he would be the 78th Director; he declines. When the rite runs, the figures are overwhelmed, Hu Tao completes it and her doll darkens. In the border she reaches a copy of the parlor where her father and grandfather greet her but say her time has not come; a plum branch falls into her hand before the Traveler pulls her out. The Traveler wakes two days later at Bubu Pharmacy; Hu Tao thanks them for saving her life.

Final Stanza detail: Xiao tells Yelan he should have taken the assignment, and she replies that she was posted at the Chasm and would have been a liability. Beidou and Ningguang talk at Yiyan Temple. Keqing tells Ganyu that Xianyun and the other adepti most likely left the anonymous information the Qixing used. At Mt. Aocang, Cloud Retainer, Zhongli and other adepti gather for tea and talk about Hu Tao.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay: Immortal Combat (rattan figures duelling with falling blocks), Drills by Lamplight (a team drill using Secret Stratagems), Custom Gift Envelopes (filling envelopes to companions' wishes, tied to seven world quests numbered 1 to 7) and Festive Travel Journal (visiting festive toy sites around Liyue Harbor). Festive Fever thresholds were 300, 600, 900, 1,200 (invite a character in Lantern-Lit Gathering) and 1,500 (Xiangling's outfit and a Crown of Insight). Selectable characters were Beidou, Chongyun, Gaming, Lan Yan, Ningguang, Xiangling, Xingqiu, Xinyan, Yanfei, Yaoyao and Yun Jin. The outfit was later sold in the outfit shop. These are game mechanics, not in-universe canon.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
The fetor sickening Liyue is connected to the earlier Wuwang Hill disaster, in which Hu Tao's father died using the Art of Homa. The Sanctification of Tao Dou is traditionally believed to require a death, because the Lone Butterfly sacrificed herself in the original rite; Hu Tao argues a scaled-down version only needs the central person to withstand a huge blast of fetor. The Traveler is invisible to the ley lines, which is why the Traveler cannot be the obscured one. No Abyss involvement is sensed.
### LARGE +
Hu Tao concealed the connection to Wuwang Hill from Ningguang and the Qixing, and Lan Yan suspected her of keeping the truth bottled up. Hu Tao expected she might die and said so to Zhongli.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
Hu Tao is the focus, shown at her most serious beneath her jokes. Lan Yan, a Chenyu Vale rattan-figure maker, is introduced here. Ningguang and Keqing coordinate the Qixing side; Baizhu and Qiqi handle the sick; Xiao and Yelan guard borders and gates; Zhongli quietly supports Hu Tao and the Traveler. Xiangling, Xingqiu, Chongyun, Gaming, Yun Jin, Ganyu, Xianyun and Beidou have smaller roles.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
Hu Tao survives, the fetor is contained and the sick recover. Ningguang's worry that a greater power may be behind the fetor is left open. Hu Tao's friends see how heavily her family history weighs on her.

## entry: Sanctification of Tao Dou
@keys: Sanctification of Tao Dou, Seven-and-Eight-Gates Array, Gate of Death
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Springtime Charms the Sanctification of Tao Dou is a rite to purge fetor, built on a Seven-and-Eight-Gates Array. The array master picks one of eight gates to subdue the evil by using the ley lines to obfuscate themselves, removing themselves from the lines and carrying the evil with them. The traditional reading is that the person dies, because the Lone Butterfly sacrificed herself; Hu Tao leads a scaled-down version in which she must withstand a huge blast of fetor.

## entry: Lan Yan
@keys: Lan Yan, rattan figure, rattan doll
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Lan Yan is a young woman from Chenyu Vale who makes rattan figures. In Springtime Charms her grandfather's divination was disturbed by the fetor, and she seeks out Hu Tao. With help from a cousin in the main family branch she links Hu Tao's rattan dolls to the ley lines.
