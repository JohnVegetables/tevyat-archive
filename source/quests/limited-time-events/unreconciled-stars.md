## summary
### LITE
Unreconciled Stars was a Version 1.1 flagship event (16-30 November 2020, with the final act released on 26 November). Meteorites fell across Mondstadt and Liyue, and anyone who touched one fell into a deep sleep, dreaming of climbing a freezing mountain. The Adventurers' Guild sends the Traveler and Paimon out with the self-styled "Prinzessin der Verurteilung" Fischl and her raven Oz; they later gain the astrologist Mona. The trail leads to Leonard, an adventurer from about two thousand years ago who never climbed Pilos Peak, and to a Fatui Harbinger who is also hunting the meteorites.
### STANDARD +
The event is mostly an investigation story told with Fischl's theatrical speech and Oz's patient translation. It was limited-time: Fischl was the event's reward character, and the story is canon but not common knowledge for characters who were not present.

## plot
### STANDARD
Act I (Unknown Star). Katheryne briefs the Traveler and Paimon: a meteor shower covered the countryside of Mondstadt and Liyue, and anyone who touches a meteorite sinks into sleep. Fischl and Oz are assigned as partners, since the Guild advises adventurers to travel in pairs. At Springvale the victims mumble about cold and rest, one remembers a snowy mountain path, and a villager asks for help finding a boy who ran to the nearest impact site. Fischl's Vision glows near the meteorite shards; clearing the shards wakes some sleepers, so the group concludes the meteorites are the cause.

Act II (The Crisis Deepens). Meteorites keep falling faster than they can be cleared. The search for a missing Dawn Winery delivery worker takes the party to Liyue, where the Millelith have turned Wangshu Inn into a relief station. A wandering Inazuman swordsman helps them briefly and leaves. At the inn Fatui skirmishers appear at the last meteorite and warn that a Harbinger is nearby. Fischl declares the sleep a curse and asks for someone expert in undoing curses; Katheryne names the travelling astrologist Mona.

Act III (What the Skies Conceal, the Water Reveals). Mona's hydromancy shows that the meteorites came from someone's constellation. At Qingce Village the party learns that the sleepers all dream of an enormous snowy mountain and feel compelled to climb it, as though the wish were put in their heads. Mona reads the name "Leonard". The Inazuman stranger returns and Mona abruptly teleports everyone away; the stranger is revealed to be the Fatui Harbinger Scaramouche, who had hoped to strike down the Traveler and orders his soldiers to keep researching the meteorites.

Act IV (Where Ancient Stars Align). Fischl and Oz step back to tend the growing number of sleepers. A book in the Mondstadt Library, "Of Mountains and Seas", written by Leonard, is half illegible until Mona reconstructs it: Leonard lived about two thousand years ago and never reached the top of Pilos Peak. Mona reasons that the mountain was levelled when Barbatos cleared the ice and is now Musk Reef. The party finds Scaramouche there, shaken, and he leaves after announcing that the stars are a lie. After fighting off his Fatui, they destroy the meteorite core, and Leonard's spirit speaks one last time before the sleepers can wake.
### LARGE +
Fischl's guesses run ahead of the evidence throughout: she names a mountain demon's curse in Act I, a cold mountain in Act I and II, and a "curse of unanswered longing" in Act II. Paimon and Mona later wryly conclude she may use the wrong method but reach the right answer. Mona's reading in Act III is deliberately incomplete: she gets the name Leonard first and the context only from the book.
### ULTRA +
In Act I Paimon points out that the Guild's "travel in pairs" advice already describes a party of four. In Act II the Dawn Winery workers Ben and Fritz are separated; Ben is rescued from monsters, and Fritz, whose cart was hit by a meteorite on the road between Dihua Marsh and Stone Gate, is found asleep by the Inazuman stranger and the Millelith. The stranger tells the party he is "a vagrant from Inazuma" and leaves quickly when the Millelith arrive. In Act III the party tracks sleepers' dream details at Qingce Village: a patient who wakes demands home cooking (a Qingce Stir Fry) before telling them about the dream.

## revelations
### STANDARD
The meteorites are fragments of Leonard's constellation, brought down from the sky, and the sleepers dream of climbing the mountain he failed to climb. Leonard was an adventurer who died without summiting Pilos Peak and instead put his efforts into designs for flying devices, the "Wings of Incompletion". The meteorites radiate elemental energy that lulls people into a hypnosis-like sleep, and a core meteorite contains a crystal holding his spirit. Pilos Peak no longer exists as a mountain: it is the small island Musk Reef.
### LARGE +
A Fatui Harbinger is investigating the meteorites. He walks out of the shared dream on his own strength and says he has found that the stars and sky are a gigantic hoax. Mona, who was taught to speak of a "false sky" in astrology, takes the claim seriously rather than dismissing it.
### ULTRA +
In his last words Leonard says that where legs fail, tools might carry people, and where tools fail, wings might, and that "against the unknown, humanity stands as one". The event itself closes by tying this to gliding: Paimon remarks that the people of Mondstadt can now glide, and that anyone can now reach the former peak by boat.

## characters
### STANDARD
Fischl and Oz lead the investigation by volume rather than method; Oz translates and keeps the party grounded. Mona supplies the astrology and an ego to match. Paimon asks the questions; the Traveler does the legwork. Katheryne dispatches them. The Harbinger Scaramouche (disguised as an Inazuman vagrant in Acts II and III) is the antagonist. Leonard appears only as a spirit in a cutscene.
### LARGE +
Ben, Fritz, Adelinde and Draff appear as Dawn Winery and Springvale locals. Millelith officers staff the relief stations at Wangshu Inn and Qingce Village. The event also names Edna, Joyce, Adler, Allan and Hartman among the Springvale villagers.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The victims wake when the core is destroyed. Fischl's event reward quest (the "Prinzessin's Pact") makes her obtainable. The Harbinger leaves Musk Reef after saying the sky is a hoax; the event does not follow up on his claim.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.1 on 16 November 2020 and available until 30 November 2020 (final act released 26 November). It follows the Prologue Archon Quest (Mondstadt chapter) in release order and has no formal story prerequisite beyond Adventure Rank 20. In story terms it takes place with Scaramouche still a serving Harbinger; the event states no exact date relative to the Prologue.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Labelled game content, not story: players collected meteorite shards for Fading Star's Might, cleared meteorite remains in Meteorite Strike Zones, and ran the Star of Destiny "Fallen Star" challenge for Fading Star's Essence. Three optional "Meteoric Wave" quests followed each of the first three phases.

## entry: Leonard and Pilos Peak
@keys: Leonard, Pilos Peak, Wings of Incompletion, Of Mountains and Seas
@spoiler: minor
@version: 1.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the Unreconciled Stars event Mona reconstructs a damaged book, "Of Mountains and Seas", by Leonard, an adventurer who lived about two thousand years ago. By forty he had conquered every domain of the world except Pilos Peak, which defeated him. Near the end of his life he put his hope into flying devices he called the Wings of Incompletion. Pilos Peak itself no longer exists above the sea; it is the small island of Musk Reef, which Mona matches to the legend that Barbatos cleared the ice and snow and reshaped Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
The Mondstadt Library holds the book; the event connects Leonard to gliding only through Paimon's closing remark, not through any stated history of the glider.

## entry: Scaramouche and the stars are a lie
@keys: Scaramouche, Balladeer
@keys2: meteorite, Mondstadt, Fischl, Mona
@spoiler: minor
@version: 1.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the event the Sixth Fatui Harbinger, Scaramouche (codename the Balladeer), first poses as a vagrant from Inazuma, then commands Fatui soldiers to keep researching the meteorites. He had planned to strike down the Traveler during the event. At Musk Reef he has been through the shared dream and says the stars and sky are a hoax, mentions "the Jester" with irritation, and leaves. Later canon identifies the Wanderer as this same person after he erased his identity from Irminsul (Version 3.3 playable); this event itself stays in Scaramouche's Fatui period.

### STANDARD
Fischl introduces herself to the Traveler and Paimon in Act I as Prinzessin der Verurteilung from the Immernachtreich, with the raven Oz as her familiar. Oz openly translates her style ("Mein Fräulein means to say: hello"). By Act IV she steps aside to tend sleepers and tells the Traveler, out of her usual style, that she enjoyed their conversations, since most Guild adventurers only talk to her for Oz's scouting reports. Her character details belong in her own entry.
