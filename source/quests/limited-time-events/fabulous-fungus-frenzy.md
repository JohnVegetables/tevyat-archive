## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 3.2
### LITE
Fabulous Fungus Frenzy was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 3.2 (10 to 28 November 2022). In Sumeru the Traveler and Paimon enter the Nilotpala Cup Beast Tamers Tournament, where contestants capture Fungi, boost them with Floral Jelly and command them through Wisdom Orbs. Behind the friendly competition sits a scheme involving the organiser Kautilya and the sponsor Elchingen, and a beast tamer named Haniyyah who wants to destroy all Fungi.
### STANDARD
The event opens at Sumeru City, where a bulletin board advertises the first Nilotpala Cup, held at Port Ormos with the Amurta researcher Kautilya as organiser and the Fontaine businessman Elchingen as sponsor (sixty million Mora in prizes). The Traveler and Paimon sign up partly to keep an eye on the Fungi, learn from the guide Haniyyah, befriend Layla and meet Yae Miko, who has come with a writer from Yae Publishing House. As the rounds go on, the Traveler switches off a Wisdom Orb, finds that a Fungus still follows them freely, learns the tournament hides a deception, and in the finale exposes Elchingen's plot. The event ends with the tournament paused and Haniyyah caring for the remaining Fungi.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest A New Star Approaches; Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and Yae Miko's Story Quest The Great Narukami Offering were recommended, with a Quick Start for those who had skipped them. The event is set while Sumeru is settling after the Archon Quest, and shows Fungi as something other than hostile monsters.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
Act I, The Most Fantastic Tournament You've Never Heard Of?: Paimon worries about the Fungi poster and the pair go to Port Ormos. Kautilya announces the cup and Elchingen's offer: whoever passes his Ultimate Trial inherits his fortune and must keep hosting the tournament. The Traveler signs up and accepts help from Haniyyah, a former Eremite beast tamer who tested the Wisdom Orb. They capture four Fungi, including a Floating Hydro Fungus that Paimon names Bongo-Head, and help the sleepy Rtawahist student Layla and her Fungus Twirly-Whirly gather her team. Yae Miko appears, entered with her own Whirling Electro Fungus, Blitzara, and warns of "potential liabilities". Act II, Nilotpala Cup: Debut Match: after the first wins, Layla wonders whether Fungi can be tamed without the orb; they switch it off and the Fungi stay peaceful. Haniyyah saves two children from wild Fungi and reveals she wants to win and wipe out Fungi, including her own partner. Act III, Dual Missions: Progress and Probe!: the Traveler investigates the Floral Jelly formula and meets the "Almighty Merchant", who is Dori. Act IV, Unfathomable Defenses: Haniyyah beats Miko in the semifinal; Miko takes the Traveler's gathered evidence; Haniyyah explains why she hates Fungi and gives the Traveler her notebook. They learn Kautilya's setup is a deception. Act V, The Strongest Opponent! The Biggest Crisis!: the Traveler beats Haniyyah's team in the final, then Elchingen leads them to a hidden area where Fatui Skirmishers wait. Act VI, Farewell, My Shroom Buddies: Miko leaves Blitzara in the Traveler's care, and Haniyyah leaves with the remaining orbs and Fungi.
### LARGE
Act I, The Most Fantastic Tournament You've Never Heard Of?. The event notice at Sumeru City's southeast entrance promises the first Nilotpala Cup Beast Tamers Tournament. At Port Ormos, the rules say contestants may not use weapons or fight each other directly; Fungi registered to a Wisdom Orb may move around the arena and the main roads of the port but not Wikala Funduq, shops or restaurants, since many locals do not know about the tournament. The Corps of Thirty referees, and gross violations can bring Akademiya punishment. Kautilya, a senior Amurta researcher, promises the orb uses Amurta's "Intellectual Boost" technology and is absolutely safe. Elchingen's prize fund is sixty million Mora, with his whole fortune offered to whoever passes the Ultimate Trial if they take over hosting. Haniyyah, who tested the orb early on and worked among the Eremites taming Sumpter Beasts, teaches the Traveler to capture Fungi; her partner is a Pyro Fungus she calls Pyro-1. Paimon names the Hydro Fungus Bongo-Head. Layla, collapsing from exhaustion because she cannot sleep enough, hopes to reach the quarterfinals, since quarter-finalists may take home Fungi of their choice. Yae Miko explains that Yae Publishing House brought its writer Souka for inspiration for a novel on friendship and rivalry; Miko enters the tournament herself.

Act II, Nilotpala Cup: Debut Match. The Traveler beats Ajilenakh Dash's team; Layla and Miko also win. Miko leads them to a "training" session that turns out to be time spent bonding with their Fungi. Layla suggests Fungi can be tamed; switching off the orb makes Twirly-Whirly and Bongo-Head wander peacefully. Cries for help lead them to two children threatened by wild Fungi; Haniyyah drives the Fungi away, then says she plans to win the tournament and destroy every Fungus. A Corps of Thirty member is seen nearby, which the Traveler takes as a sign that Cyno is watching. Souka is the next opponent.

Act III, Dual Missions: Progress and Probe!. The Traveler defeats Souka. Layla has a meeting with the Almighty Merchant and Miko hands that task to the Traveler. At sunrise they photograph the Floral Jelly formula and meet the merchant, Dori, who gives them discarded scraps and agrees to ship Layla's order. They then beat Baron Bigfoot; Layla also wins, and Paimon begins gathering evidence for Miko.

Act IV, Unfathomable Defenses. The Traveler beats Hardy Two-Blades, whose rough handling of his Fungi upsets Paimon. Miko loses the semifinal to Haniyyah, so the Traveler faces Layla, who plays fair and loses, and then Haniyyah. Miko gives the Traveler a gohei blessing after receiving the evidence. The Traveler tracks Haniyyah with Elemental Sight; she tells why she hates Fungi and gives them her notebook. They learn Kautilya's setup is a deception and that Bongo-Head follows them of its own accord, since its orb was off.

Act V, The Strongest Opponent! The Biggest Crisis!. The Traveler defeats Haniyyah's team, with Pyro-1 holding back, and asks to keep Pyro-1. Elchingen then takes them to a hidden area where Fatui Skirmishers wait and uses the Wisdom Orb's backdoor command to seize Twirly-Whirly, Blitzara and Pyro-1. Bongo-Head, which was not controlled, repels them; Miko, Layla and Haniyyah arrive and the control is broken. Elchingen flees but Sido and the Corps of Thirty arrest him and Kautilya. Haniyyah apologises.

Act VI, Farewell, My Shroom Buddies. The Traveler and Layla see Miko off; she leaves Blitzara in their care. Haniyyah, who has taken the remaining orbs and Fungi, has renamed Pyro-1 as Kindlejoy, admits taming it brought her joy, and leaves to care for them in a place she found. Afterwards the Fatui agent Yurochka can be fought near Chatrakam Cave.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler's team in the final was Bongo-Head, Twirly-Whirly, Blitzara and one Fungus of the player's choice. Ten kinds of Fungi could be awakened during play: Floating Hydro, Dendro and Anemo; Whirling Electro, Cryo and Pyro; and Stretchy Pyro, Anemo, Geo and Electro. The in-world bulletin board reports thirty-two contestants after the preliminaries and sixteen in the next phase, with audience comments debating a points system and a "Floating Fungi Protection Association".

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Elchingen, the quiet Fontaine tycoon, is revealed as a Fatuus working for Il Dottore, who wants a Fungi army to cause havoc in Sumeru. The Wisdom Orb has a hidden backdoor command, and the rules disclaimers mention possible negative effects on the body. Kautilya's role as organiser is described by the Traveler's group as a deception, and the wiki summary says Elchingen throws him under the bus. Gentler Fungi follow people who treat them well, even without an orb.
### LARGE +
Haniyyah's hatred of Fungi comes from terrible stories she grew up with, and Paimon argues she still felt compassion for her partner.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
Elchingen and Kautilya are taken away by the Corps of Thirty to face judgement. The tournament is put on hold until it can be run safely, with Haniyyah taking care of the Fungi, and the Traveler and Paimon, having technically passed the Ultimate Trial, are treated as hosts. Yurochka remains as a loose end near Chatrakam Cave.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.2 as a Flagship Event, set after the Sumeru Archon Quest. Event quests are not part of the main storyline, and many in-world people did not know about the tournament, as its own rules state.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 3.2
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Three modes unlocked in sequence: Fungus Capture, Coruscating Potential and Special Training, plus a Championship Progress tab. Mushroom Currency and Fungus Medals bought rewards such as the Nilotpala Cup Tournament Stage, a Wisdom Orb model, Crown of Insight, materials and Mora; both currencies vanished when the event period ended, and the shop stayed open a week longer.

## entry: Nilotpala Cup
@keys: Nilotpala Cup, Beast Tamers Tournament, Nilotpala Cup Beast Tamers Tournament
@version: 3.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A Fungi-commanding tournament held at Port Ormos. Contestants capture four Fungi, give them Floral Jelly and use a Wisdom Orb; weapons and direct fighting between tamers are banned, and the Corps of Thirty referees. The top eight win Mora, a title and the right to keep their ace Fungus.
### LARGE +
It was initially unknown to many locals, hence the restrictions on bringing Fungi into Wikala Funduq, shops and restaurants.

## entry: Wisdom Orb
@keys: Wisdom Orb
@keys2: Fungi, Fungus, Nilotpala Cup, Kautilya
@version: 3.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A device developed for the tournament, using Amurta's "Intellectual Boost" technology, that registers and commands a Fungus. It has a hidden backdoor command that Elchingen used against the Traveler's Fungi; Bongo-Head, whose orb the Traveler had switched off, was unaffected.

## entry: Haniyyah
@keys: Haniyyah

### STANDARD
A former Eremite beast tamer who tested the Wisdom Orb and guides the Traveler. She calls herself an illiterate nobody and plans to win in order to destroy all Fungi, including her partner Pyro-1. After the final she apologises and cares for the surviving Fungi, renaming Pyro-1 Kindlejoy.
