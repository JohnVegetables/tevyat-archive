## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Sunspray Summer Resort is a Natlan story quest released in Version 5.8 alongside a limited-time flagship event (30 July to 18 August 2025). The Traveler and Paimon holiday at the new Easybreeze Holiday Resort with Bennett, Mualani and Chasca, later joined by Venti. During the holiday Bennett meets the spirits of his birth parents.
### STANDARD +
The quest has four parts: To a Carefree Vacation!, The World Is Your Canvas!, The Surprise That Awaits Us All! and A Curtain Call for a Colorful Summer Night! The event offered four gameplay modes and rewards such as Bennett's outfit Adventures in Blazing Hue and the claymore Flame-Forged Insight. Unusually, the story quest stays playable after the event ended.
### LARGE +
The tone is light holiday comedy until Part III, which turns to Bennett's origins as the child of a retired Natlan adventurer team.
### ULTRA +
The story assumes the Traveler's heroism in Natlan as shared background (Incandescent Ode of Resurrection is recommended) and spends little time on the Archon plot.

## plot
@availability: limited
### LITE
Bennett, Mualani, Chasca and the Traveler test the new resort. After rest, painting and challenges, a blurred photograph leads Bennett to an Abyssal domain, where Venti helps him reach the spirits of his parents. A party on Tete Isle and a quiet farewell follow.
### STANDARD +
**Part I, To a Carefree Vacation!** The Traveler and Paimon receive a letter inviting them to the resort's trial operation, with food, lodging and entertainment covered. Bennett, whose luck has unexpectedly improved, greets them with a friendly Asha companion. Mualani and Chasca arrive by sea. The group tries the Dreambow Drift, buys candles for a "sun" offering at a market stall, eats at Huha Hill and holds a bonfire ritual in which each makes a wish. Bennett wishes the land healing and his dads in Mondstadt health.

**Part II, The World Is Your Canvas!** The group tackles three areas of the resort (Colorfall Cliffs, Guiztli Ridge and Wavey Bay), then visits Tete Isle, where the Traveler acts as DJ on a dance-off stage Xilonen designed and the group colours the stage with the Asha.
### LARGE +
**Part III, The Surprise That Awaits Us All!** After the challenges the group collects a prize, then finds the album's photos have blurred and the last shows four unknown people in tribal outfits. Bennett names them as Tecpatl's Flintblades, a famed adventurer team. A rift leads to a fiery domain with an Abyssal presence where a voice mocks Bennett. Venti shields the others, and a candlelit prayer brings a memory and then the spirits of Bennett's parents, Aroni and Macani. They speak outside the domain, then join the grand opening of Tete Isle, where Mavuika appears on stage.

**Part IV, A Curtain Call for a Colorful Summer Night!** The group paints their part of the resort and takes a photograph. Bennett decides to stay in Mondstadt. Venti then tells the Traveler about Istaroth and the Mare Jivari.

## revelations
@version: 5.8
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Bennett was born in Natlan to a retired adventurer couple, and the spirits of his parents are now Asha.
### STANDARD +
His parents, Aroni and Macani, belonged to Tecpatl's Flintblades. When Abyssal monsters attacked the team in the Mare Jivari, his mother stayed behind to save him. The Mondstadt adventurers who raised him gave him the name Bennett, which means "blessed". The parents lost their memories as Asha, and the album's photos were taken by Aroni.
### LARGE +
Mavuika tells Bennett the domain was an Abyssal simulation of the Mare Jivari. Venti says that 500 years ago he blew the Mare Jivari into another timeline to stop corruption spreading, making it the "Windless Land". Some souls could not escape, and others merged with Monetoo to become Asha. Mavuika opened a rift between the simulated and real Mare Jivari that let him bring them back.
### ULTRA +
Venti says he received part of Istaroth's power and authority when he met her at the Thousand Winds Temple, and ties the Mare Jivari's corruption to Time itself. He has brought memories of the dead to Bennett before. Bennett's luck will worsen again far from Natlan's ley lines, but he feels the holiday has readied him for it.

## characters
@availability: limited
### LITE
Traveler, Paimon, Bennett, Mualani, Chasca and Venti.
### STANDARD +
Also Mavuika, Rhythmos, resort staff such as the candle seller Nataba and Ukucha (who gives out the challenge prize), the Asha, and Bennett's parents Aroni and Macani. Ororon and Ifa are at the resort checking on the health of saurian staff and aphids, and Xilonen designed the stage.

## consequences
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Bennett makes peace with his past and chooses to return to Mondstadt. The group takes a commemorative picture with the cry "Lucky star!"
### LARGE +
The quest adds to what is known of the Mare Jivari and the link between Asha and lost souls.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.8 on 30 July 2025 (UTC+8) as a flagship event with timed phases. The event required Adventure Rank 18 and the Prologue Act III. After the event ended the story quest remained available at the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. It does not appear in the Archive's Story Quests list.

## entry: Easybreeze Holiday Resort
@keys: Easybreeze Holiday Resort
@version: 5.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A newly opened holiday resort in Natlan staffed by locals, with Asha as its mascots. It held a trial operation and invited the Traveler. Areas include Huha Hill, Colorfall Cliffs, Guiztli Ridge, Wavey Bay and Tete Isle.

## entry: Bennett's parents
@keys: Aroni, Macani, Tecpatl's Flintblades
@version: 5.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aroni and Macani belonged to Tecpatl's Flintblades, a Natlan adventurer team that retired when two members had a baby. After Abyssal monsters attacked in the Mare Jivari, Aroni stayed behind to save the child. Both later lost their memories as Asha. In Part III they briefly appear to Bennett and say they are proud of him.
