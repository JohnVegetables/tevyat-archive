## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
### LITE
The Chalk Prince and the Dragon was a limited-time Version 1.2 event (23 December 2020 to 5 January 2021) set on and around Dragonspine. Sucrose asks the Traveler and Paimon to find out what secret Albedo is hiding. It turns out to be a huge cursed sword that only the Traveler can wield safely, and Albedo wants it used in battle so it can "grow". Treasure Hoarders and then the Fatui come after it.
### STANDARD
Albedo's explanation is that the blade contains the ground-up remains of the black dragon Durin, whose corrosive venom curses it and drives any ordinary wielder mad; the Traveler's ability to purify corruption (as with Dvalin's tears) makes them immune. Over five short acts the sword absorbs power from defeated enemies and from a ley line disorder on the mountain, the Fatui try to seize it for Signora, and a dead Cryo Regisvine is revived by the released life force. In the end Albedo draws the purified life force out of the sword, bottles it, and lets the Traveler keep the blade.
### LARGE +
The event is a character-flavoured side story for Albedo rather than part of the main plot. It shows his curiosity, his habit of withholding details, and his faint private unease about Durin's origin, and it gives a first look at Sucrose acting on her own initiative.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Act I, Festering Desire: Back from Dragonspine, the Traveler and Paimon meet Sucrose at the adventurer camp. She believes Albedo is hiding a great alchemical secret from her and Timaeus and asks them to investigate. Albedo, rather than hiding anything, hands the Traveler a huge sword and asks them to use it in combat; he says it is cursed and only the Traveler is immune. Treasure Hoarders chase them to reclaim it. Albedo explains how he identified the sword and its link to Durin.

Act II, Archon's Rival: The sword visibly grows with use. Investigating a Treasure Hoarder camp, the group is attacked by Fatui Skirmishers as well, and Albedo concludes the Fatui are after the sword because they collect things connected to archons. He finds a ley line disorder where monsters are unusually strong, and the sword resonates with Durin's remains through the fallen enemies.

Act III, Raiders Amidst Snow Mist: Cyrus of the Adventurers' Guild stops by Albedo's camp, and Albedo keeps the sword from him. A hilichurl steals Albedo's sketchbook; the Traveler and Albedo glide across the mountain's cavern to a hilichurl camp and recover it, a little damaged. Albedo notes the mountain's monsters have been strengthened by Durin's remains.

Act IV, Uninvited Guests: Sucrose finds the Traveler, learns about the sword and asks to see it used in battle. They find the half-frozen adventurer Reckless Pallad, whom she had previously rescued, and a Fatui group led by someone named Vadim demands the sword for Signora. Albedo arrives, the Fatui are beaten and the sword grows again. When Pallad stumbles into the Traveler and knocks the sword to the ground, released life force revives a withered Cryo Regisvine, which the party must defeat.

Act V, Afterword: Albedo thanks the Traveler, checks that they feel no side effects, and extracts the purified life force from the sword into a bottle. He tells the Traveler to keep the sword as a memento, and when alone muses about Rhinedottir and Durin.
### LARGE +
Albedo says the Knights seized the sword from a Treasure Hoarder smuggling gang and no one claimed it. He found it in storage and traced it to a smith who forged it with ground Durin eyes, claws and scales; the smith probably used it in celebration, took in the venom through the hilt and went mad before dying somewhere unknown. In Act III he says he could answer the Traveler's questions but would summarise some topics. In Act IV he tells the Traveler off for letting Sucrose into the secret, then admits she could not have been kept in the dark for long.
### ULTRA +
Small details by act. Act I: Albedo ties the sword to Durin by recounting how Barbatos and Dvalin defeated the black dragon, who fell on this snow-locked mountain. Act II: Treasure Hoarders and Fatui both attack inside the hoarders' own camp, and the Fatui turn out to have been drawn by the sword rather than by the hoarders. Act III: Albedo tells Cyrus he came to gather hilichurl data with the Traveler as his assistant, and says the sketchbook's pages froze and some tore; he would have redrawn it if unsalvageable, though the Traveler argues the memories matter. Act IV: Sucrose arrives with exactly "five hours and twenty-six minutes" of free time, and later takes the shivering Pallad to safety during the Fatui fight.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Albedo's central claim is that the sword is a living-like weapon: it absorbs energy from what it kills and grows. The Traveler's purification protects them from Durin's venom, and when the venom is purified the pure life force is strong enough to revive dead plants and make them stronger. Albedo guesses that Durin's remains still influence the monsters of Dragonspine and may be resonating with the sword, and that Dragonspine's never-melting snow was perhaps chosen as a grave to seal Durin's poison (his own supposition). The Fatui interest in the sword is explained by their pursuit of archon-related things, since Durin was Barbatos' sworn enemy with power comparable to his.
### LARGE +
In the Afterword, Albedo says the dragon's life force resonates with him "because I am me" and asks Rhinedottir whether Durin is her creation. These lines link him to his master and to Durin's artificial origin without stating it outright in this event.
### ULTRA +
Albedo also tells the Traveler during Act I that the sword is "still very weak" and that, in a sense, it could be called a living thing. He is confident enough in his hypothesis to hand a dangerous weapon to the Traveler, noting the Traveler's earlier purification of Dvalin's tears is a very rare ability.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Albedo (research-minded Chief Alchemist of the Knights; gives the Traveler the sword), Sucrose (curious assistant who starts the investigation and later helps), the Traveler and Paimon. Minor figures: Cyrus (Adventurers' Guild branch master, visits in Act III), Reckless Pallad (hotheaded adventurer), Treasure Hoarders and Fatui Skirmishers, and a Fatui called Vadim who names Signora as the intended recipient. The reborn Durin and Dvalin are only referenced.
### LARGE +
Timaeus is mentioned as having left depressed that Albedo grasps data faster than he does, and Albedo calls Sucrose natural alchemist material.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Albedo ends the experiment satisfied and gives the sword to the Traveler. The event had no lasting effect on the main plot; the Fatui interest in Durin-related power returns in the Dragonspine and Albedo story content, and the sword was the event's exclusive 4-star weapon, Festering Desire, whose special effects worked only in the Dragonspine region.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.2 as a Flagship Event, after the Prologue and Albedo's Princeps Cretaceus Chapter Act I (Traveler Observation Report), with Adventure Rank 20. It is self-contained and does not depend on later Archon Quests.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Each act unlocked a challenge mode: Testing Grounds, Draconic Pilgrimage and Frostborn Miracle (a fight with the revived Cryo Regisvine), earning Glimmering, Warm and Miraculous Essences and refining the weapon Festering Desire, which gave bonuses in Dragonspine during the event. Shops sold materials, Hero's Wit and Crown of Insight.

## entry: Festering Desire (the sword)
@keys: Festering Desire
@version: 1.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A large cursed blade given to the Traveler by Albedo in the event. By his account it was made by a lost smith with the ground eyes, claws and scales of the dragon Durin; its venom drives ordinary wielders mad, but the Traveler's purification makes them immune. It absorbs energy from defeated foes and grows, and at the end Albedo extracts its purified life force and returns it.
### LARGE +
Its power leaked when it hit the ground in Act IV, reviving a Cryo Regisvine. Mechanically, the weapon's special effects applied only in Dragonspine during the event.

## entry: Dragonspine ley line disorder
@keys: ley line disorder, Durin's remains
@keys2: Albedo, Dragonspine, sword
@version: 1.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Act II Albedo discovers an area on Dragonspine where the ley lines flow abnormally and attract monsters, whose bodies become much stronger. He suspects it is linked to Durin's remains still lying on the mountain; defeating these monsters made the sword resonate with them.

## entry: Resurgent Cryo Regisvine
@keys: Resurgent Cryo Regisvine, Cryo Regisvine
@keys2: Albedo, sword
@version: 1.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
When Reckless Pallad bumps into the Traveler and the sword strikes the ground, a withered Cryo Regisvine stump comes back to life, stronger than before. Albedo explains that purified life force from Durin's corruption revived it and keeps it regenerating. It is the finale of Act IV.

## entry: Sucrose and Albedo's secret
@keys: Albedo's secret, Sucrose
@keys2: Chalk Prince, cursed sword
@version: 1.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Sucrose, with Timaeus, helps Albedo organise research data, and suspects he hides something about alchemy. She commissions the Traveler to find out, forgets about it for over a week, and then discovers the sword herself. Albedo says she was drawn to obscure knowledge and promises her safety matters more to him than her curiosity.
