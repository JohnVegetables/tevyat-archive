## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 4.2
### LITE
Thelxie's Fantastic Adventures was a Flagship limited-time event in Version 4.2 (23 November to 11 December 2023). Near Erinnyes in Fontaine, the Traveler and Paimon help Freminet finish a mechanical penguin called Thelxie, then stage a make-believe adventure of "Prince Thelxie" to comfort Zuria Destree, a mother grieving her young son. Gameplay rewards included inviting Freminet to the party.
### STANDARD
The event began as a Fontaine Fishing Association commission about vanished fish and rumours of a Water Imp. It turned out that the "imp" was Freminet's test penguin, and the story became a gentle, sad tale about Loneliness Syndrome, grief and the way fantasy can help someone say goodbye. It required Adventure Rank 20 and the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom; finishing Masquerade of the Guilty first was advised. Because it was a limited event, not every player has seen it.
### LARGE +
Three story parts (Water Imp's Conjecture, Prince's Country, Miraculous Crown) unlocked on 23, 25 and 27 November 2023. Three gameplay modes accompanied them: Motherboard Troubleshooting (circuit puzzles), Record of the Search for Glory (finding marked spots with the Echoing Conch) and Purgation Counterstrike (timed combat using Thelxie's abilities). Players collected Thelxie's Badges (100 available; 80 invited Freminet).

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Delaroche of the Fontaine Fishing Association asks the Traveler to find out why fish have disappeared from Erinnyes, warning of a Water Imp named Thelxie. At the waterside they meet Freminet, whose test penguin explains the rumour. His client, Zuria Destree, wants the penguin to become the hero of a fairytale for her son, who is ill. Over several days the group act out the tale in an underwater ruin and, once the truth about the son comes out, give Zuria a chance to say farewell.
### LARGE
Act I, Water Imp's Conjecture. Delaroche, from the Fontaine Fishing Association, explains that the fish around Erinnyes have vanished. Local stories blame a Water Imp called Thelxie, said to look like a snake-haired woman or a penguin, and a child is rumoured to have walked into the water, lured by a voice. In the waters near Erinnyes Forest the Traveler and Paimon meet Freminet in his diving helmet. He is building a mechanical penguin named Thelxie on commission and had been taking it to the water each day for testing, which explains the "penguin" and, he assumes, the child in the rumour (himself diving). Thelxie has no language module and answers only simple commands and keywords. After debugging its motherboard and collecting shells and coral for its toy weapon, they meet Freminet's client Zuria Destree. She says her son loves the Water Imps rather than fearing them, sneaks to the shore to befriend them and has built a detailed imaginary world of them. He was diagnosed at age 8 with Loneliness Syndrome, a rare illness after which a patient can no longer tell fantasy from reality. Doctors advised "guiding" the fantasy, so Zuria is drawing a picture book and wants to act out Thelxie's story at the Water Imp Kingdom, an underwater ruin in Salacia Plain. Freminet privately warns the Traveler that the illness can be fatal in the worst cases, and says he saw many such cases while at the House of the Hearth under its previous director.

Act II, Prince's Country. Two days later Thelxie has a sword and shield. Zuria draws the first page, and at the ruins Paimon plays the Guardian Goddess while the others are Prince Thelxie's companions, fighting the monsters that have taken the kingdom. The coronation cannot happen because the crown is unfinished, so Zuria draws page two with the monsters fleeing with the crown. Freminet goes to the city for parts and hopes that anyone in a dark place may see something beautiful.

Act III, Miraculous Crown. The crown is ready but Zuria does not arrive. Delaroche, worried by the lack of news, has learned that the boy in the rumour was Lescot Destree, aged eight: about a month before, fishermen saw him tie heavy shells to himself and walk into open water. His mother fell ill and left the Court of Fontaine. At her home her doctor Jalacan says Zuria has now caught the same illness through grief and is sedated after a breakdown. Freminet takes her picture book to finish it with a happy ending. They "reclaim" the crown in the ruins, and Zuria draws the last page as Paimon and Freminet narrate the coronation. The Traveler, wearing Freminet's helmet, hears Zuria speaking with Lescot through Thelxie; he asks for a lullaby so he can return in peace to his dreams. Three days later Zuria returns to the city, plans an exhibition of picture books about the illness with her doctor, and gives the Traveler a copy.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The vanished fish and the Water Imp rumours have a mundane cause (Freminet's testing), but the child who walked into the water was real: Zuria's son Lescot, who died a month earlier. Zuria's commission was her own way of coping.
### LARGE +
Zuria is herself afflicted by Loneliness Syndrome after her loss, and the picture book is the "guidance therapy" her doctor proposed. Her recovery and the final conversation are left ambiguous.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Freminet (the diver building Thelxie; his own entry carries his biography), the Traveler and Paimon, Delaroche of the Fontaine Fishing Association, Zuria Destree (an artist and the client), her late son Lescot, her doctor Jalacan, and Thelxie the mechanical penguin.
### LARGE +
Delaroche appears as a worried client and later a worried friend. Freminet is the emotional anchor: he understands the illness from his time at the House of the Hearth and insists on finishing the story with a happy ending.
