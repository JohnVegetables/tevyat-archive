## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 4.1
### LITE
Waterborne Poetry was the Version 4.1 flagship event (12 to 30 October 2023). Hu Tao and Venti co-host the "Neighboring Nations Congenial Poetry Gala" at Stone Gate in Liyue, a joint Liyue-Mondstadt gathering. While the Traveler, Paimon and friends play riddle, couplet and freestyle games, poems found on a floating leaf lead them to Callirhoe, an Oceanid who was the spring fairy of Springvale and is reunited with Old Finch.
### STANDARD
Part I introduces the gala and its riddle theme, Part II the couplet theme and an "inspiration walk," and Part III the reveal of Callirhoe's identity. The event also offered three mini-games (Hundred-Pace Hurling Rites, Inspiration Eruption, Mending Painting Prospects) that earned Poetry Gala Fervor, redeemable for the weapon Ballad of the Boundless Blue, a Crown of Insight and weapon refinement materials. After the story Hu Tao keeps trying to publish an anthology and Xiao quietly writes his first poem.
### LARGE +
The event is a warm, comic character piece that also gives the Oceanid Callirhoe a farewell, and shows how Mondstadt and Liyue knights and exorcists work together.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Part I, Congenial Gathering: at Wangshu Inn the Traveler and Paimon find Hu Tao and Venti trying to persuade Xiao, who declines to attend because of his karmic afflictions. At Stone Gate they meet Xingqiu and Chongyun, Diona (representing the Cat's Tail and Mondstadt's wine trade), and Mika and Noelle, sent by the Knights of Favonius to keep order. Hu Tao and Venti open the gala; the first theme is riddles, hung on lanterns. Chongyun mentions an investigation that Xingqiu promised to help with; it concerns something strange he saw near the water.

Part II, Tears Among the Leaves: the second theme is Liyue couplets, plus an "inspiration walk" in two groups. One group (the Traveler, Paimon, Diona, Chongyun and, joined later, Xiao) follows a poem written on a floating leaf upstream and replies with a poem of its own. The other (Xingqiu, Mika, Noelle) meets Callirhoe, a Fontaine visitor who wrote one of the riddles.

Part III, Heart of the Deep: Callirhoe is missing. A second leaf points upstream, and the party finds her surrounded by hilichurls and then transformed into a water droplet. She reveals she is an Oceanid and the spring fairy of Springvale from the story "Heart of Clear Springs," and with the group's help meets Old Finch again, reads him a poem and gives him a droplet of her power before her form fades.
### LARGE +
Part I detail: Hu Tao says she spent time petitioning Yujing Terrace and contacting the Knights of Favonius to organise the gala, building on a promise Venti made to her during Lantern Rite about writing poetry together. Venti says he is new to Liyue poetic forms. Diona says she is there as a mixologist and says she hopes to ruin the reputation of Mondstadt's wine with disgusting additions; she says she hates alcohol. Mika and Noelle find they share worries about poetry, and Noelle carried the venue's furniture from Mondstadt. Xiao says he has never written a verse and will only watch from the peaks. The first riddles are read, with the party guessing the first as dandelion, Xingqiu posing a riddle whose answer is Paimon, and the riddle writer, who is Callirhoe, accepting several answers.

Part II detail: Hu Tao and Venti set the couplet game (writing a second line to match a given one) and Venti asks the group to look out for the thing Chongyun saw. Chongyun, whose pure-yang spirit keeps evil spirits away, trains by the lake where he saw it. Xiao joins them after hearing of the exorcist's encounter. Diona mixes her drinks with Jueyun Chili, Slime Condensate and frog legs, and both Xiao and Chongyun find them delicious. Xiao spots the leaf bearing a poem about a lost love, noting the ink looks fresh, and suggests dropping replies upstream. The party writes an answering poem. Meanwhile Xingqiu, Mika and Noelle camp and discuss what makes a hero. They meet Callirhoe on a bridge and bring her back, and that night she asks them what they think of "Heart of Clear Springs," whether the spring fairy should return to the boy.

Part III detail: Chongyun reports seeing Callirhoe at the bridge near Dihua Marsh, and Mika suspects she wrote the leaf poem. The party chases a leaf with another poem about the boy by the spring growing frail, and Mika uses current and wind to guide them upstream. After they chase off hilichurls, Callirhoe appears as a large water droplet, then a human, saying she was a familiar of the former Hydro Archon and that the Oceanids left Fontaine after the archon died; she arrived in Mondstadt powerless and settled at Springvale. She remembers Diona's visits as a child, including a fish pillow named Bubbles. She tells how Finch, a boy who cried by the spring, shared nights of stories with her until she fled from a love she feared would end in regret. Mika, who knows Old Finch as a kind man who encouraged him, urges her to go. Diona fetches Finch, Callirhoe reads him a poem titled Heart of Clear Springs, gives him a droplet of her power so that he can reach her in dreams, says she has never regretted meeting him, and her form disappears. The group promises to keep her secret.
### ULTRA +
Open-world aftermath: a message board by Aunt Qiao and Pops Zhou displays the participants' poems; two lanterns by the stage hold riddles whose answers are the Glaze Lily and a Treasure Hoarder insignia; an interactable by the Springvale lake plays a happy melody; Hu Tao and Jifang argue over her planned anthology, which she touts with poems such as "Fifteen Tenderloin Steaks"; and Xiao, near Pervases' Temple, says he tried writing a poem, which sits on a table in the temple.

Gameplay: Hundred-Pace Hurling Rites (throwing darts at pots), Inspiration Eruption (defeating enemies and using Inspiring Crystals to burst Mythic Nexuses) and Mending Painting Prospects (photographing key objects for an art conservator). Requirements were Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III; A New Star Approaches was recommended. Rewards at 200 to 1,200 Poetry Gala Favor included the weapon Ballad of the Boundless Blue, Dandelion Bookmarks, weapon materials, a Crown of Insight and a furnishing blueprint. These are game mechanics, not in-universe canon.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Callirhoe is an Oceanid (Mika explains Oceanids were familiars of the former Hydro Archon), and is the real spring fairy behind the Springvale story "Heart of Clear Springs." The boy of the story is Old Finch. Callirhoe has been recovering her power slowly in Springvale for decades, can hold a physical form only briefly, and her water droplet matches what Chongyun saw. She can now meet Finch in his dreams through the droplet she left him.
### LARGE +
Xiao, previously avoiding gatherings, joins part of the walk and reveals he has trouble with poetry yet writes one afterwards. Diona's drinks may be linked to a gift from Callirhoe, which the story hints at without confirming.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Hu Tao and Venti co-host and joke together. Xingqiu and Chongyun, Diona, Mika and Noelle form the companion group; Xiao and Zhongli join for parts. Callirhoe is the emotional centre, with Old Finch at the end. Mika and Noelle show the Knights of Favonius at work, with Mika's mapping skills guiding the final search.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Callirhoe and Old Finch are reunited; she says she will meet him in his dreams. The group keeps her secret. Hu Tao pursues her anthology and Xiao finds calm in writing.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Released in Version 4.1 and set at Stone Gate in Liyue's Bishui Plain, with scenes at Wangshu Inn and in Springvale in Mondstadt. It is a flagship event outside Lantern Rite. The event story chains Congenial Gathering, Tears Among the Leaves and Heart of the Deep over three days.

## entry: Callirhoe
@keys: Callirhoe, spring fairy of Springvale, Heart of Clear Springs
@version: 4.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Callirhoe is an Oceanid who settled at Springvale after losing most of her power. She is the spring fairy of the story "Heart of Clear Springs." In Waterborne Poetry she appears as a poet from Fontaine, writes a riddle for the gala, and reunites with Old Finch, to whom she gives a droplet of her power.

### STANDARD
