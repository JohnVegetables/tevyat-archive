## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 3.7
### LITE
Duel! The Summoners' Summit! was a limited-time Flagship event in Version 3.7 (25 May to 12 June 2023), built around Genius Invokation TCG. A King of Invokations Grand Prix is being held in every nation. In Mondstadt the Traveler and Paimon meet Charlotte, a reporter for The Steambird, who is chasing a "Card Snatcher" who buys or wins cards bearing a treasure-chest back. They follow the trail to Liyue, Inazuma and Sumeru, where it ends with the Treasure Hoarders, and with a gentler discovery about who created the game.
### STANDARD
The event's four story parts follow a regional tournament each: Mondstadt (Kaeya loses the final to Timmie), Liyue (Hu Tao reaches the final), Inazuma (Sangonomiya Kokomi wins) and Sumeru (Cyno is named champion). The card backs turn out to be coded messages from Treasure Hoarders, and the Komaniya Express courier Kirara unwittingly carries them. In Sumeru, Kirara also delivers a long-undelivered parcel to Garvipidam, who with the late Gulab created Genius Invokation TCG. Gameplay modes were A Tour of Wonders, Zero Hour Invokation, Evermotion Mechanical Painting: Invoker and Heart of the Dice.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 32, the Ritou Escape Plan and the quest "Battlefield of Dice, Cats, and Cards"; recommended story quests were the Akasha Pulses Archon Quest, Mona's Astrolabos and the Raiden Shogun's Imperatrix Umbrosa Act II. Rewards included Primogems, a Crown of Insight and the weapon Ibis Piercer, bought with Invokation Coupons.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 3.7
### LITE
In Mondstadt, Diona and Margaret tell the Traveler about the King of Invokations Grand Prix. Charlotte, who researched the Traveler's deeds in detail, recruits them to find the Card Snatcher and brings a bonus payment to Mona, who writes an astrology column for The Steambird. At the finals Timmie beats Kaeya. A suspect Charlotte trails turns out to be Adrian, who sold a plain card back for triple its price to a man who said the Mondstadt ones were done and Liyue was next.
### STANDARD
In Liyue the party is greeted by Baiwen, who invites Charlotte to meet Ningguang at Yujing Terrace because Charlotte's exclusive may have offended those behind the scenes. Kazuha joins them. They help Kirara find the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, where Hu Tao is preparing for her final. She reveals that the suspect won a card back from Chongyun in a match, and the party goes to Wuwang Hill to find Chongyun and a missing child, Little Yu, while Ningguang explains that the treasure-chest card back is a rare item some players pay for as a luck charm. In Inazuma, at the Ritou tournament, the Traveler meets finalists and spectators including Ei, Yae Miko, Itto, Shinobu and Sara, then tells Kokomi what they have learned.
### LARGE +
Kokomi works through the clues: the Card Snatcher's route matches Kirara's deliveries, every parcel she carries ends up in the same place in Sumeru, and the card backs are in her bag. She infers a group acted: other members already collected card backs and shipped them by Komaniya Express while the one the party followed was too slow to use it, so he stole once and traded after that. The Traveler agrees to escort Kirara to the delivery address.

In Sumeru Cyno joins them at Caravan Ribat. Kirara delivers the package to Dedebaba, who is waiting with Pabag and Papak, three Treasure Hoarders. When they grow suspicious of her and the party, a fight follows and they are defeated. They confess that the treasure-chest card backs are signals: when dirty with wet earth and wiped off, a hidden message summons hoarders for a worldwide heist. Some fellow hoarders became fans of the game and lost the card backs to other players, so the group tried to recover them all and send them back to reprint. Cyno takes them into custody.
### ULTRA +
Kirara then brings the Traveler, Paimon and Charlotte to Vimara Village for a separate parcel the company had long been unable to deliver. Zadith, Arezo and the village head Amadhiah help the party find the recipient, a scholar named Garvipidam. The sender, Gulab, had died; the package contains a letter, and the cinematic shows Gulab giving his friend a device he made to help players find opponents nearby. Garvipidam tells them that he and Gulab were childhood classmates who painted cards together while Gulab, who had Eleazar, was in hospital. In the letter's flashback Gulab calls the device his last gift, so that his friend could make new friends through the game.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 3.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Genius Invokation TCG was created by two Akademiya scholars: Gulab, who became a Rtawahist Dastur but was bedridden with Eleazar and died, and his friend Garvipidam, who built elemental theory into the game. They developed it by painting cards during Garvipidam's hospital visits. Gulab left a final parcel with a letter, delivered late by Kirara. Garvipidam, who could not bear to play without Gulab, is moved by the letter and by the party's account of what the game has done for people.
### LARGE +
The poster seen in Mondstadt of the game's "inventors" looks young; Kaeya doubts that it is an accurate portrayal, and Charlotte says almost nothing is known about the inventors.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 3.7
### STANDARD
Charlotte is the cheerful, persistent Steambird reporter; Kirara an Inazuman courier and youkai; Cyno a serious Matra general who is nevertheless eager to pun; Kokomi a patient strategist. Mondstadt's finalists are Kaeya and Timmie. Hu Tao writes rhyming verse before her final. Ningguang hosts a meal and shares intelligence. Eichendorff, Lennard, Huffman and Mika are brief Mondstadt bystanders.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 3.7
### STANDARD
The Treasure Hoarders' plan is stopped by Cyno, Charlotte has her scoop, and Kirara is credited as the one who makes it possible. Garvipidam receives Gulab's gift.

## entry: The Card Snatcher
@keys: Card Snatcher, treasure chest card back
@keys2: Genius Invokation, Charlotte
@version: 3.7
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The "Card Snatcher" is the title Charlotte gives to a suspicious figure who took or traded for specific Genius Invokation card backs during the King of Invokations Grand Prix. He is one of several Treasure Hoarders collecting treasure-chest card backs, which secretly carry a signal for a hoarders' operation.

@keys: Gulab, Garvipidam

### STANDARD
Gulab and Garvipidam are the Akademiya scholars who created Genius Invokation TCG. Gulab, a Rtawahist Dastur with Eleazar, died; Garvipidam moved to Vimara Village and stopped playing. Gulab's late parcel carried a letter.

## entry: King of Invokations Grand Prix
@keys: King of Invokations Grand Prix, King of Invokations
@version: 3.7
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A multi-nation tournament for Genius Invokation TCG arranged through the Adventurers' Guild, with regional finals. An unofficial player tournament ran alongside. Cyno is named the Sumeru champion in the event's story.
