## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.8
### LITE
Summertime Odyssey was a limited-time Version 2.8 flagship event (15 July to 24 August 2022) on a temporary map, the Golden Apple Archipelago. After Fischl receives an anonymous invitation to a "masterless land" where she can restore her fallen kingdom, she, Mona, Xinyan, Kazuha, the Traveler and Paimon holiday there. Strange "mirages" built from each person's memories appear, and the group works through them one by one.
### STANDARD
The invitation traces back to Alice, with Venti secretly in on it. The archipelago has been seeded with a Fatui experiment, a machine named Cognitive Mimicry, which warps minds; the Fatui who tested it went mad, but the protagonists' stronger wills shaped their hallucinations into stable mirages. The story visits Kazuha's regrets over his clan, Xinyan's struggle to be a musician, Mona's burden as an astrologer and Fischl's relationship with her "Immernachtreich" fantasy. At the end the machine is repaired and switched off, and a mysterious voice, implied to be the future Dendro Archon, speaks to the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Players who collected Iridescent Flotsam during the event's challenges could invite Electro Fischl ("Prinzessin der Verurteilung!") as a character reward. The story returns to the archipelago first visited in the earlier event Midsummer Island Adventure, and its closing voice points ahead to Sumeru.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
Act I, The Golden Apple Vacation Returns!: Fischl, Mona, Xinyan, Kazuha, the Traveler and Paimon travel to the archipelago after Fischl receives an anonymous letter (really Alice's) offering her a land to restore her kingdom. On arrival, Kazuha and the Traveler find a strange Fatui machine.

Act II (Kazuha): a bonsai pot belonging to the Kaedehara family opens a domain recreating Kazuha's childhood. Act III (Xinyan): a vine-covered drum on the Broken Isle opens a domain of her memories and turns the island into an instrument again. Act IV, Immernachtreich Apokalypse (Fischl): Fischl's imagined kingdom, its three-volume book and her double. Act V, The Ancient Azure Stars (Mona): a star-chasing mirage through her years as an astrologer.

Act VI, Like Rhyme and Song, the Summer Reverie: the party finds the Fatui researcher Persikov, recovers the cores of his machine "Cognitive Mimicry", repairs and disables it, and hears the unseen voice a second time.
### LARGE +
Act I details: Katheryne tells the Traveler and Paimon that Fischl wants to see them, and Mona suspects Alice, who also made the Dodocommunication Device that Venti gives the Traveler. Venti admits he knew the plan; Xinyan and Kazuha, in Mondstadt by chance, are invited. At the archipelago Fischl and Mona race, Xinyan makes camp, and the Fatui (led by Persikov) say their machine was a failed energy generator.

Act II: The group's boat has become sentient and talks to a squirrel; Paimon names it Miitoboru. Kazuha's mirages show his father telling him to abandon the family craft, the clan dissolving after the Raiden Gokaden incident, his wandering, receiving a Vision and fleeing Inazuma on the Alcor under the Vision Hunt Decree. He realises he wants to keep the family's bonsai tradition alive.

Act III: Xinyan's mirage shows her mother and others discouraging her music and a surprise birthday gathering with Xiangling and Yun Jin; her wish is to perform with someone completely unexpected. A note by Albedo says the island is a giant instrument. The group revives a flower called Blazing Heart with Glacial Spring Water and a second, Frozen Soul, lets them dredge the mountains so Xinyan can perform. Mona admits she had already warned Fischl of the Fatui, and the trail leads to Twinning Isle, where Fischl behaves oddly.

Act IV: A raven statue and the book "Hymn of the Holy Land" are found abandoned. In the mirage the group learns of Fischl's childhood name Amy and of her parents first playing along, then telling her to give up fairy tales. Immernacht Fischl, a double, claims the Immernachtreich is a tomb for those who cannot face reality and tries to trap them, until the real Fischl accepts her insecurities and reclaims it. A hijacked call delivers the unseen voice, which says the mirages are humans' "deep dream".

Act V: Mona's mirage on Minacious Isle follows a star through her miserable early years of divination, a prediction of an adventurer's death within two years (he refused to quit adventuring), and a home filled with expensive astrology equipment. She decides she cannot change fate but can seize her destiny while still guiding others. Kazuha proposes that the machine reacts to willpower, so the strong-willed produce stable mirages.

Act VI: Persikov, drunk and ranting, is snapped out of madness. His manuscripts say the machine alters brains, is modelled on a god's power and lost its crystalline cores. The party refits them. Mona says the mirages persist, but she senses no ill intent. The voice says it was curious about a power related to it and helped design the experience; a Dendro symbol appears.
### ULTRA +
In Act VI Kazuha slams Persikov to the ground to bring him to his senses. His papers record that the Fatui did not support his research but let him test it on a deserted island with junior soldiers; it broke after a couple of days, he left his subordinates in a cave and searched for the cores himself, and succumbed to the device. After the repair two Fatui arrive no longer affected. Venti, called afterwards, says the journey has served its purpose if they had an unforgettable experience. In Act IV, raven-statue guards refuse the party at first and the final volume's castle is the toy castle Fischl once smashed in a tantrum; in Act V the group needs an Asterism Shard to reopen a pool.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.8
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Fischl's imagination is part of herself, not a flaw: Oz says Immernacht Fischl and the child Amy are two sides of the same person. Kazuha admits a wish to continue his clan's bonsai tradition. Mona decides destiny cannot be rewritten but can be seized. The Fatui machine and the unseen voice are separate causes of the mirages, and the voice's Dendro symbol foreshadows Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that the voice's timbre matches that of a Sumeru teaser at the Version 2.8 Special Program, and later Sumeru content names Nahida. Within the event the entity's identity remains an inference.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon, Fischl (and her raven Oz), Mona, Kazuha, Xinyan and Venti (by communicator). Alice and Klee are involved offstage. Persikov leads a hapless Fatui group. Raven statues of the Immernachtreich include one named Arnold, and Immernacht Fischl is the boss-like double of Fischl. The sentient boat, named Miitoboru by Paimon, appears.
### LARGE +
Albedo is present only through a note he left on the Broken Isle, and Xiangling and Yun Jin appear in Xinyan's mirage.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
The machine is switched off; the mirages remain for a while. Fischl feels secure in her imagination, Kazuha and Xinyan and Mona each resolve a private regret, and the group enjoys the rest of the holiday. The voice's final words suggest a future meeting.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.8 as a Flagship Event; players needed Adventure Rank 32 and the Chapter II Prologue (Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves), with Mona's Beyond This World's Stars recommended. It was set before Sumeru's release in Version 3.0.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 2.8
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Gameplay combined Surfpiercer boat challenges and five Phantom Realm challenges (Misty Hills, Foggy Vales; The Islands' Stirring Strings; Immernachtreich Keep; Temple of the Star Latitudes; and Initial Exploration/Direct Strike), with Iridescent Flotsam exchanged for materials and for Fischl's invite.

## entry: Golden Apple Archipelago
@keys: Golden Apple Archipelago, Klee's Camp
@version: 2.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A limited map of islands reached by a Dodoco-themed device outside Mondstadt. In the 2.8 event it holds Pudding Isle, the Broken Isle, Twinning Isle and Minacious Isle, a Fatui camp and the mirage domains. It is also where the Midsummer Island Adventure took place.

## entry: Cognitive Mimicry
@keys: Cognitive Mimicry, Persikov
@keys2: Fatui, Archipelago
@version: 2.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A Fatui machine designed by the researcher Persikov, said to be modelled on a god's power and able to alter brains. After it broke, its crystalline cores were lost. Those weak in willpower hallucinate and go mad; Kazuha, Xinyan, Mona and Fischl instead shaped stable mirages. The group retrieves the cores and fixes it.

## entry: Immernachtreich
@keys: Immernachtreich, Immernacht Fischl, Hymn of the Holy Land
@keys2: Fischl, Oz, Archipelago
@version: 2.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Fischl's imagined kingdom, here a domain with raven statues, narration and a three-volume book. It shows the child Amy and her parents' changing response to fairy tales. Immernacht Fischl, her double, calls it a tomb for those who cannot face reality, but the real Fischl reclaims it.

## entry: The unseen voice on the Dodocommunication Device
@keys: Dodocommunication Device
@keys2: Venti, Alice, Traveler
@version: 2.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Alice's prototype communicator, handed to the Traveler by Venti. In Acts IV and VI an unknown voice takes over the connection, says the mirages are the "deep dream" of humans and that it may meet the Traveler in another place; a Dendro symbol appears. The wiki treats this as implying the Dendro Archon Lesser Lord Kusanali, but the event does not say so.
