## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 6.6
### LITE
Phantasmal Pals was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 6.6 (22 May to 8 June 2026). In Inazuma, Klee, Qiqi, Sayu and Yaoyao have formed the Little Hexenzirkel and meet in a secret base. The Traveler and Paimon, together with the young Witch Hunter Prune, help them look after a strangely visible imaginary friend, Popcorn, and find the adult he belongs to.
### STANDARD
The story runs in three parts. In the first, Paimon and the Traveler meet Prune near Chinju Forest and learn that the little witches are looking after a talking Forest Boar named Popcorn, who turns out to be an imaginary friend. In the second, the Yumekui-Baku explain that Popcorn was warped by a nightmare fragment and the group collects the fragments. In the third, the nightmares are devoured, Popcorn is reunited with his now-adult human Homura, and Prune finally admits why she is searching for witches.
### LARGE +
The tone is gentle comedy built around childhood imagination and the pressures of adulthood. The two themes the event returns to are that adults also need play and rest, and that a child pushed to grow up too fast can lose the same spark.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Part I, The Forest Boar and the Little Witch. Wanting a rest, Paimon and the Traveler head for Aisa Bathhouse, overhear rumours of a rampaging Forest Boar and a strange child asking about a witch, and go to Chinju Forest. There they meet Prune, who says she is a friend of Alice and carries a bell that detects magical fluctuations. The bell leads to a Cryo Whopperflower, then to a card asking children with imaginary friends to find the Dough King. Prune suspects a trap, but the little witches' secret base turns out to be a harmless hideout. Part II, If You Should Forget the Entrance to Dreams. The Dough King, a Yumekui-Baku, and a baku named Hyakujitsuhaku Shion explain that Popcorn is bound up with a nightmare fragment. The Traveler, Paimon, Prune and the four little witches collect three more fragments around Inazuma and share a meal with the baku. Part III, The Magic That Never Fails. The baku devour the fragments and Popcorn recovers his memory. He leads the group to Homura, an overworked young adult who has forgotten him; Prune confesses her own secret, and Alice reveals the larger design behind the quest.
### LARGE
Part I, The Forest Boar and the Little Witch. After a run of hard events Paimon suggests relaxing at Aisa Bathhouse. Two passers-by mention a crazed Forest Boar near Chinju Forest and a child asking for a witch. Paimon drags the Traveler along. In the forest they meet Prune, who first claims to be Alice's friend on a "witch-related incident" and shows a mismatched set of witch's tools from Alice: a bell, a hammer and an old Spantamad textbook. The bell rings, a Cryo Whopperflower appears, and the Traveler fights it. A card nearby reads like a request for help from the Dough King, with a Forest Boar drawn on it. The bell rings again and leads to the Little Hexenzirkel's base, where Klee is playing with the boar, Popcorn. Qiqi, Sayu and Yaoyao, with Klee, introduce themselves, and Paimon joins by letting Klee give her an "introduction magic" title. Prune is invited but declines, saying she stopped playing pretend long ago. Over a tea party, the children explain that Popcorn is an imaginary friend found wandering angry and amnesiac, that he speaks of a human named Homura who needs help, and that he repeats a rhyme about bringing on the tear-bugs and booger-beasts. The meeting ends with plans to wait for the Dough King's report.

Part II, If You Should Forget the Entrance to Dreams. Next day Shion introduces the Dough King and explains that baku regulate emotions by devouring nightmares. A nightmare fragment found near Popcorn carries a signature identical to his, which explains why he is visible in the real world. Three more fragments sit at locations on a map; because devouring one now could destabilise the others, they must be gathered first. Prune tries to keep the children out of it but they come along. The party splits up to search a grove in Chinju Forest, an area east of Konda Village (where Kirara is found napping in a tree and Arataki Itto is playing hide-and-seek with the children), and a beach north of Konda Village, where the last fragment is guarded by Slimes. The quest ends with a meal at the base, during which Prune says she has an imaginary friend named Descartes.

Part III, The Magic That Never Fails. The baku eat the fragments, and Popcorn recovers his memories. He explains that Homura is now an adult worn down by work, who dreamed a nightmare when she finally rested; he tried to fight it from inside and was swept away. Shion warns that the baku sensed no effort by Homura to overcome the feelings in the fragments, a sign of emotional numbness. In Inazuma City the group watches Homura with her boss, and the little witches quote Popcorn's rhyme until Homura finds her voice and negotiates a better arrangement. Popcorn and the group then speak with her directly, and she and Popcorn reconcile. Prune confesses that she is a Witch Hunter, that she never was Alice's friend, and that Alice told her she had taken Descartes and that the only way to get him back was to defeat the witches. The little witches reassure her and she joins the Little Hexenzirkel. Descartes then reappears, along with every little witch's imaginary friend, and Alice speaks.
### ULTRA +
Small details. The event's launch quest requires the Chapter II Act I part "Ritou Escape Plan" and recommends the Archon Quest True Moon. Open-world dialogue in Part I points to the four minigames (see gameplay). In Part II, Kirara says she dozed in a tree after feeling a nearby spot was off, Prune pulls her tail by mistake and apologises, and Prune cites a book titled "Little-Known Creatures of Teyvat: Inazuma Edition" on nekomata and baku. When Itto turns up, the children hide in the Lavender Melon patch and Prune remarks that he seems the biggest kid of them all.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Popcorn is an imaginary friend who became visible and unstable after contact with a nightmare fragment, a materialised accumulation of negative emotions. His human, Homura, is now an adult overworked in her first job; the baku say her failure to resist her own nightmare signals emotional numbness. Alice tells the Traveler and Paimon that she never captured Descartes: his existence was close to fading because Prune, a precocious child who grew up too fast, was losing her childlike innocence. Alice invented the quest to send Prune to Mondstadt and the little witches, and chose Mondstadt because freedom nurtures imagination and she wanted Klee to have someone else to play with.
### LARGE +
Prune is a "Witch Hunter" and has been lying about being Alice's friend. She came to Mondstadt after being told to defeat all witches, then travelled overnight by boat to Inazuma following a trail.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Main: the Traveler and Paimon, Prune, Klee, Qiqi, Sayu and Yaoyao. Also: the Dough King (Kohaku), Hyakujitsuhaku Shion (a Yumekui-Baku), Popcorn, Homura, Homura's boss, Kirara, Arataki Itto, Alice (voice and finale appearance), and Descartes. Imaginary friends who appear in the finale include Dodoco (Klee), Gena (Sayu) and Yuegui and Tuantuan (the pair from the Yaoyao minigame), as well as Descartes.
### LARGE +
The children are all minors and the event treats them as such: they are playful, protective of each other and curious. Prune is a child who acts like an adult and is the most defensive of the group. Alice appears only as a voice at the end of Part III.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Popcorn returns to normal, is reunited with Homura, and she resolves to deal with her work situation more openly. Prune joins the Little Hexenzirkel and is reunited with Descartes. Alice makes the little witches' imaginary friends visible for the celebration. Players received the weapon appearance Super Awesome Magic Key, which stays available after the event, and the five imaginary friends and a group photo as event items.

## entry: Popcorn
@keys: Popcorn
@keys2: Forest Boar, imaginary friend, Homura, Little Hexenzirkel
@version: 6.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A talking Forest Boar who is the imaginary friend of a young adult named Homura, who works for a company in Inazuma City. He was found wandering, fast and angry, with patchy memory, after a nightmare fragment materialised him. He repeats a rhyme Homura wrote as a child and insists she is the best no matter what. After the baku devour the fragments he recovers his memory and goes back to her.

## entry: Little Hexenzirkel
@keys: Little Hexenzirkel, Little Witches
@keys2: Klee, Qiqi, Sayu, Yaoyao, Prune
@version: 6.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A children's club founded by Klee, with Sayu, Yaoyao and Qiqi as the other members, plus Paimon and later Prune. Yaoyao suggested the "Little" because they still have growing to do. Membership needs only an invitation from Klee, who can give each member an introduction magic that Alice taught her. The group meets in a secret base, shares snacks and toys, and treats the Hexenzirkel's real witches as models. Not to be confused with the Hexenzirkel itself.

## entry: Yumekui-Baku
@keys: Dough King, Hyakujitsuhaku Shion
@keys2: baku, nightmare, Popcorn
@version: 6.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Dough King, also called Kohaku, and the baku Hyakujitsuhaku Shion (who speaks of patients at a clinic and is in charge while Mizuki is away) explain that Yumekui-Baku are dough-bodied youkai who help humans regulate their emotions by devouring nightmares. They cannot easily carry tools, so they ask the Traveler's group to bring them the fragments. The baku fast beforehand and prepare the fragments with seasoning.

## entry: Nightmare fragments
@keys: nightmare fragment, nightmare fragments
@version: 6.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A fragment is a materialised accumulation of negative emotions. Popcorn carries the same signature as the one found near him, and there are three more at locations in Inazuma. Because devouring one early could make the others more volatile, the group gathers all four before the baku eat them together.

## entry: Prune and Descartes

@version: 6.6

### STANDARD
Prune presents herself as a professional investigator and a friend of Alice, but admits at the end that she is a Witch Hunter whose imaginary friend, the puppy Descartes, was gone after she lost a debate to Alice. Alice later says she did not take Descartes and had created the whole task to help Prune. Prune joins the Little Hexenzirkel and Descartes returns.
