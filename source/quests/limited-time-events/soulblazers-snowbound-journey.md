## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 6.2
### LITE
Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 6.2 (19 December 2025 to 5 January 2026). A letter invites the Traveler and Paimon to the Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge in Mondstadt, where friends from Natlan and Sumeru have formed a team of comic-book fans, the Legion of Soulblazers. The story follows the Traveler's induction into the Legion and the team's relay race, built around Minnie, a young team member who is trying to prove herself to her parents.
### STANDARD
The story has three parts. In The Shining Warriors Take the Stage, the Traveler and Paimon reach the Dragonspine camp, meet Amber and Heinz of the organizing committee, and discover that Varesa and Xilonen are competing with Alrani, Tang Yigang and Minnie as the Soulblazers. In Now's No Time for Tears, the Traveler is initiated into the Legion, trains with the team, and helps convince Minnie's parents to let her race. In Go, Fietena, Go!, Yigang falls ill, the Traveler runs in his place, and Minnie leaves the race to help an injured rival.
### LARGE +
The event is a light, warm story about fandom, mutual encouragement, and the gap between a young person's ambitions and their parents' worries for her health. It carries almost no wider lore beyond the comic series and its link to Natlan's history.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
Part I, The Shining Warriors Take the Stage. A letter announces the contest, with teams from Natlan, Sumeru, Inazuma, Liyue and Mondstadt. At the camp Amber and Heinz of the Knights of Favonius, both on the organizing committee, mention a Natlan team with a horned, hungry competitor and a girl with a striped tail; the Traveler recognises Varesa and Xilonen. Their camp is run by Alrani and Tang Yigang, members of the Legion of Soulblazers, a fan club for the comic The Soulblazer Saga. Xilonen explains she is representing her pupil Nepecha; Minnie, a Mondstadt fan, proposed the contest. Part II, Now's No Time for Tears. After a morning initiation ceremony, the Traveler trains on the course, learns of Minnie's deal with her parents, visits the Mondstadt Library, eats at Good Hunter, and watches Minnie's parents and brother ask her to come home. Xilonen then speaks to the parents. Part III, Go, Fietena, Go! Yigang is bedridden with a fever, so the Traveler runs the second leg. During the race Minnie turns back to help an Inazuman runner, the team places too low for a medal, and the committee gives an honorary medal.
### LARGE
Part I, The Shining Warriors Take the Stage. The Traveler reads the letter from the Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge Organizing Committee and goes to Dragonspine. Amber and Heinz (Field Officer, 1st Company, Knights of Favonius) give them VIP tickets; Amber asks them to borrow a Soulblazer comic for her. At the Natlan-Sumeru camp, Alrani and Tang Yigang quote The Soulblazer Saga in ceremonial tones and are disappointed that Paimon is not moved. Xilonen translates: Legion of Soulblazers means comic-book fan club. She is vice captain on Varesa's team, standing in for Nepecha, who loves the series but cannot take part in extreme sports; she also wants merchandise for Nepecha. Varesa and Minnie are training; Varesa says she and Minnie had only written letters and traded illustrations before meeting in person. Minnie wants to see more of the world but is unsure she is tough enough, and mentions a deal with her parents: train hard and get a good race time.

Part II, Now's No Time for Tears. The morning after, Varesa has finished the comics and explains the series is a 12-volume adaptation of Natlan's history with a focus on the heroine Fietena. She then holds an initiation ceremony using a Fietena mask she made, and the Traveler and Paimon join the Legion; the creed is shouted aloud. Xilonen insists on rehearsing the relay stages, so the Traveler runs the course with either Varesa or Xilonen and Minnie while monsters near the track are cleared. Minnie explains her situation: her parents oppose her wish to move abroad as an assistant comic artist or prop maker. She is the youngest in her family, was a sickly child, and her parents want a stable career. The team spends the afternoon at the Mondstadt Library, where Lisa checks out books, then lunches at Good Hunter. Minnie confides that she keeps quitting and returning to art. Her brother Patton and then her parents Sherilyn and Moritz arrive; Moritz says this race will be her last. Xilonen steps in: she tells the parents that her student Nepecha and many others admire Minnie's art and presents a group painting. She compares art to a bard's career, and Varesa assures them Minnie is sturdier than they think.

Part III, Go, Fietena, Go! The next morning Yigang has a fever and is bedridden under a medic's care. Xilonen asks the Traveler to run in his place. The referee Draff opens the race; the teams include The Sand Blasters, Lightning Strike, The Sunset Squadron, The Frostbite Friends, Dragonspine Must Be Scaled, The Mild Hunt and the Soulblazers. The order is Xilonen, the Traveler, Varesa and then Minnie. The Soulblazers fall behind early, but near the end Minnie sees an exhausted Inazuman runner, Kenzou of Lightning Strike, in trouble, and takes him to the finish line, by which point the other teams have finished. The organizing committee, in agreement with Kenzou's team, says the Soulblazers would have ranked much higher and awards a special honorary medal for putting lives first. Moritz and Sherilyn then keep their deal.
### ULTRA +
Details. The course features a Statue of The Seven at the baton hand-off between the Traveler and Varesa. The final stretch includes a fight against a Frostarm Lawachurl and Cryo Hilichurl Shooters, with a trial party that includes Amber. The comics mentioned include Soulblazer 6: Shadow of Tenebrium and a standalone special, Fietena VS Abyss Lector: Battle of the Meat Feasters. Moritz proposes making the trip abroad a family visit to the Yae Publishing House. Alrani leads a group photo posed like Fietena and Mavuika in volume 2, and the Soulblazers promise to return for the next challenge. Five Snowrealm Visitors side quests (from the Yae Publishing House, the Spark Knight and others) are accessed from the world.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Soulblazer Saga is a 12-volume comic series published by the Yae Publishing House. According to Varesa it is an entertainment adaptation of Natlan's history in which Fietena and a group of fellow warriors fight the Abyss. Fan communities of the series span Mondstadt, Natlan and Sumeru through letters and pen friends. Minnie's parents' objections come from worry about her health and her future; they relent once she meets the deal.
### LARGE +
Xilonen says she is related to Nepecha and has known her only a short time. Minnie's brother Patton works at the tavern and had heard of the Traveler's exploits.

Main: the Traveler, Paimon, Varesa, Xilonen, Minnie, Alrani, Tang Yigang. Also: Amber, Heinz, Lisa, Draff (the referee), Sara, and Minnie's family (Patton, Sherilyn and Moritz); the Inazuman contestant Kenzou; Fietena, Mavuika and others appear only in the comic sequence.
### LARGE +
Minnie is a young person and a determined runner; she has artistic and prop-making talent. Varesa and Xilonen are competing as captain and vice captain of the Soulblazers team.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
Minnie's family allows her to travel abroad on the condition that she is careful. The Soulblazers earn an honorary medal. The event ends with plans to return for the next cross country challenge; Alrani also brings Amber a copy of a Soulblazer comic.
### LARGE +
Players who reached 200 Blazing Spirit received the bow Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow; the final tier added a Crown of Insight and a Soulblazer: Fietena item.

## entry: Legion of Soulblazers
@keys: Legion of Soulblazers, Soulblazers
@keys2: Dragonspine, Minnie, Alrani, Tang Yigang
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A fan club for the comic The Soulblazer Saga. Alrani and Tang Yigang use dramatic tenets drawn from the series; Varesa says its real purpose is to share life's joys and get rid of life's woes. The members in this event are Alrani, Tang Yigang, Minnie, Varesa, and Xilonen as stand-in for Nepecha; the Traveler and Paimon are inducted in Part II.
### LARGE +
Alrani is an aspiring herbad of the Akademiya struggling with a thesis. Tang Yigang collects Soulblazer books and mecha action figures from the Yae Publishing House. Varesa leads the group as captain.

## entry: The Soulblazer Saga
@keys: Soulblazer Saga, Fietena
@keys2: Yae Publishing House, comic, Natlan
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A long-running comic series with 12 volumes in the main series and spinoffs, including an armoured-mecha spinoff, Armored Assault. The central heroine is Fietena, a warrior who becomes a commander and who fights alongside friends; the Pyro Archon also appears in it. Varesa says it adapts Natlan history for entertainment.

## entry: Minnie's deal
@keys: Minnie, Minnie's parents
@keys2: Soulblazers, Dragonspine, Xilonen
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Minnie, the youngest in her family and a sickly child, promised her parents that if her team is ranked in the race they must take her ambition of working with comics seriously. The parents are loving but anxious; Xilonen persuades them with a group painting from Nepecha's fans. Minnie stops mid-race to help an injured runner, and her father says she achieved what she set out to do, so the parents honour the deal.
