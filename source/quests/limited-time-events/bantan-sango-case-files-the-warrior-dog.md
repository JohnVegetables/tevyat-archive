## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### LITE
Bantan Sango Case Files: The Warrior Dog was a limited-time event in Version 2.3 (7 to 20 December 2021). The Bantan Sango Detective Agency in Inazuma City has received a flood of reports about missing pets and other small animals and asks the Traveler to help its field investigator Ryuuji. The culprits turn out to be both Treasure Hoarders and a swift masked dog, the "Black Shadow".
### STANDARD
Four case files unlock over several days. In each, Ryuuji marks camps in the wild; at some the Traveler fights Treasure Hoarders and other armed groups to free caged animals, and at others must sneak past the dog using a gadget, the Ubiquity Net, which Sango had made and which can catch small animals but is too weak for criminals. The final file explains the Hoarders' plan, introduces the dog's true name Kageroumaru, and ends with Kageroumaru accepting the Traveler as his ninken.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest "The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia". Rewards came from stages in Tatarasuna, Fort Fujitou, Inazuma City, Chinju Forest and Kannazuka, and included Primogems and the event's Kageroumaru reward.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### LITE
Katheryne tells the Traveler that the agency wants an experienced adventurer to accompany Ryuuji on investigations into the "large scale disappearance of many small animals". Detective Sango stays at the agency and pays a generous bounty. Ryuuji says he was knocked out by a "dark shadow" while trying to free animals at a camp, and the Traveler finds that the shadow is a dog guarding the cages.
### STANDARD
Case 1, "Mystery of the Black Shadow": the first camp is guarded by the dog; Ryuuji places smoke and fireworks devices to help the Traveler sneak in. A second camp is held by Treasure Hoarders and other armed groups. Case 2, "Stealthy Trail": Ryuuji marks new camps from Sango's deductions and, as an example of her method, recounts an earlier theft case in which the thief was caught because he sold fresh Naku Weed; Sango had reasoned he would not leave Narukami Island because boat travel was policed under the Sakoku Decree. Case 3, "The Truth (Just About) Comes to Light": the leads run cold with only about half the animals found, and Ryuuji reports Sango's puzzling instructions to investigate odd places. Case 4, "Recognition": Sango sends the Traveler to meet Ryuuji, who has been scouting Yashiori Island, where the Hoarders have just left a camp of cages behind.
### LARGE +
Ryuuji explains Sango's deduction: the Hoarders were stealing dogs to train as treasure-hunting hounds, after an earlier attempt to train weasels failed. The Traveler fights the returning Hoarders. The dog, who seems unwilling to attack them, leads the group to an old letter left by a ninken trainer, addressed to anyone who finds it, asking for someone to look after Kageroumaru, who was too weak as a young dog to be paired with a ninja yet grew strong through training. The trainer's pact instructions are crossed out. Kageroumaru then attacks in an old house, and the Traveler defeats him. Under the ninken custom Ryuuji describes, a dog must acknowledge a stronger master, so Kageroumaru now follows the Traveler.
### ULTRA +
Paimon extracts a promise that Kageroumaru will not replace her as best companion. Ryuuji asks why a dog would steal and then protect animals, and Sango answers with her hypothesis (see uncertainties). She tells the Traveler that praise will not earn a discount on future commissions, and says "sentimentality is the enemy of truth". The Ubiquity Net feedback goes to the friend who built it.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two separate groups were behind the disappearances: Treasure Hoarders who held animals in "Dens of Thieves", and the dog Kageroumaru, who kept animals in "Hiding Spots". Kageroumaru's past as a ninken trainer's abandoned pup is revealed only through the letter in the last file. The event itself offers no confirmed reason why he hid the animals.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
Sango is the agency's detective who prefers to think rather than walk; her stance is that her "flesh is an accessory to her mind". Ryuuji is her polite, practical investigator whose skill is running errands, not stealth or combat. He admits being bested by the dog and gives it nicknames such as "Hat Dog" that Paimon dislikes. Katheryne relays the commission. Paimon is awed by the bounty and persuaded by the animals' plight. Kageroumaru is a masked dog who resembles the Komore Teahouse owner Taroumaru; Ryuuji warns that, unlike Taroumaru, nothing is known about him. Ryuuji also mentions Shikanoin of the Tenryou Commission, whom the agency met before and whom he thinks can follow Sango's reasoning.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
The animals are returned to their owners by Ryuuji, the Treasure Hoarders' dog-training plan is exposed, and the Traveler gains Kageroumaru as a reward item. Sango considers the case closed.

Released in Version 2.3 and set after the Inazuma Archon Quest chapter's Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia, which is its only requirement. Not needed for any Archon Quest.

## entry: Kageroumaru
@keys: Kageroumaru, Black Shadow, Hat Dog
@keys2: Sango, Ryuuji, ninken
@version: 2.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Kageroumaru is a scarred, masked dog introduced as the "Black Shadow" in the Bantan Sango Case Files event. He was a weak puppy overlooked when ninja chose ninken, then grew strong. He guards caged small animals and attacks intruders, and finally accepts the Traveler as his master after being defeated. The wiki lists Kageroumaru as an event reward.
