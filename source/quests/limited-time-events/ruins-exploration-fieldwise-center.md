## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 6.1
### LITE
Ruins Exploration: Fieldwise Center was a limited-time Flagship Event in Version 6.1 (7 to 24 November 2025). The Traveler and Paimon return to Sumeru and join friends investigating an undiscovered ruin in the desert that left Layla and a Temple of Silence member named Djer delirious. The investigation ends with a meeting with the Ibis King Thoth, who now speaks through a cat in the sealed place called Aaru.
### STANDARD
The story has two acts. In Return From the Mysterious Realm, Collei, now an Akademiya consultant for an outdoor survival and rescue agency, brings the Traveler to Gandharva Ville, where Layla and Djer describe being swept into the ruin and repeating strange phrases. In Headlong Into the Sands, a team of Cyno, Sethos, Tighnari, Alhaitham, Kaveh and Layla enters the ruin with the Traveler and Paimon, gathers stone slabs and notes, and meets Thoth. The event's home base is the Fieldwise Center, where players start the minigames.
### LARGE +
Besides the mystery, the tone is light: Cyno's puns, Cyrus's tomatoes, Djer's fixation on Ajilenakh Nuts and Paimon's temporary persona as "Paimon Plus" run through both acts. The revelations are narrow: they affect Layla's ancestry and our picture of the Seven Pillars of King Deshret rather than the main story.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Act I, Return From the Mysterious Realm. At the Akademiya the Traveler and Paimon meet Collei and Cyrus; the Akademiya has hired Collei as a consultant for a planned survival-and-rescue agency. They learn that Cyno and Sethos have taken a strange case to Tighnari in Gandharva Ville. Layla was found delirious in the desert, repeating "How many moons are there in the sky?", and Djer, a member of the Temple of Silence found in the same state, repeated "don't eat Ajilenakh Nuts." Both heard a cat. Layla's other self fills in the gap and says the trouble started when Layla touched a device in the ruin. Act II, Headlong Into the Sands. After a two-day wait, the group enters the unknown ruin with Mehrak, who leads Kaveh and Alhaitham. They split up to find stone slabs, read Zandik's notes and meet Thoth, who explains the cat voice, corrects the memory problem, and tells his history.
### LARGE
Act I, Return From the Mysterious Realm. Paimon, newly in "Paimon Plus" mode, goes to the House of Daena for books but diverts to greet Collei and Cyrus. Collei explains the new semester means more field research and the Akademiya wants an agency guiding students on outdoor survival and rescue; the Traveler joins as a helper. In Gandharva Ville, Tighnari treats Layla while Cyno and Sethos explain the incident. Sethos and members of the Temple of Silence rescued Djer from the desert, then found Layla at the same marked spot. Djer's account: he fell through quicksand into an undiscovered ruin, touched something, and was flooded with information until a voice said not to eat Ajilenakh Nuts and a cat guided him out; he also heard "there are those from Tulaytullah who still endure." Layla remembers nothing, and mentions that she sometimes sleepwalks. Sethos, Tighnari and Cyno agree to return to the site in two days. While Paimon fetches coffee, Layla's other self recounts what the first Layla saw: the device, the flood of information, the repeated question, and the cat that led her out.

Act II, Headlong Into the Sands. Alhaitham, Kaveh and Mehrak join Cyno, Sethos, Tighnari, Layla, the Traveler and Paimon. In the ruin the party must find broken slabs and other mechanisms; the Great Vayuvyastra is activated by attacking it. Three stone slabs (priests' records) show Thoth with other Pillars and with Goudarz and Makhaira. Mehrak helps Kaveh and Alhaitham clear dust from slabs with wind. Elsewhere the Traveler, Layla and others collect four Ragged Notes and assemble them into Ordered Notes. The notes describe a student who found the ruin and was trapped; his last page is signed Zandik. The team meets a cat who speaks as Thoth. He explains the questions he put to humans, relates what became of Goudarz and Makhaira, and says the device that harmed Layla and Djer was an old tool of King Deshret for measuring human brain power. He replaced the flood of information with simple repeating phrases to protect them, a side effect that will fade. He asks the Traveler to switch off the device.
### ULTRA +
Details. Kaveh and Alhaitham's team follows Mehrak. Thoth tells the Traveler they are special among the companions. Sethos observes that Thoth's slabs show the Pillars as colleagues of Hermanubis. Cyrus has waited in Aaru Village and later recounts that the Vahumana Darshan has a "mixed record" about the Ibis King. The post-quest vignettes show Cyno, Sethos and Tighnari planning a visit, Alhaitham and Kaveh arguing over dinner, and Layla meeting Nahida and Nilou under the stars.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Thoth, the Ibis King and Scribe of Scribes among King Deshret's Seven Pillars, is imprisoned in Aaru with Sobek and Heryshaf and can occasionally speak through a cat. He explains that, when life became unbearable for the king, Deshret had him, Sobek and Heryshaf locked in Aaru, and that he could be freed if he found humans who gave the correct answers to three questions: what causes our rise and fall, what obliterates love and hate, and what determines truth and falsehood. The questions came from King Deshret, who received them from the Mistress of Flowers. Goudarz said conspiracy and betrayal and received the power to deceive; Makhaira said she had nothing left to lose and received the power to be loved. A third, unnamed woman was tested on the final question; because Thoth is still sealed, her answer must have been wrong. Thoth adds that a young man named Zandik, who lived several centuries ago, refused his third gift, and that Zandik's companion had blue hair and pointed ears, which means Layla is a descendant of Tulaytullah.
### LARGE +
Thoth says Al-Ahmar insisted the Dendro Dragon Sovereign was one of the Pillars but she rejected it, which is why there were seven Pillars with one forever absent. He describes his relationship with Sobek and Heryshaf as chess companions. He admits he tricked Zandik by saying he could not leave without his companion: it is true that only Tulaytullah's descendants may come and go as they please, but anyone can in fact enter or leave, and Thoth made up the second half to stop Zandik leaving his tired companion behind. Thoth also says gifts of former ages, whether in blood or technology, are tools and not things to revere.

## characters

@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Main: the Traveler, Paimon, Collei, Layla, Cyno, Sethos, Tighnari, Alhaitham and Kaveh, with Mehrak. Also: Cyrus (Sumeru), Djer, Thoth (as the cat), and cameos by Nahida and Nilou. Trial characters are used inside the ruin (for example Layla in Act I and several allies in Act II).

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Layla and Djer recover and Layla learns that her ancestry traces to Tulaytullah. The device that harmed them is left for the Traveler or Mehrak to switch off. Thoth remains in Aaru. Cyrus offers to ask friends for a star-related study topic for Layla.
### LARGE +
Players who reached 750 Prep Progress could invite Collei to their party; the story shows her as an Akademiya consultant for the proposed survival and rescue agency.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.1, 7 to 24 November 2025. Entry requires the Prologue Act III Song of the Dragon and Freedom; the Archon Quest An Elegy for Faded Moonlight and Cyno's Lupus Aureus Chapter are recommended. In an epilogue vignette Sethos says he has heard that the Hat Guy is taking care of something in Nod-Krai. It does not change main-story canon.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 6.1
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Three minigames: Mechanical Puzzle-Parsing Paradigm (guiding Puzzlepals with Miraculous Effects), Skillful Scouting (switching between Cuilein An-bot and Bar-drone Bunny on tracks) and Safe Engagement Training (trial objectives that grant buffs before fights). Completing them earned Prep Progress; 750 Prep Progress let players invite Collei to their party, and later tiers included Crown of Insight and a furnishing blueprint.

## entry: Thoth
@keys: Thoth, Ibis King, Scribe of Scribes
@keys2: Aaru, cat, Seven Pillars, Layla, Djer
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In this event the Ibis King speaks through a borrowed cat in the ruin beneath the desert. He is imprisoned in Aaru with Sobek and Heryshaf, is playful, proud of his chess record against his fellow prisoners, and treats ancient gifts as mere tools. He aided Layla and Djer by replacing overwhelming information with simple repeated phrases.

## entry: Thoth's three questions
@keys: Thoth's three questions, three questions
@keys2: Goudarz, Makhaira, Deshret, Thoth
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The three questions are: what causes our rise and fall, what obliterates love and hate, and what determines truth and falsehood. King Deshret gave them to Thoth, having received them from the Mistress of Flowers. Goudarz answered the first with conspiracy and betrayal and was given the power to deceive. Makhaira answered the second with vengeance and was given the power to be loved. The third person is not named.

## entry: Zandik's notes
@keys: Zandik, Ordered Notes, Ragged Notes
@keys2: ruin, Thoth, Layla
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Four Ragged Notes found in the ruin are assembled into the Ordered Notes of a student who found an unrecorded ruin, complained about the Akademiya's attitude to the desert, and wrote about the Seven Pillars before being trapped. The last page is signed Zandik. Thoth later says Zandik was clever, mistrustful and narcissistic, declined his third gift, and was tricked into staying with his companion.

## entry: Layla's lineage and the incident
@keys: Layla, Tulaytullah, other Layla
@keys2: ruin, moons, Djer, Thoth
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Layla was caught in quicksand while stargazing, entered a ruin, touched a device, and was left repeating "How many moons are there in the sky?" with no memory of the event. Her other self remembers it. Thoth tells her that Zandik's companion looked like her, with blue hair and pointed ears, which marks her as descended from Tulaytullah.
