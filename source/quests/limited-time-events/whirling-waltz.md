## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Whirling Waltz is the Version 5.6 spring flagship event. After overhearing old friends at a Court of Fontaine newsstand, the Traveler and Paimon are invited to run the Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland, a film-themed amusement park. Friends from across Teyvat arrive as visitors and consultants, and the story is told in light, character-focused Friendly Encounters rather than a quest chain.
### STANDARD +
The park has three attractions: Eclectic Escapades, Movie Masterpieces and Mellow Manor, plus a Theme Restaurant run by the chef Escoffier. The player upgrades attractions, hires staff and chooses theme dishes to raise revenue. The event's reward was the bow Sequence of Solitude.
### LARGE +
It required Adventure Rank 20, a Fontaine Statue of The Seven and the Prologue Act III, and recommended Incandescent Ode of Resurrection, Animula Choragi Chapter Act I, Dulciaria Structura Chapter Act I, Mushroom Realm Mystery Act III and a Fontaine world quest first. Many staff members are callbacks to earlier quests and events.
### ULTRA +
The park is named after Surintendante Chevalmarin, a friend of Furina's, and carries themed zones for Fontaine, Natlan and Sumeru.

## plot
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon manage the Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland. As the park grows, Varesa from Natlan sets up a fruit stall, Sigewinne and Lynette become consultants for Mellow Manor and Eclectic Escapades, Dehya's mercenary band partners with them, and Furina becomes consultant for Movie Masterpieces and invites the Traveler and Paimon to co-direct a film with Escoffier.
### STANDARD +
**Varesa.** The Natlan orchard owner comes to promote her fruit after a letter from Escoffier. She goes shopping for clockwork parts for Iansan's gym equipment, tries Movie Masterpieces and then opens the Vibrant Orchard stall.

**Sigewinne.** The Fortress of Meropide's head nurse likes animated films and shows them to inmates. She becomes Mellow Manor's consultant after being called away to treat an inmate who tried to copy a film recipe.

**Lynette and Lyney.** Lynette arrives alone because Lyney is exhausted from watching a director's horror films. They coax him to the park, and Lynette consults for Eclectic Escapades.

**Dehya.** She was a guest star in a Fontaine adventure film, and the Blazing Beasts agree to pass desert commissions to the Traveler and Paimon.

**Furina.** The park's patron surveys visitors and becomes consultant for Movie Masterpieces.
### LARGE +
**Varesa in detail.** She says Escoffier's order for her restaurant led to a chance to promote her orchard's fruit. In the Collective of Plenty she leaves a price list, a scale and a coin jar and no money ever goes missing, but Paimon warns Fontainians may not trust unattended stalls. Coach Iansan wants Fontainian clockwork meka parts to improve her gym equipment, and Varesa offers to teach Paimon beginner exercises. She plays Movie Masterpieces, loves the homages to films and comic-book heroes, and asks to be told about any future event so she can help design levels.

**Sigewinne in detail.** She heard about the park from Navia at the Beauty Association. Under the Fortress's new rules, well-behaved inmates can spend Credit Coupons on up to three films a week, and Sigewinne runs the projector herself. She has shown "My Neighbor Kokoro" and the long-running "Dom and Cherry". An inmate attempted the fictional "Magical Mouse Munchies" from three days of welfare meals and gave himself unfamiliar food poisoning; Giustina fetches her, and she returns once the patient is stable. She resolves to speak to Furina and Neuvillette about preventing copycat accidents.

**Lynette and Lyney in detail.** The siblings were invited by a director known for psychological horror. Lyney watched all of the director's films, and lost three nights of sleep. Lynette dislikes jump scares, enjoys Eclectic Escapades and likes watching guests' faces as they fall. She is also looking at animals for magic acts: Freminet's blubberbeasts and the obedient squirrels "Father" has praised. Lyney recovers at Mellow Manor and gets a new idea for an interactive magic show with a moving stage.

**Dehya in detail.** Dehya and fellow Blazing Beasts were cast in an action film; she was dismissed after wrecking half a set, while Uncle Scimitar became a star when the lead actor improvised shooting him instead of a sword fight. At the bar The Rusty Rudder she orders drinks, tells the Traveler they could be hired swords too and offers commissions for the region west of the Hypostyle Desert, north of the Desert of Hadramaveth, east of Ardravi Valley and south of the Ashavan Realm. She gives the Blazing Beasts' motto "We Protect, You Prosper!" and says she will send letters to Adventurers' Guild branches.

**Furina in detail.** She says she was first asked to approve plans for what she expected to be a small event, praises the Traveler and Paimon and says she suggested to several directors that they be named "Pioneers of the Film Industry". She says the park bears the name of her dear friend Surintendante Chevalmarin, so she runs a visitor survey and, wanting others to shine, declines to perform herself. At a dessert tasting she proposes a dramatic film about Escoffier's cooking and invites the Traveler and Paimon to direct it with her.
### ULTRA +
**Attractions.** Eclectic Escapades is a diving and gliding circuit collecting Adventure Coins, with six themed courses based on invented films such as "Journey to the Secret Sea" and "The Clockwork Express". Movie Masterpieces are action challenges with a time-slowing ability, based on invented films such as "The Champion Duelist", "RainbowEye" and "Doctor When". Mellow Manor is an animal enclosure where Cuddly Anemo Slimes, Good Little Hydro Fungi, Voracious Squirrels, Huge Golden Finches, Languorous Blubberbeasts, Placid Alpacas and Contented Capybaras are arranged for satisfaction.

**Business levels.** The park unlocks Mellow Manor at Business Level 2, a Natlan zone (Fire Tribe) at 4, a Sumeru zone (Eremite Camp) at 9 and a Fontaine zone (Classic High Street) at 14. The Theme Restaurant's dishes give temporary bonuses.

**Callbacks.** Staff include Helene, Melies and Petit Lumiere, who ran the three modes of Roses and Muskets, plus Jala, Miss Gardemetiquette and Basima the Fifth from the Dodoco, Lil' Fungi and Bing-Bang Finchball events. The wiki infers from his name and outfit that the staff member Captain Ferment O'Juice is Alphonse, a chef seen in Escoffier's character teaser.

## placement
@version: 5.6
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.6 and run from 9 May to 26 May 2025 (server time) in three phases (9, 11 and 13 May). It is a standalone event set in Fontaine, after the Fontinalia film festival of Roses and Muskets. The next Fontaine summer event, Sunny Summer Fontinalia, comes in Version 6.7.

## entry: Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland
@keys: Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland, Film Fantasyland, Surintendante Chevalmarin
@version: 5.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A film-themed amusement park in Fontaine named after Furina's friend Surintendante Chevalmarin. Its attractions are Eclectic Escapades, Movie Masterpieces and Mellow Manor. The Traveler and Paimon work as managers; Escoffier cooks for the Theme Restaurant.
