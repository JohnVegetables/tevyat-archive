## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### LITE
Shadows Amidst Snowstorms was a limited-time Version 2.3 event (25 November to 13 December 2021) set on Dragonspine during the Adventurers' Guild's winter training camp. The Traveler, Paimon, Amber, Eula and Bennett are troubled by a series of mishaps and by a person who looks exactly like Albedo, and the truth is a mutated whopperflower mimicking him. The story also reunites a boy named Joel with his missing father.
### STANDARD
Act I (The Snowy Past) starts from Paimon's wish for a fruit-juicing machine and moves up Dragonspine, where the Traveler joins a camp run by Cyrus. Act II (The Shadows Deepen) follows a group stuck on the mountain by bad luck, a night at Albedo's camp and a painting session, then an ambush by the impostor Albedo. Act III (A Secret Born From Ashes) is a story-telling session in which Paimon and Albedo share tales; Albedo's, which the Traveler takes to describe real events about the impostor, and the event ends with Joel's reunion and a last scene in Mondstadt that returns to the juicer joke.
### LARGE +
The Fellflower is the event's boss. The event is a Dragonspine character-focus story for Albedo, with significant parts for Eula and Bennett, and is a milder winter counterpart to the earlier event The Chalk Prince and the Dragon.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
Act I, The Snowy Past: Paimon asks the alchemists Timaeus and Sucrose to build a fruit juicer that keeps juice fresh; they say it is beyond alchemy and suggest Albedo, who had just left looking grim. On Dragonspine the Traveler finds Cyrus holding winter training with senior and junior adventurers. Amber has been entertaining Joel, whose father is missing, and Eula, who has never built a snowman, joins them. Cyrus has a spare instructor slot, because Fischl, originally invited, was busy elsewhere, and Eula takes it for the newcomer Gerald. The Traveler meets Albedo, who says he is on the mountain for quiet and to gather Starsilver for paint, and who invites them to learn painting at his camp. His camp has been burgled, his alchemy notes taken; the footprints lead to a cavern and Albedo goes on ahead alone. When the Traveler hears Bennett's voice, they find him caged in a Fatui camp and free him. Albedo has disappeared.

Act II, The Shadows Deepen: Back at Albedo's camp the Traveler and Paimon find Amber, Eula and Bennett have wandered the mountain for days, plagued by avalanches and ill luck. Albedo creates chairs by painting them, cooks dinner and paints Paimon's portrait with the Traveler. The next day an avalanche separates the party and Eula senses something is wrong. In a cutscene Albedo starts to make a Cryo Solar Isotoma and is run through by a second Albedo, who turns into a plant. Eula cuts the thing down, and Albedo explains it is an extremely rare mutated whopperflower and that Dragonspine's dragon-blood-saturated land makes strange life possible.

Act III, A Secret Born From Ashes: The Traveler and Paimon trade stories at Albedo's camp. Paimon tells a horror tale about whopperflowers that replace the people they eat. Albedo tells a longer story about Subject One, a synthetic human who blends in among people, and Subject Two, a failed experiment revived by a dragon's power on a snowy mountain, who copies Subject One and makes a Subject Three, intending to replace him. Albedo says it is just a story, but warns that monsters touched by Durin's blood are, like him, creatures of Rhinedottir. The group returns to the camp, where Cyrus and Pallad present Joserf, Joel's father, found without memory and recovering. A final scene in Mondstadt's alchemy shop shows Albedo again with the mark on his neck and a joke about burying fruit in Dragonspine's snow.
### LARGE +
Details. In Act I, the Traveler's earlier quest Lost in the Snow is a prerequisite, and Gerald, a new adventurer, is paired with Eula. In Act II, Bennett's bad luck is a recurring subject: he flips Treasure Hoarder Insignias hoping to guess wrong and "use up" his misfortune, while Eula admits she does not usually believe in luck but concedes he makes the case. Albedo's chair-painting shows how his alchemy treats paintings as blueprints. Just before the ambush Eula tells the Traveler she senses that something has changed. Albedo says he once ground Mist Flowers into white paint to imitate the sparkle of snowflakes; it worked well but such works need careful storage.
### ULTRA +
Act III small details: Joserf was found by Reckless Pallad after Pallad fell down a slope; Joel recognised him and called him "Dad", at which Joserf began recovering memories of Joel and Joel's mother only. Joel says goodbye to Eula, who gives him powdered rime so his snowman never melts, and gives the Traveler and Paimon a packet too. Cyrus then scolds Pallad for his breaches of discipline.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The "other Albedo" is a plant, not a human: Albedo identifies it as an extremely rare mutated whopperflower, and the event's boss is called the Fellflower. Albedo's second Act III story, framed as fiction, tells of two copies of a synthetic human; he says monsters touched by Durin's blood are creatures of Rhinedottir "just like me". When the Traveler protests that he is no monster he replies that he knows what he is and that he and the Traveler are both "different". In his story the copy erases the mark on its neck as a symbol of imperfection; in the final scene the mark on Albedo's neck briefly vanishes and returns, and the Traveler decides it was a prank.
### LARGE +
Durin is described by Albedo as an artificially created life form whose remains make Dragonspine "a cradle of life's profoundest mysteries". Albedo also muses that creation might be arrogant and asks why creators are called gods; he feels a twinge of grief when thinking of the impostor.

## characters

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
Joel and Joserf leave Dragonspine together; the winter camp ends. The impostor is destroyed. The event's weapon reward was the sword Cinnabar Spindle. No further consequences are shown in the event.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.3 as a Flagship Event; requirements were Adventure Rank 20, Prologue Act II, the Princeps Cretaceus Chapter Act I and Lost in the Snow. It is self-contained and not part of the Archon Quests.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Born of the Snow had players build Puffy Snowmen from components; Dragonspine Special Training offered Agility, Tracker and Combat training; Rise of the Fell Flower was a boss challenge against the Fellflower in which players hit Arcane Fruits back at it and picked up Arcane Essence. Rewards were exchanged for Snowstrider and Vanquisher's Emblems.

## entry: Fellflower
@keys: Fellflower, Fell Flower, mutated whopperflower
@keys2: Albedo, Dragonspine
@version: 2.3
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A very rare mutated whopperflower that took Albedo's appearance and tried to replace him on Dragonspine. Albedo says conditions there are far from typical because dragon blood seeped into the land and passed to monsters by the ley lines, speeding mutation. It is the event's boss: it summons Arcane Fruits and releases Arcane Essence.

## entry: Joel and Joserf
@keys: Joserf
@keys2: Joel, Dragonspine

### STANDARD
Joel is a boy living at the Dragonspine camp whose father had gone missing. In this event Reckless Pallad finds Joserf, who has lost most of his memory but recognises Joel and starts to recover. He survived hunting alone in a cave while his legs healed. They leave together.

### STANDARD
Throughout the event Bennett believes his bad luck rubs off on others and apologises for avalanches and falls. Eula, Amber and the Traveler reassure him it is not his fault. He jokes that guessing a Treasure Hoarder Insignia flip wrongly uses up bad luck. A miracle of misfortune with Pallad ultimately helps reunite Joel with his father.

### STANDARD
Albedo teaches the Traveler to paint at his camp and uses painted blueprints to create chairs through alchemy. The Traveler's portrait of Paimon is poor; Albedo reworks it into a flattering image that Paimon hangs up. He says alchemy can create living beings with enough research.
