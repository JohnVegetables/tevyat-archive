## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 1.3
### LITE
Lantern Rite (2021) was the first Lantern Rite flagship event, in Version 1.3, running from 10 to 28 February 2021. The Traveler and Paimon join Liyue Harbor's preparations for the festival, whose centrepiece this year is a giant Mingxiao Lantern in the shape of the adeptus Skybracer, and end up stopping a suspected arson plot and persuading the aloof Xiao to share a small Lantern Rite of his own.
### STANDARD
The event story is told in three parts, "The Origin of the Lanterns", "Currents Deep Beneath the Lanterns" and "Light Upon the Sea", which together make up the All That Glitters section. In the first the Traveler learns what Lantern Rite is and how Xiao Lanterns are made; in the second a stranger near the lantern materials turns out to be an unrelated, bumbling thief, after help from Xiao and Kaeya's informant; in the third the Traveler and the Wangshu Inn staff bring a miniature festival to Xiao, who still refuses to enter the crowded city. It is a lighthearted event with a gentle character study of Xiao at its core.
### LARGE +
Because the event is limited-time, the 2021 story is canon but not universal knowledge: most people in Teyvat know the festival, not the Traveler's part in it. The giant lantern is dedicated to Skybracer, an adeptus who in Madame Ping's account gave his life holding up Mt. Tianheng. Xiao hears about this from the Traveler and does not go to see it, though the closing scene suggests he hears the wishes of the lanterns all the same.
### ULTRA +
Lantern Rite later returned as a flagship event (Version 2.4 with Fleeting Colors in Flight, and again in Version 6.3 with A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon), each time with a different part of Liyue's celebration at its centre.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
Part I, The Origin of the Lanterns: Verr Goldet of Wangshu Inn explains that Lantern Rite is held on the first full moon of the first month to honour the heroes of the past, and that the lanterns guide them home. She quietly asks the Traveler to try to bring Xiao along. In Liyue Harbor the Traveler meets festival organiser Wang'ya, eats Grilled Tiger Fish at Yi'nian's stall, hears Madame Ping's story of Skybracer, and learns from Jingming how to make a Xiao Lantern.

Part II, Currents Deep Beneath the Lanterns: the Traveler gathers rare Plaustrite (Azurite and Vermillionite), notices a suspicious figure near the lantern materials, reports it to the Ministry of Civil Affairs, clears hilichurls for the Millelith, and with Xiao's help and a tip from Kaeya's informant finds the "plot" to be a clumsy plan to steal the giant floating Plaustrite.

Part III, Light Upon the Sea: Xiao still declines to visit the city, so Verr Goldet, Huai'an and the Traveler build a small stall below Wangshu Inn, serve Almond Tofu and Grilled Tiger Fish and hold a miniature Lantern Rite for him. He eats with them, walks the Traveler partway to the city, and stops at its edge.
### LARGE +
Part I in more detail. At the harbor a merchant, Bolai, makes a joke about buying Paimon and mentions that the lantern's lifting stone is the largest Plaustrite in twenty years. A girl called Changchang says she gave her ragdoll to the lantern because the doll wished to see the sky, and Jingming confirms that every contribution, even a symbolic one, is welcomed because the lantern is a community project. Jiangzhou explains that the Xiao Market exists to raise money for the lantern, with Mora and Plaustrite as the main donations, so no stall is meant to profit. Wang'ya sends the Traveler to Yi'nian with a Ministry document of tips from last year's festival, which Yi'nian says he will burn. The Traveler returns with the Xiao Lantern Wang'ya promised as a gift, and Paimon proposes making one for Xiao to cheer him up.

Part II in more detail. Wang'ya asks for the rarer types of Plaustrite; they are carved with wishes and donated. Back at the construction site beside the dock the Traveler and Paimon spot a man crouching near piles of paper, oil and wood, which Paimon fears are fuel for arson. Jiayi at the Ministry says a specialist, Xinghuo, is away at Guili Plains; the Traveler goes there, clears a hilichurl blockade, and Xinghuo, who has been chasing a thief, promises extra vigilance. Xiao then appears and leaves a symbol that he says the suspect dropped, warns the Traveler that the threat comes from the mortal world and so is theirs to handle, and says that if he intervened the streets might run red. Kaeya recognises the symbol as that of the Treasure Hoarders and arranges a meeting at Windrise with an informant, Nimrod. Nimrod doubts the Treasure Hoarders would risk so large a plan, and points out a branch that received an anonymous letter. At that camp Xiao appears from nowhere, which frightens the hoarders Bei'er and Crafty into handing over the letter. The two disguise themselves and go to the meeting place where the stranger turns out to be an apprentice thief hoping to impress the Treasure Hoarders. The Millelith take him away.

Part III in more detail. Verr Goldet and her colleague Huai'an are sure it would be easier to bring the festival to Xiao than Xiao to the festival. They pick a quiet spot below the inn, Huai'an builds a rough stall and decorates it with a Xiao Lantern, and Smiley Yanxiao cooks the food from the inn's pantry. When the Traveler brings Xiao down he is puzzled but eats with them. Afterwards he accompanies the party along the road toward the harbor at Paimon's request, stopping at the outskirts.
### ULTRA +
Minor beats. Paimon's order of Grilled Tiger Fish is explained as a way of honouring Pervases, who loved it. When the Traveler writes a wish on the Plaustrite it is for their sibling, a moment Paimon treats with tenderness and a promise to help look for them. Kaeya gives the password phrase "wine begets wisdom" for the informant, who then accepts a slightly wrong version because it is close enough. Xiao refuses to promise to attend but says he will answer a call for help at any time, and describes hearing "all cries for help, all prayers for peace, and all the wishes carried by the lanterns".

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
Madame Ping's account of Skybracer: the giant lantern is shaped after him, a flamboyant adeptus whose antlers were made of the essence of Rex Lapis's power and were Liyue's hardest material. In the fateful battle between gods, mountains began to collapse onto villagers below, so Skybracer had a friend cut off his antlers and used them to prop up the mountain. He fought on until his blood ran out. Mt. Tianheng still stands because of this, and his blood became the Bishui River. She frames this as a story and says the point of Lantern Rite is that people come together instead of staying alone.
### LARGE +
Xiao's inner conflict is the real revelation of the event. He says he dislikes the rabble because they are carefree and joyful, "nothing like me", and that after so long killing and carrying the burden of suffering he would only feel worse among them. Verr Goldet's description of the festival supplies the other half: the lanterns are believed to act as beacons guiding bygone heroes back to their homeland.
### ULTRA +
Nothing in the event identifies who first ties the symbol to the Treasure Hoarders beyond Kaeya's remark, and the event's "crisis" is resolved as a misunderstanding; the only real danger is the thief's incompetence. The Plaustrite is described as the main component of any Mingxiao Lantern, a floating stone, which is also the reason local gemstone merchants prize Azurite and Vermillionite so highly.

## characters

@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
Main: the Traveler and Paimon, Xiao, Kaeya (by his advice and informant), Madame Ping (Skybracer's story), Verr Goldet and Huai'an of Wangshu Inn, Smiley Yanxiao (the inn's chef). ### LARGE +
Playable characters who take part in the Stand by Me mini-game, as invitable party members, are Beidou, Chongyun, Ningguang, Xiangling, Xingqiu and Xinyan; none of them appears in the story quests themselves.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
The thief is handed to the Millelith and the festival goes ahead. The quest line ends before the lantern's release; the event's Mingxiao Convergence cinematic shows the giant lantern and the Xiao Lanterns rising on Lantern Rite night. Xiao has eaten at a small festival of his own and walks with the Traveler to the city's edge, but never enters it; the closing thread is that he will hear any call for help. The Xiao Market closes after the event with its rewards (a Crown of Insight and the "Celebration: Lantern-Light" namecard).

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.3 as a Flagship Event and set during the first Lantern Rite after the Traveler arrives in Liyue. It required Adventure Rank 23, the Archon Quest Chapter I Act I (Of the Land Amidst Monoliths) and the first act of Xiao's Story Quest (Alatus Chapter), whose Adventure Rank requirement was lowered for the event. It precedes the 2.4 Lantern Rite (Fleeting Colors in Flight) and the 6.3 Lantern Rite (A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon), both in the same series.
### LARGE +
A similar earlier version of the event was run in the second closed beta; some leftover beta text survives in the tutorial (see uncertainties).

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 1.3
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Event period 10 to 28 February 2021 (Xiao Market until 7 March), with the three story parts unlocking on 10, 14 and 18 February. Five sections: All That Glitters (the three quests above), Lantern Rite Tales (requests for Festive Fever and Primogems), Theater Mechanicus (a domain and mini-game earning Peace Talismans), the Xiao Market (spending Peace Talismans on materials, a Crown of Insight and the namecard Celebration: Lantern-Light) and Stand by Me (1,000 Peace Talismans invite one of six 4-star Liyue characters). Festive Fever progresses in three stages: We've Only Just Begun, Guests Flock From Afar, Glow of a Thousand Lanterns. During the event Xiao Lanterns could be crafted and released.

## entry: Mingxiao Lantern (2021)
@keys: Mingxiao Lantern, Mingxiao Convergence
@version: 1.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Liyue's giant lantern, released into the sky on Lantern Rite night as a joint effort of the city's people and the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Its lift comes from a huge floating Plaustrite, the largest in twenty years in 2021, chained at the Xiao Market. The 2021 design honours the adeptus Skybracer, with a stag-like figure whose antlers were a source of pride.
### LARGE +
Money and Plaustrite are donated by citizens, who often carve wishes into the stone. Even a symbolic gift such as a ragdoll is accepted, since contributions are believed to bring good luck.

## entry: Skybracer's story
@keys: Skybracer
@keys2: Lantern Rite, Mingxiao Lantern, Madame Ping, Tianheng
@version: 1.3
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Madame Ping describes Skybracer as a show-off, proud of his antlers, which were made from Rex Lapis's essence and were the hardest thing in Liyue. During a battle of the gods he gave them up, using the blood-soaked antlers as a wedge to hold up a collapsing Mt. Tianheng, and fought on until his life ran out. The Bishui River is said to be his blood. She tells the tale as a story whose truth the listener may decide.

## entry: Xiao's refusal and the small festival
@keys: Xiao Lantern Rite
@keys2: Wangshu Inn, Verr Goldet, Huai'an
@version: 1.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Xiao will not attend Lantern Rite, saying he dislikes crowds, and especially not at this time of year. The innkeepers and the Traveler instead set up a small stall with Xiao Lanterns, Almond Tofu and Grilled Tiger Fish below Wangshu Inn. He eats with them without enthusiasm, saying he does not understand human motives, and walks to the city's edge before turning back. He tells the Traveler he hears all calls for help in Liyue.
### LARGE +
His stated reason, in the third part, is that carefree, joyful people are nothing like him and being among them would only remind him of the suffering he carries from slaying demons.

## entry: The Plaustrite plot
@keys: Plaustrite thief, Treasure Hoarder letter
@keys2: Xinghuo, Nimrod, Bei'er, Crafty
@version: 1.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A suspicious man lingering near the lantern supplies is feared to be an arsonist. Trails lead via Xinghuo of the Millelith, a symbol left behind that Kaeya identifies as the Treasure Hoarders', and an anonymous letter in the hands of Bei'er and Crafty, to a meeting where the sender turns out to be an apprentice thief who only wanted to steal the giant Plaustrite by cutting its tethers and floating it out to sea. The Millelith arrest him.
