## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon is the Version 7.1 Moonchase Festival flagship event (24 September to 12 October 2026). Back at Yilong Wharf, Lan Yan of the Lan clan and the Traveler meet a seemingly eccentric old man who turns out to be the remnant soul of her clan's ancestor, Lan Junming, the Wind Reader. He is searching for the Moonchase he promised to share every year with the adeptus Silverwing, a promise that was never kept. The event also includes a lighthearted World Food Fair story, "Food Street Must Be Saved!".
### STANDARD
This year's Moonchase is organised by Chenyu Vale's associations and replaces the Masterful Chefs contest with a World Food Fair featuring cooks from four nations. While the Millelith and the Lan clan's thaumaturges fight evil spirits stirred up by the festival, Lan Yan and the Traveler help Lan Junming, a remnant soul bound to a rattan figure with a seal he placed himself, recover his lost memory over several days: a poem, a Moon-Viewing Gathering, a promise made with Silverwing. Zhongli reveals what became of her, and Lan Yan performs a final art so that he can rest. Meanwhile, Xiangling, Escoffier and the food-street cooks solve a dispute over Wangxiao Cakes.
### LARGE +
The story is told in a prologue ("Another Auspicious Moonlit Night Rolls Around"), three parts and an epilogue ("As One Might Stride Betwixt the Clouds", "On White Wings Pierce Through Cloud and Fog", "Having Met You, My Heart is Gladdened", "Next Year's Moon, Just as Bright") plus the Wondrous Anecdotes chapter. Recommended earlier quests include Dulciaria Structura Chapter Act I, Tapirus Somniator Chapter Act I and A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon (6.3), which revives Zibai.
### ULTRA +
The event's title echoes the line "Through autumn skies, the windborne silverwing chases the moon" from a poem Hu Tao recounts at the Moon-Viewing Gathering, and the epilogue's title, "Next Year's Moon, Just as Bright", matches the closing promise of a yearly food duel. The event ends with the idea that Moonchase events change every year while food and friends remain.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Prologue: Xiangling invites the Traveler and Paimon to Yilong Wharf for the World Food Fair. Escoffier is there for Fontaine, Ningguang has come on official business and Lan Yan shows the Traveler around.

Part I: Lan Yan and the Traveler meet an old man repeating that "Moonchase ought not to be like this". He is a remnant soul in a rattan figure. Tracing Zibai from Baiju Guesthouse to Iron Tongue Tian's storytelling, they find her with Zhongli, and she identifies him as Lan Junming, the Wind Reader, and Zhongli suggests the Moon-Viewing Gathering at Wangshu Inn as the Moonchase he remembers.

Part II: Travelling toward Wangshu Inn they fight possessed monsters with Hu Tao, Xingqiu, Chongyun and Gaming. At the gathering Lan Junming remembers his promise to Silverwing.

Part III: They try to summon Silverwing but she does not come. Zhongli explains her fate, and Lan Yan performs a final art that frees Lan Junming's soul.

Epilogue: Ningguang thanks Lan Yan, swallows are calmer, and the Traveler enjoys the food street.
### LARGE +
The five parts and the food chapter are described in separate entries.
### ULTRA +
Event gameplay is listed in the gameplay section. The three event world quests are Little Figure Exorcism, Battle Beneath the Spirit-Quelling Banner and Sigil Barrage Kaleidoscope.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lan Junming (the Wind Reader, also called Mad Lan) was a thaumaturge counted among the Eight Adepts. With the Red Runt (Wuwang Kid), whose Qihuang Arts he borrowed, he created a ward that repelled evil spirits. Silverwing, the Jade-Plumed Silverwing, carried sigils bearing the ward over Liyue so that he could draw on her adeptal power; each year at the Moonchase Festival the two promised to meet and renew the sigils. He used the forbidden Soul-Binding Art on himself to be able to keep the appointment. Silverwing, born from the spirit of heaven and earth, was embodied in swallows; losses on the long journeys wiped away her memory and mind, and in the end the swallows forgot their promise.
### LARGE +
Lan Junming's remnant soul had awakened before, as Lan Yu's family stories suggest. At the end he learns that the failure was not his and is helped to leave. The ward and technique he created became the evil-dispelling art every Liyue thaumaturge now learns, and the Lan clan's tradition of teaching anyone who sincerely asks comes from him.
### ULTRA +
Lan Junming never married, according to Lan Yu, and the present Lan clan descends from his two brothers; he returned in later years to Chenyu Vale and taught them everything. Zhongli compares Silverwing's fate to the Ship of Theseus.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lan Junming's remnant soul is released and returns to the Ley Lines. Lan Yan's art, completed at the pavilion east of Teatree Slope, suppresses evil spirits across Liyue, shortens the Millelith's operations and calms the swallows, which should return to nest in Chenyu Vale. Lan Yan finds a "responsibility" of her own, and her clan elders praise her. The Chenyu Vale Confectionery Guild president accepts a new Wangxiao Cake and funds more batches. Xiangling and Escoffier agree to hold a culinary duel at Yilong Wharf every year.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Released in Version 7.1 as a Flagship Event for the Moonchase Festival, running from 24 September 2026. Requirements were Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III (Song of the Dragon and Freedom). It takes place after the Frost Moon's return and the revival of Zibai in A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon (6.3), who is by now living at Baiju Guesthouse, and the World Food Fair brings cooks from Mondstadt, Fontaine and Sumeru to Yilong Wharf.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 7.1
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Three modes: The Great Chase and Dispelling (steer a rattan figure to collect adeptal power and dispel evil energy), Banner Against the Dark (defeat enemies and damage a boss for a score) and Spiritshot Salvo (fire Spiritshots with Sigils and spells). Players can also take Festive Priorities and Wondrous Anecdotes quests. Accumulating Festive Cheer (up to 1,200) gives the sword Silver Light at 200, Primogems, Tea-Scented Tassel materials, a Crown of Insight and furnishing blueprints. The story is Festive Priorities; the Food Street chapter is the Wondrous Anecdotes.

## entry: Prologue - Another Auspicious Moonlit Night Rolls Around
@keys: Another Auspicious Moonlit Night Rolls Around
@keys2: World Food Fair, Xiangling, Escoffier
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Xiangling writes inviting the Traveler to Yilong Wharf. Chenyu Vale's associations organise this year's Moonchase, and with the Lan clan's elders' agreement the Masterful Chefs contest has been replaced by a World Food Fair with representatives from Mondstadt, Fontaine and Sumeru as well as Liyue. Escoffier represents Fontaine. Ningguang says Moonchase has always been shaped by the people, that new customs are folded in and later called tradition, and that she is there on official business: around this time evil forces gather and possess monsters, so the Millelith have mobilised and the Lan clan's thaumaturges will help.

## entry: Part I - As One Might Stride Betwixt the Clouds
@keys: As One Might Stride Betwixt the Clouds
@keys2: Lan Junming, Lan Yan, Soul-Binding Art
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lan Yan, who chose the mystic arts as a hobby rather than a duty, shows Paimon and the Traveler around and mentions she is studying a swallow-related art. They meet an old man who keeps saying "Moonchase ought not to be like this", disliking the food street and calling Liyue changed. Lan Yan recognises the Soul-Binding Art, a forbidden clan technique that traps part of a soul in a rattan effigy. A seal blocks her from undoing it. He asks for help finding the Moonchase he remembers, which has nothing to do with cooking, and says Lan Yan's art steadies him. They think of Zibai at Baiju Guesthouse. At Iron Tongue Tian's, Zibai and Zhongli discuss the Moonchase Rite; Zibai recognises him as Mad Lan, the Wind Reader, Lan Junming. She says the technique was invented by Lan Junming himself, and the seal is his own work, so brute force would disperse his soul. Zhongli proposes a Moon-Viewing Gathering at Wangshu Inn in two days. Lan Junming accepts the title "Grandmaster".
### LARGE +
Lan Yan explains that when a dignitary like Ningguang visits, many clan elders come and etiquette is strict, so she greeted one uncle and left. She also tells Paimon the clan rule that children choose whether the mystic arts are a duty or a hobby, which began when the founder, the Wind Reader, answered his descendants' dilemma with "Why not both?". Lan Junming himself asks that the elders not fuss over him.

## entry: Part II - On White Wings Pierce Through Cloud and Fog
@keys: On White Wings Pierce Through Cloud and Fog
@keys2: Wind Reader, Wangshu Inn, Moon-Viewing Gathering, Jiangxue
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Two days later Lan Yan decides to walk the mountain paths with Lan Junming. Near Dihua Marsh they find Gaming, Hu Tao, Xingqiu and Chongyun besieged by possessed monsters; evil spirits born from fallen gods move from monster to monster. Lan Yan organises a spirit-quelling banner and rattan figures to sweep up the spirits. Lan Junming asks after a Tianheng Sect and a Yunlai Sect and finds the martial and thaumaturgic schools of Liyue unlike his memory. At Wangshu Inn Xiao and the fisherman Jiangxue (successor of Fish Creel, the Yunlai Angler) appear, and Xiangling and Smiley Yanxiao provide the Moon-Viewing Gathering's food. Hu Tao recounts a poetry contest in which Wuwang Kid beat Lan Junming with "Through autumn skies, the windborne silverwing chases the moon...". Lan Junming recalls Silverwing: during the Archon War he combined Qimen techniques with the Red Runt's Qihuang Arts to make a ward and inscribed it on sigils for her to carry. They promised to meet each year at Moonchase and renew the art, "seeing off the autumn swallow". The seal can only be lifted when the art is performed once more. They agree on a pavilion east of Teatree Slope two days later.
### LARGE +
Lan Junming says the Eight Adepts first met by chance at a roadside tea stop; Hu Tao proposes a modern "New Eight" and several joke titles. Xiao says he remembers most Moon-Viewing Gatherings and that their customs have stayed the same for centuries. Jiangxue refuses to join because his ancestor ordered the family to avoid Mad Lan.

## entry: Part III - Having Met You, My Heart is Gladdened
@keys: Having Met You, My Heart is Gladdened
@keys2: Silverwing, Lan Junming, Zhongli
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lan Yu, head of the Lan clan, tells the story of his ancestor: unmarried, he brought his learning home and taught his brothers, and later Moonchase stories speak of a strange man appearing in Yilong Wharf each year. At the pavilion Lan Yan learns the swallow-pattern art that once called Silverwing and, with the Traveler, gathers adeptal energy to reveal the pattern on the water. Silverwing does not come. The art is weakening. The Traveler and Paimon ask the adepti at Mt. Aocang. Nobody knows where she is, except Zhongli, who explains that she was born from the spirit of heaven and earth and that the swallows were her embodiments. In the Archon War she flew between Chenyu Vale and Guyun Stone Forest, resting at Thousand Winds Temple, and swallows died on every trip. She lost pieces of her mind and memory with them, until the swallows forgot their promise to Lan Junming. He compares her to a ship all of whose parts have been replaced.

Lan Yan decides to tell Lan Junming the truth and lift the seal. She performs a recreated version of the art at night, while Lan Junming sings of his promise and of his love of the scenery he saw in her eyes. The seal opens, his remnant soul returns to the Ley Lines, and Zibai and Zhongli say he was a rare character who found peace.
### LARGE +
Zhongli had tried to tell him before, but he could not let go. Lan Junming says he never wanted eternity, only to bid her farewell. Lan Yan answers that the art they made together now lives across Liyue in every thaumaturge's evil-dispelling art, and sings a farewell. He says Liyue was always like this and wishes them to find their own path.

## entry: Epilogue - Next Year's Moon, Just as Bright
@keys: Next Year's Moon, Just as Bright
@keys2: Lan Yan, Ningguang, Zhongli
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Ningguang thanks Lan Yan before the Lan elders, saying that an unseen force suppressed the evil spirits across Liyue and let the Millelith finish early and go home for the festival. The elders praise her ability, and she says Hu Tao and Chongyun helped. Lan Yan is rescued from her elders by the Traveler. Zhongli says the swallows seem less frantic. The group goes to the food street. Lan Yan says Moonchase events change yearly but Xiangling and Escoffier will hold a yearly duel at Yilong Wharf, so good food and good friends are guaranteed.

## entry: Food Street Must Be Saved!
@keys: Food Street Must Be Saved, Wangxiao Cake, Celestial Tea Crisp
@keys2: Escoffier, Xiangling, Chenyu Vale Confectionery Guild
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Wondrous Anecdotes chapter with three quests: The Chefs From a Foreign Land, Deliciousness Knows No Borders and A Taste Beyond Time. The Fontainian chefs Savanna and Escoffier and Liyue's Smiley Yanxiao and Xiangling test each other's cooking until the president of the Chenyu Vale Confectionery Guild demands every stall sell Five-Nut Wangxiao Cakes. The cooks and friends team up to bake a better one, the Celestial Tea Crisp, using Glaze Lily nectar and tea extract. A taste of the authentic Five-Nut cake moves him because it recalls childhood and sharing food with family, but he judges the Celestial Tea Crisp a cut above and the Guild pays for more batches. Zibai, passing by, recalls Fullmoon Cakes she ate thousands of years ago.

## entry: Soul-Binding Art
@keys: Soul-Binding Art
@keys2: Lan clan, Lan Junming, rattan figure
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A Lan clan technique that traps part of a person's soul in a rattan effigy to create a puppet the caster commands. Later generations banned it. Lan Junming used it on himself to leave a remnant soul able to keep a yearly appointment, adding a seal to bind the technique. The remnant soul slowly fades and can only be released when the art is performed once more.

## entry: Silverwing (Jade-Plumed Silverwing)
@keys: Jade-Plumed Silverwing, Silverwing swallows
@keys2: Lan Junming, Moonchase, swallow
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
An adeptus born of the energy of heaven and earth whose living manifestations were Liyue's swallows. She carried Lan Junming's wards across the land during the Archon War and kept a yearly promise to meet him at Moonchase. The toll of the journeys wiped away her mind and memory, and the swallows now fly Liyue by instinct. The wiki counts her among the Eight Adepts.
