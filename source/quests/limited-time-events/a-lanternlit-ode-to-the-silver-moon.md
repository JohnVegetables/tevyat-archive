## summary
@availability: permanent
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LITE
A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon is the Lantern Rite story of Version 6.3 (Luna IV), told in three acts and an epilogue: The Realm of Clouds, The Realm of Rocks, The Realm of People and White Horse's Leisurely Journey. In Liyue Harbor the Traveler touches a broken jade token at an antiques exhibition, meets a fragment of the legendary White Horse Adeptus, Zibai, and learns from Zhongli that Zibai died long ago and that her soul survives in three Deadly Selves. Over several days the Traveler and Paimon help gather the Selves, and on Lantern Rite night Zibai is revived, confused but alive.
### STANDARD
A curious eclipse, a photograph and an exhibition of Chasm artifacts lead the Traveler to the first of Zibai's three Selves. The second, Zizhi, possesses people who voice wishes and shows the memory of Lang-Gan's destruction; the third, Ziqiao, is won over by a day's sightseeing with Zhongli's help. On Lantern Rite night Zibai awakens. The epilogue is a lighter tour of Liyue with the recovering adeptus.
### LARGE +
The story quest was released with the Lantern Rite flagship event of Luna IV (30 January to 23 February 2026) and remains playable afterwards from the Quest Overview, under Event Quests, where it is started by enabling Focused Experience Mode. Players are recommended to have completed the Song of the Welkin Moon finale (True Moon) because the plot builds on the return of the Frost Moon and on events in Nod-Krai.
### ULTRA +
Zibai becomes a playable character in the same version, and continues in Liyue in later content; by Version 7.1 she is living at Baiju Guesthouse and appears in the Moonchase Festival event Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon. The residence at Baiju Guesthouse begins in this quest's epilogue.

## plot
@availability: permanent
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I, The Realm of Clouds: in Liyue Harbor for Lantern Rite, the Traveler hears about a partial eclipse and legends of the White Horse Adeptus, and at an antiques exhibition touches a broken jade token that carries them to a dream space where a woman tells them the way home. Zhongli explains that she is Zibai's First Deadly Self.

Act II, The Realm of Rocks: Zibai's Second Self, Zizhi, possesses Lu Xiangxiang, then Gaming, then Mountain Shaper. The adepti plan to force her out, and the Traveler wins her trust and sees the memory of Lang-Gan's destruction.

Act III, The Realm of People: Zhongli persuades the Third Self, Ziqiao, to take a day's tour of Liyue; she gives up her fragment, and on Lantern Rite night the Traveler and Zhongli revive Zibai.

Epilogue: amnesic Zibai tours Liyue with the Traveler and Paimon.
### LARGE +
The four chapters are detailed in their own entries: Act I follows the antiques exhibition and the dream space; Act II the possession of Lu Xiangxiang, Gaming and Mountain Shaper by Zizhi; Act III Ziqiao's day in Liyue Harbor and the Lantern Rite revival; the epilogue Zibai's first days back, with the tea errand, Qiaoying and Qingce villages and Wangshu Inn.

### ULTRA +
Further details. Qiqi's recollection of Baizhu's patients and the stall's Xiao Lanterns frame the eclipse as a communal worry. At the exhibition Zhongli asks Yun Jin for opera lyrics in return for the silk painting, which he says is his own copy; Beidou buys a bronze war chariot for 90 million Mora to show Ningguang; the Six Ritual Jades set is missing two pieces; and the Traveler and Zhongli learn that Zhongli had helped the exhibition because he sensed Zizhi in a jade carriage. The adepti's reaction to the Selves is explained by Madame Ping: Zizhi seems to guide those she possesses to fulfil their own wishes, not to harm them. Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver were in Liyue Harbor because Rex Lapis had asked the adepti to keep watch. In Zizhi's prison the Traveler outruns the river of time, a symbol of the curse of time. In Act III Zhongli describes Ziqiao as the most free-spirited of the Selves and says she aided him in the Archon War a thousand years earlier before retreating to the mountains; she tells the Traveler at the end that she had decided to help from her first sight of Liyue Harbor. In the epilogue Paimon likens Zibai's three temperaments to a hot-tempered Pyro Slime, a mathematical Hydro Slime and a mischievous Anemo Slime. Lu Weng's gift of Sunglo Tea and the news that the guesthouse's old name stays close the epilogue.

## revelations
@availability: permanent
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The White Horse Adeptus is Zibai, whom Lang-Gan worshipped as the God of Soil and Grain. According to Zhongli she died long ago, and Istaroth preserved the shards of her soul in her three severed Deadly Selves: Ziju (Heavenly Soul, Taiguang), held in the shadowmoon; Zizhi (Earth Soul, Shuangling), who slept beneath the Chasm; and Ziqiao (Human Soul, Youjing), who helped Zhongli in the Archon War.

### LARGE +
The false moon: the shadowmoon is a projection that hung in the sky for thousands of years until the true Frost Moon returned. Zhongli says it is disintegrating bit by bit, appearing as a black shadow over the moon, and suspects that people's wishes trigger the bouts of collapse. He expects the mass release of wish lanterns at Lantern Rite to bring it down, which will open the cage around the First Self; that is the moment to act, and also the moment when everything could be lost. The First Self and the shadowmoon have merged, so when the shadow goes she will cease to exist unless the other two souls are gathered.

The memory of Lang-Gan, shown by Zizhi: Zibai taught the people to grow grain, spin and weave, build houses and cast bronze, and descended on festival nights to hear wishes. Then the moons darkened, two shattered, and a Celestial Nail appeared over Lang-Gan. Istaroth told Zibai that places tainted by the Abyss must be purified, that she was only an emissary and had no reason for guilt. Zibai said that, as their god, she would die among them. Her Jadelight Canopy held the Nail back, giving people time to flee; Zibai fell, Istaroth caught her and told her she had done enough, and split her soul into the Three Deadly Selves. The survivors, descendants of whom now live across Liyue, looked up at the one moon that remained. Zizhi says war had spread cursed remnants from the heavens over the earth, and that she wished she had given them a little more time.
### ULTRA +
Other revelations. Zhongli's idea that wishes trigger the shadowmoon's collapse is based on timing: the Traveler's vision began just as Linlang spoke of her own wish at the exhibition. The Moonchase Rite, an adeptal ritual in which Rex Lapis tried to contact the moon (reported by Ganyu from Cloud Retainer), is understood to have been an attempt to reach the White Horse Adeptus all along. The ancient poem found on the Chasm artifacts, called "Odes of Lang: Zibai", is an ode of blessing to the maiden of the moon, naming the Eternal, Iridescent and Frost moons and what she grants each: silk, fibre for cloth, and tools to divine one's fate; then farming, harvest and wine offered to her. In the epilogue Zibai recalls the god whose remains are sealed at Wuwang Hill, known in opera as Tao Dou, as Bune, god of the underworld, a name Paimon does not know.

## consequences
@availability: permanent
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zibai is revived, confused and partly forgetful, with the temperaments of all three Selves. She takes a long-term room at Baiju Guesthouse, charged to the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, and enjoys Liyue. Lu Xiangxiang decides to stay with the archaeological team. Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver are banned from Mt. Aocang for three days for the mint plan.

### LARGE +
After the tour she is staying at Baiju Guesthouse, treats the Traveler and Paimon as her "guides", and appears in later Liyue content, including Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon (7.1).

## placement
@availability: permanent
@version: 6.3

Released in Version 6.3 (Luna IV, 14 January 2026) as the story of the Lantern Rite flagship event (30 January to 23 February 2026). ## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Four modes: Vigorous Brushstrokes (a runner stage collecting jade), Wishes Contended, Fortunes Won (combat for Charm Coins), The Moonlit Adeptus's Trail (the Traveler plays an adeptus fighting Zhanzhus in a storyteller's tale) and Bright Moon, Lantern Glow (placing Xiao Lanterns and trimming Auspicious Treasure Trees). Completing the story quests gives Primogems and materials. The story quest remains available after the event, though event gameplay does not.

## entry: Zibai's Three Deadly Selves
@keys: Three Deadly Selves, Deadly Selves, Ziju, Zizhi, Ziqiao
@keys2: Zibai, White Horse Adeptus
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zhongli's account: Zibai severed the practitioner's Three Deadly Selves, impure aspects of mind, before she died. Istaroth preserved Zibai's shattered soul in them. Ziju (Heavenly Soul, Taiguang) was in the shadowmoon, Zizhi (Earth Soul, Shuangling) slept in the Chasm and possesses people who make wishes, and Ziqiao (Human Soul, Youjing) lived freely in the mountains. When all three souls were gathered Zibai could be restored.
### LARGE +
Ziqiao is the most worldly and teases mortals; Zizhi is angry and sorrowful; Ziju is the closest to Zibai's own nature. After the revival Zibai shows all three temperaments in turn.

## entry: Memory of Lang-Gan
@keys: Lang-Gan destruction, Lang-Gan memory, Celestial Nail Lang-Gan
@keys2: Zibai, Zizhi
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The destruction of Lang-Gan as seen through Zizhi: worshippers once lit lanterns at an altar like Chiwang Terrace and prayed to the maiden on the moon, who taught farming, silk and bronze; then the moons dimmed, a Celestial Nail appeared and Istaroth said the Abyss-tainted land had to be purified. Zibai interposed her Jadelight Canopy to give her people time, was caught as she fell, and her soul was split. The survivors looked up at the single moon left.

## entry: Odes of Lang: Zibai
@keys: Odes of Lang, An Ancient Poem
@keys2: Zibai, Lang-Gan, Yun Jin
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The text on the Chasm artifacts, first called "An Ancient Poem". It praises a maiden descending from the Eternal, Iridescent and Frost moons who blesses silk, fibre and divination plants, teaches ploughing and sowing, and receives wine and a wish at harvest. Ziqiao (in character as the White Horse Adeptus) explains it to Yun Jin, and the people of Liyue Harbor recite it on Lantern Rite night as lanterns rise.

## entry: Lu Xiangxiang and the Lu family
@keys: Lu Xiangxiang, Lu Weng
@keys2: Zibai, Baiju Guesthouse, Zizhi
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A young archaeologist from a Qiaoying Village tea-growing family whose grandfather Lu Weng runs Baiju Guesthouse, named for the White Horse Adeptus who saved one of his ancestors. She helped excavate Lang-Gan relics in the Chasm, was possessed by Zizhi and overturned tables and injured two customers at Wanmin Restaurant, and recovered with no memory of it. Her family grounded her by making her raise a tea tree; Zibai replaces the seedling overnight and the family treats it as a miracle.

## entry: The White Horse Adeptus legends
@keys: Gold-Eyed Celadon Mare, Daughter of Time
@keys2: White Horse Adeptus, Zibai, Xingqiu
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Xingqiu lists the competing accounts: Hu Tao's reading as the Gold-Eyed Celadon Mare of the Eight Adepts; the elders of Qingce who say a white horse leapt from a spring and aided Rex Lapis in the Archon War; some Chenyu Vale tribes who call her the Daughter of Time; and the ancient texts that make her an emissary who carries wishes to the heavens. Madame Ping says the White Horse Adeptus always avoided the other adepti in the Archon War and she never knew why.

## entry: Moonchase Rite
@keys: Moonchase Rite
@keys2: Zibai, Cloud Retainer, Ganyu
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Ganyu reports Cloud Retainer's understanding of the ritual that gave the Moonchase Festival its name. Before it became a festival it was an adeptal rite performed by Rex Lapis to communicate with the moon, which never answered. Seeing Keqing's photo of the dark spot on the moon, Cloud Retainer concluded that the Rite had been an attempt to reach the White Horse Adeptus all along.

## entry: Act I - The Realm of Clouds
@keys: The Realm of Clouds
@keys2: Zibai, Lu Xiangxiang, Zhongli
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Yaoyao and Qiqi are making Xiao Lanterns, and Qiqi mentions that Baizhu had patients injured walking in the dark when the moon vanished. The legend of Zhanzhu the Moon-Devourer, a toad that eats the moon, is told. The huge lantern this year honours the White Horse Adeptus, who legend says lived on the moon. Lu Weng of Baiju Guesthouse insists she is real: one of his ancestors, a young herb-gatherer, fell from a cliff and was rescued by the White Horse Adeptus, who told her about the moon and the kingdom of Lang-Gan. She lost track of time and returned to find her village in ruins after countless years, but it proved to be a dream: she woke at the foot of the cliff with a basket full of rare herbs and saw a white horse leap over the cliff above. He is quarrelling with his son, who wants to rename the guesthouse. At Third-Round Knockout the Traveler meets Xiangling, Hu Tao, Xingqiu and Chongyun, who show the photograph Lu Xiangxiang dropped, the moon being covered from its centre. She had been shouting about Lang-Gan treasure and the picture, wielded a shovel and was subdued by Shenhe. Ningguang, Keqing and Lan Yan at Yujing Terrace say the Chasm artifacts were all registered with the Ministry of Civil Affairs, that more such eclipses are expected, and that the picture shows the moon above Chiwang Terrace; Lan Yan adds that the ancient people of Chenyu Vale are said to have spoken to the heavens there. Baizhu says Lu Xiangxiang is in shock and will be sedated for two days. The Traveler goes to the exhibition on the ship organised by Linlang of Xigu Antiques, where they meet Beidou and Yun Jin, Zhongli (whose painting is a copy) and the broken token. After the dream space and the Traveler's return, Zhongli takes them to Mt. Tianheng to explain.

## entry: Act II - The Realm of Rocks
@keys: The Realm of Rocks
@keys2: Zizhi, Gaming, Mountain Shaper
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Yelan investigates Lu Xiangxiang's disappearance with the Traveler; she suggests checking Xiao Lantern stalls because people pray for wishes there. Gaming, who wished for apprentices for the Mighty Mythical Beasts, is approached by Lu Xiangxiang, who offers to be his apprentice. Time freezes, Zizhi tells the Traveler to leave, and then Lu Xiangxiang comes round and does not remember. Gaming becomes aggressive, trying to force Treasure Hoarders and then two disguised adepti to be apprentices. Moon Carver (Houzhang) and Mountain Shaper (Jiehu) recognise it as malign. Zizhi moves to Mountain Shaper, who goes to plant mint at Cloud Retainer's abode. The adepti (Cloud Retainer, Madame Ping, Xiao) debate whether to fight and decide to follow Cloud Retainer's plan. Zizhi eventually accepts the Traveler's wish to know her sorrow and puts them into her prison: an unending river of time (a chase sequence). After surviving it the Traveler sees the memory and Zizhi's last request that, if anyone ever raises a banner against the heavens, her name be put on its cloth. Back at Mt. Aocang Mountain Shaper returns to himself and Cloud Retainer scolds him for the mint; Xiao tells the Traveler he will not ask what Zizhi said.

## entry: Act III - The Realm of People
@keys: The Realm of People
@keys2: Ziqiao, Zhongli, Zibai
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Two days later, at Mt. Aocang, the Geo pillar has fallen but only the Traveler can hear it. At Mt. Qingce the Traveler finds Zhongli weaving a Xiao Lantern and talking to Ziqiao, who shapes herself as a white horse. Ziqiao says she is not Zibai, reminds Zhongli that after he freed her from a painting she fought for him, helping to subdue Chenyu Vale and bring peace to Mt. Qingce, so their contract has been fulfilled, and invites a new offer. Zhongli proposes a one-day festive excursion with the Traveler and Paimon as guides. Ziqiao asks to start in Liyue Harbor, eats at Wanmin Restaurant, where Xiangling plays along with her claim to be the White Horse Adeptus, meets the now-recovered Lu Xiangxiang, discusses ambition and joy, hears Iron Tongue Tian and Yun Jin discuss the ancient poem and recites its meaning, meets Yanfei and Ganyu, jokes about the lateral gait of the giant horse lantern, has her photo taken with the Traveler and watches the sunset from Yujing Terrace. Zhongli, who has been nearby, explains that a Deadly Self cannot survive long without the soul remnant that sustained it. At the Lantern Rite release the Traveler places the Human and Earth souls in a Xiao Lantern that Zhongli holds and lets it rise. Zibai's Jadelight Canopy appears and melts away to reveal the full moon, and Zibai awakens, her eyes briefly flashing the colours of her three Selves. She does not know where she is, then recalls that she was the Envoy of the Lunar Palace named Zibai, recognises Zhongli as the Lord of Geo, "Heaven's Star", and remembers the Traveler and Paimon as her guides.

Zibai, found in Wanmin Restaurant, mistakes Shenhe for the Crane Adeptus of mint planting and lectures her about self-cultivation. Travelling in a lunar chariot of her own making she tells Paimon she was once an angel and then a moon envoy. A tea merchant, Fenglian, explains her bulk discount; Zibai extrapolates the pattern to a free twenty-one boxes and a loss for the seller, who suddenly has no stock. In Qiaoying Village she finds Lu Xiangxiang grounded with the task of raising a tea seedling and secretly transplants a full tea tree overnight, creating a "miracle". In Qingce Village she advises two children on planting rice and canola, citing the title the people of Lang-Gan gave her. At Wuwang Hill she meets Hu Tao and Zhongli, who says she was not from Liyue but perhaps will be. At Wangshu Inn she cooks Flavor of Spring for Xiao, returns the broken jade token to the Traveler and promises to make a trinket from it.
