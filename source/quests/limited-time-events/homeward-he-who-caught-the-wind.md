## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 6.4
### LITE
Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind was a limited-time Version 6.4 ("Luna V") event, running 27 February to 16 March 2026, held in Mondstadt after the Knights of Favonius' Nod-Krai expedition was winding down. Jean invites the Traveler and Paimon to a welcome event, which turns out to be Alice and Nicole's "bubble" magic: a replay of the Traveler's first adventure in Mondstadt, to be enjoyed by returning knights. Grand Master Varka comes back early to join in, and an unexpected Abyssal presence appears in the bubble.
### STANDARD
Part I sends the Traveler and Paimon to Stormbearer Coast, where they walk through a Klee-themed version of the story's opening and meet Venti, Dvalin, Alice and Nicole. Part II has volunteers replay the Holy Lyre theft and chase, with Fatui roles played by Guild members and friends. Part III shows the bubble polluted by a nascent Abyssal power that makes Dvalin a corrupted threat, which Venti, Diluc, Jean, Varka and the Traveler must defeat before a bubble-world crisis becomes real. Afterwards Jean and the Knights realise home is wherever they are together.
### LARGE +
The four event modes (Echoes of Memory, A Chat Over Fine Drinks, Lionfang's Radiant Onslaught and Favonius Falcon's Recon Log) cover the same welcome festival from different angles. The event lets Mondstadt's cast revisit old events with the Traveler.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
Part I, Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind: Paimon and the Traveler read Jean's letter (the Wild Hunt threat in Nod-Krai is neutralised and the expedition is returning) and go to the beach across from Starsnatch Cliff, the spot where their journey began. They explore a Special Story Mode full of oddities (Jumpy Dumpty mines on a tree, a Klee version of the Statue of the Seven) and meet Venti and Dvalin, then Alice and Nicole. Alice and Nicole explain that the "bubble" is a world built from Venti's memories at the Knights' request, so returning knights can catch up on events they missed. The Traveler is asked to test it. Jean explains her plans for the reception and Varka turns up unexpectedly.

Part II, For a Reunion Without Tears: With Varka, Jean, Diluc, Venti and others, the Traveler enters the bubble for the Holy Lyre der Himmel sequence. Guild members and Knights play the Fatui, Fischl plays an Electro Cicin Mage who steals the lyre, and Amber, as gliding champion of the Fatui, runs it across Mondstadt. After the performance a banquet at the Cathedral follows, where Jean and Barbara discuss home, and Varka, Venti and the Traveler talk on top of the Anemo Archon Statue.

Part III, Variations on the Song of the Dragon and Freedom: The bubble has turned Abyssal. Inside, the Traveler's group finds Mondstadt overrun and the corrupted Dvalin beyond saving, and they must kill the bubble's Dvalin in Stormterror's Lair. The real Dvalin arrives and fights alongside them. Alice then explains the Abyss's nature, and Jean sends her to confinement for blasting fish.
### LARGE +
Details. In Part I the Traveler discovers the discrepancies with Paimon's Spotter Kamera. Alice admits the odd statue was deliberate. Alice says Kleiner could not come because of trouble on "another planet" and the "border", and that Klee delighted in his call on the Dodocommunication Device. Jean recalls that she had to read two days of applications from volunteers. Varka says he left the group to arrive first, to spare Jean from planning a festival, then offers a meal at the Good Hunter's secret menu.

Part II details: Cyrus, Durin and Mona hold the three crests at the Fatui hideout in Fatui roles; Oz is an Electro Cicin; Albedo and Lisa watch from the quietest corner; Varka recovers from a hangover after drinks with Charles. At the banquet, Varka sits atop the Anemo Archon Statue and tells the Traveler he never thanked them. Venti says only the Pyro Gnosis remains free of the Tsaritsa and recalls how Signora reasoned about Morax's contract. Varka says the Knights still stationed in Nod-Krai will help if needed.

Part III details: the contaminated tears of Dvalin are gathered from the city, and Nicole's elemental devices offer support. The group sees Dvalin's tears as crystallised Abyssal power. They also learn from Alice that the bubble's unchosen events are the problem: when the original story's purification was removed, causal links produced an alternate version in which Dvalin broke his seal. Venti is prepared to kill the dragon in the fiction.
### ULTRA +
End of Part III: after Varka lands the final blow with both claymores, the bubble ejects everyone into the Grand Master's office and the real Dvalin crashes through a window into Cider Lake just as Klee enters, ready to play. The room is quickly repaired. The Knights celebrate at Diluc's wine, and Jean says she still has work to do, so Varka tells her to join later. Alice meets the Traveler in the library, thanks them for helping her keep her promise to Klee, and explains that the bubble is now looped and safe. Jean then tells Alice she must go to confinement.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Alice and Nicole's bubble magic builds an interactive world from memories, but without safeguards it can develop causal knock-on effects; here the removal of the Traveler's Dvalin purification let a nascent Abyssal power take hold. Alice describes the Abyss's essence, according to the Hexenzirkel's view and Barbeloth's interpretation, as the hatred of "fates that will not come to pass". Alice also mentions that her friend Rhinedottir's decision to tap into the Abyss ended the possibility of a future in which she stayed pure.
### LARGE +
Varka says he saw a prophecy through the Hexenzirkel's scryglass in which Venti sealed Dvalin, was injured, and the seal later failed. Alice says in real history the dragon-slayer from the story had already left for Nod-Krai, but declines to say what would have happened. Varka also tells the Traveler that their actions in Natlan reduced the Wild Hunt threat because two Abyssal powers might otherwise have joined forces.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
The bubble is stabilised and looped so the returning knights can experience it at leisure. Klee plays in it with Alice and Nicole. The Knights' welcome celebration is held in a modest form, Jean's worry that Varka would be upset about the celebration is eased, and Alice goes to solitary confinement for fish blasting at Starfell Lake. The event adds no new plot to the Archon Quest.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.4 (Luna V) as a Flagship Event, requiring Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III "Song of the Dragon and Freedom"; the Archon Quest "True Moon" and the Lupus Majoris Chapter Act I are recommended for the best experience. It takes place in Mondstadt after Varka's expedition and the Nod-Krai crisis, with the Snezhnaya arc still ahead.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 6.4
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Four modes: Echoes of Memory (find discrepancies with a Spotter Kamera), A Chat Over Fine Drinks (prepare drinks at the Knights' stand), Lionfang's Radiant Onslaught (a battle-based stage play with a shadow of the Lionfang Knight) and Favonius Falcon's Recon Log (control a Recon Falcon). They award Windscape Splendor, which unlocks the weapon appearance "Phantasmal Gift: Star Omen", Primogems and a Crown of Insight.

## entry: Alice and Nicole's memory bubble
@keys: memory bubble, bubble world, Alice's bubble
@keys2: Nicole, Alice, Mondstadt
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A magic Alice and Nicole created at the Knights' request: a small world built from Venti's memories and reproduced by volunteers, where returning knights can relive the Traveler's adventures. It is unstable and must be debugged; Nicole offers hints by communication bubble. Without a purification step the bubble developed a nascent Abyssal power and had to be repaired, after which it was set to loop.

## entry: Part III Abyssal Dvalin
@keys: bubble Dvalin, Abyssal Dvalin
@keys2: Venti, Varka, bubble
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the alternate version of events the bubble creates, Dvalin broke free of a seal and is thoroughly corrupted, so purification would kill him. Venti, Varka, Diluc, Jean and the Traveler defeat him in the bubble's Stormterror's Lair. The real Dvalin joins at the end, and Varka delivers the final blow with the Abyssal core as the target.

## entry: Varka's homecoming celebration
@keys: Varka's homecoming, welcome celebration, Windscape Splendor
@keys2: Varka, Jean, Knights of Favonius
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Varka asked that there be no grand reception, but Jean wanted the returning expedition to know they are still family. The compromise is a small Mondstadt-style festival with food, drink, sparring, glider shooting and the bubble. Varka thanks the Traveler for their help in Mondstadt and Natlan and sits with them on the Anemo Archon Statue.

## entry: Unchosen fates
@keys: unchosen fates, fates that will not come to pass
@keys2: Alice, Abyss, Hexenzirkel
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Alice's explanation that the Abyss is rooted in the hatred of the possibilities that were eliminated when something else happened; even trivial choices (a lunch order) cut off other versions of a person. She says the Hexenzirkel, and particularly Barbeloth, hold this view and that the bubble allowed some of these fates to come close to reality.
