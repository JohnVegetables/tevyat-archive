## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Summertide Scales and Tales is the Version 4.8 summer flagship event. A strangely bound book sends the Traveler and Paimon into Simulanka, a fairytale world of paper animals and toy people made by three goddesses, where Nilou, Navia and Kirara are also stranded and the Wanderer turns out to be the prophesied Hero. The "evil dragon" they must face is a lonely child named Durin, whose curse they lift by blessing him.
### STANDARD +
The story runs in three Pages and an Appendix (Afterword): The Story Begins in a Forest Without Rain, They Once Walked the Starry Sky, Until the End of the World and Afterword. It is a self-contained fairytale pastiche, light comedy until Durin's memories are shown. The event's reward character was Kirara.
### LARGE +
The event required Adventure Rank 18 and the Prologue Act III, and recommended the Interlude Chapter Act III (Inversion of Genesis) and Chapter IV Act V (Masquerade of the Guilty) first. Kirara's outfit Phantom in Boots was earned through the event's Excerpts of Bliss.
### ULTRA +
The Afterword leaves a hook for Teyvat: a flashback line says the child of a creator named "R" will one day rise from the dead, and Albedo asks the Wanderer to be ready to help if Dragonspine's Durin awakens.

## plot
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon open a mysterious book and wake in Simulanka. In the Forest of Blessings Nilou has become the prophesied Forest Fairy and Grandpa Almond believes the Traveler is the Hero. After restoring the Calligraphy Tavern's Magic Tonic they travel to Constellation Metropole, where Navia is King, and on to the end of the world to face the dragon. There the Wanderer, the real Hero, learns the dragon's story, and everyone's blessings turn him into a small, harmless Mini Durin.
### STANDARD +
**Page I.** In the Forest of Blessings the paper folk are fading because the Calligraphy Tavern, source of their Magic Tonic (ink), closed after a dragon attacked. Nilou, dressed as the Forest Fairy, learns that magic here is made of words, brews new Tonic, brings a paper hamster to life as Padisarah and reopens the Tavern.

**Page II.** By the Maritime Express the party reaches Constellation Metropole, where Navia is King. The city's tracks (a gift of the Goddess of Prophecy that rewinds people out of danger) are failing and the dragon has stolen its stars. They recover the stars and ride the Aerial Express to hang them back.

**Page III.** The Wanderer, the real Hero of Simulanka, leads them to the dragon's lair. The dragon's memories show a lonely child. After the Wanderer names him Durin, everyone's blessings shrink and uncurse him.

**Afterword.** Two days later friends from Teyvat holiday in Simulanka, and Mini Durin leaves with the Wanderer to visit Teyvat.
### LARGE +
**Page I in detail.** The Traveler and Paimon wake on the Cliff of Prophecy among paper plants and talking origami frogs. Almond explains that three goddesses created Simulanka and that an evil dragon came down in black mist, ravaged the forest and left its paper dwellers with the Fading Disorder, losing colour and memory. Nilou cannot use her Vision here. The group helps the Tavern's workers (reconnecting pipes, steadying the singer Stream's rhythm, positioning the bartender Citrus), and Paimon discovers the Tonic is ink. Nilou senses an empty space under the Tavern, a giant ink bottle, and brews the Tonic from sunsettias, snapdragons and Nilotpala Lotuses. Almond regains his memory and recalls the prophecy: the Hero from another world will descend on the Cliff of Prophecy and restore peace.

**Page II in detail.** The conductor Wheel drives the Maritime Express. Kirara is found asleep in a carriage after waking in Simulanka the day before. In the city of toy people, the Goddess of Prophecy's gift freezes or rewinds residents who are about to come to harm, but it now triggers too often, monsters are invading and the Hero is expected. Navia shares the prophecy "push the gear that connects up to the starry sky... I shall dance, and return the tracks beneath my people's feet back to the stars". Many fear losing the protection, and Navia wants the citizens to choose for themselves. The party clears monsters from the castle, recovers scattered stars, retrieves the magic thread from a frightened "conservative radical" whom Navia lets go, and asks the goddess's statue questions. It answers only about the dragon and the gear.

**Page III in detail.** A broken track throws the party off the train into a hallucinatory fog and the Wanderer catches them. He carries a "Hero's Sword" he cannot discard, had been seeking a way out rather than obey the voice telling him to save the dragon, and gives them a Fell Dragon's Scale that turns things to toy blocks. In the lair the dragon's attacks show his memories: a woman's voice naming a newborn, the child seeing his reflection and frightening the capital, his mother M telling him she will become a star and to be kind, and a flying squirrel girl whose mother calls him a kidnapper. The Wanderer, alone in the black mist, tells him nobody else defines who he is and says the name Durin.
### ULTRA +
**After the memories.** Durin's curse turned everything he touched to toy blocks and left barren footprints, and his wish to the Goddess of Fate was "to never have been born at all". Navia reads the star-taking as longing: M had told him she would become a star in Simulanka. Through a hole the Wanderer leads the party to M's study, where an old journal and a yellowed letter explain the world's makers (see revelations). Nilou asks whether Durin truly wants to live beside the people, blesses him as a dweller of the Forest, and Navia, Kirara, the Wanderer and the Traveler add theirs. Durin becomes small and no longer transforms things at a touch. Alice, appearing as a talking street lamp, welcomes the guests and explains that the boat at the Broken Sea or the giant bookmark at the Cliff of Prophecy would have taken them home all along.

**The Metropole.** A toy man tells the party "Push the gear, and the 'tracks' will disappear. We're not ready yet." Bleufond and Bleucher are rescued from falling blocks by the rewind gift in Page II. In the lair the dragon grows two extra heads and its breath bars the party's elemental skills, so toy bombs must be thrown into its open mouths.

**Afterword in detail.** Kaeya and Jean came by invitation from Alice and are at the Tavern, where Paimon tricks Kaeya into ordering the House Special (ink). Klee ran off at once, Albedo is watching over her and Fischl and Oz join in. Collei is sketching tree-houses, and Mona is scrying the Goddess of Prophecy's statue. Mini Durin and the Wanderer have built houses by the giant footprints and Durin has apologised to the Metropole with toytanium and plant oil. Albedo asks the Wanderer to consider helping if Dragonspine's Durin returns. The group names a small paper boat "The Durin". Klee calls Mini Durin her big brother because their mothers were friends. Collei mentions that something interesting is happening in Sumeru that Tighnari is working hard on, and the wiki lists An Odd Textual Mystery as the next quest. Post-quest NPC dialogue has Nahida teasing the Wanderer about his new friend.

## revelations
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The "evil dragon" is Durin, a child created and then left alone, and Simulanka was made by three friends who loved M's stories.
### STANDARD +
Durin's mother M created him with a name that would tie him to a "real-world namesake's" sad fate, hoping a happier ending would be possible in a storybook. His fear and loneliness, not malice, caused his rampages.
### LARGE +
Simulanka's creators are Alice (Goddess of Creation), B (Goddess of Prophecy, who maps fates and wanted her own statue) and M (Goddess of Fate, a Hexenzirkel mage who wrote the tales and told Durin her life would be shorter than a fairytale). The journal says Alice overruled M's wish to be "A" and gave her "M" for her middle name, and that Alice made M's origami frogs speak. M's letter says that, according to A, the Goddess of Fate in Simulanka will go on granting wishes after M is gone. The guests' blessings changed Durin's body: in this world words carry magic.
### ULTRA +
The Wanderer says Durin too was "betrayed" and "hated by people", and he alone reaches him. Mona scries that Simulanka's fate is a projection of Teyvat's, as if the world were a reflection in a mirror or lake, and recognises the Goddess of Prophecy's statue as her own master in youth. B tells M a secret: "R's child will one day rise from the dead", which the Wanderer relays to Albedo. Albedo notes that Dragonspine's Durin shows signs of slowly gathering vitality and hopes whatever wakes will not be an "evil dragon". Alice says she sent the book to the Traveler and Paimon, and the others each held a copy of M's tale when Durin made his wish.

## characters
@availability: limited
### LITE
Traveler, Paimon, Nilou (the Forest Fairy), Navia (King of Constellation Metropole), Kirara (the Nekomata-in-Boots), Wanderer (Hero of Simulanka) and Durin (Mini Durin).
### STANDARD +
Locals include Almond, Citrus, Stream, Firecracker, Padisarah, Wheel and the toy guards Montagne and Bizerte. The goddesses are Alice, B and M. Afterword guests are Kaeya, Jean, Klee, Albedo, Collei, Mona, Fischl and Oz.
### LARGE +
Navia is addressed as "Your Majesty" but acts like a partner. Kirara is teased as the "Nekomata-in-Boots" and frightens the paper animals. The Wanderer is blunt, hates the Hero title and mocks the voice that gave him orders, yet stays by Durin. Other locals: Chestnut, Ginkgo, Cedule, Bleufond, Bleucher and Olivier.

## consequences
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Durin becomes the small dragon Mini Durin, no longer cursed, and sets out to make amends. The Traveler, Nilou, Navia, Kirara and the Wanderer can leave through the Broken Sea boat or the Cliff of Prophecy's bookmark. The Wanderer promises to bring Mini Durin to Teyvat on the boat "The Durin".
### LARGE +
Albedo's request ties the fairytale to Dragonspine, and the later Paralogism quest features a dragon from Simulanka.

## placement
@version: 4.8
### STANDARD
Released in Version 4.8 on 17 July 2024 (UTC+8) and run until 28 August 2024. Page II unlocked on 19 July, Page III on 21 July and the Appendix on 23 July. It is an event story rather than an Archon Quest, though Navia (president of Spina di Rosula) takes a leading role.

## entry: Simulanka
@keys: Simulanka, Forest of Blessings, Constellation Metropole, Broken Sea, Cliff of Prophecy
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Simulanka is a storybook world created by three goddesses: Creation (Alice), Prophecy (B) and Fate (M). The Forest of Blessings is home to paper-folded animals who drink Magic Tonic (ink) to keep their colour. Constellation Metropole is a clockwork city of toy people ruled from a castle with an Astral Garden at its peak, with a statue of the Goddess of Prophecy. At the end of the world lies the Broken Sea and the dragon's lair. Players can leave Simulanka via the Broken Sea boat or the giant bookmark at the Cliff of Prophecy.

## entry: Durin of Simulanka
@keys: Mini Durin, Simulanka's Evil Dragon, The Durin
@keys2: Simulanka, Wanderer, M
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Durin is the dragon created by M, who gave him a name tied to a sad real-world namesake. He grew up lonely, frightened the capital, took the Metropole's stars because they reminded him of his mother, and lived behind the waterfall while others sheltered at the Broken Sea. The Wanderer, Nilou and the others blessed him, shrinking him into Mini Durin; he now apologises, rebuilds and befriends Klee, who calls him her big brother because their mothers were friends.

## entry: Magic Tonic and the Fading Disorder
@keys: Magic Tonic, Fading Disorder, Calligraphy Tavern
@keys2: Simulanka, Almond, Nilou
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the Forest of Blessings the paper residents' colours and memories fade unless they drink Magic Tonic, which is made from sunsettias, snapdragons and Nilotpala Lotuses brewed into ink at the Calligraphy Tavern. Nilou realises the Fading Disorder is ink running dry. A giant ink bottle sits under the Tavern, and Nilou's blessing reopens it.

## entry: The Goddesses of Simulanka
@keys: Goddess of Creation, Goddess of Prophecy, Goddess of Fate
@keys2: Simulanka, Alice, Andersdotter
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Alice made the world habitable and made origami frogs talk, B (Barbeloth) mapped every fate and has her statue in the capital, and M wrote the fairytales. M expected a short life. Mona recognises the Goddess of Prophecy's statue as her own master.
