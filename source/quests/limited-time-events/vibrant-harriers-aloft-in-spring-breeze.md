## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 4.4
### LITE
Vibrant Harriers Aloft in Spring Breeze was the Version 4.4 Lantern Rite flagship event (5 to 26 February 2024), themed on kites. In the main story Gaming, a guard and wushou dancer who has fallen out with his tea-merchant father, is helped by the Traveler, Paimon, Xianyun and her disciples to talk with him again, and at the festival Xianyun's kite lifts the whole sky. A postscript sends the Traveler to Qiaoying Village to meet Furina and other Fontaine visitors.
### STANDARD
The Lantern Rite theme combined Liyue's traditional kite-making with Fontaine's "Mechanical Vertical Lifting Device," arranged through Ningguang and the Fontaine writer Charlotte. The event also offered three festival activities (Paper Shadows A-Foraging, Joyful Beasts and Their Auspices and Iridescent Cloud-Striding) whose Festive Fever gave Xingqiu's outfit "Bamboo Rain" and a choice of 4-star Liyue character. The story is light and family-centred.
### LARGE +
It also shows Xianyun, the former Cloud Retainer, living in Liyue Harbor and mentoring Ganyu, Shenhe and others, and it provides a comic reunion with Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver, who are worried that she has left Jueyun Karst quiet.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Part I, The Orioles Bask in Spring, the Heavens Present Auspices: Keqing and Charlotte tell the Traveler that this year's festival theme is kites, combining Liyue craft with Fontaine technology, and that the Qixing are holding a contest. Two strangers who call themselves Houzhang and Jiehu turn out to be Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver, looking for Xianyun because Jueyun Karst has been unusually quiet since she moved to the harbor. The Traveler helps Gaming deliver a shipment to Yilong Wharf, where Gaming asks to avoid his family. Xianyun, told of the pair, rushes off to buy them tea and invites the Traveler to her kite workshop at Mt. Aocang.

Part II, A Cloud-Sea Appeal, a Brilliance Revealed: over dim sum at Xinyue Kiosk, Gaming says he is not rich, his father runs a small tea business that could not compete with the Feiyun Commerce Guild, and he wants to be a wushou dancer while his father disagrees. At Mt. Aocang Xianyun teaches kite-making to the Traveler, Ganyu, Shenhe and Yaoyao; a mechanical kite from the pair of adepti interrupts, and Xianyun, angry, learns it was bought in festive spirit. Hearing Gaming's story, she resolves to help.

Part III, The Glede Returns to the Pavilion: a staged "bandit" ambush lets Gaming's father, Yip Tak, see his son protect him; Zhongli helps by speaking well of Gaming and of wushou dancers. Xianyun and Shenhe nudge the two into talking, and Charlotte, the 'important client', interviews Tak about his tea; Paimon explains she will take it to Fontaine along with her article, which could bring new customers from abroad. At the festival Gaming performs a wushou dance in the dark, Xianyun lifts the grounded kites with her own, and Tak finally accepts his son's choice.

Part IV, Hustle and Bustle: afterwards the Traveler goes to Qiaoying Village to find Hu Tao and runs into Navia and Clorinde (who came with Furina), then Furina, who is gathering props for a horror film with Hu Tao's help. Neuvillette had also come and left. Zhongli and Hu Tao have been helping with the props. Back in Fontaine Neuvillette gives Wriothesley the tea Clorinde owed him.
### LARGE +
Part I detail: Zhenyuan runs a kite stall, Longzhou arranges Gaming's shipment, ambushing Treasure Hoarders are driven off on the road to Qiaoying Village, and Zhigui at the Secure Transport Agency says everyone at the wharf values Gaming but he hopes Gaming will reconcile with his family. Xianyun buys ten items after Fenglian's patter and a toy from Charbonnier, and a floating guard follows her. Mountain Shaper recalls an old event involving the Traveler, making Paimon feel guilty for doubting them.

Part II detail: Ganyu falls asleep because of overtime and Shenhe wakes her with a minty liquid. Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver admit they bought a kite because they could never make one like Xianyun. Gaming takes Shuyu and Yaoyao back to the city. Shenhe reports that he sounded dejected about kites and the ground. Xianyun has her disciples help prepare dinner and shows new outfits she commissioned.

Part III detail: Longzhou says a client needs a tea merchant escorted; the merchant is Tak. Houzhang and Jiehu ambush them at Wangshu Inn. Zhongli claims the spirit of the suanni suits Gaming and compares guiding a child to holding a kite string. Xiao, resting nearby, mistakes a ghostly kite for Hu Tao and helps test kites, saying the mechanical one was faster though he is impartial. Tak tells Gaming he thinks Gaming should live at home because the harbor could be too hard; Shenhe deliberately embarrasses herself to keep the talk going. Gaming is moved when Tak, who had earlier said nothing like it, speaks to him gently.

Part IV detail: Navia was shouting from a mountain as other visitors do, and Furina had done the same on her own and was embarrassed when discovered. Furina asks the Traveler to tell Neuvillette to take more time off. Clorinde had come mostly to accompany Navia and to buy tea for Wriothesley after losing a bet. Furina is frightened by Hu Tao and accepts Clorinde's escort. Neuvillette sampled the water at its source and gives the Traveler a ladle-like gift.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay: Paper Shadows A-Foraging (a paper-theater cooking game with Guoba and Yuegui), Joyful Beasts and Their Auspices ("Just Wushou Dance!", a four-player teabrick grabbing game) and Iridescent Cloud-Striding (racing trials around Chenyu Vale with the Golden Carp's Leap). Festive Fever thresholds were 200 to 1,200; Bamboo Rain arrived at 600 and the final tier gave a Crown of Insight. Requirements were Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue; recommended first were A New Star Approaches, Xianyun's Grus Serena Chapter Act I and, before Hustle and Bustle, Furina's Animula Choragi Chapter Act I. These are game mechanics, not in-universe canon.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
The event shows Xianyun lives in Liyue Harbor and runs a kite workshop for her disciples. It reveals Gaming's background: his father is a tea merchant whose business could not compete with the Feiyun Commerce Guild, and Gaming left home over his wushou dream. It also shows that Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver feel neglected by Xianyun.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Gaming is the emotional centre. Xianyun is mischievous and caring, organising the plan to reconcile father and son. Zhongli, Shenhe, Ganyu and Yaoyao support; Charlotte gives Fontaine's perspective. Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver (as Houzhang and Jiehu) provide comedy. Furina, Navia, Clorinde, Neuvillette and Wriothesley appear in Part IV.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Tak accepts that Gaming will pursue wushou dancing and Gaming decides to thank everyone who helped. Charlotte's plan to feature Tak's tea in Fontaine is expected, in Paimon's words, to bring him new business. Furina asks that Neuvillette take more time off, and he says he will consider it.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Released in Version 4.4 for the 2024 Lantern Rite, between the 2023 event The Exquisite Night Chimes and the 2025 event Springtime Charms. Parts I to III unlock in sequence; Part IV (Hustle and Bustle) opened on 11 February 2024.

## entry: Gaming and Yip Tak
@keys: Gaming, Yip Tak, wushou dance
@version: 4.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In this event Gaming, a guard and wushou dancer who has left home, reconciles with his tea-merchant father Yip Tak. Tak feared the dance would not sustain him as he grew older and regretted having shown him wushou when he was young; he changes his mind after seeing Gaming's performance and perseverance.

## entry: Mechanical Vertical Lifting Device

@version: 4.4

### STANDARD
Fontaine's popular Mechanical Vertical Lifting Device is combined with Liyue's traditional kite-making for the 2024 Lantern Rite after Ningguang contacts Charlotte. Zhenyuan sells it on her stall, and Xianyun lifts the rest of the kites with her own using adeptal power.
