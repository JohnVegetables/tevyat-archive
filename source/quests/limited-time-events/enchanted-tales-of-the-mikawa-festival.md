## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 5.4
### LITE
Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival was a limited-time Flagship event in Version 5.4 (14 February to 3 March 2025). Yae Miko, with the Shogunate's blessing, stages a new Mikawa Flower Festival for humans and youkai alike and names the Traveler official representative. The festival centres on a 48-hour game-making contest on the theme of dreams. A nightmare then turns up on Mt. Yougou, and the Raiden Shogun has to face the memory of someone she once had to kill.
### STANDARD
The festival is a revived youkai celebration. Teams pairing humans with bake-danuki build games: Thoma and his youkai helpers make Akitsu Harpastum, Sayu and Yoimiya make Bunshin Phantasm, and Kokomi, Mizuki and Gyoubu Koban make A Little Fox's Daydream. As contestants work, strange doppelganger rumours spread and people are found trapped in nightmares. The Tenryou Commission investigates quietly, Ei attends the festival in person at Miko's suggestion, and the cause turns out to be three frightened bake-danuki carrying a nightmare fragment from a horn buried five hundred years ago. A joint plan by the Traveler, Ei, Miko and Mizuki lures the fragment out and puts it to rest during the festival.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest "Ritou Escape Plan". The recommended story quests were Imperatrix Umbrosa Act II (Raiden Shogun), Tapirus Somniator Act I (Yumemizuki Mizuki), Dracaena Somnolenta Act I (Kokomi) and Carassius Auratus Act II (Yoimiya), plus Sayu's hangout and the World Quest Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. The reward weapon was Tamayuratei no Ohanashi.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 5.4
### STANDARD
First Night, As Though Caught in a Dream: Miko and the Shogun propose the festival and the Traveler becomes representative; Miko recruits the bake-danuki Kichiboushi, Shousei and Gyoubu Koban through Ioroi; teams form, the theme "dreams" is announced and the contest begins. That night a kairagi-like figure attacks the Traveler, who breaks out of a nightmare to thunder; Ei is watching. Second Night, As Though Beholding Illusions: the investigation, the Shogun's appearance as bait, a hot-spring conversation in which Ei speaks of the old Nightmare, Kappa found "unconscious" but made of wood, and the unmasking of three bake-danuki who had taken the places of Hitotsume Kozou, Youko and Kappa. The Traveler purifies their nightmare, and Ioroi, Ei and Miko work out what happened and plan a ritual. Final Night, As Though Surrounded by Thunder: the Traveler and Ei fight the nightmare fragments in the Plane of Euthymia while the festival prays; a cutscene shows Mikoshi Chiyo's dream broken, and the festival awards go out. After-Night, As Though Kissed by Dew: aftermath, a bath at Aisa Bathhouse with the Arataki Gang.
### LARGE
First Night. A letter from Miko brings the Traveler and Paimon to Tenshukaku, where Miko and the Shogun are arguing over a contract: a new Mikawa Flower Festival, an old youkai event of drink and games, now combining human invention with youkai arts and open to both peoples. The Traveler is chosen as neutral representative, having a deep connection to youkai and not being from Inazuma, and haggles the share of proceeds up to twenty percent. Fifty percent goes to the Shogunate. The plan is for attendees to build games in 48 hours. Miko wins over Ioroi, the Kakushigami of the bake-danuki, who is sealed in a statue in Chinju Forest, by playing on his pride, and three young bake-danuki, Kichiboushi, Shousei and Gyoubu Koban, join the Traveler. Miko feeds them a Kitsune Meal so they can speak with humans. Teams form: Thoma, whom Itto introduces to Hitotsume Kozou, Youko and Kappa, with Shousei; Sayu and Yoimiya with Kichiboushi; and Kokomi and Mizuki with Gyoubu Koban, who are discussing a new Aisa Bathhouse branch on Watatsumi Island and mean to use the contest as publicity. Kokomi sends Gorou back to Watatsumi Island to encourage islanders to attend. Ayaka volunteers to organise and Ayato oversees, while Miko adds a surprise: the theme will be "dreams" and the winner will receive the Tanuki Game Award, Mora and a sponsorship from Yae Publishing House. The Traveler is named chief judge. After play-testing and a night of food, the Traveler is attacked by a figure in Abyssal kairagi-like clothing and, after recognising a dream, hears thunder and wakes. Ei, on a cliff, wonders whether a Nightmare has returned to Inazuma. Elsewhere, Asari is left puzzled when Komura, who had been standing silent, appears beside him as though from nowhere.

Second Night. Rumours of doppelgangers spread. The Tenryou Commission has found a team of surveyors unconscious in a nightmare and traces of youkai power around Mt. Yougou. Sara and Heizou keep the investigation secret by order of the Shogun. Ei, whom the Traveler recognises at a glance, asks the Traveler to question the contestants without causing suspicion. Everyone passes: the Traveler tests Sayu and Yoimiya with shared memories and Thoma with an interview, while Kokomi vouches for Gyoubu Koban and says Mizuki left at night after a message, which turns out to be Miko's request for help. Miko then announces the Shogun's attendance; Sara and Heizou explain this will crack the culprit's composure. In the evening Mizuki gives the Traveler two Aisa Bathhouse coupons and shares her concern that Ei is hiding strain. The Traveler persuades Ei to come after midnight. In the bath Ei explains the old Nightmare: creatures that possess people and trap them in dreams. A strong enough victim does not fall asleep but wakes into a nightmare in which every precious thing seems an enemy. A person once trapped like this had to be struck down by Ei with Musou no Hitotachi, and she has no regrets. Miko and Mizuki, who arranged the bath to get her to talk, overhear. A woman named Rie reports seeing her own double in Inazuma City, then Haruto reports Kappa trapped in a nightmare. Heizou's reasoning, with the Traveler's help, shows that the doppelgangers and the nightmare are separate, that the aim is panic, and that "Kappa" is made of wood. The "Youko" and "Hitotsume Kozou" on site grow bake-danuki tails when frightened and flee with Tetsunosuke. In Chinju Forest they hide by transforming into bonfires and statues. The Traveler breaks each disguise with a leaf, Sara blocks their path, and the three bake-danuki, trapped in a nightmare, believe the Shogun sent humans to steal their land. The Traveler uses the power to purify the Abyss and sees their memory: a survey team (Kaga, Kiritani and Mizukawa) is at first ordinary, but the nightmare turns its words into threats, and then the real Hitotsume Kozou, Youko and Kappa fall unconscious. Ioroi, brought before the Shogun, recognises the three as Kinkaku, Ginkaku and Tetsunosuke who hid from a human named Kamuna Harunosuke after finding a horn full of youkai power. Mizuki suspects the horn holds a fragment of a nightmare. Ei says it is Mikoshi Chiyo's: a trusted lieutenant who raised her sword against the Shogun on Mt. Yougou while trapped in her own nightmare and was struck down, losing an arm and a horn. Miko proposes that the bake-danuki create a dreamscape that the fragment cannot tell from the real world, and Mizuki will craft a ritual. The Traveler and Ei will hold the fragment off until it takes effect.

Final Night. Ayato distributes ritual booklets, Sara adjusts security, and the contestants finish their games with help from friends: Itto and the Arataki Gang aid Thoma, Ayaka helps Sayu and Yoimiya, and Gorou and Swordfish II help Kokomi and Mizuki. Ei and the Traveler meditate. Ei says she will not use Musou no Hitotachi this time, and the Traveler promises she will not face it alone. At sunset the nightmare fragments appear on the Chinju Forest path and Ei draws them and the Traveler into the Plane of Euthymia. While they fight, voices of everyone outside chant "God of Thunder, heed our call", and the fragments weaken. A cutscene shows Chiyo's own memory: soldiers rouse her after fearing she was eaten by a beast, a nightmare makes the soldiers and two figures who call her 'Mother' (one named Mikoshi Nagamasa) look like monsters, and the Yumekui-Baku say it is beyond their skill. Ei cuts through the dream and takes her hand, and Chiyo wakes at a picnic with Makoto, Ei, Sasayuri and the Kitsune Saiguu, where Ei tells her that only sweet dreams await. In the present, Ei sits alone while Chiyo's horn fades. Night falls and the festival opens. The Traveler announces the best game (player's choice among the three) and Ei announces a special award for "most immersive game" to Kinkaku, Ginkaku and Tetsunosuke for their horror game, The Doppelganger Incident, and promises that no human will intrude on their peace without cause.

After-Night. Ei's thoughts: there were misunderstandings and hostilities, yet it ended as a celebration. Miko took Kinkaku and the others to Mizuki for a check-up. Kappa, Hitotsume Kozou and Youko recover and remember only a long dream. Itto drags friends to the Aisa Bathhouse again. In a game the Traveler beats Ayato, who the Traveler suspects lost on purpose so Ayaka could play. Outside, Aikawa Susumu says visitors tripled and the bathhouse has its best-ever result, second to Yae Publishing House, but Miko announces thirty percent of proceeds have gone to her house. Ei, watching from Tenshukaku, wishes everyone sweet dreams. Afterwards Kazuha, Kirara and Chiori can be found in the open world.
### ULTRA +
Details. Thoma explains that Akitsu Harpastum aims at everyone from children to the elderly, and that he is on holiday but helping the Yashiro Commission. Sara owns five Statues of the Almighty Narukami Ogosho and keeps that as a maximum so she can care for them properly. Kokomi privately calls Miko "Miss Fried Tofu With All the Frills". Mizuki and Miko are old acquaintances: Miko recalls racing Mizuki for the first piece of Fried Tofu at festivals when Miko was a kit and Mizuki a young baku, and Aisa Bathhouse's sales rivalry with Yae Publishing House (Aikawa Susumu calls it their 333rd sales contest) ends with the bathhouse in second place. The Aranara come up when Yoimiya says she considered putting them in her game.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 5.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Nightmare of five hundred years ago, according to Ei, trapped soldiers in dreams that even the Yumekui-Baku could not heal. Someone strong enough to resist became unable to tell dream from war and saw every cherished thing as an enemy. This was Mikoshi Chiyo, a lieutenant, called Torachiyo ("Chiyo the Tiger-Bite") by legend after she once tore apart a beast from beyond this world from within it. Tainted by darkness, she attacked the Shogun on Mt. Yougou and was struck down with Musou no Hitotachi, losing an arm, one horn and part of the mountain. The horn buried there carries a fragment of her nightmare. The three bake-danuki unearthed it while hiding from a human, used its power to hide, and have lived in its nightmare ever since, believing humans had come to destroy them.
### LARGE +
The Shogunate's survey team was only checking for youkai on the land so it could avoid or negotiate with them, as the Shogun says. Paimon recalls that Ioroi was sealed after he stole a comb that turned out to be used for Sacred Sakura Daifuku, and Ioroi admits he lost a fight to the young human involved. Ei says the Shogun's body could keep working another hundred years, yet she does not deny that she carries a burden, and Mizuki, a clinical psychologist as well as a Yumekui-Baku, tells the Traveler that humans, youkai and archons share the same emotional strain.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 5.4
### STANDARD
The fragment is put to rest and Chiyo's horn fades in Ei's lap. The festival goes on. The bake-danuki learn that humans meant them no harm and receive the special award and Ei's promise. Kokomi and Mizuki's bathhouse collaboration remains a plan. Yae Publishing House profits from the proceeds. The Traveler's purification of the nightmare, which the Traveler calls a way of dealing with the Abyss, is what first wakes the bake-danuki.
### LARGE +
Ioroi, still sealed in the statue, says the seal is almost spent and he can leave when he likes. Miko offers to release him.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 5.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.4 as a Flagship Event, set in Inazuma after the Archon Quest "Ritou Escape Plan" and as a sequel in tone to the Version 3.3 Test of Courage (Akitsu Kimodameshi), where Miko's first Mikawa Flower Festival was held on a small scale. Characters refer back to that event, to the Sacred Sakura cleansing and to Sayu's hangout. Ei places the Nightmare in the aftermath of the dark disaster five hundred years ago.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 5.4
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Three modes unlock in sequence: Akitsu Harpastum: Dreams Doubled (return harpasta fired by Harpastum youkai), A Little Fox's Daydream (collect Fried Tofu by switching dreamscapes and altering terrain) and Bunshin Phantasm (use two doppelgangers to solve puzzles). Completing them gives Festival Stamps (15 in all) toward Tamayuratei no Ohanashi (Polearm), Primogems and a Crown of Insight. In the First Night fight the Traveler uses Parry and Veilbreak instead of skills and bursts. The Final Night fight is a boss battle with the Raiden Shogun as an ally, with "prayers" from the crowd weakening the fragments.

## entry: Mikoshi Chiyo
@keys: Mikoshi Chiyo, Chiyo the Tiger-Bite, Torachiyo
@version: 5.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A trusted lieutenant of the Shogun five hundred years ago, said to have been swallowed by a tiger-bodied, serpent-tailed beast from beyond this world and to have torn it apart from inside. A nightmare made her see her subordinates, family and friends as enemies. She attacked the Shogun and was struck down, losing an arm and a horn. Ei says she would not like the nickname. A fragment of her nightmare lingers in the buried horn. She appears only in the cutscene memory near the end, where two figures call her 'Mother', one named Mikoshi Nagamasa.

## entry: Nightmare Fragment
@keys: Nightmare Fragment, Nightmare Fragments
@version: 5.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Whatever remained of the old Nightmare in Mikoshi Chiyo's severed horn, which hates joy and every good thing. It travels through the ley lines, so it appears intermittently and never beyond Mt. Yougou, and Mizuki compares it to a jibakurei. It attacks the survey team, Hitotsume Kozou and his friends, and the Traveler. The Traveler breaks out of its dream when thunder sounds.

## entry: Kinkaku, Ginkaku and Tetsunosuke
@keys: Kinkaku, Ginkaku, Tetsunosuke
@version: 5.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Three bake-danuki who hid from a human, Kamuna Harunosuke, for five hundred years using the power of a horn they found, which kept them in nightmare. Under its influence they took the place of Hitotsume Kozou, Youko and Kappa at the festival, then fled into Chinju Forest when found out. After the Traveler purifies them, they realise the humans meant no harm, receive a special award and Ei's promise, and are looked after by Miko and Mizuki.

He calls the five hundred years of hiding a long game of hide-and-seek.

## entry: Kichiboushi, Shousei and Gyoubu Koban

Each joins a team and uses transformation to build sets. Kichiboushi works with Sayu and Yoimiya, Shousei with Thoma, Gyoubu Koban with Kokomi and Mizuki.

## entry: Tanuki Game Award

The prize for the winning team, with Mora and a sponsorship from Yae Publishing House to develop and promote the game across Inazuma. Ayaka organised its production at short notice. It sits at the winning booth afterwards.

Sayu and Yoimiya's game, from Sayu's dream of making clones to do her work. Players place a hand on a board to enter a dreamscape, where body doubles help solve problems.

## entry: A Little Fox's Daydream

@version: 5.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Mizuki, Kokomi and Gyoubu Koban's game: a fox travels between sweet dreams and nightmares in search of fried tofu, with a ramen stand at the end. Miko notices the fox resembles her.

## entry: The Doppelganger Incident
@keys: Doppelganger Incident, Inazuma doppelganger
@keys2: Mikawa, bake-danuki, Heizou
@version: 5.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The scare in which people reported seeing their own doubles around Mt. Yougou and Inazuma City. An old Inazuma folk tale says people who meet their double are replaced by it within three days. Heizou deduces the doubles were separate from the nightmare and meant to cause panic. The culprits were the three bake-danuki. At the end the event honours the trio's "horror game" with a special award.
