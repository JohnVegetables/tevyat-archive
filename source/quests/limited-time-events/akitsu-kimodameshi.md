## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 3.3
### LITE
Akitsu Kimodameshi was a limited-time Flagship event in Version 3.3 (15 December 2022 to 2 January 2023) tied to the Mikawa Flower Festival. Kamisato Ayato and the Yashiro Commission hold a three-round "Test of Courage" in Chinju Forest, with prizes from the Grand Narukami Shrine. The Traveler and Paimon join, run into what seem to be ghosts, and find that the scares come from small youkai planning a festival and from a weary hagoita spirit named Hanyuuda Chizuru. Gameplay-wise it added the brick-breaking game Akitsu Yuugei.
### STANDARD
The event opened with a three-part story and a co-op minigame. Ayato describes the contest as his idea, run by three parties: merchants funding it, the Kamisato Clan lending the grounds, and the Grand Narukami Shrine advising. Each party picked one object to find, one per round. The Traveler is partnered with a friend each round, while Arataki Itto secretly tries to scare contestants for practice and Shikanoin Heizou investigates a "ghost". The ending is gentle: the ghost is a lonely youkai, the festival is held, and Chizuru is persuaded to stay and enjoy it.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest part "Ritou Escape Plan". Rewards included Primogems, a Crown of Insight, the event weapon Toukabou Shigure (Sword) and the furnishing Kimodameshi Vivaciousness, via a currency called Courage Test Tickets. The gameplay modes were Leisurely Pastime (also playable in co-op) and the harder solo Shatterdark.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 3.3
### LITE
Part I, "Test of Courage": the Traveler and Paimon hear of the contest, meet Ayato at the Yashiro Commission, and then meet Itto, who plans to scare people because his gang finds the event boring. A shrine maiden (Hitomi) runs the contest and says "Traveler and Paimon" count as one entrant, so they pick a partner from Ayaka, Gorou or Kazuha. Round one's object is Dango Milk. Afterwards Itto leaps out at three figures who are not his gang; both groups flee. Back in the forest a veiled woman warns the pair to leave, and a sweet flower scent knocks them out. Heizou wakes them.
### STANDARD
Part II, "Haunted Tales": Heizou asks them to keep quiet about what they saw, because he is investigating for a client left stranded on the beach. Round two's partners are Yoimiya, Sayu or Thoma. Itto then finds three small youkai, Youko, Kappa and Hitotsume Kozou, who explain that they are trying to hold a Mikawa Flower Festival. They built the Akitsu Yuugei game on the beach using nearly all their youkai power, inspired by a legend of the youkai Kamai Kenji and a human samurai. When the pair play Akitsu Yuugei with the veiled woman, her wish as winner is that they never cross paths again. The youkai say she is not one of them.

Part III, "The Currents of Life": the shrine maiden is missing when the third round is due, so Heizou becomes the Traveler's partner and investigates. The clues (a torn piece of black shade cloth, a smoke-blackened ball that smelled of fireworks, sleeping powder in the stones) suggest the "ghost" used props. Heizou concludes that she is a youkai born from an object, who is strong near her original body and weak away from it.
### LARGE +
At the beach the woman gives her name as Hanyuuda Chizuru. She hid there waiting to fade, and was woken when the youkai built Akitsu Yuugei. She frightened visitors away so that the young youkai would not repeat the grief she watched in the Kamai and samurai story, and she trapped the thief who wanted the game's decorations. Heizou deduces her true form: the pair of hagoita once used by Kamai and the samurai. Yae Miko, who with the shrine maiden had arranged the vanished staff and the third object, reveals more of the story and Chizuru decides to attend. Itto announces he will organise the festival, and the finale is a festival on the beach with food stalls and a yo-yo fishing game.
### ULTRA +
Details of the contest: Dango Milk was supplied by a man named Tomoki and chosen by the funders; the second object was a fan chosen by the Yashiro Commission side; the hagoita was the Grand Narukami Shrine's pick, which Heizou works out is why Miko arranged things as she did. Ayato remarks in passing that the Traveler's help might yet be needed, but the quest does not say what he expected. In the festival scene Itto, Gorou, Yoimiya, Thoma, Ayaka, Sayu and Chizuru appear in small optional conversations.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The "ghost" is Hanyuuda Chizuru, a youkai who grew from a pair of hagoita used by Kamai Kenji and a human samurai. She had watched their friendship end in regret and wanted to protect other youkai from the same grief. Yae Miko answers her by giving the rest of the tale: the samurai was Yanagibashi Takuto, founder of the Souran Isshin Art, which he developed while dueling Kamai; and five hundred years ago Kamai fought alongside Tominaga Masanari, third heir of that art, who carried a sword handed down from Takuto. Miko presents this as the resolution of Kamai's regret: friendships continue in new forms.
### LARGE +
Ei, speaking in the Kazuha partner branch, recalls that Akitsu Hazura (the hagoita game) was loved by Makoto, that Hyakkiyakou once held the Mikawa Flower Festival, and that she and Makoto once attended. The youkai's version of the game is called Akitsu Yuugei because the original game was lost to time and they rebuilt it from a merchant's description.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
Ayato proposed and watches over the event but leaves details to retainers. Itto, leader of the Arataki Gang, wants the contest to be fun for his gang and is scared by real ghosts. Heizou investigates, calmly and quickly. Yae Miko orchestrates the final round. Hanyuuda Chizuru is a kind, guarded hagoita spirit. Youko, Kappa and Hitotsume Kozou are small, eager youkai. Round partners (Ayaka, Gorou, Kazuha, Yoimiya, Sayu, Thoma) each get a short personal scene. Ei appears in the first round to comment on the games, not to scare.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
The unmasked ghost story ends with a festival held on the beach, organised by Itto with the youkai and the Yashiro Commission, and Chizuru joining it. Heizou goes to tell his client that "intent to steal" is a minor offence but cannot go unpunished. The wiki lists the event as part of the Mikawa Flower Festival event series.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.3. The event required the Ritou Escape Plan part of the Inazuma Archon Quest; players who had not done Arataki Itto's story quest Act I could enter through a Quick Start. Release order and story order are the same; no other quest requires it.

## entry: Hanyuuda Chizuru
@keys: Hanyuuda Chizuru, Chizuru hagoita spirit
@version: 3.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Hanyuuda Chizuru is the name taken by the youkai in Akitsu Kimodameshi who haunts a beach near Chinju Forest. Her true body is a pair of hagoita once used by the youkai Kamai Kenji and a samurai, which have started to rot after hundreds of years. She can use youkai power near her body and little away from it, and frightens people with props such as shade cloth, fireworks and sleeping powder. She decides to attend the festival at the end.

## entry: Kamai Kenji and Yanagibashi Takuto
@keys: Kamai Kenji, Yanagibashi Takuto, Kamai and the samurai
@version: 3.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the legend told in Akitsu Kimodameshi, the youkai Kamai Kenji welcomes a human samurai, Yanagibashi Takuto, to the Mikawa Flower Festival and proposes a duel every ten years. They became close friends over decades of drinking, travel and sparring; at their final meeting the samurai was sixty-five and too frail to finish a last game of Akitsu Hazura. Yae Miko later names Takuto as founder of the Souran Isshin Art.

## entry: Akitsu Yuugei
@keys: Akitsu Yuugei, Akitsu Hazura
@version: 3.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Akitsu Yuugei is the brick-breaking minigame at the heart of Akitsu Kimodameshi. The player controls a bar to bounce a Skill-Ball into bricks, with elemental reactions giving special effects. In the story it was built by Youko, Kappa and Hitotsume Kozou as a symbol of friendship based on the lost game Akitsu Hazura (Haneasobi), a festival game played with hagoita.

## entry: Test of Courage rounds
@keys: Test of Courage, Dango Milk
@keys2: Chinju Forest, Akitsu Kimodameshi
@version: 3.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The event's contest has three rounds, each a search in Chinju Forest for an item chosen by one organiser: Dango Milk, a fan, and a pair of hagoita. Contestants play in pairs; leaving early forfeits prizes; winning all three earns the Grand Narukami Shrine's grand prize.
