## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.4
### LITE
Fleeting Colors in Flight was the Version 2.4 Lantern Rite flagship event (25 January to 12 February 2022). Liyue's Qixing prepare a first fireworks show for the festival, with Keqing in charge and badly overworked. The Traveler and Paimon accompany her as she brings festive gifts to the adepti, help catch Treasure Hoarders who have been stealing the fireworks, and finally tell her a story about resting before the show begins.
### STANDARD
The story is told in three parts: "The Blazing Stars Ring in Fortune" (Ningguang and Keqing introduce the fireworks show, and Zhongli suggests a storytelling approach to get Keqing to rest), "Sky-Gazers, Land-Walkers" (the gift round with Keqing, then the discovery that fireworks have been stolen from Qingce Village) and "The Stars Inscribe the Year's Wishes" (the trap for the thieves, the story at the Jade Chamber and the show itself). Along the way the adepti, Qiqi, Baizhu, Yanfei, Xingqiu and Chongyun appear. The mood is warm and humorous, with an underlying theme about balancing duty and rest.
### LARGE +
The event is set shortly after the Jade Chamber's rebuilding and the defeat of Osial, which Paimon recalls in the opening; Keqing thanks Zhongli for his help in the Rite of Parting. Because it is a limited-time event, its scenes are known only to those who took part. It is the second Lantern Rite event, after the 2021 Lantern Rite.
### ULTRA +
The event continues threads from the Liyue Archon Quest: the adepti having handed Liyue Harbor to mortals, Ningguang and Keqing leading the Qixing, and Shenhe's recent arrival in Liyue Harbor. It also gives the first detailed look at how Keqing's drive affects those around her, a theme she herself admits at the finale.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Part I, The Blazing Stars Ring in Fortune: At Yujing Terrace Ningguang explains that the festival this year will include a fireworks show organised by the Ministry of Civil Affairs, with Keqing responsible. Keqing is seen revising plans, adding Qingce Village to the show's range and refusing to rest. Pengyi gives the Traveler a Launch Tube for designing fireworks. Ningguang worries about Keqing's long hours and asks the Traveler to help. At the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor and then Third-Round Knockout Zhongli advises that a story containing the message would work better than a lecture.

Part II, Sky-Gazers, Land-Walkers: Keqing asks the Traveler to introduce her to the adepti so she can deliver Qixing gifts. They visit Madame Ping, Cloud Retainer at Mt. Aocang, and Xiao at Wangshu Inn, and meet Qiqi and Baizhu on the way. Pengyi then reports that all the fireworks at Qingce Village have been stolen. The Traveler and Keqing follow footprints and meet Xingqiu and Chongyun, who have caught a Treasure Hoarder while escorting the Feiyun Commerce Guild's festival supplies. Keqing promises to find the stolen fireworks herself.

Part III, The Stars Inscribe the Year's Wishes: Keqing finds the stolen stash and waits with the Traveler for the hoarders to return. The Millelith's approach scares the hoarders into abandoning the fireworks; Keqing and the Traveler confront their leader Xingxiu and force a surrender. The Traveler meets Zhongli and Paimon invents a story with his help. At the Jade Chamber the Traveler tells Keqing a story of a chef who works himself to ruin, she recognises the message and admits she wishes she could slow time. The Fireworks Show begins, and everyone wishes each other a happy Lantern Rite.
### LARGE +
Part I in more detail. Ningguang explains that fireworks were originally firecrackers, invented in Liyue to ward off beasts and signal other farmers, and that the festival uses them to honour the toughness of Liyue's ancestors. She says Jiangzhou, who planned the festival before, has moved departments because her father is old, leaving Ningguang as head organiser and Keqing in charge of the show. Keqing criticises the budget excuse from her subordinate Pengyi and tells him the budget must serve the goal. After Ningguang leaves, Paimon and the Traveler decide to consult Zhongli because he knows the people of Liyue best. At Third-Round Knockout he is eating with Hu Tao, Xiangling and Guoba. Zhongli says that as a mere consultant his words no longer carry weight, and that a story that touches on Keqing's daily life and friends would hit harder. Keqing arrives to ask the Traveler to take her to the adepti and agrees to watch the fireworks together afterwards.

Part II in more detail. At Madame Ping's, Yanfei (working through the festival) and Madame Ping thank her for gifts of seasonal goods, silk and flower seeds, and Madame Ping notes that Keqing designed the street decorations but has not seen them. At Mt. Aocang Cloud Retainer receives a Ministry-modified firework as a gadget, admires its design, and mentions Moon Carver, who has gone travelling because he cannot enter the city, and Mountain Shaper, who left to look for treasures. Ganyu is also visiting but leaves hurriedly when Cloud Retainer begins recalling her childhood. Shenhe brings food from the city because she has learned that people share food at Lantern Rite. Cloud Retainer tells them to look after each other and gives the advice used later: test your own work like a chef tasting his food, and take rest. At Wangshu Inn Qiqi and Baizhu are collecting digestive herbs for Bubu Pharmacy, and Xiao appears silently. He accepts the Qixing's gifts unwillingly, warns that karma is harmful to mortals and refuses to join the city fireworks. Pengyi then arrives with news of the theft in Qingce Village, and because the Millelith are short-handed Keqing refuses to ask the adepti, and says she will manage. Granny Ruoxin describes the theft. Xingqiu explains that he and Chongyun are escorting festival goods; Chongyun mistook the Treasure Hoarders for evil spirits (firecrackers were once used to drive away spirits), and Xingqiu agrees to guard Qingce Village.

Part III in more detail. Keqing returns with a lead: wagon tracks and fireworks packaging led her to the stash. A hoarder, Xiuyong, and leader Xingxiu plan to steal fireworks and later firecrackers to smuggle into the city, set them off during the opening ceremony and rob Liyue Harbor while the Millelith are distracted. The Millelith, having made the captured scout talk, arrive and the hoarders scatter. Keqing and the Traveler fight the remaining hoarders. Xingxiu surrenders on recognising the Yuheng. The Traveler delivers the prisoners to Qingce Village where Pengyi and Jingcheng of the Millelith are waiting. Everyone hears an explosion, which turns out to be Keqing accidentally lighting loose fireworks while searching the site.

At Third-Round Knockout Zhongli, who has been chatting with Yun Jin, hears the views the Traveler collected and helps shape the story. At the Ministry Jingcheng sends the Traveler and Keqing off together, and at the Jade Chamber the story is told. Then a messenger, Baishi, brings an imported-style outfit that Ningguang had her tailor make for Keqing, the fireworks begin, and the finale shows friends watching from the harbor.
### ULTRA +
Details of the story the Traveler tells. A talented chef opens a restaurant near the city gate, will not take on help, believes only he can turn good ingredients into fine dishes, never eats his own food, and in six months drives away his customers and closes the restaurant for good. Paimon built it from the advice they gathered: Cloud Retainer's remark that a chef who never tastes his own food courts trouble, Yanfei's saying that a rested worker is an efficient worker, and Xingqiu's habit of handing out work to the right people. Keqing calls the ending predictable but admits she knows the lesson in theory and loses sight of it when tasks are hers. She says she was once so impatient that colleagues left within three months, that she is getting better, and that she kept her promise to share the show.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Keqing, privately, thinks Cloud Retainer is wary of mortal rule and wants a good first impression from the Qixing. Cloud Retainer asks Ganyu, Shenhe and Keqing to look out for one another, and tells Keqing that Ganyu forgets food and rest when busy and must be made to eat and sleep. Cloud Retainer says Moon Carver felt he could not simply roam into Liyue Harbor under the agreement that left the city to the Qixing, and went travelling instead. The Qixing now bear the daily running of Liyue.
### LARGE +
Xiao recognises Keqing from the fight against Osial and says her swordplay is fierce. His post-quest line says the demons have retreated and humans are safe, which he thinks Pervases would have counted as a lifelong wish come true, and says that the fireworks can be seen from anywhere and that anyone who wants him can find him. Ganyu says she planned to go home but Ningguang told her to take a proper vacation.

## consequences

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.4 as a Flagship Event (with a Co-Op mode). It required Adventure Rank 28, the Archon Quest Chapter I Act III (A New Star Approaches) and Interlude Chapter Act I (The Crane Returns on the Wind). It follows the 2021 Lantern Rite and precedes later Lantern Rite events such as A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. 25 January to 12 February 2022. Four challenge themes and a shop: Flameplume Starflowers (smelting and launching fireworks with the Launch Tube gadget, earning Affluence Talismans), The Great Gathering (recovering scattered materials in Guyun Stone Forest, including by sea on the Waverider, earning Immaculate Talismans), Wondrous Shadows (solving Shadow Lantern puzzles with Yuan Rong, earning Affluence Talismans) and Oceanic Defender (fighting Beisht, earning Conquest Talismans). Prosperous Partnerships exchanges 1,000 Affluence and 1,000 Conquest Talismans to invite a 4-star Liyue character; the Afterglow Market sells furnishings, a Crown of Insight and talent materials; and collecting enough talismans unlocks Ningguang's outfit Orchid's Evening Gown ("The Moon's Comely Brow"). The Launch Tube remains usable for launching after the event.

## entry: Fireworks Show
@keys: Fireworks Show, Launch Tube
@keys2: Lantern Rite, Keqing
@version: 2.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Ministry of Civil Affairs' first big fireworks show at Lantern Rite, organised by Keqing with Ningguang as head organiser. Fireworks are placed across Liyue, including Qingce Village, and fired together at a chosen moment. Ningguang explains that fireworks descend from firecrackers used by Liyue's ancestors for warding off beasts and signalling. The best view is said to be from the Jade Chamber.

## entry: The fireworks theft
@keys: stolen fireworks, Qingce fireworks theft
@keys2: Treasure Hoarders, Keqing, Xingxiu
@version: 2.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders steal the fireworks placed at Qingce Village and elsewhere and stash them, planning to use them to cause a distraction on opening night and rob the harbor. Xingqiu and Chongyun catch one hoarder, Kangsheng, who insists he is mistaken. Keqing finds the cache and the Millelith corner the hoarders led by Xingxiu.
### LARGE +
Xingxiu admits the hoarders had decided to steal firecrackers as well when they ran short of fireworks, since a loud bang alone would draw the Millelith away. The Feiyun Commerce Guild's capture of one hoarder makes the leader recall the rest of his gang, which leads to their meeting Keqing.

### STANDARD
Ningguang asks the Traveler to persuade Keqing to rest. Zhongli proposes a story, built from advice from Cloud Retainer, Yanfei and Xingqiu, about a chef who will not delegate or eat, falls ill and loses his customers. Keqing listens at the Jade Chamber, tells the Traveler the lesson is something she knows but forgets, and lets herself enjoy the show.
