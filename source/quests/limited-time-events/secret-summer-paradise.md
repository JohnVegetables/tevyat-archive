## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### LITE
Secret Summer Paradise was a limited-time summer event in Version 3.8 (5 July to 16 August 2023). Klee receives a letter from her mother Alice inviting her to a friend's place in Sumeru, and she, Kaeya, the Traveler and Paimon travel there and find a bottle-shaped refuge called the Veluriyam Mirage, where a spirit-like host named Idyia needs help repairing a broken central hub.
### STANDARD
The Mirage is a glass-bottle world holding an amusement-park-like village. Idyia, who looks after it, welcomes visitors, and when the Traveler's group arrives the place is failing. Over three acts they gather four missing components for a spindle-like core (one from each of several residents), meet Sangonomiya Kokomi, Eula and Collei who are also there, and discover that Idyia is an Oceanid and that the residents are Hydro Eidolon copies of earlier visitors. The core is restarted with the help of Kokomi's relic, the Shinrou Casket.
### LARGE +
Players who collected enough Fascinating Phenocrysts could invite Layla (Cryo) to the party, and Joyeux Vouchers collected in the Mirage could be exchanged for Kaeya's outfit "Sailwind Shadow", which was sold in the Character Outfit Shop after the end of 3.8. The event required Adventure Rank 18 and the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
Act I, An Invitation From Afar!: Klee, Kaeya, the Traveler and Paimon use a Magic Invite to reach the bottle-world of the Veluriyam Mirage and meet its host Idyia, who needs four components to repair the central hub. Act II, Mirage Paradise in Crisis!: the group repairs a fairground tent with Maymunah, rescues the trapped Lessig and obtains a candy-box component. Act III, Dreams and First Encounters!: they perform Zosimos's play for the last component, learn that Idyia is an Oceanid and that residents are Hydro Eidolon copies of past visitors, and restart the hub with Kokomi's Shinrou Casket.
### LARGE
Act I, An Invitation From Afar! Jean asks the Traveler and Paimon to take Klee to Sumeru. Klee has been in solitary confinement at the Knights of Favonius headquarters and has just received her mother Alice's letter, which includes a "Magic Invite" that teleports its users anywhere, but only three times. Jean sends Kaeya with them. They first go to Gandharva Ville and find Collei, and Eula, who has come after hearing that one of her Lawrence cousins in Sumeru wants to restore the clan's honour. Following the invitation, Collei works out the target is somewhere in the desert; they teleport, cannot see anything, then call for help and enter a bottle, finding a magical world inside. They meet Idyia and Sangonomiya Kokomi, who is already there. Idyia is surprised to see guests and apologises that the domain has suddenly gone downhill. The group splits up to find the four missing components, getting the first one and then helping Ferdinand, Jeroney and Haddo, the caretakers of the cart area, to get it running again.

Act II, Mirage Paradise in Crisis! Next morning Eula, Collei and Idyia head for the fairgrounds where the manager, Maymunah, a Spantamad scholar, explains that the world was suddenly turned upside down. Something falling from the central hub knocked down the Crystal Light in the big tent and trapped Hydro Eidolons, and Lessig, a Lawrence clan man who tried to help, was also trapped. Everyone sets about fixing the fallen lights. Lessig refuses help; Idyia calms things with candy and Lessig admits his mistake. Maymunah says she came to the Mirage to run her own festival. The crystal is raised, the Eidolons freed, and Maymunah gives them a central hub component, a candy box. Kokomi says she is looking for the Shinrou Casket, lost long ago from Watatsumi Island, and all clues lead to the Mirage.

Act III, Dreams and First Encounters! Lessig confesses that he is the cause of the chaos: he touched a water orb, the domain's core, without knowing. The Traveler, Klee, Kaeya and Kokomi find the last component with Zosimos, a stage director, who will give it only after they perform his play. Klee, Kaeya and Kokomi act, the Traveler moves preprint props, and Zosimos is moved to receive costumes sewn for him by Idyia; he hands over a bottle-lamp. The group places the three components in the spindle-like core, but it does not move. Kokomi senses elemental energy in all the residents, deduces they are Hydro Eidolons that can take human form, and Idyia reveals she is an Oceanid. The residents are copies of previous human visitors, made by Idyia with the Streaming Projector to realise their wishes, and the components were gifts visitors left her. Kokomi proposes borrowing the Shinrou Casket from the core; Idyia wishes that everyone who came to the Mirage achieves what they truly wish for, and the casket glows and the hub turns again. Later at the top of the wheel Idyia tells Klee and the Traveler she comes there alone whenever a visitor leaves.
### ULTRA +
Details. In Act I the group uses the preprint (a 2D side-view puzzle entered through Streaming Projectors) to free a trapped Water Droplet that leads them to the town. Eula and Collei could not make much progress at first and planned to explore the northern jungle. Idyia explains that Ferdinand, Jeroney and Haddo were rescued from a sandstorm after setting out on an adventure. In Act II, Maymunah recalls helping her father with festivities at the Grand Bazaar and says she wanted to become her true self. In Act III, Zosimos wanted to write an epic story about a thief and a mage; Paimon and Idyia give honest criticism of the play. Idyia explains that the casket listens to wishes made by those who pray to it, building "shinki", which gives it power.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Idyia is an Oceanid. The residents of the Veluriyam Mirage (Zosimos, Maymunah, the cart caretakers and others) are Hydro Eidolon replicas of previous human visitors, created to carry on the visitors' dreams. The central components are gifts from those visitors. Visitors cannot take what they make in the Mirage into the outside world; some were angry at that and destroyed everything, while others asked for a replica of themselves to stay. Kokomi's Shinrou Casket works through wishes people have prayed over it.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Veluriyam Mirage page notes that Kaveh redesigned many buildings in the domain according to Idyia, and it is only "supposedly" in Sumeru.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
The central hub turns again and the Mirage returns to normal. Lessig offers to help maintain the domain. Klee gets the ride on the Choo-Choo Cart she wanted, and Idyia lets them enjoy the domain. Kokomi's search for the Shinrou Casket is only partly resolved on screen: the casket is borrowed from the core to help repair it.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.8 as a Flagship Event, after the Sumeru Archon Quests (Song of the Dragon and Freedom is the entry requirement; Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and some Story Quests are recommended with a Quick Start). Event quests are self-contained and not part of the main storyline.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Four mini-games unlocked in phases: Spino Blaster (water-cannon marksmanship), Sojourns of the Barking Fox (speed trials with a toy fox), Dance of Flashing Thought (combat challenges earning Honed Will) and Bing-Bang Finchball (launching Finchballs for score). The Choo-Choo Cart, preprints, Gleaming Lamps and Joyeux Vouchers are exploration features of the area.

## entry: Veluriyam Mirage
@keys: Veluriyam Mirage, Bottleland
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A limited-time summer event area: a domain hidden within a bottle in the middle of the Sumeru desert. Rumour says those who enter are granted their heart's desire. It is run by Idyia and populated by Hydro Eidolons who copy past visitors. The wiki notes it is not treated as a normal Domain in gameplay.

## entry: Idyia
@keys: Idyia
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An Oceanid and the caretaker of the Veluriyam Mirage, who looks after it and likes to spend time alone at the top of the wheel when a visitor leaves. She gives visitors candy and helps them restore the central hub, and in the finale wishes for all visitors to achieve their true wishes.

## entry: Shinrou Casket
@keys: Shinrou Casket
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A relic lost long ago from Watatsumi Island that Kokomi seeks. It records the prayers of those who come to it, building "shinki" that gives it its power, and is used to restart the Mirage's central hub.

## entry: Lessig
@keys: Lessig
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A man of the Lawrence clan, cousin to Eula, who came to the Mirage to restore the clan's honour but found nobody cared about it. He triggered the disaster by touching a water orb. After being trapped and reconciling with Eula he helps maintain the domain.

## entry: Choo-Choo Cart
@keys: Choo-Choo Cart
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A cart ride in the Mirage looked after by Ferdinand, Jeroney and Haddo. Klee wishes to ride it to the top of the wheel at the end of the story.
