## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.1
### LITE
Moonlight Merriment was the Version 2.1 flagship event, starting 27 September 2021, built around Liyue's Moonchase Festival. Xiangling enters the festival's Masterful Chefs cooking competition and the Traveler and Paimon help her prepare. A side investigation into the old Stove God ends when a cracked stone statue opens to show Guoba as he once was, and Madame Ping reveals that Guoba is the Stove God Marchosius, who gave up his power to protect Liyue.
### STANDARD
The story runs in two parts, "One for the Foodies, Two for the Show" and "Three to Get Ready, and Here We Go". Part I is a hunt for inspiration and for the history of the Stove God, which pulls in Keqing, Ningguang, Beidou and several other Liyue residents. Part II is the taste-testing, the final cook-off between Xiangling and Smiley Yanxiao, and the awakening of the statue. Alongside the story the event offered the Moonlight Seeker and Trail of Delicacies challenges and a limited claymore reward, the Luxurious Sea-Lord. It is warm, food-centred and light in tone, with one quiet and moving revelation at its end.
### LARGE +
Because it was a limited-time event, the 2021 story is canon but not common knowledge in Teyvat: most people in Liyue know the festival and the competition, not the Traveler's part in it. The event's own requirements are Archon Quest Chapter I, Act III (A New Star Approaches), Trulla Chapter Act I (Mondstadt Gastronomy Trip) and Adventure Rank 28.
### ULTRA +
The Moonchase Festival has since been given a longer history in later canon (see its own entry). This entry covers only what the 2021 event says: the Stove God, the Guili Assembly, and the festival's unification by Rex Lapis in Madame Ping's telling.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Part I. Paimon pulls the Traveler to Wanmin Restaurant for a meal, where Xiangling explains that she has reached the Masterful Chefs competition of this year's Moonchase Festival (theme: "Feast of the Bounteous Land") and needs fresh ideas. They ask around the city for inspiration, and at Yujing Terrace find Beidou, Ningguang and Keqing around a strange large stone that was fished out of the sea. Nobody knows what is inside it. Keqing mentions her grandfather's research into Liyue traditions and the possibility that the Stove God is a figure separate from the Geo Archon, so the group goes to Qingce Village and then Wangshu Inn to dig through old texts. Along the way Smiley Yanxiao trades old texts for the Traveler's Satisfying Salad and turns out to be a rival entrant. Back in Liyue Harbor the adeptus Cloud Retainer appears to announce a showpiece of her own, a "supreme cuisine machine", and Keqing returns with a recipe from her grandfather's notes. Madame Ping, Xiangling's teacher, then explains the festival's history and the Stove God. The part ends with the stone beginning to crack.

Part II. With the competition narrowed to the finals, Xiangling collects more feedback. After Beidou and Xinyan, Paimon suggests someone who prefers mild food, and the Traveler calls Xiao, who has promised to come when his name is called. He praises the cooking and suggests easing the strong seasoning on the side dishes. The next day Xiangling and Yanxiao cook against each other, with Guoba cheering Yanxiao on when he falls behind. Xiangling wins. A downcast Keqing blames herself for having picked Yanxiao's Golden Shrimp Balls over Xiangling, and Xiangling reassures her. The statue has not changed, so Xiangling cooks the Chili-Mince Cornbread Buns from the recipe Ping had filled in. When Keqing eats them the stone cracks open and reveals a statue of Guoba as he once was.
### LARGE +
Part I in more detail. Before narrowing the search, the group asks Hu Tao and Baizhu for opinions. At Qingce Village Xingqiu and Chongyun are present, and Xingqiu lets Keqing borrow her grandfather's texts, which place the Stove God at the Guili Assembly. At Wangshu Inn the old texts and Keqing's own information conflict on some points. A Treasure Hoarders skirmish near the Feiyun Commerce Guild warehouse is part of the quest steps. Xiangling comes to suspect that the Stove God may have been an adeptus, which is why she takes the group to Ping.

Part II in more detail. The cook-off is a timed contest in which the Traveler helps Xiangling make three dishes, Crystal Shrimp, Triple-Layered Consomme and Jueyun Chili Chicken. Afterwards the two chefs taste each other's dishes and part on good terms. Keqing eats the buns, a dish tied to her grandfather's research that she had wanted to taste; the nostalgia and longing of that moment is what Ping says resonated with the statue.
### ULTRA +
Minor beats. Guoba greets Ping warmly. The event also added world quests (the Contraption-Contrived Cooking Course in three parts, and The Many Matters of the Moonchase Festival). Event-only NPCs include Linyang (hints for Moonlight Seeker) and Sheng (in Part I). Many ordinary Liyue NPCs, among them Chef Mao, Huai'an, Nadia and Vlad, have temporary festival dialogue.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Madame Ping's account of the festival and the Stove God, as told in the quest:
- Moonchase began as a rite of the adepti; ordinary people copied it, producing various local festivals. Rex Lapis later unified them under the Moonchase name to honour an old friend, the Stove God, Marchosius.
- Marchosius loved humanity. When the Guili Assembly was flooded and the people retreated south to Liyue Harbor through a storm, the adepti stayed by them and he cooked a flatbread with meat sauce. Centuries later, when disaster and plague returned, he placed all his power into the land to quell them, which left him much smaller and with greatly reduced wits. He told Ping and Rex Lapis about dishes that bring joy and the secrets of flame, then went into the mountains for a long slumber.
- When he awoke he ate Chili-Mince Cornbread Buns left on an offering table by a young woman in yellow. He did not remember the past, but was moved and followed her. He is now Guoba, who accompanies Xiangling.
- Ping says the real Stove God was never inside the statue; he has been with them all along.
### LARGE +
Ping calls Marchosius the closest of all the adepti to the common folk and describes his sacrifice as a god "surrendering power to the soil". She suggests that Guoba, though diminished, is carefree and at rest. Keqing points out that Guoba recognised a dish he himself invented and approved of the cook.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Xiangling is the protagonist of the event: a chef from Wanmin Restaurant who is Madame Ping's student and Guoba's companion. Keqing drives the research into the Stove God through her grandfather's notes. Madame Ping holds the Stove God's history. Smiley Yanxiao is Xiangling's rival in the final. Cloud Retainer appears briefly with a showpiece of her own. Ningguang and Beidou appear at Yujing Terrace, with Beidou and Xinyan later giving taste feedback. Xiao tastes Xiangling's dishes at the Traveler's call. Zhongli appears at the end and greets Guoba as an old friend.
### LARGE +
Hu Tao, Baizhu, Xingqiu, Chongyun, Ganyu and Qiqi also appear in Part I or the cinematic, as does Chef Mao, Xiangling's father, whose restaurant is the base for the preparations.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
No lasting plot consequence follows beyond the new understanding of who Guoba was. Xiangling resolves to take good care of him and let him eat, drink and enjoy himself. The Moonchase Festival continues as an annual occasion, and Guoba stays with Xiangling.

## entry: Masterful Chefs Cook-off
@keys: Masterful Chefs, Moonlight Seeker, Trail of Delicacies
@version: 2.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Masterful Chefs is the Moonchase Festival cooking competition organised by the Liyue Qixing. In the 2021 event Xiangling and Smiley Yanxiao reach the final. The Traveler helps Xiangling cook three dishes, Xiangling wins, and the two rivals taste each other's cooking on good terms. Moonlight Seeker and Trail of Delicacies were the event's challenge strands, unlocked in three waves from 27 September 2021, with rewards including the Luxurious Sea-Lord claymore.

## entry: Stove God Marchosius
@keys: Stove God, Marchosius, God of the Stove, Patron God of the Soil
@version: 2.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the Moonlight Merriment event, Madame Ping says Guoba is Marchosius, the Stove God, an adeptus who loved humanity and gave all his power to the land to protect Liyue from disaster and plague. His body shrank and his intellect declined. He cannot speak and remembers nothing of his earlier life, but recognises familiar flavours and old friends. The event shows a statue of him in his former form, revealed when Keqing eats a long-desired dish.
