## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
### LITE
Chromatic Ode of Candies and Roses was a limited-time event in Version 5.1 (23 October to 7 November 2024), set in Sumeru during the Sabzeruz Festival, which marks Nahida's birthday. Her friends secretly prepare a birthday surprise built around candy and the Flower Carriage Parade, while Candace's odd visions lead to half of an ancient relic, King Deshret's Wedjat Eye.
### STANDARD
The event story, told in three "gifts" (Give Her Surprises, Give Her Sweetness, Give Her Memories), follows a group of friends in and around Port Ormos and Pardis Dhyai who plan a candy-tower surprise, a Haft-Mewa feast and a Parade for the Dendro Archon without letting her find out. Alongside this, Candace's gem seems to show her events before they happen, a caravan is robbed, and Nilou disappears to learn an older version of the Dance of Sabzeruz from a Pari named Sorush. The mystery resolves into the legend of the first Sabzeruz Festival and ends with a successful Parade and a group photograph.
### LARGE +
Players who gathered 800 Festive Fervor could invite Candace to the party. Requirements were Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III Archon Quest; the Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and several Sumeru quests were recommended.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
First Gift, Give Her Surprises: friends plan a birthday surprise for Nahida in Port Ormos, and Candace begins to see things just before they happen after buying a gem. Second Gift, Give Her Sweetness: a robbed caravan, a misunderstood conversation about a candy tower, and a close call when Nahida nearly discovers the plan. Third Gift, Give Her Memories: Nilou goes missing and is found learning the original Dance of Sabzeruz from the Pari Sorush; Sethos explains the gem is half of a Wedjat Eye, and the Parade goes ahead.
### LARGE
First Gift, Give Her Surprises. Paimon and the Traveler arrive in Port Ormos just before the Sabzeruz Festival and find Tighnari, Collei, Dehya and Candace whispering about a plan: a birthday surprise for the Dendro Archon, an idea Dehya had while talking with Dunyarzad. The plan is a candy tower, a Haft-Mewa feast after the Carriage Parade, and decorations, with Kaveh doing most of the design. Candace asks the Traveler and Paimon to help shop. She haggles hard with a food stall, then, with Paimon, notices she is acting oddly: she twice reacts before accidents happen (a stove explosion, a falling crate that she stops from hitting a small girl). She explains she has been seeing things moments before they come true since buying an unusual gem that morning. They ask Dori to trace the gem; Candace bargains Dori down with a promise of food, shelter, security and tax relief in Aaru Village.

Second Gift, Give Her Sweetness. Dori reports the ore came from a nameless desert ruin and was cut into two gems because it was cracked. Meanwhile a caravan carrying supposed fireworks was robbed in a remote desert path by people with an apparent ability to predict the route. Candace and the Traveler find the caravan crew (Yakup, Hizir, Aruj), and Yakup mentions a bandit with a green stone like Candace's. At Caravan Ribat Cyno takes over the case. Back in Port Ormos Candace has another vision and overhears a seemingly sinister plot, which turns out to be Kaveh and Alhaitham talking about the candy tower. Alhaitham suggests the visions may show the past, not the future. Faruzan helps Kaveh with a model of the Flower Carriage, and when Nahida unexpectedly appears the group scrambles to hide the plan, helped by Nilou drawing her away. Tighnari moves the meeting place to Pardis Dhyai.

Third Gift, Give Her Memories. Nilou, the dancer for the Parade, is missing. Cyno, Candace and the Traveler follow her trail out of the city to the Fane of Ashvattha, where they find her with Sethos and a Pari named Sorush, who is teaching her the original Dance of Sabzeruz. Sorush and her companions were behind the caravan attack; Sethos had talked her out of going further. Sethos, who owns the second half of the gem, explains it is a cracked Wedjat Eye. The group returns to finish the tower, the Traveler is chosen as the Knight of Flowers, and the Parade proceeds. Afterwards everyone celebrates, and Nahida has a group photograph taken.
### ULTRA +
Details. In the first act Dehya's desert mercenary style and Collei's nerves provide comic relief, and Layla (worried by deadline) is only heard of in the open world. The caravan crew's comic bickering hides that their cargo was not clearly fireworks. Cyno is told a second gem went to a young man in the desert and, in Act III, checks the original seller. In Act III Nilou had left a message with the Pari Rashnu, who fell asleep before delivering it, so no one knew where she was. Sorush calls the Traveler "my Yasnapati" and Paimon "Pale Floater". Candace plays the tanbur for Nilou's dance in the Parade, and Dunyarzad asks the Knight of Flowers (the Traveler) to hang decorations on the Flower Carriage at the Sanctuary of Surasthana.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Sethos tells the legend. At the first Sabzeruz Festival, King Deshret and Lady Malikata (Nabu Malikata, the Goddess of Flowers) secretly prepared a banquet for their friend, leading her along a trail of candy to a lake where Deshret played an instrument and Malikata danced. Malikata gave her the Khvarena to protect the rainforest; Deshret gave her the Wedjat Eye, to record their shared moments. The Wedjat Eye was lost after the Khaenri'ah cataclysm and buried in the sands. Sorush adds that Kusanali made the Khvarena into a divine bird guarding the border between birth and death, until it shattered and gave rise to the Pari. The Eye still records scenes, but only resonates with people of Al-Ahmar, the descendants of King Deshret.
### LARGE +
Candace fears that what remains in the cracked memory may be mostly grief, and the group agrees with Sethos placing both halves in the care of the Temple of Silence rather than giving them to Nahida straight away. Alhaitham later says he would only examine it if it were brought to the Akademiya for certification.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
Planners: Dehya, Candace, Tighnari, Collei, Kaveh, Faruzan, Dunyarzad, Cyno, Nilou (the dancer). Others: Alhaitham (sceptical helper), Sethos, the Pari Sorush, Rashnu and Mihir, Dori (informant), and the caravan crew Yakup, Hizir and Aruj. Nahida is the target of the surprise.
### LARGE +
Cyno, Kaveh and Alhaitham provide most of the humour; Sheikh Zubayr, Nevida and a gate guard named Rukn help trace Nilou. The Wanderer and Layla appear in the open world in ambient dialogue only.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
The Parade is a success: candy explodes over the Flower Carriage like fireworks. Afterwards a party at Pardis Dhyai ends with Nahida saying this will be her most unforgettable birthday and inviting the Pari to Sumeru City, which Sorush politely declines. The Wedjat Eye halves go to the Temple of Silence. Cyno takes over the caravan case and plans to watch it under cover of protection.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.1 as a Flagship Event, set in Sumeru after Nahida's rescue and reinstatement as Archon, when the Akademiya supports the festival again. The wiki notes the Sabzeruz Festival had been marginalised under the Akademiya's earlier leadership. The three story quests were removed after the event.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Three mini-games unlocked in sequence: Rocking Carriage (steer the Flower Carriage and collect flowers), Interreflection of Reality and Dreams (help the Amurta Dastur Dharmakirti run dreamscape therapy for patients) and Shadow of the Knight's Blade (battle challenges that recreate the legend of the Knight of Flowers). Playing them earned Festive Fervor toward the rewards and Candace's invitation.

## entry: Wedjat Eye
@keys: Wedjat Eye, cracked Wedjat Eye
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
King Deshret's birthday gift to Lesser Lord Kusanali (originally Rukkhadevata) at the first Sabzeruz Festival, intended to record shared memories. It was lost after the Khaenri'ah cataclysm, later cracked and cut into two gems, one bought by Candace and one by Sethos. It records past events, resonates with Al-Ahmar's people, and gives bearers vivid glimpses. Both halves go to the Temple of Silence.

## entry: Sorush
@keys: Sorush, Bloomguard of Khvarena
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A Pari who calls herself noble and the Bloomguard of Khvarena. Elder Zurvan chose her to present the Dance of Sabzeruz to Lesser Lord Kusanali. She attacked the caravan with Mihir's thorns (and a sleepy Rashnu), then took Nilou to the Fane of Ashvattha to teach her the original dance.

## entry: Dance of Sabzeruz
@keys: Dance of Sabzeruz
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The most important part of the Sabzeruz Festival, based on the Goddess of Flowers' dance. In this event Nilou learns the older form from Sorush and performs it in the Parade to Candace's tanbur playing.

## entry: Flower Carriage Parade
@keys: Flower Carriage, Knight of Flowers, Carriage Parade
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The festival procession in which Nahida rides the Flower Carriage, attended by an actor playing the Knight of Flowers. In this event the Traveler is nominated for the part by friends representing Darshans. Kaveh's candy tower bursts over the carriage like fireworks.

## entry: Haft-Mewa Feast
@keys: Haft-Mewa
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A festival feast with seven foods on the table. The friends plan one for Nahida after the Parade, and it is the setting of the post-Parade celebration.

## entry: Candace's visions
@keys: Candace gem, Candace visions
@keys2: Wedjat Eye, gem, vision
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
After buying a strange gem, Candace sees events seconds before they occur and anticipates a stove explosion and a falling crate. Alhaitham suggests she is seeing the past; later she hears faint voices she wonders were Al-Ahmar and Malikata. The gem is half of the cracked Wedjat Eye and only works for people of Al-Ahmar.
