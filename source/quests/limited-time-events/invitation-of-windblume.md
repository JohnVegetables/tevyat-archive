## summary
### LITE
Invitation of Windblume was a Version 1.4 flagship event (19 March to 5 April 2021) celebrating Mondstadt's Windblume Festival. With no commissions to take during the festival, the Traveler and Paimon become assistants to Venti, who is selling love-poem lessons to townsfolk, then trail a string of small festival errands (a missing admirer, a buried "threat", an alchemical flower, two friends locked in a temple). In the finale the Traveler is chosen as the year's Windblume Star and makes the formal flower offering to Barbatos.
### STANDARD +
The tone is light and community-minded: the event is a catalogue of how ordinary Mondstadters celebrate love and freedom, and it reuses well-known characters in small domestic roles. It is limited-time content and not universally known in-world.

## plot
### STANDARD
Part I (Ode to Flower and Cloud). The Traveler and Paimon find that the Guild and townsfolk have no work for them during the festival. Flora, the florist, explains the Windblume Festival as a day of love and freedom and mentions how people dispute what a Windblume is: Flora favours the dandelion, while Quinn, the fruit seller, insists on the Windwheel Aster. Venti, running a poetry class in the plaza, enlists the two as assistants: the Traveler gives advice to townsfolk such as Marvin (a young man whose father opposes his sweetheart), Ellin (who wants to leave flowers for the Acting Grand Master) and Albert (head of Barbara's fan club) while Venti signals approval or caution. Kaeya enrols as a student on condition of homework and a fee of Applebloom Cider.

Part II (Missive of Cloud and Fog). Collecting homework, the Traveler runs an errand for Kaeya's drinking companion Nimrod, who wants help with a gift for his wife. Kaeya's own homework is a single line in the hilichurl tongue. At the Cathedral Barbara receives a note saying something precious to her has been stolen and buried; Rosaria calms her, the Traveler follows the map to a spot near Dawn Winery, defeats Treasure Hoarders, and digs up a box holding a gift of a Windblume from an admirer.

Part III (Realm of Fog and Wind). Sucrose and Timaeus cannot read an old alchemical recipe for a "Windblume", so the Traveler takes it to Albedo's camp on Dragonspine; his version produces a sweet flower and Sucrose claims a larger "Tetratanic Sweet Flower" is a true ancient recipe. Bennett's homework is missing because he and Razor were trapped on opposite sides of a mechanism at the Thousand Winds Temple, guarded by a Ruin Guard; once freed, Bennett recites his poem, which includes a flower name Razor suggested (Wolfhook). At Angel's Share Kaeya explains the Windblume Star: a rare honour, performed alongside the Acting Grand Master.

Part IV (Dream of Wind and Flowers). The Traveler is named Windblume Star, meets Jean in the plaza and offers a Windblume of their choice to Barbatos at the ceremony. Venti then takes them to Stormterror's Lair, where disorderly wind currents must be calmed. Venti closes by telling the Traveler that, in his view, Windblumes do not exist yet are all around, and that each citizen makes their own choice. Dvalin flies overhead at the end and finds the gift prepared for him.
### LARGE +
Across the four parts the festival is shown as chiefly a time for sentiment between ordinary people, with the Knights mostly policing it (Lisa and Jean on patrol) and Kaeya covering Jean's desk work. Venti's poetry class is both a joke on his usual laziness and a genuine teaching moment: each student's letter is tailored to the recipient. The mystery items (Barbara's letter, Bennett and Razor's disappearance) turn out to have harmless explanations.
### ULTRA +
Part I's students are listed by Venti as Albert, Ellin, Marvin, Timaeus, Kaeya and Bennett. In Part II Lisa and Jean exchange banter about Lisa's habit of zapping brawlers, and Athos is a Knight who notes Kaeya covers the Acting Grand Master's paperwork. In Part III, Bennett says he and Razor were separated by an unexpected mechanism; Razor was not at fault. The Windblume Star's duties, as Kaeya describes them, include attending the Windblume Ceremony with the Acting Grand Master and making an offering on behalf of the people of Mondstadt.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Nothing in the event changes the main story. It adds festival lore through dialogue: the Windblume Festival is a festival of love and freedom, the Windblume is a plant nobody can identify, and the Windblume Star is an honour for someone who has made great sacrifices or proved themselves over the festival period. Albedo judges the alchemical recipe, from a forgotten era, to be innovative in its thinking and worth trying. Venti's remark that Windblumes "don't exist, yet they are all around us" affirms the old Mondstadt custom of letting everyone pick their own flower.
### LARGE +
The Windblume Ode item text, available from the event shop, retells the old legend of a flower that grew stronger in the winds and became a greeting among those who resisted a tyrant in his tower; it ties the plant to people resisting a tyrant who watched over them from a tower.

## characters
### STANDARD
Venti, whose role as paid poetry teacher covers for his godhood and prompts Paimon's teasing, plays opposite Kaeya's mischief. Jean appears as Acting Grand Master and ceremony partner; Lisa, Barbara, Rosaria, Bennett, Razor, Sucrose, Timaeus, Albedo, Diluc and Katheryne have small parts. Townsfolk Flora, Quinn, Marvin, Ellin, Albert, Nimrod and Marjorie provide the festival colour.
### LARGE +
Dvalin appears at the end of Part IV, flying over Stormterror's Lair.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No lasting plot change. The event's weapon, Windblume Ode (a four-star bow), and its refinement material are obtained in the event shop.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.4, event window 19 March to 5 April 2021, with the shop open until 12 April. The prerequisite is the Prologue Archon Quest (Song of the Dragon and Freedom) and Adventure Rank 20; the story takes place in Mondstadt at the time of that year's Windblume Festival.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Labelled game content: Festive Challenges (Bullseye Balloons, Floral Freefall, Ballads of Breeze) earned Festive Tour Tickets, and the Peculiar Wonderland activities earned Peculiar Collab Coupons.

## entry: The Windblume Star

@version: 1.4

### STANDARD
In Dream of Wind and Flowers Kaeya describes the Windblume Star as one of Mondstadt's greatest honours, normally given to someone who has made major sacrifices for the city or proved themselves over the festival. The Star attends the Windblume Ceremony with the Acting Grand Master and makes the people's offering to the Anemo Archon. The Traveler holds the title for that year.

## entry: Venti's poetry class
@keys: love poem, poetry masterclass, Applebloom Cider
@keys2: Venti, Windblume
@version: 1.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the event Venti charges fees (in Mora or alcohol) to teach festival-goers to write love poems. Kaeya enrols with a deal that Venti must grade his homework and be paid in Applebloom Cider; Albert, Ellin, Marvin, Timaeus and Bennett are the other named students. The Traveler and Paimon act as assistants who give advice while Venti signals with hand gestures. The class is a comic device in the event; it is not a recurring institution.
