## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.6
### LITE
Hues of the Violet Garden was a limited-time Flagship event in Version 2.6 (7 to 25 April 2022). In Ritou, Kamisato Ayato and Yae Miko hold the Irodori Festival, a celebration of the legendary Five Kasen poets, and invite guests from other nations. The Traveler and Paimon act as guides and follow a trail of mysterious poem excerpts, the story chapter True Tales of the Violet Garden, which leads Kaedehara Kazuha to the truth about what happened to his family's sword-forging art.
### STANDARD
Across four acts the Traveler greets guests as they arrive at Ritou's harbour: Albedo and Klee from Mondstadt, with Venti arriving by accident; Xingqiu with calligraphers and Theater Mechanicus players from Liyue; then Kazuha. Each guest finds an anonymous excerpt from the legend of the Five Kasen, and the excerpts and the guests' troubles gradually connect. A new light-novel volume goes missing, Xingqiu's signature trouble is solved, and Albedo paints the poets' portraits using the guests as models. The fourth act connects the story to the downfall of the Raiden Gokaden, five Inazuman forging schools, and an old failed sword commission. At the end Ayato admits he and Miko staged the hunt to help Kazuha find the truth.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest part "Ritou Escape Plan". The recommended story quests were Ayato's The Firmiana Leaf Falls, the Raiden Shogun's Transient Dreams and Kokomi's Warriors' Dreams Like Spring Grass Renewing. This was the first event with a Quick Start option for players who had not done them. The reward character was Xingqiu.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Act I, The Strange Tale of Suikou's Tipsy Tincture: The Adventurers' Guild gives the Traveler a commission from Miko to guide Irodori Festival guests. Albedo, Klee and Venti arrive; a novel volume is missing; Sara catches Venti as a suspected intruder; Albedo takes inspiration from a Suikou excerpt and paints Venti as Suikou. Act II, What the Blue Flowers Left Behind: Xingqiu arrives with a second excerpt, and the Traveler and Albedo find that his calligraphy is why he came early. Act III, A New Painting in Pale Scarlet: Kazuha arrives with a third excerpt, the group plans author seals to help Xingqiu, and Ayaka and Venti bring a paper that reveals more writing in water. Kazuha begins to talk about his family. Act IV, The Palace, Dyed in Black: Kazuha learns the cause of the old sword failure and the group finds a hidden letter, then Albedo's last portrait is revealed.
### LARGE
Act I. Katheryne tells the Traveler and Paimon of a commission from Yae Miko at Yae Publishing House. Miko explains that the Irodori Festival honours the Five Kasen poets with a modern twist: it showcases light novels and presents Inazuma's culture to the world, now that the Sakoku Decree is lifted. In Ritou, Ayato (as head of the Yashiro Commission) asks the Traveler, who is well travelled and knows Inazuma, to greet and guide foreign guests, and hands over a ship arrival schedule. The first ship from Mondstadt brings bards and then Albedo, invited as a special guest to paint the poets' portraits, and Klee, whom he brings along; Venti is not aboard as Paimon hoped. Albedo's contact, Mr. Hirayama, is missing, and at the Outlander Affairs Agency the editors panic: the new volume of A Legend of Sword by Zhenyu has gone missing and they plan to reprint. Locals also report a suspicious individual at a warehouse. At the warehouse Kujou Sara has caught Venti, who has come without his Invitation Pass. Venti was invited by Ayato but boarded a wine ship and fell asleep drinking, waking in a crate outside Sailing Breeze. Albedo concludes the thieves had inside access, planned for a while and were several. The invitation turns out to be in Venti's hat. Venti shows a paper about Suikou he found beside him, and Albedo decides to paint the portrait of Suikou with Venti as model.

Act II. The Traveler waits at the docks for the Liyue ship. Xu of the Feiyun Commerce Guild, who serves Xingqiu, and Xingqiu himself, the real author behind "Zhenyu", disembark; Xingqiu also has an excerpt, and Paimon bluffs that it is part of a poem hunt with a chance to be an artist's model. Xingqiu realises Albedo is the painter and is happy to meet his long-time collaborator in person. Albedo notices something off with him: Xingqiu did not object to Uyuu Restaurant over Shimura's, so he knew Shimura's is open-air and probably arrived earlier. The patrons at Uyuu report neat handwriting and rough weather; Xingqiu admits he lied. Venti announces that Kujou Sara has arrested the suspect, whom Xingqiu recognises as Onodera. Onodera explains that for the festival he planned to have authors sign copies of their new novels, forgot to tell them in advance, and Xingqiu, poor at calligraphy, came early to practise. The editors rearrange the schedule. Xingqiu asks to be Albedo's next model so that he remembers the lesson, and someone unseen overhears the group.

Act III. Kazuha arrives and gives the Traveler his own excerpt about seals, which Albedo can design and Kazuha, an experienced carver, can make. They propose it to Hirayama, and two other authors, Satomi and Kadenokouji Kenzaburou, agree. Ayaka and Venti bring a strange paper that reveals more writing when dipped in water, showing that Akahito was framed. Albedo uses Kazuha and Ayaka as the last models. Paimon wonders about Kuronushi, who was missing from the tale; Albedo and Venti explain he is hidden inside the other stories as the one who framed Akahito and blackmailed Aoi. Kazuha, silent, tells them the story reminds him of his family: the Raiden Gokaden were five Inazuman forging schools tied to the Yashiro Commission, and now only the Amenoma art remains because the Isshin Art was lost, with the Kaedehara Clan its descendants. His great-grandfather lost interest, his grandfather failed to revive the business, and the last clan property was seized in Kazuha's generation. Ayaka says three of the five schools were lost in a short time and some rumoured a plot to remove them. Kazuha says an incident in his great-grandfather's day nearly crippled both his clan and the Kamisato clan, and he wants to confirm something with someone before telling them.

Act IV. Kazuha has traced an old family servant. The Isshin Art had been asked to forge an important Goshintou from an ancient diagram, but all the smiths produced only defects. At the deadline the blade was not ready and the smiths fled in fear. Kazuha's great-grandfather and the Yashiro head tracked them to the coast, where they put up a fierce resistance and escaped, and the Kaedehara Clan was punished; the Kamisato head soon died of wounds. The Kamisato Estate's copy of the diagram, dipped in water, shows it was altered, so the failure was not a lack of skill. Kazuha says his grandfather also failed to forge it and left Inazuma after an argument. Albedo notes the wording of the diagram was odd, so the great-grandfather may have known the truth. At the Kujou Estate Sara allows the group to search the seized Kaedehara property in a warehouse behind the police station; Kazuha asks that it be sold and the money given to the poor, but finds in a dead bonsai pot a hidden compartment with a blank paper that reveals a letter when dipped in water. The letter tells how the great-grandfather, Kaedehara Yoshinori, and the Yashiro head found, at a spot on Narukami Island, an eccentric who had taken responsibility for the sabotage and quickly defeated the party. The eccentric asked whether Yoshinori knew of "Niwa" and, hearing that Yoshinori was a descendant, told him to let "her" know the name Kunikuzushi, and left. He threatened to destroy the rest of the Gokaden, so the two men never spoke of it. Ayaka suspects her ancestor kept the same silence so that their families could continue. Kazuha says he has no reason to act on past wrongs but would act if the eccentric made trouble now. Back in Ritou Albedo finishes the portraits and unveils a blank one for Kuronushi. Ayato admits that Miko and he planned the hunt: he wanted to see if Kazuha could find and accept the truth, and to give Kazuha and Ayaka someone to rely on. He tells the Traveler to splash water on the portrait when alone, which reveals the Balladeer's likeness. Ayato recalls that after the Kaedehara property was seized, someone broke into the warehouse and left with nothing; Sara pretended not to notice while a Shuumatsuban member watched; the intruder gave up without learning the bonsai pot's secret, and Ayato then found the papers.
### ULTRA +
Details. Venti's role as Suikou is the first of several portraits. The five poets are known only by their colours: green for Suikou, blue for Aoi no Okina, red for Akahito, white for Sumizome and black for Kuronushi. Venti is the basis for Suikou, Xingqiu for Aoi no Okina, Kazuha for Akahito, Ayaka for Sumizome, and the Balladeer (later called Wanderer) for Kuronushi.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The old sword failure was sabotage. The forging diagram for a Goshintou had been altered so that even skilled Isshin smiths could not succeed, and the blame fell on the Kaedehara Clan. The saboteur was an eccentric who called himself Kunikuzushi, defeated the Yashiro head's party and warned that he would destroy the rest of the Raiden Gokaden if the story spread. Both families kept silent, which, as Ayaka says, let later generations exist. Ayato staged the poem hunt, partly on Miko's idea, so that Kazuha could find this out and so that the Kaedehara and Kamisato descendants could rely on each other.
### LARGE +
The final portrait is the Balladeer, Scaramouche, and the wiki summary reads Ayato's account of the warehouse break-in after the seizure as Scaramouche's doing. The Five Kasen story itself is Ayato's invention, a way to guide Kazuha to the truth.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Kazuha learns the truth about his family's failure and chooses not to pursue old grievances. Ayaka learns that her ancestor kept the same secret. Ayato's two-sided purpose is explained to the Traveler. Xingqiu's signing problem is addressed with author seals. The Irodori Festival goes ahead, with the event's poems, themed challenges and floral courtyards.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 2.6
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Four modes unlock in sequence: The Moon and Stars Inscribe (take pictures to fill Irodori poems), Theater Mechanicus (build mechanisms from Wondrous Sticks to defeat enemies), Clash of Lone Blades (duel martial artists using a Parry mechanic, without skills or bursts) and The Floral Courtyard (build furnishing arrangements to match flower themes). Completing the "Friendship in Writing" criteria invited Xingqiu as a playable character. Rewards included furnishings, recipes, Primogems and a Crown of Insight.

## entry: Five Kasen
@keys: Five Kasen, Five Kaisen, Suikou, Aoi no Okina, Akahito, Sumizome, Kuronushi
@version: 2.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Legendary Inazuman poets who took turns each year to present a poem collection to the Shogun; their works are the reason for the Irodori Festival. The poets are known by colours (green Suikou, blue Aoi no Okina, red Akahito, white Sumizome, black Kuronushi) and little is reliably known of them. In this event Ayato devised the version of the legend that guides Kazuha.

## entry: Raiden Gokaden
@keys: Raiden Gokaden
@version: 2.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Ayaka's account, the five best Inazuman forging schools, tied to the Yashiro Commission; today only the Amenoma art remains, the Isshin Art is lost although the Kaedehara Clan descends from it, and three schools vanished quickly, which fuelled rumours of a plot.

## entry: Kunikuzushi
@keys: Kunikuzushi
@version: 2.6
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The name given by an eccentric who sabotaged the Kaedehara Clan's Goshintou in Yoshinori's day, asked about "Niwa", defeated the Yashiro head's party and told Yoshinori to pass his name to "her". The wiki identifies him with Scaramouche; the event only implies this through the Balladeer portrait.

## entry: Irodori Festival
@keys: Irodori Festival, Magnificent Irodori Festival
@version: 2.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Held in Ritou in this event to honour the Five Kasen, show off modern light novels from Yae Publishing House and welcome foreign guests, with guides, poetry, performances and a flower display. Miko organises and Ayato helps as head of the Yashiro Commission.

## entry: A Legend of Sword
@keys: A Legend of Sword, Zhenyu
@version: 2.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A light-novel series by Zhenyu, a pen name of Xingqiu, illustrated by Albedo. Its latest volume disappears in Act I. Xingqiu has trouble signing copies in a neat hand.
