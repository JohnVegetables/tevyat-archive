## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### LITE
Of Drink A-Dreaming was a limited-time event in Version 2.5 (10 to 21 March 2022). Luka invited the Traveler and Paimon to take part in Bartender Academy Week at Angel's Share, the tavern in Mondstadt. They mix non-alcoholic drinks for customers and meet seven familiar guests in the Tavern Tales.
### STANDARD
Luka, who is running Bartender Academy Week, offers the Traveler and Paimon the run of the tavern's ingredients and unlimited refills as long as they are not wasteful, plus rewards when guests are happy. The bartender Charles teaches the basics and explains that beginners are no longer given alcoholic ingredients after an earlier student's mix knocked a customer out. Over three days friends from across Teyvat come to the counter, each with an order and a conversation, and a later Bartender Challenge tests the Traveler's skill. In the Afterword Luka offers them a job, which they decline to keep travelling together.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 28, the Interlude Chapter Act I The Crane Returns on the Wind, and Eula's Story Quest Aphros Delos Chapter Act I; during the event the Story Quest's Adventure Rank requirement was lowered to 28 and needed no Story Key. The event could not be completed in Co-Op Mode.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
In the opening quest Luka greets the Traveler outside Angel's Share and pitches Bartender Academy Week, which is optional. Inside, Charles trains them on a first drink, warns that customers have special requests and says conversation matters as much as mixing. The Traveler then serves guests in seven Tavern Tales: Kaeya and Rosaria; Diluc; Jean and Lisa; Zhongli; Eula; Beidou and Ningguang; and Shenhe. After the event ends, Luka thanks them in the Afterword: business has boomed, most customers came for the Traveler, and he asks whether they would stay and work there.
### LARGE +
When They Take Off Their Armor: Kaeya teases by ordering a Death After Noon, then asks for a large Moonlit Alley while Rosaria takes a small tea. Rosaria says tea reminds her of a church event she missed, when Sister Barbara saved her tea and dessert. Fine Wine From Yesterday: Diluc asks for a Gray Valley Sunset and says it was the first drink he ever mixed, when his father Crepus gave him ingredients with no instructions and he simply made a fruit punch. Dandelion, Rose, and Windwheel Aster: Lisa has dragged Jean to the tavern to relax; Lisa orders a medium extra-sweet Love Poem and Jean a medium coffee to keep her awake. Surrounded by the Aroma of Tea: Zhongli orders a strong Misty Garden and explains tea tasting to Paimon: look at the colour, smell, then sip.
### ULTRA +
Solid Ice, Soluble in Wine: Eula, after drinking at The Cat's Tail, orders a small Foamy Reef; Paimon has the Traveler water it down. Eula says her family name keeps her from being welcome in Mondstadt's taverns and asks how the Traveler makes so many friends. Docked on a Moonlit Night: Ningguang and Beidou, on a casual holiday, order a small sour Birch Sap and a large Dusk. Beidou teases Ningguang about having once sold fruit on the street and mentions her own ordinary fishing-village origins, and each invites the Traveler to bartend for them. Untainted Autumn Frost: Shenhe came after Zhongli gave her directions; puzzled by drink names, she is served a Snow-Covered Kiss and relates it to watching a sprout break through the soil. The Afterword ends with Luka inviting them back with friends.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The Tavern Tales add small character moments rather than plot. Diluc recalls inventing Gray Valley Sunset as a boy; Rosaria's dislike of tea comes from a church memory; Eula admits she usually drinks alone because of her family name; Beidou teases Ningguang about her early days as a fruit seller, which Ningguang declines to dwell on; Zhongli puts his bill on the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor's tab; and Jean and Lisa discuss Jean's difficulty relaxing.
### LARGE +
Diluc says his father gave no comment on the fruit punch at the time and notes he never thought about it afterwards. Jean concedes that learning to relax may be her biggest challenge and says she may come back. Eula says she wants to write down what the Traveler told her and Paimon predicts she may threaten them to forget it.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
Luka (event-exclusive, runs the Academy Week) and Charles (bartender at Angel's Share) frame the event. The Tavern Tales guests are Kaeya and Rosaria, Diluc, Jean and Lisa, Zhongli, Eula, Beidou and Ningguang, and Shenhe. Paimon helps the Traveler throughout.
### LARGE +
Each guest is written in character: Kaeya teasing, Rosaria dry and fond of drinks over talk, Diluc brief and a little wry, Lisa relaxed and Jean dutiful, Zhongli patient and traditional, Eula guarded until tipsy, Beidou and Ningguang friendly and competitive, and Shenhe literal and curious.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
The event does not change the main story. It leaves a standing invitation from Luka and Charles to visit Angel's Share, and Diluc says he wants to give the Traveler something once they have more bartending experience. Gameplay rewards include the furnishing Tavern Shaker: Grumous Oscillation and the namecard Celebration: Binge Vessel.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 2.5
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Drinks are made from a foundation (Coffee, Tea or Juice) plus flavourings (Milk, Cocoa Paste, Fizzy Water, Caramel, Lemon, Mint); each basic recipe uses three ingredients and special requests add extra of the matching ingredient. A mixing minigame and a pouring-size check decide the result. On the fourth day the Bartender Challenge and further recipes unlock once all Tavern Tales are done.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists 21 named recipes, among them Athenaeum, Barbatos' Boon, Birch Sap, Boreal Watch, Brightcrown, Caramel Pinecone, Dawning Dew, Dusk, Foamy Reef, Golden Eden, Gray Valley Sunset, Laughter and Cheer, Love Poem, Misty Garden, Moonlit Alley, Night of Swirling Stars, Scholar's Afternoon, Snow-Covered Kiss, Stroke of Night, Sweet Cider Lake and Tart Brilliance. Total rewards come to 420 Primogems and 460,000 Mora among other items.

## entry: Bartender Academy Week
@keys: Of Drink A-Dreaming, Bartender Academy Week, Bartender's Academy Week
@keys2: Angel's Share, Luka, Charles
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In March 2022 Angel's Share, Mondstadt's tavern run by Dawn Winery, held a Bartender Academy Week hosted by Luka. Participants had access to the tavern's ingredients, with unlimited refills if they were not wasteful, and no alcohol for beginners. The Traveler and Paimon took part.
### LARGE +
Charles trained them. Luka later said the week brought the best business he had seen and offered them jobs, which Paimon declined to keep travelling.
