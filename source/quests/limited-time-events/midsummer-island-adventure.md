## summary
### LITE
Midsummer Island Adventure was a Version 1.6 flagship event (9-28 June 2021) set on a limited-time map, the Golden Apple Archipelago. Klee receives a letter from a "Dodo-King" demanding that she bring her toy Dodoco to the archipelago. The Traveler and Paimon go with her, escorted by Jean and Barbara. As the weeks pass, Kaeya, Diluc, Albedo and Razor arrive too, each lured by a tailored letter, and the mystery ends with a recorded message: Dodo-King is Klee's mother Alice, and the islands are a gift for Klee and her friends.
### STANDARD +
The story is a light, summery puzzle-box adventure with an affectionate family reveal at the end. It was limited-time content; the archipelago returned for other summer events later (for example Summertime Odyssey, 2.8), which is a separate quest entry.

## plot
### STANDARD
Klee gets a letter from a self-styled "Dodo-King" demanding she bring Dodoco to the unknown Golden Apple Archipelago. The Traveler and Paimon go with her, guarded by Jean and Barbara, sailing through fog by lighting beacons. On the islands they find buoys, an inactive Inazuman-style machine and a memorial. Kaeya, Diluc, Albedo and Razor arrive, each lured by a tailored letter, and wake the machine, which attacks. In the last part the group collects fish scales for a mechanism at a giant conch; Klee presses it and a recording reveals that Dodo-King is her mother Alice and the islands are a summer gift.
### LARGE +
Part I (Mysterious Islands: Journey to the Unknown). Klee tells the Traveler and Paimon about a threatening letter from "Dodo-King", the self-declared strongest of the Dodo Clan, who wants her to bring Dodoco to the Golden Apple Archipelago. Nobody in Mondstadt has heard of the place, so the group asks around (Marjorie, Huffman, Cyrus) and finally Lisa, who finds a faint elemental message on the back of the letter. Using Elemental Sight on it reveals a sketch of small islands floating in fog, and Lisa recalls old accounts of islands met at sea in storms. Jean takes charge of the expedition, and the party sets out from Windrise. A boat waits in thick fog; lighting four beacons clears the way.

Part II (Summer Vacation: Proceed With Caution). The party camps on Pudding Isle. Jean and Barbara change into summer clothes chosen by Lisa and Barbara; Jean says her first duty is to protect everyone. Strange buoys have appeared overnight; destroying them sets off a quake, and Jean suspects an immense mechanical structure under the sea. On a central platform the party finds a huge humanoid machine in Inazuman dress, inactive. Klee decides it is not the real Dodo-King. Nearby, a pile of stones and small flowers looks like a memorial.

Part III (Dodo-King of the Sea: Lying in Wait). Kaeya and Diluc arrive, carried by Dvalin, each having received a letter signed Dodo-King: Kaeya's promises a sunken pirate ship with treasure, Diluc's warns of an Abyss Order stronghold, Albedo's says "I have Klee", Razor's says Klee needs help. Albedo, who had gone ahead, joins them, and Razor turns up having jumped off Dvalin because he smelled something that matches the letter. He brings a component that fits the machine, which wakes and attacks (it is fought as a Maguu Kenki). After the party defeats it, Albedo analyses it as an autonomous combat machine, probably damaged long ago, left among signs of past inhabitants; he also notes crystals made from a rare transparent ore.

Part IV (The Final Riddle: A Secret Uncovered). At dawn the group collects three fish scales that fit a mechanism at the giant conch. Klee is nervous about meeting Dodo-King; Kaeya, Razor and Albedo reassure her. When she presses the device a recording plays: the speaker, adopting Dodo-King's voice, announces she is Klee's mother Alice, explains that everything on the islands is a thank-you gift for those who kept Klee company, and tells Klee that summer is for everyone.

Part I's letter threatens, in Klee's reading, to "arbitrate" her; Paimon explains the word means judge. Part II adds the memorial and Jean's observation that this stretch of sea borders no nation. In Part III Jean asks who is overseeing the Knights, since Kaeya has come too, and is relieved to hear that Lisa is. Part IV makes clear that Alice's recording is pre-recorded: she says she will be travelling by the time it plays.
### ULTRA +
Jean doubts the invitation from the start, noting it is odd that a child would be summoned by an unknown party. The adult characters mostly came to protect Klee and to find the author. Alice's message is warm and gently teasing: she says she wants Klee to grow up with a curious yet kind heart, and Klee replies that her mother is "the bestest mom". After the reveal Diluc admits he has not been taken for a child in a long time, and Kaeya says he must meet Alice one day.

## revelations
### STANDARD
"Dodo-King" is Alice, Klee's mother, who built the archipelago, letters, boats, buoys and an energy shield as a summer present. The Inazuman-style machine is a separate, much older object left by a previous human presence, and the memorial beside it points to people who were grateful to it. After the reveal Klee still declares she loves her mother no matter what, including when her mother has blown up Mondstadt's main gate or been grounded.
### LARGE +
Albedo says the game would not have been fun if they had solved the riddle at the start, and refers to Alice as "Aunt Alice", showing he knows her.

## characters
### STANDARD
Klee is the heart of the event and the reason everyone comes. Jean and Barbara act as chaperones and relax slightly in summer clothes. Kaeya and Diluc spar over method; Albedo is quiet and observant; Razor trusts by smell and instinct. Alice appears only as a recorded voice. Dvalin carries Kaeya, Diluc, Albedo and Razor part of the way; Lisa is left holding the Knights' headquarters.
### LARGE +
Marjorie, Huffman and Cyrus give Part I's dead ends; Cyrus encourages the party to "venture into the unknown" without knowing the place.

## consequences
### STANDARD
After the event the archipelago remained visitable for the rest of Version 1.6 (players who had not raised it could do so after the event). The Dodoco Tales catalyst and the Wind-Blessed Harpastum are the event's rewards.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.6, running 9 June to 28 June 2021 (shop to 29 June). The requirements were the Prologue Archon Quest (Song of the Dragon and Freedom) and Klee's story quest "True Treasure", the first act of the Trifolium Chapter. Treat it as a summer holiday episode in Mondstadt's story.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Labelled game content: Shiny Flotsam and Mini "Harpastum" were the event currencies. The four parts unlocked challenge phases (Main Cannon, Make Ready... Fire!; Whirlpool off to Starboard... Full Speed Ahead!; Samurai Sighted... To Arms!; Harpastum Bombs Loaded... Blow 'Em Away!).

## entry: Dodo-King and the letters

### STANDARD
In the Midsummer Island Adventure event, letters signed "Dodo-King" lure Klee and her friends to the Golden Apple Archipelago. Klee's letter demands she bring Dodoco; Albedo's says the author has Klee; Kaeya's offers pirate treasure and rare vases; Diluc's claims an Abyss Order stronghold; Razor's says Klee needs help. The author is Klee's mother Alice, using the persona to give them all a holiday.
### LARGE +
The letters were written to suit each recipient; Albedo notes they were different yet all led to the archipelago, and Kaeya remarks on the strategy of choosing the right bait for the right fish. Jean considers the whole scheme an unusual way to invite a child.

## entry: Golden Apple Archipelago (1.6)
@keys: Golden Apple Archipelago, Pudding Isle, Minacious Isle, Broken Isle
@version: 1.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A cluster of small islands in a fogged sea, unknown to Mondstadt's maps and accessed in this event by a boat from Windrise. It contains buoys, beacons, a central platform with an old Inazuman-style machine, a memorial of stones and small flowers, a giant conch and a hidden recording. After the event it remained accessible until the end of Version 1.6.
