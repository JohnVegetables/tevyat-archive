## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 3.6
### LITE
A Parade of Providence was a limited-time event in Version 3.6 (27 April to 15 May 2023). The Sumeru Akademiya held its Akademiya Extravaganza and a once-in-four-years Interdarshan Championship, and the Traveler and Paimon were hired as roving commentators. Behind the games lay the story of Sachin, an absent sponsor who left part of his mind in a diadem, and of Kaveh, who won the crown and refused the fortune.
### STANDARD
The event combined the annual Wisdom Gala, where each of the six Darshans runs showcase booths, with the Interdarshan Championship, held once every four years, in which each Darshan sends one representative. The prize was the Diadem of Knowledge, bought by the wealthy researcher Sachin and donated to the Akademiya. Over three rounds (butterfly hunting in Sumeru City, switching off Ley Line Disruptors near Aaru Village, and a diadem hunt in Mawtiyima Forest) the story turns into a mystery about Sachin's hidden research and the voice in the Diadem.
### LARGE +
Players who gathered enough Gala Excitement could invite Faruzan to the party. The Traveler needed the Archon Quest A New Star Approaches; Inversion of Genesis and Alhaitham's Story Quest were recommended, with a Quick Start for those who had skipped them.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Act I, Comings and Goings: Wiqas hires the Traveler and Paimon as commentators; the six contestants (Tighnari, Cyno, Layla, Faruzan, Kaveh and the Wanderer as "Hat Guy") compete in a butterfly hunt in Sumeru City. Act II, Beginnings and Endings: a desert round to switch off Ley Line Disruptors near Aaru Village; the Traveler learns someone is targeting the sponsor Sachin. Act III, Gatherings and Partings: Sachin's son Jiwani is caught, the Diadem of Knowledge is found in Mawtiyima Forest, and Kaveh, speaking with Sachin's fragment, destroys the Diadem and donates the fortune.
### LARGE
Act I, Comings and Goings. Paimon hears about the Akademiya Extravaganza. At the Akademiya, a committee member named Wiqas explains the event and commissions the Traveler and Paimon as guest commentators because they are well known among both the city and desert folk. This also fits Lesser Lord Kusanali's hope of introducing the Akademiya's atmosphere to desert people. At the venue, the organiser Kareena opens the ceremony and the six contestants are introduced: Tighnari, Cyno, Layla, Faruzan, Kaveh and the Wanderer, who is entered under the name "Hat Guy" for Vahumana. The first round has the contestants hunt three fast "Swiftflies" among butterflies released into Sumeru City. Tighnari, Faruzan and Layla take the first three places; Kaveh and Faruzan tie and draw lots for second place.

Act II, Beginnings and Endings. In the second round, held near Aaru Village, contestants must switch off three Ley Line Disruptors in the desert. Tighnari collapses from heat and withdraws, the Wanderer also drops out, and Cyno finishes first. Layla, encouraged to know her classmates trust her, wins a place through her other self, and Kaveh and Faruzan once more tie, this time Kaveh winning the draw. The Traveler learns from Dehya and Candace that mercenaries disguised as tourists seem to be targeting Sachin, the sponsor, and from Aarav that Sachin has said he will watch the Championship to find an heir. At Alhaitham's home, Kaveh (packing to move out) finds Sachin's documents with Alhaitham's note about lofty ideals and nihilism.

Act III, Gatherings and Partings. Aarav's team identifies the culprit as Jiwani, Sachin's son, who hired Eremites to find his father and force him to hand over the estate; the Eremites were beaten by "a flying brat" and "a guy in green". The third round sends all contestants into Mawtiyima Forest to find the Diadem of Knowledge and place it on a stand. Kaveh places it first and wins the round, and a voice speaks in his head: a fragment of Sachin's consciousness. Kaveh then destroys the Diadem and donates the prize fortune to people in need. The Traveler traces Sachin's research to Aaru Village, where Alhaitham is already waiting; Nahida and the Wanderer then explain what the Wanderer was doing in the contest.
### ULTRA +
Details of the contest. The first round was resolved by method: Cyno and Layla relied on agility, Kaveh and Faruzan released mechanical butterflies to draw out the real ones, and Tighnari made an incense with ingredients from Collei. In the second round Faruzan believed a disruptor lay beneath her feet, Kaveh gave his food to a swarm of desert foxes and was overcome after leading them away, and Faruzan was trapped in a ruin; they dug the disruptor out together. Kaveh's toolbox companion Mehrak could not keep the foxes off, and Kaveh explained that Mehrak was built around a machine core from King Deshret's civilisation which he had bought. Dehya and Candace attended the Extravaganza simply to relax. Dori first appears in Act I trying to sell goods and then says she has secret information about the Extravaganza.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Sachin's fragment says that twenty-eight years earlier he went into the desert, found only conflict and tried to stop it, even coming in contact with a "Lost Darshan". He concluded that human nature can only produce conflict and destruction. He found a diadem that can hold a piece of a person's mind, stored his experience in it, asked the Akademiya to manage his estate, and ended his life in deep emptiness. He hoped the Diadem would lead him to a successor who is both genius and idealist, and judged Kaveh to be that person. Alhaitham's note on Sachin's papers reads in part that lofty ideals may be no defence against nihilism, but little decisions might be. Nahida says Sachin is not wrong but holds only one side of the truth.
### LARGE +
Jiwani adds that strange things happened around his father from the time he came back from his desert research, and more after the Diadem was bought. The Wanderer's reason for taking part is given in the finale: Nahida had him enter as Hat Guy to watch Sachin's chosen successor and stop things if they went wrong.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Contestants: Tighnari, Cyno, Layla (nominated by her Rtawahist peers), Faruzan, Kaveh and the Wanderer as Hat Guy (Vahumana). Commentators: Alhaitham and Nilou, with the Traveler and Paimon as roving reporters. Also present: Kareena (organiser), Wiqas (committee member), Sachin (absent sponsor), Jiwani (his son), Aarav (matra), Dehya, Candace, Collei, Dori, Morgiana, Nahida, and the Eremites Amur and Ganjul.
### LARGE +
Motives given on entry: Tighnari wanted to promote his ecology lecture; Cyno wanted the limited Genius Invokation TCG card in the prize pool; Faruzan wanted students and funding; Kaveh wanted money to move out of Alhaitham's house; the Wanderer gave no reason. Self-nomination (Cyno, Faruzan, Kaveh) and nomination by over half a Darshan's members (Tighnari, Layla) were the two ways in.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Kaveh is crowned champion. Because he destroyed the Diadem and gave the money away, the prize shrinks to the limited card and priority in future project proposals. He gives the card to Cyno, who pays one million Mora for it, and Kaveh spends most of it on debts and a meal for everyone. Faruzan gains a new student, Layla is admired by her Rtawahist classmates, and Tighnari's lecture draws record attendance. Nahida seals Sachin's research in Aaru Village as Kaveh's decision to keep it from spreading, while saying she would not hinder anyone who pursued it later. She also asks the Wanderer to keep studying and says she will grade "Hat Guy's" final thesis herself.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.6 as a Flagship Event. It is set after the Sumeru Archon Quests (A New Star Approaches is the entry requirement) with Alhaitham back as Scribe after serving as Acting Grand Sage, and follows the Wanderer being enrolled at the Akademiya by Nahida. Event quests are not part of the main storyline and may be unknown to people who did not witness them; Kaveh's destroying the Diadem is the main in-world consequence.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Six mini-games unlocked in phases: Gathering of Stars, Project Connectivity, Antiquity Hunt, In Truth's Steps, Concocted Reaction and Mimetic Replication. Completing them earned Gala Excitement; 1,200 invited Faruzan, and the later reward tiers included Crown of Insight and furnishing blueprints.

## entry: Interdarshan Championship
@keys: Interdarshan Championship, Wisdom Gala
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Akademiya Extravaganza has two parts: the annual Wisdom Gala, where each of the six Darshans runs booths, and the Interdarshan Championship, held once every four years, where each Darshan picks a representative. In this event both ran together, raising interest across Sumeru. The champion would wear the Diadem of Knowledge.
### LARGE +
Rounds: butterfly (Swiftfly) hunt in Sumeru City, three Ley Line Disruptors near Aaru Village, and the Diadem hunt in Mawtiyima Forest.

## entry: Diadem of Knowledge
@keys: Diadem of Knowledge
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An artifact bought by Sachin and donated to the Akademiya, and the championship prize. It can store a part of its wearer's consciousness; Sachin put his experience into it. Kaveh destroys it at the end of the event.

## entry: Sachin
@keys: Sachin
@keys2: Diadem, Akademiya, Championship, Kaveh
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A wealthy researcher and the competition's sponsor. His fragment says he went into the desert twenty-eight years before, was worn down by the conflict he found, and stored his thoughts in a diadem to look for a like-minded heir. His son Jiwani hired Eremites to find him for the inheritance.
### LARGE +
The summaries give 20 years for when the Diadem was bought and the estate given to the Akademiya (see uncertainties). Only his fragment appears in the event, and he is treated as having died.

## entry: Kaveh's choice
@keys: Kaveh crown, Kaveh refuses fortune
@keys2: Diadem, Sachin
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Offered Sachin's fortune and his research as Diadem wearer, Kaveh breaks the Diadem and donates the money. This ends Sachin's experiment through the Diadem and is the event's emotional climax.
