## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 1.6
### LITE
Echoing Tales was a limited-time event in Version 1.6 (9 June to 21 July 2021, UTC+8) on the Golden Apple Archipelago, the summer island area tied to Midsummer Island Adventure. Players collected Echoing Conches scattered across the islands, each of which replays a spoken fragment of the past. The fragments tell, out of order, of generations of stranded islanders and of shipwrecked Inazuman pirates who try to find a way out through the fog.
### STANDARD
The event needed Adventure Rank 21. There were 32 Echoing Conches in total: 5 when the islands were still submerged, 23 more after the second act of Midsummer Island Adventure raised the islands, and the last 4 after the third act. Collecting set numbers of conches unlocked rewards including Primogems, Hero's Wit, Mystic Enhancement Ore, character talent guides and Barbara's outfit "Summertime Sparkle". The recordings can be replayed from an event menu called the Conch Retrospection.
### LARGE +
After Version 1.6 the island could no longer be visited, and the outfit moved to the Character Outfit Shop at 1,680 Genesis Crystals. The area returned in Version 2.8 with a different look under the Summertime Odyssey event, with its own conch event, Resonating Visions.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
The Conch Retrospection is split into seven sets. The first, "Songs of a Distant Home", follows a family handing down an heirloom and a nursery rhyme ("five mountains hide the little village, two paths through the lonely forest, one river flowing down the middle, four winds bring the sound of joy") across generations of exile, until rising tides force them to leave the heirloom in the island's centre. The second, "Strangers in a Strange Boat", is two groups of stranded pirates bickering over a lost ship and a samurai puppet, afraid of their chief's anger. The third, "Unlucky Father", has a man attacked by hilichurls and rescued by an unseen helper. The fourth, "My 'Hero'", is a child's voice speaking to a silent figure the adults call a general. The fifth, "Grand Line", covers pirates and islanders working out how the fog moves and when the wind changes so they can sail out. The sixth, "Familiar Voices", is a set of recordings from the Midsummer Island Adventure cast, and the seventh, "Island Bygones", gives daily island life, the tide warnings and the choice to leave.
### LARGE +
In "Island Bygones", a newcomer tells the islanders that Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma exist, and the islanders, who have been trapped for generations, do not know Liyue. An elder reluctantly agrees to trust a young guide's route, and the sentries sound the alarm as the tide rises. The fifth set includes a recorded insight that the fog follows a pattern and "a mirage" is mistaken for rock. Together they show how people on the Haar Islands, as the archipelago was once called, lived and left.
### ULTRA +
Set VI's items: Albedo says the island is part of a Mondstadt mountain thrown into the sea and landing upside down, probably by Barbatos; Kaeya asks Diluc whether it is time for hunting seabirds or collecting seashells, recalling a childhood outing; Klee and Barbara plan to catch fish quietly, with Barbara recalling her sister Jean as a fun, tortoise-keeping child; and Razor, hungry, mutters about fish and water. These conches only become collectable after the Midsummer Island Adventure quest "Dodo-King of the Sea: Lying in Wait"; before that, they only murmur about the waves.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 1.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two facts come from the conches themselves: the archipelago was formed from mountain tops split off and landing inverted (Albedo's remark in set VI, attributed to Barbatos), and its former inhabitants were cut off for generations by fog and tide. The wiki ties the pirates in set II to Ako Domeki's fleet, shipwrecked after a thunder event, and the samurai puppet to the figure the child calls "General", but the conches do not state this.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
Only set VI uses named characters: Albedo, Kaeya, Diluc, Klee, Barbara and Razor, each in a short exchange. Sets I to V and VII use unnamed voices: a grandmother and child, a pirate crew, a father, a child talking to a "General", pirate and islander sailors, and islander elders and sentries.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
The event adds no plot beyond the voices; its lasting results are the outfit and the background to the Haar Islands later cited by the wiki. It unlocks nothing for later quests.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.6 alongside Midsummer Island Adventure. Its recordings are read as background to that event's story, and the 2.8 Summertime Odyssey has a counterpart event, Resonating Visions.

## entry: Echoing Conch

@keys2: Golden Apple Archipelago, Echoing Tales
@version: 1.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Echoing Tales an Echoing Conch is a collectable shell on the Golden Apple Archipelago that stores a spoken fragment of the past. The wiki lists 32 conches in seven sets. Before a conch is unlocked it only murmurs about distant waves. The later underwater Echoing Conch in Fontaine is a different mechanic.

## entry: Nursery rhyme of the Haar Islands
@keys: five mountains hide the little village
@keys2: heirloom, nursery rhyme, Echoing Tales
@version: 1.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Set I of the Conch Retrospection presents a nursery rhyme passed from grandmother to child across generations: five mountains hide the little village, two paths through the lonely forest, one river flowing down the middle, four winds bring the sound of joy. The set's final recording is titled "The Clan of Song, Scattered": the speaker orders everyone to higher ground and has to leave the heirloom in the centre of the island when the tide rises.
