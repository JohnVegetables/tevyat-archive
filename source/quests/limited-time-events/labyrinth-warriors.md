## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 2.2
### LITE
Labyrinth Warriors was a limited-time Version 2.2 event (21 October to 8 November 2021) set in Inazuma. The Tenryou Commission is overstretched by a nameless domain in Araumi that is letting out strong monsters, and the Traveler, Paimon and the Liyue musician Xinyan agree to help in exchange for Xinyan's travel permit being sorted out. Inside the domain, the Mystic Onmyou Chamber, they wake an amnesiac paper shikigami, Shiki Taishou, and help him recover the memories that explain why it exists.
### STANDARD
The domain turns out to be a training ground built long ago by the onmyouji Kamuna Harunosuke so samurai, each paired with a shikigami, could hone their skill against monsters. The Fatui Harbinger Tartaglia joins the party inside, pursuing his own interests. By the end Shiki Taishou has full control of the chamber again, clears himself with the Tenryou Commission, and gives the Traveler a small replica of himself, Shiki Koshou. Xinyan was the event's reward character.
### LARGE +
The event also doubles as a quiet character piece for Xinyan, whose rock 'n' roll becomes the way the party tests and then befriends Shiki Taishou, and for Tartaglia, who openly frames himself as a weapon who values the fight over any cause.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
Part 1, A Tricky Situation. Katheryne points the Traveler to a Liyue musician who needs help renewing an expiring temporary travel permit. It is Xinyan, stranded when the Iridescence Tour she came to play in was cancelled. The Tenryou Commission is too stretched to process the permit: Kujou Sara explains that a nameless domain in Araumi is releasing strong monsters, and promises to see to the permit in exchange for help. After guarding the domain entrance, the party finds a moving paper figure, Shiki Taishou, the domain's overseer, who has lost much of his memory. Xinyan wins his trust with a rock song, and they enter together and meet Tartaglia, who joins them.

Part 2, Pushing On. The chamber rearranges itself. Sara contains escaped monsters and later gives Xinyan a long-duration permit and lodging. The Traveler, Paimon and Xinyan then return to Shiki Taishou secretly, rejoin Tartaglia and push deeper, fusing with replicas of Shiki Taishou to recover memory.

Part 3, Away With Obsessions and Falsehood. Shiki Taishou fuses with a powerful replica, recalls the chamber's origin and Harunosuke's last memory, and decides to keep guarding it. Tartaglia reveals his own investigation.

Part 4, Path of the Taishou. The Tenryou Commission accepts the original documentation and finds Shiki Taishou and the chamber innocent. He has restored it to a domain for honing martial skill and invites the party to try it.
### LARGE +
Details of Part 1. The cancelled festival was pitched to Xinyan by a roadside recruiter as a famous Fontaine music event. She had asked Beidou to ferry her over early, and Xiangling had packed her a lunch. Xinyan offers to cover the party's other expenses with Mora Beidou gave her, if the permit fee is written off. Seeing the paper figure, she recalls the shikigami legends she collects for lyric ideas. Shiki Taishou describes hearing her song as wandering a misty path alone, wounded and afraid of what lay beyond, yet sensing someone waiting at the end. Paimon pictured people eating Grilled Fish, and Shiki Taishou then recalls grilling fish with faceless people by a fire, an answer Xinyan reads as a sign of understanding human feeling.

Details of Part 2. Tartaglia explains that he reached the domain by following monster tracks, calls it a combat training ground, and says plainly that he does not care about allegiance or right and wrong compared with the thrill of battle. Paimon doubts Shiki Taishou's sense of direction, since some doors in the chamber are false; he says he is guided by an overseer's intuition, and Xinyan points out there is no alternative to trusting him. In a memory fragment Shiki Taishou recalls a samurai ballad and the lead samurai calling Harunosuke 'Master Kamuna'. The Traveler openly asks whether Shiki Taishou would stand with them if Harunosuke proved a danger to Inazuma. He answers that his bond with the party is trust and with Harunosuke is responsibility, and does not promise.
### ULTRA +
Details of Part 3. Shiki Taishou says that Harunosuke founded an early form of onmyoudou after crossing the sea to study the arts of the adepti of Liyue, then created him as the template for all later replicas. When Shiki Taishou calls himself a weapon discarded by its owner, Tartaglia answers that he too is a weapon, chasing improvement rather than victory, and says sorrow and hesitation dull a blade; Xinyan counters that he need not abandon anything, only return to what he finds most important, as she has with rock 'n' roll, and Shiki Taishou resolves to continue. In the flashback, wounded samurai tell Harunosuke the outside situation is stabilising and ask whether the pursuit of 'ultimate attainment' should go on. Harunosuke says the targets no longer exist and those needing protection are safe, tells Shiki Taishou that giving him intelligence so he could know human emotion was the right decision, recites a line about rosy cheeks at morning and bleached bones by dusk, and sets out. After memory is restored, Tartaglia explains he came because he was looking for the Wanderer (Scaramouche in this event): the Fatui lost contact with him after he obtained a Gnosis. He says he will let that run a while longer and asks the Traveler what they will choose when they find it. Xinyan plays a second song and promises to visit, and Shiki Koshou is given to the Traveler.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 2.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Mystic Onmyou Chamber is a purpose-built training domain, not a source of evil. According to Shiki Taishou, Kamuna Harunosuke built it, and made him as its first shikigami, to defend Inazuma from the Abyss during the time of the cataclysm. Shikigami replicas were paired with samurai; they recorded the monsters defeated, and that data was fed back into the domain to create mirror monsters as training targets. Many samurai were wounded and many shikigami lost, and the Shogun's Army had already regained control of the situation, so Harunosuke disbanded the samurai and left, leaving Shiki Taishou to sleep inside.
### LARGE +
Shiki Taishou says Harunosuke's drive began when the Abyss took someone important to him, and that he studied Liyue's adepti arts before founding early onmyoudou. Shiki Taishou also recalls that Harunosuke played chess with a Tenryou Commission Supreme Commander surnamed Kujou, whose given name he cannot remember.

## characters
@availability: limited
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon are the protagonists. Xinyan, the Liyue rock musician, is the emotional centre of the cast. Kujou Sara represents the Tenryou Commission and appears in the first two parts. Tartaglia, a Fatui Harbinger, fights inside the chamber and pursues his own goals. Shiki Taishou, a polite, self-doubting paper shikigami, is the event's main new character. Katheryne gives the first commission, and Watanabe and Yata Kouki are named Tenryou Commission soldiers who briefly appear.
### LARGE +
Shiki Taishou apologises easily and speaks formally; his eyes are Inazuman glyphs that change shape with his mood. Harunosuke appears only in a memory flashback, not in the present day.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
Xinyan receives a long-lasting travel permit and lodging in Inazuma from Kujou Sara and plans to keep visiting Shiki Taishou. The Tenryou Commission accepts Shiki Taishou's account, so the domain is left as a martial training ground that may go dormant if its charm magic runs out. The Traveler keeps Shiki Koshou, a replica that stays in contact with Shiki Taishou but cannot use shikifuda or charm magic outside the chamber. Tartaglia's inquiry about the Wanderer is left open.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that the chamber's final room was later reused as a setting in another quest (Silent Seeker of Knowledge).

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. The event was a solo roguelike-style domain. A player split their characters into a Combat party and a Support party, carried chosen shikifuda, and drew Charms in rooms called emplacements. Five trial stages unlocked every three days: The Ominous Demesne, The Brutal Divergence, The Vengeful Wood, The Echoes of Extinction and The Rending Absolution. Aged Tokens, earned in the first four stages, were spent in an event shop and, with enough accumulated, to invite Xinyan (Pyro) as a reward character.

## entry: Mystic Onmyou Chamber
@keys: Mystic Onmyou Chamber, Onmyou Chamber, Ketsuroku Onmyouryou
@version: 2.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A domain in Araumi, Inazuma, first described as nameless by the Tenryou Commission. Built during the cataclysm by the onmyouji Kamuna Harunosuke as an ever-changing training ground where samurai fight monsters made of charm magic, which turn to paper charms when defeated. It slept after Harunosuke left, until an unknown power woke its monsters. Its rooms rearrange themselves, and some doors are false.
### LARGE +
Shiki Taishou oversees it and says that if its charm magic runs dry he will let it lie dormant so power can build again. The wiki notes the Japanese name uses the term for the old Yin-Yang Bureau of Japan.

## entry: Shiki Taishou
@keys: Shiki Taishou, Shiki-Taishou
@version: 2.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Event-exclusive shikigami created by Kamuna Harunosuke to oversee the Mystic Onmyou Chamber. A small paper figure modelled on a samurai in dark armour. He wakes with amnesia, wants to prevent harm, fears he was created for evil, and recovers memories by fusing with replicas of himself. At the end he recalls a purpose of protection and chooses to keep guarding the chamber.
### LARGE +
He calls himself a weapon abandoned by his master and struggles with purpose until Xinyan and Tartaglia each offer their own answer. Harunosuke says in the flashback that he gave him intelligence so he would understand human emotion.

## entry: Shiki Koshou
@keys: Shiki Koshou
@version: 2.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A replica of Shiki Taishou made as a gift for the Traveler. Shiki Taishou can communicate with it wherever it goes, and the wiki says he can hear and see what it does. Outside the Mystic Onmyou Chamber it cannot use shikifuda or charm magic, but it can still protect its holder.

## entry: Xinyan's Cancelled Tour
@keys: Iridescence Tour
@keys2: Xinyan, travel permit
@version: 2.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In this event Xinyan says she signed up for an Iridescence Tour stop in Inazuma, pitched to her by a roadside recruiter as a famous Fontaine festival, though nobody she knew in Liyue had heard of it. The tour was cancelled when the organisers ran into trouble at sea, leaving her temporary permit about to expire with no sponsor permit, which is the event's starting problem.

## entry: Tartaglia in the Chamber

@keys2: Mystic Onmyou Chamber, Shiki Taishou, Labyrinth Warriors
@version: 2.2
