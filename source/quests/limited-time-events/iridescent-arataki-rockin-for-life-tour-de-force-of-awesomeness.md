## summary
@availability: limited
@version: 4.6
### LITE
The Iridescent Arataki Rockin' for Life Tour de Force of Awesomeness was a limited-time Flagship event in Version 4.6 (6 to 27 May 2024). Arataki Itto invites the Traveler and Paimon to a rock 'n' roll festival on Watatsumi Island, which he organises with Dvorak, a Fontainian musician from the Iridescence Tour. Dvorak's festival was inspired by a legend of a dog that saved wounded animals during Inazuma's war, and Heizou starts to look into it.
### STANDARD
Dvorak's investors had cancelled his own plan for a music festival about the war, so he joins Itto in holding it with the Arataki Gang instead. Itto secures Watatsumi Island as the venue with Gorou's help, after asking Ms. Hina for advice by letter, and Kokomi supports the plan. The Traveler and Paimon fetch the supplies and music lovers needed to ready the stage over several days. At the party Itto and Paimon sing a new song about a reborn Onikabuto. Meanwhile Heizou finds traces of a wartime pet-rescue organisation, and Kokomi and Gorou privately discuss a secret joint operation with the Yashiro Commission. The Raiden Shogun, who has seen paper notes thrown towards Tenshukaku, attends with Miko.
### LARGE +
Requirements were Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest "Ritou Escape Plan". Itto's story quest "Taurus Iracundus Chapter: Act I" and the Shogun's "Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter: Act II" were recommended. The reward character was Gorou, who could be invited, together with the weapon Nightwind Horn.

## plot
@availability: limited
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
First Leg, This Ain't Your Daddy's Iridescence Tour...: an invitation leads the Traveler to Watatsumi Island, where Itto and Dvorak explain their plan and the legend of the dog. Heizou and Shinobu arrive with invitations and melons, and Dvorak gives the Traveler a checklist of tasks to prepare the venue. Glorious Finale, ...It's the Iridescent Arataki Rockin' for Life Tour de Force of Awesomeness!: the venue is ready, friends arrive, Itto and Paimon give a duet, and the final scenes reveal what Kokomi, Gorou, Miko and Ei make of it.
### LARGE
First Leg. An invitation letter from Itto in typically excitable style tells the Traveler about a new Fontainian friend who mentioned the Iridescence Tour, and asks them to come to Watatsumi Island for an even bigger party than the Drumalong Festival on Amakane Island. On Watatsumi Island Paimon admires the stage. Itto is talking with Dvorak about a legendary dog (Itto calls Dvorak "Jack-Attack"). Dvorak says that during the recent war between Narukami and Watatsumi, many animals were injured, among them a raven, a bake-danuki and a weasel thief, and that a dog like a ninken would appear out of nowhere in battle to carry the wounded to safety. He heard it in Ritou, where he was while the war was on, and meant to hold a music festival on that theme, but his investors cancelled after what they called an accident at sea; Dvorak thinks they saw poor returns and other risks. Itto offers to run it himself. He claims friends in high places, then lists them (Thoma, Kujou Sara, the three Commissions, Ayato, a fox lady, even the Shogun) in a way Paimon doubts. He says Gorou arranged the venue after Ms. Hina told him to ask the General of Watatsumi Island, and he did not know Gorou's role until then. Itto leaves to meet Gorou about the dog. Dvorak shows the Traveler the Jam Zone, where visitors can try instruments and scores from many regions including a horn from Fontaine, and the Roasted Lavender Melon Zone. At the stage Heizou and Shinobu arrive; Shinobu has been delivering invitations. Heizou brought two baskets of Lavender Melons: one from Sara, who declined to attend, and one from some Doushin and a few of Itto's former cellmates. Heizou promises to ask around Ritou about the dog but believes it is a local legend. Dvorak makes a checklist for the Traveler: invite more music lovers and gather materials to decorate the venue.

Glorious Finale. After the checklist is done, Dvorak says the party can start tomorrow. Itto, Shinobu and the Traveler talk: the dog search has stalled (Gorou and Itto's Gang found nothing), and Itto has thrown paper notes toward Tenshukaku, hoping the Shogun hands over the dog. Itto challenges Paimon to a song contest after Dvorak told him Paimon would perform. Friends gather. Kokomi and Gorou are there as supporters, saying the festival could attract tourists, and ask the Traveler if the mysterious Ms. Hina looks familiar. Thoma and Yoimiya, the latter supplying fireworks, discuss the dog; Thoma argues that if it was real, Watatsumi Island must have helped cover its tracks, since the Shogun's Army did not do rescues. Heizou says he suspects the Yashiro Commission and Sangonomiya were involved and is waiting for records from Yoriki Owada. Dvorak tells Paimon he rewrote the song for Itto by dropping the dog and writing about an Onikabuto. That night Itto and Paimon perform "I Got Reincarnated as an Onikabuto... And Flipped the World Upside Down", whose lyrics mention a raven shot through the heart, a bake-danuki, a weasel thief giving away his Mora, a General Crab reduced to a shell and an unagi, then promise rebirth as a beetle. Friends tease the result as a draw or ask who was most rock 'n' roll. Dvorak thanks everyone, and each guest gives their idea of the rock 'n' roll spirit. Thoma sees courage to grab life by the horns, Yoimiya sees world peace, Shinobu thinks of keeping the Gang safe, and Dvorak calls it hearing your own story in the music. Heizou has left a message. In the last scenes Kokomi tells Gorou the Puppy Union was a joint venture with the Yashiro Commission, a secret worth keeping but not the main aim, and that in an era of cooperation between Watatsumi and Narukami it may even help to be discovered. Miko, on a cliff, notes the rock was strong enough to stir lightning; Ei says she came because of the paper notes demanding she hand over a dog and an invitation delivered through Sara. They talk about Dvorak's songs commenting on the war: Ei says she cannot give him the answer he hopes for but that she will avoid another war, and Miko says everyone at the party would fight at her side if a bigger war came.
### ULTRA +
Details. Open-world notes show Ayato's gift of audio equipment, Thoma's tea stall from Komore Teahouse, Yoimiya's fireworks and a standing sign of Ms. Hina sent by the "Ramen Buddy" on the sign-in board, who by Itto's description is the fox lady, apparently Miko. Sayu appears in the open world as a Strange Daruma who complains she cannot grow taller. Langqing, Jifang's husband, comes from Liyue's Wanwen Bookhouse and later sells printed lyrics of the song with Dvorak's approval. Tomoki sells a "Rock 'n' Roll Dango Milk". Miko, appearing on day 4, jokes about Fortune Slips and guesses the dog was Taroumaru, the boss of Komore Teahouse, asking if he was saving only animals.

## revelations
@availability: limited
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Heizou's letters, left at the venue, trace the Puppy Union: a pet-treatment and pet-trade organisation registered with the International Trade Association during the war, whose emblem was a dog in a bamboo hat, which quickly dropped its registration after the war and was funded through at least six layers of transfers. Its hidden backers were the Foreign Trade Association, closely tied to Sangonomiya, and Komore Teahouse, with the Yashiro Commission behind the curtain. Kokomi and Gorou say it was a joint venture of Sangonomiya and the Yashiro Commission that saved some lives, including some on the Shogunate side.
### LARGE +
A post-event message from Heizou and an item, the Puppy Union Admission Details, give a codename list of rescued 'pets'. Heizou says he has the evidence but is told by Yoriki Owada to keep it quiet. Kokomi says the secret only matters if someone with an agenda finds it, and could be turned to good use now that the two islands cooperate.

## consequences
@availability: limited
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
The festival is a success, the dog's story is not resolved, and Dvorak says the feeling matters more than the dog. Itto invites the Traveler to perform next time. The Shogun and Miko discuss avoiding war. Kokomi and Gorou keep their wartime secret.

## placement
@availability: limited
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Released in Version 4.6 as a Flagship Event and set in Inazuma after the Archon Quest "Ritou Escape Plan". It follows Itto's earlier Almighty Arataki Great and Glorious Drumalong Festival on Amakane Island, and continues the Iridescence Tour that began in Fontaine and reached Liyue during the Lantern Rite. Dvorak was previously in the Version 3.4 event The Exquisite Night Chimes. The war the legend refers to is the conflict between Narukami and Watatsumi Islands that Dvorak calls not long past.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 4.6
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact. Three modes unlock in sequence: Xtreme Carnival Ensemble (a rhythm performance, with the option to compose and share tracks), Trillion Trinket Trawl (collect items from Dvorak's list to build and decorate the venue) and A Meeting of Melodies (fill in missing notes of hummed tunes). Playing them earned Iridescence Tour Tickets (800 to invite Gorou, 1,000 for a Crown of Insight) and decorating the venue gave further tickets.

## entry: Dvorak
@keys: Dvorak, Jack-Attack
@version: 4.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A musician from Fontaine and one of the main organisers of the Iridescence Tour, who travels a lot. He appeared in Liyue's Lantern Rite and now helps Itto stage the rock festival on Watatsumi Island. He heard of the rescuing dog in Ritou while the war was on and wrote a song about it, then rewrites it for Itto as a song about an Onikabuto. He tells Paimon the point of rock is hearing your own story in the music.
### LARGE +
The Puppy Union admission list includes an entry 'Unagi No. 9: Dvorak, 40 y/o'. The event never states what this means. The wiki reads it as Dvorak having been rescued during the war.

## entry: Iridescence Tour
@keys: Iridescence Tour
@version: 4.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
An international music tour covering all seven nations, begun in Fontaine, that Itto adapts into his own rock festival with Dvorak. Xinyan's Lantern Rite concert in Liyue was an earlier stop.

## entry: Puppy Union
@keys: Puppy Union, ninken legend
@version: 4.6
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the wartime story, a pet-treatment and pet-trade organisation registered in Ritou, with a dog in a bamboo hat as its emblem. Heizou traces its funding to Sangonomiya-linked and Komore Teahouse money. Kokomi and Gorou call it a secret joint venture with the Yashiro Commission that saved lives on both sides. Whether a single rescuing dog existed is left open.

## entry: I Got Reincarnated as an Onikabuto
@keys: Onikabuto, Reincarnated as an Onikabuto
@version: 4.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The song Itto and Paimon sing at the finale. It names a raven, a bake-danuki, a weasel thief, a General Crab and an unagi, then promises rebirth as a beetle. Dvorak says it grew out of the dog story and describes it as soft rock. Heizou, Thoma and Yoimiya each read it differently.

## entry: Ms. Hina
@keys: Ms. Hina
@keys2: Gorou, That's Life, Itto
@version: 4.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A popular persona in Yae Publishing House's That's Life advice column. In this event Itto says she told him to ask Gorou for help with the venue, a standing sign of her arrives as a gift from a 'Ramen Buddy' (apparently Miko), and Gorou and Kokomi both feel her face is oddly familiar. The Traveler and Paimon keep quiet. According to the Gorou wiki page, Miko invented the pen name.
