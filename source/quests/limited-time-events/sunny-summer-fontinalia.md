## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Sunny Summer Fontinalia is the Version 6.7 (Luna VIII) summer event and its three-act story quest. At Fontaine's Fontinalia Film Festival the Traveler and Paimon help Xavier's Musketeer Pictures film a story about Columbina, who is visiting Fontaine while searching for her lost best friend. On the borrowed ship Wingalet they find Sandrone alive again, calling herself Miss Guillotin, and help her reach her dead creator Alain Guillotin's flooded home, resolve the "anomalies" in her core and mend an old Fontaine wound.
### STANDARD +
The acts are Should We Meet Again, Those We Have Lost and Those We Have Kept. The event itself was an aquarium-management game (Operation Deep-Dive Treasure, Kaleido-Catch, Curio Ledger) rewarding the character Charlotte and her outfit Hurlock Variations. The story quest stays playable after the event ended.
### LARGE +
The event required Adventure Rank 20, a Fontaine Statue of The Seven and the Prologue Act III, and recommended Act X of Song of the Welkin Moon (Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana) first. Version 6.7 also released Sandrone as a playable character.
### ULTRA +
The quest follows directly on the Nod-Krai finale: Sandrone's survival, Columbina's return and the Moon Sisters are treated as shared background.

## plot
@availability: limited
### LITE
Xavier rewrites his festival film around Columbina and borrows the Wingalet from Wriothesley, but its renter, Miss Guillotin, is Sandrone. She tells the Traveler and Paimon that Alain's final gift revived her. They dive for Alain's home, find her creator's formula, and Sandrone faces her inner "anomaly". She then puts a corrupted mass of wandering souls to rest, reunites with Columbina, and the film gets its happy ending.
### STANDARD +
**Act I, Should We Meet Again.** Xavier is looking for a new subject after Columbina wanders into his auditions. Chiori and Furina support the rewrite, and Wriothesley lends the Wingalet. Aboard are Citlali (front desk worker) and Sandrone (the ship's renter, using Alain's family name). Sandrone tells the Traveler and Paimon that Dottore destroyed her core in Nod-Krai and that Alain's built-in "Final Gift" rebooted her when a Stellar Linchpin appeared. She wants to find Alain's home, now underwater, and the Traveler offers to dive with her while the crew films.

**Act II, Those We Have Lost.** The party locates the Hidden Dwelling and finds Alain's letter, a formula for meeting her inner "anomaly", and a handwritten note from her earlier years. Sandrone, with the Traveler and Paimon witnessing, faces her own grief and accepts it.

**Act III, Those We Have Kept.** Sandrone destroys the Tower of Gestalt's accumulation of wandering souls, reunites with Columbina on camera, and the film gets its ending. At a party aboard the Wingalet friends gather, and Wriothesley keeps the aquarium.
### LARGE +
**Act I in detail.** The Traveler and Paimon meet Chiori, Furina and Xavier in the Court of Fontaine. Columbina says she travelled alone after asking Nefer and Lauma for advice, and she tests friendship by punching people's arms ("Hya!"), including Wriothesley and, later, Neuvillette. Chevreuse arrives to say the Duke has agreed to let well-behaved inmates leave under supervision for the festival. Learning that the festival has booked up every good location, Wriothesley shows Xavier the Wingalet, which he has brought to the surface. In the cabin the party finds Citlali in pajamas, working the front desk for room and board: she came to Fontaine for the premiere of a light-novel adaptation, could not sleep at her hotel near the Opera Epiclese, and found every other room booked. The renter is Sandrone, who has applied for a fishing permit and business licence under the name Guillotin so she can search for the home without applying to excavate underwater ruins. She keeps her presence quiet. Charlotte's crew (director Lepine-Pauline, with Chevreuse, Navia and Emilie in the cast) is shooting "Great Detective Hurlock" on the same deck, and Xavier names the Traveler "chief lighting and Kamera specialist" for "A Homecoming".

**Act II in detail.** Sandrone and the Traveler take samples from several sites to find the entrance. While Furina and Charlotte's crews shoot on the Wingalet, Sandrone has to hide in a box for a scene. Hearing the detective scene about machines having no heart, she recalls Alain asking whether she recognised her own heart. Inside the Hidden Dwelling the party sees her garden, workbench, a photograph from the days when Alain and Rene were still together at the Institute of Natural Philosophy, a Pagoda Stack puzzle game she played as a child, and a box that holds a Multidimensional Algorithmic Core, a letter and photographs. Alain's letter says a person should be born twice, once into life and once into the soul, and gives her a formula for facing the "anomaly" within. In an inner mirror space, the "anomaly" (her emotions) recalls the tea parties with Columbina, Tartaglia, Capitano, Signora and Arlecchino, Columbina's farewell, and the loss of Pulonia. Sandrone smashes the mirror and embraces the anomaly, saying they were always one.

**Act III in detail.** Citlali pinpoints the Tower of Gestalt, in the Morte Region, as the source of resentful, severed souls. Sandrone refuses to call in Neuvillette because of the diplomatic mess, drains the tower and, with Citlali's ritual, calls the corrupted mass. When negotiation fails she runs the numerological array in reverse ("Reversion"), separating the souls so they can return to the Ley Lines. Afterwards Columbina pulls Sandrone to a lunar space and the two embrace, then are teleported in front of Xavier's Kamera; the take becomes the film's ending. At the premiere "Great Detective Hurlock" wins the Furina Award. Sandrone and Columbina visit Alain's grave, and a party on the Wingalet follows, with Neuvillette, Wriothesley, Sigewinne, Baptiste and Veronique, Lyney, Lynette, Freminet, Escoffier, Navia, Clorinde and others. Arlecchino arrives for tea with Sandrone and Columbina, and Sandrone plays the mechanical piano.
### ULTRA +
**Act I extras.** Sandrone says Alain Guillotin lived in seclusion after leaving the Institute, that she is the Seventh of the Fatui Harbingers, and that the Traveler offered to come along because they were worried about her condition. Sandrone says Columbina's senses rely on kuuvahki: strong in Nod-Krai, but elsewhere about as wide as ordinary sight unless she uses her abilities, which is how Sandrone has been avoiding her. Charlotte, Chiori and Furina discuss new awards at the festival, including Best Director, Best Costume Design and Best Kamera Operation.

**Act II extras.** In the dwelling's Pagoda Stack game the Traveler can match Sandrone's fewest-move solution, which impresses her. She reads a note she wrote as a child, "Her Highness the Clockwork Princess, Unraveller of All Reason", and blames it on Pulonia. Sandrone says she created Katheryne only as a superficial imitation of Alain's work and that some mechanics of her own core remain mysterious to her. In the aquarium she has told Paimon the fish all swam away because the old her stopped caring, and the Traveler and Paimon's care changes her view.

**Act III extras.** After the premiere Clorinde and Navia discuss Alain's legacy (founder and first director of the Fontaine Research Institute, inventor of pneuma-ousia annihilation energy), and Navia realises the Guillotin name seems familiar. Alain's headstone reads "Founding Director of the Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering, White Cavalier of Protection, Lord-Artificer". Lyney and Lynette try to borrow Citlali's Tzitzimitl helpers for their magic show.

## revelations
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Sandrone survived the Nod-Krai battle; Alain Guillotin prepared a built-in "second chance" that triggered when a Stellar Linchpin appeared.
### STANDARD +
The Stellar Linchpin let her core reboot and repair. Pulonia, the other half of Alain's work, is beyond repair, though some of its algorithms and the numerological array were imported into her body. A formula Alain left lets her meet and accept her inner "anomaly", the clash of her emotions and logic.
### LARGE +
Sandrone's account of the Stellar Linchpin is that it is a rare form of Vision linked in Fatui research to the Third Descender, whose remains the Heavenly Principles used to create the Gnoses. She says it channels elemental power with an extra, otherworldly quality. Sandrone supposes the Final Gift exists because Alain, who often spoke to her about wishes, thought she might one day wish for something enough to give up her life. The Multidimensional Algorithmic Core stores information that only she and Pulonia can access.
### ULTRA +
Sandrone explains that the Narzissenkreuz Ordo's Rene and Jakob severed souls in their research before Rene awakened; those souls could not return to the Ley Lines and corrupted over time. A query to the numerological array tells Sandrone that only one living Moon Goddess remains, and she takes this to mean Columbina made it back; later she says Columbina is now merged with the Moon Sisters. Columbina has not told Xavier, because Nefer and Lauma both advised against it.

## characters
@availability: limited
### LITE
Traveler, Paimon, Columbina, Sandrone (Miss Guillotin), Citlali, Furina, Chiori, Xavier and Wriothesley.
### STANDARD +
Charlotte, Chevreuse, Navia, Emilie, Clorinde and Lepine-Pauline appear in the film crews. Act II features Alain Guillotin in flashbacks, and Tartaglia, Capitano, Signora and Arlecchino appear in Sandrone's memories of tea parties. The finale party adds Neuvillette, Sigewinne, Lyney, Lynette, Freminet, Escoffier, Ninette, Veronique and Baptiste.
### LARGE +
Citlali is embarrassed and defensive about her pajamas, and gruff but kind. Columbina is polite, curious and devoted to Sandrone. Sandrone is sharp-tongued and dislikes bureaucracy, but admits she missed everyone.

## consequences
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Sandrone resolves her "anomalies", reunites with Columbina and the Fatui circle, and visits Alain's grave. The Wingalet's aquarium remains, since Wriothesley asks to keep some installations for the inmates at the Fortress of Meropide. Xavier's "A Homecoming" does not win the Furina Award, which goes to "Great Detective Hurlock".
### LARGE +
The Hidden Dwelling stays accessible after the quest. Sandrone says she will bring gifts to the House of the Hearth and hopes to meet in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya. Columbina wants to keep seeing Sandrone but not return to the Fatui.

## placement
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.7 (Luna VIII) on 1 July 2026 as a flagship event running to 11 August 2026 (UTC+8). It is a permanent story quest in Fontaine (the event page notes it can still be accepted after the event). It follows the Nod-Krai events and precedes the version 7.0 Snezhnaya story.

## entry: The Wingalet
@keys: Wingalet
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A large vessel kept at the Fortress of Meropide, brought up to the Court of Fontaine for the festival and rented out for extra income. Its maintenance is done by inmates. Sandrone rented it as captain and turned it into an aquarium that Wriothesley keeps afterward.

## entry: Hidden Dwelling and Alain's Final Gift
@keys: Hidden Dwelling, Final Gift, Multidimensional Algorithmic Core
@keys2: Sandrone, Alain, Guillotin
@version: 6.7
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Hidden Dwelling is Alain and Sandrone's old home, now underwater, with a garden, workbench, water-insulating sluice gates, a fireplace and a puzzle table. Alain's built-in "Final Gift" revived Sandrone and left a Multidimensional Algorithmic Core, a letter and a soul-mapping formula.

## entry: Stellar Linchpin (Sandrone's account)
@keys: Stellar Linchpin
@keys2: Sandrone, Marionette
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
According to Sandrone, a Stellar Linchpin is a rare kind of Vision, linked by Fatui research to the Third Descender, whose manifestation seems to fix impurities from his remains in place. It lets its user channel elemental energy with an unusual power from beyond Teyvat.

## entry: Wandering souls at the Tower of Gestalt
@keys: Wandering Souls' Resentment, Tower of Gestalt, soul-severing
@keys2: Sandrone, Citlali, Narzissenkreuz
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A mass of resentful, severed souls had gathered at the Tower of Gestalt in the Morte Region and was fusing with evil beings. Sandrone reversed her world-formula to separate the souls and returned them to the Ley Lines, saying she did it to fulfil Alain's wish to repair things with his friend Rene.

## entry: A Homecoming and Great Detective Hurlock
@keys: A Homecoming, Great Detective Hurlock, Musketeer Pictures
@keys2: Furina Award, Fontinalia
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
"A Homecoming" is Musketeer Pictures' festival film about a woman searching a foreign land for her lost friend, directed by Furina with Columbina as the lead. "Great Detective Hurlock", directed by Lepine-Pauline with Charlotte as the lead, adapts a popular light novel and wins the Furina Award.
