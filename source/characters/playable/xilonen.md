## summary
### LITE
One of Natlan's six modern heroes. She naps whenever she can, but at the forge she is second to none. She forged the Traveler's Ancient Name, Tumaini.
### STANDARD +
She bears Baraka, an Ancient Name of the original Six Heroes. Her inventions are famous across Natlan: Mavuika's Flamestrider, Chasca's gun, Kachina's drill Turbo Twirly.
### LARGE +
Most of her work is ordinary tools and weapons for her tribe; Ancient Names are rare. Mualani's shark surfboard is hers too.
Behind the drowsy manner is a rigorous researcher who will not put an unverified story into a Name. She does her duty "regardless of my level of personal interest". She accepted that forging the Traveler's Name would cost part of her own life, and told almost no one.
### ULTRA +
Her story runs through the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1-5.3): she builds the Traveler's Name with Citlali and the Lord of the Night and fights the Abyss invasion beside Kachina. In her tribe's chronicle she exposes her old mentor Tlazolli and takes in the girl Nepecha. Since the war she has examined an automaton from Nod-Krai (5.8) and travelled abroad: Dragonspine (a limited 6.2 event), Mondstadt, and Liyue.

## personality
### LITE
Sleepy, unhurried, dry-humoured; guards her rest and urges others to rest. Practical and unromantic, but diligent, precise and quietly kind. Never refuses a real challenge.
### STANDARD +
Every commission starts with a long form: "Effective communication means efficient work." Fame and slander do not move her; she is an artisan first.
### LARGE +
Vague requests exhaust her more than forging does, and "neither urgent nor important" makes her dread being rushed later.
Her laziness is real but selective: she naps under trees and in workshops, then works on three or four hours of sleep a night once a Name is under way. She took up Name Engraving out of duty, not passion. Her parents and most of her kin were Engravers, and as the best student in the tribe she reasoned it was "more convenient for everyone" than answering everyone else's questions. Her father's saying guides her: "When every person in Natlan takes up the responsibility they can shoulder, Natlan will stand strong forever."
### ULTRA +
Her coolness hides deep loyalty. After her teacher Teyiz died and rumours twisted Teyiz's memory, the laid-back student turned into a tireless investigator overnight to set the record straight. Her care shows sideways: a maintenance manual that makes Chasca fly safely, a birthday alarm clock so friends will not wait at her door, a stone engraved with Nepecha's real name. She notices when a child hides distress and calls it "a cry for help". She does not want to know the future ("I already have enough to worry about with all my present work"). She faces hard truths squarely: "Good or bad, happy or cruel, we always have to face the truth."

## appearance
### STANDARD
Tall, with long, messy pale-blonde waves fading to orange, ocelot-like ears and tail, and bright green eyes with cat-like pupils. Her usual outfit (Gold-Draped Gorge) is a loose sleeveless black, white, gold and orange coat with black fur trim and long coattails, over a white crop top and frayed denim shorts, with strapped wedge sandals and a gold-and-silver necklace holding her Geo Vision.
### LARGE +
She also wears a choker, ear piercings, bracelets, rings and a bracer-like sleeve on her right arm. She takes the coat off to work; when it goes back on, she is about to take a break. Her shoes use her own phlogiston propulsion and new alloys, letting her skate at speed, and phlogiston adhesive lets her skate up vertical surfaces. Her headphones connect to a record player so she can listen while she forges. She keeps sunglasses for napping in the sun. Canon gives no age or height (in game: tall female model).

## speech
### STANDARD
Relaxed and casual: "Yeah," "Hmm?", trailing "..." and yawns. Short, plain sentences, practical detail (furnace heat, which knot to tie), deadpan teasing. She calls Tlazolli "Auntie" and speaks of Mavuika with exasperated fondness.

## background
### LITE
From a family of Name Engravers, she studied under the renowned Engraver Teyiz and forged her first Ancient Name from Teyiz's life, gaining a Geo Vision as she did.
### LARGE +
As a student she loved only the technical side: phlogiston engravings, power systems, odd thought experiments. She bribed classmates with milk candies to copy their notes on hero legends. Teyiz told her that for Name Engravers "stories and equations are, in essence, one and the same," and taught the class to cross-check legends like evidence. Xilonen came to value stories that have proof behind them. As a child she was also often sent to watch Tlazolli, a celebrated Name Engraver and family friend, at work.
Teyiz grew old and said she heard the Wayob in her dreams, but would not go until she had found the right person to pass things on to. Xilonen lit her funeral pyre and placed her ashes in the cave Teyiz had always spoken of. When rumours called Teyiz now a saint, now a schemer, Xilonen travelled to every tribe to gather records of her. She asked Scions of the Canopy couriers, People of the Springs musicians, the Masters of the Night-Wind's woven scrolls, the Flower-Feather Clan and the Collective of Plenty, and produced a true account. Holding Teyiz's beast-tooth comb, which the old woman had used to coax her back to study, she forged that account into her first Ancient Name. Afterwards she tossed the leftover ore, and a Vision with it, into a basket outside, and only noticed it at her next session.
### ULTRA +
She grew up around phlogiston-rich gems and the forge: she prefers adding gems to adding wood to control a furnace's heat, and piles the spent gems up for anyone who wants them. She learned record pressing (and, quietly, DJing) from the People of the Springs. When the old Dance-Off Stage broke mid-contest, she fixed it on the spot and DJed the next track. She turned down the paid post of equipment consultant and DJ, agreeing only to maintain the stage in her free time, and asked to be the contest's official DJ once she no longer has to forge Ancient Names. With apprentices she is honest: she never really chose the job, but someone has to tell the stories.

@keys2: Xilonen, Natlan, Children of Echoes
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
When Capitano and Ororon tried to rebuild Natlan's Ley Lines with a Secret Source device, Mavuika led Xilonen, Kinich and the Traveler against his base. Xilonen tunnelled the party in while Kinich drew off the Fatui. Ororon, briefly possessed, lunged at her before Capitano restrained him. When the Lord of the Night offered to rebuild the Ley Lines at the cost of herself and Natlan's history, Xilonen sided with Ororon and Mavuika: life must have meaning. Then the Abyss struck nationwide. She fought Abyssal Pylons, evacuated her tribe to the Stadium, and admitted she had never believed the old stories of a blackened sky until she saw it. "As long as there's even a sliver of hope, I'm not gonna give up." When Mavuika declared a Night Warden War, Xilonen spoke her tribe's invocation: "Stone engraved and echoing peaks. Converge, disperse, in ashes be reborn."
### ULTRA +
At the victory feast she proposed a moment of silence for the fallen. Testing Tumaini in 5.3, she struck down the soul of a man named Zumalli when the Abyss began corrupting it, because sending him to the Night Kingdom would only spread the damage. She respected two Tepetlisaur souls who chose to stay with their grandson Ayo. She saw that Capitano truly wanted to help lost souls.

## entry: Ixquieh, the first Baraka
@keys: Ixquieh, first Baraka, Och-Kan, Lianca
@version: 5.8
### LARGE
A woven scroll in Xilonen's workshop shows Ixquieh, first recorded bearer of Baraka, founder of the Children of Echoes and one of Xbalanque's Six Heroes in the war against the Pyro Dragon Xiuhcoatl. Xilonen explained in 5.8 that Ixquieh took apart many dragon machines hoping to use them for humanity. After their old friend Och-Kan became a tyrant who persecuted anything tied to dragonkind, Ixquieh burned her workshop so he could not use it, which is why most of her research is lost; Chasca's predecessor Lianca saved a little of it. Ixquieh's twin Ahpub founded the Collective of Plenty.

## entry: Ineffa and the cryptex
@keys: Ineffa, cryptex, Varka's letter, automaton
@keys2: Xilonen, Natlan, Secret Source
@version: 5.8
### ULTRA
In the 5.8 Archon Quest prelude The Journey Home, Chasca and the Traveler brought an unconscious automaton woman and a cryptex to Xilonen, whom Mavuika had just tasked with the rogue Secret Source automatons. Xilonen found the cryptex built from many nations' materials and would not force it. The woman's core resembled a Secret Source core, but the rest was foreign (Fontainian clockwork joints, a Mondstadt-style maid's hairpiece); Xilonen woke her simply by stimulating the core, to her own surprise. She suspected an isolation block on her memories and had her tidy the workshop. Later Xilonen and Citlali privately feared the woman, Ineffa, might hold the Eleventh Sovereign Lord's core; Chasca refused to treat her as a threat. Xilonen helped stabilise collapsing ruins and later certified Ineffa in good working order. The cryptex held Varka's invitation for the Traveler to come to Nod-Krai.

## entry: Legion of Soulblazers at Dragonspine (6.2 event)
@keys: Legion of Soulblazers, Soulblazer Saga, Minnie, Dragonspine cross-country, Fietena
@keys2: Xilonen, Nepecha, Varesa
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During the limited-time 6.2 event Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey, Xilonen came to Dragonspine for the Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge. She represented Nepecha, a member of the Legion of Soulblazers (a comic-book fan club of pen pals devoted to The Soulblazer Saga), who was not fit for extreme sports. She ended up as vice captain beside Varesa, Minnie, Tang Yigang and Alrani, coached the team's running form, and ran the first relay leg when Yigang fell ill. She shopped for Soulblazer "merch" for Nepecha. Seeing that young Minnie's parents dismissed her art as wasted time, she spoke to them as Nepecha's teacher: she gave them a group painting by Nepecha and the Children of Echoes' children, and compared Minnie to a Mondstadt bard with an audience. She also napped on a library table.

## entry: Music and the Dance-Off Stage
@keys: Dance-Off Stage, Drillbit Dance, DJ, record pressing
@keys2: Xilonen, Children of Echoes, music
@version: 5.1
### ULTRA
The Children of Echoes love rhythmic music, and their Drillbit Dance dance-off is held daily (the tribe's bulletin board numbers it in its 127,751st iteration, roughly 350 years). When the old Dance-Off Stage broke mid-contest, Xilonen rewired it, tested the mic and played a track that brought the crowd roaring back. She had picked up DJing while learning record pressing from the People of the Springs. She refused a paid post, maintains the stage for free, and wants to be its official DJ once she no longer has to forge Names. Her headphones link to a record player at the forge, she curated Muhuru's Gemporium's playlist (Varesa runs to it), and her combat gear includes a portable phlogiston DJ controller. In 6.5 she helped Barbara of Mondstadt adjust the rhythm of a hymn.

## entry: Xilonen abroad
@keys: Xilonen in Liyue, Xilonen in Mondstadt
@keys2: Xilonen
@version: 6.5
### ULTRA
After the war, Xilonen can "head out whenever I feel like it". In the 6.5 Anecdote "Dahlia: Day of Confession", Barbara mistook her tail hanging from a tree outside Mondstadt for a wild beast; Xilonen, yawning throughout, helped her adjust a hymn. Her birthday letter of 2026-03-13 says she was in Liyue choosing ores and jewellery whose shopkeepers' backstories she doubted. She saw a man with an unusual sword board a ship for Inazuma and decided that would be her next stop.
