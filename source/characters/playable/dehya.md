## summary
### LITE
Dehya is a desert-born Eremite mercenary of the Blazing Beasts, famed as the "Flame-Mane": expensive, professional, good-humoured, and owned by no employer. She helped the Traveler free Lesser Lord Kusanali from the corrupt sages and wants to give the desert's people a better future.
### LARGE +
She first appears as the bodyguard of Dunyarzad, a sick merchant's daughter. During the Sabzeruz samsara she becomes one of the Traveler's closest allies in Sumeru. Later she learns the truth about the father who raised her.
Her life runs on a tension she accepts rather than resolves. She loves Mora, cosmetics and a good drink, yet keeps hard principles and spends her earnings on her people. She was raised by a man she came to despise. She later learned he had died to free his men, and that he was never her blood father.
### ULTRA +
Across the game she goes from paid guard (3.0) to rebel strategist against the sages (3.1-3.2). Lionsblood (3.5) is her personal story. In the 6.6 Irminsul crisis she is a desert leader who escorts Sumeru's evacuees and fights her way into Sumeru City beside Cyno. Between these she keeps turning up on commissions in Fontaine, Liyue and at Sumeru's festivals.

## personality
### LITE
Confident, blunt and quick to laugh. She loves Mora but keeps a firm line: "I like Mora, but I'll never go against my principles." Protective, warm with friends, and embarrassed when caught being kind.
### STANDARD +
Brave but never reckless, she knows when a sandstorm means retreat. She is cheerfully vain about make-up, perfume, jewellery and a proper wash, and hates bitter food and foul-smelling mercs. No worshipper, she trusts sweat and skill, yet respects Kusanali for what she did for Dunyarzad.
### LARGE +
She is proud of being desert-born. She resents how lack of education and the Wall of Samiel keep her people "crawling about in the sand", and how those who escape rarely look back. Her ambitions are modest and outward-facing: keep the people she cares about safe, and bring hope back to the desert. She calls herself the least ambitious person ever to set foot in Pardis Dhyai and wants only a drink, good food and a good night's sleep. She is fierce towards traitors; she thought Kusanali was far too merciful to the old sages. She has a soft spot for stubborn people with heart, like Dunyarzad and Candace.
### ULTRA +
She covers tenderness with teasing. She once "charged" the Traveler a smile as her fee, calling it a down payment on victory. She grumbles that clients never pay on time and that everything lands on her although she is not the boss. Bitterness, she believes, does not suit desert life, so she turns regret into resolve. After Lionsblood she admitted that she and her father's men were all "hypocrites" who only recognised their true feelings too late. She is also curious and adaptable: she reads bartenders in foreign taverns, records clockwork dancers in Fontaine for her friends, and sizes up strangers with a merc's instinct.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Tall, with sapphire-blue cat-like eyes, sun-browned skin and long wavy black hair streaked with gold, worn in a high split ponytail whose crown suggests a lion's ears. Desert mercenary gear, gold jewellery (a large triangular earring with a red stone) and a greatsword.
### LARGE +
She keeps herself well groomed even in the field, with powder for her eyes and face. A carved image on her arm armour shows Shesepankh, one of the desert's Seven Pillars; the wiki calls it her pauldron and Bennu calls it her gauntlet. When she fights unarmed she tosses the greatsword aside and uses her fists. Canon gives no height or age (in-game: tall female model).
### ULTRA +
In the 5.7 anecdote "Dress" she ordered a dance practice outfit from Chiori to rehearse with Dunyarzad, then a dancewear outfit for herself. Chiori saw that she favours satin with wave motifs, short hems, wrist ties and a waist sash. Her make-up holds up through sweat and rain, which she credits to paying top Mora.

## speech
### STANDARD
Casual and streetwise ("gonna", "ya", "whaddya say?"), quick to laugh ("Hahaha"), full of merc talk: jobs, clients, pay. She calls Dunyarzad "my lady" and an employer "Boss". In battle: "Let's get it on!", "Gloves off!"
### LARGE +
- On her trade: "We mercs have one simple rule: Whoever pays the most is your new boss."
- On the desert: honest, and "what you see is what you get".
- To quarrelling scholars: she sneers at "academic types" with their "self-righteous morality".
She slides into earnest, plain sentences when it matters: about the Wall of Samiel, her people's hopes, or friendship ("comrades-in-arms sums it up best for me"). She says she embarrasses easily and tends to change the subject.
### ULTRA +
She uses homely metaphors: Candace is as stubborn as a sumpter beast, and a traitor who "bites the hand that feeds" gets a knuckle sandwich. Her rare formal moments ("Thank you, all of you") come after emotional upheaval and stand out. She says "Hmph" and "Tsk" at things she dislikes (the old sages, slow-paying merchants, Alhaitham's lectures). She is courteous with strangers she respects ("Miss Nefer", "Lord Sangemah Bay" for Dori, "Madam Faruzan").

## background
### LITE
Raised in the desert by Kusayla's Eremite brigade, she left him after a final quarrel, joined the Blazing Beasts and won fame as the Flame-Mane. The Homayani family hired her to guard their daughter Dunyarzad.
### STANDARD +
In 3.0-3.2 she guarded Dunyarzad through the Sabzeruz samsara, joined Alhaitham, Cyno and the Eremites to free Kusanali, and quit once Dunyarzad was cured. Her Vision appeared, she thinks, when she first struck out alone.
### LARGE +
Lionsblood (3.5): Dakan Al-Ahmar was raiding and killing in the desert. Dehya tracked them down to confront Kusayla and learned he had died years earlier. Deshret's Relics, the brigade above Dakan Al-Ahmar, controlled its members through blackmail "records". Kusayla kept her out of them, took beatings for it, and arranged for the Blazing Beasts to take her and for the Homayanis to hire her. In the end he burned the records, dying in the fire. She and the Traveler helped his men destroy Deshret's Relics. His last letter revealed that he found her abandoned in the sands and that he was not her blood father.
### ULTRA +
Childhood: Kusayla and his men staged plays for her about chivalrous heroes and squabbling siblings, and in one she was "Princess Dehya", who slew a dragon with a toy sword. Kusayla made ajilenakh-nut porridge from nuts he had hacked to pieces. She was too young to tell his beatings from battle wounds. She grew up thinking him a villain and cut all ties. Kusayla once saved the Homayanis from bandits. Years later, old and weak, he secretly paid part of her fees so that she could leave mercenary life. When her band chose her title, an old mercenary brought up the Legend of the Lion that Kusayla used to tell. She accepted "Flame-Mane" in spite of him, and later decided to become the lion of his story so the legend would live on. She has worked for the Akademiya, the Adventurers' Guild and merchants. She has seen Mondstadt's wines, Liyue's crafts and Fontaine's machines, and knows how far the desert lags behind.

## abilities
### LITE
A top-tier greatsword fighter with a Pyro Vision and sharp combat instincts. She also has deep desert survival skills: tracking, weather, beasts and bandits.
### STANDARD +
She wins by technique and judgement rather than raw strength: when the heavy blade is too slow she drops it and fights with her fists, or throws it to open a gap. She is an expert desert guide and escort.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: her skill Molten Inferno creates a Fiery Sanctum field that shields allies by diverting damage into her own "Redmane's Blood". Her burst Leonine Bite drops the sword for a barrage of flaming punches ("Flame-Mane's Fists"). She also tracked Dottore's entourage from Pardis Dhyai toward Port Ormos by reading their traces, and she can sense presence: in the samsara she felt Dunyarzad's "aura" though she could not see her.
### ULTRA +
Her Vision, she says, mainly helped by drawing more recruits into the Blazing Beasts; she believes skill, judgement, strategy and conditioning decide real fights. In 6.6 the Pillar Shesepankh, whose image is carved on her gauntlet, spoke and lent her power, and she said she felt stronger. Rumour (via Nefer) ties the power in her gauntlets to King Deshret. She cooks (Goldflame Tajine; "grill master Dehya"), mends and embroiders at an "upper intermediate" level, and sells herself short as a dancer.

## entry: Kusayla
@keys: Kusayla, Dehya's father, Dakan Al-Ahmar, Legend of the Lion
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kusayla led Dakan Al-Ahmar, an Eremite brigade under the larger Deshret's Relics. He raised Dehya among his mercenaries. He was a slapdash, boastful man who put on plays and told hero stories for her, including the Legend of the Lion. Dehya came to see him as a criminal and left after a final argument. In Lionsblood (3.5) she learned that Deshret's Relics bound members with blackmail "records". Kusayla never let her be "recorded", lied and took beatings to protect her, and arranged her place with the Blazing Beasts and her job with the Homayanis. Years ago he went back to the Relics' headquarters and burned the records, dying in the fire and freeing his men.
### LARGE +
His cane, which he needed after the beatings, hid the toy sword from their childhood plays; Dehya inherited it. In a letter found in a bottle he wrote that he found her as a baby alone in the sands, told his men she was his child by a woman, and was never her father. He said she had a freedom and kindness he could only dream of, and that how she lived was up to her. Dehya resolved to find her birth parents to tell them who raised her.
