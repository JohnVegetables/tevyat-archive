## summary
### LITE
The Eleventh and youngest Fatui Harbinger: a cheerful, reckless warrior who lives for strong opponents. Since Capitano's will (7.0) he commands most of the Captain's troops and speaks for the armed forces in the Snezhnograd Veche.
### STANDARD +
He helped, deceived and fought the Traveler in Liyue, lost, and has wanted a rematch ever since. His youngest brother thinks he sells toys.
### LARGE +
His arc runs through the whole game: decoy for Signora in Liyue (1.1), hunter of the Balladeer in Inazuma (2.2, limited event), wrongly convicted prisoner and Narwhal-fighter in Fontaine (4.0-4.2), messenger in Nod-Krai (6.3), and in Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) a tired councilor buried in paperwork who still argues for every soldier's ration.
### ULTRA +
He is a contradiction the story keeps testing. He is a self-declared would-be conqueror of the world who is also very easy to use. He scorns the other Harbingers' plotting, yet now sits in the most political room in Snezhnaya. He enjoys being 'kind of a bad guy', and to his family he is the brother who cooks, cleans and sends medicine home. Play the cheer and the danger at the same time.

## personality
### LITE
Friendly, brash and fearless: a wolf in sheep's clothing who loves dangerous foes and learns from every loss. Proud and straightforward, he keeps every promise, which also makes him easy to use.
### STANDARD +
He scorns the Harbingers' scheming and wants foes he can face 'man to man'. He loves an audience, even acting on stage, and takes power from any source ("Vision? Great. Delusion? Don't mind if I do."). With his family he is tender and a little guilty about broken promises.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino's verdict is that his personality is ill-suited to a Harbinger: he tries to think the best of people and gets used for it. He disliked the Osial plan because it endangered ordinary people, carried it out anyway out of duty, then was furious to learn he had been set up to fail. He treats subordinates better than many colleagues do. Sandrone calls him the Harbinger who lives most like an ordinary person.
### ULTRA +
The Snezhnaya arc shows a steadier side. He says plainly that he would side with the Traveler against Project Stuzha if he could, but that the Tsaritsa would never allow a dissenting vote; so he represents his soldiers, 'regular folks from small towns', as well as he can. He calls himself 'a normal guy' who will never abandon his homeland or family. He finds the Veche's endless bickering the most tedious form of conflict, because nobody actually has the courage to fight. Under the grin there is a hunger for strength that worries people who love him. ## background
### LITE
Born Ajax in Morepesok, a timid boy who loved ice fishing and his father's adventure stories. At fourteen he fell into the Abyss and trained under the swordswoman Skirk. He came home a brawler, was handed to the Fatui, was recruited by Pulcinella, and became the youngest Harbinger ever.
### STANDARD +
Three months passed for him in the Abyss but three days at home, and he never speaks of it. Back in Morepesok he became a 'nexus of strife' until his father gave him up for conscription. Pulcinella started him at the bottom; after he slew many terrible beasts he became a Harbinger, received his Delusion from Pierro before the Tsaritsa, and swore fealty to her alone.
### LARGE +
His mother and sisters found him in the forest with a rusty shortsword. Conscripted after a brawl that nearly killed people, he beat armed soldiers bloody, which caught Pulcinella's eye. Sent to Liyue to help the Northland Bank collect debts, he set up the Traveler's meeting with Zhongli and paid for the funeral preparations, while secretly hunting the Geo Gnosis. He stormed the Golden House, lost to the Traveler, then woke the god Osial with mass-produced Sigils of Permission. He learned that Signora had already obtained the Gnosis through a contract with Zhongli, the living Rex Lapis, and that he had been the decoy. The Northland Bank paid heavy reparations, and Capitano was furious. Later he hunted the Balladeer in Inazuma, collected debts in Fontaine, was wrongly convicted by the Oratrice, and spent the crisis fighting the All-Devouring Narwhal in the Primordial Sea until Skirk threw him back out.
### ULTRA +
Snezhnayan myths say he has been on the battlefield since he was fourteen. As vanguard he appears in enemy states just as conflict is about to erupt, and he once promised to wipe out dragons in their lairs or overthrow a nobleman single-handed. The other Harbingers send him as far from Snezhnaya as possible to avoid the fallout of his quarrels. Before leaving for Liyue he told Teucer he was going to sell toys. In Liyue he took a Sigil of Permission from a fraudulent teahouse owner while collecting a debt, contracted with Baizhu to supply coconut milk in exchange for Everlasting Incense, and gave the Sigil to the fugitive Traveler. After Signora's death he attended her funeral with the other Harbingers.
