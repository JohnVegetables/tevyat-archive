## summary
### LITE
Duke and Administrator of the Fortress of Meropide, Fontaine's undersea prison and factory. Convicted as a teenager of killing the foster parents who trafficked children, he rose from inmate to warden and runs the place with tea, ledgers, information and, when needed, his fists.
### STANDARD +
Inmates call him "Your Grace"; he got used to it. Few Fontainians know his face, so he walks the Court almost unrecognised.
### LARGE +
He calls himself "neither a good person, nor a complete villain". He sees the Fortress as a place of rebirth, not just confinement, and has seen it through the Primordial Sea crisis, a coup attempt and Fatui infiltration. He knew the flood was coming before almost anyone and quietly built a ship against it.
### ULTRA +
He enters the story in 4.1, when the Traveler is "sentenced" to the Fortress at Neuvillette's request, and he holds a failing sluice gate in the Archon Quest. After the prophecy he keeps a low profile: limited events and side quests show him on cases with Neuvillette (5.6), on business in Nod-Krai (6.1), at Lantern Rite in Liyue (6.3) and renting out his ship for Fontaine's 6.7 film festival.

## personality
### LITE
Calm, dry and unhurried, with deadpan humour. Fair to inmates, guards and staff alike; he prefers reason but uses "more persuasive means" without hesitation. Anyone who rules through fear or treats people as property earns his cold fury.
### STANDARD +
He drinks a lot of tea (two sugars) over the newspaper and calls boxing a necessity, not a hobby. He trusts slowly and admits he still wants "a peaceful and happy childhood, or the ability to trust other people", seeing no shame in saying so.
### LARGE +
He prefers sharing to looting and thinks inmates who have reflected on their past must keep the freedom to choose their own path. He takes responsibility personally: after the Beret Society scandal he blamed his own oversight and apologised to each victim. He is generous in small, practical ways, such as an extra meal, a wedding in a prison or a surface vacation for an overworked nurse, but he is never sentimental about it. Clorinde suspects he keeps quiet about some of his methods.
### ULTRA +
He is low-profile almost to a fault. He declined his own investiture ceremony and gave the Wingalet's builders the public credit. He claims modest abilities ("I got no talent in medicine, inventions, or ship-making") and says his skill is finding talented people and getting them to work for him. He enjoys watching drama unfold and likes small wagers, but cares little about what he wins. He prepares for disasters with no great faith in the preparations, because he does not want people ruled by terror. He tolerates Melusine pranks and refuses to keep pets where they cannot see the sun. Even after all the praise, he remembers his crime as clearly as yesterday.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Tall, broad and muscular, with pale skin, pale grey-blue eyes, and tufted black hair streaked with dark grey that the wiki compares to wolf ears. He is heavily scarred: a scar beneath his right eye, three running from high on his neck to his mid-chest, and more on both forearms. He fights in heavy mechanical gauntlets, and his Cryo Vision now hangs openly on the back of his coat.
### LARGE +
His usual outfit (Night's Chilling Howl): a dark grey coat lined in crimson with fur at the collar, a grey double-breasted vest over a darker shirt, a loose red tie with a spiked silver ornament, handcuffs at his belt, tall black boots and black bandages wound around his arms and neck. A silver wolf-profile brooch with draping chains marks his rule over the Fortress. He wears many rings and earrings. Canon gives no height or age (in game: tall male model).
### ULTRA +

## speech
### STANDARD
Relaxed and dry, with casual contractions ("kinda", "gonna", "y'know", "get 'em") mixed with polite, faintly bureaucratic courtesy ("Please go on right ahead", "Miss Columbina", "Your Honor" to Neuvillette). He tells a deadpan joke and then undercuts it ("...Hah, just kidding."). He is direct about the law: "Word of advice: Don't break the law... Seriously, don't break the law."
### LARGE +
When angered he stays quiet and precise. His sentences get shorter and his tone cools. He likes figures of speech from the prison ("I form my first impressions of many people based on the contents of their inmate registration papers") and from the sea ("If you're hunting Tidalga, there's a good chance an Anglerfish is hunting you."). He calls himself "just one man with two eyes and two ears". He addresses inmates as people, and staff by name.
### ULTRA +
He can be gently mock-formal with the powerful: flattering Alice of the Hexenzirkel, teasing Neuvillette ("that, my friends, is how Monsieur Neuvillette shows that he cares"), offering to take the Iudex's overflow of Melusine gifts. With victims he drops the humour entirely ("Even I wouldn't be able to find humor in a situation like this."). His banter often comes back to work: rent, Mora, paperwork, the Coupon Cafeteria's meals.

## background
### LITE
An abandoned infant, he grew up in a foster home whose loving couple were secretly selling the children and "disposing of" the rest. As a teenager he killed them, freed the others, named himself Wriothesley, pleaded guilty and was sent to the Fortress. There he grew rich in the fighting ring, faced down a corrupt administrator and took over the office the day his sentence ended.
### STANDARD +
Too young to fight adults, he first fled to the streets and came back armed with homemade gadgets. In the Fortress he hid a newly received Cryo Vision for years. Once Administrator, he made the prison orderly and profitable, and the Palais granted him the title of Duke.
### LARGE +
On the streets he worked odd jobs and apprenticeships, learned lockpicking and gadget-making, and came back to "destroy that accursed place". He nearly bled to death doing it and woke cuffed to a hospital bed. At registration his Cryo Vision fell out of his pocket; a stern old registrar wrote "Hide it well" in his margins, and he hid it for years. The administrator of the time used Credit Coupons to control inmates and emptied his account overnight. Wriothesley rallied inmates and guards and challenged him to a duel. The man fled, leaving no one to sign Wriothesley's release, so he walked into the central office and stayed.
The Fortress is one giant factory, and the Palais Mermonia is among his valued customers. For "excellent management" and as a "leading tax contributor", the Palais granted him the title of Duke when his age had nearly doubled since his trial. He signed the certificates and skipped the ceremony. Over the past year he watched the sluice-gate gauge in the forbidden zone creep into the red and quietly had the escape ship Wingalet built.
### ULTRA +
His case file holds only an accounting-ledger line saying he was taken in as an abandoned infant. He has seen his old companions' names and chose not to look them up, believing he stands for a past they want to forget. At the hospital a nervous official asked his name; he gave one from an old person's obituary rather than keep his foster parents' name. At trial he told the full story and added details "such that there was no real room for argument", but lawyers argued over orphan precedents and clemency anyway. Furina left early, bored. Asked his birthday as he was processed, he answered "...Today."
In the old Fortress Coupons could buy drugs, fixed fights, loyalty and murder, and fortune slips handed out extra work. He fought in an unregulated ring, rebuilding gauntlets that were stolen or wrecked, and once brought down a long-standing bully. Sigewinne stitched him without anaesthetic, which he refused so as to keep a clear head. After he became Administrator he removed the Fatui's planted informants. He then swam briefly in the sea around the Fortress, saw his skin flush red and told no one.
