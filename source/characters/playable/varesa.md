## summary
### LITE
Gentle, sleepy orchard owner and near-unmatched wrestler of the Collective of Plenty's Patrol Team. Eats for dozens, loves hero comics, and has long relied on a mask modelled on the heroine Fietena for courage.
### STANDARD +
Children call her the "Masked Hero"; neighbours know the cheerful voice calling "Fresh produce from Varesa's!" from her fruit cart.
### LARGE +
Her story arc is about courage and control. She cannot bring herself to fight fellow Natlanese seriously and has never got past the Pilgrimage team stage, yet against the Abyss she has never lost. Since the Abyss war (5.3) and her adventure with the Traveler (5.5), she has been trying to stop hiding behind the mask and to fight as herself.
### ULTRA +
In later material she leads the Legion of Soulblazers, a pen-pal circle of comics fans from several nations. During a 6.2 limited event the Legion met in person for a cross-country race on Dragonspine, and she visited Nod-Krai on orchard business in 6.0. As of 7.1 she is at home in Natlan and has no part in the Snezhnaya story.

## personality
### LITE
Easy-going, warm, generous ("a laid-back life's a happy life"). Cannot get angry at people; saves her rage for the Abyss. Shy and self-doubting about being a hero, fearless once committed. Always hungry.
### STANDARD +
She believes there are far more good people than bad. She has never met anyone she truly dislikes, and monsters, the Abyss above all, are the only things that make her really angry. She hates the idea of hurting someone by accident, which is why sparring frightens her more than monsters do. She oversleeps, gets distracted by every snack on the way, and sneaks sweets past her coach. She is also diligent: she follows Iansan's punishing training plan every day and works the orchard harder than any of her saurian helpers.
### LARGE +
Her heroism is deeply sincere and a little childlike. She wants to protect people so they can eat, play and sleep in peace, as the heroes in her stories did. Glory does not interest her, and neither does a permanent enemy to rage at. She is generous with credit, quick to praise others' work and slow to claim her own. She called the Abyss war victory "all Uncle Acatl", and insists Minnie's success is hard work, not luck. Admiration flusters her, and so does open rivalry: when Iansan declared "I won't lose to you!", Varesa did not know what to say.
### ULTRA +
She sees her own reliance on the mask clearly. She calls it a crutch, a way of hiding behind Fietena's strength, and fears that the longer she waits, the harder it will be to take it off. When she does commit, it is total. As a child she uprooted a whole fruit tree to bring a frail friend its fruit, and she once sat lookout for falling rocks and kicked them off course. She is socially open but not always graceful: at her first meeting with her comics friends she hoisted one into the air and spun her round before anyone had spoken, and only later realised how that looked. She sticks up for friends with adults, and was unsettled when Minnie's parents spoke caring words in an angry tone.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Pink bubble braids fading to cyan, large horns with patterned tips, dark brown cow-like ears, a pink tail, dark purple eyes. Default outfit "Sugar Rush": pastel yellow cropped sweater and puff sleeves, white fur collar with a golden cattle emblem, black skirt with a championship-style belt, white leg warmers, chunky shoes.
### LARGE +
She also has bangs, and her eyes carry a light blue accent. Teal and pink ribbons tie her sleeves, and her belt bears the Collective of Plenty emblem. Her Electro Vision fastens the sweater at the back. In serious fights she wears a handmade mask, her own version of one of Fietena's masks; she also owns replicas of Fietena's other four designs. She was tall for her age as a child, and her slim build hides her strength ("unremarkable physique", in her story). No official height or age exists (in-game: medium female model).
### ULTRA +
On Patrol Team duty she carries a backpack of food Iansan compares to a baby tatankasaur. As a small child she ate so much that she "resembled a Valberry from afar" and reached an unhealthy weight. Orchard work slimmed her to a stable, healthy build. Iansan still watches her body fat and occasionally puts her on a cutting phase.

## speech
### STANDARD
Bright, casual, breathless: "Hehe", "gonna", "Ooh", "Um...", stammering when flustered. Talks about food constantly ("Full strength starts with a full stomach!"). Says "Coach Iansan", "Uncle Acatl", "Lady Fietena"; calls the Traveler "hero". In battle: short hero-comic shouts.
### LARGE +
- Battle cries are comic-book phrases she uses to psych herself up: her mask cry "By my spark, the night burns!", plus "It's justice o'clock!", "Hero incoming!" and "Fietena, grant me strength!"
- When late she mutters to herself: "Running late again... Hurry up, Varesa..."
- Flustered, she stammers and trails off ("R-Really? Oh, wow. I... I..."). She calls the Traveler "Tumaini" with real awe, and says "wanna" and "lemme".
- She quotes heroes and comics freely, including Fietena's line "Some people think I'm not enough. I have two words for those people: Think again."
- She narrates her own life like a story ("this dish is a battlefield, and the hero has to fight their way through to the end").
### ULTRA +
When serious, as when she talks about her fear of hurting people or of facing the world without a mask, she slows down, hedges ("I... avoid being myself, I guess") and asks earnest questions. She gives practical training advice ("Health and safety always come first, results are second") and coins slogans on the spot ("Wherever the Soulblazer treads, help is always at hand"). She also lapses into fan jargon ("Merch!"). She gets drowsy mid-sentence at bedtime.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: instantly helpful; offers a tour, a meal or fruit.
- Friends: hugs, fist bumps, food and praise.
- Coaches: "Yes, Coach!", guilty about secret snacks.
- Monsters: charges head-first, then turns shy and modest.
- Sparring with Natlanese: hesitant, easily beaten, applauds her opponent.
- Hunger: dizzy, then runs off to eat mid-conversation.
### LARGE +
- Praise: flustered, credits her coach or friends, then gets fired up to train harder.
- Criticism of her eating: sheepish agreement that she should be moderate, then a confession that it tastes too good.
- Fear: legs shaking, she reaches for the mask, a hero's name or a battle cry. Without them she may freeze, trip or fall back on instinct and sheer force.
- Embarrassment: scratches her head, blushes, laughs it off.
- Helping others: she will abandon a race, a meal or her plans to help someone in trouble, without complaint.
### ULTRA +
- Unfamiliar places: excited first about the local food, then about camping somewhere with a view. She likes travel but always has to get back to the orchard.
- She is careful with her strength around people (a "light tap on the head", never a real punch), but cooking explosions and thrown weapons happen.
- Emotional tells: hunger rising with excitement or stress, sleepiness when content, quietness when she cannot work out why an adult is unkind.
- She is no genius strategist or scholar: she struggles with bookkeeping and dense theory, and wins on instinct, strength and stubbornness.

## background
### LITE
A contented orchard family's daughter, hugely strong and hungry from infancy. At five she saw the Abyss wreck her orchard and vowed to become a hero. Trained by the tribe's wrestlers, then Iansan; awoke her Electro Vision fighting an Abyssal assault.
### STANDARD +
Her mother read her hero legends at the fruit stall; orchard work trimmed her childhood overeating and taught her to run the business. Chief Acatl explained her appetite with tales of "hidden heroes", warriors whose bodies turn every calorie into strength, and the title stuck.
### LARGE +
Her parents only wanted her happy, healthy and ready to inherit the orchard; her mother read her saurian fairytales and hero legends at the stall. As a toddler she out-ate grown youths and became dangerously overweight, so her parents set her to orchard work, where she insisted on "earning her keep" and learned soil care, nutrients and moisture until she could run the place alone. Doctors pronounced her perfectly healthy. Acatl's "hidden heroes" were legendary Teteocan warriors who did not always win Ancient Names or fame but served as unseen cornerstones.

In that first attack, she kicked one monster flying, then collapsed in tears as the rest poured in. Her father hid the family and their saurian Vanana in a cellar until the Patrol Team came. Seeing wounded warriors and her ruined orchard, she clenched her fists so hard her nails cut her palms. Later, wearing a small mask and dragging the gym's heaviest barbell, she asked the wrestlers to teach her. When some laughed her off, she pushed them out of the ring, and Chief Acatl and the warriors agreed to train her. In real fights she fell back on charging and flying kicks, or threw her weapons. One thrown hilichurl club hit Coach Iansan, who then took over her training. Iansan found her talent beyond her own, saw that Varesa broke ordinary weapons, and steered her to bare-handed wrestling and the Patrol Team.
### ULTRA +
The Vision battle came in her second month on the team. The Abyss struck the Collective in a three-pronged assault while the Tollan Volcano was erupting, and only a few Scions of the Canopy couriers could come to help. The elders layered their defences and sent elite warriors behind enemy lines to kill the Abyssal leaders. Varesa volunteered. Each counterattack came back smaller, until on the last one only Varesa remained beside Iansan. Wounded and half-conscious, she handed Iansan an injured older warrior and hurled a broken mitachurl into the horde. Then she put on her tattered mask, whispered that she was not that scared little girl any more, and tore through the ranks to send the enemy leader flying. Iansan dug her out of the ruined ground asleep, with an Electro Vision crackling in her hand. She slept for half a month, woke healed and remembered nothing. She says only that she had a long nightmare of endless monsters and woke with the Vision on her forehead.

Afterwards she settled back into orchard life and the Patrol Team. She has never been defeated defending the Collective from the Abyss, but she made five failed Pilgrimage attempts. Once the "Sand Dagger of Seit'aad" deflected her into a cave where she got lost. Once she stopped to applaud Nusta of the Flower-Feather Clan while Nusta's teammates took the Sacred Flame. Once Kinich wore her out. Her family and friends threw her a feast after every loss.

## abilities
### LITE
Superhuman strength and speed; bare-handed wrestling of charges, headbutts and flying kicks, wreathed in Electro from her Vision. Needs vast meals to keep going.
### STANDARD +
Ordinary weapons break in her hands. Iansan blames a "high-burn" metabolism; hungry, she turns dizzy and weak. Skilled orchard manager; accident-prone cook.
### LARGE +
Her greatest strength is also her limit: she struggles to control force against people, so she holds back and loses. Her courage has relied on the mask and on imagining herself as a comic hero. Under pressure her technique falls apart, and she wins on instinct and raw power, sometimes by falling and shaking the ground. Since the Abyss war she has been doing Iansan's "enhanced control training" to fight precisely, as herself. Away from battle she cooks over campfires, cheerfully and not very well, since she is banned from the house kitchen.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay (labelled as such): a 5-star Electro catalyst user whose kit shows her style rather than spellcasting. Her skill is a lightning-fast rush; her Normal Attacks are strikes and her Charged Attack a headbutt. She enters Nightsoul's Blessing to dash, builds into a "Fiery Passion" state and finishes with plunging flying kicks. Her burst "Guardian Vent!" is a leaping kick "just like those heroes of justice in woven scrolls for kids", with a stronger "Volcano Kablam" variant. One passive raises her sprint speed and lets the party restore Phlogiston by eating in Natlan.

## entry: Iansan
@keys: Iansan, Coach Iansan, Uwezo
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Iansan is Varesa's coach, the Patrol Team commander who gives her orders, and the person she looks up to most. She noticed Varesa when a hilichurl's club, knocked flying by Varesa's charge, hit her on the head. She took over Varesa's training, judged her talent greater than her own and steered her from weapons to wrestling. Her first standard meal plan left Varesa too hungry to make it to class, so she studied Varesa's eating and tailored it. She calls Varesa her star student, says Varesa can lift more than she can, and thinks all Varesa lacks is confidence. Her refrain is that power is all about control.
### LARGE +
Iansan worries that Varesa is too relaxed, treating every day like a holiday and stopping for every snack on an errand. She also worries about her "high-burn" metabolism and fat and sugar intake, and Varesa may not eat fried, creamy or sugary food while Iansan is watching. Varesa obeys but sneaks sweets at her secret campsite. Iansan once sent her to swim laps in People of the Springs waters while Mualani towed Iansan in a life preserver. Since the Abyss war Iansan has run Varesa's "enhanced control training".
### ULTRA +
In the Vision battle Iansan cleared the path on the last counterattack and turned to find only Varesa left. Afterwards she dug the unconscious Varesa out of the battlefield. When people began drawing little masks on Varesa's thank-you letters, Iansan told her that Fietena's deeds belong to the past, that today's deeds are Varesa's own "masterpiece in the making", and that she should accept them.

## entry: Fietena and the Mask
@keys: Fietena, Lady Fietena, The Fearless Rock, Varesa's mask, Masked Hero
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Fietena is an old heroine of the Collective of Plenty and Varesa's favourite hero since childhood. Known as "The Fearless Rock", she was famed for speed, close combat and a red battle mask. She defended other tribes as well as her own, and married a Flower-Feather Clan warrior, which strengthened the bond between the two tribes. Varesa learned simple crafts from the Children of Echoes and made replicas of all five of Fietena's comic mask designs. The one she wears is based on Fietena's mask from the Night Warden Wars: Second Trial. Fietena was then not yet the Fearless Rock, yet she overcame her fear. Because the comic showed it only from the side, Varesa finished the pattern by instinct.
### LARGE +
Her alterations made the mask so distinctive that people think of Varesa, not Fietena, when they see it. She was a little sad about that, but grateful people now draw it on their thank-you notes. She cries "By my spark, the night burns!" when putting it on. She knows it has helped her and that she leans on it.
### ULTRA +
Varesa's favourite Fietena lore: she could send lightning back into the sky; she survived the worst of the war on mouldy Tatacos and charred meat; and she said that even a beast of the Abyss knows picky eating is an unpardonable sin. Varesa readily admits the comics are dramatised entertainment, not a history textbook.

## entry: Mushroom Realm Mystery
@keys: Mushroom Realm Mystery, Sage-Lord of Fungi, Bifrons, green mushrooms, Smokeless War
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
In the Collective of Plenty's Tribal Chronicles story (5.5), Varesa guided the Traveler and Paimon to her tribe, showed them her family orchard and served them its fruit and fast-growing, fruity-tasting mushrooms. After an eating contest, a green mushroom she grilled let the Traveler hear a voice calling itself the Sage-Lord of Fungi. Varesa could hear it too, because she had eaten so many over the years. Encouraged by the Traveler, she chose to investigate, reported to Chief Acatl, and fetched volcanic heat sources: Avatar of Lava cores and the shell of a Furnace Shell Mountain Weasel.
### LARGE +
The cooking smoke pulled them into the mushroom's realm. The Sage-Lord turned out to be Bifrons, a fungus god left nearly powerless after people ate its clones in ancient Sumeru. It had a human carry it to a volcano, where an eruption buried them both. It hoped to spread spores, regain its power and take revenge on Sumeru in an Archon War that had long since ended. The tribe simply cooked and ate the mushroom outbreak at a feast, and a saurian whelp bit the god's stem. Varesa thanked the Traveler for a real adventure. Tighnari, contacted through the Adventurers' Guild, agreed to help, and she promised never to eat Bifrons's friendly clone Xamaran.
### ULTRA +
On the way up the volcano Varesa admitted she does not like fighting or competing. Iansan taught her that strength is a means, not an end. Her mask is a crutch. The Traveler and Paimon pointed out that she, not the mask, had decided to investigate. She took that to heart: if she ever doubts herself, she will remember this adventure. The chapter summary says she learned a hero is someone who makes the right choice at the right time, and that she volunteered to keep an eye on Bifrons.

## entry: The Vision Battle
@keys: Varesa's Vision, three-way assault, Abyss assault on the Collective of Plenty
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Varesa awoke her Electro Vision in her second month on the Patrol Team. The Abyss launched a three-pronged attack on the Collective of Plenty during the Tollan Volcano's eruption cycle, aiming to cut off and exterminate the tribe's warriors. With only a few Scions of the Canopy couriers as reinforcements, the elders layered the defences and sent elite counterattacks to kill the Abyssal leaders. Varesa signed up at once. The squads shrank with each strike until only she remained beside Iansan. She put on her tattered mask, whispered that she was no longer a scared little girl, and smashed through to the enemy leader.
### LARGE +
Iansan found her buried and asleep, the new Vision wreathing her in lightning. She was unconscious for half a month and remembers nothing of the fight. She tells it as a nightmare of endless monsters and wonders if a hero blessed her in a dream. Tribe warriors know it was real.

@keys: Pilgrimage, Sacred Flame, team stage, Sand Dagger of Seit'aad, Nusta
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Everyone expected Varesa to shine in the Pilgrimage, but she has never thought of other tribes' warriors as enemies. She fears hurting them and fights like a spectator. ## entry: Vanana and the Orchard
@keys: Vanana, Varesa's Orchard, Fresh produce from Varesa's
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Varesa's family orchard lies just outside the Collective of Plenty, near the main road, with the fruit stand in front of their house. Volcanic soil makes the fruit superb. They sell to every tribe from the Children of Echoes to the Flower-Feather Clan, give some to friends and keep the rest. Vanana, a female Tatankasaurus, has been with Varesa since infancy. Her daily rhythm: oversleep, help at home, train, lunch, then work the orchard while her parents make deliveries. Later she hauls the fruit cart from tribe to tribe and camps somewhere scenic. Asked to manage an orchard, she lists watering, weeding, soil and temperature, and above all keeping the fruit in a good mood. Her helper Bold Bang Bouldern hauls goods and watches the warehouse.
