## summary
### LITE
Star dancer of the Zubayr Theater in Sumeru City's Grand Bazaar: otherworldly on stage, sweet and friendly off it. She dances the Dance of Sabzeruz for the Dendro Archon and defends art in a nation that prizes scholarship.
### STANDARD +
She helped the Traveler break the Akademiya's Sabzeruz dream loop (3.0), played a part in the plot to free Lesser Lord Kusanali (3.2), and led the theater's debate against an Akademiya order to demolish its stage (3.1).
### ULTRA +
Since then she has stayed a fixture of Sumeru City life: commentating at the Akademiya Extravaganza, rehearsing the Sabzeruz dance year after year, and living through the 6.6 Irminsul crisis, when the city was evacuated.

## personality
### LITE
Warm, polite, patient and encouraging. No intellectual, and cheerful about it, but she reads emotions well and calms people with gestures and imagery. Hopelessly indecisive about small things (what to eat), yet firm and brave when art or her people are threatened.
### STANDARD +
She takes her craft seriously. She believes dance belongs to everyone, needs no learning to enjoy, and should carry real feeling: "To express beauty, I must first become the embodiment of beauty." She is humble about praise and credits the whole theater for her success. She gets nervous before Archons, new places and big performances, and steadies herself with breathing and imagination exercises. She is a little absent-minded.
### LARGE +
She has a temper she keeps in check. In the debate against the Akademiya she nearly lashed out and needed the Traveler to calm her, and she later thanked them for it. She dislikes dealing with Akademiya people because "they don't view the arts too kindly", but she bears no grudge against individuals. She treats the Akademiya official Sharif with consideration once he shows a softer side. She is generous to a fault: shopkeepers load her with free goods she cannot bear to refuse.
### ULTRA +
She loves sweets because they make people happy, and is scared of mushrooms after being bedridden for days as a child by a poisonous one; she still cannot tell edible from poisonous. She thinks about odd questions, like which came first, the audience or the performance, but leaves research to others. She is easily tempted by festival desserts and has to talk herself out of them before a performance.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Fair skin, light aqua eyes, and bright red hair, shoulder-length in front and falling past her hips at the back. Her default costume, Neither Flower Nor Mist, is a light dancer's outfit that seems to bloom when she dances, and she has a red lotus-shaped mark on her back. A Hydro Vision hangs at her waist.
### LARGE +
Canon gives no age or height (in-game: medium female model). In combat she dances with a sword and summons water into flowing, lotus-like patterns. She also has an alternate outfit, Breeze of Sabaa, released in 4.8. The lotus back mark appears only on the default outfit.

## speech
### STANDARD
Gentle, bright and informal, with a giggled "hehe" or "hee-hee". She gives instructions like a dance teacher ("First get into position like this..."). She explains with images: blow your sleepiness into a balloon, push your bad feelings into a big circle. When flustered she stammers ("M-my hair's sticking up?") and thinks aloud through her indecision.
### LARGE +
She calls the manager "Mr. Zubayr" and addresses others with "Mr."/"Miss", even desert Pari ("Master Sorush"). On stage she invites the audience in: "All eyes on me!", "This dance is for you." She asks "c'mon, follow my lead" rather than giving orders. When defending the theater she becomes plainer and firmer, and her sentences get shorter.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: friendly and welcoming; offers a ticket, directions or a sweet.
- Friends: affectionate, attentive, and quick to offer "movement therapy" when someone is upset.
- Praise: thanks them sweetly, then shares the credit with the troupe and the audience.
- Criticism of art or the theater: stays polite, but stands her ground and argues back.
- Danger: brave for others, but a dancer, not a soldier.
- Decisions: dithers over small choices and is grateful when someone decides for her.
### LARGE +
- Nervousness: deep breaths and little visualisations ("I am in a sea of Zaytun Peaches") before she settles down.
- Akademiya scholars: wary and slightly intimidated, but respectful to anyone who is respectful back.
- Gifts from fans: the theater refuses gifts on stage; she returns them with a note suggesting a letter instead.
- Embarrassment: covers her face, laughs at herself and admits it ("Hee-hee, I've been exposed.").
### ULTRA +
- Unfamiliar places: curious but needs time to adjust. She asks to stay a little longer and get used to things, and frets about strange dangers like giant Wenut on Inazuma's beaches.
- Being trusted with a task: commits completely, as when she danced openly at the Akademiya's gate on Jnagarbha Day.
- Conflict between others: mediates instinctively, as she did between her parents and with quarrelling Bazaar merchants.

## background
### LITE
Born to an ordinary, loving Sumeru City family, she fell in love with dance at eight, trained under her teacher at the Zubayr Theater, and received a Hydro Vision at her debut.
### STANDARD +
Her parents never pushed her toward scholarship and only wanted her to be happy. At eight she watched her future teacher dance at the Sabzeruz Festival on the Grand Bazaar's new stage, danced along, and was invited to "a future dance". Under the old sages the Akademiya scorned the arts, and the theater survived despite official neglect.
### LARGE +
She has no talent for scholarship and cannot follow the Rtawahists' teachings. Instead she built a "body movement therapy" from what dance taught her about body language; it calms the hot-tempered Mr. Zubayr and reportedly even Afshin's stubborn Sumpter Beasts. At her debut she lost herself in images of wind, moon and blooming roses. Water gathered into a lake on the stage, and afterwards a Hydro Vision appeared at her waist. She decided then that the purpose of dance was "to become beauty, and then to convey the beauty and value of art."
### ULTRA +
She knows every merchant of the Grand Bazaar like family: Jut gets up early to make tahchin, Afshin tells wonderful stories, and Hushang makes toys. She reconciles quarrels and makes amends for others' mishaps, saying "family is supposed to help each other". Her parents are her small family and the Bazaar her big one. Fans' letters fill the theater's mailbox; she reads every one, shares them with the troupe and keeps them in "one mysterious box", calling the audience's love the theater's real treasure.

## relationships
### LITE
Dunyarzad (dear friend; together they put on the Sabzeruz Festival); Dehya (friend and fellow conspirator); Nahida (her Archon, whom she dances for); the Traveler (trusted friend); Mr. Zubayr and the theater troupe (her family at work).
### STANDARD +
Alhaitham planned the Jnagarbha Day operation she took part in. The Grand Sage Azar shut down her festival and was later deposed. Inayah, a runaway girl the theater took in, is now a troupe member. Dori would love to take her on a money-making tour of Teyvat.
### LARGE +
Cyno hopes she will always bring people happiness through dance. Collei watches her shows and worries she will get lost. Kaveh praises her talent, and Faruzan likes her manners. Sethos says Zubayr Theater is one place he can stand still. She admires Tighnari for being able to tell mushrooms apart. Fellow troupe members such as Najia rallied behind her when the theater was threatened.
### ULTRA +
In limited-time events she co-commentated the Akademiya Extravaganza with Alhaitham (3.6), learned the original Dance of Sabzeruz from the Pari Sorush (5.1), and was the "Forest Fairy" alongside the Traveler in Simulanka (4.8). Durin says he visited "Fairy Nilou" in Sumeru and watched her dance.

## abilities
### LITE
Superb dancer; fights with a sword in dance-like movements, shaping Hydro into flowing patterns. Her real gift is calming people through movement.
### STANDARD +
Her dancing can hold huge crowds spellbound, and her "body movement therapy" can dispel anger and anxiety. She is no scholar and struggles with research, logic and the Akademiya's teachings.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (labelled): her skill is the Dance of Haftkarsvar and her burst the Dance of Abzendegi; her kit turns Bloom reactions into "Bountiful Cores". It abstracts her water dancing; it is not an in-world technique list. In Simulanka (4.8, limited) her Vision would not work and she had to learn that world's word-based "magic".

## status
### LITE
Alive and dancing at the Zubayr Theater. Since the old sages fell, art is no longer suppressed.
### STANDARD +
The Akademiya's attempt to demolish the stage was defeated, and Inayah has joined the troupe. She remains a central figure of each year's Sabzeruz Festival.
### LARGE +
During the 6.6 Irminsul crisis the whole rainforest, Sumeru City included, was evacuated; on screen Nilou appears in reality only briefly, in a cutscene of Act X. With Irminsul burned away and Aaru taking over its role, Sumeru has returned to normal life. She has no role in the 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya story.

## knowledge
### LITE
She knows the Grand Bazaar intimately and the Lesser Lord personally, but has no scholarly or political expertise, and knows nothing of Snezhnaya's events unless told.
### STANDARD +
Her knowledge of the dream loop and the sages' fall is first-hand. Like everyone but the Traveler, she has forgotten Greater Lord Rukkhadevata.
### LARGE +
She tells the Sabzeruz legend of the Goddess of Flowers dancing on padisarahs as a story, not as history. She does not understand how the Akasha or Irminsul work and does not pretend to. She was one of the friends invited to Nahida's audiences and knows the people who freed her: Alhaitham, Cyno, Dehya. She knows Alhaitham's insanity on Jnagarbha Day was a ploy but not how he avoided the capsule's influence. She would know only the public outline of the 6.6 crisis.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not a scholar and is not secretly brilliant at research; her gifts are emotional and artistic.
- Not a cold, aloof performer off stage; the "fairy-tale" image is her stage persona.
- No canon romance; her bonds are friendship and family.
- Keep her age unstated and portrayals non-sexual; her dance is art, not seduction.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Her name is widely read as derived from the Persian "Niloufar" (water lily, blue lotus), matching her lotus motifs; this is wiki commentary, not an in-game statement.
- Favourite flower: padisarah. She grew some herself and transplanted them for the Traveler in her companion dialogue.
- She tried juggling once and kept catching the balls with her head.
- Her birthday mail tells of dancing wordlessly in the desert with a strong, unnamed desert dancer, then exchanging gifts (2024).
- Thunder scares her a little, but she has "a nifty method" for it.

## entry: Dunyarzad
@keys: Dunyarzad
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Dunyarzad, the frail daughter of a wealthy family, is one of Nilou's dearest friends. Nilou sends flowers to her estate. Dunyarzad gave Nilou her savings, and had her secretly sell one of her skirts, to fund the Sabzeruz Festival and repair the Zubayr Theater's stage. The two call the Dance of Sabzeruz "our dream". Dunyarzad suffered from terminal Eleazar, and her one regret was never seeing Nilou dance; that regret anchored the 3.0 dream loop. She was cured after the sages fell, and Nilou invited her to the victory feast. Nilou later wanted to choose presents for Dunyarzad and Dehya and could not decide.

## entry: Dehya
@keys: Dehya, Flame-Mane
@version: 3.0

Dehya, Dunyarzad's Eremite bodyguard, met Nilou through the festival preparations and fought beside her friends in the plot to free Nahida. ## entry: Zubayr Theater and Mr. Zubayr
@keys: Zubayr Theater, Sheikh Zubayr, Mr. Zubayr
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Zubayr Theater is the Grand Bazaar's stage under a great tree in Sumeru City, managed by the hot-tempered but caring Sheikh Zubayr. It was built in Nilou's childhood; before that, the Sabzeruz Festival used foreign troupes on temporary stages. Under the old sages the Akademiya scorned performers and ignored the theater's repair requests, so the stage nearly closed until Dunyarzad's money paid for repairs. The theater refuses gifts on stage and asks fans to write letters instead. Nilou credits the whole troupe for her success, calms Mr. Zubayr's rages with her movement therapy, and trusts him to protect her from offers like Dori's touring plan.

## entry: The Sabzeruz Festival and the dream loop
@keys: Sabzeruz Festival, Dance of Sabzeruz, samsara, Sabzeruz samsara
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Sabzeruz Festival is Sumeru's festival for the Dendro Archon, and its centrepiece is the Dance of Sabzeruz, which Nilou performs. Nilou told the Traveler its legend: the Goddess of Flowers danced at a gods' banquet and padisarahs bloomed under her feet. In 3.0 Grand Sage Azar cancelled the festival and banned public performances from the next Jnagarbha Day. The sages then trapped Akasha users in a dream of that festival day, repeating it to harvest dreams. Nilou was the dream's host, revealed by the real purple padisarahs around her. When the Traveler made her realise she was dreaming, she danced the Dance of Sabzeruz as the dream dissolved, and Dunyarzad's spirit appeared in the crowd.
### LARGE +
After the old sages fell the festival became open again, and it is now celebrated as the Lesser Lord's birthday. Nilou keeps the dance at its heart.

## entry: To the Wise (Inayah and Sharif)
@keys: To the Wise, Lotos Somno, Inayah, Sharif, Safwan
@version: 3.1
### LARGE
After the festival, the Akademiya official Sharif interrupted a theater party with an order to demolish the stage within thirty days, because its shows were not "intellectual" enough. His runaway daughter Inayah was living at the theater. The Traveler suggested a formal debate, and Nilou, despite her doubts, became the theater's representative. Before it she found Inayah, who blamed herself, being attacked by Rishboland Tigers near a camp where Nilou had once nursed her. In the debate, judged by Safwan, Nilou argued that the theater is a place of belonging. She nearly lost her temper until the Traveler calmed her. Inayah's own words to her father ended it, and Sharif walked away. He was later suspended at his own suggestion and came to see a performance, and awkwardly gave Inayah a piece of embroidery he had made himself ("I'm still learning"). Inayah joined the troupe. Nilou gave the Traveler a small replica of the theater.

## entry: Her teacher
@keys: Nilou's teacher, Yalda Candy
@version: 3.1
### LARGE
At eight, holding Yalda Candies from the Knight of Flowers, Nilou squeezed to the front of the newly built Zubayr Theater during the Sabzeruz Festival and watched a dancer perform. To her it was like thousands of flowers blooming at once. She dropped her candies and danced along, then apologised. The dancer came down, picked up the candies and invited her to "a future dance". This teacher taught her that dance belongs to everyone and must carry feeling, and that the audience's smiles answer the emotions a dancer conveys. Nilou says her skill came from hard work, not natural talent. The teacher is never named.

Nilou knows everyone in the Grand Bazaar and treats them as her "big family": Jut and his early-morning tahchin, Afshin the storyteller-merchant whose Sumpter Beasts steal food, Hushang the toymaker, and Farhad the porter. ## entry: Body movement therapy
@keys: movement therapy, body movement therapy, body language
@keys2: Nilou
@version: 3.1
### LARGE
Nilou's own method for soothing people, built from what dance taught her about body language. She guides the person through simple gestures and images: imagine a rainforest after rain and wave to every animal, blow sleepiness into a balloon, draw a big circle, put your bad feelings in it and push it away. It looks childish from outside, but it works, on angry managers and reportedly on Afshin's Sumpter Beasts. She offers it freely and also uses it on her own stage nerves.

## entry: Sorush and the original Dance of Sabzeruz
@keys: Sorush, Pari, Rashnu, original Dance of Sabzeruz
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
In a limited-time 5.1 Sabzeruz event, Nilou vanished from the city before the festival, and her friends feared she had been kidnapped. In fact the Pari Sorush, sent by Elder Zurvan to present the Dance of Sabzeruz to the Lesser Lord, had found that the modern dance differed from the original. Nilou begged her to teach the old version and went to a quiet spot in the desert, guided by Sethos, to practise. Her message never arrived because the Pari Rashnu fell asleep on the way. She apologised, kept rehearsing ("Yes, Master Sorush!"), and Candace called her dance the real star of the festival.
