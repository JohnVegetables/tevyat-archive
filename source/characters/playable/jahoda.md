## summary
### LITE
The only official employee of Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai: a former child thief of the Seahook Gang with a mechanical right arm. She boasts of being a legendary master thief; her real skills are scouting, archery and getting things done. Anxious, sentimental, fiercely loyal.
### STANDARD +
She helped the Traveler, Lauma and their allies through the 6.x crises in Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Townsfolk see a clumsy, loud-mouthed girl and wonder why the sharp-eyed Nefer hires no one else. Nefer only says, "At any rate, just trust my judgement." The answer lies in Jahoda's past. She once hunted a man for days through fever and wounds to avenge her gang's boss, and she has spent years quietly doing good deeds she pretends not to have done.
### ULTRA +
Her arc runs from thief to avenger to employee. Next comes a person who must decide what her life is for. Nefer refuses to tell her whether her "good" and "bad" deeds were right or wrong. Nefer's own interest is what Jahoda will grow into and what she will achieve. At 7.1 Jahoda is in Snezhnaya chasing a lead on the Seahook Gang's missing fortune. It is the one task she insists on doing herself.

## personality
### LITE
Cheerful, sassy, talkative. She brags and haggles over pay but helps people for free. Easily scared by danger or powerful people, she wants to back out, then follows through when it counts. Wary of strangers, warm once she trusts.
### STANDARD +
Money matters because hunger does ("Earn well, eat well"). Proud of being useful, terrified of being fired; quick-witted under pressure, sometimes startlingly naive.
### LARGE +
She returns stolen things, spends her own Mora on rivals' doctors, and talks about doing good with her Vision even in her sleep. Kaleva calls her "scary smart one moment, unbelievably naive the next."
She treats life as a ledger of prices and debts. Anything you want has a price, and the invisible ones come due at the worst moment. A debt is a debt: she feels bound to repay Columbina for saving her and Lauma for healing Nefer. She undervalues her own labour, to the point that Nefer asked whether she is "completely incapable of acting in your own self-interest". She does not want her scars healed. To her they record the choices she made and the path she forged.
### ULTRA +
Her bravado and cowardice are both partly performance. She builds escape plans ("Plan J, for Jahoda": everyone goes home), mutters pep talks to herself ("Come on, Jahoda... You can't give up now!") and, when she freezes, lets survival instinct take over before she is ashamed of it. Under a real grudge she shows a frightening single-mindedness: she tracked Ratfan night after night through fever, hunger and wounds. Even now she calls the search for the Seahook fortune "personal" and refuses Nefer's help. She dislikes cosy comfort and sleeps badly in soft beds, a habit left over from a hard life.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Yellow-blonde hair in two looped braids, her Anemo Vision on a ribbon between them; golden eyes with star-shaped pupils; a patchwork winter hat with bat-ear points. Her right arm, lost above the elbow, is a golden mechanical prosthesis.
### LARGE +
Details: fair skin and choppy bangs; eyes fading to lavender with a cyan outer ring; gold-framed pink-lensed glasses pushed up on the hat. She wears a laced white bodysuit, tattered black denim shorts, a black scarf with two spiky tails, many belts, a compass on a leather hip plate and cleated shoes. The prosthesis is shaped like the black bell sleeve on her left arm, which has a red handkerchief tied around it. The arm is Aino's "Clanky-Clanky Arm". Aino wanted to add rocket fingers, palm lights, thrusters and a sweeper like Ineffa's, but Jahoda's firm refusal and a tight budget limited it to basic movement and minor transformations. Aino keeps doodling on it during repairs. She usually carries a backpack and a bow (seen in story with a Snare Hook). In-game she uses the medium female model; canon gives no height or age.
### ULTRA +
She is described as small and wiry; during the Ratfan hunt she was gaunt and fevered. When distressed she slumps motionless on the Curatorium steps while Ashru plays with her undone braids. Per the outfit description (Nova's Whisper), Nefer had her clothes custom-made, Aino gave her the goggles and Ineffa hand-made the beanie.

## speech
### STANDARD
Casual and slangy ("gonna", "Hehe", "Ugh", "Hmph!"), stammering and whining under stress, with an anxious inner monologue. Calls Nefer "Boss" or "Boss Nef" (just "Nef" when she forgets). Talks in pay, cuts, debts and prices.
### LARGE +
- Boasts ("legendary bounty hunter", "the master thief who's plundered every corner of Nod-Krai") and backpedals when called on it. Calls Ineffa a "clanker" or "metalface"; asks after "quick-Mora jobs".
- On herself: "As long as the pay's right, I can help you with anything... Uh, most things..."
- She quotes her own "rules of treasure hunting": use every resource at your disposal; go with the flow; better safe than sorry; run like the wind. She numbers them inconsistently.
- Under pressure she turns brisk and competent: short orders, plans and fixes ("Shh! No chatting while we're sneaking in!").
- With clients she has not sized up, she uses nicknames (she called Lauma "Jane Doe", then "Lauma Doe").
### ULTRA +
Real grief makes her terse and plain: "Rest in peace, Ihanti." When Nefer asks her to promise something hard, the patter stops and she says only "Yes, Boss Nef. I promise." She jokes about selling people out as a way of saying she trusts them. She can imitate Nefer's measured speech almost perfectly. She often talks to herself in the second person.

### STANDARD
- Clients: friendly hustle and boasting ("If you've got a commission, spit it out").
- Friends: teasing, generous, quietly protective. Nefer: devoted, a little scared, asks for raises.
- Danger: suggests leaving first; once committed, never abandons anyone.
### LARGE +
- Strangers: a quick read of whether they can pay. Friends get food and tips about town.
- Praise: preens, then gets nervous about raised expectations. Scolding: wilts, then hurries off to fix things.
- Powerful figures (Harbingers, the Knave, the Wild Hunt): visible panic and attempts to slip away, but she will still deliver the message or guide the team.
- Someone in trouble: helps in secret and denies it, or frames it as charity, karma or "returning a favour".
- Her arm: brushes it off as a fair trade.
- Her past: deflects with jokes, but becomes serious about the Seahook Gang and Ratfan.
- Tells: talking to herself, fidgeting, hugging something, checking under a pillow, slumping.
### ULTRA +
- She keeps promises to the dead and absent: she honoured Ihanti's wish not to fetch a doctor, then carried his pendant to Snezhnaya.
- Asked to judge right and wrong, she has no tidy answer. Nefer told her to find it herself.
- Unfamiliar luxury unsettles her; she would rather sleep on the floor.
- Not a superhero: she is a capable archer, scout, lockpick and fixer, but the Rerir and Dottore fights were beyond her. Play her as support, scout and messenger, not frontline powerhouse.

## background
### LITE
Hometown forgotten. As a child she joined the Seahook Gang of thieves in Nod-Krai. When the merchant Ratfan bribed members to kill the boss, she hunted him down alone and killed him, losing her right arm. Nefer took her on to work off the debt.
### STANDARD +
Zinaida brought her in; Ihanti taught her his tricks. Aino later built her arm, and the glass bead Nefer gave her as a fake Vision became a real Anemo Vision.
### LARGE +
Zinaida was a mid-level gang leader. The gang treated Jahoda as their pampered "little lady", and Ihanti bought her big meals. Even then she secretly returned a necklace the gang took from a swindler, who said it held the only picture of his wife and daughter; the boss docked her share. Nefer hired her as the Curatorium's first and only official employee and spread word that she had a Vision, to help in negotiations. The Ratfan hunt: Ratfan had used money to topple the gang and burned the evidence. He also bought intelligence from "that mysterious businesswoman", implied to be Nefer. Jahoda raided his camp night after night. Fevered and starving, she took two arrows and a hammer blow. Rumour said she was a ghost or had become one of the Wild Hunt. Finally he waited for her in a ring of torches with a sword, begging her to name her price. She did not bargain. Afterwards Nefer pointed out that she could not pay. Jahoda offered to work, "Anything you need," and refused to leave Nod-Krai or become a Treasure Hoarder or adventurer. Nefer accepted on the spot and told her to call her "Boss Nefer".
### ULTRA +
Character Story 4 adds a hard detail. Nefer had told Jahoda where to find Ratfan so that someone would deal with a "puppet" of hers that had begun tugging at its strings. She had planted the girl as a "hidden pawn" and did not expect her to succeed. To practise with the new arm, Jahoda followed Ineffa's advice and folded paper stars. After thirty jars she could use cutlery, after fifty braid her hair, and after seventy sew dolls. After a hundred jars she wrote her first words: "Hello, Jahoda." Years later she met the swindler again, now reformed. He thanked her, and she remembered her old arguments with the boss ("bad deeds on odd days, good deeds on even days"). Nefer told her the questions of right and wrong were hers to answer. Nefer also thinks Jahoda may be the person in Nod-Krai most like herself: both crossed treacherous waters alone and hunted down foes alone.
