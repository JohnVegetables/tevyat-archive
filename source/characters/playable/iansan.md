## summary
### LITE
Head coach of the Collective of Plenty's fitness club and an unbeaten Pilgrimage champion, though no taller than a child. She bears the Ancient Name Uwezo ("Power") and credits all her strength to training and nutrition.
### STANDARD +
She coaches Varesa and runs an academy open to every tribe.
### ULTRA +
Across Chapter V (5.0-5.7) she is one of the six modern Ancient Name heroes: a 5.0 Pilgrimage victor, part of the team that pulled Kachina out of the Night Kingdom, one of the voices of the Ode of Resurrection ritual (5.1), leader of the relief effort at the Flower-Feather Clan (5.2) and the Traveler's patrol partner when the Abyss Order struck the tribes (5.7).

## personality
### LITE
Disciplined, upbeat and kind; trusts routine and science over heroics. Offers everyone a training or meal plan. Answers being underestimated with proof, not anger.
### STANDARD +
Counts calories and shuns fried or sugary food ("taste is a secondary concern"). Caring rather than cruel: she tailors every plan, and hates ego-lifting, shortcuts and being pitied or protected.
### LARGE +
Underneath the routine is real ambition. She admits she once loved chasing personal records, calls that urge "my own boulder and volcano", and has asked the Traveler to pull her back if it takes over again. She breaks problems into small steps: "when facing an impossible situation, you just need to act." She blames herself when students fail: she waived Kachina's fees because she felt her adverts had over-promised.
### ULTRA +

## background
### LITE
Her family's "Blood of Egungun" keeps them child-sized; they served the tribe as medics and cooks. When the Ancient Name "Power" chose her, many scoffed. Injuries led her to careful routine and nutrition, and she rose to head coach and Pilgrimage champion.
### STANDARD +
Acatl, then chief and head coach, told her fitness is a matter of choice, not credentials. Her mother's reminder that their family could treat injuries and cook food led her to plan her own meals, bringing the science of nutrition to Natlan.
### LARGE +
Her first try at a small barbell cracked bones and kept her in bed for weeks; afterwards the club built her a training corner of her own. Copying the club's pain-chasing style kept injuring her until she switched to a strict daily plan. After about a thousand days she returned to the founder's legendary barbell, found a Vision hanging on it, removed it and lifted the bar. Along the way she flipped a rampaging Tatankasaurus chief while shielding a little girl, and later succeeded Acatl as head coach. Rivals singled her out in her early Pilgrimages; on Mavuika's advice to prove her strength, she challenged the Archon herself, and no one underestimated her again. She went on to set an all-time Pilgrimage victory record.
### ULTRA +
Envious rivals then claimed the Blood of Egungun granted strength in exchange for height. Annoyed, Iansan moved the fitness club close to the Stadium of the Sacred Flame and recruited students from any tribe. Free classes got her reported as a scammer; on Mavuika's advice she charged a fee and the classes filled up. Within a year her first students were outstanding competitors. Her flyers now read: "You need not be born with power. Toil is what truly imparts strength." She has trained Kachina, coached Varesa and run tatankasaur-safety classes. Her saurian rival Emela died fighting the Abyss while Iansan was away.
