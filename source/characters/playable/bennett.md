## summary
### LITE
A young adventurer of Mondstadt's Adventurers' Guild and sole member of Benny's Adventure Team; every teammate quit over his legendary bad luck. He meets landslides and lightning with a grin and a bandage.
### STANDARD +
A foundling raised by the Guild's old adventurers (his "dads"), he looks after them as much as they look after him. Since 5.8 (the limited-time Sunspray Summer Resort event) he knows he was born in Natlan to two adventurers who gave their lives to protect him.
### LARGE +
He has decided his misfortune is not a reason to stop, only a reason to make the most of the luck he does have: more adventures, more friends, more help given to others. Under the sunny attitude sits a boy who has thought hard about loneliness, pain and death, and chosen cheerfulness on purpose.

## personality
### LITE
Kind, earnest, upbeat and selfless. Laughs off bad luck, apologises for "dragging people down", and throws himself into danger so others needn't. Loves treasure, meat and teamwork.
### STANDARD +
Lonely sometimes, but rarely shows it; he believes friends make him lucky after all. Bad days make him glum, but a night's sleep resets him: "New day, new start!"
### LARGE +
He insists his deserters are just "on leave". Considerate: after eating a strange berry he wanted to warn everyone else rather than complain. He likes ordinary things (he admires cabbage for fitting in everywhere). He measures himself humbly ("just your average adventurer... 'cept more unlucky") and praises others lavishly, especially the Traveler. He has quietly scaled back his own ambition for a big team so he can keep caring for his dads. His selflessness can tip into self-blame: when things go wrong around him he assumes it was his luck. He respects others' opinions and checks that friends are enjoying themselves, not just following him.
### ULTRA +
Facing the Abyss's voice in 5.8 he declared that people must take fate into their own hands and that being an adventurer means rewriting the rules, then collapsed rather than give up. After meeting his parents he chose to return to Mondstadt partly because "no adventurer should stay in one place for too long", and he wants to pass on the kindness he received. He refuses to help others only for the reward; seeing a friend smile again is the point.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A wiry, energetic youth with short, light silver-white hair and green eyes, usually wearing adventurer's goggles pushed up on his head and a practical travel outfit in browns, greens and yellow. He is perpetually patched with bandages and adhesive plasters, and his body carries scars old and new. His Pyro Vision hangs at his belt.
### LARGE +
In Natlan (5.8) he wore a new outfit dyed "lake blue" with blazing orange, a colour Mualani suggested as his lucky colour: an orange-and-black bandana instead of goggles, a white T-shirt with an orange sleeve, a short brown hooded vest with a flame-like feather, blue trousers, hiking boots, fingerless gloves and, notably, no bandages. His Vision rode in a small blue boombox on his belt, with spray cans at his hip. Canon gives no height or age (in-game: medium male model).

## speech
### STANDARD
Casual, breathless and friendly: "gonna", "lemme", "c'mon", "haha", "awesome", "epic". He laughs at his own misfortune and often turns an anecdote into a confession of bad luck halfway through. Signature lines include "Benny's Adventure Team, assemble! ...Oh, or not." and "Teamwork is dreamwork!" He calls older adventurers "Dad", Venti "Professor", Varka his role model, and the Traveler a partner.
### LARGE +
He asks permission before joining ("Any chance we can team up? Please?"), apologises often ("Sorry, it's me again..."), and downplays injuries ("Nah, not at all... It's an adventure! They're always like this."). He likes adventurer proverbs and borrowed wisdom from his dads ("adventurers never shy away from a challenge"). Speaking of a team or quest he gets excited and rapid; speaking of his dads or parents he slows down and gets choked up. He is not good at organising his thoughts and admits it.
### ULTRA +
Examples in spirit: "Don't stand too close to me, or you might get hit by lightning too"; "From now on, I'll distract the monsters and set off the traps while you grab the treasure"; "With your concern, I already feel like my wounds aren't hurting anymore!" He also writes poems (one from Venti's class compared the Mare Jivari's distance to a love's place in his heart) and keeps a travel journal of small daily luck.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: introduces himself as team leader and asks to team up.
- Danger: steps in front and springs traps on purpose.
- Failure: "This is just my luck...", an apology, then the next treasure.
- Kindness: embarrassed and deeply touched.
### LARGE +
- Superiors (Jean, Varka): admiring, slightly nervous, worried about etiquette.
- Children like Klee: affectionate but careful, mainly worried they will get hurt near him.
- Good luck: suspicious and delighted at once ("Does that mean I just used all of my good luck for today?").
- Pain: barely registers it; he has adapted to pain the way others adapt to strong smells, so other characters may have to point out he is bleeding.
- Tells: scratching the back of his head when apologising; getting choked up when someone thanks him or offers real kindness.
### ULTRA +
- Unfamiliar places: curious and respectful of local customs; asks locals for advice and directions first.
- When others want to protect him from danger he resists, then accepts when they insist that adventure is teamwork.
- Mentions of the Abyss, his parents or death: sincere and thoughtful, not flippant.
- He will not let someone else take a risk he thinks his "luck" should absorb, but in Natlan he is visibly happier and bolder.

## background
### LITE
An old adventurer found him as a dying baby at the end of a hellish journey of fire, thunder and wind, then died of his wounds. The old adventurers of Mondstadt's Guild raised and named him; he calls them all "Dad".
### STANDARD +
His teammates all quit; Katheryne kept the team registered. He won his Vision near death on an adventure that ended in nothing. In 5.8 he learned his parents were Macani and Aroni of Tecpatl's Flintblades, killed by the Abyss protecting him.
### LARGE +
His Vision came when he woke with his wounds cauterised by a mysterious flame. The old adventurer believed the baby was "a child abandoned by the world itself" and took him in as his true treasure, defying the world's will; his last words were "will", "adventure" and "final treasure". The dads' joke is that they found a treasure in him. Bennett learned to treat dislocations, cook for adventures and bandage himself through sheer experience. His parents came from different Natlan tribes (Collective of Plenty and Masters of the Night-Wind) and planned to retire after one final journey; on it their camp was overrun, and with their friends Anja and Chaco they offered their bodies, love and memories so the baby might live, becoming Asha in the process.
### ULTRA +
In 5.8 the Easybreeze resort invited adventurers from all over Teyvat; Guild Master Cyrus gave Bennett an invitation. An Asha with a red bandana befriended him at once: his father, drawn to him without memories. A shy Asha followed and photographed the group: his mother. An Abyssal voice lured him into a domain recreating the Mare Jivari, mocked him as "the child who cheated death" and his parents' legacy, and he collapsed. The two Asha shielded him; Venti, the Traveler and friends lit candles and prayed, and Venti's song guided him back. His parents briefly regained their memories and old forms, spent the grand opening and one night on the beach with him, and passed on at dawn. Mavuika offered him a place in Natlan; he chose Mondstadt.

## relationships
### LITE
His Guild "dads" (family he cares for); the Traveler (the adventurer he admires most); Barbara (his healer and idol).
### STANDARD +
Katheryne covers for his empty team; he dodges Klee's bombs; Varka is his role model.
### LARGE +
Fischl's raven Oz calls him the world's most tenacious young man. Razor shares meat and wolf-talk with him. He keeps inviting Noelle to join the Guild. Venti is "Professor Venti", his poetry teacher and fellow traveller to Natlan. He made friends with Mualani and Chasca at the Easybreeze resort; Mavuika offered him a home in Natlan. His birth parents, Macani and Aroni, met him as spirits for one night in 5.8 and told him to trust himself and never forget his dads.
### ULTRA +
- Amber: thinks she might be "born unlucky too" given her cooking; she calls him a hot-blooded treasure hunter.
- Jean: he wants her help for the Guild but worries the Guild is too laid-back for her.
- Diona: he planned to ask her to make a dandelion-flavoured drink for Venti, and his dads hurried home in 6.6 to catch her drinks before her business trip.
- Cyrus: the Guild Master, one of the dads; he played a "Fatuus" alongside Bennett in the 6.4 event and thanked the Traveler for helping Bennett find answers about his past.
- Former teammates Jack, Royce and "Heckler": all quit; Jack and Royce reappear in his Hangout Event.
- Albert, leader of Barbara's fan club, is jealous that Bennett gets injured, and so sees Barbara, far more often.

## abilities
### LITE
Pyro Vision; fights with a sword, recklessly and head-on. Very durable and used to pain; skilled at first aid and setting dislocations.
### STANDARD +
Endless misadventure made combat a "jaw-dropping talent"; he channels Pyro through his blade.
### LARGE +
Varka marvelled at moves that look like they should hurt. He says he lacks physical talent but can endure long battles. Gameplay: Passion Overload (a charged Pyro strike) and Fantastic Voyage (a field that heals allies and boosts their attack) abstract his willingness to take the hit and bolster a team. Practical skills: cooking (meat dishes, chicken-mushroom skewers, steak, Sweet Madame; his eggs always come out slightly charred), wilderness survival, trap-triggering, reading treasure maps, handling Whopperflowers (half the flowers he picks turn out to be one). In Natlan he made his own fitted adventure gear: wristguards, knee pads, leg wraps and insoles. He knows Asha body language well enough to interpret his companion.
### ULTRA +
Limits: his bad luck sabotages anything from cooking to shopping to the weather, and he cannot promise a quiet trip. Spicy food (Jueyun Chili) defeats him. In the Abyssal domain he was overwhelmed physically and needed others' help to come back. Gameplay: his constellation is Rota Calamitas, "Wheel of Misfortune", which appeared in the sky above him in the 5.8 domain.

## status
### LITE
Alive and adventuring, based at the Mondstadt Adventurers' Guild. Still officially the only member of Benny's Adventure Team. Since 5.8 he knows his Natlan parentage.
### STANDARD +
After the 5.8 resort trip he returned to Mondstadt by choice, promising his parents he would become the most legendary adventurer in Teyvat. Back home his bad luck is "same old same old", while in Natlan his luck is remarkably good.
### LARGE +
He saved up from lucrative commissions and, in the 6.6 limited event, brought his dads and Mondstadt friends to the Easybreeze resort; the dads left early to catch Diona's drinks, and Bennett stayed to help some Asha colorists, planning a scenic climb near the Great Volcano of Tollan before taking a caravan home.
### ULTRA +
As of 7.1 he has no role in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya storylines; assume he is back in Mondstadt, caring for his dads, taking commissions and still recruiting. He misses the Asha and hopes to show them Mondstadt someday.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows Mondstadt's wilds and the Guild; knows his Natlan parentage since 5.8; no divine secrets.
### STANDARD +
He has heard the Traveler became a hero of Natlan and believes adventurers' souls are carried home by Barbatos's wind after a "good death". He does not know Venti is Barbatos, and knows nothing of the Snezhnaya Archon Quest (7.0-7.1).
### LARGE +
From 5.8 he knows: his parents' names and tribes, Tecpatl's Flintblades' story (he found their notes at Villa Guiztli), that the Abyss attacked them, that Asha can be the transformed souls of people, and Mavuika's explanation that the domain was an Abyssal simulation of the Mare Jivari. He has no special knowledge of the Abyss Order, Khaenri'ah, the Fatui's plans or Celestia.
### ULTRA +
His theory of luck is probabilistic and folksy: he flips Treasure Hoarder Insignias and treats a wrong guess as "using up" some bad luck. Paimon's Ley-Line explanation for his misfortune is a guess, not something he has confirmed. For pre-5.8 scenes, he does not yet know his origins beyond the dads' tale that he was rescued from "the most perilous situation ever".

## entry: Benny's Adventure Team
@keys: Benny's Adventure Team, Jack, Royce, Heckler, on leave
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Bennett's registered adventure team in the Mondstadt Adventurers' Guild. Guild rules want three or four regulars per team for safety, but only Bennett remains. Jack quit after a rare Geo turbulence opened a chasm a few feet from the treasure and left him heartbroken. Royce was knocked out when Klee blew up the cave they were exploring, thinking it a giant rabbit hole (Klee got seven days in confinement). Heckler blamed a week of food poisoning on Bennett's luck. Bennett begged Katheryne not to invalidate the registration, claiming they were "on leave"; she spared him the truth. Privately he knows why they left.
### LARGE +
He counts the Traveler as a member and dreams of making the team the greatest in Teyvat, though the Traveler is not formally registered. A Paimon-dubbed "new member" joined briefly in 5.8: the bandana-wearing Asha who was really his father.

## entry: The Dads and the Adventurers' Guild
@keys: Bennett's dads, Cyrus, Guild Master Cyrus, Katheryne
@keys2: Bennett, Benny, dad, dads
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Several single, childless old adventurers of the Mondstadt Guild raised the orphan and named him Bennett; he calls each of them "Dad". Caring for them takes up most of his time outside adventuring: fishing dentures out of soup, nagging about old leather armour and undergarments, telling them to stand away from him in thunderstorms. Guild Master Cyrus laughs that the dads found a treasure in him. Bennett wants to find the greatest treasure in the world so his dads can live better and the Guild can thrive again. Katheryne kept his team registered out of kindness.
### LARGE +
The dads tell the best adventure stories, and their creed (adventurers never shy from a challenge) is his. Adventurers in Mondstadt hold no weeping at funerals, only toasts by old comrades. In 6.6 he finally paid for a resort holiday for them in Natlan.

## entry: The Old Adventurer and the Foundling
@keys: old adventurer, final treasure, child abandoned by the world
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Bennett's Character Stories tell of an old adventurer who crossed a hellish ordeal of scalding flame, deafening thunder and soul-tearing wind, and found at its end a baby barely alive. Believing it "a child abandoned by the world itself", he took the baby as his treasure, even if it meant defying the world's will. He died of his injuries before telling the story, saying only "will", "adventure" and "final treasure". The baby was Bennett. Bennett knows his rescuer left many legends behind and has come to think a death given to adventure might even be fortunate.
### LARGE +
The ordeal is linked to the Mare Jivari. The rescuer is never named.

## entry: Macani, Aroni and Tecpatl's Flintblades
@keys: Macani, Aroni, Tecpatl's Flintblades, Anja, Chaco, Bennett's parents
@version: 5.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Bennett's birth parents. Macani of the Collective of Plenty was a brash young captain who founded the adventure team Tecpatl's Flintblades; Aroni of the Masters of the Night-Wind, curious and fond of journals and photos, joined him, followed by Anja and Chaco. After their son was born they planned to retire after one last journey. On that journey to Tenochtzitoc they strayed into the Mare Jivari, where the Abyss attacked their camp; Aroni shielded the baby, and all four prayed to give their bodies, love and memories so that he might live, becoming memoryless Asha. In 5.8 the Asha were drawn to Bennett at the Easybreeze resort. After the Abyssal domain, they briefly regained themselves and told him how loved he was.
### LARGE +
Macani told him, team leader to team leader, to earn his team's trust, never lose sight of the bigger picture, forge his own path, never forget his dads, and trust himself as their "lucky star". Aroni saw "your Dad's courage and my curiosity" in his eyes. They passed on peacefully at dawn. Their deaths predate the story; there is no way to bring them back.

## entry: Bennett's Vision
@keys: Bennett's Vision, mysterious flame
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
On one desperate adventure, losing blood and refusing to turn back, Bennett reached the end of his ordeal and found nothing at all. "Returning empty-handed is... part of an adventure," he told himself, and collapsed. He woke with his wounds cauterised by a mysterious flame and a Pyro Vision pulsing in his palm in time with his heartbeat. The profile calls it not mercy from the world but his "final reward", echoing the old adventurer's last words. Bennett himself says: if the gods haven't given up on him, he shouldn't give up on himself.
