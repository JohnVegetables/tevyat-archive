## summary
### LITE
Chaac is a legendary Natlanese hero said to have been the first human to receive the primal fire, Phlogiston, from the Sage of the Stolen Flame. Stories call him brave, wise and fearless, though one fable calls him a fool.
### STANDARD +
He is said to have shared the fire with other humans, helped found the first tribe and, at the end of his life, lit a flame on a mountain whose answer was the birth of the first Wayob.
### ULTRA +
Both source texts are in-universe tales. Records of Hanan Pacha is labelled a fable that scholars treat as not historical.

## background
### LITE
In the oldest tales, humans wandered ignorant while dragons ruled the land and the Lord of the Night ruled the night realm. Chaac took Phlogiston from the Sage of the Stolen Flame and taught his fellow tribespeople to use it, which led to the first settlements and the first tribe.
### STANDARD +
Records of Hanan Pacha adds that Chaac, a human, accompanied the Sage to the red and black lands. They shot a vain winged dragon in the eye with a bamboo blowdart, then posed as doctors and replaced its eyes, fangs and claws with grainfruit. The Sage broke open the dragon's rainbow crystal, Pyrophosphorite, and gained Phlogiston. Together they raised land into the sky as Hanan Pacha, where Chaac passed the fire on and a tribe settled.
### LARGE +
The fable says the Sage urged the tribe to rise higher and eat nothing grown on the land, to escape the Lord of the Night's control. Chaac did not want to leave Natlan. The Lord of the Night, disguised as a tribesman, gave Chaac sweet water that was in fact alcohol; he shared it with everyone, so the tribe was drunk when the Sage returned. The Sage expelled them from Hanan Pacha and left Natlan. Chaac then followed the Lord of the Night's guidance and founded permanent tribes on the ground.
### ULTRA +
Myths of the Night Realm has a different ending beyond the shared outline. With the primal fire Chaac and his companions defeated a raging, wicked dragon and founded the first tribe. As his companions died one by one, his tribe scattered and no one sang of him. On his last night he climbed a high mountain, lit the first flame and called to his friends, and the god of the night realm answered with the birth-cry of the first Wayob.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; a legend of an age before the first Pyro Archon. He is remembered through the first Wayob and the stories of the fire.
### STANDARD +
No appearance or mention was found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content as of 7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
The Sage of the Stolen Flame is his teacher or partner in the theft and, in the fable, the clever foil to his foolishness. The Lord of the Night appears as the god who both tricked him and later guided him, and who answered his last call.
