## summary
### LITE
Froderock Fronkonsteen was a Fatui professor and subordinate of Il Dottore who commanded the Fatui Desert Expedition Team in the Desert of Hadramaveth, Sumeru. He studied the Eremite Tanit tribe and is thought to have been killed when Jeht broke free.
### STANDARD +
He is never met; the Traveler learns of him from soldiers' remarks and the expedition's notes.

## background
### LITE
His interest in the Eremites led him to take charge of the expedition to the desert, where the Fatui sought a way into the Eternal Oasis and he researched the Tanit.
### STANDARD +
His study of Tanit language and reading habits convinced him they were not poorly educated: they obtained experimental records discarded by the Akademiya and built a self-taught body of knowledge. Captain Zoya Snezhevna notes that he held overall command over the team, though she was irritated with him. A warehouse record shows he ordered capacitors, Fire-Water and sweet Fontaine wine, and that he could not function without wine.
### LARGE +
When Babel broke off cooperation, he demanded a live sample and his subordinates sought a way to capture a Tanit. Babel offered them Jeht, so the Fatui ambushed her at the spot where she had been told the Traveler was dealing with them. Froderock was excited about her health, and Lieutenant Ruslan went to find him when she needed to be freshened up.

## relationships
### LITE
He answered to Dottore and commanded Captain Zoya's company in the desert.
### STANDARD +
The wiki reads a Sumeru quest as indicating he defied the Doctor's orders, apparently from jealousy; this is not independently confirmed here.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead. The expedition was wiped out when Jeht broke out of her cage, killed soldiers and rampaged.
### STANDARD +
A fleeing Skirmisher asks where the Professor is; the other says he is in the cave and everywhere. Neither he nor the expedition is mentioned again.

### ULTRA +
Zoya's irritation with him shows in a readable in the Make Bright the Arrows quest, where she crossed out his suggestion to continue their research in the desert and replied with an implied threat, according to the wiki.
