## summary
### LITE
The "Master" of the secret Narzissenkreuz Ordo, about four hundred years before the main story: a made being holding the mind of the scholar Rene de Petrichor, meant to merge all Fontainians and escape a foretold apocalypse. The Traveler and Ann defeat him in In the Wake of Narcissus.
### STANDARD +
He is not a conventional villain. By his own account he never acted from selfishness, and the people he harmed were hurt by a plan that began as an attempt to save everyone. His followers knew him by a title ("Master") rather than a personal name. The name Narzissenkreuz is also that of the hero in a story (the hero Narzissenkreuz and the evil dragon) that Alain had Rene and his friends act out as children.

## background
### LITE
Rene and Jakob Ingold calculated a world-ruining flood and founded the Ordo to survive it by merging many wills. Rene volunteered as first subject and discarded his personality; the ritual failed and left him bodiless, so Jakob built a body and named it Narzissenkreuz.
### STANDARD
Rene and Jakob, who later joined the Institute of Natural Philosophy, drew the formula from data at ruins tied to Khaenri'ah: it predicted that the world's birthing waters would dry up and a great flood would follow. Jakob's body for the Master combined refined essence taken from Lyris, an Oceanid who had directed the Narzissenkreuz Institute, the corrupted remains of Carter Scherbius, and the pocketwatch Alain Guillotin had given Jakob, used as the cornerstone of the rite. Jakob made sure Narzissenkreuz would not remember the two previous failed attempts or the time they had cost. Narzissenkreuz believed the first attempt had worked, though he knew part of his life was missing, and held that this would not matter, since he would have all the time in the universe.
### LARGE +
The Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz, a ritual blade said to embody the concentrated wills of countless people, was part of the plan. As Master he led ceremonies with the Holy Blade and held radical views: according to Caterpillar, he believed that one's own will is an obstacle, and that accidentally receiving a Vision while seeking the path is a guaranteed path to destruction. He created Caterpillar by giving a hilichurl human intelligence and memory and teaching it speech. He sent Jakob and Lyris to four locations on a secret mission involving the Seal of Chymical Marriage. The Court of Fontaine came to treat the Ordo as an illegal extremist group. Agents sent to investigate it were dissolved and absorbed; three had been taken by the time of a late page, among them the Champion Duelist Marfisa and Emanuel Guillotin, and he gained their memories and skills. He then used the Book of Revealing to show Alain the coming apocalypse and asked him to join. Alain refused, saying it seemed there was no going back. In his notes the Master dismissed Alain as a genius only with machines, but wrote that he would create a future with a place for Alain and Mary-Ann.
### ULTRA +
The conflict ended when the Marechaussee Hunters, including Alain, Mary-Ann and Seymour, defeated the Ordo and its followers among the remnants of Elynas. An allegorical artifact text (Fell Dragon's Monocle) has the defeated "evil dragon", read as Rene, tell the "hero", read as Alain, before falling into the chasm that dissolves all things, that he will surely return to save all souls and be reborn as a new universe. Caterpillar meanwhile had failed a task and been confined in the Fortress of Meropide. The Ordo's tower was sealed by the authorities and the Master remained beneath it, in the Primordial Sea. In Rowboat's Wake he says that during the first ten years he focused all his power on keeping his will from dissipating, and that he then used that will to build a universe within himself to resist his fate.

## status
@version: 4.2
### LITE
In Rowboat's Wake (4.2) he guided the Traveler and Ann to Annapausis, was recognised by Mary-Ann as Rene, then, his power spent, said farewell and was left to rest there. Nothing later is recorded as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
After his defeat in the Primordial Sea (Waking from the Great Dream) he lost control of it and called his work an empty dream, wishing to return to the time before the Narzissenkreuz heroes set out. He agreed to help Ann.

## relationships
### LITE
Jakob made him and serves him. Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin were Rene's friends. Ann forgave him.
### STANDARD +
Alain stayed estranged, though he and Rene had once been close; Narzissenkreuz says he hoped for a place for Alain and Mary-Ann in his future. Mary-Ann, on learning who he was, said she could not yet forgive "Narzissenkreuz" but could forgive "Rene". Caterpillar calls him "Master" with some irony and tells him he finds treating life as a toy despicable, yet still describes him as an absolutely reliable guide. Seymour, Alain's creation, he once saw as a possible achievement beyond ancient golems.
### LARGE +
Lyris was brought into the Ordo and her essence used as the base of his body; he speaks of her as someone who clings to Mary-Ann without understanding what happened. The Traveler is for him a "variable" who changed his world-formula. He half-offered Ann his legacy, and she declined at once.

## abilities
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
In the Primordial Sea he fights with a technique he calls the "Rain of Blades". He could dissolve enemies and absorb their consciousnesses, taking their memories, skills and intelligence, and he could give a hilichurl human reason. After his defeat he still coached Ann to reshape and drain the waters of Annapausis. His grip on the Primordial Sea failed once the reforged Holy Blade cut through the surrounding will.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The wiki lists three forms for the character: Hydro Tulpa, Hydro Eidolon and human. In the Primordial Sea he is a giant being, and in the final quests a dwindling presence rather than a solid figure.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not treat him as simply Rene: Rene asked to be reborn without his old personality, and Mary-Ann separates the two names. Do not claim he is dead or a god. Do not confuse him with the hero of the childhood story; that is a separate tale Alain had the friends act out, and Jakob recalls that Rene used to play the evil dragon in it.

## entry: Rene and the Ritual of Rebirth
@keys: Rene de Petrichor, ritual of rebirth
@keys2: Narzissenkreuz, Ordo
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Rene chose to be the "first subject" and have his personality removed so that a purer being might emerge. The ritual used the Holy Blade and part of Lyris's essence but left him bodiless; Jakob then built the body that carried his mind and called it Narzissenkreuz.

## entry: The World-Formula
@keys: world-formula, Doomsday Clock, second deluge
@keys2: Narzissenkreuz, Rene
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
The calculation predicts that the world's birthing waters will run dry and that a flood hundreds of years later will ruin the world. Jakob describes the apocalypse as the second deluge, which the wiki equates with the Prophecy of Fontaine. Narzissenkreuz is credited with using it to make the Doomsday Clock. The Traveler's arrival changed the values in it.

## entry: Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz
@keys: Holy Blade, Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz
@keys2: Narzissenkreuz, Ordo, Jakob
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
In Search in the Algae Sea the Traveler, Ann, Seymour and Caterpillar reforge it before Jakob can. It cuts through wills born of water; in Rowboat's Wake it is used on the barrier at the real Annapausis, after which Narzissenkreuz says its purpose is fulfilled.

## entry: Waking from the Great Dream
@keys: Waking from the Great Dream, Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe
@keys2: Narzissenkreuz, Primordial Sea
@version: 4.2
### LARGE
The fight takes place in the Primordial Sea. Narzissenkreuz demands the Holy Blade and says he will save as many as he can even at the cost of his life force. He calls the Traveler someone equal to a world, then admits defeat. Ann tells him she is willing to forgive him, and he apologises.

## entry: Caterpillar
@keys: Caterpillar
@keys2: Narzissenkreuz, Ordo, Master
@version: 4.2
### LARGE
Caterpillar was a hilichurl rogue given human intelligence and speech by the Master, then confined in the Fortress of Meropide after a failed task. Earlier, Jakob's Hydro Eidolons had held a humanoid form, and one sentient being lived only 32 days before dissolving.

## entry: Annapausis and Mary-Ann

@keys2: Narzissenkreuz, Rene, Mary-Ann

In Annapausis, a dream-like place held together by Mary-Ann's will, he steps forward and admits who he is. Mary-Ann forgives Rene, withholds forgiveness from Narzissenkreuz, and invites him to travel. He declines, saying there is still something he must do.
