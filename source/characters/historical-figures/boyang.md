## summary
### LITE
Boyang was a thaumaturge who fought beside the Millelith against the monsters pouring out of the Chasm during the Cataclysm, about five hundred years ago. He and his younger brother Rongzhao, both ancestors of Yelan, carried a space-bending catalyst called the Fantastic Compass. Boyang stayed in the Chasm and never returned, and is known today from a note he left behind.
### STANDARD +
Without a Vision, he relied on ritual and the compass. The Traveler's party learned his story in the Interlude Chapter quest At Tunnel's End, Light (version 2.7).

## background
### LITE
Two brothers, Boyang and Rongzhao, were given the Fantastic Compass by someone of great importance and brought it to the Chasm to join the war against the monsters of Khaenri'ah. Boyang fought alongside the Millelith and the rogue yaksha Bosacius.
### STANDARD +
They devised a plan to trap the beasts in the depths of the Chasm and seal them there with the compass. The device could only work when a mortal and an adeptus fed it power together, so Boyang and Bosacius stayed inside with the monsters and sealed themselves in; Rongzhao helped close the domain from outside. Boyang's last message was left in the Note of Unknown Origin, which tells that he remained there for good.
### LARGE +
Cutscene text in the quest says Bosacius, who had lost his memory and mind, often mistook Boyang for his old comrades. As time passed Boyang understood that he could never rejoin his family and that his only companion was nearing death. The sealed space, which draws on the memories of those within it, held echoes of the two men; Yelan, Xiao and Yanfei used the compass again by joining a mortal's and an adeptus's power to escape.

## status
### LITE
Dead for roughly five hundred years; he died or was lost in the sealed space beneath the Chasm. Nothing further about him appears in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content found.
### STANDARD +
His descendant Yelan investigated his fate during the Interlude Chapter, and Xiao later said that one more person would remember Bosacius for having seen what happened to them.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Rongzhao was his younger brother, who survived but lost his reason, which Yelan and Xiao ascribe to Bosacius's karma. Bosacius was the yaksha he shared the seal and his last days with. Yelan is his descendant.
### LARGE +
The brothers' sacrifice and madness are why Yelan's family history is tied to the Chasm; Xiao draws a parallel between Bosacius's end and his own burden of karma.
### ULTRA +
Yelan notes that the compass recorded the voices of Bosacius and her ancestor making the seal together, invoking the gods of the five regions with yaksha and mortal taking the device in hand. Do not give Boyang a personality or dialogue beyond this: canon shows only his deeds and a few recorded words.

## entry: Fantastic Compass
@keys: Fantastic Compass
@version: 2.7
### STANDARD
A catalyst able to manipulate space, given to Boyang and Rongzhao and used to seal the Chasm's monsters beneath the ground. It needed a mortal and an adeptus to channel power at once; the Traveler's party reactivated it that way to escape the sealed space, and expected it to vanish when time resumed.
