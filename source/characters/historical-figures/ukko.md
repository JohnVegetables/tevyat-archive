## summary
### LITE
Ukko is a figure from the final days of Sal Vindagnyr, the civilization of Dragonspine. The Princess' Box text names Ukko as someone the dying princess felt sorry to have seen her in her state. Ukko is probably the scribe whose bitter words fill the Scribe's Box and who recorded the fall in the Ancient Carvings, and the last survivor.
### STANDARD +
Canon never states Ukko's role directly; the scribe identification is an inference.

## background
### LITE
Nothing is given about Ukko's origin or life before the disaster of the Skyfrost Nail, beyond being among the people of Sal Vindagnyr and close to its princess.
### STANDARD +
As the people died, Ukko stayed. Inference from the Ancient Carvings and related item text: the princess died trying to graft a branch of the shattered Frostbearing Tree onto a healthy one, and Ukko found and buried her.

## personality
### LITE
Protective, grieving and bitter. Ukko blames Imunlaukr as the outsider who abandoned the princess, and wishes the world ruined.
### STANDARD +
The Scribe's Box wishes the frosty skies to burn, that all might turn to dust on the wind to find the outsider, and that the black dragon of the princess' dreams would cover the land in scarlet poison. It ends with Ukko as the last one with no need to keep watch, and with hope for a new nation without gods.

## relationships
### LITE
The Princess of Sal Vindagnyr, whom Ukko attended and probably buried; Imunlaukr, whom Ukko blames.
### STANDARD +
Ukko is unaware that Imunlaukr kept seeking a way to end the disaster, according to other artifact text; that is Ukko's mistaken belief.

## status
### LITE
Dead for thousands of years, last survivor of Sal Vindagnyr. A Frostarm Lawachurl bearing the name Ukko can be fought on Dragonspine, but canon does not confirm it is the same person. No connection to 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content found.
### STANDARD +
The Scribe's Box mention of Khaenri'ah, a new nation without gods, is among the oldest in-game references to it.

## chronology
### LARGE
Ordered by inference from the item texts: the Skyfrost Nail disaster ends Sal Vindagnyr; the princess attempts to graft a branch onto a healthy tree and dies of cold; Ukko finds and buries her; Ukko, last survivor, records the fall in carvings and voices bitterness in the Scribe's Box. No dates are given beyond thousands of years ago.
### ULTRA +
The Princess' Box and Snow-Tombed Starsilver share part of her monologue, which marks it as her last words. The Scribe's Box mentions the black dragon of the princess' dreams, which the wiki links to Durin. Ukko learned of people building a nation without gods, an early in-game nod to Khaenri'ah.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not state Ukko's gender, title or the scribe role as certain, and do not confirm the Lawachurl link. Ukko did not know that Imunlaukr kept trying to stop the disaster.
