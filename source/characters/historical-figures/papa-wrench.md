## summary
### LITE
Papa Wrench was the name taken by Mr. Shesternin Sr., a Snezhnayan who lost his memory and drifted through Nod-Krai with a caravan before settling by Blue Amber Lake on Lempo Isle. A gentle, generous mechanic who charged little or nothing for repairs, he was Aino's teacher. He is dead by the time of the story and appears only through other people's accounts.
### STANDARD +
He never appears in person. His workshop, built from an underground warehouse beneath Blue Amber Lake, and the machines he made for a sick child remain as his legacy; they are the setting of the world quest The Tale-Telling Heart.

## background
### LITE
Father of Danyusha Shesternin and father-in-law of Zhorik. Ten years before the quest he vanished from the ship Fortunate One and was presumed lost overboard. He survived with amnesia, took the name Papa Wrench, travelled Nod-Krai with a caravan, and settled by Blue Amber Lake as a mechanic.
### STANDARD +
The caravan leader looked after him, and the caravan's children loved the endless stories he told. When a girl named Tinet, who was ill and temperamental, could no longer travel, the caravan camped at Blue Amber Lake and he stayed behind to care for her. There he found an underground warehouse containing the Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask, one of the Proofs of the Cognoscenti, and converted it into a workshop. He built mechanical devices to cheer Tinet up, and the children were allowed to play with the mask. Tinet did not survive; he later died of chronic illness and grief.
### LARGE +
His collection also held a lavish goblet that Tolfin believes the caravan leader brought from Snezhnograd; he never could bring himself to use it. Tolfin, who met him through dealings with the caravan, remembers him looking strange on seeing his own obituary and insisting he could not recall the details of ten years earlier. He left the workshop and belongings to Tolfin as Papa Wrench, but as Mr. Shesternin Sr. his family had a legal claim to the estate, which is why Tolfin lured Danyusha and Zhorik to the lake with the promise of a Proof of the Cognoscenti.

## personality
### LITE
Remembered as kind, patient and charitable: he charged only token fees for building or repairing machines and often worked free. He was good with children, a natural storyteller, and warmly encouraging toward people with big ideas.

## relationships
### LITE
Aino was his student and he took her ideas seriously. Verna claims to be his apprentice. Tolfin was his friend and heir. Danyusha is his daughter and Zhorik his son-in-law, who in The Tale-Telling Heart is revealed to have pushed him off the ship.
### STANDARD +
Tolfin, who had set up the confrontation as a test for Zhorik, let him go afterwards, saying he would have forgiven him had he changed, as Papa Wrench wished. Tinet, the sick child he stayed for, was the reason for his workshop. Aino remembers him patting her head and calling her ideas wonderful; the Clink Clank Clash event story says an acclaimed teacher recognised her passion as genius.

### ULTRA +
Verna's claim to be his direct apprentice comes from the world quest For a Green Island..., where she boasts of it while insisting she is the finest mechanic on Hiisi Island; it is her own boast and no other source confirms it. The Aino material is separate: her voice-over describes how he rubbed her head and said he could not wait to see her finished projects, and the Clink Clank Clash story (a limited-time event in Version 6.0) has Ineffa retell that an acclaimed teacher found Aino and recognised her genius. Both sources speak of him as her mentor, not as a relative.

## abilities
### LITE
A skilled mechanic and tinkerer who, even after losing all personal memory, still remembered every specification of machine parts (per Aino).
### STANDARD +
He built mechanical devices in his workshop beneath Blue Amber Lake, including contraptions meant to bring joy to Tinet.

## status
### LITE
Dead. He died before the events of Version 6.0, of chronic illness and grief following Tinet's death. As of 7.1 nothing further is told about him.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Wiki description: blue eyes; in his later years short gray hair, a mustache and a chinstrap beard.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
He is a historical figure, not a speaking character. After his amnesia he had no memory of being Mr. Shesternin Sr., of his family, or of the ship.

## chronology
### LARGE
- About ten years before The Tale-Telling Heart: aboard the Fortunate One he vanishes; Zhorik and Chief Stalanov are also on the ship.
- Afterwards: he survives with amnesia, becomes Papa Wrench, and travels with a caravan across Nod-Krai.
- The caravan camps at Blue Amber Lake because of Tinet's illness; he finds the underground warehouse and the Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask.
- Later: Tinet dies, then he dies as well.
- Version 6.0: his story is told in The Tale-Telling Heart; Aino and Verna reference him.

### STANDARD
The workshop under Blue Amber Lake began as an underground warehouse. Papa Wrench converted it and filled it with machines built to cheer Tinet. It also held the Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask, one of the Proofs of the Cognoscenti, and a lavish goblet. In The Tale-Telling Heart Tolfin guides the Traveler through it with Huginn.

## entry: Fortunate One incident
@keys: Fortunate One, Zhorik Shesternin, Danyusha Shesternin
@keys2: Papa Wrench, Stalanov, overboard

### STANDARD
Ten years before the quest, Mr. Shesternin Sr. disappeared on the ship Fortunate One, where Zhorik and Police Chief Stalanov were also passengers. Zhorik acted suspiciously but there was no hard evidence, so it was treated as an accident. The Tale-Telling Heart shows that Zhorik pushed him overboard to gain the Shesternin wealth.
