## summary
### LITE
Kitain Hideie was the last Prefect of Yashiori, last of the old Kitain Clan of Yashiori Island. Inazuma remembers a Kitain hero who was lost in the cataclysm; his own letter reveals that he lost his nerve in the fighting, deserted his men and the clan's cross-shaped spear, and spent the rest of his life in guilty exile.
### STANDARD +
He died of old age and exhaustion in the Veluriyam Mirage, where the domain's keeper let him see his battlefield one last time. All of this is known from limited-time event material (Secret Summer Paradise, 3.8), so ordinary Inazumans do not know it.

## background
### LITE
His mother, once a shrine maiden, gave him the Shinrou Casket from Mouun Shrine when he was summoned far abroad as Prefect of Yashiori, hoping it would ease the pain of battle. He boasted that as heir to the Kitain name he would never need it.
### STANDARD +
Kitain was a family that hunted tatarigami and served as Prefects of Yashiori for generations, and the Kitain Cross Spear was made by an ancestor for his own style of spearmanship. Hideie carried the casket to the battlefield of the cataclysm, about 500 years ago, where he followed the Raiden Shogun. His letter describes burning skies, warped monsters, dark hounds and a friend who rescued him from them and then sank into the mire, still looking at him. He dropped the spear, left the troops and fled like one driven mad.
### LARGE +
He never returned to Yashiori, ashamed of having betrayed the Shogun's trust and the name of his ancestors. He wandered abroad as a fugitive, tormented by nightmares of that field and finding relief only in the illusions of the Shinrou Casket. When he was old he heard of a domain that could grant wishes, crossed the desert to the Veluriyam Mirage and begged its keeper Idyia to send him back to that day to save his friend and follow the Shogun to the end. He had misjudged what the mirage could do. Idyia saw he was gravely ill and recreated the battlefield from his mind in the bottle instead; he died looking at it, smiling.
### ULTRA +
His testament, left in Silver Bottle Courtyard, speaks of finally returning to the place he dreamed of on countless nights. Idyia later told the Traveler she did not know why a man would wish to see so frightening a scene in his last moments, and that the casket helped restart the domain's central hub, so she owed him thanks.

## status
### LITE
Dead. Public memory says he fell in the cataclysm; in fact he deserted and died in exile in the Veluriyam Mirage.
### STANDARD +
The Shinrou Casket was lost with him inside the mirage, and the pearl that should have sensed it went dark; In the 3.8 event the casket is found again and helps restart the domain's central hub. The Kitain Clan line ended; the wiki suggests his desertion is the cause, but canon does not say so.
### ULTRA +
Orobashi's Legacy has Kaji lament that the Kitain who destroyed so many evils will never return, and the quest rewards the diagram for the Kitain Cross Spear. It implies the family died out for reasons the world does not know.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His friend on the battlefield and his mother, the former shrine maiden, mattered most to him; neither is named. He considered the Raiden Shogun the sovereign he betrayed. Kitain Bunsou was his ancestor.
### LARGE +
No living person appears in his story. The only contact he had in his last days was Idyia, who looked after him in his final hours and who in the event story is grateful to him for the casket.

## entry: Absolution-Seeker's Testament
@keys: Absolution-Seeker's Testament, Testament of Kitain Hideie
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A fragile letter found in Silver Bottle Courtyard, Veluriyam Mirage, during the Secret Summer Paradise event. In it Kitain Hideie confesses that he fled the cataclysm battlefield and abandoned the cross-shaped spear and his troops, and that he has come to the mirage to atone and finally follow the Shogun.

## entry: Shinrou Casket
@keys: Shinrou Casket
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A relic kept at Mouun Shrine that Hideie's mother sent with him to the war. Kokomi says it listens to the prayers of those who come to it, builds up shinki, and draws its power from that. Hideie used its illusions as comfort in exile. It was lost in the Veluriyam Mirage until the 3.8 event, when it helped restart the domain's central hub.

## entry: Kitain Cross Spear
@keys: Kitain Cross Spear
### STANDARD
A four-star polearm whose weighty cross shape was made by Kitain Bunsou for his own style. Its text says the Kitain were once demon hunters, hunters of Tatarigami, and Prefects of Yashiori for many generations. Players forge it from a diagram earned in the world quest Orobashi's Legacy: Part V.
