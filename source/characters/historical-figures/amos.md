## summary
### LITE
Amos was a huntress of Old Mondstadt who became the favoured executor and lover of its tyrant god Decarabian. She later joined the rebellion against him and died in the fight about 2,600 years ago. Amos' Bow is named after her.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through weapon and artifact text and a short cutscene, so her story comes with poetic framing. She is dead and does not appear in any present-day quest.

## background
### LITE
Her clan offered her to Decarabian, and her skill with the bow won her a place as his favoured counsellor. She served him as executor, dealing with enemies, rebels and prisoners. Having known neither love nor hate before him, she took his harshness as his form of love and showed the same to others.
### STANDARD +
Artifact text says she once roosted with owls and ran with hawks on the plains before she was handed over, and that the Lord of Wind raised her to his side because of her bow. As she sought his love she came to see that he did not love her as he loved his subjects. Her weapon text imagines her dreaming of ocean waves, forests and boars in berry bushes, words he never heard.

She joined the rebellion that the Nameless Bard led, with the wind spirit and the knight Gunnhildr. The Minnesang circlet says she did it to claim his gaze one last time; the Dawn's Brilliant Oath, by contrast, imagines she had been forced to obey him and longed for a gentler dawn. She scaled his tower and loosed an arrow at him while his winds tore at her.
### LARGE +
Heldenepos's Unspoken Tale describes her as taciturn and has her final arrow break through his wind-wall as she died. The Dawn's Brilliant Oath calls her ineloquent by nature but true of heart, supposes that a few soft words from the bard turned her from the tyrant. The passage that follows says 'the silent warrior' cast aside duty and name and gathered the scattered winds for the boy; the plume is described as a guard's who gave up his name, so that line most likely refers to the Red-Haired Warrior and should not be attributed to Amos. The passage is a guess by its narrator, not a record.

Her bow, used for hunting boars as well as her attack on the god, outlasted her and became the legendary Amos' Bow. The wiki notes that she appears to draw her bow with her left hand in the cutscene, an observation from the video rather than a statement in any text.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Her long white hair is the only detail the cutscene gives; artifact text adds a jade and crystal ornament in her pale braids, a gift from the Lord of Storm. She was barefoot on snow in the weapon's imagery.
### LARGE +
Amos' Bow's text calls her a maiden following the Lord of the Tower's footsteps, and the Minnesang circlet describes wine in golden cups and jade on pale braids once she was at court, with silver 'falling like snow' about her feet in place of the splinters of silver snow she had walked on barefoot. Dawn's Brilliant Oath calls her an iron-cold huntress, grim of tongue. No text gives her age, height or eye colour.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 2,600 years, killed in the rebellion against Decarabian. Her bow survives as a weapon.
### STANDARD +
She does not appear in any quest as a living character and, as far as checked, is not mentioned in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 story content beyond item texts. Her burial and any remains are not described.
### ULTRA +
Her legacy is the bow and three artifact texts: Amos' Bow (1.0) and, from the 6.3 artifact set A Day Carved From Rising Winds, the Minnesang of Love and Lament, Heldenepos's Unspoken Tale and Dawn's Brilliant Oath. She also appears as a character in the Genius Invokation TCG (an Arcane Legend event card, Day of Resistance: Moment of Shattered Dreams, lists her among its featured figures).

## relationships
### STANDARD
Decarabian was both her devotion and her undoing: she gave him blind loyalty, he gave her nothing she recognised as love, and the two struck at each other at the end. The Nameless Bard, the wind spirit and Gunnhildr were her comrades in the rebellion.
### LARGE +
Freedom-Sworn's text lists the group that stood before the tower as a youth, a spirit, an archer, a knight and a wandering flame-haired warrior; the archer is read as Amos and the knight as Gunnhildr. The Minnesang circlet shows her devotion in deeds (it says she pierced throats in his dungeons and razed rebel villages and whispered counsel in his ear) and says that none of it won her the love he gave his commoners. In the circlet's closing image the god and the huntress tore each other apart without ever knowing each other's heart.
### ULTRA +
The same circlet text has her serve in his tower while a craftsman who offered Decarabian a golden singing bird for his life had both hands cut off, so that no such toy could be made again; it is cited here as an example of the court she served, not as an act the text assigns to her. Her relations with the wind spirit and the boy are not described in any text beyond their shared place among the rebels, so do not invent conversations between them.

## entry: Amos' Bow
@keys: Amos' Bow, Amos's Bow
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A five-star bow said to draw power from everything in the world, stronger the further its wielder is from what the heart desires. The text ties it to Amos's hunt of Decarabian and to a love that went unanswered.
### LARGE +
The bow's lore line says Amos originally wielded it to hunt boars and used it in her attempt to slay Decarabian. Its description has her follow the Lord of the Tower barefoot in the snow, tell him in a soft voice of dreams of ocean, sand, forests and boars in berry bushes, and, at the end of the Archon War, climb the spire in company of a nameless young man, an 'elf' (the wind spirit) and a knight, saying 'Finally, I shall hold his gaze.' It was not until her arrow flew that she realised how far apart they were. The knight in the text is unidentified (the Red-Haired Warrior was later separated as a fifth rebel, so Gunnhildr is the likelier reading).

## entry: A Day Carved From Rising Winds
@keys: A Day Carved From Rising Winds, Minnesang of Love and Lament, Heldenepos's Unspoken Tale, Dawn's Brilliant Oath
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
This artifact set's texts retell the rebellion against Decarabian. They give the most detail on Amos's inner life but disagree on her motives, so state who says what.
### LARGE +
Minnesang of Love and Lament (circlet) is the huntress's own tragedy of blind devotion and what she hoped to win by dying in his sight. Heldenepos's Unspoken Tale (goblet) gives the battle's end from outside: the taciturn archer's last arrow breaks the wind-wall, Decarabian falls, a final storm threatens, and the boy walks into it playing the lyre. Dawn's Brilliant Oath (plume) is mostly about the Red-Haired Warrior and the boy's song and mentions Amos only as the god's most favoured confidant, speculating about her heart.
