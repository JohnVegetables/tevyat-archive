## summary
### LITE
Cochanina was an ancient Pyro Archon of Natlan, remembered as the surfing champion of the People of the Springs. She is best known for driving the Volcano Lord out of her tribe's land with a trick, and her last known act was to help contain the Bakunawa in the Mare Jivari.
### STANDARD +
Both stories are told in the same period of Natlan's long tradition of mortal Archons. She is not a living figure: she appears only in tales and in a surviving note, and as one of the voices the Abyssal Gosoythoth mimicked.

## background
### LITE
Cochanina is described as a kind, fun-loving mortal who became Pyro Archon. When the Volcano Lord fouled the springs of her tribe, she went unarmed with only a surfboard, lured him into a race to a "treasure" in the sea, outsurfed him, and tossed a pufferfish as a decoy. He charged it, fell off his board and never again troubled the People of the Springs.
### STANDARD +
The elder of the People of the Springs came to her because the Volcano Lord had dumped his filth into the springs and driven away the Flowcurrent Birds; she left her renowned blade Unu Pachacuti at home. She flattered him into the race, and the pufferfish struck him as he lunged; he made it ashore clinging to reeds.
The Volcano Lord, a huge black and purple salamander-like creature, had taken the empty Great Volcano of Tollan while the older dragons were away and had been beaten by earlier Pyro Archons before, only to slip back below. She did not drive him out of Natlan entirely. She baited him with the supposed Iridescent Chalice of the hero Uenuku, the tribe's legendary treasure, which he could only reach by surfing.
### LARGE +
Later she was one of the Pyro Archons who contained the Bakunawa, a creation of Rhinedottir, in the Mare Jivari. A weathered note written in her name in the Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc quest (5.8) speaks of sealing the borders and containing the Mare Jivari as a task "not for just one generation", says her time runs short, and asks that her thoughts be put into the Sacred Flame. A newer note beside it, from the Pyro Archon Isaja, says the task is complete.
### ULTRA +
In the Chapter V finale Where All Hopes Lie (5.3), the Abyssal Gosoythoth took the voices of Cochanina, Ndenge, Xbalanque and Xumucul. Its lines through her voice were bleak: the flame has been seen dozens of times and, once the wood is spent, night returns, and "why do you struggle when you are so weary?" These are the monster's mimicry and not her recorded views.

## status
### LITE
Dead for centuries. She left a note asking that her thoughts be put into the Sacred Flame, and the Pyro Archons after her kept up the containment work in the Mare Jivari until the task was declared complete in a later note.

## relationships
### STANDARD
The **People of the Springs** (Meztli) remember her as their surfing champion and defender. **Gosoythoth** echoed her voice in its last fight with the Traveler and Mavuika. She is linked to **Mavuika** and the rest of the Pyro Archons only through the office and the long containment of the Bakunawa; canon shows no meeting.

## entry: The Volcano Lord Ousted

@keys2: Surf's Up, surfing, pufferfish, Springs
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the weapon story of Surf's Up, the Volcano Lord, bored in the dark volcano, decides to wash his possessions in the cleanest springs in Natlan, those of the People of the Springs, fouling them with smoke and filth. Cochanina visits him unarmed, tells him that the treasure of Uenuku lies under the sea, and challenges him to surf to it. As he flounders in her wake she throws a drifting pufferfish and cries that it is the key. He chases it, is stung, and tumbles off. The prize for the real First All-Tribe Surfing Championship, the Surf's Up catalyst, is held for safekeeping at the Leisurely Puffer by Mualani.

## entry: The Bakunawa Containment

@keys2: Cochanina, Mare Jivari, Pyro Archons, Isaja
@version: 5.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Several Pyro Archons, including Cochanina, Shulevitz, Gloharry and Isaja, are recorded as working to contain the Bakunawa in the Mare Jivari; a temple was built to hold it. Cochanina's note, found in the Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc quest, calls it a multi-generation task, warns that her time is short, and entrusts her thoughts to the Sacred Flame. Isaja's note announces that the task is finished.
