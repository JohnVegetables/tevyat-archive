## summary
### LITE
Lovia was the last Archpriestess of the Frostmoon Scions, the Nod-Krai people descended from exiles of Hyperborea. About five hundred years ago she tried to force the birth of a Sacred Heir through murder and outside alliances, was killed by her own granddaughter Aila, and was struck from the record for blasphemy.
### STANDARD +
She survives only as a cautionary figure. Only priestess-rank Scions are meant to know her name.

## background
### LITE
An orphan from outside the Scions, though she never knew it, adopted by the previous Archpriestess. She took the staff during a period of waning faith and at first led through generosity and self-sacrifice. After her adoptive sister died shielding her, she devoted herself to the Sacred Heir Project at any cost.
### STANDARD +
When the Maiden chosen to bear the heir eloped with a Snezhnayan officer, Lovia asked Rotwang to retrieve her; the officer died and the Maiden took her own life. Lovia then raised the twin girls Alia and Aila in seclusion as her granddaughters and instructed them to kill other Scions and drink their blood to raise their purity.
### LARGE +
The Scions' moonlight blessing (kuuvahki) was dwindling and their rites were losing power, and the project was believed to restore Hyperborea's glory. Lovia told Aila she must never wed an outlander. Alia later left Hiisi Island; Aila, now the sole focus of Lovia's plans, deeply resented it.
### ULTRA +
Her self-image shifts across the texts. Sacrificer's Staff shows a gentle, compassionate young woman who believed in feeding the hungry and mending suspicion with sincerity, until her sister's death convinced her that the kindness of the weak was a delusion and that lies spun into a gentle dawn, backed by absolute authority, would serve her flock better. She then took the antlered crown as if wrapping herself in a cocoon. By Moonweaver's Dawn she calls Aila "my heart's child" while ordering dreadful punishments cloaked in soft words, never noticing the resentment she bred.

## personality
### LITE
Soft-spoken and certain, she described every cruelty as love. She was devoted to a single goal and sure that only her path was right.
### STANDARD +
Moonweaver's Dawn describes her, from Aila's side, as a kind and gentle old grandmother whose soft words hid dreadful punishments. Lovia seems to have taken silence for consent.

## status
### LITE
Dead. Aila killed her about five hundred years ago, before the Cataclysm, and her name was then struck from public record.
### STANDARD +
Afterwards Aila became the first Moonchanter, abolished the Archpriestess office and the old Hyperborean rites, and sealed Lovia's records and Rotwang's letters in the Court of the First Edict (the Sanctum of the Oathbearer).
### LARGE +
The Traveler and Paimon learned the name only through Aila's recordings in the world quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past (6.0), and Verna, a Frostmoon priestess, then told them what little she knew, since Lovia's deeds were erased from the record. Aila's notes call Lovia's long quest pointless, because the new moon's birth had nothing to do with her actions. Nothing further was revealed through 7.1.

## relationships
### LITE
Aila (granddaughter who killed her), Alia (the other twin), Rotwang (outside collaborator), Solovei (the man she wanted dead).
### STANDARD +
Avrelian's mother (the chosen Maiden, whose flight upset the project) and her son Avrelian. Her unnamed adoptive sister died protecting her, and the previous Archpriestess was her unnamed adoptive mother.

## entry: Lovia's death
@keys: Lovia's death, killed Lovia, Aila stabbed Lovia
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lovia, wishing Solovei dead, denounced him to Aila as a treacherous murderer and ordered her to thrust a sword through his heart from behind. Solovei had told Aila that Lovia was lying. Aila stabbed Lovia through the back instead, and Lovia died without understanding the betrayal. Aila pointed out that her blood ran red, not the silver of a Frostmoon Scion.
### LARGE +
In a recording Aila says she holds no resentment toward Lovia but must bury her sins so the children are not stained, and that the Lady of the Underworld (Ronova) guided her hand. Treat that last claim as Aila's own.

## entry: Sacred Heir Project
@keys: Sacred Heir Project, Sacred Heir
@keys2: Lovia, Frostmoon Scions, Hyperborea
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Scions believed a child of their purified bloodline would be born to rebuild the first great cause and rule Elysium. Lovia's pursuit of it led her to Rotwang, to murder among the Scions, and to the drinking of Scion blood to raise the twins' purity. Aila later wrote that the new moon's birth had nothing to do with it.

## entry: Letter for Lovia
@keys: Letter for Lovia, Sampo Mill
@keys2: Lovia, Rotwang
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In this letter in the Sanctum of the Oathbearer, Rotwang reports the Maiden's suicide, claims to share Lovia's wish to wake the Sampo Mill and forge an Elysium for the people of Nod-Krai, and flatters her by saying that even at the court of the Belyi Tsar a lady of such foresight is rare. Revelation's Toll shows he was really seeking to open the gate to a "pitch-black Elysium", read as the Abyss. Aila sealed the letter away as evidence.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is a human leader, not a goddess or saint.
- She is not Aila, who reformed the Scions after killing her.
- She was not a silver-blooded Scion in fact; her blood was red.
- Do not invent the full extent of her blasphemy, her age, or why the Maiden fled.
