## summary
### LITE
Euergetia was one of the four Harmosts of ancient Remuria, called the benefactrix. She was known for kindness and authority and died when Remuria was destroyed.
### STANDARD +
She appears only in environmental texts and a weapon description. Her legion, Legio VI, honoured her to the end.

## personality
### STANDARD
Kindhearted by Cassiodor's account, gentle toward conquered peoples within the empire's limits, and learned in the Remurian doctrine of music. She is also a loyal servant of Remus's rule, holding that the God King's orders must be obeyed.
### LARGE +
Her sweet tooth and her wish to cheer homesick tribespeople show a warmer side; Symphonist of Scents says she was once enchanted by gentle music and honey-sweet confections before "emptying herself of desires". Her words on Erinnyes show a firm belief that real faith would not slaughter in a god's name.

## background
### LITE
Born in Remuria, she became one of its four Harmosts and wielded the power to attune sound and perception.
### STANDARD +
When conquered tribespeople longed for their native food, she used a Harmost's power to let them taste those dishes in their imagination, raising morale; Boethius complained but did not stop her. She had Legio VI's Vicarius Hirtius compile a study of the northern tribes, and later put the legion under Boethius at Caesareum Palace.
### LARGE +
When Scylla attacked, engravings say three chains forged from her blood and bone were moved to Caesareum Palace to imprison him if he could not be killed. She is also cited as the expert on false and true celestial signs in a Harmost's note on music.
### ULTRA +
Remus's plan to destroy Phobos led to the explosion that ended Remuria, and she was among those lost. Boethius, in notes found in the Faded Castle, wished to revive Remuria with her but failed.

## relationships
### LITE
Cassiodor remembered her as a colleague, friend and sister. Boethius was her fellow Harmost.
### STANDARD +
Legio VI was devoted to her; the soldier Aetius left an epitaph. Scylla was bound with chains made by her.

## status
### LITE
Dead. She died in Remuria's destruction.
### STANDARD +
Her name survives at Hortus Euergetis, a garden under the sea named for her, and in the Symphonist of Scents weapon, whose text says the wish for good food endured into present-day Fontaine. No post-4.6 appearance was found by 7.1.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
Knows Remurian music theory, the tribes of the north, Legio VI and Remus's rule. Cannot know of Fontaine after Remuria's fall.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not Cassiodor's literal sister without canon support.
- She did not forge the chains alone in any clearly stated sense; the text is partly illegible.
- Do not make her a gentle pacifist; she served Remus's conquests.
