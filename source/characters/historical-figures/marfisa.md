## summary
### LITE
Marfisa was a Champion Duelist who lived over 500 years ago. Born in Fleuve Cendre, she rose through self-taught spear fighting, took justice as her rule after losing to a Marechaussee Hunter, and in the end was dissolved by the Narzissenkreuz.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through weapon and artifact texts. She is dead and is not a living character in any quest.

## background
### LITE
She grew up in the sunless undercity of Fleuve Cendre, taught that the weak become prey and that one must accept one's fate as ashes to ashes, dust to dust. A girl in odd attire (read as Parsifal the Great) gave her a Lumidouce Bell flower and invited her to a surface performance. Dazzled, she wished to walk in sunlight too, and she became a spear fighter of renown.
### STANDARD +
Without a teacher, she learned every spear technique she had seen and fought without mercy, with a cold, noble stage presence. Her streak ended when the Marechaussee Hunter Emanuel Guillotin tricked her into revealing a flaw with a deliberately slowed rhythm and struck her arm. He told her that her rules made her stiff and weak to changing hearts, and suggested she take 'justice' as the unchanging rule. She swore revenge, but later decided justice was the reason he had beaten her. She dropped her old rules and became a purple-clad Champion Duelist, upholding justice 'as it ought to be' for the court.
### LARGE +
After the Siege of Poisson the Great Magician Parsifal requested trial by combat over an 'unexpected transgression', and the court chose Marfisa to represent the prosecution; newspapers speculated about her past connection to Parsifal. Parsifal presumably died. Marfisa then set out to find Emanuel for a final duel, only to learn he had retired, masked, his old provocative voice gone and drinking heavily. She took it as a betrayal of his past life and tracked him herself, finding a mask he had left behind.
### ULTRA +
There the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, whom Emanuel had been investigating, met her. Its Master said her fine spear-arm would be of use; a subordinate remarked that she was already the second and the next would follow. She fell into the Narzissenkreuz's waters and her memories dissolved. Lumidouce Elegy says she never learned that the Lumidouce Bell's real meaning in the language of flowers was not hope.

## status
### LITE
Dead for over 500 years: dissolved and absorbed by the Narzissenkreuz.
### STANDARD +
As far as checked she is not part of any 5.x, 6.x or 7.0-7.1 story, and she is not a living character. Her spear technique is described as now belonging to the Narzissenkreuz.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Parsifal gave her hope and, years later, faced her in the arena. Emanuel Guillotin changed her creed and became the hunt that led to her death. The Narzissenkreuz is the entity that ended her.
### LARGE +
Rightful Reward calls Emanuel her 'fated opponent'; the same text says that after her defeat she meant to meet him again and give it her all in a final duel.

## entry: Rightful Reward
@keys: Rightful Reward
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
A four-star polearm whose text tells how Marfisa met Emanuel, discovered his retirement, and then met the Narzissenkreuz at the end of her pursuit. She is the 'spear-wielder' of the passage.
### LARGE +
Its opening says that thieves remain thieves, crimes must be publicly punished and lies publicly exposed; the wiki attributes that creed to Marfisa.
