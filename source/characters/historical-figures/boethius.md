## summary
### LITE
Boethius was one of the greatest musicians of ancient Fontaine and one of the four Harmosts of the God King Remus. His fanatical loyalty and the corruption of the Grand Symphony turned him into the architect of Remuria's final war.
### STANDARD +
He is remembered in Fontaine as a master of music and, in Remuria's own record, as a zealot. In Version 4.6 his shattered spirit returns beneath Petrichor, where it is destroyed along with Phobos.
### ULTRA +
The game never shows the original man alone: the Boethius of the 4.6 quests is his fragmented will bound into the Symphony and speaking for it. Treat any statement of his as the mixture it is.

## personality
### STANDARD
Proud, eloquent and certain he is right. He speaks of his foes as primitives, insects or barbarians, praises order and permanence, and cannot accept that anything Remuria built could be a mistake. He is devoted to Remus personally even when he disobeys him.
### LARGE +
He mixes real grief for his people with contempt for others. He insists that giving Remurians eternal bodies and souls inside Phobos is a gift, and that anyone who refuses is ungrateful. Cassiodor, who once knew him as a noble Harmost, says he was not always this way.

## background
### LITE
Born in a reed-built village on an isle conquered by Remuria, he followed the invading warriors home, rose through his musical talent, abandoned his tribe's customs and was raised by Remus to Harmost.
### STANDARD +
As Harmost he learned of the Primordial Sea, the prophesied flood and Remus's growing madness, and still carried out his orders: hunting barbarians and Lochfolk and corrupting primordial water in the hope of dissolving the Remurians into Ichor. When Scylla led a rebellion he ordered every barbarian tribe destroyed, ending Remuria's golden era.
### LARGE +
Remus woke to the agony of the tribes and tried to undo the damage with a final order. Boethius took this as betrayal, rallied what armies remained, took the golden Ichor and sealed Scylla beneath the Domus Aurea. Remus, Boethius and Scylla were all swept away when the Grand Symphony's self-destruct order raised the tsunami that drowned Remuria.
### ULTRA +
Afterwards, according to Cassiodor, Boethius gathered the survivors as the Golden Troupe to save Phobos and bring peace to the world through the Symphony, and wove his own shattered soul into it. The cost fell on Fontainians: souls plundered, rulers turned into pawns, polities newly born brought to the brink. Cassiodor and the Marechaussee Hunters sealed both him and the sea that had swallowed his nation. In 4.6 the border between past and present thinned, and his spirit seized Petrichor's people in a dream.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Cassiodor was his friend and brother and became his greatest enemy. Remus was his god king. Scylla, the dragon prince he imprisoned, helps free the people from him. The Traveler and Paimon, whom he calls outsiders to Phobos's plan, destroy him.
### LARGE +
- Cassiodor: Boethius brought the warrior into the empire, so he reads Cassiodor's mercy as treachery and holds up the 'brand of the Sebastos' that Cassiodor can never remove.
- Phobos: said to have eaten him; in the end it is Phobos that wears his face.
- Erinnyes: from the same conquered tribe as Cassiodor, she refused Remuria and went to serve Egeria.

## status
### LITE
Dead. His spirit and Phobos were destroyed in Fortune Plango Vulnera (4.6), and the Requiem released the souls bound in Ichor.
### STANDARD +
No later appearance by 7.1. He is still honoured in Fontaine as a great musician; his book The Flying Outlander survives and was passed to Tailleferre.
### LARGE +
Scylla expects the freed souls to be reborn one day as mortals. Boethius's story serves Fontaine's artists as a reminder of the 'inescapable cycle' of Teyvat.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not a god and not the Sebastos; he is a Harmost.
- Do not present him as a villain from the start: Cassiodor and the artifact texts describe a once-excellent Harmost.
- The 'Boethius' fought in 4.6 is partly Phobos; do not give Phobos's claims, such as having eaten him, to the man himself.
- He is not the Golden Hunter; that title is Cassiodor's.
### LARGE +
- Do not call the Golden Troupe a Fontainian organisation: it was made of Remurian remnants.
- Do not invent a family, spouse or romance for him.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Remuria's expansion: taken from his island home; becomes Remus's servant and later Harmost.
- Golden era ends: Scylla's rebellion; Boethius orders the tribes destroyed.
- Fall of Remuria: seals Scylla with the Ichor; swept away by the tsunami.
- Over the following millennia: Golden Troupe in the Faded Castle; opposed by Cassiodor and the Marechaussee Hunters; sealed.
- 4.0: named in Golden Troupe artifact texts.
- 4.6: appears in Underwater Nocturne, The Last Day of Remuria, Fortune Plango Vulnera; destroyed.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Paimon's nicknames for him include Mr. Super Rude, Mr. Impolite and Mr. Evil-and-Rude.
- In Fortune Plango Vulnera he is fought in three forms: 'The Immortal Sebastos's Second Coming', 'Bleeding Idol' and then Phobos itself.
- His name echoes the Roman philosopher Boethius; 'Dominus' was a Roman imperial title.

## entry: The Golden Troupe
@keys: Golden Troupe, Faded Castle, Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Remuria fell, Boethius gathered its remnants into the Golden Troupe in the Faded Castle. They studied spirit and fate, built musical devices like those of the golden age and aimed to rescue Phobos so music could unite the world. The Marechaussee Hunters were founded to fight them and their enslaved golems. The Troupe also, per the wiki, practised child sacrifice.
### LARGE +
Later the master Ruggiero and Cassiodor sealed the Faded Castle under rebuilt Petrichor and spread false stories to hide its secrets.

## entry: Harmost of Remuria
@keys: Harmost of Remuria, four Harmosts, Remurian Harmost
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Harmosts were four officials whom Remus promoted and shared his power with. They helped oversee Remuria and could alter Phobos's melody, which only they and the Sebastos could do. Boethius and Cassiodor were two of them; the wiki lists the other two as Aurelius and Euergetia.

## entry: Scylla
@keys: Scylla, Fell Dragon Prince, Bane Dragon
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The dragon prince led barbarians and vishaps against Remuria. Boethius had the Initium Iani shut and sealed him beneath the Domus Aurea with the golden Ichor, where he stayed for thousands of years. Scylla told Cassiodor that the attack was not betrayal but part of Remus's plan, and that Boethius was the real traitor.

## entry: Phobos
@keys: Phobos, Grand Symphony, the Symphony
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Symphony that governed Remuria gained a persona through the Remurians' wishes and became Phobos. It says it devoured Boethius and made him its instrument. In Fortune Plango Vulnera it seeks to raise Boethius as a second Remus; Cassiodor traps it in a Legatus Golem body so the Traveler can destroy it.

## entry: Cassiodor and Boethius
@keys: Cassiodor and Boethius, brothers of Remuria, brother Harmosts
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
They met in the war against Cassiodor's tribe and became close friends, calling each other brother. Boethius accused Cassiodor of a soft heart, Cassiodor accused Boethius of ignoring the voices of those below him. Their quarrel in the artifact text 'Golden Bird's Shedding' foreshadows the rebellion.
