## summary
### LITE
Director of the Institute of Natural Philosophy in the cataclysm era about 500 years ago and a childhood friend of Basil Elton, Emanuel Guillotin and Karl Ingold. He recruited gifted young researchers, backed Alain Guillotin's work that led to pneumousia energy, and closed the Institute during the cataclysm to keep its young members from the fighting. His fate is unknown.
### STANDARD +
He is known entirely from the Institute's Ancient Notes and two artifact texts. The notes show a practical, dry-humoured administrator who cared about talented youths and post-disaster reconstruction.

## background
### LITE
Born before the cataclysm and linked by the wiki to the Narzissenkreuz Institute as a child, where he made friends with Basil, Emanuel and Karl. He later directed the Institute of Natural Philosophy and recruited child prodigies, among them the playwright Coppelius.
### STANDARD +
When the cataclysm struck he closed the Institute for everyone's safety, since the young researchers wanted to go out and fight; he hoped they would one day see why rebuilding mattered more than marching off to die. After the first flood period he picked land near the Fountain of Lucine for the relocated Institute, then learned that the land would host the Opera Epiclese, which he mocked bitterly.
### LARGE +
After Basil's death the friends paid respects at her grave. Emanuel presented his adopted son Alain to Dwight, who met the newly appointed Iudex and thought him more reliable than Leon and the other clowns. Alain's clockwork meka work won Dwight's attention and funding; later results on Khaenri'ah war machines and primal-construct energy raised that investment further. Dwight noticed Carter Scherbius, Alain's assistant, taking frequent leave and had him watched.
### ULTRA +
Alain introduced Rene de Petrichor and Jakob Ingold, whom Dwight enrolled in Alain's lab, worrying about the effect on other researchers. When Carter lay ill and unconscious, Dwight took his blood for a possible cure. Rene then broke from Alain's project and asked to study the Abyss, Durin's blood, Elynas's remains and Dark Mud from Liyue; Dwight approved it, seeing its value against the toxins that remained in Fontaine's soil and water and were producing deformed Oceanids, and found out they were Karl's adopted sons. When Alain achieved pneumousia energy, Dwight judged it the biggest breakthrough in a century that would change all of society. A report about the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and its leader, called the Master, reached him; according to the wiki he entrusted the investigation to Emanuel.

## relationships
### LITE
Close friends Basil Elton, Emanuel Guillotin and Karl Ingold; protege Alain Guillotin; researchers Rene, Jakob, Carter and Coppelius.
### STANDARD +
He treats his old friends as family, funds Alain, and approves Rene's project. He judges the Narzissenkreuz Ordo's secrecy worrying enough to ask Emanuel to look into it.
### LARGE +
The Forgotten Vessel text recalls the four childhood friends playing hero-and-dragon games, with Emanuel as the hero and Karl as the dragon. The Veteran's Visage text mentions Basil proposing a get-together with Lasker and Ingold when she returned, and entrusting Alain and Mary-Ann to friends if she did not.

## status
### LITE
Unknown. His present whereabouts are not stated. The Institute he led is long gone.
### STANDARD +
In the present the Traveler finds the key to his office in a chest in the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and finds rifthounds in the office. Nothing says whether he died, was lost to the flood or lived on.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not the Master of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo; he received a report about the Ordo and the matter went to Emanuel.
- Do not give him a death, a later position or a motive; canon is silent.
- Do not confuse him with the "Director" of the artifact texts, whom the wiki links to Lyris.
- Basil Elton is a woman and a Marechaussee Hunter; the dead friend is not male.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Childhood: befriends Basil, Emanuel and Karl at the Narzissenkreuz Institute (per wiki).
- Before the cataclysm: directs the Institute of Natural Philosophy; notices Coppelius's talent.
- The cataclysm 500 years ago: closes the Institute; later, post-flood, plans a new site.
- After: pays respects at Basil's grave; funds Alain; enrols Rene and Jakob; approves Rene's Abyss project; Alain's pneumousia breakthrough.
- Later: receives the Ordo report; Emanuel investigates.
- Present: the Traveler finds his office key (Version 4.0).
