## summary
### LITE
Irmin was the one-eyed last Vinster King of Khaenri'ah's Eclipse Dynasty. Once sane, he sank into an obsession with Abyssal power and knowledge "from beyond the skies". He named the Traveler's sibling his heir, then used them as a vessel for Abyssal power, setting the Five Sinners and the cataclysm in motion. He is known only through others' accounts.
### STANDARD +
His court was led by trusted servants such as the sage Hroptatyr and the seer Vedrfolnir. When Vedrfolnir warned him that Khaenri'ah would fall, Irmin had him blinded and imprisoned, which provoked the palace raid by Dainsleif and the future Five Sinners.
### ULTRA +
Khaenri'ahns who lived under him describe him in sharply different ways: Thorkell calls him "the all-seeing Vinster King" guiding the nation toward prosperity, while dissidents call him a blind fool and a raving lunatic. Both views come from 6.1 Nod-Krai flashbacks.

## background
### LITE
He ruled underground Khaenri'ah some five hundred years ago. Under him the kingdom's machines were rebuilt with otherworldly secrets, the Universitas Magistrorum turned to Abyss research, and a secret agent hunted the last Crimson Moon descendants. He hailed the newly arrived golden-haired outsider as heir and saviour, then led them to the kingdom's depths to absorb unlimited Abyssal power.
### STANDARD +
Around that time he blinded his court seer Vedrfolnir for prophesying the fall of Khaenri'ah. Vedrfolnir's brother Dainsleif, captain of the royal guard, gathered Rhinedottir, Rerir, Surtalogi and Hroptatyr to storm the palace; in the chaos the five seized the Abyssal power that had been poured into the heir and became the Five Sinners. The cataclysm followed. A bulletin later declared Knight Marshal Anfortas regent because of "King Irmin's present indisposed state".
### LARGE +
Under Irmin, the Universitas Magistrorum answered directly to the throne. One undated namecard records it reporting orbital predictions to its "Almighty Sovereign". An unsigned letter found in Gavireh Lajavard begged the Knight Marshal to ask the king to preserve some machinery built to the old standard, because some Khaenri'ahns trusted "human will" more than the new otherworldly methods. Pierro's artifact text says he could not win the ruler's favour against the sages and failed to stop them tearing away "the veil of sin". An artifact text released in 6.0 adds that the study of the stars became forbidden in his era, and only a select few were allowed to break the ban to serve their "one-eyed ruler".
### ULTRA +
The Abyss research had a human cost even before the cataclysm. In memories shown in the Mirror of Remembrance (7.1), Hroptatyr blames the incurable, fatal illness of Professor Mimameith, teacher to him and Eftirvit (Pierro), on "chronic complications from His Majesty's research project", the Abyss project she had worked on. Hroptatyr also remarks that summoning the essence of Celestia would need "a king ambitious enough to permit it". Pierro later reveals that, at the time he pulled Paimon down from Celestia, Hroptatyr summoned the Traveler's sibling. A 7.0 collaboration glider text adds that as Irmin's rule grew more tyrannical and relations with the surface soured, the price of grain and vegetables, already scarce underground, soared.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Irmin's relationships are known only from other people's memories. He trusted two men above all: Hroptatyr "The Wise", who encouraged his ambitions and rose quickly under him, and Vedrfolnir "The Visionary", whom he blinded and jailed as soon as the seer's prophecy displeased him. He raised the Traveler's sibling to crown prince or princess and the hope of the nation, then treated them as an instrument. His agent Rerir carried out the purge of Crimson Moon descendants for him and came to hate him. Dainsleif, sworn captain of his guard, led the raid against him.
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir, hearing of Vedrfolnir's arrest, wondered whether the king's circle of trust had shrunk further. Hroptatyr answered that it was not about trust but about what the king wanted. Rerir's view was harsher: the king's suspicions had been building for a long time, and he blinded the seer because he did not want visions of what he refused to see. Pierro (as Eftirvit) was a royal mage who never won Irmin's favour, and the king showed no interest when Eftirvit presented him with Paimon.
### ULTRA +
Lesser figures fill out the picture of his reign. Tholindis, a Moon Marrow researcher, saw her team's resources diverted to the Abyss and blamed his growing madness on the day he met the outsider heir; since she could not kill the king herself, she even hoped to go back in time and stop that meeting by killing the outsider who brought those new possibilities (6.3). Orlog, a Crimson Moon loyalist, called him a petty man "with a miser's heart" whose eyes were clouded by powers from beyond this world. Thorkell praised him as the all-seeing Vinster King guiding the nation to prosperity. Later, the Alberich Clan, who had no royal blood, ruled as regents once his strength failed.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon says only that he was one-eyed. Kaeya's secret family teaching (2.8) and an artifact text (6.0) both use the phrase. No portrait, age, height, clothing or which eye he lacked has been given. Do not describe him beyond that.
### LARGE +
Some statuettes dropped by Abyss Order monsters show a one-eyed figure that some players take to be Irmin. Others suggest the one-eyed regent Anfortas. This is speculation, not canon.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead for about five hundred years; his reign ended with the palace raid and the cataclysm. No scene shows his death, though item text lists "regicide" among that night's events.
### STANDARD +
Officially, Khaenri'ahn records said only that he was "indisposed" when Anfortas took the regency. As of 7.1 no living character claims he survived, and he does not appear in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya stories except in flashbacks and others' accounts.
### LARGE +
His legacy remains active: the Abyssal power he had poured into his heir became the Five Sinners' power, and the purge he ordered shaped Rerir's fall and the fate of the Crimson Moon's descendants, which the Traveler relives through simulations in Nod-Krai (6.1).

## chronology
### LARGE
- Eclipse Dynasty era: the Eclipse ("dark sun") supplants the Crimson Moon. Irmin becomes the last Vinster King (date unknown).
- During his reign: the Universitas is turned toward Abyss research; Professor Mimameith falls fatally ill from that project (revealed 7.1).
- Eftirvit pulls Paimon from Celestia and presents her to the king, who is not interested; at the same time Hroptatyr summons the Traveler's sibling (revealed 7.0-7.1).
- The sibling is proclaimed heir and saviour of Khaenri'ah in a great ceremony that the young knight Dainsleif helps police (revealed 5.2).
- The purge of Crimson Moon descendants intensifies under the agent Rerir (revealed 6.1).
- Vedrfolnir warns that the kingdom will fall and is blinded and jailed. Dainsleif's party storms the palace, the five seize the Abyssal power, and the cataclysm begins (revealed 4.7, 5.2, 6.1).
- Anfortas declares himself regent, citing the king's "indisposed state" (revealed 3.6).
### ULTRA +
Release order of what players learned: the Pale Flame text about the "previous ruler" (1.5); his name and one eye in Hidden Strife (2.8, limited event); the Universitas namecard (4.0); the Anfortas bulletin (3.6); Dainsleif's "Vinster King" (4.7); the heir ceremony and the vessel (5.2 glider, 5.7 quest); the Nod-Krai flashbacks of his court and the purge (6.0-6.3); Paimon's presentation (7.0); Mimameith's illness (7.1).

## trivia
### ULTRA
- "Vinster" is an old Germanic word meaning dark; the Chinese title means "Black King". These are wiki etymology notes, not in-game statements.
- The Chinese name of the dynasty means "Black Sun", matching the "dark sun" that replaced the crimson moon in Khaenri'ahn legend.
- Like the later regent Anfortas, Irmin was a one-eyed ruler of Khaenri'ah.

## entry: The Vinster King's heir
@keys: Prince of Khaenri'ah, Princess of Khaenri'ah, Savior of Khaenri'ah, crowned heir, golden-haired heir
@keys2: Irmin, Vinster King
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Traveler's sibling arrived in Khaenri'ah, Irmin named them his heir. He and the Court of Khaenri'ah loudly proclaimed the strength and hope the new crown prince or princess would bring, and the people hailed them as a saviour. Dainsleif, then a young knight, kept order in the palace plaza that day. Looking back, he calls the title nothing but the Vinster King's lust for power (5.7). The king later led the heir down to the kingdom's deepest depths to serve as a vessel for unlimited Abyssal power.
### LARGE +
Tholindis dates the king's worst excesses to his meeting with the heir: only afterward did his Abyss research go too far (6.3). In 7.1 Pierro reveals that the sibling had been summoned from Celestia by Hroptatyr. Whether the king ordered it is not said.

## entry: The blinding of Vedrfolnir
@keys: Vedrfolnir, The Visionary
@keys2: Irmin, Vinster King, blinded, gouged
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Vedrfolnir, the court seer, was one of the two men the king trusted most. After he told the king at court that Khaenri'ah would fall if he continued on his path, Irmin had both his eyes gouged out and threw him into a filthy cell. In prison, Vedrfolnir said what surprised him was the king's sheer ignorance and how unfairly the world answers earnest counsel. His brother Dainsleif responded by planning the palace raid that created the Five Sinners.
### LARGE +
Talking to Rerir before his arrest, Vedrfolnir drew a line between "our king as he once was" and the man he had become, and warned that a king can be the most virtuous of rulers or a tyrant worse than any dragon. Rerir guessed the king blinded him hoping to stop visions he did not want to see.

## entry: The purge of the Crimson Moon
@keys: Crimson Moon descendants, Rächer of Solnari, purge
@keys2: Irmin, Vinster King, Eclipse Dynasty, Rerir
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Long after the Crimson Moon Dynasty fell, Irmin still coveted the secrets it had left behind and saw its surviving bloodline as a threat. He secretly had his agent Rerir, the "Rächer of Solnari", hunt the remaining descendants. Rerir's files include a teacher as well as orphaned teenagers and children. The purge spread from the direct line to distant offshoots, and when Rerir found his own fiancee Tholindis among them, he cried out against "Irminsul" and turned on the king.
### LARGE +
Rerir himself noted that the king never considered a gentler approach. The purge grew harsher as the king became lost in his research. The Nod-Krai simulations of 6.1 show these events through Rerir's memories.

## entry: Abyss research under King Irmin
@keys: secrets from beyond the skies, veil of sin, Abyss project
@keys2: Irmin, Vinster King, Khaenri'ah, Universitas Magistrorum
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Irmin backed machinery built with "secrets from beyond the skies", which a letter writer feared would replace the trust Khaenri'ahns placed in "human will". He diverted the Universitas Magistrorum's resources to the Abyss, leaving researchers like Tholindis, who warned against relying on one poorly understood power, on their own. Rumour said he had hidden mechanisms installed in the palace and city, and on the night of the raid Rerir suspected they had been triggered and ran on Abyssal power (6.1).
### ULTRA +
The cost was felt early: Hroptatyr blamed his teacher Mimameith's fatal illness on the king's Abyss project (7.1). The study of the stars was banned except for a few allowed to serve the one-eyed ruler (6.0 artifact text).

## entry: The palace raid against the Vinster King
@keys: palace raid, storm the palace, Five Sinners' night
@keys2: Irmin, Vinster King, Dainsleif
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
To free Vedrfolnir, Dainsleif, captain of the royal guard, gathered Rhinedottir, Rerir, Surtalogi and Hroptatyr and broke into the palace. He said he did not want treason, only to make the king see the error of his ways. Surtalogi told a guard that the king himself had betrayed the throne. In the raid the others, Vedrfolnir included, betrayed Dainsleif and took the Abyssal power the king had channelled into his heir. Dainsleif later said the six of them should have been the ones to stop the Vinster King from shaking the foundations of the world (4.7).
### ULTRA +
The glider text that tells this story lists "regicide, righteousness, ruthlessness, treachery" among that night's deeds. It is the strongest hint about the king's end, but it never says who killed him.

## entry: Regency after King Irmin
@keys: Anfortas, Marshal Regent, indisposed king
@keys2: Irmin, Vinster King, regent
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
A barely legible bulletin in the ruins of Gavireh Lajavard announces that Knight Marshal Anfortas declared himself regent because of King Irmin's "present indisposed state" and unknown threats to the kingdom. Kaeya's family teaching agrees that the Alberich Clan, though not royal, ruled as regents when the king's strength failed.

## entry: Hidden Strife letter about the one-eyed king
@keys: one-eyed king Irmin, Alberich teaching
@keys2: Kaeya, Alberich
@version: 2.8
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
During the limited-time event Hidden Strife (2.8), an optional box hidden by Kaeya contained a family teaching: the Alberich Clan, who had no royal blood, became regents when "the strength of the one-eyed king Irmin failed". This was the first time Irmin was named, and it is the only source for his single eye apart from a 6.0 artifact text.

## entry: Paimon and the Vinster King
@keys: Paimon's origin, gift to the Vinster King
@keys2: Paimon, Pierro, Eftirvit
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Pierro told the Traveler (7.0) that he first used Khaenri'ahn sky-pulling technology over five hundred years ago, and that it brought Paimon down from Celestia. He presented her to the Vinster King, who took no interest in the gift, so she lived in Pierro's palace until the cataclysm. Paimon remembers none of this.
