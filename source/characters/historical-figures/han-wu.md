## summary
### LITE
Han Wu was a master weaponsmith of Liyue who lived around the time of the cataclysm, five hundred years ago. With the smith Yun Hui he developed the Prototype weapon series, and he is also tied to the Blackcliff series.
### STANDARD +
He was fiery-tempered until a disaster in the Chasm changed him. He is known only through the flavour text of weapons, not through any quest or voiced appearance.
### ULTRA +
His story is told across several weapon descriptions: the Prototype and Blackcliff texts give the cave-in, the Slasher text his guilt and shrine, and the Pole text how his son inherited his designs. Treat all as weapon lore, not eyewitness history.

## background
### LITE
After the cataclysm monsters still roamed Teyvat, and people turned to supernatural arts but found existing weapons broke easily. Wu and Yun Hui joined forces to build stronger ones, producing the Prototype series. Wu had a son, Han Ce, who wanted no part in the family trade.
### STANDARD +
Believing the ores available to him limited his weapons, Wu took a mining crew into the Chasm, using one of Hui's connections, to find crystallised blackcliff. A cave-in sealed them inside. As his men fell into madness and despair, Wu used his prototype sword to carve a way out; when it struck rock it released a deafening roar that none of the survivors ever spoke of. He came out much calmer than before.
### LARGE +
Later the Chasm suffered great earthquakes and was closed. Wu was racked with guilt, believing he had disturbed a dragon beneath it. He rose from his sickbed to forge Blackcliff Slasher and told Han Ce to build a shrine in the cave and place the sword there to ward off evil and rebuke the dragon.

## status
### LITE
Dead. He died peacefully in his sleep a few years after forging the Slasher.
### STANDARD +
On the day he died the shrine emerged from the cave and the sword glowed as if grieving. Han Ce retrieved it that night, found his father's prototype designs, made the Blackcliff Pole from them and decided to carry on the family trade.
### ULTRA +
The Tianshu of that age, per the Han Ce page, engraved an inscription on the Slasher after Ce retrieved it.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Partner of Yun Hui in the Prototype series. His relationship with Han Ce was strained over Ce's refusal to take over the forge until the Chasm incident softened Wu; the two reconciled, and Ce later continued the work.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
He knows smithing, the Prototype designs and what happened in the Chasm from his own experience. He believes his cave-in woke something dragon-like beneath the Chasm; this is his belief, never confirmed.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Around the cataclysm, five hundred years ago: develops the Prototype series with Yun Hui.
- Some time later: the Chasm mining expedition and cave-in; reconciles with Han Ce.
- Several years later: earthquakes close the Chasm; he forges Blackcliff Slasher.
- A few years after that: dies; Han Ce takes the sword and continues the Blackcliff line.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Han Ce, not Han Wu, made most of the Blackcliff weapons; Wu made the Slasher.
- Do not state what roared in the Chasm; the text leaves it open.
- He is not a card character; do not give him quotes or catchphrases.
