## summary
### LITE
Cassiodor was a Harmost of ancient Remuria who, after its fall, took the name Ouranides of Ouranopolis and became the Golden Hunter, founder of the Marechaussee Hunters and keeper of the sunken realm. He guided the Traveler through the Canticles of Harmony quests in a white cat's body.
### STANDARD +
He is the opposite of his old friend Boethius: both rose from conquered tribes, but where Boethius clung to Remuria, Cassiodor chose to live among Fontainians and keep the past sealed. In Version 4.6 he gives up his own existence to destroy Phobos.
### ULTRA +
Most of what is known comes from his own account in the quests and from artifact texts. He is a witness and a participant, not a neutral narrator, and what he says about Scylla and about the final years of Remuria changes as he learns more.

## personality
### STANDARD
Formal, dry and humane. He speaks in old-fashioned phrases and can be sardonic, yet he plainly refuses to sacrifice innocents. He says a name is only a label and that one's will and soul matter more than the body. He admits that Remuria's pride and forgetting of its own origins brought about its fall.
### LARGE +
Boethius called his mercy a soft heart. It is the reason Remus trusted him with a guardian's duty. Cassiodor holds contradictory loyalties without hiding them: he still reveres the Sebastos and the ideal of freeing people from fate, and he is certain Remuria's methods were wrong. He tells Este, in the dreamlike memory of the legion, to trust the heart's wisdom and not hand judgment of right and wrong to Phobos or any Harmost.
### ULTRA +
In the form of a cat he is a little prickly about it: he admits that animal behaviour sometimes overbears him, objects to being compared to the town cats, and responds to being petted with a sigh or a remark that the Traveler is getting carried away.

## appearance
### STANDARD
His own appearance is never shown. In Version 4.6 he is met as a white cat with silver tabby stripes and different-coloured eyes, with gold sparkles and blue runes while possessed, and a gold pendant with a blue diamond-shaped charm that he says the Sebastos gave him. Remurians in general have stone bodies with Ichor.
### LARGE +
The pendant is, in his words, a fragment of the great Symphony and his last link to Remuria; while it remains, Boethius cannot fully master the Symphony. After he leaves the cat, the pendant is gone and Osse is an ordinary, if unusual, cat.

## speech
### STANDARD
Courteous and slightly archaic ('nay', 'I shall', 'do not hesitate to ask'), with measured explanations and the occasional dry joke. He addresses the Traveler and Paimon by name, calls Boethius by name or 'that fellow', and calls Remus the Sebastos or the Imperator.
### LARGE +
He becomes plain and warm when he is certain. In the Finale he speaks of Remus's plans with joy and calls the Traveler 'my friend', and he quotes a Remurian saying about water, soil and the value of life.

## background
### LITE
He was a warrior of a tribe that Remuria's armies destroyed. He joined the empire, befriended the musician Boethius and rose to Harmost. After Remuria sank he founded the Marechaussee Hunters to oppose Boethius and the Golden Troupe.
### STANDARD +
He and Erinnyes were the only people of their tribe to escape. She went to Egeria and Aremorica; he followed Boethius to Remuria's capital, finished its trials and was raised to a prideful master. As Harmost he doubted the order to destroy the tribes. He was elsewhere when the capital drowned, and afterwards took many bodies to guard the old realm and joined Egeria's people in a new form.
### LARGE +
Remus separated his soul from the Grand Symphony and called him Ouranides of Ouranopolis, 'the city of the future', so that he could act on his own judgment. After the fall he hunted the Golden Troupe's phantoms and golems under the name Golden Hunter, which he disliked. With the Lochknights and Remurian survivors such as Este he formed the Marechaussee Hunters. Boethius, now wrapped in the broken Symphony, was sealed along with the sea that had swallowed his nation.
### ULTRA +
Over the millennia he guided future generations. He helped the master Ruggiero reach and seal the Faded Castle beneath rebuilt Petrichor and spread false stories about it, according to Ruggiero's Notes. In 4.6, as the border between past and present thinned, he woke inside a white cat in Petrichor, brought the Traveler and Paimon to the Faded Castle, learned from Scylla the truth of the assault on the capital, and in Domus Aurea pretended to rejoin Boethius, then joined the Symphony to bind him and Phobos in one Legatus Golem body.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Boethius was his sworn brother and became his enemy. Remus was his god king. Erinnyes was his tribeswoman. Este, a Remurian who followed him, became a Fontainian ancestor. Scylla was a hated enemy and then a useful ally. The Traveler and Paimon were his last companions.
### LARGE +
- Este (golem): a friend from the legions, saved from the fall, who had children in Fontaine; his descendant Este later cared for Osse.
- Neuvillette's Marechaussee Phantom, the Hunters' modern form, goes back to his founding; the Hunters now investigate crimes and include Melusines.
- Ruggiero: a scholar who suspected the Golden Hunter was a Remurian remnant.
### ULTRA +
His title Dominus appears as a Remurian form of address when golems greet him. A nameless golem still guarding Scylla's chains in the sea greets him and dies content. In the sunken memory of the legion, Este greets him as Dominus Cassiodor, and a legionnaire says the Sebastos had sent him to Aremorica.

## abilities
### STANDARD
He can cross between bodies: he took many forms to carry out his duty, including a white cat. As a spirit-keeper he can manifest a form inside a space made by the Symphony, call up a seal on a hidden path and operate the golden devices of the old realm. He led the first Marechaussee Hunters.
### LARGE +
Remurian bodies are cast of stone with Ichor for blood. Ichor-bound souls are tied to their obsession; he says that is why they cannot rest. Because his soul was cut from the Symphony he is not a part of Phobos, and could rejoin the Symphony as Harmost and seize control from within.

## status
### LITE
Dead. In Fortune Plango Vulnera (4.6) he possessed the Legatus Golem body that held Phobos, and the Traveler destroyed both. His soul was released by the Requiem.
### STANDARD +
Scylla expects the freed souls to return to the Primordial Sea and one day be reborn as mortals. The cat Osse, whose form Cassiodor used, returned to Petrichor and is cared for by the descendant Este. There is no appearance after 4.6 in story content by 7.1.
### LARGE +
Cassiodor said this world would no longer have a Cassiodor or a Golden Hunter. He asked the Traveler to complete the Requiem for him and for the Sebastos's wish, and he parted with a hope that they might meet again in a fertile land full of flowing water.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
He knows Remuria's history, the Harmosts, the Symphony and Ichor, the ritual Boethius planned, and the layout of the sunken capital. He did not know of Remus's secret plan with Scylla, or that Phobos had a persona, until the Finale.
### LARGE +
He did not know where Remus went after the fall or why Boethius turned, and he searched for answers for millennia. He says souls bound to Ichor are held there by their own obsession.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not Boethius: Cassiodor is the Golden Hunter, Boethius the Bleeding Idol.
- He is not a cat; Osse is the cat, and Cassiodor only used its body. After 4.6 Osse is an ordinary cat.
- He is not a villain; Boethius calls him a traitor, but Scylla, who has her own grievance, names Boethius as the real traitor, and Cassiodor comes to agree.
- He is not a member of the modern Marechaussee Phantom; he founded the Hunters millennia earlier.
### LARGE +
- He did not kill the Remurians. He sealed Boethius and the sea; the Requiem freed the souls.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Remuria's expansion: his tribe is destroyed; he joins the empire and befriends Boethius.
- Later: raised to Harmost; sent by Remus to Aremorica; his soul is separated from the Symphony and he is renamed Ouranides of Ouranopolis.
- Fall of Remuria: he is absent from the capital.
- Afterwards: possesses many bodies, founds the Marechaussee Hunters, helps seal Boethius, later helps Ruggiero seal the Faded Castle.
- 4.0: named in artifact texts (Hunter's Brooch, Golden Song's Variation).
- 4.6: appears as Osse in Canticles of Harmony; destroyed with Phobos.
- 5.8: featured on a Genius Invokation TCG equipment card.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Paimon's nicknames: 'Osse' is her short form of 'Ouranopolis', and Monsieur Os is her more polite name for him. 'Dominus' is a Roman honorific.
- The wiki notes that Cassiodor is named after the Roman scholar Cassiodorus, and that 'Pharsalia's Rhapsody' alludes to a civil war of ancient Rome that parallels Cassiodor and Boethius's clash.
- He sees the name Marechaussee Hunter, taken from his own phrase about pursuing phantoms, as a humiliation, according to the artifact text.

## entry: Osse
@keys: Osse, Monsieur Os, glowing cat, talking cat
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
A white cat with silver stripes whom Cassiodor possessed for the Canticles of Harmony. Paimon shortened his name Ouranides of Ouranopolis to 'Osse' and later called him Monsieur Os. When Cassiodor left, the cat was given to Este in Petrichor, where the Traveler can pet it.

## entry: The Marechaussee Hunters
@keys: Marechaussee Hunters, Golden Hunter, Hunter's Brooch
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Cassiodor founded the original Marechaussee Hunters after Remuria's fall to fight the Golden Troupe and its enslaved golems. The name comes from his phrase about pursuing phantoms in the dark and expelling them, a title he thought humiliating. Remurian survivors such as Este and Walter were the first Hunters. The Hunters later reintegrated into society and their methods were forgotten, according to a quest-text summary on the wiki.
### LARGE +
The modern Marechaussee Phantom descends from them; it is led by Neuvillette and now investigates crime, with many Melusines among its ranks. The Hunter's Brooch was a medal for feats of arms in Fontaine's defence, one that some awardees hid or threw in the water.

## entry: Ouranides of Ouranopolis
@keys: Ouranides of Ouranopolis, Ouranopolis, Vivarium's Heir
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In a memory of Remus (the speaker's name is hidden, but Cassiodor and the wiki identify it as him), the Imperator separates his Harmost's melody from the Grand Symphony as a contingency against mishaps and gives him the name 'Ouranopolis, the city of the future', so that he may judge for himself. Cassiodor later says that is why he has had many names and bodies.

## entry: Cassiodor and Erinnyes
@keys: Erinnyes, butterfly wing, Aremorica, Hauteclaire
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Per the wiki, Cassiodor and Erinnyes were the only survivors of their tribe. Remus later sent him to Aremorica, where he took part in a fighting tournament in disguise and tried to persuade her to end the war. He was discovered and his artificial body cut to pieces; Erinnyes promised to free him from the golem body and said she had not forgotten their oath. Her faith was crushed after Remuria fell and Egeria told her the truth about the original sin.

## entry: Ruggiero and the Faded Castle
@keys: Ruggiero, Ruggiero's Notes, Consilium Sapientum
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
In Ruggiero's Notes the master wrote that the Golden Hunter knew far more about Remuria than he expected. Ruggiero helped him keep Boethius from awakening the seals to Remuria and sealed the Faded Castle, after failing to find the relics he sought, under the rebuilt Petrichor. They set up Petrichor's ruling Consilium Sapientum and spread false stories about the castle.

## entry: Fortune Plango Vulnera
@keys: Fortune Plango Vulnera, Domus Aurea, Requiem, Legatus Golem
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Domus Aurea, Cassiodor, who has seemed to join Boethius's ritual, uses his Harmost's authority to disrupt the conductor from inside the Symphony. Phobos reveals itself and says it devoured Boethius; Cassiodor traps it in the Legatus Golem body, takes the same body and asks the Traveler to destroy them. After the fall of Boethius and Phobos, the Traveler plays Remus's Requiem, and the souls bound to Ichor return to the sea.
### LARGE +
Cassiodor says Remus aimed to free everyone from the Symphony and from hatred through his own sacrifice. He is glad to finish that wish. The quest takes its name from a movement of Orff's Carmina Burana.
