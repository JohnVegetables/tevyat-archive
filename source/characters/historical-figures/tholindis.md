## summary
### LITE
A Khaenri'ahn researcher in the Universitas Magistrorum's Moon Marrow lab and the woman Rerir loved. She secretly descended from the Crimson Moon Dynasty, helped other descendants escape, and vanished into the moon's reflection on the day the Cataclysm began.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through memories in the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest chapter (Nod-Krai, 6.1) and as a surviving soul in 6.3. Her story explains why Rerir became a Sinner.
### ULTRA +
Canon shows her as practical, wry and loyal: she hides her ancestry, keeps loving a man whose job is hunting people like her, and tries to fix history alone rather than ask him to choose.

## background
### LITE
Tholindis worked in the Moon Marrow lab at the Universitas Magistrorum. She was a distant relative of a Crimson Moon branch that had cut ties generations before, and only her teacher knew. She was in a relationship with Rerir, a royal agent.
### STANDARD +
She hoped Moon Marrow could give Khaenri'ah an alternate energy source. When King Irmin moved most of the Universitas to Abyssal research, her team was reassigned and she was left alone. She found that Moon Marrow works as a gateway to a place where time runs backwards, but could not hold it open with the power available. Once she learned what Rerir did, she kept the secret, spared him a confrontation and quietly aided fellow descendants.
### LARGE +
Before the Cataclysm she gave Frithr, a young Crimson Moon descendant leaving the city with her parents, a piece of the moon to keep, saying the bloodline would never die out. On the day Dainsleif led the assault to free Vedrfolnir and Abyssal energy flooded Khaenri'ah, monsters appeared in the Universitas and she had no weapons to hand. She opened the gate and jumped. Rerir arrived as she did, was told that she had found a way to end it all, and could not stop her.
### ULTRA +
In the reflection her body was torn apart and only her soul remained. For about five hundred years she wandered without finding an exit. Columbina reached her in 6.3, bringing news of Rerir's remorse and his message; Tholindis shared everything she had learned about the reflection, then accepted that she and Rerir share the same space but cannot meet.

## status
### LITE
Dead in body since the fall of Khaenri'ah. As of 7.1 her soul persists in the moon's reflection, separated from Rerir by time.
### STANDARD +
Her research helped Columbina move through the reflection toward the past. Nothing after 6.3 shows her again.

## relationships
### LITE
Rerir: her partner, loved despite his work. Frithr: a descendant she protected. Columbina: gave her news of Rerir and received her research.
### STANDARD +
King Irmin and Vedrfolnir shaped her fate from afar; she blamed Irmin's obsession for the disaster.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A young woman with short green-blonde hair and a golden hairpiece. She wears a white short dress with detached sleeves under a dark blue layer with gold trim, belted at the waist, and white ankle boots. Her eyes are ordinary purple rather than the Crimson Moon's red, which let her hide her lineage.

## speech
### STANDARD
Her lines are brisk, teasing and warm with Rerir ("You can be such a child sometimes"), formal in lab reports, and anxious in private. She calls Columbina "Miss Columbina". No fixed catchphrase.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
If ever portrayed: practical and curious about the world beyond the gateway; guarded about her ancestry; protective of people she shelters; honest in hindsight about her own secrets. In the reflection she is resigned, grateful and gentle toward Rerir.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She did not die by Rerir's hand. She jumped into the gate herself.
- She was not an Abyss follower or a member of the Eclipse Dynasty.
- She did not belong to the main Crimson Moon line.
- Do not give her a wedding date or an answer to the proposal.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the Cataclysm: works in the Moon Marrow lab; meets Rerir; he proposes.
- She learns his real job and keeps going; helps descendants hide.
- Khaenri'ah's last day: she jumps into the moon's reflection (shown in 6.1).
- About 500 years later: Columbina meets her soul in 6.3 (True Moon).

## entry: Moon Marrow research

@keys2: research, gateway
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tholindis's work found that Moon Marrow, a remnant of Crimson Moon power tied to a god, serves as a gateway. Opening it takes a specific energy source and then a great amount of power to keep it open, far beyond the Universitas's instruments. Her logs show her wish to see what lay beyond. After Irmin shifted resources to the Abyss, she worked alone.
### LARGE +
The place beyond the gateway, the moon's reflection, turned out to flow backwards in time. Her notes, shared later with Columbina, describe golden energy there that carries a traveller faster.

## entry: The jump into the moon's reflection
@keys: Tholindis, moon's reflection, Moon Gate
@keys2: Rerir, jumped
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
On the day Abyssal energy erupted, monsters reached the Universitas lab. Tholindis opened a gateway and leapt in, telling Rerir she had found a way to give him back a normal life and that their wedding would have to wait. Rerir saw her fall and was broken by it, which led him to take Abyssal power as a Sinner.
### LARGE +
She later admitted she had planned to use the reflection to reach the day the king met the Traveler's sibling. She also wondered whether fear, or the Abyss, had pushed her.
