## summary
### LITE
Phobos is the Grand Symphony Remus created from Sybilla to free Remurians from fate. It became a corrupt guide that led Remuria to war and ruin, and was destroyed in 4.6.
### STANDARD +
Called the "Discordant Symphony", it took in Remurian souls through golden Ichor and gave their owners immortal golem bodies. Its guiding voice is the origin of the war that sank the nation.
### ULTRA +
It is a rare case in canon of a plan against fate that itself became the instrument of doom.

## background
### LITE
Remus and Sybilla made it to grant humanity's wishes, as a system outside the Ley Lines and Heimarmene. It was created from Sybilla's wisdom, but she had no will and fulfilled any request, so Phobos inherited the wish to make all humans happy and satisfied even the Remurians' worst desires.
### STANDARD +
Ascended Remurians heard its instructions. Some believed their eternal lives and golem bodies made them superior to others. Boethius's desire to join the Symphony led it to consume him, and through him it pushed Remuria to war against the Fontaine tribes. Its melodies grew harsh, and Remus, sensing sorrow in the Symphony, woke.
### LARGE +
Remus realised the flaw lay in Sybilla's nature and freed Cassiodor from the Symphony as a failsafe. He composed the Requiem as a self-destruct order and gave it to Scylla, who would feign betrayal and invade the Domus Aurea. Phobos, seeing the plan as a betrayal of humanity's happiness, had Boethius sabotage it. Scylla was sealed, and Remus released the symphony anyway; without Scylla to channel it, massive waves submerged Remuria and killed both Remurians and attackers.
### ULTRA +
Survivors split: Boethius's Golden Troupe tried to revive Phobos while Cassiodor's faction opposed them, and Cassiodor broke Boethius's soul into fragments sealed in the Sea of Bygone Eras. The Belyi Tsar believed the Remurian escape from the heavens' gaze loosened Fate, letting arts pass on through the Golden Troupe and the Marechaussee Hunters.

## status
### LITE
Destroyed for good in Fortune Plango Vulnera (4.6), freeing the souls it held.
### STANDARD +
Before that, it re-emerged beneath Petrichor and replaced locals' souls with Remurian ones. The Traveler played the Requiem for Scylla, allowing Cassiodor, Boethius and the other Remurians to pass on.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Remus made it; Sybilla's nature shaped it; Boethius was its voice; Cassiodor and Scylla opposed it.

## entry: The Requiem
@keys: Requiem, Remus's Requiem
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
The Requiem was Remus's final symphony, an order for Phobos to self-destruct. Scylla was meant to guide the released power and return Remurians to flesh and blood. In 4.6 the Traveler plays it on Scylla's behalf to destroy Phobos.
