## summary
### LITE
Ix Kemonel Jasjatem Oox, the Weaver of Secrets, was the Third of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords. She ruled a territory in the Tezcatepetonco Range and, unlike Lords who oppressed human tribes, allied with those who sought her protection. She aimed to weave an eternal dream-realm in the Night Kingdom for her declining dragons.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through records and item text. She was learned in divination and destiny, and she took a humane, if controlling, line towards her dragons.

## background
### LITE
She understood that the Heavenly Principles fix human fates, and called humans soulless creatures bound to star-signs. She still pitied her dragon subjects, who were devolving into beasts after the War of Funerary Flame. She hoped to harness the souls of humans and dragons to build a blissful dream-realm.
### STANDARD +
Her friend, the Priestess of Floral Candles and Skyborne Feathers, feared the plan would bring humans more suffering. The Weaver nonetheless made a pact with her tribes, forerunners of the Masters of the Night-Wind: she would build a mirror labyrinth and mist fortress if they appointed a Holy Sovereign to put the dragons into a dream. She then merged her consciousness into the Night Kingdom.
### LARGE +
As the Priestess predicted, the Holy Sovereign suffered: candidates needed a star-bright soul approved by the Lord of the Night, and were blinded and had limbs removed, dying young. The last, a little girl later called Maghan's Master, was tricked by Kukulkan into the deep mist. The Weaver had foreseen this and led her to the Night Kingdom, showed her visions of a peaceful future Natlan, told her to distrust Kukulkan and the Lord of the Night, and to record history on tapestries so the labyrinth would endure.
### ULTRA +
The girl then guided others to the Night Kingdom, including her servant Maghan, whom she sent to serve Xbalanque. Under Och-Kan the pact was dropped. The dying Maghan told his successor, the young priest Wagadu, to restore it; Wagadu returned Maghan's bow to the Night Kingdom and met the Weaver. She praised Maghan and asked for two things: respect for the Saurians, and that her name be struck from all tribal records so Kukulkan could not plot against her. The first Iktomisaurus is said to have emerged then. Her name uses Mayan-language words (wiki etymology): woman, weaver, secret or whisper, three, soul.

## status
### LITE
Unknown. She merged her consciousness into the Night Kingdom, and canon sources read do not say if she persists. No appearance in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 was found.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Key ties are the Priestess (a friend who objected), Kukulkan (an enemy she outfoxed), her pact tribes, and Maghan's Master and Wagadu, whom she guided and instructed.

### STANDARD
The covenant between the Weaver of Secrets and her human tribes: a labyrinth of mirrors and a fortress of mist in return for a Holy Sovereign to place the dragons in a dream. It cost its sovereigns blindness, limb loss and early death. It lapsed under Och-Kan and was renewed through Wagadu.
