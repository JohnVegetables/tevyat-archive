## summary
### LITE
Skybracer was an adeptus of Liyue, a deer-formed illuminated beast, who died in the Archon War. He is remembered for a legend told at the Lantern Rite: to save the village below Mt. Tianheng from a collapsing mountain, he had his own antlers, a gift of Morax's divine essence, cut off and driven in to hold the peak up, then went back to fight until he died. He is known only through that legend and the deer-shaped Mingxiao Lantern of the 1.3 Lantern Rite.
### STANDARD +
Nothing is recorded of his personality, earlier life or any quest of his own. Everything about him is told second-hand by Madame Ping, who knew him, by the storyteller Xiaoyan and by the scholar Soraya, and the tellings do not fully agree.

## background
### LITE
His antlers, his pride and joy, were by Madame Ping's account made from the essence of Rex Lapis's divine power and were the hardest material in Liyue. When gods' blows made Mt. Tianheng start to collapse onto the village below, he had a friend cut them off and wedged them under the mountain, then kept fighting until his blood and life ran out.
### STANDARD +
Madame Ping adds that the blood he lost turned into the Bishui River, a legend rather than an event anyone witnessed. Soraya, researching the festival, reports that old texts agree he is dead and that he once fought beside Rex Lapis, but disagree on when and against whom. The people of the mountain valley are said, in Xiaoyan's telling, to have thought more of him than of the Geo god, though Xiaoyan is an entertainer and embroiders freely.
### LARGE +
The storyteller's version includes a masked adeptus companion who struck the antlers off with a polearm, and describes the sprayed blood and the grateful villagers. These details come only from him and are not repeated by Madame Ping.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the Archon War; only the legend and the Lantern Rite lantern remain.
### STANDARD +
In the 1.3 Lantern Rite event the city's Mingxiao Lantern was dedicated to him, a huge deer-shaped lantern. Madame Ping hoped the builders made the antlers large enough, since they were his pride, and joked that he was a show-off who would be pleased. Xiao, asked whether he would see it, brushed it off as a short-lived human creation. A search of the soil of Mt. Tianheng in the event found no sign of antler fragments. No later appearance was found as of 7.1.
### LARGE +
A wiki search found no mention of him in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His one confirmed bond is to Morax (Rex Lapis, now Zhongli): the antlers were the Geo god's gift, and he is said to have fought at his side for a time. Madame Ping, who is still alive, remembers him affectionately as vain and proud. Xiao, another adeptus, shows no sentiment toward the memorial lantern. His unnamed friend who cut off the antlers is never identified.
### LARGE +
In the legend his sacrifice is made for the villagers below the mountain, not for the god he served.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Archon War era (over two thousand years ago): Skybracer fights for Liyue; a god's blow splits the base of Mt. Tianheng and he props it up with his antlers, then falls in battle.
- 1.3 Lantern Rite (limited event): his story is told by Madame Ping and Xiaoyan, and a Mingxiao Lantern in his likeness is built. @availability limited.

### ULTRA +
Mt. Tianheng and the Bishui River (which runs through Bishui Plain) are linked to him only by Madame Ping's telling at the Lantern Rite, so treat the connection as local legend. Fellow deer-formed adeptus Moon Carver shares his animal form, but no relationship between the two is stated.
