## summary
### LITE
Petronilla was a Marechaussee Hunter who took in and trained the child Clorinde, later Fontaine's undefeated Champion Duelist. By night she was the black-cloaked vigilante known as the "Protector of Justice". She disappeared on Clorinde's tenth birthday and her fate is unknown.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through memory: Clorinde, Navia and Furina recall her in the Silent Night story quest (4.7), and Clorinde's character stories add detail. She never appears in person.

## background
### LITE
By her own account Petronilla rescued Clorinde, abandoned by her birth parents, from people of ill repute. She was a Marechaussee Hunter and passed on the Hunters' art of marksmanship, close-quarters swordsmanship, tracking and concealment. In the city she was a hero of the night whom the Gardes could never catch.
### STANDARD +
Clorinde remembers her as fascinating but inexperienced at raising a child. Her first toy was a short sword, and at five she was given a Geovishap Hatchling brought from Liyue as a pet, with the rule that any dispute be settled by a fight and the loser was "the real pet". Clorinde lost once and won a week later. Training included a room of rotating pillars studded with cloth-capped wooden blades. By six Clorinde could find her way through a forest and by seven she hunted monsters alone. In between, Petronilla took her travelling to meet friends.
### LARGE +
Clorinde believes Petronilla never knew her own parents, which she thinks is why the older woman knew no other way to raise a child; she never heard her speak of them. Petronilla also told Clorinde of a Hunter who, after the last monsters were gone, lost his purpose and left the Hunters to keep hunting alone; her comment was that a sword that forsakes its scabbard can only rust away. She once said that if Clorinde could beat her, she would hear out Clorinde's dissenting views.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Nothing official is known of her face or build; the wiki's appearance section is empty. Clorinde remembers her wearing a black cloak on her night-time outings, and wanted posters of the vigilante covered the city walls.

## status
### LITE
Missing since the night of Clorinde's tenth birthday. As of Version 7.1 canon has not said what happened to her.
### STANDARD +
She did not come to collect Clorinde from Navia's house that night. Clorinde searched her rooms and old training grounds and waited several days before asking Callas for help, but even he could find nothing. Petronilla left Clorinde a dark blue cape with the Marechaussee Hunter insignia (not the black one she wore), a necklace engraved with Clorinde's name, her sword, and one of the Hunter keys handed down through the generations.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's account of Buried Honor, Clorinde later used Petronilla's key, together with the key of the Hunter Florian and two others, to open the Marechaussee Hunters' vault, which held a stone tablet of Hunter names and their old, broken weapons. Clorinde's outfit incorporates the inherited cape. Furina dates the vigilante's activity to about twenty years ago.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Clorinde was her ward and apprentice, and the bond is the centre of her story. Callas, founder of the Spina di Rosula, was a close friend who knew her secret; through her visits Clorinde met his daughter Navia.
### LARGE +
The girls learned the "Protector of Justice" secret when they overheard Callas address Petronilla by the title. They pretended not to have heard, though Clorinde nearly let it slip once. Navia remembers a shy young Clorinde hiding behind Petronilla at their first picnic. The Gardes whom the vigilante outwitted would have recognised the black cloak and wanted posters.
