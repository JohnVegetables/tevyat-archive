## summary
### LITE
Uenuku, the Treader of the Rainbow, was a hero of the People of the Springs and the first known bearer of the Ancient Name Umoja, "unity". Legends about his bond with a water sprite disagree on how his story ended.
### STANDARD +
He is counted among the original Six Heroes of Natlan, who stood beside Xbalanque against the dragonlord Xiuhcoatl. Later Meztli lore calls him the Rainbow King; his descendant Tupac and, five hundred years after Tupac, Mualani carried his Name.

## background
### LITE
Uenuku was a young warrior from the People of the Springs who set out to protect the weak. A water sprite from a distant land blessed his weapon and travelled with him. He joined the Grand Alliance but eventually left it.
### STANDARD +
The legends have four main beats. A fairy, an Oceanid messenger of the Mistress of Many Waters, met him under the moonlit springs and offered a blessing and her company, asking only that when he became king she receive "the spring where vicious beasts prowl". He saw that she meant to use him but agreed to fight with her until they betrayed each other. She advised him to abandon his village; the justice he followed was the kind that could "unite" the weak. Dozens of winters later, the text says, he realised that the faith he held at the start was what he regretted most.
### LARGE +
Notes found in the Old Core of Chu'ulel say Uenuku left the Grand Alliance for a reason left blank, and that the Oceanids who followed him left with him. The same notes show that Och-Kan, the Alliance's leader, wished they had stayed. In Heretic's Molten Blade he is a teasing voice among Xbalanque's drinking companions, when the heroes boast over who did the most against the dragons. His quip to the flute player Maghan is the only line attributed to him.
### ULTRA +
Modern Meztli keep his example alive: a dive-obsessed young man, Aukai, quotes him and says he plunged into the sea to rejoin his beloved, and a surfing-training enthusiast, Rongo, attributes to him a saying about winning hearts being as hard as conquering the tides. The Footprint of the Rainbow text says the shells seen today are fragments of the rainbow that fell into the sea, and that their two connected halves symbolise the wish to be reunited. Treat both as folk memory.

## status
### LITE
Long dead as a historical person; canon does not say how he died. He lives on as the first bearer of Umoja and in Meztli legend.
### STANDARD +
Nothing about him has appeared in canon since the 5.0 texts, and nothing places him in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His central tie is to the Oceanid, the Hineila (or Ianeira) of the legends. His descendant Tupac, Xbalanque and the Grand Alliance's leaders are the only other figures linked to him.
### LARGE +
Whether the Oceanid loved him, used him or both depends on the version told. In the dragon-flame version she shields him and leaves a rainbow; in the Meztli scrolls he is the one who pushed her away in suspicion.

## entry: The Oceanid and the Rainbow
@keys: Hineila, Ianeira, Uenuku's Oceanid, Rainbow King legend
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two accounts exist. In the first, the water sprite hoped to use a worthy king to purify Natlan's springs of Phlogiston. She grew fond of Uenuku despite herself, and when a dragonlord's fire was about to engulf him she took the flames in his place, leaving only rainbow-coloured vapour. In the Meztli scrolls, the two fell in love beneath the moon but were separated by his suspicion; after decades of searching he followed a rainbow she raised to the far side of the sea, where, blessed by the Sacred Flame, they were never parted again. The weapon text itself says the legends have many origins.

## entry: Umoja
@keys: Umoja, Ancient Name Umoja
@keys2: Uenuku, Tupac, Mualani
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Umoja, "unity", is the Ancient Name of the People of the Springs. Uenuku was its first bearer; Tupac held it in the cataclysm, and Mualani holds it now. Footprint of the Rainbow ties the idea to Uenuku's justice that could unite the weak.
