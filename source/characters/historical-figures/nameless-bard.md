## summary
### LITE
A boy of Old Mondstadt, about 2,600 years ago, who played the lyre and longed to see birds fly beyond the hurricane wall of the God of Storms Decarabian. He raised the revolt against the tyrant with a tiny wind spirit and a handful of allies, and died as the tyrant fell. The spirit became Barbatos, the Anemo Archon, and remade himself in the boy's likeness.
### STANDARD +
He has no voiced lines of his own and no recorded name in plain text. Everything about him comes from Venti's own story quest, the character stories, and weapon and artifact texts. The wiki calls him 'the Nameless Bard'.
### ULTRA +
He is the hidden centre of Venti's story: the reason the Anemo Archon has a human face, plays a lyre, wanders as a bard and treats freedom as something that must be chosen. A 6.3 artifact text adds that his last act was a melody that became a nation.

## background
### LITE
He lived inside Decarabian's storm wall in Old Mondstadt, a city ringed by gales so fierce even birds could not pass. He played the lyre, wanted to write the greatest poem, and had never seen a blue sky, an eagle or green grass. He befriended a nameless wind spirit, one thread of the Thousand Winds, who would later become Barbatos.
### STANDARD +
He wished aloud to see birds in flight and asked the spirit to come along. The spirit found him an eagle's feather, but he fell in the war for freedom before it could be given. His songs, once played in the shadows of dark alleys to give courage to the downtrodden, spread resistance. When the uprising came, he led it with the spirit, the huntress Amos, a knight (identified by the wiki as Gunnhildr) and a red-haired warrior. Decarabian died believing his people loved him, and his tower fell, ending the storm wall.
### LARGE +
Sources differ on details of his death. Character Story 4 says he fell in the fighting 'for song, sky and birds'. The 6.3 texts Heldenepos's Unspoken Tale and A Note in Spring's Leich say that after Decarabian was cast down, his dying storm threatened the survivors; the frail boy, unskilled in war, strode into the wind playing his lyre, and the bard strummed the strings of the Master of Time, stunning even the Mother of the Thousand Winds, and turned the storm into a thousand-year song named Mondstadt. His mortal body could not carry a nation's song and fell with the collapsing tower, and his name was swept away by time. The wind spirit claimed a strand of time and the name.
### ULTRA +
Item texts add texture. Freedom-Sworn describes a drinking song whispered in cellars and alleys, later sung by the weak who could not climb the tower, which carried the group upward. Dawn's Brilliant Oath says the warrior sent to smother the rebels laid down his sword after hearing his lyre. The final lines of A Note quote a farewell, evidently his: do not grieve, for folk will go on singing of tomorrow while the winds blow. The 6.2 sword The Daybreak Chronicles lists "the Nameless Shepherd" among the bearers of the rebel moniker Kreuzlied, and says the final Kreuzlied (Barbatos) was born from the wish of a certain youth. In the 1.0 Archon Quest, Venti says that before he became an archon, a friend taught him the meaning of freedom.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Described only as a frail youth or boy with a lyre. The wiki notes from a cinematic that he wore a three-petaled cecilia on his vest and plucked his lyre with his left hand. Canon gives no height, age or hair colour in the sources read. Venti's body is built in his likeness.
### LARGE +
The cecilia detail comes from a wiki trivia note on the flower, not an in-game text read here. His strong eyes 'filling with light' is Venti's own rhyme.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Venti, the wind spirit, was his closest friend and carries his shape. The huntress Amos, the knight Gunnhildr and the red-haired warrior fought beside him; Decarabian was the tyrant they overthrew.
### LARGE +
Istaroth, the Master of Time, owned the melody he borrowed at the end. The artifact text places the spirit's claim on his name on the day the Mother of Time and Wind bestowed her authority; the wiki reads this as Time granting authority to the spirit. Venti keeps him as a private grief: in the Carmen Dei story quest he falls silent after reciting a song first heard from this friend.
### ULTRA +
The red-haired warrior and Amos are linked to him by item texts rather than by direct scenes. Venti's own ballads about Barbatos are invented, per Character Story 2, but in the Carmen Dei quest he tells the true story of the boy to the Traveler and Paimon, and after the Traveler sends Paimon off, recites the boy's song to the Traveler alone.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 2,600 years. He died in or just after the fall of Decarabian's tower. No grave, remains or ghost are shown. Nothing in canon through 7.1 revives or contradicts his death.
### STANDARD +
He is remembered only through Venti and a few item texts. Mondstadt's history credits the Anemo Archon, not him. His final melody is described as the origin of Mondstadt as a nation and as an ideal: 'all who hold freedom in their hearts are its musicians'.
### ULTRA +
The 6.3 artifact set A Day Carved From Rising Winds, released in Nod-Krai's Luna IV, is his latest appearance. No 7.0-7.1 material was found to mention him.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not give him a name stated in plain text. The name Venti is strongly implied, not spoken.
- Do not give him a voice, catchphrases, an age or a romance. He is a mentioned figure only.
- He is not Barbatos, and Venti is not 'possessing' him. The spirit chose to wear his shape.
- Do not turn him into a chosen one or Vision holder; canon gives him neither.
- He was one of five rebels, not the sole hero. Amos, the knight (Gunnhildr, per the wiki) and the red-haired warrior matter too.

## chronology
### LARGE
- About 2,600 years ago: boy lives in Old Mondstadt and befriends the wind spirit; shown in Venti's flashback (1.0).
- During the Archon War: he raises revolt with the spirit, Amos, a knight (identified by the wiki as Gunnhildr) and the red-haired warrior (Amos' Bow, 1.0; Freedom-Sworn, 1.6).
- Decarabian falls; Amos dies loosing the last arrow; the bard plays the Master of Time's melody against the dying god's storm and dies as the tower collapses (6.3 texts).
- Immediately after: the spirit ascends as Barbatos, remakes himself in the boy's likeness, scatters the snow, and declares Mondstadt a city with no king (Character Story 4).
- Present day: Venti tells the Traveler the story in the Carmen Dei Chapter (1.0) and the artifact lore deepens it (6.3).

## entry: The Last Song
@keys: A Note in Spring's Leich, A Day Carved From Rising Winds, symphony of a thousand years
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In A Note in Spring's Leich, the Nameless Bard strums the strings of the Master of Time as Decarabian's rule collapses. A violent storm becomes a thousand-year poem of wind and colour, named Mondstadt, and 'all who hold freedom in their hearts are its musicians'. His body cannot bear a nation's song; it falls with the tower and his name is lost.
### LARGE +
The text says even the Mother of the Thousand Winds was stunned. The wind spirit, on the day the Mother of Time and Wind bestowed her authority, claimed a strand of time and the name time could not save. It ends with a farewell: take this breeze, with a yearning for joy and dreams of liberty, and do not grieve.

## entry: The Eagle's Feather
@keys: eagle's feather, feather of an eagle
@keys2: Venti, Barbatos, Nameless Bard
### STANDARD
Venti obtained an eagle's feather for the boy who had never seen a bird. The boy died before he could receive it, and the feather stayed in the spirit's breast pocket as he watched Decarabian fall. Canon does not say whether this is why Venti's Anemo often appears as feathers.

## entry: The Rebels of Old Mondstadt
@keys: Freedom-Sworn, Amos' Bow, Dawn's Brilliant Oath, rebellion against Decarabian
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
Item texts name five who stood before Decarabian's tower: a youth, a spirit, an archer, a knight and a flame-haired warrior. Amos' Bow lists a nameless young man, an elf and a knight with the huntress, while Freedom-Sworn adds the red-haired warrior as a fifth. The weak who could not climb sang the rebels' drinking song, which echoed through the city.
### LARGE +
Dawn's Brilliant Oath (6.3) says the warrior yielded to his songs, and that Amos, the taciturn huntress, gathered 'the winds scattered across the long night' for the boy. Heldenepos's Unspoken Tale (6.3) says Amos loosed the last arrow at the cost of her life. Amos and the warrior have their own entries.

## entry: Fly, Fly Away
@keys: fly, fly away, bird in the sky
@keys2: Venti, Nameless Bard, Barbatos
### STANDARD
At the end of the Carmen Dei story quest, Venti says he first heard a song from his lost friend and recites four short lines: fly away like a bird in the sky; see the world on my behalf; may you fly to the heavens. The scene goes quiet. It is the only song of his that Venti quotes.

## entry: Venti's Face
@keys: Venti's face, Barbatos' first act, took the form of a friend
@keys2: Nameless Bard, wind spirit
### STANDARD
Barbatos' first use of his new power, per Character Story 4, was to remake himself in the boy's likeness, so that he could play the lyre the boy loved. Venti says he took 'the form of a friend', comparing it to the false Stanley who borrowed another man's name, a parallel the quest does not spell out further.
