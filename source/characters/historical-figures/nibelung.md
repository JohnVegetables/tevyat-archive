## summary
### LITE
Nibelung was the Dragon King, a primeval dragon born with Teyvat who ruled it before the Heavenly Principles. He made the Three Moons to govern while he searched the stars for a defence against the Abyss. He came back bearing the Abyss, waged the war of funerary flame about 6,000 years ago, and was destroyed.
### STANDARD +
He is counted as the Second Descender, and Aksinya calls him the true Primordial Human, a seat that no one has filled since. He never appears in person. Everything known comes from dragons who served him, the moon goddesses he made, murals and old books, and these sources disagree about whether he was a saviour, a tyrant or both.
### LARGE +
His war shaped the present world. The Heavenly Principles won only at great cost. They dropped Celestial Nails that ended the unified civilization, and they were weakened enough that they later made the Gnoses to keep the world in check. Many dragons still revere him; Neuvillette thinks he was wrong.

## background
### LITE
Born alongside the planet, he ruled the dragons and the Seven Sovereigns. Warned that the Abyss would destroy his world, he refused to abandon his people. He raised three moons from the crust of what is now Nod-Krai to run the world's cycles, then left for the sea of stars to find an answer.
### STANDARD +
The warning came from Anacharsis, a mind-travelling chronicler from beyond the stars who had watched the Abyss destroy many civilizations. She urged the "kind, gentle king" to leave with her. He replied that his people were the meaning of the universe to him, that his bones would shield the realm, and that he would lead them to the stars. The dragons' pursuit of space travel grew from that vow. The moon goddesses, Aria, Canon and Sonnet, were made as shadows of the planet and extensions of his will, and held his authority over the Light Realm. For thousands of years they waited in vain for him to return. Meanwhile the Heavenly Principles arrived, remade the world and took over the vows by which the moons turned it.
### LARGE +
During his absence, Xiuhcoatl went to the three lunar thrones to ask where the king had gone and met only silence. Dragon researchers on the Frost Moon kept praying for him: a terminal there reads "May the Creator be forever crowned with supreme glory". The pioneer Ibni-Belum built a deep-space receiver after catching a signal he believed was Nibelung's. Records call him "Father Nibelung". When he did return, he tore open the false sky. He brought the Abyss with him and woke the dragons to a war of vengeance. Apep says he gained the power of darkness from outside the world and led them against the outsiders' order. Kukulkan says that when the Dragon King descended "from beyond the circles of this world", the Abyss called Xiuhcoatl forth from his feigned death. Quest visions show a once-grand figure, now pitiable, sunk into shadow: gargantuan dark wings and black flames scorching the land while the white tree nearly withered.
### ULTRA +
The war was the Funerary Year of Before Sun and Moon, when "the second throne of the heavens came, and war was rekindled". The heavens collapsed and the earth split, and that is how Enkanomiya sank. When the moon sisters argued over whether to follow him, he judged them disloyal, imprisoned all three in the "moon's reflection", and turned their moons into weapons of terror. The Heavenly Principles took this as a breach of their peace. They shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and flung the Frost Moon beyond the false sky, and the goddesses later died. In 6.2 Nicole said no one knew whether they ever escaped his prison; in 6.3 the sisters, made mortal, escaped it with Columbina's help and went back to the war, where their moons' fall followed. Xiuhcoatl woke from his feigned death at his old master's call, stopped the Thirteen Sovereign Lords and Kukulkan from exploring the stars and trying to forge another moon, and Natlantea's ley lines burned as its angels were devoured. Kukulkan says the reaver annihilated the "great black-winged lord at great cost". In that moment Xiuhcoatl regained a brief, tragic clarity, but by then he had already become a breeding ground for darkness.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no full description. After the Abyss took him, quest text describes gargantuan wings "from beyond the world" and a once-glorious figure sunk into shadow. Apep remembers his scales shining bright, and a mural in Ochkanatlan shows a pale dragon above the Three Moons, so he was apparently not always dark. Any portrayal should keep him vast and draconic, and alien after his corruption.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Heavenly Principles: usurpers who took his world and his moons' vows while he was away; his enemy in the war that killed him.
- The Three Moon Sisters (Aria, Canon, Sonnet): his creations, who waited for him for ages, then hesitated when he returned. He imprisoned them and weaponized their moons.
- The Seven Sovereigns and dragonkind: his vassals. Apep stayed loyal beyond his death, Xiuhcoatl was his old servant, and many dragons still call him Creator or Father.
- Anacharsis: the star-voyager who warned him and asked him to flee; he refused.
### LARGE +
- Hyperborea and the Belyi Tsar: humans who tried to create a god equal to "The One" from the Cryo Dragon's corpse. The Tsar was the incomplete result (see the Primordial Human entry).
- Neuvillette: a reborn Hydro Sovereign. He quotes old words calling the world the realm "of the Formidable Father" now ruled by "a cluster of filthy feathers", yet privately thinks King Nibelung was wrong, and that the Abyss can be opposed only if all life stands together.
- Columbina: the sisters he imprisoned passed their lunar authority on to her. Before meeting Neuvillette she called him "another dragon who puts people in prison", a jab at Nibelung's jailing of the sisters.

## status
### LITE
Dead. The Heavenly Principles destroyed him at the end of the war of funerary flame, about 6,000 years ago.
### STANDARD +
His seat as Primordial Human has stood vacant ever since, according to Aksinya. Attempts to fill it include Hyperborea's Belyi Tsar project (7.0). The Abyss he brought into Teyvat is still there.
### LARGE +
As of 7.1 nothing suggests he survives. His legacy is still active: the surviving Frost Moon has since been brought back inside the false sky, Columbina holds the moons' authority, Apep and other dragons keep their grudge, and the Heavenly Principles remain weakened by the war.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not a playable or living character, and he never speaks on screen. Do not write him dialogue as if canon supplied his voice; the only words attributed to him are his reply to Anacharsis in an artifact text.
- Not simply evil: his sources call him a kind king who refused to abandon his people, and also a destroyer who brought the Abyss and jailed his own creations. Keep both.
- Do not confuse him with Durin (the black dragon of the cataclysm 500 years ago) or with any Dragon Sovereign.
- Do not say the Heavenly Principles killed him cheaply; every account stresses the great cost.
### LARGE +
- His motive for embracing the Abyss is not settled; do not state that he was purely corrupted or purely calculating.
- He is not one of the Seven Sovereigns; he was their king.
- Do not invent names, appearance details, family or a romance. "Father" and "Creator" are titles of reverence.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Primordial age: born with Teyvat; rules the dragons and Seven Sovereigns.
- Anacharsis warns him of the Abyss; he vows to lead his people to the stars.
- He raises the Three Moons over present-day Nod-Krai and departs for the sea of stars.
- Thousands of years pass; the Heavenly Principles arrive and take over the moons' vows.
- About 6,000 years ago (the Funerary Year): he returns bearing the Abyss; the war of funerary flame. He imprisons the moon sisters and weaponizes the moons, then is annihilated; Celestial Nails fall.
- Revealed in game: 2.4 (allusion in Before Sun and Moon); 3.6 (named by Apep); 4.1 (Neuvillette); 5.0-5.5 (Natlan murals, Kukulkan, Anacharsis artifact); 5.8 (Fractured Halo); 6.0-6.3 (Nod-Krai Archon Quest and the moon sisters); 6.5-6.7 (Istoria; Moon Gazing); 7.0 (Primordial Human revelation).

## trivia
### ULTRA
- The Frostmoon Scions' hymn calls him Takoja Ianikuinen, Primeval Blacksmith and Eternal Workman, "lord of two natures", and both begs and fears his wrath. Its imagery of a forged bow, chariot and moon-shuttle echoes the Finnish smith Ilmarinen, who in the Kalevala bears the same epithet. The name Nibelung comes from Germanic legend and Wagner's Ring cycle, which also gave Teyvat the names Alberich and Rhinedottir.

## entry: Anacharsis's warning
@keys: Anacharsis, Seutervoinen, Deep Gallery's Distant Pact
@keys2: Nibelung, Dragon King, lord of dragons
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
An artifact text tells how a chronicler from the sea of stars, whose body slept far away while her mind travelled, was woken from ages of meditation by the brilliance of Teyvat and its primeval dragon. She told its ruler she had seen the Abyss tear through star clusters and silence all songs, and asked him to leave his doomed world with her. He thanked her but refused: to him his people were the meaning of the universe, his bones would shield the realm, and he would lead them to the stars. When she returned, he had vanished "like drifting smoke", the land was bound by four shackles, white light had been split into seven elements, and the Winged One ruled the three moons. ## entry: The moon sisters' imprisonment
@keys: moon's reflection, Moon Sisters, Three Moon Goddesses
@keys2: Nibelung, Dragon King, imprisoned, weapons
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
According to Nicole, the moons were born of Nibelung's will. The Heavenly Principles let them stay because they kept the planet working. When he returned, the sisters split: one wanted to follow the one who first hung them in the sky, and another feared that taking sides would ruin the world, especially as he now carried the Abyss. He discovered their disloyalty, imprisoned all three and made the moons weapons that terrified all living things. The Heavenly Principles shattered two moons and cast the Frost Moon beyond the sky.
### LARGE +
In 6.3, inside the moon's reflection, where time runs backwards, Columbina met the sisters at the moment of their imprisonment. Canon told her this was where "Teyvat's original ruler, the Dragon King Nibelung" had jailed them. Aria said he had never taught them an order like the Heavenly Principles', and that they had only just decided on countermeasures against it when he came back. They took the news of his destruction and their moons' fate calmly and chose to help Columbina.

## entry: The Primordial Human seat
@keys: Primordial Human, Narayana, The One
@keys2: Nibelung, Dragon King, Belyi Tsar, Hyperborea
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 7.0 Aksinya told the Traveler that Hyperborea had tried to forge the Belyi Tsar from the Primordial Cryo Dragon's flesh to give him "authority equal to The One". The One was Nibelung: a will born alongside Teyvat, the master of the Light Realm's power, and King of all Dragon Lords. She said the place of Primordial Human, or Narayana, has been vacant since his destruction. The experiment ran out of control, froze Snezhnaya and drew down a Celestial Nail.
### LARGE +
The Notes by a Certain Noble Individual found in the Pale Crown Palace tell it differently. Hyperborea did not know that "the will which once ruled the ancient universe" was not yet extinguished, so no one could take the throne it had prepared. The Notes also trace the noble writer's own birth to a primordial first light. Both accounts agree the project failed to make a true successor.

## entry: The dragons who waited (Project Moongazer)

@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
On the Frost Moon, dragon researchers kept faith with their absent king. The pioneer Ibni-Belum caught a mysterious deep-space signal and believed it was Nibelung's. He built a transceiver station to catch it again, but it never returned and his students later converted the station for lunar monitoring. Another, later base was built "from the will" of the "Lord of Darkness Who Returned From the Sea of Stars": it drew on the moon's core to turn the moon into a space-based weapon, and the Traveler took this to mean the returned Nibelung.

## entry: Apep's loyalty
@keys: Apep
@keys2: Nibelung, Dragon King
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Apep told Nahida that the dragons once believed only forbidden knowledge could defeat the Heavenly Principles, and that the Dragon King brought back the power of darkness and led them against the outsiders' order. The victors won the right to shape the world. After his death Apep kept gathering forbidden knowledge until a Celestial Nail turned her forests to desert. Some dragons have since befriended humans and forgotten, but she says his scales and will still shine in her heart, and she will follow them to the end.
