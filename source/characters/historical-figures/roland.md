## summary
### LITE
Roland, a noble-born Knight of Favonius under Arundolyn 500 years ago, was taught by Rostam, the Wolf Pup. The White Knight was expected to become the mightiest Knight of Boreas, but blood blackened his armor, he became the Bloodstained Knight and left Mondstadt for good.
### STANDARD +
Item texts trace the rest: he found Khaenri'ah's cursed people, called their fate the ultimate injustice and pledged himself to the Abyss. In 6.4 an Abyssal imitation wearing his name leads the Wild Hunt in Nod-Krai and Mondstadt, while his real final act, sealing his last comrades' resting place, is shown in a spirit scene.
### LARGE +
He is the second time the Boreas lineage nearly died out. Few knights today know his story; it is kept by high-ranking Knights of Favonius, and the public mostly knows him only through artifacts and tales.

## background
### LITE
Noble-born, trained by Rostam in chivalry, justice and swordsmanship, he leapt at every wrong, saved a maiden who loved him and took only a white flower. Slaying monsters, he came to feel his kills as justice served and ignored a warning that slaughter in justice's name is still slaughter.
### STANDARD +
After the cataclysm 500 years ago, which cost him many comrades including Rostam, he and loyal knights went west. His armor, sword and ornaments turned black with blood, and the people he saved fled from him as from a monster. A few knights and one pale-furred wolf kept following; he asked them to stop, then allowed it because he had no right to forbid them. They died in battle near Kipumaki Cliff, and he sealed their resting place with a trace of Boreas's power.
Going on to the fallen kingdom meaning to die fighting its monsters, he learned the monsters were its cursed people, named that the ultimate injustice and swore himself to the Abyss.
### LARGE +
At some point after this, he met a hunter whose long slaughter of Abyssal beasts had made her beast-like, judged her no different from a monster and killed her before continuing west to answer the crime of turning people into beasts (Hunter's Path text; the wiki names her the Viridescent Venerer).
Because he gave himself to the Abyss, the Abyss in turn learned everything from him, which is how it can cast his image.
### ULTRA +
In the Final Hour text, the timepiece he kept deep in the Abyss, he has recognised that there is no place for him on the earth. The Bloodstained Chevalier's Goblet text recalls the turn: he returned from a battle to find the people he had come to rescue gone from the burning houses, and vowed to rid the world of monsters, poverty and evil. The Iron Mask text adds that a person he had just helped cowered from him, making him realise he had become a monster himself.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the real Roland is presumed dead, with no recorded end after he joined the Abyss in Khaenri'ah. In 6.4 only an Abyssal imitation of him fought Varka, and it dissolved.
### STANDARD +
His sword is among the swords laid out in the Ley-Line space Varka crosses, standing alone among the memorial blades of earlier Knights of Boreas and Grand Masters. Varka left Rostam's unsent letter at its foot. A wiki search found no 7.0-7.1 content about him.
### LARGE +
Wild Hunt leadership shifted in 6.4: after Rerir was expelled, the Abyss created the imitation to lead it, and with the imitation gone the Wild Hunt cannot consolidate for the foreseeable future (Varka).

## relationships
### LITE
Rostam, his mentor; Arundolyn, the Lion of Light, who could not move him; Boreas, who respected him; Varka, the current Knight of Boreas, his unmet heir.
### STANDARD +
Rostam's lessons are the source of both his strength and his rigidity. Rostam and Arundolyn, Varka insists, never forgot him. His only followers at the end were a few knights and a wolf.
### LARGE +
The Maiden Beloved texts record the woman he saved: she waited for him for years, and never finished telling him how she felt (the text breaks off at 'But my heart is already-').
### ULTRA +
The Black Sword's tale and the Bloodstained Chivalry texts agree that the people he saved came to fear him. Boreas, by contrast, names him a man whose lifespan 'should be no longer than any other of his kind'.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The White Knight wore burnished white armor with a mirror-bright sword; he later wore an iron mask on a noble face. Blood stained the armor, sword, cape and a raven-feather plume black. Texts do not give his face, hair or age.
### LARGE +
The Black Sword's guard resembles the circlet from the Bloodstained Chivalry set. In the 6.4 monument scene he removes his mask, but the camera hides his face.

## chronology
### LARGE
- 1.0: The Bloodstained Chivalry, Maiden Beloved and Black Sword texts first tell his story without always naming him.
- 5 centuries before the main story: serves under Arundolyn and Rostam; the cataclysm.
- After the cataclysm: goes west; deaths of his followers at Kipumaki Cliff; seals their rest; journeys to Khaenri'ah and pledges himself to the Abyss.
- 6.4 (Luna V): named in Triumphant Warrior, Lonely Warrior and the Nameless Bloodstained Monument.
### ULTRA +
- 3.0: Deepwood Memories and Hunter's Path texts add the blind brother and the hunter.

## entry: The Abyssal imitation
@keys: Roland's imitation, Wild Hunt, Lonely Warrior
@keys2: Roland, Varka, Boreas
@version: 6.4
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Rerir was expelled, the Abyss made an imitation of Roland to lead the Wild Hunt and infested Andrius's remains. It speaks of rectifying the world in the name of the Bloodstained Knight and keeps a frozen heart and mind from Roland's peak. In Lonely Warrior, Varka lets it assimilate his soul along with Boreas's, which the imitation had taken as a decoy; the thoughts of friends in Mondstadt then pull him out, and the imitation dissolves. It tells him no words can make a knight doubt his convictions.
