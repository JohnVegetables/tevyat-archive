## summary
### LITE
Founder and first president of Fontaine's Spina di Rosula and father of Navia. A respected, generous man who was charged with murder, called "Callas the Unfaithful", and killed in a trial by combat three years before the Fontaine story. Navia later proved he had been framed.
### STANDARD +
He is never seen alive in the game, only through the memories of those who knew him. Spina members, Clorinde and Neuvillette all speak of him with deep respect.

## background
### LITE
A tall, broad-shouldered, blond man of Poisson with an anchor-marked eyepatch, who built the Spina di Rosula into an organisation that helped people and looked for the truth where the authorities would not. He sponsored Fontaine's aquabus network, married Clementine and raised Navia. A murder charge and a duel ended his life.
### STANDARD +
He refused an honorary title the Palais Mermonia wished to grant him after the aquabus lines were completed; Navia guesses he feared a title would set the Spina apart from the common folk it worked with. His partnership with the Court of Fontaine and the Fontaine Research Institute over the aquabus split the Spina: Romeuf and many others left on bad terms, resenting his whole family. Three years before the story he banned the drink Sinthe, which was secretly diluted Primordial Seawater, and began investigating its sellers.
### LARGE +
His reasoning for the aquabus, as old Spina members recount it, was that cooperating with the Court and the Institute would build valuable relationships and help Poisson; after Clementine died he treated finishing the lines as her final wish, which ended hopes of reconciling with Romeuf's group. The Spina was prospering in those years and he was generous with it. He won over Jacques, a Sinthe seller, who gave him information; Jacques's superiors ordered him killed, and when Jacques could not do it, another assassin was hired.
### ULTRA +
At a countryside banquet he went out for air with Jacques following. Two shots rang out, and the guests found Callas holding a gun and Jacques dead. His own gun had in fact been left on the table (a Spina member checked); the real sequence, as Navia reconstructed it, was that the assassin shot Jacques, turned on Callas, and was killed when Callas wrestled the gun away. The mastermind then had the assassin's body dissolved in Primordial Seawater, which the public did not know could happen to a person, so no evidence of the true events remained. Facing the court at the Opera Epiclese he would have had to testify against the Sinthe producers, which would have put Navia at risk; instead he chose a trial by combat with Clorinde, refused to yield even when badly hurt, and died.

## relationships
### LITE
Navia (daughter, now second president of the Spina); Clementine (wife, died after Navia's birth); Clorinde (the duelist who killed him, to whom he entrusted his daughter).
### STANDARD +
Marcel posed as his devoted benefactor while secretly running Sinthe, and was unmasked by Navia. Melus, Silver and veteran Spina members stayed loyal to his memory. Neuvillette admired him and later went to his grave to apologise.
### LARGE +
Romeuf and his faction left the Spina over the aquabus funding dispute. Colter and Florent, old Spina members, speak warmly of his generosity and trust; Florent says he keeps calling him "Mr. Callas" in spite of the public epithet, and credits Callas's trust for his own rise in the Spina.

## status
### LITE
Dead. Killed by Clorinde in a trial by combat about three years before the Fontaine Archon Quest. His name was cleared when Navia exposed Marcel at the Chapter IV trial.
### STANDARD +
After the trial the Spina held a long-delayed memorial for him, attended by everyone in Poisson and many people from the Court; afterwards Navia and the Traveler visited his grave and found Neuvillette there paying his respects. In the story quest Shimmering Memories (4.3), Navia and Poisson residents plan a statue of Callas and Clementine funded by donations; it had not been possible while he was known as "the Unfaithful".
### LARGE +
No appearance or new information about him was found in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content read for this entry. His legacy lives on in the Callas Line of the aquabus and in the Spina under Navia, who says she intends to lead the Spina of today rather than copy her father.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He did not kill Jacques; the second assassin did, and Callas killed that assassin.
- He did not lose the duel through weakness: he chose to die and would not yield.
- Do not give him an age, birthday or Vision; none are stated.
### LARGE +
- Do not write him as present or speaking in current scenes: he has no voiced appearance, and what is known comes from others.
- He refused the honorary title the Palais Mermonia offered; he never held a noble title.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the story: founds the Spina di Rosula; marries Clementine; backs the aquabus project, and the Spina splits over it.
- After Navia's birth: Clementine dies; he resolves to complete the aquabus as her wish.
- About three years before the Fontaine story: bans Sinthe and investigates it; a banquet shooting leaves Jacques dead; he is charged and called "Callas the Unfaithful"; he dies in the duel with Clorinde.
- 4.0: his story is revealed in the Chapter IV Archon Quest, ending with Marcel's unmasking and his memorial.
- 4.3: Navia's story quest includes the planned statue.
### ULTRA +
- Neuvillette says he did not stop the duel because he believed an innocent man would never throw away his life, and that Callas's sacrifice later allowed the serial disappearances case to be solved.

## entry: Callas the Unfaithful
@keys: Callas the Unfaithful, Callas' case, Callas's case
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The nickname Poisson gave Callas after he was arrested over Jacques's death at a banquet. Benefactors withdrew support and the epithet outlived him for years, so a statue of him would have been too controversial. Navia, the Spina and old friends such as Florent refused to use it. Navia cleared his name at the Chapter IV trial.

## entry: Callas and Clorinde's duel
@keys: Callas duel, trial by combat, Clorinde duel
@keys2: Callas
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Callas opted for a duel with the Champion Duelist Clorinde instead of standing trial at the Opera Epiclese, and asked her to look after his daughter before it began. He did not yield even when wounded, knowing that Clorinde's honour would then require her to finish the duel. Clorinde swore later that he never intended to leave the ring alive. The duel left Navia with years of confusion and a strained relationship with Clorinde, because Callas had told her nothing.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette says he did not intervene because he believed an innocent man would never give up his life, and that Callas proved him wrong.

## entry: Sinthe and Marcel
@keys: Sinthe, Marcel, Vacher
@keys2: Callas
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Sinthe, sold in Poisson, was Primordial Seawater watered down, with severe long-term effects on drinkers. Callas banned it. Marcel, who owed his business to Callas and professed loyalty, was secretly behind it. Callas's silence kept Navia safe: Marcel did not move against her for years because Callas's associates held information they could pass to the authorities if she were harmed.

## entry: Aquabus and the Callas Line
@keys: Callas Line, Clementine Line, aquabus
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Spina sponsored the construction of Fontaine's aquabus system at Callas's urging. The Callas Line is named for him, the Clementine Line for his wife and the Navia Line for his daughter. The Callas Line, which ran to the Fontaine Research Institute, is out of service.
