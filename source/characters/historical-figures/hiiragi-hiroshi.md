## summary
### LITE
Head of the Hiiragi Clan and Kanjou Commissioner about five hundred years ago. He is remembered for creating the trading port of Ritou on a deserted island, a feat that impressed the Shogun.
### STANDARD +
He wanted foreign trade centralised under Inazuma's government and proved the benefits by importing foreign inventions. He is known only through item text, a voiceover line and a quest.
### LARGE +
His friendships crossed the human and spirit worlds: a Kitsune Saiguu counted him a friend, and a famed swordsman taught him.

## background
### LITE
Born into the Hiiragi Clan, he rose to lead it and the Kanjou Commission. He built Ritou from an empty island.
### STANDARD +
To show that a government-run port would pay, he imported foreign inventions. These included a Snezhnayan blast furnace, used to make the hot springs of Tenshukaku as a gift for the Shogun, and a special Kamera that recorded memories and emotions. The Kamera was built with a demon-exorcising mirror that the Kitsune Saiguu had given the Hiiragi Clan.
### LARGE +
After the cataclysm, while still young, he trained in swordsmanship under Iwakura Michihiro, known as Douin, the Kujou Clan's sword instructor. He joked about the swordsman's secret blade and said that, if the Kujou had not moved first, he would have recruited him.

### ULTRA +
The Ornate Kabuto text shows him as playful in his youth, asking his teacher whether the sword could cut lightning. The Hakushin Ring text, in the Kitsune Saiguu's last thoughts, is the source tying him to her.


## status
### LITE
Dead; a historical figure of about five hundred years ago.
### STANDARD +
His clan survives and still heads the Kanjou Commission, and Ritou remains Inazuma's trading hub.
### LARGE +
No appearance or mention was found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content up to 7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
The Kitsune Saiguu (friend), Iwakura Michihiro (sword teacher), the Shogun (ruler he impressed).
### LARGE +
The Kitsune Saiguu remembered him among the friends she made as a mortal, naming the Kanjou who worked hard for the city's prosperity. The Hiiragi Clan held her gift of a mirror.

## knowledge
### LITE
He lived about five hundred years ago and knows nothing of later events.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not the modern NPC Hiroshi.
- Do not give him an age, a death or a personality beyond the confident, teasing manner in the Kabuto text.
- Ritou was not a natural port: it was built from a deserted island.

## chronology
### LARGE
- About 500 years ago (after the cataclysm): trains under Iwakura Michihiro.
- Builds Ritou; imports the blast furnace and Kamera.
- Later: the Kitsune Saiguu recalls him as a friend in her last moments.
