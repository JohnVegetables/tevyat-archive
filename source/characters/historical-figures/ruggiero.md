## summary
### LITE
Ruggiero was a Fontainian scholar who set out to prove the Grand Symphonies existed, came to Petrichor looking for the legendary Faded Castle, and later rebuilt the town and founded the Consilium Sapientium, becoming its first Doge. He is long dead.
### STANDARD +
Today's Doge, Tiepolo, credits him with restoring the town's fortunes. Scholars like Bravais admired him, though Bravais thought Fontaine's poor academic climate meant his work was never fairly judged.

## background
### LITE
Ruggiero collected special conches as part of his search for proof of the Grand Symphonies. He sensed a strange energy lingering around Petrichor, then a run-down fishing village, and went there hoping to find the Faded Castle. There he met the Golden Hunter.
### STANDARD +
The Hunter's knowledge of Remuria contradicted what was commonly taught, since he linked the Marechaussee Hunters, Remuria and the Lochknights as one era although history placed them apart. Ruggiero privately suspected he was a surviving Remurian but could not explain his immortality, and raised the Lochknights only obliquely because the Hunter seemed unwilling to discuss them. The pressing task was stopping something stirring at the seals, so the history questions were left for later.

Searching the Castle, Ruggiero found only ordinary storehouse relics; the Hunter said the thing they first sought had sunk beneath the sea with Capitolium, and most relics were the Golden Troupe's. The priority became sealing the place. He marked on a map where the relics lay in case someone might return.
### LARGE +
In his final note Ruggiero says he understands everything: people should not control knowledge beyond their abilities, for knowledge pursues people rather than the reverse. He proposed rebuilding the abandoned town, drawing in refugees and spreading other versions of the legend so that they would guard the rebuilt seals of their own accord, an idea he never told the Hunter before the Hunter left. A wealthy adventurer, Juliano, says in an inscription that Ruggiero's teachings eased his emptiness and that he spent his fortune helping Ruggiero rebuild the town, hiding the remainder in four chests.

## status
### LITE
Dead by the present day; he died several hundred years before it.
### STANDARD +
His legacy is the Consilium Sapientium, the elders' council that assesses all of Petrichor's affairs and from which the Doge is chosen, and the town itself. His notes in the Faded Castle, and Bravais's annotated copy of his research, survive.
### LARGE +
Tiepolo says townspeople of the time blamed the old decline on too-close ties with the outside world. How far today's customs follow Ruggiero's design is not stated.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Cassiodor, the Golden Hunter, was his partner in the Faded Castle and left Ruggiero to look after Petrichor. Juliano financed the rebuilding and honoured him as a sage.
### LARGE +
Bravais, a later scholar, annotated Ruggiero's research notes, calling him a true great master in his time. Tiepolo, Petrichor's current Doge, is his successor in title only.
### ULTRA +
In The Fundamentals of Music (a hidden exploration objective), Tailleferre says that records left by a Ruggiero several hundred years ago place something she needs within the Faded Castle, showing his writings are still used by researchers. This detail is cited via the wiki's infobox and was not read in the quest itself.

## entry: Consilium Sapientium
@keys: Consilium Sapientium, Doge of Petrichor
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Petrichor's council of elders, founded by Ruggiero. Its most trustworthy member is chosen Doge. Tiepolo describes it as upholding tradition in a quiet, custom-bound town.
### LARGE +
Petrichor's Venetian-style titles recall the Republic of Venice, a link the wiki notes as a probable reference rather than anything in canon.

## entry: Ruggiero's Notes
@keys: Ruggiero's Notes
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Three notes in the Faded Castle: one on the Golden Hunter's puzzling knowledge of Remuria, one on the failed search for relics and the decision to seal the place, and one on his decision to give up the search and rebuild Petrichor.
