## summary
### LITE
Menogias, titled General Kapisas, was one of the Five Foremost Yakshas who fought for Morax against the demons of the Archon War. Beyond his battle record he is remembered for a stubborn, refined hobby: designing elaborate clothing, which Rex Lapis admired.
### STANDARD +
He died long ago, probably in a fight with fellow yaksha Bonanus. The people who knew him, Xiao and Xianyun among them, recall his taste and obstinacy more than his deeds in war. A lantern in his image was the star of Liyue's 2024 Lantern Rite (limited event).

## background
### LITE
Menogias answered Rex Lapis's summons to cleanse the demons and plagues born of fallen gods. He was counted among the five strongest yakshas, and like Bonanus and Indarias he fell to the karmic debt the work piled up: canon implies he and Bonanus killed each other.
### STANDARD +
The book Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti presents him as one of the five who followed Rex Lapis into battle until the evil was wiped out at its source. Off the battlefield he was the one yaksha with a taste for ornament. He tailored garments for other adepti, choosing fabrics, colors and accessories with care, and held firm opinions on them. Xiao later says the clothes were intricately made but inconvenient to wear, and that Bosacius never hid his distaste.
### LARGE +
Rex Lapis was fond of his designs and kept many. Xiao says (in a limited event) that the suit Zhongli wears in his mortal life was designed by Kapisas, though Xiao never saw him wear it during the war. Xianyun tells (limited event) of a skirt he made for Bonanus: she complained quietly to other adepti that its length would hinder her in battle, but he insisted the train would heighten her adeptal dignity, and he would not be moved even by Rex Lapis on such matters. In Bosacius's memory in 2.7 he is shown trying to coax the four-armed Bosacius into a purple robe with two sleeves.
### ULTRA +
Xiao quotes a saying of his: live in the present and enjoy each pleasant surprise. Xiao is unsure whether the general meant it as advice about clothes for people around him. The wiki's reading that he and Bonanus killed each other rests on the memory cutscene in which Bonanus is shown impaling him; the book itself says only that three of the five perished. When Moon Carver misses old friends in the 4.4 Lantern Rite event, he says he wishes Menogias lived to see the peace; he imagines him running a kite stall that would earn profit and esteem, and bringing him wine to drink together on days he could not join mortal affairs (limited event).

## relationships
### STANDARD
Fellow Foremost Yakshas: Bosacius, Bonanus, Indarias and Alatus (Xiao). Rex Lapis was his patron and best-known customer. Bonanus wore his work and grumbled about it; Bosacius resisted his suggestions. Xiao and Moon Carver miss him.
### LARGE +
Xianyun remembers him in connection with adepti gatherings where his dress designs were discussed. Mountain Shaper tells Moon Carver those days are now fond memories. No Zhongli voice-over checked mentions him, though the outfit Zhongli wears as a mortal is Menogias's work.

## abilities
### STANDARD
One of the five strongest yakshas, so a formidable warrior. Canon gives no details of his fighting. His association with Geo rests on cinematic imagery and a voice actor's statement. His craft, tailoring and design, is the skill canon actually describes.

## status
### LITE
Dead, long before the present story. Not mentioned in 6.x Nod-Krai or 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya content.
### STANDARD +
His name survives mainly through Xiao, Xianyun, Moon Carver and the clothing he designed. At the 2024 Lantern Rite (limited event) the Mingxiao Lantern was made in his yaksha form, and the in-game character Musheng, asking who the lantern commemorates, is told it is Menogias, a deadly yaksha who was also well-versed in clothing design and tailoring.

## entry: Menogias and the Mortal Suit

@availability: limited
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
In the Exquisite Night Chimes event (3.4, limited), a gathering Hu Tao hosts at Xinyue Kiosk, Xiao tells the Traveler that Rex Lapis liked Kapisas's designs and collected many, and that the outfit he now wears as a mortal was designed by the general. Xiao never saw Rex Lapis wear it during the war and had not expected him to start later. The wiki lists the outfit under Zhongli's Hermit of Mortal Life look.

## entry: The Skirt for Bonanus
@keys: Bonanus's skirt, train of the skirt, Menogias tailor
@keys2: Menogias, Kapisas, Xianyun
@availability: limited
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
In the 4.4 Lantern Rite event quest (limited), Xianyun, searching for tailors for the festival, recalls that Menogias made Bonanus a skirt that she told other adepti was too long to fight in. When asked, he said the train would bring out her adeptal bearing as a mark of elegance. Xianyun adds that not even Rex Lapis could out-stubborn him when clothing was concerned.

## entry: Bosacius's Last Memories
@keys: Fantastic Compass memory, At Tunnel's End Light, Nameless Yaksha memories
@keys2: Menogias, Kapisas, Bosacius
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the 2.7 Chasm quest At Tunnel's End, Light, the Fantastic Compass preserves Bosacius's dying mind. In his maddened state he calls those around him by his comrades' names, Menogias among them. His clearing memories show the five yakshas walking together, Menogias trying to put a purple robe on him, and the others' fates, including Bonanus impaling Menogias. Xiao identifies the five generals by title: he is Alatus, Menogias is General Kapisas.
