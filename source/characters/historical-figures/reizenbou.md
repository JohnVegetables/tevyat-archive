## summary
### LITE
Reizenbou was the Great Tengu of Mt. Yougou and chieftain of the Yougou Tengu about five hundred years ago. She mentored three young trainees (the Yougou Three), bore a daughter, Teruyo, and served as bodyguard to the Kitsune Saiguu after losing a race to her. Her bow Thundering Pulse, a gift from the Shogun, passed to the swordsman Takamine the Mistsplitter through a bet.
### STANDARD +
When the cataclysm took the Kitsune Saiguu, Reizenbou blamed her own failure to protect her, left her daughter behind and withdrew into the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates, refusing to face humanity again. She appears only in item texts and an underground puzzle area, never in person.

## background
### LITE
She became head of the Yougou Tengu at some point after the earlier Great Tengu Sasayuri died. She was given Thundering Pulse, a bow made by the Raiden Shogun. Young people came to Mt. Yougou to learn tengu arts from her, including "true magic" taught in secret. After the Kitsune Saiguu beat her in a race (suspecting Reizenbou let her win) she guarded the fox envoy.
### STANDARD +
Three trainees, Asase Hibiki, Kamuna Harunosuke and Mikoshi Nagamasa, studied under her at Mt. Yougou; Hibiki and Harunosuke also grew familiar with the Grand Narukami Shrine and the Kitsune Saiguu in those years. Takamine, a wandering swordsman, bet his young body against Thundering Pulse; the contest ended in a draw (three wins each), so he became her servant but kept the bow, and she taught him tengu archery, which she apparently did not teach the Yougou Three. She gave birth to Teruyo around the time she was teaching them. A hamayumi bow text records that Hibiki felt sorry toward "her Master, the Tengu" for using the magic she had taught.
### LARGE +
Her mark on the age is also left in boasts. The Redhorn Stonethresher text has the Otogi Kongou Lion King claiming that even the Great Tengu of Mt. Yougou feared his weapon and would not face him; the text itself says the children never believed these tales. The Hopeful Heart text, in the voice of the shrine maiden Hibiki, calls the tengu "inhuman" and says she used to kill all day for fun, and implies that having Teruyo brought out her gentler side.

## personality
### STANDARD
Item texts call her rambunctious and fond of fighting (Shimenawa's Reminiscence) and impatient (Thundering Pulse). She apparently let the Kitsune Saiguu win their race, which suggests loyalty and some generosity beneath the bluster. These are all second-hand impressions from other people's recollections.
### LARGE +
A shrine maiden's recollection credits her motherhood with drawing out gentleness, in the Lady Kitsune's teasing words ("had the mother inside her brought out... just a little"). Her wrath after the cataclysm was directed at herself, not at others: she was enraged at her own incompetence as protector.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Master of tengu archery, wielding the thunder-charged bow Thundering Pulse, which Takamine remembered as fierce and unpredictable yet beautiful: soaring through clouds and loosing thunderbolts with each pulse of the string. She also taught a form of "true magic" and swordplay of the tengu.
### LARGE +
Thundering Pulse is said to retain its lustre though soaked in darkness; it was made by the Raiden Shogun for her. After she lost it in the bet, she was no longer known to hold it, and the bow later passed to Hibiki.

## relationships
### LITE
Close with the Kitsune Saiguu, whom she protected. Teacher to the Yougou Three; mother of Teruyo; opponent turned master of Takamine.
### STANDARD +
Reizenbou left Teruyo behind when she went into exile. Teruyo, who later renamed and duelled Iwakura Michihiro on Narukami Island, inherited her mother's "beauty and skill" according to Takamine's remark in the Storm Cage text. The Shogun's gift of Thundering Pulse implies that she had earned the favour of the Raiden Shogun, though how close she was to Makoto or Ei is not stated.

## status
### LITE
Not seen alive in the present. After the cataclysm she withdrew into the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates beneath Araumi and refused to appear before people again. Her ultimate fate is not stated.
### STANDARD +
The Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates is a one-time domain whose description calls it a tengu warrior's final abode, and says the boat became the cage in which the person spent the rest of their days. Shaft of Remembrance records that she went into this exile enraged at her failure as Lady Saiguu's protector, leaving Teruyo behind. Earth Kitsune Statue lines near Araumi record Harunosuke and others worrying about where "Master Tengu" had gone while she was out fighting.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the cataclysm: becomes chieftain of the Yougou Tengu after Sasayuri; is given Thundering Pulse.
- Loses a race to the Kitsune Saiguu; becomes her bodyguard.
- Mentors the Yougou Three; bears Teruyo; makes the bet with Takamine and takes him on as servant and archery pupil.
- Cataclysm, five hundred years ago: fights the monsters; goes with the Kitsune Saiguu to the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates (traces of their presence are found there by Hibiki and Harunosuke); the Kitsune Saiguu is lost.
- After: self-imposed exile in the Empty Boat; leaves Teruyo behind.
- 2.0: first appears in the game through the Inazuma item texts and the Araumi area.

### ULTRA +
Source-by-source: her motherhood and chieftaincy come from Shimenawa's Reminiscence and the wiki's Reizenbou page; the bet and the bow from Thundering Pulse; the race from Hakushin Ring (in the voice of the Kitsune Saiguu, who calls herself the victor and adds that Reizenbou "must have let me off easy"); the exile from Shaft of Remembrance and the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates domain text. None of these is a first-person account by Reizenbou herself, so her motives are always reported by others.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not turn her into a villain: she is a grieving guardian, not an enemy. Do not give her any lines in the present day; canon does not show her alive. Do not assume she is the "Tengu" of the Otogi King's boasts in a literal sense. Do not state that she is dead; canon leaves her fate open. Do not identify the Shogun who gave her the bow as Ei or Makoto without noting the ambiguity.

## entry: Reizenbou and Thundering Pulse
@keys: Reizenbou, Thundering Pulse
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Thundering Pulse is a thunder-crackling longbow made by the Raiden Shogun and gifted to the Great Tengu Reizenbou. She lost it in a bet to the swordsman Takamine, who left it with Asase Hibiki as the stake in a promise to return from the Abyss. Hibiki received it after he did not come back and later used it to pierce him when his darkened remains returned.

## entry: The Yougou Three
@keys: Yougou Three, Asase Hibiki, Kamuna Harunosuke, Mikoshi Nagamasa
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Hibiki, Harunosuke and Nagamasa travelled to Mt. Yougou to learn tengu arts under Reizenbou. After the cataclysm, Reizenbou went into exile, Harunosuke left for another country in grief, and Nagamasa joined the Shogunate to clear the Mikoshi name.

## entry: Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates
@keys: Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A one-time domain below Araumi on Narukami Island, reached through underground passages. The description calls it the final abode of a tengu warrior and the cage in which she spent her despairing remaining days; a puzzle area outside shows traces of her and the Kitsune Saiguu.
