## summary
### LITE
Bosacius, titled Marshal Vritras, was the eldest and leader of the Five Foremost Yakshas, the adepti who served Rex Lapis against the monsters born of fallen gods. A four-armed Electro warrior and a commander, he was consumed by karmic debt, forgot his own name, and died sealed in The Chasm during the cataclysm. Legends of that battle remember him only as the Nameless Yaksha.
### STANDARD +
Xiao, the last of the Five, calls him both a formidable fighter and a talented commander. Before his madness he withdrew from the world and left a sealed hoard in Minlin; at the end he chose to hold the line against the monsters of Khaenri'ah at the cost of his life.
### LARGE +
His fate stayed unknown for centuries and troubled Xiao, who finally found the truth in The Chasm in the Interlude Chapter (2.7). There, Bosacius survives only as memory: an illusion of him fights Xiao, and his last hours are replayed through a catalyst called the Fantastic Compass.
### ULTRA +
Almost every detail comes through Xiao, the spaces that read minds, or items of folk lore, so accounts disagree on small points. Treat him as a proud, dutiful leader whose mind failed before his body did, and who regained himself in the final moments.

## background
### LITE
When the Archon War left the remains of dead gods to fester into monsters and plague, Morax summoned the yakshas to destroy them. Bosacius, Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus (later Xiao) became the five strongest, and he led them by strength and seniority. The yakshas' work stained them with karma, and one by one most were lost.
### STANDARD +
According to the book "Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti", the Five followed Rex Lapis wherever battle went until the plague was purged; after about a thousand years three of the five had died and one had vanished. Bosacius was the one who vanished. His tablet at Minlin says he followed his Lord to fight the pestilence, that Liyue is now at peace, that only he and Alatus remain, and that he wishes to leave this world; he left his treasure sealed there for anyone fated to take it. Xiao says the last time he saw Bosacius was the day he lost his mind, after which he simply went away and was never heard of again.
### LARGE +
Five hundred years ago the monsters of Khaenri'ah invaded Liyue through The Chasm, and Bosacius, drawn by the bloodshed (Xiao's guess), joined the Millelith in defending it. Wiki accounts, built from the quest memories, say he fought beside the thaumaturge brothers Boyang and Rongzhao, and that to stop the invasion they used the Fantastic Compass, a space-bending catalyst that needs a mortal and an adeptus to power it. Bosacius and Boyang stayed behind, sealed in with the monsters. Bosacius's artifact lore sings of a four-armed yaksha who arrived at The Chasm to the tribes' praise, entered it blade in hand, and was lost as the gorges collapsed.
### ULTRA +
The Compass recorded the sealing; its recording has Bosacius say he will hold the line by sealing the surface. Down in the sealed space he often called Boyang 'Menogias' or 'Alatus' and could not say who he was, while Boyang tried to recall him to himself. At the end he saw his four lost siblings carrying lights, remembered them, and gave his name and his destiny in one breath. An illusion of him in the Realm of Snaring Illusions shouts that monsters must not reach the surface and orders people to hold the line sixty miles from The Chasm.

## status
### LITE
Dead. He died in the dark beneath The Chasm during the cataclysm, about five hundred years ago, and only illusions remain. No appearance in any content up to Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Xiao confirmed in the Interlude Chapter that the Nameless Yaksha of the battle was Bosacius. He did so by fighting a phantom of him, which dissolved, and by taking a stone from The Chasm that he meant to place in the temple of Pervases in his memory.
### LARGE +
His body was the only one of the Five never found before then, and Xiao learned of his end only through the Compass's memory. No later content (6.x Nod-Krai, 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya) mentions him.

## relationships
### LITE
Leader and eldest of the Five Foremost Yakshas; Xiao (Alatus) is the survivor who sought his fate. Served Morax. Died alongside the Millelith thaumaturge Boyang.
### STANDARD +
He was proud and dutiful in Xiao's memory, and once made the yakshas promise to look after each other and to know each other's fate to the end. In his last visions Bonanus, Indarias, Menogias and Alatus call to him as family.
### LARGE +
Boyang, an ancestor of Yelan, was his companion in the dark; he also watched several comrades kill one another through karmic madness, a history that weighed on him.

## entry: The Nameless Yaksha of The Chasm

@keys2: Bosacius, Chasm, yaksha
@version: 2.7
### STANDARD
Legend tells of an unknown yaksha who fought fiercely beside the Millelith when the monsters of Khaenri'ah invaded The Chasm. He was Bosacius, who no longer remembered his own name, so survivors could not say who he was. Xiao investigated in the Interlude Chapter and confirmed it.
### LARGE +
Xiao reasoned at first that Bosacius, being proud, would have announced his name had he joined that war, and that the unknown fighter could not be him. The Compass memory proved otherwise.

## entry: The Fantastic Compass and the sealing of The Chasm
@keys: Fantastic Compass
@keys2: Bosacius, Boyang, Chasm
@version: 2.7
### STANDARD
A catalyst of Boyang's clan that can manipulate space. Bosacius and Boyang used it to seal the invading monsters, and themselves, in the depths of The Chasm. It needed constant power from both a mortal and an adeptus, and it recorded their last moments.
### LARGE +
The Compass is also what lets the Realm of Snaring Illusions replay memories. In the quest it is found whole in a space built from the Traveler's own memory, and Yelan recognises its design as like the catalysts of her clan.
