## summary
### LITE
Yelena was a former Fatui member and the elder sister of Kirya. To spare him the rapid aging that a Delusion inflicts, she deserted with him and settled in Fontaine, where she posed as a Sumeru scholar named Vijava. She grew the flower used in the Auguste perfume, which drove Fontaine's 'Perfume Mania' about twenty years ago, and died in a fire she set to hide the secret.
### STANDARD +
Her story is told entirely in retrospect through Emilie's Story Quest, 'Floral Debt, Blood Due' (4.8), mainly via Kirya and the surviving partners.

## background
@version: 4.8
### LITE
Yelena and Kirya were Fatui. A Delusion had already begun aging her body, and she took her younger brother and fled to Fontaine. There she used the Delusion to cultivate a flower with distorted elemental energy.
### STANDARD +
She worked with the Fontainian perfumer Edgar and the merchant brothers Sylvain and Lucien. Their perfume, Auguste, sold at prices up to ten million Mora, spurring an economic bubble. Kirya's existence was kept a closely guarded secret.
### LARGE +
The Marechaussee Phantom investigated the extravagant prices. Fearing exposure of the Fatui past, and too weakened by overusing her Delusion to flee, she burned the flowerbeds and died in the fire, so no trace of a Delusion would remain. Edgar, Sylvain and Lucien were imprisoned for the bubble; Kirya escaped.
### ULTRA +
Her efforts to hide her deteriorating condition from Kirya are why he blamed the other three for her death. Over the next two decades he plotted both to take revenge and to learn the truth behind her suicide, culminating in the events of 'Floral Debt, Blood Due'. His student Emilie foiled his attempt to end his own life with Auguste as atonement.

## relationships
### LITE
Her bond with Kirya defined her. Sylvain says she doted on him. She hid how badly her health was failing, which led Kirya to blame her partners for her death.
### STANDARD +
Her business partners Edgar, Sylvain and Lucien knew her as Vijava; they have no separate entries in this archive.

## status
@version: 4.8
### LITE
Dead for about twenty years as of the Pomum de Ambra Chapter; she died by self-immolation to protect the perfume's secret.
### STANDARD +
Weeks after the quest, Emilie released a modified Auguste named 'Yelena' in her honour. Kirya concluded her death was ultimately his own fault, since she died protecting him. Nothing in versions through 7.1 adds more.

## entry: Yelena and Auguste
@keys: Yelena, Vijava, Auguste perfume
@version: 4.8
### STANDARD
Auguste was the most popular product of Fontaine's Perfume Mania, built on a Delusion-cultivated flower. Yelena, as 'Vijava', supplied it. Her death to hide its origin set off Kirya's twenty-year revenge plot.
### LARGE +
Emilie later reproduced Auguste's accord with improved techniques; its three scents are Rainbow Rose, Glaze Lily and Cecilia.
