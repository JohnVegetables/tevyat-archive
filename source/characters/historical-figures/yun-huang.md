## summary
### LITE
Yun Huang was the eighth head of the Yun Family, a Liyue house of weaponsmiths, about five hundred years ago. The only child of Yun Hui, she was a headstrong martial artist who took over the forge herself when her father joined the Liyue Qixing. With Han Ce she is one of the two central figures in the Blackcliff weapon descriptions.
### STANDARD +
She is known only from those item texts: a woman who inherited a family business at a hard time and, with Han Ce, found a way to keep it alive.

## background
### LITE
Huang had been a headstrong martial artist since childhood. When her father stepped down to join the Qixing, family custom said she should marry and let her husband take over; she refused and took the forge herself, which caused gossip. Soon afterwards Han Wu's mining accident in the Chasm and a run of earthquakes closed the blackcliff mines on which the Yun business depended, and she feared the family legacy would end with her.
### STANDARD +
One sleepless night she prayed for a miracle. Han Ce, Han Wu's wandering son, then came with a wooden box of his father's revised Prototype plans and a bow of blackcliff, saying that as the trouble came from blackcliff it would end with blackcliff, and suggesting she try the bow since the Yun house liked archery. Her arrow roared across the sky like thunder and she took it as her prayer answered.
### LARGE +
Later a man who called himself a thaumaturge came to Qingce Village, where she was living, looking for the Yun and Han craftsmen. She sent for Han Ce, and the thaumaturge gave them a piece of crimson Cor Purum as thanks for their fathers' work on Prototype Amber. With it they made Blackcliff Agate, a catalyst that glowed to warn of tectonic movement along the ley lines; it was placed at the foot of Mt. Tianheng for people to see. When the earth quietened, the Agate vanished without a trace.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; her death is not described. What remains is the Blackcliff line of weapons and her house, which later drifted from smithing towards the performing arts.
### STANDARD +
Yun Jin is a later member of the same family, whose generation has few forgers left.

## relationships
### LITE
Her father Yun Hui raised her as a martial artist. Han Ce, son of her father's partner Han Wu, became her ally and co-creator.
### STANDARD +
Han Ce had a rift with his father over the family trade and healed it after the Chasm accident, then vowed to keep the forge fire alive. He and Huang made the Agate together as the children of the two men behind Prototype Amber.

## chronology
### LARGE
- After the Cataclysm: her father and Han Wu make the Prototypes; Hui later joins the Qixing and Huang takes the forge.
- A time of earthquakes: Han Wu's accident in the Chasm, then the mines close.
- Han Ce brings her the Blackcliff Warbow.
- The thaumaturge's gift: Blackcliff Agate is made.
### ULTRA +
The sources give no dates for any of these steps and do not say how long she led the forge. A wiki search found no Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content mentioning her as of 7.1.
