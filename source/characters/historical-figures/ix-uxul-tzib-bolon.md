## summary
### LITE
Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon, nicknamed Ixlel, was the Ninth of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords and ruler of Chichen Uctokah, the city later renamed Ochkanatlan. Och-Kan sealed her in a stone statue in the Old Core of Chu'ulel for thousands of years. In Open Your Heart to Me (5.2) the Traveler freed her soul at her own request.
### STANDARD +
She is a quest-only figure with no playable form. Proud, formal and sharp-tongued, she insists on her rank, yet near her end she is weary and bitter about the memories that bound her.

## background
### LITE
She ruled Chichen Uctokah since before human civilisation took hold in Natlan. When Och-Kan took the region and raised Ochkanatlan, he excavated the pit where she lay, found her, and imprisoned her for good. He tore out her heart, which holds her memories, and hid the pieces.
### STANDARD +
According to the wiki's reading of Holy Sovereign's Notes and related item text, she first named Och-Kan the fated Sovereign for his royal blood and shared her knowledge of the ancient dragon empire, including a ladder to the firmament and the collapse of the Three Moons. Och-Kan found this terrifying: he believed humanity would be crushed if dragons regained power, and decided to strip her of authority. Ochkanatlan's systems were modelled on those of her city, so only a dragon could run them. For a time he drained her energy for his own city until her defiance made him build a new Core of Chu'ulel that he could control.
### LARGE +
Workers digging out the well suffered hallucinations and disease; all but their leader Manqu died, and Och-Kan had the survivors burned. Artisan's Memo text describes her appearing to Manqu in human form as a young woman with crystal skin and turquoise eyes whom he found unsettling. After her confinement, Abyss-aligned monsters, the Tenebrous Mimiflora among them, appeared around the Old Core and a strange illness began to transform people nearby. Canon does not say whether she caused this.
### ULTRA +
Her pride runs through everything she says in the quest: she tells Paimon to use her title, calls Paimon an elemental floating object, and says she would have received the Traveler in a Dragonlord's or mortal's guise had the statue not held her. Paimon's nickname Ixlel sticks over her objection. She says destruction is the destined path of the Lords.

## status
### LITE
Dead. In Open Your Heart to Me (5.2) she asked the Traveler to gather the fragments of her heart. She poured her last energy into the heart to restore it, and the Traveler took it. This released her soul. She has no known appearance in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Och-Kan is the central figure: first her chosen sovereign, then her jailer. She regards Ajaw and the other lost Lords as colleagues and the Traveler as the Chosen of Dragons, uniquely entitled to release her. The wiki links her creation to Xiuhcoatl through the Smoldering quest.

## entry: Open Your Heart to Me

@keys2: Ixlel, Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon
@version: 5.2

### STANDARD
In this world quest the Traveler enters the flooded depths beneath Ochkanatlan, lowers the water, finds Ixlel's statue and collects three Ancient Jadestone Fragments of her heart. She asks them to cut out her restored heart so that she can die free; her energy has about thirty winters left and she refuses to die as a prisoner. The quest rewards the Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon.

## entry: Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon
@keys: Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon, Clustered Storage Array
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
A flawless piece of jade that was once Ixlel's heart. It held the authority to command devices in Ochkanatlan and stored her memories, but its energy is now exhausted.
