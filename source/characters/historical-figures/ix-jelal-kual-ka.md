## summary
### LITE
Ix Je'lal K'ua'l Ka', the Jade Maiden, was the Second of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, a dragon ruler of ancient Natlan. She is remembered as a hard-line councillor and as the artisan who forged the bow Chain Breaker for the human girl Lianca. She is known only through records and item text, never in person.
### STANDARD +
She appears in Council Records found in the Chamber of Deliberation. There she is severe about weakness: she judged fallen Lords unworthy and voted to execute a colleague on the smallest suspicion of betrayal.

## background
### LITE
After the Seven Sovereigns fell, Je'lal and her fellow Lords tried to copy moon forging to reach other worlds, until Xiuhcoatl stopped them. The wiki lists her as an artificial ruler created by him.
### STANDARD +
With Kukulkan, they had tried to reach worlds beyond Teyvat; Xiuhcoatl, who had feigned death, stopped them when Nibelung returned.
When Kukulkan's plan to give phlogiston to humans came out, she proposed the joint impeachment of the Regent. It passed, but the session ended when Xiuhcoatl entered the Chamber of Deliberation. Awanyu later says the Lords did not truly believe Kukulkan had harmed dragons and wanted his authority.
### LARGE +
At some point the Eighth Lord asked her to forge a bow for her human daughter Lianca. Je'lal was outraged, yet did it, producing Chain Breaker, whose item text credits a dragon artisan. After Lianca's bow defeated the Sixth Lord, Je'lal told the council he had been felled by a human, and said the Tenth Lord, sealed after Ahpub and Ixquieh copied her jade token, fell through his own weakness; she declared both unworthy to remain sovereign rulers.
### ULTRA +
In the vote on executing the Eighth Lord for raising Lianca, she voted in favour. She did not think the Eighth Lord a traitor, but held that even a tiny chance of betrayal was intolerable, and the Eighth Lord respected that logic. Her name is built from Mayan-language elements (wiki etymology): woman, beauty, jewel or emerald, two, and soul.

## status
### LITE
Presumably dead. The wiki reads Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment as placing her among the ten Lords who fell when Xbalanque besieged the Sacred City of Tollan. No appearance in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 was found.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her ties are mostly political: the Eighth Lord (forged the bow for her, later voted to execute her), Kukulkan (impeached), Xiuhcoatl (creator per wiki) and Lianca (her bow's wielder, whose victory she resented).
