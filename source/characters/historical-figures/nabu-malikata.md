## summary
### LITE
Nabu Malikata, the Goddess of Flowers, was a god of Sumeru's desert in the Archon War era and a former Angel who created the Jinn and the first Khvarena. With King Deshret and Rukkhadevata she formed the Three Eternal Companion-Gods. Celestia burned her to ashes for giving Deshret forbidden knowledge.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through the memories of others, item text and relics. She is said to have had beautiful horns and wore an amethyst crown, and in the Chinese and Japanese text the Sabzeruz Festival is named after her ("Birthday of the Goddess of Flowers"). Remains of her body ended up in Collei and were removed in 6.6.
### LARGE +
Much of the desert's later history hangs from her death: Deshret's grief and his Mausoleum, the Jinn's servitude, the Pari, the Orchard of Pairidaeza and, thousands of years on, Aaru replacing Irminsul as the hub of Teyvat's memory.
### ULTRA +
Canon tells her story in layers and from biased voices. Legends, a song attributed to her, Deshret's servants and in-world notes all describe her, and they agree on little about her motives except that she may have chosen her death with open eyes.

## background
### LITE
Made an Angel by the Heavenly Principles, she joined her sisters' failed rebellion and hid beneath the Great Red Sand, where the Jinn were born. She later built Ay-Khanoum with Deshret.
### STANDARD +
Origins: the Angels were made in the era of Teyvat's unified civilisation. Item text calls Ay-Khanoum the City of the Moon Maiden and a paradise for migrants from the moon, and calls her the Moon Maiden; the wiki infers from this that she became a Moon Envoy of the Lunar Palace. She joined her elder sister Koitar's rebellion and helped hide the youngest Angel, Nicole. As punishment the Angels were cursed to lose strength and memory if they loved an individual over humanity, and almost all became Seelies. Nabu escaped by hiding.

The desert: a legend says she wandered the wastes for seventy-two nights, springs bursting from her wounds. Streams became gardens where water lilies grew, and the first Jinn were born from them. The Jinn begged her to stay, and where she rested purple moon-like flowers called Padisarah bloomed.

Her rule: during the Archon War she met King Deshret, then Rukkhadevata. Deshret shared his throne with both goddesses. They were friends who pursued different ideas of wisdom. Nabu Malikata and Deshret built Ay-Khanoum as an earthly paradise.
### LARGE +
The festival: she is the originator of the Sabzeruz Festival (its namesake in the Chinese and Japanese text), which first marked Rukkhadevata's birthday. On one such birthday she foresaw the cataclysm and made a shard of Khvarena, which she called her daughter, and gave it to Rukkhadevata, who she thought shone more brightly than herself. Rukkhadevata made it the bird Simurgh, a daughter of both goddesses. Simurgh was destined to sacrifice herself in the cataclysm to save the Girdle of the Sands, and her Khvarena alighted on the remains of Egeria and produced the Pari, called daughters of all three goddesses.

The turning point: she warned Deshret to "seek not the Master of the Four Shades, and inquire not of the mysteries of the sky and the abyss" (Amethyst Crown), telling him what had happened to the Angels. He answered with his ambitions, and she agreed to help; the wiki reads the Belyi Tsar's notes as showing she had long meant to carry on the Angels' rebellion. Rukkhadevata was heartbroken at the plan, took her own people east to make an oasis and left Nabu waiting for a return that never came.

Her death: using what remained of her tie to Celestia she gave Deshret divine knowledge and, in Thoth's telling, was burned to ashes by the flames of wrath for it. Texts suggest she knew what would follow and acted so that the bitterness of her death would preserve her legacy; Deshret, who loved her, never grasped that. Her remains were scattered; Deshret gathered what he could and buried it in the Eternal Oasis, but some pieces were never found. The original Padisarah went extinct, and Rukkhadevata could never reproduce its purple.
### ULTRA +
Aftermath: Deshret told the Jinn that she merely slept and promised to help find her if they served him, binding them to his service. He had the Orchard of Pairidaeza made as her resting place and set the mortal kingdom of Gurabad to guard it. With the divine knowledge she had given him he raised his Mausoleum around Aaru. Thoth says Deshret first wanted Aaru to rival the world itself, but his friendship with the two goddesses limited it to a monument of the past while Nabu still lived.

Later: Dottore tracked down her unaccounted remains and used them on Eleazar and other subjects; only Collei merged with them. Cyno sealed them before the game's present, per the wiki's account of the manga. In 6.6 Liloupar sacrificed herself to take the last fragment out.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- King Deshret: her friend and co-ruler, who loved her and never got over her death. Whether she loved him is never stated.
- Greater Lord Rukkhadevata: her closest friend among the gods, and the one she trusted with her daughter Khvarena.
- The Jinn and Liloupar: her creations. Deshret bound them after her death.
### LARGE +
- Koitar and Nicole: her elder sister who led the Angels' rebellion, and the youngest sister she helped shield.
- Collei: unwilling host of her remains until 6.6.
### ULTRA +
- Simurgh and the Pari: her gift to Rukkhadevata and the descendants of that bird, who are called daughters of all three goddesses.
- Dottore: the Harbinger who found her scattered remains and used them in experiments.

## abilities
### STANDARD
She was a divine envoy of Celestia and then a god of the desert. She could create life in the form of the Jinn and the first Khvarena, shape oases and flower gardens, foresee the cataclysm, and pass divine knowledge to Deshret through her remaining link to Celestia. In Liloupar's memories she looks ineffably beautiful, yet meeting her gaze shows only nothingness. Her remains inside Collei took the form of a large magenta and purple serpent wreathed in black flame.
### LARGE +
Limits: the Angels' curse took strength and memory from any who loved one person over humanity. She could not stand against Celestia, which killed her for passing knowledge on.

## status
### LITE
Dead for thousands of years. Her last stray remains, inside Collei, were removed in 6.6.
### STANDARD +
Nothing of her acts on its own. Her legacy lives on in the Jinn, the Pari, the Sabzeruz Festival and Aaru.
### LARGE +
After Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana (6.6) Aaru replaced Irminsul as the hub for Teyvat's memories and a new anchor for the Dendro Archon's existence. The Belyi Tsar's notes in the Pale Crown Palace (7.0) say the Goddess of Flowers sought to build a system independent of the Ley Lines and Heimarmene; the wiki reads Aaru's new role as the completion of that plan.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Legend (Flower of Paradise Lost): she survived the Angels' fall by hiding, wandered seventy-two nights and gave birth to the Jinn through water lilies.
- Deshret's story to the Jinn: she sleeps and can be found.
- Thoth (6.6): her death followed from the divine knowledge she gave Deshret.
- Item text (Secret-Keeper's Magic Bottle, Oasis Garden's Truth): she chose to die so that her bitterness would preserve her.
- The Belyi Tsar's notes (7.0): she sought to build a system independent of the Ley Lines and Heimarmene. Treat the author as biased and unverified.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not Rukkhadevata. They were two separate goddesses and close friends.
- She was not Deshret's established lover; he loved her, and her feelings are never stated.
- She was not one of The Seven, and canon never gives her a Gnosis.
- Do not give her spoken lines; canon records her only second hand, apart from a song attributed to her and the Scroll of Streaming Song.
- Padisarah still grow in Sumeru, but the original purple Padisarah went extinct with her; Rukkhadevata's recreation never matched its colour.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Era of unified civilisation: created as an Angel by the Heavenly Principles.
- The Angels' rebellion under Koitar; Nabu hides beneath the Great Red Sand.
- Archon War era: meets Deshret and then Rukkhadevata; Ay-Khanoum is built.
- Rukkhadevata's birthday: she foresees the cataclysm and makes the first Khvarena.
- She agrees to help Deshret's defiance; Rukkhadevata leaves.
- She is burned to ashes by Celestia; her remains are scattered.
- Deshret binds the Jinn, builds the Orchard of Pairidaeza and the Mausoleum.
- Modern era: Dottore uses her remains on Collei (before the game's start).
- 3.0: the Goddess of Flowers is introduced; 3.4: she is named Nabu Malikata (wiki change history).
- 6.6 (Luna VII): Collei's remains are revealed as hers; Liloupar removes the last fragment.
### ULTRA +
- Collei's visions of Irminsul's burning and corruption (Winter Night's Lazzo teaser; Where Flowers Awaken) are tied by the wiki to the goddess's remains. The first came true in Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Nilou's default crown has horns made to echo the goddess's.
- Her name is written in Deshret Script as NABU MLKTA; the wiki notes its resemblance to the Middle Persian ideogram for queen. These etymologies are the wiki's reading.
- The wiki links the series name Dirge of Bilqis to the Queen of Sheba tradition; this is out-of-game inspiration, not canon.

## entry: Ay-Khanoum
@keys: Ay-Khanoum, City of the Moon Maiden, City of Amphitheaters
### STANDARD
Ay-Khanoum was the earthly paradise Nabu Malikata and King Deshret built in the desert. Item text calls it the City of the Moon Maiden and the City of Amphitheaters, a home for migrants from the moon.

## entry: Padisarah
@keys: Padisarah, Padisarahs
### STANDARD
Purple moon-like flowers said to bloom wherever Nabu Malikata rested. The original went extinct with her death; Rukkhadevata later tried to recreate it and could not match its purple.

## entry: The Jinn and the goddess
@keys: Jinn, Jinni, Liloupar
@keys2: Nabu Malikata, Goddess of Flowers
### STANDARD
The first Jinn were born from water lilies in gardens fed by the goddess's wounds. After her death King Deshret told them she merely slept and promised to help find her in return for their service. Liloupar was a Jinni of hers; in 6.6 she gave her life to remove the goddess's last remains from Collei.

## entry: Sabzeruz Festival origin
@keys: Sabzeruz Festival, Sabzeruz
@keys2: Nabu Malikata, Goddess of Flowers
### STANDARD
In the Chinese and Japanese text the festival is named after Nabu Malikata, the "Goddess of Flowers"; she began it as a celebration of her friend Rukkhadevata's birthday, and on one such birthday she foresaw the cataclysm. After Rukkhadevata's death it came to mark Lesser Lord Kusanali's birthday.

## entry: Her remains in Collei
@keys: Collei
@keys2: Nabu Malikata, god remains, Goddess of Flowers
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Dottore used her unrecovered remains in experiments on Eleazar and others. Collei was the only subject to merge with them but could not control them, and the remains' lingering malice harmed those near her until Cyno sealed them. They gave her visions of Irminsul's fate. Liloupar took out the last fragment in Your Past Is Like a Garden.
