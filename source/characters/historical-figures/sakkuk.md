## summary
### LITE
Sakkuk of the Nanatzcayan was the first hero to join the Scarlet-Eyed Youth in the rebellion against King Och-Kan, about 2,000 years ago. She outlived every companion, became Chief of the Children of Echoes, and at the youth's request erased his name from history.
### STANDARD +
She considered herself the weakest of the heroes, yet she was the last one alive. In old age she gathered treasures that her lost friends had left, honoured their graves each year, and finally vanished into the night with the Craftsman's genuine works.
### ULTRA +
Her legend is bound up with the mines: the hammer-strokes of the Children of Echoes still keep the rhythm of her songs, though the ballads she kept are lost.

## background
### LITE
Born to the Nanatzcayan tribe, she lost her father, executed for protecting the tribe's Saurians, and hated Och-Kan for it. She joined the Scarlet-Eyed Youth first among the heroes, found the Masterful Craftsman for him, and helped overthrow the king.
### STANDARD +
The youth, a man of the Flower-Feather Clan, gathered the heroes in turn: Sakkuk first, then the twins Atawallpa and Waskar, then Yupanqui, who brought up the Craftsman because he knew Ochkanatlan better than anyone. On the youth's orders she found the Craftsman in a ruined alley, his face scarred by fire, and he joined her cause at once. When the rebellion began she acted on behalf of the tribes' leaders and sent the wandering spirits back to the night realm; the youth burned the corroded city and freed his clan.
### LARGE +
The wiki reads her as the first to love the youth, while the Craftsman, who loved her in turn, held his tongue. The war cost her nearly everyone: the Craftsman died under the "black beast" before it ended, Yupanqui and Waskar fell in battle, Atawallpa died soon after his brother, and the youth returned to the sacred flames. She had promised the Craftsman a turquoise crown for remaking the Faded Emerald Tail; it was never worn.
### ULTRA +
Long afterwards the six tribes learned that the old chief paid dearly for jewellery signed by the Craftsman. She took forgeries too, saying she wanted to stop crude copies staining his name, then quietly punished the forgers. Every year, before the Embercore Flowers bloomed, she went alone to the Tequemecan Valley with dew-kissed flowers for her friends. At the end she left the crown at a tree and walked into the night with the real pieces; the tribes swore to destroy anything bearing the Craftsman's name, as she asked. The youth had asked her to erase his own name so that every human might believe they could become the Pyro Archon.

## relationships
### LITE
The Scarlet-Eyed Youth (leader she loved); Och-Kan (hated tyrant); the twins Atawallpa and Waskar and the hero Yupanqui (fellow heroes); the Masterful Craftsman.
### STANDARD +
The Craftsman, an artisan scarred by fire, served her with open loyalty; he knew from their first meeting that someone else had her heart. She regarded herself as the weakest of the group and lived on to miss them.

## status
### LITE
Dead for roughly 2,000 years, presumably of old age, after disappearing into the night. Her name survives on the Peak Patrol Song, the Children of Echoes' mining rhythm and the Unfinished Reverie artifacts.
### STANDARD +
Her titles of "Peace-Reforger" and Chief of the Children of Echoes survive in the item texts and wiki. No appearance or mention was found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content as of 7.1.

### STANDARD
The nameless second Pyro Archon, a man of the Flower-Feather Clan banished by his elder for protecting Saurians. After trials in the Night Kingdom he gathered the heroes against Och-Kan, burned the city, and returned to the Sacred Flame. He asked Sakkuk to erase his name; the weapon text Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage records the plea.
