## summary
### LITE
Kaedehara Kazuha's grandfather, from the Kaedehara line of the Isshin Art. He failed to restore his family's swordsmithing, left Inazuma, and died before the start of the game.
### STANDARD +
He is known from Kazuha's memories in the limited Summertime Odyssey event (2.8). He later took up rock gardens, as his own father had kept bonsai.
### LARGE +
Kazuha remembers him as a quiet man who stood for long periods before his father's bonsai, and who wanted his grandson to see more of the world.

### ULTRA +
The wiki's family tree places him as the son of Yoshinori and father of Kageharu; Kazuha's own account gives the most detail on him, and no Hisamichi appears in the base game outside these events.

## background
### LITE
Son of Kaedehara Yoshinori. He tried to revive the Isshin Art after the Case of the Eccentric, but could not produce the Goshintou, as the smiths before him had not.
### STANDARD +
Yoshinori's remark that the art should fade if he could not find the secret within enraged him, and he left Inazuma; the two never reconciled before Yoshinori died. Kazuha recalls that Hisamichi travelled widely in hope of saving the clan and returned too late. Afterwards he made rock gardens instead of plants, and brought sword-testing stones home from the forging station.
### LARGE +
He kept a backyard full of such stones. He once told his young grandson that a Kaedehara blade is not forged to harm people, and that the clan's way is to live in society while the heart yearns for nature.

## status
### LITE
Dead before the game begins.
### STANDARD +
The household ended when Kazuha dissolved it after his own father's death. No canon mention after 7.1 was found.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Yoshinori (estranged father), Kageharu (son), Kazuha (grandson).
### LARGE +
Kageharu, who also took to bonsai, was inspired by him. Hisamichi worked long hours and said he regretted how little time he had for Kazuha.

## knowledge
### LITE
He did not know the truth behind the Case of the Eccentric.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not Yoshinori; Hisamichi is the middle generation.
- Do not make him a master smith who succeeded; he failed to produce the Goshintou.
- Do not invent a wife, cause of death or age.

## chronology
### LARGE
- After the Case of the Eccentric: tries to revive the Isshin Art and fails.
- Leaves Inazuma after the quarrel; his father dies before they meet again.
- Later: makes rock gardens; speaks to his grandson Kazuha.
- 2.6 and 2.8 (limited events): his story is revealed through letters and memories.

### STANDARD
In a Summertime Odyssey memory, Hisamichi carves a small rock garden modelled on scenery he saw abroad, to place in his bonsai. Kazuha later remembers that some of the stones in the yard were sword-testing stones from the forging station.
