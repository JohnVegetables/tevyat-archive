## summary
### LITE
The Scarlet-Eyed Youth was the second Pyro Archon, a man of the Flower-Feather Clan from Tlalocan who rose against Och-Kan, the Python King of the Cinder City, about 2,000 years ago. He gathered heroes, burned Ochkanatlan, and afterwards returned to the sacred flames. At his request his name was erased from history.
### STANDARD +
Natlan remembers him as the proof that a human can become a god, but not by name. Even his divine name, Haborym, is a title shared by Pyro Archons in the sources.

## background
### LITE
Under Och-Kan, those who sympathised with the Saurians were persecuted. The Flower-Feather Clan's elder, who only pretended to obey the king, banished the young man so that he could escape capture. With red eyes that smoulder like the sunset, he travelled on alone; poets later argued about whether he was a mortal or a thread of the first Pyro Archon's will.
### STANDARD +
Item texts tell that he passed many trials. For his last, he cut out his own heart with a golden dagger and faced mortality as Xbalanque had done. Such a sacrifice was an honour reserved for victors, and he was allowed to come back from the Night Kingdom. He then called heroes together: first Sakkuk, then the twins Atawallpa and Waskar. Yupanqui backed his saving of the Saurians and introduced the Masterful Craftsman, who knew the ineffable city. During the revolt he summoned the divine wrath of fire and burned the city, freeing the Flower-Feather Clan. A vision seen by Sanhaj showed him defeating the king on the stairs before an empty throne.
### LARGE +
Having finished his work, he returned to the sacred flames and left only a warm afterglow. Sakkuk, who outlived the heroes, kept collecting what they left behind; Crownless Crown says she had loved him and that he returned to the sacred flames after completing his calling. The weapon story Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage says he asked the Nanatzcayan chief to remove his name from history so that others would take up his mantle. Several rival ballads exist, and no single song is accepted as definitive.
### ULTRA +
The weapon text gives two rival traditions. The Children of Echoes' version has a red vulture guiding him to Sakkuk; the other poets deny he was an ordinary mortal and call him a thread of the thought of Xbalanque, the One Entombed With the Primal Fire, returned from the Night Kingdom. The text calls the vulture guide an invention of those who doubted he travelled alone. All agree only that he ascended to divinity, which is why Natlan accepts that humans can become gods.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago. By the 7.1 cutoff he is a legend and an unnamed predecessor of Mavuika; a poem foretells that he will emerge from the flame when Natlan faces crisis again, but this is a poets' belief, not an event.
### STANDARD +
Mavuika proclaims the Night Wars in the name of Haborym, the title of the Pyro Archon.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Xbalanque preceded him as Pyro Archon. Och-Kan was his enemy; Sakkuk, the twin heroes and Yupanqui were his companions and supporters. The Flower-Feather Clan was his people, and his banishment from them was an act of protection.
### LARGE +
Later heroes of Natlan, including Sanhaj in the Night Kingdom, saw him in visions of the past.

## chronology
### LARGE
- About 2,000 years ago: banished from the Flower-Feather Clan; passes his trials; becomes Pyro Archon; gathers allies; burns Ochkanatlan; returns to the sacred flames.
- Later: Sakkuk keeps his memory and removes his name from records at his request.
- 4.6: first referenced through the Unfinished Reverie artifact texts.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He has no confirmed name. Do not invent one.
- He was not the first Pyro Archon; that was Xbalanque.
- Do not state that Sakkuk and he were lovers; only her feelings for him are mentioned.
- Do not use the red-vulture guide as fact.

The divine name of the Pyro Archon. A weapon text suggests it was held by Xbalanque and so by all later Pyro Archons, and Mavuika uses it in her declaration of the Night Wars.
