## summary
### LITE
Priscilla was a servant in Old Mondstadt with a past she hid, taken in by the illegitimate heir Eberhart and bound to him as an accomplice in his plot against the aristocracy. She was to light a warning beacon on the cape if the plot was discovered. The nobles found out first and she is presumed to have died.
### STANDARD +
She is known only from a Dragonspine journal, a Hidden Exploration Objective and a weapon text; she has no profile or model and never speaks in game.

## background
### LITE
Nothing is known of where she came from; the weapon text implies she had committed sins in her past. Eberhart took her as a servant and said he did not care about them. In the household she came to know several nobles, among them Luther.
### STANDARD +
Without most nobles knowing, she served as a co-conspirator in his plan to remove the corrupted aristocracy, even at the cost of other lives. The agreement was that, if word of the plot reached Mondstadt while the Dragonspine expedition was under way, she would light a torch at Stormbearer Point to warn him.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead, killed by the nobles who uncovered Eberhart's plot, a thousand years ago. No later appearance.
### LARGE +
Luther, writing his final journal entry as he bleeds out on Dragonspine, names her in an apology for not returning to Mondstadt. Eberhart's own whereabouts after the expedition are unknown.
### ULTRA +
Outside the limited-event weapon text, the permanent record holds only a name in Luther's journal and a promise in Eberhart's notes; everything about her inner life and death comes from that one event-sourced text, in a poetic register, so treat it as an in-world retelling and not a plain report.

## relationships
### LITE
Eberhart is the whole of her story: master, and in her eyes the leader of a coming change, who in practice used her. She appears to have been a willing instrument.
### STANDARD +
Luther knew her from the household. The nobles, whom she had served, were the ones who caught the plot.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Aristocratic period, Vennessa's rebellion: becomes Eberhart's servant and co-conspirator.
- During the Dragonspine expedition: waits in Mondstadt to light the beacon at Stormbearer Point; the plot is found out and she is presumed killed.
- 1.2: the Ancient Investigation Journal and Ragged Records name her.
- 3.1: the Missive Windspear, from the limited event Of Ballads and Brews, adds her story.

### STANDARD
Eberhart's signal arrangement: if the nobles learned of the plot, Priscilla was to light a torch at Stormbearer Point as a warning for him. His Ragged Records note the agreement; the weapon text says no ship was ever seen from the cape.

### STANDARD
A polearm from the limited event Of Ballads and Brews whose text tells Priscilla's story: a silver eagle that guides the traveller home, Eberhart's offer of a place at his side when the winds of change blow, the beacon instruction, and her quiet end beneath the moon among mocking nobles. In it she dreams of standing beside her 'real young master' and tells him not to mourn her.
