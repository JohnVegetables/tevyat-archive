## summary
### LITE
Basil Elton was the Admiral of the Fontaine Navy, later the last Vice Director of the Narzissenkreuz Institute. She was kind and easygoing, loved by the Institute's children, and she died leading her crew against the beast Elynas during the cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
Her flagship the Sponsian became the white wreck that the Traveler later meets in memory. She is known mostly through the recollections of her childhood friends and the objects left behind.
### ULTRA +
Her name also marks the Elton Trench, where much of the wreckage lies, and echoes the narrator of a well-known horror story, a coincidence the wiki treats as a deliberate reference.

## background
### LITE
She grew up at the Narzissenkreuz Institute alongside Emanuel Guillotin, Karl Ingold and Dwight Lasker, and the friendship lasted her life. She rose to Admiral of the Navy, then retired after the Armada was disbanded and the Sponsian wrecked, and was chosen Vice Director of the Institute, a post held only by people of exceptional moral quality and ability.
### STANDARD +
At the Institute she was beloved by the children despite her military past, was known for making good drinks and refreshments, and learned baking during her few years there. In youth she had been a poor cook. Long before becoming admiral she once beat the Emperor of Fire and Iron when it attacked a ship, sending it back to the depths. Her First Mate, Nathaniel Pickman, suggested the name Sponsian, after a historical usurper; she first called it awful, then adopted it, saying she did not mind the sense of unreal reality it gave.
### LARGE +
When the cataclysm came, both the Director and Basil were called away. Expecting the Institute to flood and neither to return, she sent the children to trusted people: Mary-Ann and Alain to Emanuel Guillotin, and Rene and Jakob to Karl Ingold. The Armada then repaired the Sponsian and followed Elynas for about thirty-five days while Abyssal monsters gathered around them.
### ULTRA +
As the hunt grew more dangerous she sent her Second Mate and most of the crew back to safety, keeping only Pickman, the Chief Engineer and those who chose to stay. A machine aboard showed there was no chance of survival, and that forecast came true.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her closest bonds were with the friends she grew up with: Emanuel Guillotin, Karl Ingold and Dwight Lasker. She treated the Institute's children almost as her own to protect, which is why she entrusted them before leaving.
### LARGE +
She stayed friends with all three even after the Siege of Poisson. Dwight, later director of the Institute of Natural Philosophy, still honours her grave, and the melusine Ahes, whom she trusted with the wreck, keeps its memory.

## status
### LITE
Dead. She was lost in the final battle against Elynas during the cataclysm, and the Sponsian broke in two, one half on the shore and one inside the beast.
### STANDARD +
Her grave is visited by old friends. In the world quest The Lone Phantom Sail, the Traveler, with Paimon and the melusine Ahes, hears her voice in a memory of the ship's last moments. Canon through 7.1 gives no later appearance.

### STANDARD
The Sponsian was Basil's white ironclad flagship, called the pride of the Fontaine Navy. It was wrecked once, repaired for the last hunt against Elynas, and finally broken in two. Ahes describes it as a ship that sails through dreams.

### STANDARD
As Vice Director, Basil was known for kindness. Before leaving for the cataclysm she placed Mary-Ann and Alain with Emanuel Guillotin and Rene and Jakob with Karl Ingold, the arrangement that shaped the next generation's lives.
