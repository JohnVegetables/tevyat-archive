## summary
### LITE
A legendary Aranara of Vanarana in the Dharma Forest, told of as the strongest Ararakalari user after the Dendro Archon. He crushed ruin machines during the cataclysm, locked many beneath stone seals and then vanished from the forest.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through stories and the stone locks he left. Aranara such as Arakarman and Arayuta call his tales their favourites, and Arabalika trains harder because Aradasha's power is "not in the now".

## background
### LITE
The Aranara say Aradasha was not strong from the seed: he began by destroying mushrooms and worked up to shattering mountain-sized iron monsters, eighteen foes in all, each win adding another story. When the iron monsters (ruin machines) and Marana came, he defeated many and locked them under stone seals, bringing peace to the forest.
### STANDARD +
Later he joined Aramuhukunda's adventure. With the golden-haired human Nara Varuna the three beat the monsters that the Aranara alone could not, and soon after the birds and flying flowers lost his trail. The Aranara guess he went away with Aramuhukunda.
### LARGE +
Arayuta tells the tale: when things were most perilous, the great Aradasha used Ararakalari to defeat many "Big Iron Chunks" and seal them with stone locks, so that Nara and Aranara could sleep in the forest at night. Arasata adds the flaw: even the hardest earth erodes, and cracked locks become Valuka (sand), so guardians must watch them. The tale predates the game by several hundred cycles of trees.
### ULTRA +
The Aranara keep his story as a training tale: Arakarman says even weak Aranara want to cultivate Ararakalari after hearing it, and sighs that it would be wonderful to see Aradasha's Ararakalari again. Arabalika, who trains to be able to defeat iron monsters outright, says he would beat a Big Iron Chunk before one could blink. In Legends of the Stone Lock the four young Aranara describe the monsters as mountain-tall and fire-spewing, and admit they could contain the strongest, the Bulky Iron Chunk, only with their own Ararakalari.

## abilities
### LITE
Master of Ararakalari, the Aranara's way of using memory to channel the forest's power. His signature use was trapping and destroying huge metal monsters, leaving behind stone locks.
### STANDARD +
Before him only the Dendro Archon's Ararakalari was stronger; after him his was the greatest. Arama later reused this technique in Old Vanarana to smash a regenerating Ruin Grader, and described the effort as hitting it "very, very hard", losing some memories in the process. Gameplay: Legends of the Stone Lock and the Stone Pillar Seals in the Dharma Forest are the visible legacy.

## status
### LITE
Whereabouts unknown; spoken of in the past tense. He disappeared after the cataclysm-era fighting and has not been seen in living memory.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 canon gives no sign that he has returned. Four Aranara (Aradashan, Arayuta, Arasata and Arasaha) with the Traveler cleared his remaining stone locks and the sealed Perpetual Mechanical Array (the "Bulky Iron Chunk") in Legends of the Stone Lock.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Aramuhukunda was his partner on the great adventure; the Dendro Archon (earlier called Queen Aranyani) made him; the human Nara Varuna fought beside them. Arama, Arabalika and the four Aranara of the stone-lock quest honour him as a model.

### STANDARD
Aradasha sealed ruin machines under stone locks in the Dharma Forest. The Aranara fear that time cracks the locks into sand. In the hidden world quest Legends of the Stone Lock, the Traveler broke sixteen sealed machines and then a sealed Perpetual Mechanical Array. Aradashan, Arayuta, Arasata and Arasaha (young Aranara who revere the legend) want to grow strong enough to defeat such foes without needing seals.
