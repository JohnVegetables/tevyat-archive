## summary
### LITE
Carter Scherbius was a kind and optimistic research assistant at the Institute of Natural Philosophy and a friend of the prodigy Alain Guillotin. A terminal illness, and the Abyssal attempt to save him, set off the break between Alain and his friends Rene and Jakob.
### STANDARD +
He was an adult working beside children far more gifted than himself, and he accepted that without bitterness. Today he is remembered chiefly through logs and pages left behind and through a shadow of him in the Book of Revealing.
### ULTRA +
His idea of a thinking kamera lived on in Alain's Recon Log Mek and in Mary-Ann's assistant Seymour.

## background
### LITE
Born before the cataclysm into a poor family, Carter lost his family and friends after something went wrong and entered the Narzissenkreuz Institute. As an adult he became a research assistant to Alain Guillotin, who was younger and more talented but whose resources Carter did not resent. Struck by a terminal illness, he was eventually left alive but broken after an attempt to cure him with Abyssal power.
### STANDARD +
A minor symptom and a knocked-over Primal Construct showed his illness early; Alain tamed the Construct. Rene and Jakob later joined the team, and Carter felt ashamed that he could not follow Alain and Rene's discussions. Together they worked on Primenergy Machinery, Alain and Rene intending to build a new kinetic core from ousia and pneuma. Carter and Mary-Ann once took the four children on a research pretext to Petrichor for a picnic, with a Kamera borrowed from the Institute.
### LARGE +
As his health worsened, Alain and the others visited him often. Inspired by their talk of a virtual neural network, Carter proposed a thinking Kamera that could keep taking photos after he was gone for those who mattered to him. Alain thought it pointless at first, though he saw uses in security. Carter witnessed Alain's first controlled annihilation reaction, the lighting of the bulb, and accidentally knocked over an ink bottle in his excitement. Alain advised him to seek medical help in Sumeru.
### ULTRA +
Director Dwight Lasker took blood samples hoping for a cure. Rene took samples of blood and skin, and a swift catspaw of the Ordo carried them to Sumeru, but the cause was never found. Jakob then persuaded Rene to use the same Abyssal method that had once saved Jakob's life. Carter's tissue began to collapse; Rene halted it, but Carter remained partly conscious with a voice still heard from what was left. Rene tried to silence it by cutting the vocal cords, but the organs regrew within days.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Carter's closest bonds were with Alain, Rene and Jakob, who visited him through his illness. He admired Alain's gift without envy and felt both shame and affection toward the younger members of the team. Mary-Ann treated him as a friend.
### LARGE +
The attempt to save him cost Alain's friendship with Rene and Jakob: Alain cut ties with both after the experiment. Jakob and Rene acted out of love, but their method turned Carter into a ruined, preserved body.

## status
### LITE
Dead. Rene's Abyssal method left him preserved but ruined, and the wiki says Jakob later apparently used his remains; his shadow persists in the memory realm of the Book of Revealing.
### STANDARD +
In the Book of Revealing the shadow, appearing as a puppy in Canotila's perception, urged her, the Traveler and Caterpillar to leave the unstable realm. It said that it was born from Rene's and Jakob's obsession with saving him and that, despite how they changed, he did not hate them and was grateful for their friendship.
### LARGE +
Alain commemorated him by naming the Suppression Specialist Mek after him, after the clash between the Marechaussee Hunters and the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. The wiki also says Jakob apparently used Carter's remains in the experiments that created Narzissenkreuz, and leftovers in the making of Caterpillar.

## entry: Carter's Illness and the Abyssal Method
@keys: Carter Scherbius, Carter
@keys2: Rene, Jakob, Abyss, illness
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Carter suffered from an unidentified terminal illness. After Sumeru's physicians could not diagnose it, Rene used the Abyssal method that had once saved Jakob on Carter. It halted his collapse but left him preserved and partly conscious, an outcome that ended Alain's friendship with Rene and Jakob.
