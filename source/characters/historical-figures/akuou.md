## summary
### LITE
Akuou, born nameless and known as Touzannou, 'Viceroy of the East', was a Watatsumi general in the Narukami-Watatsumi War about 2,000 years ago. Orobashi made him ruler of conquered Yashiori Island; he was killed with Orobashi by the Raiden Shogun's Musou no Hitotachi.
### STANDARD +
He is the namesake of the claymore Akuoumaru. Watatsumi remembers him as a valiant king of the eastern mountain; Yashiori Island's legends remember a brutal Wicked King.

## background
### LITE
An orphan of unknown origin with no name or clan, he was taken in by Orobashi and apprenticed to the shrine maiden Mouun and her twin sister Ayame, from whom he learned whalesong and Watatsumi's rites. He developed the sword styles Getsumouun and Yuushio.
### STANDARD +
Mouun invited him to swim by moon and starlight, and their night excursion is where the story places his understanding of the two sword styles. He had no descendants, but Watatsumi soldiers still use the styles, and outstanding warriors are called scions of Touzannou.

He joined the invasion of Yashiori Island, which lay under Makoto's jurisdiction, led by Orobashi, Ayame and Mouun. Watatsumi sought the island's resources to offset its own natural deficiencies, but Akuou also boasted that he would duel the Great Tengu Sasayuri and bring back their mask as a gift for Mouun and Ayame.
### LARGE +
The invaders conquered and annexed Yashiori. For his achievements Orobashi named him Touzannou. Makoto was occupied at home, so Ei led a force to subjugate the invaders. Sasayuri fought in the war and died after a duel with Akuou. In the end Ei's Musou no Hitotachi split Yashiori Island, creating Musoujin Gorge, and killed Akuou and Orobashi.

Mouun was killed by Sasayuri's followers and her body taken; Ayame vanished into the sea. Watatsumi Island was left to the Divine Priestess, who had stayed home.
### ULTRA +
The Akuoumaru text recalls him as a young man hardened by slaughter whose only lasting wish was one made over the moon on the water by the shrine: to stand above Mt. Yougou overlooking the Lord of Thunder's palace and fight Sasayuri on Tenshukaku's roof. It says the waves washed his dreams away like towers in sand. The Debates book notes that he never joined himself to the one he loved, nor did they share a peaceful future.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed with Orobashi about 2,000 years ago when the Raiden Shogun ended the war.
### STANDARD +
The Formation Estate stands on the mountain he conquered. His fame lives on in Watatsumi tradition and in the weapon Akuoumaru. As far as checked, he is not named in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Orobashi raised him to rank; Mouun and Ayame taught him and shaped his life, and he loved Mouun. Sasayuri was his chosen rival, and the Raiden Shogun killed him.
### LARGE +
The Yuna Clan's songs call him the legacy of moonlight and tide, and a forgotten child of the moonlight. Yashiori's people see an invader; Watatsumi sees a beloved champion.

## entry: Akuoumaru
@keys: Akuoumaru
### STANDARD
A five-star claymore (named after him) said to have been used by a mighty Watatsumi general who knew only the Getsumouun and Yuushio styles yet never lost a duel or a battle against a mortal. Its description tells the whole arc: his ambition, his wish to duel the tengu, and his defeat by the lightning.

## entry: Orobashi's expedition
@keys: Orobashi, Narukami-Watatsumi War
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Orobashi, the Great Serpent of Watatsumi, challenged the storm clouds of Narukami to war to fulfil his people's dreams of a richer land. Akuou led the vanguard to Yashiori, the Eastern Mountain.
