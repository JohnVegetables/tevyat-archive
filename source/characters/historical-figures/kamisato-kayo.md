## summary
### LITE
Kamisato Kayo was the mother of Kamisato Ayato and Kamisato Ayaka of the Kamisato Clan, and is deceased. Her children remember her as dignified, elegant and always smiling under heavy clan duties. She died while Ayaka was young, which pushed Ayaka to mature early and take a larger part in the clan's affairs. She has no on-screen appearance; she is known through voice-overs and Ayaka's Story Quest.
### STANDARD +
In private she took the alter ego "Tsubaki", a name under which she imagined life without the Kamisato Clan's responsibilities.

## background
### LITE
Kayo belonged to the Kamisato Clan, which runs the Yashiro Commission, and bore its burdens of ceremony and administration. Her husband is mentioned as deceased and is never named.
### STANDARD +
Ayaka says her mother handled many obligations in the clan without visible strain, and that her death ended Ayaka's childhood sheltering. Kayo comforted Ayaka's childhood fear of thunder by saying the sound was the Almighty Shogun's voice; the child began listening closely for it ever after.

## status
### LITE
Dead; no date, cause or place is given. Her notebooks and her secret meeting place remained after her, and her children preserved her memory.
### STANDARD +
Her hidden notebook was found by Ayaka in the Grus Nivis Chapter and led Ayaka to fulfil one of her mother's unfulfilled wishes: attending a festival as an ordinary person.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Ayaka idolised her and feels she lacks her mother's perfect composure; Ayato, as the clan's current head, shares the memory of her. Kayo's wish for freedom mirrors Ayaka's own wish to be treated as an ordinary person.
### LARGE +
Ayaka took the notebook as a reason to live "every day without regrets", and later drew on a prayer panel at the festival for an Inazuma where everyone is happy.
### ULTRA +
The riddle in Kayo's notebook told the reader to bring one fresh flower, sew four rays of moonlight, face east and call Tsubaki's name seven times; Ayaka decoded it as directions (knocking on a hidden wall once, four times and seven times). Ayaka brought the Traveler in place of Ayato, and a kimono she designed and a foreign dish as gifts for Tsubaki. This quest also shows Ayaka had few friends in childhood, because her rank made ordinary families wary of her, an isolation Kayo's own constrained life mirrors.

## entry: Tsubaki

### STANDARD
Tsubaki was Kayo's private persona, a way to imagine being an ordinary woman. As Tsubaki she liked pretty kimonos, wanted to taste food from other places and longed to see sights she had never seen. Her duties kept her from visiting a local festival as she wished. Ayaka first mistook Tsubaki for a friend, possibly a fox envoy, before discovering the truth.

### STANDARD
In this Grus Nivis quest Ayaka and the Traveler follow riddle-like directions in her mother's notebook to a hidden spot beneath Tenshukaku. A concealed box held a second notebook revealing that Kayo and Tsubaki were one person, with many unfulfilled desires. Ayaka then took the Traveler and Paimon to a festival on Amakane Island as the fulfilment of both her own and her mother's wish.
