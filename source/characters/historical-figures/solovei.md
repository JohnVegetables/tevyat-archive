## summary
### LITE
Silvestr Petrovich Solovei, the Torchforger, was a Snezhnayan noble who, about five hundred years ago, fought the Abyss in Nod-Krai with the priestess Aila's aid, won the borderlands' autonomy from the new Cryo Archon and founded the Lightkeepers. Long dead, he survives in a statue, item text, letters and a recording.
### STANDARD +
He was the childhood friend of Avrelian, the first Reed Miller, and acted out of loyalty to that friend's dream of a "paradise" for Nod-Krai's poor. He became the Lightkeepers' first Starshyna. The Lightkeepers' oath and the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, given to him by Aila, are his legacy.

## background
### LITE
Born to House Solovyov, a nightingale-emblem family raised to the gentry by the Belyi Tsar. His elder brother, guard of the "sacred chariot", was killed by beasts after glimpsing Hvergelmir's secret research; Solovei inherited the name and eventually forsook his homeland. With his friend Avrelian, the first Reed Miller, he promised a paradise; when the Cataclysm struck he rode to defend Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
In their youth he and Avrelian pried the location of Governor Prokhor Trubetskoy's hidden hoard out of the governor's five-year-old son Brahe, emptied it and handed the spoils to the street children of Nod-Krai (The Raven's Legacy flashback, where he is called Silve). In the war against the Abyss, Aila, who had foreseen him, became his benefactor. Alia, Aila's elder twin, who had taken up the Reed Miller's mask after Avrelian's death, worked beside him against the monk. After the Cataclysm and the Tsaritsa's rise, he went to her court, argued for the autonomy of the borderlands with Brahe giving up his claim, and received permission to found the Lightkeepers. He later died of age, apparently peacefully.
### LARGE +
Frostmoon records and the Lightkeeper's Pledge text differ on how the darkness was broken: Aila went to the Pillar of Embla at his request and ended the catastrophe, or he raised the first beam of light with power borrowed from her. His enmity with the monk Rotwang (Rauthvangi) was personal: the monk's plot killed many across Nod-Krai, Alia among them.

## status
### LITE
Dead; apparently died peacefully of old age centuries before the present, having founded the Lightkeepers. A statue of him stands in Piramida. Nothing in canon through 7.1 brings him back.
### STANDARD +
The Lightkeepers honor him as their founding Starshyna. His funeral, held by the Lightkeepers he trained, was quiet at his own request; Brahe attended and Aila did not.

## relationships
### LITE
Avrelian was his closest friend; Aila his benefactor; Alia a companion who died for the cause; Lovia his enemy; Brahe an old acquaintance turned friend.
### STANDARD +
He and Avrelian remained bound by the childhood promise, and after the execution he treated defending that dream as an oath. Aila's trust in him led her to kill Lovia instead of him. Alia's last words, which he relayed to Aila, asked that Aila live in happiness and freedom.

## appearance
### STANDARD
In youth, slim with pale skin, olive-green eyes and dark chestnut hair nearly to the shoulders. The statue shows him as an adult broad-shouldered and well-built with waist-length hair.

## speech
### STANDARD
In the recording he is polite but plainspoken: thanks Aila repeatedly, declines to second-guess the past and says he does not believe in fate, "I'll just do what I can." His letters use courteous, formal address and open directly on practical matters. Avrelian called him Silve.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the Cataclysm: boyhood and youth with Avrelian; the Brahe hoard episode (The Raven's Legacy, 6.3); family elevated; elder brother killed.
- Avrelian is executed as a notorious bandit; Alia later takes up the Reed Miller's mask.
- Cataclysm: comes to Nod-Krai; Aila lights a beacon or lends power; works with Alia against the monk; Lovia is killed by Aila.
- After Alia's death: writes to Aila, meets her in person, vows to find the monk. (Recording unsealed in Echoes of an Unfinished Past, 6.0.)
- After the Cataclysm: petitions the new Cryo Archon Anastasya, with Brahe renouncing his claim; founds the Lightkeepers and becomes first Starshyna.
- Later life: trains Lightkeepers; dies; funeral attended by Brahe.

## trivia
### ULTRA
The Piramida statue carries a plaque in Snezhnayan Script that the wiki translates as "We will tear the snowclad veils, we will give the wind to the wilderness"; Ratniki at the statue recite it as "We shall part the snow-curtain of the sky, and blast the winter winds across the wasteland." His surname is the Russian word for nightingale.
