## summary
### LITE
Kujou Shigeyori was a mortal general of Inazuma, valued by the Raiden Shogun, who according to legend built the battlefront fortress of Kujou Encampment in Kannazuka in a single night during the cataclysm hundreds of years ago. He is the namesake of the Kujou Clan in the wiki's listing.
### STANDARD +
Almost nothing else about him is recorded in canon. He is a legend recalled in one in-game viewpoint text and does not appear in any quest.

## background
### LITE
Little is known: he was a general who raised a fortress on the Kannazuka front in one night.
### STANDARD
In the viewpoint's telling he flew the banner of the Electro Mitsudomoe high and fought valiantly against the forces of darkness from the fortress he raised. The text uses this to explain why the Kujou of today are known for their military engineering. The Kujou Encampment is now the Shogun's Army headquarters under the Tenryou Commission, set in Kannazuka.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Inazuma history puts his fortress-building among the events of the cataclysm, alongside campaigns by Takamine the Mistsplitter and Mikoshi Chiyo with the Shogun against the dark forces. Those are separate accounts of other figures; none links their deeds to his.

## status
### LITE
Dead for hundreds of years. His fortress still stands as the Kujou Encampment.
### ULTRA +
Canon gives no date, age or manner of death. Do not give him a family, rank or personality beyond what the viewpoint states; the Kujou Clan's present-day members, such as Kujou Takayuki and Kujou Sara, are separate entities.

## relationships
### STANDARD
The Raiden Shogun held him in high regard. The Kujou Clan that serves her and heads the Tenryou Commission descends from, or at least honours, his name.
### LARGE +
The Kujou family's inherited skill with fortifications is the only link the text draws between him and later Kujous. No text names his descendants.
