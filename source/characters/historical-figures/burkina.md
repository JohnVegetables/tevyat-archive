## summary
### LITE
Burkina was a young hero of the Scions of the Canopy in Natlan, bearer of the Ancient Name Malipo, "Turnfire". He was one of the six warriors who joined Tenoch in the war against the Abyss during the cataclysm, fighting beside his Yumkasaur companion Kongamato. He died in that war, though how is told two ways.
### STANDARD +
He is the ancestor Kinich cites when talking about the Mare Jivari campaign, and Kinich carries the Name Malipo today.

## background
### LITE
Burkina was a courier and fighter of the Scions of the Canopy, the tribe of Coatepec Mountain. Fruitful Hook lore shows a proud young man who set out to harvest Flamegranates from the mother tree on a high peak to prove his agility to a mocking Yumkasaur, trained for a season, and was saved mid-fall by that same Yumkasaur, Kongamato. They became companions. When the cataclysm struck Natlan, he joined Tenoch and five other warriors in the war against the Abyss.
### STANDARD +
Before the war he trained with the winged-Saurian clan, with the mountain people below the volcano and with the Masters of the Night-Wind, and Tenoch, a friend, helped him make the weapon (the Fruitful Hook) he used for the climb in return for a debt Burkina half-expected to repay. As the fourth warrior recruited into Tenoch's fellowship (Talking Stick), he volunteered; scars on Tenoch's body convinced him youth should "bleed for burning justice". In Mavuika's memories he is shown as an energetic youth boasting about being the first of his peers to earn an Ancient Name.
### LARGE +
In the war he fought beside Kongamato in a campaign at the Mare Jivari. The Abyss's energy badly infested Kongamato; in a rage he killed Burkina, who passed some of his power and blood to the Yumkasaur before dying. The tribe's own stories say he fell defending Natlan. Five centuries later the Last Turnfire Night shows Kongamato, still alive, begging to be killed for "Huitztlan's shame" until Kinich, bearing the same Ancient Name, reaches him.
### ULTRA +
A cinematic in the Turnfire Night quest shows a remembered Burkina speaking the tribe's creed: they will answer to the fire for all their deeds, and if he falls behind the others should never look back. Kongamato later says he sees Burkina in his dreams, urging him to back the hero of the present, and notes the Traveler's presence resembles Tenoch's.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about five hundred years. His Ancient Name lives on in Kinich, and his Yumkasaur companion still lives, severely changed by Abyssal corruption, as the Mountain King of the Scions of the Canopy.

## relationships
### STANDARD
**Kongamato** was a mocking rival turned companion, and later the cause of his death. **Tenoch** was a friend and the one who gathered him for the war. **Kinich** inherits his Ancient Name and speaks of him as an ancestor. **Mavuika** remembers him as a boastful peer. The **Scions of the Canopy** keep his name in their tribal chronicles.

### STANDARD
Kongamato is a huge Yumkasaur who lived through the cataclysm. Burkina's companion, he took severe Abyssal infection while fighting at his side and, in rage, killed him. Ashamed, he has asked since to be put down. The Scions of the Canopy hold regular Turnfire rituals to drain some of his energy; in the Last Turnfire Night quest he fought the Traveler, then Kinich shielded him with his Ancient Name, and Kongamato chose to live with his shame and train future heroes.

### STANDARD
The Fruitful Hook is a claymore-class hooked sword that Burkina made with Tenoch's help to harvest Flamegranates from the mother tree on a peak above the clouds. Burkina trained with the winged Saurians, the mountain people and the Night-Wind priests, then climbed. A gust nearly threw him, until Kongamato rescued him with its tongue; Burkina shared the fruit, and the best Flamegranates are named "Mountain Kings" in the Yumkasaur's honour.
