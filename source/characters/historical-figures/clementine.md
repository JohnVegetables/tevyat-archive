## summary
### LITE
The wife of Callas, first president of Poisson's Spina di Rosula, and the mother of Navia. She worked inside the organisation as a mediator between its members and died of complications after giving birth to Navia. She is never seen alive; the aquabus Clementine Line is named for her.
### STANDARD +
Everything known about her comes from other people: Navia, old Spina members such as Colter, and the aquabus tour guides. She was gone long before the Fontaine story begins.

## background
### LITE
A member of the Spina di Rosula who handled disputes between its members. She married Callas, backed his plan to put the Spina's money into the aquabus network, and died giving birth to their daughter Navia.
### STANDARD +
During the dispute over the aquabus, Callas wanted to cooperate with the Court of Fontaine and the Fontaine Research Institute, while Romeuf wanted the money spent on improving Poisson itself. Clementine sided with her husband. Romeuf and his followers, claiming that her pregnancy and her support for Callas had cost her the standing of an impartial mediator, left the Spina on very bad terms. The Spina did go on to sponsor the aquabus, and the Clementine Line takes her name.
### LARGE +
Colter recalls that the Spina had good times in the years when members could bring their disagreements to her. After her death, Callas decided to see the project through as her wish. The aquabus lines were named for the two most important women in his life, Clementine and Navia, according to tour-guide commentary.
### ULTRA +
Navia says her father hid how her mother died; when she found out, it added to her frustration with a father who had told her little. In the 4.3 story quest Rosa Multiflora, Romeuf's faction plots to bomb the Clementine Line so its rubble would bury Poisson; Navia says she did not want to lose another link to her parents.

## relationships
### LITE
Callas (husband) and Navia (daughter). Romeuf's faction left the Spina partly over her stance.
### STANDARD +
- Callas: husband; she supported his aquabus plan and he named a line after her.
- Navia: daughter; Clementine died because of complications of her birth, and Navia learned the truth only later.
- Romeuf and his faction: opposed her stance and left the Spina; their quarrel with Callas was partly about her.
### LARGE +
Colter, Florent and other veteran Spina members remember the early Spina in which she acted as mediator; they supply almost everything canon says about her role.

## status
### LITE
Dead, from complications of childbirth, years before the Fontaine story. Her memory survives in the Clementine Line.
### STANDARD +
In the 4.3 story quest, Navia and the people of Poisson plan a statue of Callas and Clementine funded by donations. As of 7.1 nothing further about her is known.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not alive and has no voiced lines, personality profile or appearance in canon; do not invent them.
- The funding dispute was between Callas and Romeuf; her support and her pregnancy were Romeuf's grievance, not the cause of the quarrel.
- Her surname, age and the date of her death are not stated in the quests cited.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before Navia's birth: marries Callas; takes part in the Spina's aquabus dispute; Romeuf's faction leaves.
- At Navia's birth: dies of complications.
- Afterwards: Callas finishes the aquabus, naming lines after her and Navia.
- 4.0: Navia mentions her mother's death in the Chapter IV Archon Quest.
- 4.3: Spina members recall her in Rosa Multiflora, where the Clementine Line is threatened.

## entry: Clementine Line
@keys: Clementine Line, Clementine aquabus
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The north-south aquabus line named for Clementine, narrated by a tour guide named Aeval. Its stops include the Court of Fontaine. It belongs to the network Callas built with the Court of Fontaine and the Fontaine Research Institute. In the 4.3 story quest, Romeuf's faction tries to destroy its supports.

## entry: Romeuf's break with the Spina
@keys: Romeuf
@keys2: Clementine, Callas, Spina di Rosula
@version: 4.3
### STANDARD
Romeuf wanted Spina funds spent on Poisson rather than the aquabus; he left with many members after Callas pressed ahead. His followers argued that Clementine could no longer be a fair mediator because she backed Callas and was pregnant.
