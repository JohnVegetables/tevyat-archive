## summary
### LITE
A human Garde captain of Fontaine roughly 400 years ago who worked beside the Melusine Carole, killed those who framed her, was found guilty by Neuvillette, and founded the Mutual Aid Network in prison.
### STANDARD +
Cold and stern on the surface, he was a loyal and earnest officer who quietly came to share Carole's dream of humans and Melusines living together.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette still remembers the trial with regret; Wriothesley later argued that Vautrin's apparent hatred of Neuvillette was a deliberate act to prove the Iudex's fairness.

## background
### LITE
He led the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol when Neuvillette brought the Melusines into Fontainian society. He worked with the Melusine Carole of the Marechaussee Phantom for five years. After hardliners framed her and she died, he killed them, was convicted at the Opera Epiclese and imprisoned in the Fortress of Meropide.
### STANDARD +
He had a younger sister, Illaria, who died when he was very young, and saw her in Carole's innocence and kindness. Carole called him a "blockhead" and he first told her to give up on coexistence, but her spirit won him over. Both received Medals of Peace from Neuvillette for their efforts.
### LARGE +
Hardliners of the old regime, including Thibert and Suavegothe, lured Carole to a crime scene, accused her of murder and incited a mob. Carole took her own life to prove her innocence. Learning the results of the investigation, Vautrin told his men to restrict public access to the information, asked for the list of suspects and said he would take responsibility for everything.
### ULTRA +
At his trial the audience supported him, and the Oratrice agreed with the Chief Justice that he was guilty: he had abused his authority and carried out informal executions. He answered with outrage, then said a short goodbye. In the Fortress of Meropide he was disciplined and never violent, and founded the Mutual Aid Network, whose badge copies his Medal of Peace. Neuvillette says Carole's medal burned and his is the only one left.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Neuvillette: trusted superior who judged him. Carole: partner and the person who changed him. Illaria: the lost sister. Wriothesley: the later Fortress overseer who studied his file.
### LARGE +
His staff, such as Rastignac, obeyed his order to hide the investigation. The Mutual Aid Network kept his ideals of mutual help and just dealings alive.

## personality
### STANDARD
Stern, blunt and earnest. Carole found him stone-faced and only interested in work, but he stepped in to protect her from a hostile crowd and helped her hand out flyers. He warns against false hopes and says only "There's still a long way to go".
### LARGE +
Pragmatic about Fontaine's prejudice, and fiercely protective once he cares. He keeps feelings buried, then acts decisively. He concealed his sister's story from Carole.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about four centuries; remembered through Neuvillette's memories and the Mutual Aid Network.
### STANDARD +
His Medal of Peace design survives as the Network's badge. Neuvillette still regrets the verdict.
### LARGE +
No living person in the game has met him. The Network continues in the Fortress of Meropide, where its members stand up for each other; in the 4.1 quest they had stepped in against Domenico's attempt to incite a riot.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not a villain; Neuvillette himself called his revenge a kind of justice.
- His courtroom anger at Neuvillette was likely an act, not hatred.
- Do not invent romance with Carole; canon calls them partners and friends.
### LARGE +
- Do not make Vautrin a killer of anyone other than the hardliners behind the plot.
- Do not give the Mutual Aid Network a criminal agenda.

## entry: The Trial in the Opera Epiclese
@keys: Vautrin's trial, Vautrin guilty, informal executions
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Carole's death, Vautrin killed the hardliners who framed her. At the trial the crowd cheered for him, but Neuvillette, saying justice by law differs from personal justice, found him guilty and the Oratrice agreed. Vautrin raged, then said goodbye and went to the Fortress of Meropide.

## entry: Medal of Peace
@keys: Medal of Peace, Mutual Aid badge
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Neuvillette made two medals for Carole and Vautrin after five years of service to Melusine-human relations. Carole's was lost in a fire; Vautrin's is the only one left. The Mutual Aid Network wears a badge modelled on it.

## entry: Illaria
@keys: Illaria, Vautrin's sister
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Illaria was Vautrin's younger sister who died young. He told Rastignac that Carole was like her in every way: innocent, kind and always believing the best of people.
