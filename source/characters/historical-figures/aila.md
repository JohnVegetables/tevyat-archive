## summary
### LITE
Aila was the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, living about five hundred years ago in the time of the Cataclysm. Raised by the Archpriestess Lovia to be a pure-blooded vessel, she obeyed cruel orders for years before killing Lovia, sealing away the old rites and rebuilding the faith around moonlight and kindness to outsiders. She is known today through relics, notes and a recorded image in the Sanctum of the Oathbearer.
### STANDARD +
She sees threads of past and future, foresaw the birth of the new moon goddess Kuutar and offered her the first drop of silver blood. With the hero Solovei she sealed the undying serpent Rotwang (Rauthvangi) and gave him the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, the weapon on which the Lightkeepers were founded.

## background
### LITE
Aila and her older twin Alia were born to the Frostmoon Scions and raised on Hiisi Island by their grandmother Lovia, who kept them in isolation and had them kill other Scions and drink their blood to purify their own. When Alia left the island, Aila bore the full weight of Lovia's attention. She carried out every command until Solovei warned her what Lovia truly was; ordered to lure and murder him, she killed Lovia instead.
### STANDARD +
Afterwards Aila took leadership as Moonchanter, abolished the Archpriestess role and the old Hyperborean rites, and turned the Scions toward the Frost Moon, reverence for nature and kindness to outsiders. She sealed the Court of the First Edict (the Sanctum of the Oathbearer) to bury Lovia's corruption, leaving a recording for a destined outsider to find five centuries later. During the Cataclysm she and Solovei drove Rotwang's followers back to Kipumaki Cliff and sealed him a second time. She also carved the Idol of the New Moon showing Kuutar's original form.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; no date or manner of death is given. Her faith, seal and prophecy survived her, and the Traveler and the priestess Verna broke the seal in the quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past (Nod-Krai).
### STANDARD +
Her bloodline-faith lives on under Lauma, the current Moonchanter. When Solovei died of old age many years later, Aila declined to attend his funeral and turned away the Lightkeeper Brahe on Hiisi Island, having foreseen his visit.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Lovia was grandmother and jailer; Aila hid her hatred, obeyed, then killed her. Alia, her older twin, shared a childhood promise to "fly together like white birds" and broke it by leaving; Aila resented the leaving but said she would not resent her sister. Solovei was the one person whose kindness she tried to spare from her own hatred, and her partner against Rotwang. She loved the unborn goddess Kuutar as the one hope that the Scions could be redeemed.
### LARGE +
Aila also saw the Traveler as the destined slayer of the undying serpent, though their thread "would not catch" in the loom of the world and their fate was unclear to her, and she guided them to the sanctum with stones she called as out of place in this world as they were.
### ULTRA +
Her private notes in the Sanctum record that she lied to Solovei when he asked whether she felt hatred, believing no one should carry another's suffering. She wrote that all walls, towers and temples should be torn down save the old sanctuary pillar, so that isolation would never breed another Lovia, and that the new goddess must never be made into a "perfect deity" or owed repayment.
