## summary
### LITE
Ako Domeki Zaemon, the Deathless Oni of Seirai, was a pirate and bandit captain of Seirai Island who lived about 500 years ago and led a rebellion against the Inazuma Shogunate. He commanded over ten ships from his flagship, the Seiraimaru, and wielded the barbed spear later known as The Catch.
### STANDARD +
After the rebellion ruined Seirai, he left to see the world in place of an old shrine maiden, Asase Hibiki. He is known from item text and a few quests; nothing says when or where he died.

## background
### LITE
After the cataclysm, the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki retired from the Grand Narukami Shrine to Asase Shrine on Seirai. Domeki was a regular there: he called her a naggy, fretful aunt, but still listened to her stories and helped look after the shrine. Neko, the shrine's cat, thought him a very tall, loud human and an undignified glutton.
### STANDARD +
Some years later, once the city of Ritou had been built, he led a rebellion against the Shogunate. In one battle he defeated and carried off a Maguu Kenki puppet, a machine modelled on Iwakura Michihiro; his fame spread across Narukami Island, though Hiiragi Hiroshi doubted he could match the real Doukei, then the Kujou Clan's sword instructor.

Hibiki tried to keep him out of the fight, but ended by helping his cause: she unsealed the Thunder Manifestation to destroy the Shogunate's ships. It did, but it also created an Electro storm that wrecked most of Seirai. Hibiki and Domeki both headed into it, leaving Neko behind.
### LARGE +
The Hamayumi's text shows Hibiki's side: she resolved not to let him die and told him women were a hindrance on the ship, which only made her smile at his gruffness. In the storm's aftermath Domeki and his crew were swept away; his chief mate Janome and other crew, along with the ship that held the Maguu Kenki, protected him but were stranded on the Golden Apple Archipelago. They eventually escaped it with the island's other inhabitants and returned to Seirai.

Domeki also came back to Seirai on his own, only to find it in ruins and Hibiki gone. He made his final vow: to see the world in her place. He sailed away again.
### ULTRA +
The Catch's text records his words in first person: he was once master of the Seiraimaru and commanded over ten ships, was now a leaf on the waves, and would not have made it home without Janome and the people stranded on the Golden Apple Archipelago. He claimed he was the freest man in the world and told Hibiki, who had spoken of Kamuna and Konbumaru (Takamine the Mistsplitter), that he would go and see the edge of the world for her and tell her all about it where all ship routes end.

## status
### LITE
Last seen sailing away from the ruins of Seirai Island about 500 years ago; his fate is not stated.
### STANDARD +
His memory survives as Seirai's most famous pirate. No living appearance is attested, and nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 story content was found. The ship Kosekimaru, which held the Maguu Kenki, reappears in a later event (see the entry below).

## relationships
### STANDARD
Hibiki was the closest person to him: a scolding but protective elder who gave her magic to save him. His chief mate Janome and his crew stayed loyal through the storm. The Shogunate whose ships he fought was his enemy.
### LARGE +
Neko, the cat of Asase Shrine, regarded him as a glutton but is part of the shrine household he frequented. Iwakura Michihiro's Maguu Kenki puppet was his captured prize.

## entry: The Catch
@keys: The Catch, Ako's Sake Vessel
### STANDARD
A 4-star polearm, originally a fishing harpoon, that was Ako Domeki's favourite weapon. Its text records his farewell speech, and Ako's Sake Vessel, his liquor flask, is the weapon's refinement material.

## entry: Asase Hibiki
@keys: Asase Hibiki, Asase Shrine
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
Retired Grand Narukami Shrine maiden and Domeki's confidante. Her sacrifice to save him is told in the Hamayumi's text and the Seirai Stormchasers quests; Neko was left behind at the shrine.

## entry: Kosekimaru and Miitoboru
@keys: Kosekimaru, Miitoboru
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Kosekimaru was the ship that held the Maguu Kenki puppet and was stranded with Janome; during the Summertime Odyssey event the wrecked ship temporarily gained sentience and was named Miitoboru. This is event-only canon.
