## summary
### LITE
The God King and Sebastos of Remuria, an empire that once ruled the waters of ancient Fontaine. A god from Sumeru, he founded Remuria, created the Grand Symphony to escape a prophesied fall, and brought about the very ruin he feared.
### STANDARD +
Remuria's golden age is credited to him: farming, great cities and temples, music and art. He shared rule with four human Harmosts. Remuria is named for him, and by the time of the Traveler's visit he has been dead for millennia, remembered through legend, books and dream-memories in Petrichor.
### LARGE +
In the dream-memories of Remuria's last days, the "barbarians" fighting the empire call him "Dark Lord Remus", a title Remurians such as the "Bellringer" regard as blasphemy against "the Sebastos Remus, our mighty Imperator". After the fall his history blurred into legend. Canon shows him as a well-meaning ruler whose wish to free humanity from fate was warped by his own creation.

## background
### LITE
Remus was a "singer of the oases" in Sumeru. When King Deshret conquered his land he refused to serve and sailed for the High Sea to find a place to die. He found the ruins of a forgotten city and the Golden Bees. They told him he was destined to found a prosperous nation whose desire for justice would doom it. He founded Remuria upon his ship.
### STANDARD +
Reluctantly accepting that destiny, he saw the Irminsul turn into a golden ship, and from it founded the empire. He taught his people agriculture, building and music, and used the Fortuna to call other tribes into Remuria, conquering those that refused. Fearing the foretold fall, he sought Egeria in the Primordial Sea. Scylla, her jailer, fought him for thirty days and nights, then let him pass. Egeria could not change Fontaine's fate, which stemmed from her original sin of making a new race of humans with the Primordial Sea, but she gave him a goblet of primordial water. He vowed to make a world of endless prosperity without conflict, and Scylla left the sea to join him.
### LARGE +
He mixed that water with an immortal stone to make golden Ichor, which could dissolve a body while holding its soul and mind so they could be moved into undying golems. These "ascended" Remurians were attuned to the Grand Symphony (Phobos), which would eventually even command Fortune and give humans true free will. His great ship became the Domus Aurea, a harp-like palace from which the Symphony was played. Sybilla joined her wisdom to his power and gave her own life to create Phobos.
### ULTRA +
The plan failed. The ascended Remurians, freed from mortality, grew proud and came to regard other peoples as less than human. Because Sybilla had no will of her own, Phobos took on the Remurians' own prejudices and began serving their selfish desires, driving wars on Lochfolk, vishaps and other peoples in the belief that this was Remus's will. Other Remurians could no longer hear it and panicked. Resistance gathered under Erinnyes (the Lochknights). The Harmost Boethius and his followers decided that freeing humanity meant subjugating non-Remurians.

Seeing this, Remus resolved to end the Symphony. He freed Cassiodor from it so Cassiodor could lead the "blind" Remurians, and without Cassiodor's knowledge gave Scylla the Requiem, the Symphony's self-destruct order, planning to sacrifice himself to activate it. Scylla was to "betray" Remuria and lead an invasion so that he could enter the Domus Aurea, contain the released primordial power and return the Remurians to flesh and blood. Phobos learned of this and had devoured Boethius, who sealed Scylla. Remus's last orders to end the war were rejected, and presumably he activated the Requiem; with Scylla sealed, no one could control the flood and Remuria sank, Remurian and invader alike.

## status
### LITE
Dead for thousands of years. He was lost when Remuria sank. In Petrichor's Canticles of Harmony quests (4.6) his memories reach the Traveler as dreams, and his Requiem finally destroys Phobos.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler shows a particular affinity for his power. Together with the freed Scylla, now in an Ichor body that still holds the Requiem's power, they carry out his last wish and destroy the Grand Symphony. The souls of the Remurians trapped in it, Cassiodor's among them, move on to be reborn as mortals in the future.
### LARGE +
Since the fall, Remuria's history has dissolved into legend: "Fortuna" no longer names destiny but his ship. Boethius survived and formed the Golden Troupe to revive Remuria and Phobos; Cassiodor, as the Marechaussee Hunter, opposed it and eventually shattered Boethius's soul, sealing the shards in the Domus Aurea. No sign was found that he appears in the 6.x or 7.0-7.1 stories.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Sybilla (the Golden Bee who foretold his nation's fate and gave her life to make Phobos); Scylla (the vishap prince who fought him, then became his friend and the keeper of his Requiem); Egeria (the imprisoned Hydro Sovereign who could not save him); the Harmosts Boethius (who betrayed him) and Cassiodor (whom he freed); Erinnyes and the Lochknights (who rose against his empire); King Deshret (who conquered his homeland).
### LARGE +
Remus and Scylla remained close in spirit: Scylla in the Traveler's time still speaks of Remus's will and bitterly of Boethius. Cassiodor served as one of the four humans who shared governance of Remuria, after joining from a tribe Remus had conquered.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before Remuria: a god and "singer of the oases" in Sumeru; his land falls to King Deshret.
- He sails to the High Sea and finds the forgotten city with Sybilla and the Golden Bees.
- He founds Remuria on the Irminsul-turned-ship; golden age.
- He visits Egeria, fights Scylla, and receives primordial water.
- He creates Ichor and Phobos; the Domus Aurea is built.
- Phobos is corrupted; wars and the Lochknight resistance follow; Remus plans the Requiem with Scylla.
- Boethius seals Scylla; Remuria sinks.
- 4.6: his story is revealed in the Canticles of Harmony quests; the Grand Symphony is destroyed.

## entry: Phobos (the Grand Symphony)
@keys: Phobos, Grand Symphony, Golden Symphony
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Remus's attempt to defy fate: a music-based will that guided ascended Remurians and was meant to take control of Fortune itself. It was made from Remus's power and Sybilla's wisdom and life. Lacking a will of its own, it absorbed Remurians' biases and began serving their selfish and destructive wishes.
### LARGE +
Boethius was devoured by it and became its puppet. Its attempt to restart in the present brought the 4.6 events of Petrichor, ending when the Traveler and Scylla destroyed it using Remus's Requiem.

## entry: The Requiem
@keys: Requiem, Remus's Requiem, final symphony
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Remus's last symphony, the Grand Symphony's self-destruction order, which he gave to Scylla. His plan was to sacrifice himself to unleash it while Scylla entered the Domus Aurea to control the primordial energy and restore the Remurians. The plan failed when Scylla was sealed, and Remuria sank. The Requiem's power remained in Scylla's Ichor body.

## entry: Sybilla and the Golden Bees
@keys: Sybilla, Golden Bees, Golden Bee
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The former envoy who guarded the Irminsul at the forgotten city Remus found. She predicted he would found a nation whose passion for justice would cause its end, but considered avoiding the fall futile because civilisations rise and fall by the law of Fortuna. She nonetheless pointed him toward Egeria and eventually gave her life to create Phobos.

## entry: Scylla
@keys: Scylla, Prince of Vishaps, fell dragon
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Remus's first adversary and later friend. After thirty days and nights of battle at Egeria's prison, the exhausted pair talked, and Scylla let Remus pass and later joined his cause. Remurian tradition remembers him as an evil "fell dragon" who betrayed Remus; in the Traveler's visions he was sealed by Boethius while trying to carry out Remus's plan.

## entry: Harmosts
@keys: Four Harmosts, Harmost, Harmosts
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Remus shared governance with four humans of great capability, called the Four Harmosts; two were Boethius and Cassiodor. Boethius pursued domination of non-Remurians; Cassiodor was freed from the Symphony by Remus to lead the other Remurians.

## entry: Remuria
@keys: Remuria, Remurian, Remurians
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
The empire Remus founded over the waters of ancient Fontaine, with its capital divided into the outer city Machimos and the inner Capitolium, with the Domus Aurea at its heart. It fell in a single destructive wave when the Requiem was released. See its own entry for the place.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- "Remus" echoes the legendary co-founder of Rome, and "Sebastos" is the Greek rendering of the Roman imperial title Augustus (wiki etymology notes).
- Aliases used in canon include Sebastos, God King, Imperator, King of Fortuna, Dark Lord, Philharmonic God, Glorious King and King of the Immortals.
