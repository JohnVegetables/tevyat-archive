## summary
### LITE
The youngest of the three ancient Moon Goddesses, ruler of the Iridescent Moon. Terrified of dying after her moon was shattered, she fused her soul with the Abyss; the result was the Crimson Moon that Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty revered. She finally died after Columbina and the Traveler freed her marrow.
### STANDARD +
Her story is one of fear and pain turned into a source of power: the Crimson Moon was worshiped as a conduit to the Abyss, while the goddess behind it was suffering.

## background
### LITE
Sonnet was made by the Dragon King Nibelung to govern the third and smallest of the Three Moons while he was away from Teyvat. Her light was the faintest of the three, but when she shone it made fleeting halos.
### STANDARD +
The Moon Sisters shared the night sky, each holding sovereignty in turn, and lived in a lunar palace. When the Heavenly Principles first arrived they were received as guests; when the Principles began imposing their order, Sonnet was the only sister to speak against it, then deferred to her sisters' decision to simply watch. Later she was horrified by the harsh way the Principles punished any challenge.
### LARGE +
Nibelung returned from the Abyss and fought the war of funerary flame against the Heavenly Principles. The sisters argued over whether to take a side; Nibelung read their hesitation as disloyalty, imprisoned them in the moon's reflection and turned their moons into weapons. The Heavenly Principles shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and hurled the Frost Moon beyond the false sky. Without their moons the goddesses lost their reason to exist in Teyvat and faded.
### ULTRA +
Sonnet remained trapped in the reflection, unaware of the war's outcome, until a future Columbina visited and told her. The three sisters then agreed to give their lunar authorities to Columbina, and at their request she sent their now mortal bodies back to the world. Sonnet was the most afraid of dying and leaned on her sisters. Experiencing her moon's shattering was agonizing; in desperation she merged her fading soul with the Abyss. This kept her barely alive at the cost of perpetual torment and turned her into the Crimson Moon.

## status
### LITE
Dead. She died in the Crimson Moon's hidden domain, reached from Nod-Krai, after the Traveler purified her marrow in the Crimson Moon's hidden domain (6.1).
### STANDARD +
Her marrow, the Iridescent Moon Marrow, passed to Arlecchino. Dottore later stole it, and Columbina took it back and returned it to Arlecchino after defeating him (6.2-6.3).
### LARGE +
Her bloodline survives in the descendants of the Crimson Moon Dynasty, including Arlecchino, whose Balemoon Bloodfire is Sonnet's former power fused with the Abyss.

## relationships
### LITE
Her sisters Aria and Canon, who helped Columbina; Columbina, whom she mistook for a future self; Nibelung, her creator and jailer.
### STANDARD +
The Crimson Moon Dynasty of Khaenri'ah worshiped her corrupted remains without knowing her story. Arlecchino is among the last of its bloodline and a keeper of her marrow.
### LARGE +
In the 6.3 prison scene, Sonnet said she and her sisters knew what was coming. In 6.1 she apologized to Columbina for having lied out of fear, said she wished she had her courage, and said everything happens for a reason before dying. Columbina assumed she had mistaken her for Canon, because their auras are similar.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Like her sisters, a tall, fair maiden with long pink hair and pink eyes, and a pink crescent moon on her back. White robes edged in purple and pink, golden sandals laced to the knee, and gold jewelry including a laurel-like circlet and arm bands. The Iridescent Moon itself is a red crescent wreathed in crimson flame in its pure form; Abyss-corrupted, it hung in the sky as a broken husk.

## chronology
### LARGE
1. Primordial era: created by Nibelung; shares the sky with Aria and Canon.
2. Arrival of the Heavenly Principles; Sonnet alone objects.
3. War of funerary flame: imprisoned in the moon's reflection; the Iridescent Moon is shattered.
4. She merges with the Abyss and becomes the Crimson Moon.
5. The Khaenri'ahn Crimson Moon Dynasty worships it; later the Eclipse Dynasty overthrows that dynasty and, under the Vinster King, hunts the survivors.
6. 6.1: Arlecchino opens the domain; Sonnet dies after the Traveler purifies the marrow.
7. 6.2-6.3: the marrow is stolen by Dottore and recovered; a future Columbina meets the sisters in the prison.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Her name is the English word for a fourteen-line poem; the wiki traces it to the Italian for 'little song'.

## entry: Crimson Moon
@keys: Crimson Moon, Crimson Moon Dynasty, Balemoon Bloodfire, Two Worlds' Flames
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Crimson Moon is the shattered Iridescent Moon fused with the Abyss. Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty used it as a conduit to the Abyss and hid its marrow in a domain that opens only through a ritual with Balemoon Bloodfire. The royal bloodline carries that power as Balemoon Bloodfire, a mix of Sonnet's former power and Abyssal energy, called the Two Worlds' Flames. Arlecchino says the dynasty's sages branded orphans from beyond the world with its light.
### LARGE +
The Eclipse Dynasty seized power from the Crimson Moon Dynasty; the Vinster King later sent Rerir, the Rächer of Solnari, to hunt the remaining followers, called the Dregs of the Crimson Moon.

## entry: Iridescent Moon Marrow
@keys: Iridescent Moon Marrow, Iridescent Moon's marrow, Moon Marrow
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
The Iridescent Moon Marrow is Sonnet's remains, left when a goddess dies. It was hidden in an Abyssal domain and could not be found by the people of Teyvat. In 6.1 the Traveler severed its Abyssal fetters and purified it, Columbina used it to regain strength, and Arlecchino agreed to keep it. Dottore stole it, and after he was defeated Columbina gave it to Arlecchino again.
