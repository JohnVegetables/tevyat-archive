## summary
### LITE
Knight Marshal of the Schwanenritter, the order of knights of fallen Khaenri'ah. When the cataclysm struck and King Irmin was left "indisposed", he proclaimed himself temporary Regent and led survivors up out of the kingdom into Sumeru.
### STANDARD +
He is known only from notices, letters, chronicles and a coded message left in ruins. No one speaks his lines and no one meets him; everything about him is what others recorded.
### ULTRA +
His name is borrowed from the Fisher King of Wolfram von Eschenbach's Parzival, the wounded guardian king, which suits a regent who lost an eye. This is an etymological note, not a story fact.

## background
### LITE
He held the rank of Knight Marshal in Khaenri'ah's knightly order, the Schwanenritter. Little else of his earlier life is known, including his clan.
### STANDARD +
Before the cataclysm he had enough standing to be counselled directly: an unfinished letter in the ruins beneath Sumeru begs an "Eminent Marshal" to ask the king to preserve some older machinery made before forbidden knowledge. The wiki reads it as addressed to him.
### LARGE +
The knights fielded early-model Ruin Golems, an older design than the usual Ruin Guards, which suggests the order either distrusted the forbidden-knowledge technology or lost faith in it when the disaster came.

## status
### LITE
Last recorded fighting on alone in Sumeru, long ago. His fate is not stated; he is presumed dead.
### STANDARD +
Nothing about him has appeared in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content. His legacy survives in the Order of Skeptics' admiration for the knights and in a hidden achievement named for him.

## relationships
### LITE
Hadura, his own Knight Commander, whom he executed for betraying the order. King Irmin, whose duties he took over.
### STANDARD +
His clan is never stated; a regency by non-royal Alberichs in Kaeya's childhood lessons makes the Alberich Clan the likely answer, but this is only a deduction.
### LARGE +
Nagarjuna and the scholars who became the Order of Skeptics later fought beside the knights in Dharma Forest, called them heroes of Dahri, and carried on their aim of ending the cataclysm. The records imply that none of the knights went with them.

## chronology
### LARGE
1. The cataclysm: Abyssal beasts ravage Khaenri'ah. Irmin is left unable to rule and Anfortas, as Knight Marshal, announces he will serve as Regent and lead the Regnum Concilium Ultimum until the kingdom is stable again.
2. A Knight Commander, Hadura, wins a successful stalling operation and is given the title "Sentinel of the Golden Hall"; the Schwanenritter then evacuate able-bodied people upward with their Ruin Golems.
3. In Sumeru the survivors begin to sicken with a disease that turns them into monsters, and some who had not been touched choose to go back underground.
4. The knights keep fighting. Hildric dies of multiple-organ failure; Ynghildr is lost in battle, leaving only her signet ring and a second keepsake.
5. Hadura betrays the order and wrecks a machine beyond repair; Anfortas kills them and loses his left eye. He buries them with knight's honours as the last of his compatriots.
6. A coded message in Ruin Guards around Dharma Forest records that the Schwanenritters fought to the last one. He is last seen fighting alone.
### ULTRA +
Ynghildr did not die as the chronicle thought: she became a Shadowy Husk and was found guarding a fallen golem's control module in the Vimana Agama quest. Anfortas could not have known this.

## entry: Schwanenritter
@keys: Schwanenritter, Schwanenritters, Swan Knights
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Schwanenritter ("Swan Knights" in German) were a knightly order of Khaenri'ah's Eclipse Dynasty era, led by Knight Marshal Anfortas. They fought the dark beasts of the cataclysm and brought Khaenri'ahn survivors aboveground to Sumeru, using Ruin Golems of an older design. Named members include Hadura, Hildric ("Beard-Father") and Ynghildr ("Damsel of the Dale"). The Order of Skeptics honoured them as heroes of Dahri.
### LARGE +
Three surviving Ruin Golems mark their route: one lost in the Desert of Hadramaveth, one abandoned in the Valley of Dahri and one that reached Devantaka Mountain; parts from the second kept the third running. The Devantaka golem holds the chronicles of the knights' deaths.

## entry: Hadura
@keys: Hadura, Sentinel of the Golden Hall
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Hadura was a Knight Commander of the Schwanenritter, honoured as "Sentinel of the Golden Hall" for stalling the Abyssal beasts, and one of the last two knights alive. They betrayed the order by destroying a machine beyond repair, and Anfortas executed them, losing his left eye. Their motive is unrecorded. A later Order of Skeptics member, Klingsor, claimed Hadura as an ancestor.

## entry: Regnum Concilium Ultimum
@keys: Regnum Concilium Ultimum, Final Kingdom Council
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The "Final Kingdom Council", a body Anfortas announced he would lead as temporary Regent while King Irmin was incapacitated. The notice found beneath Sumeru is badly weathered, and its last lines promising instruction to the citizens are unreadable.

## entry: In the Name of Anfortas
@keys: In the Name of Anfortas, Cryptic Message in Sumeru
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A hidden exploration objective in Sumeru: five damaged Ruin Guards near Dharma Forest carry fragments of an enciphered message, and the cipher's key is the name ANFORTAS. Decoded, the message states that the Schwanenritters fought to the last one. Finishing it grants the achievement "In the Name of Anfortas".
