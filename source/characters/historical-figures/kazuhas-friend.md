## summary
### LITE
Kazuha's friend was an Electro Vision bearer and close companion of Kaedehara Kazuha. After losing a duel before the throne to Kujou Sara, he was executed by the Raiden Shogun under the Vision Hunt Decree. His name is not given in the game.
### STANDARD +
His death shaped Kazuha: the Vision he left behind, known as the Masterless Vision, is why Kazuha carries two Visions in the Omnipresent God cinematic.

## background
### LITE
Kazuha once explained the Musou no Hitotachi to him as the height of the Shogun's skill, shown only when she delivers divine punishment. He made it his goal to overcome that technique and challenged the Vision Hunt Decree's enforcers to a duel at Tenshukaku.
### STANDARD +
Sara answered the challenge on the Shogun's behalf, with the loser to face divine punishment. He lost and was executed. Kazuha, who was fleeing the Decree himself, arrived after the duel and snatched the dying Vision before it could be confiscated.

## status
### LITE
Executed before the game's events. His broken blade lies as a mound north-east of the Grand Narukami Shrine, with an epitaph, and his Vision rests beside Kazuha's as a memorial. No later developments in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Their bond was close travel and trust; his ambition grew from a conversation with Kazuha. Sara and the Shogun were the opponents of the duel, the Decree itself being the cause of his death.
### LARGE +
Kazuha later tried to find someone who could resonate with the Masterless Vision, even asking Beidou to offer it as the Crux Clash prize. No one could, the Traveler included. Beyond that the Vision responded only when Kazuha used it during the Omnipresent God cinematic, where he blocked the Shogun's strike with both Anemo and Electro. Inazumans who heard of it assumed a Delusion or doubted it. Wanderer found it amusing (voice-over).
### ULTRA +
His dying words echoed again when the Vision stirred in the cinematic: that there will always be those who dare to brave the lightning's glow. After the Shogun's blade flung it away the Electro Vision went dark once more.

## chronology
### LARGE
- During the Vision Hunt Decree: the friend dies after losing the duel.
- Afterwards: Kazuha travels with the Vision and offers it at the Crux Clash (Liyue).
- 2.0, The Omnipresent God: Kazuha wields the Masterless Vision and blocks the Shogun's attack.

### STANDARD
After the owner's death the Electro Vision became a Masterless Vision. It would not resonate with anyone Kazuha tried, but responded when Kazuha himself used it, giving him both Anemo and Electro.

## entry: Broken Blade Mound

### STANDARD
His sword was left in a ravine north-east of the Grand Narukami Shrine. After The Omnipresent God the Masterless Vision is placed beside it. The inscription speaks of thunder consuming body and bone so that both may rest in nourishing the earth.
