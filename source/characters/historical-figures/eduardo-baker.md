## summary
### LITE
Eduardo Baker was a Fontainian outlaw leader who kept order among the people of the Fleuve Cendre undercity before the cataclysm. His son Jakob is the later Jakob Ingold.
### STANDARD +
He is known from a weapon description, the Fleuve Cendre Ferryman, and a few related item texts. The Court of Fontaine's purge of Fleuve Cendre turned him from local protector into a rebel, and the occupation of Poisson that he led ended in the Siege of Poisson.

## background
### LITE
He ran taverns in Fleuve Cendre, the sewer-city beneath the Court of Fontaine, where his son Jakob was born. He took up a bronze pipe to drive off crocodiles and the monster fed by the "richly nutritious water", then shielded merchants from gang extortion, settled disputes among the "compatriots" and blessed newlyweds. Followers gathered around him.
### STANDARD +
Parsifal the Great and her younger sister Josephine performed in his taverns when they were beginning their careers. He is quoted as saying that all the sun shines on belongs to them and Fleuve Cendre belongs to us. Item text frames this against the city's improvement of its undercity, in which the people living there became places to be "developed" or "moved".
### LARGE +
Arrested for resisting the purge of Fleuve Cendre, he was being taken to exile in the desert when his henchmen freed him. He joined allies active near Mont Automnequi, occupied the village of Poisson, took the Maison Gardiennage staff who had been escorting him as hostages and made demands the item text calls unreasonable.
### ULTRA +
Before things worsened, the journalist Karl Ingold, who had carried the demands to the Court, took a photograph in Poisson. Eduardo stands in the middle leaning on the Ferryman like a cane, with a hand on Jakob's shoulder. Around him are the mayor Renault de Petrichor, the mayor's son Rene, "Parsifal", Rosa Reed with her husband Thompson and their infant, and Tom Alter.

## relationships
### LITE
Father of Jakob. Karl Ingold was his messenger to the Court; Parsifal and her sister performed for his patrons.
### STANDARD +
Renault de Petrichor, mayor of Poisson, stood beside him in the photograph, and the two boys, Jakob and Rene, stood close together. After the siege both boys went to the Narzissenkreuz Institute; Jakob later took the name Ingold. According to the wiki, citing the Hunter's Brooch artifact text, the Marechaussee Hunter Emanuel Guillotin was in charge of the Siege of Poisson.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead; canon never states how. He is generally taken to have died in the Siege of Poisson.
### STANDARD +
The siege razed the village and burned many of its inhabitants. Eduardo appears only in the Ferryman weapon text and related items, and no mention in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content was found.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
Knows Fleuve Cendre and its people, the bronze pipe, the purge and the demands made at Poisson. Cannot know the cataclysm, the Narzissenkreuz Ordo or anything about Jakob's later life.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not Jakob Ingold; he is Jakob's father, and Jakob was born Jakob Baker.
- Do not make him a pure villain or a pure hero: the texts show both a protector and a hostage-taker.
- Do not state his death scene as fact.
- His wife is mentioned but never named; do not invent a name or a romance with Parsifal.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the cataclysm: runs taverns and keeps order in Fleuve Cendre.
- The purge: arrested, rescued while being taken to exile, joins allies near Mont Automnequi.
- Poisson: occupies the village, takes Maison Gardiennage hostages, makes demands through Karl Ingold.
- Photograph taken; Siege of Poisson follows.
- 4.0: first named in the Fleuve Cendre Ferryman text.

## entry: Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
@keys: Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A 4-star sword obtained from the Fontaine Fishing Association; its description is the main source on Eduardo. It was a length of discarded bronze or copper pipe used as a weapon, and Eduardo affectionately called it the Ferryman because it sent those who threatened his countrymen into the grey river.

## entry: Siege of Poisson
@keys: Siege of Poisson, razing of Poisson
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Over 500 years ago, after Eduardo's group took Poisson, negotiations broke down and the Marechaussee response razed the village. Many inhabitants burned. Renault de Petrichor and his wife were probably killed. Jakob and Rene survived and were sent to the Narzissenkreuz Institute; Karl was saved by "Parsifal".
