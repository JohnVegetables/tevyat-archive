## summary
### LITE
Asase Hibiki was a shrine maiden of Asase Shrine on Seirai Island about five hundred years ago. She trained at the Grand Narukami Shrine, loved the samurai Takamine the Mistsplitter, and is believed to have been lost in the Electro storm she unleashed to defend Seirai from the Shogunate.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through item text (Shimenawa's Reminiscence, Emblem of Severed Fate, Hamayumi, Thundering Pulse) and through the cat Neko, who still waits for her. She does not appear in person in any present-day quest.

## background
### LITE
Hibiki left Asase Shrine to hone her skills at the Grand Narukami Shrine on Narukami Island. There she was mentored by the Kitsune Saiguu and grew close to Takamine, the servant of the tengu Reizenbou. Takamine and the Saiguu were lost in the cataclysm, and Hibiki later returned to Seirai.
### STANDARD +
She distrusted the Kitsune Saiguu at first but came to respect her deeply. Hibiki, Kamuna Harunosuke and Mikoshi Nagamasa trained under the Yougou Tengu and became known as the Yougou Three. Takamine taught her the tengu archery he had learned, and she hid her affection behind the nickname "the idiot Konbumaru". He later became a hatamoto of the Raiden Shogun but married another samurai's daughter, kept gambling and wandering, and left her exasperated.

When the cataclysm came, Takamine joined Ei's expedition into the Abyss and entrusted his bow, Thundering Pulse, to Hibiki as a wager on his safe return. He did not return in time. The Saiguu fell defending Inazuma, Reizenbou went into exile, Harunosuke left for Liyue, and the Yougou Three never met again.
### LARGE +
Thundering Pulse's text says that long after, a lone returnee came out of the Abyss and met Hibiki at their promised place in Chinju Forest. Hibiki, who believed the Abyss's corruption could not be undone and had long sworn to end his folly and obsession, shot him with a barbed arrow. The bow was hers as the wager's prize.

She retired to Seirai Island, ran Asase Shrine with the cat Neko and the pirate captain Ako Domeki, told them of her wish to see the world, and never spoke of how her friends had ended. When Domeki rebelled against the Shogunate, she helped him because of their friendship and his resemblance to Takamine. Using the true magic she had secretly learned on Mt. Yougou, she broke the great barrier that held the remaining resentment of Kanna Kapatcir to wreck the Shogunate's ships. The result was the Electro storm that still ravages Seirai, and she never came back from it.
### ULTRA +
The Hamayumi text frames her last choice as a vow not to let "this person" die again, and says she regretted using Reizenbou's teaching this way and hoped the old cat would stay away from the storm. Domeki and some of his crew survived; his chief mate Janome and the ship carrying a defeated Maguu Kenki were blown to the Golden Apple Archipelago. He later vowed to Janome and Hibiki to live as the freest person in the world. Asase Shrine alone was spared by the storm.

## relationships
### LITE
Takamine the Mistsplitter (love, archery teacher, later killed by her); the Kitsune Saiguu (mentor); Ako Domeki (friend and ally).
### STANDARD +
Reizenbou taught her true magic; Harunosuke and Nagamasa were her fellow trainees. Neko, the shrine cat, still waits for her.
### LARGE +
Domeki called her "Aunt"; canon leaves open whether that was blood or courtesy. She never told Neko or Domeki how Takamine and her other friends ended.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead for about five hundred years: she never returned from the Electro storm over Seirai Island.
### STANDARD +
Neko, made functionally immortal for reasons canon does not explain, still waits at Asase Shrine and does not realise a human would have died long ago. In The Narukami Trail (as summarised by the wiki), the shrine maiden Inagi Hotomi nearly tells Neko that Hibiki is likely dead, and the Traveler softens the answer.
### LARGE +
Her Hamayumi, Thundering Pulse and the artifact sets told from her view are the only direct voices of her that survive. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content found changes her status.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Assuming the Shimenawa's Reminiscence card depicts her, she had brown hair in a ponytail and wore the red-and-white shrine maiden garb of the Grand Narukami Shrine. She carried a warbow in her Hamayumi story.

### STANDARD
Takamine was Reizenbou's servant, a gambler and wanderer who became a hatamoto. Hibiki learned archery from him and held his bow, Thundering Pulse, as the prize of their wager. The two did not marry; he wed another samurai's daughter. Thundering Pulse's text says that when he came back from the Abyss, Hibiki killed him.

## entry: The Seirai storm

### STANDARD
By the item text, Hibiki unsealed the great barrier holding the resentment of Kanna Kapatcir's remains to destroy the Shogunate's ships and shield Ako Domeki's rebellion. The Electro storm over Seirai Island that resulted has lasted to the present; the island was evacuated and Asase Shrine alone was untouched.
