## summary
### LITE
Venerare led the Lawrence Clan in the early days of Mondstadt, roughly 2,600 years ago. Under her guidance the people raised a tall statue in the city plaza for the Anemo Archon's descent, with an oath to protect Mondstadt carved beneath it. She is known from item text only.
### STANDARD +
She is a named ancestor, not a voice: no quest or voice-over features her. Her descendants later betrayed the ideals she stands for.

## background
### LITE
The Royal Grimoire says the Lawrence Clan was led by her in Mondstadt's early days and that she directed the building of the statue. The oath beneath it was inscribed by the leader of all the clans, not by her alone.
### STANDARD +
The clan she led was one of the prominent Mondstadt clans after Barbatos took over, alongside the Gunnhildr and Imunlaukr clans. Wiki summaries describe the early Lawrences as pioneers who survived the frozen wilderness outside Decarabian's city.
### LARGE +
Leonard's journal, recovered in Where Ancient Stars Align, thanks "help from Venerare" while recording how he crossed to and climbed Pilos Peak; part of the line is obscured until Mona restores it. The wiki infers from this that she lived before and after Barbatos rose, which is where the roughly 2,600-year date comes from.
### ULTRA +
The Royal Grimoire goes on to say that, over the years, the clan abandoned its forebears' noble aspirations and the statue was taken down, with the royal mages no longer speaking of history or the oath. The rebuilt statue lacks the inscription. Vennessa's rebellion later overthrew the aristocracy.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 2,600 years; the manner of her death is not recorded.
### STANDARD +
She does not appear in any present-day quest, and no text for 6.x or 7.0-7.1 mentions her.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her one individually recorded tie is to the adventurer Leonard, whose journal thanks her. Otherwise she is defined by the clan she led and the Anemo Archon she honoured.
### LARGE +
The tie to Leonard is unexplained in canon. The later Lawrences are her inheritors in name only: the Royal Grimoire contrasts their fall with the noble aspirations of their forebears.

## entry: Royal Grimoire
@keys: Royal Grimoire
@keys2: Venerare, Lawrence
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A four-star catalyst whose lore describes the research of Mondstadt's royal mages. It is the only source for Venerare building the Anemo Archon statue and the lost oath beneath it.
### LARGE +
The text also explains that the royal mages tended water and soil, fought monsters and tutored nobles, and that the people exiled them with the nobles when the royals were overthrown, for becoming like the nobles.
