## summary
### LITE
Xiuhcoatl, the Flamelord, was the Pyro Dragon Sovereign. He ruled Natlantea, ancient Natlan's dragon civilisation, and wielded light, heat, wisdom and the primal flame (Phlogiston).
### STANDARD +
In his last, Abyss-corrupted era he built Huitzilopochtli, meant to burn all Natlan into pure Phlogiston so the Abyss could never touch it. Kukulkan and Xbalanque opposed it. Saurians with the Flamelord's Blessing carry his bloodline.
### LARGE +
Most of what players know about him comes from Kukulkan in the 5.5 world quest An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty. Kukulkan is a biased witness: he loved his brother, opposed him, and in the end took over his plan.
### ULTRA +
Nod-Krai content (6.0 and 6.7) shows an earlier, gentler side of him. Before the Heavenly Principles came, he often visited the dragon researchers on the Frost Moon. He took part in their work on life and consciousness and was remembered for his "characteristic gentleness." The tyrant-machine of Natlan's last dragon era and the curious scholar-king of the moon logs are the same being, at different times.

## background
### LITE
Born from the primal flame on the Sacred Mountain, he shaped the Sacred City of Tollan there, gave his brother Kukulkan life, and created the Thirteen Sovereign Lords. The Heavenly Principles froze him in lava in a "false death"; he woke when the corrupted Dragon King Nibelung returned, and the Abyss then slowly ate his mind. About two thousand years ago Xbalanque pierced his one weak point, an eye he had made into a device's control hub; both fell, and only Xbalanque rose again, as the first Pyro Archon.
### STANDARD +
Using secret source technology, Xiuhcoatl built the Thirteen Sovereign Lords. They were artificial-intelligence dragonlords, said to be cleverer than the Pyro dragons, and each one governed a territory of Natlantea. While he lay in false death, Kukulkan ruled as regent. When Xiuhcoatl awoke during Nibelung's war of vengeance, he stopped Kukulkan and the Lords from exploring the stars and trying to forge a new moon. Then the dragons overthrew the civilisations the angels had built in Natlan; one artifact text says the newly returned ruler destroyed their "blasphemous citadel". The surviving dragonkind enjoyed what one artifact calls freedom "by the grace of the one who ruled over flames." Meanwhile he was already becoming a shell.
### LARGE +
When Nibelung was killed by the Heavenly Principles, Xiuhcoatl had a short, tragic moment of clarity. He saw that his realm, once filled with primal flame, was now covered in Abyssal sores. He also knew that he himself had become a breeding ground for darkness and that his lucid spells were growing shorter. With the time and wisdom he had left, he rebuilt the dragon city once more. He made the Tzitzimimeh (Astral Assemblages), miniature stars of compressed Phlogiston, and finally Huitzilopochtli. A nameless Secret Source Servant says it was a prototype built to help him record information, and that when he rebuilt Tollan's centre he dug downward to create a stable experimental environment. Kukulkan was horrified when the plan was revealed to him, but no Dragonborn who served Xiuhcoatl could refuse his orders, so he looked for another path among humans.
### ULTRA +
The Lords and the Three Princes once tried to impeach Kukulkan for giving Phlogiston to humans. Kukulkan's disguise Awanyu says they knew their king was only feigning death and that their real aim was to seize Kukulkan's authority. Then Xiuhcoatl, "only occasionally clear-minded," appeared in the flesh, the accusers hastily took back their words, and the trial collapsed into farce. Later the Three Princes died fighting humans. Ten of the Thirteen Lords died when Tollan fell. The last words of four Lords survive as the Iridescent Legacy notes. Several of them call on "my king" as they die, and one admits that their kind had grown enfeebled and decayed. Kukulkan says his brother was all but unharmable: he commanded the primal flame and could plunge his whole body into it. The eye he had to use to "monitor and anchor reality" for Huitzilopochtli was his only weakness. In the vision the Traveler sees in Revelations from the Past (5.0), a giant dragon with pierced pupils and a tiny human fall together while Tollan collapses. Then a man rises from the dead dragon's heart.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His most important bond was with **Kukulkan**. Xiuhcoatl created him on his own birthday, called him brother and made him regent. Kukulkan loved him, said no Dragonborn who served him could refuse his orders, and still worked against Huitzilopochtli: he gave Phlogiston to humans and allied with **Xbalanque**. Xiuhcoatl once told him that in his final moments he would understand his king's desire, and in 5.5 the dying Kukulkan said he finally did. **Xbalanque** was his killer and the founder of the Pyro Archon line. He gave the **Thirteen Sovereign Lords** their existence and their authority to rule. Towards **Nibelung**, the Dragon King, he was a loyal servant: he searched the moons for him and woke to answer his call.
### LARGE +
**Och-Kan**, Kukulkan's half-dragon son, was raised among humans. Kukulkan hoped he would slay Xiuhcoatl and become a new Sovereign uniting dragons and humans. Och-Kan joined Xbalanque instead and later carried the Flamelord's Blessing. The Eleventh Lord, **Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk**, once followed Xiuhcoatl to the three moons. After his defeat she split her mind in despair, and her kinder half became **Ineffa**. Another Lord survives as **Ajaw**.
### ULTRA +
On the Frost Moon he got along well with the dragon researchers. A 6.7 record says he "has always favored" Ungien and the researcher Maagees-Geti, and another hints that a rival only dared to move against them with powerful backing. The recorder Nu-ur-Gestug spoke with him at length about Lusalim's plan to engrave a researcher's mind into data that could re-emerge forever. Xiuhcoatl gave practical suggestions, which were passed on to Lusalim. **Gosoythoth** never met him in life. It stole his remnant memory from the ley lines and wore his form.

## abilities
### LARGE
He held the Pyro elemental Authority and presided over light, heat and wisdom. He was master of the primal flame, Phlogiston, which Kukulkan describes as a kind of "computronium": an atom that can carry information and imitate the essence of anything. He could plunge his whole body into the primal flame unharmed. He passed flaming light and wisdom to his kind through his horn. He created life (Kukulkan and the Dragonborn), artificial minds (the Thirteen Lords and the Secret Source Servants), whole cities (Tollan) and star-like weapons (the Tzitzimimeh). According to legend he turned one of his eyes into "a conduit of supreme authority by which all flames might be governed." That eye was the control hub for Huitzilopochtli, and it was his only exploitable weakness.
### ULTRA +
His limits were real. The Heavenly Principles beat him once and took at least part of his authority. The Abyss corrupted him from within until he could rarely think clearly. And by anchoring Huitzilopochtli to his own eye, he handed a mortal a way to kill him.

## appearance
### LARGE
He is known only through art, a namecard, murals and imitation. Historical depictions show a red dragon with golden accents on its wings. Gosoythoth's copy, the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire, is a colossal serpentine dragon with eight huge flaming wings, clawed arms, horns, long antenna-like feelers and blackened, Abyss-marked patches; it dual-wields enormous swords of Pyro and Abyssal energy. Treat that boss as a corrupted imitation, not a portrait. His actual remains, with a gigantic sword, can be seen in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain near Huitzilopochtli.

## status
### LITE
Dead. His remains lie in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain beneath the Great Volcano of Tollan; no new Pyro Dragon Sovereign exists as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
During the 5.5 world quests, the Traveler and the Saurian Little One, who carries his blood, brought both Golden Entreaties to Huitzilopochtli. Kukulkan offered Little One the chance to become the Pyro Dragon Sovereign renewed, but the activation was cancelled and the array shut down. In the 5.3 Archon Quest, Gosoythoth briefly took his form and was defeated by Mavuika and the Traveler.
### LARGE +
One record says the Pyro vishaps have devolved into Saurians and lost the primal flame's purity, so the next Pyro Sovereign will not be born among them. Machines still greet visitors in his name: the Frost Moon seal (6.0) and an ancient terminal (6.7) say "Welcome, Master of Flame and Wisdom, Your Majesty Xiuhcoatl" when they detect a matching dragon energy signature.

## legacy
### LARGE
In Natlan, folk memory of him is split. Some people believe he still sleeps in the volcano. Others say he is only a husk burned hollow by Xbalanque's fire. His name survives in the Flamelord's Blessing on certain Saurians and in the Golden Entreaties. The Pyro Archon line itself began with his death.
### ULTRA +
A hidden layer of Natlan's history is his doing too. Kukulkan's long plot, which included the false legend of the Blessing's cure, the Jade of Return rumour and the fell dragon over Ochkanatlan, all served to bring the split eye and an heir of Xiuhcoatl back to the array he built.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the Heavenly Principles: born from the primal flame on the Sacred Mountain; creates Kukulkan the same day, then the Dragonborn; shapes Tollan; creates the Thirteen Sovereign Lords; visits the Frost Moon and its researchers.
- After Nibelung leaves for the stars: visits the three moons with the Eleventh Lord looking for him and gets only silence.
- Conquest of the Seven Sovereigns: frozen in lava in a false death; Kukulkan becomes regent.
- War of vengeance (War of Funerary Flame): wakes when Nibelung returns; stops the star-voyage and moon-forging projects; the dragons overthrow the angels' civilisations in Natlan.
- After Nibelung's death: a brief clarity; builds the Tzitzimimeh and Huitzilopochtli and converts an eye into its control hub; becomes increasingly lost to the Abyss.
- About 2,000 years ago: Xbalanque's alliance besieges Tollan; Xiuhcoatl and Xbalanque fall together; Xbalanque revives as Pyro Archon; Xbalanque and Kukulkan split the eye into the two Golden Entreaties.
- Release: 5.0 (first mentions, the duel vision), 5.2 (Och-Kan and the Blessing), 5.3 (Gosoythoth's imitation), 5.5 (Kukulkan's full account, Huitzilopochtli), 5.8 (Fractured Halo, Smoldering), 6.0 and 6.7 (Frost Moon records).
### ULTRA +
- 5.5, Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment: the failed impeachment of Kukulkan and the deaths of the Lords and Princes.
- 5.5, In Search of a Hidden Heart: a nameless Secret Source Servant, built to help him record information, still follows his edict on entry to the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.
- 6.6: the Il Dottore weekly boss summons Xiuhcoatl's Pyro sword as an attack. This is a gameplay detail with no story explanation.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- His name comes from the Nahuatl "turquoise serpent" or "fire serpent" of Aztec myth.
- The 5.0 namecard Natlan: Fiery Dragon describes the dragon king as a "sharer of light, heat, and wisdom" waiting in silence for a coming visitor.

## entry: Huitzilopochtli
@keys: Huitzilopochtli, Sweeping Skyfire, Sunquake, Natlan Phlogistonization Array
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Xiuhcoatl's last creation, in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain. It is a "Natlan Phlogistonization Array" that would burn everything and copy all its information into Phlogiston units, turning Natlan into a realm of pure primal flame. Living beings would die or become Phlogiston-based lifeforms, with draconic ones ruling the "new world." The Tzitzimimeh would then destroy any approaching Abyss through mutual annihilation. Its control hub was one of Xiuhcoatl's pupils, later the two Golden Entreaties. Activation needs three confirmations. Kukulkan led Little One there in 5.5 to activate it, but the sequence was cancelled.
### LARGE +
Towers were built across Natlan to extend its coverage. Och-Kan had most of them destroyed, but their fragments still carry the Iridescent Inscriptions it needs.

## entry: Golden Entreaties (Xiuhcoatl's eye)
@keys: Golden Entreaty, Golden Entreaties, Jade of Return
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Two white discs with golden patterns, made when Xbalanque's sword split Xiuhcoatl's control-hub pupil. Xbalanque and Kukulkan took one each and swore an oath of peace between humans and dragons ("Only the enemy cloaked in shadow shall not be forgiven"); Kukulkan says the alliance was meant to keep Huitzilopochtli from being activated. Xbalanque's half passed to Och-Kan, who hid it on the Tonatiuh, and Kukulkan hid his. Uniting both at the volcano wakes the array.

## entry: Flamelord's Blessing
@keys: Flamelord's Blessing, Flamelord Blessing, Chosen of Dragons
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
A mark on the horn of some Saurians born of Xiuhcoatl's line. It gives them ancient dragon power that their bodies cannot bear; it can drive them mad or kill them, and the Wayob and Night Kingdom reject them. Tradition said bringing both Golden Entreaties to the volcano would wash it away. Kukulkan admitted in 5.5 that he invented this legend to lure a human and a Blessed Saurian, the "Chosen of Dragons," to Huitzilopochtli. ## entry: Lord of Eroded Primal Fire
@keys: Lord of Eroded Primal Fire, The Slain Dragon Molded
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
This is not Xiuhcoatl. During the 5.3 Archon Quest, Gosoythoth, the Reaper of the Abyss, pulled the remnant will of a dead dragon sovereign from the ley-line memories and used it as the base of a fabricated body. It fought Mavuika and the Traveler in this form and was defeated. The boss archive describes a once-dead dragon trapped forever in its last day, fury having consumed its reason.

## entry: Xiuhcoatl and the Frost Moon
@keys: Frost Moon, Nuur Stone Circle, Maagees-Geti, Nu-ur-Gestug
@keys2: Xiuhcoatl, King Xiuhcoatl
@version: 6.7
### LARGE
Nod-Krai records show King Xiuhcoatl often visiting the dragons' Frost Moon research sites before the Heavenly Principles came. He wrote a paper on "the Schema of the Self" based on computronium and hoped to create life with Phlogiston to bring his surface kin "some small relief." He gave suggestions that reached Lusalim's project. After Nibelung vanished, he and the Eleventh Lord asked the three moons for help finding him and got only silence (Fractured Halo, 5.8).
