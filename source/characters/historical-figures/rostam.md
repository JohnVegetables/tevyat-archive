## summary
### LITE
Rostam, the 'Wolf Pup', was a Knight of Favonius and Knight of Boreas in Mondstadt 500 years ago. He devised Favonius Bladework, the sword style the Knights still practise, and stood at Grand Master Arundolyn's side as his closest friend and deputy. He loved the singer Rosalyne, and died in the cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through item texts and Varka's 6.4 story; he never appears as a living character. What survives is a stern, dutiful knight who did the quiet work for the Lion of Light, and whose death turned two people close to him, Arundolyn and Rosalyne, into very different people.
### LARGE +
His reach is long for a man with so little on-screen presence: his sword style survives, a disciple's tragedy runs through the Bloodstained Chivalry set, and his grief-stricken lover became La Signora.

## personality
### LITE
Grave, dutiful and dry. Item texts show a knight with a permanent frown and an unsmiling manner that his cheerful superior teased him about; the flask he carried never held a drop of wine. He worked from the shadows and cared for Mondstadt above himself.
### STANDARD +
He prepared for the worst ('always have a backup plan' is the creed of the Guardian's Band) and taught it to the knights. Only one thing softened him: a maiden's song in the square. He worried more about the future of Mondstadt and his friends than about himself.
### LARGE +
He also took a hard line on evil; the Guardian's Sigil shows him disguising crest and face at night to do what an upright knight could not. Whether this was literal secret-society work is unconfirmed (see uncertainties). He mentored with the same principles, which his disciple later followed to ruin.

## background
### LITE
Born to a peasant family in Mondstadt, he grew up as the playmate of Arundolyn, heir to a line of knights. Both became Knights of Favonius; Arundolyn became Grand Master with the Lion title, Rostam his assistant with the Wolf title. He created Favonius Bladework and died in the cataclysm after twenty-three years of service.
### STANDARD +
As boys the friends played in the wolves' forest, chased boars with slingshots and followed a seelie to a small yellow flower buried for a thousand years; Brave Heart's texts mention a feather and an hourglass the two friends gave each other. He received the title Wolf Pup at twenty-seven. Knights' tradition reserved the Lion and Wolf titles for those with leadership potential, but Rostam did not live to lead.
He was a Knight of Boreas, one of the few recognised by the wolf god Boreas himself. He mentored Roland, the noble-born White Knight, and fell in love with Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter, who sang in the city square. Before she left for the Sumeru Akademiya he gave her a Hydro timepiece.
### LARGE +
When the cataclysm came and monsters poured out after Khaenri'ah's fall, he marched with the Knights against them. Texts describe the knights facing the horror in steady formation. On the eve of battle Arundolyn drank with his friend and promised to toast him if he did not return. Rostam died in the fighting, thinking in his last moments that Rosalyne was safely abroad and that Arundolyn and Roland were still alive.
### ULTRA +
Roland left Mondstadt after the death and wandered Teyvat. Rostam's unsent letter to him was kept at the Knights' headquarters for five centuries, since Arundolyn never found his disciple. The Bell notes a rumour that Rostam took over Kreuzlied's old secret society, formed to help Vennessa overthrow the aristocracy a thousand years earlier, without telling Arundolyn.

## abilities
### LITE
A swordsman of legendary skill with a light, swift, precise style. No Vision is mentioned.
### STANDARD +
Legend says he could cut each raindrop from the sky and that the wind of his sword dance could split a rose and put out a torch. He founded Favonius Bladework, whose single-handed form survives; no practitioner is said to have matched him. The style's essence is defence of Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
Unlike the Lion of Light's lost two-weapon style, his bladework could be taught and still is. His skill was complemented by preparation and the quiet work described in the Guardian's items, if those texts refer to him.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Arundolyn: friend, rival, superior; two halves of one team, described as sword and dagger.
- Rosalyne (La Signora): his lover; she went on to become the Crimson Witch of Flame and then a Fatui Harbinger.
- Roland: disciple, later the Bloodstained Knight.
### LARGE +
Varka, the present Knight of Boreas, carries Rostam's story forward in 6.4. Rosaria names Rostam, Ravenwood and Varka as the few recognised by Boreas himself.
### ULTRA +
In the Lonely Warrior quest, inside a space built from the entangled consciousnesses of Boreas and Roland, Varka finds a Favonius Sword. Rostam's voice asks the 'lonely warrior' to take him to see 'him', and the blessing 'Rostam's Longing' later shields Varka with the words 'You must deliver it'. Varka obliges.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed in the cataclysm 500 years ago after twenty-three years of service.
### STANDARD +
His sword style and the Knight of Boreas title live on in the Knights of Favonius. In 6.4, Varka leaves Rostam's unsent letter at the base of the Black Sword, a shadow of Roland, and tells it that Rostam and Arundolyn never forgot him.
### LARGE +
The Brave Heart, Defender's Will and Elegy for the End texts keep his story alive. Research found no appearance of him in Nod-Krai (6.x) beyond the 6.4 Varka quests, and none in Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Childhood: plays with Arundolyn in the wolves' forest.
- Youth: both become knights; Rostam mentors Roland.
- Age 27: receives the Wolf Pup title.
- Before the cataclysm: gives Rosalyne a Hydro timepiece as she leaves for Sumeru.
- Cataclysm, about 500 years before the main story: fights the monsters and dies after 23 years of service.
- Afterwards: Arundolyn stops fighting; Rosalyne returns to find him dead; Roland wanders.
- 1.0: his story is told in weapon and artifact texts.
- 6.4: Varka's Lupus Majoris quests carry his unsent letter and his sword's longing.

## trivia
### LARGE
- The wiki notes that Rostam is the name of a Persian hero who slew a dragon; no link to any Genshin dragon is known.
- The blessing granted by the Favonius Sword in Varka's story quest is called 'Rostam's Longing'.
- Varka's seventh character story recalls a legend that Rostam once listened to a tender nocturne beneath the starlight and drifted off to sleep, one of the verses added over the centuries to the Knight of Boreas homecoming song.

### STANDARD
Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter sang in the square in Mondstadt, and the stern knight Rostam loved her. He gave her a Hydro timepiece before she left for the Sumeru Akademiya. She came home to a ruined city and his death, and became the Crimson Witch of Flame, later La Signora.
