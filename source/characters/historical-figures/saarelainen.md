## summary
### LITE
The Third Descender, a visitor from beyond Teyvat who loved Anastasya (the future Cryo Archon) and fathered the line that became the Khaenri'ahns. To save her he let the Heavenly Principles turn his body into the seven Gnoses, and was erased from memory.
### STANDARD +
Almost nothing of him survives except through his consequences: the Gnoses, the Archon system built on them, Stellar Linchpins and the Khaenri'ahns' descent. By 7.1 the Tsaritsa treats him as the origin of the objects she collects.
### ULTRA +
He is known by two different kinds of record. The Snezhnayan account in the 7.1 Archon Quest gives a calm, tragic bargain recounted by Rhinedottir. The Frostmoon Scions' hymn, preserved in Nod-Krai, remembers him as a cheerful young suitor and a saint to be prayed to. The two do not fully agree, and the hymn is known to contain errors, so say which one is being used.

## background
### LITE
He came to Teyvat from beyond the world, probably from the sea of stars, more than 6,000 years ago and settled in ancient Snezhnaya. He and Anastasya had a son. After the Belyi Tsar incited them to open a gate to the Abyss, the Heavenly Principles erased her; he ascended to Celestia and traded his body for her life.
### STANDARD +
The bargain, as Rhinedottir tells it: the Heavenly Principles agreed to restore Anastasya and never to lay a hand on his descendants. In exchange his remains were split into seven pieces to consolidate their system of rule. Because his power, knowledge and final acts made him dangerous in their eyes, they insisted that everything about him be forgotten, and used Irminsul to erase him. To hold them to their word he laid a curse that would take effect only if the promise were broken. (Neuvillette's Vision story separately says the usurper, its functions ruined in the war of funerary flame, created the Gnoses together with "one who came after".)
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir says his body had an unparalleled affinity for the power of the human realm and could amplify the elements, and that this was his bargaining chip. Anastasya concludes that the Cataclysm in Khaenri'ah 500 years ago triggered the curse, and that this is why the Heavenly Principles are asleep. The Belyi Tsar, who escaped punishment by fleeing into the Shadow Realm, kept some memory of him and told Anastasya the lost story; she did not trust the Tsar, though Rhinedottir thinks his account was in all likelihood true. Rhinedottir says she had read the Celestia records about the incident.
### ULTRA +
Between the bargain and the present his remains passed through the hands of the gods; Ronova calls them their playthings. The Belyi Tsar felt guilty over his part in the Descender's fate and, in the wiki's reading of his notes, this fuelled his drive to become the Primordial Human. Anastasya inherited the guardianship of a seal Saarelainen left beneath the palace, which went unnoticed by the Heavenly Principles. The Jester says Stellar Linchpins, like the Fatui's transcription prisms, contain his power.

## relationships
### LITE
Anastasya, his lover and the mother of his son; the Belyi Tsar, who incited him and alone remembered him; the Heavenly Principles, whom he bargained with and cursed; the Khaenri'ahns, his descendants.
### STANDARD +
Anastasya has no memory of him and says the Tsar's account felt like someone else's story. Rhinedottir, a Khaenri'ahn herself, pays her respects to Anastasya as "Mother of Khaenri'ah" for this reason. Anastasya did not know how to face the Khaenri'ahns.
### LARGE +
The Traveler and Paimon learned of him through Neuvillette (4.2) and later from the Tsaritsa; Paimon was unsettled by the comparison between the Descender's special body and the Traveler's. Skirk is the one who first named the remains.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed by the Heavenly Principles after his bargain; his remains are the seven Gnoses. The Tsaritsa holds six of them as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Anastasya says that Gnoses cannot coexist for long with the Ley Lines and one day will be driven out; she hopes to gather all seven to open his seal. The Pyro Gnosis, which the Traveler reports (citing Mavuika) has returned to the Ley Lines, was still not in her hands in 7.1.
### LARGE +
His legacy is felt in the Stellar Linchpin and the Fatui's transcription prism, which the Jester says both contain his power and are therefore free of some of Teyvat's laws.

## chronology
### LARGE
- More than 6,000 years ago: arrives and meets Anastasya during the unified civilization; they have a son.
- Later, after the Belyi Tsar's incitement: the gate to the Abyss is opened; Anastasya is erased; he ascends to Celestia and bargains; he is killed and erased from Irminsul.
- 500 years ago: the Cataclysm triggers his curse and the Heavenly Principles fall dormant.
- 4.2: Skirk's message names his remains as the Gnoses.
- 6.0 (Nod-Krai): the Hymns of the Far North preserve his prayer song.
- 7.1: the Tsaritsa reveals his seal and Rhinedottir narrates his bargain.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- "Saarelainen" is a Finnish surname meaning "islander", and the wiki notes a probable link to Ahti Saarelainen (Lemminkainen) of the Kalevala, who journeys to Pohjola to woo a daughter of Louhi.
- The hymn's title for him, the "Stranger of Winter's Day", is also the second title in the wiki profile.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not Anastasya's father, brother or a Snezhnayan fae; he is a Descender from beyond Teyvat.
- He did not willingly "become" the Archon system: he gave up his remains in a deal to save Anastasya.
- No canon voice, appearance or personality exists for him, so do not invent lines for him.
### LARGE +
- The hymn's claim that Anastasya was the Belyi Tsar's daughter is wrong.
- Do not treat the Wings of the Silent Moon folk tale as history.
- Do not say what his seal contains.

## entry: The bargain in Celestia
@keys: Saarelainen, Third Descender, Stranger of Winter's Day
@keys2: bargain, Celestia, curse, Gnoses, Anastasya
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Belyi Tsar incited Anastasya and the Third Descender to open a gate to the Abyss, the Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya as punishment. Saarelainen ascended to Celestia and offered his remains to be split into seven pieces to consolidate their system of rule, asking in return that Anastasya be resurrected and that his descendants never be harmed. The Heavenly Principles accepted but required that all of him be forgotten. He bound them with a curse that would take effect only if the promise were broken.
### LARGE +
Anastasya called him naive for trusting a higher being, but Rhinedottir replied that the curse made his deal enforceable. The Cataclysm in Khaenri'ah, which struck his descendants, set it off. This is the only detailed account of the bargain; Rhinedottir says it came from Celestia's records, and Anastasya says her own memory of events is unreliable.

## entry: Gnoses and Visions as his remains
@keys: Gnosis, Gnoses
@keys2: Third Descender, Saarelainen, remains, Stellar Linchpin
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Skirk told Neuvillette in 4.2 that a Gnosis is the "remains of the Third Descender", and Neuvillette told the Traveler. In 7.1 Anastasya says the Gnoses do not originate in Teyvat's system, but from him, and that they amplify their bearers' power.
### LARGE +
Anastasya says that Gnoses cannot coexist with the Ley Lines for long. The Jester says Stellar Linchpins and transcription prisms also contain his power.

## entry: The Third Descender's seal
@keys: Third Descender's seal, seal beneath the palace, Zapolyarny Palace seal
@keys2: Saarelainen, Third Descender, Anastasya
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In a hidden basement of Zapolyarny Palace there is a seal left by the Third Descender, the last thing he did before he was killed. The Belyi Tsar kept it first and Anastasya after him. Only a Gnosis responds to it; every other attempt to open it has failed. Anastasya thinks the secret inside is vital and may be revealed only when the remains are no longer scattered, which is one of her two reasons for collecting the Gnoses.

## entry: Prayer Song XVI of the Hymns of the Far North
@keys: Hymns of the Far North, Prayer Song XVI
@keys2: Saarelainen, Stranger of Winter's Day, Pohjola, Frostmoon Scions
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Nod-Krai the Frostmoon Scions keep a hymn to him as a healer of wounds and sickness. It calls him a cheerful, kind-hearted, noble-born youth who rode a starry steed past Hiisi Island and Pohjola to court the daughter of Pakkaisukko. In this version Pakkaisukko demands that he open the gates of Pohjola, and the Lord of the Seven Calamities carves him into seven pieces. The priestess Ehrnrooth notes that Aila, the first Moonchanter, later simplified the prayer.
### LARGE +
The same hymnal curses the "poisonous plot" of Pakkaisukko, whom the wiki identifies with the Belyi Tsar. Later quests show that Anastasya was a herald of the Tsar's court, not his daughter, so treat the details as folk memory distorted by the Heavenly Principles' changes to Irminsul.

## entry: Wings of the Silent Moon tale
@keys: Wings of the Silent Moon, Snow-King
@keys2: Saarelainen, traveler, frost spirit
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A fae folk tale says a snowland king had to cross a realm of pitch-black silence, behind a door deep in the earth, to kill an undying enemy, so a spirit of frost and "a traveler from beyond the palace walls" wove him two wings of ancient moonlight. The wiki connects the pair to Anastasya and Saarelainen. The text itself admits the tale may be a later invention to explain a wind glider.

## entry: Stellar Linchpins and his remains (Fontinalia event)
@keys: Stellar Linchpin, Stellar Linchpins
@keys2: Third Descender, Saarelainen, remains, impurities
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
During the limited-time Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7), Marionette told the Traveler that Fatui research links Stellar Linchpins to the Third Descender: the Heavenly Principles used his remains to make the seven Gnoses to consolidate the Archon system, so Visions, which come from that system, are to some extent related to him; and when a Stellar Linchpin manifests, impurities from his remains are believed to get fixed in place, which would explain the power from beyond Teyvat that Linchpin users often show.
