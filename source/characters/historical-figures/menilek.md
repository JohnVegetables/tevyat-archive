## summary
### LITE
Menilek was a hero of the Flower-Feather Clan who bore the Ancient Name Vuka and rode with his Qucusaur companion Ngoubou. He was one of the six heroes who joined Tenoch against the Abyss and died in the last battle about 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
Clan songs remember him for adventures with Ngoubou, a long friendship with Tenoch, and a boisterous love of drink. Chasca inherited his Name after he acknowledged her in 5.1.
### ULTRA +
His legend sits between triumph and tragedy: the same texts that praise him as a wilful, hopeful hero also record a promise he could not keep to Ngoubou.

## background
### LITE
A warrior of the Flower-Feather Clan, he and the Qucusaur Ngoubou made pilgrimages together, joined Tenoch's fellowship as its second member, and with five other heroes fought the dark tide of the cataclysm. He died with Wanjiru and Sundjatta; Tupac lived just long enough to carry Mavuika to the Sacred Flame.
### STANDARD +
Flower-Wreathed Feathers tells that an old sage urged Menilek to end a dying Saurian whelp, saying a calamity older than his Name ran in its veins; he ignored the advice, healed the whelp with elixir, and they set out together. The clan's songs say he pulled mountains apart with his bowstrings, that Ngoubou used cunning to pierce a wicked python's throat, and that together they fought a huge monster for forty days and defeated it with a pike. Early on he contended against Tenoch beneath burning rocks and came to admire his courage.
### LARGE +
In Talking Stick he is the second to join Tenoch, calling him a hero among heroes and a brother despite the tribes' quarrels. In Sunset, on the night before the final battle, he drinks with Sundjatta, Tupac and Wanjiru, toasts Sanhaj and Burkina, and falls asleep before the last toast. In the battle he fights beside Mavuika; Tupac later says Menilek's headlock outlived him and smothered a giant beast.
### ULTRA +
His last words in the bow's story are to Ngoubou, asking him to rest while he finishes, promising to return and see the flowers on the high cliffs. In Chapter V of the Archon Quest, when Chasca's sister Chuychu dies, a golden Menilek speaks from Chasca's Ancient Name: he regrets how unfair life was to her and asks what she will find if she rises above her pain. Then he says the epitaph "My life's suffering shall be my epitaph" with her.

## relationships
### LITE
Ngoubou (his companion Qucusaur); Tenoch (friend and fellow hero); Chasca (the later bearer of Vuka); Tupac, Sundjatta and Wanjiru (fellow heroes).
### STANDARD +
Mavuika, the Pyro Archon he trusted, whose plan he toasted.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 500 years. His Ancient Name Vuka belongs to Chasca, and his memory survives in the Flower-Feather Clan's songs.
### STANDARD +
His Ngoubou outlived him as the mist-dwelling tyrant, according to the boss text. No new appearance found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content as of 7.1.

## entry: Holawaqa Ngoubou
@keys: Ngoubou, Holawaqa Ngoubou, Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The text archived for the Golden Tyrant of the Broken Oath calls him a Qucusaur whose body took on a supernatural form from forces modern Saurians cannot control. It says the giant of the mountain fog once followed the heroes who saved Natlan, and that one of them agreed to make the pilgrimage to the volcano with him to pull his mind back from the brink; his present title suggests this did not end well. Flower-Wreathed Feathers ties the story to Menilek.

## entry: Vuka
@keys: Vuka, Transcendence
@keys2: Menilek, Chasca
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
Vuka is an Ancient Name of the Flower-Feather Clan, rendered "Transcendence" in the Flower-Wreathed Feathers text. Menilek was an earlier bearer; Chasca holds it now, with the epitaph "My life's suffering shall be my epitaph; to remember the pain I endured, and finally, transcended."
