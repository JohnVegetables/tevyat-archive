## summary
### LITE
Lianca was a woman raised by dragons who founded the Flower-Feather Clan as a refuge for the weak and outcast. She was the first bearer of the Ancient Name Vuka and one of Natlan's Six Heroes. She died fighting Och-Kan after her son betrayed her.
### STANDARD +
She is known mainly through Chain Breaker's legend and flashbacks in the 5.8 quests Flamelets and Searing. She refuses titles and thrones: the clan's people are, in her words, crownless kings.

## background
### LITE
An abandoned infant, she was adopted by Ix Ajkotz'i'j Iq'ismal Waxak, the Eighth Sovereign Lord, and taught to kneel to no one. At the usual age she set out alone to find her own path, saw how rare freedom was, and resolved to build a place where the weak could live without fear. Outcasts, orphans and banished dragons gathered around her; she turned none away. This became the Flower-Feather Clan.
### STANDARD +
She told Xbalanque she was no king and would travel with him only if he sincerely wanted a place where the weak were equals. When Och-Kan raised Ochkanatlan, her clan joined the Grand Alliance, but she came to doubt him and refused to recruit more labourers for his dig. She later had a son, who was her chosen successor, and two daughters.

The wiki says she could also fight capably with a staff. She wielded the bow Chain Breaker, a gift forged at her mother's request by the Second Sovereign Lord.
### LARGE +
After Xiuhcoatl's death, the Eleventh Lord asked Lianca to save them as she had her mother. The Eleventh Lord had split into one half longing for a home and another swearing vengeance on humanity, and tried to trick Lianca into destroying the kind half. She saw through it, sealed the vengeful half in the Temple of Cascading Colors, and sent the other half out of Natlan with a Moon Wheel in her core. In Searing (5.8) flashbacks she tells Ixquieh she trusts not the Eleventh Lord's malfunctioning half but her own path, and believes every conscious being has a right to freedom.
### ULTRA +
Her son, a secret adviser to Och-Kan, urged him to remove the hidden danger of the Flower-Feather Clan before it could rebel. Och-Kan gathered his forces. The Chain Breaker text says she shot her son first, then Och-Kan's minions, and nailed her own body to the bow with her last arrow rather than kneel. In Fires of Autumn Twilight (as summarised by the wiki), her second daughter later set a fire that killed herself and a former friend who sought to marry into the chiefdom.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the Ochkanatlan era, killed in the clash with Och-Kan. Her clan and her Ancient Name Vuka live on. The 5.8 quests show voice-only flashbacks of her, and the Priestess of Floral Candles and Skyborne Feathers says her adoptive mother survived the curse because Lianca saved her.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her dragon mother, the Eighth Lord, shaped her creed. Xbalanque and Ixquieh were allies and friends; Och-Kan became her enemy. Ineffa exists because Lianca spared and hid one half of the Eleventh Lord.
