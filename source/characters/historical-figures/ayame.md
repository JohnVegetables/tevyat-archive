## summary
### LITE
Ayame, called Umigozen, was a diver and warrior of Watatsumi Island's Yuna Clan, younger twin of the shrine maiden Mouun. About two thousand years ago they led the first Watatsumi navy in the war against the Narukami Shogunate. She vanished at the war's end and her fate is unknown.
### STANDARD +
She is known through weapon and book text only. Her naginata survives as Wavebreaker's Fin, and she appears in no present-day quest.

## background
### LITE
The Yuna Clan, whose line traces back to Enkanomiya, was known for whalesong and closeness to sea life. Mouun entered Sangonomiya Shrine while Ayame became a pearl diver and sold what she gathered. Folk-tunes call Ayame valiant and cheerful, strong enough to wrestle fierce sea creatures.
### STANDARD +
The Watatsumi people suffered famine and disease on an island with little farmland, and looked to invade neighbouring Yashiori. Their god Orobashi hesitated out of fear of Ei's power but gave way. The Divine Priestess put the twins in command of the first navy. They also took in a nameless orphan, later Akuou, as their apprentice and taught him Yuna arts such as whalesong. The Watatsumi forces conquered Yashiori and made Akuou its king, which drew the Narukami Shogunate.
### LARGE +
Ei came with her tengu general Sasayuri. After a brutal war the Narukami forces prevailed, though Sasayuri died. Ei slew Orobashi and Akuou, and Mouun was ambushed and killed by Sasayuri's subordinates while retreating. Ayame stood on to the end and disappeared. Her mansion, the Palace in a Pool beneath Suigetsu Pool, was left empty and later sealed by seawater. Her naginata was taken by the tengu.

## relationships
### LITE
Mouun (older twin sister); Akuou (apprentice, lover of Mouun); Orobashi (god of her people).
### STANDARD +
Her enemies were Ei, the Raiden Shogun of the time, and the tengu general Sasayuri.

## status
### LITE
Missing for about two thousand years; presumed lost at sea. Her fate is left to legend.
### STANDARD +
Wavebreaker's Fin records two versions: a last stand among the tengu, or a voyage into the Dark Sea. Canon chooses neither.
### ULTRA +
The Life of Mouun the Shrine Maiden says she vanished into a blood-red ocean after a valiant stand; the weapon text adds that her song may still echo in seashells and the bellies of whales. No present-day quest shows her or her palace occupied.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No canon text describes her face or build. Her weapon is a naginata whose edge glows with the phosphorescence of the deep sea.

### STANDARD
Mouun, Ayame's older twin, was a Sangonomiya shrine maiden who persuaded the giant whale Daikengyou to join the Watatsumi cause. She and the whale were killed in the retreat after Orobashi and Akuou fell, and the Shogunate took their remains.
