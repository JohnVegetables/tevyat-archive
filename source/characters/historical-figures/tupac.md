## summary
### LITE
Tupac, called the Giant, was a mountain-sized warrior of the People of the Springs. He was the last of six heroes to join Tenoch against the Abyss, fought in the final battle, and brought the fallen Mavuika to the Sacred Flame before dying on his feet.
### STANDARD +
He bore the Ancient Name Umoja, "unity", which now belongs to Mualani. Tribal songs remember him for hot springs raised from the ground and a fight with a two-tailed whale; in the animated short he is cheerful, loyal and unafraid of death.
### ULTRA +
He is known only through a projection of his Name, item texts and the 5.3 short. Nothing places him in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## background
### LITE
A warrior of the People of the Springs, descended from the hero Uenuku, Tupac was said to have no equal in Natlan. Bored by peace, he crossed the sea in search of beasts and treasures, and in his youth fought Tenoch in a long rivalry over his drinking horn.
### STANDARD +
When the Abyss attacked, Tenoch (now expelled from the tribes) visited him, and the two former enemies laughed over their scars and joined hands. He was the sixth and last of Tenoch's recruits, one from each major tribe. He fought through the final battle five hundred years ago, carried Mavuika to the Sacred Flame and died.
### LARGE +
Weapon texts tell of a Tupac who felt born too late to stand beside the ancient hero Xbalanque or to fight the Python King, and who waded into the sea instead. He took a winged dragon's wings for sails, a whale's bones for a boat and a sea serpent's fang for his trusty weapon, then ate the serpent with his drink. A voice at the sea's edge told him to keep his oath until the exile who was once his foe returned, and the text presents this as the moment he understood his duty. His Ash-Graven Drinking Horn, kept for his kin, was never used in battle.
### ULTRA +
Another ballad credits him with breaking mountains in a rage and thrusting rocks into the earth to make the hot springs seen in Natlan's southwest. Scars were his trophies: when Tenoch arrived he was counting more than three hundred scars, twenty broken bones and over a hundred obsidian shards left by challengers. The storyteller's lesson from his tale is that unity and promises must never be forsaken. Five centuries later the projection of his Name appeared to Mualani in the Night Kingdom and passed her his memories, including those of Mavuika's plan.

## status
### LITE
Dead. He died on the steps of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, still standing, about 500 years before the present day. His Ancient Name Umoja is now borne by Mualani.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 nothing contradicts his death. He survives as a Name projection, tribal ballads, item texts and the memory of the other cataclysm heroes.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Tenoch was his greatest foe and became his comrade. He fought beside Wanjiru, Menilek and Sundjatta, mourned Sanhaj and Burkina, and gave his last strength to Mavuika. Uenuku was his ancestor; Mualani carries his Name.
### LARGE +
The comrades were openly fond of each other: they drank, joked and comforted one another the night before the battle. Tupac wished Mavuika could have joined them; Wanjiru answered that she needed time alone.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Enormous strength and size. The wiki records a hammer in his battle depiction and a boomerang-like catalyst in item text, and notes he was shown controlling Hydro, but canon does not say he had a Vision.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A giant, many times taller than most people, with a sandstone-like body and armour. Texts say no mount could carry him and that tables served as his chairs. He carried a huge hammer on his back and was covered in scars from challengers.
### LARGE +
When his Name manifested to Mualani he appeared as a dark form with a golden aura, and the characters of "Umoja" glowed on his chest.

## entry: Final Battle and the Sacred Flame
@keys: Tupac last stand, Tupac Sacred Flame, Tupac carried Mavuika
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the "Sunset" short, Tupac drinks with Wanjiru, Menilek and Sundjatta the night before the last battle and jokes about it. In the fight, Menilek dies with a monster in a headlock, Sundjatta falls to a blow meant for Wanjiru, and Tupac, gravely wounded, carries the unconscious Mavuika up to the Sacred Flame. He tells her she is their flame and must burn on and never go out. By the time she wakes he is standing motionless and dead. She then places her life in the flame.
### LARGE +
Weapon text says that to his final breath he did not betray his oath. The wiki records that the other three heroes died in the same battle; Wanjiru's death is not shown on screen.

## entry: Ash-Graven Drinking Horn
@keys: Ash-Graven Drinking Horn, Tupac's drinking horn
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
A ritual vessel passed down among the Meztli (People of the Springs). The weapon text, a storyteller's song, has the young Tenoch demand it from Tupac and Tupac answer that he must wrestle him first. The horn was a treasure much valued and never used in battle. In the song, Tupac later offers the horn before the Pyro Archon, a gesture of his last hope.

## entry: Waveriding Whirl
@keys: Waveriding Whirl, Tupac's sea voyage
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
A boomerang made from a giant sea serpent's fangs, said to be the weapon the giant Tupac called trusty. The text recounts his voyage on a whale-bone boat with a dragon's wings for sails.

## entry: Umoja and Mualani
@keys: Umoja, Mualani and Tupac
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mavuika's plan: each tribe's Wayob would acknowledge one hero who inherits an original hero's Ancient Name. After Mualani helped Kachina in the Night Kingdom, Tupac appeared to her, told her that her Name would let her unite the tribes, touched fists with her and vanished, transferring his memories to her. Mavuika later confirmed that Umoja means "unity" and is the Name once held by the hero of Mualani's tribe.
