## summary
### LITE
Guizhong, divine name Haagentus, was the God of Dust. She was not a strong god but a brilliant inventor, and she ruled the ancient Guili Assembly north of Mt. Tianheng with Morax (now Zhongli). She died in the Archon War about 3,700 years ago. Her legacy includes the Guizhong Ballista, the stone dumbbell called Memory of Dust, and the name of the Guili Plains.
### STANDARD +
To her friends among the adepti she was the centre of every gathering: extroverted, joking, fond of banquets and of building machines. Her death scattered her people and led Morax to found Liyue Harbor. Her closest friends, Madame Ping and Cloud Retainer, still grieve for her.
### LARGE +
Most of what is known about her comes from other people: a few lines on ancient stone tablets, a weapon description written from Morax's memory, and Cloud Retainer's and Madame Ping's reminiscences during the 3.4 Lantern Rite. Ordinary Liyue people barely know her name. To scholars like Soraya she is the author of a riddle, and to Treasure Hoarders the ballista is an obstacle.
### ULTRA +
She matters in roleplay as an absence. She sets the mood of the Guili Plains and of the Glaze Lily. She is a grief that Zhongli, Ping and Cloud Retainer carry differently, and her belief that guided humans can grow to equal the gods foreshadowed Liyue's later story. Describe her through those who remember her; she cannot appear in the present.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Morax remembers her as a young woman with billowing sleeves, who acted solemn but looked joyful. In official depictions (the 3.4 event cinematic and story teaser) she has long grey hair fading to blue, worn partly in a bob with a long tail held by a dark-blue hairpin. Her long sleeves hide her hands, lined with a starry-night pattern and embroidered with Glaze Lilies. Her white, blue and navy dress frays into dust-like fragments at the hems. She goes barefoot with a blue anklet.
### LARGE +
Unlike sterner adepti such as Cloud Retainer, she is usually drawn smiling. In-game text describes only the sleeves, her solemn-but-joyful bearing and her lonely last smile. The rest comes from official art and video, not dialogue.

## personality
### STANDARD
Cloud Retainer describes her as outgoing and in love with both social gatherings and inventions. She kept a table of friends, joined their arguments for fun or broke them up with absurd suggestions, and could say outrageous things without giving offence. She was competitive: she boasted that neither squabbling adeptus could ever beat her.
### LARGE +
She was self-aware about her weakness. She told Morax that since the gulf in strength between them was so great, she would rely on technique and wisdom instead. She first saw humans as fragile beings, "as small and fragile as dust", to be protected by her wisdom. Living among them changed her mind. She came to marvel at their spirit and warned that a fully developed human might equal an adeptus. Madame Ping remembers a saying of hers that it takes every blade of grass and every flower to make a homeland.
### ULTRA +
She believed machines could make simple, fine melodies even if they could never replace human composers, a view that sparked her long debate with Ping. At her death she made light of her own token, asking Morax to forget the stone dumbbell. Cloud Retainer credits part of her victory over Cloud Retainer's own inventions to her "sheer eloquence".

## background
### LITE
She descended at an unknown time and led her people to the plains north of Mt. Tianheng, where she taught them farming. Allied with Morax, she named the land the Plains of the Returning and Departing (Guili) and set a great crossbow on the mountain to guard it. The Archon War destroyed the Guili Assembly and killed her.
### STANDARD +
Under her the farms reportedly stretched as far as Stone Gate. She chose the name because her uprooted people had found a new home there, as if they had returned. The name joins a character from her name with one from the name Morax used then (the "li" also found in "Zhongli"). She laid down Four Commandments for her people: teach with wisdom, be bound by virtue, fortify the bones, unite in ambition. Marchosius, the Stove God, also served the Assembly. In the war the adepti could not hold the plains. Guizhong fell, a flood followed, and Morax led the survivors south on a storm-beaten march of more than ten days ('a dozen days' in the English text) to found Liyue Harbor.
### LARGE +
She first met Morax in a field of Glaze Lilies and gave him a stone dumbbell holding all her wisdom, calling it both the mark of their pledge and a challenge to him. There was no formal contract; they were two people walking the same path for their own reasons. She hosted banquets at a great stone table for adepti such as Cloud Retainer, Streetward Rambler (Madame Ping) and Sea Gazer, with Marchosius cooking and Morax bringing cups Cloud Retainer thought too square. At Cloud Retainer's abode on Mt. Aocang a stone table still has a chair carved "Here sits Guizhong", with a bowl and chopsticks set before it.
### ULTRA +
Cloud Retainer once invited Rex Lapis to judge their rival inventions, and he ruled Guizhong's Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism the better. Guizhong and Ping argued over a bell that could compose and play music until Rex Lapis confiscated it for ceremonial use. Afterwards the two met often in the mountains to talk about music, mechanics and mortal affairs. Guizhong and Cloud Retainer jointly built Ping a potted-plant mechanism. One winter they all rode Cloud Retainer's snow mechanism until Moon Carver joined and crashed it into a snowbank (Xianyun's voice-over). The domain text says Guizhong also gathered ancient, evil artifacts while researching mechanics, and the adepti made the Realm of Clouds at Luhua Pool to contain them; she is named its former ruler. Her manuscripts lie unfinished there. When war reached the plains she was overpowered. Ping and Cloud Retainer arrived too late and found only her body among the ruins. Morax remembers her last words among the Glaze Lilies: their journey together had ended, and he should forget the dumbbell. Her form dissolved into dust.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Morax (Zhongli): co-ruler and partner in a pledge without a contract; she was the brains, he the brawn. Madame Ping (Streetward Rambler): her closest friend after a long rivalry over music. Cloud Retainer (Xianyun): friend and rival inventor who finished her ballista. Marchosius (Guoba): fellow god of the Assembly.
### LARGE +
Morax still remembers the dumbbell he could never unlock and the Glaze Lilies that have since dwindled; In his birthday voice line Zhongli gives the Traveler a dried Glaze Lily. Ping took the Cleansing Bell into her keeping and, for a long time after the death, preferred to be alone with her zither. Cloud Retainer avoided the Guili Plains for millennia because of the sorrow, and still wishes Guizhong were here to solve her mechanical puzzles. Sea Gazer was a regular guest whose treasures she liked to outdo. Ganyu met her a few times.
### ULTRA +
The Mistress of Chenyu Vale was an enemy of the Assembly: she made war on its gods and tried to drown it with the Bishui River. Canon does not say she killed Guizhong. Moon Carver belonged to the same old circle. The scholar Soraya, a modern researcher of her legacy, solved her Four Commandments with the Traveler and doubts that she was a particularly powerful god.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Her strength was invention, not force. She designed the Guizhong Ballista, an automatic siege crossbow that fired on large monsters near Tianheng Pass, and she made the Cleansing Bell and the Memory of Dust. An old tablet says her reach "shrouded the skies for thousands of miles around"; nothing else describes her power in battle.
### LARGE +
The Cleansing Bell is a mechanism that can both compose and perform music. It was later used in Rites of Parting, and its sound is said to purify the mortal realm. The Memory of Dust is a stone puzzle-lock that she said held all her wisdom; Morax never opened it. The ballista still worked thousands of years later: Zhongli repaired it with its stored spare parts in 1.0, and Ningguang's replicas, manned by adepti, fought Osial.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay: Memory of Dust is a 5-star catalyst in the Liyue weapon series; its shield and attack bonuses are game mechanics, not lore. Her element is never stated.

## status
### LITE
Dead: killed in the Archon War about 3,700 years ago. She does not appear in the present and has no known remnant or reincarnation as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
She is remembered chiefly by the adepti and Zhongli, and in Lantern Rite Madame Ping counts her among the heroes the festival honours. Her ballista still stands at Tianheng Pass, guarded by the Millelith.
### LARGE +
In the 3.4 Lantern Rite (limited event) Ping admitted she had not completely moved on, though grief had grown easier with time. Ganyu wondered aloud about replanting a sea of Glaze Lilies on the plains. The Guili Plains remain a ruined wilderness.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not make her a love interest or Morax's wife; canon calls their bond a pledge between two people on the same path. Memory of Dust explicitly says there was no formal contract.
- Do not give her an element, Vision or combat style; none is stated.
- Do not name her killer or state that she will return. Both are unknown or unsupported.
- "Guizhong's Lullaby" is a fan name for the soundtrack piece Lovers' Oath, not canon.
### LARGE +
- She is not a solemn or tragic figure in life: she was the joker and host of her circle.
- Most Liyue people do not know her story; Soraya had to reconstruct it from ruins. Characters should not cite details like the Cleansing Bell's origin unless they were there or were told.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Unknown date: descends; meets Morax among the Glaze Lilies and gives him the stone dumbbell.
- Archon War era: the Guizhong Ballista is set up at Mt. Tianheng; her people farm the plains north of the mountain; the Guili Assembly is founded with Morax and Marchosius and the Four Commandments are laid down.
- Around 3,700 years ago: a battle ravages the Guili Plains and Dihua Marsh, she is killed and a flood follows. The survivors go south and Liyue Harbor is founded.
- 1.0: the Stone Tablet text, the Mt. Aocang stone chairs, Treasure Lost, Treasure Found, and the Archon Quest named "Guizhong" (Zhongli repairs the ballista). In Turning Point the ballista replicas fight Osial.
- 1.1: Memory of Dust released, telling of her first and last meetings with Morax.
- 2.1: Moonlight Merriment (limited) has Ping tell how the Assembly was lost to a flood.
- 3.4: The Exquisite Night Chimes (limited) reveals the name Haagentus, her friendships and the Cleansing Bell's history.
- 4.4: Xianyun's voice-overs recall Guizhong; Chenyu Vale lore shows its goddess warring on the Assembly.
### ULTRA +
- 2026: the limited event Where Waves Meet the Reef has Beidou demonstrate a new Guizhong Ballista from her ship; Guizhong herself is not discussed.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- The Chinese title translates roughly as "Dust-Lord God". The Chinese name of Memory of Dust means "Lock of the Mortal World", and its design is a burr puzzle.
- Haagentus follows the pattern of gods named after demons of the Ars Goetia (Haagenti), per the wiki etymology. This is an observation about naming, not in-world lore.

## entry: Memory of Dust and the pledge with Morax
@keys: Memory of Dust, stone dumbbell, Guizhong's pledge
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Guizhong met Morax in a field of Glaze Lilies and gave him a stone dumbbell. She called it the mark of their pledge and a challenge, saying all her wisdom was hidden inside and beginning a sentence ("If you can unlock it...") that she never finished. They had no formal contract. She thought humans were as fragile as dust and grew clever because they were afraid; since she was far weaker than Morax, she would use technique and wisdom, his brawn and her brains building a great city together. Dying among the lilies, she smiled a lonely smile, said their journey had ended and asked him to forget the dumbbell.
### LARGE +
Morax never unlocked it and never learned the rest of the sentence. The wild Glaze Lilies dwindled until none were left. The account is told from his memory in the weapon description. Use it to colour Zhongli's quiet grief, not to cast him as a widower.

## entry: The Guizhong Ballista
@keys: Guizhong Ballista, Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism, Tianheng Pass crossbow
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A giant crossbow turret on Mt. Tianheng, at Tianheng Pass, built in the distant past to shoot large monsters and protect Liyue. It works on its own and once nearly skewered a Treasure Hoarder. In the 1.0 Archon Quest the Treasure Hoarders under Freckle Huang had disabled it. Zhongli repaired it with spare parts from an old supply post. Its sudden, unexplained repair later led the Qixing to post Millelith around it, and the Yuheng Keqing came to investigate. Its maintenance notice calls it "an intricate machine of divine conception", built by mortal craftsmen.
### LARGE +
Credit for it varies: the tablet history names Guizhong, Zhongli says an adeptus, and Cloud Retainer says she crafted it and later finished Guizhong's device in her memory. In Turning Point, Ningguang produced upgraded replicas that Cloud Retainer, Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper manned against Osial; Osial smashed them. Mass-produced variants were later deployed at Guyun Stone Forest against Beisht.
### ULTRA +
In 3.4 Cloud Retainer recalled that Rex Lapis judged Guizhong's Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism superior to her own work. The wiki equates that mechanism with the ballista, but canon does not state it. During the limited event Where Waves Meet the Reef (2026), Beidou's exhibition project demonstrated the firepower of a new Guizhong Ballista fired from her ship.

## entry: The Cleansing Bell
@keys: Cleansing Bell
@keys2: Guizhong, Haagentus, Madame Ping, Streetward Rambler, Rite of Parting
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In 1.0 Madame Ping lent the Traveler the Cleansing Bell, an adeptal bell whose lasting sound purifies the mortal realm, for Rex Lapis's Rite of Parting. She said she had pestered an old friend for it, and Cloud Retainer later explained that the friend was Rex Lapis. In the 3.4 Lantern Rite event Cloud Retainer revealed that Guizhong made it. It was one of her proudest works, able to compose and perform music, and it began the argument with Ping over whether machines can make music. Rex Lapis confiscated it for ceremonial use. After Guizhong died, Ping asked him for it and he gave it to her. Ping rang it to play the tune Guizhong had composed on it, not funeral music.
### LARGE +
Ping later spent years composing a melody of her own in Guizhong's memory and played it only once, to the clouds; that performance is how she came to save the drowning ancestor of Dvorak, a visitor from Fontaine. She explained that with the one attuned to her soul gone, no one else could understand the tune.

## entry: Guizhong's Four Commandments
@keys: Four Commandments, Teach with wisdom, unite in ambition, fortify the bones
@keys2: Guizhong, Guili, Soraya
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Tablet fragments in the Guili Plains ruins record that Guizhong gave her people four commandments: teach with wisdom, be bound by virtue, fortify the bones, unite in ambition. Together they would be known as the Guili Assembly. In the world quest Treasure Lost, Treasure Found, Soraya, a Sumeru Akademiya researcher, reads them as a riddle about a treasure hidden in Guili. The Traveler solves it by finding jade plates among the ruins.
### LARGE +
The ruin transcriptions phrase each commandment as a maxim. Shared ambition is steadfast for all time; wisdom nourishes like water and reflects truth; strong bones keep movement supple; virtue grows like a tree. They also say the battle on the plains left "black dust" choking the heavens. Soraya believes Guizhong ruled peacefully for a long time and doubts she was a very powerful god.

## entry: The fall of the Guili Assembly
@keys: Guili Assembly, Guili Plains
@keys2: Guizhong, Haagentus, God of Dust, flood, Archon War
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the Archon War the gods fought for dominance. The adepti defended the Guili Plains but could not stop the war, which ravaged the plains and killed Guizhong. The tablets say black dust choked the heavens and a thousand rocks splintered. A flood then swept the Assembly away and Dihua Marsh became a swamp. The survivors went south and, after a storm-beaten march of more than ten days ('a dozen days' in English) in which Marchosius cooked for them, founded Liyue Harbor under Rex Lapis. They never returned to the plains.
### LARGE +
Chenyu Vale lore (4.4) adds that its goddess, maddened by the war, attacked the Assembly's gods and tried to flood it with the Bishui River. Canon still does not say who struck down Guizhong. In Cloud Retainer's cinematic account Guizhong was overpowered by the enemy, and Ping and Cloud Retainer arrived to find her body among the ruins.
