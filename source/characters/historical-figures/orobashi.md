## summary
### LITE
The coral-bodied Great Serpent who was god of Enkanomiya and then of Watatsumi Island, known as Watatsumi Omikami. He fell to Ei near the end of the Archon War, and his remains still scar Yashiori Island.
### STANDARD +
Watatsumi's people still worship him as their guardian, even though he died at the hands of the Raiden Shogun who now rules them. He is known only from legends, books and ruins; he never appears alive in the game.

## background
### LITE
An ancient serpent god, he was beaten in the Archon War and fled Teyvat into the Dark Sea. A child in Enkanomiya's caves asked him to be their god and he accepted. Later he led the people to the surface to found Watatsumi Island, then fought and lost a war with the Raiden Shogun, who killed him.
### STANDARD +
In the Dark Sea the people of Enkanomiya were ruled by the Sunchildren and their puppeteers, and menaced by Bathysmal Vishaps. Orobashi's coming ended the Sunchildren's rule and reduced the vishap threat. In the Oathsworn Eye's text (a weapon from a limited-time event) he says he fled because he could not best either Deus Auri (Morax) or Narukami, and was tired of being shamed for blasphemy.

When he brought the people up to the sunlit world, he broke off the coral growing from his body to make Watatsumi Island and taught them to farm and smelt. They were deeply grateful and worshipped him.
### LARGE +
In Enkanomiya he came across the book Before Sun and Moon, which recorded a history that Celestia wished hidden. The wiki says he and Watatsumi were judged guilty of profanity and deceiving living souls, and that he took the sins upon himself so his people would be spared and returned to Celestia's rule. Wiki sources also say he personally directed experiments on the Dragonheirs of the Depths and bred vishaps able to grow Bloodbranch Coral.

Watatsumi Island could not feed its people: its geology made farming hard and hunger and disease spread. They begged to take fertile Yashiori Island. He was long silent, knowing how the Raiden Shogun had slain the gods she defeated, but his people's repeated pleas finally moved him (the wiki also cites a decree from Celestia). He trained them as warriors, built a navy under the twin shrine maidens Mouun and Ayame, and invaded Yashiori; Mouun's disciple Touzannou (later called Akuou) became its vassal ruler.
### ULTRA +
Ei gathered her troops and waged a brutal war that cost many lives on both sides, including her cherished tengu general Sasayuri. Near its end she slew Orobashi with her signature Musou no Hitotachi. The blow opened Musoujin Gorge and left his remains scattered across Yashiori; he died with his gaze fixed on the sky. Touzannou was severed by the same slash; Mouun was ambushed and killed by Sasayuri's troops as she tried to retreat; Ayame went missing.

After his death, according to the Shogunate historian Kujou Michizane's Sangonomiya Chronicles, Sangonomiya sent envoys to surrender and has recognised the Shogunate as legitimate ruler ever since; the island is still governed by its shrine under the Divine Priestess. Its people nonetheless kept worshipping Orobashi rather than the Shogun. The Preliminary Study says he had ordered Enkanomiya's chronicles sealed in the depths, and that he had the people adopt Narukami-style names.

## appearance
### STANDARD
In legend he had a fluorescent body covered in many-coloured coral. Enkanomiyan carvings show him as a "Coral-Adorned Serpent", and an item text from the Three Realms Gateway Offering event (limited) calls him a giant white snake. Another says his body was as long as the winding shoreline and his voice so loud that only the Dark Sea could take it in.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Ei (the Raiden Shogun): his conqueror. She killed him but let Watatsumi keep worshipping him so that he could still receive prayers.
- The people of Enkanomiya and Watatsumi: he saved them from the Sunchildren and the dark, and they revere him as a guardian deity.
- Mouun and Ayame: twin shrine maidens who commanded his navy. Akuou (Touzannou) was Mouun's disciple, made vassal ruler of Yashiori.
### LARGE +
The Sangonomiya clan leads the people who still honour him, and Sangonomiya Kokomi commands the Watatsumi Resistance. Tension between the Narukami faithful and Watatsumi worshippers has not faded; some Watatsumi residents find peace with the Shogunate hard to accept because it feels like his sacrifice was in vain.
### ULTRA +
In the Vision Hunt Decree war, a radical faction in the Watatsumi Army, at the suggestion of the Fatui mole Nathan, tried to revive him by destroying the Narukami Pillars on Yashiori. Nathan was actually working to release the Tatarigami and to harm the resistance's chances. Centuries earlier, Dottore, under the alias Escher, introduced Crystal Marrow-based smelting at the Mikage Furnace on Tatarasuna, and its Tatarigami fumes caused a disaster there (revealed in the Wanderer's story). In the present day Crystal Marrow was also used to make Delusions at the Delusion Factory.

## status
### LITE
Dead since about the end of the Archon War, roughly 2,000 years ago. His remains lie on Yashiori Island, with his skull at Serpent's Head.
### STANDARD +
His lingering power became the Tatarigami, the phenomenon of storms, lightning, corruption and madness on Yashiori Island. The Shogunate later used his Crystal Marrow in smelting at the Mikage Furnace. Watatsumi still worships him; its Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri, which halts the spread of Holy Soil, uses a Jade Coral Branch said to come from his body.
### LARGE +
As of Version 7.1 nothing shows him alive or returning. In the Orobashi's Legacy world quests the Traveler repairs the ancient wards on Yashiori that suppressed the Tatarigami. In the final parts the Traveler defeats a Maguu Kenki guarding the last ward, and the Tatarigami storm ends. No content in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0 to 7.1) chapters changes his status.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- Common account (accepted on both Narukami and Watatsumi): his starving people pleaded for years for the fertile eastern lands until he relented and invaded.
- The Shogunate's account (Sangonomiya Chronicles): the Great Serpent had kept to the west in peace, then suddenly crossed the boundary and invaded without warning.
- Other legend: he had sinned by fleeing into the Dark Sea to avoid the Archon War and led his people out at heaven's instruction, so perhaps he went to his death on heaven's order; the Preliminary Study says this cannot be known.
- Hypothesis from hidden inscriptions (recorded by Shihab Purbiruni in A Preliminary Study of Sangonomiya Folk Belief): a shrine maiden hid a prophecy of certain defeat; he knew he could not win and went to war to sacrifice himself, so his people would weave their memories into an enduring faith. The author stresses that Sangonomiya's history is poorly recorded.

## entry: Tatarigami
@keys: Tatarigami, Orobashi's remains, Serpent's Head
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
In Inazuma, Tatarigami means the corpse of a god and the odd phenomena it produces. Orobashi's lingering power became the Tatarigami on Yashiori Island, where it brings storms, lightning and corruption, and can drive people there mad. Crystal Marrow, which grows on his bones, carries a sliver of that power and strengthens metal when smelted into it.
### LARGE +
The Shogunate later used it in the Mikage Furnace on Tatarasuna. In Orobashi's Legacy the Traveler helps the researcher Kaji restore ancient Narukami wards that held the Tatarigami back.

## entry: Oathsworn Eye
@keys: Oathsworn Eye
@keys2: Orobashi, Serpent God
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The weapon's text, from the Three Realms Gateway Offering event (limited), shows the serpent speaking to the child who asked him to become a god. He said that he fled because he could not beat Deus Auri or Narukami. He gave the child a jewel like a snake's eye to swear an oath on, saying he had used it to make pacts with his coral servants. Some of the people protest, citing Master Spartacus's teaching never to worship a god, and the serpent respects their will. Later, with the island complete and the vishaps repelled, he takes the remaining people to the surface, 'to fulfill his oath to the heavenly city'.

## entry: The Slaying at Yashiori
@keys: Musoujin Gorge, Yashiori Island war
@keys2: Orobashi, Ei, Raiden Shogun
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Ei killed Orobashi on Yashiori Island with Musou no Hitotachi, and the slash left the great rift of Musoujin Gorge. The war also took the lives of Mouun, Touzannou (Akuou) and Sasayuri. It is the root of the old bitterness between Narukami and Watatsumi.
