## summary
### LITE
Varuch was the priest of Sal Vindagnyr, a mountain nation that once stood on what is now Dragonspine, and the father of its princess. The wiki treats him as probably its last king-priest. He is known from a single item text in which he climbs the peak for guidance while his nation freezes, and he is never heard from again.
### STANDARD +
He has the least recorded about him of the figures in Sal Vindagnyr's story; most of what is said of him comes through his daughter's texts. He is presumably dead and does not appear in any quest.

## background
### LITE
Almost nothing is known of his life before the Skyfrost Nail fell. His daughter was born under the Frostbearing Tree, an ancient silver-white tree tied to the Irminsul, and the whole nation rejoiced at her blessing.
### STANDARD +
When the nail's fragment came to rest over the tree and shattered it, snowstorms covered Sal Vindagnyr. Varuch asked his daughter to paint frescoes of the nation, believing that if she could paint thawing snow and ice they would be safe. She stayed in the great hall to paint while he went up the mountain to find answers, having not received any on earlier tries.
### LARGE +
His Priest's Box text shows a man who feels he has failed his people: he had expected the young to grow strong like cypresses, now sees them withering, and fears that their trust in him will fall through. He plans to climb the peak once more and be back by the time the second painting is done. The wiki notes he was among four figures of Sal Vindagnyr lore, with the princess, Ukko and Imunlaukr.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead, thousands of years ago, probably on the mountain.
### STANDARD +
His traces vanish after he sets out for the summit; no source says he returned or what he found.
### ULTRA +
He has no appearance in any quest, in the 6.x Nod-Krai content or in 7.0-7.1 as far as checked. His text was added with Dragonspine's hidden objectives in version 1.2.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His daughter is the only person linked to him, and the link is a hope: he leaned on her paintings to save the nation, and she apologised in her last words for being unable to finish them.
### LARGE +
The two texts do not meet. He never learns she could no longer remember green grass and clear skies, and she writes of him in the present tense, waiting for his return. The scribe Ukko and the outlander Imunlaukr belong to her story rather than his.

## entry: Priest's Box
@keys: Priest's Box, Princess' Box
@keys2: Dragonspine, Sal Vindagnyr, Varuch
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
One of three boxes of the hidden objective Dragonspine's Last Trio, with the Scribe's Box and the Princess' Box. Its description is narrated by Varuch and is the main source on him.
### LARGE +
The box sits in a chest on top of a tower above the Entombed City. In it the priest laments that his hopes for the young have failed, says he will climb the peak again, and hopes the third painting will show thawing snow and ice. Together the three boxes open a secret room whose carvings continue the story.
