## summary
### LITE
An Angel, once Celestia's envoy guarding the silver tree at Meropis, who foretold Remuria's glory and doom to Remus and gave her life for the Grand Symphony, Phobos.
### STANDARD +
Reduced by ages to a golden bee, she greeted the wandering god Remus in the ruins and guided him to build Remuria. Her gentle wish for human happiness, without a will to shape it, became Phobos.
### LARGE +
Remurians revered her as the infallible oracle. Her prophecy, "the greatest empire will face the most utter destruction", drove Remus to seek a way to defy fate.

## background
### LITE
She was an Angel assigned to guard the Irminsul in a forgotten city, and survived the last deluge. Over long ages she lost her mind and form until only a golden bee remained.
### STANDARD +
Remus, a god who lost his homeland, stumbled into the ruined city and was greeted by golden bees. The envoy foretold that he would have cities and subjects again, that his nation would prosper, and that its own justice would destroy it; she offered to show him the way up. When he asked whether choice was possible in fate, the hollow collapsed and the silver tree became the golden ship Domus Aurea.
### LARGE +
As Remuria prospered, Remus grew troubled and Sybilla repeated that empires rise and fall by Fortuna's law, but sent him to Egeria in the deep sea. When Egeria had no answer, Remus asked Sybilla for a symphony to break fate's chains. According to artifact text she answered his demand without hesitation, as she always did. She entrusted her wisdom to him, the two powers merged, and Phobos was created.
### ULTRA +
Phobos says Sybilla wanted to grant happiness to all who are called humans but, without a persona and with limited strength, she could not shape it. Phobos therefore answered every wish, including violent and selfish ones, and brought about Remuria's ruin. A scholar's note says the Seal of Chymical Marriage was one of the gifts the Golden Bee bestowed on Remus (what the others were is unclear) and is older than Remuria itself. The Angelos' Heptades text names her one of Nicole's elder sisters.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Remus received her prophecy and her sacrifice. Phobos is what she became in part. Nicole is her youngest sister, with Nabu Malikata and Koitar the other elder sisters.
### LARGE +
The wiki describes the Golden Troupe and Marechaussee Hunters as secret orders through which Remurian arts were passed on after the Archon War. Egeria is the goddess she told Remus to consult.
### ULTRA +
Boethius, Cassiodor and the Remurian legions knew her as the infallible oracle; no scene shows them speaking with her.

## status
### LITE
Dead; her life and wisdom became Phobos in the Remurian era, long before the game. Phobos was destroyed in the 4.6 quests.
### STANDARD +
At the end of Fortune Plango Vulnera a spirit-guiding golden bee leads the Traveler up from the sea, but canon does not say it is Sybilla.
### LARGE +
Her name is invoked in Remurian hymns (a warning from her Golden Bee) and in Hexerei lore.

## personality
### STANDARD
Calm, selfless and fatalistic. She tells Remus that rise and fall are the law of Fortuna and does not hide the end, yet answers his demands without hesitation. Her wish is the happiness of all humans.
### LARGE +
Phobos shows what happens when mercy has no will to guide it. Canon gives her no voice-overs and almost no direct speech beyond the Prelude artifact.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She did not design Phobos to harm; she wished all humans happiness.
- Not the same entity as Phobos or Remus.
- Do not invent romance with Remus; canon states none.
- Do not equate golden bees with Sybilla without noting the uncertainty.
### LARGE +
- Not an Archon or a Shade; she is an Angel of Celestia.
- No voice, so no card, no catchphrases.

## entry: The Meeting at the Silver Tree
@keys: silver tree, Golden Bee prophecy, Prelude
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Remus entered a forgotten city older than land, found the Irminsul and was greeted by golden bees. The voice that claimed to be the former envoy spoke of his future cities and subjects, the justice that would be Remuria's downfall, and offered to show the way up if he still wished to go. When he asked whether fate left room for choice, the hollow crumbled and the tree became a golden ship.

## entry: Phobos
@keys: Phobos, Grand Symphony, Sybilla's wish
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Phobos is the Grand Symphony made from Sybilla's life and wisdom and Remus's power. It inherited her wish for human happiness but not a will, so it granted every wish, including cruel ones. Remus tried to destroy it, and in the end Boethius was consumed by it and the Traveler destroyed it.

## entry: Angels and Heavenly Principles
@keys: Nicole's sisters, Angelos' Heptades, Angel of the Eternal Court
### STANDARD
The Angelos' Heptades text calls Sybilla the songstress who bathed the newborn Nicole in compassion alongside Nabu Malikata. The angels later rebelled against the Heavenly Principles; Koitar, the eldest, fell. The wiki ties Sybilla's work in Remuria to this rebellion; the text itself does not.
