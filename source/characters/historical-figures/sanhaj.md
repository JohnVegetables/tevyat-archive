## summary
### LITE
Sanhaj Kompore was a Border Master and high priest of the Masters of the Night-Wind and one of the six warriors who joined Tenoch against the Abyss in the cataclysm, about 500 years ago. He was born silent, became a storyteller of ritual and warning, and died with Tenoch when a miniature Night Kingdom was detonated at the Mare Jivari. His Ancient Name, Bidii, now belongs to Ororon.
### STANDARD +
He is remembered as cunning, quiet by nature and gifted at weaving old tales into warnings, and as a man who shaped what people were allowed to remember.

## background
### LITE
Sanhaj was born unable to speak. The elder who tended the tribe's ritual fire said half of his soul had been lost in the night flames. On a birthday he set out for the Night Kingdom to find it, accompanied by the Iktomisaurus Mahamba, the only one willing to go. There he was shown memories that were not his; he returned knowing the past and able to see into the future, and cried out his first words.
### STANDARD +
In the Night Kingdom he saw the first Pyro Archon swearing a vow before the Great Volcano of Tollan, and the second Pyro Archon, the Scarlet-Eyed Youth, defeating the king Och-Kan. He came to understand that Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night, had arranged these visions. At the end he saw the shadow of the cataclysm approaching the tribes. He woke by a blue bonfire, Mahamba's eyes flashing, and the elder gave him a ceremonial staff. From then on he told tales of the past to warn the heedless about coming danger, and deliberately wove falsehoods into his stories to cover a past he judged should not be touched again. He forged the ceremonial sword Calamity of Eshu, used in rituals that blurred the line between the Night Kingdom and the waking world.
### LARGE +
When the Abyss tide rose, Tenoch sought him out as the third of his six companions. The weapon text says Sanhaj had once been persuaded by Tenoch's club-borne 'talking stick', and that Tenoch agreed to give him the spoils before he would join. Sanhaj had foreseen his own end with Tenoch and the birth of what would later be called the Mare Jivari, and answered with a shrug: it mattered little, and perhaps the scoundrel famed for cleverness might yet become a hero. He had a family; the researcher Conulaf is described as one of his descendants.
### ULTRA +
Sanhaj was a high priest, revered mystic, border master and a teller of tales about tribal warriors. Among the Masters of the Night-Wind he was held to be a master of border spells beyond even Citlali, who calls herself no amateur. The greatest Border Masters can widen the Night Kingdom's borders and manifest part of it in the real world. Burkina fell around the same time as Sanhaj, leaving Menilek, Sundjatta, Tupac and Wanjiru to drink to both dead men in the 'Sunset' short; Wanjiru noted that Sanhaj had never been talkative, yet the news of his death rang loud and clear.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the cataclysm, about 500 years ago. His spirit persists in Natlan's clairvoyant traces: it appeared to Ororon when he awakened Bidii and again when Mavuika and the six heroes drew on the divine throne.
### STANDARD +
Citlali describes his remaining spirit as still very strong, so strong that contacting it during clairvoyance startled monsters nearby. The Traveler later found his final resting place in the Pyro Archon Temple, during The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc.
### LARGE +
Nothing after Version 7.1 is recorded. As of 7.1 he is a spirit and a legend, not a living participant. Bidii has passed to Ororon, whose story is told in his own entry.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Tenoch recruited him and died beside him in the Mare Jivari spell. His Iktomisaurus companion Mahamba shared his dangerous journey. Ororon inherited the Ancient Name Bidii and receives Sanhaj's knowledge. The surviving four heroes (Sundjatta, Tupac, Wanjiru and Menilek) remembered him as a hero who rarely spoke.
### LARGE +
Mavuika placed her trust in the six heroes' plan; Sanhaj's death was one of its early sacrifices. Citlali treats him as a legendary elder of her own tribe.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Border spells: he could draw the Night Kingdom into the real world. Legend credits him with summoning a miniature Night Kingdom and exploding it to destroy the approaching dark calamity at the Mare Jivari, a spell that cost his life and Tenoch's. He also had foresight, gained from the night visions, and was shown using a bow (Chain Breaker) in battle. He forged Calamity of Eshu but used it only ceremonially.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Childhood: born silent; journeys to the Night Kingdom with Mahamba.
- Cataclysm: recruited by Tenoch as the third of six companions.
- Battle of the Mare Jivari: dies with Tenoch; shortly before the events of 'Sunset' the other heroes toast him.
- Present day (5.1 onward): his spirit appears to Ororon (Beneath the Secret Source) and in Despair Engulfs the Heavens.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not make him talkative; sources stress his silence and cunning.
- He is not Tenoch's rival or an enemy; he joined the fellowship willingly.
- Do not treat the miniature Night Kingdom story as fully proven history; it is presented as legend.

## entry: Calamity of Eshu

### STANDARD
A curved ceremonial blade forged by Sanhaj, used in rituals that blurred the border between the Night Kingdom and the world. Its weapon text, released through the Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls event, is the main account of his silent birth and night visions.

## entry: Battle of the Mare Jivari
@keys: Battle of the Mare Jivari, miniature Night Kingdom
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
Legend holds that Sanhaj's border mastery turned the tide against the approaching dark calamity by summoning and detonating a miniature Night Kingdom. The cost was his life and Tenoch's. The Talking Stick text says Sanhaj had foreseen the birth of the place later called the Mare Jivari.
### LARGE +
The accounts are legend as told by Citlali centuries later and by weapon texts; no first-hand description of the battle is given in the sources read.
