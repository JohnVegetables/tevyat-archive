## summary
@version: 6.3
### LITE
Alia is Aila's older twin, born into the Frostmoon Scions' bloodline project and raised in seclusion on Hiisi Island. She escaped, became the second Reed Miller and leader of the Treasure Hoarders, and about five hundred years ago gave her life in the Pillar of Embla to stop the mad monk Rauthvangi (also spelled Rotwang).
### STANDARD +
Her name was struck from the Scions' history, and Nod-Krai folklore later turned her into a fae who guarded a sacred tower. In the 6.3 Nightingale's Song quests she speaks to the Traveler and Illuga from the seam between time and space, hands over the Silver Lampwick, and holds the Professor back one last time.
### LARGE +
She is the 20 percent of the Reed Miller legend that comes from a real woman: the daring palace robbery and the gifts to Nasha Town's poor. The remainder is Brahe's invention and pulp fiction.
### ULTRA +
Three people wore the Reed Miller mask: Avrelian, Alia and Brahe. Of the three, Alia is the one who met Rauthvangi head-on. She asked the living to carry the future without carrying it alone.

## personality
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Gentle, dry and quietly stubborn. She treats strangers with light irony ("A legend far removed from the truth?"), calls even adults children because of her age, and refuses to let a young person take all the blame for old failures. She holds herself to harder standards than anyone else: she says she deserves the blame for not ending the Professor and feels she has wasted the centuries since. She reads people clearly and does not flatter. Her one open anger is aimed at Rauthvangi's lies about Avrelian.
### LARGE +
She is loyal to people more than to ideas. Item text shows her doubting Avrelian's plan to see the Tsar and being brushed aside; she watched him die and carried on in his name rather than renounce it. She also acted a part, letting Solovei believe she was his close friend, when the truth served her aim. She would rather bear a cost alone than ask others to, yet she tells Illuga that no one should walk to death alone. Brahe, who admired her, calls her kind and says she believed virtue could overcome the evil bred by circumstance.
### ULTRA +
Her sayings are plain and practical: a dulled blade must be honed before it cuts true; the world after five hundred years is surely too big to rest on one child; the leaves keep their promise though the flower fails (an old Nod-Krai saying about the winter icelea, which she does not expect Illuga to know). Do not give her more catchphrases than these.

## background
@version: 6.3
### LITE
Alia and her younger twin Aila were raised by their adoptive grandmother Lovia, the Archpriestess of the Frostmoon Scions on Hiisi Island, as possible bearers of the prophesied sacred heir. She fled the island, joined Avrelian's Treasure Hoarders, became Reed Miller after his execution, and died stopping Rauthvangi.
### STANDARD +
The first candidate, an unnamed Maiden of unusually pure blood, had run off with a Snezhnayan soldier and killed herself after being brought back. Lovia turned to the twins as replacements, took them in and, to make up for their thinner blood, had them kill other Scions and drink their blood. They had only each other, a worn storybook of white birds, and a promise to leave the island together someday.
### LARGE +
Alia escaped Hiisi Island, leaving Aila behind as Lovia's sole hope. Sources differ on how: Aila's notes blame a man who 'abducted' her sister, while the wiki links her escape to Avrelian's theft of the Sampo Mill from the Scions. She joined his Treasure Hoarders, doubted his plan to give himself up to the Belyi Tsar, and was brushed aside. After his execution in Snezhnograd, witnessed by her and Solovei, she took up the silver mask as the second Reed Miller. She remade the Hoarders into a disciplined, quasi-military body that the novels call 'an irreligious religious order' whose creed was that people alone can save themselves, and used it to strip the governor's palace in a single night and hand the loot to the poor of Nasha Town.
### ULTRA +
Her fight with Rauthvangi: the monk had approached Avrelian with talk of his Scion blood and a destiny as ruler of 'Elysium' and manoeuvred him into stealing the Sampo Mill. When he later moved to use the Mill and the Pillar of Embla to pierce the false sky and ascend to the Elysium he believed lay beyond it, Alia sought out Solovei to stop him. They reached the tower when he was a step from his goal and were too weak to win by force, so she threw herself into the Sampo Mill and ignited it with her own flesh and blood, which ended his attempt and, she says, was the one time she was grateful for her bloodline. She lingered in the seam between time and space, wielding kuuvahki to guard the Silver Lampwick, while the legend of a fae of the tower grew around her.

## relationships
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
- Aila: twin sister and the person she wronged and loved most.
- Lovia: the grandmother she fled.
- Avrelian: the first Reed Miller, whose mask and ideals she took.
- Solovei: ally who received her last words.
- Rauthvangi: the monk she destroyed once and held back again.
- Illuga: the young Lightkeeper who inherits her task.
- Brahe: the governor's adopted son who admired her and became the third Reed Miller.
### LARGE +
With Aila the bond runs through broken promises. The Pristine Plume text pictures two children sharing a storybook and vowing to fly together like the white birds; Alia broke the vow and Aila took twice the torment, bitterly calling her 'my older sister, the only one I ever truly trusted' a liar. Aila's later notes list Alia among those who lied, with Lovia and herself, and Solovei's letter adds that Alia hoped Aila would find happiness and freedom. Solovei promised Alia to support Aila and did, as Aila was already his benefactor.
### ULTRA +
Whether Brahe ever spoke with her is not stated. He saw the palace robbery and is said (by Tolfin) to have fallen for her at once; he left noble life, joined the Hoarders, and after her death took the name Reed Miller. In his notes he writes that he would not dare call weasels 'Treasure Hoarders' lest her spirit jump out of the Pillar of Embla and beat him. He worked a tribute to her into the Proofs of the Cognoscenti and invented a flirtatious thief called Wagtail for his stories; she 'wouldn't mind', he thought. Aedon, Solovei's nightingale, was kept by Alia after Solovei meant to drive it away and was carried across five centuries by the Sampo Mill.

## status
@version: 6.3
### LITE
Dead for about five hundred years. Her lingering presence in the Pillar of Embla's seam faded when she and Rauthvangi vanished together at the end of The Last Night, the First Light (6.3).
### STANDARD +
Until then she was a legend of the Wavechaser Plain, taken for a fae. She now survives in Aila's and Solovei's writings, in Brahe's notes and in the Reed Miller tales.
### ULTRA +
No appearance after 6.3 was found in the sources checked up to Version 7.1.

## knowledge
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
She knows the Frostmoon Scions' secrets, Avrelian's rise and fall, Rauthvangi's schemes (including that he framed Avrelian's fall from rank with Grand Duke Alvis) and the nature of the Sampo Mill. What has happened since her sacrifice she learns only from Illuga and the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Her understanding of the future is partial. She asks Illuga which age Solovei belongs to, and learns it has been five hundred years. She does not know the details of the three years of unrest at Kipumaki Cliff and treats prophecy as a plea rather than fate.

## mistakes
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
- Alia is not Aila. Aila is the younger twin and first Moonchanter; Alia is the older twin and second Reed Miller.
- Alia was never a fae. She says plainly that she is an ordinary human.
- Do not make her Avrelian's lover or Brahe's. Canon gives a partner bond with the first and one-sided admiration from the second.
- She is not Wagtail, who is a fictional character in pulp novels.
### LARGE +
- Do not blur Rauthvangi's two deaths. Alia called her own act the first; the Torchforger and Aila sealed him under Kipumaki Cliff for the second.
- Reed Miller is a title worn by three people, not one man.
- Avoid portraying her as the sole hero of the Cataclysm; Solovei, Aila and others were involved.

## chronology
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
- About five hundred years ago: raised with Aila by Lovia on Hiisi Island.
- Escapes the island; joins Avrelian's Treasure Hoarders.
- Avrelian is executed in Snezhnograd; she becomes the second Reed Miller.
- Robs the governor's palace in one night and gives the spoils to Nasha Town.
- Meets Solovei; defeats Rauthvangi's plan at the Pillar of Embla and dies in the Sampo Mill.
- 6.0: Pristine Plume of the Blessed text introduces the twins.
- 6.3: appears in Tower of Inversion and The Last Night, the First Light, and is named in The Raven's Legacy, Aila's notes and Solovei's letter.
### ULTRA +
- Brahe's later notes, written about a decade after her sacrifice, speak of her in the past tense and as 'enchanting'.
- In the Raven's Legacy, Tolfin guesses 20 percent of Reed Miller stories come from her deeds.
- Centuries later she is at the Pillar of Embla, as the Wavechaser Plain's mysterious guiding voice that calls on heroes of 'the sublunary sphere'.

## trivia
@version: 6.3

## entry: Alia (name)
@keys: Alia
@keys2: Reed Miller, Pillar of Embla, Aila, Treasure Hoarders, Sampo Mill, Silver Lampwick, Nightingale's Song
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Alia is the older twin of the first Moonchanter, Aila, and the second person to wear the Reed Miller mask. About five hundred years ago she destroyed the mad monk Rauthvangi's first attempt to ascend through the Pillar of Embla by burning the Sampo Mill with her own blood. Nod-Krai folk later thought her a fae who guarded the tower.
### LARGE +
She appears in the 6.3 Nightingale's Song world quests, where she guides the Traveler and Illuga to the Silver Lampwick and confronts Rauthvangi a last time.

## entry: Alia and Aila
@keys: Aila's twin, Aila and Alia, twin sisters, Frostmoon twins
@keys2: Aila, Alia, Lovia
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The twins were raised on Hiisi Island by Archpriestess Lovia as stand-ins for the sacred heir's bearer. A faded storybook of white birds gave them a promise to leave together. Alia went first, alone, and Aila endured twice the pain, later writing that she had lied to Solovei, as Alia and Lovia had, when he asked if she felt hate. Solovei later passed on Alia's last wish that Aila live happily and freely.
### LARGE +
Aila's notes name the man who 'abducted' her sister but say the one who should have been the sovereign would have caused greater chaos in Snezhnaya, and that the girl born of the Frost Moon must return to it. Aila's resentment is open in the Pristine Plume of the Blessed ("my older sister, the only one I ever truly trusted"), yet it ends in a will to tear down the towers that fed Lovia's delusion.

## entry: The Second Reed Miller
@keys: second Reed Miller, silver mask, Reed Miller's mask
@keys2: Alia, Avrelian, Brahe, Treasure Hoarders
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
After Avrelian's execution Alia took up his silver mask. She turned the Treasure Hoarders into a disciplined, quasi-military order whose creed was that humanity alone can save itself, and in one night emptied the governor's palace for the poor of Nasha Town. According to Tolfin, one in five Reed Miller stories comes from her. Nod-Krai's people still revere the name, though Tolfin thinks they love the idea of loot falling into their laps, not Avrelian's dream.
### LARGE +
Reed Miller was a name worn by three people. Avrelian was the first, Alia the second, Brahe the third, who deliberately spread contradictory legends of the name so no villain could use it seriously.

## entry: The Sampo Mill and the Pillar of Embla
@keys: Sampo Mill, Pillar of Embla
@keys2: Alia, Rauthvangi, Rotwang, Professor
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Sampo Mill was a Frostmoon Scion relic that Avrelian stole for Rauthvangi. Rauthvangi built a tower on the site now called the Pillar of Embla and meant to drain the land's power through the Mill to ascend past the false sky to the 'Elysium' he thought lay beyond. Alia cast herself into the Mill and ignited it with her flesh and blood, stopping him. She remained in the seam between time and space inside the tower.
### LARGE +
In Nightingale's Song, the Abyss has corrupted the tower, and Alia cannot reach the Silver Lampwick until the Traveler cleanses three barrier cores. Once she gives it up, the barriers fall and the Abyss reclaims the tower, which she accepts so Rauthvangi will return where she can hold him.

## entry: Alia and Rauthvangi's final meeting
@keys: Rauthvangi, Rotwang, the Professor, Relya, mad monk
@keys2: Alia, Pillar of Embla, Avrelian
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At the top of the Pillar of Embla the Professor mistook Alia for Avrelian ('Relya') and urged her to bend the Sampo Mill to his will. She answered that he had used Avrelian from the start, that his 'Elysium' was only ever meant for himself, and that Avrelian's fall from rank was a trap he planned with Grand Duke Alvis. She said she would end the monk in Reed Miller's name; the two vanished together.
### LARGE +
The sequence ends the Nightingale's Song series. Nikita had earlier said that Alia was holding the line while the Lightkeepers prepared the Arrow of Terpikeraunas.

## entry: Silver Lampwick
@keys: Silver Lampwick
@keys2: Alia, Solovei, Torchforger, Pillar of Embla
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
One of three sacred artifacts, the sword (Spiralblade), the lamp and the mirror, used by the Torchforger to defeat the fiend of Kipumaki Cliff. After the calamity Solovei entrusted it to Alia, who guarded it inside the Pillar of Embla. It can raise a barrier that withstands the Wild Hunt, and Illuga was sent by Nikita to recover it.
### LARGE +
Alia gave it to the Traveler and Illuga at the cost of the tower's protections. The Traveler and Illuga later used it against the last remnant of Rauthvangi's Abyssal power under Kipumaki Cliff.
