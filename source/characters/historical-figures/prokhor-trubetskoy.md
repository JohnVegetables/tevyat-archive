## summary
### LITE
Prokhor Trubetskoy was a Snowland Fae Grand Duke and Governor of Nod-Krai before the Cataclysm. He is remembered as greedy and unpopular, and as the adoptive father of Brahe, who became the third Reed Miller.
### STANDARD +
He is known only second-hand, through the quest The Raven's Legacy, Brahe's notes at Reed Miller's Great Treasure and a remark by Euphrosiniya.

## background
### LITE
Governor of Nod-Krai in the Belyi Tsar's time, seated in what is now Voidsea Outlook; the Lightkeepers' base Piramida stands on the foundations of his former palace. His self-serving nature made him unpopular.
### STANDARD +
When Brahe was five, Avrelian and Solovei tricked the boy into revealing Trubetskoy's hidden hoard and gave it to the poor. The duke recovered the wealth, and more, within three days, and Brahe was punished. Brahe later lamented that his cunning stopped at money: the duke never suspected Rotwang was using him.
### LARGE +
After Avrelian's execution as the first Reed Miller, his follower Alia took the name and led the Treasure Hoarders in robbing the duke's palace entirely. Brahe met her during that event and fell for her. After Alia's death, Brahe gave up his noble identity and became Reed Miller himself. Decades on, Brahe spread tales of the "new Grand Duke Trubetskoy" (himself) failing to find the treasure, which suggests the elder duke was already dead.
### ULTRA +
Brahe's tales describe the "greedy father" and the new duke scheming for the treasure, as part of a falsified legend meant to ensure evildoers would never be mistaken for serious idealists. Treat these as Brahe's inventions, not history.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Brahe is his adoptive son; some speculated he was the duke's illegitimate child. Euphrosiniya heads House Trubetskoy today.
### LARGE +
Hiljetta and Tolfin are listed as Brahe's descendants, and by extension the duke's adoptive line.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago. Euphrosiniya says he fell fighting alongside the Belyi Tsar.
### STANDARD +
His palace site now holds Piramida. Nothing in canon to 7.1 shows him alive.
