## summary
### LITE
Indarias, titled General Musatas, was one of the Five Foremost Yakshas, the strongest of the adepti warriors Morax called upon to destroy the demons born from fallen gods. She died long ago; canon records almost nothing of her beyond her rank and a single anguished moment in a dying comrade's memory.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through others. Xiao, the last surviving yaksha, names her when recalling the five, and the Chasm quests briefly show her. No living character tells a story about her.

## background
### LITE
When the Archon War left demons, plagues and monsters in its wake, Rex Lapis (Morax) summoned the yakshas, illuminated beasts of fearsome looks and warlike temper, to cleanse them. Indarias was among the five judged strongest. The work ate at the yakshas: the hatred they suppressed became karmic debt that bred rage and madness, and she, like Bonanus and Menogias, perished.
### STANDARD +
The book Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti says the five followed Rex Lapis into battle many times until the evil was wiped out at its source, and guarded his rule for a very long time. It dates the collapse to about a thousand years of fighting, after which three of the five were dead and one had vanished. Indarias is one of the three. The book does not say how she fell.
### LARGE +
Xiao later tells the Traveler the five were called the guardian adepti and gives each general's title: he is Alatus, Menogias is General Kapisas, Bonanus is General Chizapus and she is General Musatas. In the memory fragments of the maddened Bosacius, recorded by the Fantastic Compass, he sees her scream after surrendering to her karmic debt, in the same recollection that shows Bonanus striking Menogias down. That is the only glimpse of her end.
### ULTRA +
Nothing places her death in a specific year. The yaksha order was whittled down over generations by battle, madness and desertion before Bosacius too withdrew and Xiao was left alone. By the cataclysm five hundred years ago only a maddened Bosacius still fought, so her death falls somewhere between the Archon War and then. Xiao says the fate of his fallen comrades has long stung his heart like a thorn.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Bosacius, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus (Xiao) were her fellow Foremost Yakshas, and Rex Lapis her commander. In Bosacius's last memories she calls him 'Brother', and she stands beside Bonanus in several scenes, which suggests they were close; canon states nothing more.
### LARGE +
Xiao is the only one of the five still alive, and his remarks show he regards the others as comrades he lost. Zhongli, who was Rex Lapis, has eased Xiao's karmic pain, but no quest has him speak of Indarias in particular.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Counted among the five strongest yakshas, so a formidable warrior. Canon gives no description of her fighting style, weapon or power. Her association with Pyro rests on cinematic imagery and a voice actor's statement, not in-game text.

## status
### LITE
Dead, long before the present story. No confirmed manner of death. Not mentioned in 6.x Nod-Krai or 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya content.
### STANDARD +
She survives only in the yakshas' legend and in Xiao's memory. Each spring at Lantern Rite, Liyue's people honor the surviving Conqueror of Demons, not her.

## entry: The Karmic Debt of the Yakshas
@keys: karmic debt, karma of the yakshas
@keys2: Yaksha, Yakshas, Indarias, Musatas
### STANDARD
The yakshas had to cleanse the lingering hatred of fallen gods, and every battle left a tally of karmic debt on their souls. Some were consumed by rage, others by fear-driven madness; many turned on one another. Of the Five Foremost Yakshas, Indarias, Bonanus and Menogias are dead, Bosacius went mad and died fighting at the Chasm, and only Alatus (Xiao) remains. Indarias's end is described in the book only as part of this collapse.
### LARGE +
Xiao carries the same debt and nearly succumbed to it himself. The deaths of his comrades have stayed with him.

## entry: Bosacius's Last Memories
@keys: Fantastic Compass memory, At Tunnel's End Light, Nameless Yaksha memories
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the 2.7 Chasm quest At Tunnel's End, Light, the Fantastic Compass preserves Bosacius's dying mind. His clearing memories show the five yakshas walking together, Indarias screaming under her karmic debt, and the other comrades' fates. Just before he dies he recognizes his comrades, and Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus appear around him and call him brother. Xiao says afterward that he can finally put the matter to rest.
