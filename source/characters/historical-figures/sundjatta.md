## summary
### LITE
Nare Magan Sundjatta was the 52nd chief of the Children of Echoes (the Nanatzcayan miners and smiths) and one of the six heroes who followed Tenoch against the Abyss in the cataclysm, about 500 years ago. He bore the Ancient Name Baraka, now held by Xilonen, and died in the heroes' final battle.
### STANDARD +
Canon shows him as a leader who preferred stability among the tribes, a craftsman who put his knowledge into writing, and a comrade who raised a toast to his own coming death.

## background
### LITE
Sundjatta succeeded Mbande as chief of Nanatzcayan. His companion was the Stegosaurus Muhuru. When Tenoch came to recruit him as the fifth of six warriors, Sundjatta reminded him that he had once petitioned the Pyro Archon to vote for Tenoch's expulsion, to keep the tribes at peace. He agreed to go because this might be the last battle of their generation, but asked that his tribe not be involved.
### STANDARD +
He was also a scholar-smith. A scroll attributed to him, Sundjatta's Phlogiston Forge Notes, introduces himself as the 52nd chief and says he will weave ancient texts into a tapestry like Daurama, Nayoga and Sanhaj. It argues that the forging of phlogiston itself, which Sakkuk's scrolls trace to ancient dragons and which the first Baraka Ixquieh may have mastered, is beyond ordinary craftsmen of his time; he advises using phlogiston as an aid for tempering metal and jade. He closes with the claim that the legacy of Nanatzcayan is a blessing made by mortal hands, not the gift of gods.
### LARGE +
In the Sunset short, on the eve of the last fight, he sits with Tupac, Wanjiru and Menilek. He proposes toasts: to Sanhaj and Burkina, who were already dead; to the Pyro Archon, Mavuika, in whom they all trusted; and last to themselves and their coming death, 'both grim and glorious'. He refills Wanjiru's cup as he says it. The scene shows he knew the stand would likely end in defeat for them but meant their sacrifice to feed the Sacred Flame and pave the way for later victory.
### ULTRA +
The battle itself is shown only in summary: Menilek, Wanjiru and Sundjatta died in the last stand against the Abyss, while Tupac survived long enough to bring Mavuika to the Sacred Flame. The wiki summary of Sunset adds that Sundjatta took a lethal hit meant for Wanjiru. He is shown controlling Geo and using a heavy bludgeoning weapon.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the cataclysm. His spirit appears in the present, in Despair Engulfs the Heavens, as one of the six heroes whose power Mavuika and the modern heroes channel in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame.
### STANDARD +
Xilonen inherited Baraka. In that scene the pairs speak together; Sundjatta and Xilonen's line is 'Bearing an ancient blessing'.
### LARGE +
Nothing beyond 7.1 is recorded for him. The Ancient Name Baraka was first borne by Ixquieh, founder of the Children of Echoes, then by Sundjatta, then by Xilonen.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Tenoch: once an opponent over tribal peace, then a comrade. Sanhaj, Burkina, Menilek, Tupac and Wanjiru: fellow heroes; he led the toast to the dead. Muhuru: his Stegosaurus companion. Xilonen: present-day Baraka. Sakkuk: the Children of Echoes' great-mother, whose scroll he cites.
### LARGE +
Mavuika: he trusted the Pyro Archon's plan and toasted her in Sunset.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the cataclysm: becomes 52nd chief after Mbande; clashes with Tenoch over tribal peace.
- Cataclysm: recruited by Tenoch as the fifth companion; the heroes' last toast; dies in the final battle.
- 5.1: his spirit appears in Despair Engulfs the Heavens. 5.8: his forge notes can be read in the Flamelets quest.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not give him a Vision or romance; neither is established.
- He was not a war-lover: he sought stability and agreed only because this would be the last battle.
- Sakkuk is his tribe's ancestor, not his contemporary.
