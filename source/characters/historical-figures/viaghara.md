## summary
### LITE
Viaghara was the first lord of the forest, a divine "true tiger" born in Dharma Forest in the age when Greater Lord Rukkhadevata founded her rainforest. It was both her friend and the ruler of the forest's animals, and it helped protect the Varuna Contraption.
### STANDARD +
Its stripes were said to resemble blazing flames, or to change constantly like the forest's branches. After Viaghara, a line of other lords of the forest followed, each reshaping the palace of the labyrinth, until the last of them died defending the forest during the cataclysm 500 years ago. Rishboland Tigers have borne the "tiger" name since.
### LARGE +
Viaghara's title list is long: Lord of the Forest, Greater Lord of the Labyrinths, Owner of the Moon-Mirrored Spring and Lord of All Animals. It is known only through legends in item text, with no surviving voice.

## background
### LITE
When Rukkhadevata left King Deshret and the Great Red Sand and went east, she built the Varuna Contraption to nurture a rainforest. The moon's reflection on the water made the pattern of a labyrinth, and from that labyrinth the tiger Viaghara was born.
### STANDARD +
Rukkhadevata gave it a crown, and the two exchanged tokens of friendship. When the Aranara, the children of the pomegranate, appeared, Viaghara blessed them under the first Vasara Tree and made a pact to share the labyrinth with them and to forbid the animals from harming them.
### LARGE +
Together the lords of the forest and the Aranara guarded the "secret of sun and rain": the lords with hedges and streams, the Aranara with dreams and illusions. The crown passed on to a girl named Cinnamon, Viaghara's squire and the first maiden to follow the lords' path. She taught children of the forest to navigate the labyrinth and follow nature's rules, founding the line of forest guardians; Viridescent, who lived through the cataclysm, came from that lineage but abandoned it to take revenge on Abyssal monsters.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A tiger whose stripes were like blazing flames and kept changing, resembling branches of the forest.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago. How Viaghara died is not recorded.
### STANDARD +
Successor lords of the forest continued the line and the last died in the cataclysm. Afterwards Rishboland Tigers took up the "tiger" name and still protect the forest and the Aranara in imitation of the old lords, with only a fraction of their might.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Greater Lord Rukkhadevata: friend and patron, gave it its crown.
- The Aranara: blessed by it, sharing the labyrinth under a pact.
- Cinnamon: its squire, who inherited the crown and began the forest guardians' tradition.
### LARGE +
- The Rishboland Tigers: descendants in name only, said to keep the promise to protect the forest and never harm the Aranara.

## mistakes
### LARGE
- Do not give Viaghara dialogue, a personality or a known death.
- Do not equate it with the Rishboland Tigers, with Viridescent or with Tighnari's fauna lore.
- Do not state that Viaghara is the king in the Faramarz story; that is the wiki's assumption.

## chronology
### LARGE
- After Rukkhadevata parted from King Deshret: Dharma Forest founded, Varuna Contraption built, Viaghara born.
- After the Aranara were born: pact under the first Vasara Tree.
- Unknown dates: Cinnamon becomes squire and inherits the crown; successor lords follow.
- 500 years ago: the final lord of the forest dies defending the forest in the cataclysm.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- The name is read as a form of Sanskrit vyaghra, "tiger"; the wiki also notes a possible resemblance to the tiger deity Waghoba. Neither is canon.
- The Laurel Coronet artifact text and the domain entrance text for The Coordinates of Sun and Rain tell parts of the same legend.

## entry: Faramarz and the Huntress

### STANDARD
In weapon and artifact lore, a fallen desert prince named Faramarz tried his luck in the forest after King Deshret's kingdom fell. The lord of the forest, sensing blood on his hands, repeatedly sent a huntress with a white bow (now Hunter's Path) to drive him away. She refused to kill him in hope of redemption, and the king had her lead him into the maze to pick a white branch. His end is unclear, with some accounts saying he died in the forest. The wiki assumes the king was Viaghara; canon does not name him.
