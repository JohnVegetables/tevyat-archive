## summary
### LITE
Arundolyn, the 'Lion of Light', was Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius when the cataclysm struck about 500 years ago. He had no Vision, yet fought with a greatsword and a longsword at once. After his closest friend Rostam died in the cataclysm, he never fought again.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through weapon, artifact and book texts and through Varka's story; he never appears on screen.
### LARGE +
His legacy is a sword style that died out because almost nobody had the strength to use it, and a childhood friendship that Mondstadt remembers in the Brave Heart artifacts.

## background
### LITE
Born into a long line of knights in Mondstadt, he grew up beside Rostam, a peasant's son, and the two dreamed of heroism. Both became knights of Favonius; Arundolyn rose to Grand Master with the title Lion of Light and Rostam stood beside him as the Wolf Pup.
### STANDARD +
As boys they slipped out at night into the wolves' forest, chased boars with slingshots, fought imaginary monsters with sticks and followed a seelie to a small yellow flower buried for a thousand years. They played out Vennessa's fight with the aristocracy's champion, and the future Lion won. His toy greatsword was modelled on that of the Knight of Boreas Ravenwood.
In adulthood he led the Knights while Rostam did the quieter work. When the cataclysm came he led the knights to fight for Mondstadt, and on the eve of battle he shared wine with his old friend and deputy.
### LARGE +
Rostam later died in the cataclysm after twenty-three years of service. Fortitude of the Brave says the knights fought on an expedition in a foreign land and the hero came home without his old friend (the wiki identifies it as the expedition to Khaenri'ah; Elegy for the End places Rostam's death in a Mondstadt valley as Durin fell). Arundolyn and, per the Favonius Greatsword's story, never again displayed his strength, because fighting monsters was no longer a source of glory. The wiki adds that he gave up the Grand Master title and his weapons. At home he found the childhood yellow flower unwilted, the one thing unchanged in his life.
### ULTRA +
Brave Heart's texts add small details: the boy gave his timid friend a songbird's feather to hearten him; Rostam gave him a miniature hourglass; and a crude wreath of flowers from their game seemed to him worth more than the power of a master of knights. These are private memories; canon records no battle record or speech of his.

## relationships
### LITE
Rostam, his childhood friend and deputy, whose death ended his fighting life; Roland, Rostam's disciple.
### STANDARD +
The two friends are described as sword and dagger: Arundolyn the model knight and public face, Rostam the Wolf who worked from the shadows. Varka's story says no one inherited his genius after him; Varka set out to recreate his dual-blade style and vowed to surpass him.
### LARGE +
Rostam's lover Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter, later the Crimson Witch of Flames and then La Signora, grieved the same loss; canon does not describe any meeting between her and Arundolyn. After Rostam's death he wanted to deliver Rostam's unsent letter to Roland but never found him, so it stayed at the Knights' headquarters (per Varka in 6.4).

## abilities
### STANDARD
Strong and fast without a Vision, through natural gifts and hard training. He fought with a greatsword in one hand and a longsword in the other in a blade dance Varka's story calls close to divine. The style demanded strength and technique; the Knights' records describe its ferocity but not its technique, so the full form faded.
### LARGE +
Varka found brute strength was not enough to dual-wield. Later knights have learned both blades but none have wielded them as extensions of the body as he did.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago; the manner of his death is not recorded.
### STANDARD +
He is remembered only in item lore and in Varka's story as the Grand Master who stopped fighting, and his style is lost.
### LARGE +
Varka's Vision story (6.4) has Varka try to recreate his dual-wielding without relying on his own Vision, vowing to surpass him. In the 6.4 Lupus Majoris quest Varka visits a small graveyard of swords that includes Arundolyn's and tells Roland's image that Rostam and Arundolyn never forgot him. The Knights' title line runs from Vennessa through Arundolyn to later Lionfang Knights such as Jean.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the cataclysm: grows up with Rostam; both become knights.
- Becomes Grand Master and Lion of Light; Rostam becomes the Wolf Pup.
- Cataclysm: leads the Knights, including an expedition in a foreign land (Khaenri'ah, per the wiki); Rostam dies.
- Afterwards: stops fighting; his later life and death are unrecorded.
- 5.2 (limited event) and 6.4: later references to him by Phonia and in Varka's stories.

## trivia
### LARGE
- Ferrous Shadow is a replica of his boyhood greatsword, itself modelled on Ravenwood's.
- The wiki's Welsh etymology for his name is a guess, not canon.

## entry: Arundolyn and Rostam
@keys: Rostam, Wolf Pup
@keys2: Arundolyn, Lion of Light
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Childhood friends and rivals, Arundolyn (the Lion) and Rostam (the Wolf Pup) were called sword and dagger. Rostam originated Favonius Bladework and died in the cataclysm after twenty-three years of service. Arundolyn gave up fighting afterwards.
### LARGE +
On the eve of battle they drank together, one saying he would not mourn if the other failed to return and the other answering that he would toast him instead. Rostam's unsent letter to his disciple Roland stayed at the Knights' headquarters until, in 6.4, Varka laid it at the base of Roland's Black Sword in a vision, beside a small graveyard of swords that included Arundolyn's.

### STANDARD
