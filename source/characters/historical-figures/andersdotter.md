## summary
### LITE
Anya "Marie" Andersdotter, code name M, was a human writer from Mondstadt and a member of the Hexenzirkel. She wrote the storybook The Boar Princess, which every Mondstadt child knows, and she created the character Durin. She is long dead.
### STANDARD +
She was the one ordinary mortal among the witches: she never learned magic or combat. Alice calls her an exceptional writer whose prose taught Alice what grief means.
### ULTRA +
Her legacy runs through three projects: The Boar Princess, the world of Simulanka (where the people called her the Goddess of Fate) and the relay story The Little Witch and the Undying Fire. Durin's Character Stories call her his mother.

## background
### LITE
She was the last to join the Hexenzirkel and the first to leave. As she aged she told the witches that her lifespan was far shorter than theirs and asked them to keep her storybook and pass it to future generations. She refused Alice's offer to extend her life with magic and chose to end her life as a human.
### STANDARD +
Alice calls her the author of the tale that never ends. Nicole says her stories, whether a mere idea or a finished manuscript, made readers gasp with joy and that Anya had a magic of her own: weaving love and compassion into words.

In Simulanka she wrote a fictional boy named Durin. Barbeloth warned that he would meet the same fate as the historical Durin, but Anya wanted him to have a long, happy life and kept writing. She continued into old age and, before she died, gave Alice a magic pen so the story could go on. Alice, Barbeloth and Rhinedottir later used astrology, alchemy and magic to let her Durin exist as a person.
### LARGE +
In Durin's Story Quest (The Magic Known as Storytelling, 6.2), Alice telepathically sends the Traveler to meet Durin and then opens an unfinished book made by all the witches, saying that Anya died before finishing her part and left a great regret; before she died Anya made sure Alice would hand the unfinished book to Durin, whom she believed to be the only one who could resolve it. A flashback in the final quest shows Anya refusing to give up on bringing Durin into Teyvat for a happy life. She knew she would have to keep writing and that the story would have to end at some point, and she wanted Durin to write his own story so that it could continue after her. Durin later meets a manifestation of his mother, who entrusts him with her pen, and the witches' work lets him live as a separate person. A fragment of her consciousness then says goodbye to Alice, pleased at how her child had grown.

During the Windblume's Breath event (3.5, limited), a cinematic shows the witches' tea parties. Anya is represented there by the cover of The Boar Princess and by a boar princess with a fox and a turtle; the cinematic's captions have her say that her lifespan is nothing compared to the others' and that she wishes to leave them her storybook, which they may pass on to their children.
### ULTRA +
Simulanka (shown in the 4.8 limited event Summertide Scales and Tales) was made by three goddesses who were Hexenzirkel members, M among them as the Goddess of Fate. Her old journal explains the code name: she would by rights have been A, since A is the first and last letter of her name, but Alice outranked her by seniority and gave her M for her middle name. She writes that Alice's magic made one of her origami frogs speak, which led her to write a story about paper animals; that Barbeloth mapped the fates of everything in Simulanka, which she felt spoiled the story; and that she bet Barbeloth the people would one day carve their own destiny. A yellowed letter to her sisters says her pen will soon run dry, thanks them for the world they made for her, and says their time together was the most wonderful youth she could have asked for.

Durin's Character Stories fill in her last years. He arrived in Simulanka while she was alive, accompanied her around the unfinished world and watched her and his two aunts argue over designs. One aunt loved adding thrilling devices; his mother worried, because she cherished the people of Simulanka like her own children, but the arguments never lasted. One day she set down her pen, asked him whether he would want to leave the world of the story, accepted his solemn agreement to the task she gave him and said she had now written the most important passage of all. Not long after, the stories she and her sisters had set in motion began to turn, and she was gone.

Durin Character Story 5 adds that at a tea party where seven witches were present Alice proposed a storytelling relay on the theme 'The Undying Fire' and gave Anya the last chapter. Anya said she wished, as an author, to leave an undying fire through her stories and was still searching for her own answer. By the time the relay reached the fifth volume she had died; Alice had misjudged how differently witches and humans experience time. Octavia, rarely seen at gatherings, came because the Hexenzirkel had never lost a member before. Alice took up the ending, and when Durin took flight beside Dvalin she concluded that the undying fire Anya left was Durin.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The wiki describes her as slender with pale skin and medium-length mint-coloured, softly curled hair under a large hat, in a navy and white star-themed dress with a sash that trails like a cape and high-heeled blue shoes. In the 3.5 tea-party cinematic she is represented by the cover of The Boar Princess and by a boar princess with a fox and turtle.
### LARGE +
This description comes from how she appears in The Magic Known as Storytelling (6.2) and from the Simulanka statue; the wiki also lists an ink pot with her likeness in the cinematic and a Dodoco version of her in the Story Quest's story world. Her middle name Marie is revealed in that Story Quest by Alice.

## status
### LITE
Dead, roughly 500 to 400 years ago by the wiki's estimate. Her book and the people it shaped live on, notably Durin.
### STANDARD +
She is not in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 as a living character; she is remembered by the Hexenzirkel and appears in flashbacks and statues.
### LARGE +
In Durin's Story Quest her manifestation appears once, to say farewell to Alice. Nicole speaks of her as her greatest literary inspiration in The Silent Theater (6.6), and Anya is the featured figure of the Imaginarium Theater card Lunar Arcanum III: The Empress (introduced with Luna I, 6.0). Canon states no grave, memorial or exact date of death.
### ULTRA +
An Imaginarium Theater fortune slip describes her as simply ordinary compared with the other Hexenzirkel members. The wiki infers that she died after the Cataclysm from a flashback in which she is old and Alice remarks that things are finally returning to normal after the terrible calamity. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 refers to her.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Alice is the closest witch to her, the keeper of her pen and the one who most feels her loss. Barbeloth and Rhinedottir contributed to Durin's second chance, and Nicole reveres her stories. Durin is the character she wrote and in a sense her child.
### LARGE +
Alice proposed the storytelling relay, trusted Anya with its ending, and recalls a promise she made to her when she takes up the ending herself. Durin's memories mention a pale-blue aunt who visited Anya with teacups and sweets while she wrote, and who looked at the new scales growing on young Durin with a complicated expression, and a fiery-red aunt who loved adding thrilling devices to Simulanka's designs; his profile does not name them.
### ULTRA +
Nicole says Anya taught her many tricks and techniques for writing compelling tales, though her biggest enemy has always been herself. Octavia and I. Ivanovna N. also wrote volumes of the relay story; the Ivanovna volume is described as partly rescuing a tale that had veered wildly. In Alice's closing spell for Durin's story, the Traveler is called the 'reader' and Durin the 'writer'.

## entry: The Boar Princess
@keys: The Boar Princess, Boar Princess
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A seven-volume storybook found around Mondstadt: a fairy tale of a forest kingdom of boars, friendship, love and death, set in the myths of ages past. Mondstadt children are said to know it well, and it is credited to Andersdotter.
### LARGE +
Volume 1 describes a forest kingdom of boars under a Boar King, whose kind young daughter shares her fruit and whom the boars bless along with the gentle Anemo Archon. Volume 2 moves to a frozen tundra to the north that the playful Barbatos had not reached, where a lone wolf pup lives; volume 3 introduces an evil squirrel sorcerer. Childish handwritten notes are scribbled in the margins of some volumes. Alice counts it among her favourite stories.

## entry: Mages' Tea Party and M
@keys: Mages' Tea Party, mages' tea parties, code name M
@keys2: Hexenzirkel, Alice
@version: 3.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the 3.5 event Windblume's Breath, Alice explains that a four-line prophecy was a farewell letter the witches wrote to Ioanna Ivanovna the day before her wedding, with M as the legend that never ends. The same event gives the cinematic of the tea parties.
### LARGE +
Alice says that when a member of the group had to leave to spend the rest of her days with a beloved, the others wrote this kind of prophecy to invite her to a final gathering. The four phrases stand for R (Rhinedottir, the flower not of this world), N (Nicole, the guide who never gets lost), A (Alice, the one who would never lie) and M (the legend that never ends). Alice adds that the human M used her prose to teach her what grief means.

## entry: Durin's second chance
@keys: Durin's story, unfinished book, magic pen
@keys2: Andersdotter, Anya, Alice
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
Andersdotter wrote Durin into a happy story, and her pen and the combined work of the Hexenzirkel allow him to exist as a person in Teyvat. The witches did this as a promise to her.
### LARGE +
In Durin's Story Quest the Traveler, Paimon and Durin follow clues in an unfinished storybook. They find Anya's sources of inspiration in the Mondstadt library, revisit tea-party scenes populated by Dodoco versions of the witches, and finally break out of the story with help from Albedo. Alice's magic, Barbeloth's astrology and Rhinedottir's alchemy let Durin live as a separate entity rather than the evil Durin of history, and afterwards Barbeloth no longer sees his fate among the stars.

## entry: Goddess of Fate
@keys: Goddess of Fate, Simulanka goddess, statue of M
@keys2: Simulanka, Durin, Anya
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Simulanka's lore M is the Goddess of Fate, one of three goddesses who made the world. A statue of her exists there, distinct from the goddess statue in the Metropole. Her journal and letter, read during the limited event Summertide Scales and Tales, show her fondness for Simulanka's people and her wish that they might carve their own destinies.

## entry: The Little Witch and the Undying Fire
@keys: The Little Witch and the Undying Fire, Undying Fire, storytelling relay
@keys2: Anya, Andersdotter, Hexenzirkel, Alice
@version: 4.7
### STANDARD
A seven-volume relay story written by Hexenzirkel members: Alice, Barbeloth, Rhinedottir, I. Ivanovna N., Nicole, Octavia and, partly, Andersdotter. Durin's Character Story 5 says Anya was assigned the final chapter but died before her turn, and volume 5 ended abruptly in a single line. Alice later wrote the ending.
