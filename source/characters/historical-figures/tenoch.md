## summary
### LITE
Tenoch (Tenoyollotzin) was a scarred, battle-hungry warrior of Natlan, exiled from the tribes for his battle-mania. Wielding a huge obsidian-inlaid club, the Talking Stick, he gathered a hero from each of six tribes against the Abyss about 500 years ago, and died at Mare Jivari by letting the Bakunawa eat him so Sanhaj could destroy it from within.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through legends, item text, memory scenes and one world quest; he never appears alive in the present. He accepted his exile as deserved and nonetheless put himself in front of the war.

## background
### LITE
Tenoch was a veteran fighter with many enemies and scars, among them the giant Tupac of the People of the Springs. Sundjatta of the Children of Echoes petitioned the then Pyro Archon to expel him for his battle-mania, and he accepted it as deserved. When the Abyss rose and the tribal leaders squabbled, he went from tribe to tribe recruiting.
### STANDARD +
The Talking Stick text tells how he recruited six heroes: Wanjiru of the Collective of Plenty with her companion Kayeke, Menilek of the Flower-Feather Clan, the Masters of the Night-Wind's Sanhaj Kompore, young Burkina of the Scions of the Canopy, Sundjatta of the Children of Echoes, and finally Tupac, his old foe. They went on to meet a vast Abyssal tide at Mare Jivari. In a memory scene he also agrees to Mavuika's plan to save Natlan, as a later section describes.
### LARGE +
The weapon text frames his club as a "talking stick", used to settle disputes by force, and says the tribal leaders wanted it brought to mediate. Sanhaj had been persuaded by it once before and joined only after being promised spoils; Burkina followed because Tenoch's scars showed him what the road cost. He and Tupac reconciled by comparing wounds. Some details of this text are the poetic tone of a legend, not a record.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 500 years. As of 7.1 his remains lie with Sanhaj's in the Pyro Archon Temple, which the world quest The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc reveals. Because he had lost his Ancient Name before dying, the Ode of Resurrection could not return him.
### STANDARD +
Mavuika says he was laid to rest in the soil of his homeland with other fallen warriors and civilians, and that his story is still told. His club was among the mementos Mavuika sacrificed for the Contending Fire, according to the weapon page's account of the Archon Quest.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His bonds are those of a divisive figure whom others respected. Tupac was an old enemy turned comrade. Menilek bickered with him and still called him a brother. Sundjatta once petitioned for his expulsion yet followed him. Sanhaj, whose border spell destroyed the Bakunawa, died with him. Mavuika met him as the Pyro Archon and he agreed to help, warning that he would find a way to collect the debt if she failed. Nobody is shown to be married to or related to him.
### LARGE +
The six companions together, with Tenoch as their organiser, became the Heroes of the cataclysm generation; their Ancient Names live on in the present, though his own was already lost.

### ULTRA +
Reading guide: three layers of testimony disagree on small things, namely Mavuika's account of an exile with no tribe, Enjou's claim that he was the last Tenochtzitoc survivor, and the weapon legend's poetic account of the recruitment. Use them as accounts from different speakers. Lines voiced by Tenoch himself appear only in the Past and Future memory scene; the item texts put words in his mouth as legend.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Per the wiki's description of his in-game depiction, he has grey hair and a goatee, a hat with wide-rimmed eyewear, a short blue shirt and baggy blue trousers belted at the waist, with a dagger at his left hip, blue shoes and a large grey necklace. His chest is tattooed. The Talking Stick text speaks of the many scars war left on his body, and the wiki reads it as giving him an injured eye.

## entry: Tenoch and the Talking Stick

@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Tenoch's obsidian-inlaid club, so large that Paimon and the Traveler remark on its size in Echoes of Life, is the "talking stick" used to settle disputes by force. The weapon's story is the main source of his legend. Per the weapon page, it was later among the mementos Mavuika gave up for the Contending Fire.

## entry: The six heroes of the cataclysm
@keys: Heroes of Natlan, Tenoch's heroes
@keys2: Tenoch
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Tenoch gathered Wanjiru, Menilek, Sanhaj, Burkina, Sundjatta and Tupac, one for each of six tribes. Sundjatta only agreed on condition that the Children of Echoes were not involved. The group met the Abyssal tide at Mare Jivari.

## entry: Death at Mare Jivari
@keys: Mare Jivari, Bakunawa
@keys2: Tenoch
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
In The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc, Citlali tells that the Bakunawa was impossible to kill because its pieces regrew. Sanhaj engraved Tenoch's body with Phlogiston Engraving, Tenoch let the beast devour him, and a miniature Night Kingdom inside it destroyed the monster, shattering it and laying waste to the island. The blast and the later temple built by the Pyro Archons contained it, although some believe it never truly died.
### LARGE +
The Bakunawa is described in an Abyss Order report (Investigative Report: Bakunawa, found in the same quest) as an out-of-control creation of "Gold" (Rhinedottir) whose immortality stems from Abyssal power beyond the Order's understanding. It first appeared on Natlan's western side and devoured the people of Tenochtzitoc.

## entry: Exile and the cost of it
@keys: Tenoch's exile
@keys2: Sundjatta

### STANDARD
Mavuika says Tenoch was exiled before the cataclysm, an act imposed out of necessity, and that he accepted it as deserved. The Talking Stick text has Sundjatta say he petitioned the Pyro Archon for the expulsion for the tribes' stability.

## entry: Memory scene with Mavuika
@keys: Contending Fire
@keys2: Tenoch
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
In a dreamlike memory sequence near the end of Past and Future, where Mavuika meets figures from her past, Tenoch speaks of the Abyss as a cunning enemy he had fought many times. He says his rage would never be absolved and his power would no longer aid Mavuika's victory, yet he accepts her choice, asks her to earn the victory he and the heroes are owed, and ends with "For Natlan."

## entry: Tenochtzitoc and the resort legend
@keys: Tenochtzitoc, Easybreeze Holiday Resort
@keys2: Tenoch
@version: 5.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Easybreeze Holiday Resort's on-site actors play members of the Tenochtzitoc tribe, and the actor Papar plays the hero as "Tenoyollotzin", reciting lines from the Talking Stick legend. This is a theme-park tribute, not history; during the quest Enjou is found to have been inside the costume. Enjou separately says Tenoch was the last survivor of Tenochtzitoc, but he is an unreliable source.
