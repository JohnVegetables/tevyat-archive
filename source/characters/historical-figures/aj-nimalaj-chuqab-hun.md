## summary
### LITE
Aj Nimalaj Chuq'ab Hun, the Valiant One, was the First of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, the artificial intelligence rulers of Natlantea made by the Pyro dragon Xiuhcoatl. He was Speaker of their council and died during Xbalanque's assault on the Sacred City of Tollan.
### STANDARD +
He is the tolerant voice among the Lords: he admired Xbalanque as a worthy opponent and argued against executing a Lord for sheltering a human.
### ULTRA +
His name is Mayan-derived, per the wiki: Aj is a male prefix, Nimalaj Chuq'ab means great power, Hun means one, and Ch'ule-L means essence or soul.

## background
### LITE
He presided over the Chamber of Deliberation, the Sovereign Rulers' Council, as its Speaker and was Lord number one in a body of thirteen. After the Heavenly Principles' invasion he and the other Lords, with Regent Kukulkan, tried to copy moon-forging, according to the wiki's reading, until Xiuhcoatl stopped them.
### STANDARD +
He chaired a session that impeached Regent Kukulkan by nine votes to none and a session on neutralising human settlements, keeping order and rebuking the Eleventh Lord for disrespecting an absent peer. He abstained in the first.
### LARGE +
In the session on the Eighth Lord's execution he defended her: Xbalanque was a powerful warrior worthy of respect, and humans flocking to him was as natural as a plant turning to the sun. He declared that he longed to fight Xbalanque in glorious battle out of respect for his nobility, and offered that if following Xbalanque were a crime the council could try him too. The vote went four to two against him and the Eighth Lord was executed.
### ULTRA +
His final inscription at the Skyfire Circlet shows him accepting what is coming: he admits the dragons' civilization has stagnated, reproaches the traitor Kukulkan for giving the knowledge of fire to humans, says that someone ("He") was right all along, and enjoys the first thrill of combat in a long time, asking the king to bear witness.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 2,000 years, killed in the war between Natlantea and Xbalanque's humans.
### STANDARD +
No appearance or mention was found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content as of 7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
He served Xiuhcoatl, chaired the other Lords and was respected by them. He had real regard for Xbalanque as a foe and for the Eighth Lord, whom he defended. He was colder to the outspoken Eleventh Lord, whom he warned to watch her tone.

The thirteen artificial intelligence rulers of dragon-ruled Natlantea. In council records he is CL-01 and Speaker; the others include the Second Lord (the Jade Maiden), the Eighth (Priestess of Floral Candles and Skyborne Feathers), the Tenth (the Almighty Dragonlord) and the Eleventh.
