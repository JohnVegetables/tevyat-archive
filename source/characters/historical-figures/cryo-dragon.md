## summary
### LITE
The Cryo Dragon was one of the Seven Sovereigns, the dragon-lords who ruled Teyvat before the Heavenly Principles, and it was defeated in the war against Celestia. Its corpse, kept in Snezhnaya, froze the land and later produced the Belyi Tsar.
### STANDARD +
As of Version 7.1 the Nail of Celestia holding its authority in check has been destroyed, leaving the corpse free to hallow soil across the world.

## background
### LITE
Alongside the other Sovereigns, the Cryo Dragon fought Celestia and lost. Nothing else is recorded about its life.
### STANDARD +
After its death, the people of Hyperborea used its corpse in an attempt to make a Primordial Human, a being whose authority would equal that of Nibelung, the king of the dragon-lords. The unchecked authority turned Snezhnaya into a variant of Holy Soil covered in primordial ice and turned the region's ley lines into veins of pure crystal. The project spun out of control, and Hyperborea was struck by a Celestial Nail and sunk into the Shadow Realm.
### LARGE +
The experiment was not a total failure: a thousand years afterwards, the Belyi Tsar emerged from the dragon's remains. The corpse had remained above ground, and the Celestial Nail suppressed its authority. The Hyperboreans had tried to forge his body from the dragon's flesh and blood.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1, after Celestia revoked her Authority and Pierro mortally wounded her, Ronova went to the corpse, fused Eitr she had taken from the Shadow Realm with the Nail of Retribution hanging over it, and let the Nail crumble. The corpse is now free to hallow the soil of the whole world.

## relationships
### LITE
Fellow Seven Sovereign of Nibelung; raw material for Hyperborea's experiment; the origin of the Belyi Tsar.
### STANDARD +
Celestia defeated it; Hyperborea used its corpse; the Belyi Tsar hatched from its remains. Ronova's last act in 7.1 released its authority.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago; its corpse, no longer pinned, is spreading its authority.
### STANDARD +
The Nail over the corpse was destroyed at the end of Chapter VII Act IV (7.1). No successor is known.

### STANDARD
The Seven Sovereigns were dragon-lords of the old world. The Primordial One fought them and, after forty winters and forty summers, vanquished them, as recorded in a Byakuyakoku collection text in Enkanomiya.

## entry: Hyperborea's experiment
@keys: Hyperborea, Primordial Human
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aksinya states that the Hyperboreans attempted to make a Primordial Human from the Cryo Dragon's flesh and blood so that he would have authority equal to Nibelung. The experiment's unchecked authority turned Snezhnaya to ice before Celestia's Nail struck Hyperborea.
