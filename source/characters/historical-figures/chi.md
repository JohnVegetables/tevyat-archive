## summary
### LITE
Chi, called Qingce in the ancient language, was a serpent god that nested in the area now holding Qingce Village. Morax fought and beat it, but its power kept seeping into the land, so he pinned each aspect of it in a different place. It survives only as legend, Geo statues and a mystery in the Vault of Qingce.
### STANDARD +
The word chi is also used in Liyue for dragon-like sea monsters. The Skipper's greatsword Serpent Spine was made from the spine of one such chi, a separate creature.

## background
### LITE
Thousands of years ago Chi took control of the Mt. Qingce region. Morax fought it at a great height and prevailed, but it could not die entirely.
### STANDARD +
A document at Qingce records how Rex Lapis conquered each facet in turn: spirit bound in the north, bones pinned in the south-east, flesh imprisoned in the north-west, soul fettered in the north-east and form crushed in the south-west. He taught the villagers to make Geo statues, said by Xiao to bear Morax's likeness, to crush the remaining power; adeptus amber nearby also suppresses it.
### LARGE +
Legend later grew that Chi's body became Mt. Qingce, its scales the terraced fields, and that the blood-fed trees of Bishui Plain grew from its blood, their groves marking its eyes. Zhongli denies the mountain's origin in this way. In the world quest The Chi of Yore the Traveler opens a vault in north-west Qingce, but finds no trace of Chi in the glowing orb inside.

## status
### LITE
Defeated and sealed in the ancient past; whatever remains is confined by Morax's statues and amber. Nothing about it appears in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content found.
### STANDARD +
In The Heart of Healing the ethereal remains in a cave were made into medicine by Jiangli for her husband Jialiang, but were too potent for mortals and made both ill; Baizhu destroyed them, which cleansed the poison from Jiangli.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Morax is its conqueror. Qingce Village's people were taught to keep its power down and still tell its legends. Another chi, killed by the Skipper, supplied the spine for Serpent Spine.
### ULTRA +
Portrayal note: Chi is not a speaking character in canon. The five-direction binding and the village's statues are the main established facts; the mountain-body legend is folk belief, which Zhongli corrects in open-world dialogue.
