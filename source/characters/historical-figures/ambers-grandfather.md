## summary
### LITE
Amber's grandfather was a Liyue-born mercenary captain who became a Knight of Favonius, founded the Outriders and trained every member himself. He raised Amber in his methods and mentored the young Eula. About four years before the story begins he left his coat of arms and sword in his office and vanished without a word.
### STANDARD +
Canon never gives his name. He is remembered by those he taught as a quiet, forgiving man: Amber's Character Stories describe his love as silent and unconditional, and Eula credits him with teaching her open-mindedness.

## background
### LITE
He came from Liyue Harbor and led a mercenary group that guarded merchant caravans. On one routine escort a monster attack destroyed the caravan and he alone survived, saved by a doctor of the Knights of Favonius. Ashamed to go home and wanting to repay the debt, he joined the Knights, founded the Outriders and trained each member personally.
### STANDARD +
He later fell in love and started a family in Mondstadt. Young Amber used to watch his drills in secret and sneak out at night to copy them; moved by her enthusiasm, he taught her everything he knew. He often cooked Liyue dishes for her after a day of training, and sometimes Eula joined them for dinner and complained about it while visibly enjoying it. Amber was a handful as a child, and he was the one who ended up smoothing over her mischief, such as when she dropped a bird's nest on his head.

He also took in Eula, a young member of the disgraced Lawrence Clan who struggled under the shadow of her family's past. The two were alike, one an outcast of Liyue and the other an exile of Mondstadt. His example of patience and openness helped her discover who she was before she faced the question of her clan, and her Vision appeared as she set out on that path.
### LARGE +
The Favonius Warbow's item text says the Outriders were once a formidable team of archer scouts who passed down his lifetime of knowledge and were invaluable to the Knights, and that the unit became a shell of itself after its first Outrider left the order for reasons the Knights never learned. Amber's Character Story 3 says he had been the heart and backbone of the division, and that it grew undisciplined without him. Amber, newly admitted, found herself almost alone and resolved to inherit his role.

Amber's Character Story 1 also says she understood the full weight of his responsibilities only on the day he resigned from the Knights.
### ULTRA +
In Character Story 2 he is quoted telling the young Amber that once Mondstadt accepted him it became his homeland, and that he decided to watch over this new home; he hoped she might one day inherit that duty but added that nobody knows what the future holds. The same story has him settle in Mondstadt and start a family only shortly after founding the Outriders, and describes him leading them on missions himself.

After he vanished, according to Character Story 3, the Outriders lost discipline and failed a few operations, and the division was kept as part of the Knights in name only. Experienced members transferred or resigned until too few remained to cover daily patrols, and rumours that he had defected made recovery harder. Amber had only just been admitted when this happened; she wanted to prove she had inherited his determination, to find out where he had gone, and above all to inherit his role as a loyal protector of her homeland.

## status
### LITE
Missing as of 7.1. He disappeared about four years before Prologue, and canon has not shown him since.
### STANDARD +
A Qingce Village bulletin-board message describes an old friend, a former hired hand on the same caravans, who returned after forty or fifty years and glides with a wind glider; the wiki reads this as probably him, but it is unconfirmed.
### LARGE +
Amber's Character Story 3 records that rumours of his defection circulated among the Knights after he left, and that she herself wanted to learn where he went. Neither the Knights nor Amber are shown learning the answer in any quest, and no page checked for 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content mentions him.
### ULTRA +
The message-board writer says the old friend worked beside him as hired muscle on board caravans when they were young, that he later went to Mondstadt to become some kind of knight, and that he now returns old, without a word, and is seen skulking around Qingce without anyone knowing where he lives. This matches the Liyue-born caravan guard of Amber's Character Story 2, but the board never names him and gives a span of forty or fifty years. Wiki trivia additionally links his disappearance to the Eroch affair, but that is inference (see uncertainties), and in the manga he is only the absent owner of the office where the conversation takes place.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Amber is his granddaughter and heir to his work; she is now the last Outrider and holds to his teachings. Eula was his student and became a close friend of Amber, and Eula still recalls him as the outcast of Liyue who trained her. The Knights of Favonius owed him the Outriders, and he owed them his life.
### LARGE +
Eula's Character Story 3 calls her the beloved mentee of Amber's grandfather, "the first Outrider", and says that is why Amber trusts her completely; Amber and Eula had been friends long before Eula joined the Knights. Amber's own voice-over says she joined the Knights because of her grandpa and stayed because of Jean.
### ULTRA +
Between Amber and her grandfather the canon tone is quiet: his love is described as silent and unconditional, he took responsibility for her childhood mischief, and his main shown acts of care are training and cooking. Nothing shows how Jean, Diluc or Kaeya regarded him; do not invent it.

## entry: Outriders

### STANDARD
The Outriders were the Knights of Favonius' scouting archers, founded and trained by Amber's grandfather. After he vanished the division declined; Amber, the only Outrider in the present, keeps the title alive.
### LARGE +
The Favonius Warbow's text says the founder, a mercenary leader from Liyue, passed his whole store of knowledge to the unit, whose tracking skills and danger sense were unique among the Knights. When the first Outrider left the order for reasons unknown to the Knights, the team became a shell of itself: structure and name preserved, expertise gone. A few, Amber among them, still work to maintain the dignity of the title.
