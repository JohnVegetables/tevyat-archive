## summary
### LITE
Waskar was the younger of the twin heroes of Natlan's Collective of Plenty. He joined the Scarlet-Eyed Youth against the insane king of Ochkanatlan, stood first against the darkness on the day of the revolt, and was killed in the fighting.
### STANDARD +
Every detail comes from Unfinished Reverie artifact text. His elder brother Atawallpa mourned him loudly and bitterly.

## background
### LITE
Waskar's back, the text says, "bore the brunt of the tyrant's lash", so he knew the king's cruelty before the revolt. He and Atawallpa were the second to answer the Scarlet-Eyed Youth's call, after Sakkuk and before the silent Yupanqui and the Masterful Craftsman.
### STANDARD +
The brothers shared a habit of joking. When the craftsman told a fanciful tale of what he had seen in the flames of the fire he had survived, the twins asked giggling if the golden tear of the stone head was the source of his stories. In the sundial text Waskar is the champion of the Collective of Plenty, whose people saw the revolt as a chance for the oppressed to rise from the earth once more.
### LARGE +
On the day the heroes struck, each tribe's champion acted: Sakkuk sent wandering spirits back to the Night Kingdom, Yupanqui shattered the shackles on a Saurian, Atawallpa rekindled the memory of past glories, and Waskar stood first against the torrent of darkness. The Scarlet-Eyed Youth then burned the corroded city with the fiery wrath of the god.

## status
### LITE
Dead. He was slain in the war against the king of Ochkanatlan, long before the Cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
Atawallpa died sometime afterwards, and Sakkuk, whom the text calls the frailest of the heroes, outlived them all.
### LARGE +
No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content mentions him. His only trace is in the artifact set Unfinished Reverie.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes the pair's names echo the Quechua names of two half-brothers of Inca history, and hero twins are a widespread mythic motif; this is real-world etymology, not in-game lore.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Atawallpa, his elder twin: a talker with sharp lips and teeth who joked with him and howled in grief at his death.
- The Scarlet-Eyed Youth: the leader he followed into rebellion.
- Sakkuk and Yupanqui: fellow heroes of the revolt.
### LARGE +
- The Masterful Craftsman: the architect of the plan, who told the twins his visions around the fire.
- Och-Kan: the insane king he fought.

## entry: The Heroes of the Revolt
@keys: Scarlet-Eyed Youth heroes, Unfinished Reverie heroes, twin heroes
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
The Scarlet-Eyed Youth gathered Sakkuk first, then the twins Atawallpa and Waskar, then Yupanqui and the Masterful Craftsman, to overthrow the insane king of Ochkanatlan. The plan was hatched over wine at a fire. When the day came, each hero acted in turn; the youth burned the city and returned the land to the tribes.
### LARGE +
Waskar fell in the fighting, Atawallpa followed later, and Sakkuk lived longest. Sakkuk, grown old, lamented how dull the years were without her friends.
