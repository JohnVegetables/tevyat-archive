## summary
### LITE
Renault de Petrichor was the last of the de Petrichor family to govern the town of Petrichor, and later the mayor of Poisson. He was the father of Rene de Petrichor and died with his wife in the Siege of Poisson during the cataclysm 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
He is a mentioned figure only: Tiepolo, Petrichor's present-day Doge, tells his story, and he appears in the description of a photograph taken by the reporter Karl Ingold.

## background
### LITE
The de Petrichor family had ruled Petrichor since the first ruler appointed by Egeria, and Tiepolo calls them just and benevolent leaders who brought the town ever-increasing prosperity. Renault was the family's last ruler there, then for reasons canon does not give was transferred to be mayor of Poisson.
### STANDARD +
After the purge of Fleuve Cendre, Eduardo and the other survivors were exiled to the desert, then rescued and occupied Poisson. In the photograph of the time Renault stands among them in formal dress, his collar askew, with his son Rene in front of him, absorbed by the new Kamera. Others in the picture include Eduardo, Jakob, the magician Parsifal, Rosa Reed and her husband with their infant, and Tom Alter.
### LARGE +
The Siege of Poisson ended in bloodshed that killed Renault and his wife. Rene, writing years later in the pages found across Fontaine, speaks of 'Father and Mother' and blames the Maison Gardiennage's men, and saying he still cannot trust Uncle Guillotin, who was with them. Tiepolo says that with Renault's death in Poisson the family tree withered.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed at Poisson about 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
With the line gone, Petrichor drifted toward becoming a forgotten fishing village until Ruggiero, its first Doge, rebuilt it. Townspeople at the time blamed closer ties with the outside world for the decline.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His son Rene is his main tie. He also appears beside Eduardo, the survivors of the Fleuve Cendre purge and their friends in Poisson. His wife is unnamed.
### LARGE +
The Siege killed both parents, and Rene's later distrust of the Maison Gardiennage and Emanuel Guillotin traces to it. Whether Renault actively sheltered Eduardo's group or simply governed a town they occupied is not stated.

## entry: Siege of Poisson
@keys: Siege of Poisson
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A bloody confrontation in Poisson during the cataclysm in which Renault and his wife died; Rene later blamed the Maison Gardiennage.
### LARGE +
The wiki credits Emanuel Guillotin with starting it. The group photograph was taken shortly before it.
### ULTRA +
The Moment of Judgment artifact text recalls Karl Ingold crossing between Poisson and the Court of Fontaine many times in those months, full of hope, before the cries that followed; it does not name Renault's death directly, so his fate rests on Tiepolo's account and Rene's writings.

## entry: De Petrichor family
@keys: de Petrichor family, Petrichor family
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
A line of nobles who governed Petrichor from Egeria's appointment until at least five hundred years ago. Renault and Rene are the only known members.
