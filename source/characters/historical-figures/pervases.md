## summary
### LITE
Pervases was a junior Yaksha, one of Rex Lapis's adepti, who died in the Archon War. His spirit lingered in a crane statue at a ruined adepti temple near Mt. Tianheng, where the Traveler met him during the quest Butterfly's Dream.
### STANDARD +
He gave up the censer and seven-star lamps he had used for the Mortal Lingering ritual so the Traveler could perform the Dream Trawler ritual against the impostor Starsnatcher.

## background
### LITE
Rex Lapis gave him the name Pervases. Canon does not say how he came to serve or what he did before. He fell in the Archon War, and his spirit stayed in his temple statue.
### STANDARD +
On the anniversary of his death he performed the Mortal Lingering ritual to see modern Liyue. Meeting the Traveler, he sensed Xiao's aura and karma on them, told the truth about himself and let them take the ritual items though that ended his own lingering.

## status
### LITE
Dead; his spirit has gone dormant again in the statue.
### STANDARD +
Wang Ping'an, repentant, later restored the temple. In the Hereafter quests the Traveler brings grilled fish to pay respects there; Xiao is also found at the temple, and the wiki says he returns there to ponder the Yakshas' fate.
### LARGE +
Pervases also appears as a cameo in Lantern Rite material and a Story Teaser, grilling fish. The wiki notes the Archon personally named him and that a statue of him stands in his temple near the foot of Mt. Tianheng.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's reading is that his temple was run down and overrun with Treasure Hoarders before Wang Ping'an's restoration. Xiao says in the Hereafter quest that the Yakshas never acted for recognition.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Close to the Prime Yaksha Bosacius, and bound to Xiao by their shared order; he left a message for Xiao. Grilled fish was his favourite food, apparently a favourite among adepti.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
He knows the Yakshas' duty and his own death; he did not know modern Liyue until the ritual.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not a surviving Yaksha; he is dead.
- Do not invent the details of his death or his message to Xiao beyond what the quest says.
