## summary
### LITE
Taika was the strongest fighter of the Flower-Feather Clan, the mountain tribe of Natlan. When the Cataclysm began he left his pregnant wife and became an adventurer, leading a band to Ochkanatlan in search of a fabled weapon against the Abyss. He never returned.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through remnants: an artifact's flavor text, the memory of Och-Kan and an NPC's remark about "our leader". His daughter Bona later followed his trail.

## background
### LITE
Before the Cataclysm he dominated his clan's masked combat tournaments, wearing a falcon-feathered mask, until the contests bored him. When monsters and disappearances in the Night Kingdom began, he accepted that the strong must carry more, bade farewell to his wife and unborn child, and went to war. He later led the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team to the ruined city in search of the Tonatiuh and its "Jade of Return".
### STANDARD +
In the artifact text his departure is framed as the moment he first felt "the weight of destiny" after seeing true war. The legend he followed said an ancient weapon of the dragons, hidden in a city above the clouds, could quell the Abyss; nobody who sought it had ever returned. In Och-Kan's memories a teammate credits him with finding the blueprints that led the team through the city.
### LARGE +
At his last parting he told his wife that a daughter should be named Bona, and that she would surely be an outstanding warrior. In his final moments, the artifact text says, he offered his blood and wished to leave "the key to those gates" with his child. The wiki identifies that key as Pyrophosphorite.
### ULTRA +
In the memory scene the team stands at the Altar of Primal Flame, and a comrade jokes about celebrating with his newborn daughter once the adventure ends. The legend of the Jade of Return turned out to be a lure; Bona's wiki page describes it as a falsehood spread by Waxaklahun Ubah Kan to draw strong warriors into a trap. Taika's team was one of many drawn in.

## status
### LITE
Dead. He and most of his team died in Ochkanatlan during the Cataclysm era, long before the present.
### STANDARD +
A Flower-Feather Clan rescue team may have been sent for the survivors, but canon does not say if it arrived in time. Only the young mystic Liriwu is recorded as having returned alive.
### LARGE +
His legacy lives on through Bona, who came to Ochkanatlan a few decades later and died there; her memory was later met by the Traveler and Paimon. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 adds to him.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Bona, his daughter: born after he left. She followed him into Ochkanatlan, believing his legend, and learned it was a trap.
- His wife: the mother of Bona, unnamed.
- The Ochkanatlan Adventure Team: he led it; named teammates include Quenin, Copan, Legba, Titu and the young mystic Liriwu.
### LARGE +
Quenin recalls him as mightier than himself, saying he is not "as mighty as our leader". Copan, Titu and Legba speak of him with warmth and some teasing in Och-Kan's memory.

## entry: Ochkanatlan Adventure Team
@keys: Ochkanatlan Adventure Team, Cinder City expedition, Jade of Return
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
A group of adventurers from several tribes, led by Taika, who entered Ochkanatlan after the Cataclysm seeking a legendary weapon against the Abyss. The name is unofficial. They found the city overrun with corruption. Some members went mad, others were replaced by Tenebrous Mimiflora, and the mission failed. Liriwu was the only one known to have come back.
### LARGE +
Titu hid the young Liriwu in a safe tower. Taika, Copan, Legba and Titu reached the Tonatiuh, where the expedition ended. Quenin later helped cover the retreat of those who fled.
