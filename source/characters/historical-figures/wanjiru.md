## summary
### LITE
Wanjiru, "the great runner", was a warrior of the Collective of Plenty and the first of six heroes to join Tenoch against the Abyss. She bore the Ancient Name Uwezo, "power", with her Tatankasaurus companion Kayeke, and died in the final battle five hundred years ago.
### STANDARD +
She was Mavuika's friend and wrestling partner before Mavuika became Pyro Archon. Iansan now carries her Name.

## background
### LITE
Wanjiru and Kayeke were the first to answer Tenoch when he travelled Natlan gathering fighters. Her body was scarred from the journey, and the constant quarrels among the tribes had worn on her, but his call to unite against the Abyss revived her fighting spirit.
### STANDARD +
Weapon text describes a young Wanjiru whose strength shattered century-old ironwood, crushed raw ore in her palm and bent metal like mud. Hearing tribal tales of the dragon Xiuhcoatl's tomb, she dared the Great Volcano of Tollan, passed its guardians and took a bone longsword from the ruins within, the Sturdy Bone. She rarely drew it, because, she said, her hands were enough. Mavuika's memory shows her as a close friend of the future Archon, one who lost wrestling matches until her last one.
### LARGE +
In the "Sunset" short she toasts the dead Sanhaj and Burkina and calls their deaths a rallying cry. She senses Mavuika's grief at a solitary five-century journey. On the eve of the last fight she jokes about certain defeat, then watches Menilek sleep through it. She fought on the cliff beside the Abyss with the others and later vanishes from the account; Sundjatta died shielding her.
### ULTRA +
A later weapon text places a young girl, Fietena, in Wanjiru's shadow, listening to her recount heroic legends. After the heroes fell, Fietena took up a half-broken bone blade and rallied the tribes. This fits the Sturdy Bone's legacy but the weapon text does not say the blade was Wanjiru's.

## status
### LITE
Dead, about 500 years before the present day; her Name Uwezo now belongs to Iansan.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through memories, a Name projection tied to Iansan, item texts and the 5.3 short. Nothing places her in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Kayeke is her companion. Tenoch recruited her first. She was close to Mavuika, Tupac, Menilek and Sundjatta, and Iansan inherited her Name.
### LARGE +
Her bond with Mavuika is playful and rooted in wrestling: she wins once, jokes about queueing up behind the Archon's duties, and worries the Sacred Flame may change her friend.

A longsword of bone that grew harder with every blow. Weapon text says Wanjiru took it from the ruins in the Great Volcano of Tollan and rarely needed to draw it. The text ends with her gripping the blade on the eve of battle.

### STANDARD
In a memory shown in A Fuel Named "Fate", Wanjiru laughs at having beaten Mavuika in a wrestling bout for once, just before Mavuika became Pyro Archon.
