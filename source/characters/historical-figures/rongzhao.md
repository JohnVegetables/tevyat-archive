## summary
### LITE
Rongzhao was an exorcist of Liyue, the younger brother of Boyang and an ancestor of Yelan. He fought in The Chasm during the cataclysm about five hundred years ago and came home alive, but without his sanity.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through the Interlude Chapter (2.7), where Yelan goes looking for the ancestor who vanished and learns what happened to both brothers.
### LARGE +
Rongzhao himself never speaks or appears. Everything about him comes from Boyang's notes, Yelan's account and Xiao's reading of it.

## background
### LITE
He and his elder brother Boyang were given the Fantastic Compass by someone of great importance and ordered by the Qixing to take it to The Chasm to aid the Millelith against the monsters of Khaenri'ah. They fought for days beside a maddened yaksha, then helped seal the monsters underground.
### STANDARD +
The brothers found that the monsters weakened near a strange underground palace revealed when The Chasm's surface was damaged. Boyang planned to lure the beasts into it with the Compass and the yaksha and seal himself in; Rongzhao agreed to help from the outside, so the seal would hold. Boyang's own notes record that he chose to leave Rongzhao on the surface.
### LARGE +
Rongzhao was among those who closed the domain from above. He survived the war, but he was the one who "made it back minus his sanity," in Yelan's words. Yelan's family kept the story, and her elders still teach that the Vigilant Yaksha deserves the utmost respect.
### ULTRA +
Boyang's notes also record that the pair, on the Qixing's orders, brought the Compass to aid the Millelith, and that without the yaksha's help the brothers might have been killed at once. Yelan cautions that the underground space acted like a living thing that replays the minds of the dead, so what the Traveler sees there of the brothers is a reflection, not Rongzhao himself.

## status
### LITE
Long dead as of the present day. No account of his death exists in canon.
### STANDARD +
Yelan's family lore says that an ancestor of theirs found brief relief from his madness only when passing Wangshu Inn, where Xiao lives. She suggests it was the comforting, evil-warding presence of the yaksha.

## relationships
### LITE
Boyang (elder brother, sealed underground); Bosacius (the mad yaksha who fought beside them); Yelan (descendant).
### STANDARD +
Yelan and Xiao read his madness as the effect of Bosacius's karma on a man with no Vision. His relief at Wangshu Inn ties him to Xiao, though the two never meet in canon.

## entry: Boyang and the Fantastic Compass
@keys: Boyang, Fantastic Compass
@version: 2.7
### STANDARD
Boyang was the older brother and a Millelith thaumaturge. The brothers were gifted the Fantastic Compass, a catalyst that manipulates space, and were told by the Qixing to carry it to The Chasm. The Compass needed a steady flow of power from a mortal and an adeptus together, so Boyang and the yaksha Bosacius sealed themselves in the underground palace with the beasts, while Rongzhao worked on the outside. Boyang's notes were found in The Chasm's Bed in the Interlude Chapter.

## entry: Rongzhao's madness
@keys: Rongzhao's madness, Yelan's ancestor
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Yelan tells the Traveler that only one of her two ancestors came back, and without his sanity. Xiao answers with a single word, "Karma." Yelan agrees it is a likely cause but calls it one possibility: neither brother had a Vision, so neither could resist the yaksha's karma for long.
