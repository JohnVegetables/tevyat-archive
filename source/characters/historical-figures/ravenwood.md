## summary
### LITE
Ravenwood was the second Knight of Boreas, a Mondstadt blacksmith's son who, as a boy, followed the nameless Wandering Knight around, and years later took up the heavy claymore under Boreas's name. His valor earned the Wolf Spirit's recognition, and when the old aristocracy tried to return he defended the city, making the title a lasting role in the Knights of Favonius.
### STANDARD +
He is known from a weapon text, a character story and Varka's Lupus Majoris quests; he has no profile, model or lines of his own beyond brief voice echoes. His greatsword became the model for later replicas, including the Ferrous Shadow.

## background
### LITE
The son of the city's blacksmith, the man who gave the Wandering Knight his sword for free, he admired the knight as a boy. After the knight left and the Knights of Favonius drifted back under the aristocracy's influence, he picked up a newly forged sword and went into the wilds.
### STANDARD +
Gest of the Mighty Wolf says he carried that sword to a fortress gate where an old man recognised the techniques once used by 'Andrius', and the crowd cheered that Andrius had returned. The Varka profile adds that the Knights' tavern-hummed homecoming hymn was revived once more when he claimed the title, and has endured in bardic versions ever since.
### LARGE +
He watched the knight practise in silence and heard his sunset stories of a lost warrior kingdom with undying flowers and rivers of mead. After the title was secured, it passed down within the Knights, though Rosaria notes few holders have been truly recognised by Boreas.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; no text gives the manner or date. His sword symbol is among those at the memorial Varka crosses in 6.4, and his echo shields and guides Varka through the Ley Lines.
### STANDARD +
Rostam and, much later, Varka also held the title. In the Lonely Warrior quest Ravenwood's voice says the title of Boreas must find an heir, and later his Ley-Line shield replaces Rostam's.
### ULTRA +
Varka's closing scene shows a small graveyard of swords marked with the symbols of the Wandering Knight, Ravenwood, Vennessa, Arundolyn and Rostam, past Knights of Boreas and Grand Masters; Varka asks whether he is doing a decent job as Grand Master. Ravenwood's sword there is the Ferrous Shadow, which the quest's trivia ties to him as the second Knight of Boreas.

## relationships
### LITE
The Wandering Knight was his hero and model; Boreas recognised him; later holders such as Rostam and Varka inherit his title.
### STANDARD +
He is remembered more through others' stories than through his own words: Jean and Rosaria cite him as the one who made the Boreas title permanent, and the Ferrous Shadow text uses his sword as the pattern for the replica Arundolyn swung as a boy.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No canon description of his face, build or age. He is shown in text only as a boy with a blacksmith's background and later as a knight with a heavy greatsword.

## chronology
### LARGE
- About a thousand years ago: as a boy follows the Wandering Knight around Mondstadt.
- Years later: picks up a newly forged sword; defends the city against the returning aristocracy; wins Boreas's recognition.
- Later still: his greatsword becomes the model for replicas, one of which Arundolyn swings as a boy about the time of Rostam.
- 1.0: the Ferrous Shadow text names him.
- 6.4: the Gest of the Mighty Wolf text, Varka's profile and the Lupus Majoris quests expand his story.

### STANDARD
A character story in Varka's profile: the first Knight of Boreas used to hum one tune in the tavern every time he returned from a hunt, so the song became a signal of victory; after he left, it faded until Ravenwood claimed the title and the crowds sang it again. Bards have kept it alive since.
