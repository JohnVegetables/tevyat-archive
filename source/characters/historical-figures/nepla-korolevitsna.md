## summary
### LITE
Nepla Korolevitsna was a human woman warrior from before the Archon War who guarded a human clan. Defeated by the young Monomakh and Filipov, she became Filipov's wife and died of illness long before him.
### STANDARD +
The Snezhnayan chronicles remember her as a formidable champion, and her memory was later used against Filipov by Rotwang and by the Abyss.

## background
### LITE
The legend says she carried a war hammer heavier than the two youths combined, rode a fire-breathing horse, and by a single shout brought Filipov's warhorse to its knees.
### STANDARD +
When Monomakh threw Filipov at her as a venomous serpent, she caught it by the tail and hurled it back. Filipov at last unhorsed her at the cost of an eye. By the law of that age the victor could kill or enslave her clan; Monomakh refused and sent them to settle at what became Port Belovodye. Filipov offered to marry her so Monomakh need not fear his resentment, saying her arrow had taken one eye and her valour and beauty the other.
### LARGE +
Monomakh warned that fae live long and humans briefly; Filipov answered that the shortness of a winter's day mattered little. The marriage ended sooner than expected when a sudden sickness took her.

## relationships
### LITE
She was the wife of the fae Filipov Vseslavyevich, and her clan was spared by Monomakh.
### STANDARD +
Filipov counted losing to his wife among the only three defeats of his long life, the others being to Monomakh and to the Abyssal calamity.

## status
### LITE
Long dead. Thousands of years later, Filipov trusted the monk Rotwang after he summoned her apparition, and during the Cataclysm the Abyss took her form to tempt Filipov.
### STANDARD +
Filipov exposed that imitation by falsely claiming she had a mole behind her ear; the copy produced one, and he laughed and refused her.

### ULTRA +
Filipov later abandoned Monomakh's army only when word came of the Tsar's death, and his whereabouts are unknown. The tale of the false Nepla ends with his remark that all the world longs to be deceived.
