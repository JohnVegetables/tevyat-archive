## summary
### LITE
She was the daughter of the high priest Varuch and the last princess of Sal Vindagnyr, a mountain kingdom buried by snow long before the Archon War. Gifted with prophetic dreams, she painted what she foresaw as frescoes; when the Skyfrost Nail fell and the great tree shattered, she tried to save it and died in the cold.
### STANDARD +
Her name is lost, and canon knows her only through the things she left on Dragonspine: three treasure boxes, the carvings, and the texts of the weapons Frostbearer and Snow-Tombed Starsilver. She is a figure of quiet, unfinished hope rather than of deeds.
### ULTRA +
Her story reaches the present only in fragments and through other people's voices. The Princess' Box is narrated by her, the Priest's Box by her father, the Scribe's Box by Ukko, and the weapon texts by a later narrator. The archive treats her as a narrated memory, not as someone who can speak in the present.

## background
### LITE
Born beneath the great silver-white tree of Sal Vindagnyr, she was blessed with sight of the future, which she recorded as frescoes. When the Skyfrost Nail brought endless blizzards, her father sent her to paint a coming thaw; she could not remember what spring looked like. She took a surviving branch of the tree to a small island to graft it, failed, and died there.
### STANDARD +
Frostbearer's text says the kingdom rejoiced at her birth under the tree, which was originally an Irminsul-linked tree that had helped the nation prosper. One of her visions, shown in a carving, was of a black dragon that darkened the sun, which she took as an omen of doom. The Ancient Carvings say the people of Sal Vindagnyr were refugees who found a green mountain and named their monument there.
### LARGE +
After the nail fell, her father Varuch went up the mountain to seek an answer from Celestia and told her to keep painting, hoping the third fresco would show thawing ice. In her box she says she could no longer tell which colours to use for a thaw, that by his return everything would be as before but she could not bear the cold, and she asks forgiveness of Varuch and Ukko; she also says she wishes she could see Imunlaukr again.
### ULTRA +
The Snow-Tombed Starsilver text adds that she gave the outlander Imunlaukr a Starsilver greatsword to shatter the ice, told him a fourth fresco was prepared with his likeness and promised to wait and pray for his return, but the howling wind swallowed her words. The Skyfrost Nail then split in three; its fragments froze the great tree and she took its most complete branch to a small island and tried to graft it. It did not take, and she withered with it. The Ancient Carvings mention a scribe who tried to heal the Leylines, found the tree already dead, and buried her. Frostbearer adds that long after, when the black dragon (Durin) was killed by Dvalin and his poisoned blood soaked the land, the dead tree's roots drank it and, after the Traveler gave it a crimson essence, grew one fruit for those who died there.

## status
### LITE
Dead, thousands of years ago; she died in the fall of Sal Vindagnyr. Nothing in canon places her in any later era.
### STANDARD +
Her grave is believed to lie near the entrance to Starglow Cavern on Dragonspine, where the Scribe's Box is found; the small island where she is said to have died has a sword in the ground, owner unknown.
### LARGE +
Her prophecy of a black dragon was fulfilled: Durin descended on the land, and Dvalin killed him. As of 7.1 canon adds nothing further about her.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Varuch, her father and the high priest, who trusted her paintings to point to salvation.
- Ukko, the scribe, who mourned her and buried her.
- Imunlaukr, the outlander she cared for and waited for; their last words to each other were never heard.
### LARGE +
Ukko's bitterness toward Imunlaukr, whom the scribe believed had abandoned her, is recorded in the Scribe's Box. Imunlaukr in fact kept searching for a way to end the disaster and returned too late. She herself shows no blame for anyone in her own words.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Birth beneath the white tree; the nation celebrates.
- Her vision of a black dragon; she records it as a fresco.
- The Skyfrost Nail falls; blizzards begin; Imunlaukr leaves with the Starsilver greatsword.
- Varuch climbs the peak; the Nail splits and shatters the tree.
- She grafts a branch on a small island and dies there; the scribe buries her.
### ULTRA +
- Long afterward: Durin comes, Dvalin kills him, and the tree takes in the dragon's blood.
- 1.2: all of the above first appears in Dragonspine content (Dragonspine's Last Trio and Dragonspine's Glacial Secret).

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She has no given name. Do not call her by one.
- She is not the priestess; her father Varuch is the high priest (Frostbearer's English text says "priestess" by mistranslation).
- She is not active in the present era and has no Vision in the modern sense.
### LARGE +
- Do not state that she and Imunlaukr were lovers; canon says only that they were close and that she wished to see him again.
- Do not state that she took her own life; the sources say she withered with the grafted branch in the cold.

## entry: Princess' Box
@keys: Princess' Box, Priest's Box, Scribe's Box, Dragonspine's Last Trio
@keys2: Dragonspine, Sal Vindagnyr
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Three boxes found on Dragonspine, each narrated by a different person from Sal Vindagnyr's last days. The Princess' Box holds her admission that she cannot finish the fresco and her apology to her father and to Ukko; the Priest's Box holds Varuch's hope that the third painting will show thawing ice; the Scribe's Box holds Ukko's bitter wish to see the heavens burn. Together they open a secret room.
### LARGE +
Ukko also says in the Scribe's Box that they had heard of people building a "new nation without gods" that might stand against the world, a reference to Khaenri'ah.

## entry: Frostbearing Tree
@keys: Frostbearing Tree, white tree of Sal Vindagnyr, undying silver-white tree
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The great silver-white tree beneath which the princess was born, once linked to an Irminsul tree. A fragment of the Skyfrost Nail shattered it. After Durin's blood soaked into the ground, the tree stirred and, when the Traveler offered a crimson essence, bore a single fruit as a memorial to the dead of Sal Vindagnyr.

## entry: Imunlaukr and the Starsilver greatsword
@keys: Imunlaukr, Snow-Tombed Starsilver, Imunlaukr Clan
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
A hero from another land who lived in Sal Vindagnyr and was close to the princess. She gave him a Starsilver greatsword to shatter the ice; he left to find a way to save the nation, and returned to find everyone dead. He left the sword among the frescoes and went to look for war to lose himself in.
