## summary
### LITE
Pir Kavikavus was a celebrated scholar of the Akademiya's Kshahrewar darshan in Sumeru, a prodigy who became a Herbad before turning thirty. He is remembered as the last scholar to enter a Ruin Golem for systematic research; an accident there lost his findings, and Kshahrewar has been in decline since.
### STANDARD +
He is idolised by current Kshahrewar researchers such as Jazari, and a research prize for scholars under forty bears his name.
### ULTRA +
His name is a matter of record rather than rank: Aranara address him as Nara Pir Kavikavus, and the Pir Kavikavus Prize carries the same name. Wiki editors note the name echoes a Sufi title for a spiritual guide and the legendary Persian king Kay Kavus, but that is an etymological observation, not stated in-game.

## background
### LITE
Some time after the Cataclysm he joined Kshahrewar and rose quickly, earning the rank of Herbad before thirty. He researched the Ruin Golem on Devantaka Mountain, a Khaenri'ahn construct. He was accompanied by an Aranara friend, Aralohita.
### STANDARD +
During his work he is said to have found that the Akasha System has no signal on Devantaka Mountain. The Akademiya's records name him as the last person to get inside the Ruin Golem for systematic study; after his accident nobody has been able to enter.
### LARGE +
After the accident Aralohita saved part of his research and locked it behind puzzles and riddles, as Aranara like to do. In the Until Vana is Healed quest the Aranara Ararycan leads the Traveler and Paimon to one of those puzzles, where a research paper by Kavikavus is found on the Ruin Golem's Chaos Core. Jazari uses it to seal the core, which weakens the Abyss Mages who try to interrupt him and greatly lessens the Abyss Order's threat to the forest.
### ULTRA +
The paper's title, as given in its item description, is "Theory and Practice: Enhancing the Safety of the Chaos Core in Unstable Conditions Using the Khaenri'ah Ruin Golem of Devantaka Mountain as an Example." Its body is so full of jargon that anyone who is not a senior Kshahrewar researcher will find the argument unfathomable, which is why the Traveler and Paimon must hand it to Jazari. The wiki speculates that Aralohita helped him achieve so much at a young age; the quests do not say this.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead; his research was lost in the accident. His reputation lives on in Kshahrewar, and the rediscovery of his paper in Version 3.0 revived hope in the darshan.
### STANDARD +
The Akademiya has not raised Kshahrewar's funding in the years since his passing, according to a public message board. Jazari hopes to win the Pir Kavikavus Prize and to follow in his footsteps.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Aralohita is the Aranara friend who helped him and preserved his research. Jazari admires him as a predecessor and aims to repeat his study of the Ruin Golem. The Aranara call him Nara, a respectful term for humans they accept.
### LARGE +
Ararycan, another Aranara, tells the Traveler and Paimon about Aralohita and Kavikavus; Paimon recalls Jazari's earlier account of his genius.
### ULTRA +
Aralohita's own fate is unknown. Ararycan was able to locate Aralohita's last known position and unearth the research papers.
