## summary
### LITE
Kaedehara Kazuha's father. He died before the game begins, leaving a household in debt and a son he urged to live free of the clan's burdens.
### STANDARD +
He is known only from the limited event Summertime Odyssey (2.8), through Kazuha's memories, a note and a letter. He loved bonsai.
### LARGE +
Kazuha wrote a letter to him and planned to burn it on a hilltop so that the smoke might reach him.

### ULTRA +
His voice actor is credited on the wiki, but canon gives no further portrait: no appearance, trade, age or death is described, so portray him only through the lines above.

## background
### LITE
Son of Kaedehara Hisamichi. He kept notes on forging techniques and bonsai designs.
### STANDARD +
He wrote that his son Kazuha turned six, named as his wife wished; her death came close to Kazuha's birth. He found his own father's decline hard to bear. He told Kazuha that he had failed to revive the clan and that Kazuha should abandon the family craft.
### LARGE +
After his death Kazuha dismissed the servants, dissolved the household and became a wanderer. The Tenryou Commission later confiscated an old family relic.

## status
### LITE
Dead before the game.
### STANDARD +
No later mention appears in canon through 7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Hisamichi (father), Kazuha (son), a late wife (unnamed).
### LARGE +
Kazuha's letter recalls a windy mountain spot to which his father once brought him.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knew nothing of Kazuha's later life.
