## summary
### LITE
A Snowland Fae Grand Duke, the Belyi Tsar's sworn Synaimos and first Commander of the Druzhna, and the most gifted shapeshifter of his people. He is remembered as a cunning strategist and a grieving widower, and he disappeared when the Tsar died.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through books and item text. Chronicles pair his name with cunning, quick wit and victory, and credit him with losing only three times in a long life.

## background
### LITE
He challenged the young Monomakh and, after seven shapeshifting defeats, swore loyalty and became his closest friend. He married the human warrior Nepla Korolevitsna, who died young of illness; only his friendship and oaths to the Tsar kept him alive. He vanished when the Tsar fell in the cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
Filipov met Monomakh soon after Monomakh left his teacher Yalyane Morevna, scorned the offered friendship and challenged him. Each time he was thrown over one more mountain. The General History of Snezhnaya calls this duel the most credible of several accounts. The forms were weasel, falcon, white wolf, great bear, bull, a mountain-sized giant, then Monomakh himself. Tales add that the pair sailed into a whale's belly and fought a dragon for seven days; the book says these cannot be verified. Filipov was said to have been beaten only three times: by Monomakh, by his wife, and by the Abyss.
Filipov helped win the war with the Volkodlak king Miscislav. A Brief History of the Pale Starborn says that during the second campaign he infiltrated Miscislav's army by transformation and learned of an ambush planned by Grand Duke Skoldyryt of Nyholmgarthir, which let Monomakh adjust his plan. He also visited Vaselisa Strivozha after she broke with the Volkodlaks, bringing a sword as the Tsar's gift. She accepted and held a seven-day banquet.
### LARGE +
He was not yet styled Grand Duke in the account of the Miscislav war, so the title is thought to have come later (a wiki inference). Brahe's Notes (II) names Dmitri Dubrovich Sukhotin, a third-class military officer and Chief Instructor of the Snezhnayan Royal Military Academy until the Khaenri'ahn disaster, as the Grand Duke's trusted aide.
### ULTRA +
In the Tsar's last years, when Rotwang had become a court favourite, a tale spread through the Tsar's court that the monk Rotwang had publicly silenced Filipov's scepticism by summoning his dead wife's soul. Filipov accepted it and no longer cautioned the Tsar against Rotwang. In the cataclysm he joined the Tsar's expedition to Khaenri'ah, the campaign in which Zver and its chimeric monsters also fought the Abyss under the Tsar's banner. There the Abyss took Nepla's shape to call him away from the army. He kept fighting until news of the Tsar's death reached him.

## status
### LITE
Missing since the cataclysm roughly five hundred years ago. He left his army when the Tsar died, and canon says nobody knows his fate. He has not appeared in the present day through 7.1.

## relationships
### LITE
Monomakh was the one tie to the world after Nepla's death. Nepla was the great love of his life and the defeat he accepted gladly. Rotwang exploited his grief, and Filipov half-knowingly let himself be deceived.
### STANDARD +
Vaselisa Strivozha matched his wit in the sword-gift exchange. Item text describes him as deeply trusted by the White Emperor.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Master of transformation, called the progenitor of the shapeshifters. He turns into animals and giants, can imitate a person closely enough to infiltrate an army, and is a noted strategist and spy. Canon gives him no Vision or element.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no description beyond the loss of one eye, which the General History attributes to his wife's arrow.

## entry: The First Duel with Monomakh
@keys: Filipov, Synaimos, seven mountains
@keys2: Monomakh, Belyi Tsar, duel, challenge
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Filipov refused Monomakh's gift of crystals and demanded submission instead. He fought as a weasel (breaking Monomakh's weapons), a falcon, a white wolf, a bear, a bull, a giant and finally as Monomakh. Each time Monomakh seized him by the scruff of the neck and hurled him over one more mountain, up to seven. Filipov then accepted Monomakh's offer and became his Synaimos, a fae word roughly meaning comrade-in-arms, brother or kinsman. The General History calls this the most credible of many versions.

## entry: Nepla Korolevitsna and Filipov's Grief
@keys: Nepla, Nepla Korolevitsna, Filipov's wife
@keys2: Filipov
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Monomakh and Filipov met Nepla Korolevitsna when she guarded a human clan. She threw Filipov, in serpent form, back over three mountains, and he lost an eye to her arrow before unseating her. Monomakh spared her clan, and Filipov chose to marry her, though Monomakh warned that the fae live far longer. She died of illness sooner than expected. Filipov wished to follow her in death but stayed alive for the sake of his friendship and oaths.

## entry: Filipov and the Mad Monk
@keys: Rotwang, mad monk
@keys2: Filipov
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Court gossip recorded in the Vessel of Plenty says Filipov called Rotwang's research a hoax and Rotwang answered by raising the soul of Filipov's late wife. The General History, without naming the sorcerer, says Filipov believed the 'itinerant charlatan' almost at once when shown his dead wife's phantom, and thereafter stopped warning the Tsar about the mad monk. No one ever confirmed the stories with Rotwang himself.

## entry: All the Living Desire to Be Deceived
@keys: desire to be deceived, mole behind her ear
@keys2: Filipov, Abyss, Khaenri'ah
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the cataclysm, the Abyss took Nepla's form and urged Filipov to desert. He said his own shapeshifting was finer, since the real Nepla had a mole behind her ear. When the imitation swept aside its hair and bade him look again, he laughed until he cried and admitted he had invented it. He said everyone wants to be deceived, himself included, and went back to the battle. The Memorata-Cognoscenti's Jest item quotes the same maxim as a saying of the fae lord Filipov.

## entry: The Sword for Vaselisa Strivozha

### STANDARD
At the end of the war with Miscislav, Filipov put a long sword on Vaselisa's table and traded proverbs with her about wind and winter. He then explained the sword was Monomakh's trophy gift, sent in hope she would stay an ally. She accepted and held a banquet lasting seven days and seven nights.
