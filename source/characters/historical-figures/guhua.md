## summary
### LITE
Guhua is a legendary martial nomad of Liyue remembered as a peacekeeper and the namesake of the Guhua Clan. He trained on Mt. Lingmeng and devised three techniques, and the Rainslasher is said to have been his weapon. He never led the clan that bears his name.
### STANDARD +
His surviving notes show a man whose training repeatedly detoured into other interests, such as fish and mutton cooking.

## background
### LITE
Guhua trained at Lingmeng Chamber on Mt. Lingmeng, later called Wangshan Hall. There he developed the Three Guhua Greats: Rain Cutter for the sword, Light Piercer for the spear and Life Ender combining both.
### STANDARD +
He suffered many setbacks. After falling off the Plumwatcher Piles too often he built a pond beneath them, raised fish for food, and filled ten pages of notes with fish recipes. When the boy Baixuan visited while Guhua was recovering and brought a mutton-and-fish dish, Guhua taught him Life Ender in exchange for the recipe and then filled pages with mutton recipes.
### LARGE +
Guhua renovated the library and built a hidden underground chamber beneath it, sealed with elemental scrolls readable only with Elemental Sight. An engraving there, apparently his, is grave in tone: it laments that the heavens are unfaithful and fate capricious, and asks future generations what they will do at a dead end. Inscriptions at the chamber's exit boast of slaying dragons and drinking their blood, killing tigers at Mt. Tianheng and skewering a scaled beast at the Sea of Clouds.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago; the date is unknown. A legend says he ascended as an adeptus and became a star at Huaguang Stone Forest, but nothing else supports it. No relevant 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content found.
### STANDARD +
The Guhua Clan, founded after his death by Baixuan, still teaches in his name. He is a founder in memory only: he never took a disciple.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Baixuan, the boy he favoured, founded the clan and learned Life Ender. The Guhua Clan treats Guhua as founder though he was not its master.
### ULTRA +
Guhua's voice survives only in two registers: cheerful, distractible notes about training and recipes, and a solemn engraving about fate and dead ends. The wiki flags the contrast in tone; treat both as his, without inventing speech beyond them.
