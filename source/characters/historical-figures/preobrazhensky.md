## summary
### LITE
Preobrazhensky (V. A. Preobrazhensky) was a high-ranking researcher of the Fatui's Armory Palace who studied Kuuvahki and fallen moon fragments in Nothing Passage on Lempo Isle, Nod-Krai. He was the bitter rival of Professor Anton Zarkovich Karnatsky. A surviving soldier's note blames the experiments of the two men for a catastrophe that killed them and almost the entire Fatui 6th Company.
### STANDARD +
He appears only through written traces found in the Colors of Emptiness world quests (6.0): a furious signed note, camp diaries and a dying soldier's last letter. He never appears in person and has no profile text of his own.

## background
@version: 6.0
### LITE
Preobrazhensky held a senior position in the Armory Palace and was stationed with his staff at the Nothing Passage research site. He and Karnatsky each wanted to be first to a breakthrough on moon fragments so as to earn the Tsaritsa's praise.
### STANDARD +
Soldiers' diaries describe the professors as openly at each other's throats. The captain of the 6th Company suspected Preobrazhensky and his underlings of playing tricks when strange marks and sounds troubled the camp, and had extra traps and defensive Landcruisers set up.
### LARGE +
His signed note accuses Karnatsky of using an authorization terminal to lock Preobrazhensky's people out and says Karnatsky's northern-side experiments had produced no progress. He states that he had already warned Karnatsky seven times and that this was a final warning. It shows an irascible, proud man who treated the dispute as a personal feud.
### ULTRA +
The surviving soldier's letter says the pair's experiments 'birthed the catastrophe' and that something inside the fallen moon rocks was awoken by Karnatsky and Preobrazhensky 'each in turn'; the writer did not know what blasphemy they committed. The 6th Company was wiped out. Only Private E. Smirnova escaped, at the cost of Fourrier A. Volkov's life, to send the Design Bureau a distress call. Elsewhere, a Design Bureau archive volume of casualty and punishment records contains an intriguing section about Nothing Passage, an offhand reference to the incident.

## relationships
### LITE
His one defining relationship is rivalry with Karnatsky, whose competitiveness fed the disaster. His staff worked under him; the soldiers guarding the site regarded the professors' quarrels with weary resentment.
### STANDARD +
Fatui soldiers of the 6th Company, under Captain Volkov, were assigned to the site and resented the resources spent on defence. Whether he had a family is not established (see uncertainties).

## status
@version: 6.0
### LITE
Dead. He died in the Nothing Passage catastrophe, before the events of Colors of Emptiness; the Traveler finds only his notes in an area afflicted by Kuuvahki phenomena.
### STANDARD +
Nothing is known of his remains or burial. Canon through 7.1 adds no further information about him.

## entry: Nothing Passage catastrophe
@keys: Preobrazhensky, Nothing Passage catastrophe
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Two rival Armory Palace professors, Karnatsky and Preobrazhensky, experimented on fallen moon fragments while the Fatui 6th Company guarded them. According to a dying soldier's note, they awoke something in the rocks; a 'color' devoured the company, and only Private Smirnova escaped. The note also clears Smirnova of desertion.
### LARGE +
The site lies on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai and holds strong Kuuvahki environments that the Traveler explores in the Colors of Emptiness quests.
