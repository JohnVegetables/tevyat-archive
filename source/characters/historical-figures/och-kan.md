## summary
### LITE
Och-Kan was the half-dragon Python King and Sacred Lord of ancient Ochkanatlan. A follower of Xbalanque, he became a tyrant and was overthrown by the Scarlet-Eyed Youth; in 5.2 the Traveler meets what remains of him.
### STANDARD +
He was a dragon with a human soul, bred by his father Kukulkan to be the next Pyro Sovereign, who instead gave his life to protecting humans. Before his fall he split his mind from his body: the mind became the dragonspirit Cocouik, the body an Abyss-twisted dragon.
### ULTRA +
Canon presents him through unreliable witnesses: the people's fear, Kukulkan's reproach, his own memories and the notes he left behind. Treat him as a sincere, self-hating protector who became a tyrant, not as a simple villain or a martyr.

## background
### LITE
Kukulkan raised him, a dragon with a human soul, to slay Xiuhcoatl and unite the two peoples. He served Xbalanque instead, then ruled Ochkanatlan as Sacred Lord.
### STANDARD +
As a child he was thrown into a vishap nest in the Ruined Armament Workshop. An experiment log there, reporting to Kukulkan, records strength and regeneration beyond an adult human's, no fear, and a habit of sharing his food with the vishaps; he escaped, and the order to hunt him was withdrawn after Kukulkan personally subdued him. Later Xbalanque's kindness earned his loyalty and the tribe took him back. He joined the war against the dragons, deciphered iridescent inscriptions for the warriors and, after a raid, received the Heretic's Molten Blade from Xbalanque.
Xbalanque, not Och-Kan, killed Xiuhcoatl and became Pyro Archon, taking the future the Heavenly Principles had written for Och-Kan. When Xbalanque entered the Sacred Flame, Och-Kan took power and called himself Sacred Lord, but held that Xbalanque should stay the only god.
### LARGE +
His rule grew harsher. He broke Xbalanque's commandments on peaceful coexistence and raised a city where heaven and earth were separated. The tribes gave authority to the Grand Alliance, dragons were hunted, live sacrifices were banned and worship of the Night Kingdom forbidden so that humanity would not be "strung up" by the Lord of the Night's strings. He used the Turnfire against opponents.
He ordered artisans to dig up huge constructs below the city, then burned the site and those who knew of it. Notes found in the ruins suggest he caged the dragon Ixlel in a statue, split her heart in three to power the city, and, after she turned defiant, built a new Core of Chu'ulel from his own draconic aspect.
### ULTRA +
Kukulkan returned to rebuke him, demanded the Golden Entreaty, and was told it was hidden. Och-Kan's father left, warning he would return for his soul. Yupanqui, who had stayed beside him, joined the Scarlet-Eyed Youth in the rebellion.
At the end Och-Kan tore off his mask to reveal a draconic, golden-flame face. The Youth took his sword and stabbed him; he fell into the dark mire and closed his eyes as the palace collapsed. He survived because he had already sealed his mind in the Core of Chu'ulel to protect the people. Later his body was used by Kukulkan as an Abyss conduit.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Dragon strength and regeneration far beyond a human's, which he disliked using. He preferred to command iridescent inscriptions like a mage. A weapon text says he could fight a dragon bare-handed but was no swordsman.
### LARGE +
As the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash he fights with Pyro attacks and a void barrier; while his mind (as Cocouik) fought the corrupted body, it unleashed the Golden Flame, a beam of light Paimon dubs a "Super Awesomesauce Laser".

## appearance
### STANDARD
He was said to be half-human with golden patterns on his face and hated how he looked. After his release he wore a mask. Over time his pupils turned vertical, and when the Scarlet-Eyed Youth saw him tear off his mask, his face showed gaping holes, "fearsomely draconic", that spewed golden flames.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Kukulkan was his father and, in the end, his adversary and his puppeteer. Xbalanque was the person he served and the only god he accepted. Yupanqui was his one surviving friend. The Scarlet-Eyed Youth overthrew him. Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon (Ixlel), a dragon of Natlantea, was advisor and then prisoner of his city. Bona freed his mind and named it Cocouik. See the header for the dynamics.
### LARGE +
Bona was an adventurer who found the mind in the Core of Chu'ulel after the Cataclysm, and they searched for the Jade of Return together. She died fighting Kukulkan after learning his plot, and Och-Kan regrets not saving her. The Bona the Traveler travels with in 5.2 is a memory of her from hundreds of years earlier. In 5.2 he told the Traveler that Little One, who carries the Flamelord's Blessing like him, is "most similar" to him, and left him the Golden Entreaty.

## status
### LITE
Dead. In Moment of Awakening (5.2) he merged his mind and body, died and left his remains in Ochkanatlan. As of 7.1 no sign he is alive.
### STANDARD +
His spirit rejoined the Night Kingdom, where in Journey of Stars and Flames (5.3) he and Bona helped the Traveler's party against the corroded depths, saying the light would guide the way through the endless darkness.
### LARGE +
The city of Ochkanatlan bears his name.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Ancient era: born as a dragon with a human soul; raised among human tribes; imprisoned by dragons; escapes.
- Xbalanque's war: joins the party; tricks Yupanqui into joining; deciphers dragon scripts; receives the sword.
- After Xbalanque's ascension: rules Ochkanatlan as Sacred Lord; the Scarlet-Eyed Youth overthrows him.
- After the Cataclysm: Bona frees his mind and names it Cocouik.
- 4.6: an unnamed king of the ineffable city in artifact text. 5.0: named in A Mysterious Visitor From Huitztlan. 5.2: Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm. 5.3: the Night Kingdom aid. 7.0: the Heretic's Molten Blade text.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- His name is a Yucatec Maya phrase for the boa constrictor and echoes the Vision Serpent.
- His other titles echo Maya court titles such as Yajaw K'ahk', Q'atal Tzij, Ti'sakhuun and Kalomte.

## entry: Cocouik
@keys: Cocouik, Coco
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The small dragonspirit who travels with Bona and, later, the Traveler is Och-Kan's separated mind. It cannot speak plainly until the final battle, when it merges with the body and becomes Och-Kan again.

## entry: Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash
@keys: Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, evil dragon, fell dragon, Tainted One
@keys2: Och-Kan, Ochkanatlan
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Och-Kan's body after the Abyss claimed it. It attacks the party repeatedly and steals the Jade of Return (really a Golden Entreaty, which Och-Kan had hidden on the Tonatiuh) just as they find it, until Cocouik strikes it down and merges with it.

## entry: Heretic's Molten Blade
@keys: Heretic's Molten Blade
@keys2: Och-Kan, Xbalanque
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The sword Xbalanque had made for him from dragon materials, after a campfire argument over the best weapon. Och-Kan swore to hunt heretics with it for a realm of humans. The Scarlet-Eyed Youth later took it from him.

@keys2: Och-Kan
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Och-Kan said the Entreaty "is as important as it is dangerous". He claimed to have hidden it from Kukulkan, kept it on the ship, and in the end entrusted it to Little One, saying it must go to the sacred mountain.
