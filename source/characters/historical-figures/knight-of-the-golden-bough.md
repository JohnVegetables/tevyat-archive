## summary
### LITE
The Knight of the Golden Bough, named Gunnhildr, was the prophesied hero and last hope of Arcadia. After the nation fell the knight went north to Snezhnaya for the Golden Bough, found it ruined, and led Arcadian survivors south instead.
### STANDARD +
The wiki calls the knight the namesake of Mondstadt's Gunnhildr Clan, a claim that sits uneasily beside the older Biography of Gunnhildr.
### LARGE +
All of this comes from the 6.5 weapon text Golden Frostbound Oath and the Pillar Hall area, not from any quest featuring the knight.

## background
### LITE
A knight of Arcadia, a paradise whose people believed its glory would last forever. When calamity buried the capital in winter, a prophecy named the knight as hero: only a pure, untainted Golden Bough could revive the withered wood and keep the sealed paradise alive.
### STANDARD +
The wiki's account: the knight wandered the frozen north and found a sacred grove with an Irminsul tree that had survived, but no priests tended it. A self-proclaimed king of the Jack Frost fae guarded it, setting trials and giving answers that were not answers. The knight beat the Jack Frost King in a duel by striking the crown from its head. The Golden Bough, already stripped of its blessings by the spread of Holy Soil, was a withered remnant. The knight left a longsword beneath the tree and turned south.
### LARGE +
In later years the knight gathered Arcadians willing to leave their ruined capital and led them to the high tower where the wicked dragon had been cast down, seeking shelter under Decarabian. The weapon text adds that the Jack Frost King, unaware of the loss, handed a hollow Golden Bough to the Traveler at the end, and hopes that whoever set it there will find a way home. The wiki also says the knight lost a beloved in Arcadia's destruction.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; the knight's exact fate is unknown, and the lineage is said to continue as the Gunnhildr Clan.
### LARGE +
As of 7.1 there is no further information.
### ULTRA +
The knight appears only through item and area text, never in a quest as a living figure. The Golden Frostbound Oath text, the wiki page and the Gunnhildr Clan page agree on the outline; the wiki adds the beloved and the clan-origin claim.

## relationships
### STANDARD
The Jack Frost King, who tested and was defeated by the knight; Decarabian, under whose rule the survivors settled; and the Gunnhildr Clan, the knight's descendants.
### LARGE +
The knight and the clan priestess Gunnhildr share a name but canon does not explain their connection.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
The knight acted on prophecy and did not know the Bough was dead. A longsword left under the withered tree marks the abandoned quest.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not treat the knight as the priestess of the Biography of Gunnhildr, or as Frederica Gunnhildr.
- Do not give the knight a gender, age or death; sources do not.
- The duel with the Jack Frost King was not a killing; the king survived and kept guarding the grove.
- Do not assume the Golden Bough of Nemorensis was the knight's sword; that is only a wiki suggestion.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Arcadia falls; the prophecy names the knight.
- The knight crosses northern Snezhnaya, endures the Jack Frost King's trials and wins the duel.
- Learns the Golden Bough is spent; leaves the sword beneath the tree.
- Returns south, gathers Arcadian survivors and seeks shelter under Decarabian.
- Descendants continue the Gunnhildr Clan (the wiki's account).
- 6.5: the story is revealed in Golden Frostbound Oath.
