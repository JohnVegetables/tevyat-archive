## summary
### LITE
She was the last Holy Sovereign of the valley tribe in the Tezcatepetonco Range that would later be called Mictlan. Chosen as a child for her star-bright soul, blinded by smoke and lamed at the ankles, she bore a role that was in effect a sacrifice. She was the mistress of Dinga, who became the dragon slayer Maghan, and she became her people's Wayob after he killed her at her own request.
### STANDARD +
Canon gives her no personal name; she is known only through the text of two weapons, the Flute of Ezpitzal and Starcaller's Watch (named for the statuette she gave Dinga). Her last wishes tied Mictlan to the land of Natlan and set the course of Maghan's life.

## background
### LITE
She lived near the end of Xiuhcoatl's reign. Her valley village was protected by the dragon Ix Kemonel Jasjatem Oox, the Weaver of Secrets, in return for a Holy Sovereign who would guide the dragons into a shared dream. Chosen as a child, she lived with a flute and a silent attendant, Dinga.
### STANDARD +
The office was cruel by design. A candidate needed a soul bright as a star, approved by the Lord of the Night's messenger. She was crowned with eagle feathers, her eyes dyed with burning smoke and her ankles cut with a flint blade so that she could not leave. Item text implies earlier Holy Sovereigns had gone the same way.

Kukulkan, posing as a sage, despised the dragons' retreat into dreams. He planned to turn the young Sovereign against them, stoking her suffering with talk of hope and sending Dinga far away so that war would fall on the valley. He took her to look into the Night Kingdom, expecting her to see nothing in the mist and smoke.
### LARGE +
The Weaver of Secrets had foreseen this and guided her through. In the Night Kingdom the girl received visions and instructions. She told Dinga that he would become a hero and a fated Sovereign who would replace the old pact with a new one bringing true peace; that his descendants would live without fear; that a city of jade would be built and a master of flames would watch over humans and dragons.

She told him not to trust Kukulkan, not to depend on the Lord of the Night or the benevolence of the saints, and that humanity must in the end rely on itself. She charged him to follow Xbalanque, the first rising dawn, to spread the stories of heroes among the tribes and record their memories on woven scrolls, so that the people would be anchored to the land later named Natlan. She gave him a statuette, which became Mictlan's sacred relic Starcaller's Watch.
### ULTRA +
She also told him that when they met again he should pierce her heart, so that her life would help stabilise the boundary and her name be taken by the beacon fires. Flute of Ezpitzal text has her guide him through the mist and mirrors with flutesong before she embraced him and spoke these wishes, addressing him by his old name, Dinga, as dragon slayer and fated Sovereign Maghan.

Some years later, when Xiuhcoatl had fallen and Cinder City was founded, Och-Kan declared the valley's people traitors for having worked with dragons. Maghan led the attack, shattered the labyrinth and fortress, and killed everyone inside, including her. Starcaller's Watch says he used the Python King's golden dagger. Afterwards he retired and built Mictlan. Kukulkan's plan had misfired: the betrayal he had seeded came with the song of the newborn Wayob from the depths of the Night Realm.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; she became Mictlan's Wayob, and her statuette survives as the relic Starcaller's Watch. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 appearance was found.
### STANDARD +
Maghan's successor as priest, Wagadu, is said to have renewed her pact with the Weaver of Secrets, giving rise to the first iktomisaurus (Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow text). In the current era she is known only from item text and tribal memory.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her bond with Maghan (Dinga) is the centre of her story: he was her only companion, and her last commands to him, to remember the heroes, to build a new peace, to take her life, shaped his later career. She was close to the Weaver of Secrets in the Night Kingdom, and wary of Kukulkan and the Lord of the Night. No meeting with Och-Kan is described, but his order ended the valley.
### LARGE +
Wiki trivia notes parallels with Och-Kan, who also bears the title Holy Sovereign, and speculates on a link; this is not stated in canon.
