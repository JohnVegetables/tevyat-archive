## summary
### LITE
Sohreh was a Dastur of the Sumeru Akademiya's Amurta Darshan. Roughly four hundred years ago she joined a field research team to Ardravi Valley, partnered with the Trainee Dastur Zandik, and died during the expedition. She is known only through notes and records left in the rainforest.
### STANDARD +
Her own note is cheerful: she praises the area's biodiversity, names a micro-scarab species the "Nahidad Scarab" ("the Dendro Archon's Boon"), complains that her partner's rigid Darshan has nothing in common with Amurta, and then records a pleasant evening of talk and a planned picnic. Later records describe her injuries and death.
### LARGE +
She matters mostly as an early marker of the Zandik of these notes, the man the wiki identifies as the future Dottore: after her death he was suspected of murdering her among other misconduct, and then vanished.

## background
### LITE
An Amurta scholar of the Akademiya, assigned to survey the jungle of Ardravi Valley and Devantaka Mountain with a small investigation team.
### STANDARD +
The team found Khaenri'ahn-era machinery, a Ruin Golem and related guards. Zandik secretly entered the golem and resolved to keep what he found from the rest of the team. Sohreh, meanwhile, catalogued wildlife and discovered the new micro-scarab species.
### LARGE +
According to the record left near the Damaged Ruin Guard, Zandik had acted without authorisation for a third time while Sohreh lay badly injured by Rishboland Tigers. The team was then attacked by the guard, and she died. She was buried in an unrecorded place and later exhumed by unnamed Akademiya members.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about four hundred years. Her cause of death was never settled in the records that survive.
### STANDARD +
The autopsy notes list lacerations, bruising and a fractured hyoid bone, with mechanical asphyxia as the stated mechanism, yet the investigators could not determine what killed her. Zandik was later suspected of murdering her, and disappeared after that.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Zandik (later Dottore, per the wiki): her expedition partner. She found him young, handsome and too rigid; they clashed in method but enjoyed one long conversation about evolution.
- Amurta: her Darshan, with a life-sciences outlook that clashed with Zandik's.
### LARGE +
- The wiki connects Zandik's later behaviour in the Akademiya, including research on Eleazar, to the misconduct list that mentions her death. Canon does not narrate her death on screen.

## mistakes
### LARGE
- Do not state as fact that Zandik murdered her; the records only record suspicion.
- Do not give her a personality beyond what her note shows, a warm, curious field researcher; no voice lines exist.
- Do not make her a living NPC or place her after the 400-year mark.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Some time after the cataclysm 500 years ago and before about 400 years ago: the Akademiya investigation team surveys Ardravi Valley.
- During the expedition: Sohreh's note, the picnic plan, Rishboland Tiger injuries, the Damaged Ruin Guard attack and her death.
- Afterwards: exhumation and autopsy, Zandik suspected, Zandik disappears.
- 3.0: the notes appear in Sumeru as open-world interactables.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- The wiki suggests her name may derive from the Persian feminine name Shohreh, meaning "famous". This is an etymology guess, not canon.
- Sohreh's Note is found in the cave behind the Ruin Golem; the Ragged Attendance Record lies beside the Damaged Ruin Guard south of Vimara Village.

## entry: Sohreh's Note
@keys: Sohreh's Note, Nahidad Scarab, Nahidad Scarabs
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A moss-covered page from a research log, torn out and left in the cave behind the Ruin Golem in Ardravi Valley. In it Sohreh admires the jungle's biodiversity, mentions that the Withering was already touching the area without yet threatening it, and names new micro-scarabs "Nahidad Scarabs". She grumbles that Zandik's rigid Darshan shares nothing with Amurta, then notes a good discussion on plant and animal evolution and a picnic planned for that night.

## entry: The death of Sohreh

@keys2: Zandik, Dottore, Ardravi Valley, Ruin Guard
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Records found around Sumeru piece together her end: Zandik's repeated unauthorised actions, her injuries from tigers, an attack by a Damaged Ruin Guard, her burial, and later an autopsy that found a fractured hyoid bone and "mechanical asphyxia" without determining a cause. Zandik was suspected but nothing is proven.
