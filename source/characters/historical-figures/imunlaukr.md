## summary
### LITE
A warrior from outside Sal Vindagnyr who loved the mountain city's princess. He left to find an Irminsul branch to save it, returned to find everyone dead, and wandered into war. His descendants became the Imunlaukr Clan.
### STANDARD +
His story is told in weapon descriptions and in the murals of Dragonspine, not in a quest. It is a tragedy about arriving too late.

## background
### LITE
Called an outlander by Sal Vindagnyr's people, Imunlaukr settled in the city and grew close to the priest Varuch's daughter. When the Skyfrost Nail fell and buried the green land in snow, he set out to find a branch of the Irminsul to save it.
### STANDARD +
The princess gave him the Starsilver greatsword to shatter the ice and snow and promised to paint his likeness as the fourth fresco and wait. He found no answer in time. When he returned, the city and its people, her included, were dead, and the great hall held only echoes. Despairing, he left the greatsword between the frescoes, spoke bitterly to the gods above and went down the mountain to find a war to lose himself in.
### LARGE +
The later weapon text says he abandoned his despair at the first moonfall after leaving, when he instinctively tried to save a maiden under attack, and hurled a lance into a dark tide of beasts. That blow, which songs call dolorous, is credited in legend with shattering mountains and leaving three kingdoms desolate; the maiden died too. Survivors hunted him as the enemy of their peoples. At that moment he stopped longing for salvation and turned from the warrior's life for good.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; his end is unrecorded. Only his descendants are attested.
### LARGE +
He left behind a lineage that became the Imunlaukr Clan, a clan of brave warriors who fought for the amusement of the gods. The Sacrificial Greatsword text says they eventually found something to fight for as Mondstadt took shape.
### ULTRA +
Nothing ties him to a specific Mondstadt-era event. The wiki suggests, as inference only, that black blood on his weapon hints at fights with Abyssal creatures, comparable to the Khaenri'ah expedition's description in the Brave Heart set. As of 7.1 no new information has surfaced in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His bond with the princess defines him: she gave the greatsword, he promised to return, and her last words never reached him. The clan descended from him treated battle as a duty to entertain the gods.

## entry: Imunlaukr Clan
@keys: Imunlaukr Clan
### STANDARD
One of the founding clans of Mondstadt. The Sacrificial Greatsword text describes it as brave, gifted warriors who fought monsters and outlaws without caring whether they returned, yet who found a purpose as the city formed.

## entry: Snow-Tombed Starsilver
@keys: Snow-Tombed Starsilver, fourth fresco
### STANDARD
The greatsword the princess gave him, left between the frescoes of the Mural Room in Dragonspine. The Hidden Exploration Objective Dragonspine's Glacial Secret unlocks the room.
