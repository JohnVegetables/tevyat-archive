## summary
### LITE
The Hydro Dragon Sovereign was the ruler of the Vishaps with sovereignty over water, one of the Seven Sovereigns. After dying in the war against the Heavenly Principles it was reborn as the human Neuvillette.
### STANDARD +
Hydro Vishap legend calls it the beating heart that ruled over the Primordial Sea.

## background
### LITE
One of seven dragon-lords who ruled the nations of the old world. With the other sovereigns it fought the Heavenly Principles and lost, then withered away and died. The Heavenly Principles took a portion of each sovereign's power and gave it to the archons.
### STANDARD +
Enkanomiya's records predicted that a new Hydro Dragon would be born in human form, because the dragon-lords need a pure vessel and the Bathysmal Vishaps, originally Hydro, had become impure by taking on Electro and Cryo. A character in The Last Day of Remuria adds that no new Hydro Dragon could be born while Egeria slept in the deep sea.
### LARGE +
A Fontainian legend recounted by Freminet says a Hydro Dragon once lived in Fontaine and that the sky rains when it weeps; children are told to shout, Hydro Dragon, don't cry. In 4.2 Focalors says the same words to Neuvillette, and rain falls when Neuvillette is deeply grieved.

### ULTRA +
The portion of power the Heavenly Principles took was bestowed on the archons, which is why Neuvillette needed the Hydro Archon's authority returned. The Wings of Merciful, Wrathful Waters text records the Hydro Vishap view of the dragon as the beating heart of the Primordial Sea.

## relationships
### LITE
Neuvillette (its reincarnation), Egeria (divine counterpart), the Heavenly Principles (enemy).
### STANDARD +
Focalors held its authority as Hydro Archon.

## status
### LITE
The original dragon is dead. As Neuvillette it lives again.
### STANDARD +
Before the events of The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur, Neuvillette lacked the old power and memories, including his link to the Primordial Sea. Needing the Archon's authority returned, he thought the solution unworkable, since it would require the Hydro Archon's death. Focalors executed herself so that he regained his full powers and memories, and he used them to make Fontainians fully human, preventing the prophecy's dissolution.
### LARGE +
After Furina abdicated, the duties of the Hydro Sovereign and the Iudex coexist in Neuvillette. He still gives out Visions in recognition of human will.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
Canon gives the original dragon no voice or recorded thoughts; information comes through legends, books and Neuvillette's recovered memories.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not treat the Hydro Dragon as a separate living being from Neuvillette; it is his previous incarnation.
- Do not claim Egeria and the dragon are the same figure; they are counterparts.
- Do not give it a name, gender or a death scene beyond canon.

### STANDARD
The dragon-lords who ruled the seven nations of the old world. The Hydro Dragon was one of them. All fought the Heavenly Principles and lost.

## entry: Hydro Dragon, don't cry
@keys: Hydro Dragon don't cry, weeps and it rains
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
A children's saying in Fontaine, learned by Freminet, that rain falls whenever the Hydro Dragon cries. It proved true: the sky rains when Neuvillette grieves.
