## summary
### LITE
Kamuna Harunosuke was an onmyouji of Inazuma from the Kamuna Clan who lived about five hundred years ago. With Asase Hibiki and Mikoshi Nagamasa he trained on Mt. Yougou under the tengu, and the three are remembered as the Yougou Three. After the cataclysm he went to Liyue to learn the adepti arts, later sealing the troublesome tanuki Ioroi in stone for the Shogunate.
### STANDARD +
He created the art of Onmyoudou by combining adepti arts with his own techniques, made the shikigami Shiki Taishou and owned the Mystic Onmyou Chamber domain. The Kamuna arts, paper seals that coat weapons with elemental energy even without a Vision, are used by the Kairagi today.

## background
### LITE
Harunosuke, Hibiki and Nagamasa studied under the Great Tengu Reizenbou of Mt. Yougou, living for a time in Araumi. In the cataclysm the Raiden Shogun's allies went to battle. The Kitsune Saiguu died, Takamine disappeared and Chiyo, Nagamasa's mother, was corrupted. Grieving, the three parted: Harunosuke for Liyue, Hibiki to Asase Shrine and Nagamasa to the Shogunate.
### STANDARD +
Some decades later he returned and, sent by the Shogunate, sealed Ioroi and many bake-danuki in stone statues as a lesson, though not all were sealed properly and some escaped. He also performed a minor Sacred Sakura Cleansing, ending with sacred words that included the Kitsune Saiguu's love of fried tofu.

## status
### LITE
Unknown. Wiki sources say he left for unknown parts after finishing his work. His domain, the Mystic Onmyou Chamber, and his shikigami remain.
### STANDARD +
Shiki Taishou eventually persuaded him to let go of his burdens, as the Shogun's own protection of Inazuma made the chamber's purpose pointless; he then left to seek a new purpose.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Hibiki and Nagamasa shared his training and parted with him in grief; Reizenbou taught them. Saiguu remembered him fondly as an easygoing young man. Ioroi, whom he sealed, calls him a "young human" and bears him a grudge.
### LARGE +
Shiki Taishou, his creation, believes he could never have been careless with Onmyoudou.
### ULTRA +
In A Tricky Situation the Traveler's party fuses Shiki Taishou with a mass-produced replica of himself and recovers a memory of Harunosuke. Childe speculates that Harunosuke might have been evil; Taishou rejects this and Xinyan sides with Taishou. This is speculation by a character, not canon fact.

### STANDARD
Through the Memento Lens, the Traveler sees a Mysterious Man (identified as Harunosuke) protecting a ward near the abandoned shrine northeast of Konda Village with sacred words beginning "In the name of Narukami's envoy". He left three shikigami, the Mysterious Shadows, to guard it. He met Hanachirusato, mistook her for Saiguu and chose not to purify her, since she posed no danger.

## entry: Mystic Onmyou Chamber

### STANDARD
Harunosuke owned this domain and created Shiki Taishou, who calls him the first onmyouji of Inazuma. Taishou does not remember his master's face or his reasons, but is sure he would not have been irresponsible.
