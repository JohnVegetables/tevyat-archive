## summary
### LITE
Egeria, the Mistress of Many Waters, was the first Hydro Archon of The Seven and Fontaine's god from about 2,000 to 500 years ago. She replaced the fallen God King Remus, united the surviving tribes and founded Fontaine's rule of law. Out of compassion she gave her Oceanid followers human bodies, a "sin" that brought Celestia's prophecy of drowning upon Fontaine. She died at Tunigi Hollow in the cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
Her body became the Amrita, a pool of pure dew in Sumeru's desert. Her chosen successor Focalors spent five centuries undoing the prophecy she left behind.
### LARGE +
She was not born a god of a nation. She was made as the new heart of the Primordial Sea after the Hydro Dragon was removed, then imprisoned in those depths for her sin, and freed to rule only when Remuria collapsed. Fontaine remembers her as gentle, merciful and just; her ancient enemies called her a usurper.
### ULTRA +
Most of what is known comes from secondhand testimony: Oceanids, artifact and weapon texts, Remurian survivors, a Frostmoon Scions hymnbook from Nod-Krai and Focalors's account in 4.2. She never speaks on screen in a quest. Players hear her voice only in the 3.7 overture teaser and see her only on the stone slates of the 4.2 Archon Quest.

## background
### LITE
Created to be the Primordial Sea's heart, she gave the Oceanids human form with that sea's water. Celestia called it a sin and sealed her away. When Remuria fell, Celestia sent her to rule Fontaine, and she became the Hydro Archon.
### STANDARD +
Origin: after the first Hydro Dragon was removed as the sea's heart, a Shade sent by Celestia made a new one. That heart was Egeria: a dragon's nobility without the dragon's form and a god's majesty without a divine duty, made wholly from Teyvat's own matter. Legend says she wept, and her first tear became the first Oceanid. She told the Oceanids to understand every kind of life and to love every creature.

The sin: some Oceanids envied human life. Egeria could not create life herself, so she poured Primordial Seawater into their blood vessels as human blood. Their descendants, the people of Fontaine, would dissolve back into water if the Primordial Sea touched them. Celestia judged this a theft of the sea's power, sealed her in the depths, and decreed the prophecy: the waters would rise, Fontaine's people would dissolve, and only the Hydro Archon would remain, weeping on her throne.

Her reign: after Remuria's destruction about 2,000 years ago she settled the tribes' feuds and had them build new cities around springs. She received a Gnosis and joined the first Seven, who gathered in Liyue after the Archon War.
### LARGE +
While she was imprisoned, the vishap prince Scylla guarded her. Remus, warned by the prophet Sybilla that his empire would fall, fought Scylla for thirty days and nights to reach her. Egeria told him the truth: Fontaine's doom came from her own unforgivable sin and could not be undone. He took a cup of her purest water and tried to cheat fate with Ichor and bronze bodies, and Remuria fell anyway. Above the waves, the Aremorican queen Erinnyes claimed to hear Egeria's voice, united the tribes in her name and fought Remuria. When Egeria was freed, Erinnyes begged her for a way out. Egeria told her what she had told Remus, and Erinnyes threw down her sword and walked out of history.

As ruler she exiled all convicted criminals. Some repented and prayed to her through the waters. She pitied them and set them to guard her secret beneath the waves, a sealed path to the Primordial Sea. That is the forgotten origin of the Fortress of Meropide. Knowing she could not oppose Celestia, she chose the Oceanid Focalors as her successor in the hope that Focalors could find a way to deceive the heavens.
### ULTRA +
Fontaine's titles for her include Noble Navigator, Ruler of Rivers and Seas, Queen of All Waters, Mother Goddess, Mistress of Rivers and Oceans, Sovereign of the Waters and Mother of Many Springs. A history of Remuria written in Fontaine's early years praises her as the queen who kept Fontaine from barbarism and annihilation and gave it laws. She also offered the defeated Remurians a place in her new nation if they gave up old grudges, and some, like the Harmost Cassiodor, joined her people. Erinnyes's warriors swore never to raise a sword against anyone who kept faith with Egeria, and their tribes came to treat all their old gods as aspects of her. One legend has her granting a guilt-stricken knight's wish for exile, judging that a fair verdict was the greatest leniency for a selfless soul; another has her giving the last Lochknight a lily as pure as lake-light, which is why Fontainians still give Lakelight Lilies to travellers.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Focalors: her Oceanid familiar, made human and chosen as her successor. Focalors called the inheritance a colossal mess but kept faith with it: "existence" was Egeria's justice, its "continuation" hers.
- The Oceanids: her first creations and most loyal devotees. After her death many refused Focalors, went looking for her in Sumeru, and scattered across Teyvat.
- The Hydro Dragon (later Neuvillette): she took its place as the sea's heart, and no new Hydro Dragon could be born while she slept in the deep.
- Remus and Scylla: the God King who sought her answer in vain, and the guardian of her prison.
- Greater Lord Rukkhadevata: the Archon who turned her remains into a living seal.
### LARGE +
- Naberius, the Shade who rules Life, created her; a Nod-Krai hymn (6.0) praises Naberius as the mother of waters who birthed a heart for the primordial ocean.
- Erinnyes fought in her name for decades and was broken by her answer.
- Furina, Focalors's human half, says she is not Egeria and does not know her predecessor's secrets.
- The Pari of the Vourukasha Oasis worship the god in the Harvisptokhm, that is, what remains of Egeria.
### ULTRA +
- Scylla, who had served the Hydro Dragon, called her the "Usurper of Many Waters" and returned to her side in the Primordial Sea after Remuria's end.
- Cassiodor, once a Harmost, served her people after the fall and founded the Marechaussee Hunters.
- Oceanids who left after her death include Rhodeia of Loch, the strongest, who refused Focalors and wanted only to keep the old dreams alive; Idyia, who fled because the waters turned bitter with pain and hatred; and Callirhoe, who settled in Springvale. The Oceanid Lyris's post as orphanage director was created in Egeria's honour.

## abilities
### STANDARD
As the Primordial Sea's heart and later Hydro Archon she held vast Hydro power and a Gnosis. She could shape Oceanids but could not create life outright; her human Fontainians were made by borrowing the sea's life-giving power, which Celestia had not allowed her to use. She heard prayers carried through the waters.
### LARGE +
Even dead, her remains have power: the Amrita purifies Abyssal defilement, especially when joined with Khvarena. Awakened in 3.6, her consciousness flooded the land with Amrita and cleared the Sign of Apaosha.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the cataclysm 500 years ago. Her body became the Amrita at Tunigi Hollow in Sumeru.
### STANDARD +
Corrupted by the Abyss and unable to be reborn in a purer plane, her divine consciousness was anchored in the Gaokerena lotus inside the Harvisptokhm, which Rukkhadevata grew from the Amrita to seal the rift. In the 3.6 quest Khvarena of Good and Evil, the Traveler, Paimon and the Pari Sorush purified her defiled consciousness and woke it, and the tree regrew.
### LARGE +
The prophecy she left was resolved in 4.2: Focalors's plan let Fontaine's people be judged, the Hydro Archon was sentenced, Focalors executed herself, and Neuvillette, the restored Hydro Dragon, absolved the people's sin so the Primordial Sea no longer dissolves them. Egeria's throne therefore no longer exists. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes her status.

## appearance
### LARGE
Canon gives almost no description. In 4.2 she is shown on ancient stone slates: one shows her turning Oceanids into people, another shows her kneeling before an island in the sky as if begging forgiveness. Her voice is heard in the 3.7 teaser "The Final Feast" (English voice: Courtney Steele).

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before Remuria: created as the Primordial Sea's new heart; the Oceanids are born from her tears. She gives some of them human form, is judged guilty by Celestia and is sealed in the deep.
- Remuria's age: Remus fights Scylla to reach her and learns the curse cannot be undone. Erinnyes unites the tribes in her name.
- About 2,000 years ago: Remuria falls. Celestia frees her to rule Fontaine. She becomes Hydro Archon, founds the lawful nation and attends the first Seven's gatherings in Liyue.
- During her reign: criminals in exile build Meropide to guard her secret. She chooses Focalors as successor.
- 500 years ago: slain at Tunigi Hollow. Her body becomes the Amrita, Rukkhadevata grows the Harvisptokhm, and Simurgh's sacrifice creates the Pari. Focalors becomes Hydro Archon, and many Oceanids leave Fontaine.
- Over 400 years ago: Rene de Petrichor finds that his and Jakob's constitutions resemble the Gaokerena's.
### ULTRA +
Release order: first mentioned as "the previous Hydro Archon" in Life Flows On (II) (1.4); Lord of Amrita and the Harvisptokhm (3.6); voice in the Final Feast teaser (3.7); named in Fontaine, with prophecy rumours and the glider lore (4.0); Meropide's secret (4.1); the stone slates and Focalors's account (4.2); Remus, Scylla and Os (4.6 Canticles of Harmony); Naberius named in the Hymns of the Far North (6.0).

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Her Chinese title for Lord of Amrita means "Master of Sweet Dew".
- Her name comes from the water nymph of Roman myth who counselled King Numa.
- Some scholars credit her with the Spring of the First Dewdrop, a pure Ichor in the Morte Region; the archive says there is no reliable evidence.
- Neuvillette believes Egeria wanted to keep Fontainians' true origin secret, which is why ancient law forbids forcibly changing a creature into a human (Sigewinne's story).

## entry: Egeria and the Oceanids
@keys: Oceanid, Oceanids, first tear, Rhodeia, Endora
@keys2: Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters, Hydro Archon
@version: 1.4
### STANDARD
Legend says the first Oceanid was born from Egeria's first tear, though one Fontaine researcher notes evidence that Oceanids are older than her. She told them to understand every kind of life and love every creature, and her desire to connect the world as all water is connected sent them across Teyvat's waters. Some longed to live on land, and she gave them human bodies. Fontaine's people descend from them, and Focalors was one of them.
### LARGE +
After her death, many Oceanids refused to recognise Focalors as Hydro Archon, and Fontaine's waters, they say, turned bitter with pain and hatred. They made a pilgrimage to the Harvisptokhm looking for her, found only her remains, and scattered: Rhodeia to a quiet lake in Liyue, Idyia to Sumeru's desert, Callirhoe to Springvale. Since her death most of Fontaine's waters can no longer naturally produce Oceanids.

## entry: The original sin and the Prophecy of Fontaine
@keys: original sin, Prophecy of Fontaine, weeping on her throne
@keys2: Egeria, Focalors, Hydro Archon, Fontaine
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Egeria made Fontainians by putting Primordial Seawater in Oceanids' veins. Celestia ruled that she had stolen the sea's power and decreed that the waters would rise, the people would dissolve, and only the Hydro Archon would remain, weeping on her throne. In 4.0 Neuvillette cites a rumour that the prophecy came from Egeria's last words. In 4.2 the stone slates and Focalors revealed the real cause.
### LARGE +
Focalors's answer: the Heavenly Principles had themselves stolen the dragons' power, so Egeria's theft was no worse. Focalors split into a divine self and a human Furina, staged 500 years of deception, had the Hydro Archon tried and condemned, and gave the authority back to the Hydro Dragon. Neuvillette then forgave the people's sins.

## entry: Remus in Egeria's prison
@keys: Remus, God King, Scylla
@keys2: Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters, prison, Primordial Sea
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
At Sybilla's suggestion, Remus went down into the Primordial Sea to ask the imprisoned Egeria how to escape the prophecy that Remuria would fall. Scylla, the vishap prince guarding her prison, fought him for thirty days and nights, then mocked his hope and let him pass. Egeria told him every terrible secret, including that her sin had made the curse irrevocable. He left with a cup of her purest water, resolving to seal his people's souls in insoluble Ichor and forge them bodies of bronze and stone.
### LARGE +
Scylla later reminded Remus that he had promised the "Usurper of Many Waters" he would use her water to build a world of endless prosperity without conflict, and told him he had failed. Monsieur Os (Cassiodor) said no new Hydro Dragon could be born while she slept in the deep, so Remuria's "fell dragon" was only an unusually strong vishap.

## entry: Erinnyes and the Lochknights
@keys: Erinnyes, Lochknight, Lochknights, Pure Grail, Aremorica
@keys2: Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters, Queen of All Waters
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Erinnyes of Aremorica claimed to hear Egeria's voice, united Fontaine's tribes in her name, and led warriors, later called Lochknights, against Remuria. They swore to protect the Oceanids and never to fight anyone devoted to Egeria. She hunted for the Pure Grail to free Egeria and wash away her people's sin but never found it. When Celestia freed Egeria after Remuria fell, Erinnyes begged her for a way to save her people. Egeria told her what she had told Remus. Erinnyes's faith collapsed; she dropped her sword and left the valley, never to be seen again.
### LARGE +
A rival version in the same weapon text says pure-hearted knights did win the Pure Grail and freed her, and that she granted one knight's plea for exile and blessed her sword before it sank into a lake.

## entry: Fontinalia Festival and Egeria
@keys: Fontinalia, Fontinalia Festival
@keys2: Egeria, Lochknight, Lochknights, Hydro Archon
@version: 4.3
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The Fontinalia Festival commemorates the legendary Lochknights, who searched for the Oceanids and in the end welcomed Egeria to Fontaine. Celebrants wore costumes, raised golden cups and went door to door asking for pure water. In recent years Furina turned the pure-water part into a call for candy. This custom is explained in the limited-time event Roses and Muskets (4.3).

## entry: Meropide and Egeria's secret
@keys: Fortress of Meropide, Meropide, forbidden zone
@keys2: Egeria, secret, Primordial
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Under Egeria every convicted criminal was exiled to the cold, barren seaside. Some repented and prayed to her; she took pity and told them to guard her secret beneath the waves. They built what became the Fortress of Meropide, whose real purpose was to seal a path to the Primordial Sea behind a sluice gate. The purpose was forgotten until Wriothesley learned it from an elderly historian (4.1).

## entry: Death at Tunigi Hollow and the Harvisptokhm
@keys: Tunigi Hollow, Harvisptokhm, Gaokerena, Amrita, Amrita Pool, Lord of Amrita
@keys2: Egeria, Hydro Archon, Rukkhadevata, Pari, Sorush, Simurgh
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
In the cataclysm several gods fought the Abyss pouring out of the rift at Tunigi Hollow. Egeria was slain, and her body became a sea of pure dew, the Amrita. Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from it to seal the rift, absorb the defilement and anchor Egeria's tainted consciousness in the Gaokerena lotus. When even the Amrita began to fail, the Khvarena bird Simurgh drank it and burst into countless Khvarena, the origin of the Pari, who worship the tree's god.
### LARGE +
Centuries later unpurified defilement raised the Sign of Apaosha over the tree. In Khvarena of Good and Evil (3.6) the Traveler, Paimon and Sorush, helped by Mihir and Rashnu, entered through the Rite of Chinvat, freed Spenta Hearts from the Defiled Chambers of her consciousness and bloomed the Flower of Sanctity. The awakened consciousness poured Amrita across the land, and the Harvisptokhm regrew. Sorush recalls their god saying, "May the eternal Khvarena guide us on the upward path."

## entry: Naberius and the heart of the sea
@keys: Naberius, Maadteraahka, heart of the Primordial Sea
@keys2: Egeria, Hydro Dragon, Primordial Sea
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Oceanid tradition, recorded in Fontaine's reputation glider text (4.0), says that after the Hydro Dragon was removed as the Primordial Sea's heart, Celestia's envoy who was charged with creating life made Egeria as a new heart, wholly from Teyvat's matter. Humanity's longing for a god, the text muses, is why she was then given a shard of the "one who was first" and a divine duty late in life. A Frostmoon Scions hymnbook found in Nod-Krai (6.0) names that envoy: Maadteraahka, that is, Naberius, the mother of waters who birthed a heart for the primordial ocean.
