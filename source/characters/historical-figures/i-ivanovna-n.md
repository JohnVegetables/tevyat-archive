## summary
### LITE
A human politician of Snezhnaya who lived centuries ago: Hexenzirkel member J (the Tricksy-Witch) and the first Speaker of the Snezhnograd Veche, appointed by the Tsaritsa. She left the witches to marry and mentored the future Harbinger Pulcinella.
### STANDARD +
She had no magic or combat training and called herself and Andersdotter simply ordinary; her power was political. The line of successors she left behind lasted into the Traveler's time.
### LARGE +
She is never seen alive in play: she appears as a voice and figure in a cutscene of the 3.5 Windblume event and is otherwise known from item texts, a fortune slip and other characters' recollections.

## background
### LITE
A Hexenzirkel member under the codename J. A few years into the Tsaritsa's reign she became first chairperson of the new Snezhnograd Veche and Mayor.
### STANDARD +
The Veche was founded in the sixth year of the 799th Cycle, per a book. While in office she asked Alice for help with various small matters in Snezhnaya; in exchange Alice asked her to write a passage in a fortune slip meant for Andersdotter. The passage is dry and unsentimental: she contrasts herself with the sentimental Barbeloth, says she does not believe in a world after death, and says she writes to herself alone. She recalls that Alice once dragged her through waterways, blowing up furnaces and catching a cat inside a Domain.

When the fae Mirko Radovidovich Veleshek applied to the Fatui, he was told to report to the Mayor and became her pupil. Outsiders read the arrangement as the Tsaritsa warning the old fae order by making a fae answer to a human. Artifact text calls Ivanovna exceptionally skilled at weaving conspiracies while erasing every trace of her own part.
### LARGE +
She fell in love and chose to leave the Hexenzirkel when she married. The day before the wedding, the witches sent her a group letter phrased as a prophecy, really a request to see them again; its four descriptions stand for Rhinedottir, Nicole, Alice and M (Andersdotter). At a tea party she told the others that, the day before, she had ended the life of her husband, who had grown old and gravely ill, because he loved her and she wanted to end his pain. Her husband is never named.

She is credited as author of Volume 4 of The Little Witch and the Undying Fire, a volume on diplomacy and politics whose author says she has no knowledge of magic.

## status
### LITE
Dead; she passed away centuries ago. Her offices passed to Pulcinella, who is Speaker of the Veche in the present.
### STANDARD +
Scarlet Proof artifact text mentions Pulcinella thinking about his own successor after her death, and an old noble of the Veche, Golitsyn, says his house owes its standing to the grace of Lady Ioanna Ivanovna. Her line of successors continues; the latest, Scarlett, tailed the Traveler to the floating island and lit the Lantern of Utmost Joy in the 3.5 Windblume event.
### LARGE +
Later material adds detail without revising her story: Nicole recalls her political machinations among the tea-party stories in a Nod-Krai quest; Durin's Story Quest (6.2) shows Alice calling her the tricksy-witch who held nearly all the snowy lands with none but the Tsar above her, too busy now to join their gatherings; Durin's character story names her author of the fourth volume of the witches' shared storybook. A Lunar Arcanum card in the Imaginarium Theater (The Lovers) features her.

## relationships
### LITE
Hexenzirkel friends (Alice above all); Pulcinella, her pupil; the Tsaritsa, who appointed her; Scarlett, her latest successor.
### STANDARD +
Alice is the closest tie in canon: she traded favours with Ivanovna and joined the letter sent to her. Pulcinella learned politics under her, struggling at first to keep up; the artifact text credits humans generally with teaching him loyalty and the sowing of discord, strategy, and how to conceal a foul temper. He later watched the young Tartaglia grow the way she had watched him.
### LARGE +
Alice acknowledged none of Ivanovna's successors until Scarlett.
### ULTRA +
The other witches wrote the pre-wedding letter together as their customary invitation to a final get-together before a member left to live with her beloved. Alice later wanted Scarlett to tell her all about J's married life in her hometown. Nicole recalls Ivanovna's political machinations among the stories she heard at the first tea party she attended, which she only observed.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Date not given: she becomes a Hexenzirkel member with the codename J. A storybook scene suggests she was already powerful in Snezhnaya under a Tsar (see uncertainties).
- Sixth year of the 799th Cycle, a few years into the Tsaritsa's reign: she is named first chairperson of the Snezhnograd Veche.
- Her tenure: she trades favours with Alice and writes her fortune-slip passage; Mirko becomes her pupil on joining the Fatui.
- Before her wedding (order relative to her mayoralty unknown): the group letter ("prophecy") is written to her; she leaves the Hexenzirkel.
- Late in her husband's life: she ends his life and relates it at the tea party the next day.
- Centuries ago: she dies; Pulcinella succeeds her.
- 3.5 (March 2023): Windblume's Breath reveals her through the letter and the tea-party cinematic.

## entry: Hexenzirkel letter (the Windblume prophecy)
@keys: Windblume prophecy, Lantern of Utmost Joy, Hexenzirkel letter
@keys2: Ivanovna, J
@version: 3.5
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
During Windblume's Breath (3.5, an event), Collei and Sucrose investigated a prophecy found in Mondstadt, built on four descriptions (a flower not of this world, a guide who will never get lost, one who would never lie, a legend that never ends) and pointing to the Lantern of Utmost Joy. Scarlett, a successor of J, admitted slipping it into a storybook. Alice explained that it was a letter the witches sent to J, Ivanovna, the day before her wedding, asking her to come and see them again; the four descriptions stand for Rhinedottir, Nicole, Alice and M (Andersdotter).

## entry: Pulcinella as her pupil
@keys: Pulcinella, Mirko Radovidovich Veleshek, The Rooster
@keys2: Ivanovna, mayor

### STANDARD
The Domovoy Mirko applied to the Fatui early in the Tsaritsa's reign, after the Cataclysm, and was told to report to the Mayor, Ivanovna. He learned politics from her, struggling at first to keep her pace, and succeeded to her offices as the Harbinger Pulcinella.

## entry: Snezhnograd Veche founding
@keys: Snezhnograd Veche
@keys2: Ivanovna, first chairperson
### STANDARD
Created by the Tsaritsa in the sixth year of the 799th Cycle to handle administration after the fae princes withdrew. Ivanovna was its first chairperson and Speaker. See the Veche's own entry for its present state.
