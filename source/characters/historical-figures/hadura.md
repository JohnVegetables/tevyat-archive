## summary
### LITE
A Knight Commander of the Schwanenritter in Khaenri'ah's last days, titled "Sentinel of the Golden Hall" for a successful operation against the Abyssal beasts. Later Hadura betrayed the order and was executed by Knight Marshal Anfortas.
### STANDARD +
Hadura is known only through orders and chronicles left in Sumeru's ruins. Nobody speaks for Hadura and no motive is given, so the reason for the betrayal is open.
### ULTRA +
The records make the same person both a celebrated defender and a traitor, and the later Abyss Herald Klingsor leaned on the first half of that legacy.

## background
### LITE
Hadura was a Khaenri'ahn knight of the Schwanenritter during the cataclysm. As a Garrison Commander, Hadura ordered a proving ground's self-defence systems activated so the evacuation of citizens to the surface could continue; as a Knight Commander, Hadura led an operation that stalled the dark beasts and was conferred the title "Sentinel of the Golden Hall".
### STANDARD +
The Schwanenritter used Mechanical Wardens (Ruin Golems) to hold the beasts back and led survivors out through the Girdle of the Sands toward Dharma Forest. The wiki places the successful operation around the Valley of Dahri and says Anfortas bestowed the title. Canon does not say which of Garrison Commander and Knight Commander was the higher rank.
### LARGE +
By the time the last golem reached Devantaka Mountain, Hildric had died of multiple-organ failure and Ynghildr had gone missing in battle, leaving only Hadura and Anfortas. Nagarjuna's Akademiya Darshan fought alongside the knights and called them heroes of Dahri; whether Hadura was among them is only a guess.

## status
### LITE
Dead, executed by Anfortas roughly five hundred years before the main story, and still given a knight's funeral because Hadura was the last of the other knights.
### STANDARD +
Nothing about Hadura appears in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content in the pages read. Hadura lives on through the chronicle in the Devantaka Ruin Golem and through the descent claimed by Klingsor.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Anfortas, the Knight Marshal who honoured Hadura and then killed Hadura for treachery, losing his left eye in the fight. The Schwanenritter, whom Hadura served and betrayed. Klingsor, a descendant.
### LARGE +
Klingsor, a ranger of the Order of Skeptics hundreds of years ago, prized the descent. Resenting how Khaenri'ahns were treated for the cataclysm, he pointed to Hadura's efforts and came to doubt the Order's origin story and suspect that Nagarjuna had hidden the truth. Hadura's relatives presumably followed Nagarjuna to the Girdle of the Sands and joined the Order of Skeptics; this is inferred.

## chronology
### LARGE
1. The cataclysm: as Garrison Commander, Hadura orders self-defence systems at a proving ground activated to cover the evacuation of citizens.
2. As Knight Commander, Hadura leads an operation that stalls the dark beasts and receives the title "Sentinel of the Golden Hall".
3. The knights cross to Sumeru; Hildric dies and Ynghildr goes missing, leaving only Hadura and Anfortas.
4. Hadura betrays the order and destroys a machine beyond repair. Anfortas executes Hadura, loses his left eye, and still grants a knight's funeral.
5. Centuries later Klingsor, a descendant, becomes an Abyss Herald, setting off the events of the Khvarena of Good and Evil quest.
### ULTRA +
The order of events 1 and 2 follows the wiki's reading; the two documents themselves are undated.

In the Ruin Golem on Devantaka Mountain, the chronicle for Hadura names the Schwanenritter and the title "Sentinel of the Golden Hall", records execution by the Knight Marshal for betrayal that caused irreversible damage to a machine, notes that the Knight Marshal lost his left eye, and says a proper knight's funeral was granted because Hadura was the last of the compatriots.

## entry: Klingsor and Hadura
@keys: Klingsor, Hadura's descendant
@keys2: Hadura
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Klingsor, an Order of Skeptics ranger who called Hadura his ancestor, resented the Khaenri'ahns' reputation after the cataclysm and cited Hadura's efforts against the monsters. This made him doubt the Order's origin stories. He later joined an Akademiya expedition to Khaenri'ahn ruins, embraced the Abyss, and became an Abyss Herald.
