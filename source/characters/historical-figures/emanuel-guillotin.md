## summary
### LITE
Emanuel Guillotin was a Fontainian hunter of the Marechaussee Phantom about five centuries ago. After directing the bloody Siege of Poisson he retired, scarred and drinking, until a childhood friend left two children in his care.
### STANDARD +
He is remembered through the Marechaussee Hunter artifact set. He was the first person dissolved by the Narzissenkreuz.

## background
### LITE
He grew up at the Narzissenkreuz Institute with Basil Elton, Dwight Lasker and Karl Ingold, joined the Marechaussee Phantom as an adult, and led its forces in the Siege of Poisson. The bloodshed marked him in body and mind and he retired.
### STANDARD +
As a boy he played the hero in games where Karl was the dragon Jabberwock. He was said to have been lighthearted and carefree until Poisson. Afterwards he hid his scarred face behind a mask and sank into drink. When Basil expected the Institute to be threatened, she asked him to guard Alain and Mary-Ann; he agreed reluctantly although he had no love for children.
### LARGE +
After Basil died he introduced Alain to Dwight Lasker, director of the Institute of Natural Philosophy, hoping for support, but Mary-Ann refused it. Dwight then asked him to investigate the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and its leader, the Narzissenkreuz; he accepted to protect the two children. He was dissolved by the Master, and Marfisa, who had been following him, was dissolved second.
### ULTRA +
In his last moments he recalled the Institute games, the Director's embrace, the faces twisted by hatred in the flames of Poisson, and the realisation that he had also sent innocent children to the Institute. He regretted that he never became a proper father to the siblings but wished to keep the memories of watching them grow. The artifact texts describe all of it dissolving into the waters, matching the fate of the Narzissenkreuz's victims.

## relationships
### LITE
Basil Elton (childhood friend who entrusted him with her wards), Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin (his adoptive children).
### STANDARD +
Dwight Lasker and Karl Ingold were fellow Institute childhood friends. Marfisa, once defeated by him in a duel, hunted him for years.

## status
### LITE
Dead. Dissolved and absorbed by the Narzissenkreuz about 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
Nothing is known of him after that. Alain and Mary-Ann's own stories appear in the Marechaussee Hunter and related content.

## abilities
### STANDARD
A highly skilled swordsman. Rightful Reward says his slow, deceptive rhythm beat Marfisa, then the Champion Duelist, in a duel in her youth. He was an effective commander at Poisson.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Youth: Institute games with Basil, Dwight and Karl.
- Adulthood: joins the Marechaussee Phantom, directs the Siege of Poisson, retires.
- Later: becomes guardian of Alain and Mary-Ann at Basil's request.
- After Basil's death: investigates the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and is dissolved.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not a villain; the sources treat him as a tired, remorseful veteran.
- Do not give him a romance, a first-hand voice-over or a named sword.
- Not the guillotine inventor; the surname only echoes a historical French name.
- He is not alive in the Traveler's era.

## entry: Siege of Poisson
@keys: Siege of Poisson, Poisson incident
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The event that broke him. He directed the Marechaussee Phantom's action in Poisson; an Institute record says it potentially led to the deaths of Rene de Petrichor's and Jakob Ingold's parents. He afterwards left the Marechaussee.
