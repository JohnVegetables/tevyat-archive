## summary
### LITE
Yun Hui was the seventh master of the Yun Family, a Liyue house renowned for forging weapons. After the Cataclysm he and the smith Han Wu designed the improved Prototype weapons, and he later left the forge to his daughter Yun Huang when he joined the Liyue Qixing.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through weapon descriptions: the Prototype series, Prototype Amber (the catalyst he named), and the Blackcliff weapons in which his daughter and the Han family appear.

## background
### LITE
Hui lived through the Cataclysm five hundred years ago. Afterwards monsters still roamed Teyvat, and people turned to supernatural arts to fight them, but the weapon-making methods of the time were old and the weapons broke easily. Hui and Han Wu, another famous weaponsmith, combined their knowledge to create the Prototype weapons.
### STANDARD +
Thaumaturges then came to Liyue and begged Hui to design a new catalyst. He added one to the series, a wood-and-ore disc with a piece of Cor Purum given by the thaumaturges at its centre. Its warm golden glow reminded him of amber, so after consulting them he named it Prototype Amber, and later Liyue catalysts followed its pattern. Later he helped Han Wu form a crew to dig for crystallized blackcliff in the Chasm; a cave-in trapped them, and Han Wu came out changed.
### LARGE +
The Yun Family wiki summary says the Prototype set began as a longsword, a greatsword and a polearm. The polearm was dedicated to Hui's father because its tip glowed like a star, and a bow was added at Hui's request.

## relationships
### LITE
Han Wu was his friend and partner in the Prototype series. His only child, Yun Huang, took over the Yun forge. He became a Qixing member and left the family business.
### STANDARD +
The Yun family custom was that a daughter's husband would become head; Huang, raised by a martial artist, chose to take the role herself, and the weapon text notes that this started gossip.

## status
### LITE
Long dead. Nothing describes his death; his work survives as the Prototype weapons and the Yun Family's later history.
### STANDARD +
Over the centuries the Yun Family drifted from weaponsmithing towards the performing arts, and by Yun Jin's generation few members still forged.

## chronology
### LARGE
- After the Cataclysm: with Han Wu designs the Prototype series.
- Later: names Prototype Amber for the thaumaturges.
- Later: helps Han Wu arrange the Chasm crew; the cave-in follows.
- At some point: joins the Qixing and passes the Yun forge to Huang.
### ULTRA +
The order of these events is not dated in the sources; the weapon descriptions give only that the Prototype series and Prototype Amber came first, the Chasm cave-in later, and that Huang already led the forge when earthquakes closed the mines. Hui does not appear in any quest, voice-over or later story as of 7.1, and a wiki search found no Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content that mentions him.
