## summary
### LITE
Raiden Makoto, Ei's elder twin, was the first Electro Archon and the original Raiden Shogun. She ruled Inazuma for about 1,500 years, with Ei secretly serving as her body double, until she died in Khaenri'ah during the cataclysm 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
The world saw the twins as one god, Baal. Her death drove Ei into the frozen "Eternity" of the Shogun puppet; her lingering will freed Ei from it in 2.5.
### LARGE +
Few Inazumans know she existed. Yae Miko told the Traveler the truth in 2.1, and Ei told the rest in her 2.5 Story Quest: Makoto's preserved realm of consciousness, a fragment of her will sealed in the sword Musou Isshin, and the seed of the Sacred Sakura. That tree, by canon's own paradox, was planted in the present and took root in Inazuma's past.
### ULTRA +
She is seen only in memories and cinematics: the Story Teaser "Promise of a People's Dream" in the 2.1 Archon Quest, the light from Musou Isshin in Radiant Sakura (2.5), and Mikoshi Chiyo's dream in a limited 5.4 event. She speaks on screen only once, in her farewell to Ei. Everything else about her comes from Ei, Yae, the Shogun, a blind tea master's memory and an old novel.

## personality
### LITE
Gentle, no fighter, devoted to the present moment and to her people's dreams.
### STANDARD +
She loved Inazuma's scenery, food and stories and told Ei all about them. Both twins understood erosion, but Makoto said a scene should be enjoyed all the more because it is "but a fleeting shadow". Ei says she was not concerned with outcomes.
### LARGE +
Her view of Eternity was Ei's opposite. To her, the most precious things were dreams, the yearning of living beings for a better future. A single ambition ends and is replaced, but the drive that keeps producing new ones is innate and therefore eternal. Ei once thought this naive. In 2.5 she called it simple on the surface but more mature and far-sighted than her own. Makoto's gaze was always on her people, and Ei came to see it as joy and hope, not just duty.
### ULTRA +
The blind tea master Furuyama called her "quite the romantic at heart" and "a true dreamer". Over tea she described Inazuma's most beautiful places to him so vividly that he felt he had travelled there, and taught him to live without regrets. She was also a strategist: she foresaw how Inazuma's future might unfold, even allowing for Ei's stubbornness, and set up a plan that took 500 years to finish. She felt indebted to Ei for protecting her, apologised for leaving so suddenly, and was openly happy at the end.

## appearance
### LARGE
Ei's identical twin, so she looks almost exactly like her: fair skin, purple eyes and long dark-violet hair that lightens at the ends. In cinematics her hair is braided only halfway, tied with a pink cord in a bow, with the rest loose.
### ULTRA +
She wore a light lavender short kimono with a crimson obi tied in a bow at the back, close to the Raiden Shogun's own outfit, plus white socks and black platform sandals. She carried a purple wagasa umbrella hung with wisteria. Her hair ornament, a kanzashi with a large purple flower and lavender wisteria, now belongs to Ei, according to the wiki. She seems to lack Ei's beauty mark, though this may only be the art style. Canon gives no height, age or birthday. In Radiant Sakura she does not take bodily form; she is a light and a voice rising from Musou Isshin, and Ei says she only heard her and would not call it seeing her.

## background
### LITE
The twins won the Archon War together. Ei, the warrior, judged Makoto the better ruler; Makoto became Archon with Ei as her kagemusha. In the cataclysm Makoto went to Khaenri'ah without telling Ei, who arrived only to hear her die.
### STANDARD +
The novel Treasured Tales, which Ei calls largely accurate here, says Ei gave up her body so Makoto could ascend, and Makoto rebuilt it. Makoto handled the realm; Ei handled warfare, including killing Watatsumi's invading god Orobashi in her name. Makoto attended the first Seven's gatherings in Liyue. Dying, she passed Musou Isshin to Ei, and Ei saved her collapsing realm of consciousness.
### LARGE +
Five hundred years ago black fog and monsters poured over Inazuma, with rifthounds as their advance guard. Makoto sensed that Khaenri'ah "was not somewhere that any archon could afford to ignore" and went alone. Ei says Makoto, no martial artist, had always let Ei face such crises in her place; this time she went first, perhaps to put herself in front so Ei stayed hidden. Ei followed, leaving the Kitsune Saiguu to defend Inazuma. She came only in time to hear her sister's last breath. Makoto was already unconscious, so Ei entered her mind for a last farewell. Before that realm of consciousness collapsed, Ei saved it and brought it home. Makoto's sword, Musou Isshin, passed into Ei's bloodied hands, and the kagemusha became the true Raiden Shogun.
### ULTRA +
Back in Inazuma, Ei found a great sakura on Mt. Yougou that everyone insisted had always been there. She could only call it a miracle of Makoto's consciousness. Its power let her finally defeat the monsters. Her grief and her fear of erosion became her resolve to pursue Eternity. She sealed the torii-gate entrance to Makoto's realm beneath the Grand Narukami Shrine, built the Shogun puppet and withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia. Earlier, peaceful days: Makoto, Ei and their friends (the Kitsune Saiguu, Mikoshi Chiyo, the tengu) played karuta under the sakura trees; Makoto enjoyed the hagoita game Akitsu Hazura (Haneasobi) and once attended the youkai's Mikawa Flower Festival with Ei (as Ei recalled during a limited-time event). The first Seven met in Liyue to drink with Rex Lapis. Of them only Barbatos still holds his throne; Makoto was one of four Archons lost in the cataclysm, alongside Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, Egeria and the Belyi Tsar.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Ei (Beelzebul) was her twin, shield and successor; Makoto never saw her as a mere weapon, and losing Makoto was Ei's greatest loss. Yae Miko knew her a little and remembers her as gentle. The Kitsune Saiguu and Mikoshi Chiyo were friends of the twins.
### LARGE +
- Furuyama: a blind tea master in the Shogun's retinue who usually served tea to Makoto, not Ei, and absorbed her outlook.
- The first Seven: Morax, Barbatos and the other early Archons, with whom she gathered in Liyue after the Archon War. Ganyu notes five of the seven died before Rex Lapis.
- Orobashi and Akuou: Watatsumi's invaders of Yashiori Island, under her rule, crushed by Ei on her behalf.
- Istaroth: Ei suspects this "higher power" helped Makoto's plan with the Sacred Sakura.
### ULTRA +
- The Traveler and Paimon: witnesses to her farewell in her realm of consciousness. In 3.0, hearing that Sumeru's first Dendro Archon vanished in a great calamity centuries ago, the Traveler privately thinks it sounds just like what happened to Raiden Makoto.
- The Shogun puppet: created after her death. After its final duel with Ei it promised to be Ei's shadow "just as you were once Makoto's shadow". The Shogun says Makoto's body perished but she became the Sacred Sakura, which is also a form of Eternity.
- Inazuma's people: soldiers' memories leaking from the Sacred Sakura call the absent Shogun "Almighty Shogun". Most never knew there were two.

## abilities
### LARGE
As Electro Archon she held divine Electro power and the authority of Inazuma's throne. She was not a warrior; Ei handled the fighting. Her sword Musou Isshin was born from her divine might but never sharpened, a symbol of true peace rather than a weapon.
### ULTRA +
Her greatest feats came at her death. She left a faint fragment of her will in Musou Isshin, timed to wake when Ei truly understood her and unleashed the sword's full power. She also kept a realm of consciousness that Ei could preserve. Through that realm she gave Ei the seed of the Sacred Sakura, a tree that absorbs and purifies filth in Inazuma's Ley Lines and holds back the cataclysm's corruption. Her farewell describes what the seed does: when eternity and dreams "shine in unison", the Sacred Sakura blooms from the darkness, free of the Heavenly Principles' grasp. Inside her realm, time ran forwards and backwards. Ei's long duel with the Shogun there lasted centuries from the inside but almost no time outside, and Yae thinks the realm's turbulence carried the Traveler to the right moment. Ei and Yae doubt Makoto did all this alone and suspect Istaroth. Canon never says how much power she had compared to other Archons.

## status
### LITE
Dead for 500 years; Ei rules as Baal.
### STANDARD +
In 2.5 her last fragment of will spoke from Musou Isshin, gave Ei the Sacred Sakura's seed and said a final goodbye.
### LARGE +
Her legacy endures in the Sacred Sakura on Mt. Yougou (in the Shogun's words, the form her eternity took) and in Musou Isshin, which Ei still carries. Nothing in the 6.x Nod-Krai content or the 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya Archon Quests adds to or changes her story as of 7.1.

## knowledge
### LARGE
Her lingering will knew Khaenri'ah was a danger no Archon could ignore and foresaw how Ei might cling to stillness. It could not perceive time and did not know how long had passed or what Inazuma had endured. If she appears in a memory or flashback scene, she knows nothing after the cataclysm except what her final message in 2.5 shows.

## mistakes
### LARGE
- Makoto is not Ei and not the Shogun puppet. Makoto is the dead elder twin; Ei is the living Archon; the puppet was built after Makoto's death.
- Baal is Makoto's divine name, yet Ei still rules under it, and other Archons call Ei "Baal". Ei's own divine name is Beelzebul.
- She was no fighter. Do not give her Ei's swordsmanship or Musou no Hitotachi; her sword was never sharpened while she held it.
- She is dead and has said her final farewell. Do not bring her back or treat her as speaking from the Sacred Sakura.
### ULTRA +
- Her Eternity was not Ei's stillness. She cherished fleeting moments and dreams.
- Do not invent the details of her death, a killer, a romance or her exact age; canon gives none.
- Do not resolve Istaroth's involvement; it is Ei's guess.
- Ei's memories and the novel Treasured Tales disagree on details; Ei says the book becomes "wishful thinking" after the Watatsumi war.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Archon War: twin gods; Ei conquers rival gods and they unite Inazuma's islands.
- About 2,000 years ago: Ei gives up her body; Makoto ascends as Electro Archon, founds the Shogunate and rebuilds Ei as her kagemusha. The first Seven gather in Liyue.
- Her reign: Watatsumi invades Yashiori Island; Ei crushes Orobashi in her name.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- "Makoto" means true; "Ei" means shadow. Treasured Tales calls the twins "Shin" and "Kage", light and dark.
- Her titles include Narukami Gongen, First Shogun and True Shogun. In her time, people addressed the Shogun as "Her Excellency" and "Almighty Shogun".
- English localisation calls her realm "the Plane of Euthymia" in one weekly boss material text (The Meaning of Aeons), which wonders whether Makoto's true meaning was ever conveyed; the quests themselves just call it her realm of consciousness.
- She shares her voice actors with the Raiden Shogun.

## entry: Ei, the kagemusha
@keys: Ei, Raiden Ei, Beelzebul, kagemusha
@keys2: Makoto, twin, sister, previous Shogun
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ei is Makoto's younger twin. Treasured Tales says Ei, a peerless warrior who thought she understood little of mortal hearts, gave up her body so Makoto could ascend; Makoto rebuilt it and made her a kagemusha (shadow warrior, body double). Together they were the one Raiden Shogun, light and shadow. Ei did the fighting so Makoto would not have to. Makoto's death in Khaenri'ah was, in Yae's words, Ei's greatest loss and the start of Ei's change.
### LARGE +
Ei never understood why Makoto went to Khaenri'ah first and kept her in the dark. By 2.5 Ei had come round: "What Inazuma needs more than a warrior is someone like Makoto". She still says she can never become her. Makoto's last words thanked Ei for the polearm that had shielded her from countless calamities.
### ULTRA +
Ei says that in the vast majority of cases the one Furuyama brewed tea for was Makoto, because Ei spent her free time on martial practice. Ei suspects Furuyama sensed there were two Shoguns but never said so.

## entry: Makoto's death in the cataclysm
@keys: Makoto's death, death of Makoto, Raiden Makoto
@keys2: cataclysm, Khaenri'ah, five hundred years ago, 500 years ago
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When monsters overran Inazuma 500 years ago, Makoto went to Khaenri'ah alone without telling Ei, sensing the place could not be ignored. Ei followed, leaving the Kitsune Saiguu to defend Inazuma, but arrived only in time to hear Makoto's dying breath. Ei entered her unconscious sister's mind to say goodbye, cried bitterly and resolved to pursue Eternity. Ei saved Makoto's collapsing realm of consciousness and carried it back to Inazuma.
### LARGE +
In Ei's 2.1 memory cutscene, she watched "a nation stride forward and lose everything to the Heavenly Principles" and, with each step of progress, lost something more, "in the end, even her". The Kitsune Saiguu also died defending Inazuma.

## entry: Musou Isshin
@keys: Musou Isshin
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Musou Isshin was born from Makoto's divine might and named by her; she wanted it to witness "an Inazuma as lovely as a dream". Makoto was no fighter, so it was never sharpened while she held it; it stood for true peace. On the day she died it passed into Ei's hands, already stained with blood. Ei kept its name and has fought with it ever since.
### LARGE +
Makoto hid a faint fragment of her will in it, to be released only when Ei used its full power. That happened in 2.5, when the sword stirred during Ei's last duel with the Shogun. Ei felt Makoto beside her and remarked that a blade Makoto barely wielded brought her closer to her sister.

## entry: Makoto's realm of consciousness
@keys: Makoto's realm of consciousness, realm of consciousness, torii gate
@keys2: Makoto, Raiden Makoto, Grand Narukami Shrine, Ei
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The realm Ei rescued from Makoto's dying mind lies behind a torii gate in a cave beneath the Grand Narukami Shrine. Ei sealed it with her own hands. It resembles the Plane of Euthymia but differs in some way Yae cannot pin down. Yae calls it the foundation of the Sacred Sakura. In 2.5 the Shogun opened it so the two could duel at full strength; the duel lasted centuries inside and almost no time outside.
### LARGE +
Inside, the Traveler saw the seasons, the rise and fall of things and time moving forwards and backwards at once. After Makoto's farewell, the turbulence Yae sensed there vanished. The Traveler took a Dream Sakura from the realm.

## entry: The Sacred Sakura and Makoto's seed
@keys: Sacred Sakura
@keys2: Makoto, Raiden Makoto, seed, Ei
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Makoto's lingering will gave Ei a seed of light: "the miracle tree that blesses the people". Ei planted it in Makoto's realm in the present, and it took root in Inazuma's past, growing into the Sacred Sakura that purified the cataclysm's filth and saved the nation. That is why everyone remembers the tree as ancient while Ei alone saw it appear after Makoto's death. Ei once failed to convince Yae of the link, because the seed had not yet been planted.
### LARGE +
Makoto's words: eternity extends time to infinity and dreams light up each moment within it; when both shine together the Sacred Sakura blooms out of darkness, free of the Heavenly Principles. She meant it to buy Ei time until she was ready to "embrace the new". Ei and Yae suspect a higher power (Istaroth) helped. Yae credits generations of kitsune for the tree's present shape.

## entry: Makoto's Eternity
@keys: Makoto's Eternity, dreams, fleeting moment
@keys2: Makoto, Raiden Makoto, Ei, Eternity
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
Both twins served Eternity, but differently. Ei sought stillness to resist erosion. Makoto cherished the present precisely because it passes ("If nothing else, we have the present moment," Ei recalls her saying) and held that dreams, the endless human drive to want a better future, are what is truly eternal. Ei calls Makoto's view simple on the surface but deeper and more far-sighted than hers. Accepting it ended the Sakoku Decree's logic.

## entry: Furuyama, the blind tea master
@keys: Furuyama, blind tea master
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
Furuyama served tea in the Raiden Shogun's retinue 500 years ago, nearly always to Makoto. Blind for years, he travelled Inazuma through Makoto's descriptions of her dreams. He repeated her creed that good things do not last, so one must enjoy life while it lasts and leave no regrets. When the cataclysm came he stayed behind as others fled, content. In 2.5 his memory leaked from the Sacred Sakura's roots, and he mistook Ei for the Shogun he knew. Ei brewed a final pot of tea in his memory.

## entry: The twins' circle of friends
@keys: karuta, Kitsune Saiguu, Mikoshi Chiyo, Sasayuri
@keys2: Makoto, Raiden Makoto
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
In quieter times Makoto, Ei and their friends sat under the sakura trees playing karuta, with the Kitsune Saiguu offering prizes. Ei, always the most wooden player, pestered Makoto and Mikoshi Chiyo into practice matches until she finally won. Ei still remembers that tree and wishes time had stopped there.

## entry: Baal and the two Raiden Shoguns
@keys: Baal, two Raiden Shoguns, two Shoguns, first Raiden Shogun
@keys2: Makoto, Ei, Beelzebul, twin, kagemusha, Electro Archon
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Yae Miko: "there were two twin gods, Baal and Beelzebul." Baal was Makoto. Because the twins ruled jointly, the world knew only one god, and both "Baal" and "Raiden Shogun" referred to both of them until Makoto died. Ei then took the Shogunate and kept the name. Few Inazumans know there was ever a change of Electro Archon; Ganyu hinted at it in Liyue, and Yae told the Traveler the full story in 2.1.
