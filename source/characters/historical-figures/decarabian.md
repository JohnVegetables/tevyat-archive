## summary
### LITE
Decarabian was the God of Storms, who ruled Old Mondstadt from his high tower during the Archon War. He wrapped his city in a wall of gales to shield it from Boreas's blizzards, and his subjects came to see it as a prison. About 2,600 years ago they rose against him and killed him.
### STANDARD +
He is not one of The Seven. After he fell, the wind spirit who had helped the rebels ascended as Barbatos and founded a free Mondstadt. His ruined city and tower later became Stormterror's Lair.

## background
### LITE
During the Archon War, which Mondstadt's histories call the Age of Kings, Decarabian and the wolf god Boreas were the "Kings of Ice and Frost" at war. To protect his capital from Boreas's blizzards, Decarabian raised a storm wall around it that let nothing in or out; not even Boreas could scratch it. Inside, he planned the city down to each resident's quarters and banned chords and tunes that might signal resistance. He thought his reign good and believed his people bowed out of love.
### STANDARD +
Weapon text calls his high tower the place where a wicked dragon had once been cast down; his territory is said to have spread across the Brightcrown Mountains and Windwail Highland, with a major settlement around Cecilia Garden. Ruins in the lair suggest the city once had gateways to the northeast and northwest, closed off afterwards. At some point he took a human lover, Amos, who came to see that he did not love her as she loved him. His people's bowing was forced by the wind, not given in respect.

Around 2,600 years ago frustration became rebellion. Rebels used the windblume flower as a quiet symbol. Their leaders, as listed by the wiki from item and story text, were a lyre-playing boy (the Nameless Bard), a wind spirit, Amos, the knight Gunnhildr and a wandering Red-Haired Warrior. The Gunnhildr Clan had left the city earlier and was sheltered by the wind spirit. Decarabian was killed, and his death blew apart part of his tower.
### LARGE +
Boreas, in the same period, had come to believe his own power kept life from flourishing in Mondstadt and chose death, so the blizzards ended; canon does not say whether Decarabian knew. When the god fell, the wall dissolved and the rebels saw the blue sky beyond for the first time. Item text says the "song of freedom" shattered the city in an instant and that his dream would otherwise have lasted forever. The victory cost lives: Amos and the Nameless Bard died. Venti's Character Story 4 says Decarabian fell believing he had given his people a city free of the bitter cold.
### ULTRA +
Legacy: the wind spirit ascended as Barbatos, shaped Mondstadt into a free city with no king and moved its people to Cider Lake, leaving the old city abandoned. At some later point (canon does not say exactly when) a heavily damaged Field Tiller, a Khaenri'ahn prototype Ruin Guard that had fought Boreas's spirit, took shelter in his tower and rained fire on anyone approaching Old Mondstadt for about a year. Mondstadters thought it a curse of the dead god and called it the Tyrant's Final Fury; the attacks stopped on their own and the Church later judged the theory improbable. The Traveler identifies the Field Tiller as the cause in the 1.4 Archon Quest act We Will Be Reunited. Around 500 years ago the dragon Dvalin, poisoned in battle, made the tower his lair, and Old Mondstadt became Stormterror's Lair. The Prologue Archon Quest (1.0) brings the Traveler, Venti, Diluc and Jean there to free Dvalin.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed by the rebels about 2,600 years ago. His city and tower survive as ruins in Stormterror's Lair, and his name lives on in Decarabian-style ruins and weapon ascension materials. As of 7.1 he plays no active role in the story.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Venti (as the wind spirit) helped overthrow him and became Barbatos; the Nameless Bard was among the revolt's leaders and died; Amos, his former lover, joined the rebellion; Gunnhildr fought against him; Boreas was his rival in the Archon War.

## entry: Decarabian's Storm Wall
@keys: Decarabian's storm wall, storm barrier, storm-walled Mondstadt, Old Mondstadt
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Decarabian ringed his capital with gales so strong that Boreas's war on the city failed. Inside, the wind ground soil and rock to dust and the people lived under constant bowing winds. He saw a refuge from the cold; they saw a cage. When the rebels killed him the wall vanished and the sky showed.

## entry: Tyrant's Final Fury
@keys: Tyrant's Final Fury, Field Tiller
@version: 1.4
### STANDARD
At some point after the god's death a damaged Field Tiller took refuge in his tower and rained fire on anyone who approached Old Mondstadt for about a year. Locals, unfamiliar with Ruin Guards, took it for a curse of the God of Storms and named it the Tyrant's Final Fury. It ceased on its own, and the Church later judged the curse theory improbable. In the 1.4 Archon Quest (Dishonorable Trial, A Soul Set Apart) Barbara recounts the story and the Traveler, Paimon and Dainsleif link it to the Field Tiller atop the tower.

## entry: Fall of Decarabian's Tower
@keys: A Note in Spring's Leich, Decarabian's fall, fall of Decarabian
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Artifact text added in 6.3 (A Note in Spring's Leich) retells his end: a tempest raged before his crumbling throne, and the Nameless Bard, playing his lyre a last time with strings that "once belonged solely to the Master of Time" (Istaroth), turned that storm into the long song that is Mondstadt. The bard's body could not bear that power and fell with the collapsing tower, and his name was lost; the wind spirit, when the Mother of Time and Wind bestowed her authority on him, quietly kept a strand of time and that name. The text addresses Decarabian as a "sorrowful tyrant". How this account fits Venti's Character Stories (which have Barbatos ascend right after the fall) is not spelled out.

### ULTRA
Weapon text added in 6.5 (Golden Frostbound Oath) tells of a knight of fallen Arcadia who, after failing to win the Golden Bough, led surviving Arcadians to the high tower where a wicked dragon had once been cast down, to stand before its lord and seek shelter. The wiki identifies this knight as the Knight of the Golden Bough, namesake of the Gunnhildr Clan; canon does not describe how Decarabian received them.
