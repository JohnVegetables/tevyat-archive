## summary
### LITE
Kanna Kapatcir, the Great Thunderbird, was a vast Electro bird of prey, called a monster in item text, that Tsurumi Island's old tribe revered as its guardian. She destroyed the island and its people in grief and rage after a boy she had befriended was sacrificed.
### STANDARD +
She is known through memory, artifact text and the Traveler's Tsurumi quests, not through any appearance. Her power outlived her as the Thunder Manifestations.

## background
### LITE
Long ago she lived on and above Tsurumi Island. She noticed strange objects fall from the heavens and make the island's fog but did nothing about it. The tribe on the island feared and thanked her, offered blood sacrifices and read her flight as divine revelation. She thought of them as no different from animals. A boy named Ruu sang so well that she landed to hear him, and he named her Kanna Kapatcir. When she returned after promising to hear him again, he was dead and his blood was in the sacrificial cup. She destroyed the tribe with lightning and cursed the land until she could hear his song again.
### STANDARD +
Ruu had believed his death served the tribe, but she saw only the murder of an innocent and a broken promise. Years later she was hunted down and killed by Ei, who regarded her as an obstacle to progress. Her remnants were sealed at the Asase Shrine on Seirai Island.
### LARGE +
A thousand years afterwards Asase Hibiki released the barrier, loosing the Thunder Manifestation on the Shogun's ships. The island broke apart and some ships were swept far out to sea. In the Sun-Wheel quest the Traveler and Paimon brought Ruu back to the place of his sacrifice, another Thunder Manifestation appeared there, and Ruu, now knowing the truth, went to Seirai and sang to it. A new feather then appeared before him, a sign that she had heard the song and the curse was lifted.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed by Ei long before the Traveler's time. What remains is her residual power, sealed and later released as Thunder Manifestations, and a feather that answered Ruu's song. No later developments in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Ruu is the centre of her story: the only person whose song reached her and the one whose death broke her. The tribe saw her as divine; she saw them as fleeting. Ei killed her as a monster, so she is both the victim of the tribe's cruelty and the cause of its extinction.
### LARGE +
Her name was given to her by Ruu, and she had forgotten it, and him, by the time of her death. Her curse ended not by being fought but by a promise kept.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Ancient era: strange objects fall on Tsurumi Island; the fog forms; the tribe worships her.
- Ruu meets and names her; he is sacrificed; she razes the mountain and curses the land.
- Years later: Ei kills her; remnants sealed at the Asase Shrine on Seirai Island.
- A thousand years later: the seal is released and the Thunder Manifestation wrecks Seirai Island's fleet.
- 2.2: the Traveler learns her story in The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna and Ruu's song ends the curse.

### STANDARD
Kapatcir ("Great Eagle of the Storm") was a giant violet-feathered thunder bird, called a monster, who ruled the skies above Tsurumi Island and was worshipped there. After a boy named Ruu was sacrificed she destroyed the tribe, then was killed by Ei. Her feathers and remnant power persist as Thunder Manifestations.
### LARGE +
Item text says thunder was her breath and human fate was as the weather to her; the tribe's worship meant nothing to her. Her appearance is never shown in game.
### ULTRA +
Trivia: Kapatcir is among the names the game will not let the Traveler give to the Little One; Paimon remarks that it piles on too much pressure.
