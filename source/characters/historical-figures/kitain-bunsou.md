## summary
### LITE
Kitain Bunsou, called Kitain the Serpent, was one of Inazuma's celebrated warriors and a spearman. He belonged to the Kitain family of Yashiori Island, which hunted the Tatarigami for generations and served as Prefects of Yashiori. He made the Kitain Cross Spear for his own style of fighting. He is dead and little else is recorded about him.
### STANDARD +
A children's song once listed him among Inazuma's great warriors, with Arataki of the Front Gate, Iwakura the Successor and Takamine the Mistsplitter; the weapon text says many other names on the list have been lost.

## background
### LITE
Wiki summaries count him among the Yashiori Island Guardians who subdued the Tatarigami that emerged after the death of the evil god Orobashi. The spear's text says his family long served as Yashiori's Prefects and hunted Tatarigami.
### STANDARD +
The spear's text adds that after so long as a demon hunter, a hunter could hardly avoid taking in some of the miasmic, tainted blood. A wiki summary treats him as the probable founder of the Kitain School of spearmanship that survives in Inazuma, but that is not firmly stated.

## status
### LITE
Dead; no date or manner of death is given. His spear survived as a weapon design, recovered in the world quest series Orobashi's Legacy.
### STANDARD +
His line later died out, for reasons legend does not give. His descendant Kitain Hideie left a written testament of guilt after fleeing the cataclysm battlefield.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His clearest tie is to the Kitain family and its inheritance of the Prefect role. Kaji, in Orobashi's Legacy, speaks of the Kitain's deeds as an age of heroes now gone, and gives the Traveler the spear's diagram.

### LARGE +
In Orobashi's Legacy: Part V, Kaji gives the diagram to the Traveler after the final ward at Fort Mumei is repaired, which suppresses the Tatarigami's spread there. Kaji says the ruins of Fort Mumei are what remains of an ancient city razed when the Raiden Shogun fought the evil god Orobashi.
### ULTRA +
The same quest has Kaji lament that the Kitain, who once destroyed so many evils, will never return, and compare the Traveler's work to the old Prefects' deeds. Kaji says the Prefects of Yashiori once wielded the spear.

## entry: Kitain Cross Spear
@keys: Kitain Cross Spear
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A strangely shaped, heavy polearm made by Kitain Bunsou for his own spear style. It is hard to handle untrained but can unleash great destruction in expert hands. In Orobashi's Legacy: Part V, Kaji gives its diagram to the Traveler, saying a skilled smith could recreate its former glory.

## entry: Kitain Hideie testament
@keys: Kitain Hideie, Absolution-Seeker's Testament
@keys2: Kitain
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In a letter found in the Veluriyam Mirage (a limited-time event area, Secret Summer Paradise), Kitain Hideie, a Prefect of Yashiori, confesses that he abandoned his troops and the cross spear in the cataclysm. Ashamed to return to Yashiori, he resolved to go back to the battlefield to seek atonement. He is the only named descendant of Bunsou.
