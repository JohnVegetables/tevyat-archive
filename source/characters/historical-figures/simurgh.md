## summary
### LITE
Simurgh was a divine bird made from a shard of Khvarena, the purifying light Nabu Malikata created to oppose the coming cataclysm. When the Abyss threatened the Amrita, she gave up her form and became countless Khvarena, the ancestors of the Pari.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through artifact texts and the words of the Pari. She has no recorded speech in the game.

## background
### LITE
Nabu Malikata, the Goddess of Flowers, foresaw a coming darkness and separated a pure shard of her own Khvarena, which she called her innocent daughter. She entrusted it to her friend Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. After Nabu Malikata and King Deshret were gone, Rukkhadevata shaped that Khvarena into the divine bird Simurgh and charged her with guarding the border between new birth and death. Simurgh slept until the cataclysm, then awoke.
### STANDARD +
When disaster arrived, the Hydro Archon Egeria was slain and her body became the Amrita, from which Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm, a mother-tree. The Abyss began to corrupt both the Amrita and the tree. To keep the Amrita pure, Simurgh drank from it, shattered her own form and scattered into countless Khvarena, which fell as flowers across the Vourukasha Oasis. Only the Pari, her heirs, became sentient.
### LARGE +
Artifact texts add the emotional frame. Nabu Malikata warned the Khvarena of a coming trial of "division and death", asked her not to flee or let her light dim, and said immortality would follow, though a harder road than death. The set describes Simurgh's form as holding the colours of countless birds and the song of many flowers, with the oasis of flowers "hidden within" her body. The same texts say that once the cataclysm came she awakened "and went to her inevitable doom", and that reborn Khvarena flowers would no longer be troubled by death. Rukkhadevata, the texts say, had known Simurgh's fate since the night she slept beside the Goddess of Flowers.
### ULTRA +
The Pari give two slightly different framings. Zurvan, the first Pari, says Simurgh drank the Amrita and mixed her Khvarena with it, creating countless beings. Sorush says Simurgh transformed herself into all kinds of Khvarena, and that those Khvarena then took the bird's place in defending the land and sealing painful memories that leaked out. Pari believe each of them has a fated duty and live to serve it; Sorush explicitly sets out to imitate Simurgh's sacrifice in the 3.6 quest, and the Pari call worthy successors Bloomguards.

## relationships
### LITE
Nabu Malikata made the Khvarena from which she came, and Rukkhadevata gave it a form. Egeria's Amrita is what she drank. The Pari are her descendants.
### STANDARD +
Nabu Malikata called her "my Khvarena, my pure daughter" and asked her to love and be loyal to "another mother", Rukkhadevata. The artifact texts speak of three mothers of the flower sea: verdure (Rukkhadevata), sweet dew (Egeria) and fresh flowers (Nabu Malikata). The Pari remember her as the master of Khvarena.

## status
### LITE
Dead as an individual. Her form was shattered during the cataclysm roughly five hundred years ago, and her Khvarena lives on in the Vourukasha Oasis and in the Pari.
### STANDARD +
Her last wishes were carried out by Zurvan, who gathered Khvarena and fought Abyssal monsters in the wilderness. In the 3.6 quest Khvarena of Good and Evil the Traveler helped cleanse the Harvisptokhm and seal the Sign of Apaosha. Nothing found for versions up to 7.1 changes her status.
### LARGE +
Plumes of Purifying Light, the remains of dead Pari, are returned to the Amrita Pool, continuing the cycle her sacrifice began. Tainted Pari are rejected by the oasis and become Fravashi Trees.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the cataclysm: Nabu Malikata separates a Khvarena shard and gives it to Rukkhadevata.
- After Nabu Malikata and King Deshret died: Rukkhadevata forms the Khvarena into Simurgh, who sleeps.
- About 500 years ago (the cataclysm): Egeria dies and the Amrita forms; Rukkhadevata grows the Harvisptokhm; Simurgh drinks the Amrita and becomes countless Khvarena, and Zurvan, the first Pari, is born.
- 3.6: the Khvarena of Good and Evil quest and the Vourukasha's Glow artifacts reveal her story.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Her name echoes the Persian simurgh, a benevolent mythical bird tied to purifying water and fertility.
- Two Sumeru soundtracks carry her name, "Simurgh's Dream" and "Where Simurgh Rests", but contain no lore.
- The Vourukasha's Glow artifact texts are the main source of her story.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not an Archon, nor a goddess with a recorded rank.
- She is not alive as a bird. Do not have her speak; canon records no lines.
- The Pari are her descendants, not a continuation of her mind.
- Do not claim canon says why she was chosen beyond the artifact texts.

## entry: Khvarena and the Pari
@keys: Khvarena, Pari, Vourukasha Oasis
@keys2: Simurgh, divine bird
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Khvarena is the light Nabu Malikata made to oppose the cataclysm. It can purify Abyssal energy and Forbidden Knowledge. When Simurgh shattered her form, her Khvarena spread through the Vourukasha Oasis; only the Pari became sentient. Pari that die turn into Plumes of Purifying Light and are returned to the Amrita Pool.
### LARGE +
Zurvan, the first Pari, found Dainsleif after Khaenri'ah fell, and with Nagarjuna and the Order of Skeptics performed the first Rite of Ab-Zohr at the Harvisptokhm, which purified the skies and created the other Pari.

## entry: The Amrita and the Harvisptokhm
@keys: Amrita, Harvisptokhm, Amrita Pool
@keys2: Simurgh, Egeria
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Amrita is what remained of Egeria after she was slain at Tunigi Hollow. Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from it so Egeria's consciousness could stay and seal the Abyssal rift. The Amrita began to be corrupted, which led Simurgh to drink it and break her form to purify it.
