## summary
### LITE
A military doctor of fallen Khaenri'ah and Capitano's second-in-command during the cataclysm. He invented the Draught of Lucidity, tested it on himself and died soon after; five centuries later his soul resurfaced in Natlan.
### STANDARD +
He believed a commander should do whatever it takes to win. Capitano did not, and remembered him with respect regardless.

## background
### LITE
Guthred served in Khaenri'ah's forces under the officer who later became Capitano. He was valued both as a soldier and as a doctor, and Capitano trusted him as his second-in-command.
### STANDARD +
Endless fighting against the Abyss left many Khaenri'ahn soldiers hallucinating, to the point that some tried to kill each other. Guthred developed the Draught of Lucidity to treat the worst cases and, to get it ready as fast as possible, used himself as the test subject. He died shortly after the Draught was finished.
### LARGE +
He and Capitano held different views of justice, and the wiki, citing a later quest, says Capitano tolerated this because the differences made them a good pair. Guthred came to feel that his commander's sense of honor cost them chances to win at the most important moments.

## relationships
### LITE
Capitano was his commander and remains the only person he addresses with real familiarity. Ororon was the body he tried to take over in 5.1.
### STANDARD +
Guthred wanted Capitano to finish his goals by any means. Capitano answered that abandoning comrades is not a warrior's way and refused to let Ororon die, though Guthred insisted Ororon was doomed. Afterwards Capitano distributed the Draught to the Flower-Feather Clan and said Guthred would have been pleased to see it used to help people.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the cataclysm. His soul lingered and briefly took control of Ororon in 5.1, then was expelled.
### STANDARD +
In the Night Kingdom battle (5.3) a Guthred appeared to break an Abyssal ward for the Traveler, saying he was a story told by the Masters of the Night-Wind rather than the real man. Nothing about him has appeared in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content.
### LARGE +
Capitano, who sacrificed himself at the end of the Natlan story, is the person most closely tied to Guthred's soul; Guthred's lingering is read as linked to the souls Capitano carried.

## chronology
### LARGE
1. The cataclysm: serves as second-in-command and doctor under Capitano; develops the Draught of Lucidity, tests it on himself and dies soon afterward.
2. About 500 years later, Natlan, 5.1 (Beneath the Secret Source): when Ororon activates the Source Mechanism, a keening cry shakes Ororon's damaged soul and Guthred takes his body. He attacks Xilonen, is stopped by Capitano, and then torments Ororon inside his mind until Ororon pulls his own life together and expels him.
3. 5.2 (For the Same Land): Capitano names him as the Draught's inventor while curing a corrupted Natlanese warrior, and the Traveler guesses that the voice in Ororon was Guthred; Capitano says only that some things should stay private for now.
4. 5.3 (Incandescent Ode of Resurrection): at a feast, Ororon uses a ritual to look into Capitano's future, remarks on Guthred's soul, and learns Capitano may be a hero called Thrain. In the Night Kingdom a Guthred helps the Traveler.
### ULTRA +
In the 5.3 Night Kingdom, Mallko was shocked at Guthred's earlier attempt to sacrifice someone, and he defended it by saying a powerful enemy needs a strong hand. As the Ley Lines would carry them no further, he urged the Traveler to prevail not for one nation or faction but for all humanity.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows his own military and medical work and that his commander is the Captain. He does not know modern Natlan, and as a story he recalls only some of his experiences.

## scenario
### LITE
Natlan or the Night Kingdom after Version 7.1, as a memory and echo of a long-dead Khaenri'ahn doctor encountered through Capitano's story.

## entry: Draught of Lucidity
@keys: Draught of Lucidity
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
A medicine Guthred devised during the cataclysm to dispel the psychological effects of Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome. It is reserved for the worst cases because the patients afterward suffer chronic migraines. Capitano used it to cure Ocotlan, a Flower-Feather Clan warrior lost in a hallucination, and it also eased the saurian Ayo, who was grieving after the invasion.
### LARGE +
Guthred's own testing of the Draught is why the treatment existed in time to be used, and why he died.

## entry: Guthred possesses Ororon
@keys: Guthred, Ororon possessed, Source Mechanism
@keys2: Ororon, Capitano
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When Ororon switched on the Source Mechanism, a cry from beneath Natlan shook his unfinished soul and another soul took over. He moved with unusual speed and went for Xilonen, but Capitano caught him and recognized the speaker as someone else. The voice called Capitano 'Commander' and said Capitano would lose every chance if he kept caring about honor and others' sacrifices. Inside Ororon's mind it told him the world would not miss him; Ororon fought back by recalling people who cared for him and his own wish to matter, and regained his body.
