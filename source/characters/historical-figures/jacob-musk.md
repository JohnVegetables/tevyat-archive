## summary
### LITE
Jacob Musk was a celebrated Mondstadt ecologist who lived at an unknown time. He wrote the books Hilichurl Ballad Selection and Hilichurl Cultural Customs, and is an ancestor of the scholars Ella and Edith Musk.
### STANDARD +
Mondstadt's libraries credit him as the Poet Laureate of Hilichurlian. His work is the nearest thing the city has to an ethnography of hilichurls.
### LARGE +
His books are the main in-world source for how Mondstadters understand hilichurl tribes: Shamans as elders, Lawa as solitary giants, and tribes with distinct customs such as the Meaty, Sleeper and Eclipse tribes of Dadaupa Gorge.

## background
### LITE
An ecologist and researcher from Mondstadt. No canon text gives his birth, family tree or dates.
### STANDARD +
To compile Hilichurl Ballad Selection he travelled across the continent to visit hilichurl tribes and even went deep into their settlements. He was praised as the Poet Laureate of Hilichurlian for the book, but the book's introduction says neither he nor the hilichurls much liked that honour. Though keen on hilichurl studies, he disliked being associated with them even late in life.
### LARGE +
Hilichurl Cultural Customs, in four volumes, covers social structure, spirituality, the solitary Lawa and a survey of tribes at Dadaupa Gorge. The ballad book prints song texts in Hilichurlian with his commentary, sometimes dry (he remarks on the smell of the elderly hilichurls). The passage on the Wind Dragon in Breeze Amidst the Forest is credited to him in English but may be a misattribution.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago. Only his books remain, in the Knights of Favonius Headquarters library and with Ella Musk.
### LARGE +
As of 7.1 nothing new has been added about him.
### ULTRA +
Not mentioned in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content; the English attribution of the Wind Kingdom excerpt remains the one disputed detail about him.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His descendants Ella Musk, who studies Hilichurlian linguistics in Mondstadt's library, and her younger sister Dr. Edith. No other relationship is attested.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
His conclusions are those of a mortal observer: he could not explain how hilichurls control elements without Visions, nor why they camp in ruins. He thought they lived only in the present.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not Ella Musk or Dr. Edith; he is their ancestor.
- Do not give him a date, age, wife, children or a death scene.
- Do not claim he spoke fluent Hilichurlian; his texts show him recording songs he did not fully understand.
- The Wind Kingdom customs excerpt may belong to another Musk; hedge when attributing it.

## entry: Hilichurl Ballad Selection
@keys: Hilichurl Ballad Selection
@keys2: Musk, hilichurl
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A poetry collection of hilichurl songs compiled by Jacob Musk. Volume 1 sits in the library of the Knights of Favonius Headquarters; volume 2 is obtained through a dialogue with Ella Musk. Each song is given in Hilichurlian with his remarks, from a mournful elders' song to a rowdy battle chant.
### LARGE +
He admits he did not yet grasp the literal meaning of some songs and judged them by feeling. He calls one battle song vulgar after seeing hilichurls brawl once it is sung.

## entry: Hilichurl Cultural Customs
@keys: Hilichurl Cultural Customs
@keys2: Musk, hilichurl
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Four volumes of his field notes. They describe tribes as extended families led by a Shaman, hilichurl worship of elemental power in the abstract rather than the Archons, and the Lawa, large solitary hilichurls who are revered but do not lead.
### LARGE +
Volume 4 profiles tribes at Dadaupa Gorge: the Meaty tribe (feasting, a boar pen, a combat arena), the Sleeper tribe (cunning, comfortable huts) and the Eclipse tribe (occult, a throne for the strongest shaman).
