## summary
### LITE
Sumeru's first Dendro Archon and God of Wisdom, avatar of Irminsul, whom the Aranara called Queen Aranyani. She died in the cataclysm 500 years ago after creating her successor, Nahida. In 3.2 she had Nahida erase her from Irminsul, and only the Traveler still remembers her.
### STANDARD +
Before 3.2 Sumeru revered her and the sages kept her cult alive while confining her small, weak-seeming successor. Afterwards every record and memory changed: the Aranara's queen, the sages' idol and the gods' old colleague all became Nahida.

## background
### LITE
She was one of the Three Eternal Companion-Gods with King Deshret and Nabu Malikata. She broke with them, grew a rainforest and ruled Sumeru with the sages of the Akademiya. She helped Deshret contain forbidden knowledge. In the cataclysm that knowledge polluted Irminsul and her. She built the Akasha, made Nahida from Irminsul's purest branch and died.
### STANDARD +
When Deshret's pursuits let forbidden knowledge into his kingdom, she had temples built and filled them with her divine power of life. The effort shrank her to a child's form, and Deshret's own sacrifice ended the outbreak. In the cataclysm the other Archons were summoned to Khaenri'ah, but she stayed to guard Irminsul. Through the Akasha she borrowed her people's dreams and almost cleansed it. Then she found that her own existence was polluted, so the corruption would outlive her in Irminsul's memory, and she could not erase herself. She left clues beginning "Let the world completely forget me". After her death the sages found the child-like Kusanali, judged her no wiser than an ordinary child and confined her in the Sanctuary of Surasthana.
### LARGE +
Earlier history from item lore and quests: a Celestial Nail had turned most of Sumeru into the Great Red Sand, and she first appears in legend as princess of a surviving forest. Nabu Malikata admired her answers to riddles and allied with her, and Deshret shared his throne with them both. When the two planned to turn against Celestia, she refused and went back to the forest. There her rain-calling Varuna Contraption gave rise to the tiger Viaghara, the first lord of the forest. She created the Aranara, and her followers (later the Amurta Darshan) spread the rainforest. After the Archon War she was among the first Seven, who met in Liyue to drink with Rex Lapis.
### ULTRA +
After Deshret's death his civilisation broke into short-lived city-states. Later she reshaped a Khvarena that Nabu had entrusted to her into the bird Simurgh, and promised Apep's creatures to erase the forbidden knowledge within the dragon. In the cataclysm, the "Black Tide" of Withering, she grew the Harvisptokhm from the fallen Hydro Archon Egeria's remains. In the following centuries the Akademiya turned her Akasha into a public knowledge service, and the false story that she betrayed Deshret set forest against desert.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Nahida (Lesser Lord Kusanali) is her incarnation "in the next samsara". They met once, in her remnant, and Nahida erased her as she asked. King Deshret and Nabu Malikata were her fellow Companion-Gods. She left them but saved Deshret's people. The Aranara are her creations. The Akademiya grew from her followers and idolised her. The Traveler is the only one who still remembers her.
### LARGE +
Simurgh guarded the border between birth and death. Viaghara was her friend. Nahida kept her old promise to Apep's creatures without knowing whose it was. She anchored Egeria through the Harvisptokhm. The surviving Archons now remember "Buer" in her place.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Her remnant looks exactly like Nahida: an ancient god in a child-sized form, so alike that Nahida at first asks whether the figure is herself. Describe her factually and without any sexual framing. According to Kasala's account she once had a grown form, before the temple-building shrank her.

## abilities
### LARGE
She was God of Dendro and wisdom. She held the Dendro Gnosis and her consciousness was linked to Irminsul, which brought her knowledge and let her feel its corruption as pain. She raised forest from desert, created life (the Aranara, Simurgh), worked through dreams, and poured her power of life into the land to hold forbidden knowledge back. Each effort drained her. Her own acts left traces in Irminsul, so she could never erase herself; someone else had to do it. Her works include the Akasha, the Varuna Contraption, the Harvisptokhm, an unstable Fire Seed, and a meditation device in the Sanctuary of Surasthana.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the cataclysm and erased from Irminsul (3.2). Teyvat now remembers Nahida as the only Dendro Archon; the Traveler keeps her secret.
### STANDARD +
The erasure ended the crazed whispers, the Withering and Eleazar. Her name survives only by chance: on the RUKDVTA throne in the Orchard of Pairidaeza, in a mushroom's description and in a few old item texts.
### LARGE +
Echoes since: in 3.6 Nahida kept the promise to Apep that she no longer remembered. In 5.3 the Traveler privately called her a friend who now exists only in their memory. In 6.6 Nahida burned the corrupted Irminsul and Aaru took over its role. As of 7.1 nothing shows her memory returning, and she has no part in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya stories.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- After 3.2 no one native to Teyvat remembers her, including Nahida, Paimon, the Archons and the Aranara. Only the Traveler does.
- She and Deshret were never enemies; she did not betray him or create the desert.
- Queen Aranyani is her, not a separate goddess.
- She is not alive and cannot return. No canon age, height or birthday exists.
### LARGE +
- Post-3.2 lines about the "Lord of Verdure" mean Nahida.
- By her own account she did not fight in Khaenri'ah.
- She did not design the Akasha as a tool of control; the sages repurposed it later.
- Canon does not say the 6.6 burning of Irminsul restored her memory.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Ancient era: a princess of the surviving forest, then a Companion-God with Deshret and Nabu Malikata. She breaks with them and revives the rainforest.
- Thousands of years ago: she contains Deshret's forbidden knowledge and shrinks to a child's form. Deshret dies.
- About 2,000 years ago: the Archon War ends and the first Seven gather in Liyue.
- 500 years ago: the cataclysm. She guards Irminsul, builds the Akasha, makes Nahida and dies.
- 3.0: named in Chapter III. 3.1: her true history with Deshret is revealed. 3.2: her remnant meets Nahida and the Traveler, and she is erased.
- 3.6: Apep is healed. 5.3: the Traveler remembers her. 6.6: Irminsul is burned.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Her Chinese name means roughly "Great Benevolent Tree Lord". "Rukkhadevata" comes from a Pali term for a tree spirit, and Aranyani is a Hindu forest goddess.
- Other titles in item and quest text include Lord of Trees, Lord of Dendro, Lord of Wisdom, God King and "Benevolent, Wise, and Eternal Princess".
- She is the second god shown to shrink after pouring power into the land; the first is Guoba (Marchosius).
- English voice actress: Chloe Eves.

## entry: Her Remaining Consciousness: Character and Voice
@keys: Rukkhadevata's consciousness, Greater Lord's Remaining Consciousness, Rukkhadevata's remnant
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The only time she speaks directly is as a remnant in her last memory (3.2). She is gentle, serene and warm. She laughs softly ("Hehe"), praises good reasoning plainly ("You are very smart, indeed") and explains huge matters in calm, short sentences, often through plant images: a dead tree's branch taking root, a branch growing into a different tree, wisdom blooming if you wait quietly. She puts Irminsul and the world above her own feelings ("my feelings weren't important"). She admits pain and fear, and calls her borrowing of people's dreams selfish. She is humble before Nahida, hoping she will become "an even greater archon" and seeing in her a determination she herself lacked.
### LARGE +
She does not soften hard truths. When Nahida resists, she addresses her as Buer, the God of Wisdom, says there is no other way, and tells her to feel joy at having found the answer. Her farewell is a slow, lyrical chant of everyone dreaming beneath the great tree of wisdom, ending in a blessing of sweet dreams for the people of Sumeru. Her remnant knows it is a memory, guesses from Nahida's appearance that about 500 years have passed, and knows nothing of later events unless told. Legend gives the same picture: in Aranara stories she loved every living thing equally, as leaves of one tree, and item lore has her choosing "the path of defending life" over majesty.

## entry: Nahida, Lesser Lord Kusanali
@keys: Nahida, Lesser Lord Kusanali, Kusanali, Buer
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rukkhadevata created Nahida from the purest branch of Irminsul to be her self "in the next samsara". Nahida's birth and Rukkhadevata's death happened at about the same time, so they never met while Rukkhadevata lived. In 3.2 Nahida used the Dendro and Electro Gnoses to reach her predecessor's last memory, learned why she had been made, and reluctantly erased her from Irminsul. Afterwards Nahida came out in tears without knowing why. She now believes she has always been the Dendro Archon and lost her memories and power in the cataclysm.
### LARGE +
Before the erasure Nahida admired her predecessor and the sages compared the two constantly, to Nahida's disadvantage. After it, Nahida still senses a duty she cannot remember (the Fire Seed in 3.6), and her Character Story 5 recalls the advice of someone whose name has been redacted.

## entry: King Deshret and the Temples
@keys: King Deshret, Deshret, Scarlet King, Kasala, Three Eternal Companion-Gods
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Long ago Rukkhadevata, King Deshret and Nabu Malikata ruled together as the Three Eternal Companion-Gods. She later split from them, but when Deshret's search for an ideal world let forbidden knowledge into his kingdom, she came to help. She had the priests build temples and filled them with divine life to suppress the knowledge, and was weakened into a child's form. Deshret's own sacrifice finally ended the outbreak. In Secret of the Scorching Desert (3.1) the ancient temple guardian Kasala's recorded message shows this truth to the Traveler, Cyno, Alhaitham, Dehya and the Deshret loyalist Rahman, against the belief that she betrayed him.
### LARGE +
The Akademiya allowed the betrayal story to spread among the Eremites, and the resulting feud between forest and desert helped the sages stay in control. The rift Deshret's death left in his realm, and Apep's devouring of his corrupted body, set up the promise she later made to Apep's creatures.

## entry: "Let the world completely forget me"
@keys: Let the world completely forget me, Boat of Consciousness, Greater Lord's Remaining Consciousness, That Day Under Irminsul
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies (3.2) Nahida and the Traveler crossed a realm of consciousness on the Boat of Consciousness and reached Irminsul as it stood 500 years ago, where Rukkhadevata's remnant waited. She explained the cataclysm, the Akasha and her pollution, and gave Nahida the full sentence behind her trail of clues: "Let the world completely forget me." Her farewell blessed the people of Sumeru with sweet dreams. Nahida erased her, and the Traveler alone kept the memory.
### LARGE +
The erasure rewrote the world. Sumeru's history now says Kusanali used her power in a great disaster, lost her wisdom and memories, and was abandoned by the Akademiya. The Aranara speak of the Lord of Verdure. The surviving Archons recall "Buer" as an old colleague. Item and book texts changed. At the victory feast in A Toast to Victory, the Traveler realised they might be the only one in Teyvat who remembers and resolved to keep her secret.

## entry: The Akasha
@keys: Akasha, Akasha System, Akasha Terminal
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rukkhadevata created the Akasha during the cataclysm to compile human wisdom, borrowing people's dreams and joining them with her own power to cleanse Irminsul. It cleared almost all the forbidden knowledge, but not the pollution in her own existence. Powered by the Dendro Gnosis, it became her legacy. After her death the Akademiya turned it into the public information service of Sumeru City, and the sages later tried to use it for their own ends.

## entry: Queen Aranyani and the Aranara
@keys: Queen Aranyani, Aranara, Aranyaka, Vanarana, Varuna Contraption, Nara Varuna
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Aranara, small dream-wielding forest beings, are Rukkhadevata's creations and called her Queen Aranyani. In their stories she loved Aranara, humans, mushrooms and Rishboland Tigers alike, as leaves of one tree. She went far away to heal the world and never came back; perhaps, they guessed, she became a tree that bears healing fruit. They and the lords of the forest guarded her rain-calling Varuna Contraption. Since 3.2 they too have forgotten her, and their words now refer to the "Lord of Verdure".

## entry: Nabu Malikata and the Khvarena
@keys: Nabu Malikata, Goddess of Flowers, Khvarena, Simurgh
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Nabu Malikata, a Seelie survivor, sought out the young forest princess and was won over by her answers to riddles. Seeing the cataclysm coming, Nabu gave Rukkhadevata a part of herself, a Khvarena of flowers. Nabu chose to die leading Deshret toward forbidden knowledge in the hope of one day overturning the Heavenly Principles; Rukkhadevata found the plan unacceptable and left. Nabu waited for a return that never came. Much later, Rukkhadevata reshaped that Khvarena into the divine bird Simurgh, who in the cataclysm drank the tainted Amrita and dissolved into Khvarena, from which the Pari and other life arose. A chair with her name inscribed still stands in the Orchard of Pairidaeza.

## entry: Apep's Creatures and the Fire Seed

@version: 3.6
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
After Apep devoured Deshret's corrupted body, forbidden knowledge lived on inside the Dendro dragon and its creatures. The weakened Rukkhadevata promised one creature to erase this "apocalypse", gave it life force to survive in the Chasm, and made with Apep's creatures a Fire Seed to restart the Heart of Oasis inside Apep. In the Sapientia Oromasdis Chapter (3.6) Nahida found the seed without remembering it, followed the elemental fungi and healed Apep, keeping her predecessor's promise. The Grounded Geoshroom recognised something familiar in her and asked if she had "got smaller again".

Inside the Sanctuary of Surasthana stood a device Rukkhadevata used to shut herself away for meditation. Five hundred years ago a Grand Sage changed it so it could be controlled only from outside. ## entry: Traces That Survived the Erasure
@keys: RUKDVTA, Rukkhashava Mushrooms, Orchard of Pairidaeza, Viridescent Venerer, God of the Woods, Fruit of Fulfillment
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Some traces of her escaped Irminsul's rewrite: the RUKDVTA throne in the Orchard of Pairidaeza; the Rukkhashava Mushrooms, whose description now says people think "Rukkhadevata" was the name of a giant tree; and older weapon and artifact texts such as Viridescent Venerer's Determination (the death of the "God of the Woods") and Fruit of Fulfillment. ## entry: The Folio of Foliage and Rival Accounts of Her End
@keys: The Folio of Foliage, Black Tide, final journey
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
Accounts of how she died differ. The Folio of Foliage (in its pre-3.2 text) told of the "Black Tide" of Withering and of a final journey she made with some followers, from which she never returned. The Aranara said she went far away to heal the world. Her own remnant says she was at Irminsul, not in Khaenri'ah, and died cleansing it. The sages never found her. Treat these as separate in-world accounts.

## entry: The Valuka Shuna
@keys: Valuka Shuna, Tighnari's ancestors, Valuka
@version: 3.0
### ULTRA
After Deshret's fall, the fox-like Valuka Shuna, once his desert subjects, survived only by Rukkhadevata's grace and moved into the rainforest. Over time their fur turned green and the dark-furred among them lost their tolerance for desert heat. Tighnari is descended from them.
