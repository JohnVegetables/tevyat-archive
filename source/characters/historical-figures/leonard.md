## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Leonard was an adventurer of ancient Mondstadt whose lifelong dream was to climb Pilos Peak, the tallest and most dangerous mountain in his world. His book, "Of Mountains and Seas", and the meteorites of the Unreconciled Stars event (Version 1.1, limited) preserve his will.
### STANDARD +
He never conquered the peak, but he left designs for wings so that others might go where legs could not. The wiki credits him as the inventor of the Wind Glider.

## background
@availability: limited
### LITE
By age forty, he says, he had conquered every domain except Pilos Peak. He wrote of the climb with help from Venerare, a young generation too scared to join him, and a fellow climber named Alrik.
### STANDARD +
His book records Alrik agreeing to climb with him, then dying on the mountain from the cold and age; Leonard buried a bottle of Alrik's favourite wine beside him. Injuries followed: a broken leg, two frostbitten fingers on his left hand and a near-fatal slide in a hailstorm that he survived by clinging to sharp rock. After one blue-sky day he watched a falcon rise toward the summit and wished for wings of his own.
### LARGE +
In the message the core meteorite plays when it breaks, an aged Leonard says that ropes let people scale mountains and boats cross seas, but Pilos Peak defeated him. Its beauty and indifference made him afraid, and an adventurer who loses courage cannot climb. He therefore turned to a greater ambition: better tools, then wings. He hoped a friend, his children, students or friends would one day reach the top.
### ULTRA +
His supplies were provided by a tribe, and he noted that younger adventurers were put off by older ones who called the mountain a nightmare from which one could not awaken. He sought a kindred spirit before finding Alrik. In the recorded message he says humanity stands as one against the unknown, that to be alive is to seek, and that the wind has not yet come for his soul, a line that fits his wish for wings.

## status
@availability: limited
### LITE
Dead for about two thousand years or more. His will lingered in meteorites that fell on Mondstadt during the Unreconciled Stars event, and the Traveler destroyed the core with Mona's help.
### STANDARD +
The sleeping sickness the meteorites caused ended when the core broke. Pilos Peak is now Musk Reef, a small island that anyone can reach by boat, and Mondstadters glide on wings that follow his vision.

## relationships
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Venerare helped him cross to Pilos Peak. Alrik was his climbing companion who died on the mountain. The unnamed 'dear friend' received his designs.
### LARGE +
Centuries later, Mona restored his book, and the Traveler and Paimon helped her reach the core on Musk Reef. Scaramouche entered the dream earlier and left without knowing who Leonard was.

## knowledge
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
He knows Pilos Peak as a living, dangerous mountain and his own limits as a climber. He knows nothing of Barbatos, the Traveler or the meteorites.

## abilities
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A resourceful mountaineer and tinkerer: he planned climbs with ropes, stakes and wind-thrown hooks and later designed gliding wings. His will was strong enough to affect others in dreams. This power belongs to the event's meteorites, not to Leonard himself.

## mistakes
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
- Do not claim he climbed Pilos Peak or that he was a god or Archon.
- Do not say he lived in Decarabian's time as fact; the dating is inferred.
- Do not treat the Adventurers' Guild as his organisation.
- Do not invent a family; he mentions children, students and friends only as hopes.
- He is not a source for the Fatui's plan or the 'false sky' remarks, which are Scaramouche's.

## entry: Pilos Peak and Musk Reef
@keys: Pilos Peak, Musk Reef
@keys2: Leonard, Unreconciled Stars
@version: 1.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Pilos Peak, in Leonard's time the highest peak around, is smooth like a cut gem with no clear way up. According to Mona, Barbatos later blew the ice and snow from the land, changed Mondstadt's geography, and the mountain sank into the sea, leaving its summit just above water as Musk Reef.

## entry: Wings of Incompletion
@keys: Wings of Incompletion, Wind Glider designs
@keys2: Leonard
@version: 1.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The designs Leonard left to a friend in the core's recorded message, which Paimon links to the Wind Gliders Mondstadters use today with the Anemo Archon's help. The wiki names Leonard the Wind Glider's inventor, though the quest shows only the designs.

## entry: Unreconciled Stars
@keys: Unreconciled Stars, meteorites, sleeping sickness
@keys2: Leonard, Mona
@version: 1.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A limited-time Version 1.1 event (2020-11-16 to 2020-11-30). Meteorites carried Leonard's will and put people into a dream of climbing a mountain. The final quest, Where Ancient Stars Align, sends the Traveler and Mona to Musk Reef to destroy the core, which plays Leonard's message and wakes the sleepers.
