## summary
### LITE
Lyris was the Oceanid director of the Narzissenkreuz Institute, the orphanage of the cataclysm era, and later the Narzissenkreuz Ordo's "Red Empress". Her merging with Mary-Ann Guillotin produced the Oceanid Mary-Ann of Annapausis.
### STANDARD +
Fontaine's Court recorded her as warm and devoted to the children but of below-average intelligence for an Oceanid. Her story is told only in the Fontaine world quests of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo (4.0) and the memories they unlock.
### ULTRA +
Nothing in canon shows her speaking for herself in a scene; every account of her comes through records, memories and the dream-world she left behind.

## background
### LITE
She lived around 500 years ago and directed the Institute, an orphanage whose director post honored the previous Hydro Archon, Egeria. After the cataclysm destroyed the Institute and the Oceanids vanished, she came back to Fontaine and circled its underwater ruins, longing for the days of playing with the children. Rene and Jakob, former wards, persuaded her to join the Ordo despite her doubts.
### STANDARD +
Before the cataclysm she took in the children and often played games with them. Vice-Director Basil Elton ran the real administration, though she regarded him as her assistant. The Institute was destroyed and later disbanded. In the Ordo, Rene and Jakob used her ability to extract people's egos for their gestalt consciousness; Rene noted that the original Oceanids were gone apart from "inferior mutants". Her condition worsened, adding urgency to the project.
### LARGE +
Rene became the first subject to be merged into the gestalt. The attempt nearly lost him, and Jakob rebuilt his personality using the Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz, Lyris's Oceanid essence, Carter Scherbius's remains and a pocketwatch Alain Guillotin had given Rene; Rene was then reborn as Narzissenkreuz. While in the Ordo she revisited the Institute ruins, where Mary-Ann and her robotic dog Seymour found her depressed. Mary-Ann told her the fairy tale of Princess Lyris, who gave up her "time" to save her friends and slept until they could carry her future; it was meant to show the future was not hopeless. Lyris was more interested in the story itself, so Mary-Ann gave her the start of a new chapter and suggested she write the ending.
### ULTRA +
The Court of Fontaine then branded the Ordo a criminal group and issued a high-priority arrest warrant against Lyris. Narzissenkreuz sent Jakob, Lyris and many others to Elynas as bait to buy time, and there the Marechaussee Hunters led by Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin fought them. Lyris absorbed Mary-Ann's personality in the battle. The merged Oceanid built the dream-world Annapausis, hid in a fairy tale and waited for the Institute's children to return. In the process she accidentally gave rise to Ann.

## status
### LITE
Lyris no longer exists as a separate person; her identity lives on in the hybrid Oceanid Mary-Ann, who now has a fresh start.
### STANDARD +
Narzissenkreuz still calls the hybrid more Lyris than Mary-Ann. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 shows her again.

## relationships
### STANDARD
The children of the Institute, especially Rene, Jakob and Mary-Ann, were her family; Basil Elton was her assistant and administrator. See the header relationships for the dynamics.

## entry: Princess Lyris (fairy tale)
@keys: Princess Lyris, Red Empress
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
"Princess Lyris" is the figure in the hero Narzissenkreuz fairy tale that Alain had the Institute children act out. Mary-Ann told Lyris a version in which the princess sacrificed her "time", fell asleep and promised her friends would carry her future. The Oceanid Ann calls herself the hero rescuing Princess Lyris. "Red Empress" is her Ordo title; Caterpillar calls the head-taking a metaphor for her power to dissolve and extract wills.
