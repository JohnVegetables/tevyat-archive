## summary
### LITE
Shirin was the last queen of Gurabad, in the Great Red Sand, Sumeru: the half-Jinni daughter of the tyrant Ormazd and the Jinni Liloupar, wife of Parvezravan and mother of Shiruyeh. Legend casts her as the poisoner of her father and a demon queen. Sumeru quest canon shows her instead as a pawn in her mother's revenge, who lost her mind.
### STANDARD +
Two accounts compete: a popular legend, preserved in the book The Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin, and the sadder account Liloupar herself gives the Traveler.

## background
### LITE
Ormazd, a shepherd who rose with Liloupar's help to unite many desert city-states under Gurabad, became an ordinary tyrant instead of the enlightened ruler she hoped for. Liloupar answered by planning ruin across three generations. Shirin was the first piece: a daughter given to Ormazd with three prophecies and three admonitions.
### STANDARD +
The prophecies said that Shirin would love a hero whose child would surpass him, that her relatives would meet a sweet end, and that only she would be given Gurabad. The admonitions told Ormazd that her joy would bring him tears, that he should no longer feast at her table after her wedding, and that her child would be an ill omen for the land.
She married Kisra Parvez, Ormazd's adopted son, who unknown to them was Liloupar's own child. At some point she unknowingly served Ormazd poisoned honey and killed him. Realising what she had done, she went mad. Kisra, appalled yet glad to become king, took the throne as Parvezravan. With Liloupar's help he drew out Shirin's broken mind to form misshapen Jinn, which he trapped in machines to expand the kingdom.
### LARGE +
Fearing that his son Shiruyeh would one day surpass him, Parvezravan banished him once grown. Little is known of Shirin's final years; Shiruyeh is said to have fled to her side while Parvezravan became a dark monster. Gurabad was then swallowed by the earth and corrupted by the Abyss, and the fates of all three are unknown.
### ULTRA +
The Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin tells it differently. In the book, Shirin took revenge on a loveless marriage by having slaves serve poisoned honey to Ormazd and his 300 offspring at a banquet, then went mad and withdrew from public view. Banished, Shiruyeh wore a brass mask, and Shirin disguised herself as a priestess and gave him a false prophecy to push him to kill his father. Shiruyeh later vanished into a chasm from which a plague, Shiruyeh's Plague, spread and emptied the city. The book says both mother and son became evil apparitions, with Shirin bottled by King Deshret or still wandering.

## status
### LITE
Her fate is unknown. After Gurabad fell she is not recorded dying; the legend places her as a restless spirit, while Liloupar says her children are still in Gurabad and lonely. Nothing in canon through Version 7.1 settles it.
### STANDARD +
Liloupar enters the gate of Gurabad alone at the end of Memories of Gurabad, saying her children, Shirin and Shiruyeh, are probably lonely. She later takes her own life in the Sumeru Archon Quest, apparently without finding them.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Liloupar is the mother who orchestrated her tragedy and later grieves most for her. Ormazd, her father, died by her hand unknowingly. Parvezravan is her husband and, per Liloupar, her younger brother, who used her mind to make Jinn. Shiruyeh is her son, whom she loved and who vanished.
### LARGE +
Liloupar expresses her greatest remorse over Shirin in Nameless Tablet (II), asking her daughter's forgiveness.

### STANDARD
A two-volume in-world book told by a Jinni to a shepherd boy as a sequel to The Shepherd and the Magic Bottle. It names Shiruyeh as a vassal ruler of a plague and gives the legendary version of Shirin's crimes. Its account diverges from the quest evidence and should be treated as folklore.

### STANDARD
Liloupar punished Ormazd's tyranny by shaping three generations: Shirin, Parvezravan (her own son) and Shiruyeh. In Memories of Gurabad she admits that she punished them with schemes of blood, honeyed execution and ambition.
