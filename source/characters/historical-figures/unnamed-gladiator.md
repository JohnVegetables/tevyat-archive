## summary
### LITE
The Unnamed Gladiator was Eberhart's slave champion in Old Mondstadt, about a thousand years ago, a peerless fighter in the arena who nearly won his freedom. He kept fighting out of attachment to his master and fell to Vennessa, a gladiator of the Lawrence Clan. His story is told by the Gladiator's Finale artifact set and the polearm Deathmatch.
### STANDARD +
Canon gives conflicting accounts of how his last fight ended.

## background
### LITE
He belonged to Eberhart and fought in the arena that the aristocracy ran at the Thousand Winds Temple. How and when Eberhart acquired him is not stated.
### STANDARD +
The Nostalgia text recalls him as a silent slave walking in the garden with his young master, who gave him a flower. He wore it as a brooch and it came to stand for his gentler side. He once dreamed of seeing wild flowers on a journey of his own.
### LARGE +
He began fighting for himself, blood and battle-lust, but at some unknown time he began to kill for his master. The Deathmatch text shows Eberhart granting freedom after the promised number of victories, then asking him to continue as a free man for their glory. The Intoxication text blames the lavish gifts, such as a golden goblet, for tying him down.

## personality
### LITE
Disciplined, silent and proud, devoted to his young master. He found glory in the cheers, and mistook the master's gifts for care.
### STANDARD +
He built and flipped an hourglass before each fight and put it aside in the arena. The accounts describe him as someone who lost sight of freedom through loyalty, which proved his tragedy.

## relationships
### LITE
Eberhart, his owner, whom he cherished; Vennessa, who killed him.
### STANDARD +
Dying, he looked to Eberhart's seat for acknowledgement but found it abandoned, with a goblet and silver platter toppled, and understood he had been used.

## abilities
### LITE
An invincible champion of the arena who slew men and monsters. His weapon was a crimson polearm, Deathmatch.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about a thousand years. Most texts say Vennessa pierced his heart in his last bout. Luther's journal on Dragonspine fears the same fate for himself. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content connects to him.
### STANDARD +
Gladiator's Destiny gives a different ending, in which he is spared and sets off free; treat these as unresolved accounts.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No face or build is described. Items mention a feathered helmet, a brooch flower, a golden goblet and a crimson polearm. The Longing text calls him old by the time of the last fight.

## chronology
### LARGE
From item texts, no dates: the master gives him a flower in the garden; he rises to legend; he nears freedom; Eberhart offers freedom for the promised victory count; he fights on; Vennessa kills him in his last match.
### ULTRA +
The pieces disagree on details: Longing calls the last opponent a young girl with the ferocity of a young lioness, Nostalgia a foreign girl, Triumphus says his helmet falling made the farce plain. Luther later cites his fate in a journal as a warning.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not name him, and do not resolve whether he was freed or killed. Do not claim Eberhart loved him; his master's regard is exactly what the texts question.
