## summary
### LITE
Nikolay Snezhevich, called Kolya, was a corporal in the Fatui's Ninth Company, sent to investigate the Chasm. He was an orphan of the House of the Hearth and the brother of Katarina. He disappeared in the depths and is presumed dead.
### STANDARD +
He is never met in person. The Traveler learns of him through Katarina, fragments of his diary and a letter he wrote her.

## background
### LITE
Raised in the House of the Hearth with his sister Katarina. Before the Ninth Company went to the Chasm she was transferred into his regiment, and he wrote her a letter saying how happy he was to be reunited with her.
### STANDARD +
In the Chasm the Company took heavy losses from Abyss monsters, its Mokosh Device (meant to absorb Abyssal corruption) was overloaded and destroyed, and a team that disturbed the Chasm Nail woke a Ruin Serpent and retreated with losses. In the chaos Nikolay used himself as bait to draw enemies away from his squad and vanished.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead. His last diary entry records a severe leg wound and being found by a Shadowy Husk, ending with an apology to Katarina.
### STANDARD +
A fallen rock blocks the path where he was last known to be. Katarina went to look herself and was not found; only his treasured letter lay on the ground.
### LARGE +
Anton, the officer whose group withdrew to the main mining area, believed Nikolay a lost cause. The Fatui action logs the wiki cites record the Company's disastrous losses but do not give Nikolay's fate.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler finds only diary fragments and never his body. Treat his death as probable, not shown; Katarina's own disappearance afterwards is likewise unresolved on the page.

## relationships
### STANDARD
His bond with Katarina is the whole of his story: she refused to give up on him even when Anton, who led the group that withdrew, regarded him as a lost cause.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
He knew his squad's mission and the dangers of the Chasm. He could not have known that Katarina would stay behind to look for him.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not confirmed dead on-screen; say presumed.
- He is not a Harbinger or high officer; he is a corporal.
- Do not invent his personality; canon gives only his courage and his warmth toward his sister.
