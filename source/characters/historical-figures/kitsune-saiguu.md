## summary
### LITE
The Kitsune Saiguu, also called the Hakushin Kitsune, was a kitsune of the Hakushin Clan whom Inazuma worshipped as a fox goddess. She led the Grand Narukami Shrine, befriended Ei and others of the Raiden era, and vanished into the darkness defending the land during the cataclysm 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
She is a figure of memory: the Traveler never meets her, only a shrine maiden formed from her remembrance, her weapon text, and the people and spirits she left behind.
### LARGE +
Her last act was to guard the Sacred Sakura and its roots from Abyssal defilement. What survived of her became Hanachirusato, who completed the ritual with the Traveler in the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual world quests (2.0).

## personality
### STANDARD
Warm, playful and sociable. The Hakushin Ring text shows her enjoying races, music, sword dances and festival nights, admiring others' talents, scheming with good humour (against Ioroi and, to win him a place, against the Shogun) and keeping her own cares quiet. Yae Miko calls her nonsensical and insufferable, with evident fondness.
### LARGE +
Beneath the mischief she took duty seriously. Before the cataclysm she warned that a disaster would engulf heaven and earth and chose to stand at the Shogun's side. In her last moments she asked the darkness consuming her to spare her memories for the land she loved. She was a big fan of fried tofu and, per Hanachirusato, knew 1,111 short chants.

## background
### LITE
A descendant of the Hakushin Clan's matriarch Hakushin, she was worshipped as a kitsune goddess and served at the Grand Narukami Shrine. She kept company with Ei, the oni Chiyo, the tengu chieftain Reizenbou and the humans of Mt. Yougou. About 500 years ago she moved to Tenshukaku, warning that disaster was coming, and later vanished fighting it.
### STANDARD +
Her early life is unrecorded, and her lifespan is unknown beyond kitsune living longer than humans. She won a race against Reizenbou, who then became her bodyguard. She schemed Ioroi into serving the Shogun as Protector of the Grove. She befriended the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki, Kamuna Harunosuke, the Kanjou Hiiragi Hiroshi, the Naganohara family of firework makers and the kitsune writer Urakusai. Urakusai's unexplained mistake angered her, she banished him, and she moved to Tenshukaku to stay near the Shogun.
### LARGE +
When the cataclysm came, the Raiden twins left Inazuma one after the other (the wiki says for Khaenri'ah); she and those who stayed, Reizenbou among them, worked to protect Inazuma. Legend says she led human refugees into the underground halls of Araumi. She set barriers to guard the Sacred Sakura and the Thunder Sakura roots and charged the Konda Clan with keeping one. Before her final parting from Ei she gave the motto 'do not be blinded, do not waver, keep walking the path you believe in'. She was then consumed by a 'dark will'. She begged it to spare her memories, and they settled into the soil as filth, from which Hanachirusato later formed.
### ULTRA +
Her departure scattered her circle. Reizenbou went into exile, Kamuna left for Liyue, the Earth and Sky kitsune turned to stone awaiting her return, and Hibiki took over the shrine. Her old shrine in the Hakushin lands fell into neglect. Ioroi, still believing the hide-and-seek game continues, made trouble to lure her out and was sealed in stone by Kamuna. Hanachirusato recalls that Saiguu gave a catalyst to the Kanjou head Hiiragi Hiroshi (Inagi Hotomi speaks of a demon-exorcising mirror she gifted the Hiiragi Clan, whose blessed lens became the Memento Lens) and that Inagi Misato of the Grand Narukami Shrine was her friend; the Konda, Saimon and Inagi lines descend from people she protected.

## appearance
### STANDARD
White hair and fur, and the shrine priestess clothing seen in the Raiden Shogun character teaser. Hanachirusato wears a white fox mask, but that is her own look, not Saiguu's.

## relationships
### LITE
Ei and Makoto were her closest allies; Chiyo and Reizenbou her friends; Asase Hibiki her mentee; Yae Miko her doted-on younger kin; Ioroi her rival and ward.
### STANDARD +
Her bond with Ei is the emotional centre: she talked Ei into sparing Ioroi and, before their final parting, left her a motto she hoped Ei would remember. Reizenbou's loyalty ended in guilt. Yae Miko, born after Saiguu first left the Hakushin lands, grew up on tales of her and says little of her now. Hanachirusato is not her but is made of her.

## status
@availability: limited
### LITE
Dead. She disappeared into the darkness during the cataclysm and never returned. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes this.
### STANDARD +
Statues of the Earth and Sky kitsune still wait for her. Her catalyst, the Hakushin Ring, carries a memory of her. Hanachirusato was cleansed in 2.0, leaving a mask that became the Hakushin Ring's blueprint.
### LARGE +
In the 5.4 Mikawa Festival event (limited) she appears only in Chiyo's dream, at a picnic with Makoto, Ei and Sasayuri.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not Yae Miko (her kin and successor at the shrine), nor Hanachirusato (a remnant of her memories).
- Her personal name is unknown; 'Saiguu' is a title.
- Do not claim she is alive or that the Traveler met her.
- Hibiki, not Yae, is the one described as her mentee.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Unknown date: wins a race against Reizenbou; tricks Ioroi into serving the Shogun; befriends Hibiki, Harunosuke, Hiroshi, the Naganohara family and Urakusai.
- Before the cataclysm: banishes Urakusai and moves to Tenshukaku.
- About 500 years ago: the twins leave for Khaenri'ah; Saiguu protects Inazuma, shelters refugees at Araumi, sets barriers around the sakura roots, plays hide-and-seek with Ioroi, parts from Ei, and is lost.
- After: Hanachirusato forms; Kamuna seals Ioroi; a man very likely Kamuna later renews a ritual ward and spares Hanachirusato (Memento Lens visions).
- 2.0: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual world quests and the Hakushin Ring.
- 5.4: appears in a limited-event cinematic only.

## entry: Hanachirusato
@keys: Hanachirusato
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A masked shrine maiden who describes herself as filth condensed from the Kitsune Saiguu's memory. She carried 1,111 short chants and her motto, called the Traveler the 'Destined One', and gave them the Memento Lens. She was purified at the end of the ritual, leaving a mask as the Hakushin Ring's blueprint.

## entry: Hakushin Ring
@keys: Hakushin Ring
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A 4-star catalyst whose description is narrated as Saiguu's last thoughts: her friends, a race, a dance, a prayer that the barrier hold, and a plea that the dark will return her memory to the land.

## entry: Reizenbou
@keys: Reizenbou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Yougou Tengu chieftain who became Saiguu's bodyguard after losing a race. She mentored the Yougou Three, held the bow Thundering Pulse, and went into exile after failing to protect Saiguu.

## entry: Ioroi
@keys: Ioroi, Protector of the Grove
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A bake-danuki who competed with Saiguu. She had Ei take him as Protector of the Grove, then tricked him into hide-and-seek in the cataclysm so that he would not fight. He believes the game is still on and was sealed in stone by Kamuna.

## entry: Urakusai
@keys: Urakusai
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Kitsune author of the original Chronicles of the Six Kitsune and a tea expert. She admired his writing, then banished him over a mistake no one remembers.
