## summary
### LITE
A Muratan enslaved in Mondstadt about a thousand years ago, she fought as a gladiator, then with Barbatos' help led the rebellion that ended the aristocracy and founded the Knights of Favonius as its first Grand Master.
### STANDARD +
Her legend lives in the song The Legend of Vennessa, Jean's title, the sword Aquila Favonia and the great oak at Windrise. She is a mentioned figure only.
### LARGE +
Two threads run through her story: a captive who refused freedom as a gift ("freedom given freely is only a guise for another shackle", as the manga has her say) and a founder who built an order meant to keep tyrants from ruling again. Later Mondstadters remember the first more readily than the second.

## background
### LITE
A Muratan of Natlan descent, enslaved with her tribe by Mondstadt's nobles, she became an undefeated arena fighter. Barbatos helped her overthrow the aristocracy; she then rebuilt the city and founded the Knights.
### STANDARD +
The Muratans descend from Natlanese exiled after an ancient civil war; this group accepted exile and wandered abroad (Enjou's tale in 5.8). Vennessa's clan was hounded and starved by the corrupt dragon Ursa, then fled into Mondstadt, where the Lawrence-led nobles enslaved them. She fought in the arena for her people and her sister Lind (manga), becoming the Lionfang Knight for her undefeated streak.
After the rebellion, most nobles and collaborators such as the royal mages were exiled; houses that proved uncorrupted, like the Gunnhildrs, stayed. She restored the Church of Favonius and founded the Knights of Favonius so that no tyrant could rise again.
### LARGE +
The rebellion as the in-game sources tell it: Venti's Character Story says Barbatos returned after 1,600 years, heard the slave girl Vennessa's cry and raised the revolt with her. The song The Legend of Vennessa has the wind spirit descend to her, offer friendship and promise to weave her name into a song, then has the dragon flee and the aristocrats hide as she marched. A forged treaty did much of the work: soldiers serving the nobles found what looked like a deal selling Mondstadt to Rex Lapis and turned on their masters. Only many years later did historians learn it was fake; Barbatos had forged it.
The official manga adds the opening scene: during Ludi Harpastum Venti provoked the heir Barca Lawrence and sheltered behind Vennessa, whose reputation made Barca back down. Revenge followed: the Lawrences bought her whole clan and set them against Ursa the Drake in the arena, with her people's freedom, and then the city, as the prize. Barbatos appeared in his divine form, empowered her and drove Ursa off, and ordered Lord Lawrence to hand over the city. The rebellion proper is described as a larger affair than that single fight.
### ULTRA +
Her gladiator years: wiki history says the champion belonging to the noble Eberhart (the Deathmatch-wielding gladiator of the Gladiator's Finale set) died at her hands. Item texts stress that every gladiator wore shackles and that Vennessa was not truly held by hers, since Mondstadt's ordinary ore and lack of Natlan's divine flame could not match her strength; what held her was duty to her tribe.
Allies: Ragnvindr, a noble's knight who turned after befriending a member of the Wanderer's Troupe; Kreuzlied, a Lawrence defector who had kept an underground resistance alive; the Gunnhildr Clan, which refused to follow the others into cruelty; and believers of the Church of Favonius. A weapon text calls her the final push that toppled an already crumbling tower, which fits the picture of a rotten aristocracy.
After victory she led the city's reconstruction. One item text imagines her burdens growing from her tribe to Mondstadt, to the very idea of freedom, and finally to the whole world, and her hope that the world might grow stronger.

## personality
### STANDARD
Proud, self-reliant and fiercely dutiful to her people; she refused help that would simply hand her freedom, insisting it be won. Devout in the song's telling (her soul never ceased to pray for her people, for Mondstadt and for freedom). Warm enough to befriend a strange bard and gladly return his friendship.
### LARGE +
Item texts stress responsibility: she carried her tribe's welfare, then the city's. She is remembered as unyielding to injustice and deception alike.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Per the wiki's description of the manga art: athletic, tanned, amber-eyed, with long vermilion Muratan hair braided at the back, and as a gladiator a wrist cuff and an ankle shackle. Songs call her the fiery-headed dame; Venti likens her build to a brick wall. No height or age is given.

## abilities
### STANDARD
An undefeated arena swordswoman and later the first Grand Master. Her blade, Aquila Favonia, is said to have witnessed all her hardships and feats. Item texts describe a scorching blade and strength enough that ordinary Mondstadt chains could not hold her. In the manga her power against Ursa was boosted by Barbatos.
### LARGE +
Leadership mattered as much as strength: she rallied the city, then founded an order and restored the Church. No Vision, Delusion or elemental power is stated for her.

## relationships
### LITE
Venti/Barbatos (ally and friend), Jean (holds her Dandelion Knight title), Ragnvindr (rebel ally), Lind (sister, manga only).
### STANDARD +
Venti met her at the start of the rebellion and tells of her in song. Jean considers her a hero and goes to her tree in hard times. The Lawrence Clan were her enslavers and enemies; Ursa the Drake had starved and hunted her tribe.
### LARGE +
The Lion of the South, a seat among the Four Winds, passes through the Grand Masters and Acting Grand Masters via the Dandelion Knight or Lionfang Knight title; Vennessa is listed as its first holder. The Falcon of the West is usually said to be hers too, though how and when she held it is disputed.

## status
### LITE
Long dead; said to have ascended to Celestia at Windrise, where a vast oak now stands. Mondstadt honours her as its hero.
### STANDARD +
The great oak, called the Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero, is thought to have grown where she ascended; Dr. Edith repeats the legend that she became the goddess who protects Mondstadt for eternity. Legend says she became the Falcon of the West, one of the Four Winds. The Temple of the Falcon at Windrise is dedicated to that title.
### LARGE +
No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 appearance is known. In 5.8 Natlan's Enjou speaks of the Muratans who left for Mondstadt, the nearest canon connection to her homeland. Dainsleif's remark that the Falcon of the West only hovers beneath the gods' light is often read as a pointer to her, but he names no one.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- No Vision is stated for her and she is not an Archon (her power against Ursa came from Barbatos in the manga), and she is not stated to be Jean's ancestor by blood.
- She has no canon husband, children or surname.
- Lind is a manga-only character, not a game character.
- Do not state the arena duel with Ursa as the whole rebellion; it is only the manga's opening, and the in-game accounts describe a wider revolt.
- The "falcon" title is the English rendering; the wiki notes that the Chinese bird word is closer to eagle (Aquila Favonia means eagle of the west wind).

## chronology
### LARGE
- About 1,010 years ago (wiki dating): born into the Muratan tribe of the southern plains; her tribe is hunted by Ursa the Drake and flees to Mondstadt, where they are enslaved. Her sister Lind is named in the manga.
- Years of gladiatorial fighting; undefeated, she is called the Lionfang Knight.
- About 1,000 years ago, during Ludi Harpastum (manga): she meets Venti, is purchased by the Lawrences and sentenced to the arena.
- Barbatos intervenes; the aristocracy falls through a rebellion in which a forged treaty turns the soldiers.
- She founds the Knights of Favonius, the Church is restored and the Four Winds are named.
- At the end of her life she ascends to Celestia at Windrise; the oak stands there.
- Game versions: first referenced in 1.0 (Venti's story, Aquila Favonia, The Legend of Vennessa); no new content in 6.x or 7.0-7.1; Muratan context in 5.8.

## entry: Ursa the Drake
@keys: Ursa the Drake, Ursa
@keys2: Vennessa
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Ursa was a corrupted dragon, a devolved Angel, which (in the official manga) hounded Vennessa's wandering clan for four days and starved them before they fled to Mondstadt. In the manga it commands hilichurls in the final arena battle and is driven off when Barbatos empowers Vennessa. It was later sealed away, and the Knights of Favonius maintained the seal.

## entry: The Forged Treaty
@keys: forged treaty, secret treaty, Rex Lapis treaty
@keys2: Vennessa, aristocracy, rebellion
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
During the rebellion a secret treaty was discovered that seemed to sell Mondstadt and its people to the Geo Archon, bearing Rex Lapis' seal. The soldiers who had served the nobles turned against them. According to Venti's Character Story, historians learned only many years later that it was fake; the story reveals that Venti himself had forged it, having once practised forgery to play pranks on the Geo Archon.

## entry: Aquila Favonia
@keys: Aquila Favonia
@keys2: Vennessa, Knights of Favonius
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The sword said to have been Vennessa's, called the soul of the Knights of Favonius. Its text says it witnessed her hardships and her deeds, and tells that the blessing of Favonius came from a distant tribe.

## entry: Windrise and the Great Oak
@keys: Windrise, Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero, great oak
@keys2: Vennessa, ascended, ascension
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
At Windrise a huge oak is said to have grown where Vennessa ascended. Dr. Edith says it grew in the year she left; Jean's Character Story says she left a sapling that became the tree over centuries. Jean visits it for reflection, and in the Prologue Venti recovers there from Abyssal poison and from Signora's attack.

## entry: Dandelion Knight and the Four Winds titles
@keys: Dandelion Knight, Lionfang Knight, Lion of the South, Falcon of the West
@keys2: Vennessa
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Vennessa was the first Dandelion Knight, also called the Lionfang Knight. The title passes among Grand Masters and Acting Grand Masters, and Jean holds it now. The Lion of the South among the Four Winds is tied to this title; Vennessa is also said to be the Falcon of the West. Early English text said "Liontooth Knight".

## entry: The Muratans
@keys: Muratan, Muratans
@keys2: Vennessa, Natlan
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
The Muratans are Natlanese exiles from an ancient civil war who accepted exile and left their homeland; their kin who stayed became the Tenochtzitoc. In the 5.8 quest The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc, Enjou says the exiles who headed to the "true land of wind" were known there as Muratans. Vennessa's people were Muratans (manga), who by her day had passed down survival and combat skills more than their history.

Lind is Vennessa's younger sister, mentioned only in the official manga. Vennessa goes to buy herbs for her on the day she meets Venti. Nothing else is established.
