## summary
### LITE
Anton Zarkovich Karnatsky was a senior Snezhnayan researcher of the Armory Palace who studied the kuuvahki energy of fallen moon fragments in Nothing Passage on Lempo Isle, Nod-Krai. He and his rival Preobrazhensky caused a catastrophe that killed nearly the whole Fatui 6th Company and both of them.
### STANDARD +
He is never met in person; the Traveler learns of him from the diary of a private of the 6th Company and from research papers left in the Fatui camp.

## background
### LITE
A high-ranking Armory Palace researcher, he was posted with Preobrazhensky to Nod-Krai to research moon fragments, with a Fatui company as guard while recruits gathered materials for the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau.
### STANDARD +
His notes claim he extracted the purest primal light from a moon shard and that the energy, close to a primordial form of life, had been taught to feed by killing and consuming living things, though it showed faint altruistic tendencies he meant to remove. He planned to concentrate it into a pure kuuvahki life form that could plug the gaps Abyssal corruption leaves in fragile Nod-Krai, and expected the credit and rich rewards, such as a share of Port Belovodye's income, for himself.
### LARGE +
The private's diary records that he called the soldiers dim-witted lackeys, then one day held forth on his discovery, his gaunt face flushed in an expression the writer found unnatural. He declared he would tear open the veil of the world and ignite the first spark for the Tsaritsa's wishes.

## relationships
### LITE
His enemy was fellow Armory Palace researcher Preobrazhensky.
### STANDARD +
Both wanted to be first to break through with the moon fragments and win the Tsaritsa's praise, and their quarrel was the camp's only entertainment. Karnatsky openly called a man the diary describes as his daughter's fiance a lunatic, apparently Preobrazhensky, and his own notes mock Preobrazhensky as a pitiful old fool and fae whelp who had questioned the practicality of his work back at the academy.
### LARGE +
Preobrazhensky met one such tirade by going white and reciting a diatribe in the Fae tongue, which the soldier writing the diary could not follow.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Canon shows him as a theoretician and experimenter in kuuvahki and moon-shard energy, not a fighter.

## status
### LITE
Dead before the Traveler arrives; the dying private blames Karnatsky and Preobrazhensky by name.
### STANDARD +
Their experiments awakened the moon fragments in turn and the resulting 'color' consumed the 6th Company; only Private Elena Smirnova is said to have escaped the gorge. After the Colors of Emptiness quest series the whole passage becomes a strong kuuvahki environment.

### ULTRA +
The Design Bureau's archive later holds a casualty and punishment record whose Nothing Passage section the Traveler finds intriguing (Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot quest). Karnatsky's full given name and patronymic is confirmed by a note of Preobrazhensky's.

## entry: Nothing Passage disaster
@keys: Nothing Passage, 6th Company, Private's Notes
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Fatui company was posted at Nothing Passage while Karnatsky and Preobrazhensky quarrelled and experimented. Private Pyotr Orlov's diary records strange drag marks, a wail only one man heard, and unnatural cold and mould before the end. His last note, asking that Smirnova be cleared of desertion, says the two researchers' experiments birthed the catastrophe.
