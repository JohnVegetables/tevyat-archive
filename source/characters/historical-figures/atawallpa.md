## summary
### LITE
Atawallpa was the elder of the hero twins who rose against King Och-Kan about 2,000 years ago, alongside his brother Waskar, the Scarlet-Eyed Youth, Sakkuk, Yupanqui and the Masterful Craftsman. The item texts call him eloquent and a chatterbox.
### STANDARD +
In the rebellion's decisive hour he rekindled his people's longing for past glories. He is known only from the Unfinished Reverie artifacts and speaks nowhere in person; every detail comes from other voices.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the twins' names echo the Inca half-brothers Atahualpa and Huascar; the archive treats this as naming inspiration, not canon.

## background
### LITE
Atawallpa and his younger brother Waskar were the second to join the Scarlet-Eyed Youth, after Sakkuk and before Yupanqui. They were close and liked joking together. Moment of Attainment ties him to the people of the spring's source, the Meztli.
### STANDARD +
Dark Fruit of Bright Flowers describes him as having lips and teeth sharper than any blade, which is the only account of his appearance. When the craftsman's strange visions were recounted over wine, the twins giggled and teased him for it.
### LARGE +
Moment of Attainment gives each hero a role on the day the tribes took back their authority: Sakkuk sent wandering spirits back to the Night Kingdom, Yupanqui broke the shackles on the Saurians, the youth burned the corroded city, and Atawallpa rekindled the desire of all for past glories. Waskar was first to stand against the torrent of darkness. In the wiki's reading the brothers' tribes differ, Atawallpa with the Meztli and Waskar with the Collective of Plenty.
### ULTRA +
When Waskar was killed by their adversary, Atawallpa's screams of despair left his throat hoarse. Sakkuk, the last survivor, later recalled that he too died before her, and mused that she, the frailest of them, had outlived all.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 2,000 years. He died after his brother and before Sakkuk; the cause is not given.
### STANDARD +
No appearance or mention was found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content as of 7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Waskar was his younger twin and partner in jokes; his grief at Waskar's death is the one emotional moment recorded for him. He followed the Scarlet-Eyed Youth with Sakkuk, Yupanqui and the Craftsman. Sakkuk remembered him fondly as an incorrigible chatterbox.
