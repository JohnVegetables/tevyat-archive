## summary
### LITE
Sea Gazer was an adeptus of Liyue who lived long before the present and is dead. He liked to sit and look at the ocean, travelled and dug up treasures, and made the Fantastic Compass, a device for sealing evil monsters. Shenhe says he was very close to Mountain Shaper.
### STANDARD +
He is known by his epithet alone. Liyue's folk histories remember his lost abode in Lisha, which holds his rarest finds, among them a great piece of Sunset Vermillionite sought for the Jade Chamber's rebuilding.
### LARGE +
Everything about him is second-hand: a quest, a cutscene's video caption, and a few remarks from Cloud Retainer and Shenhe. No scene of him alive is shown.

## background
### LITE
By his own words (a line carried in an event video's description) he spent much time gazing at the sea and seldom exerted himself, though he was glad to help others in need. He befriended a mortal thaumaturge and gave him the Fantastic Compass to use in battle; together they exorcised demons with it.
### STANDARD +
He was a skilled explorer who built a collection of treasures found or excavated over time, proud of each one. According to Cloud Retainer, he was usually mild but could be quite a braggart when showing off his newest find at Guizhong's social gatherings at the Guili Assembly, which would prompt Guizhong and Cloud Retainer to bring out a mechanical creation to match it.
### LARGE +
He built an abode in Lisha for his "rarest and most exquisite treasures." Xingqiu, citing "Records of Drifting Clouds," says the abode was abandoned after his death and its location lost. Shenhe adds that he would not have minded someone taking a treasure he no longer needed.
### ULTRA +
In the Interlude Chapter (2.4) the Traveler, Paimon and Shenhe entered the abode, which still had automatic defences powered by adeptal energy, to retrieve Sunset Vermillionite for the Jade Chamber project. During the Lantern Rite event "The Exquisite Night Chimes" (limited-time) the Mingxiao Lantern was modelled on him.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago; the cause and time are not given.
### STANDARD +
His abode in Lisha is abandoned but its defences still work. He is remembered by a few adepti and by scholars who read old records.

## relationships
### LITE
Mountain Shaper (close friend); a human thaumaturge friend who received the Fantastic Compass; Guizhong and Cloud Retainer (hosts of the gatherings he attended).
### STANDARD +
The Compass later reached Yelan's ancestors Boyang and Rongzhao, who used it at The Chasm during the cataclysm. Canon does not say how it travelled from Sea Gazer's friend to them.

## entry: Fantastic Compass
@keys: Fantastic Compass
@version: 2.7
### STANDARD
A magical device made by Sea Gazer to seal away monsters. He gave it to a human friend as a catalyst, and the pair exorcised great demons with it in the mountains. Five hundred years ago Boyang and Rongzhao, ancestors of Yelan, carried it to The Chasm. Used there by a mortal and an adeptus together, it sealed a place beneath The Chasm, and it has been missing since. The Interlude Chapter's wording on it says that when mortal and adeptal powers combine, heaven and earth can be shaken, and that the device proves the two can coexist.

## entry: Sea Gazer's Abode
@keys: Sea Gazer's Abode, Sea Gazer's treasures, Sunset Vermillionite
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
An abandoned adeptus abode in Lisha holding Sea Gazer's rarest treasures, including a massive chunk of Sunset Vermillionite. Defence mechanisms powered by adeptal energy still deter trespassers. Anyone who needed a treasure would not have been blamed for taking it, per Shenhe. At the end of the domain a Seelie settles beside another already at rest in its court.
