## summary
### LITE
Karl Ingold was a Fontainian journalist who lived around the cataclysm. He carried Eduardo Baker's demands to the Court of Fontaine before the Siege of Poisson and left journalism afterwards.
### STANDARD +
He later raised Jakob and Rene after Basil Elton asked him to take them in, and appears only in item texts.

## background
### LITE
Karl grew up with Basil Elton, Emanuel Guillotin and Dwight Lasker. He became a journalist and, when Eduardo Baker took Poisson, volunteered to bring his demands to the Court of Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
He passed between Poisson and the Court many times in those months, carrying hope. The talks failed and the Marechaussee response razed Poisson. Karl was disillusioned and vowed never to work as a journalist again, keeping instead the company of explorers and ruins, though he kept his photographs.
### LARGE +
He photographed Eduardo's group in Poisson before the situation deteriorated. During the siege "Parsifal" used her trick to save him, and he later regretted not recording her final duel with Marfisa. Years afterward Basil Elton invited him to the Narzissenkreuz Institute, where he photographed the members.
### ULTRA +
When the cataclysm threatened to flood the Institute, Basil left Jakob and Rene in the care of trusted people; the wiki identifies Karl as the one who took them and says Jakob took the name Ingold. Rene's notes describe an expedition to the Girdle of the Sands during which Karl foraged nuts for jam.

## relationships
### LITE
Friend of Basil, Emanuel and Dwight since childhood; guardian of Jakob and Rene.
### STANDARD +
Rene's notes record that Karl grew suspicious when Jakob forgot something, and that he was not particularly close to the boys.

## status
### LITE
Unknown. No source says what became of him after the expedition.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
Knows the Poisson standoff first-hand and the Institute's circle. He is not shown to know of the Abyss research Rene and Jakob pursued.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not give Karl a death, a romance or a family beyond Jakob and Rene.
- He is not Jakob's biological father; that is Eduardo Baker.
- Do not make him a member of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the cataclysm: journalist; carries demands between Poisson and the Court of Fontaine.
- The Siege of Poisson; saved by "Parsifal"; retires from journalism.
- Basil invites him to the Institute; he takes a group photograph.
- Around the cataclysm: takes Jakob and Rene; expedition to the Girdle of the Sands.
- 4.0: first named in the Fleuve Cendre Ferryman and Marechaussee Hunter texts.
