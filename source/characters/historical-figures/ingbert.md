## summary
### LITE
Ingbert was a Mondstadt aristocrat of the Aristocratic period, about a thousand years ago. As Landrich's legitimate son and Parsifal's brother, he loved exploration and adventure. He joined an expedition to Dragonspine and was killed there by his half-brother Eberhart.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through the Ancient Investigation Journals, which the household servant Luther wrote on the expedition.

## background
### LITE
He grew up with Luther and his brothers Parsifal and Eberhart. Landrich organised a Dragonspine expedition, partly to dampen desire to rebel against the aristocracy; the party included Ingbert, Eberhart, Luther, Nick and some unnamed members.
### STANDARD +
Ingbert hoped to see a fresco and ancient weapons in Dragonspine's ruins. A snowstorm separated the unnamed members from the rest, and a cave-in before a sealed round door killed Nick and buried the supplies he carried. The survivors returned to the South Base Camp, then went to a cellar in the southwestern ruins as Eberhart proposed.
### LARGE +
In the cellar, Eberhart took out a polearm. Ingbert was wounded but struck back with his Vision, hurting Eberhart badly. He pursued Eberhart and was killed. Luther, injured himself, left a last journal entry blaming Eberhart's ambition as an illegitimate child, and asked that Landrich be warned.

### ULTRA +
Luther's second journal notes that Ingbert especially wanted to see the fresco and the ancient weapons behind the sealed doors, and that the party returned without them. The third journal says Landrich was already planning to give Eberhart the clan name after the expedition, and Luther wonders whether Eberhart knew and killed Ingbert to move up the line of succession regardless. Luther's last wish, addressed to Priscilla, was that someone warn Landrich that Eberhart, also hurt, might still make it back.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed in the ruins of Dragonspine about a thousand years ago.

## relationships
### LITE
Parsifal is his brother; Eberhart, the illegitimate half-brother, killed him; Landrich is his father; Luther was his companion and chronicler.
