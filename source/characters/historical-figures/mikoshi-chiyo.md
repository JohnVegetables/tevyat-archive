## summary
### LITE
An oni warrior of the Mikoshi Clan who fought beside the Raiden Shogun Ei in the cataclysm 500 years ago. Swallowed by a beast of sin, she tore her way out but came back tainted, drew her sword on Ei, and was struck down.
### STANDARD +
Later tradition remembers her as "Chiyo the Tiger-Bite", worn down over the years into "Torachiyo", and often as a male rebel. Her disgrace stained the Mikoshi name, yet Ei still remembers her as a friend.
### LARGE +
Her nightmare outlasted her. In the limited 5.4 event Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival, a severed horn buried at Mt. Yougou was found to carry a fragment of that nightmare, and Ei brought it to rest.
### ULTRA +
She is a minor figure told through scattered item texts and one event, so treat her as a tragic example of what the cataclysm's darkness did to the strong, not as a detailed character.

## background
### LITE
Chiyo was an oni of the Mikoshi Clan, five hundred years ago a valued ally of Ei, the Raiden Shogun. She had a biological son, Michihiro, and fostered another, Nagamasa. Fighting the dark forces of the cataclysm, she was swallowed by a tiger-bodied, snake-tailed beast and escaped by tearing it apart, earning her name. Inside it she saw her comrades torn to pieces and was "stained by a deep sin".
### STANDARD +
Her son's artifact text says she wished to overcome the destiny of life and death and win an eternal name for the thinning blood of the war-oni. She fought under the Electro Mitsudomoe emblem on her back and wore a white juunihitoe as battle dress. Ei had given her a treasured sword. After the beast, nightmares twisted her sight so that family, friends and subordinates looked like enemies or like victims of monsters. On Mt. Yougou she raised that sword against Ei, who cut off her sword-arm and one horn with the Musou no Hitotachi, cutting away part of the mountain itself.
### LARGE +
Earlier, a small tale from the Redhorn Stonethresher text: the eccentric Otogi King wrestled "the petite oni" who was in the mood for a tussle, with results the text calls too tragic to watch: the children remembered the dainty oni hurling him into the sky. After Mt. Yougou, she fled into the forest and vanished. Legends say a tengu, the Shuumatsuban, or a young Iwakura took her for a monster and killed her; another says she met the oni-masked puppet near the giant serpent's corpse; a folktale on an oni mask says she slew herself in a fit of madness. Only the tsuba of the sword Ei gave her was sent back to the Mikoshi.
### ULTRA +
Her fall left the clan in shame. Michihiro retreated to a village, was renamed Iwakura by a tengu named Teruyo, and left the Mikoshi name behind, while Nagamasa worked to restore the family honour. The Mikoshi in Inazuma City today are said to descend from Nagamasa. Mask of the Kijin adds a wider warning: few who fought the Abyss then escaped pitch-dark dreams, and those who slew monsters and became them were not rare.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Described as petite and dainty, "as lovely as the moon" in item text. In the Raiden Shogun teaser she has two large horns, red irises with golden pupils, long grey-purple hair with red highlights and a white juunihitoe with red and pink trim.
### LARGE +
Corrupted, her eyes glow red and her face bears two glowing red scratches on the left side. Ei cut away her sword-arm and one horn; the wiki reads the teaser as showing her right-handed, so the right arm.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Ei (ally, then the one who struck her down); Michihiro (son, who became Iwakura); Nagamasa (foster son); the Kitsune Saiguu and Makoto (friends in the circle around the twins).
### LARGE +
Ei: in the 5.4 event Ei says the nightmare twisted every joy of Chiyo's life into enemies in need of destruction, and that once Chiyo set her sights on a target she never wavered. Ei also says she would not like the name Tiger-Bite. The cinematic shows the nightmare making both sons look like enemies.
### ULTRA +
Yae Miko and Yumemizuki Mizuki worked with Ei in 5.4 to subdue the nightmare tied to her horn; the bake-danuki Tetsunosuke, Kinkaku and Ginkaku had carried it for centuries.

## abilities
### STANDARD
A fierce warrior of the oni, strong enough to tear a tiger-and-serpent beast apart from inside and to challenge Ei. Her sword was a gift from Ei. No Vision is mentioned.

## status
### LITE
Gone for 500 years. Ei says Mikoshi Chiyo is gone; legends disagree on how she died.
### STANDARD +
Only a fragment of her nightmare, tied to a severed horn, remained, until it was put to rest in the limited 5.4 event.
### LARGE +
She does not appear in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) material found. The Mikoshi Clan continues in Inazuma City through Nagamasa's line.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not a man; the "Torachiyo" tales are a distortion of her name.
- Do not state one definitive death; canon leaves it open.
- She is not Michihiro's sister or wife; she is his mother.
- Do not call her Ei's enemy from the start: she was an ally who was twisted.

## chronology
### LARGE
- About 500 years ago: the cataclysm; Chiyo swallowed and corrupted; she attacks Ei on Mt. Yougou, loses an arm and a horn, flees and disappears.
- 2.0: first mentioned in item texts.
- 5.4 (limited): Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival; the nightmare fragment on her horn is calmed and Ei tells her only sweet dreams await.
### ULTRA +
- After her fall: Michihiro leaves for seclusion, later renamed Iwakura; Nagamasa joins the Shogunate.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- The wiki suggests "Torachiyo" echoes the childhood name of a historical Japanese warlord; this is the wiki's etymology, not canon.

## entry: The beast of sin
@keys: beast of sin, tiger's body and a serpent's tail
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The beast that swallowed Chiyo had a tiger's body and a serpent's tail and came from beyond this world. She tore it apart from within, but the darkness inside it marked her. The wiki notes it may be a nue, a monstrous creature of Japanese folklore; canon does not name it.
### LARGE +
Item text hints that corruption of this kind may not be one-directional, since the border between worlds had grown fragile.

## entry: The fight on Mt. Yougou
@keys: Musou no Hitotachi, Mt. Yougou, Chiyo's betrayal
@keys2: Chiyo, oni
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Chiyo, twisted by nightmares, attacked Ei on Mt. Yougou. Ei answered with the Musou no Hitotachi, which severed Chiyo's sword-arm and one horn and cut away part of the mountain itself. Chiyo fled into the forest.
### LARGE +
In 5.4 Ei suspects this is when the horn was buried there.

## entry: The nightmare fragment of the horn (5.4)
@keys: Nightmare Fragment, Nightmare Fragments, Chiyo's horn
@version: 5.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the limited 5.4 event Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival, three bake-danuki who had found the buried horn on Mt. Yougou had come to see humans as monsters. The horn carried a piece of Chiyo's nightmare, which fed on fear and anxiety for nearly 500 years.
### LARGE +
Yae Miko and Mizuki prepared a ritual, the bake-danuki wove a dreamscape to fight illusion with illusion, and the Mikawa Flower Festival served as the lure; the Traveler and Ei fought the fragments in the Plane of Euthymia. In the cinematic Chiyo wakes at a picnic and Ei tells her only sweet dreams await; in the present Ei sits alone as the horn fades.

## entry: The Mikoshi Clan
@keys: Mikoshi Clan, Mikoshi family
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A prominent Inazuman clan with oni blood around the time of the cataclysm. Chiyo's treachery tarnished it; Michihiro left to found the Iwakura Clan. The Mikoshi still in Inazuma City descend from Nagamasa.

## entry: Chiyo's sons
@keys: Michihiro, Iwakura Michihiro, Mikoshi Nagamasa
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Michihiro, her eldest son, secluded himself in a village from shame and was later renamed Iwakura by the tengu Teruyo. Nagamasa, her foster son, became an inspector at Tatarasuna and served the Shogunate to restore the Mikoshi name. See their own entries.
