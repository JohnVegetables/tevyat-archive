## summary
### LITE
Parsifal the Great was the stage name of a Fleuve Cendre thief who became a famous Great Magician in Fontaine more than 500 years ago, performing with her sister, who played the assistant 'Josephine'. She joined Eduardo Baker at Poisson and, after the siege, asked for a trial by combat against the Champion Duelist Marfisa, probably dying in it.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through item texts and has no speaking role.

## background
### LITE
Born in Fleuve Cendre, she and her sister survived by cheating and stealing from the opera house's audience, until the stage's comic knight stories dazzled them. They took their names from one of Coppelius's plays, whose thief Parsifal was a master of appearing and disappearing, and their first shows were in Eduardo Baker's taverns and beside sewers.
### STANDARD +
Their act moved from food stalls to the surface court's theatres, with tricks such as 'Feathers into Butterflies', 'Paradise', 'Fire in the Mirror' and 'The Golden Fish'. Parsifal was the famous one. When Fleuve Cendre was purged, the sister who played Josephine ran back to find destruction and designed a 'grand magic trick' of their own: a speech about a world without aristocrats, kings or swords, and without birth into inescapable poverty.
### LARGE +
Parsifal joined Baker's occupation of Poisson. In the photograph by journalist Karl Ingold she leans on a railing with her stage smile. During the Siege of Poisson she saved his life, and she was later brought to court for her part in it. She requested trial by combat; newspapers spread the news, and the court chose Marfisa, with whom she had a past connection, to represent the prosecution.
### ULTRA +
Ingold, who had quit journalism, wished years later he had recorded the final duel; he remembered a girl using a trick to shift herself to a dark, safe cavern. Lumidouce Elegy says Marfisa remembered the flowers that bloomed under the dazzling spotlights of the magician's act; the sister called Josephine appears there as the assistant on stage.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead, after her last duel more than 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
She is not a character in any quest and was not found in 5.x, 6.x or 7.0-7.1 story content.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her sister Josephine is her stage partner and fellow thief; Eduardo Baker's taverns gave them their start; Marfisa met her as an audience member and faced her later in the arena.
### LARGE +
Baker's slogan about Fleuve Cendre belonging to its people frames the photograph at Poisson, where she stands beside Rosa and Thompson Reed. Karl Ingold knew her, and regretted not recording her last fight.
