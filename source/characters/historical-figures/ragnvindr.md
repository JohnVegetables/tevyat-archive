## summary
### LITE
Ragnvindr, the Dawn Knight, lived in Mondstadt about a thousand years ago under the Aristocracy. A squire to a noble knight, he came to loathe the nobles' neglect of the poor, joined Vennessa's rebellion and later turned the aristocrats' baths into a library. The Ragnvindr Clan, owners of the Dawn Winery, bear his name.
### STANDARD +
He is known through ballads attributed to him and through the texts of items such as The Flute and the Favonius Codex, not through any scene in the game. His title came from a sword-dancing gladiator who inspired him.
### ULTRA +
Different texts give him different roles: a squire in the Ballads of the Squire and The Flute, a rebel in the Flute and Windblume Ode, a library founder in the Favonius Codex, and a winery keeper in a limited-event song description. Present them as one man, with the unresolved joints listed in the uncertainties.

## background
### LITE
Born into the upper ranks of Mondstadt's society during the Aristocratic period, he served as a knight's squire. Watching the poor suffer while his peers boasted of protecting the city left him disillusioned. After witnessing the enslaved Dawnlight Swordswoman's final fight he chose the name Dawn Knight, and when Vennessa rebelled he took her side.
### STANDARD +
The Ballads of the Squire, said to be his, describe patrolling the city at night, hearing the chains of the oppressed and seeing that the knights' proud speeches did not match the street corners. Vol. 2 recalls a dancer in chains, of the Wanderer's Troupe, who answered his question about why she wanted the nobles' fall by asking if he, as one who called the wind his companion, had not once known freedom too. Her last fight in the aristocrats' arena ended short of perfection, and a nameless knight carried her sword from the field and buried it where the gentle winds meet. The Flute's text says he went to the arena with his master and named himself in her honour.
### LARGE +
After Vennessa's rebellion succeeded (with Barbatos' aid), he turned the aristocrats' indoor baths into a library, which grew over time into the largest collection of literature in the northern half of the continent. That library burned once in the Great Fire of Fall Equinox and now holds a fraction of its former collection. The Windblume Ode text, which ends its tale in Decarabian's age, calls him another red-haired knight who would follow the Red-Haired Warrior and bring light to the dark history of the nobility. A song description in the limited Invitation of Windblume event (1.4) says that after the war the sword-wielding knight returned to watch over the winery and tend the orchards.
### ULTRA +
The same text predicts that his clan will always live in darkness and bring forth the flame of dawn. His descendants include Crepus and, through him, Diluc; Kaeya was later adopted into the family. It is not recorded whether the Dawn Winery originated with Ragnvindr.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about a thousand years. His name survives in the Ragnvindr Clan, the Dawn Winery and the ballads attributed to him.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 the Ragnvindr Family still runs the Dawn Winery,.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- The Dawnlight Swordswoman: the gladiator who inspired his title and his turn against the Aristocracy.
- Vennessa: leader of the rebellion he joined.
- The Ragnvindr Clan: his descendants, among them Crepus and Diluc.
### LARGE +
- The Red-Haired Warrior: the earlier rebel whom a Windblume Ode text calls his predecessor in spirit, with the two linked by red hair rather than by a stated family tie.
- The Knights of Favonius: the order that Vennessa founded. Ragnvindr's library appears in its history, but his own membership is not stated.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Aristocratic period, about 1,000 years ago: serves a knight as a squire; sees the Wanderer's Troupe's failed uprising and its swordswoman's enslavement.
- Attends her final match in the arena, buries her with her sword and names himself the Dawn Knight.
- Joins Vennessa's rebellion, which wins with Barbatos' help.
- After the war: converts the baths into a library; tends the Dawn Winery and orchards.
### ULTRA +
- 1.0: first mentioned in the ballads and weapon texts available from release.
- 1.4: Windblume Ode (limited event reward) connects him with the Red-Haired Warrior.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not one of the Knights of Favonius' founders by canon; do not claim he was.
- He did not lead the rebellion. Vennessa did.
- Do not give him a personal romance with the Dawnlight Swordswoman. Canon says she inspired him; it states nothing more.
### LARGE +
- Do not make him the Red-Haired Warrior. They lived 1,600 years apart.
- Do not invent a given name, age or fate for him.
- Do not claim he founded the Dawn Winery; canon leaves its origin open.

## entry: Ballads of the Squire
@keys: Ballads of the Squire, Breaking Dawn
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Two volumes of verse handed down from the Aristocratic period, said to come from Ragnvindr himself. Vol. 1 is a squire's night patrol through a city of luxury and misery, where the poor weep, the knights boast of starlight and the wind carries the grief of the oppressed against the palace walls. Vol. 2 tells of a chained dancer from the Wanderer's Troupe whose silence was a song of freedom, who told her lonely listeners tales of the nobles' divine-powered ancestors, angels, gods and dragons, and whose final sword performance in the arena was cut short.

## entry: The Dawnlight Swordswoman and The Flute
@keys: Dawnlight Swordswoman, Sword Dancer, The Flute, Wanderer's Troupe
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A sword-dancer of the Wanderer's Troupe, who fought the Aristocracy and, after the uprising failed, was enslaved as a gladiator. Her sword, The Flute, sang when swung with skill. Ragnvindr saw her final match, buried her with the sword and took the name Dawn Knight in her honour.

## entry: Mondstadt Library
@keys: Mondstadt library, Great Fire of Fall Equinox, Favonius Codex
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
According to the Favonius Codex text, when the Knights of Favonius was founded, Ragnvindr turned the aristocrats' indoor baths into a library. Gifts from bards, scholars and travellers made it the largest collection in the northern half of the continent. It later burned in the Great Fire of Fall Equinox, so only part of the collection survives; a poplar door in the basement is said to predate the library, and the Knights say the restricted section lies behind it.

## entry: Windblume Ode
@keys: Windblume Ode, secret sign of Windblume
@version: 1.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A weapon text that came as a reward from the limited Invitation of Windblume event (2021). It tells how the Windblume flower became a secret sign among those resisting Decarabian, mentions the Red-Haired Warrior, and then names Ragnvindr as another red-haired knight of the Aristocratic age whose clan "shall ever live in the darkness and bring forth the flame of dawn."

## entry: Ragnvindr Clan and Dawn Winery
@keys: Ragnvindr Clan, Ragnvindr Family, Dawn Winery
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
An influential Mondstadt family named after the Dawn Knight, and one of two known noble families not exiled after the aristocracy fell (with the Gunnhildr Clan). They run the Dawn Winery, Mondstadt's premier winery. Crepus and Diluc are descendants; Kaeya was adopted into the family.
