## summary
### LITE
Katsuragi was a yoriki of the Inazuma Shogunate who lived at Tatarasuna roughly four hundred years ago, serving Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa, who had saved his life. He was known as kind and loyal, and befriended a nameless wandering eccentric (the Kabukimono, later Scaramouche and the Wanderer). After a disaster at the Mikage Furnace he took the blame in Nagamasa's place and was cut down by Nagamasa's own blade.
### STANDARD +
His death is remembered in the name of the sword Katsuragikiri Nagamasa, "Nagamasa who cut Katsuragi". Records of what really happened were suppressed, so for centuries most people knew only that Nagamasa had killed his retainer for some supposed misconduct.
### LARGE +
Almost all that is known of Katsuragi comes second-hand: surviving notes by other Tatarasuna workers, the weapon's description, and the Wanderer's Character Stories. He appears only as a figure in written record and in quest flashback.

## background
### LITE
Little of his life before Tatarasuna is recorded beyond the fact that Nagamasa saved him once, which bound him to Nagamasa in deep loyalty. He served Nagamasa at Tatarasuna and knew some smithing.
### STANDARD
Becoming Nagamasa's yoriki as he rose to Inspector, Katsuragi was posted with him to Tatarasuna, and he talked metalwork late into the night with Vice Armory Officer Miyazaki Kaneo. Nagamasa had joined the Shogunate to wash away the shame his adoptive mother, Mikoshi Chiyo, brought on the Mikoshi name; Katsuragi followed him to the forge town. There they were joined by Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide, Miyazaki, and the workers Kinjiro and Nozomu. Nagamasa took up weaponsmithing, and his temper was seen to soften; with Katsuragi's and Miyazaki's help he forged a nagamaki called the Daitatara Nagamasa, his great achievement. Nozomu, moved by its beauty, painted it, and Katsuragi performed a sword dance with the Kabukimono.
### LARGE +
While investigating a landslide caused by Crystal Marrow miners, Katsuragi found the Shakkei Pavilion and the nameless young man inside it. He brought him to Tatarasuna and introduced him to Nagamasa. Seeing the small golden feather the stranger carried, he judged that the man was no ordinary person and had reasons to keep his origin hidden, so he left out the pavilion and said instead that the man had been found at Nazuchi Beach, asking him to play along. The Tatarasuna folk then taught the newcomer to read, write, cook and forge.
A colleague's note says that Katsuragi may have committed wrongdoing, but out of the goodness of his heart.
### ULTRA +
In "The Isle of Darkling Clouds", an unfinished novel by the Inazuman writer Sawada read in the 3.3 quest, Katsuragi is a warm, slightly flustered presence among friends: he groused good-naturedly to Niwa and Miyazaki when asked how Nagamasa's blade was coming, unwilling either to shame his master or lie to his two friends; he urged the shy newcomer to eat his share; and when the furnace crisis worsened he held Nagamasa back from the flames. The game itself calls the novel fantastical, so these scenes are fiction within the world, not surviving records.

## relationships
### LITE
Nagamasa is the one who matters: savior, master, and in the end the man whose blade killed him. The Kabukimono, whom he rescued and sheltered, remembered him as a friend.
### STANDARD +
Niwa Hisahide and Miyazaki Kaneo were friends and colleagues at the forge. Kinjiro and Nozomu worked there too; Nozomu died after retrieving the melted blade from the furnace, and Kinjiro hid the blade's remains and Nozomu's painting in the Arsenal. The surviving Tatarasuna notes speak well of Katsuragi and of Nagamasa alike.
### LARGE +
Katsuragi was the first at Tatarasuna to show the Kabukimono kindness, which is why the Wanderer's Character Stories mention him by name when recounting the town's tragedy.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about four hundred years, cut down at Tatarasuna by Nagamasa. As of 7.1 nothing changes this; no new appearance was found in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.
### STANDARD +
The sword that killed him was thrown into the furnace afterwards. His memory survives chiefly as an epithet: both the blade and Nagamasa came to be called Katsuragikiri Nagamasa.
### LARGE +
Because Niwa imposed an information blackout during the disaster, few records of the Tatarasuna Mystery exist, and most Inazumans only knew that Nagamasa had slain his yoriki. The fuller truth surfaced in The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call (3.3); afterwards the Wanderer, believing history could be changed, used his remaining power to erase himself from Irminsul (The Kabukimono's Finale). Wiki summaries say the record was rewritten so that a foreign mechanic stands where the Kabukimono was; Katsuragi's role and death are described as otherwise unchanged.

## entry: Tatarasuna Mystery
@keys: Tatarasuna Mystery
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Tatarasuna Mystery is the name for the events at the Mikage Furnace about 400 years ago. A catastrophe at the furnace killed many and left Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide missing; it was widely thought that he had fled in disgrace. That placed responsibility on Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa, next in rank. Katsuragi offered himself as the one to blame, and Nagamasa, who needed to live to restore the Mikoshi name, struck him down with the Daitatara Nagamasa and threw the blade into the furnace. Nozomu pulled the melted remains out and died of his burns; Kinjiro hid them, with Nozomu's painting of the blade, in the Arsenal.
### LARGE +
The Wanderer's Character Stories attribute Niwa's death to the foreign mechanic Escher (in truth The Doctor), whose persuasion pinned the incident on the local overseer. Only Katsuragi's part is summarised here; see Niwa Hisahide for the rest.

## entry: Daitatara Nagamasa
@keys: Daitatara Nagamasa, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Daitatara Nagamasa was a nagamaki that Nagamasa forged at Tatarasuna with Katsuragi's and Miyazaki's help. The sword was renamed Katsuragikiri Nagamasa after it killed Katsuragi. In the game it survives as a forgeable four-star claymore whose description calls it a heavy, tough blade once made in Tatarasuna.
### LARGE +
The notes by Tatarasuna workers describe the day it was completed as a joyful one, with Nagamasa in high spirits, Nozomu drawing the sword and Katsuragi dancing with the Kabukimono.

## entry: Katsuragi and the Kabukimono
@keys: Kabukimono
@keys2: Katsuragi, Tatarasuna, Nagamasa
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The nameless young man whom Katsuragi found in the Shakkei Pavilion went by "Kabukimono", an Inazuman word for an eccentric stranger. Katsuragi covered for his unusual origin with the story of Nazuchi Beach, and the people of Tatarasuna accepted him as a friend. He is the future Scaramouche, later the Wanderer, and the happiest memories of his early life are tied to this town.
