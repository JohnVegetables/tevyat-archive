## summary
### LITE
Erinnyes was the High King of Aremorica in Remuria's last years. A former singer, she led the northern tribes against the empire and founded the Lochknights.
### STANDARD +
She claimed to hear the goddess Egeria and sought the Pure Grail to save her people from the prophecy of dissolution. Egeria's answer ended her quest.

## background
### LITE
Erinnyes came from the same tribe as Cassiodor. A Remurian army slaughtered it, leaving only the two of them. Cassiodor went over to Remuria, while she was taken in by the chieftain of Aremorica to guard the waters the God King had not yet seized.
### STANDARD +
She became High King of Aremorica. Servius notes that nobody knew how, as she was not the heir of Cunoricus, the earlier chieftain who surrendered to Remuria. She said she was an oracle who heard Egeria, united Fontaine's warring tribes by making them pledge to Egeria, and led them against Remuria. Remurians saw her as a madwoman and atrocity-committing rebel.
### LARGE +
She and her knights swore never to draw swords against those who pledged to Egeria, never to compromise with evil and to protect the oceanids; they became known as the Lochknights. An oceanid messenger told her of the prophecy that Fontainians would be dissolved in the Primordial Sea for their original sin, and of the Pure Grail, which grants wishes and washes away sin. She searched for the Grail and the means to free Egeria from her prison and did not find it.
### ULTRA +
Remus sent Cassiodor in disguise to Aremorica, where he won a fighting tournament and gained an audience with her. His identity was discovered and the artificial body his soul was in was cut to pieces; as his consciousness was about to return she held him and said she would free him and had not forgotten their oath. She razed his palace at Petrocorii (now Petrichor). After Remuria sank and Celestia freed Egeria to rule Fontaine as Hydro Archon, Erinnyes asked her how Fontainians could avoid their fate. Egeria said that she herself was responsible for the original sin, having created humans from oceanids. Erinnyes threw down her sword, left the valley and was never seen again.

## relationships
### LITE
Cassiodor (sworn companion turned enemy), Egeria (the goddess she served and questioned), Remus (enemy).
### STANDARD +
The Lochknights followed her. Her ally was the Aremorican chieftain who took her in.

## status
### LITE
Fate unknown. She left the valley after meeting Egeria and was never seen again.
### STANDARD +
The island and forest named Erinnyes are probably named after her. A play and the book La Chanson d'Erinnyes retell her life, the book with romantic liberties.

## abilities
### STANDARD
A leader, singer and warrior with no demonstrated supernatural power. Her scepter Splendor of Tranquil Waters is a relic of authority over the seas, according to its description.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Early life: tribe destroyed by a Remurian army.
- Raised by Aremorica; becomes High King; unites the tribes.
- War with Remuria; Pure Grail quest; tournament and Cassiodor's capture.
- After Remuria sank: meets Egeria; abandons her sword; vanishes.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not present her as a proven oracle; canon preserves the doubt.
- Her real name may be a title, Errighenth.
- La Chanson d'Erinnyes is a romanticised version of her end.
- The location Erinnyes Forest is separate from her.

## entry: Cassiodor

### STANDARD
Her tribe-mate and sworn companion. He once pinned a butterfly wing on her ear, which in their people's custom means a promise not to part until death. After their tribe was killed he joined Remuria under Boethius and later became the Remurian ruler's servant. When he was cut down in Aremorica she told him she had not forgotten the oath.

### STANDARD
A grail that can grant any wish and wash away sin. An oceanid messenger told Erinnyes of it as a way to save Fontainians from the prophecy. The Lochknights swore to find it; she never did.
