## summary
### LITE
Monomakh Snezhnyi, the Belyi Tsar ("White Tsar"), united the northern fae, founded the Pale Star Court and became the first Cryo Archon and God of Love. He died fighting the Abyss in the cataclysm 500 years ago; the Tsaritsa Anastasya succeeded him.
### STANDARD +
He thought he was a fae, but he was an incomplete Primordial Human that Hyperborea made from the Cryo Dragon's remains. He secretly worked to complete himself and overthrow the Heavenly Principles, and he admitted opening the gate through which the Abyss seeps into the world.
### LARGE +
Snezhnaya remembers him as a conqueror and a merciful "father". His own notes show a ruler racked by guilt, who took the throne Celestia offered so that the sacrifices of the forgotten would not be wasted. His research institute, Hvergelmir, and his foundling ward Aksinya carried the plan on after his death. In 7.0 the Traveler stopped that plan in the ruins of his palace.

## appearance
### LARGE
Fae who met him describe a handsome man of ordinary human height. Weapon lore (Breezeborne Refrain) gives the young Tsar sky-blue eyes. Among fae he took a fae form; his own body was something else. His true body lay under the Pale Crown Palace for five centuries and kept growing into the "Tsar's White Birch", a pale, tree-like mass of "nerves" that blocked doors and spread through the ruins. The faces of Hvergelmir's Proto-Man, which became the Eye of Graeae, were modelled on a painting of the Tsar as a child. His spear, Frostbreath, was a weapon of solid ice.

## personality
### LARGE
His own notes show a scholar's patience and a heavy conscience. He went back into the Shadow Realm hundreds of times, revised his conclusions as evidence came in, and wished he alone could bear the sin of the opened passage. He saw duty as his reason for existing: "this has always been my calling".

His mercy was real but political. He returned slain enemy dukes' bodies to their clans, spared a defeated human clan instead of enslaving it, and forgave Zver for disobeying its creator. Yet he also excused Zver's unauthorised execution of a human noble who had seen Hvergelmir's secrets, because it spared his researchers trouble. Early on he despised Hyperborea's "usurpers" and the prophet Anacharsis as deluded fools. Later he came to see them as people who had fought a doom decreed long before. He wanted the sacrifices of the forgotten remembered in "the songs of the people". The Tsaritsa, who knew him, says she never much trusted him.

## background
### LITE
Born from the Cryo Dragon's remains, he trained under Yalyane Morevna, founded Kitezh and killed King Miscislav of the Volkodlak, after which all fae clans swore to him. He later admitted humans, ruled as Cryo Archon, and fell against the Abyss in Khaenri'ah.
### STANDARD +
Hyperborea's experiment to make a successor to Nibelung from the dragon's flesh ran wild, froze Snezhnaya into Holy Soil and ended in Hyperborea's fall. A thousand years later he hatched, incomplete. He wandered the north for centuries with companions such as the shapeshifter Filipov, his "Synaimos" (sworn comrade). Miscislav's war on Kitezh ended when the Tsar's thrown spear killed him and two Grand Dukes; Monomakh then took the titles "lord of all fae" and Belyi Tsar.
### LARGE +
The war: seventy-six years after Kitezh's founding, his envoys told the Vila to stop paying Miscislav tribute. Miscislav answered with an ultimatum thirty times larger, and the Emperor chose war rather than submit. Over three battles at Kitezh, his spear, thrown like an arrow, killed Miscislav and the Grand Dukes Skoldyryt and Vodyanoy.

The passage and the throne: in the time of the Pale Star Court he incited Anastasya, then a herald of his court, and the Third Descender Saarelainen to open a gate to the Abyss. The Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya. Saarelainen traded his life for her return, and his remains became the Gnoses. Because the Tsar had fled into the Shadow Realm, he alone kept his memories of Saarelainen, and he later told Anastasya the story. Inside the Shadow Realm he found the ruins of Hyperborea, and over hundreds of forays he learned what the fae and he himself truly were. At last, around the end of the Archon War, he accepted Celestia's offer of the Cryo throne and the Gnosis. In his own words, he did so to keep the sacrifices of "the survivors and the forgotten" from being in vain. He moved his seat from Kitezh to Snezhnograd and admitted humans to his realm, an act fae chroniclers call "pardoning" humanity.
### ULTRA +
His later reign: in a "pioneering age" he had Nod-Krai explored and industrialised with cities, ports and factories, and he raised the Solovyov family of spies to the gentry. The Khaenri'ahn exile Rauthvangi, calling himself Professor Rotwang, became his favourite at court. In the Shadow Realm he found a Hyperborean life-preservation capsule that still worked, with a living infant inside: Aksinya. He raised her and later confided his dream to her. He founded the Hvergelmir institute and charged Aksinya, Alvis, Pavlina and Koshchei to "seek the stairway leading to the vacant seat in the court of the stars". The goal was a Proto-Man who could complete him as the Primordial Human and bring about the Court of Eternal Bliss, a world of unchanging, everlasting love. His last notes say the passage had opened anew and that the world inside the "celestial shell" might end within centuries. He set out for war, telling Aksinya he would return victorious with Zver at his side. He led fae and chimeric monsters into Khaenri'ah against the Abyss and was killed there. Zver, badly wounded, dragged his body back. Filipov abandoned the army when the news came, and Anastasya Feodorovna was crowned Tsaritsa in the first year of the 799th Cycle.

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Anastasya (the Tsaritsa): once his court herald, then his victim in the Abyss-gate affair, then his successor; she never much trusted him.
- Aksinya: the Hyperborean foundling he raised, his confidant and head of Hvergelmir.
- Zver: the spared chimeric creature who served him in life and guarded his corpse for 500 years.
- Filipov: his shapeshifter Synaimos and first Commander of the Druzhna.
- Yalyane Morevna: his spear teacher; Miscislav: his greatest enemy.
### LARGE +
- Saarelainen, the Third Descender, whose sacrifice became the Gnoses and whom only the Tsar remembered.
- Rotwang (Rauthvangi), the alchemist-astrologer favourite whom fae nobles called the "mad monk". Rotwang thought the Tsar's ambition too small, saying he sought only to rule all living things beneath the moon and never looked to the stars.
- Koshchei, Alvis and Pavlina, the fae lords of Hvergelmir.
- Vaselisa von Strivozha, the saint of Veneter, who fed his righteous anger with staged tears while keeping her own counsel.
### ULTRA +
- Nepla Korolevitsna, the human warrior whose clan he spared and settled at what became Port Belovodye; she married Filipov.
- Grand Duke Seriviiki of Cirnareka, a former enemy whom he later honoured with a ceremonial tap of the sword.
- The Solovyov family, whom he ennobled; Solovei later founded the Lightkeepers.
- An unnamed old acquaintance (blotted out in his notes) who was in the Hyperborean ruins before him and could not tell him what he knew.

## abilities
### LARGE
He held Cryo authority and, as Archon, the Gnosis. He was a spearman of legendary strength: his thrown spear could kill enemy commanders across a battlefield, and legend says he once hurled Filipov over seven mountains. He could travel in the Shadow Realm, which hid him from the Heavenly Principles' judgment and protected his memories from Irminsul's edits. Frostbreath's text says that even with all his might, his throw reached only half of Snezhnaya, short of the spear his teacher described. He realised late in life that she had probably meant firearms. His body was a Hyperborean creation with "a soul bestowed through light itself". It stayed biologically alive after his consciousness died, which is why his body could later become the Primordial Human.

## status
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Dead since the cataclysm 500 years ago. His body lay in the Pale Crown Palace, guarded by Zver, and slowly grew into the Tsar's White Birch.
### STANDARD +
In 7.0 the Traveler recovered the Eye of Graeae's power fragments, including one at the palace, where they defeated Zver. Aksinya then used the Tsar's body to create the Primordial Human, "Svarod, Velikan of Light", with the Proto-Man's consciousness from the Eye of Graeae as its core. The Traveler, aided by the now-empty Eye of Graeae, defeated it. Afterwards the giant form was shed: the Eye's former will, now in an ordinary body bearing the young Tsar's appearance, fell into the Shadow Realm with Aksinya, and the Tsar's body was gone from the palace. The Tsar himself did not return.
### LARGE +
In 7.1 Rhinedottir confirmed to Anastasya that the Tsar had incited the Abyss gate and that his account of the Third Descender was probably true. The Tsaritsa answered that the past mattered less to her than living in the present. Snezhnaya's Snowland Fae, once his ruling aristocracy, have lost most of their former influence under the Tsaritsa.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He was not a fae, though he and everyone else thought so for most of his life.
- He did not conquer the north single-handedly: fae histories credit Filipov's spying, Alvis's strategy and the whole empire's war effort.
- Do not make him Anastasya's father or lover; the "daughter of Pakkaisukko" hymn is a hostile legend.
- Do not name the acquaintance blotted out in his notes, or say who killed him.
- He is dead; the Primordial Human that the Traveler fought in 7.0 used his body, not his mind.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Hyperborea's Primordial Human experiment on the Cryo Dragon's remains runs out of control; Hyperborea falls.
- A thousand years later he hatches from the remains; he is trained by Yalyane Morevna and wanders the north with Filipov.
- 4th year of the 417th Cycle: he founds Kitezh, the first city of Snezhnaya.
- 3rd-7th years of the 428th Cycle: the Vila tribute dispute and the war with Miscislav; he becomes the Belyi Tsar.
- During the Pale Star Court: he incites the opening of the Abyss gate; the Gnoses are created; he begins his forays into the Shadow Realm.
- About 2,000 years ago: he becomes Cryo Archon, opens his realm to humans and moves his seat to Snezhnograd.
- Later: the pioneering age in Nod-Krai; Rotwang at court; Aksinya found; Hvergelmir founded.
- 500 years ago: he dies in Khaenri'ah fighting the Abyss; in the first year of the 799th Cycle Anastasya is crowned.
- 7.0: his body becomes the Primordial Human and is defeated; 7.1: his role in the Abyss gate is confirmed to the Tsaritsa.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- "Belyi" means white. After his victory over Miscislav, the fae chronicle has him call himself "heir to the Pale Star, and protector of all fae".
- A tale passed down among the fae says he defeated an undying enemy whose life was hidden in an egg, reached through a door in the eye of a hare inside a raven perched on a downward-growing tree. The glider text that tells it (Wings of the Silent Moon) notes that wind gliders were invented long after his age, so the tale may have been made up to explain the glider.
- The Frostmoon Scions remember him as "Pakkaisukko" and curse his "poisonous plot" at Pohjola.

## entry: The Abyss gate and the Third Descender
@keys: Belyi Tsar, Monomakh, Pakkaisukko
@keys2: Saarelainen, Third Descender, Abyss gate, passage, Pohjola, Gnoses
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the days of the Pale Star Court, the Belyi Tsar incited his herald Anastasya and the Third Descender Saarelainen to open a gate to the Abyss. Rhinedottir calls this the source of a millennium of Abyssal disasters. Anastasya was erased by the Heavenly Principles; Saarelainen bargained his life for her return, and his remains became the seven Gnoses.
### LARGE +
The Tsar's own notes admit he "foolishly" opened the passage. He describes the Shadow Realm as a film between Teyvat and the Abyss, through which corruption has seeped ever since. Hidden in that realm, he kept the memories of Saarelainen that Irminsul erased from everyone else, and he later told Anastasya, to whom the story felt unreal. A Frostmoon Scion prayer tells a hostile version in which "old Pakkaisukko" promised Saarelainen his daughter's hand if he opened Pohjola's gates.

## entry: Hyperborea and the Primordial Human
@keys: Belyi Tsar, Monomakh
@keys2: Primordial Human, Hyperborea, Cryo Dragon, Proto-Man, Narayana, Court of Eternal Bliss
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hyperborea tried to make a Primordial Human from the Cryo Dragon's remains to fill the seat left by Nibelung. The project ran wild and froze Snezhnaya into Holy Soil, and a thousand years later Monomakh hatched from the remains, incomplete. In the Shadow Realm ruins he learned the truth and accepted the mission. He had Hvergelmir build a Proto-Man as the "Key" to complete him. His aim was the Court of Eternal Bliss, a world of unchanging love free of sorrow.
### LARGE +
In his notes he calls himself "the posthumous child of that primordial first light", born from the same light Anacharsis came to Hyperborea to seek. He concluded that only by ascending to "the seat high up in the court of the stars" could total destruction be averted. After his death Aksinya pursued the plan; in 7.0 it produced the Primordial Human, which the Traveler defeated.

## entry: The war with Miscislav
@keys: Miscislav, Battle of Kitezh, Volkodlak Alliance, Miscislavli
@keys2: Belyi Tsar, Monomakh, Pale Star
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
After decades of peace, Emperor Monomakh's envoys persuaded the Vila to stop paying tribute to King Miscislav. Miscislav demanded thirty times that tribute from the Pale Star Court. Monomakh refused, telling his people that giving in would only bring a greater war on their children. Miscislav's host attacked Kitezh, and in three battles Monomakh's thrown spear killed Miscislav and the Grand Dukes Skoldyryt and Vodyanoy. He returned their bodies to their clans, every fae clan then submitted, and he named himself the Belyi Tsar. Miscislav's bear skull, pierced by the spear, is kept in the Zapolyarny Palace Treasury.

## entry: Zver and the Pale Crown Palace
@keys: Zver, Pale Crown Palace, Tsar's White Birch
@keys2: Belyi Tsar, Tsar, Monomakh
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zver was a discarded prototype of the alchemist Koshchei, a two-headed winged lion with a mind of its own. The Tsar spared it and named it "Beast". It led his chimeric monsters against the Abyss and carried his body home from Khaenri'ah. For five centuries it guarded the corpse in the Pale Crown Palace, which became his mausoleum, until the Traveler defeated it in 7.0. Dying, it asked to follow its master at last.

## entry: Aksinya and Hvergelmir
@keys: Hvergelmir, Court Jester
@keys2: Belyi Tsar, Monomakh, Aksinya
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aksinya was a Hyperborean infant whom the Tsar found alive in a life-preservation capsule in the Shadow Realm. He raised her at court, gave her a pair of earrings when she was grown, and in return told her of his dream. She led the Hvergelmir institute with the fae lords Alvis, Pavlina and Koshchei. When he left for his last war, she promised to have everything ready by the time he came back victorious.

## entry: Filipov, the Tsar's Synaimos
@keys: Filipov, Filipov Vseslavyevich, Synaimos
@keys2: Belyi Tsar, Monomakh
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Filipov, a fae shapeshifter, first scorned the young Monomakh and challenged him. He changed shape after shape; each time Monomakh threw him over more mountains, up to seven. Moved by his purity of heart, Filipov became his sworn comrade, chief strategist and the first Commander of the Druzhna. Filipov later grew trusting of Rotwang after the monk summoned his dead wife's phantom, and stopped warning the Tsar against him. He deserted the army on hearing of the Tsar's death and has not been seen since.
