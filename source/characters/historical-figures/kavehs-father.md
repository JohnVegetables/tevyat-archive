## summary
### LITE
An unnamed scholar of the Rtawahist Darshan at the Sumeru Akademiya, husband of the architect Faranak and father of Kaveh. About twenty years before the game he took part in the Interdarshan Championship, fell short, and went into the desert, where he died in an accident.
### STANDARD +
Kaveh remembers him as kind and generous, a man who promised to bring something nice back for him. Faranak says he was a good-hearted man who made her happy. His death shaped Kaveh's guilt, his architecture-loving childhood and his habit of refusing help.

## background
### LITE
He met Faranak over thirty years before the game, when she was a young architect often quarrelling with clients and had many admirers. He won her over by supporting her despite not understanding her art. They had Kaveh, bought him block puzzles and a sketchbook; he also showed Kaveh how to make fatteh.
### STANDARD +
When Kaveh, still very young, told him about the Interdarshan Championship and the unusual Diadem of Knowledge, he said he might win it so that Kaveh could play with it for a few days. He became a favourite but missed the title "by a hair", returned home deeply depressed, and soon left for the desert, saying he wanted to investigate something there. News of his death in an accident followed.
### LARGE +
Kaveh's Character Story says the family home was a place where "words were not necessary". After the death his wife could not draw or smile and Kaveh grew up bearing the guilt of having mentioned the contest. His mother later left for Fontaine for work; Kaveh enrolled in Kshahrewar and lived alone.
### ULTRA +
Kaveh's later memories: he sees his father's laugh and promise to bring something nice back every time he closes his eyes. A book of Faranak's that Kaveh keeps has a hidden page with a drawing of a person falling into quicksand and a note asking a father's forgiveness (Kaveh's Character Story). The wiki cites the Hangout 'A Whole New Style' (3.7) for how the letter was lost, and notes that Kaveh's Specialty Dish, The Endeavor, is derived from the fatteh his father taught him.

## personality
### LITE
Kind, warm and generous to a fault; idealistic enough to be called an idealist in the past, and quietly serious about suffering he could not fix.
### STANDARD +
Kaveh says he was "always too kind for his own good". He wrote, unfinished, to his son even while in the desert, and he supported others without needing to understand them.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about twenty years, killed in an accident in the desert. No remains or grave are mentioned.
### STANDARD +
His belongings did not reach his family; the unfinished letter was lost. His memory survives in Kaveh, in Faranak's drawing (a blond man pictured with three other men) and in the stories of Aaru Village.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Kaveh is his son and carries the weight of his death; Faranak is his wife; Rishti remembers him in Aaru Village; Arakasyapa is an Aranara he once taught to write; Sachin's research is what the event quests suggest unsettled him.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Never invent his name, age or appearance beyond the blond, neatly styled hair in Faranak's drawing.
- Kaveh did not cause the death; it was an accident, and Kaveh's guilt is his own conclusion.
- He is not Sachin, though the wiki notes parallels; do not merge them.
- He died before Kaveh's Vision or the Palace of Alcazarzaray; the father never saw Kaveh's career.

## entry: The Interdarshan Championship

@version: 3.6

### STANDARD
Twenty years before Chapter III the Akademiya held the Interdarshan Championship, whose prize was the Diadem of Knowledge. Kaveh's father, a favourite, did not win; during the 3.6 event A Parade of Providence (limited), Kaveh recalls him coming home "extremely depressed" and later leaving to investigate the desert. The event implies, but does not show, that he met the consciousness fragment of the researcher Sachin stored in the diadem. Kaveh later shattered the diadem.

## entry: Aaru Village and the lost letter

@keys2: Kaveh's father
@version: 3.6

### STANDARD
In the limited event, Rishti of Aaru Village says a young man from the Akademiya taught villagers to cope with Wenut and monsters, bought medicine with his own money and nursed the sick. He often frowned, told children of teaching a strange creature to write, explored the desert and asked after the Nagarjunites. He later died in quicksand after finding a caravan trapped there, saving many people. His half-finished letter to his child was taken by Rishti's son, who was killed years later, and the letter was lost.

## entry: Arakasyapa
@keys: Arakasyapa
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Arakasyapa is an Aranara whom Kaveh's father taught to read and write as a child. In the event quests it hides the Diadem of Knowledge in a tree hollow after the final round, leaving a note that it wishes to protect the man who taught it, then vanishes. The wiki and quests suggest, rather than state, that this man is Kaveh's father.
