## summary
### LITE
Alvis was the Grand Duke of the Domovoy, a very old fae who ruled the mining city of Kitezhgrad, served the Belyi Tsar from before the unification of Snezhnaya, and mentored the human girl Aksinya. He is now dead.
### STANDARD +
He was general and councillor to the Belyi Tsar, lord of the city that held Snezhnaya's mines, and a member of the Hvergelmir circle. In the end he betrayed Aksinya and paid for it.

## background
### LITE
A long-lived Domovoy, Alvis joined Monomakh Snezhnyi when Kitezhgrad was founded and soon controlled its mining. He helped win the Tsar's war against King Miscislav, later took the human foundling Aksinya as pupil, and after Kitezhgrad was destroyed in the cataclysm 500 years ago he founded Novokitezhgrad.
### STANDARD +
During the Miscislavli war he lured Grand Duke Vodyanoy of Dalegrad and his army away from the advantage of the Frozen Lake, which let the Tsar slay Vodyanoy. After the Tsar moved his seat to Snezhnograd, pardoned humanity and invited the best minds of both peoples to court, Alvis took part in the Hvergelmir project to reach a "vacant seat" among the stars. He remained a fae who feared and resented humans, yet set that aside for the Tsar's dream.
### LARGE +
The Frostmoon Scions' quest records an accusation, spoken by Alia, that he and the Khaenri'ahn researcher Rotwang laid a trap that drove the officer Avrelian out of the Royal Military Academy and into banditry so that Avrelian would steal the Sampo Mill. When Kitezhgrad was destroyed by the dark tide, Alvis was expected to be consumed by building the new capital, and Izmaylov used that time to reach the new sovereign first and take over the Energy Commission. Under the Tsaritsa's regime Alvis, himself of the old fae nobility, kept up in council a feud with Izmaylov.
### ULTRA +
When the Hvergelmir group built the Eye of Graeae, the Eye saw that Aksinya's final plan would be ruinous and persuaded Alvis, Koshchei and Pavlina to help divide the Primordial Human's body and cast Aksinya into the Shadow Realm. Aksinya later escaped with Izmaylov's help. In the modern day she came to Alvis, retrieved a piece of the Eye (the Pebble of Crystalline Essence) from him, and ensured he met an unwelcome fate.

## relationships
### LITE
The Belyi Tsar, whom he served; Aksinya, his pupil and later victim; Izmaylov, his Domovoy rival.
### STANDARD +
He disliked Aksinya when she was a foundling and came to feel an elder's warmth toward her, freely sharing what he knew and advising her. Izmaylov, a younger Domovoy he looked down on, outmanoeuvred him more than once.
### LARGE +
The Tsaritsa was merciless toward the fae who once held real power, and Alvis answered her regime with caution; he used the building of Novokitezhgrad as the cornerstone of his standing.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Canon shows him as a strategist and administrator rather than a fighter: a cunning commander in the unification wars, and a lord who held a city's mining industry for centuries. No elemental powers are described.

## status
### LITE
Dead as of 7.1, killed in the present day after Aksinya returned from the Shadow Realm.
### STANDARD +
A note left by Aksinya says Alvis paid the price for defiling the sacred form and that the flesh and blood the covetous Domovoy took was recovered from Novokitezhgrad.

## entry: Izmaylov rivalry
@keys: Izmaylov, Foundryman, Royal Energy Commission
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Alvis and the Domovoy Izmaylov, who led the Royal Energy Commission, argued bitterly in every council session, so convincingly that some thought it staged. Over the railway's Transport Bureau, Alvis wanted a noble fae at its head while Izmaylov, who knew the Tsaritsa's wishes, listed only humans; Alvis stepped back to wait for Izmaylov to fail against the Burner of Cities. The young Domovoy instead forged weapons for the Luchkin, helped crush the rebellion and founded the High Table.
### LARGE +
Earlier, Izmaylov had taken advantage of Alvis's absence in Novokitezhgrad: when the Grand Duke returned, the Energy Commission had a new master.

## entry: Hvergelmir and the betrayal of Aksinya
@keys: Hvergelmir, Eye of Graeae, Koshchei, Pavlina
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Hvergelmir circle gathered under the Belyi Tsar to seek the vacant seat in the court of the stars. Alvis toasted Aksinya as its triumphant leader, though his old distrust of humans had never entirely left him. Later the Eye of Graeae, speaking as the Primordial Human, persuaded Alvis, Koshchei and Pavlina to divide him and throw Aksinya into the Shadow Realm.
