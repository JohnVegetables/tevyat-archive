## summary
### LITE
Huitzilin was a legendary shaman and scroll-weaver of the Masters of the Night-Wind, who lived about two hundred years ago. She mastered using colour as a medium for memory, and her technique became the backbone of how the tribe weaves its history into scrolls. She was Citlali's fellow student, rival and friend.
### STANDARD +
She is met only through voice, flashback and scroll depictions in Citlali's quests, never in the present day.

## background
### LITE
She and Citlali studied together as young shamans. Huitzilin repeatedly challenged Citlali and lost, then began bringing snacks on later visits; their twenty-year contest ended in a draw. Her saurian companion was a young iktomisaurus called Claw, later known as Elder Tlapo.
### STANDARD +
Huitzilin held that memory is the strongest weapon against the Abyss, since Abyssal foes are those who lost the capacity for memory, love and compassion. She found the medium for it in colour: the more cherished a memory, the more vivid its colour, and colour fades first as memories vanish. In the Battle of the Seven Colors she used this to build a maze of memory that trapped the Abyssal forces and brought her tribe victory.
### LARGE +
In later life she took many disciples. As training she had them leave provocative graffiti outside Citlali's home, a challenge Citlali could not ignore; Huitzilin did it out of admiration, believing only those brave enough to face Citlali deserved her legacy. No student ever won, yet those who tried often went on to remarkable work, and her disciples and descendants kept up the custom after her death.

Her body of work was so large that she divided it among different disciples, so no one student holds every technique, and some knowledge has been lost with the decline of certain shaman families, such as Etchemin's. The current shamans Aywastin, Besuci, Caholom and Chogan are counted among her school's practitioners.
### ULTRA +
In a flashback in The Color of Memory she is old and ill and tells Citlali she has no time for another contest. She says the tribe believes death is the start of a new existence in the Night Kingdom, a place made of memories, and explains why woven scrolls are coloured orange (so the dead can feel the setting sun) and studded with grainfruit kernels (so they can enjoy the harvest). Citlali later recalls this while weaving.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about two centuries. Her legacy lives on in the woven scrolls of the Masters of the Night-Wind, the colour-memory technique Citlali carries on, and the memory maze of the Seven-Hued Ruins. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 appearance was found.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A woman with fair skin, long braided blonde hair and brown eyes, wearing Masters of the Night-Wind shaman attire. Woven scrolls show her with a circlet and glowing ginger hair. In old age she has shorter beige hair and the same attire and circlet.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Citlali is the heart of her story: rival, friend and heir to her technique. Huitzilin's challenge tradition kept their bond alive after her death. Her companion Claw, as Elder Tlapo, remains a figure of the tribe.

### LARGE
In the Battle of the Seven Colors, Huitzilin turned colour into a maze of memories that trapped Abyssal forces: without the capacity for memory, they could not find their way across. The tribe won. Chief Biram believes a scroll depicting the battle was her own work. The Seven-Hued Ruins preserve a space of this kind, with a trace of her consciousness in it.
