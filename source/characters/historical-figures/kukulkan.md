## summary
### LITE
A dragon prince of ancient Natlantea known to humans as the Sage of the Stolen Flame. He brought Phlogiston to the tribes, grew disillusioned when they used it for war, and tried to trick a chosen successor into activating the Huitzilopochtli array.
### STANDARD +
Canon shows him mostly through records, fables and two quests (Moment of Awakening in 5.2, An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty in 5.5), where he wears human faces and manipulates people he claims to want to save.
### ULTRA +
He is neither the benevolent culture hero of Natlanese legend nor a simple villain: the records stress his foresight, his contempt for the dragon court and his real, repeatedly disappointed hope in humanity.

## background
### LITE
Born from an offshoot of Xiuhcoatl's primal flame, he became regent after the Heavenly Principles froze his brother. Foreseeing his kin's fall, he stole a seed of Phlogiston and taught it to humans, who later turned it to war.
### STANDARD +
He fought in the war with the Heavenly Principles and was appointed regent of Natlantea. He tried with the Thirteen Lords to repeat moon-forging until Nibelung's return and Xiuhcoatl's waking stopped them. He then took Phlogiston to find a land "yet to know the touch of filth and decay", taught the human Chaac first, and raised Hanan Pacha (now the Upper Sanctum) with him. Pilgrims who passed his trials became Natlan's first permanently settled tribe. He built the Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits to train warriors in Phlogiston.
He had a son, Och-Kan, made from a dragon's body and a human soul, hoping he would slay Xiuhcoatl and become a new sovereign for dragons and humans together.
### LARGE +
The dragon court discovered the theft. The Thirteen Lords and the Three Princes tried to impeach him, accusing him of helping Och-Kan escape and ally with Xbalanque; Xiuhcoatl's appearance halted the hearing, and the records suggest the real aim was to seize his authority. He worried Och-Kan would only follow his father's script and considered whether it would be better for him to leave.
After Xbalanque's victory and the ascension of the first Pyro Archon, he and Xbalanque each took half of the Golden Entreaty and swore to prevent Huitzilopochtli. Yet he watched the tribes use Phlogiston as a weapon, Och-Kan turn tyrant, and humanity fail to unite against the Abyss, and lost his faith. He withdrew from history while dragons dwindled. Xiuhcoatl had predicted he would one day walk the same path.
### ULTRA +
In the end he planned to use a human and a Saurian bearing the Flamelord's Blessing, together the "Chosen of Dragons", to unseal the Great Volcano of Tollan and bring him the Golden Entreaties. He offered Och-Kan the chance to activate the array; Och-Kan refused, hid the Entreaty on Tonatiuh and was overthrown by the scarlet-eyed youth. Kukulkan then fed Och-Kan's mind-severed body with Abyssal energy to create the fell dragon that circled Ochkanatlan, invented the legend of the Jade of Return, and lured travellers into Tonatiuh. Souls the fell dragon consumed were made into masks for his human disguises. The Ochkanatlan Adventure Team and Bona were drawn there; all but Liriwu died.
With Little One he completed the rite and then revealed himself in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain. When Little One cancelled the activation, he stayed by the array as its maintenance failed, saying he finally understood how Xiuhcoatl felt at the end of his days, that deep down he had still wished to believe, and that Little One had made the same choice he and Och-Kan once made; he then faded away.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Xiuhcoatl is his elder brother, who gave him life, and the dragon whose plan he opposed and later tried to carry out. Och-Kan is his son, whom he used and ruined. Xbalanque was his oath-bound ally. Chaac is his first human disciple in the fables. Little One, the Traveler's Tepetlisaurus companion, is the chosen successor he tried to make choose annihilation.
### LARGE +
The Thirteen Lords and Three Princes saw him as a traitor who gave humans Phlogiston. The Lord of the Night appears in the fables as the power that feared humanity leaving Natlan. Accounts in weapon texts also place him in conflict with the Weaver of Secrets and Maghan's master; treat these as wiki inference.

## status
### LITE
Gone. He faded away at the end of An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty (5.5) after Little One refused to activate Huitzilopochtli, content with the choice.
### STANDARD +
Natlanese tradition still tells of the Sage, often as a human. Hanan Pacha and the Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits are abandoned. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 appearance has been found.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Brown and beige scales, a humanoid upper body and a legless serpentine lower body ending in a green-feathered tail. Long flat horns, bundles of two-toned green feathers at the sides of his face, four taloned fingers, white and grey robes with triangular and scale patterns, and two Phlogiston-charged stone structures floating at his back. He can disguise himself as a human and has been seen as Manqu and Bona.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- The Sage is not a human hero in canon, though many Natlanese believe so.
- He did not steal Phlogiston for love of humans alone: records show scheming and detachment.
- Kukulkan is not Xiuhcoatl. Xiuhcoatl created Huitzilopochtli; Kukulkan first opposed it and only later tried to activate it.
- Do not invent romance, wives or other children; only Och-Kan is canon.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Age of the Seven Sovereigns: born as an offshoot of Xiuhcoatl's flame; fights the Heavenly Principles; becomes regent.
- War of Funerary Flame: Xiuhcoatl wakes and halts the moon-forging attempt.
- Steals Phlogiston; teaches Chaac; raises Hanan Pacha; fathers Och-Kan.
- About 2,000 years ago: allies with Xbalanque after Xiuhcoatl's death.
- Centuries later: ruins Och-Kan, builds the Jade of Return legend, lures the Adventure Team.
- 5.2: appears as the voice and disguise behind the Tonatiuh trap.
- 5.5: revealed to the Traveler and Little One; fades away.

## entry: Hanan Pacha and the Phlogiston Fable
@keys: Hanan Pacha, Upper Sanctum, Records of Hanan Pacha, Sage of the Stolen Flame fable
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Natlanese tradition says the Sage and his foolish companion Chaac raised the floating land of Hanan Pacha so that curious people would come and learn Phlogiston. The records are explicitly a fable that historians dismiss; in that tale the Sage is human, steals the Pyrophosphorite from a vain winged dragon by trickery, and leaves after the people are made drunk by sweet water the Lord of the Night offered. The legend is that Phlogiston was scattered across Natlan, and the Sage set out on a "road home" that might mean he will return. Chikya believes the Sage was human, but the Upper Sanctum is shown to be Kukulkan's work.

## entry: Impeachment of the Regent
@keys: Impeachment of the Regent, Silver Prince, Thirteen Lords impeachment, Impeachment Inquiry into Regent Kukulkan
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
A council record in the Chamber of Deliberation shows an impeachment inquiry into Regent Kukulkan convened on the Second Lord's initiative; the argument is erased, and the vote shows nine lords in favour and four abstaining. Shadows replayed in the chamber (Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment, 5.5) call him the "Silver Prince", accuse him of giving humans Phlogiston and of letting an escaped test subject from his laboratory reach the human federation. In another session the Eleventh Lord sneers that the Fourth Lord shares the Regent's "facile obsession with technology". The Secret Source Servant calling itself Awanyu says the denunciations were about seizing his authority, not genuine belief, and that when Xiuhcoatl appeared in the flesh the lords withdrew their words and the trial dissolved into farce.

## entry: Huitzilopochtli and the Golden Entreaties
@keys: Huitzilopochtli, Golden Entreaty, Golden Entreaties, Sweeping Skyfire, Sunquake
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Huitzilopochtli, the Natlan Phlogistonization Array, would transform Natlan into Phlogiston and annihilate the Abyss by mutual annihilation, at the cost of all existing life. Two Golden Entreaties made from Xiuhcoatl's control-hub eye activate it after three confirmations. Kukulkan swore with Xbalanque never to activate it, then plotted to do so himself, and after Little One cancelled the activation he faded away.

## entry: Och-Kan and the Fell Dragon
@keys: Och-Kan, Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, Jade of Return
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Och-Kan was Kukulkan's half-dragon son and the king of Ochkanatlan. Kukulkan twisted his body into the fell dragon in the ruins and used the Jade of Return legend to draw people to Tonatiuh. Och-Kan later lamented that his spirit and body had been used by "that person" for such crimes.
