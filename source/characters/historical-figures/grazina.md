## summary
### LITE
Grazina Myklukho was a Senior Researcher in the Post-Apocalyptic Elysium, an apocalypse bunker in Nod-Krai built under the Belyi Tsar. She led the plant-breeding work that produced the plants later called Mandragoras, and learned too late that her supervisor was using her research for Rotwang.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through the logs, notes and recordings the Traveler can find in the bunker during the world quest Return to Sender. Her voice in the recordings is credited as a Gentle Female Voice.

## background
### LITE
About 500 years ago, around the Cataclysm, she lived in the bunker under Supervisor Morozov. Building on earlier research that kuuvahki makes certain plants mutate, she and her team bred a plant that absorbs kuuvahki and purifies Abyssal corruption. She hoped the bunker's descendants would one day walk the surface again.
### STANDARD +
She asked Morozov to add pipelines to the ventilation system to circulate kuuvahki from the plants; he agreed. Afterwards he ordered tests on Abyssal creatures, and breaches killed several people and made children ill, among them a child named Payva, who died. She pleaded with him to stop. She had also asked about a missing maintenance worker, Kotelnikov, who had been silenced after he discovered the outside world was intact.
### LARGE +
Brahe, the third Reed Miller, infiltrated the bunker hunting Rotwang's followers and told her the truth. Without proof, she chose to believe him. She sent Morozov an angry message and then an apologetic one asking to meet in person. Brahe took the surviving children out to the hunter Solovei and the new Moonchanter Aila, together with plant specimens. Before going to confront Morozov she recorded a toast to the dead "from those soon to die".
### ULTRA +
Morozov died in the confrontation, but Brahe warned that the bunker's contamination would one day revive him as a mindless monster. The broadcast signal and doors could not be shut, and destroying the facility would release the Abyssal corruption. She declined Brahe's offer to leave, stayed to keep Morozov in and outsiders out, and wrote in her private journal that the deaths were as much her fault as Morozov's or Rotwang's. She let Brahe choose the name Mandragora, after a fictional plant from northern legend that he said Alia once told him about.

## personality
### STANDARD
Methodical and hopeful as a scientist, formal in writing, and willing to be blunt when lives were at stake. Her logs show anger that she later regrets, and a harsh sense of personal responsibility. She chose to trust Brahe on hope rather than proof. These traits come from her written records only.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Brahe (Reed Miller) is the outsider who told her the truth and carried her work and the children to safety. Morozov was her supervisor, whom she trusted and later killed; the children (including Payva and Lily) mattered to her deeply.

## status
### LITE
Presumed dead around 500 years ago. She sealed herself in the bunker; the Traveler finds her records in Nod-Krai's Wavechaser Plain.
### STANDARD +
Morozov was resurrected as the Ultimate Calamity and the Traveler defeats it in Return to Sender (or at Piramida, depending on quest order). Mandragoras survive outside the bunker as her lasting legacy.

## entry: Post-Apocalyptic Elysium

### STANDARD
A bunker in Wavechaser Plain built during the Belyi Tsar's reign in expectation of the Cataclysm. Its researchers and families were made to believe the outside world had been destroyed. Grazina worked there under Supervisor Morozov; the Traveler can enter after the world quest Return to Sender begins.

## entry: Mandragora origin
@keys: Mandragora, Mandragoras
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Grazina's kuuvahki-absorbing plant had no name. Brahe, a self-styled master thief, "stole the naming rights" and called it Mandragora, after a fictional plant in northern legend. Grazina notes that in the language of flowers it symbolises happiness and hope, and calls it a bitter irony.
