## summary
### LITE
Ixquieh was one of the original Six Heroes of Natlan, the first bearer of the Ancient Name Baraka and a master of flame-forging. With her twin Ahpub she joined Xbalanque against tyrant dragons, then led the Children of Echoes, and died long ago after a split with her brother.
### STANDARD +
The Ring of Yaxche describes her as a calm, unflappable young woman. Sundjatta's notes call her Hero of the Forged Flame, the first to master smelting phlogiston itself.

## background
### LITE
She and Ahpub were born while dragons ruled Natlan. Their father died through a Dragonlord's whim and their mother sacrificed herself to protect them; the twins were separated and suffered many trials. They reunited before an evil dragon's corpse and vowed to end death, destruction and pain.
### STANDARD +
They tricked a great dragon, Aj Imuch' Paxlaq Lahun, the Tenth Sovereign Lord, with a forged imitation gem and a draught that drained his power, and he was buried alive in the mountains. They swore to the warrior Xbalanque and, later, to Och-Kan's Grand Alliance. Ahpub became Chief of the Collective of Plenty, she of the Children of Echoes. Sundjatta writes that she had no parents to name her, so she gave herself a draconic name, and that her phlogiston-forging was a more advanced art than later smiths know. After Och-Kan became king her view of dragons softened while Ahpub's hatred grew.
### LARGE +
When Och-Kan ordered the pursuit of fleeing dragons, only Ahpub obeyed. He brought his army to her territory and demanded she give up the dragons she sheltered, but she refused. After several days they met privately and argued: he accused her of betraying the Alliance, while she said he and Och-Kan had abandoned Xbalanque's ideals for bloodlust. The Ring of Yaxche says Ahpub's sword accidentally cut her throat and she died in his arms.
### ULTRA +
In Searing (5.8) flashbacks she is a loyal friend to Lianca, unsure of the Eleventh Lord's malfunctioning half and still working to restore the core that became Ineffa. After their deaths, their adventures inspired traditions of both the Collective of Plenty and the Children of Echoes.

## status
### LITE
Dead long ago. Two accounts disagree: the Ring of Yaxche says Ahpub accidentally killed her in their quarrel; Sundjatta's notes say she took her own life and burned her workshop so that her writings and legacy would not fall to Och-Kan or be stained. Little of her work survives, and a friend of hers preserved some. She appears only in flashbacks in Flamelets and Searing (5.8).

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her twin is Ahpub; Xbalanque is the warrior-leader they both followed; Och-Kan was an ally who became a rival or an enemy. She was friends with Lianca, shielding Ineffa's core. The Children of Echoes, including Xilonen, still honour her and her Ancient Name.
