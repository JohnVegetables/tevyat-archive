## summary
### LITE
Bonanus, titled General Chizapus, was one of the Five Foremost Yakshas, the adepti who followed Rex Lapis into battle against the demons born of fallen gods. She is long dead; only Xiao, who was Alatus, survived the Five.
### STANDARD +
Canon names her but says little. Wiki summaries record her as the Hydro yaksha of the Five, and Xiao remembers her saying that few yakshas find rest for their souls.
### LARGE +
Her story survives only in fragments: the in-game book on the Five, Xiao's account in the Interlude Chapter, a dying memory of Bosacius, and a Lantern Rite anecdote from a limited-time event.
### ULTRA +
Because canon is so sparse, any portrayal should stay within three facts: she was a yaksha of the first rank, she belonged to a tight group of siblings-in-arms, and she was lost to karmic debt. Everything else is open.

## background
### LITE
When the Archon War left divine remnants that spawned monsters and plagues, Morax summoned yakshas to destroy them. Of the many, five were the strongest: Bosacius, Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus. Bonanus was one of them.
### STANDARD +
The in-game book "Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti" says the yakshas followed Rex Lapis wherever fighting went until the plague was purged, and that over about a thousand years most of them were broken by rage, fear-born madness or delusion. Three of the Five died, one vanished and one survived. Bonanus was among the three who died; her death is not narrated (see status).
### LARGE +
A memory sequence in the 2.7 Interlude shows the Five as close companions: Menogias fussing over a purple robe that the four-armed Bosacius could not wear, Bosacius drawing on a sleeping Alatus's face, and Bonanus calling to the dying Bosacius as "Brother Bosacius". During the 4.4 Lantern Rite event (limited), Xianyun recalled that Menogias once made Bonanus a dress with a long train for an adepti gathering; Bonanus privately complained that it would hinder her in battle, but Menogias was too stubborn to be talked out of it, even by Rex Lapis. That is the only glimpse of her personality canon offers.
### ULTRA +
Xiao says the Five used to talk of living a mortal's life once the world was at peace, and that he was the only one who did not hope for it. He also tells the Traveler that, "as Bonanus said", it is rare for a yaksha to find repose for the soul; the exact original words are not recorded in the sources read.

## status
### LITE
Dead. She was one of the yakshas lost to karmic debt long before the cataclysm. Not seen alive in any content up to Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Xiao states that Bonanus, Menogias and Indarias all perished while Bosacius's fate was unknown; Bosacius was later found to have died in The Chasm during the cataclysm. Canon only implies how Bonanus died.
### LARGE +
Bosacius's dying memory shows an image of Bonanus impaling Menogias, and wiki summaries read this as the two killing each other in a karma-driven fight. Treat it as strongly implied but not spelled out. No 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) material mentions her.

## relationships
### LITE
Fellow Foremost Yakshas Bosacius (leader), Indarias, Menogias and Alatus (Xiao); served Morax (Zhongli).
### STANDARD +
Menogias dressed her in a gown she did not like; the two are implied to have killed each other. Bosacius was the eldest, and she called him "brother". Xiao remembers her saying that few yakshas find peace.
### LARGE +
Xianyun, who spoke of Bonanus and Menogias in the 4.4 event, is the only living adeptus in canon to recall her.

### STANDARD
The five strongest yakshas were Bosacius (Electro, leader), Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus. Alatus alone survived and later took the name Xiao. The others died to karmic debt: Bonanus, Menogias and Indarias in the aftermath of the Archon War, Bosacius in The Chasm during the cataclysm.
### LARGE +
Xiao told the Traveler that the Five used to hope for a mortal's life once the world was at peace, and that he alone did not share the wish.
