## summary
### LITE
Kreuzlied is the name given to the last known member of the Wanderer's Troupe, a Mondstadt-born fighter who wielded the greatsword The Bell. Born into the ruling Lawrence Clan, he turned against it and was disowned.
### STANDARD +
After the Troupe's failed uprising against the Mondstadt Aristocracy he survived, spared the worst thanks to his birth. Legend says he then founded a secret society to topple the aristocracy and helped Vennessa's later revolt.
### LARGE +
His name appears only in artifact and weapon texts. Kreuzlied is an alias; his real name and ultimate fate are unrecorded.

## background
### LITE
Born to the Lawrence Clan, the ruling house of Mondstadt's aristocracy, he rejected it and became a traitor and outcast in its eyes.
### STANDARD +
He travelled with the Wanderer's Troupe, whose members were skilled in music and combat. Per the goblet text, he was met after the Conductor, the Sojourner and the Dawnlight Swordswoman. The Troupe ended its journey in Mondstadt and rose against the aristocracy; the Conductor and Sojourner died, the Dawnlight was enslaved, and his punishment was not made public.
### LARGE +
The Bell's text says aristocrats trembled at the sound of his blade, the toll of a bell of judgment, while the nobility preferred ignorance of history. The greatsword has a clock set in it whose ticking served as a rhythm for fighting alongside his friends. After the Troupe disbanded it was soaked in acid and rusted.

## status
### LITE
Long gone; his fate is unknown.
### LARGE +
No text confirms a death. As of 7.1 nothing further has appeared.
### ULTRA +
Not mentioned in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content as far as checked. The Bell text only says his punishment was never made public, which it treats as proof of his privileged origins.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Troupe comrades (the Dawnlight Swordswoman, the Sojourner, the Conductor), the Lawrence Clan he left, and Vennessa, whose revolt he supported.
### LARGE +
The Dawnlight Swordswoman's influence on Ragnvindr, later the Dawn Knight, is a separate thread; Kreuzlied's legacy is the underground society.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Kreuzlied is an alias, not a confirmed birth name.
- He did not found the Troupe and was not its leader; the Conductor was.
- The rumour tying his society to Rostam is a rumour, not fact.
- Do not claim he was executed, exiled or killed; the text says his punishment was kept secret.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the Troupe: born into the Lawrence Clan.
- With the Troupe: last known to join.
- Ludi Harpastum, Mondstadt: the Troupe's uprising fails.
- Afterwards: spared harsh punishment, forms a secret society.
- Later: legendary aid to Vennessa's revolt.

### STANDARD
The Bell is a 4-star claymore from the Wanderer's Troupe series, a greatsword with an embedded clock. Its text says the sound of its clockwork set the combat rhythm for its wielder and friends and that it was later soaked in acid. It is the main source on Kreuzlied.
