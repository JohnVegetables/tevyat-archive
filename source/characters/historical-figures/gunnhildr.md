## summary
### LITE
A woman of the Gunnhildr Clan in Decarabian's age. As the clan chief's daughter she prayed to a wind spirit in a blizzard, and her faith made the spirit strong enough to become Barbatos. Her descendants named the Gunnhildr Clan for her.
### STANDARD +
Most of what survives comes from a book compiled by a modern clan descendant, so it is a family tradition rather than neutral history.

## background
### LITE
Gunnhildr's father led a clan that once served the tyrant Decarabian. Disillusioned by his aloof rule, he took them out through the storm walls into the blizzards of Boreas. As the clan despaired, the young Gunnhildr prayed, and a formless wind spirit answered. Her sincere plea and her people's cries became faith that fed the spirit's strength, and it made a small shelter for them and gave her the power to protect.
### STANDARD +
After her father died she led the clan and became its first priestess. When the spirit rose against Decarabian alongside the Nameless Bard, Amos and the Red-Haired Warrior, she led her people into battle. After the victory, with the spirit now Barbatos, she crowned him with laurels. Her clan became one of the foremost clans of the new Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
The wiki assumes she followed Barbatos to Cider Lake, where the city was established, but little is recorded of the rest of her life. The Biography says the clan was nomadic, calls her the strongest leader it ever had, and describes her descendants as upholding a motto, "for Mondstadt, as always." In the later revolt against the Aristocracy they sided with the people and avoided exile, unlike the Lawrence Clan. A Sacrificial Sword text adds that the clan staged ceremonies enacting Mondstadt's defence, a rite now lost.
### ULTRA +
The wiki and Biography agree on the outline but differ in emphasis: the book frames her as chosen protector, while the weapon text shows a clan with a wider religious life. Nothing in canon gives her a husband, children, age or a death scene.

## status
### LITE
Long dead. She appears only in the Biography of Gunnhildr and in the clan's legacy; no ghost, relic or later sighting is attested.
### LARGE +
Her name survives in the Gunnhildr Clan, whose members include knights and clergy of the Church of Favonius. As of 7.1 no new information has appeared in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Her bond with the wind spirit is the centre of her story: she gave it faith and it gave her protection. She stood against Decarabian beside the other rebels. Her clan remembered her as the model of protecting Mondstadt.

## entry: Biography of Gunnhildr
@keys: Biography of Gunnhildr, Eckhard Gunnhildr
### STANDARD
A history book found in the Dawn Winery, compiled by the modern historian Eckhard Gunnhildr from legends passed down in the clan. It places Gunnhildr three thousand years ago and traces the clan's ties to the wind spirit and its guardianship of Mondstadt.
