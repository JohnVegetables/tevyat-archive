## summary
### LITE
Mary-Ann Guillotin was a Narzissenkreuz Institute orphan who became a Marechaussee Phantom agent and Alain Guillotin's younger sister. She was fatally wounded at Elynas, where the Oceanid Lyris absorbed her will.
### STANDARD +
She was the cheerful, approachable one of her friend group. Alain later shaped the puppet Sandrone in her likeness and memory.

## background
### LITE
Raised at the Institute until the cataclysm, she and Alain were entrusted by Vice-Director Basil Elton to the former Marechaussee Hunter Emanuel Guillotin before Elton left to fight Elynas. Alain joined the Institute of Natural Philosophy and she became a Marechaussee Phantom agent.
### STANDARD +
Alain built her the mechanical dog Seymour. She often helped her brother when his assistant Carter fell ill. Rene and Jakob liked her; Carter noted she acted like their elder sister despite being the youngest. Their friendship ended after Rene and Jakob's radical Abyss and Remurian research, including the failed experiment on Carter.
### LARGE +
When the Court deemed the Narzissenkreuz Ordo a threat, she, Alain and Seymour tracked Jakob and other members to Elynas's remains. The battle caused an explosion with human casualties and destroyed many metal animals. She was fatally injured and Lyris dissolved and absorbed her; Seymour survived badly damaged. She was presumed dead afterwards. Earlier she had visited Lyris in the Institute ruins and told her the Princess Lyris fairy tale based on the hero Narzissenkreuz game Alain had made the children play.
### ULTRA +
Her adoptive father Emanuel died hoping to make a safer world for them. As a child she is described as having fair skin, chin-length brown hair and blue-gray eyes.

## status
### LITE
Dead. Her will lives on only as part of the hybrid Oceanid Mary-Ann, who is aware she is not the human.
### STANDARD +
A memorial park bears her name, and Sandrone, a Harbinger of the Fatui, wears her likeness.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Alain was her brother and protector; Emanuel her adoptive father; Rene, Jakob and Carter her friends. Lyris was her childhood director and her ending.

## entry: Hybrid Mary-Ann and Ann
@keys: Oceanid Mary-Ann, Annapausis Mary-Ann
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Oceanid Mary-Ann is not the human: a merged consciousness of Lyris and Mary-Ann who built Annapausis. She accidentally gave rise to Ann, who carries both sets of memories. Both later found a fresh start while knowing they are not the human Mary-Ann Guillotin.
