## summary
### LITE
She was the Eighth of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, the dragon rulers created by Xiuhcoatl, and presided over Ch'upikaq Tajin, later Flower-Feather Clan territory. She adopted the human girl Lianca, and the Sovereign Lords' council voted to execute her for it. She stayed loyal to dragonkind to the end.
### STANDARD +
Her proper title is the Priestess of Floral Candles and Skyborne Feathers. Her name is Mayan in origin; the wiki glosses it as a priestess who places flowers and candles at shrines, with wind, feathers and the number eight.

## background
### LITE
After the war of funerary flame she came to rule Ch'upikaq Tajin in the time when ancient dragons ruled Natlantea. At some point she took in the abandoned infant Lianca and raised her as a daughter.
### STANDARD +
Before that, after the Heavenly Principles invaded Teyvat and the Seven Sovereigns fell, she was among the Sovereign Lords who, with Kukulkan, tried to replicate moon forging to reach worlds beyond Teyvat (Fractured Halo text). Xiuhcoatl, imprisoned in lava, revived on Nibelung's return and stopped them.

She asked the Second Lord, Ix Je'lal K'ua'l Ka', to have a jade longbow forged for Lianca, and told her to go and find her own path as dragons do, to kneel to no one, human or dragon. She supported Lianca's choice even if mother and daughter might one day oppose each other.
### LARGE +
In council record B-CL-13097, on neutralising human settlements, she argued that drastic measures are inefficient and voted against the motion, saying her actions were sanctioned by the Sovereign and that she owed explanations to no weakling. She was accused by the Seventh Lord of favouring humanity over dragons, in particular for having the longbow forged for her 'human pet'.
### ULTRA +
The Twelfth Lord, Ix Roq'ratik'obala' Ka'alahun, later moved for her execution in record B-CL-16130. He called Lianca a reaver and harbourer of exiled degenerates who had joined Xbalanque in killing dragonkind, and asked why the Eighth Lord tolerated her. She refused to defend herself, said she remained loyal to the Sovereign and that Lianca had chosen her own path. The First Lord said that following Xbalanque is no crime. The Second Lord said she did not call her a traitor but would not risk any speck of impurity and voted with the motion; the Eighth Lord agreed she would do the same in her place. The vote was 4 for (Second, Fifth, Seventh and Twelfth Lords), 2 against (First and Eighth) and 1 abstention (Third).

## status
### LITE
Dead: executed by her own council during the siege of Tollan, though she stayed loyal to dragonkind until her last breath. Her territory became the Flower-Feather Clan's land. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 appearance was found.
### STANDARD +
In Smoldering (5.8), Citlali tells the party that records claim she was executed by her own kind for secretly raising a human; Chasca notes that legends of Lianca do not name her dragon mother. A Little Robot tells Ineffa to return to the Eighth Lord's territory to restore her memory data.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Lianca is the core of her story. Within the council she was respected and feared: the First Lord defended her, the Second Lord admired her but would not spare her, the Seventh and Twelfth Lords resented her. Her loyalty was to Xiuhcoatl, not to dragon custom as the others read it.

### STANDARD
Lianca was an abandoned infant adopted by the Eighth Lord, who raised her among dragonkind and told her she was her daughter and an honourable princess who kneels to no one. Her longbow Chain Breaker was forged at the Eighth Lord's request. Lianca later founded the Flower-Feather Clan and died fighting Och-Kan.
