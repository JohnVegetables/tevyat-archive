## summary
### LITE
Rene de Petrichor was a brilliant young Fontainian from the cataclysm era. He believed his calculations pointed to a coming apocalypse, founded the Narzissenkreuz Ordo to survive it, and finally dissolved himself into a being known as Narzissenkreuz, or the Master.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through the notes, Enigmatic Pages and artifact text he left behind, through what the Master later says and does, and through later accounts such as Sandrone's. He was the son of the mayor of Poisson, a close friend of Jakob Ingold, and a childhood friend of Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin.
### LARGE +
Rene's writing is arrogant, affectionate, scientific and desperate by turns. He admires almost no one's intellect but Alain's, yet he cares deeply about Jakob and wants to save every Fontainian, not only himself.

## personality
### LITE
Gifted, intense, impatient with those he thinks slow ("adults are too pedantic"), openly proud of his own reasoning but loyal to the few he loves. He fears for the future and bends his conscience around it.
### STANDARD +
He is sure of his world-formula and dismisses rival views, yet he is also hurt by Alain's refusal and bears him no ill will, assuming Alain simply has not seen what he has seen. His notes mix scientific detail with small domestic touches (sweets, jam, tea at the Institute), and he is furious on Alain's behalf when others envy him.
### LARGE +
He treats people as variables he can arrange, as with the Institute members he turned into "believers", but he still feels the weight of his failures, such as Carter's condition and Jakob's tears. He is willing to erase his own personality if it helps the project.

## background
### LITE
Rene was the son of Renault de Petrichor, mayor of Poisson. After the Siege of Poisson he and Jakob were taken in by the Narzissenkreuz Institute, where they befriended Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin. He later researched Khaenri'ahn ruins and founded the Narzissenkreuz Ordo.
### STANDARD +
As a child prodigy he deduced a "world-formula" from Khaenri'ahn records. After the Institute disbanded he travelled with Jakob and Karl Ingold to the Girdle of the Sands, studying the Gaokerena of the Harvisptokhm, in which the former Hydro Archon Egeria's consciousness lingers. He found its makeup similar to his own and Jakob's bodies, and noted that Khvarena resembled something the text censors, which the wiki reads as the Abyss.
### LARGE +
Back in Fontaine, he and Jakob sneaked into the remains of the giant beast Elynas and used its flesh to strengthen Jakob, and later explored Remurian ruins. At Elynas they met Alain, Mary-Ann and Emanuel Guillotin by chance; Rene hid Jakob's secret, and Alain recommended them to the Institute of Natural Philosophy. Seeing the Institute's members turn into followers, he formed the Ordo to give them structure. The Ordo gathered at the Tower of Ipsissimus in the Morte Region. He coined the names Scarlet Quartz (for a red stone from Dragonspine), Beastly Rift and Rifthound.
### ULTRA +
His plan was to merge the wills of countless people through the Sword of Narzissenkreuz and a rebuilt circle of Four Orthants, so that Fontainians could transcend the limits of separate bodies and survive the end of the world. He gave up the idea of simply moving the Institute's Director to a new home, and persuaded her to come to the gestalt under construction. He then obtained the sword, left the pocket watch Alain once gave him behind, and dissolved himself, intending to merge with her consciousness. According to the Ordo's log and Sandrone's later account, the attempt failed and his body dissipated; Jakob experimented for years (using defectors as subjects), then built a Hydro Tulpa body from the Director's essence, Carter's corrupted remains and the pocket watch, restored Rene's consciousness as the Master, and erased the failure from his memory. The Master absorbed Marechaussee Phantom agents and Emanuel Guillotin, and later made the Doomsday Clock at the Ordo's meeting place.

## status
### LITE
Dead as an individual; his consciousness lived on only in Narzissenkreuz, the Master, who faded away to rest in Annapausis at the end of the 4.2 "In the Wake of Narcissus" quests.
### STANDARD +
Before that, the Master was defeated by the Traveler and Ann in the Primordial Sea, helped them reach Mary-Ann in Annapausis, and was forgiven by her as Rene. Centuries earlier the Ordo had been ruled an illegal extremist group, its tower sealed, and the Master left in the Primordial Sea beneath it. His legacy outlives him: in the 6.3 Nod-Krai Archon Quest, Sandrone (the Marionette) has studied Rene's records and Alain's notes on him, adopted some of his ideas, and tries to use his Four Orthants theory to predict the world's future.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Jakob Ingold is his closest friend and the person he saved; Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin are old friends who refused to join; his father Renault died in the Siege of Poisson; the Director Lyris merged with him to become the Master.
### LARGE +
Karl Ingold looked after the children and became suspicious of his research. Emanuel Guillotin was distrusted for his part in the Siege of Poisson. Carter Scherbius, Alain's assistant, was the subject of an experiment that broke the friendship with Alain.

## entry: Narzissenkreuz Ordo
@keys: Narzissenkreuz Ordo
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rene and Jakob founded the secret society in Fontaine more than 400 years ago. It aimed to use the power of the Primordial Sea to fuse many wills into one being and so survive the prophesied destruction. It met at the Tower of Ipsissimus, was later declared an illegal extremist group and had its tower sealed. Members held clearance levels from one to four.
### LARGE +
After the Ordo's collapse, its leader, the Master, remained in the Primordial Sea. The Ordo also clashed violently with Mary-Ann at Elynas, which ended in her death.

## entry: The world-formula
@keys: world-formula, world formula, Doomsday Clock
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rene's calculation, built from Khaenri'ahn records, that predicted the drying of the world's waters and, after centuries, a second flood. He believed no new civilization could rise afterwards. Alain did not believe it. The Master later used the formula to create the Doomsday Clock.
### LARGE +
Rene needed outside "variables" to change the result, which led him to Abyssal power, to the Descender idea and to the ancient method of the Golden Troupe.

## entry: Jakob Ingold
@keys: Jakob, Jakob Ingold
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rene's closest friend, whom he met in childhood. Jakob nearly died during their travels; Rene saved him with Abyssal power and turned him into a "neohuman" who can take the form of an Iniquitous Baptist. Jakob then helped design the Ordo's rituals and later built the vessel that housed Rene's consciousness.

Childhood friends from the Narzissenkreuz Institute. Alain refused Rene's requests for help, and Rene says he does not blame him. Mary-Ann made Rene happy simply by being near. Both refused to join the Ordo.

## entry: Rene's Investigation Notes
@keys: Rene's Investigation Notes, Fell Dragon's Monocle, Enigmatic Page, Pages From Some Lost Notes

### STANDARD
Rene's notes, found in the Sumeru quest "As the Khvarena's Light Shows", record his research on Khvarena and the Gaokerena; torn "Pages From Some Lost Notes" in the Khaenri'ahn ruins under Gavireh Lajavard compare Khvarena, Azosite and the Abyss. The Enigmatic Pages found in Fontaine and the Fell Dragon's Monocle artifact text, added later, tell the rest of his life.

## entry: Rene's legacy in Nod-Krai
@keys: Rene de Petrichor, Rene
@keys2: Sandrone, Marionette, Four Orthants, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the 6.3 Archon Quests "Tracing the Footsteps of the Past" and "The First Sliver of Moonlight", Sandrone (the Marionette) studies Rene's draft lectures and records and Alain's old notes on him. She says Alain despised Rene's theories but never found better alternatives, so she adopted some of Rene's ideas, and that Rene "once foretold the fate of the world", probably using the Primordial Sea. She ran into problems implementing his Four Orthants theory, and observes that his world-formula, full of constants, shows he thought the world's fate predetermined. The Knave, surprised, asks whether Alain and Rene were not "mortal enemies".

## entry: Soul-severing
@keys: soul-severing, severed souls
@keys2: Rene, Narzissenkreuz, Sandrone, Fontaine
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
During the limited-time 6.7 event "Sunny Summer Fontinalia" (quest "Those We Have Lost"), Sandrone explains that Rene devised soul-severing as the first step towards letting souls live on forever and so saving the world. His imperfect Four Orthants theory caused a failure that dissipated his physical form; Jakob, trying to restore him, severed the souls of people who stood in his way. Souls severed after Rene awoke merged into his consciousness, but the earlier ones were left wandering Fontaine's waters and in time became corrupted. Sandrone, who never knew Rene, deals with them because Alain regretted not helping his friend and wished to mend things with him. She also shows a photo of Alain and Rene at the Institute of Natural Philosophy from before their falling-out.
