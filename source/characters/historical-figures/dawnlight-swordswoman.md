## summary
### LITE
The Dawnlight Swordswoman, also called the Sword Dancer, belonged to the Wanderer's Troupe about a thousand years ago, at the end of Mondstadt's Aristocratic period. She wielded The Flute, a sword that sang as it moved. After the Troupe's attack on the city failed she was enslaved as a gladiator and kept fighting, and her final match moved the future Dawn Knight Ragnvindr.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through item and book texts, never through a quest.

## background
### LITE
The Troupe took in wanderers from across Teyvat, so her origin is unknown. She met the Conductor and the Sojourner, later Kreuzlied, and with them formed the Troupe, which performed music and fought the oppressive aristocracy. Their last act was to storm Old Mondstadt; it failed, and she was enslaved as a gladiator.
### STANDARD +
Her sword had holes and fine engravings and made a flute-like sound depending on its swing angle. Item text says her blade sang with the radiance of morning light, which earned her the name Dawnlight. The sword was buried when the Troupe disbanded and, unearthed years later, no longer sings.
### LARGE +
Even after losing hope and companions, she kept singing of freedom in the arena. In the Ballads of the Squire she asks Ragnvindr why he lifts others' lives up while claiming the wind as his companion and forgetting freedom, words the wiki presents as strengthening his resolve against the aristocracy.

### ULTRA +
The Flute's text says Ragnvindr was a retainer in a knight's household when his master took him to watch the gladiator match; her magnificent finale left him sure of what he had to do next. The Wanderer's Troupe artifact set text, as summarised on the wiki, is the source for the failed storming of the city and her enslavement. Tolfin's tale that she may have been Fontainian is the only hint of origin and is not confirmed.

## status
### LITE
Dead. She died in the arena in front of Ragnvindr, who buried her with her sword and later took the title Dawn Knight in tribute to her.

## relationships
### LITE
Her Troupe companions were the Conductor, the Sojourner and Kreuzlied. Ragnvindr, then a young retainer, was her witness and heir in spirit.
