## summary
### LITE
The Burner of Cities was a Vila of the old fae nobility who led a short-lived rebellion against the Tsaritsa. She and her followers used Snezhnaya's new railways to raid across the land, and she died when her plan to destroy the Kresnik's Torches was stopped.
### STANDARD +
She is a figure of legend, known only from the texts of weapons and artifacts. Her fall led to the founding of the High Table.

## background
### LITE
After the Tsaritsa took control of Snezhnaya and sharply cut the privileges of the old fae aristocracy, a female Vila grew bitter. She gathered resentful fae, took the epithet "Burner of Cities", and launched raids by rail, hoping to restore the glory of the fae nobility.
### STANDARD +
Texts describe her as beautiful as a pearl, with a proud bearing. Before her rebellion she fought a human later called "the guiding one", beat him, and gave him a name she called sacred, one that belonged to "the Princess's descendants". He laughed off his defeat and went on to be revered for fighting injustice. When they met again, now in a fight for the citizens' safety, she lost; he offered reconciliation and she refused, vowing that she and her followers would never submit to Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
She allied with a wolf fae from Cirnareka and a pale fae called "the wise one", who helped her inflame followers with the idea of overthrowing the Tsaritsa and creating a new world. Her ranks included a Domovoy named Varenukha, who was exposed as the guiding one's spy and executed, and a Volkodlak named Rafalovich, who grew disillusioned and defected to the other side. The Vila raiders were drawn from descendants of the old Vila nobility, and the Vila people split over their cause. The rebellion was soon suppressed.
### ULTRA +
In a final bid to destroy Snezhnaya, and herself with it, she planned to wreck the Kresnik's Torches. The guiding one, Manechka and the Luchkin, aided by weapons forged by the young Domovoy Izmaylov, thwarted the plan; the guiding one and the Burner of Cities perished in the confrontation and what exactly happened was lost to time. The wolf fae was cut down by Rafalovich, and the wise one was slain by their former friend Eprokin, who became Director of the Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau. Izmaylov took the credit, to the surprise of his rival Alvis, and swore an oath with the surviving heroes that created the High Table and the Law.

## relationships
### LITE
The Tsaritsa, whose rule she fought; the guiding one, the human she first beat and who later bested her; Izmaylov, who armed her enemies.
### STANDARD +
The Burner of Cities and the guiding one held a mutual respect. She refused his appeal for forgiveness, and he died fighting her.
### LARGE +
Her followers included the hunchbacked Domovoy Varenukha, the warrior Rafalovich and a wolf fae; the "wise one" was a mastermind who urged her on. The wolf fae and the wise one both fell afterwards.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A young woman of the Vila, lovely as a pearl, constantly wearing a proud countenance.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Canon presents her as a formidable fighter who beat the guiding one in their first duel, and as a leader who used the railway network to strike across the country. Details of her Vila powers are not given.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about five centuries. Her rebellion failed.
### STANDARD +
She is remembered in the lore of weapons and the Luchkin and in the archive entry on Winged Halberd-Knights. Some Vila who rose in small-scale rebellions in the century after the Tsaritsa's ascent were her kind.

## entry: Guiding one and Luchkin
@keys: guiding one, Luchkin, Manechka
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The guiding one is the human hero who founded the Luchkin, a group that fought injustice and later guarded the ashlands. The Burner of Cities once defeated him; he later fought her again out of compassion rather than cunning, and both died in the battle at the Kresnik's Torches. His young follower Manechka pursued the Burner underground with him and then led the surviving Luchkins.
### LARGE +
After the death of the guiding one, Manechka returned alone and kept watch over the ashlands for years, a vigil that children of the ashlands still sing about.

## entry: Founding of the High Table
@keys: High Table, Izmaylov
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The young Domovoy Izmaylov forged weapons for the Luchkin heroes. After the Burner of Cities' scheme was crushed, he took the modest renown the heroes had earned and swore an oath with the survivors in narrow alleyways, establishing the High Table and the Law.
