## summary
### LITE
Havria was the God of Salt, a gentle and relatively weak god of Liyue before and during the Archon War. She refused to fight, gave shelter to people displaced by the war, and was killed by one of her own followers at Sal Terrae, leaving only a flower of salt.
### STANDARD +
She held two relics, a bottomless salt chalice and a ruler that raises salt from the ground. Her death turned her unfortunate worshippers who stayed behind into salt; the survivors fled to Liyue and the protection of Morax. Zhongli knows her story and counts it as a warning from the war.
### LARGE +
Her memory now exists in two clashing versions: the account Zhongli and the diary of the adventurer Roald give, and the grievance preserved in Yinyuan Hall that Morax murdered her.
### ULTRA +
Her ruin was sealed and later reopened in the Sal Flore story quest; afterwards Zhongli put her relics beyond mortal reach.

## background
### LITE
Havria lived in Liyue before the Archon War and stood among its many gods without the strength to win a place among The Seven. When the war broke out, she would not fight, but fled and made concession after concession until she held only a small haven, now Sal Terrae.
### STANDARD +
Roald's diary describes her as the gentlest of the gods, who took in the dispossessed and worked for peace between the gods. Her people settled in the haven and lived humbly for several centuries. Zhongli says that in her last days she had not a single weapon with which to defend them.
### LARGE +
The diary says the first king of the people, and its last, grew to believe that gentleness was unfit for the times and wanted military strength; to prove it he killed the defenceless god with a sword. Zhongli, in the quest, says the fall was framed by some of her people as a kinder end than defeat, and that he believes she held no hatred toward them. Her homeland was probably the Guyun area: Zhongli calls the return of her relics there a homecoming.
### ULTRA +
A tale in Customs of Liyue credits her with handing out flowers as a blessing or comfort, which her scattered followers may have turned into Liyue's Flower Ball custom; the text itself warns the legend has lost its detail.

## status
### LITE
Dead, killed before the game's events. Only a single flower of salt remains in a sealed domain at Sal Terrae. Not seen or mentioned in content after Sal Flore up to Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Her body dissipated into the salt flower. Followers who could not flee were turned to salt statues in the ruin, and those who escaped later returned to break the sword that killed her. In the quest Zhongli took her chalice and ruler to Guyun Stone Forest and cast them into the sea above Osial's prison, saying another spark of divinity had left Liyue.

## relationships
### LITE
Zhongli (Morax) knew of her fate; Yinyuan Hall descends from her followers; her killer was one of her own people.
### STANDARD +
Yinyuan Hall's version says Morax killed her out of jealousy of her power. Zhongli says she was never able to resist and would not have placed a curse on her people.
### LARGE +
The descendants of the survivors feared her wrath, returned to break the murder sword and set it with her relics, then resealed the place. Zhongli sees Wanyan's faith as the faith in a god who has already passed away.

## entry: Sal Terrae and the death of the Salt God

@keys2: Havria, God of Salt

### STANDARD
Sal Terrae is the last haven Havria held. A sealed domain, Of Mysteries and Salt, holds the statues of her salinised followers, the broken sword, and the sal flore at the place she died. It is opened in Zhongli's story quest by activating a set of Electro monuments in a certain order.
### LARGE +
The sword was broken by her descendants to appease her, and the chalice and ruler were left in the temple as offerings. Wanyan finds the sword and refuses to believe Zhongli's account of it.

## entry: Havria's relics
@keys: salt chalice, salt ruler
@keys2: Havria, Zhongli
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
The chalice is only half full yet never empties, so it yields endless salt; the ruler, pushed into the ground, covers the area with salt like a rising tide, deeper meaning more salt. Zhongli took both and cast them into the sea at Guyun Stone Forest.

## entry: Yinyuan Hall's version of the story
@keys: Yinyuan Hall, Wanyan
@keys2: Havria, God of Salt, Morax
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
Yinyuan Hall, the salt trade of the Eight Trades, remembers Havria as a god who was assassinated by Morax out of envy. Wanyan, a hall member posing as an archaeologist in Sal Flore, wanted proof of this. Zhongli's contract and the ruin's evidence led her to a different truth, and she left unable to accept it.
