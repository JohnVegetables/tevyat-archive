## summary
### LITE
Hakushin was a Great Kitsune, matriarch and namesake of the Hakushin Clan that lived around Mt. Yougou in Inazuma. The Kitsune Saiguu and Yae Miko are among her descendants.
### STANDARD +
Canon says almost nothing else about her. She appears only in book text and clan lore, and in no present-day quest.

## background
### LITE
She led a prominent kitsune clan that dwelt around Mt. Yougou and managed a shrine there with her apprentices.
### STANDARD +
New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune says that in her time she had six apprentices, all skilled in magic and shape-shifting, who helped her run the shrine and defend the mountain. The eldest was the Black Fox Itaru, a strong and unruly kitsune. After Itaru got drunk and wrecked a shintai representing the Shogun, Hakushin banished her from the mountain to reflect. Itaru ignored her warnings. The wiki says the Grand Narukami Shrine is implied to have existed already in Hakushin's day.
### LARGE +
The Hakushin Lands lie at the foot of Mt. Yougou. After the Kitsune Saiguu died in the cataclysm, the shrine and surrounding area were left abandoned. Both the Saiguu and, later, Yae Miko trace blood to the clan. The Hakushin symbol appears on a comb in Chinju Forest and on Ioroi's Ward.

## relationships
### LITE
Ancestor of the Kitsune Saiguu and Yae Miko; teacher of six apprentices including Itaru the Black Fox.

## status
### LITE
Unknown. She lived long ago, and canon does not say what became of her.
### ULTRA +
The Hakushin Ring, a catalyst carrying the Kitsune Saiguu's memory, and the soundtrack title Hakushin's Lullaby are the only other things that bear her name. The Saiguu's clan background is the main reason she matters to the story of Yae Miko.

## entry: Hakushin Clan
@keys: Hakushin Clan, Hakushin Lands
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A prominent kitsune clan that managed shrines around Mt. Yougou and takes its name from Hakushin. Known members are Hakushin, the Kitsune Saiguu (five hundred years ago) and Yae Miko (the present).
