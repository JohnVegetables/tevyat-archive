## summary
### LITE
Clervie was Crucabena's biological daughter and Peruere's closest friend in the House of the Hearth. She died at sixteen by her own choice, in a duel against Peruere, who later became Arlecchino.
### STANDARD +
Two Clervies exist in canon: the living girl, seen in the animated short The Song Burning in the Embers and recalled in the Ignis Purgatorius story quest, and a six-year-old shadow of her that haunted the House in Version 4.6. The shadow found peace at sunrise.
### ULTRA +
Her death is the human centre of Arlecchino's rise. It was the first of two killings that turned a quiet orphan into the director of the House, and it is the reason Arlecchino speaks about 'a real home' and about giving the children freedom to choose their own fate.

## personality
### STANDARD
Cheerful, gentle and stubborn. In every account Clervie is kind to the children around her, bad at hiding her feelings, and unwilling to give up on freedom. Arlecchino calls her cheerful and passionate with a tenacious spirit, and her ideas somewhat naive. She remained attached to Crucabena as her mother even while seeing what she was.
### LARGE +
She dreamed of a real family where nobody was killed or sacrificed. When she could not escape and would not kill her mother, she decided the only freedom she had left was the freedom to choose death. The shadow Clervie is warmer and more childlike: curious about the world, trusting of 'Perrie', frightened of being sent to Dottore, and still sure the House cannot be escaped.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Light pink hair and light green eyes, a white headband and a white dress with blue bows. The wiki also describes a blue necklace shaped like a Lumidouce Bell. The short shows her from childhood to the duel; the shadow appears as a small child.
### LARGE +
Crucabena mocked her death by pointing at a wilted blue flower, which the short and the wiki connect with a flower Clervie used to wear. The shadow cannot step into direct sunlight, and she appears only in shadows and does not eat real food.

## background
### LITE
Crucabena brought Clervie into the House of the Hearth at six to prove that all children were treated equally. She saw the House for the purgatory it was, befriended the orphan Peruere, tried to escape and to reform it, and was punished for it.
### STANDARD +
Crucabena ran the House as a contest in which the children dueled to be crowned 'king', sending the maimed to the Doctor or on deadly missions. Clervie tried to unite them to duel to a draw. At sixteen she faced Peruere in a duel and let her kill her, trusting Peruere to be the one who could do what she could not.
### LARGE +
Clervie opposed Peruere's plan to kill Crucabena, because Crucabena was a powerful Harbinger and still her mother. After her death, Peruere killed Crucabena a year later at the same place, was pardoned by the Tsaritsa and took the names Knave and Arlecchino. Peruere's flames, which leave shadows of what they consume, kept a copy of Clervie as a six-year-old.
### ULTRA +
The living Clervie's years run roughly as follows. At six she enters the House. Over the next years she befriends Peruere, is told to look after her when her curse flares, suggests seeing the aurora in Snezhnaya together, attempts escapes and is tortured as an example. At sixteen she is made to duel Peruere. A year later Peruere, using the curse and a newly gained Vision, kills Crucabena and is imprisoned in Snezhnaya before being pardoned.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Peruere/Arlecchino is her best friend and her killer. Crucabena is her mother, who used her for show and punished her. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet, who knew her only as a shadow, try to help her; Freminet sheltered her. The Traveler and Paimon keep her company on her last night.
### LARGE +
- Peruere: Clervie says 'Perrie' would vouch for her, but the group has never heard that name. Arlecchino tells the shadow the truth and says Clervie 'died' and that she herself is Perrie. Clervie says she is proud of her and calls her a great 'king' and 'Father'.
- Chapleau: Clervie's help after he was poisoned saved his life; he says he owes her and the Traveler.
- Filliol and Nanteuil: the shadow influenced them, and Arlecchino planned to punish them with her.
### ULTRA +
Arlecchino's voice-over about Lyney recalls that Clervie once asked her what a 'real home' looks like and she had no answer. Arlecchino's Character Story 5 says that in the moment of her death Clervie was truly happy, because she had found her freedom.

## status
### LITE
Dead. The living Clervie died more than a decade before the game's events; her shadow was released in Version 4.6 and has not reappeared by 7.1.
### STANDARD +
At sunrise at the ruin on Mont Esus East, Arlecchino declared the shadow 'expelled' from the House, freeing her from its rules. The sunlight reached her, she said goodbye to Peruere, and she faded into blue petals. Her presence is no longer felt by the House.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not a ghost or a spirit; the quest calls her a shadow, and she is a six-year-old who does not know she has died.
- Do not give her a romance with Peruere: canon presents a close friendship.
- The living Clervie was sixteen when she died; do not confuse her with the shadow.
- Clervie chose to let Peruere end her life in the duel; do not present it as a simple murder.
### LARGE +
- Crucabena never loved her. The wiki and Arlecchino's own account say Crucabena used her to show impartiality; do not invent maternal affection.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Age six: brought into the House by Crucabena.
- Over the next years: befriends Peruere, tries to escape, is punished.
- Age sixteen: dies in a duel with Peruere.
- A year later: Peruere kills Crucabena and becomes Arlecchino.
- 4.5 (April 2024): appears in the animated short The Song Burning in the Embers.
- 4.6: her shadow appears in When the Hearth-Flame Goes Out and is released in 'Into the Sunlight'.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Her name is the French form of Klervi, according to the wiki, which links it to Creirwy, daughter of the Welsh enchantress Ceridwen, who also gave her name to Crucabena.
- In her last conversation she says she once dreamed of being a bard, playing the lute and singing into the winds of freedom, and that if she had Mora she would buy three dresses.

## entry: Into the Sunlight
@keys: Into the Sunlight, shadow of Clervie, When the Hearth-Flame Goes Out
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Arlecchino takes the Traveler and Paimon to the ruin on Mont Esus East, tells the story of Clervie and Peruere, and then duels Lyney, Lynette, Freminet and the Traveler, who defend the shadow's presence in the House. Afterwards Clervie and Arlecchino speak at last. Clervie learns she died, says she would choose the same again, and asks about the world. At dawn Arlecchino releases her.
### LARGE +
Clervie's fear of Dottore and her belief that the House could not be left explain why sunlight failed to reach her before. Arlecchino says the difference between Crucabena and herself lies in the rules she makes, not in enforcing them, and that children who want to leave will have the House's secrets wiped from their memory and be allowed to live in Fontaine, an arrangement she discussed with Neuvillette.

## entry: The Song Burning in the Embers
@keys: The Song Burning in the Embers, animated short, Peruere
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
An animated short released on 2024-04-17 that shows the House of the Hearth under Crucabena. Clervie brings cake to Peruere's spider's grave, invites her to see the aurora in Snezhnaya, and dies in her duel saying 'I'm sorry... thank you'. Peruere then fights Crucabena, who calls innocence and kindness 'useless', and wins. The short ends with Pierro's pardon and Peruere taking in a young child to raise as a strict 'Father'.
