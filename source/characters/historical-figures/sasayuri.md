## summary
### LITE
Sasayuri was a beloved tengu general of Inazuma, known as the Great Tengu of Yougou, who served the Raiden Shogun and was killed in the war against the Watatsumi people of the Great Serpent Orobashi.
### STANDARD +
Sasayuri is known only through second-hand text and brief cinematic glimpses; no dialogue exists.

## background
### LITE
Sasayuri served as a tengu general under Ei, titled for Mt. Yougou. Before the invasion of Yashiori Island the Watatsumi champion Akuou vowed to duel the Great Tengu on the roof of Tenshukaku. Instead the tengu mask was broken in the confused fighting on the island, and Sasayuri died in the war.
### STANDARD +
After Sasayuri's death the title of Great Tengu of Mt. Yougou appears later attached to Reizenbou, though canon does not say it was formally handed over.
### LARGE +
In the Nightmare teaser Sasayuri sits with Ei, Mikoshi Chiyo and the Kitsune Saiguu at a picnic; when Ei recalls the battle against Orobashi, Sasayuri is the first to vanish from the scene. The 5.4 event cinematic (limited-time Mikawa Festival) repeats this gathering.
### ULTRA +
The Ode of Floating Dreams story teaser is also listed on the wiki page for Sasayuri; its content was not checked here. Canon gives no personality, voice or funeral, so none should be invented.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the war against Orobashi, long before the game's present. Remembered by Ei and in Watatsumi's accounts. No later developments in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Loyalty to Ei is shown by a place at the Shogun's table and by Ei's recollection. Akuou treated the Great Tengu as the foe he most wished to meet; his promise to bring back the tengu's mask was a boast made to Ayame and Mouun that the battle ended before he could keep.
### LARGE +
Akuou's wager with Mouun, found in the weapon texts, shows the Watatsumi side held the Great Tengu as a legendary opponent.
