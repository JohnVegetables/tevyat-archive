## summary
### LITE
Born Mikoshi Michihiro, eldest son of the oni warrior Chiyo and heir to the Mikoshi Clan, he withdrew from his disgraced clan after his mother's betrayal and was renamed Iwakura by the tengu Teruyo. He developed the Tengu Sweeper, founded the Iwakura Art and became the Kujou Clan's sword instructor under the title Douin.
### STANDARD +
He is known only through item text and the folk song "Iwakura the Successor". His legacy was a school that lasted five centuries and a puppet swordsman that copied his style.
### LARGE +
The artifacts of the Emblem of Severed Fate set tell most of his story: the shame, the exile, thirteen years of duels with a tengu who named him, and the day he finally touched her. Her last gift to him was a surname and a farewell.
### ULTRA +
He also appears for a moment in a cinematic of the limited 5.4 Mikawa Festival event, in the nightmare that torments his mother.

## background
### LITE
He was Chiyo's legitimate heir. During the cataclysm five hundred years ago she was corrupted in a battle with a beast of sin and turned her sword on the Raiden Shogun, which stained the Mikoshi name. Michihiro withdrew to seclusion near Konda Village, where the tengu Teruyo gave him the new name Iwakura.
### STANDARD +
Teruyo told him that if he could touch her in a duel he could name his secret blade Tengu Victor, and they met every spring for thirteen years. In the end his stroke cut her feathers and destroyed both the ruined branch shrine where they fought and his blade, giving birth to the Tengu Sweeper. The Kujou Clan then took him as instructor and he took the title Douin.
### LARGE +
The Amenoma master of his time forged the sword that would pass to every holder of the In title, named Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma at his request. He gave a spare copy, a kageuchi, to the Saimon Clan of Konda Village in thanks for sheltering him in his retreat. The Tengu Sweeper, they say, can be used only with a mind free of confusion.
### ULTRA +
Ornate Kabuto records a conversation between him and the young Kanjou head Hiroshi, in which he answers wryly that his sword might strike a tengu in flight but no one has ever brought one down. The Maguu Kenki puppet, built to imitate his technique and modelled on him, went out of control and was discarded in his lifetime.

## status
### LITE
Long dead or presumed dead; nothing says how he died. The sword school he founded outlived him by centuries.
### STANDARD +
In the present day the Iwakura Art was led by Mitsunari, but the Vision Hunt Decree scattered the clan, and the Traveler's encounters in Inazuma left no known members alive; only the Maguu Kenki still fights in his style.
### LARGE +
In the 5.4 event Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival (limited), he appears silently in a cinematic as part of Chiyo's dream. Nothing about him appears in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Chiyo (mother), Nagamasa (adoptive brother, who stayed and repaired the name), Teruyo (the tengu who renamed him), the Kujou Clan (employer) and the later Iwakura Art.
### LARGE +
Katsuragikiri Nagamasa's text says that, although Michihiro abandoned him and disappeared, Nagamasa cherished the Mikoshi name all the more and worked to clear it. Canon records no meeting between the brothers afterwards.
### ULTRA +
Teruyo's final words in Sundered Feather ask him to forget her and to use his sword for the Iwakura bloodline, to open a path that belongs to the Iwakura alone.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows his mother's fall, Teruyo's lessons and the Tengu Sweeper. He knows nothing of the Archon War's end, the Vision Hunt Decree or the Traveler.
### STANDARD +
He believes, with Teruyo, that his oni blood was a curse to be put behind him; his later feelings are not recorded.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He is not Iwakura Mitsunari, who lived centuries later and held the title Kouin.
- He was not Chiyo's adopted son; Nagamasa was the foster child.
- Do not give him speech, personality traits or age; item text gives only a few lines of conversation.
- "Doukei" and "Michihira" are earlier spellings; the current name is Michihiro.
- The Iwakura Clan did not fall in his lifetime.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Five hundred years ago: the cataclysm; Chiyo is corrupted and strikes at the Raiden Shogun.
- After: withdraws to Konda Village; meets Teruyo; thirteen years of duels; Tengu Sweeper; Iwakura Art founded; becomes the Kujou instructor and takes the title Douin.
- Later: his technique inspires the Maguu Kenki.
- 2.0: mentioned in Inazuma item texts.
- 3.3: "Doukei" is revised to Michihiro.
- 5.4 (limited): appears in the Mikawa Festival cinematic.

## entry: Tengu Sweeper
@keys: Tengu Sweeper, Tengu Victor, Iwakura Art
@keys2: Michihiro, Iwakura
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The secret sword technique of Iwakura Michihiro, said to be fast enough to strike a tengu in flight and usable only with a clear mind. It was first used in the courtyard of an abandoned small shrine, where it destroyed the building and broke his blade. The name sounds like Tengu Victor, the title Teruyo promised if he touched her.
### LARGE +
Over the centuries, masters bearing the In title used it against evils across the islands. The Maguu Kenki is built on the same sword art.

## entry: Teruyo of Mt. Yougou
@keys: Teruyo, Yougou Tengu
@keys2: Michihiro, Iwakura

### STANDARD
A black-winged tengu, daughter of the Great Tengu Reizenbou, who gave Michihiro the name Iwakura, "the seat of rock", and challenged him each year. She was raised on Mt. Yougou and stayed on Narukami Island after Reizenbou's exile. Her farewell asks him to forget her and keep to the human path.

## entry: The Mikoshi Clan's disgrace
@keys: Mikoshi Chiyo, Chiyo, Mikoshi Clan
@keys2: Michihiro
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In a battle against a tiger-bodied, serpent-tailed beast, Chiyo was swallowed, tore it open from within, and was left stained with sin. In the end she drew her sword on the Almighty Narukami Ogosho. She fled wounded; the only thing returned to the clan was the guard of her sword.

## entry: Heirloom blade of the In
@keys: Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma, Amenoma Kageuchi
@keys2: Michihiro, Iwakura
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The sword forged by the Amenoma master for Michihiro and passed to each master of the In title. It has no special powers; its owners' skill came from training, and none held a Vision. A spare copy, Amenoma Kageuchi, went to the Saimon Clan.
### LARGE +
The original ended up rusted in Enkanomiya, near the Serpent's Bowels, after the Iwakura Art's downfall, where a hidden objective lets players place it on the clan's blade-mound.

## entry: The Maguu Kenki
@keys: Maguu Kenki
@keys2: Michihiro, Iwakura

@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A mechanical swordsman built from the memory of the first master of the Tengu Sweeper, but it malfunctioned and was thrown away. Some copies were found on Yashiori Island and one was carried off to the Golden Apple Archipelago by Ako Domeki's crew.
