## summary
### LITE
Aria was the Goddess of the Eternal Moon, the first-born and eldest of the three Moon Sisters, whom the Dragon King Nibelung made to look after Teyvat in his absence. She died in the war of funerary flame after the Heavenly Principles shattered her moon. What remains of her is the Eternal Moon's Moon Marrow, kept in Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
She is not one of The Seven and has no Vision or Gnosis. Her moon, the largest of the Three Moons, is said to have caused the ocean tides through its great pull. Her only direct appearance is a conversation with Columbina in the moon's reflection (Archon Quest, 6.3).

## background
### LITE
Nibelung raised the three moons and set a goddess over each to govern the cycles while he travelled the sea of stars. Aria governed the Eternal Moon, the largest, and shared the night sky with her sisters in rotation. When the Heavenly Principles arrived, she and Canon waited to see whether its order would help Teyvat, while Sonnet opposed it. When Nibelung returned, the sisters argued over whether to side with him.
### STANDARD +
The sisters held primordial authority but saw their task as protecting the world rather than ruling it, and divided their power equally so that none became too strong. Aria later admitted that the sisters were horrified by the Heavenly Principles' celestial nails, which punished civilizations that tested its rules, and that they had been about to act when Nibelung returned. Nibelung read their hesitation as disloyalty and imprisoned them in the moon's reflection, a lunar palace built to keep them from interfering in his war, then turned their moons into weapons. The Heavenly Principles shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and threw the Frost Moon beyond the false sky.
### LARGE +
Weapon and artifact text suggests the dragons saw the sisters' accommodation with the Heavenly Principles as betrayal and cheered when the moons fell; item text also notes that the sisters stayed silent when the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl came asking for word of Nibelung, and says it is unclear whether they would not or could not speak. Artifact text describes the Eternal Moon's fall as throwing heaven and earth into chaos. Shielded in the moon's reflection, the sisters knew nothing of the war outside until Columbina Hyposelenia, travelling back in time from her own era to escape Dottore, reached them.
### ULTRA +
In The First Sliver of Moonlight, Aria is the calm, steadying voice of the three. She reassures the shy Columbina that no one can hurt her there, names the Heavenly Principles as the "great ruler of the firmament", and admits that she and Canon had wanted to see whether its order was good. On learning that only the Frost Moon survived, she tells Columbina to speak plainly because the sisters are "remnants of a bygone age". She says the sisters owe the world an apology for arguing instead of acting, warns that no moon goddess can leave the prison alive, and urges Columbina to carry on the story rather than end it there. Once the sisters agree to hand their authority to Columbina so she can return to her own time as the Trilune Goddess, Aria says that they were the tides in the river of time and now subside while Columbina rises. She comforts Sonnet as they dissolve and says goodbye to Columbina by name.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The wiki's description of her depiction: a tall, fair woman with long blonde hair and green-to-yellow gradient eyes, in white and turquoise robes with laced footwear to just below the knee, a feather-like hair ornament and golden jewellery. A soft yellow crescent moon is shown behind her as the symbol of the Eternal Moon.

## status
### LITE
Dead since the war of funerary flame, about 6,000 years before the present by Nod-Krai content. Her Moon Marrow passed from the Frostmoon Scions to Dottore and back, and is now in the Moonchanter Lauma's care. As of 7.1 she appears only in memory and relics.
### STANDARD +
Fragments of the Eternal Moon rained onto Teyvat when it was shattered; the rest were hidden beyond the false sky until Mavuika's strike during the Natlan Archon Quest briefly broke it open and revealed the broken moon. Some of those fragments are said to have been pulled down into Nod-Krai by the Fatui.

## relationships
### STANDARD
She is the eldest of the Moon Sisters with Canon (Frost Moon) and Sonnet (Iridescent Moon). She and Canon were of one mind about waiting on the Heavenly Principles, while Sonnet objected. Nibelung was their creator and jailer. Columbina, a later Frost Moon goddess, is the sister to whom she gave her authority.

## entry: Eternal Moon Marrow

### STANDARD
After Aria died, her power coalesced into the Eternal Moon's Moon Marrow, which looks like a small moon ringed with debris. The Frostmoon Scions found it and sealed it in a secret chamber opened once a year during Moon-Prayer Night. The Traveler carried it for a time. Dottore stole it to try to become a false Moon God, and Columbina recovered it in the final confrontation. It can show glimpses of the past; Lauma used it to look back.

## entry: The Moon's Reflection
@keys: moon's reflection, lunar palace, Lunar Palace
@keys2: Aria, Canon, Sonnet, Moon Sisters
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Nibelung built a prison of warped space and time for the three goddesses so they could not interfere in his war. Inside, the sisters were protected from the destruction of their moons but could not leave alive or learn what was happening outside. Columbina entered it to flee Dottore, travelled back in time and met the sisters there.
### LARGE +
Because a moon goddess cannot live without her moon, the sisters were doomed the moment they left. They chose to leave anyway after transferring their lunar authorities to Columbina, who used them to become the Trilune Goddess; the sisters then returned to the war and their fall.
