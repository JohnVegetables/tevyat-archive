## summary
### LITE
Avrelian was the first person to use the name Reed Miller. A Snezhnayan military-academy graduate turned outlaw, he founded the Treasure Hoarders in Nod-Krai, dreaming of an Elysium for the hungry. About five hundred years ago he went to Snezhnograd to explain himself to the Belyi Tsar and was executed without ever seeing the Tsar.
### STANDARD +
Tolfin, a storyteller, says the name Reed Miller was never one person's: Avrelian was the first, Alia the second and Brahe the third. His friend calls him Relya, and item texts name him Ruler of Elysium, a destiny Rotwang persuaded him of.

## background
### LITE
Son of a Frostmoon Scion maiden and a Snezhnayan officer, he was orphaned and adopted by Mikhnenko, a rank-and-file military officer, then studied at the Royal Military Academy beside his friend Solovei. After graduating he was recorded dead and resurfaced in Nod-Krai as the outlaw Reed Miller.
### STANDARD +
As cadets Avrelian and Solovei went on treasure hunts between drills, taking from the rich for the poor. The boy Brahe, the governor's adopted son, told them where one treasure lay. In The Raven's Legacy flashback he is on Bounty Island, angry at the governor's extravagance, vowing to rise by any means and rewrite unjust law, and giving the loot to street children. In Nod-Krai he raised the black raven emblem and gathered the forsaken, issuing Iron Coins so that people would master wealth rather than be mastered by it.
### LARGE +
Rotwang, the mad monk, urged him to cast aside his melancholy and follow, saying both were chosen by fate, and dangled an Elysium where the proud fae would kneel to him. Avrelian believed him and stole the Sampo Mill from the Frostmoon Scions, which also freed Alia. After a noble friend, Solovei, urged him to leave his band and explain himself to the Belyi Tsar, he walked into Snezhnograd, was seized on the spot and publicly executed.
### ULTRA +
Item texts add a darker frame: his mother, the girl chosen to bear the sacred heir, fell for a Snezhnayan officer and abandoned her duty; captured by a spy of the mad monk, she took her own life beside the wounded officer. Her plan thwarted, the Archpriestess Lovia turned to a lesser branch of the bloodline and the twins Alia and Aila were born. Another text calls him the prophesied son who rejected the crowns that fate ordained, and says the priestess Aila buried the far north's last wish of golden grandeur in his empty grave. His last recorded words, left for Alia, asked her not to weep for a future yet to arrive and to trust his plan.

## personality
### LITE
Idealistic and defiant; he called the poor his brothers and sisters. Item text calls him a master thief who never lied. He trusted Rotwang more than he should have.
### STANDARD +
He promised Alia never to endanger himself or break his word. Whether his convictions were faith or private grievance, canon does not say.

## status
### LITE
Dead: executed in Snezhnograd about five hundred years ago, before the Cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
His Iron Coins became worthless scrap, though Hiljetta still hopes to restore the Treasure Hoarders' golden age. In the 6.3 quest The Last Night, the First Light, Rotwang mistakes Alia for him, calling her Relya.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Solovei was his closest friend; Alia his follower who took his name; Brahe a younger admirer who became the third Reed Miller. Rotwang manipulated him with a prophecy, and the Belyi Tsar never met him.
### LARGE +
Tolfin says the raven emblem was derived from the family crest of Solovei. Alia doubted Solovei's advice that Avrelian surrender but was rebuffed; Solovei later deeply regretted it. A weapon text shows his mother telling her unborn child never to set foot in Nod-Krai or trust the dream of Elysium, a warning he did not heed.

## knowledge
### STANDARD
He believed in Elysium, in Rotwang's account of his destiny and that the Belyi Tsar would hear him. He did not know he was being used, and knew nothing of the Cataclysm.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Seen only as a young man in the quest flashback; canon text gives no age or height. Alia later wore a silver mask as Reed Miller.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat him as a historical figure, not a voice: a generous thief, a chosen king betrayed by flattery and hope.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Reed Miller was not one man: do not credit Avrelian with Alia's raid on the governor's palace or Brahe's Great Treasure legend. He was Rotwang's dupe, not a knowing partner in the monk's designs, and he did not lead the Lightkeepers or live to see the Cataclysm. He was executed in Snezhnograd and did not escape the gallows; that claim was made later by Alia, masked as him.

## chronology
### LARGE
Some five centuries ago: born to a Frostmoon maiden and a Snezhnayan officer; adopted by Mikhnenko; attends the Royal Military Academy. After graduating as a non-commissioned officer he is recorded as having died in the line of duty and resurfaces in Nod-Krai a few years later. He founds the Treasure Hoarders, steals the Sampo Mill at Rotwang's urging, goes to Snezhnograd and is executed, leaving the name to Alia. Not long afterwards the Cataclysm begins.

### STANDARD
Reed Miller was an alias used by at least three people: Avrelian, then Alia, then Brahe, plus many later pretenders. In Nod-Krai folklore the name is revered, and most cheap novels about the master thief tell Alia's or Brahe's tales or are pure fiction.

## entry: Iron Coins
@keys: Iron Coin, Iron Coins
@keys2: Avrelian, Reed Miller
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
A currency issued by Avrelian, based on his belief that people should master wealth rather than wait for gods to share it out. After his death they became worthless scrap, though they remain collectors' curiosities.

## entry: Great Treasure and the Proofs of the Cognoscenti
@keys: Proof of the Cognoscenti, Proofs of the Cognoscenti, Great Treasure
@keys2: Reed Miller
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
In legend, Reed Miller left a great treasure "East of the Moon, West of the Sun". Brahe, the third Reed Miller, created the story and the five Proofs of the Cognoscenti (including a rusted flower and a black feather) to keep treasure hunters hooked, as the quest The Raven's Legacy shows. The hoard turned out to be a mountain of Iron Coins.

## entry: Ruler of Elysium prophecy
@keys: Ruler of Elysium, King of Elysium, prophesied Ruler
@keys2: Avrelian, Rotwang
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The Frostmoon Scions' millennia-long plan was for a pure-blooded maiden to bear a sacred heir destined to reign over Elysium; item text calls Avrelian that prophesied son, who rejected the crowns fate ordained. Rotwang told him both were chosen by fate, urging him to cast aside his melancholy and follow, and Avrelian's last words to Alia say "If I am indeed the prophesied Ruler of Elysium". Alia later accuses Rotwang of spinning every promise and lie to make him seize the Sampo Mill.
