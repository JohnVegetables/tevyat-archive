## summary
### LITE
Akame Kanenaga was a swordsmith of the Akame School, one of the three families of the Isshin Art in the Raiden Gokaden, about a century ago. Caught up in the Case of the Eccentric, he fled Inazuma for Snezhnaya, and there he forged the sentient blade Kagotsurube Isshin out of regret.
### STANDARD +
His story survives mainly in the weapon text of Kagotsurube Isshin and a wiki summary of the quest that gives the weapon. He died in the snow of Snezhnaya and does not appear alive in any quest.

## background
### LITE
The Akame School was known for blades made to kill, and the authorities took away its leadership of the Isshin Art only three generations after Akame Mitsunaga won it. Kanenaga lived several generations after Mitsunaga and never became head blacksmith. When the Raiden Shogun's smiths were ordered to forge an important blade from an Ancient Forging Blueprint that Scaramouche had tampered with, they could not finish it by the due date, and many fled in fear of punishment. Kanenaga was one of them.
### STANDARD +
The exiles went under the Fatui's protection in Snezhnaya. The weapon text says he changed his name and made a humble living there. Back in Inazuma, Kaedehara Yoshinori and the Kamisato head had learned the truth but covered it up and took the blame; the Kamisato were spared thanks to Yae Miko, while Yoshinori closed the Kaedehara forge and the Isshin Art, left without practitioners, lost its prestige.

When Kanenaga learned of this he was filled with remorse. The wiki says he also came to understand that the Fatui had tricked him. He spent his remaining years on one last sword.
### LARGE +
The weapon text frames his life as the pursuit of the Isshin Art's vanity: he says he spent half his life on it only to end up another Kaedehara, a bearer of a famous name that came to nothing. He wished to prove to Inazuma that the art was still trustworthy and to win back the Kaedehara name, and he died amid the snow.

He forged the blade, Kagotsurube Isshin, using the Tatarigami, which gave it a consciousness. On his deathbed he told it his wish. The Tatarigami's inherent malice twisted that wish, during the blade's journey back to Inazuma, into an obsession with proving itself against the gods.
### ULTRA +
The weapon text sets his craft against the Tatarigami: the Isshin Art means a mind still and free of distraction, yet a blade that kills is forged from resentment of the living, so the Akame School's pursuit of lethality tended to leave its members withdrawn and short-spanned. The wiki lists Kagotsurube Isshin's quest as Ere the End, a Glance Back; the blade's story there involves Kazuha, a descendant of the Kaedehara line.

## status
### LITE
Dead, having died in Snezhnaya after forging Kagotsurube Isshin.
### STANDARD +
The Akame School's line may have ended with him, but canon does not say so. His blade survives and reached Inazuma; it appears in no 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content found.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Scaramouche (the Wanderer) set the scheme that broke the Isshin Art; Kanenaga fled it rather than caused it. Kaedehara Yoshinori and his heirs bore the shame, and Kanenaga's last work was meant to restore their name. His sword gained a will of its own.
### LARGE +
The Fatui sheltered the fleeing smiths but, according to the wiki, deceived them. Akame Mitsunaga, the school's earlier master, was one of the three apprentices of Futsu Masayoshi alongside a Kaedehara and a Niwa; Kanenaga is the later and less honoured Akame.

## entry: Kagotsurube Isshin
@keys: Kagotsurube Isshin, Prized Isshin Blade
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
A 4-star sword described as blood-red and ominous, forged in Snezhnaya by Kanenaga using the Tatarigami. It has a mind of its own, born of its maker's remorse and the Tatarigami's malice, and travelled to Inazuma bent on proving itself.
### LARGE +
Its description says the hamon looks like flowing blood and tears in moonlight. On the way home the blade used humans as tools, possessing them until they were of no use. By the time it reached Inazuma its power was greatly diminished.

## entry: Akame School
@keys: Akame School, Akame Mitsunaga
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
One of the three families of the Isshin Art, known for lethal blades. Its early master Akame Mitsunaga became head of the art; three generations later the authorities revoked the school's leadership, calling its blades undesirable.

## entry: Case of the Eccentric
@keys: Case of the Eccentric
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
About a century ago, Scaramouche (Kunikuzushi) arranged for the Isshin Art smiths to fail the Raiden Shogun's commission by tampering with the blueprint. Many fled to Snezhnaya, Kaedehara Yoshinori ended the family forge, and the Isshin Art lost its standing. The Kamisato were spared after Yae Miko intervened.
