## summary
### LITE
Vaselisa von Strivozha, called the Pure One, was the first Grand Duchess of the Vila and ruled the Silver Court of Veneter, a haven as renowned as Kitezhgrad. Many fae hailed her a merciful saint.
### STANDARD +
In the story told by the weapon Breezeborne Refrain, her gentleness hid a calculated plan to keep the wind free.

## background
### LITE
She founded House Strivozha and the Silver Court of Veneter. Her court paid tribute to the Miscislavli Alliance, but she stayed neutral in the war against Monomakh's Pale Star Court and tried to save the innocent.
### STANDARD +
Because of the tribute, history counted the Vila among Miscislav's force. Monomakh defeated her alone, and she then denounced Miscislav and his envoys, ended the tribute and joined the Pale Star Court's campaign to unify the Snowland Fae.
### LARGE +
Another account is a weapon text about a human general of Miscislav's army, Alrekr Jarizleivich. He had opposed slaughtering the allied Water Imps and lost his titles, and his family and people were killed. He came to Veneter to surrender. Vaselisa wept, welcomed him and gave him mead, and killed him. She had his body carried to Monomakh with her maidservant Gluvasha as an assassination by Miscislav's spies. She hoped the emperor's anger would destroy the Blood-Devourer.
### ULTRA +
She told her attendant Photius why: the Volkodlak king and the Pale Star emperor each wanted the wind of the Vila as a weapon, one as a blade, the other as a holy sword, and she wanted it to belong to no one. She advised Photius to learn from Gluvasha, and warned that pride is a poison to a clan. A General History of Snezhnaya quotes a Songbook of Saint Strivozha, said to be composed by her, with a myth about Hyperborea and the Mother of Night. Her house stayed powerful: Beyond the Chrysalis shows a Grand Duke Photius, perhaps the same man, dying of a stroke, and Gluvasha's heir holding Veneter after the Cataclysm.

## relationships
### LITE
Monomakh Snezhnyi, who beat her; Miscislav, whom she paid tribute and then betrayed; her house and heirs.
### STANDARD +
Gluvasha, her maidservant or child (accounts differ) and later heir; Photius, her young attendant, who prepared elixirs.
### LARGE +
Photius was chosen for his skill with elixirs and feared that Monomakh would learn the truth; she mocked his habit of deferring to those he thought wise.

## appearance
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Jade-green eyes. She was famed for perfumery and dressed in white robes.

## abilities
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
She was famed for perfumery and, as the weapon text shows, for deception: she killed Alrekr with a cup of mead and wept convincingly before the emperor. Canon gives no details of Vila powers beyond a connection to the wind.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about three thousand years. Her house and the Vila still exist.
### STANDARD +
House Strivozha kept power under the Pale Star Court and after the Cataclysm. Her songs and her sainthood are remembered. Her fate is not stated.

## entry: Silver Court of Veneter
@keys: Silver Court, Veneter, Silver Court of Veneter
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Vaselisa's court was, before the Pale Star's torch was lit, a haven comparable to Kitezhgrad. Fae of many clans praised the Lady of the Vila for banishing cold and easing pain. She was known in later songs as pure, benevolent and true, though the weapon text suggests that this reputation was partly her own work. Veneter stayed with House Strivozha, and after the Cataclysm fell to a Strivozha heir again.

## entry: Alrekr Jarizleivich
@keys: Alrekr Jarizleivich, Alrekr
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A human general and war hero of the Miscislavli Alliance who routed Pale Star armies. He urged Miscislav not to slaughter the Water Imps and was stripped of rank; his wife and children were killed by the Blood Eagle. He asked Vaselisa for passage to Monomakh to surrender, and was killed by her; his death was presented as poisoning by Miscislav's spies.

## entry: Miscislav and the tribute
@keys: Miscislav tribute, Blood-Devourer tribute
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
In the book's account the Vila paid tribute to Miscislav until the emperor's envoys told them to stop, and he then demanded far more from the emperor. A scholar notes that the demand was thirty times the Vila's tribute.
