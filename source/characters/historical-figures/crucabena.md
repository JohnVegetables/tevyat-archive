## summary
### LITE
The previous Knave of the Fatui Harbingers and head of the House of the Hearth in Fontaine. To the orphans she was a smiling, story-telling "Mother"; in truth she ran the House as a lethal contest. She is dead, killed by her successor Peruere, who became Arlecchino.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through Arlecchino's memories, character stories and the animated short "The Song Burning in the Embers". She appears in person only in flashback.
### ULTRA +
She matters because of what she left behind: the orphans Arlecchino turned into a family on different rules, Clervie's shade, which lingered at Mont Esus East until Arlecchino released it in 4.6, and an old plan her subordinate Efim tried to keep alive. Arlecchino says the difference between them lies in how the rules are made, not how firmly they are kept.

## background
### LITE
Crucabena headed the House of the Hearth as the Knave. She told the children of the House fairy-tale versions of cruelties and pitted them against each other until one proved worthy to be "king" and inherit her title. Children who survived badly hurt were sent to Dottore or onto dangerous missions. An orphan named Peruere killed her and took her place.
### STANDARD +
She brought her daughter Clervie into the House at six to show the others that everyone was treated equally. Clervie saw it as a purgatory, tried to soften it, and repeatedly tried to escape; she was punished, publicly, to deter others. Most of the children were convinced the House was a happy family. Peruere, an orphan of unknown origin, saw through it. Crucabena favoured Peruere more than her own daughter, yet had Clervie duel Peruere; Clervie let herself be killed. A year later Peruere fought Crucabena at the same spot (the ruined site at Mont Esus East). Though the wiki calls Crucabena the stronger fighter, she died, and Peruere took the title.
### LARGE +
The wiki's account of Freminet's stories says that in the months after Clervie's death she agreed to take in Freminet, a boy whose family was in debt, shielding him from the creditors, but coldly told him his mother had sold him. She held his mother's safety over him to ensure obedience. Peruere later said this was a lie. Arlecchino's accounts say that Crucabena's experiments took place out of sight, so almost nobody knew of them, and so Peruere was widely seen as a madwoman who had committed matricide. Arlecchino let the rumour stand and added to it.
### ULTRA +
In the animated short she tells the children a story of being born alone into the world and growing strong in the House, with the goal of defeating all brothers and sisters to become "King". She notes Peruere's absence from the story and speaks of her "curse" flaring; she sends Clervie to see to her. In the fight with Peruere she uses Hydro, including turning aside Pyro-like attacks and forming a sword to hurl. Her grave stands in Mont Esus East and is the entrance to the Scattered Ruins; Arlecchino wrote the eulogy on the small tombstone at the site. Peruere took up the title, a Delusion and the name Arlecchino after meeting the Tsaritsa, and was commuted to the rank by Anastasya (per the wiki). Arlecchino then rebuilt the House as "Father".

## status
### LITE
Dead. Killed by Peruere (Arlecchino) more than a decade before the story. Nothing after 7.1 is stated about her.
### STANDARD +
Her grave is at Mont Esus East in Fontaine. In Version 4.6 she is seen only in Arlecchino's flashback; she had been introduced by overworld dialogue in 4.0 (unnamed) and named in the 4.5 animated short.
### LARGE +
Her influence persists: Efim Snezhevich, once an instructor in her House, kept following her directives, pretending to serve Arlecchino. He tried to use Lyudochka Snezhevna in Inazuma, and even considered making new orphans useful to train, until he was recalled to Snezhnaya before the plan could proceed. (Wiki summary of overworld and quest content.)

## relationships
### STANDARD
- Arlecchino (Peruere): favoured pupil, then killer and successor. Arlecchino says her own rule differs from Crucabena's in what the rules are, not in strict enforcement.
- Clervie: biological daughter, treated no better than the other children, killed in a duel to end the cycle.
- Children of the House: she was "Mother" to all of them, kind on the surface.
### LARGE +
- Freminet: protected from creditors and held in line by threats.
- Efim Snezhevich: instructor in her House, later her self-appointed continuer.
- Dottore: where the badly injured survivors of her contests were sent.

## abilities
### STANDARD
A Fatui Harbinger of great strength, whom Clervie called an "unimaginable" power and a "famous Harbinger". She is shown using Hydro in the animated short and may also have used Cryo; the source of her powers is not shown.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not Clervie's fantasy of a loving mother; the warm manner was a front.
- Do not call her Arlecchino without caveat: that name is probable but not stated for her.
- Do not give her a backstory, age, appearance beyond the wiki description, or motive canon does not provide.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Long dark pink hair in a braided side ponytail, dark green-grey eyes, crimson lipstick, a white blouse under a dark cloak, a grey-purple tie and brooch, black gloves and boots, a long slit skirt (wiki description of the animated short).

## entry: Crucabena and the House of the Hearth contest
@keys: Crucabena, previous Knave, "Mother" of the Hearth, king of the House
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Under Crucabena the House of the Hearth looked like a family of kind adults and friendly children. She taught the children to fight, set them against each other in duels, and planned to crown the child who proved most worthy as "king" to inherit her title. The wounded who survived were sent to Dottore or on dangerous missions. She told stories of cruelty as if they were fairy tales.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino's Character Story 3 says she never saw the Hearth as her home and treated the sacrifices as an interesting experiment. The result Arlecchino predicted a decade earlier came true: Peruere outlasted everyone and killed Crucabena. Arlecchino then renounced the title "Mother" and rebuilt the House as "Father".

## entry: Crucabena and Clervie
@keys: Crucabena, Clervie, Mother and daughter
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Clervie was brought to the House at six to show the others that the Mother treated everyone equally; Arlecchino says Crucabena cared little for her. Clervie failed to change her mother's mind and tried to flee or expose the truth, earning beatings that were used as an example. Peruere proposed killing Crucabena, but Clervie refused because she still thought of Crucabena as her mother. Crucabena had Clervie duel Peruere, who killed her. Clervie died at sixteen; her shade, reverted to her six-year-old self and missing most of her memories, lingered until Arlecchino formally expelled her from the House in Into the Sunlight (4.6), freeing her to fade.
