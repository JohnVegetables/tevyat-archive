## summary
### LITE
Fontaine's legendary Lord-Artificer: a prodigy of the Narzissenkreuz Institute, Marechaussee Hunter and founder of the Fontaine Research Institute, who created Sandrone and Pulonia.
### STANDARD +
His clockwork meka are still built in Fontaine's factories. He lived in the generation after the cataclysm and is spoken of as a genius, yet much of what is told of him is legend, and Sandrone, who knew him as an old man, is the main witness. He appears in Fontaine content only as a flashback figure in Neuvillette's memories and through artifact and item texts, and in Nod-Krai through Sandrone's testimony.
### ULTRA +
His story mirrors Rene's: two Institute children who dreamed of saving the world, one through transcendent knowledge and the Abyss, the other through machines built purely on human intellect.

## background
### LITE
Raised in the Narzissenkreuz Institute with his sister Mary-Ann, then adopted by Emanuel Guillotin. A prodigy at the Institute of Natural Philosophy, he broke with friends Rene and Jakob, joined the Marechaussee Phantom, defeated Narzissenkreuz, founded the Research Institute and died old after creating Sandrone.
### STANDARD +
The Carter incident: Rene and Jakob secretly experimented with Elynas's remains and, in an attempt to save the dying Carter, subjected him to a procedure that sped his decline. Carter died, and Alain cut off both men.

The clash: Narzissenkreuz showed Alain the calculated destruction after the cataclysm and asked him to join; he refused, saying there seemed to be no going back. In the battle at Elynas's remains Seymour was gravely damaged and Mary-Ann is presumed to have died. Alain defeated Narzissenkreuz, who fell into the chasm that dissolves all things.

### LARGE +
Prodigy and envy: At the Institute of Natural Philosophy he was revered and envied. After Rene and Jakob returned to Fontaine he recommended them to the director, and the three collaborated on his project. His sickly research assistant Carter Scherbius, who was older than he was, joined the circle. Alain studied Khaenri'ah machines such as ruin graders and also technology from the era of King Deshret. A mechanical breakthrough of his was hailed as Fontaine's energy revolution.

Marechaussee: He and Mary-Ann joined the Marechaussee Phantom. Inspired by Carter's idea of a thinking kamera, he built a prototype assistant, Seymour, to accompany and protect Mary-Ann. When the Court of Fontaine named the Narzissenkreuz Ordo a threat, the siblings were assigned to capture its members.

Later life: He founded the Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering, named the Suppression Specialist Mek after Carter, and in old age retired to his workshop. There he built a puppet modelled on Mary-Ann and, later, Pulonia.

Marionette recalls that after the first storm raised by Rene he lost his friends and family, and that his work was sustained afterward by the same dream that drew his closest friend into the Abyss. He rejected transcendental knowledge and power, fearing it for what it did to Rene, and found comfort only in formulas and machines born from human intellect. In his last years he set the formulas aside for the fairytales that the Institute's director once read the children at bedtime, accepting that the Institute's story needed a human being to see it through to the end.
### ULTRA +
In the Neuvillette flashback of In a New Light (4.1) he kneels beside Seymour at Elynas and says the Ordo plans to reanimate the monster and that the Chief Justice must be told. Fell Dragon's Monocle imagines the hero's fate: after the hunt he would lose what was most precious to him, never again trust anything beyond the sum of human knowledge, and conceive of a kingdom powered by machines and non-elemental energies. In the Honor to Your Devotion text (7.0), the Palais Mermonia pays Sandrone a large sum in compensation after the death of 'Duke Alain Guillotin'.

## personality
### LITE
Rigid, serious, devoted to research and close to almost no one but his sister; he distrusted anything beyond human knowledge.
### STANDARD +
Asked whether wanting to save oneself justified trying to save the world, he answered briefly: yes, it is enough. Late in life he was gentler: he told Sandrone she could choose her own birthday, laughed with her, and let his composure slip when she said she would stop discarding his notebooks. He did not like tea (Sandrone kept the tea set a subordinate brought him). During the limited Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7) Sandrone recalls that he built a mechanical piano, though by then he rarely played it, and owned a collection of classical music. He admitted to her that he once dreamed of saving the world when young, but firmly refused to hand that wish on to her.
### LARGE +
He dismissed the idea of Sandrone inheriting his burdens and considered his hope of her travelling for him a failure once she became his colleague. He kept his late research unpublished and wanted his notebooks destroyed after his death. He was reluctant to speak of the thinking machine, answering questions with 'It's a great shame. There's nothing to be said.'
### ULTRA +
His epitaph ends with a variation of the same words: 'Regrettably, there's nothing more to say.' In the Institute children's games and in Fell Dragon's Monocle he is cast as the 'hero' who slays the dragon Rene.

## relationships
### LITE
Mary-Ann: his sister. Sandrone: his creation, encouraged to be herself. Rene and Jakob: friends turned enemies.
### STANDARD +
Carter was his assistant, whose death ended the friendship. Emanuel Guillotin was his adoptive father, later dissolved by Narzissenkreuz; Basil Elton entrusted the siblings to him. Pulonia was left to Sandrone.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette is thought by the wiki to have known of the funeral, because the heavy rain lasted until midnight; this is an inference only. Alain gave identical pocket watches to his friends. According to the wiki, Rene's held a childhood photo and was later found by the Traveler, Jakob's watch was used for the body of Narzissenkreuz, and Mary-Ann's watch was worn by the Oceanid Ann. In the Nymph's Dream set, Nymph's Constancy describes a pocket watch first owned by a 'tiny champion who loved machines' (identified with Alain on the wiki) that fell with the friend it was given to into the primordial liquid.
### ULTRA +
The Fontaine Research Institute he founded later split over competing dreams, a rift Marionette says had nothing to do with him (see the Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles).

## status
### LITE
Dead for centuries; he died of illness after Sandrone's activation and was buried near the former Narzissenkreuz Institute ruins.
### STANDARD +
His tombstone names him Founding Director of the Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering, White Cavalier of Protection and Lord-Artificer; Sandrone attended the burial while heavy rain fell.
### LARGE +
His legacy: the large lifts of Romaritime Harbor and Lumidouce Harbor follow his designs, the Version 4.0 Special Program credits him with the Arkhe system, and legends of his thinking machine inspired Fontaine's engineers to pursue artificial intelligence, though none has succeeded. As of Version 7.1 he is present only in memory and item text. He appears in Sandrone's flashback in Descending Moon (6.3), where his question 'Is there anything you wish for?' returns during her last stand against Dottore, and Columbina speaks of the 'Lord-Artificer' who made Sandrone in Welkin Moon's Homecoming (6.3). Riddles Awaiting Answers (4.1) recalls him as 'Lord-Artificer' Guillotin and the White Cavalier of Protection. In the limited Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7) Sandrone revisits their old home, reads the last letter he left her, and his grave (Alain Guillotin's Resting Place, near Annapausis) becomes permanently accessible. Scarlet Proof (7.0) mentions the death of Duke Alain Guillotin.

## abilities
### STANDARD
No Vision or divine power: an engineer and energy researcher who invented the clockwork meka and the prototype Seymour.
### LARGE +
He also designed 'virtual neural network' tools for thinking and surveillance, and built a mechanical piano. As a Marechaussee Hunter he fought beside Mary-Ann and Seymour at Elynas and was the one who defeated Narzissenkreuz.

## appearance
### STANDARD
As a young man he wears the Marechaussee Phantom uniform in Neuvillette's memory. Official art shows him young, adult and old. Until Sandrone's education was largely finished he wore his hair long in a ponytail; after completing Pulonia he had the back cut short.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
He is dead; treat him as remembered in anecdote. In a flashback, portray him as reserved and precise.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He did not create Sandrone to be Mary-Ann; he told her she was not and wanted her to be her own person.
- He was never Narzissenkreuz's ally; he refused the offer and defeated him.
- According to Marionette he did not sacrifice his family for his cause.
- No canon source gives his age at death or his exact birth date.
- Do not confuse him with Emanuel Guillotin, his adoptive father.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Childhood: raised at the Narzissenkreuz Institute with Mary-Ann, Rene and Jakob.
- During the cataclysm: Basil Elton leaves; Emanuel Guillotin adopts the siblings.
- Years at the Institute of Natural Philosophy: clockwork breakthrough, collaboration with Rene and Jakob, Carter's illness and death.
- Marechaussee years: builds Seymour; clash at Elynas; Narzissenkreuz's defeat.
- Later: founds the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering.
- Old age: retires to his workshop; creates Sandrone on his 65th birthday and later Pulonia; dies and is buried; Sandrone continues his work and later goes to Snezhnaya.
### ULTRA +
Release order of his appearances: Nymph's Dream artifact texts (3.6); Marechaussee Hunter artifacts and Book of Esoteric Revelations pages (4.0); In a New Light flashback (4.1); Echoes of Fate account (6.1); Marionette's Notebook, Descending Moon and Welkin Moon's Homecoming (6.3); the animated short The Final Gift (6.6, released 2026-06-15); the limited Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7), with his last letter to Sandrone and his grave; the Scarlet Proof text (7.0). The titles Lord-Artificer and White Cavalier of Protection are first spoken in Riddles Awaiting Answers (4.1).

## trivia
### LARGE
His surname echoes Joseph-Ignace Guillotin, the French physician after whom the guillotine was named. The thinking-machine detail has been compared by fans to Alan Turing; this is interpretation, not canon.

## entry: Sandrone and Alain's Creation

@keys2: Alain, puppet, creator, created, father

### STANDARD
Alain finished Sandrone on his 65th birthday after modelling her on his dead sister. He agreed she was very different from Mary-Ann and wanted her to act on her own will. She learned fast, kept notebooks, and became his colleague. Before he died he asked her to burn his manuscripts; she read them first and kept the genius alive.

## entry: Pulonia
@keys: Pulonia
@keys2: Alain, Sandrone, attendant
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Pulonia was Alain's last creation: a purely mechanical assistant designed as an algorithm for calculating the unknown. Alain was unsatisfied, since even he did not know what the unknown was. He left it to Sandrone, who kept it close and passed it off as her attendant until Dottore destroyed it.

### STANDARD
The Marechaussee Phantom assigned Alain and Mary-Ann to capture the Ordo's members. At the remains of Elynas the two sides fought; an explosion damaged Seymour and likely killed Mary-Ann, and Alain defeated Narzissenkreuz.
