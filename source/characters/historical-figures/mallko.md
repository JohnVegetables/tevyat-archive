## summary
### LITE
Mallko was a Scion of the Canopy and a bearer of an Ancient Name. He died in a Night Warden War five years before the Natlan Archon Quests. He appears in Black Stone Under a White Stone as a temporarily reassembled soul in the Night Kingdom.
### STANDARD +
He was a strong fighter and the childhood friend of Vichama. As a soul he is steady and willing to sacrifice himself.

## background
### LITE
Mallko's parents died when he was young, and he grew up with Vichama. They became known by combining his strength with Vichama's scouting and reading of the battlefield. They promised to fight together to the end, even if the Abyss was never defeated in their lifetimes.
### STANDARD +
Five years ago, he, Vichama and Pisac won the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame and were chosen for the Night Warden Wars. On the eve of departure the Abyss attacked the Scions of the Canopy and Vichama was hurt rescuing someone. Mavuika allowed him to stay behind. The rest faced more enemies than expected and made a fatal misjudgement. Pisac died before the Riftwolves reached them and Mallko was slain by them.
### LARGE +
No one returned alive, so the Ode of Resurrection could not be used. His soul decayed in the Night Kingdom and his Ancient Name was lost. He was also a friend of Atea.
### ULTRA +
Vichama believed his presence might have prevented the miscalculation. Mallko himself, as a revived soul, described the link between them as feeling like their souls were connected. The wiki notes the name echoes an Inca-mythology figure; this is trivia, not lore.

## status
### LITE
Dead. In the Archon Quests, Vichama helped Kachina's rescue team look for Ancient Names, hoping to recover Mallko's. It could not be recovered, but the attempt reassembled parts of his soul in the Night Kingdom. He guided the team, explained the Wayob, and stayed behind to stall Comonqui, who scattered him. The wiki says his soul recovered when the Night Kingdom regained strength after the Abyss invasion was repelled, and he and Vichama then helped the Traveler in battle. Vichama later found peace on hearing of his sacrifice, and died in The Rainbow Destined to Burn.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Vichama is the defining bond: shared promise, guilt on one side. Mavuika, Atea and Pisac are teammates and friends; Kachina's team are those he helped.
