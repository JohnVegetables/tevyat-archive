## summary
### LITE
Suavegothe was a hardliner of Fontaine's 'old regime' who opposed the Iudex Neuvillette for letting Melusines live and serve in the Court of Fontaine. In a memory shown in the 4.1 quest Ever-Surging Memories she helps the plot that frames the Melusine detective Carole, and is later killed by the avenging Vautrin.
### STANDARD +
She is known only through that memory, which Neuvillette's Hydro power stirs up in the Fountain of Lucine. Nothing is stated about her life outside the conspiracy.

## background
### LITE
She appears as one of the conspirators around Thibert, a high-ranking figure of the old regime. Alarmed that Melusines had been given official posts, they chose to strike at Neuvillette through the Melusines rather than directly.
### STANDARD +
Overheard as Medals of Peace are awarded to Carole and Vautrin, she voices doubt: even with all their powers combined they are no match for Neuvillette. Thibert replies that political rules protect them. Thibert has already planned a murder to blame on the Melusines, and she gives in, saying that Neuvillette forced their hand and should be taught a lesson.
### LARGE +
Afterwards she is one of those inciting the crowd, shouting that Melusines cannot be trusted and that this goes for Neuvillette too. The plot succeeded in its immediate aim: Carole was drawn to the crime scene and accused, the Gardes were held off by the mob, and Carole gave her own life to calm it. Vautrin, who had seen in Carole his dead younger sister, took the suspect list from Rastignac and went after those responsible, which brought him to trial before Neuvillette.
### ULTRA +
Rastignac's report in the memory found no direct evidence but concluded the instigators were old-regime supporters whose interests the reform had damaged, and that their real aim was to force Neuvillette to confess error and yield power. Vautrin judged that they avoided Neuvillette and himself and went for Carole all along. Vautrin ordered public access to the information restricted and took the suspect list, saying he would bear responsibility. Suavegothe's two spoken scenes (the private doubt, then the public shout) are all canon gives of her.

## relationships
### STANDARD
Thibert is the plot's organiser and she follows his lead; Clotaire is another who stirs the crowd. Her opposition to Neuvillette and to Carole is political, not personal, as far as canon shows.
### LARGE +
Vautrin never faces her in the memory. Per the wiki he killed the whole group, then was found guilty in a trial that he secretly welcomed as a show of Neuvillette's fairness, and he founded the Mutual Aid Society after being sent to the Fortress of Meropide.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about four hundred years, killed by Vautrin according to the wiki. The remaining trace of her is the memory stored in the Fountain of Lucine.
### STANDARD +
She is not mentioned in later Fontaine Archon Quest content or in 6.x and 7.0-7.1 content as read for this file.

## entry: Carole framing incident
@keys: Suavegothe, Thibert, Carole's frame-up, Medal of Peace
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Fountain of Lucine memory seen in Ever-Surging Memories, old-regime hardliners including Thibert and Suavegothe trick the Melusine Carole of the Marechaussee Phantom into a murder scene and accuse her. A crowd demands she be detained; she sacrifices herself to calm it. Vautrin avenges her by killing the conspirators and is convicted by Neuvillette. This is the origin of the Mutual Aid Society's Medal of Peace badge, according to the wiki.
