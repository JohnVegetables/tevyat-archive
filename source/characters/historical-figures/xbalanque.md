## summary
### LITE
Xbalanque (divine name Haborym) was the first Pyro Archon and God of War of Natlan, about 2,000 years ago. He began as a mortal outlander, slew the Pyro Dragon Xiuhcoatl, and bargained with Ronova, the Shade of Death, to write the rules Natlan uses against the Abyss. Natlanese remember him as "The First Sun".
### STANDARD +
Natlan's records describe him as wise, brave and upright. When he returned for one day in 5.3, he turned out to be warm, joking and informal. He introduced himself as "Dear Beloved Sun", or "Lanque" for short, and called himself an old man.
### LARGE +
His rise depended on a war band of heroes. The group included the half-dragon Och-Kan and Lianca, Uenuku, Maghan, and the siblings Ahpub and Ixquieh. His legacy has a darker side too. Och-Kan revered him and later became the tyrant of Ochkanatlan. Kukulkan swore an oath of peace with him. A weapon text, however, says Xbalanque never meant to keep that oath.
### ULTRA +
Writings from 6.x and 7.0 present him as a symbol of humans who defy fate. The Belyi Tsar's notes in Snezhnaya say that Natlan's throne went to someone other than the "Sacred Lord" whom Fate had anointed. Varka's character story calls him the "human king" who led heroes to the divine throne without the favour of a Vision. A Nod-Krai thief's notes list him among idealists who could not stop their ideals from being twisted after their deaths.

## background
### LITE
An unprophesied champion, he conquered the feuding tribes into an armistice and was crowned by the Night Kingdom. He struck Xiuhcoatl's reality-anchoring eye, fell to his death with the dragon, and rose from its heart as Pyro Archon.
### STANDARD +
Ronova then showed him the coming Abyss. From the divine throne he bargained hard for her power, which created the Ode of Resurrection, the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame and the path by which mortals ascend as Pyro Archon. She agreed on condition of secrecy, and he added a bet: if humanity won, he could see the new world for one day.
### LARGE +
Ronova called him the greediest human and fiercest negotiator she had met, warned that if the wielder of her power could not conquer the fear of death innocent lives would pay instead, and promised to claim that "that treacherous Xbalanque" stole it. Xbalanque cut the dragon's eye in two. The halves became the two Golden Entreaties, which could set off the Huitzilopochtli array and destroy Natlan if they were ever joined. He and Kukulkan each kept one half and swore to keep peace between humans and dragons. He also set up the system that lets ordinary humans become Pyro Archon, and gave the Ancient Name Kiongozi ("Leader") its first meaning.
### ULTRA +
His followers founded the Eternal Empire of Ochkanatlan. Item text says that when Xbalanque "arose from the throne to enter the Sacred Flame", Och-Kan took power. Och-Kan believed Xbalanque should stay the one and only god. He broke Xbalanque's commandments on peace with the dragons until the scarlet-eyed youth overthrew him. The youth became the second Pyro Archon. Mavuika says the "woven scrolls" depict him as wise and upright, and that no source ever mentioned his sense of humour.

## relationships
### STANDARD
**Ronova**: his bargaining partner, not his friend. His demands interested her enough that she became an observer of Natlan's story. **Mavuika**: his distant successor and the current bearer of Kiongozi. He respects her deeply. After their duel he called her "the newborn sun, greater and more radiant", and in a moment of solemnity, "my dear child". **Kukulkan**: an ally of necessity. They shared the Golden Entreaties and an oath, but accounts disagree on whether Xbalanque ever meant to honour it. **Och-Kan**: the half-dragon he once treated with compassion. Och-Kan's devotion later became a tyranny carried out in Xbalanque's name. **Xiuhcoatl**: the dragon he killed, and died with, to end the dragons' rule.
### LARGE +
His war band treated him as both a leader and a friend. In a campfire scene from 7.0, the heroes argue drunkenly about which weapon is best. He settles it by stuffing their mouths with grilled meat. Then he asks the silent Och-Kan which weapon he wants, and later forges him a sword from materials taken off the dragons. Lianca joined him only after he convinced her that he wanted equality for the weak, not a crown.
### ULTRA +
In 5.3, the people of present-day Natlan treat him as a legend. Citlali's Wayob sensed his return and worried her. Ajaw, Kinich's dragon companion, calls him a punk. Mualani tried to "fish" for him. Iansan made the boy's body he was borrowing do double warm-ups. Mausau is the sickly tribal boy whose body he borrowed. Xbalanque was careful not to harm him, and the boy took it all as a vivid dream.

## abilities
### LARGE
As a mortal, he conquered every tribe with a great obsidian blade and killed a Dragon Sovereign. As Pyro Archon, he held divine authority and used the divine throne to borrow Ronova's power over death. Natlan's rules came from that power: the Ode of Resurrection, the Pilgrimage, and the way Pyro Archons ascend. He claims he could shake cliffs with a single punch and scale mountains in one leap. During his 5.3 return he created a "land of the sun" from his own soul, a spiritual arena where he and Mavuika could fight without their bodies. He could also hear the prayers of the dead in the Night Kingdom and repeat their words.
### ULTRA +
Limits: inside Mausau's untrained body he had less than a third of his strength. He failed to reach the last 32 of the Pilgrimage tournament. In his spirit form, he lost to Mavuika by the slimmest margin. The remaining life force Ronova gave him for the visit could have let someone else return for a short time. He offered it to Mavuika, and she declined. He says it is otherwise very hard to meet the dead outside the Night Kingdom.

## status
@version: 5.3
### LITE
Dead; as of 7.1 his soul is in the Night Kingdom. In 5.3, after Natlan defeated the Abyss, Ronova honoured his bet and he returned for a single day in a borrowed body, then departed at sunset.
### STANDARD +
He possessed a boy named Mausau, entered the Pilgrimage tournament, dueled Mavuika and rode her flamestrider across Natlan. Before he left, he said he would take Mavuika's words, that the peaks reached by earlier generations become the everyday goals of later ones, back to share in the Night Kingdom. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 brings him back on-screen. Later content mentions him only in texts and memories.

## appearance
### LARGE
His true form appears only in murals, the "Natlan: Divine Army" namecard and a short duel cutscene. The murals show a tall man wearing a large headpiece and gold earrings, a gold collar, gauntlets and a loincloth. A 7.0 weapon text calls him red-haired, with "flaming hair". The Crown of the Saints describes a crown woven from golden flowers and a great obsidian blade. During his 5.3 visit he looked like Mausau, a small red-haired tribal boy. In the spirit duel he appeared with glowing shoulder pieces and gauntlets.

## speech
### LARGE
In 5.3 he talks casually and plays the fool. He uses self-deprecating jokes ("long-dead guy on his first trip home"), complains about his rough first morning back (snagged by a fishing line, caught in a trap), and stays cheerful when people tease him. He calls Mavuika a "considerate gal" and announces "serious mode" before he gets to the point. When it matters, his voice becomes solemn and archaic. He speaks of suns, flames and hope, and calls the Traveler and Paimon "Blessed One-One" and "Blessed One-Two". In the 5.1 flashback with Ronova he is curt, demanding and sure of himself, a ruler driving a hard bargain. Use the playful voice for the old man revisiting the world and the commanding one for scenes from his reign.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- He was human, not a dragon or a born god. He became Archon by killing Xiuhcoatl and rising again.
- He is dead and back in the Night Kingdom. Do not keep him alive after his one day in 5.3.
- The menacing "Xbalanque" lines in the final Natlan Archon Quest battle (5.3) come from Gosoythoth imitating him, not from Xbalanque himself.
### LARGE +
- Mausau is not Xbalanque. The boy only lent his body for a day and remembers it as a dream.
- Do not turn him into a solemn, humourless statue. He was disappointed to find that no record called him "totally hilarious".
- Ronova did not hand over her power freely. He negotiated for it, and part of the deal was that she would deny involvement.
- Och-Kan's tyranny went against Xbalanque's commandments; Xbalanque did not order it.
- Ganyu only says that "the first Seven" drank in Liyue. Do not invent scenes between him and particular Archons.
- Do not give him a Vision, a known age, a height or a family. Canon gives none of these.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before about 2,000 years ago: the tribes, taught Phlogiston by Kukulkan, fall into civil war. Xbalanque, an outlander, gathers companions (Lianca, Och-Kan and others) and forces an armistice. The Night Kingdom crowns him.
- About 2,000 years ago: he dies killing Xiuhcoatl in the Great Volcano of Tollan, rises as the first Pyro Archon, splits the dragon's eye into the Golden Entreaties and makes an oath with Kukulkan.
- Straight afterwards: Ronova shows him the coming Abyss. He bargains with her at the Throne of the Primal Fire, Natlan's rules are written, and he places his bet.
- Later: his followers found Ochkanatlan. He enters the Sacred Flame, Och-Kan takes power, and the scarlet-eyed youth eventually overthrows Och-Kan and succeeds him.
- 5.0: first mentions in game (Archon Quest Chapter V, item texts, world quests).
- 5.1: heard in a flashback negotiating with Ronova (A Fuel Named "Fate").
- 5.3: Gosoythoth imitates his voice during the final battle. Later that version, in the Sol Invictus Chapter story quest As the Blazing Sun, he returns for one day in Mausau's body.
- 7.0: the Belyi Tsar's notes and the Heretic's Molten Blade text add to his legend.

- His name is pronounced roughly "shib-a-LAHNG-kay", as the 5.3 quest After the Peace voices it. It comes from one of the Maya Hero Twins.
- Other titles: Human Archon, Leader of Mortals, Human God, Blazing Sun.
- On his visit he got his trouser leg caught in a fishing line, apparently from Mualani and Kachina's fishing trip, and stepped into an unattended pit trap. A later birthday letter from Citlali describes Ororon digging a pit and saying the Traveler had told him it was how to catch "Xbalanque's fate".
- After riding the flamestrider, he joked that he might never have become Archon if such toys had existed in his day.

## entry: Xbalanque's pact with Ronova
@keys: Ruler of Death, Xbalanque's bargain, Xbalanque's bet
@keys2: Xbalanque, Haborym, First Pyro Archon
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Xiuhcoatl's death, Ronova showed Xbalanque the Abyss that would come. He used the divine throne to ask for her power over death. The rules she first wrote into the Night Kingdom were not enough for him, and he made her offer more. She warned that if the wielder failed to overcome the fear of death, innocent lives would pay the price. He answered that Natlan would bow neither to the Abyss nor to her rules. She agreed, as long as the deal stayed secret, and promised to claim that "that treacherous Xbalanque" had stolen her power. Mavuika later used this power against the Abyss, and Ronova came to claim payment in 5.3. Xbalanque's side bet won him one day in the living world, which he collected in 5.3.

## entry: The slaying of Xiuhcoatl
@keys: Xiuhcoatl, Golden Entreaty, Golden Entreaties
@keys2: Xbalanque, First Pyro Archon, Haborym
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Xbalanque led humanity against the Pyro Dragon Sovereign in what legend places at the Great Volcano of Tollan. Kukulkan says the dragon used one eye to watch over and anchor reality, and that this eye was its only weakness. Xbalanque struck it, and dragon and man fell from a great height together. He died, then rose from the dragon's heart wreathed in flame as Pyro Archon. The eye split into the two Golden Entreaties. He and Kukulkan each kept one and swore to keep peace between humans and dragons.

## entry: Xbalanque's war band
@keys: Xbalanque's companions, Xbalanque's party
@keys2: Xbalanque, Lanque
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Xbalanque did not win alone. Item texts name a band of heroes around him. Lianca joined because he wanted equality for the weak. Och-Kan had mastered the dragons' golden script. Ixquieh lured enemies into traps with Phlogiston. Ahpub fought dragons in mid-air. Maghan played the flute badly, and Uenuku complained about it. After one raid, by the fire, Xbalanque settled their argument over the best weapon and promised Och-Kan a sword forged from dragon materials. He enjoyed throwing absurd questions at bystanders, a habit Och-Kan found aggravating.

## entry: The one-day return (As the Blazing Sun)
@keys: Mausau, As the Blazing Sun, Hearken, Ye Who Have Returned
@keys2: Xbalanque, First Pyro Archon, Lanque
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
After the Abyss was purged, the Wayob told Citlali that Xbalanque would return. He woke in the body of Mausau, a sickly tribal boy. He entered the Pilgrimage tournament and dropped out before the last 32. Mavuika found him and he explained his bet with Ronova. In a spirit arena created from his soul, they fought a duel for as long as one brazier stayed lit, and he narrowly lost. He offered her his remaining life force to bring back someone she missed. She refused, as he had expected, and he passed on messages from her family and comrades in the Night Kingdom. After a flamestrider ride past Ochkanatlan and the People of the Springs, he thanked them and left at sunset. Mausau woke inspired to train for the next Pilgrimage.

## entry: Kiongozi, the first Ancient Name
@keys: Kiongozi
@keys2: Xbalanque, Mavuika, Ancient Name
@version: 5.3
### LARGE
Kiongozi ("Leader") is Xbalanque's Ancient Name, the first one, in the words of A Thousand Blazing Suns. He asked the Lord of the Night to record it. Over many generations it passed down to Mavuika. Xbalanque decided that the strength built up through this inheritance was why she beat him.

## entry: Och-Kan and Xbalanque's commandments
@keys: Och-Kan, Sacred Lord, Ochkanatlan
@keys2: Xbalanque, First Pyro Archon, commandments
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Och-Kan was half-dragon. Xbalanque showed him compassion, and Och-Kan devoted himself to him and came to think of himself as Xbalanque's "most perfect weapon". He swore to make a realm for humans alone. After Xbalanque entered the Sacred Flame, Och-Kan ruled Ochkanatlan. He believed Xbalanque should remain the only god and broke the commandments of peace with the dragons. In the 7.0 notes, the Belyi Tsar calls Och-Kan the "Sacred Lord" whom Fate had anointed and says Xbalanque took that role from him.

## entry: Gosoythoth's mimicry of Xbalanque
@keys: Gosoythoth, Lord of Eroded Primal Fire
@keys2: Xbalanque, Pyro Archon
@version: 5.3
### ULTRA
In the final battle of the Natlan Archon Quest, Gosoythoth pulled up memories of past Pyro Archons and taunted Mavuika and the Traveler in their voices, Xbalanque's among them. The threats of darkness and conquest in those lines are the Abyss's distortion. They do not reflect what Xbalanque believed.
