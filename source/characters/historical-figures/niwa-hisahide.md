## summary
### LITE
Niwa Hisahide was Armory Officer of Mikage Furnace in Tatarasuna, Inazuma, about 400 years ago, and head of the Niwa Clan of the Isshin Art. He disappeared during the Tatarasuna Mystery and was blamed for it; in truth Dottore killed him.
### STANDARD +
He is known to the present only through documents, local belief and the memories Nahida shows the Traveler and the Wanderer in the Interlude Chapter (3.3). Official records say he was modest, clever and gifted at running people and land.
### ULTRA +
He matters less for his own deeds than for what his death was used to do: Dottore's lie that he had fled became the second great betrayal in the life of the future Scaramouche, and fed the grudge behind the Case of the Eccentric centuries later.

## background
### LITE
A swordsmith of the Niwa Clan, one of the three Isshin Art clans, who rose to Armory Officer and administrator of Tatarasuna's Mikage Furnace.
### STANDARD +
The Niwa Clan is distantly related to the Kaedehara Clan. Aqaba's paper records Niwa as inheritor of the Isshin Art and successor to his clan; the quest's narrated account adds that he never quarrelled with his siblings and that his post reflected his standing with those in power. His second-in-command was the Vice Armory Officer, Miyazaki Kaneo. His clan elders in the city disliked his plan to adopt new forging techniques, and he went ahead regardless.
### LARGE +
Under Niwa, a mechanic calling himself Escher, a supposed Fontainian working with Akame of the Akame Clan, introduced a new smelting method. Niwa noted that every piece of ore was now smelted this way. Soon the furnace gave off black smoke, output faltered and workers tired and died. He says they kept the truth from spreading outside Tatarasuna. When people sent for help did not return, he accepted that he would take Escher's device into the furnace himself.
### ULTRA +
In the memory, Niwa had long suspected the connection between Escher and the disasters but resisted believing it. Before entering the furnace he told Escher to drop the act. Escher stabbed him. As he lay dying Niwa worked out that Escher served the Fatui and had given Tatarasuna the technique to release the evil in the Crystal Marrow. He left a message for the kabukimono, that both he and Nagamasa saw him as one of them and he had nothing to prove. Escher, who revealed himself as Dottore, called him dead and, per Nahida's narration, placed Niwa's heart in the device.

## relationships
### LITE
He took the nameless puppet that became the Wanderer in as one of the smiths, and told him he was human. Dottore, as Escher, murdered him.
### STANDARD +
He was close to Miyazaki, Katsuragi and the inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa. After his disappearance Nagamasa answered for Tatarasuna, and Katsuragi took the blame and was executed.
### LARGE +
Katsuragi's companions left notes suggesting they believed Katsuragi had committed some offence, so Niwa's friends did not know the whole story. Niwa's dying words to the kabukimono were never delivered; Dottore replaced them with the claim that Niwa had run.

## status
### LITE
Dead for about 400 years, killed in the Tatarasuna Mystery. As of 7.1 no new information about him has appeared.
### STANDARD +
Public belief at Tatarasuna and in Aqaba's paper was that he vanished or fled. The Wanderer learned the truth when Nahida showed him Dottore's own memories.
### LARGE +
After the Wanderer erased himself from Irminsul, the Traveler hears from Amenoma Tougo (who learned it from Kazuha) a new version in which Niwa and another swordsmith died leaping into the furnace to stabilise it. The Traveler also reads that a foreign mechanic replaced the kabukimono as the chief suspect, but Niwa's disappearance is unchanged, so Dottore remains his killer in the revised history too. The original version is still treated as the true story, and the Wanderer chooses to take back his memories of it.

## chronology
### LARGE
- About 400 years ago: Katsuragi finds the puppet at Shakkei Pavilion and brings him to Tatarasuna; Niwa tells him he is human.
- Same period: "Escher" arrives, the new smelting technique spreads and the Tatarigami begins to spread; the kabukimono goes to Narukami Island.
- Niwa confronts Escher before entering the furnace and is killed.
- Afterwards: Niwa is presumed to have fled; Katsuragi is executed in Nagamasa's place.
- 3.3: the Traveler and the Wanderer see these events in memory in the Interlude Chapter.

## entry: Niwa's death at Mikage Furnace
@keys: Niwa's death, Tatarasuna Mystery, Escher, Mikage Furnace
@keys2: Niwa
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Nahida's memory Niwa tells Escher that he has tried not to link the furnace's evil to the new technology. He sets out the losses: black smoke, worker casualties, a death from the filth, and no reply from those sent for help. He plans to carry Escher's device inside, likely to his death. Escher stabs him; Niwa realises the Fatui are behind it, and dies asking that the kabukimono be told he belonged with them.
### LARGE +
Escher later gives the kabukimono the device with Niwa's heart inside and says Niwa ran, leaving a gift taken from an innocent servant. The kabukimono, enraged, leaves Tatarasuna. Nahida points out that Niwa never meant him to be the one to enter the furnace.
