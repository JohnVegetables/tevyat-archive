## summary
### LITE
A Mt. Aocang crane who became human to help Liyue in a drought, married an exorcist, and lost her memories. In Version 4.4 she regains them and returns to crane form.
### STANDARD +
She lives with her granddaughter Shuyu, who thinks she has dementia. The Traveler, Paimon and Xianyun (Cloud Retainer) retrace her youth.
### LARGE +
Her story is the first act of Xianyun's story quest, A Thousand Moonlit Miles. Afterwards she is a non-sentient crane again.

## personality
### LITE
Gentle, vague and unselfconscious, carrying an unexplained guilt.
### STANDARD +
Underneath is a love of family strong enough that she stayed human rather than return to her master. Playful in her confusion (she asks for braised earthworms) and good-humoured about looking young. With her memories restored she is clear-eyed and at peace, resolving to train until she is sentient again and one day an adeptus.
### LARGE +
She was moved by stories of Cloud Retainer's deeds in an ancient drought and wanted to do likewise. Shame kept her from facing her master, so she sent a letter.

## background
### LITE
A Mt. Aocang crane taught by the adeptus Cloud Retainer. About fifty years before the Traveler's visit she drank a Human Mimicry Potion to help in a Liyue drought.
### STANDARD +
The potion would last ten years; staying human past that meant forgetting her past and becoming neither human, beast nor adeptus. She met an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng, and they worked as a formidable pair. She chose to stay with him and sent her master a letter.
### LARGE +
Ill at Wangshu Inn, she was given Soul-Revitalizing Teapills by a merchant who was Cloud Retainer in disguise, the pills made with her own blood. On the night her son was born, the couple were chased by fog of divine remains and the monsters it drew; her husband raised a barrier around an abandoned house while Cloud Retainer fought outside. Her son later had Shuyu.

## relationships
### LITE
Cloud Retainer/Xianyun (master); Shuyu (granddaughter).
### STANDARD +
Xianyun first disapproved of the husband's weak thaumaturgy, then respected his devotion, and did not drag Yuandai home: everyone leaves one day but may return.

## abilities
### LITE
A sentient crane; as a human she and her husband cleared evil spirits and bandits.

## status
### LITE
Now a non-sentient crane training on Mt. Aocang.
### STANDARD +
Seen on Qingyun Peak at night. Shuyu became Xianyun's first disciple in the human world. Canon through 7.1 shows her no further.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is a crane, not a cursed human.
- Her husband is unnamed; do not name him.
- She has not become an adeptus; she promises to train toward it.
- Xianyun is Cloud Retainer's human name; Yuandai knew her as Cloud Retainer.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Centuries ago: gains sentience; Cloud Retainer tells her stories.
- About 50 years before the visit: drought; takes human form.
- Years after: marries the exorcist and stays human; sends a letter.
- Illness at Wangshu Inn; disguised master supplies pills.
- Night of her son's birth: fog and monsters; baby's cry at dawn.
- 4.4: meets Xianyun, the Traveler and Paimon; memories return on Mt. Aocang; reverts to a crane.

## entry: Human Mimicry Potion
@keys: Human Mimicry Potion, Soul-Revitalizing Teapills
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Cloud Retainer prepared the Human Mimicry Potion for Yuandai with a ten-year limit. Yuandai stayed human and slowly lost her memories. Cloud Retainer later gave her pills made with her own blood, disguised as a merchant at Wangshu Inn, to suppress the effects.
### LARGE +
Her blood in the pills drew the fog of divine remains and monsters on the night of Yuandai's labour.

## entry: The nameless heroine legend
@keys: nameless heroine, mysterious heroine
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Xingqiu told the Traveler of novels about a nameless heroine who, fifty years ago in a drought, cleared evil spirits and bandit camps, then lived quietly with an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng. He marked Wangshu Inn, Qingyun Peak and an area north of Jueyun Karst; these became the stops where Yuandai's memories returned.

## entry: Shuyu
@keys: Shuyu
@keys2: Yuandai, grandmother, crane
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Yuandai's granddaughter, orphaned young. Once she learned the truth she asked Xianyun to take her as a disciple. When she once had a fever, a pure-white crane flew her to the adeptus abode and healed her; Cloud Retainer later admitted she had done so. Shuyu has her own entry.

## entry: Wangshu Inn and the abandoned houses
@keys: Wangshu Inn, abandoned villages, Jueyun Karst
@keys2: Yuandai, nameless heroine
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Wangshu Inn is where Yuandai recuperated and met the disguised merchant. North of Jueyun Karst stand abandoned houses where she once stayed and where Shuyu's father was born; Treasure Hoarders had to be driven off first.
