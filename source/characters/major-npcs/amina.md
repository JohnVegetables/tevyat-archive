## summary
### LITE
Amina is the chief of the People of the Springs, the hot-spring tribe of Natlan. She is earnest, inventive and responsible, the self-declared serious one among carefree people.
### STANDARD +
She took over from her grandfather after the elders voted for her, and she tries to lead the tribe towards a different future.
### LARGE +
She is a gracious host to visitors who come to bathe, and she carries her tribe's losses in the Abyss war with hope rather than despair.
### ULTRA +
Her story passes through the 5.0 attack on her tribe, the death of the elder Atea, the Traveler's help against a swindler, and the sending-off feast before the final descent to the Night Kingdom (5.3).

## personality
### LITE
Hands-on, earnest and warm. She says she is far more serious than the rest of her tribe, fusses about slackers and accidents, and has a constant stream of new ideas. She also keeps a positive view even when grieving.
### STANDARD +
Positivity is her guiding star, a lesson from her grandfather. She is quick to apologise when she thinks she sounded naive.
### LARGE +
She promises to take responsibility as chief if anyone finds someone lazing about.
She struggles with the details of organising: she can handle the big picture but not the fine points of party planning, which Atea used to do. Logistics and waste bother her, and she accepts good advice readily (Mavuika's plan for the feast).
### ULTRA +
Her honesty about her limits is part of her character: after the war she says she "just hope[s]" the dead reunited with Atea, then admits that might sound naive. She was ashamed that outsiders were being scammed in her tribe's name and wanted to fix it rightly, not just punish quickly.

## background
### LITE
She became chief of the People of the Springs after the death of her grandfather. The tribe's elders voted for her because her many new and unusual ideas suggested she could lead them to a different future.
### STANDARD +
At first she had no idea what to do as chief; Atea encouraged her and helped her work out what went wrong. In 5.0 the tribe was attacked by the Abyss while she was helping plan a surprise party for Atea.
### LARGE +
Afterwards she dealt with swindlers luring visitors to a "mysterious island" with the Traveler's help, redesigned patrols and a new stage, and later organised a farewell feast for the Traveler and Mavuika. When the Abyss attacked Natlan again, she oversaw defence for her tribe and survived, but mourned the large losses.

## relationships
### LITE
She respects Atea deeply, works with Mualani and trusts the Traveler. Mavuika, Kachina and Kinich help with her sending-off feast.
### STANDARD +
Atea was like a mentor, "Auntie Atea". Her tribemates Marokau, Hikueru and Manua appear in the party planning; Manua's ideas worry her.
### LARGE +
Mavuika's advice at the feast improves Amina's handling of surplus supplies and relieves her. She has warm dealings with visiting friends from Sumeru in a limited-time event.

## speech
### STANDARD
Open and friendly, with short, lively sentences and gentle teasing. She asks about the weather or whether you have had a hot spring bath today.
### LARGE +
She is informal: "I'm doing my job with all seriousness, mind you!" When she mentions Atea her voice grows softer.

## entry: Atea and the surprise party
@keys: Atea surprise party, Atea's party, hot spring refurbishment
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Amina, Mualani, Marokau and Hikueru refurbished an abandoned hot spring for a surprise party for Atea, wrongly believing she was recovering. The party was postponed a day because Atea was on patrol, and the Abyss then attacked, but was repulsed without casualties.
### LARGE +
Amina told patrol members to keep up their duties even while planning the party, saying the Abyss could attack at any moment. After the party, Atea died of the Abyssal corrosion she already carried; Amina was one of the few who knew.

## entry: The Legend of the Mysterious Island
@keys: mysterious island, Kaulu, Amina's Coin Pouch
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Scammers lured people to a "mysterious island" and took their Mora as travel costs. Amina had no proof, so she gave the Traveler marked coins to lure them out. Kaulu claimed innocence until Amina's marked Mora was found on him; she thanked and compensated the Traveler.

## entry: The sending-off feast
@keys: sending-off feast, Amina's feast, Night Kingdom send-off party
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
Before the descent to the Night Kingdom, Amina held a sending-off feast; Mualani and Kachina relayed the invitation and Kinich brought supplies. Too many well-wishers came, so Mavuika advised inviting everyone, with leftovers bought by the Speaker's Chamber for redistribution. Here the Traveler learned Atea had died.

## entry: Atea's lessons
@keys: Auntie Atea, Amina Atea, Atea mentor
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Atea encouraged Amina through her early uncertainty as chief and helped her analyse failures. After the war Amina hopes the dead meet again in the Night Kingdom.
