## summary
### LITE
A retired Kshahrewar architecture professor of Sumeru City, brisk and warm, who taught Kaveh's mother Faranak and knew Kaveh as a child.
### STANDARD +
She is an expert in formal garden design and the author of essays Kaveh studied. She now spends her days near the Bimarstan, running a feud with Cyrus in a vegetable-growing contest among retired scholars. In Kaveh's Hangout Event she tells him what Faranak was like and helps him find the password to Faranak's notebook.

## personality
### LITE
Direct, cheerful and a little mischievous, with a teacher's habit of sizing people up. She is affectionate toward the young people she knew and asks them to drop formalities.
### STANDARD +
Competitive and loud when she thinks she has been wronged (the tomato), then genuinely kind once she sees familiar faces. She is observant about people and sympathetic about the price of genius.
### LARGE +
She is candid about Kshahrewar's hard truths: the architects who burn brightest rarely find happiness, because they fight for their vision with clients. She says this gently to Kaveh because he shares his mother's traits.

## background
### LITE
She taught at the Akademiya's Kshahrewar Darshan and wrote on formal garden design. Faranak studied in her class.
### STANDARD +
Her class was known as the hardest in Faranak's diary. She watched Faranak graduate, argue with clients daily, meet the man who became Kaveh's father, and raise Kaveh. She stayed close to the family.
### LARGE +
She is now retired and joins other retirees, including Cyrus, in a vegetable competition where the loser must perform on stage for the winners. The news-crier Sir Arthur announces that she will give a new concept construction exhibition.

### ULTRA +
Faranak's notebook, as Kaveh reads it, records the diary-writer's remark that Zaha Hadi's class was the toughest and that structural mechanics was a pain she pushed through. Zaha herself does not read the notebook; she only supplies the memories that point Kaveh to the password.

## relationships
### LITE
Teacher of Faranak; family friend of Kaveh; sparring partner of Cyrus.
### STANDARD +
She remembers Faranak as quiet, perfectionist and admired, and thinks Faranak chose Kaveh's father partly because he stayed by her and listened. She tells Kaveh he can come to her to talk, including about architecture.
### LARGE +
Cyrus, an ex-sage and Cyno's adoptive father, is her rival over the tomato; he claims it was not him and mocks her. He hits back by saying she sneaked into Jannat's garden one night; she lets him off the hook for now. The two bicker like old acquaintances, though canon does not describe their history.

## abilities
### LITE
Expert in architecture, especially formal garden design; an experienced teacher.
### STANDARD +
Her essays were influential enough that Kaveh learned from them. No combat ability is shown.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A short elderly woman with white hair and glasses (per the wiki).

## speech
### STANDARD
Warm, bustling and plain-spoken. She calls the young "kids", "child" or "youngsters", teases ("You've grown taller again!"), and says "Hehe" or laughs. She asks to be addressed without formality and quotes people's remembered words accurately. When angry she shouts and issues challenges ("Just come out and face me, you coward!").

## entry: Faranak
@keys: Faranak, Kaveh's mother
@version: 3.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Zaha Hadi remembers Faranak as a young, golden-haired student who sat in the first row, was admired by many and worked with a frown and a hand on her forehead. Faranak once said that true art cannot be understood, though an artist wishes someone would. Zaha thinks the heartbreak of genius is finding someone who understands their ideas.
### LARGE +
Zaha suggests Faranak chose her husband, who could not understand her designs, because he stayed beside her. Kaveh uses this to guess that the notebook's password is companionship.
