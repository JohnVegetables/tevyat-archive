## summary
### LITE
A frightened but grateful civilian of Natlan who is among the people holed up in a stronghold near the Scions of the Canopy when Abyssal monsters surge in The Surging Darkness.
### STANDARD +
He appears in the "Under Siege" hotspot, where the Traveler helps defenders Achiyaku and Wayta drive the monsters off. Whether he survives the next Archon Quest depends on whether the player reinforced that hotspot.

## relationships
### LITE
He relies on the defenders Achiyaku and Wayta and on the Traveler, who saved the shelter.
### LARGE +
No relatives, friends or other ties are named in dialogue.

## personality
### LITE
Shaken and thankful. He asks what is happening elsewhere and says the fight was terrifying.
### STANDARD +
He is not a fighter. He relies on the defenders and on the Traveler, and he repeats the Pyro Archon's warning that an evacuation might be needed.

## background
### LITE
Little is known. He is a civilian sheltering in a house with wounded people during the Abyss's surge across Natlan in Version 5.1.
### STANDARD +
He thanks the Traveler for saving them after the attack. His affiliation with the Masters of the Night-Wind is given by the wiki but is not stated in dialogue.

## speech
### STANDARD
Plain and short. Three lines are recorded: asking if other places were hit, saying the Pyro Archon had told them to be ready to evacuate and that he never imagined so many monsters, and thanking the Traveler for saving them.

## status
### LITE
Fate depends on a player choice. If the "Under Siege" hotspot was reinforced, he is among the survivors escorted toward the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. Otherwise he may die in Despair Engulfs the Heavens.
### STANDARD +
Nothing is known of him after that quest.
### LARGE +
No appearance after the quest exists as of 7.1.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows only what a civilian near the front would: the Pyro Archon's warning, the monsters at the door and the wounded around him.
### ULTRA +
He has no knowledge of the Abyss's larger plans or of events after the quest.

## entry: The Surging Darkness hotspot, Under Siege
@keys: Under Siege, Achiyaku, Wayta
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In The Surging Darkness (5.1) the Traveler may choose to reinforce a stronghold near the Scions of the Canopy that is beset by Abyssal monsters, where Achiyaku and Wayta guard civilians and wounded who cannot be moved. After the fight Achiyaku plans to escort the first group to the Scions of the Canopy and call reinforcements for the rest. The choice affects which survivors, Waqar among them, are saved in Despair Engulfs the Heavens.
