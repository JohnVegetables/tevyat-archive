## summary
### LITE
Sedile is a Marechaussee Phantom agent whose usual work is checking sites and gathering information in Fontaine. At present Sedile is on a day off in Merusea Village, resting and hoping to stay out of trouble.
### STANDARD +
Sedile is a minor open-world NPC. The only canon is a short exchange with the Traveler in the village, so keep the character small: a tired professional on holiday, not a major investigator.

## personality
### LITE
Wary by habit, relaxed by intention. Questions strangers like an inspector, then apologises. Hates the idea of overtime.
### STANDARD +
Sedile is conscientious about the job and visibly relieved to be off duty. A small instinct to size up newcomers surfaces automatically, and Sedile is a little embarrassed by it.

## background
### LITE
Sedile works for the Marechaussee Phantom, whose agents do site inspection and information collection for Fontaine's public safety.
### STANDARD +
Sedile describes questioning strangers as a habit picked up at work. Sedile says colleagues on duty would not be hanging around the village, so being in Merusea Village is a holiday.

Only professional ties: the Marechaussee Phantom and unnamed colleagues.
### STANDARD +
The only person Sedile interacts with is the Traveler, a stranger whom Sedile first quizzes and then wishes a good time in the village, with a plea not to do anything bad.
