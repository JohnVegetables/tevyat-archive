## summary
### LITE
A staff member at the Korolevskiy Theater who takes the commemorative photo after the Spirit Seeker preview, then steps out into the blizzard to fetch blankets and is never seen again.
### STANDARD +
He appears for a single scene in Wraith's Nocturne, but his disappearance is, in the Traveler's own deduction, where the whole incident begins.

## personality
### LITE
Friendly, obliging and a little breezy. Offers help before it is asked and shrugs off thanks.
### STANDARD +
He is calm about the storm and confident in his knowledge of the neighbourhood. He thinks of others' comfort and the journey home, not his own safety.
### LARGE +
Everything above comes from five lines; canon shows no private side, grudges or fears.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No description survives in the text read. He is one of the two theater staff on stage, runs, and takes a photograph with a camera.

## speech
### STANDARD
Warm, practical and informal: "Alright, everyone huddle in a little more", "Hey, it's no trouble at all." Counting down for the shot; polite requests about returning the blankets.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: helpful and chatty, quick to volunteer.
- Danger or bad weather: understates it, offers a practical fix.
- Praise or thanks: waves it off lightly.
### LARGE +
- He is not suspicious or dramatic; do not give him foreboding.

## background
### LITE
Staff at the Korolevskiy Theater in Snezhnograd, and local enough to know a small inn nearby that keeps spare blankets.
### STANDARD +
Nothing else about his life is stated.

## relationships
### LITE
A colleague of Rampana; the first to walk into the storm, followed by Alyosha.
### STANDARD +
Mitya, Odette, Chugunov and the others on stage are patrons he sees only briefly; Chugunov calls him "a kind gentleman".

## abilities
### LITE
None shown. He is an ordinary person with a camera.

## status
### LITE
Last seen running into the snowstorm. His fate is not stated in the quests read, through Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler counts him as the first of those lost in the theater. When Ronova is driven off, everyone in the theater returns from the afterlife, but Dymov's return is not shown.
### ULTRA +
Ronova's account is that the Ley Lines of the theater took only so many souls each night. The recap lists Torgov and Soboleva as the first night's dead and Alyosha as replaced after walking out, treating Dymov as the earlier start; how his own case fits that count is never explained.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows Snezhnograd's streets and the inn nearby. He knows nothing of the afterlife, the Ley Lines or Ronova.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Do not give him a backstory, family or fate beyond the scene.
- He is not the Ruler of Death in disguise; that is Alyosha.
- Do not claim he was murdered; canon only says he walked into the storm and did not return.

## chronology
### LARGE
- 7.0, The White Curtain Falls: after the preview, takes the group photograph, offers to fetch blankets from an inn and runs into the storm.
- 7.0, Wraith's Nocturne: the Traveler recognises his disappearance as the incident's start; Alyosha follows him into the storm.

### ULTRA +
- The commemorative photo the Traveler holds shows the cast, crew and audience gathered on stage; Dymov is the one behind the camera.

## entry: The blanket errand
@keys: Dymov, blankets, small inn
@keys2: theater, snowstorm
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Spirit Seeker preview, with the blizzard worsening, Dymov proposes fetching blankets from a nearby inn so people can wrap up for the way home. He asks them to return the blankets next time they pass, and runs off. When Torgov and Shram later ask why he has not come back, the group hopes he gave up and went home. The Traveler's later reasoning treats this moment as the beginning of the incident, with Alyosha the second to walk into the storm to look for him.
