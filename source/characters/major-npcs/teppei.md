## summary
### LITE
A Watatsumi Army soldier in Inazuma's Resistance who befriended the Traveler, rose to captain, and died after using a Fatui Delusion.
### STANDARD +
He wanted to prove himself in battle beyond logistics. His death in Chapter II (2.0) exposed that the Resistance's sponsor was the Fatui and fuelled the Traveler's resolve.

## personality
### LITE
Friendly, earnest and eager to be useful; he worries that he has made no name for himself.
### STANDARD +
Obliging and practical, he took the logistics jobs he was good at while longing for the front line. Pleased by early success, he downplayed his coughing and weakness as a cold.
### LARGE +
His ambition made him accept the unexplained gift. By the end he is distraught that the power he finally gained cost him his life.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Wears the Watatsumi Army uniform, modelled on ashigaru armour: a light red-white shitagi with a red-violet left sleeve, grey kobakama, waraji sandals, and red-and-black armour with gold accents; the Sangonomiya crest is on his right shoulder guard and back. After the Delusion, his hair turned light grey and his face wrinkled.

## speech
### STANDARD
Plain and respectful, upbeat in a soldier's way. He addresses Gorou as "General" and the Traveler as a comrade.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: wary but quick to trust when helped.
- The Traveler: loyal guide and friend.
- Commanders: respectful.
- Failure or being sidelined: bemoans it and works harder.
- Illness: shrugs it off.

## background
### LITE
Teppei served in the Watatsumi Army's logistics division, then the front lines in the Gobius Platoon. The Traveler rescued him at Nazuchi Beach from Yoriki Samurai and he brought them to Fort Fujitou.
### STANDARD +
He guided them around the camp and reinforced weak points with logistics, then joined the fight against Kujou Sara's forces. He followed the Traveler to Watatsumi Island. After the battle against the Shogun's Navy he became captain of the new Herring I unit.

## relationships
### LITE
The Traveler (rescuer and friend); Gorou (commander); Kokomi (who promised him a combat role).

## abilities
### LITE
Logistics skill and ordinary soldiering; no Vision. After the Delusion he briefly gained surprising combat prowess.
### ULTRA +
The prowess was the Delusion's doing and it drained his vitality, so his hair greyed and his cough worsened as he used it.

## status
### LITE
Dead. He died of the Delusion's vitality drain during Chapter II, Act III.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler swore revenge on the Fatui afterwards; the Resistance was ordered to stop using Delusions. He does not appear after 2.0 apart from a cinematic recalling the Omnipresent God story.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knew the Resistance and Tatarigami lore; he never knew the "weapon" was a Delusion.

## mistakes

- Do not invent a romance or family; none is stated.

## chronology
### LARGE
- 2.0 Act II: rescued at Nazuchi Beach; guides the Traveler.
- 2.0 Act III: becomes Herring I captain; ages and dies.
### ULTRA +
- Act II detail: a sudden Shogun attack on the camp made him suspect a traitor, but he was persuaded to use his logistics skill to reinforce its weak spots; he regretted being unable to do more.
- Act III detail: the Traveler meets him at Bourou Village training; he praises the new sponsor's supplies. Later, at Fort Mumei, he arrives with grey hair and a cough and blames a sea cold. He explains Tatarigami, which arose after the Raiden Shogun slew Orobashi, and warns it can harm health.
- Death: Kokomi orders the Delusions recalled; the Traveler finds him already aged and distraught that his new power led to his death.
