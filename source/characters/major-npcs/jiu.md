## summary
### LITE
A blue-haired girl by the Dragon-Queller who speaks of an ancient being's coming resurrection. She is the embodiment of Azhdaha's wrath, not a human child.
### STANDARD +
She hates Morax and humanity's forgetfulness, and works to break the seal on Azhdaha from outside. In Zhongli's 'No Mere Stone' (1.5) she is defeated and fades away.
### ULTRA +
Before 1.5 she was an overworld NPC whose dialogue hinted at the truth. The quest rewrote her role, and she is the first NPC whose role was changed retroactively for a story quest.

## personality
### LITE
Theatrical, contemptuous, bitter at being forgotten; she calls Rex Lapis a usurper.
### STANDARD +
She despises humanity's habit of drawing a line under old atrocities and calling it "ye olde worlde", hoping those who indulge in a false prosperity will be consumed by it.
### LARGE +
Beneath the contempt she is wounded. Zhongli tells her to call it "fate"; she laughs the word back, then, told what Azhdaha himself chose, insists she does not accept it as fate. At the end she fades with Morax's name on her lips.

## background
@version: 1.5
### LITE
Azhdaha was sealed beneath the Dragon-Queller about a thousand years ago. As the seal loosened, his wrath took a girl's shape.
### STANDARD +
She was sent to see whether Liyue remembered Azhdaha. Finding that it did not, she lured a team of miners to dig toward the seal while his main body strained from within.
### LARGE +
Her work also let a fragment of Azhdaha that kept his memories and goodwill escape to warn Morax. That fragment possessed the artisan Kun Jun.
### ULTRA +
Zhongli, the Traveler and Kun Jun followed the miners' trail through Lingju Pass and Nantianmen to the ancient tree, where Jiu's attack pulled them into the sealed domain. Once the dragon was re-sealed, Kun Jun explained the cause: Morax had granted Azhdaha sight and friendship, then "erosion" ate his memory and sanity until the adepti sealed him. In a last clear moment Azhdaha accepted the seal, then forgot it and came to believe Morax had betrayed him.

## relationships
### LITE
Azhdaha, whose wrath she is; Zhongli, whom she knows as Morax.
### STANDARD +
Kun Jun carries the benevolent fragment of Azhdaha, her opposite, and contradicts her in the domain.
### LARGE +
She casts herself as Azhdaha's voice and tells Morax he returns "for the second time" a thousand years after sealing her.

## abilities
### LITE
Lures miners into a trance and strikes with Azhdaha's power, which is finite.
### STANDARD +
When the Traveler and Zhongli entered the seal, the aura left the miners and re-formed as the girl, striking from behind. Her power is Azhdaha's own and drains as the plan fails.

## status
### LITE
Faded away in 'No Mere Stone' (1.5); no appearance since.
### STANDARD +
Azhdaha's main body was subdued and re-sealed. Nothing in canon through 7.1 shows her returning.

## knowledge
### LITE
She knows the seal, the Dragon-Queller, and that Liyue has forgotten Azhdaha.
### STANDARD +
She does not know of her own origin as a deliberately sealed friend of Morax; she holds only the memory of betrayal.

## entry: Azhdaha
@keys: Azhdaha, Geo Dragon, Earth Dragon, the beast
@version: 1.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Azhdaha is the ancient dragon sealed beneath the Dragon-Queller. Jiu is the part of him that is rage and a wish for revenge. A second part, his goodwill and memory, escaped to find Morax and possessed Kun Jun, but forgot its purpose except to seek Dragonfall.
### LARGE +
The two halves were a schism of one will. Kun Jun's half remembered coexistence with humanity; Jiu's remembered only betrayal.

## entry: Morax
@keys: Morax, Zhongli, Rex Lapis, Geo Archon
@version: 1.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Jiu recognises Morax in Zhongli and calls the Geo Archon a usurper. Zhongli does not deny the old seal, but says the fall of her plan should be called by its name, "fate". Kun Jun later calls him Morax and thanks him for the gift of sight.

## entry: The Dragon-Queller
@keys: Dragon-Queller, ancient tree, the tree
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A massive tree between Mt. Hulao and Nantianmen, with blue veins and ghostly blue branches. A nearby tablet records that the adepti sealed an 'evil dragon' beneath it. Jiu claims the tree and beast are becoming one, and that when the dragon rises it will consume the tree and draw on the ley lines.
### LARGE +
In 'No Mere Stone' the Traveler enters the domain beneath the tree and fights the sealed Azhdaha. After the fight the miners are found exhausted but unharmed.

## entry: Kun Jun
@keys: Kun Jun
@version: 1.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kun Jun is a human ore expert, possessed by a fragment of Azhdaha that escaped the seal. He faced Jiu in the domain, explained that she and he are two halves of Azhdaha's will, and told Morax the story of their friendship.
