## summary
### LITE
A folklorist's grandson who posed as the adeptus Starsnatcher, was shamed by Xiao and now tends Pervases' Temple.
### STANDARD +
His old fraud made him a small following near Wangshu Inn. After Xiao's Dream Trawler ritual he gave up the act, returned his takings and began writing about the Yakshas.
### ULTRA +
He is an ordinary man, not a hidden adeptus. Portray him as a sincere penitent with a scammer's habits of speech.

## personality
### LITE
Eager, talkative and a little cowardly; an enthusiast of adepti lore who once let greed warp it, now trying hard to be honest.
### STANDARD +
As Starsnatcher he was grandiose and spoke of himself as 'one'. Out of the mask he is anxious and quick to explain himself. He is embarrassed by his past and hates it being mentioned in front of real adepti.
### LARGE +
He is earnest about atonement: he spends his own money on the temple. He keeps collecting folklore, but admits that many tales lack evidence and that confirming sources takes ages. He also sees the opportunity in everything, such as advertising the temple to bring in visitors.
### ULTRA +
He turned his knowledge of cons into a guidebook to catch other cheats, which the Ministry of Civil Affairs found useful.

## background
### LITE
He grew up on his grandfather's tales of the adepti. When his grandfather died he found a Sigil of Permission among his things, began imitating an adeptus for fun and then for money and fame.
### STANDARD +
As Starsnatcher he collected a small group of followers who stopped short of Wangshu Inn and demanded payment for wishes. Xiao's Dream Trawler ritual showed him the real Conqueror of Demons, and he swore off the act.
### LARGE +
In 2.4 he returned with workers to fund the repair of Pervases' Temple, which he found while researching a book on the Vigilant Yaksha. He pays from his own pocket; the Ministry of Civil Affairs paid him well for his anti-scam guidebook.
### ULTRA +
After the temple was finished he stayed on as its keeper, offering incense to visitors and working on his book.

## relationships
### LITE
Xiao: the adeptus whose forgiveness reformed him. The Traveler and Paimon: the pair who exposed him and later helped him.
### STANDARD +
Pervases: the dead Yaksha he honours. The Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Millelith: his unexpected patrons.
### LARGE +
Verr Goldet, Wangshu Inn's boss, knew him as the guest who signed in under the name Wang Ping'an.

## abilities
### LITE
Folklore and a persuasive manner; no combat skill.
### STANDARD +
He posed as an adeptus and exorcist with a Sigil of Permission, which makes real adepti refrain from harming its holder, but it could not drive off the tainted hilichurls and, per Xiao, was what drew them.
### LARGE +
He cannot fight off hilichurls or Treasure Hoarders and runs from them.

## status
### LITE
Alive; keeps Pervases' Temple in the mountains near Liyue Harbor.
### STANDARD +
He is still working on his book about the Yakshas, slowly.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows adepti folklore well and now knows Xiao is real. He does not know the Traveler's greater journey.
### STANDARD +
He remembers the Traveler and Paimon as the pair who exposed him and does not want the old name mentioned.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Short black hair and the green everyday clothes of a Liyue man. As Starsnatcher he wore an ornate mask like a Yaksha mask.

## speech
### STANDARD
Friendly and a little breathless, apologetic, fond of self-justifying explanations. As Starsnatcher he spoke in the grand third person ('one'). He says 'Be careful out there' when parting.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Reminded of his past: flustered, urgent to change the subject.
- Real adepti: prostrate and reverent.
- Folklore: lights up and talks at length, then admits what he cannot prove.
### LARGE +
- Hilichurls or Treasure Hoarders: panics and calls for help.
- He no longer claims any power; he is quick to deflate himself.

## entry: Starsnatcher, the false adeptus
@keys: Starsnatcher, Master of Stars, false adeptus
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
Starsnatcher was a masked man outside Wangshu Inn who claimed to be an adeptus, granted wishes in exchange for Mora, and used a Sigil of Permission. His followers vouched for him. When asked to bring something for his help, the Traveler brought food, and Paimon and the Traveler caught him buying a book about the Yakshas and then failing to drive off hilichurls. Xiao had never heard of any such adeptus.
### LARGE +
The Dream Trawler ritual drew his spirit before them, and the sight of Xiao made him swear an oath. He left a letter, a repayment of his takings and the sigil at Wangshu Inn.

## entry: Pervases' Temple
@keys: Pervases' Temple, temple of Pervases
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
A run-down temple to the late Yaksha Pervases in the mountains near Liyue Harbor. Wang Ping'an paid for its repair as atonement, with the Millelith guarding the work. After the Hereafter quests the temple is restored and visitors can offer incense there.
