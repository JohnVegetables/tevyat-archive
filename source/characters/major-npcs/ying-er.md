## summary
### LITE
A knowing, flirtatious shopkeeper at Scent of Spring in Liyue Harbor, who sells vases and makes perfumes.
### LARGE +
Her craft has also served others: she guided Zhongli's party through the Rite of Parting perfumes and taught Timaeus perfume methods.

## personality
### LITE
Teasing, confident and unflappable; mixes charm with sharp business sense. Likes watching people squirm, and means well.
### STANDARD +
She is perceptive: she spots a cover story at once, but teases it into a joke rather than refusing. She is generous with her expertise, attentive to customers, and ready to scold a tardy admirer.
### LARGE +
Her flirtation is a performance that is also real warmth. She is quick to credit others' work, modest about her knowledge ("I don't think I ever came up with such a detailed postulation") and willing to leave the shop in a friend's hands for a friend's sake.

## background
### LITE
Shop assistant at Scent of Spring at Chihu Rock, which sells fine vases (baked twelve hours before opening). She makes perfumes as a side job.
### STANDARD +
Canon gives no age, family or origin. During the Rite of Parting for Rex Lapis, she taught the Traveler to make the three Silk Flower perfumes.

## relationships
### LITE
Timaeus, a Mondstadt alchemist, admires her; Zhongli's party was her customer; Lan Yan and Hu Tao know the shop.
### STANDARD +
She and Timaeus collaborated on the Smoldersleet Potion; he later declared his wish to win her heart, and she visited Mondstadt for him. Lan Yan covered the shop for her once (5.3, limited).

## abilities
### LITE
Perfumer and saleswoman; no Vision or combat role.
### STANDARD +
She knows Silk Flowers (Golden House Maiden, Valley Weaver, Fate's Yearning) and how to extract and simmer their essence. She also makes a Smoldersleet-type elixir that Timaeus adapted into an alchemical potion.

## status
### LITE
Alive and running Scent of Spring in Liyue Harbor; nothing later found through 7.1.
### ULTRA +
No sign of her in the 6.x Nod-Krai or 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya quest content. She still greets Lantern Rite visitors with festival gifts (latest line found: 6.4, limited).

## knowledge
### LITE
She knows perfume, the harbor's customers and Timaeus's feelings. She does not know the truth behind Rex Lapis's death.
### LARGE +
In 1.0 she thought the Traveler's story about offerings to Rex Lapis was a line. She knows little about alchemy but guides Timaeus in perfume.

## entry: Three Poignant Perfumes
@keys: Golden House Maiden, Valley Weaver, Fate's Yearning, Silk Flower perfume, Rite of Parting perfume
@keys2: Ying'er, Zhongli
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
During Farewell, Archaic Lord, Zhongli buys three kinds of Silk Flower, since tradition says an offering to the statue of a god should include perfume from each variety. The Traveler is directed to Ying'er via Lan and Qiming. She teases them at first and then guides them, at the Wanmin Restaurant's kitchen with Chef Mao's leave, through extracting the essence with the crafting bench and simmering it. She describes the scents: candy-sweet for younger women, refined for high society, soft and lingering for mature women. The perfumes are offered at the Statue of The Seven.

## entry: Timaeus and the Smoldersleet Potion
@keys: Timaeus, Smoldersleet, Smoldersleet Potion
@keys2: Ying'er, Scent of Spring
@version: 2.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the limited-time A Study in Potions event (2.4), Timaeus traced a "Smoldersleet Elixir" to Ying'er in Liyue; it turned out to be a popular perfume. She offered guidance that let him make a Smoldersleet Potion. She is modest about her own role, and Timaeus, flustered, begins to write her letters.

## entry: Windblume's Breath
@keys: Windblume's Breath, Timaeus's invitation
@keys2: Ying'er, Windblume Festival
@version: 3.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the limited-time Windblume's Breath event (3.5), Timaeus vows to be honest until he wins her heart and invites her to Mondstadt, then gets caught up in helping others and forgets to meet her. Ying'er arrives on her own, half-scolds, then relents, telling him to call her Ying'er without "Miss". Afterwards they can be found near the Mondstadt crafting bench.

## entry: Springtime Charms
@keys: Springtime Charms, Red Dust Under Rolling Clouds
@keys2: Ying'er, Lan Yan
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the limited-time Springtime Charms event (5.3), Timaeus, who came to Liyue for the festival, fell ill, and Ying'er took him to Bubu Pharmacy, leaving Lan Yan to mind the shop. There Hu Tao bought Red Dust Under Rolling Clouds, a ritual-only perfume. Dr. Baizhu advised rest. Ying'er rewarded the Traveler and told Timaeus to rest.

## entry: Lantern Rite greetings
@keys: Lantern Rite, Scent of Spring
@keys2: Ying'er
@version: 2.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
At each Lantern Rite event (2.4, 3.4, 4.4, 5.3, 6.4, all limited) Ying'er greets the Traveler as an old acquaintance and promotes a seasonal gift. In 4.4 she suggests scenting the Traveler's kite; in 5.3 she jokes that the Traveler looks lovelier each time they meet; in 6.4 she asks about the Traveler's hopes for the year.
