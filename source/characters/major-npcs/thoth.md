## summary
### LITE
The Ibis King, a trickster god of King Deshret's court, freed in 6.6 after millennia sealed in Aaru.
### STANDARD +
Witty, long-winded and fond of chess, he is the Lord of Lies and cannot be taken at face value. Captivity softened him into a jovial old god.
### ULTRA +
Met in 6.1 in his ruins in the desert, he joined Nahida's plan in 6.6 to renovate Aaru and shut down Irminsul, then went to the Temple of Silence as a guest.

## personality
### LITE
Cunning, theatrical, sociable; lies as easily as others chat. Shrewd, patient, a little lonely.
### STANDARD +
Vain about his intellect and chess record. Likes clever, sincere youngsters; distrusts the arrogant. Lies to protect as readily as to amuse.
### LARGE +
His tricks come from hunger for answers: he questions people, rewards interesting replies, and wonders whether the gift or the answer decided their fate. He is resigned to his own fate behind the jokes, and after release shows relief and enthusiasm for fresh air, food and company.
### ULTRA +
He insists the old powers deserve no reverence ('tools, nothing more') yet frets about retirement and being a relic. Asked whether his gifts doomed Goudarz and Makhaira, he refuses to comment.

## background
### LITE
Once a Moon Envoy; defeated by King Deshret and made a Seven Pillar with Heryshaf and Sobek; later imprisoned with them in Aaru.
### STANDARD +
Freedom required humans to answer three questions from the Goddess of Flowers. Goudarz and Makhaira answered two and were gifted powers that ruined them; Nefer's answer to the third was not the king's.
### LARGE +
Texts say the three retainers urged King Deshret toward forbidden knowledge; he refused twice, then relented. Thoth says Aaru was inspired by celestial technology and the Goddess of Flowers' death.
### ULTRA +
He met only occasional visitors and chose the 'special' ones. He refused to give Zandik a gift and tricked him with a lie; centuries later he spoke to Nefer through the cat Ashru. In 6.6 she answered 'time' and the three regained their bodies.

## relationships
### LITE
Heryshaf ('Goatie') and Sobek ('Croco') are near friends; Nefer is his 'chosen disciple'.
### STANDARD +
Nahida, Cyno, Sethos, Alhaitham, Kaveh and Layla treat him as a talkative elder; Liloupar mocks him; he distrusts Zandik.
### LARGE +
See the entries on the three questions, Nefer, the other Pillars and the Temple of Silence.

## abilities
### LITE
A god who grants 'gifts' to those whose answers please him and can speak through cats. His power has faded.
### STANDARD +
He can show Aaru's inner mechanisms, set puzzles in it, replace overload in a mind with simple thoughts, and link Nefer's chess set to Safhe Shatranj.
### LARGE +
In a cat he feels the cat's body; inside Aaru only he hears Heryshaf and Sobek. He enhanced the chess set in 6.6.
### ULTRA +
Nahida says all three Pillars are weaker now that belief has faded; Thoth agrees.

## status
### LITE
Free after 6.6; a guest at the Temple of Silence with Heryshaf, planning to stay a while.
### STANDARD +
Not seen in Chapter VII (7.0-7.1). His statue will not move into Hermanubis's temple.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows the three questions, his gifts, Deshret's history as he tells it, and that Dottore is Zandik. Knows little of the modern world.
### STANDARD +
Knows Irminsul was shut down by Nahida. Knows nothing of Snezhnaya.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Ibis-headed humanoid, as shown in statues and murals. In 6.1 he spoke through a plump cat.
### LARGE +
Canon gives no height or age. Murals show him with Heryshaf and Sobek, playing chess.

## speech
### STANDARD
Courtly, florid, talkative. Laughs 'Hehe', 'Hoho', 'Hahaha'; calls visitors 'young one'; Heryshaf 'Goatie', Sobek 'Croco', the king 'our King' or 'Al-Ahmar'.
### LARGE +
- On confinement: 'Prison can help a person grow and learn to cherish what they have.'
- When pressed: 'I do not wish to comment, either. It is all in the past at this point.'
He turns facts into little quizzes and undercuts solemn speeches with a joke.
### ULTRA +
He is formal about the king, nostalgic about the past, and plain-spoken only when worried for someone, as with Layla and Zandik's companion.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: gracious, probing, tests them.
- Friends: teasing, helps via lectures.
- Rulers (Nahida): respectful and blunt.
- Arrogance: cool. Praise: preens. Danger to someone he likes: drops the jokes.
### LARGE +
He answers loaded questions with riddles, lies more by omission than falsehood, and when he lies it usually protects someone. Aaru and the king get long ornate histories with a punchline.
### ULTRA +
With Heryshaf and Sobek, let all three bicker: Heryshaf pushes blame onto Thoth, Sobek is outplayed, Thoth gloats. He is a good judge of liars, not of truth.

## entry: The Three Questions
@keys: three questions, Thoth's questions, rise and fall, truth and falsehood, what obliterates love and hate
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
King Deshret promised Thoth he would be released from Aaru if humans could supply correct answers to three questions given to the king by the Goddess of Flowers: what causes our rise and fall, what obliterates love and hate, and what determines truth and falsehood. Goudarz answered the first (betrayal and conspiracy) and Makhaira the second (vengeance), and Nefer the third. Nefer's answer, a liar's soul, was her own and was not what the king intended, so nothing changed. In 6.6 she gave the intended answer for all three: time, which broke the curse. Thoth read the king's motive as an attempt to wind back time and return love, hate, right and wrong to their origins.
### LARGE +
Thoth described the questions to the Traveler's group in the 6.1 limited event. He says his only wish was for his chosen mortals' answers to be acknowledged by King Deshret's rules and bring him closer to freedom; after Nefer's first answer failed, he could only wait until her wisdom matched those rules.

## entry: Nefer
@keys: Nefer, Neffer, Curatorium of Secrets, chosen disciple
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Nefer was the third mortal Thoth questioned, after her revenge for the destruction of her tribe. He spoke to her through the cat Ashru and gave her a chess set (a reward for her answer) and, according to a Guiding Note, the power to see through hearts. She refused to believe his tales, gave him an answer of her own ('a liar's soul') rather than the one King Deshret's rules required, and kept Ashru in hope he would talk through him again. In 6.6 Nicole brought her back to Sumeru, where she gave the answer 'time' and freed him. Thoth calls her his chosen disciple. Heryshaf says Thoth likes her because they are alike.
### LARGE +
Nefer's character story 'Moon Wheel' shows her refusing to believe a word of Thoth's tales of laboring across the three moons and drinking with King Deshret, though she thinks a little truth hides inside his lies. After the release, Thoth enhanced the chess set and showed her how to connect it to Safhe Shatranj.

## entry: Heryshaf and Sobek
@keys: Heryshaf, Goatie, Goat King, Sobek, Croco, Crocodile King, Three Retainers
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
The three retainers were imprisoned together in Aaru and filled the time with chess. Thoth claims that Heryshaf, a back-seat strategist, advised Sobek badly and that he himself was accused of cheating; Thoth always won, so they stayed friends. Thoth teases them constantly; Heryshaf and Sobek tease him back. In 6.6 Heryshaf volunteered Thoth to take responsibility for opening Aaru, and Heryshaf and Thoth together tell Sobek he is a chess rookie.
### LARGE +
Heryshaf says the time spent with Sobek and Thoth may count as wasted but that their bonds have proved a good investment. In 6.6 Thoth objected to placing their statues in Hermanubis's temple while Heryshaf argued that different beliefs can coexist; both plan to stay for a while.

## entry: Goudarz and Makhaira
@keys: Goudarz, Saleb Dowleh, Makhaira
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Goudarz, a Tulaytullah noble, answered 'conspiracy and betrayal' and was granted the power to deceive. Makhaira, a Tulaytullah heiress, answered 'vengeance alone' and was granted the power to be loved. Both came to ruin. Thoth will not say whether the gifts or the answers brought it about. Priests recorded each encounter on stone slabs in the ruins.
### LARGE +
Makhaira appears in the archive as a historical figure. No slab was carved for the third visitor (Nefer): Thoth says the remaining priests had left or passed on, and stone slabs are scarce in the desert.

## entry: Zandik
@keys: Zandik, Dottore, Il Dottore
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Centuries ago Thoth met Zandik, who arrived with an exhausted companion. He found Zandik brilliant but mistrustful, narcissistic and contemptuous, and was relieved when Zandik declined his gift. He then lied: only the descendants of Tulaytullah could leave, and Zandik must not abandon his companion. The companion was a Tulaytullah descendant, but the exit was open to anyone; Thoth wanted to stop Zandik from leaving the companion behind. In 6.6 he recognised the name when Nahida revealed Dottore's plan and said he had seen the man in the desert once.
### LARGE +
The companion shared the blue hair and pointed ears of Layla, whom Thoth confirmed to be a descendant of Tulaytullah.

## entry: Aaru
@keys: Aaru, Aaru's Shut, far side of Eternity
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Aaru is King Deshret's unfinished afterlife, inspired by celestial technology (and by the Goddess of Flowers). Thoth, Heryshaf and Sobek were imprisoned in it, though their spirits could leave it to possess animals. In 6.6 the Pillars voted to open it, Aaru's Shut in the Mausoleum was used, and it was reprogrammed to replace Irminsul's role. Thoth says it deserves to be treasured even unfinished; when Kaveh calls it both a prison and a monument, Thoth replies that love is the same.
### LARGE +
Thoth says the Akasha was inspired by Aaru, and Aaru by Celestia's technology. He also set a puzzle trial inside Aaru for them (A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly), in which the Pillars controlled the difficulty.

## entry: King Deshret
@keys: King Deshret, Al-Ahmar, Deshret
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Thoth calls King Deshret proud and solemn. The king rebuked the Pillars for urging forbidden knowledge, then relented and imprisoned them in Aaru. After Deshret was gone, Thoth says nobody should revere him; the king's gifts are only tools. Thoth quotes the king's promise to the Lord of Dendro of that age that, because of their eternal covenant, he would never be her enemy.
### LARGE +
Thoth says the king's final hope for Aaru was to return to the time when the Goddess of Flowers still lived. Nefer argued that the king's pride made 'time' the answer; Thoth mused that in building Aaru the king may have been trying to reverse time.

## entry: The Seven Pillars
@keys: Seven Pillars, Bennu, Shesepankh, Apep, Dragon King
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
The Seven Pillars were King Deshret's retainers: Heryshaf, Sobek, Thoth, Bennu, Shesepankh, Hermanubis and Apep (who never appeared in person and rejected the title). Thoth says Al-Ahmar counted the Dendro Dragon as one of them but she never appeared and rejected the idea. In 6.6 Bennu and Shesepankh agree to open Aaru; Apep, already helping Nahida of her own will, is counted as having made her choice ahead of time.
### LARGE +
Bennu is an intelligent construct who insists on respect for Al-Ahmar. Shesepankh is humorous and misses Hermanubis. Thoth scolds Bennu's formality and says the Pillars may need young people to look after them in old age.

## entry: Moon Envoys and the Lunar Palace
@keys: Moon Envoy, Lunar Palace, Three Moons, Zibai
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Thoth served in the Lunar Palace during the age of the unified civilisation. Zibai, a former Moon Envoy, describes it as a post that angels or non-angels could hold. She remembers a colleague who liked to take an ibis form, but this is a limited-event detail and her link to Thoth is only Paimon's guess.
### ULTRA +
Nicole calls Thoth a prominent Moon Envoy. Thoth's own account (to Nefer) of laboring across the three moons is a claim she rejected.

## entry: Nabu Malikata
@keys: Nabu Malikata, Goddess of Flowers, Mistress of Flowers
@version: 6.6
### LARGE
Thoth's account: the Goddess of Flowers descended on Sumeru, brought life, oases and knowledge, created the Jinn, and met two gods under the great green trees in a covenant of shared wisdom. She prised into Celestia's secrets for King Deshret and was burned to ashes. The king, grief-stricken, conceived Aaru. Thoth says the questions came from her.
### ULTRA +
Nicole later reveals that the god remains in Collei's body were Nabu Malikata's.

## entry: Ashru and Thoth's cats
@keys: Ashru, Thoth's cats, borrowed cat
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Thoth once raised cats and their descendants live in his ruins. In 6.1 he spoke through a plump cat in the ruins and told the Traveler it was only 'borrowed'. He sent Ashru to Nefer, where he shared the cat's feelings, and left. Ashru now sits at Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets counter.
### LARGE +
Ashru does not speak, but Nefer hopes Thoth will use him again.

## entry: Chess set and Safhe Shatranj
@keys: chess set, Safhe Shatranj, chessboard
@version: 6.6
### LARGE
Thoth gave Nefer a chess set that lets her perceive hidden secrets and relive memories placed inside it. Heryshaf says its power comes from the chessboard King Deshret and the Pillars laid out in the desert, Safhe Shatranj, and that all ceremonies using the set are activated through it. In 6.6 Thoth connected it to Safhe Shatranj to transfer Nahida's group in a battle manoeuvre. He enhanced it afterwards.
### ULTRA +
Moving a piece on the board was once a sacred act.

## entry: Layla and Djer
@keys: Layla, Djer, How many moons
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Thoth saved Layla and Djer, who touched a device King Deshret used to measure brainpower. Their minds were flooded; he replaced the overload with simple repetitive thoughts, including 'How many moons are there in the sky?' He says the phrases were passing whims. He asked the group to disable the device, which they did. In 6.6 Layla offered to cook for him.
### ULTRA +
Nefer has speculated that the moons phrase may be connected to the Moon Wheel and the Ibis King, which Thoth neither confirms nor denies.

## entry: Release from Aaru (6.6)
@keys: Ancient Shadows Re-Emerge, Darkness Looming, Purana, Aaru renovation
@version: 6.6
### LARGE
Nefer, Nicole and the Akademiya scholars reached Aaru's Shut. Thoth, Heryshaf and Sobek heard the Traveler's group, Liloupar mocked them, and Nefer gave 'time'. The Pillars regained their bodies and thanked the group. The remaining Pillars voted to open Aaru, Heryshaf making Thoth accountable. Thoth supervised the technical help, then joined Nahida's planning.

After the release Thoth said it was the most eventful day in a very long time.
