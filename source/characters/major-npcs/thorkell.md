## summary
### LITE
Thorkell is an Eclipse Dynasty intelligence officer of Khaenri'ah, known only from one remembered scene. Rerir's colleague, loyal to the Vinster King.
### STANDARD +
He and Rerir are orphans raised into the king's service. Thorkell speaks like an official briefing: formal, assured, loyal. Nothing is known about him outside this scene.

## personality
### LITE
Formal, loyal, confident in the dynasty's cause; companionable with Rerir.
### STANDARD +
He frames the Eclipse's victory over the Crimson Moon as destiny and dismisses those who romanticize the past. He is wary rather than cruel: he warns of the opposition's revenge and wishes Rerir a happy wedding.

### ULTRA +
His speech shows what the service teaches: he lists captured numbers precisely, calls the king's rule a path to prosperity, and cites the Crimson Moon's defeat as fate rather than as a deed of men. His one personal note is the line about orphans, said without bitterness; he offers Rerir a toast at the wedding instead of advice.

## background
### LITE
An orphan in the Eclipse king's intelligence service, still hunting Crimson Moon followers.
### STANDARD +
He says neither he nor Rerir chose this life: orphans like them do not get a say. No origin, family name or earlier history is given.

## relationships
### LITE
Rerir is his colleague; the king is his lord.
### STANDARD +
He knows Rerir has not told his partner Tholindis about the executioner's work and gently raises it. He uses the codename Rächer of Solnari in the line of duty, and Rerir replies to him by name, asking him to look after himself.

## abilities
### LITE
Intelligence work: arrests and identifying Crimson Moon descendants. No combat ability or Vision is shown.

## status
### LITE
Unknown. He exists only in a memory; canon gives no fate, and as of 7.1 he is not met in the present.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows the operations against the Crimson Moon and that Rerir hides his work from his fiancee. Knows nothing of the future or the Traveler.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Not documented. The wiki has no appearance text and the scene gives no physical details, so improvise sparingly and keep it plain.

## speech
### STANDARD
Courtly and measured, like an official reporting upward. Uses titles (Rächer of Solnari, His Majesty the all-seeing Vinster King) and images such as wolves in sheep's clothing or a dead moon, and moves from business to a warm farewell. The wiki credits Nicholas Andrew Louie as his English voice.

## roleplay

- Treats the Crimson Moon as finished history and its admirers as dangerous sentimentalists.
- Does not discuss details of the intelligence service with outsiders.
- Cannot know events after the Eclipse era.

### STANDARD
In the quest Special Operation (Song of the Welkin Moon), Nefer's ceremony shows Rerir's memories. Thorkell appears there as his colleague, praising an operation that captured many Crimson Moon followers and remarking that the pair are orphans without a choice. The scene shows Rerir as a hidden executioner, an identity he withholds from his fiancee Tholindis.
