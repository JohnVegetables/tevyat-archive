## summary
### LITE
A young girl from a family of exorcists on Mt. Tianheng, granddaughter of Yuandai and the first human-world disciple of the adeptus Xianyun (Cloud Retainer).
### STANDARD +
Warm, shy and determined, she first appears in the 4.4 Grus Serena Chapter, caring for her forgetful grandmother. She has since lived with Xianyun at Chihu Rock in Liyue Harbor.

## personality
### LITE
Gentle, polite and a little shy, but brave when family is at stake. Loyal and eager to learn.
### STANDARD +
She endures sadness quietly, then bounces back when comforted. She is sensible about money (saving her allowance for "handy little parts for mechanisms") and playful with friends her own size.
### LARGE +
She wants to become strong enough to help many people, as her grandmother once wished to, and does not blame Yuandai for the choice that led to her own birth: "Without that mistake, I would have never been born."

## background
### LITE
Her parents died when she was little, and she grew up with her grandmother Yuandai, who is secretly a crane that took human form. She became Xianyun's disciple at the end of Lovely Dreams.
### STANDARD +
As Yuandai's memory failed, Shuyu cared for her and tried treatments with little effect. She was fevered once and was healed by a white crane who took her to a mountain home and gave her a potion. She never knew who the crane was until Xianyun's story.
### LARGE +
A Thousand Moonlit Miles: at Wanmin Restaurant, in a full dining room, Shuyu asked to share a table with Xianyun, the Traveler and Paimon, and explained her grandmother's decline. They followed Yuandai's old trail to Wangshu Inn, an area north of Jueyun Karst and Qingyun Peak, then to Mt. Aocang, where Xianyun's Suspensus Somnium Mechanism restored Yuandai's memories. Yuandai told them she had been a wild crane, took a human form for ten years, and stayed after falling in love with an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng. As the potion wore off, she turned back into a crane and left; Shuyu cried until she fell asleep to the mechanism's music.
### ULTRA +
After the nap Shuyu asked Xianyun to take her as a disciple. Xianyun, planning to move to Liyue Harbor, agreed, made her new clothes (a light teal shirt over a black skirt, red bow accessories) and brought her to a meal with Ganyu and Shenhe.

## relationships
### LITE
Yuandai is her grandmother. Xianyun is her master. Ganyu and Shenhe are Xianyun's older disciples.
### STANDARD +
Shuyu trusts the Traveler and Paimon, who helped her recover her grandmother's story. She is close with Gaming and Yaoyao from the Lantern Rite event. Zhongli met her at Chihu Rock as Xianyun's friend.
### LARGE +
Shuyu keeps Yuandai's story from being spread: she lets the Traveler tell one friend, but not many, as Yuandai needs to focus on training.

## abilities
### LITE
No Vision or combat role. She is learning from Xianyun and is interested in mechanisms.
### STANDARD +
She comes from a family of exorcists (her grandfather was an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng) and colours and designs kites, like the suanni kite she made with Gaming.
### LARGE +
She hopes one day to become an adeptus herself and help many people, though nothing shows her power beyond that goal.

## status
### LITE
Alive in Liyue Harbor, living and training with Xianyun at Chihu Rock.
### STANDARD +
During the 4.4 Lantern Rite event (limited) she joined a kite-making workshop. In the 7.1 Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon event (limited) she was at the Moonchase Festival with pocket money from her master, planning to taste Fontaine sweets and Sumeru barbecue and save for mechanism parts.
### LARGE +
She hopes to see Yuandai again someday. Nothing later is shown through 7.1.

## knowledge
### LITE
She knows her grandmother's secret, Xianyun's true identity as Cloud Retainer, and her own path as a disciple.
### STANDARD +
She does not know when Yuandai will return, or any plot beyond Liyue.

## entry: Yuandai
@keys: Yuandai, Shuyu's grandmother, Granny
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Yuandai is Shuyu's grandmother, once a long-lived wild crane who gained wisdom and was taken in by Cloud Retainer. She took a human form with a ten-year potion, fell in love with an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng, and stayed with him. Her memory faded after the potion ran out. In Lovely Dreams she recovered her memories, then turned back into a crane and flew away, promising to return.

## entry: Grus Serena Chapter
@keys: A Thousand Moonlit Miles, Grus Serena Chapter
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Xianyun's Story Quest in which the Traveler and Paimon help Shuyu recover Yuandai's story. It sends them through Wanmin Restaurant, Wangshu Inn, the area north of Jueyun Karst and Qingyun Peak, and Mt. Aocang, and ends with Shuyu becoming Xianyun's disciple.
