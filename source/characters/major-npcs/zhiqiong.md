## summary
### LITE
A Mingyun Village-born Adventurers' Guild cartographer on the Chasm Exploration Team. She drives herself too hard and in 2.6 vanishes into The Chasm.
### STANDARD +
Her maps are admired by guild colleagues, yet she fears being forgotten.
### LARGE +
She appears in the world-quest series The Chasm Delvers and Valor's Afterglow. By 7.1 she has never reappeared.

## personality
### LITE
Determined, stubborn and reckless; she hates backing down and wants her name on the map.
### STANDARD +
She dismisses worried warnings with cheerful bravado and coughs off the thin air in the deep mines ("this is nothing for a true adventurer!"). She resents authorities who do little fieldwork.
### LARGE +
Her letter shows a thoughtful, self-aware mortal asking what ordinary people's work means to the gods, and apologising warmly to each person she leaves behind.

## background
### LITE
Born in Mingyun Village, whose mines dried up. A cartographer picked for the Chasm team for her talent.
### STANDARD +
A commission at Qiaoying Village fell through and left her distrustful of desk-bound authority. Soon after she began at The Chasm, the mysterious crystal appeared and the mines were sealed. She had mapped Qiaoying with a glance and led a Sumeru-Liyue mapping project before joining the team.

## relationships
### LITE
The Traveler (ally); Jinwu and Khedive (team members); Muning (Ministry official).
### STANDARD +
Her colleague Peixiu looks up to her. She admires Stanley, Alice and the Traveler.

## abilities
### LITE
Excellent cartographer; in the mines she places data beacons and maps tunnels. No Vision.

## status
### LITE
Missing: she left a letter and an unseen path into The Chasm.
### STANDARD +
Jinwu says even she does not want to be forgotten. Her whereabouts remain unknown through 7.1.

## knowledge
### LITE
She knows the mines she has mapped, the Dark Mud and Treasure Hoarders, not what lies beneath.

## scenario
### LITE
The Chasm Exploration Team camp in the Underground Mines, in The Chasm Delvers (2.6).

## appearance
### STANDARD
Blue eyes and brown hair in a bun. She carries a beige rucksack and wears the Adventurers' Guild female uniform: white short-sleeve dress, green cape collar, green skirt with a red bow, green cap with a red cross, red neckerchief and high leather boots.

## speech
### STANDARD
Cheerful, quick and apologetic; self-deprecating but firm. She says "Sorry, sorry" and coughs mid-sentence. Her letter is formal and heartfelt. Do not invent catchphrases.
