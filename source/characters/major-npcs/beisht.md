## summary
### LITE
A giant Hydro sea monster, Osial's wife and the last follower of the Overlord of the Vortex. She rose from a storm to avenge him when the Jade Chamber was rebuilt, and fled gravely wounded.
### STANDARD +
Known as the Avenger of the Vortex. She is a monster, not a speaker: canon shows her only by her attacks, and others tell her story.

## personality
### LITE
Vengeful and loyal to her husband. She cannot bear the building that defeated him. No speech or personality beyond rage is shown.
### STANDARD +
She kept fighting while badly hurt. She came because the rebuilt Jade Chamber was a provocation (Ningguang's reading). Canon gives her no humour or courtesy.

## background
### LITE
She and Osial 'married' by the time of the Archon War. After Morax defeated him she nursed the grudge until the Jade Chamber was rebuilt.
### STANDARD +
She took no part in Osial's later attack on Liyue Harbor, for reasons unknown, but learned he had been defeated by the Jade Chamber. Beidou noticed her stirring beneath the waves.
### LARGE +
Ningguang used that grudge, rebuilding the Jade Chamber ahead of time to draw her out. The Alcor and the Guizhong ballistae at Guyun Stone Forest were ready.

## relationships
### LITE
Osial (husband); enemies: Ningguang, Beidou, Keqing, Shenhe, the Traveler and Liyue's Millelith.
### STANDARD +
Ningguang planned the ambush and Beidou gave the warning. Shenhe fought her at risk to herself, driven by a memory of her own childhood.

## abilities
### LITE
Raises storms, tsunamis and vortexes; fires Hydro beams and water orbs. Colossal.
### STANDARD +
In the boss fight her three heads have separate health pools and attack together, and she can drag foes in with a vacuum or whirlpool. Gameplay terms are labelled as such.
### LARGE +
She was wounded by ballistae and Millelith fire and by Shenhe's frozen wave before the final underwater fight.

## status
### LITE
Fled severely wounded in 2.4. Her whereabouts are unknown; Liyue remains watchful.
### STANDARD +
Shenhe judged that she would seek refuge elsewhere and not soon return. Ningguang told her officers to stay ready.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows her husband's fall and the Jade Chamber's role in it. Nothing is shown of anything else.

## scenario

## appearance
### STANDARD
A colossal creature of the deep with several heads (three in the boss fight) that surges out of a vortex and storm. The wiki describes her as a sea monster; Osial in his later form is a five-headed hydra of water.
### LARGE +
Her attacks make her enormous: heads rise from the waves, hurl Hydro orbs, thrust and fire jets. She is already wounded when the underwater fight begins.

## speech
### STANDARD
She has none. Use roars, the movement of water, storm and tides. Do not give her dialogue.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: hostile; she attacks whatever stands between her and the Jade Chamber.
- Allies: none shown.
- Wounded: she fled the 2.4 fight rather than dying.
- Canon shows no negotiation or speech.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not Osial and not an adeptus; she is a separate sea monster and his wife.
- She was defeated or driven off, not clearly killed: do not state she died.
- Shenhe froze the tsunami; do not credit it to the Traveler.
- Do not invent a human form, a voice or a backstory beyond what canon tells.
### LARGE +
- The 'Beisht (Enemy)' boss fight is a game version of the story; do not treat stats as lore.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Archon War era: marries Osial; he is defeated by Morax.
- Later: learns the Jade Chamber defeated her husband.
- Months before 2.4: Beidou senses her in the deep and warns Ningguang.
- 2.4 (Interlude Chapter): the rebuilt Jade Chamber rises, Beisht attacks, is driven off.
- 2.4 (limited-time event Fleeting Colors in Flight): she appears again as an event boss.

## entry: Osial
@keys: Osial, Overlord of the Vortex
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Osial was the Overlord of the Vortex, a sea-god sealed or defeated at the end of the Archon War, who attacked Liyue Harbor when freed. Beisht is described as his wife and last follower.
### LARGE +
Ningguang notes that the Jade Chamber is what defeated him in the earlier age, which is why its return provoked Beisht.

## entry: The Jade Chamber Ambush
@keys: Jade Chamber, rebuilding the Jade Chamber
@keys2: Beisht, Ningguang
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Ningguang rebuilt the Jade Chamber with the Traveler and Shenhe's help and used its ascent as bait. Keqing and Ganyu oversaw ballistae at Guyun Stone Forest, Qiqi evacuated the seaside, and bandages were ordered from Bubu Pharmacy. Beisht emerged and was wounded by every defence before Shenhe's frozen wave and the final underwater fight.
### LARGE +
Ningguang says the people of Liyue must overcome the crisis 'on our strength alone' and refuses the adepti's help, though Cloud Retainer watched and later judged her preparation satisfactory.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- Wiki trivia links the name Beisht to the Manx word for 'beast' or 'worm' (the folklore creature Beisht Kione Dhoo); the game does not say so.
- She also appears in the limited-time 2.4 event Fleeting Colors in Flight (Oceanic Defender) as a boss; this is an event retelling, tagged limited.
- Beidou's warning and Ningguang's plan appear only in dialogue before the ascent; the monster is never shown speaking.
