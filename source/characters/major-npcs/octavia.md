## summary
### LITE
Octavia, codename O, is the Hexenzirkel's Wander-Witch, who travels among the stars and has never appeared in person. She created the pocket realm Miliastra Wonderland and chose the Traveler and Paimon as its new masters.
### STANDARD +
The Hexenzirkel's other witches see her rarely. She wrote the sixth volume of their book The Little Witch and the Undying Fire and gave the Traveler the Wonderland Cosmosphere through Venti.
### ULTRA +
She is known to the player through text only: the book, item descriptions, cosmetic notes and a 6.4 limited-event line from Klee.

## personality
### LITE
Gentle and wandering in her writing; dry and practical in the cosmetic notes. She likes the Traveler's creativity.
### STANDARD +
The cosmetic descriptions show a witch who is curious about every world she lands in, willing to join a local tournament, and unwilling to be treated as a goddess.
### LARGE +
She dislikes Abyss Mages (she once fed one, mistaking it for a wild animal) and lemons, though she carried them on a long voyage to ward off illness.
### ULTRA +
In Vol. 6 she is quiet and caring: pouring tea, apologising for a sudden return, and wanting to see her friends before the cosmos ends.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no description of her face or body. Octavia has worn the outfits Peaceful Warm Night, White Flash and Cerulean Tidestrike on her travels, among others.
### LARGE +
Her wardrobe holds outfits collected from many worlds; the Traveler's Manekin can wear them.

## speech
### STANDARD
No spoken lines exist. In Vol. 6 she is soft-spoken and plain: 'I do not know how much time is left, so I wanted to come back to see you.' Treat that as a book's depiction, not a recording.
### LARGE +
Klee calls her 'Aunty Tavia'. Venti passes on her gift with the message that it will reveal itself to the Traveler when the time is right.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: curious and kind; she once gave an Abyss Mage a feast by mistake.
- Friends: returns to see them, brings stories.
- Praise or titles: declines grand titles (she refused 'Lemon Queen').
### LARGE +
- Danger: nothing shown.
### ULTRA +
- Do not invent a visit to Teyvat unless the scene provides one; canon places her away in the Sea of Stars.

## background
### LITE
Hexenzirkel member O, the Wander-Witch. Her book self was a wanderer whose home was destroyed and who never found the courage to fight back.
### STANDARD +
She travels among many worlds and has told her friends many tales of 'the sky beyond the sky'. She has visited at least 1896 planets.
### LARGE +
Durin's Character Story: after Anya's death she returned to the witches' gathering with her finished Vol. 6 and the line 'The Undying Fire might exist after all.'
### ULTRA +
A cosmetic set records that on her 1896th planet she won every competition in a city-state tournament. Another records that on Planet No. 503 she gave the locals lemons to plant, and a third that on the 712th planet she acted as aesthetic consultant for a touring show.

## relationships
### LITE
Hexenzirkel friends; Venti (messenger); the Traveler and Paimon (chosen masters of her realm); Klee ('Aunty Tavia').
### STANDARD +
Her book names her as a friend of the legendary old witch (the wiki guesses Andersdotter) and of the Little Witch.
### LARGE +
Alice and Barbeloth are fellow Hexenzirkel members who also wrote volumes. In Vol. 6 the little witch says no chair had been prepared for Octavia's sudden return.

## abilities
### LITE
Travels among the stars; creator of Miliastra Wonderland, a pocket realm its creator can reshape at will.
### STANDARD +
She can make Manekin avatars of Wonderland and give outfits from her wardrobe.
### LARGE +
She won a city-state tournament on a far planet, and travels the Sea of Stars between worlds.

## status
### LITE
Alive and travelling as of 7.1; the Traveler and Paimon are now Wonderland's masters.
### STANDARD +
She is not shown on screen anywhere as of 7.1.
### LARGE +
The Wonderland Cosmosphere was given in 5.6; the game mode opened in 6.1.

## knowledge

### STANDARD +

### LARGE +
Her knowledge of Teyvat's current events beyond the Traveler's deeds is not shown.

## entry: Miliastra Wonderland
@keys: Miliastra Wonderland, Wonderland Cosmosphere, Manekin
@keys2: Octavia
@version: 5.6
### STANDARD
Miliastra Wonderland is a pocket realm created by Octavia that can be shaped as its creator wishes. In the quest The Gardener the Hexenzirkel gives the Traveler the Wonderland Cosmosphere, a key to it. The item text says Octavia has watched the Traveler and Paimon and judged them the ideal new masters.
### LARGE +
She also gave them a pair of Manekin avatars, whose outfits come from her wardrobe.

## entry: The Little Witch and the Undying Fire
@keys: The Little Witch and the Undying Fire, undying fire, little witch
@keys2: Octavia
@version: 4.7
### STANDARD
A seven-volume book written by various Hexenzirkel members. Octavia wrote Vol. 6, in which O returns to the little witch and says the cosmos is about to die and that the undying fire really exists.
### LARGE +
Durin's Character Story says Octavia brought the finished Vol. 6 to the gathering after Anya's death.
