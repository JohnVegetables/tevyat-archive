## summary
### LITE
Tugarin is Alyosha's black-and-white husky, once a starving wild dog of the tundra. He tracks, scouts and attacks on command.
### STANDARD +
Alyosha adopted him after the dog helped finish a man-eating bear. He is stubborn, does not like being petted, and responds to Alyosha's whistles and signals.

## personality
### LITE
Loyal, keen and prickly; he does not like being petted.
### STANDARD +
He is a skilled tracker and rabbit-catcher, but he misreads some orders: he once could not count to ten when scouting. Later Alyosha reports he now can.
### LARGE +
Because he follows Alyosha's secret code of whistle and plate signals, he occasionally comes up with commands his owner did not teach him on purpose, to Alyosha's surprise.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A husky with black and white fur and blue eyes. He wears a red scarf with white outlines at the left side of his fur collar.

## background
### LITE
A starving wild dog, he got two pieces of sausage from Alyosha, who was hunting a man-eating bear. He later helped finish the bear, and Alyosha adopted him.
### STANDARD +
When Alyosha's men began turning on innocents because of Project Stuzha's supply strains, Alyosha left Tugarin with his parents while he dealt with them. After Alatyr was abandoned, Tugarin followed him to Sretomorozsk. Later Alyosha joined the Rokot, and they often visited the Korolevskiy Theater, where Tugarin got to know Vodyanitsa.
### LARGE +
Tugarin was already trained to some degree; he continues to learn from Alyosha and to be tested with new challenges.

## relationships
### LITE
Alyosha is his owner. Vodyanitsa is a familiar scent from the theater. The Traveler worked with him in 7.0.
### STANDARD +
Alyosha treats him with great trust, praising him ("Good job, boy.") and feeding him at the end of a job.

## abilities
### LITE
Tracking by scent, scouting, distracting, tackling on command. Alyosha's Burst, Hunter's Advance, summons him.
### STANDARD +
He tracked the stolen Kresnik Kristall's trail in Gunfire in the Silent Lands, drew Fatui attention so Alyosha and the Traveler could slip past, and tackled a fae while Alyosha disarmed a detonation switch in Hunter on the Snowfields.

## status
### LITE
Alive and by Alyosha's side in Sretomorozsk and Snezhnograd as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Alyosha also breeds dogs and offers puppies for adoption, though Tugarin remains his partner.

## knowledge
### LITE
He understands commands and scents, not politics.
