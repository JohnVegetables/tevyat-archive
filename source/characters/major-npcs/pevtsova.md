## summary
### LITE
Pevtsova is a resident of Svetloledovka, the town beside the Prime Ice cavern, and one of the people the Traveler and Odette rescue from the cave. She remains deeply grateful.
### STANDARD +
In 7.1 she meets the Traveler and Odette again in Snezhnograd while buying the last of her town's rebuilding supplies. She says Svetloledovka wants to repay the help it has received, and Vesna turns that into a trip to collect Seals of Life there.
### LARGE +
She is the voice of ordinary Snezhnayans in the Svetloledovka scenes: thankful for the Veche's and army's help, worried for the injured, eager to repay.
### ULTRA +
Her two scenes bracket the recovery of Svetloledovka, from the cave-in and rescue in 7.0 to the rebuilding that is almost complete in 7.1.

## personality
### LITE
Grateful, community-minded and earnest. She remembers the people who saved her and wants to give something back.
### STANDARD +
She is open and talkative with people she trusts, and speaks of the town's needs more than her own.
### LARGE +
She is dependable and practical: she goes to Snezhnograd to buy supplies for the town and does not forget the Traveler.
### ULTRA +
She regrets that the injured are recovering slowly, and speaks of it with sadness rather than anger.

## background
### LITE
A Svetloledovka resident who was among the five people trapped in the Prime Ice cavern when a monster attacked.
### STANDARD +
Sandrone, hearing that disaster has struck in Svetloledovka, goes there and the Traveler goes into the cave with Odette. Pevtsova, Gornova and Ryabushinsky are among the survivors, and the monster flees.
### LARGE +
After the rescue the town is rebuilt with help from the Veche and the military. Sparks Converging Upon the Tundra shows that process nearly done.

## relationships
### LITE
Rescued by the Traveler and Odette; fellow Svetloledovka residents include Gornova and Ryabushinsky.
### STANDARD +
Odette keeps a friendly tie with the town; Gornova tells her to come back whenever she likes.
### LARGE +
At Svetloledovka station after the Seals of Life trip she asks the visitors to stay a few more days; Danica declines for the group.

## entry: Rescue in the Prime Ice cavern
@keys: Prime Ice cavern, Svetloledovka cave-in, Svetloledovka rescue
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Five people were trapped in the Prime Ice cavern near Svetloledovka when one of the giant excavators broke down and a monster attacked. The Traveler and Odette found them, fought the creature, and escorted them out. The Traveler also found a mysterious sword at the cave's star, the Exaiphanes Blade.
### LARGE +
Pevtsova shouted a warning as the monster approached, then thanked the rescuers after it fled. Odette said the beast was small but ferocious and sees people as food.

## entry: Seals of Life drive in Svetloledovka
@keys: Svetloledovka Seals of Life, Seals of Life drive
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Pevtsova tells Vesna that Svetloledovka wants to contribute. Vesna proposes a trip there to collect Seals of Life, and the group travels by train. Pevtsova asks whether they can stay longer; Danica declines.
