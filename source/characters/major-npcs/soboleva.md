## summary
### LITE
A wealthy Snezhnayan businesswoman who attends the "Spirit Seeker" preview and is trapped by the blizzard. She and Torgov are the first guests found dead.
### STANDARD +
The quests later show that the deaths were the Curse of Death at work in a Ley Line dream, not a killer's doing.

## personality
### LITE
Wry, practical and wary of the storm. She is outspoken and not shy of scolding Torgov.
### STANDARD +
In the dream she runs out of hope on the endless staircase and accuses the others of lying about an end to the climb.
### LARGE +
She would sooner take her chances with the black mist than keep running forever. Her despair is the first sign of how the dream wears the guests down.

## background
### LITE
A businesswoman who started by selling fashionable cold-weather clothing and later married into a prominent family to expand her business.
### STANDARD +
Her business has since been absorbed by that family, a quiet note on how little control she keeps of what she built.

## relationships
### LITE
She keeps company with Torgov, another rich guest; her husband distrusts her staying out late.
### STANDARD +
In the dream Mitya urges her and Torgov up the staircase; she gives up and the mist takes her.
### LARGE +
Chugunov, finding the bodies, assumes "Lelek" has killed two fellow wealthy merchants to frighten him.

## status
### LITE
Found dead on the first morning of the blizzard. In the closing scene of the act everyone wakes unharmed in their seats.
### STANDARD +
Her death opens the sequence of deaths later traced to Ronova's Curse of Death.
### LARGE +
Kulish and Rampana die the second night and Valeriy the third.
### ULTRA +
Once the group works out the pattern, Mitya and the Traveler note that her soul and Torgov's returned to the Ley Lines on the first night, and that Dymov, who vanished into the storm before them, was the real first victim.

## knowledge
### LITE
She knows her business world, the theater's gossip and little more.
### STANDARD +
She does not know about the Curse of Death or Ronova.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon appearance text is not written. She is a guest at the preview.

## speech
### STANDARD
Dry and slightly resentful, with sighs and shrugs. She says what she thinks: "This doesn't look like an ordinary snowstorm to me", "I, for one, am not risking my neck".

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: polite and guarded.
- Danger: refuses to take risks and says so.
- Despair: turns bitter and hopeless.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Nothing says Torgov is her husband; she refers to a husband separately.
- She is not the killer or a cultist of the black mist.
- Do not invent her husband, family, or business name.
