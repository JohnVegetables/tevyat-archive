## summary
### LITE
Chief of the Scions of the Canopy in Natlan, an easygoing risk-taker who warns others that adventure is a double-edged sword.
### STANDARD +
He has the most adventuring experience in his tribe and dislikes being called Uncle. He supported the effort to rescue Kachina and oversaw tribe responses to Kongamato and the Abyss invasion.
### ULTRA +
He appears in the Natlan Archon Quest (5.0), the Yupanqui's Turnfire story quest and a world quest.

## personality
### LITE
Genial, joking and fond of extreme sports, but quietly cautious. He laughs at himself ("Oh man, now isn't this complicated?") and is sensitive about being called old.
### STANDARD +
He tried to talk the Traveler's group out of a dangerous mission, then helped when they were resolved. He values every outcome of a leap: some return, some land safely, some fly farther.
### LARGE +
He takes pity on stranded creatures: after finding a Yumkasaur guarding a chest, he arranged daily feeding instead of punishing anyone.

## background
### LITE
Chief of the Scions of the Canopy, whose village hangs from the cliffs of Coatepec Mountain. His earlier life is not documented.
### STANDARD +
The Scions are explorers and messengers; he organised them to bring real-time intelligence across Natlan during the Abyss war and evacuated when the village was overwhelmed.

## relationships
### LITE
The Traveler, Chasca and Mualani sought his help. Citlali's stone was in his keeping. Kinich and Trinidad are tribe members.
### STANDARD +
Vichama was his recommendation to find Kachina's Ancient Name. Chuychu visited him afterwards.

## abilities
### LITE
Experienced adventurer and organiser. No Vision or fighting ability is documented.
### STANDARD +
He runs messenger networks and the Turnfire Ritual that kept Kongamato in check.

## status
### LITE
Alive, chief of the Scions of the Canopy, back in his village after Mavuika stopped the Abyss invasion.
### STANDARD +
The annual Kongamato purification is no longer needed, so the ritual he oversaw has ended. He sends people to feed the Yumkasaur at Teticpac Peak daily.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows his tribe, the borrowed stone, and the Traveler's trip to the Night Kingdom.
### STANDARD +
He has heard of the Traveler's phlogiston use. He knows nothing of events beyond Natlan.

## entry: Spiritspeaker Stone
@keys: Spiritspeaker Stone, Vichama
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Wayna had borrowed Citlali's stone. He gave it to the Traveler's group, advising them to ask Vichama, who found Kachina's Ancient Name. Chasca destroyed the stone afterwards; he expected Citlali to scold him but valued the life saved.

## entry: The Last Turnfire Night
@keys: Turnfire Ritual, Kongamato
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Wayna oversaw the ritual keeping Kongamato in check. Kinich planned to use Enjou's Beastly Rift instead; Wayna agreed, but Trinidad smashed the device. A shell later appeared around Kongamato, ending the need for annual rituals.
