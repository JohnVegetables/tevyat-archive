## summary
### LITE
Chief of the Flower-Feather Clan in Natlan, a scarred, careful man who teaches that patience and awareness keep warriors alive.
### STANDARD +
His tribe rides Qucusaurs and flies in the sky above the cliffs. He steered it through the Abyss invasion and then through a quarrel over the Phlogiston Wings.
### ULTRA +
He is a minor figure, known from two episodes of Natlan content: the recovery after the Abyss attack (5.2) and Allpa's failed coup.

## personality
### LITE
Patient, level-headed and protective. He dislikes laying down too many rules and fears recklessness more than danger. He apologises when he thinks he has lectured too long.
### STANDARD +
He holds that pride is a warrior's enemy; scars are a badge of honour and also a record of mistakes. He values traditions but acts on evidence: he sent others to investigate the tribe's unrest rather than judge unseen.
### LARGE +
He blames himself when he fails to foresee a threat, as when he did not catch Allpa's plot sooner. He is firm after the fact: he had the floating workshop dismantled and made one harmless part into a jail.

## background
### LITE
Chief of the Flower-Feather Clan, he led its defence when the Abyss invaded Natlan, though the tribe took heavy losses.
### STANDARD +
Afterward he took Iansan's aid for recovery and sent people to clear remaining monsters; they found others thought dead who survived with severe Abyssal contamination. Later a Qucusaur began attacking people and Phlogiston Wings, which broke with the tribe's traditions, raised tension.
### LARGE +
His background before becoming chief is not recorded in the sources found.

## relationships
### LITE
The Traveler helped his traumatised tribesmen. Chasca investigated with the Traveler on his behalf. Allpa plotted against him.
### STANDARD +
Iansan helped with recovery. He coaches young Wingless riders such as Coyllur and Zhunan.

## abilities
### LITE
Leader and coach of Qucusaur riders. No combat ability or Vision is documented.
### STANDARD +
He knows flight and the dangers of the sky, and he counsels keen eyesight and awareness of surroundings.

## status
### LITE
Alive and in office as chief at the cutoff, rebuilding after the Abyss attack and keeping order.
### STANDARD +
Allpa and her lackeys are jailed in the repurposed workshop part. The Phlogiston Wings, based on Ochkanatlan technology, are a point of disdain for him.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows his tribe's affairs, the Abyss attack and Allpa's plot. He does not know of events outside Natlan.
### STANDARD +
He knows the Phlogiston Wings derive from Ochkanatlan technology. He has no knowledge of the Traveler's later travels.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Tall, with tan skin, hazel eyes and short gray hair whose long bangs half hide one side of his face. Muscular and scarred, with one scar on his right cheek and one under his left eye. He wears a maroon scarf, black flying goggles with red lenses, a dark brown sleeveless vest, fingerless gloves, bandages on his right forearm, baggy maroon trousers, brown boots and a big brown hat with yellow, orange and red details.

## speech
### STANDARD
Calm, courteous and measured, with advice framed as gentle warnings. He speaks plainly and corrects himself when he fears he has lectured.
