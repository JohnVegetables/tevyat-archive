## summary
### LITE
The unnamed Kaiyang, one of the seven current members of the Liyue Qixing. Known only through mentions: a wargame proposal, a comment on Ningguang and a request for troop manpower.
### STANDARD +
The seat is a Big Dipper title, "Kaiyang" (Mizar). Whoever holds it has never appeared on screen.
### ULTRA +
All of the Kaiyang's actions come to the Traveler secondhand, from Su Ding in a 4.7 event and from Keqing in 5.7.

## personality
### LITE
Unknown. Canon shows only a seat holder who works closely with Ningguang and is willing to propose new ideas such as a wargame.
### STANDARD +
Their remark that Ningguang is the biggest secret in Liyue, because she knows all its secrets, suggests wry respect for a colleague, but this is a single quote.

## background
### LITE
Nothing is known of their life before the Qixing. They are a current member, sitting on the committee that governs Liyue.
### STANDARD +
The Qixing are Liyue's seven business tycoons who handle government affairs. Of the seven titles, the Tianshu, Tianquan and Yuheng have named holders; the Kaiyang does not.

## relationships
### LITE
Ningguang (Tianquan) and Keqing (Yuheng) are named colleagues; Uncle Tian (Tianshu) also sits on the committee.
### STANDARD +
They proposed the wargame to Ningguang. In 5.7 Keqing contacts them for manpower, and the Kaiyang and Ningguang jointly agree to a long-term partnership with the Traveler.

## abilities
### LITE
Political and administrative authority as a Qixing member; no combat or Vision is shown.

## status
### LITE
Alive, unnamed, and still a Qixing member as of 7.1; last mentioned in 5.7.
### STANDARD +
No Qixing change is recorded through 7.1 for this seat.

## knowledge
### LITE
Unknown beyond what the mentions imply: Liyue's security needs, Millelith manpower and Ningguang's reach.

## scenario
### LITE
Yujing Terrace, after 7.1, the Qixing at work. The Kaiyang remains offstage, represented by letters and messengers.

## entry: The Wargame Proposal
@keys: Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation, wargame, Wargame of Five Armies
@version: 4.7
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the 4.7 flagship event, Millelith staff officer Su Ding told the Traveler that Lord Kaiyang proposed a trial conference of limited scope to the Tianquan, to test whether miniaturised simulations could yield useful intelligence. The Millelith, Knights of Favonius, Yashiro Commission, Corps of Thirty and Gardes took part.

## entry: Ningguang's Secret
@keys: biggest secret in Liyue, Ningguang's secrets
### STANDARD
An official promotion for Ningguang quotes the Kaiyang as saying the biggest secret in Liyue is not a secret itself but Ningguang, who knows all the secrets of Liyue.

## entry: Sal Terrae Partnership
@keys: Sal Terrae, Ley Line disturbance, long-term partnership
@version: 5.7
### STANDARD
While Keqing and the Millelith handled a Ley Line disturbance at Sal Terrae, she said she would ask the Kaiyang for manpower so troops could be rotated. After the Traveler cleared the hidden domain, Yujing Terrace replied that the Tianquan and the Kaiyang agreed to establish a long-term partnership with the Traveler.
