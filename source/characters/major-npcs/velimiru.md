## summary
### LITE
An artificial Fatuus made by Harbinger Sandrone, he took part in a three-man Fatui mission to Natlan that was destroyed by the machine it tried to control.
### STANDARD +
He appears only in the 5.8 Archon Quest act The Journey Home (Smoldering and Searing). He is dead by its end.

## personality
### LITE
Smug, patient, calculating. He issues polite ultimatums, craves his creator's approval and shrugs off setbacks with dry self-mockery.
### STANDARD +
He lets his louder teammates bluster and states the terms himself, treating people and machines as pieces.
### LARGE +
He is cautious when warned: after Sandrone's caution about the Traveler he calls off an attack as "not worth the risk". But when a mission report is at stake he gambles, and will destroy a prize rather than lose it.

## background
### LITE
Created by Sandrone, he joined Fajeyi and Gulebu in a Fatui special-ops group gathering knowledge of ancient civilisations. The team went to Natlan on its own initiative to take the dragons' knowledge, hoping to earn her recommendation.
### STANDARD +
Sandrone warned them of the danger and of the Traveler, but did not forbid it. The team infiltrated the Temple of Cascading Colors (Ch'upikaq Tajin) and used the merchant Zhuoxin to bring Ineffa from Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
They staged automaton attacks near the Flower-Feather Clan to draw Ineffa out, offered to write off Zhuoxin's Northland Bank debt, and handed him a replica control device built with Sandrone's technology to capture the Eleventh Lord. When he refused, they cut him loose and sprang the trap anyway.

## relationships
### LITE
Created by Sandrone, whom he calls Marionette. Works with Fajeyi and Gulebu. Treats Zhuoxin as a pawn and the Traveler as the dangerous variable.
### STANDARD +
Fajeyi and Gulebu are blunt where he is calm; who leads the group is left unclear. Both die in the temple core. Ineffa is the prize.
### LARGE +
His last words call him "just one of her many projects", implying Sandrone has others like him.

## abilities
### LITE
Planning and control rather than fighting. A replica device built on Marionette's Secret Source technology lets him command automata and, at the end, reawaken the Eleventh Lord.
### STANDARD +
Being artificial, he survives wounds that kill his comrades instantly. The Traveler notices he is not flesh and blood.
### LARGE +
His team commandeered at least half of the ruins' systems and used their power to hold a cage around Ineffa and the Traveler. They monitored the party from the moment it entered.

## status
### LITE
Dead: he reawakened the Eleventh Lord with his last strength and the Traveler struck him down.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in canon as of 7.1 shows him returning.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows the Natlan operation and Sandrone's warning about the Traveler. Nothing after his death.
### STANDARD +
He believes a former sovereign ruler holds the key to the temple's core, and that Ineffa can be used to reach it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
An ordinary-looking man in a Fatui uniform, blue eyes, hair falling over his right eye.
### LARGE +
Fajeyi is dressed like a Cryogunner Legionnaire without the freezer pack or gun. Gulebu's gear is not described.

## speech
### STANDARD
Measured, with a low "Hehehe" when pleased. He names things by title (the Eleventh Lord, the sovereign ruler, the variable) and calls Sandrone Marionette.
### LARGE +
He turns refusals into polite ultimatums ("I think it's time... we parted ways"). Under strain his sentences shorten and trail off, but the dry wit stays.

## roleplay

### LARGE +

## entry: Fajeyi and Gulebu

### STANDARD
Fajeyi and Gulebu are the other two members of Velimiru's team. Fajeyi (called "boss" by Gulebu) is mocking and clinical; Gulebu is loud. Both are crushed by Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk in the temple's core.
### ULTRA +
Fajeyi's gear resembles a Cryogunner Legionnaire's. Gulebu taunts Zhuoxin about his hopes of being "the world's best father".

## entry: Zhuoxin's blackmail
@keys: Zhuoxin
@keys2: Velimiru, Fatui, Ineffa, Northland Bank
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Zhuoxin is a Liyue merchant whose wife Aamu and daughter Adieta were with him near the Flower-Feather Clan. According to the Fatui he owed money to the Northland Bank and was forced to bring Ineffa to Natlan, and then to the temple, under threat to his family. In Searing, Velimiru handed him a replica control device to use against the Eleventh Lord, and when he refused, abandoned him.
### LARGE +
The Fatui offered to write off his debt and let him return to Liyue, and Fajeyi reminded him that the contract had a termination clause.

## entry: Sandrone's special-ops team
@keys: Sandrone's special-ops team, Marionette
@keys2: Velimiru, Fajeyi, Gulebu, Natlan
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Sandrone (called Marionette by her creation), a Fatui Harbinger, assigned this team to search for knowledge of ancient civilisations for an unrevealed purpose. When they proposed going to Natlan, she warned them of the danger and of the Traveler as an unpredictable variable, but did not forbid it. Velimiru repeats her phrase as he dies.

## entry: The Eleventh Sovereign Lord
@keys: Mirror of the Dawning Morn, Eleventh Lord, Eleventh Sovereign Lord
@keys2: Velimiru, Ch'upikaq Tajin
@version: 5.8
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Eleventh Lord, Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk, is one of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, dragon-era artificial intelligences. In Searing it appears in the temple's core as the Mirror of the Dawning Morn and crushes Fajeyi and Gulebu, badly wounding Velimiru. When it is damaged, Velimiru reignites it with his controller as a last gamble, and it speaks of burying everyone in the ruins. Citlali had earlier suspected Ineffa's core might be this Lord's; his scenes do not confirm that.
