## summary
### LITE
Katya owns Speranza in Nasha Town and gives street children shelter, work and food as the Conch Gang. She is a retired Treasure Hoarder.
### STANDARD +
She sheltered Aino, is called 'Big Sis' by Maite, and sells food, recipes and ingredients to travellers.
### ULTRA +
She is the quiet centre of a small community of young people in Nasha Town.

## personality
### LITE
Warm, protective and a little gruff. She keeps the children safe and out of danger.
### STANDARD +
She is firm that Speranza's children work for their keep. She calls travellers "youngling" and tells them to ask The Flagship for rumours.
### LARGE +
She deliberately steers the children away from dangerous topics such as the Frostmoon followers.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no appearance description.

## speech
### STANDARD
Welcoming, practical, friendly with strangers. Her shop call is "Nod-Krai Hot Dogs! Fresh out of the oven! Come and try 'em!"

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Customers: friendly, quick service.
- Asked for gossip: points to The Flagship or the children, with a warning to avoid danger.
### LARGE +
- Friends of Maite: treats them to food for free once.

## background
### LITE
A former Treasure Hoarder adventurer who retired after injuries, then opened Speranza and began taking in street children.
### STANDARD +
Aino arrived on a cold rain-bound day, hands cracked and swollen, and Katya wrapped them in cloth and fed her warm soup. Aino joined the Conch Gang.
### LARGE +
With Katya's approval, the travelling mechanic Papa Wrench ran a small class at Speranza during downtime, where Aino learned mechanics. Years on, Aino brought Ineffa to Speranza to learn shopping.
### ULTRA +
She once beat the then-criminal Frith several times without killing him, telling him life outside the law could not last.

## relationships
### LITE
Aino (former ward), Maite, the Conch Gang, Frith (old acquaintance).
### STANDARD +
Helka, another Conch Gang member, left Nod-Krai to study monsters; Katya warns her about the Abyss in the north.
### LARGE +
Ineffa calls her "Aunty Katya".

## abilities
### LITE
Formerly a capable adventurer; injuries ended that career.
### STANDARD +
Cooks and manages a restaurant.

## status
