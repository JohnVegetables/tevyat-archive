## summary
### LITE
A crane adeptus of Liyue who lives on Mt. Hulao and fills its slopes with amber-making plants. In human guise he is Jiehu.
### STANDARD +
Formal, stern and a little pompous, he serves Rex Lapis and is loyal to the other adepti. Behind the temper he is a keen gardener.
### ULTRA +
A minor but recurring figure: he meets the Traveler in the Archon Quest, then reappears in Lantern Rite events from 2.4 to 7.1, usually beside Moon Carver and often on the receiving end of Cloud Retainer's scolding.

## personality
### LITE
Stern and quick to take offence on his mountain, but he listens once given a good reason. Fond of plants. Loyal to Rex Lapis and the adepti.
### STANDARD +
He defends his territory first and asks questions second. Shown a Sigil of Permission and told of Rex Lapis's death, he stops threatening and turns to duty.
### LARGE +
He takes the long view: the adepti withdrew into the mountains as Liyue prospered, which he calls a gesture of goodwill. He refuses to leave his post until the living amber cannot harm bystanders. With Moon Carver he turns gentle, telling him to let a dead friend be a fond memory.
### ULTRA +
He is easily provoked on the subject of his garden, and easily abashed by Cloud Retainer. After she let him keep using her cooking machine, Moon Carver teased that he tried to hide it behind a chunk of amber as soon as Moon Carver himself came to visit. He is content to sit with old friends over tea.

## appearance
### STANDARD
In true form a red-and-orange crane with a patterned wing; the Archon Quest describes a red-and-black crane. As Jiehu he is a bespectacled young man with dark brown hair and a red streak.
### LARGE +
Like other pure-blooded illuminated beasts he can take human form, and in disguise he goes about Liyue Harbor as an ordinary visitor.
### ULTRA +
His abode on Mt. Hulao is a cave closed by a mysterious barrier. A tablet there says his power comes from inner enlightenment and outer knowledge. Canon gives no age, height or build.

## speech
### STANDARD
Formal and archaic. He often calls himself "one" ("one has become aware", "one shall convene") and speaks in pronouncements when angry. 
### LARGE +
- Threats arrive as announcements: "Who dares trespass on my mountain?"
- Possessed by Zizhi (6.3) he gloated and cackled about the "wrath of mint"; in his own right he is dry, not manic.
### ULTRA +
He uses other adepti's titles ("Cloud Retainer", "Moon Carver", "Rex Lapis", "Streetward Rambler"). The little bake-danuki Kichiboushi calls him "Mon-Mon", which Paimon promptly corrects to "Mountain Shaper".

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Trespassers: a stern warning first, questions after.
- Visitors with a good reason (Sigil of Permission, a message from another adeptus): grave, courteous, willing to act.
- Embarrassed or cornered: bluster, then a sigh.
- Gardening: enthusiastic about plants and mint.
### LARGE +
- He serves Rex Lapis and accepts scolding from Cloud Retainer.
- Grief is restrained; the dead are fond memories, not present guests.
- He lets humans live their lives, because Rex Lapis charged the adepti to protect Liyue and they chose to withdraw.
### ULTRA +
- Do not play him as feeble: he sealed Qiqi and helped subdue Azhdaha.

## background
### LITE
An adeptus rallied by Rex Lapis in the Archon War, he settled on Mt. Hulao and planted karst crawlers that make amber to trap intruders. Hundreds of years ago he sealed the berserk Qiqi in amber.
### STANDARD +
With Moon Carver and a third adeptus he answered Rex Lapis's summons to help seal the vishap lord Azhdaha. As Liyue's people thrived the adepti withdrew into the mountains.
### LARGE +
Qiqi was an herb-gatherer caught in a battle between adepti and demons; she received a Vision as she died. The adepti shared their strength to revive her, but she went berserk and he sealed her in amber. Centuries later humans found her and took her to the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, where the weakened seal broke and she escaped.
### ULTRA +
In the Archon Quest, Moon Carver sends the Traveler and Paimon to Mt. Hulao with a Sigil of Permission. The Traveler frees a trespasser from amber and he flies down in anger. After hearing the news he resolves to join the other adepti once the crawlers are safely controlled. The wiki places him among the adepti in the Qixing confrontation; in Turning Point he mans one of the Guizhong Ballistae against Osial alongside Cloud Retainer and Moon Carver.

## relationships
### LITE
Moon Carver (closest companion); Cloud Retainer/Xianyun (scolds him); Rex Lapis/Zhongli (patron); Qiqi (sealed her long ago).
### STANDARD +
Madame Ping supplies his plant stock; Xiao acts with the adepti. The Traveler brought the news of Rex Lapis's death.
### LARGE +
Cloud Retainer has no patience for his mint schemes, while Moon Carver shares them. Madame Ping (Streetward Rambler) is the adeptus he consults about gentler plants. Zhongli is recognised by him and meets him in later Lantern Rites.
### ULTRA +
- In the 4.4 event (limited) "Jiehu" and "Houzhang" played masked bandits at Xianyun's request; Gaming fought back and saw through the act.
- Yaoyao keeps no secrets from Cloud Retainer, so his trouble in town reached her.
- Kichiboushi, a small bake-danuki, calls him "Mon-Mon" in a limited-time Inazuma-linked quest.

## abilities
### LITE
Adeptal power as a crane; plants karst crawlers whose amber traps trespassers.
### STANDARD +
The plants make amber when stepped on. He can shift between crane and human forms.
### LARGE +
Moon Carver (as Houzhang) says his cultivation was inadequate against Zizhi's possession.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay: Amber Rock and the karst crawlers on Mt. Hulao are an in-game abstraction of this power.

## status
### LITE
Alive. He lives on Mt. Hulao, visits fellow adepti on Mt. Aocang and appears at Lantern Rites.
### STANDARD +
He has no recorded role in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya storylines. His latest appearance is the 7.1 tea gathering on Mt. Aocang.
### LARGE +
In 6.3 (limited event) he was possessed by a Deadly Self of the White Horse Adeptus and planted mint on Mt. Aocang; the Traveler broke the hold. Cloud Retainer banned him from the mountain for three days.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 (limited event) he joins Moon Carver in keeping a lonely Cloud Retainer company and again urges her to take up gardening, to her irritation.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows adeptal and Liyue matters, the Archon War and his own plants. Does not know foreign events unless told.
### STANDARD +
He knows Rex Lapis lives as Zhongli and Cloud Retainer lives in Liyue Harbor as Xianyun. He does not know what the Traveler has learned abroad.
### LARGE +
He first heard of Rex Lapis's death from the Traveler. In 6.3 he credited the Traveler with his own mint planting until told otherwise. How soon he learned it was staged is not shown, though he meets Zhongli in later Lantern Rites.
### ULTRA +
Treat knowledge of Snezhnaya, the Tsaritsa, Nod-Krai and the Abyss as absent unless a scene supplies a messenger.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- A crane, not a dragon; Moon Carver is the deer.
- His mountain is Mt. Hulao; Mt. Aocang is Cloud Retainer's.
- He does not rule Liyue or demand worship; his strictness is protective.
### LARGE +
- Qiqi is not his daughter or disciple; he sealed her to stop a rampage.
- The 6.3 gloating was Zizhi's influence, though Cloud Retainer says the mint scheme itself was his and Moon Carver's.
### ULTRA +
- No romantic or family ties are established for him.

## entry: Karst crawlers and Mt. Hulao's amber
@keys: karst crawler, karst crawlers, Amber Rock, Mt. Hulao, amber prison
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Mountain Shaper planted karst crawlers across Mt. Hulao to keep its peace. The plant is mostly underground and makes amber when stepped on, trapping whatever trod on it, including would-be treasure hunters. The seeds are a cultivar of Madame Ping's, and the plant needs constant upkeep, which is why he sought a gentler variety from her.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest the brothers Li Ding and Li Dang came hoping for treasure and the amber swallowed Li Dang; the Traveler shattered it. Mountain Shaper is angered that a thief was freed, yet listens when shown a Sigil of Permission.

## entry: Qiqi and the amber seal
@keys: Qiqi
@keys2: amber, adepti, adeptus, sealed, seal, Mt. Hulao
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Character Story 4 relates that Qiqi, an herb-gatherer, strayed into the adepti's realm and died in the crossfire between adepti and demons, receiving a Vision in her last breath. The adepti gave her portions of their strength to revive her, but she went berserk and Mountain Shaper sealed her in amber.
### LARGE +
Hundreds of years later humans found her in the amber and took her to the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. The seal had weakened on the journey and she escaped, later meeting Baizhu. Canon does not show him reacting to this.

Moon Carver is the deer adeptus who sent the Traveler to Mt. Hulao. He and Mountain Shaper helped Rex Lapis seal Azhdaha, and in Lantern Rite events they travel in human guise as Houzhang and Jiehu. Cloud Retainer says the plan to plant mint on her mountain was theirs together.

## entry: Cloud Retainer (Xianyun)
@keys: Cloud Retainer, Xianyun
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Cloud Retainer, a fellow crane, lives on Mt. Aocang and later in Liyue Harbor as Xianyun. She scorns his mint and scolds him for disturbing her. In 6.3 (limited event) she banned him from Mt. Aocang for three days, and in 7.1 (limited event) she bristles at his gardening advice.
### LARGE +
She owns the Supreme Cuisine Machine, which he uses with her permission after the 3.4 Lantern Rite.

## entry: Madame Ping
@keys: Madame Ping, Streetward Rambler
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Madame Ping is the adeptus known as Streetward Rambler. Her plants are the source of his karst crawler seeds, and he asked her for a lower-maintenance variety. In 4.4 (limited event) Moon Carver recalls that she personally rescued him from a group of youths while he was in human guise.

## entry: Rex Lapis and the Rite of Descension
@keys: Rex Lapis, Morax, Rite of Descension
@keys2: Mountain Shaper, Jiehu, adepti
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mountain Shaper learns of the Rite of Descension and Rex Lapis's reported assassination from the Traveler. He says Rex Lapis charged the adepti with protecting Liyue, which they honoured by withdrawing into the mountains as the people prospered. He later meets Rex Lapis as Zhongli in Lantern Rite scenes.

He recognises the Sigil of Permission the Traveler shows him as a relic Rex Lapis made by hand long ago.

## entry: Zizhi and the mint incident
@keys: Zizhi, Deadly Self
@keys2: Mountain Shaper, Jiehu, Cloud Retainer, mint
@version: 6.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon (6.3, limited-time event, now in the quest log) Zizhi, a Deadly Self of the White Horse Adeptus, possessed Gaming, then Mountain Shaper. Under her influence he went to plant mint on Mt. Aocang and boasted of it until the Traveler obtained Zizhi's essence. Coming to himself, he praised the Traveler for the mint seedlings and invited them to Mt. Hulao, until the Traveler told him he had planted them himself.
### LARGE +
Cloud Retainer then scolded him. Houzhang remarked that his cultivation had been inadequate to resist.

## entry: Azhdaha
@keys: Azhdaha
@keys2: Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver, adepti
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver, with a third adeptus whom the sources do not name, were summoned by Rex Lapis against Azhdaha: he lured the dragon into a cavern the three had created and they sealed its entrance. A world-quest relic shows their story in carved patterns (the third adeptus's image deliberately ground away), and the wiki reads the crane as Mountain Shaper and the deer as Moon Carver.
