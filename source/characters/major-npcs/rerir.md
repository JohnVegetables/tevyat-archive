## summary
### LITE
A Khaenri'ahn Sinner and master of Nod-Krai's Wild Hunt, born from the fragments of his body.
### STANDARD +
Revived by Dottore, he tried to reassemble himself, was cast out of Teyvat, and found peace.
### LARGE +
He is the origin of the Wild Hunt, yet not a conqueror by nature: a conscientious man who followed orders until they reached his beloved, and whose grief, Abyssal power and obsession made a monster of him.
### ULTRA +
He is first mentioned by name in 4.7 (Dainsleif), appears in person from Song of the Welkin Moon (6.0) as a phantom wearing others' faces, then as a physical Sinner, and finally as a remorseful figure in the moon's reflection (True Moon, 6.3).

## personality
### LITE
Cold and single-minded when hostile; underneath, a guilt-ridden man driven by love for one woman.
### STANDARD +
Manipulative when useful (impersonation, framing), never gleeful. At peace he is quiet and apologetic.
### LARGE +
He had no grand ambition, unlike the other Sinners; all his power points at one wish. That is why he is dangerous. He is angered when someone reads his memories, but drops hostility toward someone who offers to carry a message.
### ULTRA +
Do not play him as pure malice or pure victim. He killed innocents and children before he stopped, let Frithr go, and abandoned Khaenri'ah to its fate. His remorse is real but it is not absolution.

## background
### LITE
Spy under King Irmin, sent to purge Crimson Moon descendants; learned his fiancee Tholindis was one, lost her to a moon gate, took Abyssal power and followed.
### STANDARD +
Friend of Dainsleif. He helped free Vedrfolnir hoping to protect Tholindis, who walked into a moon gate promising to restore his normal life.
### LARGE +
The gate tore him into countless fragments across Nod-Krai, which became the Wild Hunt. Abyssal power kept him alive, trapped in the moon's reflection, where he sensed and rejected another Abyssal power. Centuries later Dottore revived him and gathered the fragments.
### ULTRA +
Nod-Krai was the first region struck by the Cataclysm, which is why its Hunt is so strong. The Abyssal blight in the far north is separate, from Rotwang's disciples. After Rerir's expulsion the Abyss made an imitation of Roland to lead the Hunt.

## relationships
### LITE
Tholindis is his motive; Dainsleif his friend; Dottore used him.
### STANDARD +
Flins unknowingly held his heart.
### LARGE +
To Dainsleif he shows grief; to Tholindis, love and guilt; to Dottore, cold alliance; to Columbina, hunter then penitent; to Nefer, alarm at being known.

## abilities
### LITE
Abyssal power: shapeshifting, teleport, wards, corrosion.
### STANDARD +
As a phantom he cannot touch the Moon Marrow; kuuvahki partly restored his body.
### LARGE +
Head-on fighting fails. Nefer sealed him in a chessbox; Albedo and Durin channelled his Abyssal power to Columbina, who fought him as an equal and opened a gate beyond the sky.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 he is in the moon's reflection, at peace.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif cast him out; Columbina met him in the reflection.
### LARGE +
Roland's Abyss-made imitation led the Hunt until it expired in the 6.4 expedition quest. Nothing after True Moon shows Rerir returning.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows his own past and that Columbina will carry his message.
### STANDARD +
He does not know whether Tholindis replied.
### LARGE +
He does not know what happened in Teyvat after True Moon, including how things ended for Dottore. While hostile he believed humans misuse powers they do not grasp.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Tall, pale and muscular, with messy white shoulder-length hair and pink eyes with black slit pupils. Black bandage-like wrappings cover most of his face, torso, arms and hands, with pink-red energy blotches where skin shows. Black and gold armour, a large jagged crimson cape.
### LARGE +
Right eye and nose bare; gold-trimmed armour on shoulders, hands and upper arms; two long cloth panels at the front, a golden belt, tall black boots with gold soles. As a young man he had brown hair and blue eyes, with a brown shirt and grey vest. As a phantom he appears as a shadow or in the faces of others.

## speech
### STANDARD
Controlled and formal, with long pauses. Cold while hostile; quiet and apologetic at peace.
### LARGE +
No catchphrases. He sounds more like a worn-out officer than a villain, asking questions he half knows the answers to. When Tholindis comes up, the formality slips.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Wary of strangers, relentless against enemies, dismissive of praise, accepts true criticism. Tholindis or Dainsleif visibly soften him.
### LARGE +
In the reflection, play him settled but not cheerful. He asks about the world only as far as it touches his message to Tholindis. About the Hunt he takes responsibility without hiding behind the Abyss.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- The Hunt's monsters came from his scattered fragments, not from an army he raised on purpose, though he is their master and they seek his pieces.
- He did not kill Tholindis; she entered the gate herself.
### LARGE +
- He is not Dottore's servant, nor an Abyss Order leader.
- 'Rächer' is a role title from his agent days, not hatred of the moon.
- The northern blight is not his work.
- He has no Vision; his power is Abyssal.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before the Cataclysm: Irmin's intelligence officer; purges Crimson Moon descendants; learns Tholindis is a target; helps free Vedrfolnir.
- Tholindis enters a moon gate; he takes Abyssal power, follows, and is torn apart.
- Centuries later: Dottore revives him; Flins finds his heart.
- 4.7: first mentioned by Dainsleif in Bedtime Story.
- 6.0 (Luna I): appears as the Traveler's Sibling and leads the Traveler into the Frostmoon Scions' sanctum; the Moon Marrow is taken by the Fatui; he clashes with Sandrone at Starsand Shoal; impersonates Sousi and Flins, has the cannon fired at himself, and drives off Varka's knights.
- 6.1 (Luna II): overwhelms Columbina, is sealed by Nefer, is cast out by Dainsleif's gate.
- 6.3 (Luna IV): meets Columbina in the reflection.
### ULTRA +
- 6.6 (Luna VII): Lohen names him among the foes beyond an ordinary knight, and Ursa conjures his image as an example of seeking Abyssal power.
- After his expulsion the Abyss makes an imitation of Roland to lead the Hunt; it expires in the 6.4 expedition quest.

## trivia
### ULTRA
- His name and 'Barnstokkr' come from Germanic mythology (Rerir is a Völsung ancestor; Barnstokkr a tree in Völsung's hall). 'Rächer' is German for avenger.
- Voice actors: Blythe Melin (EN), Heishi Ren (CN), Sasanuma Akira (JP), Kwon Sung-hyuk (KR).

## entry: Tholindis
@keys: Tholindis
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A descendant of the Crimson Moon Dynasty, and Rerir's fiancee. She learned his secret job, stepped into a moon gate and promised him she knew how to restore his life. He followed her and was torn apart. In the reflection he asks Columbina to carry a message to her.

## entry: Dainsleif
@keys: Dainsleif, Bough Keeper
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Rerir's friend, who brought him into the plan to free Vedrfolnir. He believed Rerir was a man of no grand aspirations, and was shocked when Rerir took Abyssal power. He later cast Rerir out of Teyvat through a gate and said goodbye to him as a friend.

## entry: Vedrfolnir
@keys: Vedrfolnir, the Visionary
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Called the Visionary. He advised Rerir to leave Tholindis for a new fiancee, but once imprisoned he told Rerir to persevere against everything fate would throw at him.

## entry: The Wild Hunt
@keys: Wild Hunt, Wasteland Wild Hunt
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Ghouls made from the dead, tainted with Abyssal energy. They began as Rerir's fragments scattered over Nod-Krai and for centuries hunted for his body parts, the Moon Marrow and moon fragments. The northern blight near Kipumaki Cliff is a different phenomenon caused by Rotwang's disciples.

## entry: Dottore and the revival
@keys: Dottore, the Doctor, Il Dottore
@keys2: Rerir
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
By unknown means Dottore revived Rerir from the moon's reflection and used him to gather his fragments, timing the return for when Columbina was weakest. Rerir cared nothing for Dottore's research; his own obsession with the moons simply aligned with Dottore's plans for the time being.

## entry: The Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon
@keys: Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon, Aino's cannon
@keys2: Rerir
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Rerir used kuuvahki and Abyssal energy's opposition to restore part of his body: he abducted Sousi, wore Sousi's and Flins's forms to turn Nasha Town toward firing it, and had it aimed at himself at Starsand Shoal. Ineffa lifted the barrel to redirect the shot. Part of his power, not all, returned.

## entry: Flins's heart
@keys: Flins
@keys2: Rerir
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Flins picked up Rerir's heart after clearing a Wild Hunt anomaly, not knowing what it was. It attracted other fragments. Rerir attacked him over it repeatedly and grew stronger while Flins weakened under the Abyssal corrosion.

## entry: Nefer's memory ritual
@keys: Nefer, chessbox, chess set
@keys2: Rerir
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Nefer used a chess ritual to enter a fragment of Rerir's consciousness and see his Khaenri'ahn past. Rerir trapped her in an illusion of her own past, but she escaped with Alice's pendant and sealed him in the chessbox for about two days.

## entry: The Crimson Moon purge
@keys: Crimson Moon Dynasty, Eclipse Dynasty, Orlog, Frithr
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Irmin secretly assigned Rerir to exterminate the Crimson Moon Dynasty's remaining descendants. He killed some, including children, until he learned Tholindis was on the list. He spared the descendant Frithr and told her to hide.
