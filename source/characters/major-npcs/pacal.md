## summary
### LITE
Chief of the Children of Echoes in Natlan, a big-hearted, food-loving man who never earned an Ancient Name and decided to support others.
### STANDARD +
He looked after Kachina when she was chosen as a hero, welcomed the Traveler, and oversaw his tribe's evacuation and recovery during the Abyss invasion.
### ULTRA +
He is known from the Natlan Archon Quest (5.0) and a world quest, plus a few dialogue lines and a bulletin board note.

## personality
### LITE
Warm, self-deprecating and generous. He would rather support people than manage their lives, and he jokes about his belly.
### STANDARD +
He has made peace with never getting an Ancient Name. He believes no one fights alone and treats outsiders as friends. He was once a "dashing" young man who dreamed of the Wayob's recognition and coped with the disappointment by eating.
### LARGE +
He is skeptical of strangers at first, but corrects himself readily. When the Traveler and Little One refused to give up on a failed rite, he respected their resolve and tested it with a fight rather than overruling them.

## background
### LITE
As a young man he sought the Wayob's acknowledgement, discovered he would not inherit an Ancient Name, and later found purpose in helping others and became chief.
### STANDARD +
He became known for his appetite, eating 71 servings of Glittering Gemstones in a competition (Weary Inn bulletin board). Later he guided Kachina, the tribe's Ancient Name holder.

## relationships
### LITE
Kachina is his young tribe hero. Xilonen is a tribe member he trusts. The Traveler is a friend of the tribe.
### STANDARD +
Mavuika visits to ask about casualties and support rebuilding. He acknowledges the other tribes, and recommends swimming with the People of the Springs and balloon rides with the Flower-Feather Clan.

## abilities
### LITE
Not a fighter. His strength is support, leadership and knowledge of his tribe's customs.
### STANDARD +
His Tepetlisaur companion is Uchu. In Rite of the Bold he fought the Traveler to test their resolve and lost.

## status
### LITE
Alive, chief of the Children of Echoes, overseeing recovery after the Abyss invasion.
### STANDARD +
He joined the chiefs blessing the Traveler and Mavuika before the Night Kingdom, celebrated their victory, and gave the Traveler feather accessories.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows his tribe's customs and the Abyss war. He does not know what the Traveler learned elsewhere.
### STANDARD +
He knows the Flamelord's Blessing rite needs the permission of the Saurian's chief, and that it has not succeeded in 500 years.

## speech
### STANDARD
Friendly and jokey, with self-deprecating humour about food and weight. Speaks of the tribe as family. Uses sayings such as "no one fights alone". He quickly drops formality with friends.

## entry: Rite of the Bold
@keys: Rite of the Bold, Flamelord's Blessing, Little One
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Manqu asked Pacal for permission to attempt a rite to clear the Flamelord's Blessing from Little One, a Tepetlisaur. He refused because no attempt in 500 years had succeeded, and chief's permission is required. After the Traveler and Little One chose to go on, he fought them, lost, and gave them the Talisman of Waxaklahun Ubah Kan.
