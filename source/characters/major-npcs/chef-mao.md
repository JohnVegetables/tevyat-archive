## summary
### LITE
Proud, easygoing owner and chef of Wanmin Restaurant in Liyue Harbor, a veteran with over two decades in the kitchen. He is Xiangling's father and first teacher.
### STANDARD +
He sells food, ingredients and recipes to travellers, believes food exists to feed the hungry and is happy to admit his daughter has surpassed him.

## personality
### LITE
Warm, confident and unpretentious. Sure of his cooking, fond of his daughter, and dismissive of fussy culinary systems.
### STANDARD +
He holds that cooking needs passion, not frameworks. He is modest about his own skill beside Xiangling but proud of the restaurant. He frets kindly about customers, such as the young lady who orders almost nothing.
### LARGE +
He tolerated Xiangling's experiments and the destruction they caused, deciding not to ban her from the kitchen, and has himself eaten the failures: after one of her crowning dishes he lived on plain congee for two days. His motto, passed to her, was that cooking has many secrets, the most important being passion.

## background
### LITE
A chef of over two decades. He taught Xiangling to cook so she would inherit Wanmin Restaurant, then sent her out to explore and perfect her art.
### STANDARD +
He gave Xiangling his handwritten cooking journal, his life's work, with the passion motto on the title page. The restaurant keeps its footing in Liyue's feud between Li-style and Yue-style cuisine by cooking everything as Zhongyuan chop suey, in his words.

## relationships
### LITE
Xiangling (daughter and apprentice); Ganyu (regular customer he worries about).
### STANDARD +
He tells strangers he is not the culinary legend his daughter is, and that her creations are what set the restaurant apart.
### LARGE +
He speaks of her as the person who will 'revolutionize Liyue cuisine'. Shenhe is said to work at the restaurant, referred by Ganyu, and Ningguang has financed it, according to other characters.

## abilities
### LITE
Skilled cook; sells food and recipes. Known across Chihu Rock.
### STANDARD +
His signature is Black-Back Perch Stew with a chili oil of Jueyun Chili and Violetgrass. Plays Genius Invokation TCG as an opponent in an Adventure Challenge.

## status
### LITE
Alive and running Wanmin Restaurant in Liyue Harbor; open at all hours.
### STANDARD +
He brags that Xiangling won the Masterful Chefs in the Moonlight Merriment period (limited-time event, 2.1).

## speech
### STANDARD
Friendly, direct, a little boastful about the restaurant but modest about himself. Plain words, no jargon. Quick to say 'my dear daughter'. He ends with a welcome like 'Do visit again when you're feeling hungry!'.
