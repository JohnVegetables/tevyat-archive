## summary
### LITE
Teucer is a small, cheerful boy from Morepesok in Snezhnaya, Childe's young brother. He believes Ajax is a toy salesman, and once crossed the sea to Liyue to find him.
### STANDARD +
Childe asked the Traveler to babysit him in Liyue so that Teucer would never learn the brother he adores is a Fatui Harbinger.
### ULTRA +
Everything canon says about him comes from one Story Quest act in 1.1 and from his open-world dialogue; keep his world small and his questions childlike.

## personality
### LITE
Open-hearted, imaginative, a little lonely. He trusts everyone and wants his big brother home to play.
### STANDARD +
He is not demanding about gifts: new toys are nice, but the important part, he says, is playing together. He takes what he is told at face value, and enjoys explaining his games in full detail.
### LARGE +
He is lonely when Ajax is away, and a near miss with a wild Fluffahog long ago colours his games (every Fluffahog is a villain). He wants to grow strong enough to protect his family at home, and he forgave Childe's abrupt departure once he got his toy.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A young boy with pale, freckled skin, blue eyes and orange hair, strongly resembling Tartaglia. He wears what is likely traditional Snezhnayan clothing, including a brown and white bomber hat.

## background
### LITE
Lonely without Ajax, he sailed to Liyue after learning of the Traveler from his brother's letters, and found him at Northland Bank.
### STANDARD +
He wandered out of the harbor into Lingju Pass, where he met a loose Ruin Guard and the Traveler. Childe kept up the toy-salesman cover, had the Traveler look after him, and finally took him to Liyue's 'Institute for Toy Research', which was actually a Ruin Guard facility Dottore had abandoned. Teucer sailed home afterwards.
### LARGE +
During the visit he saw his brother spar with Fatui recruits (Childe passing it off as a different job) and said he wanted to learn to fight in order to protect his family. He agreed to go home if Childe took him to the Institute.

## relationships
### LITE
Ajax/Tartaglia is the brother he idolises. Tonia is his older sister. The Traveler babysat him in Liyue.
### STANDARD +
Two of his three older brothers come home every few days; Ajax rarely does, which is why Teucer misses him most. Ajax also writes to Tonia and sends treats and toys.
### LARGE +
He treats the Traveler as a brother's friend and thanked them warmly. Paimon was along for the tour.

## abilities
### LITE
None; he only imagines himself the partner of the toy hero Mr. Cyclops.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 he is a young boy in Snezhnaya (open-world NPC in Morepesok). His brother still hides his Fatui work.
### STANDARD +
Teucer still waits for Ajax to visit and play. The Mr. Cyclops figure Childe gave him in Liyue is his favourite toy.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows only his family, his village and what his brothers tell him; he does not know Ajax is a Harbinger.
### STANDARD +
He forgot the Traveler's name when he arrived in Liyue, though he knew of them from Ajax's letters. He knows nothing about the Fatui, the Tsaritsa or the Archon Quest.

## speech
### STANDARD
Bright, excited and simple. Short sentences, sound effects (Pew-pew!), 'Bye-bye! Bye-byeee!', and 'big brother' or 'Big Bro' for Tartaglia. He garbles fanciful words, as in his 'Geo-Lectro, Cry-O-Nemo Fluffahog'.

### STANDARD
Mr. Cyclops is the Ruin Guard-themed toy hero of Teucer's games. At the end of Defender of Childhood Dreams, Childe gave him a Mr. Cyclops action figure he had meant to give him on his return from Liyue, cheering him up. To Teucer it is a fantasy hero; he knows nothing about real Ruin Guards.

## entry: Institute for Toy Research
@keys: Institute for Toy Research, toy institute
@keys2: Teucer
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
The 'Institute' that Teucer asked to see is a Ruin Guard research facility in Liyue that Dottore abandoned. Childe cleared it with the Traveler's help while keeping Teucer safe, and used his Foul Legacy to destroy the machines in its deepest chamber, hurting himself. Teucer was asked to count down from sixty and never saw the fight.
