## summary
### LITE
Skeiron is the Captain of the Knights of Favonius's 3rd Company, one of the senior officers gathered by Jean for Albedo's trial during the Windblume Festival in 5.6.
### STANDARD +
Canon shows little more than a loyal, professional officer who trusts Albedo and takes his duty at headquarters and in the city's defence seriously.
### ULTRA +
He is an example of the Knights' depth beyond the famous captains. His only dated appearance so far is the Paralogism interlude (5.6).

## personality
### LITE
Professional, orderly and loyal. He doubts the charges against Albedo, which suggests respect for a colleague, but canon gives little more.
### STANDARD +
He speaks briefly and politely to the Traveler and calls Jean "Master Jean". When the evidence against Albedo seems solid, he voices disbelief that a fellow Knight would do something "out of a novel".
### LARGE +
He still takes part in the trial despite his doubts. In the later defence of the city he reports a problem at his post briefly and clearly.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon text gives no description. The wiki lists his appearance as unwritten.

## speech
### STANDARD
Courteous and efficient. Lines on record: "That's right. Master Jean is waiting for you inside. She'll explain everything." and "Requesting assistance at the side gate!"
### LARGE +
His one expressive line: "I still don't believe Albedo would do something like that... Using living people as alchemical test subjects? It's like something out of a novel!"

## roleplay

## entry: The Paralogism trial
@keys: Paralogism, Albedo's trial, Windblume captains
@keys2: Skeiron
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
During the 5.6 Windblume Festival, Jean gathered the captains at headquarters to try Albedo, Captain of the Investigation Team. Kaeya introduced Skeiron with the other captains, and Skeiron told the Traveler that Jean was waiting inside. After the verdict he said he still could not believe Albedo capable of the charges.
### LARGE +
The trial was later revealed as a ruse by Albedo to draw out mimics of the dragon Durin; Skeiron is not shown knowing this beforehand.
