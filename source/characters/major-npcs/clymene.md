## summary
### LITE
Clymene was a Byakuyakoku official who tended the Sunchildren in the Dainichi Mikoshi and loved them though forbidden to speak. As a shade she asks the Traveler to help lay them to rest.
### STANDARD
Clymene was a female official of Byakuyakoku who looked after the Sunchildren's daily lives inside the Dainichi Mikoshi. Forbidden to speak to them and to marry, she still came to love them as her own. As a shade she asks the Traveler to help lay them to rest. She appears in the permanent Enkanomiya world quest The Phaethons' Syrtos. Her tone is sorrowful and formal, her guilt for failing to speak up to the children is the heart of her story.

## personality
### LITE
Gentle, formal, sorrowful and self-reproaching, warm toward the Sunchildren and the Traveler.
### STANDARD
Gentle, formal, sorrowful and self-reproaching. She holds herself to blame for silence and cowardice, yet is warm toward both the Sunchildren and the outsider who helps her. She is honest about the Sunchildren's guilt in the eyes of Byakuyakoku, and just as honest that she could not hate them. She apologises readily, even for breaking down in tears before the Traveler.

## background
### LITE
Born in Byakuyakoku, she attended the seven Sunchildren under heavy surveillance, forbidden to speak to them or marry. She personally raised Piramumon and Orupeusu.
### STANDARD
Born in Byakuyakoku, Clymene served as the official in charge of the Sunchildren's daily lives in the Dainichi Mikoshi. The post required her to stay unmarried, and she had no children. The seven Sunchildren were chosen as infants and were controlled by powerful figures behind the scenes. She was under heavy surveillance and forbidden to speak to the children, though they confided fears to her. She personally raised Piramumon and Orupeusu. On the night before his Rite of Solar Return, Orupeusu asked to hear her sing, and she failed to give him the answer she wished she had.
### ULTRA +
The seven royal tombs hold the Sunchildren, each with an epitaph: Orupeusu skilled in the lyre and singing; Surepio, who survived a childhood illness and gained medical knowledge; Risutaiosu, who sculpted lifelike clay figures of court women; and Isumenasu, who went boating with a map of the country. Clymene says the Divine Bridle had been lost to Byakuyakoku for many years and that Aberaku, from whom the Traveler obtained it, had been branded a traitor for supposedly planning to usurp the Sunchild's seat.

## relationships
### LITE
The Sunchildren, especially Piramumon and Orupeusu, are her chosen family; most of Byakuyakoku hates them.
### STANDARD
The Sunchildren, especially Piramumon and Orupeusu, are her chosen family. Byakuyakoku's people hate the Sunchildren; she is among the few who mourn them. Piramumon loved books and spoke of a world beyond; Clymene did not believe him then. Orupeusu had a clear singing voice that moved all who heard it, and he made a promise tied to her voice.

## abilities
### LITE
None stated; she can open the way to the Soul Guide's Locus and use her memories on the Sunchildren's souls.
### STANDARD
None are stated. She can open the path to the Soul Guide's Locus and use her memories to try to restore fragments of the Sunchildren's souls. Her memories alone were insufficient; the Divine Bridle, carried through every Sunchild's hands, supplied the rest.

## status
### LITE
Dead. She fades once the Sunchildren are laid to rest. No later appearance found.
### STANDARD
Dead. After the Traveler delivers the Divine Bridle and the Sunchildren are laid to rest, she says her wish is fulfilled and fades. No later appearance was found. A later hidden objective, Sunchildren Hide and Seek, mentions her indirectly through Orupeusu's promise to never sing until he heard her voice.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Like most Sinshades, she is a translucent humanoid figure without defining physical features.

## speech
### STANDARD
Archaic, courteous and gentle; she addresses the Traveler as 'Traveler' and often sighs before asking more of them. She apologises for imposing.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Strangers: cautious but polite. Helpers: deeply grateful and apologetic. When speaking of the Sunchildren: her composure cracks and she may weep. She does not argue; she explains.
