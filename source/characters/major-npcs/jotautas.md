## summary
### LITE
A Fatui Oprichnik stationed in Nod-Krai who first served Columbina, then Dottore, and tells the Traveler's party what he knows about the Doctor.
### STANDARD +
He is loyal to the Tsaritsa but has lost trust in Dottore. He appears in one scene in the 6.3 Archon Quest, fleeing the Doctor's research facility.

## personality
### LITE
Wary and tired, loyal to the Fatui's cause rather than to any one commander. He speaks reluctantly, then candidly once he is persuaded.
### STANDARD +
Honest about his unease and quick to deflect to institutional loyalty ("my loyalty will always belong to the Tsaritsa"). He admires strong, direct leaders such as the Captain, and he finds Dottore's coldness unsettling.
### LARGE +
He observes people more than he admits: his picture of Columbina as detached and always looking at the sky, and of Dottore watching her until she grew wary of him, is detailed. He will talk to enemies if the stakes are shared.

## background
### LITE
One of the Fatui sent to Nod-Krai in three units, under Dottore, Marionette (Sandrone) and the Damselette (Columbina). He belonged to the Damselette's.
### STANDARD +
When Columbina left, saying she had completed Her Majesty's request, her unit merged into Dottore's.
### LARGE +
Dottore later told them he would return to Snezhnaya, then reappeared sooner than expected with two gemstones.

## relationships
### LITE
Columbina, first commander; Dottore, current one, whom he distrusts; the Tsaritsa, to whom he swears loyalty.
### STANDARD +
He meets the Traveler's party as enemies and leaves as an informant. The Traveler suggests he seek refuge with Arlecchino's unit at the Design Bureau.

## abilities
### LITE
Ordinary Oprichnik training. He flees rather than fights; no Vision, weapon or combat record is given.
### ULTRA +
His only value to the story is as a witness: he knows the unit's structure, Columbina's habits and Dottore's stated priorities, not technical details.

## status
### LITE
After talking to the party he leaves the facility. Whether he reached the Design Bureau is not shown.
### STANDARD +
The Bureau is later engulfed by one of Dottore's energy fields. His fate is unknown as of 7.1.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows Dottore wants all three moons, or will make the third himself if he has two.
### LARGE +
He knows that Dottore brought two gemstone-like spheres back from his supposed trip home, and that two Harbingers had already gone into the facility. He does not know how the artificial moon would work.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Wears a Fatui Oprichniki uniform. Canon gives no other details.

## speech
### STANDARD
Clipped, tense, hesitating at first (ellipses, half-sentences), then more fluent once he decides to speak. Refers to superiors by Fatui titles: "The Doctor", "The Damselette", "The Captain", "Her Majesty". Sardonic about Dottore's chances of being stopped ("Good luck with that").

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Enemies: guarded and short-tempered at first; gives ground if the argument is about common stakes.
- The Doctor: dread and distrust, never open defiance.
- The Tsaritsa and Fatui: unwavering.
### LARGE +
- Columbina: respectful puzzlement rather than anger.
- If asked what to do next, he defers to others.
His testimony is from the perspective of a low-ranking soldier; avoid giving him strategic or magical knowledge he would not have.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Before 6.3: sent to Nod-Krai with the Damselette's unit; after she leaves, folded into Dottore's.
- 6.3, "Moonless Night": runs into the party inside the Doctor's research facility, shares what he knows and leaves.
- 6.3, next quest: the Design Bureau, to which he might have gone, has been engulfed by an energy field.

## entry: Columbina's Unit
@keys: Damselette's unit, Columbina's unit, three units
@keys2: Jotautas, Nod-Krai, Fatui
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Jotautas says three Fatui units were dispatched to Nod-Krai, led by the Doctor, Marionette and the Damselette. In his memory Columbina's unit was left alone, as she seemed detached from the world and rarely interacted with her subordinates. He recalls Dottore watching her at first, and the two getting along before she grew wary and avoided him. She left the Fatui, telling her unit she had finished what Her Majesty asked of her, and then the unit merged with Dottore's.
### LARGE +
This account is a subordinate's view and is not the full story of Columbina; see her own entry.

## entry: Dottore's Moon Plans
@keys: Dottore's plan, three moons, artificial Moon Marrow
@keys2: Jotautas, Doctor
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Jotautas tells the Traveler's group that Dottore is building something extraordinarily ambitious, that his top priority is obtaining the three moons, and that he has said he would create a third if he could get only two. The Moon Marrow spheres he carries were taken from Columbina, as Lauma notes. Nefer concludes he is close to completing an artificial Moon Marrow.
