## summary
### LITE
Anthon is one of Tartaglia's younger siblings, the second-youngest of the family: elder sister Tonia is above him and little brother Teucer below. He never appears on screen; he is known from Tartaglia's letters and from Tartaglia calling him and Tonia great kids.
### STANDARD +
His only detail in canon is a joke at his expense: he apparently believed Liyue's people were made of stone, and possibly that they ate stones, until Tartaglia wrote home to correct him.

## personality
### LITE
Not described in canon. The only hint is a child's literal-minded curiosity about a faraway land.
### STANDARD +
Tartaglia calls him and Tonia great kids of whom he is very proud. Anything beyond that is the writer's extrapolation: keep him a curious, well-loved child who takes his big brother's stories seriously.

## background
### LITE
He is part of Tartaglia's family in Snezhnaya, along with Tonia, Teucer, their parents and Tartaglia. Tartaglia left to serve the Tsaritsa and sends letters and gifts home.
### STANDARD +
While Tartaglia was stationed in Liyue, he sent home kites, a rattle drum, porcelain dolls from Inazuma and snacks, and asked Tonia to tell Anthon that Liyue's people are ordinary humans.

## relationships
### LITE
Tartaglia is his older brother, Tonia his elder sister, Teucer his younger brother.
### STANDARD +
Tartaglia's letters go to the family through Tonia. Tartaglia later invites the Traveler to his home to meet Tonia and Anthon.

### ULTRA +
Because canon gives him only these two lines, do not add more: his role is as a name in Tartaglia's family and a small joke about Liyue.

## entry: The Liyue stone misconception
@keys: Anthon stone, Liyue made of stone
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
In Tartaglia's letters home (character story), Tartaglia asks Tonia to tell Anthon that Liyue's residents are ordinary humans, not made of stone, and do not eat stones. The wiki reads this as meaning Anthon had believed both things. The line is a small joke and the only characterisation of Anthon in canon.

### STANDARD
Tartaglia has three younger siblings: Tonia (the elder sister), Anthon, and Teucer, the youngest. Near the end of Defender of Childhood Dreams Tartaglia invites the Traveler home to meet Tonia and Anthon, calling them great kids he is very proud of.
