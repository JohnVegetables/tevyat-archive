## summary
### LITE
A small yellow-brown Jack Frost who follows Linnea everywhere, eating shiny things and fighting with Geo hammers.
### STANDARD +
Most Jack Frosts are blue and sociable; Lumi's earthen looks got her bullied, so she left with Linnea. She communicates through variations of "Ya-ho".
### ULTRA +
She is in effect Linnea's family from the tundra: two outcasts who chose each other. Her appetite for minerals and shiny things is both comic and, in Linnea's story, a clue to her Geo nature.

## personality
### LITE
Playful, greedy for anything shiny, fearless of heat and cheerful. Affectionate with Linnea.
### STANDARD +
She is a glutton for gleaming objects and will swallow valuables, ores and Linnea's finds if not watched. She loves games with Aguara, and responds to the Moon Wheel with unusual quiet fascination. Lumi seems to understand human speech.
### LARGE +
Behind the silliness is an outcast's longing to belong: she once made a cardboard-box disguise, cut eyes into it and painted it blue to be accepted by other Jack Frosts. She left that disguise behind when she joined Linnea, though at least one Jack Frost seemed sad to see her go.

## background
### LITE
Lumi was bullied by other Jack Frosts for her earthen colouring until Linnea found her and invited her along.
### STANDARD +
In the Jack Frost Village, her fellows chased her from sleeping spots and stole her food. In desperation she made a blue-painted cardboard box disguise. Linnea, on a trip to the tundra, met this yellow Jack Frost, learned her situation and plucked up the courage to ask her to come along; Lumi agreed and left the disguise at her old home.
### LARGE +
Travelling with Linnea, Lumi turned out to tolerate heat without issue, even following her into the deserts of Sumeru to study the Wenut, and grinning while nearly falling into lava. On another trip she swallowed a rare fossil Linnea had scavenged from the seafloor. Linnea shook it out of her and found it was a Moon Wheel, which Lumi gazed at in fascination until Linnea kept it as an ornament.

## relationships
### LITE
Linnea is her friend; other Jack Frosts bullied her.
### STANDARD +
Linnea calls her too cute but warns people to guard their valuables. In the Spirit Envoy Championship Lumi befriended Aguara, the Traveler's Tepetlisaur Whelp companion. Linnea's worry is how Lumi will cope outside the tundra, which Lumi has answered.
### ULTRA +
Aedon, Illuga's nightingale, is scared of Linnea's enthusiasm; he has no stated relationship with Lumi. Linnea eventually concludes that Lumi needs another Jack Frost with her tastes for company.

## abilities
### LITE
Geo attacks with a pair of maces; tolerates heat unusually well.
### STANDARD +
In Linnea's kit she is summoned by Countermeasure: Lumi's Battle Cry! and Memo: Survival Guide in Extreme Conditions, striking with pounding hammers in several forms (gameplay). When idle she lives in Linnea's backpack. During the Championship she grew to gargantuan size and nearly crushed Celaeno and Haemus before Linnea stopped her.
### LARGE +
Linnea concludes her Geo power and quirks come from eating earth-like objects, a theory drawn from the energy accumulation. Jack Frosts in general can tolerate heat with some discomfort and withstand heavy compression.

## status
### LITE
With Linnea in Nod-Krai, still carrying on as her companion.
### STANDARD +
No change is recorded through 7.1. Linnea's plan to find another Jack Frost like her is unfulfilled.

### LITE
She knows Linnea, her backpack and the shiny things in the world; she cannot explain herself in words.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A smaller Jack Frost with no torso, but the usual round, fluffy body. Earthen yellow-brown rather than blue, with golden eyes, brown cheeks, a black face with two tooth-like points at her mouth, an orange-yellow hat with a star and a bow tie on her back. She floats, with two stubby black feet underneath. In Linnea's kit she can enlarge to a huge form.
### LARGE +
She is also shown in an enraged form and a travel-size form in the wiki infobox.

## speech

## entry: The Moon Wheel

@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
In Linnea's Character Story, Lumi swallowed what Linnea thought a fossil from the seabed. Linnea shook her upside down until it popped out, and it proved to be a glowing crystal called a Moon Wheel. Lumi stared at it with fascination and Linnea decided to keep it as an ornament.

## entry: Spirit Envoy Championship
@keys: Spirit Envoy Championship, Celaeno, Haemus
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Lumi entered the competition with Linnea. Against Celaeno and Haemus she grew huge and almost crushed them; Linnea stopped her at the last moment. Afterwards Linnea concluded that Lumi's quirks came from eating earthen objects and resolved to find a similar Jack Frost for her.
