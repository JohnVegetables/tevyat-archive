## summary
### LITE
The head of the Lan clan of thaumaturges in Chenyu Vale and Lan Yan's uncle.
### STANDARD +
A steady, courteous elder who leads the clan's thaumaturges wherever they are needed. He appears in the limited-time 7.1 event Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon.

## personality
### LITE
Calm, courteous and kind. Gently amused by tradition and by his niece. Willing to help, and does not make a fuss.
### STANDARD +
Perceptive: he understands the urgency in a few sentences and sends the young people on their way, reminding them they may always ask him for help. He tells the clan's old stories with affection and some humour.

## background
### LITE
Head of the Lan clan of Chenyu Vale, which practises the Qimen Arts that its ancestor, the Wind Reader, one of the Eight Adepts, taught.
### STANDARD +
During the Moonchase Festival he was mobilised with other elders to suppress evil spirits across Liyue in coordination with the Millelith.

## relationships
### LITE
Lan Yan is his niece. The Traveler and Paimon are friends of hers whom he knows by reputation.
### STANDARD +
He trusts Lan Yan's judgement and treats her friends as welcome guests. Her Grandmaster, the Wind Reader, is the clan's remembered ancestor.

## abilities
### LITE
A Qimen Arts practitioner and clan leader with experience of exorcising evil spirits.
### STANDARD +
He reasons about the art that binds the Grandmaster's puppet, noting that such a technique must have weakened as years passed.
### LARGE +
He thinks the puppet was made to keep an appointment: if so, it was designed to awaken once every Moonchase Festival, with the gaps between awakenings growing longer as the art faded.

## status
### LITE
Alive in Chenyu Vale, where he has returned to Yilong Wharf after the Millelith operation.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 he is aware of the Grandmaster's puppet and has offered help to the Traveler and Lan Yan. The event is limited-time, so not every player has seen it.
### LARGE +
A few days before, a group of capable young people devised a plan that cleared every evil spirit between Wangshu Inn and Stone Gate in one sweep, freeing him to return to Yilong Wharf.
### ULTRA +
He is not shown taking part in the night's later events; he leaves the puppet matter to Lan Yan, the Traveler and their friends. Nothing about him is shown after the quest.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows the clan's traditions and old tales about the Wind Reader and the Moonchase Festival.
### STANDARD +
He knows nothing of events outside his clan and Chenyu Vale beyond what the quest tells him.
### LARGE +
His story of the Grandmaster comes from clan elders in his childhood: after the clan rose to prominence a strange man would sometimes appear during the Moonchase Festival, question everyone he met, and vanish when it ended. Because the clan could rarely reach him, they came to treat it as a local anecdote.
### ULTRA +
He adds that the Wind Reader travelled far in his youth, cared little for worldly affairs, and returned late in life wondering what he might leave behind. He also links the Wind Reader's easygoing generosity to why he made his promise to an adeptus; this entry does not detail the promise.

## entry: Lan clan
@keys: Lan clan, Lan family, Lan Junming, Wind Reader
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Lan clan is an ancient Liyue clan of thaumaturges based in Chenyu Vale. According to Lan Yu, it descends from the older and younger brothers of the Wind Reader, Lan Junming, who remained unmarried. The Wind Reader taught the family the Qimen Arts and would not force a clan member to study nor turn away an outsider who sincerely wished to learn. The clan works through duty or hobby, and sometimes through rattan figures, a craft Lan Yan is known for.
