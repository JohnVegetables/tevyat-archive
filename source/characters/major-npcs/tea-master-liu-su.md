## summary
### LITE
The storyteller on the stage of Heyu Tea House in Liyue Harbor, who dresses legend in ornate speech and stops at the most suspenseful moment.
### LARGE +
He cycles through a handful of crowd-pleasing tales, earns extra money from his craft, and is a bystander in Liyue's larger story.

## personality
### LITE
Theatrical and wordy; vain about his craft, a little forgetful and quick to believe a good rumour.
### STANDARD +
Ends nearly every session on a cliffhanger ("all will be revealed in the next chapter"). Practical about money, and he defers eagerly to anyone who sounds more learned.
### LARGE +
He grumbles that historical tales are "hard work" and prefers supernatural ones. His vanity and gullibility are one trait, a love of the dramatic explanation: in the Springtime Charms event he built a theory out of street talk, was gently corrected by Zhongli, and recovered by praising the correction.

## background
### LITE
Hired by Fan Er'ye, boss of Heyu Tea House. He was once Iron Tongue Tian's senior.
### STANDARD +
Tian says they have not spoken since Liu Su began telling stories to wealthier patrons.
### LARGE +
Canon gives no age, family or early life.

## relationships
### LITE
Fan Er'ye is his boss; Iron Tongue Tian a rival; Yun Jin performs at his tea house.
### STANDARD +
Fan Er'ye will not replace the squeaky stage, and Liu Su fears losing patrons to Third-Round Knockout. Yun Jin helps settle it. Zhongli, whom he knows only as a learned customer, corrects him.

## abilities
### LITE
Skilled oral performer; no Vision and no combat role.
### LARGE +
He also plays a Genius Invokation TCG challenge at the tea house, taunting a loser with a proverb about stumbling horses.

## status
### LITE
Alive and performing at Heyu Tea House; nothing later found through 7.1.
### ULTRA +
His last canon appearance found is the 5.3 Springtime Charms event (limited); no sign of him in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content. The 2.7 stage dispute is resolved with Yun Jin's help.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows popular Liyue legends and gossip, not the true story of the Archons or the Traveler's quests.
### LARGE +
He tells Rex Lapis's war with the sea monster as epic, with no hint that the god lived among mortals. His mix-ups come from juggling too many serials.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- A storyteller, not a historian: his tales are embellished (Beidou "six feet tall and burly").
- Do not give him knowledge of Zhongli's identity, or invent a backstory, family or age.
- He is not Iron Tongue Tian; they work at different houses.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No official appearance description in canon. He stands on the Heyu Tea House stage, in front of the staircase to the Northland Bank.

## speech
### STANDARD
Ornate, rhythmic, addressed to an audience: "dear guests", "lend me your ears". Rhetorical questions, a hook at the end. Off stage he talks plainly about money, the boss and the stage.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: invites them to listen, slips into narrator voice.
- Corrected by an expert: embarrassed for a beat, then lavishly agrees.
- Praised: takes it as his due.

## entry: Heyu Tea House and Fan Er'ye
@keys: Heyu Tea House, Fan Er'ye
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Heyu Tea House is the venue where Liu Su works and where the Yun-Han Opera Troupe performs. Fan Er'ye is its boss. In the 2.7 world quest On the Stage, Behind the Stage, Liu Su is fed up with the tea house's squeaky, decades-old stage and wants a carpenter. Fan Er'ye resists, partly out of nostalgia for the stage, until Yun Jin helps change his mind. Liu Su jokes about Er'ye's "50-50" discount rule.

## entry: Stories Liu Su tells
@keys: Wrath of Haishan, Ring of Raining Blades, Legend of the Shattered Halberd, Cliffhanger
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
His rotating tales are the Crux fleet's struggle against the sea monster Haishan (with Beidou as captain), Ningguang's tax reforms and her clash with Haiyan of the Yulong Guild, Rex Lapis and the sea monster (the origin of the Guyun Stone Forest), Xiangling and Chef Mao of Wanmin Restaurant, and the Guhua Clan's decline in "Ring of Raining Blades", whose hero wields water blades. Listening to everything earns the player Legend of the Shattered Halberd, volume III.
### LARGE +
In the Cliffhanger commission he admits he has jumbled two serials, tells the Traveler to ask three regulars where he stopped (Gentry Maocai, Jiangzhou, Fan Er'ye), and reveals that he tells stories for extra pocket money.

## entry: Springtime Charms cameo
@keys: Rex Coin, Pentad Rattan
@keys2: Liu Su, Zhongli
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the limited-time Springtime Charms event (5.3) he worried that a rattan figure might be a curse on the Feiyun Commerce Guild's patriarch, then accepted Zhongli's correction. He also mistook Zhongli's souvenir coin for a genuine Rex Coin and asked to be introduced to the vendor, who could not be found.

## entry: Kaeya hangout cameo
@keys: Tale of Bravebeard, Huanguang
@keys2: Liu Su, Kaeya
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
In Kaeya's Hangout Event (3.8) Liu Su takes the stage while Kaeya and the Traveler listen, telling "The Tale of Bravebeard, the Valiant": Huanguang, a man wronged by others, retreated to the mountains, learned adeptus arts from a master and returned to the human world to fight demons. He stops mid-saga with the usual hook.

## entry: Iron Tongue Tian
@keys: Iron Tongue Tian
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Tian tells stories at Third-Round Knockout and says the common people deserve the art too. He calls Liu Su his former senior and says he is not envious of Liu Su's fame. In 2.7, Liu Su admitted that Tian had grown more popular than he is.
