## summary
### LITE
Hiiragi Chisato is the daughter of Hiiragi Shinsuke of the Kanjou Commission in Ritou and the sweetheart of Kujou Kamaji. After her father's imprisonment she became the household's leading figure.
### STANDARD
Hiiragi Chisato is the daughter of Hiiragi Shinsuke, head of the Hiiragi Clan that leads the Kanjou Commission in Ritou. She secretly loves Kujou Kamaji of the rival Kujou Clan. After her father's imprisonment she became the household's leading figure and took her duties more seriously. She is polite, earnest and still learning to run the estate. Her personal life is hemmed in by the political balance of the Tri-Commission.

## personality
### LITE
Courteous, sincere and shy about her feelings, principled about duty and generous with thanks.
### STANDARD
Courteous, sincere and cautious. She is shy about her feelings but principled about duty, and she thanks people generously. Earlier she was a worried young woman desperate to get a letter to her sweetheart. After the plot against her failed she grew serious and focused on practical reform, starting with simple matters and building trust with the retainers.

## background
### LITE
A Ritou native of the Hiiragi family, she asked the Traveler to deliver a secret letter to Kamaji against her father's wishes. After the Fatui scheme was uncovered, her father was jailed and she was left heading the household.
### STANDARD
Chisato grew up in Ritou in the Hiiragi family. Her father opposed her relationship with Kamaji, so she asked the Traveler to deliver her letter in exchange for help leaving Ritou. After the Fatui scheme was uncovered her father was imprisoned, and she was left heading the household. Some retainers sought to remove her by sending a false love letter that pushed marriage into the Kujou Clan, which would have absorbed her and ended her influence. The Traveler and the Yashiro Commissioner foiled it.

## relationships
### LITE
Her imprisoned father Shinsuke, her sweetheart Kamaji, who promised to wait for her, and the Traveler, whom she thanks with small gifts.
### STANDARD
Her father Shinsuke is imprisoned and she worries for him but accepts accountability. Kamaji is her sweetheart; they write rarely because of their clans' rivalry, and he promised to wait for her. The Traveler is a trusted helper she thanks with small gifts. She accepts the Yashiro Commissioner's help without resentment.

### STANDARD
No combat ability. She has administrative and social skills in running an estate and event preparations.
