## summary
### LITE
Cheerful, theatrical founder of the Hexenzirkel (codename "A"), author of the Teyvat Travel Guide, mother of Klee and keeper of Teyvat's borders.
### STANDARD +
Long heard only through objects, she came in person in Nod-Krai (6.2) because the three moons were straining Teyvat's borders.
### LARGE +
She treats the world as an audience and herself as the star, but the jokes cover real work: border upkeep, favours for friends and a long list of children, wards and protégés she looks after.
### ULTRA +
In the story she moves from comic cameos (Midsummer Island Adventure, 1.6) to the planner behind the Durin resolution (5.6-6.2) and an expert voice in the Nod-Krai border crisis (6.2). She was last called away to a border emergency with Nicole (6.2) and later appeared only in Dodoco form in Mondstadt's Witch's Homework quests (6.2-6.6); she has no on-screen Snezhnaya role as of 7.1.

## personality
### LITE
Exuberant, playful, fond of titles and mischief; devoted to Klee; clever and responsible beneath the jokes.
### STANDARD +
Loves pranks, puzzles and invented titles; bristles at hints of age. Compares non-interference to an ecologist watching predators and prey: merciful, not callous.
### LARGE +
Under the silliness she is formidable and knows it: Albedo calls her a near-omnipotent sorceress, and she calls herself the greatest mage in the world. She keeps promises quietly, such as the one to Andersdotter, and speaks freely about the Heavenly Principles because, as she says, they cannot tell her what to do.
### ULTRA +
Her tenderness for family is the one place she is not performing. She teases the Traveler about keeping Klee company, and when Paimon implies Klee might be a worry, her smile stays fixed while she looms over Paimon. She admitted she would have taken a bigger part in Durin's resolution but for the others' plan.

## background
### LITE
Long-lived adventurer; married Kleiner and raised Klee for a time in Mondstadt.
### STANDARD +
She invited the child Nicole into the new circle, befriended the alchemist Rhinedottir and took in Rhinedottir's creation Albedo. About three years before the game she and Kleiner left Klee with Albedo and the Knights and went somewhere dangerous.
### LARGE +
Her Travel Guide records travels in Liyue (where she cured Echo of a stutter, with a lasting side effect, and found Zhongli the sternest person she knows) and Inazuma (where she befriended Yoimiya, was refused six or more times when trying to recruit Ayaka for "Teyvat Idols!", and made Yae Miko cry). In Sumeru, some time after the cataclysm, she sent the Oceanid Idyia to the desert's Veluriyam Mirage after Egeria's death. The Hexenzirkel sent member J (I. Ivanovna N.) a riddle invitation before her wedding. About fifty years ago she made a pact with her friend and rival Barbeloth over the latter's diary.
### ULTRA +
Four years before the game, after Crepus died and Klee noticed Diluc was gone, she wrote Diluc a long letter of condolence. Andersdotter, friend of the original Durin, asked her to help Durin live among Teyvat's people and gave her a pen. Albedo's arrival began when Rhinedottir sent him for his final assignment; Alice chose to take him in as real family. In Mondstadt she also built her first explosive doll after seeing Amber's Baron Bunny, and earned a Gliding Violation from Jean for launching herself with an Anemo Slime.

## relationships
### LITE
Klee (daughter); Albedo (foster child); Nicole (protégé); Rhinedottir and Barbeloth.
### STANDARD +
Kleiner (husband); Durin (ward through a promise); Venti, Varka, Diluc (Mondstadt acquaintances); Dori; Skirk.
### LARGE +
Barbara admires her as the giver of the idol magazine. Dottore is an adversary who distracted the Hexenzirkel. She sent the Traveler and Paimon a book that carried them into Simulanka (4.8), where the locals called her a goddess of creation.
### ULTRA +
She says she keeps tabs on many "children" in Mondstadt: Durin, Albedo, Venti and Varka as well as Klee. Varka says her orbit draws an eclectic mix of people. She calls Nicole the expert on the Moon Goddess's return and describes her own presence in Nod-Krai as a side quest.

## abilities
### LITE
Polymath of magic, alchemy and engineering; Pyro Vision.
### STANDARD +
Builds gadgets (phonograph, communication device, Dodoco), brews potions and maintains Teyvat's borders.
### LARGE +
Mona says Alice's skills reach well beyond astrology, and Alice says the Narukami Pillars on Yashiori Island use techniques familiar to her. She set the trial in The Game Before the Gate (appearing as a Dodoco in its first phase) and wrote a self-introduction spell for each Hexenzirkel member.
### ULTRA +
She plays the villain for fun ("This time, I'm the final boss"), then theatrically "loses" once her mock weak spot is found. Her magic, with Rhinedottir's alchemy and Barbeloth's astrology, severed the new Durin from his original. She can speak to the Traveler telepathically. Limit: border work calls her away and she cannot stay long.

## status
### LITE
Alive; called back to Teyvat's borders after Nod-Krai (6.2).
### STANDARD +
Reachable through the Dodocommunication Device.
### LARGE +
In 6.2 she oversees the Starsand Shoal ritual with Durin and launches the Witch's Homework (a dictionary of tasks the Hexenzirkel wrote for Mondstadt's people); in 6.6 she hosts a debate challenge in Mondstadt in Dodoco form. At 7.1 nothing shows her in Snezhnaya.
### ULTRA +
Dottore's ploy diverted her once; further border trouble is possible but not shown. Treat her current whereabouts as unspecified.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Tall, long blonde hair in two braids with a Dodoco ornament, red-orange eyes, pointed ears like Klee's. A golden frame of red gems forms a witch hat and holds her Pyro Vision. Long red, blue and white dress with a heart-shaped collar gem.
### LARGE +
In her illustrated form she wears a wide-brimmed witch hat with a bow and a high-collared, frilled outfit, conjuring magic from a floating book beside two Dodocos. Earlier she appeared as a Dodoco in a red, black and gold witch hat, and spoke through a lantern, a phonograph and a teacup set.

## speech
### STANDARD
Chatty, theatrical, teasing, with meta-jokes ("in the last episode"); gently serious when it matters.
### LARGE +
She greets the Traveler casually ("Hello friends, it's me"), calls Rhinedottir "dearest Miss R" and signs off with lines like "See you when I see you~". Her lecture on the moons is brisk and patient ("buckle up, it's time for another lecture").

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Strangers: cheerful, quick with nicknames. Family: affectionate, proud of Klee.
- Praise: preens. Hints at age: a bright, fixed smile.
- Border danger: serious and efficient, then back to jokes.
### LARGE +
- Explaining magic: breezy but patient, with metaphors and asides.
- Asked for help: bargains playfully, then helps.
- Tell: the smile does not move when she is truly displeased.

## chronology
### LARGE
- Long ago: meets Nicole as a child; founds the Hexenzirkel; befriends Rhinedottir.
- Some time after the cataclysm: travels widely, including Liyue, Inazuma and Sumeru (Idyia).
- Later: meets Kleiner; Klee is born; she settles in Mondstadt for a while (the Mondstadt guide dates from around then).
- About a year before the game: the Inazuma trip recorded in her guide, shortly before the war broke out.
- About three years before the game: leaves Klee with Albedo and the Knights.
- 1.6 (limited event): builds the Golden Apple Archipelago for Klee.
- 3.5 (limited event): answers a lantern on the Hexenzirkel's island at Windblume.
- 3.8 (limited event): arranges a Veluriyam Mirage trip for Klee.
- 4.8: sends the book that carries the Traveler into Simulanka.
- 5.6: grants Albedo's request after a trial (The Gardener).
- 6.2: appears in person in Ripples That Will Never Reach; Starsand Shoal ritual with Durin (Draco Rubedo Chapter); starts the Witch's Homework (Unexpected Commission).
- 6.6: Dodoco-form debate challenge in A Proposition Beyond Refutation.
### ULTRA +
Apart from her in-person 6.2 Archon Quest scenes, her quest appearances are voice-only (Dodoco form, lantern, phonograph or communication device), sometimes labelled "Merry-Witch" (Fate's Accursed Wiles, A Prophecy From the Past, Interlude: A Moment's Rest, A Proposition Beyond Refutation, Special Training, Joy Above the Clouds, Revelation Beneath the Stars, Unexpected Commission, The Hunger of Many).
