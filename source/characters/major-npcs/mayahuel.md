## summary
### LITE
A doctor of the Masters of the Night-Wind and mother of Tepoz, who braves the Abyssal invasion to treat the wounded. She exists as an optional rescue.
### STANDARD +
Her story depends on a player choice in Version 5.1: rescued, she works at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame; otherwise she does not appear there.
### ULTRA +
She appears only in two 5.1 Archon Quests and in no later content read up to 7.1.

## personality
### LITE
Conscientious, anxious and stubbornly dutiful. She cannot sit back and do nothing while people are hurt, even at risk to herself.
### STANDARD +
She is practical about danger (she agrees to stay with warriors) and proud of her son's judgement. She works without rest.
### LARGE +
The Stadium staff worry she is overworking herself.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No official text describes her. Her remark that her age keeps her from the frontlines suggests she is no longer young.

## speech
### STANDARD
Warm, practical and a little tired; "Just doing my part." She asks about supplies and bargains for her share.
### LARGE +
She worries aloud, then moves on to what needs doing.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Patients and wounded: focused and calm.
- Her son: anxious, then proud.
- Strangers: grateful for help, quickly back to work.
### LARGE +
- She promises sensible precautions, such as travelling with warriors, but keeps going.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- She is not a fighter.
- Do not treat her death as canon; it depends on the player's choice.
- Do not invent a tribe role beyond doctor.

## background
### LITE
A doctor who travels the wilds treating wounded and evacuees. Her son Tepoz fights in the reinforcements.
### STANDARD +
She was surrounded by hilichurls while helping evacuees and was saved by the Traveler. If both she and the potion courier Awhina are rescued, Awhina's potions go to her patients.
### LARGE +
Guaman thanked her for coming a long way to help.

## relationships
### LITE
Tepoz (son), the Traveler and Paimon (rescuers), Awhina and Qhispe (co-workers).
### STANDARD +
Tepoz asked the Traveler to tell her to shelter; she promised to write and to travel with warriors. At the Stadium, Qhispe is grateful for her expertise.

## abilities
### LITE
Medicine; treats the wounded and distributes potions.

## status
### LITE
Branching: alive at the Stadium treating the wounded if rescued; otherwise she does not appear.
### STANDARD +
She has no word from her son but trusts him.
### LARGE +
The wiki states that she may die during Despair Engulfs the Heavens if not rescued; canon does not show this.

## knowledge
### LITE
Knows the invasion and her son's whereabouts; nothing later.

## chronology
### LARGE
- 5.1, The Surging Darkness: rescued from hilichurls (if the player chooses).
- 5.1, Despair Engulfs the Heavens: treating the wounded at the Stadium.
