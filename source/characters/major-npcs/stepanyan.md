## summary
### LITE
Stepanyan, codenamed Onyx Agate, is a disciplined Fatui officer of the 1st Grenadier Guards. He survived the Wild Hunt's attack on the Design Bureau in Nod-Krai and now commands a company in Glupov.
### STANDARD +
Courteous and even-tempered, he is the star student of Major Valeriy. He sees himself as an unremarkable soldier striving to be a dependable war machine.

## personality
### LITE
Polite, calm and respectful of authority. He prefers settling conflicts by talk but enjoys a fair challenge, and he is modest about his service.
### STANDARD +
He stresses willpower and the discipline of constant training. Obeying rank comes naturally, partly from Valeriy's influence. Even after a mishap such as being pelted with eggs, he reasoned with the offenders rather than lashing out. He has been angry only once in recent years: on learning that Sandrone had met with a mishap.
### LARGE +
Promotion has made him realise that command is a different skill from fighting: paperwork, scheduling and coordination now matter more than combat. He says he still prefers the front line, but wants to be capable in every respect.

## background
### LITE
A decorated soldier of the 1st Grenadier Guards, stationed in Nod-Krai at the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau as a poruchik in the 3rd Company. He later returned to Snezhnaya and became a company commander.
### STANDARD +
Under Sandrone's overall command in Nod-Krai, though never receiving a direct order from her, he fought when the Wild Hunt and Rerir assaulted the Design Bureau. His confirmed kills in that battle totalled 61, and though his weapon shattered and his legs were broken, he crawled to the rally point. Later he narrowly escaped an energy field cast by Dottore.
### LARGE +
In hospital he learned that Dottore had destroyed Sandrone's body, leaving only her mechanical core, which left him shocked and terrified. After recovery he was placed back under Major Valeriy, signalling the end of his Nod-Krai tour.
### ULTRA +
Before leaving Nod-Krai he entered the Technical Martial Challenge in Nasha Town, finishing as a finalist beside Gaumaru and the Diminished Seventh Arpeggio. He later became a company commander, and in 7.0 stands in Glupov, the Fatui's satellite city.

## relationships
### LITE
Major Valeriy is his respected superior and mentor. He admires Sandrone's Landcruisers.
### STANDARD +
He remembers fighting the Traveler and tells them he is nothing special.
### LARGE +
He mentions his comrades, whose envy he expects when he reports that he sparred with the Traveler.

## abilities
### LITE
A decorated front-line soldier. In the limited event he fights with a blade and Pyro and Electro attacks.
### STANDARD +
In the event he uses Pyro skills and Electro, setting up Overloaded. He credits technique and timing as much as strength.

## status
### LITE
Alive and commanding a company in Glupov as of Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Recently handed company affairs, he is still adjusting to administrative work.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knows military routine, Valeriy's teachings and what he saw of the Wild Hunt battle.
### STANDARD +
He knows the Traveler by reputation and has fought them; he does not claim to understand the larger events of Snezhnaya.

## appearance
### STANDARD
In the open world he looks like an ordinary Oprichniki Fireblade Shock Trooper. During the event he wore the inverted colour scheme of the Battle-Hardened Fireblade Shock Trooper. No further canon description exists.

## speech
### STANDARD
Formal, polite, restrained. He greets people with courtesy, quotes Valeriy's teachings. Before the event bout he told the Traveler, "You can beat me up until I end up on all fours, and that would suit me just fine."

## entry: Trials of the Brave Warrior

### STANDARD
This was a limited-time Version 6.6 event (June 2026) in Nasha Town. The Technical Martial Challenge was overseen by Sieciech, with Kaedehara Kazuha as a special guest judge. Onyx Agate, Gaumaru and the Diminished Seventh Arpeggio were three finalists locked in stalemate until the Traveler arrived. The Traveler tested each of them, and the player then chose the champion.
### LARGE +
Stepanyan said he would be happy to be thrashed, calling the bout bracing and noting that technique and timing matter as much as strength. If chosen champion, he deflected the honour, said the Bombardiers would gladly organise a similar event and promised to write a proposal to Valeriy.
