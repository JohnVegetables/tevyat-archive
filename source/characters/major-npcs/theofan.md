## summary
### LITE
Theofan was a Fatui delegate in Liyue, Yusupov's second-in-command, who gave Zhiyi poison and slipped away with the evidence.
### STANDARD +
He is only talked about, never seen. Yelan wanted to turn him into a pawn but the Harbinger Regrator had already got involved and he was gone.
### ULTRA +
His name matters for two facts: the Fatui's record of the Zhiyi affair was in his hands, and he supplied the poison Zhiyi used against Yusupov.

## personality
### LITE
Unknown. His actions show a cautious, opportunistic operator.
### STANDARD +
The wiki calls him ambitious toward Yusupov's post. Canon shows only that he helped a plot against his superior and escaped when it failed.

## background
### LITE
A Fatui delegate in Liyue, second to the Snezhnayan ambassador Yusupov.
### STANDARD +
Yusupov tried to place a Fatui pawn in the Liyue Qixing by having Zhiyi slowly poison Uncle Tian. Zhiyi, resenting the Fatui's hold, contacted Yusupov's associates and used poison from Theofan to kill Yusupov and bury the evidence.

## relationships
### LITE
Yusupov: his superior (the wiki says he coveted Yusupov's post). Zhiyi: the candidate he supplied with poison. Pantalone: the Harbinger who stepped in.
### STANDARD +
Yelan intended to turn him against the Fatui.
