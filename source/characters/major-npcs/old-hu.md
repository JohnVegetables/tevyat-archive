## summary
### LITE
The late 75th director of Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, Hu Tao's grandfather, remembered for his saying "Live in life, and die in death".
### STANDARD +
A big, muscular, plain-speaking man, he taught his granddaughter the parlor's arts, and is the one who steadied things after the Wuwang Hill disaster. He appears as a spirit at the border of life and death in a limited-time 5.3 event.

## personality
### LITE
Open, honest, stubborn and without regrets. He believed a funeral director must let go, and lived as he taught.
### STANDARD +
Warm toward his granddaughter, to whom he was always generous with praise: "Our little girl is the best." An old woman at the border once told Hu Tao her stubbornness was exactly like Old Hu's.
### LARGE +
He kept few belongings, in keeping with a tradition that directors burn their possessions on passing, leaving only his hat.

## background
### LITE
He directed Wangsheng Funeral Parlor as its 75th director, had a son, the 76th director, and a younger brother who left the family business.
### STANDARD +
He and his son went to Wuwang Hill to burn away a large accumulation of fetor with the Art of Homa. The son's lingering regrets made him falter and he died on the spot; Old Hu dealt with the aftermath, but the village was lost and the hill changed. He did not reach sixty.
### LARGE +
His younger brother, later Baizhu's master, left to practise medicine after a trip to Chenyu Vale and the brothers never spoke again. Old Hu died ten days before his funeral, which he had asked Hu Tao to conduct; at thirteen she arranged a grand ceremony.
### ULTRA +
After the funeral the thirteen-year-old slipped out at night with a few rations and travelled for two days to the border, where an old woman told her that no director of the parlor ever lingers there, and to go home. Hu Tao concluded that Old Hu had crossed at once, leaving no regrets, and found a Pyro Vision in her bag when she returned.

## relationships
### LITE
Hu Tao is his granddaughter. His son was the 76th director. Baizhu's master was his younger brother.
### STANDARD +
He taught Hu Tao the arts and told her she was gifted for mastering them at eight. After his death she travelled to the border to find his spirit and did not, concluding that he had crossed over with no regrets.

## abilities
### LITE
Master of the Wangsheng arts, including the Art of Homa, used to burn away fetor.
### STANDARD +
He contained the fetor at Wuwang Hill after his son's failure, though it cost him his health.

## status
### LITE
Dead. Appears only as a spirit.
### STANDARD +
In the 5.3 Springtime Charms event (limited), when Hu Tao was overcome by fetor and carried to the border, he appeared with her father; her father praised her work as director, and Old Hu said her time had not yet come before they faded away.

## knowledge
### LITE
He knew the parlor's secrets and teachings. As a spirit he shows little interest in current affairs.
### STANDARD +
He is shown knowing that Hu Tao is director; her father is the one who says the day of reunion will come in good time.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Not a villain or a ghost seeking vengeance; he is at peace.
- He is not the one who failed at Wuwang Hill; his son was.
- Do not invent his first name, his wife or lines beyond his known sayings.
### LARGE +
- He is not Baizhu's master; that is his younger brother.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Large and musclebound, with a head two sizes bigger than Hu Tao's. He wore the hat Hu Tao now wears, and has the same eyes she inherited.
### ULTRA +
The hat bears the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor's insignia and is hard-brimmed. Hu Tao spent a day and night altering it to fit her smaller head, and tells people it is a magical hat that upholds good and repels evil.

## speech
### STANDARD
Plain and affectionate. His known sayings: "Live in life, die in death. Follow your heart, do what you can." He speaks in short, proud sentences to Hu Tao.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
- Hu Tao or her friends: gentle, encouraging, tells them there is no hurry.
- Of death: calm, practical; letting go is a craft.

## entry: Wuwang Hill Disaster
@keys: Wuwang Hill, Wuwang Hill Disaster
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Over a decade before the Traveler's arrival, fetor leaked from the border and gathered at Wuwang Hill. Old Hu and his son went there to burn it away with the Art of Homa. The son, held back by attachments, faltered and was killed at once. Old Hu managed the fallout, but a nearby village was lost and the hill is still shrouded in fog and blue ghost lights. Details come from the 5.3 Springtime Charms quest, a limited-time event.
